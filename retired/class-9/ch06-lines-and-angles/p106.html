Example 4 · Single correct

A transversal cuts two parallel lines, making alternate interior angles of $(2x + 25)^\circ$ and $(3x - 5)^\circ$. What is each angle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $30^\circ$
  2. $95^\circ$
  3. $55^\circ$
  4. $85^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1Alternate interior angles are equal: $2x + 25 = 3x - 5$, so $x = 30$.
Step 2Each angle is $2 \times 30 + 25 = 85^\circ$.
Answer(d) $85^\circ$
Example 5 · Single correct

The angles of a triangle are in the ratio $3 : 5 : 7$. What is the largest?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $84^\circ$
  2. $36^\circ$
  3. $60^\circ$
  4. $105^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1$3 + 5 + 7 = 15$ parts make $180^\circ$, so one part is $12^\circ$.
Step 2The largest is $7 \times 12^\circ = 84^\circ$.
Answer(a) $84^\circ$
Example 6 · Single correct

An exterior angle of a triangle is $3x$, and the two interior opposite angles are $x$ and $40^\circ$. What is the exterior angle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $20^\circ$
  2. $60^\circ$
  3. $40^\circ$
  4. $120^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1An exterior angle equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles: $3x = x + 40$.
Step 2$x = 20$, so the exterior angle is $60^\circ$.
Answer(b) $60^\circ$
