Example 12 · Numerical answer

The angles of a triangle are $x$, $x + 10$ and $x + 20$ degrees. Find the largest, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$3x + 30 = 180$, so $x = 50$.
Step 2The angles are $50^\circ$, $60^\circ$ and $70^\circ$.
Answer70
Example 13 · Numerical answer

The supplement of an angle is $30^\circ$ more than twice its complement. Find the angle, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$180 - x = 2(90 - x) + 30 = 210 - 2x$.
Step 2$x = 30$. Check: the supplement is $150^\circ$ and twice the complement is $120^\circ$.
Answer30
Example 14 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) The complement of $35^\circ$(1) $35^\circ$
(Q) The supplement of $35^\circ$(2) $325^\circ$
(R) The reflex angle of $35^\circ$(3) $55^\circ$
(S) The angle vertically opposite $35^\circ$(4) $145^\circ$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1

Solution.

Step 1$90^\circ - 35^\circ = 55^\circ$ and $180^\circ - 35^\circ = 145^\circ$.
Step 2$360^\circ - 35^\circ = 325^\circ$, and vertically opposite angles are equal.
Answer(d) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
