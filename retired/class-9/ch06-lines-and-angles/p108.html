Example 9 · Multiple correct

In triangle $ABC$, side $BC$ is produced to $D$, $\angle ACD = 115^\circ$ and $\angle A : \angle B = 2 : 3$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $\angle A = 50^\circ$
  2. $\angle B = 69^\circ$
  3. $\angle ACB = 65^\circ$
  4. $\angle A + \angle B = 115^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1$\angle A + \angle B = \angle ACD = 115^\circ$ in 5 parts of $23^\circ$: $\angle A = 46^\circ$ and $\angle B = 69^\circ$.
Step 2$\angle ACB = 180^\circ - 115^\circ = 65^\circ$.
Answer(b), (c), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Which statements about a quadrilateral $ABCD$ are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its angles add to $360^\circ$.
  2. If $\angle A + \angle B = 180^\circ$, then $AD \parallel BC$.
  3. It can have four obtuse angles.
  4. It can have three right angles and one acute angle.

Solution.

Step 1A diagonal cuts it into two triangles: $2 \times 180^\circ = 360^\circ$. In (b), $AB$ is a transversal making co-interior angles that add to $180^\circ$.
Step 2Four obtuse angles add to more than $360^\circ$, and three right angles leave exactly $90^\circ$ for the fourth.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

Five angles around a point are $2x$, $3x$, $4x$, $5x$ and $6x$. Find the largest, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Angles around a point add to $360^\circ$: $20x = 360$, so $x = 18$.
Step 2The largest is $6 \times 18 = 108$.
Answer108
