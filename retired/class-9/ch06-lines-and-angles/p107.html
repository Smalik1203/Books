Example 7 · Multiple correct

A transversal cuts two parallel lines, and one of the eight angles is $40^\circ$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Four of the angles are $40^\circ$.
  2. Four of the angles are $140^\circ$.
  3. Each pair of co-interior angles adds to $180^\circ$.
  4. One of the angles is $50^\circ$.

Solution.

Step 1At each line the angles are $40^\circ$, $140^\circ$, $40^\circ$, $140^\circ$, and corresponding angles match, so four of each.
Step 2Co-interior angles are one of each: $40^\circ + 140^\circ = 180^\circ$. No angle is $50^\circ$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which facts are enough to show that two lines are parallel?

Select all correct options.

  1. A transversal makes equal alternate angles with them.
  2. A transversal makes co-interior angles of $110^\circ$ and $70^\circ$.
  3. A transversal makes corresponding angles of $65^\circ$ and $115^\circ$.
  4. Both lines are perpendicular to a third line.

Solution.

Step 1Equal alternate angles, co-interior angles adding to $180^\circ$, and two right angles as corresponding angles each prove the lines parallel.
Step 2Corresponding angles must be equal; $65^\circ \ne 115^\circ$.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
