Example 15 · Matching

Match the angles of each triangle with its largest angle. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Angles in the ratio $1 : 1 : 2$(1) $84^\circ$
(Q) Angles in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$(2) $60^\circ$
(R) Angles in the ratio $3 : 5 : 7$(3) $90^\circ$
(S) Angles in the ratio $1 : 1 : 1$(4) $80^\circ$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The largest is its share of $180^\circ$: $\frac{2}{4} \times 180^\circ = 90^\circ$ and $\frac{4}{9} \times 180^\circ = 80^\circ$.
Step 2$\frac{7}{15} \times 180^\circ = 84^\circ$ and $\frac{1}{3} \times 180^\circ = 60^\circ$.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
