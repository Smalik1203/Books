Example 1 · Single correct

The supplement of an angle is five times its complement. What is the angle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $67.5^\circ$
  2. $45^\circ$
  3. $22.5^\circ$
  4. $75^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1With the angle $x$: $180 - x = 5(90 - x) = 450 - 5x$.
Step 2$4x = 270$, so $x = 67.5$.
Answer(a) $67.5^\circ$
Example 2 · Single correct

Two angles are supplementary, and the larger is $38^\circ$ more than the smaller. What is the smaller?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $109^\circ$
  2. $71^\circ$
  3. $76^\circ$
  4. $52^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1$x + (x + 38) = 180$, so $2x = 142$.
Step 2$x = 71$; the angles are $71^\circ$ and $109^\circ$.
Answer(b) $71^\circ$
Example 3 · Single correct

Lines $AB$ and $CD$ cross at $O$, and $\angle AOC = 70^\circ$. The ray $OE$ bisects $\angle BOD$. What is $\angle AOE$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $35^\circ$
  2. $110^\circ$
  3. $145^\circ$
  4. $70^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1$\angle BOD = \angle AOC = 70^\circ$ (vertically opposite), so $\angle BOE = 35^\circ$.
Step 2$\angle AOE = 180^\circ - 35^\circ = 145^\circ$, since $AOB$ is a straight line.
Answer(c) $145^\circ$
