Example 11 · Numerical answer

Solve $\frac{x - 2}{3} + 1 = \frac{x}{2}$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Multiply every term by 6: $2(x - 2) + 6 = 3x$.
Step 2$2x + 2 = 3x$, so $x = 2$. Check: $0 + 1 = 1$.

Example 12 · Numerical answer

A show sells adult tickets at ₹50 and child tickets at ₹30. It sells 12 more child tickets than adult tickets and takes ₹2760 in all. How many adult tickets are sold?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1With $a$ adult tickets: $50a + 30(a + 12) = 2760$.
Step 2$80a + 360 = 2760$, so $80a = 2400$ and $a = 30$.
Answer30
Example 13 · Numerical answer

The angles of a triangle are $x$, $2x$ and $(3x - 30)$ degrees. Find the largest angle, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$x + 2x + 3x - 30 = 180$, so $6x = 210$ and $x = 35$.
Step 2The angles are $35^\circ$, $70^\circ$ and $75^\circ$.
