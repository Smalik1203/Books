What is the solution of $4(2x - 3) = 3(x + 6)$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 1.2
  2. 6
  3. 3
  4. 30

Solution.

Step 1Expand both sides: $8x - 12 = 3x + 18$.
Step 2$5x = 30$, so $x = 6$. Check: $4 \times 9 = 36$ and $3 \times 12 = 36$.
Answer(b) 6
Example 3 · Single correct

What is the solution of $\frac{x}{4} - \frac{x}{6} = 3$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 18
  2. 72
  3. 36
  4. 12

Solution.

Step 1Multiply both sides by 12, the LCM of 4 and 6: $3x - 2x = 36$.
Step 2$x = 36$. Check: $9 - 6 = 3$.
Answer(c) 36
Example 4 · Single correct

What is the solution of $\frac{3x + 1}{4} = \frac{x + 5}{2}$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 7
  2. 3
  3. 11
  4. 9

Solution.

Step 1Multiply both sides by 4: $3x + 1 = 2(x + 5) = 2x + 10$.
Step 2$x = 9$. Check: $\frac{28}{4} = 7$ and $\frac{14}{2} = 7$.
Answer(d) 9
