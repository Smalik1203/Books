Example 8 · Multiple correct

For which equations is $x = 3$ a solution?

Select all correct options.

  1. $3 - 2x = 15 - 6x$
  2. $3(x - 1) = 2x + 2$
  3. $4(x + 3) = 4x + 12$
  4. $5(x - 2) = 3x + 2(x - 4)$

Solution.

Step 1$3 - 6 = -3$ and $15 - 18 = -3$. (c) is true for every $x$, so for 3 as well.
Step 2$3 \times 2 = 6$ but $6 + 2 = 8$, so (b) fails at 3 (its solution is 5), and (d) has no solution at all.
Answer(a), (c)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

A mother is 26 years older than her son. In 4 years she will be three times as old as he will be. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The son is 9 years old now.
  2. The mother is 35 years old now.
  3. In 4 years the son will be 14.
  4. The equation is $s + 26 + 4 = 3s + 4$.

Solution.

Step 1With the son $s$ years old: $s + 30 = 3(s + 4) = 3s + 12$, so $2s = 18$ and $s = 9$; the mother is 35.
Step 2In 4 years the son is 13. The bracket in $3(s + 4)$ cannot be dropped, so (d) is wrong.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Consider $4(x + k) = 4x + 12$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. With $k = 3$ it is true for every $x$.
  2. With $k = 1$ it has no solution.
  3. With $k = 1$, $x = 2$ is a solution.
  4. With $k = 3$, $x = 100$ is a solution.

Solution.

Step 1$4(x + k) = 4x + 4k$ matches $4x + 12$ for every $x$ when $k = 3$; with $k = 1$, $4x + 4 = 4x + 12$ is never true.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
