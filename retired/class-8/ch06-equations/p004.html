Example 1

Solve $x + 7 = 12$.

What stands in the way is the $+7$. Undo it by subtracting $7$ — from both sides.

Solution.

Step 1 $x + 7 - 7 = 12 - 7$ $-7$ both sides
Step 2 $x = 5$
Check $5 + 7 = 12$ true
Answer $x = 5$

The middle line is written out here and will not be again. Once the habit is formed, it is enough to write what you did in the margin and give the result.

Example 2

Solve $7x + 4 = 25$.

Solution.

Step 1 $7x = 21$ $-4$ both sides
Step 2 $x = 3$ $\div 7$ both sides
Check $7(3) + 4 = 25$ true
Answer $x = 3$

Two things stood in the way and came off in reverse order — the $+4$ first, then the $\times 7$. To dress, socks then shoes; to undress, shoes then socks.

Negative numbers and fractions come out of the same steps without any special handling. There is no rule saying an answer has to be a whole number, and an answer that is not one is not a sign that something has gone wrong.
