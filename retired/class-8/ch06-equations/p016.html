1. Find the value of $k$ that makes $2x + k = 2x + 9$ true for every number. What happens for every other value of $k$?
  1. Find the value of $k$ for which $kx - 4 = 5x - 4$ has more than one solution.
  1. Write an equation of your own that has no solution, and one that every number solves. Say how you built each.
  1. A taxi firm charges ₹$50$ and then ₹$12$ a kilometre. A second charges ₹$80$ and then ₹$12$ a kilometre. For what distance do they cost the same? Explain your answer in words as well as symbols.
  1. A third firm charges ₹$20$ and then ₹$15$ a kilometre. For what distance does it cost the same as the first firm in the last question?
  1. Somebody solves $3x + 5 = 3x + 5$ and writes $x = 0$. Explain what they have misunderstood, and check whether $x = 0$ is a solution.
  1. Can a linear equation in one variable have exactly two solutions? Argue from the three cases of this section rather than by trying examples.
