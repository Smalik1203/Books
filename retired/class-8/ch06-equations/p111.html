Assertion and reason

In Questions 15 to 18, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $x = 0$ is a solution of $5x = 2x$.

    Reason (R): Both sides of an equation may always be divided by $x$.

  1. Assertion (A): Every number is a solution of $4(x - 1) = 4x - 4$.

    Reason (R): The two sides of the equation are the same expression written in two ways.

  1. Assertion (A): The equation $2x + 3 = 2x + 7$ has no solution.

    Reason (R): Adding the same number to both sides of an equation does not change its solution.

  1. Assertion (A): The solution of $\dfrac{x}{2} = 3$ is $x = 1.5$.

    Reason (R): A fraction is cleared by multiplying both sides by its denominator.
