Example 5 · Single correct

The sum of three consecutive odd numbers is 63. What is the largest of them?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 23
  2. 21
  3. 25
  4. 19

Solution.

Step 1Let the numbers be $n - 2$, $n$ and $n + 2$. Their sum is $3n = 63$, so $n = 21$.
Step 2The numbers are 19, 21 and 23.
Answer(a) 23
Example 6 · Single correct

For which values of $x$ is $5(x - 2) = 3x + 2(x - 4)$ true?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $x = 1$ only
  2. no value of $x$
  3. every value of $x$
  4. $x = 0$ only

Solution.

Step 1Both sides simplify: $5x - 10 = 5x - 8$.
Step 2Taking $5x$ from both sides leaves $-10 = -8$, which is false whatever $x$ is.
Answer(b) no value of $x$
