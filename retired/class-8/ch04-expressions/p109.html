Example 15 · Matching

List IList II
(P) $101^2$(1) 9999
(Q) $99^2$(2) 9996
(R) $101 \times 99$(3) 9801

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–3, R–1, S–4
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  3. P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2
  4. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2

Solution.

Step 1$(100 \pm 1)^2 = 10\,000 \pm 200 + 1$, and $(100 + a)(100 - a) = 10\,000 - a^2$.
Answer(d) P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
