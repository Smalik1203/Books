Example 12 · Numerical answer

If $x + y = 8$ and $x^2 + y^2 = 34$, find $(x - y)^2$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$2xy = (x + y)^2 - (x^2 + y^2) = 64 - 34 = 30$.
Step 2$(x - y)^2 = x^2 + y^2 - 2xy = 34 - 30 = 4$.
Answer4
Example 13 · Numerical answer

Simplify $3x(2x - y) - 2y(x - 4y) + xy$, and find its value when $x = 2$ and $y = 1$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$6x^2 - 3xy - 2xy + 8y^2 + xy = 6x^2 - 4xy + 8y^2$.
Step 2When $x = 2$, $y = 1$: $24 - 8 + 8 = 24$.
Answer24
