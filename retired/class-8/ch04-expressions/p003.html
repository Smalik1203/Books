$$\frac{5}{x} + 2, \qquad \sqrt{x} - 1, \qquad x^{-3}$$

are not, because $\dfrac{5}{x}$ is $5x^{-1}$, and $\sqrt{x}$ is $x^{1/2}$, and neither $-1$ nor $\tfrac12$ is a whole number. That condition is not just a piece of vocabulary. Everything in this chapter — collecting terms, multiplying out, the three identities — works because the powers are whole numbers and can be added by the index laws of Chapter 2.

4.2.2 Degree

The degree of a term is the total number of letters multiplied into it — that is, the sum of the powers of all its variables. The degree of a whole expression is the largest degree among its terms.

$5x^2y$ $x$ twice and $y$ oncedegree $3$
$-7xy$ $x$ once and $y$ oncedegree $2$
$4y$ $y$ oncedegree $1$
$-9$ no letters at alldegree $0$

So $5x^2y - 7xy + 4y - 9$ has degree $3$. A constant has degree $0$ rather than no degree, because $-9$ can be written $-9x^0$ — and $x^0 = 1$, which is exactly what Chapter 2 arrived at.
