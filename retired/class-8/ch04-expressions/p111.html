Long answer
  1. Show that $(a + b)^2 + (a - b)^2 = 2(a^2 + b^2)$. Use it to find $107^2 + 93^2$ without squaring either number.
  1. A square garden of side $x$ m is made $3$ m longer one way and $3$ m shorter the other. Is its area now more or less, and by how much? If the new area is $112$ m², find $x$.
  1. Show that $(2n + 1)^2 - (2n - 1)^2$ is always a multiple of $8$. Then find the two consecutive odd numbers whose squares differ by $96$.
