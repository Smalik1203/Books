Example 5

Simplify $2a(a - 3b) - 5b(2a - b)$.

Expand each bracket first, then gather.

Solution.

Step 1 $2a(a - 3b) = 2a^2 - 6ab$ the first bracket
Step 2 $-5b(2a - b) = -10ab + 5b^2$ every sign reversed
Step 3 $2a^2 - 6ab - 10ab + 5b^2$ put them together
Answer $2a^2 - 16ab + 5b^2$

The $-5b$ turned the $-b$ inside into $+5b^2$. Two negatives multiplied give a positive here for the same reason they do in arithmetic.

One case of this is worth a mention of its own, because it answers a question that sounds harder than it is: by how much does a product grow when one of its two numbers goes up by one? Writing it out settles it in a line.

one number up by 1 $a(b + 1) = ab + a$
both up by 1 $(a+1)(b+1) = ab + a + b + 1$

A product grows by the other number. So $23 \times 28$ is $23 \times 27$ with another $23$ added, and $24 \times 28$ is $23 \times 27$ plus $23$ plus $27$ plus $1$ — which is worth checking on the two products themselves, since $621 + 23 + 27 + 1$ is $672$.
