Example 3 · Single correct

A square field has a diagonal of 30 m. What is its area?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $450 \text{ m}^2$
  2. $900 \text{ m}^2$
  3. $225 \text{ m}^2$
  4. $600 \text{ m}^2$

Solution.

Step 1A square is a rhombus, so its area is half the product of its diagonals.
Step 2$\frac{1}{2} \times 30 \times 30 = 450 \text{ m}^2$.
Answer(a) $450 \text{ m}^2$
Example 4 · Single correct

A garden roller is a cylinder 1.4 m across and 2 m long. What area does it level in 25 full turns? Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Choose one correct option.

  1. $22 \text{ m}^2$
  2. $110 \text{ m}^2$
  3. $440 \text{ m}^2$
  4. $220 \text{ m}^2$

Solution.

Step 1One turn levels its curved surface: $2\pi r h = \frac{22}{7} \times 1.4 \times 2 = 8.8 \text{ m}^2$.
Step 2In 25 turns: $25 \times 8.8 = 220 \text{ m}^2$.
Answer(d) $220 \text{ m}^2$
Example 5 · Single correct

The curved surface of a cylinder is $660 \text{ cm}^2$ and its height is 15 cm. What is its radius? Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Choose one correct option.

  1. 7 cm
  2. 14 cm
  3. 3.5 cm
  4. 21 cm

Solution.

Step 1$2\pi r h = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times r \times 15 = 660$.
Step 2$r = 660 \times \frac{7}{660} = 7$ cm.
Answer(a) 7 cm
