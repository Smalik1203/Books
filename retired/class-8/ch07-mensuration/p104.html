$r = 3.5$, $h = 16$ $2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 3.5 \times 19.5 = 429$ cm2
$r = 7$, $h = 4$ $2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 11 = 484$ cm2
$r = 14$, $h = 1$ $2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 14 \times 15 = 1320$ cm2

The tallest of the three uses least, and the flattest more than three times as much for the same contents. Taller is not always better, though: a tin of radius $2$ cm would have to be $49$ cm tall and would need about $641$ cm2. The least metal goes on a tin that is neither very flat nor very thin. A tall thin packet is usually a decision about shelves rather than about metal.

Example 1 · Single correct

A trapezium has area $180 \text{ cm}^2$ and height 12 cm. One parallel side is 6 cm longer than the other. What is the longer parallel side?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 12 cm
  2. 15 cm
  3. 18 cm
  4. 24 cm

Solution.

Step 1Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times (a + b) \times 12 = 180$, so $a + b = 30$.
Step 2With $b = a + 6$: $2a + 6 = 30$, so $a = 12$ and $b = 18$.
Answer(c) 18 cm
Example 2 · Single correct

The diagonals of a rhombus are in the ratio $3 : 4$, and its area is $384 \text{ cm}^2$. What is the longer diagonal?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 24 cm
  2. 32 cm
  3. 16 cm
  4. 48 cm

Solution.

Step 1Let the diagonals be $3k$ and $4k$. Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times 3k \times 4k = 6k^2 = 384$.
Step 2$k^2 = 64$ and $k = 8$: the diagonals are 24 cm and 32 cm.
Answer(b) 32 cm
