Example 15 · Matching

A model water tank is made to a scale of $1 : 20$ and holds 2 litres. Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Times longer, each length(1) 400
(Q) Times larger, each area(2) 16000
(R) Times larger, the volume(3) 20
(S) Litres the real tank holds(4) 8000

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–3, R–4, S–2
  2. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Lengths grow 20 times, areas $20^2 = 400$ times and volumes $20^3 = 8000$ times, so 2 litres become $16\,000$.
Answer(b) P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
