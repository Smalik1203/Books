Example 9 · Multiple correct

A cylindrical water tank is 2.8 m across and 2 m high. Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its radius is 1.4 m.
  2. Its volume is $12.32 \text{ m}^3$.
  3. It holds 12 320 litres.
  4. Its curved surface is $17.6 \text{ m}^2$.

Solution.

Step 1$\pi r^2 h = \frac{22}{7} \times 1.4 \times 1.4 \times 2 = 12.32 \text{ m}^3$, and $1 \text{ m}^3$ is 1000 litres.
Step 2$2\pi r h = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 1.4 \times 2 = 17.6 \text{ m}^2$.
Answer(a), (b), (c), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

A field 40 m by 25 m receives 5 cm of rain. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $50 \text{ m}^3$ of water fell on it.
  2. That is $50\,000$ litres.
  3. That is 5000 litres.
  4. It fills a 5 m by 5 m tank 2 m deep.

Solution.

Step 1$40 \times 25 \times 0.05 = 50 \text{ m}^3 = 50\,000$ litres, just what a 5 m by 5 m by 2 m tank holds.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A container 30 cm by 25 cm by 20 cm is full of juice. How many 250 mL cartons can be filled from it?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Volume $= 30 \times 25 \times 20 = 15\,000 \text{ cm}^3 = 15\,000$ mL.
Step 2$15\,000 \div 250 = 60$ cartons.
Answer60
