Example 12 · Numerical answer

A metal pipe is 70 cm long, with inner radius 3 cm and outer radius 4 cm. Find the volume of metal, in $\text{cm}^3$. Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The metal is the outer cylinder less the hollow: $\pi \times (4^2 - 3^2) \times 70$.
Step 2$\frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 70 = 1540$.
Answer1540
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A wall is 5 m long and 3 m high. It has a door 1 m by 2 m and a window 1.5 m by 1 m. Find the area to be painted, in $\text{m}^2$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The wall is $5 \times 3 = 15 \text{ m}^2$; less the door and window, $15 - 2 - 1.5 = 11.5 \text{ m}^2$.
Answer11.5
