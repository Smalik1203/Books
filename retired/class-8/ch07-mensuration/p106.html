Example 6 · Single correct

Every edge of a cube is halved. What fraction of the old volume is the new volume?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{1}{2}$
  2. $\frac{1}{4}$
  3. $\frac{1}{8}$
  4. $\frac{1}{6}$

Solution.

Step 1Volume is edge $\times$ edge $\times$ edge, and each of the three is halved.
Step 2$\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8}$.
Answer(c) $\frac{1}{8}$
Example 7 · Multiple correct

A closed box measures 50 cm by 40 cm by 30 cm. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its volume is $60\,000 \text{ cm}^3$.
  2. Its total surface area is $9400 \text{ cm}^2$.
  3. The area of its four side faces is $5400 \text{ cm}^2$.
  4. It holds 6 litres.

Solution.

Step 1$50 \times 40 \times 30 = 60\,000$, and $2(50 \times 40 + 40 \times 30 + 50 \times 30) = 2 \times 4700 = 9400$.
Step 2The side faces: $2(50 + 40) \times 30 = 5400$. $60\,000 \text{ cm}^3$ is 60 litres, not 6.
Answer(a), (b), (c)

  2. Its volume becomes an eighth.
  3. New surface area: $972 \text{ cm}^2$.
  4. New volume: $1458 \text{ cm}^3$.

Solution.

Step 1The new side is 9 cm: surface $6 \times 81 = 486$, a quarter of $1944 \text{ cm}^2$, and volume $729$, an eighth of $5832 \text{ cm}^3$.
