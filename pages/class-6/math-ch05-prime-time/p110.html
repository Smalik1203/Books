Case-based questions

  1. For a school function, 84 chairs and 126 stools are to be arranged in rows. Every row must have the same number of seats, and a row holds only chairs or only stools.

    1. Write the prime factorisation of 84 and of 126.
    2. Which row lengths are possible?
    3. With rows of 14 seats, how many rows of chairs and how many rows of stools are there?
    4. Can the rows hold 4 seats each? Use the prime factorisations to explain.
