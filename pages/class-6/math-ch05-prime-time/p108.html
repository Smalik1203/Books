Assertion and reason

In Questions 13 to 16, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): 35 and 48 are co-prime.

    Reason (R): 35 and 48 are both composite numbers.

  1. Assertion (A): 7248 is divisible by 8.

    Reason (R): 248 is divisible by 8.

  1. Assertion (A): Every prime number is odd.

    Reason (R): 2 is a prime number.

  1. Assertion (A): The first common multiple of 15 and 28 is 420.

    Reason (R): 15 and 28 are co-prime.

  1. Write all the factors of 45.
  1. Write the first three common multiples of 8 and 12.
  1. Is 1 a prime number? Give a reason.
  1. Write the prime factorisation of 98.
  1. Find the smallest prime number that is greater than 50.
