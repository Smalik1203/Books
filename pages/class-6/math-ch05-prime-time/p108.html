Example 10

At a mela, a red light flashes every 4 seconds and a yellow light flashes every 6 seconds. Both lights flash together when they are switched on.

  1. The two lights next flash together after
    1. 10 seconds
    2. 12 seconds
    3. 24 seconds
    4. 2 seconds
  2. Numerical answer. In the first 60 seconds after they are switched on, how many times do the two lights flash together?
  3. Numerical answer. A blue light that flashes every 10 seconds is switched on with them. After how many seconds do all three first flash together again?

Solution.

Step 1Of the multiples of 6, the first that is also a multiple of 4 is 12.
Step 2So they flash together every 12 seconds: at 12, 24, 36, 48 and 60 seconds, which is 5 times.
Step 3Of the times 12, 24, 36, 48 and 60, the first that is a multiple of 10 is 60.
Answer(i) (b) 12 seconds; (ii) 5; (iii) 60
