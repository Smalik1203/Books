Co-prime art

Fig. 5.7 shows some thread art. Pegs are set evenly around a circle and numbered. A thread starts at the top peg and is tied to every 4th peg, going round until it comes back to the start. We say that the thread-gap is 4.

The first picture has 12 pegs and a thread-gap of 4. The second has 13 pegs and a thread-gap of 3. The third has 16 pegs and a thread-gap of 6, and the last has 24 pegs and a thread-gap of 6.

In some of the pictures, the thread is tied to every peg. In others, it is not. Is this connected to whether the number of pegs and the thread-gap are co-prime? Look closely at the pictures and talk about it in class.
