By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Find the common factors of 36 and 60.
  1. Write the first three common multiples of 8 and 12.
  1. Is 1 a prime number? Give a reason.
  1. Write the prime factorisation of 98.
  1. Find the smallest prime number that is greater than 50. Give a reason.
  1. Without dividing, find whether 7236 is divisible by 4 and whether it is divisible by 8. Give a reason for each.
  1. Are 45 and 64 co-prime? Justify your answer.
  1. The first common multiple of 4 and another number is 28. The other number is less than 10. Find it.
  1. Find the remainder when 3457 is divided by 5, and when it is divided by 2.
  1. Find the number of factors of 49. Is 49 a prime or a composite number?
Short answer
  1. Write the prime factorisations of 105 and 88, and use them to find whether the two numbers are co-prime.
  1. Find the largest 3-digit number that is divisible by both 4 and 5. Show how you found it.
  1. Use the test for 8 to find whether 2016 is divisible by 8. Then check your answer with the prime factorisation of 2016.
  1. Find all the common factors of 48 and 72. Hence find the largest jump size in Jump Jackpot that lands on both 48 and 72.
  1. Show that 8 and 225 are co-prime, and that $8 \times 225 = 1800$. Find one more pair of co-prime numbers, both greater than 1, whose product is 1800.
  1. Explain why the product of two different primes has exactly four factors. Use $3 \times 7 = 21$ as your example.
  1. A number lies between 50 and 100. It is a multiple of 3 and also a multiple of 4, and the sum of its digits is 9. Find the number.
  1. Find the smallest number that is a multiple of 6, of 9 and of 12. Also find the smallest such number that is greater than 100.
