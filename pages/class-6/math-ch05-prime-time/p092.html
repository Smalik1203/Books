Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): 7248 is divisible by 8.

    Reason (R): A number is divisible by 8 if the number made by its last three digits is divisible by 8.

  1. Assertion (A): 35 and 48 are co-prime.

    Reason (R): 35 and 48 are both composite numbers.

  1. Assertion (A): 91 is a prime number.

    Reason (R): A prime number has exactly two factors.

  1. Assertion (A): 2 is a prime number.

    Reason (R): Every prime number is odd.

  1. Assertion (A): 1 is neither a prime nor a composite number.

    Reason (R): 1 has only one factor.

Case-based questions

  1. On sports day, 48 girls and 60 boys are put into teams for a relay. Every team has the same number of girls and the same number of boys, and every child is in a team.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Write the prime factorisations of 48 and 60.
    2. Can there be 8 teams? Give a reason.
    3. Find the largest possible number of teams. How many children are there in each of these teams?

  1. Two buses leave a bus stand in Jaipur together at 6:00 am. The city bus leaves every 15 minutes, and the airport bus leaves every 20 minutes.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Write the first four times after 6:00 am when the city bus leaves.
    2. At what time do both buses next leave together?
    3. How many times do both buses leave together from 6:00 am to 10:00 am, counting both of these times?
