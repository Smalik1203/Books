1. Kabir writes the numbers from 51 to 70. He crosses out every multiple of 2, of 3, of 5 and of 7 in his list.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Is 57 crossed out? If so, by which prime?
    2. How many numbers are left, and what are they called?
    3. His friend crosses out the multiples of the same four primes in a much longer list. Find the smallest composite number that is not crossed out.

  1. A village fair is held every 3 years, and a cattle show is held every 4 years. Both were held in 2024.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. In which two years after 2024 is the fair held next?
    2. In which year are both held together next?
    3. How many times are both held together from 2025 to 2100?

  1. In a school library, a book whose number is divisible by 8 goes on shelf A. A book whose number is divisible by 4 but not by 8 goes on shelf B. Every other book goes on shelf C. Rani has books numbered 4520, 2316 and 7134.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. On which shelf does book 4520 go?
    2. On which shelf does book 2316 go?
    3. Rani changes the last digit of 7134 so that the book goes on shelf A. Find every digit she can use.
