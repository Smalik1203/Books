Now Grumpy makes the game harder. He puts two treasures on two different numbers. Jumpy must keep the same jump size all the way, and he wins only if he lands on both treasures. He still starts at 0.

Grumpy puts the treasures on 14 and 36, and Jumpy chooses 7. He lands on 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, … He reaches 14, but he jumps past 36. So he does not win. Which jump size should he have chosen? List the factors of both numbers.

Factors of 14: 1, 2, 7, 14

Factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36

Only 1 and 2 are in both lists. These are the jump sizes that land on 14 and also on 36.

Common factors

A number that is a factor of two numbers is a common factor of them. The common factors of 14 and 36 are 1 and 2.

In Jump Jackpot, the jump sizes that reach both treasures are the common factors of the two numbers.

Take care not to mix up the two ideas. The common multiples of two numbers go on for ever: the common multiples of 3 and 5 never stop. The common factors of two numbers are only a few, and none of them is bigger than the smaller number. The common factors of 14 and 36 are just 1 and 2. And 1 is a common factor of any two numbers, because 1 is a factor of every number.
