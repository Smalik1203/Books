Example 9

A prime puzzle has 2 rows and 2 columns of squares, and each square holds a prime. The top row has a product of 6, and the bottom row a product of 35. The left column has a product of 10, and the right column a product of 21.

  1. The prime in the top left square is
    1. 2
    2. 3
    3. 5
    4. 7
  2. Numerical answer. What is the product of all four primes in the grid?
  3. Numerical answer. What is the sum of all four primes in the grid?

Solution.

Step 1$6 = 2 \times 3$, so the top row holds 2 and 3. $10 = 2 \times 5$, so the left column holds 2 and 5.
Step 2The top left square is in both, so it holds 2. Then the top right holds 3, the bottom left 5, and the bottom right 7, because $35 = 5 \times 7$.
Step 3The product is $6 \times 35 = 210$, and the sum is $2 + 3 + 5 + 7 = 17$.
CheckThe right column is $3 \times 7 = 21$. The columns give $10 \times 21 = 210$, the same product.
Answer(i) (a) 2; (ii) 210; (iii) 17
