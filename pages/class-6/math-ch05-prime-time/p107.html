Why the other options are wrong

B1 Both statements are true. But being composite does not make two numbers co-prime: 4 and 6 are both composite, and they share the factor 2. What makes 35 and 48 co-prime is that $35 = 5 \times 7$ and $48 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3$ have no prime in common. So the answer is (b), not (a).
B4 The first common multiple of 15 and 28 is their product, 420, because they have no common factor to share. Anshu’s observation in Section 5.3 is exactly this, so R explains A and the answer is (a).
