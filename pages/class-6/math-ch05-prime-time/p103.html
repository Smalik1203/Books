Example 3

Find the common factors of 48 and 60.

Solution.

Step 1 Factors of 48: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 48
Step 2 Factors of 60: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15, 20, 30, 60
Step 3 the numbers in both lists are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

Example 4

Is 187 a prime number?

Solution.

Step 1 187 is odd 2 is not a factor
Step 2 187 does not end in 0 or 5 5 is not a factor
Step 3 $187 \div 3$ leaves 1, $\ 187 \div 7$ leaves 5 try the next primes
Step 4 $187 = 11 \times 17$
Answer No. 187 is composite

Ruling out 2 and 5 is not enough. A composite number can have larger primes inside it, and 187 has two of them.

Example 5

How many prime numbers are there between 60 and 80?

Solution.

Step 1 leave out the even numbers and the multiples of 5
Step 2 61, 63, 67, 69, 71, 73, 77, 79 are left
Step 3 $63 = 7 \times 9$, $\ 69 = 3 \times 23$, $\ 77 = 7 \times 11$ these three are composite
Answer 5, namely 61, 67, 71, 73 and 79
