Example 3

Which of these pairs are co-prime?

  1. 8 and 15
  2. 14 and 21
  3. 25 and 36
  4. 17 and 34

Solution.

Step 1$8 = 2 \times 2 \times 2$ and $15 = 3 \times 5$ share no prime.
Step 2$25 = 5 \times 5$ and $36 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3$ share no prime.
Step 3$14 = 2 \times 7$ and $21 = 3 \times 7$ share the prime 7. And $34 = 2 \times 17$ shares the prime 17 with 17.
Check8 has the factors 1, 2, 4 and 8, and 15 has 1, 3, 5 and 15. Their only common factor is 1.
