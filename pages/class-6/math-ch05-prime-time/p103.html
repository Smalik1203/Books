Which number has exactly three positive factors?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 21
  2. 25
  3. 27
  4. 35

Solution.

Step 125 has factors 1, 5 and 25.
Step 221 and 35 each have four positive factors; 27 has 1, 3, 9 and 27.

Example 4 · Single correct

The four-digit number 5a24 is divisible by 9. What digit is a?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 0
  2. 3
  3. 5
  4. 7

Solution.

Step 1Its digit sum is 5 + a + 2 + 4 = 11 + a.
Step 2For a digit from 0 to 9, the only multiple of 9 in this range is 18. Thus a = 7.
Answer(d) 7

Which is the smallest positive number divisible by both 15 and 28?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 210
  2. 420
  3. 840
  4. 43

Solution.

Step 115 = 3 × 5 and 28 = 2 × 2 × 7 share no prime factor.
Step 2Their least common multiple is 15 × 28 = 420.
Answer(b) 420
