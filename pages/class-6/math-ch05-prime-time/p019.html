Special numbers

Here are four numbers: 9, 16, 25 and 43. Which of them looks special to you? Why do you say so? Here is what some of Guna’s classmates said.

Karnawati: 9 is special, because it is a single-digit number. All the others have 2 digits.

Gurupreet: 9 is special, because it is the only multiple of 3.

Murugan: 16 is special, because it is the only even number. It is also the only multiple of 4.

Gopika: 25 is special, because it is the only multiple of 5.

Yadnyikee: 43 is special, because it is the only prime number.

Radha: 43 is special, because it is the only number that is not a square number.

All of them are right. A number can stand out from the others for many different reasons. Fig. 5.11 has four more boxes, each with four numbers in it.

A number can also be special in more than one way. Look at 43 again. It is the only prime, it is the only number that is not a square number, and it is the only number greater than 40. Which of these reasons did you think of first?
