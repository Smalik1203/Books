Jump Jackpot

Jumpy and Grumpy play a game on a number line. Grumpy puts a treasure on a number, such as 24. Jumpy chooses a jump size. He starts at 0 and jumps along the line, landing only on the multiples of his jump size. If he lands on the treasure, he wins it.

Suppose Jumpy chooses 4. He lands on 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 and then 24, so he gets the treasure (Fig. 5.2). Jump sizes of 2, 3, 6, 8 and 12 also land on 24. So do jump sizes of 1 and 24.

Each of 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 and 24 divides 24 exactly. You already know that these are the factors of 24. So a jump size lands on the treasure when it is a factor of the treasure’s number.
