Divisibility by 4

Divisibility by 4 can be checked quickly too. Look at the multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, … For 10, 5 and 2, the last digit was enough. Does the last digit work for 4? Try it with a few numbers before you read on.

It does not. Look at 12 and 22. They have the same last digit, but 12 is a multiple of 4 and 22 is not. In the same way, 14 and 24 have the same last digit, but 24 is a multiple of 4 and 14 is not. The same thing happens with 16 and 26, and with 18 and 28. So the last digit alone cannot tell us whether a number is a multiple of 4.

Would looking at more digits help? Make a list of the multiples of 4 from 1 to 200, and look for a pattern. Circle the ones between 100 and 200. Then compare each circled number with the number that is 100 less than it: 104 with 4, 112 with 12, and so on. What do you notice? Is 100 itself a multiple of 4?

Now find the numbers that are divisible by 4 between 330 and 340. Do the same between 1730 and 1740, and between 2030 and 2040. What do you see? Is 8536 divisible by 4? What about 8538?
