Type 5 · Divisibility tests

Example 12

Which of 7324, 6136, 5148 and 9420 is divisible by 8?

Solution.

Step 1 only the last three digits matter
Step 2 $324 = 8 \times 40 + 4$, $\ 148 = 8 \times 18 + 4$, $\ 420 = 8 \times 52 + 4$ each leaves 4
Step 3 $136 = 8 \times 17$
Answer 6136

All four end in two digits that make a multiple of 4 — 24, 36, 48 and 20 — so the test for 4 cannot separate them. A number divisible by 4 need not be divisible by 8.

Example 13

Is 3492 divisible by 4? Is it divisible by 8?

Solution.

Step 1 last two digits: $92 = 4 \times 23$ so 4 divides it
Step 2 last three digits: $492 = 8 \times 61 + 4$ so 8 does not
Answer Divisible by 4, not by 8
Example 14

Find the largest 4-digit number that is divisible by both 5 and 8.

Solution.

Step 1 a multiple of 8 is even, so it cannot end in 5; it must end in 0 the test for 5
Step 2 $999 = 8 \times 124 + 7$, so 9992 is the largest 4-digit multiple of 8 last three digits
Step 3 count down in 8s: 9992, 9984, 9976, 9968, 9960 stop at a 0
Answer 9960
