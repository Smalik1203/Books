Example 12 · Numerical answer

What is the least positive number that leaves remainder 1 when divided by 4, 6 and 9, and is greater than 1?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Subtracting 1 must give a positive common multiple of 4, 6 and 9.
Step 2Their least common multiple is 36, so the required number is 36 + 1 = 37.
Answer37
