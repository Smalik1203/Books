Divisibility by 8

Checking for divisibility by 8 can be made simple too. Can the last two digits be used again? Look at 112 and 212, which end in the same two digits. Is each of them a multiple of 8? Now find the numbers that are divisible by 8 between 120 and 140, between 1120 and 1140, and between 3120 and 3140. What do you see? Then change the last two digits of 8560 so that the new number is also a multiple of 8.

Think and Reflect

Do you agree with each statement? Why, or why not? Talk about it in class.

  1. Only the last three digits matter when you decide whether a number is divisible by 8.
  2. If the number made by the last three digits is divisible by 8, then the whole number is divisible by 8.
  3. If the whole number is divisible by 8, then the number made by its last three digits is divisible by 8.

So a long division is not always needed to check whether a number is a factor. By looking closely at multiples, we found quick tests for 10, 5, 2, 4 and 8. Are there quick tests for other numbers too? There are, for 3, 6, 7 and 9, and you will meet them in later classes.
