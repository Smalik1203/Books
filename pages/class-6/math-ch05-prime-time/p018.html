So a long division is not always needed to check whether a number is a factor. By looking closely at multiples, we found quick tests for 10, 5, 2, 4 and 8. Are there quick tests for other numbers too? There are, for 3, 6, 7 and 9, and you will meet them in later classes.

Divisibility tests
  • A number is divisible by 10 if its last digit is 0, by 5 if its last digit is 0 or 5, and by 2 if its last digit is 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8.
  • A number is divisible by 4 if its last two digits make a multiple of 4, and by 8 if its last three digits make a multiple of 8.
