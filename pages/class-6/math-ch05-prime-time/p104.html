  2. 12
  3. 45

Solution.

Step 1$10 = 2 \times 5$, $45 = 3 \times 3 \times 5$ and $18 = 2 \times 3 \times 3$ use only primes the number has.
Step 2$12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3$ needs two 2s, but the number has only one.

Example 5

How many factors does 72 have?

Solution.

Step 1Write 72 as a product of two numbers in every way: $1 \times 72$, $2 \times 36$, $3 \times 24$, $4 \times 18$, $6 \times 12$ and $8 \times 9$.
Step 25 and 7 are not factors of 72, and after $8 \times 9$ the pairs repeat. The 6 pairs give $6 \times 2 = 12$ factors.
Check$72 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3$, and every factor listed is made from some of these primes.
Answer12
