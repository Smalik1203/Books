Example 6 · Single correct

Two primes have sum 39. What is their product?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 74
  2. 76
  3. 114
  4. 152

Solution.

Step 1An odd sum of two primes requires one even prime, which must be 2.
Step 2The other is 39 − 2 = 37, also prime. Their product is 2 × 37 = 74.
Answer(a) 74
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which pairs are co-prime?

Select all correct options.

  1. 8 and 15
  2. 14 and 21
  3. 25 and 36
  4. 17 and 34

Solution.

Step 18 has only prime factor 2, while 15 has 3 and 5; they share none.
Step 225 has prime factor 5, while 36 has 2 and 3; they share none. The other pairs share 7 and 17 respectively.

Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which statements about 360 are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It is divisible by 8.
  2. It is divisible by 9.
  3. Its prime factorisation is 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5.
  4. It has exactly three positive factors.

Solution.

Step 1360 ÷ 8 = 45; its digit sum 9 also shows divisibility by 9.
Step 28 × 9 × 5 = 360 verifies the factorisation. Even 1, 2, 3 and 4 already give more than three factors.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
