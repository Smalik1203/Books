Problem 4

Which of these numbers is divisible by 8?

  1. 7324
  2. 6136
  3. 5148
  4. 9420
Solution

Only the last three digits matter. Check whether each number made by them is a multiple of 8.

$324 = 8 \times 40 + 4$

$136 = 8 \times 17$

$148 = 8 \times 18 + 4$

$420 = 8 \times 52 + 4$

The answer is 6136, option (b). All four numbers are divisible by 4, because 24, 36, 48 and 20 are multiples of 4. So the test for 4 cannot decide here. A number divisible by 4 need not be divisible by 8.

Problem 5

How many numbers from 1 to 100 are multiples of both 4 and 6?

  1. 4
  2. 8
  3. 16
  4. 41
Solution

Find the first common multiple of 4 and 6. Every common multiple is a multiple of it.

multiples of 6: 6, 12, …, and 12 is a multiple of 4

common multiples: 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72, 84, 96

$96 = 12 \times 8$, and $12 \times 9 = 108$ is past 100

The answer is 8, option (b). Option (a) counts only the multiples of $4 \times 6 = 24$, which misses 12, 36, 60 and 84. Option (c) counts all 16 multiples of 6. Option (d) adds the 25 multiples of 4 to the 16 multiples of 6.

Compare this with 4 and 5. They are co-prime, so their first common multiple is their product, $4 \times 5 = 20$. From 1 to 100, the numbers that are multiples of both 4 and 5 are 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100, which is 5 numbers. But 4 and 6 share the factor 2, so their first common multiple, 12, is less than their product, 24. Counting the multiples of the product works only when the two numbers are co-prime. For 4 and 6 it gives 4 numbers, which is half of the right answer. The number to count in steps of is the first common multiple, not the product.
