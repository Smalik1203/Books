Example 6

Find the smallest number greater than 1 that leaves a remainder of 1 when it is divided by 4, by 6 and by 9.

Solution.

Step 1Take 1 away from the number. What is left is a common multiple of 4, 6 and 9.
Step 2Of the multiples of 9, the first that is a multiple of 4 and of 6 is 36.
Step 3So the number is $36 + 1 = 37$.
Check$37 = 4 \times 9 + 1 = 6 \times 6 + 1 = 9 \times 4 + 1$
Answer37
