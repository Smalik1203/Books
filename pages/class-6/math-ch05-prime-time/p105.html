Example 9

Find the prime factorisation of $84 \times 50$ without multiplying first.

Solution.

Step 1 $84 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 7$
Step 2 $50 = 2 \times 5 \times 5$
Step 3 put the two lists together order does not matter
Answer $2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5 \times 5 \times 7$

Multiplying gives $84 \times 50 = 4200$, and the seven primes above multiply to 4200 as well.

Example 10

Which of these pairs is co-prime: 21 and 35, 39 and 91, 25 and 36, 33 and 55?

Solution.

Step 1 $21 = 3 \times 7$ and $35 = 5 \times 7$ both have 7
Step 2 $39 = 3 \times 13$ and $91 = 7 \times 13$ both have 13
Step 3 $25 = 5 \times 5$ and $36 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3$ no common prime
Step 4 $33 = 3 \times 11$ and $55 = 5 \times 11$ both have 11
Answer 25 and 36

91 looks like a prime and is not. Two composite numbers can still be co-prime, as 25 and 36 are.
