Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which statements are true for every positive integer n greater than 1?

Select all correct options.

  1. If n is prime, it has exactly two positive factors.
  2. If n is odd, it is prime.
  3. If n is divisible by 6, it is divisible by both 2 and 3.
  4. If n is composite, it has a factor other than 1 and itself.

Solution.

Step 1Exactly two positive factors defines a prime; having more defines a composite.
Step 2A multiple of 6 is a multiple of both 2 and 3. The odd number 9 is composite, disproving the second statement.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

For the numbers 18 and 30, which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Their greatest common factor is 6.
  2. Their least common multiple is 60.
  3. 90 is a common multiple.
  4. Their common positive factors are exactly 1, 2, 3 and 6.

Solution.

Step 118 = 2 × 3 × 3 and 30 = 2 × 3 × 5, so the common-factor part is 2 × 3 = 6.
Step 2The least common multiple is 2 × 3 × 3 × 5 = 90. The common factors are the factors of 6.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
