Case-based questions

  1. For a school function, 84 chairs and 126 stools are to be arranged in rows. Every row must have the same number of seats, and a row holds only chairs or only stools.

    1. Write the prime factorisation of 84 and of 126.
    2. Which row lengths are possible?
    3. What is the largest row length that can be used?
    4. With that row length, how many rows of chairs and how many rows of stools are there?

    2. After how many minutes do all three ring together again?
    3. At what time is that?
    4. Between 9:00 am and 10:00 am, how many more times do all three ring together?
