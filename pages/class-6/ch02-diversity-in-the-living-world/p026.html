2.3 Living Things and Their Surroundings

Compare your local records with records from another place. You may find both shared and different kinds. Differences in water, temperature, food and shelter can help explain the comparison.

Activity 2.9
1.

Make one shared class table on the blackboard using Table 2.6.

2.

As a class, fill it with plants and animals you have seen, read about, or heard about at home.

3.

Now look at each column. Ask: what is that place like? Hot or cold? Wet or dry? Windy? Is water easy to find there?
