Let Us Enhance Our Learning

1. Here are two seeds — a wheat grain and a kidney bean. Use what you know about these flowering plants to predict their root type and venation. State what evidence supports the prediction and how you could check it.

2. Sort these animals by habitat. Draw the diagram in your notebook. Put the aquatic ones in region A, the terrestrial ones in region B, and any that use both in the middle part, C.

Horse · Dolphin · Frog · Sheep · Crocodile · Squirrel · Whale · Earthworm · Pigeon · Tortoise

3. A radish is pulled out of a kitchen garden. Examine one. What kind of root is it? Now predict the venation of a radish leaf. Then check whether you were right.

4. A shopkeeper arranges her stock by price. A customer who wants a birthday gift finds this arrangement unhelpful. Neither of them is wrong. Explain why, using what you learnt about grouping.

5. Sort these animals into two groups, using any feature not used in this chapter — cow, cockroach, pigeon, bat, tortoise, whale, fish, grasshopper, lizard. Write your rule clearly.
