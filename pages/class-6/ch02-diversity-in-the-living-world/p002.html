Practise looking closely in the investigation below. Tables 2.1 and 2.2 show possible records; your own entries may be different.

Activity 2.1

You will need: a notebook, a pencil, a water bottle.

1.

Before the visit, copy the headings of Tables 2.1 and 2.2 into your notebook. Leave room for your own rows.

2.

With your teacher, spend one period in a garden, park or school ground. Record the place, date and time.

3.

Walk slowly, then stop at a safe place. Stand still and listen for thirty seconds. What different sounds can you hear?

4.

Add a row for each kind of plant or animal you observe. If you do not know its name, describe or sketch it. For example: "small plant, three leaves together, near the tap".

5.

Record only what you can establish. Write "not seen" for a feature you looked for but did not see. Do not pluck leaves or flowers or disturb animals.

Compare your records with a partner. Which living things were difficult to find or describe? What made them difficult?

Safety: Stay with your group. Do not touch any animal. Do not put your fingers into holes or under stones.
