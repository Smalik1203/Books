Investigate
1.

Go out to the same place as before. Choose six plants of clearly different sizes.

2.

Stand next to each one. Is it shorter than you, about your height, or taller?

3.

Look at the stem near the ground. Is it green or brown?

4.

Bend the stem very gently with one finger. Does it bend, or is it stiff? Stop as soon as you feel it resist. A broken stem can kill the plant.

5.

See where the branches start. Near the ground, or higher up?

6.

Record these features using the headings in Table 2.3. Group your plants by shared stem features and explain your rule.

Did height and stem features give you the same groups? Which plants were difficult to place?
