Herbs, Shrubs and Trees

Compare your records with the descriptions below. Your sample may not contain all three forms, and young or pruned plants may be harder to classify.

Compare stems and branching
TreesShrubsHerbs
Trees are woody plants that usually develop a main trunk supporting a crown of branches. Mango is an example. A young mango is still a tree even when it is shorter than a mature tomato plant. Shrubs are woody plants that usually branch close to the ground, often with several main stems. A rose bush is an example. Observe the branching as well as the height. Plants with soft, non-woody stems are called herbs. Many are small, such as mint, coriander and tomato, but height alone does not define a herb.
