Plants That Cannot Stand Up

Not every plant can hold itself up.

Compare growth habits
ClimbersCreepers
Some plants have stems that are too weak to stand. So they climb up something else — a wall, a fence, a stick, or another plant. These are called climbers. A money plant is a climber. So is a grape vine. Other plants also have weak stems. But instead of climbing, they spread flat along the ground. These are called creepers. Pumpkin and watermelon plants are creepers.

Climbing and trailing describe growth habits, not a separate woody/non-woody division. A grape vine is a woody climber; a pumpkin has non-woody trailing stems. A plant can therefore be described in more than one way.
