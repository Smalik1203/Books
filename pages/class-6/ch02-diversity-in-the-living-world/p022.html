Two Camels, Two Deserts

Compare the two kinds of camel in the illustration. Which visible differences could matter in cold weather?

Camels live in the hot sandy desert of Rajasthan. Camels also live in the cold, high, rocky desert of Ladakh. Both places are deserts. Both are dry, with little food and little shelter. But they are dry in very different ways. And the two camels are not the same.

Compare body form, feet, humps and coat
Rajasthan camelLadakh camel
  • The Rajasthan camel is tall, with long legs.
  • Its two-toed feet have broad, spreading pads. They spread its weight, so it does not sink into loose sand.
  • It has one hump.
  • Its coat is thin.
  • The Ladakh camel is shorter and heavier.
  • Its sturdy legs also end in broad, padded, two-toed feet.
  • It has two humps.
  • It grows a thick shaggy coat, especially at the head and neck, for winters that fall far below freezing.

You may have heard that a camel stores water in its hump. That is not true. The hump stores fat. The camel lives off that fat when there is no food. When a camel uses much of this stored fat, its humps can become smaller and less upright.
