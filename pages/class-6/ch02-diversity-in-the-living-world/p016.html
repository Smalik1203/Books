Under the Ground

Roots hold plants in place and take up water and dissolved minerals. You can observe their growth as seeds germinate.

Activity 2.5

You will need: a handful of chana (chickpea) seeds, a handful of wheat seeds, two shallow trays or plates, cotton cloth or cotton wool, water.

1.

Soak both kinds of seed in water overnight.

2.

Spread a wet cloth in each tray. Put the chana seeds in one tray. Put the wheat seeds in the other.

3.

Keep both trays in the same place. Keep the cloth damp, not flooded, and check it every morning for six days.

4.

From day three, inspect the seedlings daily. Record when each germinates and sketch the roots without tearing them.

5.

On day six, compare several seedlings of each kind. Keep any that germinated late and observe them for a few more days. Do not pull roots out of the cloth; examine them gently.

Draw what grew from each kind of seed in your notebook. Use the same scale for both drawings.

Could drawings alone tell you which plant is harder to pull out? What would you need to keep alike in a comparison of root anchorage?
