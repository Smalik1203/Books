Woody stems support branches and can thicken as a plant grows. Look at the base of a tree and then a herb: what differences can you describe without damaging either?

A banana plant is a large herb. Its trunk-like structure is a pseudostem, made from tightly overlapping leaf bases rather than a woody trunk. Its height does not make it a tree.

Plants That Cannot Stand Up

Not every plant can hold itself up.

Compare growth habits
ClimbersCreepers
  • Some plants have stems that are too weak to stand. So they climb up something else — a wall, a fence, a stick, or another plant. These are called climbers.
  • A money plant is a climber. So is a grape vine.
  • Other plants also have weak stems. But instead of climbing, they spread flat along the ground. These are called creepers.
  • Pumpkin and watermelon plants are creepers.

Climbing and trailing describe growth habits, not a separate woody/non-woody division. A grape vine is a woody climber; a pumpkin has non-woody trailing stems. A plant can therefore be described in more than one way. A climber and a creeper both have stems that need support or spread along the ground.
