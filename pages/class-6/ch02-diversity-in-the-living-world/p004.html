A moving animal may disappear before you finish recording it. Try watching from one place as well as walking slowly. Which method helped you find which animals?

Separate what you saw from what you infer. The crow was seen on a wall and in a tree; that does not tell us every place it lives. "Pecking at something" does not identify its food. What further observation could help?

The variety of living things found in one region is called its biodiversity.

Your school ground has its own biodiversity. So does a pond. So does a forest, a seashore, and even the soil in a flowerpot.
