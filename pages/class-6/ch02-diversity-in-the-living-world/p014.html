A rubbing is a record made by a method. A faint patch may mean the paper moved or the pencil missed it. Check that patch on the actual leaf before describing its veins. Keep an unclear print beside your improved one so you can explain why you changed the record.

Compare the vein pattern separately from the leaf outline. Two leaves can have different outlines yet share a vein pattern. Point to the lines that support your grouping; another group should be able to follow your rule without knowing the plant names.
