A rubbing is a record made by a method. A faint patch may mean the paper moved or the pencil missed it. Check that patch on the actual leaf before describing its veins. Keep an unclear print beside your improved one so you can explain why you changed the record.

Two Patterns

Compare your prints with these examples. Two common patterns are shown here; your sample may contain only one.

Compare vein patterns
Reticulate venationParallel venation
  • In reticulate venation, veins branch to form a net.
  • A thick middle vein is visible in the hibiscus leaf; finer veins branch and join around it.
  • Mango, neem and peepal also have net-like venation.
  • In other leaves, many veins run alongside one another. This pattern is called parallel venation.
  • In a grass blade they run along the length of the leaf. A banana leaf has a strong middle vein, with many parallel side veins running out towards its edges.
  • Parallel venation does not mean that a leaf has no middle vein.
