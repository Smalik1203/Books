The letters N and S are a useful record of the poles. If the letters rub off, the poles remain. You can identify them again by comparing the magnet with a marked magnet or by letting it turn freely.

4.3 Finding Directions

We can now go back to the sailors in the storm.

Investigate

You will need: a bar magnet, thread, a table or a stand, a sheet of paper.

1.

Tie the thread around the middle of the bar magnet.

2.

Hang it from the edge of a table, or from a stand. Move the thread a little until the magnet hangs level.

3.

Make sure nothing made of iron or steel is nearby. Move away steel chairs, keys and pencil boxes.

4.

Wait until the magnet stops turning on its own.

5.

On the floor below, mark the spot under each end of the magnet. Join the two marks with a straight line.

6.

Now give the magnet a gentle push so that it spins. Wait until it stops again.
