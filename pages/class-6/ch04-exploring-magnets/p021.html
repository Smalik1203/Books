A clip in water. Drop a steel paper clip into a glass of water. Get it out using a magnet, without wetting your fingers or the magnet.

Watch the clip as you slide the magnet up the outside of the glass. Keep the glass still and record where the clip follows and where it stops.

Two racing cars. Secure a bar magnet on each toy car with removable tape or an elastic band. Push them towards each other. Now turn one magnet around and try again. What changes?

For the racing cars, identify the two ends that face each other before you let the cars move. Then turn just one magnet around. This changes the facing poles while keeping the cars and their wheels the same.

Think It Through

In the maze and in the glass of water, the magnet never touches the thing it moves. Which activity in this chapter already explained that?

In each game, name the object that feels the magnetic force. The card, water or glass can lie between the magnet and that object. Moving the magnet farther away may make the effect too weak to move the object, even when the material between them has not changed.
