Think It Through

Are the filings spread evenly? Or do they collect in some places more than others? What does this tell you about the magnet underneath?

Compare the pattern before and after gently tapping the paper. Tapping helps grains move; it does not create poles. Keep the amount of filings and the paper position the same.

Two Ends, Two Poles

The filings do not spread evenly. They gather in thick bunches at the two ends of the bar magnet. In the middle, there are hardly any.

How It Works

The pattern suggests a stronger effect near the ends. It is not a force meter: the filings become temporarily magnetised, turn into alignment and attract one another into chains.
