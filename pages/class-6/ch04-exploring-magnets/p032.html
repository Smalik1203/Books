5. You are given three metal bars that look exactly alike. Two of them are magnets. One is a plain iron bar. You have nothing else to help you. How will you find out which one is the plain iron bar?

6. A magnet has no N or S marked on it. You also have a second magnet with its poles clearly marked. How will you use the second magnet to find the poles of the first?

7. Now suppose you have no second magnet at all. You have only a piece of thread. Can repeated suspension identify the North pole without an independent direction reference? State what extra information you need.

8. The north end of a compass needle is a North pole. It turns towards the Earth's north. But you learnt that like poles repel and unlike poles attract.
