How It Works

The round pencils reduce rubbing between magnet A and the table. Without them, the magnetic force might be present but too weak to overcome the friction. A magnet that does not move immediately has not necessarily stopped exerting a force.

Like Poles and Unlike Poles

Compare your observations with the pole markings.

So we can say it simply:
Unlike poles: attractionLike poles: repulsion
When the North pole of one magnet faces the South pole of another, the two are pulled together. This pulling together is called attraction. When two North poles face each other, they push apart. Two South poles do the same. This pushing apart is called repulsion.
Unlike poles attract. North and South pull together. Like poles repel. North and North push apart. South and South push apart.
