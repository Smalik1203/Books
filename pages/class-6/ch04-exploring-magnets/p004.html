Think It Through

Compare the two columns. Where were you right? Where were you wrong? Look only at the objects that stuck. What do those objects have in common?

Test a part of the object whose material you know. A pencil may have a metal ferrule, and a sharpener may have a steel blade inside a plastic body. If only one part sticks, record that part rather than calling the whole object magnetic.

An object that does not stick may simply be too far from the magnet. Bring the magnet close without forcing the object against it. If a result surprises you, repeat the test and compare it with a known steel paper clip.
