What Makes the Needle Work?

How It Works

The stroking magnet does not transfer a magnetic substance to the needle. Its field helps align small magnetic regions inside the steel. Some alignment remains after the magnet is removed. This is magnetisation.

Using one pole and repeating the same stroke encourages a consistent alignment. Thirty to forty strokes is a starting procedure, not a guaranteed threshold: the needle material and magnet strength affect the result.
