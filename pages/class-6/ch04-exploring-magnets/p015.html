Activity 4.4
1.

Before stroking, test whether the needle attracts a pin. Record the result in your notebook, then lay the needle flat on a wooden table.

2.

Hold one pole of the bar magnet at one end of the needle. Press lightly and slide it along the needle to the other end.

3.

At the end of the needle, lift the magnet right up and away.

4.

Bring the same pole back to the same starting end. Slide it along again in the same direction.

5.

Repeat this thirty to forty times. Always stroke in one direction only. Always lift the magnet clear at the end of each stroke.

6.

Put the bar magnet well away. Repeat the pin test and compare with your before-test. Attraction alone is not conclusive; check the floating needle next.

7.

Ask an adult to secure the needle flat on top of the cork with a small piece of tape, so it stays level.

8.

Float the cork in the bowl of water. The needle should stay above the water.

9.

Wait for it to stop turning.
