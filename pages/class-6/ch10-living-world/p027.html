Two influences, one growing plant

Roots generally grow in the direction of gravity. Shoots generally grow upwards and can bend towards light. These responses occur in growing parts while the plant stays rooted in one place.

Keep the comparison fair

A plant moved nearer the camera looks larger without having grown. Keep the pot, camera and ruler fixed. Record elapsed time and actual ruler readings; also trace the tip’s direction because height alone can miss growth along a curve.
