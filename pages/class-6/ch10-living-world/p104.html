Problem 1

Which observation gives the strongest evidence that a dry seed has germinated?

  1. It darkens when wet.
  2. It gains weight on soaking.
  3. A root emerges and grows.
  4. Its coat peels off.

Explanation

Answer: C. Germination involves renewed growth of the embryo. An emerging root that grows is evidence of that process. The other observations can result from water entering and softening a seed, even if the embryo is no longer alive.

A good answer identifies what is being tested. The question concerns germination, not simply whether water entered the seed.

Now distinguish the start of growth from the needs of an established seedling.
