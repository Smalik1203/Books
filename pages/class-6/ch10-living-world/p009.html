New organisms, not repaired objects

Reproduction produces new organisms of the same kind. A bean plant forms seeds that can give rise to more bean plants. A frog produces offspring that develop into frogs. Reproduction connects one generation with the next, so a kind of organism can continue even though each individual has a limited life.

A young child is living before becoming able to reproduce. Some adults never reproduce. Reproduction is a characteristic of living kinds; it is not a test that every individual must pass at the moment we observe it.

Resting is different from dying

A sleeping animal still respires. A dormant seed has very low activity. Death is the irreversible end of an organism’s life processes. Lack of a visible response during a short observation is not enough evidence to declare something dead. Equally, giving water to a dead organism cannot restart its life.

Use a pattern of evidence

Movement, nutrition, growth, respiration, excretion, response and reproduction describe the living world together. An organism does not display every characteristic visibly at every age or in every condition.
