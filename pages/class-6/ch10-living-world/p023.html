10.11From pond eggs to a frog

A jelly-like cluster of frog eggs is called spawn. An embryo develops within each viable fertilised egg. In the pond-frog pattern here, a tadpole hatches into water, then develops legs as its body changes.

Compare the legs and tail rather than image size. Hind legs appear before the front legs emerge. The tail is gradually absorbed as the froglet develops; it is not simply cut off.

Identified eggs, embryo and adult are common frogs (Rana temporaria). The tadpole, hind-leg and froglet photographs have no verified species identification. These comparisons illustrate a pond-frog pattern, not every frog species.
