10.4 Respiration and removing wastes

Place a hand lightly on your chest while resting. Air moves into and out of your body as you breathe. Breathing is the movement of air; respiration is the process in living cells that releases usable energy from food. They are connected, but they are not the same process.

In familiar animals, breathing brings in oxygen needed for respiration and helps remove carbon dioxide. During active movement, muscles need more energy. Breathing usually becomes faster. A parked vehicle does not respire: burning fuel in an engine is not the activity of living cells.

Activity 10.3

Watch a breathing pattern

1. Prediction: will ordinary walking change your count? Procedure: sit comfortably and count your ordinary breaths for one minute. Count one breath in and out as one cycle.

2. Record: if ordinary walking is comfortable for you, walk gently for a minute, then count again. A classmate may record while you count.

3. Conclusion: rest and repeat the count. Describe the pattern in words; different people need not have the same numbers.
