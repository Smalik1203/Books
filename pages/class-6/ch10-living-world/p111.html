The mosquito activities use recorded observations and adult-led checks of containers. The photographs show real organisms; stage images compare different individuals. Some reference photographs have no verified species identification and are used only for general appearance. The opener, opened-bean plate and three apparatus comparisons are photorealistic generated reconstructions. They explain structures or possible responses and are not records of performed experiments. Life-cycle photographs show real specimens.
