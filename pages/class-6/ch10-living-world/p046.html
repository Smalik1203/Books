Read a photograph carefully

A photograph preserves one view at one moment. It can show a root, a leaf or an animal’s body form, but cannot by itself show how quickly that organism grew or what happened next.

Choose two photographs from this chapter. For each, write one feature you can actually see and one question the picture cannot answer. For example, an adult frog photograph shows legs, but does not reveal the date on which its tail disappeared. A dated series of observations would provide better evidence for that change.
