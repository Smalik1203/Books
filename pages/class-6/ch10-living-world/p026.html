Activity 10.7

Follow the light

Question: will the shoot bend toward one-sided light? Prediction: mark its expected new direction.

1. Place an upright seedling in a ventilated cardboard box with one side opening. An adult should cut the opening. Use indirect daylight; avoid direct sun and overheating.

2. Keep the box closed except for that opening and light-shielded ventilation. Set a similar uncovered seedling nearby. Keep moisture and temperature similar.

3. Mark the pot position. Briefly open the front to sketch both plants daily from the same viewpoint, then close it.

Record: dated shoot outlines and the opening’s direction. Conclusion: compare new growth with the starting outline and the uncovered plant. Restore suitable light after the investigation.

Making small changes visible

Bose studied plant responses and developed highly sensitive instruments. His crescograph magnified tiny growth movements so that they could be recorded.

Measurements revealed changes too small for the unaided eye to follow. Your dated sketches use the same principle: compare evidence across time.
