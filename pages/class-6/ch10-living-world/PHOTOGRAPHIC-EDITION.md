## Authentic photographs

28 photographs are credited with source links and licences on p. 39 and in [credits.json](../../../figures/class-6/living-world/credits.json). The 23 retained photographs are joined by bean specimens in sequence (B. Domangue), a young bean plant (Rasbak), mature bean plants (H. Zell), frog embryos (Mnolf / B kimmel), and tadpoles with hind legs (Formul).

Real photographs retain biological structure. Resizing, layout cropping and label overlays are editorial changes. CC BY-SA images and crops retain their source licence. Formul's right-hand tadpole is isolated by a layout crop; its long tail and developing hind legs remain visible. A less legible candidate was rejected during grayscale proofing.

Bean stages compare different common-bean specimens and varieties at different scales. The mature-seed photograph is reused to represent a new generation, as disclosed in Figure 10.5. The flowering parent and embryos in new seeds are distinguished.

The mosquito sequence uses a Culex egg raft, elongated larvae, aquatic pupa and adult male Culex pipiens. The pupa's species is unverified; the adult male is explicitly not an egg-laying female. The images do not track a single organism. The pupa crop preserves its curved abdomen and breathing structures. Water and air are labelled.

Frog eggs, embryos and adult are identified as Rana temporaria; other stages have unverified species identification. Figure 10.7 compares different individuals, depicts a familiar pond-frog pattern and does not generalise to every frog. Hind legs precede front legs; the tail is gradually absorbed. The adult-to-eggs return means reproduction.
