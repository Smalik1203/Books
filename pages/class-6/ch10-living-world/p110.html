Set C

1. A. A pod can contain seeds of a new generation. The parent remains distinct from its offspring. New embryos develop inside seeds, not as roots growing directly inside a pod.

2. B. The young aquatic stages include larvae and pupae. An adult is one of four stages, so the reason is false.

3. C. A pupa is a living developmental stage. Not feeding at a particular stage does not establish death.

4. D. The conclusion must remain within the temperatures and seed kind tested. A generalises beyond the trial; B confuses delayed germination with death. C ignores the water provided to both groups.

5. A. A tracked transition links stages directly. Counts alone can change because of hatching, death or movement. The other records do not show the required transition.

6. B. Both undergo major development, but their stages differ. Both begin as eggs; the mosquito adult flies and the pond-frog adult uses water and land.

Three things to carry forward

Look for a connected pattern of life processes. Match a conclusion to the comparison actually made. Read life cycles as development of individuals linked by reproduction between generations.
