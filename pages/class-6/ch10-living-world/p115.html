Set A

1. A. Respiration releases usable energy in cells. Breathing moves air, nutrition supplies materials, and excretion removes wastes.

2. B. The touch is the stimulus. Folding is the response. The changed position or shape of a leaf describes a response, not its trigger.

3. C. The coat protects the interior. Cotyledons hold most stored food; the embryo’s young root and shoot grow into the seedling. The coat does not become these organs.

4. D. The adult emerges from a pupa. Egg and larva occur earlier. A winged mosquito is already in the adult stage.

5. A. The muscular tail propels a young tadpole. Gills support breathing; egg jelly surrounds the embryo. Undeveloped legs cannot do the swimming work shown.

6. B. Carbon dioxide is a waste made during respiration. Taking in food or oxygen is not waste removal; passing undigested food differs from excretion.
