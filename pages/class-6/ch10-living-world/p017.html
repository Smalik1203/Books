10.9A plant reaches a new generation

A bean seedling develops into a mature flowering plant. After successful reproduction, the flower’s ovary develops into a pod containing new seeds. The parent plant and its offspring remain distinct.

Which image shows the parent, and which shows its offspring?

Check your reasoning. The flowering plant is the parent. The embryos inside the new seeds are offspring. A pod is a fruit of the parent plant, not a new plant.
