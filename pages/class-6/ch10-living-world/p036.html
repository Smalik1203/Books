10.13 Keep the ideas connected

Keywords

Observation: something noticed or measured. Prediction: what you expect before a check. Experiment: a planned investigation of a question. Conclusion: what the evidence supports, with limits stated.

Stimulus: a change that prompts a response. Germination: the beginning of seed growth into a seedling. Habitat: the place and conditions in which an organism lives. Metamorphosis: major changes of body form during development.

Summary

Recognise life through connected processes. Dormant seeds can be alive.

Nutrition supplies food and materials; respiration releases energy. Breathing moves air.

Excretion removes metabolic wastes and excess substances. Organisms also respond to stimuli.

Growth and development change individuals. Reproduction connects generations. Death ends a life.

Viable seeds need water, oxygen and a suitable temperature. Light requirements depend on seed kind.

Roots generally grow down; shoots generally grow up and may bend towards light.

Plants can reproduce through seeds or from plant parts.

Mosquitoes develop through egg, larva, pupa and adult. Frogs in the pond example develop through egg, tadpole, froglet and adult.
