Think It Through

Read a sample record

The observed interval from tadpole to froglet is 54 days; from froglet to tailless young frog it is 20 days. A young frog is not necessarily a reproducing adult. These are intervals between recorded sightings, not exact stage durations, because a change may have occurred between visits.

To estimate durations more closely, observe more frequently. Do not conclude that all frogs follow this timetable. Species, water temperature and food supply can change the timing.

The cycle in words and pictures

Make a six-frame strip: eggs, embryo in egg, tadpole, tadpole with hind legs, froglet and adult. Draw a final arrow from the adult to new eggs and label it “reproduction”. Write one original sentence under each frame. You can turn the sentences into a short poem, but check that every stage remains scientifically accurate.
