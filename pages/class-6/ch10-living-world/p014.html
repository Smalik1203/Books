Four seed groups

Prediction: write which groups you expect to sprout and why, before starting.

1. Label four shallow plastic containers A–D. Place five seeds on tissue in each. Keep A dry; cover B’s seeds with water; moisten C and D without flooding.

2. Put C in a ventilated dark box with light-shielded vents. Keep A, B and D in the same indirect light and all groups at the same room temperature.

3. Maintain each water condition. For 7–10 days, count seeds with an emerging root daily. Inspect C briefly, then close its cover. Record dates, counts and mould separately.

Conclusion: compare A with D for water, C with D for light, and B with D for flooding.

B still contains dissolved oxygen. Flooding limits air access and can encourage decay; it is not a measured “zero oxygen” treatment. Record observations in the table on the next page.

Your observation notebook

Record real results, even if they differ from expectations. A fair investigation includes its uncertainties. The seed packet may be old, the room unusually cold, or a tissue allowed to dry between observations.
