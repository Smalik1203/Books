Inside a bean seed

The two thick cotyledons are part of the embryo. They store food that supports early growth. Opening the seed reveals a tiny young shoot and root along the inner edge.

The embryo includes both cotyledons, the young shoot and the young root. The cotyledons are not a separate packet beside the young plant. The thin outer seed coat protects the embryo.
