Problem 2

Two groups of the same viable seeds are kept equally moist and warm. One is in light and one in darkness. Both germinate. What is supported?

  1. These seeds can sprout in darkness.
  2. Darkness makes germination faster.
  3. Light is needed for the first root.
  4. Later growth needs no light.

Explanation

Answer: A. The result concerns the tested seed kind and the beginning of growth. It does not describe every plant or later development. B claims a speed difference that was not reported. C contradicts the dark group’s germination. D confuses germination with the light needs of continued growth.

The strongest conclusion is often the one whose scope matches the experiment. A broader statement is not automatically a better explanation.
