Not every new plant begins with a seed

Seeds are one route to a new plant, but they are not the only one. A potato tuber can sprout from a bud, and a suitable stem cutting, such as money plant, can form roots. These are examples of reproduction from plant parts. A florist’s cut flower and a rooted cutting therefore should not be judged only by whether they are detached from a plant.

A seed is missing from the story

Rohan grows a money plant from a stem piece with a node. He says, “It cannot have a life cycle because I never sowed a seed.” His conclusion is mistaken. The cutting can establish a new plant, grow and develop. A seed-to-seed diagram describes the bean example, not every possible route by which all plants reproduce.
