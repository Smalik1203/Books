The surroundings matter

Food availability, water, temperature and the condition of a habitat can affect how organisms grow and develop. Compare records within one species before drawing conclusions. A cooler week may slow insect development; a drying pond can threaten aquatic stages. Dates belong to observations made under particular conditions.
