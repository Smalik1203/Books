10.5 Responding and continuing life

A change that produces a response is called a stimulus; the plural is stimuli. A sudden sound may make you turn your head. Light, touch, temperature and gravity can act as stimuli. The response is what the organism does after the change. Distinguish the cause from the action that follows it.
