Set B

1. C. Motion can be mechanical and a seed can be dormant. None of the definite classifications follows from motion alone.

2. D. The trial shows that soil was not required for initial sprouting. It does not remove the need for oxygen or establish claims about later nutrition.

3. A. Stored reserves supply the developing seedling. Water is needed but is not its food reserve. Soil food is not a requirement for sprouting, and growth needs energy.

4. B. Legs develop while the tail is gradually absorbed. The tail does not become legs or drop off before the legs develop.

5. C. C changes water while controlling seed kind and temperature. The other comparisons change extra conditions or compare different materials.

6. D. Living origin and present life are different questions. B and C incorrectly use movement as a decisive test.

Set C

1. A. A pod can contain seeds of a new generation. The parent remains distinct from its offspring. New embryos develop inside seeds, not as roots growing directly inside a pod.

2. B. The young aquatic stages include larvae and pupae. An adult is one of four stages, so the reason is false.
