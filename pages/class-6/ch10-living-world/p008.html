Activity 10.3

Watch a breathing pattern

1. Prediction: will ordinary walking change your count? Procedure: sit comfortably and count your ordinary breaths for one minute. Count one breath in and out as one cycle.

2. Record: if ordinary walking is comfortable for you, walk gently for a minute, then count again. A classmate may record while you count.

3. Conclusion: rest and repeat the count. Describe the pattern in words; different people need not have the same numbers.

Care while exploring.

Plants respire too

Plants have no lungs, but their living cells need energy. Gases can pass through tiny pores called stomata on leaves and through other plant surfaces. Respiration occurs in living plant parts during both day and night. Photosynthesis requires light; respiration does not stop when photosynthesis starts. A teacher may show stomata on a prepared microscope slide.
