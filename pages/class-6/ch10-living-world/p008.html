Compare growth with respiration

CaseVisible size increase?Respiration?
A growing seedlingYesYes
A healthy adult at restLittle or noneYes
A pile as sand is addedYesNo
A metal spoonNoNo

The seedling builds its own living body. The pile becomes bigger because material is added from outside. The adult still maintains its body even without a visible increase in size. This is why growth and respiration must be interpreted together rather than treated as interchangeable signs.

10.6Inside a resting seed

A dry seed may look like a small packet of stored food, but a viable seed also contains an embryo, a young plant. The outer seed coat protects it. In a bean, the large seed leaves, or cotyledons, hold much of the food reserve. These reserves support early growth before the seedling has enough working leaves. The cotyledons are part of the embryo, along with its young root and shoot.
