10.10A mosquito’s hidden stages

The flying mosquito is only one stage. In the Culex example, a female lays a floating egg raft. Larvae and pupae live in water; a winged adult emerges from the pupa.

Different individuals, not a time-lapse. Egg raft and larvae: Culex; adult: male Culex pipiens. The pupa photograph shows general mosquito form; its species is unverified. It is not evidence that all four specimens belong to one species.

Look for the larva’s long segmented body and the pupa’s rounded front and curved abdomen. The adult photograph cannot show egg laying: the individual pictured is male.
