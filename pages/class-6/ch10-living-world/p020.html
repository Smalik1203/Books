Turn and compare

Question: how does growth respond after turning? Prediction: draw separate arrows for the root and shoot.

1. Germinate gram seeds on moist tissue. Place similar seedlings against the walls of two shallow clear observation containers, supported by damp tissue, above collected water.

2. Keep one upright. Turn the other container a quarter-turn so the root and shoot start sideways. Support it in this new position. Do not pin or bind living tissue.

3. Keep both evenly lit and moist. Mark container corners and draw dated outlines from the same viewpoint for several days.

Record: trace old parts and new tips separately. Conclusion: compare root and shoot directions with the upright control. Record no change if none is visible.
