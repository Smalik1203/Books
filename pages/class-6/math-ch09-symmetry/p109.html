Example 10

Ravi folds a paper strip 40 cm long in half, so that its ends meet. He punches one hole through both layers, 6 cm from the fold, and opens the strip.

  1. The two holes are
    1. 6 cm apart
    2. 12 cm apart
    3. 20 cm apart
    4. 26 cm apart
  2. Numerical answer. How far is each hole from the nearer end of the strip, in centimetres?
  3. Numerical answer. On a new strip, he wants the holes 18 cm apart. How far from the fold should he punch, in centimetres?

Solution.

Step 1each hole is 6 cm from the fold: $6 + 6 = 12$ cm
Step 2the fold is $40 \div 2 = 20$ cm from each end: $20 - 6 = 14$ cm
Step 3the fold is halfway between the holes: $18 \div 2 = 9$ cm
Answer(i) (b) 12 cm apart; (ii) 14; (iii) 9
