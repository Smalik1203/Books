Example 10 · Multiple correct

A design’s smallest positive matching rotation is 60°. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its rotational order is 6.
  2. A rotation of 120° brings it onto itself.
  3. A rotation of 30° brings it onto itself.
  4. A rotation of 300° brings it onto itself.

Solution.

Step 1360° ÷ 60° = 6. Every whole multiple of 60° is a matching rotation.
Step 2120° and 300° are such multiples; 30° cannot match because 60° is the smallest positive matching angle.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
