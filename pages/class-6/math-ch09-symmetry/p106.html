Type 4 · Angles of symmetry and order

Example 7

Find the angles of symmetry of each figure in Fig. 9.40, turned about the marked point. What is the order of rotational symmetry of each? Does either figure have a line of symmetry?

Solution.

Step 1 (a) a quarter turn carries each outer square to the next one's place $90^\circ$ fits
Step 2 (a) angles of symmetry: $90^\circ$, $180^\circ$, $270^\circ$, $360^\circ$
Step 3 (b) a quarter turn stands the block on end; a half turn fits only $180^\circ$ fits
Step 4 any fold puts an outer square where there is none no line fits
Answer (a) order 4 (b) order 2, with angles $180^\circ$ and $360^\circ$; neither has a line of symmetry
Example 8

A figure has rotational symmetry of order 20. Find its smallest angle of symmetry. Are $126^\circ$ and $100^\circ$ angles of symmetry?

Solution.

Step 1 smallest angle $= 360^\circ \div 20 = 18^\circ$ order 20 shares the full turn
Step 2 $18 \times 7 = 126$ so $126^\circ$ is one
Step 3 $18 \times 5 = 90$ and $18 \times 6 = 108$ no multiple gives 100
Answer $18^\circ$; $126^\circ$ is an angle of symmetry, $100^\circ$ is not
