Example 5

A point and its reflection in a straight line are 13 cm apart. How far is the point from the line, in centimetres?

Solution.

Step 1The line of symmetry is halfway between a point and its reflection.
Step 2So the point is half of 13 cm from the line.
Step 3$13 \div 2 = 6.5$ cm
CheckThe reflection is also 6.5 cm from the line, and $6.5 + 6.5 = 13$.
Answer6.5
Example 6

A wheel has 36 equal spokes, equally spaced. Every third spoke is painted. What is the order of rotational symmetry of the painted wheel?

Solution.

Step 1The spokes are $360^\circ \div 36 = 10^\circ$ apart.
Step 2The painted spokes are $3 \times 10^\circ = 30^\circ$ apart, so the smallest angle of symmetry is $30^\circ$.
Step 3The angles of symmetry are $30^\circ$, $60^\circ$, and so on up to $360^\circ$: $360 \div 30 = 12$ of them.
CheckThere are $36 \div 3 = 12$ painted spokes, and each angle of symmetry moves the first painted spoke onto a different one.
Answer12
