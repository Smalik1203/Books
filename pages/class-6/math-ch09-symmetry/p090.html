By the Book
Very short answer
  1. A point P is 3 cm from a line of symmetry. Find the distance between P and its reflection in the line. Give a reason.
  1. The smallest angle of symmetry of a figure made of equal radial arms, equally spaced, is $20^\circ$. Find the number of arms and the order of rotational symmetry of the figure.
  1. A figure has 12 equal radial arms, equally spaced. Is $50^\circ$ an angle of symmetry of this figure? Give a reason.
  1. A figure has 9 equal radial arms, equally spaced. Find its smallest angle of symmetry and its order of rotational symmetry.
  1. Find the number of lines of symmetry of the capital letter S. Does the letter S have rotational symmetry? Give a reason.
  1. A figure has rotational symmetry of order 4. Find its smallest angle of symmetry, and write its other angles of symmetry.
  1. A wheel has 20 equal spokes, equally spaced. Find its smallest angle of symmetry. Is $54^\circ$ one of its angles of symmetry? Give a reason.
  1. The word WOW is written in capital letters, with the O drawn as a circle. Does the word have an up-and-down line of symmetry through its middle? Give a reason.
  1. A circle is drawn with one of its radii. Find the number of lines of symmetry and the order of rotational symmetry of this figure.
  1. The order of rotational symmetry of a figure is 1. Does the figure have rotational symmetry? Give a reason.
Short answer
  1. A figure has 3 equal radial arms, with angles of $80^\circ$, $140^\circ$ and $140^\circ$ between them. Explain, with a picture, why this figure does not have rotational symmetry.
  1. Ravi says, “Every figure that has a line of symmetry also has rotational symmetry.” Give a capital letter that shows he is wrong. Also give a capital letter that has rotational symmetry but no line of symmetry.
  1. A figure has 30 equal radial arms, equally spaced. Find the angle between two arms next to each other. Every third arm is now painted red. Find the smallest angle of symmetry and the order of the painted figure.
