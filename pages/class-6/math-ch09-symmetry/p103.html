Behind Each Answer

Problem 1

The number of lines of symmetry of a regular hexagon is

  1. 3
  2. 6
  3. 12
  4. 2
Solution

A regular hexagon has 6 corners and 6 sides. Fold through opposite corners, and through the middles of opposite sides.

through opposite corners: 3 pairs of corners, so 3 lines

through the middles of opposite sides: 3 pairs of sides, so 3 lines

$3 + 3 = 6$ lines

The answer is 6, option (b). Option (a) counts only one of the two kinds of line. Option (c) counts each line twice, once from each of its two ends. Option (d) counts only the up-and-down and across lines.

Problem 2

The smallest angle of symmetry of a figure is $72^\circ$. Its angles of symmetry are

  1. $72^\circ$, $144^\circ$, $216^\circ$, $288^\circ$, $360^\circ$
  2. $72^\circ$ and $360^\circ$ only
  3. $72^\circ$, $144^\circ$, $288^\circ$, $360^\circ$
  4. $72^\circ$, $180^\circ$, $360^\circ$
Solution

The angles of symmetry are the multiples of the smallest one, up to $360^\circ$.

$72 \times 1 = 72$, $\;72 \times 2 = 144$, $\;72 \times 3 = 216$

$72 \times 4 = 288$, $\;72 \times 5 = 360$

The answer is option (a). Option (b) forgets that two turns of $72^\circ$ also fit. Option (c) skips 216, which is $72 \times 3$. Option (d) includes $180^\circ$, which is not a multiple of 72.

The five angles in option (a) are the angles of symmetry of a regular pentagon. A turn of $72^\circ$ carries each corner of the pentagon to the next corner, and five such turns make a full turn of $360^\circ$.
