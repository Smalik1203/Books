Example 1 · Single correct

What is the smallest positive rotation that brings a regular octagon exactly onto itself?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 30°
  2. 45°
  3. 60°
  4. 90°

Solution.

Step 1The eight identical positions divide a full turn equally.
Step 2360° ÷ 8 = 45°.
Answer(b) 45°
Example 2 · Single correct

A rectangle has unequal adjacent sides. How many lines of reflection symmetry does it have?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 4
  4. 0

Solution.

Step 1The lines through the midpoints of opposite sides are the two symmetry lines.
Step 2Its diagonals are not symmetry lines because the unequal side lengths would have to exchange places.
Answer(b) 2
Example 3 · Single correct

A point is 4 cm to the left of a vertical mirror line. How far is it from its reflected image?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 2 cm
  2. 4 cm
  3. 6 cm
  4. 8 cm

Solution.

Step 1Its image lies 4 cm to the right of the line at the same height.
Step 2The separation is 4 + 4 = 8 cm.
Answer(d) 8 cm
