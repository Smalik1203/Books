1. Lakshmi draws a kolam on a square grid of 4 rows of 4 dots. She joins the four corner dots to make a big square, and the four middle dots to make a small square inside it.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many dots are there in the grid?
    2. How many lines of symmetry does her kolam have?
    3. She now also draws one diagonal of the big square. Find the number of lines of symmetry and the order of the kolam now.

  1. A toy shop in Delhi has a signboard that reads TOYS, in capital letters. The O is drawn as a circle.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Which letters of the word have an up-and-down line of symmetry?
    2. Which letter has rotational symmetry but no line of symmetry?
    3. Ravi says the letter S has a line of symmetry across its middle, because its top half looks like its bottom half. Is he right? Justify your answer.
Objective questions
  1. The number of lines of symmetry of a figure made of 10 equal radial arms, equally spaced, is
    1. 5
    2. 10
    3. 20
    4. 36
  1. The order of rotational symmetry of a figure is 12. Its smallest angle of symmetry is
    1. $12^\circ$
    2. $24^\circ$
    3. $30^\circ$
    4. $36^\circ$
  1. A point is 7 cm from its reflection in a line. Its distance from the line is
    1. 3.5 cm
    2. 7 cm
    3. 14 cm
    4. 0 cm
  1. The capital letter that has rotational symmetry of order 2 and no line of symmetry is
    1. O
    2. S
    3. C
    4. U
  1. Equal radial arms, equally spaced, are $12^\circ$ apart. The number of arms is
    1. 12
    2. 24
    3. 30
    4. 36
  1. For a figure with 20 equal radial arms, equally spaced, which of these is not an angle of symmetry?
    1. $36^\circ$
    2. $54^\circ$
