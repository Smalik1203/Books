Case-based questions

  1. A wall clock has 12 equal marks round its edge, one for each hour, equally spaced. It has no numbers. Leave out its hands.

    1. What is the angle between two marks next to each other?
    2. What is the order of rotational symmetry of the ring of marks?
    3. The hour hand moves from 12 to 5. Is the angle it turns through an angle of symmetry of the ring of marks?
    4. How many lines of symmetry does the ring of marks have?

  1. A shop sells wheel covers with equal spokes, equally spaced. The table shows three designs.

    DesignNumber of spokes
    P12
    Q15
    R20
    1. Find the smallest angle of symmetry of each design.
    2. Which designs look the same after a turn of $72^\circ$?
    3. Which designs look the same after a turn of $90^\circ$?
    4. A new design S has its spokes $12^\circ$ apart. How many spokes does it have?
