Reflection symmetry
  • A line of symmetry reflects a figure onto itself.
  • So a figure with a line of symmetry has reflection symmetry.
Example 1

In a triangle PQR, the sides PQ and PR are equal. Its line of symmetry passes through P and the middle of QR. The triangle is reflected in this line. Where do P, Q and R go?

Solution.

Step 1 P lies on the line of symmetry, so it stays where it is a point on the line does not move
Step 2 Q is on one side of the line, so it goes to the matching place on the other side, where R was
Step 3 in the same way, R goes to where Q was
Check PQ lands on PR, and PR lands on PQ. They are equal, so the triangle fits exactly on itself
Answer P stays where it is, and Q and R change places.

Making shapes that have a line of symmetry

You have seen figures that are symmetrical and figures that are not. How can you make a symmetrical figure yourself? Here are three ways.

Ink blots. You may have done this in Class 5. Fold a sheet of paper in half and open it. Drop a few drops of ink or paint on one half. Press the two halves together, and open the paper again.

?
Think and Reflect
  1. What do you see? Is the ink pattern symmetrical?
  2. If it is, where is its line of symmetry?
  3. Is there any other line along which you can fold the paper to get two parts that match? Make a few more patterns and check.
