1. Assertion (A): A point 4 cm from a line of symmetry is 8 cm from its reflection.

    Reason (R): A line of symmetry is halfway between a point and its reflection.

  1. Assertion (A): The capital letter T has exactly one line of symmetry.

    Reason (R): Every capital letter that has a line of symmetry also has rotational symmetry.

  1. Assertion (A): A figure of 3 equal radial arms, with angles of $90^\circ$, $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ between them, has rotational symmetry.

    Reason (R): A full turn is $360^\circ$.

  1. Assertion (A): A square has 4 lines of symmetry.

    Reason (R): A square has 4 equal sides.

Case-based questions

  1. For Onam, Anju makes a pookalam, a rangoli of flowers. It is a circle cut into 16 equal parts. She fills the parts with yellow, orange, white and orange flowers, in that order, again and again round the circle.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. What is the angle at the centre of each part?
    2. What is the smallest angle of symmetry of the coloured pookalam?
    3. Her sister makes a pookalam coloured in the same way, but its smallest angle of symmetry is $120^\circ$. Into how many equal parts is her circle cut? Show how you found it.

  1. For Diwali, Tara makes a star lantern with 10 equal points, equally spaced round its centre. Seen from the front, the lantern looks the same after certain turns.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. What is the angle between two points next to each other?
    2. What is the order of rotational symmetry of the lantern?
    3. Tara ties a small bell to every fifth point. Find the smallest angle of symmetry and the order of the lantern with its bells.

  1. A chessboard has 8 rows of 8 squares. The squares are black and white in turn, along every row and every column. Rohan looks at the pattern of colours.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many squares are there on the board?
    2. What is the order of rotational symmetry of the pattern?
    3. How many lines of symmetry does the pattern have? Justify your answer.
