9.1Line of Symmetry

Look at the triangle in Fig. 9.2(a). Imagine folding it along the dashed line. One half of the triangle covers the other half exactly, circles and all. The two halves are called mirror halves.

Now look at Fig. 9.2(b). If you fold it along the dashed line, the left half does not fit over the right half. The tab on the left piece is at the top, but the tab on the right piece is at the bottom. So these are not mirror halves.

Line of symmetry

A line of symmetry of a figure is a line that cuts the figure into two parts that fit exactly on each other when you fold along the line.
