Example 4

Which of these figures have exactly 2 lines of symmetry?

  1. a plus sign, arms crossing at their middles, with the across arm longer
  2. an oval, like a circle stretched sideways
  3. two equal circles that touch at one point
  4. 4 equal radial arms, equally spaced

Solution.

Step 1The plus sign and the oval fold up-and-down and across, but not on a slant, as their width and height differ: 2 lines each.
Step 2The circles fold along the line through both centres, and along the line through the touching point at right angles to it: 2 lines.
Step 3The 4 arms fold along each arm and halfway between two arms: 4 lines.
