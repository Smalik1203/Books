Type 3 · Folding, cutting and punching

Example 5

A sheet of paper 16 cm wide is folded in half from left to right. A hole is punched 3 cm from the fold. When the sheet is opened, how far apart are the two holes? How far is each hole from the nearer side of the sheet?

Solution.

Step 1 the fold is $16 \div 2 = 8$ cm from each side
Step 2 each hole is 3 cm from the fold, one on each side of it the fold reflects it
Step 3 distance apart $= 3 + 3 = 6$ cm
Step 4 distance from the nearer side $= 8 - 3 = 5$ cm
Answer the holes are 6 cm apart, and each is 5 cm from the nearer side
Example 6

A sheet of squared paper is folded once and cut along the dashed line, as in Fig. 9.39 (left). Describe the hole you see when the sheet is opened. How many sides does it have?

Solution.

Step 1 the cut runs 3 squares along, 1 down, 2 back, then 2 down to the fold
Step 2 opening the sheet adds its mirror image beyond the fold the fold reflects it
Step 3 the hole is a bar 6 squares wide over a stem 2 squares wide
Answer a T-shaped hole with 8 sides; its only line of symmetry is the fold
