Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which rotations bring an unmarked square exactly onto itself?

Select all correct options.

  1. 45°
  2. 90°
  3. 180°
  4. 270°

Solution.

Step 1The smallest positive matching rotation is 90°.
Step 2Its multiples 90°, 180° and 270° all match; 45° does not.
Answer(b), (c), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which statements about reflection in a straight mirror line are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. A point on the mirror line stays fixed.
  2. A point and its image are equally distant from the mirror line.
  3. The image of a segment always has a different length.
  4. Reflecting twice in the same line restores the original figure.

Solution.

Step 1A reflection preserves distances and fixes the mirror line point by point.
Step 2Each off-line point moves to the opposite side at equal perpendicular distance. Repeating the reflection moves it back.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which figures have exactly four lines of reflection symmetry?

Select all correct options.

  1. An unmarked square
  2. A non-square rectangle
  3. A regular pentagon
  4. A regular four-sided polygon

Solution.

Step 1A square has two diagonal symmetry lines and two lines through opposite side midpoints.
Step 2A regular four-sided polygon is a square. A non-square rectangle has two lines, and a regular pentagon has five.
Answer(a), (d)
