Exercise Set 9.3
  1. Find the angles of symmetry of each figure in Fig. 9.26, for a turn about the marked point.
  1. Which of the figures in Fig. 9.27 have more than one angle of symmetry?
  1. Give the order of rotational symmetry of each figure in Fig. 9.28.

Let us list the angles of symmetry of the figures we have met.

Exactly 2 angles of symmetry: $180^\circ$, $360^\circ$

Exactly 3 angles of symmetry: $120^\circ$, $240^\circ$, $360^\circ$

Exactly 4 angles of symmetry: $90^\circ$, $180^\circ$, $270^\circ$, $360^\circ$

Do you see something the lists have in common? The angles in the first list are all multiples of 180. In the second list they are all multiples of 120, and in the third they are all multiples of 90. In each list, the angles are the multiples of the smallest angle.
