Example 12

Fig. 9.41 has 8 arms, equally spaced, and long and short arms take turns. Find its angles of symmetry and its order.

Solution.

Step 1 angle between arms $= 360^\circ \div 8 = 45^\circ$
Step 2 a turn of $45^\circ$ carries each long arm onto a short arm no fit
Step 3 a turn of $90^\circ$ carries each long arm onto the next long arm two places on
Answer $90^\circ$, $180^\circ$, $270^\circ$ and $360^\circ$; the order is 4

Only every second arm is alike, so the figure behaves like one with 4 arms.

Example 13

For each digit in Fig. 9.42, find the number of lines of symmetry and the order of rotational symmetry.

Solution.

Step 1 0 and 8 fit a fold across the middle and a fold up and down 2 lines each
Step 2 2 and 5 fit neither fold side bars on opposite sides
Step 3 a half turn fits all four: top bar onto bottom bar, upper right onto lower left $180^\circ$ fits
Step 4 a quarter turn fits none taller than wide
Answer 0 and 8: 2 lines, order 2; 2 and 5: no line, order 2
