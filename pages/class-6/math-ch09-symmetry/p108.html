Example 9

A rangoli has 12 equal petals, equally spaced, coloured red, yellow and blue in turn.

  1. The smallest angle of symmetry of the coloured rangoli is
    1. $30^\circ$
    2. $60^\circ$
    3. $90^\circ$
    4. $120^\circ$
  2. Numerical answer. How many angles of symmetry does the coloured rangoli have?

Solution.

Step 1Petals are $30^\circ$ apart, and the colours repeat every 3 petals, so a colour comes back after $3 \times 30^\circ = 90^\circ$ of turning
Answer(i) (c) $90^\circ$; (ii) $360 \div 90 = 4$
