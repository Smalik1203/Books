Why the other options are wrong

A1 Fold a long rectangle along a diagonal and the corners do not meet, so the diagonals are not lines of symmetry. Only the two folds through the middles of opposite sides work, which gives (b). Option (c) would be right for a square.
B2 The two triangles are the same size, so R is true. But one triangle is only turned onto the other, not folded onto it. When you fold along the diagonal, the corners miss, so A is false and the answer is (d).
