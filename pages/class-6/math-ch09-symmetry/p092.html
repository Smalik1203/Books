1. A figure is a square of side 4 cm with a triangle of equal sides, each 4 cm, on every side, pointing outwards. Find its perimeter, its number of lines of symmetry and its order of rotational symmetry. The triangles on two opposite sides are removed. Find the number of lines of symmetry and the order of what is left.
  1. Points A and B lie on the same side of a line $l$, on a line at right angles to $l$. A is 2 cm from $l$ and B is 5 cm from $l$. They are reflected in $l$ to give A′ and B′. Draw a figure. Find the distances AB, A′B′, AB′ and BB′.
  1. A clock face has 12 hour marks, equally spaced, and no hands. Find the angle between two marks next to each other, and the order of rotational symmetry of the ring of marks. The minute hand is added, pointing at 12. Find the number of lines of symmetry and the order of the face now. Through what angle does the minute hand turn from 12 to 4, and is this an angle of symmetry of the ring of marks alone?
  1. A garland has 20 beads, equally spaced round a circle, coloured red and white in turn. Find the angle between two beads next to each other and the smallest angle of symmetry of the garland. Find the number of lines of symmetry of the garland. A second garland is coloured in the same way, and its smallest angle of symmetry is $40^\circ$. Find the number of beads on it.
  1. A square sheet of side 16 cm is folded in half along its up-and-down line of symmetry. A square of side 2 cm is cut out of the folded sheet, with one side along the fold and its top 3 cm below the top edge. The sheet is opened. Find the length and breadth of the hole, its area and its perimeter. How many lines of symmetry does the sheet with its hole have?
  1. A floor is made of 16 square tiles, in 4 rows of 4. The tiles along both diagonals of the floor are black, and the others are white. Find the number of black tiles, the number of lines of symmetry and the order of rotational symmetry of the floor. A black tile in one corner breaks and is replaced by a white tile. Find the number of lines of symmetry and the order of the floor now.
  1. An up-and-down line $l$ and a line $m$ across it meet at the point O. A point P is 3 cm to the left of $l$ and 2 cm above $m$. P is reflected in $l$ to give Q, and Q is reflected in $m$ to give R. Find the distances PQ and QR, and say where R is. Show that a half turn about O also takes P to R.
Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): A figure made of 9 equal radial arms, equally spaced, has $40^\circ$ as its smallest angle of symmetry.

    Reason (R): The 9 equal angles between the arms make up $360^\circ$, and $360 \div 9 = 40$.
