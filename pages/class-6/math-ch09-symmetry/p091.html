1. A figure looks exactly the same after a turn of $90^\circ$, and also after a turn of $120^\circ$. Show that it also looks the same after a turn of $30^\circ$.
  1. On a grid of 3 rows of 3 squares, the centre square and the four corner squares are shaded. Find the number of lines of symmetry and the order of rotational symmetry of the shaded pattern.
  1. Anil folds a sheet of paper in half along an up-and-down fold. He punches one hole 4 cm from the fold and 6 cm from the top edge. Find the number of holes when he opens the sheet, the distance between them and the distance of each hole from the top edge.
  1. A figure has 15 equal radial arms, equally spaced. Find its smallest angle of symmetry. Which of the turns $48^\circ$, $90^\circ$ and $120^\circ$ bring it back onto itself? Justify your answer.
  1. A square sheet of side 6 cm is folded along one of its lines of symmetry, and the folded sheet is a rectangle. Find the length and breadth of this rectangle. Along how many lines of symmetry can the square be folded to give a triangle instead?
  1. A figure has 10 equal radial arms, equally spaced. Find the number of its angles of symmetry, and write the three largest of them.
  1. A figure has 4 equal radial arms, equally spaced, and one arm is painted. Find its order of rotational symmetry. Now two opposite arms are painted instead. Find its order of rotational symmetry now.
Long answer
  1. A square sheet of side 20 cm is folded in half along its up-and-down line of symmetry. Two holes are punched in the folded sheet, each 3 cm from the fold: one 4 cm from the top edge and one 4 cm from the bottom edge. The sheet is opened. Draw the opened sheet. Find the number of holes and the distance between the two holes in each row. Also find the number of lines of symmetry of the pattern of holes.
  1. The wheel of a bullock cart has 16 equal spokes, equally spaced. Find the angle between two spokes next to each other. Is $157\frac{1}{2}^\circ$ an angle of symmetry of the wheel? A painter paints every fourth spoke red. Find the smallest angle of symmetry and the order of rotational symmetry of the painted wheel.
  1. Meena draws a rangoli with 9 equal petals, equally spaced round its centre. Find its smallest angle of symmetry, and write all its angles of symmetry less than $180^\circ$. She then colours 3 of the petals red, equally spaced. Find the smallest angle of symmetry and the order of the coloured rangoli.
