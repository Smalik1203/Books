Type 2 · Reflecting in a line

Example 3

Copy Fig. 9.37 on squared paper. Draw the reflection of triangle PQR in the line that runs past the squared paper.

Solution.

Step 1 P is 4 squares left of the line, so its image is 4 squares right of it same row
Step 2 Q is 1 square left, so its image is 1 square right same row
Step 3 R is 3 squares left, so its image is 3 squares right same row
Answer join the three images: that is the reflected triangle
Example 4

In Fig. 9.38, A is 4 squares to the right of the point B, which is on the slanting line. Where is the reflection of A in the line?

Solution.

Step 1 from A, step 1 square left and 1 up until you reach the line: 2 steps straight to the line
Step 2 take 2 more steps the same way same distance beyond
Step 3 in all, 4 squares left and 4 up from A
Answer the reflection of A is 4 squares directly above B

For this slanting line, across and up change places.
