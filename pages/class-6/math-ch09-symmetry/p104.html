Example 4 · Single correct

A design has rotational symmetry of order 5. How many positions, including the starting position but not counting the full-turn repeat, coincide with the original?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 10

Solution.

Step 1The order counts the distinct matching positions in one turn.
Step 2They occur at 0°, 72°, 144°, 216° and 288°, giving five positions.
Answer(b) 5
Example 5 · Single correct

Which figure has rotational symmetry of order 2 but no line of reflection symmetry?

Choose one correct option.

  1. A square
  2. A non-square rectangle
  3. A parallelogram with unequal adjacent sides and no right angles
  4. An equilateral triangle

Solution.

Step 1Every parallelogram matches itself after a half-turn.
Step 2The stated parallelogram is neither a rectangle nor a rhombus, so it has no reflection symmetry. The other choices have reflection lines.
Answer(c) The stated parallelogram
Example 6 · Single correct

A square sheet is folded in half along its vertical centre line and then along its horizontal centre line. One hole is punched through all four layers, away from every fold and edge. How many holes appear when it is unfolded?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 4
  2. 2
  3. 8
  4. 1

Solution.

Step 1The two folds create four layers.
Step 2A hole away from the folds gives four distinct reflected positions after unfolding.
Answer(a) 4
