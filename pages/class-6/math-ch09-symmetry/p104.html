Example 3

Which of these are true for a reflection in a straight line?

  1. A point on the line does not move.
  2. A point and its reflection are equally far from the line.
  3. A segment and its reflection can differ in length.
  4. Reflecting twice in the same line gives back the figure.

Solution.

Step 1A point on the fold stays where it is, and folding carries any other point to the same distance on the other side; folding back returns it. So (a), (b) and (d) are true.
Step 2The two halves fit exactly, so lengths do not change: (c) is false.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
