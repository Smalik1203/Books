Exercise Set 9.2
  1. In each sheet in Fig. 9.10, a hole was punched in a folded square sheet, and then the sheet was opened. For sheets (a), (b) and (c), find the line along which the sheet was folded. Sheet (d) was made by punching only one hole. How was it folded?
  1. In each figure in Fig. 9.11, the dashed line is a line of symmetry, and one hole is shown. Copy each figure and mark where the other hole must be.
  1. Take a sheet of paper. Give it a vertical fold, and then a horizontal fold. How many layers of paper are there now? Punch one hole through all the layers, away from the edges. How many holes will you see when you open the sheet? Check by doing it.
  1. Each sheet in Fig. 9.12 has been folded and is cut along the dashed line. The thick edges are folds. Sheet (c) was folded twice. Draw how each sheet will look when it is opened. Then cut paper in the same way and check your drawing.
