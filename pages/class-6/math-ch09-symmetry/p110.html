Assertion and reason

In Questions 13 to 16, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): A figure whose smallest angle of symmetry is $30^\circ$ has 12 angles of symmetry.

    Reason (R): $30 \times 12 = 360$.

  1. Assertion (A): A circle has rotational symmetry.

    Reason (R): A circle has exactly 360 angles of symmetry.

  1. Assertion (A): A figure made of three equal radial arms, with angles of $100^\circ$, $130^\circ$ and $130^\circ$ between them, has rotational symmetry.

    Reason (R): These three angles add up to $360^\circ$.

  1. Assertion (A): The letter H, drawn with straight lines, has rotational symmetry of order 2.

    Reason (R): The letter H has a line of symmetry.
