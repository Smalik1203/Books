A game on a grid

Here is a game for two players. Draw a grid of 6 rows of 6 squares. The players take turns. In each turn, a player covers two squares that share a side by drawing a line joining their centres. The line can go across or up and down. A square can be covered only once, so two lines may not meet or cross. The first player who cannot draw a line loses the game.
