Example 6 · Single correct

Five identical cakes are shared equally among eight children. How much does each child receive?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{5}{8}$ of a cake
  2. $\frac{8}{5}$ of a cake
  3. $\frac{3}{8}$ of a cake
  4. $\frac{1}{8}$ of a cake

Solution.

Step 1Cut each cake into eight equal pieces and give one piece from each cake to each child.
Step 2Each receives five eighths of one cake.
Answer(a) $\frac{5}{8}$ of a cake
