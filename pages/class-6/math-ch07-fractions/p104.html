Type 3 · Equivalent fractions and lowest terms

Example 6

Find the numbers that go in the boxes: $\frac{3}{7} = \frac{\square}{28} = \frac{15}{\square}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $28 = 7 \times 4$ denominator times 4
Step 2 $3 \times 4 = 12$, so $\frac{3}{7} = \frac{12}{28}$ numerator times 4 too
Step 3 $15 = 3 \times 5$ numerator times 5
Step 4 $7 \times 5 = 35$, so $\frac{3}{7} = \frac{15}{35}$
Answer 12 and 35
Example 7

In one group, 4 rotis are shared equally by 5 children. In another group, 8 rotis are shared equally by 10 children. How much roti does each child get in each group? Are the shares equal?

Solution.

Step 1 Cut each of the 4 rotis into 5 equal parts. Each child gets one part of every roti one part of each
Step 2 $4 \times \frac{1}{5} = \frac{4}{5}$ roti each, so $4 \div 5 = \frac{4}{5}$
Step 3 In the second group each roti is cut into 10 parts: $8 \times \frac{1}{10} = \frac{8}{10}$ roti each
Step 4 The second group is two of the first put together, so the shares are the same twice the rotis, twice the children
Answer $\frac{4}{5}$ roti and $\frac{8}{10}$ roti. The shares are equal, so $\frac{4}{5} = \frac{8}{10}$
Example 8

Write $\frac{108}{144}$ in lowest terms.

Solution.

Step 1 108 and 144 are both divisible by 4 test for 4
Step 2 $\dfrac{108}{144} = \dfrac{108 \div 4}{144 \div 4} = \dfrac{27}{36}$
Step 3 $\dfrac{27}{36} = \dfrac{27 \div 9}{36 \div 9} = \dfrac{3}{4}$ 9 divides both
Step 4 3 and 4 have no common factor except 1
Answer $\frac{108}{144} = \frac{3}{4}$

Dividing by $4 \times 9 = 36$ in one step gives the same answer, because 36 is the largest common factor of 108 and 144.
