Example 4

On a number line, the length from 0 to 1 is cut into 12 equal parts. Which of these are correct?

  1. The 3rd mark after 0 stands for $\frac{1}{4}$.
  2. The 8th mark after 0 stands for $\frac{2}{3}$.
  3. The 10th mark after 0 stands for $\frac{5}{6}$.
  4. The 6th mark after 0 stands for $\frac{1}{6}$.

Solution.

Step 1The marks stand for twelfths: $\frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}$, $\frac{8}{12} = \frac{2}{3}$ and $\frac{10}{12} = \frac{5}{6}$.
Step 2The 6th mark stands for $\frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$, not $\frac{1}{6}$.
CheckThe mark for $\frac{1}{6}$ is the 2nd one, since $\frac{1}{6} = \frac{2}{12}$, and the mark for $\frac{1}{3}$ is the 4th one, since $\frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{12}$.
