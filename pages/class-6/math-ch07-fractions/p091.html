Long answer
  1. A school field is shared out for sports day: $\frac{1}{3}$ of it for football, $\frac{1}{4}$ for kabaddi and $\frac{1}{6}$ for a flower garden. The rest is left open. Find the fraction of the field that is left open. Is the open part bigger than the garden? Find the difference between them.
  1. Draw a fraction wall with rows for thirds, sixths and twelfths, and use it to show that $\frac{2}{3} = \frac{4}{6} = \frac{8}{12}$. Using this, find $\frac{2}{3} + \frac{5}{12}$ and write the answer as a mixed number.
  1. Rahul has a rope 5 m long. He cuts off $1\frac{2}{3}$ m for a swing and $2\frac{1}{4}$ m for a clothes line. Find the length of rope left. Is it enough for a skipping rope $1\frac{1}{4}$ m long? Justify your answer.
  1. Three teams repair a road 1 km long. In one week, team A repairs $\frac{3}{10}$ of it, team B repairs $\frac{1}{4}$ and team C repairs $\frac{2}{5}$. Find which team repaired the most. Find the fraction of the road repaired in all, and hence the fraction still to be repaired.
  1. Draw a rectangle and cut it into 12 equal squares. Shade $\frac{1}{3}$ of the rectangle in one colour and $\frac{1}{4}$ of it in another colour, with no square shaded twice. Find the fraction of the rectangle that is shaded, and the fraction that is not shaded.
  1. 7 rotis are shared equally among 3 children. Find each child’s share as a mixed number. Then 3 more rotis are made and 2 more children join, and all the rotis are shared equally again. Does each child now get more or less than before? Find by how much.
  1. Meera wants to compare $\frac{5}{12}$ and $\frac{3}{8}$. She uses the common denominator 96. Show that 24 is also a common denominator, and compare the two fractions using it. Find the difference between them, and check that 96 gives the same difference.
  1. Draw a number line from 0 to 2, and cut each unit into 6 equal parts. Mark $\frac{1}{3}$, $\frac{5}{6}$, $1\frac{1}{2}$ and $\frac{11}{6}$ on it. Hence write the four numbers in descending order, and find the distance between the smallest and the largest.
  1. A water tank is $\frac{5}{8}$ full. In the morning, water filling $\frac{1}{4}$ of the tank is used. In the afternoon, water filling $\frac{1}{2}$ of the tank is pumped in. Find the fraction of the tank that is full at the end of the day. Is the tank full? If not, find the fraction that is empty.
  1. Show, with a strip folded into eighths, that $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{8} + \frac{1}{8} = 1$. Using this, find $1 - \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{4}$, and write $\frac{7}{8}$ as a sum of three different fractional units.
