Example 5

A class has 48 students, and $\frac{5}{8}$ of them come to school by bus. How many students do not come by bus?

Solution.

Step 1The whole class is $\frac{8}{8}$, so the students who do not come by bus are $\frac{8}{8} - \frac{5}{8} = \frac{3}{8}$ of the class.
Step 2One eighth of 48 students is $48 \div 8 = 6$ students.
Step 3So three eighths of the class is $3 \times 6 = 18$ students.
CheckFive eighths of the class is $5 \times 6 = 30$ students. The two groups together make $30 + 18 = 48$ students, the whole class.
Answer18
Example 6

The fraction $\frac{7}{12}$ is written as an equivalent fraction with denominator 84, and then as one with denominator 120. What is the sum of the two numerators?

Solution.

Step 1$84 \div 12 = 7$, so the denominator was multiplied by 7.
Step 2The numerator must be multiplied by 7 too: $7 \times 7 = 49$.
Step 3$120 \div 12 = 10$, so the other numerator is $7 \times 10 = 70$.
Step 4The sum of the numerators is $49 + 70 = 119$.
Answer119
