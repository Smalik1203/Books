7.7Comparing Fractions

Which is greater, $\frac{4}{5}$ or $\frac{7}{9}$? It is hard to tell by looking. So write both with the same denominator. 45 is a multiple of 5 and of 9, so use 45.

Example 2

Compare $\frac{4}{5}$ and $\frac{7}{9}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{4}{5} = \dfrac{4 \times 9}{5 \times 9} = \dfrac{36}{45}$ write in forty-fifths
Step 2 $\dfrac{7}{9} = \dfrac{7 \times 5}{9 \times 5} = \dfrac{35}{45}$
Step 3 36 forty-fifths is more than 35 forty-fifths compare the numerators
Answer $\frac{4}{5} > \frac{7}{9}$

The common denominator does not have to be the product of the two denominators. Any common multiple will do.

Example 3

Compare $\frac{7}{9}$ and $\frac{17}{21}$.

Solution.

Step 1 63 is a common multiple of 9 and 21 choose the unit
Step 2 $\dfrac{7}{9} = \dfrac{7 \times 7}{9 \times 7} = \dfrac{49}{63}$
Step 3 $\dfrac{17}{21} = \dfrac{17 \times 3}{21 \times 3} = \dfrac{51}{63}$
Step 4 $\frac{49}{63} < \frac{51}{63}$ compare the numerators
Answer $\frac{7}{9} < \frac{17}{21}$
Comparing fractions

Step 1. Change the fractions to equivalent fractions with the same denominator.

Step 2. Compare the numerators. The fraction with more fractional units is greater.
