Why the other options are wrong

A5 $\frac{21}{28}$ and $\frac{6}{8}$ are both equal to $\frac{42}{56}$, but each still has a common factor: 7 in the first and 2 in the second. Only $\frac{3}{4}$ is in lowest terms, so the answer is (c). Option (d) is not equivalent at all.
B4 Both statements are true. A is true because $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{6}{12}$ and $5 < 6$. A numerator smaller than the denominator shows only that a fraction is less than 1: $\frac{2}{3}$ also has one, and it is more than a half. So R does not explain A, and the answer is (b).
