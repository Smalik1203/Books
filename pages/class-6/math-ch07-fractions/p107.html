Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–2, R–1, S–3
  3. P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Divide to obtain the whole part and remainder: 17 = 3 × 5 + 2; 23 = 3 × 6 + 5.
Step 2Also, 19 = 4 × 4 + 3 and 11 = 3 × 3 + 2. The remainder stays over the original denominator.
