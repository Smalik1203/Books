Example 9

A cake is cut into 12 equal pieces. Mohan eats $\frac{1}{4}$ of it and Neha eats $\frac{1}{3}$ of it.

  1. How many pieces does Mohan eat?
    1. 3
    2. 4
    3. 6
    4. 2
  2. Numerical answer. How many pieces are left?

Solution.

Step 1One quarter of 12 pieces is $12 \div 4 = 3$ pieces.
Step 2One third is $12 \div 3 = 4$ pieces, so $12 - 3 - 4 = 5$ are left.
Answer(i) (a) 3; (ii) 5
