Example 8

Find $2\frac{1}{3} - \frac{3}{4}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $2\frac{1}{3} = \frac{7}{3}$ mixed number as a fraction
Step 2 $\dfrac{7}{3} - \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{28}{12} - \dfrac{9}{12}$ write in twelfths
Step 3 $\dfrac{28}{12} - \dfrac{9}{12} = \dfrac{19}{12} = 1\dfrac{7}{12}$
Answer $1\dfrac{7}{12}$

Check by adding back: $\frac{19}{12} + \frac{9}{12} = \frac{28}{12} = 2\frac{4}{12} = 2\frac{1}{3}$, which is where we started.

7.9A Pinch of History

In ancient India, a fraction was called bhinna in Sanskrit, which means broken. It was also called bhaga or ansha, which mean a part or a piece.

The way the whole world writes fractions today began in India. The Bakshali manuscript, from about 300 CE, writes one half as a 1 placed above a 2, very much as we write it. Aryabhata (499 CE), Brahmagupta (628 CE), Sridharacharya (about 750 CE) and Mahaviracharya (about 850 CE) all wrote fractions this way.
