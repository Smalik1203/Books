Fractions with any numerator, together with rules for adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing them, were first used in India. The Sulba-sutras show that people knew rules for working with fractions even in Vedic times. Brahmagupta set them out in full, in the form we still use. They reached Europe through the Arabs, and were in common use there by the 17th century.

A puzzle with fractional units

Equal units make 1 easily, as in $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3} = 1$. Can you make 1 from units that are all different?

Two different units will not work. The largest fractional unit is $\frac{1}{2}$, and $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = 1$. To make the two units different, one $\frac{1}{2}$ must be swapped for a smaller unit, and then the sum is less than 1.

Try three units. Start from $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3} = 1$. One $\frac{1}{3}$ must get bigger, and the only bigger unit is $\frac{1}{2}$. What is left is $1 - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}$, which is $\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4}$. Again one $\frac{1}{4}$ must get bigger, and the only choice other than $\frac{1}{2}$ is $\frac{1}{3}$. The last unit must then be $1 - \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{6}$. So there is only one answer, shown in Fig. 7.17.

What this chapter showed
  1. Each equal part of one unit is a fractional unit. More parts make smaller units.
  2. In $\frac{5}{6}$, 5 is the numerator and 6 is the denominator. The fraction is 5 fractional units of $\frac{1}{6}$.
  3. Every fraction has a place on the number line.
  4. A mixed number has a whole part and a fractional part less than 1, like $2\frac{3}{4} = \frac{11}{4}$.
  5. Equivalent fractions stand for the same share or length, like $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{3}{6}$.
  6. A fraction is in lowest terms when its numerator and denominator have no common factor except 1.
  7. To compare, add or subtract fractions, first give them the same denominator.

Before you compare, add or subtract two fractions, ask what fractional unit each one counts. If the units are different, make them the same first.
