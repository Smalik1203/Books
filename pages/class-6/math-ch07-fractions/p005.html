Measuring with fractional units

To say how much a fractional amount is, count how many fractional units it is made of.

Reading fractions

We usually read $\frac{3}{4}$ as three quarters or three upon four. Reading it as 3 times $\frac{1}{4}$ tells you more. It shows the fractional unit, $\frac{1}{4}$, and how many of those units there are, 3.

Numerator and denominator

In the fraction $\frac{5}{6}$, the top number 5 is the numerator. The bottom number 6 is the denominator.

The denominator names the fractional unit. The numerator counts how many of those units there are.
