Fractional units
  • When one unit is cut into equal parts, each part is a fractional unit, or a unit fraction.
  • The more equal parts, the smaller each unit: $\frac{1}{2} > \frac{1}{3} > \frac{1}{4} > \frac{1}{5} > \cdots$

Think and Reflect
  1. Which is bigger, $\frac{1}{7}$ or $\frac{1}{10}$? Explain your answer using shares of a roti.
  2. Can a fractional unit be bigger than $\frac{1}{2}$? Why?

Fractions have been used and named in India for a very long time. The Rig Veda calls the fraction $\frac{3}{4}$ tri-pada. Many Indian languages still use a word with the same meaning. In Hindi, people say teen paav, and in Tamil they say mukkaal.
