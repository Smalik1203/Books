7.2Fractional Units as Parts of a Whole

A chikki is a sweet made of jaggery and peanuts. The chikki in Fig. 7.2 is marked into 24 small squares. It breaks into two pieces. How much of the whole chikki is each piece?

The smaller piece has 6 small squares. Four pieces of this size would make the whole chikki, so the smaller piece is $\frac{1}{4}$ chikki. Now look at the bigger piece. It is made of 3 blocks, and each block is the same size as the smaller piece. So the bigger piece is 3 pieces of $\frac{1}{4}$, and we say it is $\frac{3}{4}$ chikki. We have measured the bigger piece using the fractional unit $\frac{1}{4}$.

You can check this by counting squares. The bigger piece has 18 of the 24 squares. One quarter of 24 squares is 6 squares, so three quarters is $3 \times 6 = 18$ squares. Both ways give the same answer.

The same chikki can be cut into 6 equal pieces in more than one way. Fig. 7.3 shows two ways. Each piece is one sixth of the chikki.
