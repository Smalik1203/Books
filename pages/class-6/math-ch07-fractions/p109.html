Assertion and reason

In Questions 13 to 17, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{4}{7} > \frac{4}{9}$.

    Reason (R): When the same amount is shared among more people, each share is smaller.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{12}{18}$ is in lowest terms.

    Reason (R): 12 and 18 are both even numbers.

  1. Assertion (A): $3\frac{1}{2} = \frac{7}{2}$.

    Reason (R): 3 wholes are the same as 6 halves.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{5}{12} < \frac{1}{2}$.

    Reason (R): The numerator of $\frac{5}{12}$ is smaller than its denominator.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$.

    Reason (R): To add two fractions, add the numerators and add the denominators.
