By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Write $\dfrac{23}{6}$ as a mixed number. Show how you found it.
  1. Write $4\dfrac{2}{7}$ as a fraction. Show how you found it.
  1. Is $\dfrac{18}{24}$ in lowest terms? Give a reason, and write it in lowest terms if it is not.
  1. Find the number that goes in the box: $\dfrac{5}{8} = \dfrac{\square}{40}$.
  1. Which is greater, $\dfrac{5}{7}$ or $\dfrac{7}{10}$? Show your working.
  1. Find $\dfrac{3}{8} + \dfrac{1}{6}$.
  1. Find $\dfrac{5}{6} - \dfrac{3}{10}$ and write the answer in lowest terms.
  1. On a number line, the length from 0 to 1 is cut into 8 equal parts. Which fraction does the 6th mark after 0 stand for? Write it in lowest terms.
  1. The sum of two fractions is $\dfrac{7}{8}$. One of them is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Find the other.
  1. Kiran has a ribbon 2 m long. She cuts off $\frac{3}{4}$ m to tie a gift. Find the length of ribbon left, as a mixed number.
Short answer
  1. Write $\frac{3}{5}$, $\frac{7}{10}$ and $\frac{2}{3}$ in ascending order. Also find the difference between the largest and the smallest of them.
  1. Explain, with a drawing of two paper strips of the same length, why $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{6}$.
  1. Ramesh walks $1\frac{1}{2}$ km to the bus stop and then goes $2\frac{3}{4}$ km by bus. Find the total distance he travels. Also find how much farther he goes by bus than on foot.
  1. Write three fractions equivalent to $\frac{4}{9}$. Also find the fraction equivalent to $\frac{4}{9}$ whose denominator is 63.
  1. Is $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{5}$ more than 1 or less than 1? Find by how much.
  1. Sunita eats $\frac{2}{9}$ of a cake and her brother eats $\frac{1}{3}$ of it. Find the fraction of the cake they eat together. What fraction of the cake is left?
  1. Write $\frac{11}{3}$ and $\frac{17}{5}$ as mixed numbers. Hence find which of the two fractions is greater.
