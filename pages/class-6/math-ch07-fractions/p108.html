Example 18

Write $\frac{5}{6}$ as the sum of two different fractional units.

Solution.

Step 1 The largest fractional unit is $\frac{1}{2}$, and $\frac{1}{2} < \frac{5}{6}$ start with the largest
Step 2 $\dfrac{5}{6} - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{5}{6} - \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}$ what is left
Step 3 $\frac{1}{3}$ is a fractional unit, and it is not $\frac{1}{2}$
Answer $\frac{5}{6} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3}$

A sum like this is an Egyptian fraction, from the chapter’s Pinch of History.

Example 19

The perimeter of a triangle is $5\frac{1}{3}$ cm. Two of its sides are $1\frac{1}{2}$ cm and $2\frac{1}{4}$ cm long. How long is the third side?

Solution.

Step 1 The third side is the perimeter minus the other two sides perimeter is the sum
Step 2 $5\frac{1}{3} = \frac{16}{3}$, $\ 1\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$, $\ 2\frac{1}{4} = \frac{9}{4}$
Step 3 $\dfrac{16}{3} - \dfrac{3}{2} - \dfrac{9}{4} = \dfrac{64}{12} - \dfrac{18}{12} - \dfrac{27}{12} = \dfrac{19}{12}$ write in twelfths
Step 4 $\dfrac{19}{12} = \dfrac{12}{12} + \dfrac{7}{12} = 1\dfrac{7}{12}$
Answer $1\frac{7}{12}$ cm
