Example 10

A strip of paper is 1 m long. One edge is marked in eighths of a metre, and the other edge in sixths of a metre.

  1. Which two marks are at the same place along the strip?
    1. $\frac{3}{8}$ and $\frac{2}{6}$
    2. $\frac{4}{8}$ and $\frac{3}{6}$
    3. $\frac{6}{8}$ and $\frac{5}{6}$
    4. $\frac{2}{8}$ and $\frac{1}{6}$
  2. Numerical answer. Counting both ends, how many marks on one edge are at the same place as a mark on the other edge?
  3. Numerical answer. How far is the $\frac{5}{8}$ mark from the $\frac{5}{6}$ mark, in twenty-fourths of a metre?

Solution.

Step 1In twenty-fourths, eighths are at 0, 3, 6, …, 24 and sixths at 0, 4, 8, …, 24.
Step 2They meet at 0, 12 and 24: at 0, $\frac{1}{2}$ m and 1 m. So $\frac{4}{8} = \frac{3}{6}$.
Step 3$\frac{5}{8} = \frac{15}{24}$ and $\frac{5}{6} = \frac{20}{24}$: $20 - 15 = 5$.
Answer(i) (b); (ii) 3; (iii) 5
