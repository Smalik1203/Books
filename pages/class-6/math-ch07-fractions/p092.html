Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{3}{7} > \frac{3}{10}$.

    Reason (R): When the same number of things is shared among more people, each share is smaller.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{5} = \frac{3}{8}$.

    Reason (R): To add two fractions, first write them with a common denominator.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{15}{20}$ and $\frac{9}{12}$ are equivalent fractions.

    Reason (R): 20 and 12 are both multiples of 4.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{9}{4}$ lies between 2 and 3.

    Reason (R): A fraction is more than 1 only when its denominator is bigger than its numerator.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{24}{36}$ in lowest terms is $\frac{2}{3}$.

    Reason (R): 12 is the largest number that divides both 24 and 36.

Case-based questions

  1. A rangoli is drawn at the doorway of many homes at Diwali. Lakshmi draws her rangoli on a square grid of 16 equal squares. She colours 4 squares red, 6 squares yellow and 2 squares green, and leaves the other squares white.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. What fraction of the rangoli is red? Write it in lowest terms.
    2. What fraction of the rangoli is yellow or green?
    3. Is the white part of the rangoli more than, less than or equal to the red part? Justify your answer.

  1. Many families travel from Jaipur to Delhi by road and rail. Arun makes this journey. He travels $\frac{1}{2}$ of the distance by train and $\frac{1}{3}$ of it by bus. He travels the rest of the way by car.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. What fraction of the distance does he travel by train and bus together?
    2. What fraction of the distance does he travel by car?
    3. Is the bus part of the journey longer or shorter than the car part? Find the difference.
