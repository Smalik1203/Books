Example 3 · Single correct

Riya reads $\frac{2}{5}$ of a book on Monday and $\frac{1}{3}$ of the whole book on Tuesday. What fraction remains unread?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{3}{8}$
  2. $\frac{4}{15}$
  3. $\frac{7}{15}$
  4. $\frac{11}{15}$

Solution.

Step 1She has read $\frac{2}{5}$ + $\frac{1}{3}$ = $\frac{6}{15}$ + $\frac{5}{15}$ = $\frac{11}{15}$.
Step 2The remainder is 1 − $\frac{11}{15}$ = $\frac{4}{15}$.
Answer(b) $\frac{4}{15}$
Example 4 · Single correct

Which is the greatest fraction?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{7}{12}$
  2. $\frac{5}{8}$
  3. $\frac{2}{3}$
  4. $\frac{11}{16}$

Solution.

Step 1Using denominator 48, the fractions are $\frac{28}{48}$, $\frac{30}{48}$, $\frac{32}{48}$ and $\frac{33}{48}$.
Step 2The largest numerator is 33, so $\frac{11}{16}$ is greatest.
Answer(d) $\frac{11}{16}$
Example 5 · Single correct

A ribbon is $\frac{5}{6}$ m long. A piece of length $\frac{1}{4}$ m is cut off. What length remains?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{4}{2}$ m
  2. $\frac{7}{12}$ m
  3. $\frac{2}{3}$ m
  4. $\frac{13}{12}$ m

Solution.

Step 1Use twelfths: $\frac{5}{6}$ = $\frac{10}{12}$ and $\frac{1}{4}$ = $\frac{3}{12}$.
Step 2The remainder is $\frac{10}{12}$ − $\frac{3}{12}$ = $\frac{7}{12}$ m.
Answer(b) $\frac{7}{12}$ m
