Example 3

On a number line, each unit is cut into 4 equal parts. A bar starts at 0 and ends at the seventh mark after 0. How long is the bar? Is it longer than 1 unit?

Solution.

Step 1 Each part is $\frac{1}{4}$ unit long 4 equal parts
Step 2 The bar covers 7 parts: $7 \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{7}{4}$
Step 3 $\frac{7}{4} = \frac{4}{4} + \frac{3}{4} = 1 + \frac{3}{4}$ 4 quarters make 1
Answer The bar is $\frac{7}{4}$ units long, so it is longer than 1 unit

Type 2 · Mixed numbers

Example 4

Write $\frac{23}{6}$ as a mixed number.

Solution.

Step 1 6 sixths make 1 whole
Step 2 $23 = 18 + 5$ and $18 = 3 \times 6$ take out the wholes
Step 3 $\dfrac{23}{6} = \dfrac{18}{6} + \dfrac{5}{6} = 3 + \dfrac{5}{6}$
Answer $\dfrac{23}{6} = 3\dfrac{5}{6}$
Example 5

Write $5\frac{2}{7}$ as a fraction.

Solution.

Step 1 Each whole is 7 sevenths
Step 2 5 wholes are $5 \times 7 = 35$ sevenths
Step 3 $35 + 2 = 37$ sevenths add the 2 left over
Answer $5\frac{2}{7} = \frac{37}{7}$
