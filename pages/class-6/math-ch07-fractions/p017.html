Example 3

Compare $\frac{7}{9}$ and $\frac{17}{21}$.

Solution.

Step 1 63 is a common multiple of 9 and 21 choose the unit
Step 2 $\dfrac{7}{9} = \dfrac{7 \times 7}{9 \times 7} = \dfrac{49}{63}$
Step 3 $\dfrac{17}{21} = \dfrac{17 \times 3}{21 \times 3} = \dfrac{51}{63}$
Step 4 $\frac{49}{63} < \frac{51}{63}$ compare the numerators
Answer $\frac{7}{9} < \frac{17}{21}$

In both examples, the fractions were first written with the same denominator. After that, only the numerators had to be compared.

Comparing fractions
  • Write the fractions as equivalent fractions with the same denominator.
  • Then the fraction with the bigger numerator is greater.
