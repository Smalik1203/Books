7.8Addition and Subtraction of Fractions

Meena’s father made some chikki. Meena ate $\frac{1}{2}$ of it, and her younger brother ate $\frac{1}{4}$ of it. How much of the chikki did they eat together?

Cut the chikki into two halves. Meena ate one of them. Cut the other half into two equal pieces. Each of these is $\frac{1}{4}$ of the whole chikki, and her brother ate one. Fig. 7.17 shows the two pieces they ate.

$\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{4} = 3 \times \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{3}{4}$

Meena’s half is the same as two quarters. So together they ate three quarters. To add the two amounts, we first wrote both of them in the same unit, quarters.
