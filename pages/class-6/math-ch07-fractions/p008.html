Writing fractions greater than one as mixed numbers

You saw that $\frac{3}{2} = 1 + \frac{1}{2}$. Other fractions can be written the same way. Take $\frac{11}{4}$. Four quarters make a whole, so eight quarters make 2 wholes, and 3 quarters are left over.

$\dfrac{11}{4} = \dfrac{8}{4} + \dfrac{3}{4} = 2 + \dfrac{3}{4}$

We read this as two and three quarters, and we write the whole part and the fraction side by side.

Mixed numbers

A mixed number, or mixed fraction, has a whole number, called the whole part, and a fraction less than 1, called the fractional part. In $2\frac{3}{4}$, the whole part is 2 and the fractional part is $\frac{3}{4}$.

Writing a mixed number as a fraction

Jaya wants to write $3\frac{3}{4}$ as a fraction. She says, “$3\frac{3}{4}$ means $1 + 1 + 1 + \frac{3}{4}$. Each 1 is 4 quarters.”

$3\dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{4}{4} + \dfrac{4}{4} + \dfrac{4}{4} + \dfrac{3}{4}$

$= \dfrac{4 + 4 + 4 + 3}{4} = \dfrac{15}{4}$

So $3\frac{3}{4} = \frac{15}{4}$. The 3 wholes give $3 \times 4 = 12$ quarters, and 3 more quarters make 15.

Any mixed number can be changed in the same way. In $2\frac{2}{5}$, each whole is 5 fifths, so the 2 wholes are 10 fifths. Add the 2 fifths that are left over.
