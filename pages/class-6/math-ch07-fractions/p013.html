Writing a fraction in lowest terms

Lowest terms

A fraction is in lowest terms, or in its simplest form, when its numerator and denominator have no common factor except 1.

To write a fraction in lowest terms, find the equivalent fraction with the smallest numerator and denominator.

Example 1

Is $\frac{16}{20}$ in lowest terms? If not, write it in lowest terms.

Solution.

Step 1 Both 16 and 20 are divisible by 4 a common factor
Step 2 $\dfrac{16}{20} = \dfrac{16 \div 4}{20 \div 4} = \dfrac{4}{5}$ divide both by 4
Step 3 4 and 5 have no common factor except 1 nothing left to divide
Answer No. The simplest form of $\frac{16}{20}$ is $\frac{4}{5}$

You can also do it in steps. Both 36 and 60 are even, so divide both by 2. Keep going until no common factor is left.

$\dfrac{36}{60} = \dfrac{18}{30} = \dfrac{9}{15} = \dfrac{3}{5}$

The steps divide by 2, then by 2 again, then by 3. You could also notice that $36 = 3 \times 12$ and $60 = 5 \times 12$, and divide by 12 in one step. Both ways give $\frac{3}{5}$. Dividing by the largest common factor is quickest, but steps are often easier to spot.
