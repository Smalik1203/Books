$\dfrac{36}{60} = \dfrac{18}{30} = \dfrac{9}{15} = \dfrac{3}{5}$

The steps divide by 2, then by 2 again, then by 3. You could also notice that $36 = 3 \times 12$ and $60 = 5 \times 12$, and divide by 12 in one step. Both ways give $\frac{3}{5}$. Dividing by the largest common factor is quickest, but steps are often easier to spot.

7.7Comparing Fractions

Which is greater, $\frac{4}{5}$ or $\frac{7}{9}$? It is hard to tell by looking. So write both with the same denominator. 45 is a multiple of 5 and of 9, so use 45.

Example 1

Compare $\frac{4}{5}$ and $\frac{7}{9}$.

$\dfrac{4}{5} = \dfrac{4 \times 9}{5 \times 9} = \dfrac{36}{45}$

$\dfrac{7}{9} = \dfrac{7 \times 5}{9 \times 5} = \dfrac{35}{45}$

36 forty-fifths is more than 35 forty-fifths. So $\frac{4}{5} > \frac{7}{9}$.

The common denominator does not have to be the product of the two denominators. Any common multiple will do.

Example 2

Compare $\frac{7}{9}$ and $\frac{17}{21}$. 63 is a common multiple of 9 and 21.

$\dfrac{7}{9} = \dfrac{7 \times 7}{9 \times 7} = \dfrac{49}{63}$

$\dfrac{17}{21} = \dfrac{17 \times 3}{21 \times 3} = \dfrac{51}{63}$

Since $\frac{49}{63} < \frac{51}{63}$, we get $\frac{7}{9} < \frac{17}{21}$.
