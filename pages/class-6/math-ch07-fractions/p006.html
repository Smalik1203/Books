Reading fractions

We usually read $\frac{3}{4}$ as three quarters or three upon four. Reading it as 3 times $\frac{1}{4}$ tells you more. It shows the fractional unit, $\frac{1}{4}$, and how many of those units there are, 3.

Read other fractions in the same way. Each one is a number of fractional units.

$\dfrac{2}{5} = 2 \text{ times } \dfrac{1}{5}$

$\dfrac{7}{8} = 7 \text{ times } \dfrac{1}{8}$

$\dfrac{5}{3} = 5 \text{ times } \dfrac{1}{3}$

Numerator and denominator
  • In $\frac{5}{6}$, 5 is the numerator and 6 is the denominator.
  • The denominator names the unit. The numerator counts the units.
