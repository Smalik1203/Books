1. Many schools cook a mid-day meal for their students. In one school kitchen, the cook uses $2\frac{1}{2}$ kg of rice on Monday, $3\frac{1}{4}$ kg on Tuesday and $2\frac{3}{4}$ kg on Wednesday.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Write the amount used on Tuesday as a fraction.
    2. On which of the three days is the most rice used?
    3. Find the total amount of rice used in the three days, as a mixed number.

  1. Sugarcane juice is sold at stalls in many towns in summer. At his stall, Rafiq has a jug of 1 litre of juice. He pours $\frac{1}{4}$ litre into one glass and $\frac{1}{5}$ litre into another glass.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Which glass has more juice?
    2. How much juice has he poured in all?
    3. How much juice is left in the jug? Can he fill two more glasses of $\frac{1}{4}$ litre each? Justify your answer.

  1. Kites are flown all over Gujarat at Makar Sankranti. Tara has a reel of 10 m of kite thread. She uses $3\frac{1}{2}$ m of it on Monday and $4\frac{2}{3}$ m of it on Tuesday.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Write $4\frac{2}{3}$ as a fraction.
    2. On which day does she use more thread?
    3. Find the length of thread left on the reel, as a mixed number.
