Example 12

Rani walks $1\frac{1}{2}$ km to the market, and then $2\frac{2}{3}$ km to her aunt’s house. How far does she walk in all?

Solution.

Step 1 $1\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$ and $2\frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{3}$ mixed numbers as fractions
Step 2 $\dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{8}{3} = \dfrac{9}{6} + \dfrac{16}{6} = \dfrac{25}{6}$ write in sixths
Step 3 $\dfrac{25}{6} = \dfrac{24}{6} + \dfrac{1}{6} = 4\dfrac{1}{6}$ 24 sixths make 4
Answer $4\frac{1}{6}$ km
Example 13

A jug holds $2\frac{1}{4}$ litres of milk. Meera pours out $\frac{3}{4}$ litre, and then $\frac{2}{3}$ litre more. How much milk is left in the jug?

Solution.

Step 1 $2\dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{9}{4} - \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{6}{4} = \dfrac{3}{2}$ the first pour
Step 2 $\dfrac{3}{2} - \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{9}{6} - \dfrac{4}{6} = \dfrac{5}{6}$ the second pour
Step 3 $\dfrac{5}{6} + \dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{10}{12} + \dfrac{8}{12} + \dfrac{9}{12} = \dfrac{27}{12}$ check by pouring back
Answer $\frac{5}{6}$ litre

The check works: $\frac{27}{12} = 2\frac{3}{12} = 2\frac{1}{4}$ litres, which is what the jug held at the start.

Example 14

What must be added to $\frac{5}{8}$ to make $1\frac{1}{4}$?

Solution.

Step 1 $1\dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{5}{4} = \dfrac{10}{8}$ write in eighths
Step 2 $\dfrac{10}{8} - \dfrac{5}{8} = \dfrac{5}{8}$ take away what is there
Step 3 $\dfrac{5}{8} + \dfrac{5}{8} = \dfrac{10}{8}$ check by adding
Answer $\frac{5}{8}$
