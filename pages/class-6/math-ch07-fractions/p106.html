Example 13 · Numerical answer

A rope is 6 m long. How many pieces of length $\frac{3}{4}$ m can be cut from it, with no waste?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Six metres contains 24 quarter-metre units.
Step 2Each piece uses three such units, so 24 ÷ 3 = 8 pieces.
Answer8
