$\dfrac{36}{60} = \dfrac{18}{30} = \dfrac{9}{15} = \dfrac{3}{5}$

The steps divide by 2, then by 2 again, then by 3. You could also notice that $36 = 3 \times 12$ and $60 = 5 \times 12$, and divide by 12 in one step. Both ways give $\frac{3}{5}$. Dividing by the largest common factor is quickest, but steps are often easier to spot.

7.7Comparing Fractions

Which is greater, $\frac{4}{5}$ or $\frac{7}{9}$? It is hard to tell by looking. So write both with the same denominator. 45 is a multiple of 5 and of 9, so use 45. Then the two fractions count the same unit, forty-fifths, and you only need to compare the numerators.

Example 2

Compare $\frac{4}{5}$ and $\frac{7}{9}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{4}{5} = \dfrac{4 \times 9}{5 \times 9} = \dfrac{36}{45}$ write in forty-fifths
Step 2 $\dfrac{7}{9} = \dfrac{7 \times 5}{9 \times 5} = \dfrac{35}{45}$
Step 3 36 forty-fifths is more than 35 forty-fifths compare the numerators
Answer $\frac{4}{5} > \frac{7}{9}$

The common denominator does not have to be the product of the two denominators. Any common multiple will do. For 9 and 21 the product is 189, but 63 is also a common multiple, and it is smaller and easier to use.
