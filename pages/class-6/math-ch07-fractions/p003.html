Think and Reflect
  1. The pieces in the two ways have different shapes. Are they the same size? How can you tell?
  2. Can you find a third way of cutting the chikki into 6 equal pieces?

The chikki can also be broken into 3 equal pieces, each 2 squares wide and 4 squares tall. Three such pieces make the whole, so each piece is $\frac{1}{3}$ chikki. To name a piece, ask how many pieces of that size make the whole.
