Adding fractions with the same denominator

To find $\frac{2}{5} + \frac{1}{5}$, draw two strips, each cut into 5 equal parts. Shade 2 parts of one and 1 part of the other, as in the top row of Fig. 7.15. Every part is $\frac{1}{5}$, and 3 parts are shaded in all. So $\frac{2}{5} + \frac{1}{5} = \frac{3}{5}$.

Example 4

Find $\frac{4}{7} + \frac{6}{7}$.

Solution.

Step 1 Both fractions count sevenths the same unit
Step 2 Together there are $4 + 6 = 10$ sevenths, as the bottom row of Fig. 7.15 shows
Step 3 $\dfrac{4}{7} + \dfrac{6}{7} = \dfrac{10}{7} = 1 + \dfrac{3}{7}$ 7 sevenths make 1
Answer $1\dfrac{3}{7}$
