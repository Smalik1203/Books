7.6Equivalent Fractions

Equal lengths on a fraction wall

Look again at the folded strips in Fig. 7.6. The piece $\frac{1}{2}$ is exactly as long as two pieces of $\frac{1}{4}$, and also as long as four pieces of $\frac{1}{8}$. So $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{4}{8}$.

Equivalent fractions

Fractions that stand for the same length, or the same share, are equivalent fractions. They use different fractional units.

Strips cut into 3, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10 equal parts can be added below the others. Laid one under another, they make a fraction wall, like the one in Fig. 7.15. Make your own wall with paper strips. Then use it to check whether $\frac{1}{3}$ and $\frac{2}{6}$ are equivalent.
