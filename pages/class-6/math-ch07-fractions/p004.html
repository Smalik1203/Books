Naming a piece of a whole
  • If 4 pieces of the same size make the whole, each piece is $\frac{1}{4}$ of the whole.
  • Pieces of different shapes are the same fraction when they are the same size.

7.3Measuring Using Fractional Units

Take a strip of paper, and call its length one unit. Fold it into two equal parts and open it out again. The crease cuts the strip into two parts, and each is $\frac{1}{2}$ unit long.

Fold the strip in half, and then in half again. Now there are 4 equal parts, and each is $\frac{1}{4}$ unit long. Fold it once more to get 8 equal parts. Fig. 7.6 shows all four strips.

Count the quarters from the left. Two quarters make a length of $\frac{2}{4}$, three quarters make $\frac{3}{4}$, and four quarters make $\frac{4}{4}$, which is the whole strip.
