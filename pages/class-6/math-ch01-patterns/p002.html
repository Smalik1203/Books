1.2Patterns in Numbers

Some of the most important patterns in mathematics are patterns in the whole numbers:

$0,\ 1,\ 2,\ 3,\ 4,\ 5,\ \ldots$

The three dots at the end mean that the numbers go on for ever. The part of mathematics that studies patterns in whole numbers is called number theory.

A list of numbers made by following a rule is called a number sequence. Table 1.1 shows ten number sequences. You will meet all of them again, in this chapter and in later ones.

Table 1.1 Ten number sequences
Number sequences
All 1s
$1,\ 1,\ 1,\ 1,\ 1,\ 1,\ 1,\ \ldots$		Counting numbers
$1,\ 2,\ 3,\ 4,\ 5,\ 6,\ 7,\ \ldots$
Odd numbers
$1,\ 3,\ 5,\ 7,\ 9,\ 11,\ 13,\ \ldots$		Even numbers
$2,\ 4,\ 6,\ 8,\ 10,\ 12,\ 14,\ \ldots$
Triangular numbers
$1,\ 3,\ 6,\ 10,\ 15,\ 21,\ 28,\ \ldots$		Square numbers
$1,\ 4,\ 9,\ 16,\ 25,\ 36,\ 49,\ \ldots$
Cube numbers
$1,\ 8,\ 27,\ 64,\ 125,\ 216,\ \ldots$		Virahānka numbers
$1,\ 2,\ 3,\ 5,\ 8,\ 13,\ 21,\ \ldots$
Powers of 2
$1,\ 2,\ 4,\ 8,\ 16,\ 32,\ 64,\ \ldots$		Powers of 3
$1,\ 3,\ 9,\ 27,\ 81,\ 243,\ 729,\ \ldots$

The Virahānka numbers are named after Virahānka, a scholar who studied the rhythms of poetry. He wrote about these numbers in India more than 1300 years ago.
