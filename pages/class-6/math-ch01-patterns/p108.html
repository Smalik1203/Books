Example 18

The first shape of the Koch snowflake has 3 straight lines. At each step, every straight line becomes 4 straight lines. How many straight lines does the 5th shape have?

Solution.

Step 1 1st shape: 3 lines
Step 2 $3 \times 4 = 12$, $\ 12 \times 4 = 48$ 2nd and 3rd shapes
Step 3 $48 \times 4 = 192$, $\ 192 \times 4 = 768$ 4th and 5th shapes
Answer 768
Example 19

Every corner of a regular hexagon is joined by a straight line to every other corner. How many lines are there? How many of them are not sides of the hexagon?

Solution. The 6 corners and the lines joining them make a complete graph with 6 points.

Step 1 6 points have 15 lines the 5th triangular number
Step 2 6 of these lines are the sides
Step 3 $15 - 6 = 9$
Answer 15 lines, and 9 of them are not sides

Check the method on smaller shapes. A triangle has 3 corners and 3 lines, and all 3 lines are sides. A square has 4 corners and 6 lines: the 4 sides and the 2 lines that cross its middle. A pentagon has 5 corners and 10 lines, so 5 of its lines are not sides.
