Example 10

A child builds a staircase from blocks. The first step is 1 block, the second step is a column of 2 blocks, the third is a column of 3 blocks, and so on.

  1. How many blocks are in a staircase of 6 steps?
    1. 21
    2. 36
    3. 18
    4. 28
  2. Numerical answer. How many blocks are in a staircase of 15 steps?

Solution.

Step 1The blocks make the triangular numbers: $1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 = 21$.
Step 2Two staircases of 15 steps fit together as a rectangle of $15 \times 16 = 240$ blocks. One staircase is half of that: 120.
Answer(i) (a) 21; (ii) 120
