1.6Shapes That Count

Shape sequences are often connected to number sequences. When you know one, it can help you understand the other.

Look at the regular polygons in Fig. 1.8. The numbers of sides are 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, … This is the sequence of counting numbers, starting at 3. It is also where the names come from: a triangle has 3 sides, a quadrilateral 4, a pentagon 5, a hexagon 6, a heptagon 7, an octagon 8, a nonagon 9 and a decagon 10.

The word regular means that all the sides of the shape have the same length, and all its corners are the same too. A regular quadrilateral is a square. The size of a corner is called an angle, and you will learn about angles in Chapter 2.
