Look at how each shape grows from the one before it. Some of these sequences, such as the regular polygons and the stacked squares, can be drawn for as long as you like. The Koch snowflake is different. Its edge gets longer and more crinkly at every step, but the whole snowflake never grows bigger than a circle drawn around the first triangle.
