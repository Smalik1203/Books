The whole picture is a square with 6 rows of 6 dots, which is 36 dots. So

$1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 + 11 = 36$

You can draw the same picture for a square of any size. Each new L-shape adds a row and a column, turning each square into the next. That is why adding the odd numbers from 1 always gives a square number, and why the pattern never stops.

Odd numbers and squares
  • Adding the odd numbers in order from 1 always gives a square number.
  • The first 3 odd numbers give $1 + 3 + 5 = 9$, which is $3 \times 3$.

Adding up and then down

Here is a second pattern. This time, do not stop when you reach the biggest number. Add the counting numbers up to some number, and then add them back down again to 1. Count the biggest number only once. Here are the first few lines:

$1 = 1$

$1 + 2 + 1 = 4$

$1 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 9$

$1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 16$

$1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 25$
