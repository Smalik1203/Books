  1. A gardener lays square tiles to make a square path. To go from one square to the next, she lays an L-shape of tiles along two of its sides. Her squares have 1, 4, 9, 16, … tiles, and the L-shapes she adds have 3, 5, 7, … tiles.

    1. How many tiles are in the L-shape that turns the square of 16 tiles into the next square?
    2. How many tiles are in the square with 12 tiles along a side?
