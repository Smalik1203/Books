In Questions 13 to 16, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): 36 dots can be arranged as a square.

    Reason (R): 36 is the sum of the first 6 odd numbers.

  1. Assertion (A): 15 is a triangular number.

    Reason (R): 15 is an odd number.

  1. Assertion (A): Every square number is also a triangular number.

    Reason (R): 36 is both a square number and a triangular number.

  1. Assertion (A): The sum of the first 7 odd numbers is 49.

    Reason (R): 49 is a triangular number.
