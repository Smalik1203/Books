  1. A shopkeeper stacks tins in rows: 1 tin in the top row, 2 in the row under it, 3 in the next, and so on. Every row must be full. She has 60 tins. Find the number of full rows she can make and the number of tins left over. How many more tins does she need to finish one more row?
  1. A pattern of dots has 1 dot in the centre. The first ring around it has 6 dots, the second ring 12 dots, the third ring 18 dots, and so on. Find the number of dots in the pattern with 5 rings. Show that this number is 1 more than 6 times a triangular number. Hence find the number of dots in the pattern with 10 rings.
  1. The Koch snowflake of Fig. 1.11 begins as a triangle with 3 straight lines. At each step, every straight line is replaced by 4 shorter ones. Find the number of straight lines in the 5th shape. Which shape is the first to have more than 10 000 straight lines?
  1. Riya saves ₹1 on the first day, ₹2 on the second day, ₹4 on the third day, and so on, saving double each day. Find the amount she saves on the 10th day, and her total savings over the 10 days. On which day do her total savings first go above ₹1000?
  1. Columns of blocks go up and then down: they hold 1, 2, 3, … blocks up to the tallest column, and then 3, 2, 1 blocks again. Draw the columns when the tallest has 4 blocks, and show that the total is a square number. Hence find the number of blocks when the tallest column has 15 blocks. How tall is the tallest column when 400 blocks are used?
  1. Hexagons in a row are made with matchsticks: 1 hexagon needs 6 matchsticks, 2 hexagons need 11 and 3 hexagons need 16. Find the number of matchsticks needed for 25 hexagons. Show that the number of matchsticks is always 1 more than 5 times the number of hexagons. Hence find how many hexagons can be made with 100 matchsticks, and how many matchsticks are left over.
  1. The stacked triangles shape with 1 row has 1 small triangle, with 2 rows has 4, and with 3 rows has 9. For the shape with 10 rows, find the number of small triangles in its bottom row and in the whole shape. How many of the small triangles in the whole shape point upwards? Show your working.
