A cube is built from 64 small cubes, and its six outside faces are painted. Which statements are correct?

  1. Each edge has 8 small cubes.
  2. Each layer has 16 small cubes.
  3. Exactly 8 small cubes have no paint.
  4. Exactly 56 small cubes have paint.

Solution.

Step 164 = 4 × 4 × 4, so each edge has 4 cubes and each layer has 16.
Step 2The unpainted interior is 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 cubes; 64 − 8 = 56 have paint.

Example 5

Find the sum 11 + 13 + 15 + … + 29.

Solution.

Step 1The sum of the first 15 odd numbers is 225.
Step 2Remove the first five odd numbers, whose sum is 25: 225 − 25 = 200.
CheckPair them from the ends: 11 + 29 = 40, and 10 numbers make 5 pairs, so 5 × 40 = 200.
Answer200
