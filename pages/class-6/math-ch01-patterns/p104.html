Type 3 · Adding odd numbers

Example 6

Find $1 + 3 + 5 + \cdots + 49$ without adding the numbers one by one.

Solution. First find how many odd numbers are being added.

Step 1 $(49 + 1) \div 2 = 25$ add 1, then halve
Step 2 49 is the 25th odd number
Step 3 25 odd numbers make a square of 25 rows of 25
Step 4 $25 \times 25 = 625$
Answer 625

Adding 1 and halving works because the odd numbers are 1 less than the even numbers: 49 is 1 less than 50, and 50 is the 25th even number.

Example 7

Salma adds the odd numbers in order, starting from 1, and stops when her total is 121. What is the last number she adds?

Solution.

Step 1 $121 = 11 \times 11$ a square of 11 rows
Step 2 so she adds the first 11 odd numbers
Step 3 the 11th odd number is $2 \times 11 - 1 = 21$
Answer 21
Example 8

Find $11 + 13 + 15 + \cdots + 29$.

Solution. The sum does not start at 1. Start it at 1, and then take away what was added.

Step 1 29 is the 15th odd number, so $1 + 3 + \cdots + 29 = 15 \times 15 = 225$
Step 2 9 is the 5th odd number, so $1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 = 5 \times 5 = 25$ the part to remove
Step 3 $225 - 25 = 200$
Answer 200

In the picture of L-shapes, this is a square of 15 rows with a square of 5 rows cut from its corner.
