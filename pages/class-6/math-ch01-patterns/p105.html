Example 6

Every team in a tournament plays every other team exactly once. There are 15 matches. How many teams take part?

Solution.

Step 1With 6 teams, the numbers of new pairings are 5, 4, 3, 2, 1.
Step 2Their sum is 15. Adding any further team increases the count, so there are exactly 6 teams.
Check15 = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 is a triangular number, as a handshake count must be.
