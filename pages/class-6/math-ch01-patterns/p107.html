Why the other options are wrong

B1 The L-shapes in Fig. 1.5 build a square of 6 rows of 6 dots out of 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11 dots. So R is exactly the reason that 36 dots make a square, which gives (a). Option (b) would fit only if R were true but had nothing to do with A.
B3 A is false. 4 is a square number, but the triangular numbers go 1, 3, 6, 10, so 4 is not one of them. R is true. One number that is both kinds does not make every square number a triangular number, so the answer is (d).
B6 There are 4 sides with 10 dots each, which makes 40. But each corner dot sits on two sides, so it has been counted twice. Take away the 4 extra corner dots to get 36, option (d). Option (a) forgets about the corners, and option (b) counts every dot in the square, not just the edge.
