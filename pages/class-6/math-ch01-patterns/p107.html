Type 6 · Counting in shape sequences

Example 15

Meena bends a wire 72 cm long into a regular polygon. Every side is 8 cm long. Which polygon of Fig. 1.7 has she made? She then bends the same wire into a regular hexagon. How long is each side now?

Solution. Every side of a regular polygon has the same length.

Step 1 $72 \div 8 = 9$ sides
Step 2 a regular polygon with 9 sides is a nonagon nona means 9
Step 3 a hexagon has 6 sides: $72 \div 6 = 12$ cm hexa means 6
Answer a regular nonagon; each side of the hexagon is 12 cm
Example 16

In a kho-kho tournament, every team plays every other team once. 15 matches are played in all. How many teams are there?

Solution. Draw each team as a point, and each match as a line joining two points. The picture is a complete graph.

Step 1 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 points have 1, 3, 6, 10, 15 lines triangular numbers
Step 2 15 lines go with 6 points
Answer 6 teams

The same count answers any question in which every pair meets once: handshakes at a party, matches in a league, or lines joining points. Each new point is joined to every point already there, so the counts grow by 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, …, which is how the triangular numbers grow.

Example 17

A shape in the stacked triangles is made of 64 small triangles. How many rows does it have? How many of its small triangles point up?

Solution.

Step 1 the rows hold 1, 3, 5, 7, … small triangles the odd numbers
Step 2 so the total is a square: $64 = 8 \times 8$, which means 8 rows
Step 3 the rows hold 1, 2, 3, …, 8 triangles that point up
Step 4 $1 + 2 + \cdots + 8 = 36$
Answer 8 rows; 36 triangles point up

The other $64 - 36 = 28$ triangles point down, and 28 is the triangular number just before 36.
