Example 9

A king puts 1 grain of rice on the first square of a board, 2 grains on the second, 4 on the third, and so on, doubling the grains on each new square.

  1. How many grains are on the 8th square?
    1. 64
    2. 128
    3. 256
    4. 16
  2. Numerical answer. How many grains are on the first 8 squares together?

Solution.

Step 1The squares hold the powers of 2: 1, 2, 4, 8, and so on. The 8th holds 128.
Step 2The powers of 2 up to 128 add to one less than 256: $256 - 1 = 255$.
Answer(i) (b) 128; (ii) 255
