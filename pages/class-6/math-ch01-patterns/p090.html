By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Find the 12th number of the sequence 4, 7, 10, 13, … Show how you found it.
  1. Is 100 a triangular number? Give a reason.
  1. Find the sum of the first 15 odd numbers without adding them.
  1. Write the rule of the sequence 1, 3, 9, 27, … and find its next two numbers.
  1. The first few odd numbers, starting from 1, add up to 169. How many odd numbers were added?
  1. Is 64 both a square number and a cube number? Give a reason.
  1. Find $1 + 2 + 3 + \cdots + 9 + \cdots + 3 + 2 + 1$ without adding term by term. Give a reason.
  1. A complete graph has 7 points. Find the number of lines it has, and show how you found it.
  1. The 5th and 6th Virahānka numbers are 8 and 13. Find the next two.
  1. A regular polygon has a perimeter of 36 cm, and each side is 4 cm long. Find the number of its sides, and name the polygon.
Short answer
  1. Arjun arranges chairs in rows: 1 chair in the first row, 3 in the second, 5 in the third, and so on. Find the number of chairs in the 10th row. Also find the number of chairs in the first 10 rows together.
  1. Explain, with a picture of dots, why adding a triangular number to the next one gives a square number. Use $6 + 10$ as your example.
  1. Find $21 + 23 + 25 + \cdots + 39$ without adding term by term.
  1. A sheet of paper is folded in half again and again. After 1 fold it is 2 layers thick, and after 2 folds it is 4 layers thick. Find the number of layers after 8 folds. After how many folds is it first more than 1000 layers thick?
  1. Triangles in a row are made with matchsticks: 1 triangle needs 3 matchsticks, 2 triangles need 5 and 3 triangles need 7. Find the number of matchsticks needed for 20 triangles. How many triangles can be made with 51 matchsticks?
  1. Write the rule of the sequence 2, 5, 8, 11, … and find its 10th number. Is 50 in the sequence? Justify your answer.
  1. Find $1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + \cdots + 128$ without adding. Explain the pattern you used.
