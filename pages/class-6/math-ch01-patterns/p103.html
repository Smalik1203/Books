Example 2

A wire 72 cm long forms a regular polygon with side 8 cm. It is then reshaped into a regular hexagon. Which pair gives the original polygon and the new side length?

  1. Octagon; 12 cm
  2. Nonagon; 12 cm
  3. Nonagon; 8 cm
  4. Decagon; 12 cm

Solution.

Step 1The original polygon has 72 ÷ 8 = 9 sides, so it is a nonagon.
Step 2The hexagon has six equal sides, each 72 ÷ 6 = 12 cm.
Answer(b) Nonagon; 12 cm

Example 3

A large upward-pointing triangle is divided into 8 rows of equal small triangles. The rows contain 1, 3, 5, … small triangles. Which statements are correct?

  1. There are 64 small triangles.
  2. 36 of them point upwards.
  3. 36 of them point downwards.
  4. The bottom row has 15.

Solution.

Step 1The total is the sum of the first 8 odd numbers: 8 × 8 = 64.
Step 2The upward-pointing triangles number 1 + 2 + … + 8 = 36, leaving 28 downward-pointing triangles.
Step 3The eighth odd number is 2 × 8 − 1 = 15.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
