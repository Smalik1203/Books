Example 3

Find the 12th Virahānka number.

Solution. Each Virahānka number is the sum of the two numbers just before it.

Step 1 the first seven are 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21 from Table 1.1
Step 2 $13 + 21 = 34$, $\ 21 + 34 = 55$, $\ 34 + 55 = 89$
Step 3 $55 + 89 = 144$, $\ 89 + 144 = 233$
Answer 233

Example 4

A triangle of dots has 11 dots in its bottom row. How many dots does it have? How many more dots make the next triangle?

Solution.

Step 1 its rows have 1, 2, 3, …, 11 dots
Step 2 two copies make a rectangle: $11 \times 12 = 132$ 11 rows of 12
Step 3 one triangle: $132 \div 2 = 66$
Step 4 the next triangle adds a row of 12: $66 + 12 = 78$
Answer 66 dots; 12 more dots make the next triangle
Example 5

A hexagon of dots is drawn like the hexagonal numbers 1, 7, 19, 37, …, with 6 dots along each side. How many dots does it have?

Solution. Build the hexagon from the centre, one ring at a time.

Step 1 the centre is 1 dot
Step 2 the first ring has 6 dots, and each ring has 6 more than the one inside it
Step 3 rings with 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 dots on a side: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30
Step 4 $1 + 6 + 12 + 18 + 24 + 30 = 91$
Answer 91 dots

Check with the sequence: $1 + 6 = 7$, $\ 7 + 12 = 19$, $\ 19 + 18 = 37$. The rings give the hexagonal numbers in order.
