1. In a poem, a short sound (S) takes 1 beat and a long sound (L) takes 2 beats. A line of 1 beat can be filled in 1 way, a line of 2 beats in 2 ways (SS or L), and a line of 3 beats in 3 ways. List the ways of filling a line of 4 beats, and show that their number is the sum of the numbers for 2 and 3 beats. Hence find the number of ways for a line of 8 beats.
  1. At a meeting, every person shakes hands once with every other person. Find the number of handshakes when 8 people meet. Show that when one more person joins, the number of handshakes goes up by the number of people already there. Hence find the number of people at a meeting with 66 handshakes.
  1. Two copies of a triangle of dots fit together to make a rectangle of dots. Draw this for the triangle with 4 rows, and write the number of rows and columns of the rectangle. Use this to find the 20th triangular number and the 50th triangular number.
Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): 36 dots can be arranged as a square.

    Reason (R): 36 is the sum of the first 6 odd numbers.

  1. Assertion (A): 15 is a triangular number.

    Reason (R): 15 is an odd number.

  1. Assertion (A): $1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + 16 = 31$.

    Reason (R): The powers of 2, starting from 1, always add up to 1 less than the next power of 2.

  1. Assertion (A): The sum of the first 7 odd numbers is 49.

    Reason (R): 49 is a triangular number.

  1. Assertion (A): Every square number is also a triangular number.

    Reason (R): 36 is both a square number and a triangular number.

Case-based questions

  1. Kolam is drawn at the doorway of many homes in the south of India. Meena draws a kolam of dots in the shape of a triangle: 1 dot in the top row, 2 in the next, 3 in the next, and so on. Her kolam has 8 rows.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many dots are in the bottom row?
    2. How many dots are there in the whole kolam?
    3. Her sister draws a kolam of 55 dots in the same pattern. How many rows does it have? Show how you found it.
