The Virahānka numbers are named after Virahānka, a scholar who studied the rhythms of poetry. He wrote about these numbers in India more than 1300 years ago. They turn up in nature too. Look again at the sunflower on page 1: its seeds lie in spirals, some turning one way and some the other, and the two numbers of spirals are almost always two Virahānka numbers that sit next to each other in the list.
