1. On sports day, a school has 6 house teams. In the tug-of-war, every team pulls against every other team once. Mr Rao, the sports teacher, has to plan the order of the matches.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many matches does each team play?
    2. How many matches are there in all?
    3. Next year the school will have 8 house teams. How many more matches will Mr Rao have to plan? Show your working.

  1. Farhan lays square tiles as the base of a rangoli. He starts with 1 tile. To make the next square, he adds an L-shape of tiles along two of its sides. The L-shapes he adds have 3, 5, 7, … tiles.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many tiles are in the L-shape added to the square of 16 tiles?
    2. How many tiles are in the square with 12 tiles along each side?
    3. Farhan has 200 tiles. Find the biggest square he can make, and the number of tiles left over.

  1. On Monday, Anil teaches a new song to 2 friends. On every day after that, each person who learnt the song the day before teaches it to 2 new friends.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many people learn the song on Wednesday?
    2. Name the sequence that the numbers of new learners make.
    3. Counting Anil, how many people know the song after Saturday?

  1. Astronomers in India divided the sky into 27 parts, called nakshatras. The moon moves into the next nakshatra about once a day, and comes back to the same nakshatra after about 27 days.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. The moon is in a nakshatra on day 1. On which day is it next back there?
    2. After about how many days has the moon come back to it 4 times?
    3. On which of these days is the moon in the day 1 nakshatra again: day 50, day 55, day 82? Justify your answer.
