1.3Numbers as Pictures

Many number sequences can be drawn. To draw a number, arrange that many dots or small cubes in a shape. A picture often shows a pattern more clearly than a list of numbers does, and it can show things the number alone hides. For example, 6 dots can be arranged as a triangle, but 7 dots cannot.
