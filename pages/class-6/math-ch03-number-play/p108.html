Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) One Collatz step from 18(1) 0
(Q) One Collatz step from 9(2) 999
(R) One Kaprekar step from 2111(3) 28
(S) One Kaprekar step from 5555(4) 9

Each entry has exactly one match

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–3, R–2, S–4

Solution.

Step 118 ÷ 2 = 9 and 3 × 9 + 1 = 28.
Step 22111 − 1112 = 999; 5555 − 5555 = 0. Leading zeros are retained when arranging digits.
Answer(b) P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
