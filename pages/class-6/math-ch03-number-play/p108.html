C4 A player can add up to 10, so the key number is 11. Whatever the other player adds, you add enough to make 11 in that turn. The first player cannot say 11 at once, so the second player wins by saying 11, 22, 33 and so on up to 99, option (c).
C5 An ordinary year moves the first day of the next year on by 1 day, and a leap year by 2. From Thursday 2026, the years start Fri, Sat, Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu (2032), Sat, Sun, Mon, Tue, Thu (2037). 2032 starts on a Thursday, but it is a leap year, so its calendar is different from March on. 2037 is the answer, option (c).
C6 Supercells cannot sit next to each other. The most cells with no two next to each other are the 4 corners and the centre. The grid 9, 1, 8 and 2, 7, 3 and 6, 4, 5 has all 5 as supercells, so the answer is (b).

Most of these questions did not need long working. They needed you to notice one fact: that neighbours cannot both be bigger, that a palindrome is fixed by half of its digits, or that the smallest and largest cases set the limits. Once you saw the fact, the answer followed quickly.
