Example 15

You add a 4-digit number and a 3-digit number. How many digits can the sum have?

Solution.

Step 1 Smallest sum: $1000 + 100 = 1100$, which has 4 digits. smallest plus smallest
Step 2 Largest sum: $9999 + 999 = 10{,}998$, which has 5 digits. largest plus largest
Step 3 Any other sum, such as $4000 + 500 = 4500$, lies in between.
Answer The sum has either 4 digits or 5 digits, and never 3 or 6.
Example 16

A square pattern has 5 rows of 5 boxes. Every box holds 30, except the middle box, which holds 80. Find the total.

Solution.

Step 1 if all $5 \times 5 = 25$ boxes held 30, the total would be $25 \times 30 = 750$ all the same first
Step 2 the middle box holds $80 - 30 = 50$ more
Answer $750 + 50 = 800$

Example 17

How many steps does the Collatz rule take to reach 1 from 24? How many from 48?

Solution.

Step 1 24 is even, so the next number is 12
Step 2 sequence (a) in Section 3.10 starts at 12 and takes 9 steps already worked out
Step 3 so 24 takes $1 + 9 = 10$ steps
Step 4 48 is even, and half of 48 is 24, so 48 takes $1 + 10 = 11$ steps
Answer 10 steps from 24, and 11 steps from 48
