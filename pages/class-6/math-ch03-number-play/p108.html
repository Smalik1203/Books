Example 9

Eight houses stand in a row along a road. From left to right, their heights in metres are 6, 9, 4, 7, 12, 5, 8 and 3. A house is a lookout if it is taller than each house next to it.

  1. How many lookouts are there?
    1. 2
    2. 3
    3. 4
    4. 5
  2. Numerical answer. The house that is 7 m high is made taller by a whole number of metres. By at least how many metres must it grow to become a lookout?
  3. Numerical answer. After that change, how many lookouts are there?

Solution.

Step 19 is taller than 6 and 4, 12 is taller than 7 and 5, and 8 is taller than 5 and 3. The two end houses each have a taller neighbour.
Step 2The house must be taller than 4 and 12, so at least 13 m high: $13 - 7 = 6$ m more.
Step 3The heights are now 6, 9, 4, 13, 12, 5, 8, 3. The lookouts are 9, 13 and 8, because 12 now has a taller neighbour.
CheckA house 12 m high would not do: it would not be taller than its neighbour of 12 m.
Answer(i) (b) 3; (ii) 6; (iii) 3
