Type 7 · The Collatz rule

Example 13

How many steps does the Collatz rule take to reach 1 from 24? How many from 48?

Solution.

Step 1 24 is even, so the next number is 12
Step 2 sequence (a) in Section 3.10 starts at 12 and takes 9 steps already worked out
Step 3 so 24 takes $1 + 9 = 10$ steps
Step 4 48 is even, and half of 48 is 24, so 48 takes $1 + 10 = 11$ steps
Answer 10 steps from 24, and 11 steps from 48

Type 8 · Estimation

Example 14

A train has 18 coaches, and each coach seats 72 people. About how many people can the train seat?

Solution.

Step 1 18 is close to 20, and 72 is close to 70 easy numbers
Step 2 $20 \times 70 = 1400$
Answer about 1400 people

The exact number is $18 \times 72 = 1296$. The estimate is a little high, because 18 was taken as 20.

Type 9 · Winning strategies

Example 15

Two players start at 0 and take turns adding 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 to the total. The first player to reach 40 wins. Which player can always win, and which numbers should that player say?

Solution.

Step 1 whatever the other player adds, you can add enough to make 6 in that turn 1 more than 5
Step 2 so count back from 40 in 6s: 34, 28, 22, 16, 10, 4
Step 3 4 can be said on the very first turn 4 is allowed
Answer the first player, by saying 4, 10, 16, 22, 28, 34 and 40
