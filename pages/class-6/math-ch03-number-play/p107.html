Why the other options are wrong

A1 12 beats its one neighbour, 7. 15 beats 7 and 3. 10 beats its one neighbour, 9. So the answer is (a). Option (b) forgets that a cell at the end has only one neighbour, so 10 is a supercell too.
A4 A 2-digit number has a digit sum of at most $9 + 9 = 18$, so 3 digits are needed. The first digit should be as small as possible, so the others must be 9s: 299, option (d). Option (a) uses the same digits but puts the 9s first, which makes the largest such number.
B1 The largest number in a row is bigger than everything, so it is bigger than its neighbours too. R is the reason A is true, which gives (a).
