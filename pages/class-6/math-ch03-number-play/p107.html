Example 13 · Numerical answer

A game starts at 0. Two players alternately add 1, 2 or 3 to the total. The player who first reaches exactly 22 wins. What should the first player add to guarantee a win with correct play?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Start with 2, then reply to each opposing move with enough to make the pair of moves total 4.
Step 2The first player then reaches 2, 6, 10, 14, 18 and finally 22.
