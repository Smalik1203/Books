Example 10

A 4-digit palindrome has a digit sum of 18. Its thousands digit is double its hundreds digit. Find the number.

Solution.

Step 1 the units digit equals the thousands digit, and the tens digit equals the hundreds digit it is a palindrome
Step 2 so the thousands and hundreds digits add up to $18 \div 2 = 9$ each digit appears twice
Step 3 two digits that add up to 9, one double the other, are 6 and 3 $6 = 2 \times 3$
Answer 6336, and $6 + 3 + 3 + 6 = 18$

Example 11

Start with 1000. How many Kaprekar rounds does it take to reach 6174?

Solution. Each step below is one round, written as $A - B = C$.

Step 1 $1000 - 0001 = 0999$ keep four digits
Step 2 $9990 - 0999 = 8991$
Step 3 $9981 - 1899 = 8082$
Step 4 $8820 - 0288 = 8532$
Step 5 $8532 - 2358 = 6174$
Answer 5 rounds

Reading 0999 as 999 would give $999 - 999 = 0$, and the steps would stop there.
