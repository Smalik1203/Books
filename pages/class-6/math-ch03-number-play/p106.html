Solution.

Step 1$8 + 0 + 9 + 7 = 24$, and 3021 reversed is 1203.
Step 21001 is the smallest 4-digit palindrome. For the largest 3-digit number with digit sum 4, put all of the 4 in the hundreds place: 400.
