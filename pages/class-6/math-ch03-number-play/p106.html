Example 12 · Numerical answer

On a number line, A represents 125 and B represents 305. The segment AB is divided into six equal parts. What number is at the fourth division point after A?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The gap is 305 − 125 = 180; each part is 180 ÷ 6 = 30.
Step 2The fourth point is 125 + 4 × 30 = 245.
Answer245
