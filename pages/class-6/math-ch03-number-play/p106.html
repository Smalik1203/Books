Type 5 · The Kaprekar steps

Example 10

Start with 1000. How many Kaprekar rounds does it take to reach 6174?

Solution. Each step below is one round, written as $A - B = C$.

Step 1 $1000 - 0001 = 0999$ keep four digits
Step 2 $9990 - 0999 = 8991$
Step 3 $9981 - 1899 = 8082$
Step 4 $8820 - 0288 = 8532$
Step 5 $8532 - 2358 = 6174$
Answer 5 rounds

Reading 0999 as 999 would give $999 - 999 = 0$, and the steps would stop there.

Type 6 · Adding and subtracting large numbers

Example 11

Use 10,000, 2,500 and 600, as many times as you need, adding or subtracting, to make 26,900.

Solution.

Step 1 $10{,}000 + 10{,}000 = 20{,}000$ start close to it
Step 2 $20{,}000 + 2{,}500 + 2{,}500 + 2{,}500 = 27{,}500$
Step 3 $27{,}500 - 26{,}900 = 600$ 600 too big
Answer $26{,}900 = 10{,}000 + 10{,}000 + 2{,}500 + 2{,}500 + 2{,}500 - 600$
Example 12

A square pattern has 5 rows of 5 boxes. Every box holds 30, except the middle box, which holds 80. Find the total.

Solution.

Step 1 if all $5 \times 5 = 25$ boxes held 30, the total would be $25 \times 30 = 750$ all the same first
Step 2 the middle box holds $80 - 30 = 50$ more
Answer $750 + 50 = 800$
