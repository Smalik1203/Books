There are many versions of this game. Here is another one that people often play.

Game 2. The first player says a number from 1 to 10. Then the players take turns adding a number from 1 to 10 to the number the other player said. The first player to reach 99 wins.

Play this game many times with a classmate. Can you find the winning strategy this time? Which player can always win, and which numbers should that player say?

Example 9

Here is a smaller game. Two players take turns adding 1 or 2 to a total that starts at 0. The first player to say 10 wins. Which numbers should a player say to be sure of winning? Should that player go first or second?

Solution.

Step 1 Work backwards. If you say 7, the other player can only reach 8 or 9. Then you reach 10.
Step 2 In the same way, saying 4 lets you reach 7, and saying 1 lets you reach 4.
Step 3 The winning numbers are 1, 4, 7 and 10. Each is 3 more than the number before it.
Answer Go first, and say 1, 4, 7 and 10. Whatever the other player adds, add enough to make 3 in that turn.

You can also make up your own version. Decide how much a player may add on each turn, and which number wins. Play your game several times. Then work out the winning strategy, and which player can always win.

Winning strategies
  • A winning strategy is a way of playing that always wins, whatever the other player does.
  • To look for one, start from the winning number and work backwards.
