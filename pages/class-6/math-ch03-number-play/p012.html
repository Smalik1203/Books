Example 3

You subtract a 2-digit number from a 4-digit number. How many digits can the difference have?

Solution.

Step 1 Smallest difference: $1000 - 99 = 901$, which has 3 digits. smallest minus largest
Step 2 Largest difference: $9999 - 10 = 9989$, which has 4 digits. largest minus smallest
Step 3 Any other difference, such as $5000 - 50 = 4950$, lies in between.
Answer The difference has either 3 digits or 4 digits, and never 2 or 5.

Every other difference lies between 901 and 9989. We did not need to try any of the differences in between.
