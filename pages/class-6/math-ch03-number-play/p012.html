3.7Clock and Calendar Numbers

Some times on a 12-hour clock make pretty patterns, such as 4:44, 10:10 and 12:21. In 4:44 every digit is the same. In 10:10 the hours and the minutes are the same. And 12:21 reads the same from both ends.

Manish was born on 20/12/2012. Look at the digits of his date of birth: 2, 0, 1, 2, and then 2, 0, 1, 2 again. His sister Meghana was born on 11/02/2011. Her date of birth reads the same from left to right and from right to left, just as the time 12:21 does. Fig. 3.13 shows how the digits pair up.

Palindromic times and dates
  • A time or a date is a palindrome if its digits read the same from both ends.
  • Leave out the colon and the slashes: 11/02/2011 is read as 11022011.
Example 6

Which of these times on a 12-hour clock read the same from both ends: 5:15, 11:11, 12:12 and 9:59?

Solution.

Step 1 Leave out the colons: 515, 1111, 1212 and 959.
Step 2 Read each from right to left: 515, 1111, 2121 and 959.
Step 3 Only 1212 has changed. not a palindrome
Answer 5:15, 11:11 and 9:59 read the same from both ends. 12:12 does not.
