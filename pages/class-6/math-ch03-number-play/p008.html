Digit sum

The digit sum of a number is the total of its digits.

Example 3

Find the digit sums of 40,506 and 3981. Which number has the bigger digit sum?

Solution.

Step 1 $4 + 0 + 5 + 0 + 6 = 15$ digits of 40,506
Step 2 $3 + 9 + 8 + 1 = 21$ digits of 3981
Answer 3981 has the bigger digit sum, though 40,506 is the bigger number.
