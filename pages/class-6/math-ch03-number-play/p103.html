Example 1 · Single correct

Using 0, 2, 5 and 8 exactly once, what is the difference between the greatest and least four-digit numbers?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 6471
  2. 6462
  3. 6522
  4. 5940

Solution.

Step 1The greatest is 8520. The least cannot begin with 0, so it is 2058.
Step 28520 − 2058 = 6462.
Answer(b) 6462
Example 2 · Single correct

A three-digit palindrome has digit sum 17. Its tens digit is 2 more than its hundreds digit. What is the number?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 575
  2. 737
  3. 464
  4. 656

Solution.

Step 1Let the end digit be a. The middle digit is a + 2, so 3a + 2 = 17.
Step 2Thus a = 5 and the middle digit is 7. Checking: 5 + 7 + 5 = 17.
Answer(a) 575
Example 3 · Single correct

For a four-digit Kaprekar step, arrange all digits in descending and ascending order, retaining leading zeros, then subtract. What is obtained from 4203 after one step?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 3087
  2. 4086
  3. 4176
  4. 4266

Solution.

Step 1Descending order gives 4320; ascending order gives 0234.
Step 24320 − 234 = 4086.
Answer(b) 4086
