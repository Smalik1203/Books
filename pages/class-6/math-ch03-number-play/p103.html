Example 1

Using the digits 0, 2, 5 and 8, each exactly once, what is the difference between the largest and the smallest 4-digit numbers?

  1. 6471
  2. 6462
  3. 6522
  4. 5940

Solution.

Step 1The largest is 8520. The smallest cannot begin with 0, so it is 2058.
Step 2$8520 - 2058 = 6462$
Check$2058 + 6462 = 8520$
Answer(b) 6462
Example 2

A 3-digit palindrome has a digit sum of 17. Its middle digit is 2 more than its first digit. What is the number?

  1. 575
  2. 737
  3. 464
  4. 656

Solution.

Step 1The first and last digits are equal, and the middle digit is 2 more. So 17 is three equal digits and 2 more.
Step 2$17 - 2 = 15$ and $15 \div 3 = 5$. So the first digit is 5, and the middle digit is 7, which is 2 more.
Check$5 + 7 + 5 = 17$
Answer(a) 575
