Example 5

How many 3-digit palindromes have a digit sum of 12?

Solution.

Step 1The first and last digits are the same. So the digit sum is twice the first digit, plus the middle digit.
Step 2First digit 2 needs a middle digit of 8, which gives 282.
Step 3First digits 3, 4, 5 and 6 give 363, 444, 525 and 606.
Step 4First digit 1 would need a middle digit of 10, and a first digit of 7 or more makes the sum too big.
Check$6 + 0 + 6 = 12$ and $5 + 2 + 5 = 12$
Answer5
Example 6

On a number line, A is at 125 and B is at 305. The part from A to B is cut into 6 equal parts. What number is at the 4th mark after A?

Solution.

Step 1$305 - 125 = 180$ and $180 \div 6 = 30$, so each part is 30.
Step 2The 4th mark after A is 4 parts along from A.
Step 3$125 + 4 \times 30 = 245$
CheckTwo more parts reach B: $245 + 2 \times 30 = 305$
Answer245
