Example 7

How many digits do you write when you write all the numbers from 1 to 100?

Solution.

Step 1 1 to 9 are 9 numbers of 1 digit each: $9 \times 1 = 9$
Step 2 10 to 99 are 90 numbers of 2 digits each: $90 \times 2 = 180$ $99 - 9 = 90$
Step 3 100 has 3 digits
Answer $9 + 180 + 3 = 192$ digits

Example 8

Reverse and add, starting with 87. How many steps does it take to reach a palindrome?

Solution.

Step 1 $87 + 78 = 165$
Step 2 $165 + 561 = 726$
Step 3 $726 + 627 = 1353$
Step 4 $1353 + 3531 = 4884$ reads the same backwards
Answer 4 steps, ending at 4884
Example 9

A 4-digit palindrome has a digit sum of 18. Its thousands digit is double its hundreds digit. Find the number.

Solution.

Step 1 the units digit equals the thousands digit, and the tens digit equals the hundreds digit it is a palindrome
Step 2 so the thousands and hundreds digits add up to $18 \div 2 = 9$ each digit appears twice
Step 3 two digits that add up to 9, one double the other, are 6 and 3 $6 = 2 \times 3$
Answer 6336, and $6 + 3 + 3 + 6 = 18$
