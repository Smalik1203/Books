Look at Fig. 3.14. What can you say about the numbers and the arrows?

The numbers in the middle column are added in different ways to make the numbers at the sides. You may use a middle number as many times as you need. The arrows show two of the sums:

$38{,}800 = 25{,}000 + 400 + 400 + 13{,}000$

$3{,}400 = 1{,}500 + 1{,}500 + 400$

Draw arrows to make the other numbers at the sides. Try to find each sum in your head before you write anything down.

Think and Reflect
  1. Can you make 1,000 using the numbers in the middle? Why not?
  2. Now make 14,000, 15,000 and 16,000. Each of them is possible.
  3. Which thousands cannot be made at all?
