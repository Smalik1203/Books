Digits and operations

Adding two 5-digit numbers can give another 5-digit number. Subtracting one 5-digit number from another can also give a 5-digit number. Here is an example of each, and in both of them every number has exactly 5 digits.

$12{,}350 + 24{,}545 = 36{,}895$

$48{,}952 - 24{,}547 = 24{,}405$

Does this always happen? To find out how many digits an answer can have, look at the smallest and the largest answers that are possible. Example 7 shows how to do this when the two numbers have different numbers of digits.

Example 7

You subtract a 2-digit number from a 4-digit number. How many digits can the difference between them have?

Solution.

Step 1 Smallest 4-digit minus largest 2-digit: $1000 - 99 = 901$ 3 digits
Step 2 Largest 4-digit minus smallest 2-digit: $9999 - 10 = 9989$ 4 digits
Step 3 Any other difference, such as $5000 - 50 = 4950$, lies in between.
Answer The difference has either 3 digits or 4 digits, and never 2 or 5.

Every other difference lies between 901 and 9989. We did not need to try any of the differences in between.
