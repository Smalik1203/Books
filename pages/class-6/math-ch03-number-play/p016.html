3.12Games and Winning Strategies

Numbers can be used to play games, and to find ways to win them. A way of playing that always wins is called a winning strategy. Here is a well-known game called 21. Play it with a classmate, and then try it at home with your family.

Game 1. The first player says 1, 2 or 3. Then the two players take turns. On your turn, add 1, 2 or 3 to the number the other player said, and say the total. The first player to reach 21 wins.

Play the game many times. Do you begin to see how to win?
