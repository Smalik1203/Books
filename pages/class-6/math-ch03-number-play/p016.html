How many digits?

To find how many digits a sum or a difference can have, work out the smallest and the largest answers. Every other answer lies between them.

Exercise Set 3.6
  1. Write an example for each of these, wherever you can.
    1. Add two 5-digit numbers to get a 5-digit sum more than 90,250.
    2. Add a 5-digit and a 3-digit number to get a 6-digit sum.
    3. Add two 4-digit numbers to get a 6-digit sum.
    4. Add two 5-digit numbers to get a 6-digit sum.
    5. Add two 5-digit numbers to get 18,500.
    6. Subtract two 5-digit numbers to get a difference less than 56,503.
    7. Subtract a 3-digit number from a 5-digit number to get a 4-digit difference.
    8. Subtract a 4-digit number from a 5-digit number to get a 4-digit difference.
    9. Subtract a 5-digit number from a 5-digit number to get a 3-digit difference.
    10. Subtract a 5-digit number from a 5-digit number to get 91,500.
  1. Could you find an example for every case in Question 1? For each case where you could not, explain why no example is possible. Then make up more questions like these, and try them on your classmates.
  1. Always, sometimes or never? Decide whether each statement is always true, only sometimes true, or never true. Give your reasons, and discuss them with the class.
    1. A 5-digit number plus a 5-digit number gives a 5-digit number.
    2. A 4-digit number plus a 2-digit number gives a 4-digit number.
    3. A 4-digit number plus a 2-digit number gives a 6-digit number.
    4. A 5-digit number minus a 5-digit number gives a 5-digit number.
    5. A 5-digit number minus a 2-digit number gives a 3-digit number.
