1. A shop gives each customer a token with a 4-digit number. A token is lucky if its digit sum is 10. Sana gets the token 3421.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Is Sana’s token lucky?
    2. What is the smallest number a lucky token can have?
    3. How many lucky tokens are there from 1000 to 1100? Justify your answer.

  1. On sports day, the four houses of a school have 118, 96, 104 and 89 students. Every student gets one juice box. The juice boxes come in packs of 24.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Estimate the total number of students, taking each number to the nearest ten.
    2. Find the exact number of students.
    3. How many packs of juice boxes are needed? Justify your answer.

  1. Kavya plays a game on her way to school. She takes the number of her bus, reverses its digits and adds, and keeps going until she reaches a palindrome. Today her bus is number 87.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the first sum.
    2. Is the first sum a palindrome? Give a reason.
    3. Find the number of steps she needs, and the palindrome she reaches.

  1. D. R. Kaprekar taught mathematics at a school in Devlali, in Maharashtra. In his steps, A is the largest number made from the digits of a 4-digit number, B is the smallest, and $C = A - B$. Sunita starts with the year 2025.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Write A and B for 2025.
    2. Find C.
    3. Find the number of rounds 2025 takes to reach 6174.
