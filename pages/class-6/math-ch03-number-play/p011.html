Example 5

Start with 6382. Follow the Kaprekar steps.

Solution.

Step 1 Round 1: $A = 8632$, $B = 2368$, $C = 8632 - 2368 = 6264$
Step 2 Round 2: $A = 6642$, $B = 2466$, $C = 6642 - 2466 = 4176$
Step 3 Round 3: $A = 7641$, $B = 1467$, $C = 7641 - 1467 = 6174$
Check $6174 + 1467 = 7641$
Answer 6382 reaches 6174 in 3 rounds.

The digits of 6174 give $A = 7641$ and $B = 1467$ again, so every round after this gives 6174 once more.

Now take some 4-digit numbers of your own, and follow the steps. What happens? Try at least three different numbers, and check with your friends what they got.

The Kaprekar constant

Start with any 4-digit number whose digits are not all the same, and repeat the steps. You always reach 6174. This number is called the Kaprekar constant.

What about 3-digit numbers? Follow the same steps with a few 3-digit numbers, such as 321. Which number starts to repeat? Keep three digits all the way: 99 is read as 099. Compare what you find with a friend.
