To make 39,800, start from 40,000, which is close to it. 40,000 is 200 too big. Taking away 800 and adding back 300 twice takes away exactly 200, because $800 - 300 - 300 = 200$. Digits and operations

Adding two 5-digit numbers can give another 5-digit number. Subtracting one 5-digit number from another can also give a 5-digit number. Here is an example of each, and in both of them every number has exactly 5 digits.

$12{,}350 + 24{,}545 = 36{,}895$

$48{,}952 - 24{,}547 = 24{,}405$

Does this always happen? To find out how many digits an answer can have, look at the smallest and the largest answers that are possible. Example 3 shows how to do this when the two numbers have different numbers of digits.
