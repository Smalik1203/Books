Comparing numbers
  • Along a number line, the numbers get bigger from left to right.
  • To compare two 4-digit numbers, compare their digits from the left: 9950 is bigger than 9590.

On some number lines, only a few of the marks are labelled. When the marks are equally spaced, you can find the numbers at all the other marks, as Example 2 shows.

Example 2

A number line has ten equally spaced marks. The 3rd mark is 7080 and the 7th mark is 7120. Find the numbers at the 1st mark and the 10th mark.

Solution.

Step 1 From the 3rd mark to the 7th mark there are 4 gaps. count the gaps
Step 2 $7120 - 7080 = 40$ and $40 \div 4 = 10$, so each gap is 10.
Step 3 1st mark: $7080 - 2 \times 10 = 7060$. 10th mark: $7120 + 3 \times 10 = 7150$.
Answer The 1st mark is 7060, and the 10th mark is 7150.
