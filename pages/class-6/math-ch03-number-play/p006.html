3.5Pretty Palindromic Patterns

Look at these numbers: 66, 848, 575, 797 and 1111. What do they have in common? Read each one from left to right, and then from right to left. You get the same number both ways. A number like this is called a palindrome, or a palindromic number.

Palindromes from 1, 2 and 3

The numbers 121, 313 and 222 are 3-digit palindromes made from the digits 1, 2 and 3. A digit may be used more than once, and a palindrome does not have to use all three digits.

Write down every 3-digit palindrome that uses only the digits 1, 2 and 3. How can you be sure that you have found them all? You could start with the palindromes that begin with 1, then those that begin with 2.
