Example 3

The Collatz rule halves an even number, and multiplies an odd number by 3 and adds 1. Which statements are correct?

  1. An odd number always gives an even number next.
  2. An even number always gives an odd number next.
  3. Starting at 12, the rule reaches 3 after two steps.
  4. Starting at 5, the rule reaches 16 after one step.

Solution.

Step 1An odd number times 3 is odd, and an odd number plus 1 is even. So statement (a) is correct.
Step 28 is even, and half of 8 is 4, which is even too. So (b) is wrong.
Step 312 gives 6 and then 3. 5 gives $3 \times 5 + 1 = 16$. So (c) and (d) are correct.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 4

Which statements about the digit sums of 3-digit numbers are correct?

  1. The largest possible digit sum is 27.
  2. A digit sum of 1 is possible.
  3. A digit sum of 28 is possible.
  4. Two different numbers can have the same digit sum.

Solution.

Step 1999 has the largest digit sum, $9 + 9 + 9 = 27$. So 28 is not possible.
Step 2100 has the digit sum 1. 123 and 132 both have the digit sum 6.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
