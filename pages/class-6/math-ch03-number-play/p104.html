Example 4 · Single correct

Apply the Collatz rule: halve an even number; multiply an odd number by 3 and add 1. Starting at 13, what is the number after four steps?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 40
  2. 20
  3. 10
  4. 5

Solution.

Step 1The successive steps give 13 → 40 → 20 → 10 → 5.
Step 2The starting number is not counted as a step.
Answer(d) 5
Example 5 · Single correct

In the row 18, 43, 27, 61, 55, 72, 39, an interior entry is a supercell if it is greater than both immediate neighbours. How many supercells are there?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 5

Solution.

Step 143 exceeds 18 and 27; 61 exceeds 27 and 55; 72 exceeds 55 and 39.
Step 2Neither 27 nor 55 exceeds both neighbours. End entries are excluded by the definition.
Answer(b) 3
Example 6 · Single correct

A number is rounded to the nearest hundred and gives 4700, with halfway values rounded upward. What is the greatest whole number it could be?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 4700
  2. 4749
  3. 4750
  4. 4799

Solution.

Step 1The numbers from 4650 through 4749 round to 4700.
Step 24750 is halfway to 4800 and rounds upward, so 4749 is the greatest.
Answer(b) 4749
