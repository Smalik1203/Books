Example 10

To reverse and add, write a number’s digits in the opposite order and add the two numbers. Repeat with the answer until you reach a palindrome. Anita starts with 39.

  1. The first sum is
    1. 132
    2. 93
    3. 121
    4. 66
  2. Numerical answer. How many steps does Anita need to reach a palindrome?
  3. Numerical answer. Her friend starts with 93. How many steps does he need?

Solution.

Step 1$39 + 93 = 132$
Step 2132 is not a palindrome. $132 + 231 = 363$, which is one. So Anita needs 2 steps.
Step 3$93 + 39$ is the same sum, 132, so her friend also needs 2 steps.
Check363 read from right to left is 363, so it is a palindrome.
Answer(i) (a) 132; (ii) 2; (iii) 2
