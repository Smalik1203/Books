Digit sums

Komal adds up the digits of some numbers. She notices that different numbers can give the same total. The total you get by adding the digits of a number is called its digit sum.

$6 + 8 = 14$

$1 + 7 + 6 = 14$

$5 + 4 + 5 = 14$

So 68, 176 and 545 all have the same digit sum, 14.
