By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Find the supercells in the row 42, 57, 31, 68, 90, 74. Give a reason for each one.
  1. 5995 is a palindrome. Write the palindromes that come just before it and just after it.
  1. Do one Kaprekar round on 5294. Write A, B and C.
  1. Write the Collatz sequence that starts at 11, up to 1. Find the number of steps it takes.
  1. A 2-digit number has a digit sum of 11. Its tens digit is 3 more than its units digit. Find the number.
  1. Find the number of digits you write when you write all the numbers from 1 to 30.
  1. Estimate $412 + 289 + 596$ by taking each number to the nearest hundred. Is the exact sum more or less than your estimate?
  1. Write the largest 4-digit number whose digits are all different. Find its digit sum.
  1. Using 3, 0, 7, 1 and 9 once each, write the largest and the smallest 5-digit numbers.
  1. On a number line with equally spaced marks, two marks next to each other are labelled 4250 and 4275. Find the numbers at the next two marks after 4275.
Short answer
  1. Start with 68. Reverse its digits and add, and keep going until you reach a palindrome. Find the number of steps it takes and the palindrome you reach.
  1. Find the supercells in the row 15, 22, 18, 30, 25, 12. Then change one number so that the row has 3 supercells.
  1. Explain why two cells next to each other can never both be supercells. Hence find the largest number of supercells in a row of 11 cells.
  1. Find the smallest 4-digit number whose digit sum is 20. Also find the largest 4-digit number whose digit sum is 20.
  1. A 3-digit number is subtracted from a 4-digit number. Find the smallest and the largest difference. Hence write how many digits the difference can have.
  1. Meera buys 48 packets of biscuits at ₹21 each. Estimate the total cost. Find the exact cost, and the difference between the two.
  1. In a Collatz sequence, which numbers can come just before 10? Which numbers can come just before 3? Give reasons.
