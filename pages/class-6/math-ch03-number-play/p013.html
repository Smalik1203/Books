3.9Playing with Number Patterns

The numbers in Figs. 3.13, 3.14 and 3.15 are arranged in patterns. Find the total of the numbers in each pattern. Should you add them one at a time, or is there a quicker way?

Some of the patterns are made of boxes with a number written in each. Others are made of dice faces. In a dice face, the square stands for the number of dots on it, so a face with five dots counts as 5.

Share the method you used for each pattern with your class. Did your classmates find different quick ways? Which way did you like best, and why? Before you add, you could also guess which pattern has the largest total, and then check.
