Jeevan was looking at this year’s calendar. He wondered why people buy a new calendar every year. Could an old calendar be used again? Last year’s calendar was different from this year’s, and next year’s will be different again. But will a year’s calendar ever come back, with every date falling on the same day of the week? Think about how many days there are in a year, and how many weeks that makes. Then look at the day of the week on which your birthday falls this year and on which it fell last year.
