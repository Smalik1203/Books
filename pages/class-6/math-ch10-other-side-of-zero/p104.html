Example 4

A diver is 12 m below sea level. Heights above sea level are positive. Which of these statements are true?

  1. Her height is $-12$ m.
  2. Rising 7 m, she reaches $-5$ m.
  3. 3 m further down, she is at $-9$ m.
  4. She is 12 m from sea level.

Solution.

Step 1She is at $-12$ m, 12 m below sea level. Rising 7 m gives $-12 + 7 = -5$ m.
Step 2Going 3 m further down gives $-12 - 3 = -15$ m, not $-9$ m.

Example 5

How many integers lie between $-8$ and 6, not counting $-8$ and 6?

Solution.

Step 1The integers are $-7, -6, \ldots, -1$, then 0, then $1, 2, \ldots, 5$.
Step 2That is 7 negative integers, zero and 5 positive integers: $7 + 1 + 5 = 13$.
CheckFrom $-7$ to 5 is $5 - (-7) = 12$ steps, so there are 13 integers.
Answer13
