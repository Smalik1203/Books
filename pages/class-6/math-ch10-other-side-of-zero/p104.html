Example 6 · Single correct

A sequence starts at −17 and increases by 6 each time. What is its fifth term?

Choose one correct option.

  1. −11
  2. 1
  3. 13
  4. 7

Solution.

Step 1The five terms are −17, −11, −5, 1 and 7.
Step 2Four increases follow the first term, so −17 + 4 × 6 = 7.
Answer(d) 7
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which inequalities are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. −9 is less than −4.
  2. −2 is greater than 1.
  3. 0 is greater than −6.
  4. −11 is less than −10.

Solution.

Step 1Numbers farther right on a number line are greater.
Step 2Thus −9 < −4, 0 > −6 and −11 < −10, while −2 < 1.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which expressions have value −6?

Select all correct options.

  1. −9 + 3
  2. 4 − 10
  3. −2 − 4
  4. −8 − (−2)

Solution.

Step 1Compute each: −9 + 3 = −6; 4 − 10 = −6; −2 − 4 = −6.
Step 2Subtracting −2 means adding 2, so −8 − (−2) = −8 + 2 = −6 as well.
Answer(a), (b), (c), (d)
