Floor $+3$ is lower than Floor $+4$, so $+3 \lt +4$, or $+4 \gt +3$. Floor $-4$ is one floor further below the ground than Floor $-3$, so $-4 \lt -3$, or $-3 \gt -4$.

Every negative floor is below Floor 0, so every negative number is less than 0. Every positive floor is above Floor 0, so every positive number is greater than 0.
