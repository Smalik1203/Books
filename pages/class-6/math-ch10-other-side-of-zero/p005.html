Comparing numbers using floors

?
Think and Reflect

Who is on the lowest floor? Use Fig. 10.2.

  1. Jay is in the Art Centre, so he is on Floor $+2$.
  2. Asin is in the Sports Centre. Which floor is she on?
  3. Binnu is in the Cinema Centre. Which floor is she on?
  4. Aman is in the Toy Store. Which floor is he on?

Floor $+3$ is lower than Floor $+4$. So we write $+3 \lt +4$. We can also write $+4 \gt +3$.

Floor $-4$ is lower than Floor $-3$, because it is one floor further below the ground. So $-4 \lt -3$, and we can also write $-3 \gt -4$.

Every negative floor is below Floor 0, so every negative number is less than 0. Every positive floor is above Floor 0, so every positive number is greater than 0.
