10.5A Pinch of History

Like fractions, integers, including zero and the negative numbers, were first thought of and used in Asia, thousands of years ago. Much later, they spread across the world.

The earliest known uses of negative numbers were in keeping accounts. One of the most important mathematics books of China is The Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art. It was finished by the first or second century CE. It showed positive and negative numbers with red and black rods, much as we used green and red tokens.

India also had a strong tradition of keeping accounts. Around 300 BCE, Kautilya wrote a great deal about credit and debit in his Arthaśāstra, and he knew that a balance could be negative. Negative numbers are used in accounts in many old Indian works. One of them is the Bakhshālī manuscript, from around 300 CE. In it, a negative number was shown by a special mark written after the number, not before it as we write today.
