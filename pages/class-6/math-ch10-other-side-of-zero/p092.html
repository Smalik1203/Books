Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $(-8) - (-3) = -5$.

    Reason (R): Subtracting a negative number is the same as adding the matching positive number.

  1. Assertion (A): $-10 \lt -2$.

    Reason (R): $10 \gt 2$.

  1. Assertion (A): $-3 \gt -8$.

    Reason (R): $-3$ is further from 0 than $-8$ is.

  1. Assertion (A): $-5 \gt -2$.

    Reason (R): On the number line, $-5$ lies to the left of $-2$.

  1. Assertion (A): $(-6) + (+6) = 0$.

    Reason (R): A number plus its inverse is always zero.
