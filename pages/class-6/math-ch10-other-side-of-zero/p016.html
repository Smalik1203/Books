You can think of credits as positive numbers and debits as negative numbers. The total of all the credits and debits is your balance. It can be positive or negative. It is usually better to keep it positive.

Heights above and below sea level

The heights of mountains, plateaus and deserts are measured from sea level. The height at sea level is 0 m. Heights above sea level are written as positive numbers, and heights below sea level as negative numbers. For example, the top of a hill 300 m above sea level has a height of $+300$ m. The bed of a lake 50 m below sea level has a height of $-50$ m.

Sea level is the zero for heights, just as the ground floor is the zero of the Building of Fun.
