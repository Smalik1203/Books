The number $+3$ does two jobs. It is the floor number of the Book Store. It is also the number of floors you move when you press $+3$. In the same way, $-3$ is a floor number, and it is also a movement of three floors down.

Positive and negative numbers
  • A number with a $+$ sign in front is a positive number, and a number with a $-$ sign in front is a negative number.
  • Zero is neither positive nor negative, so we write it with no sign.

Floors above the ground floor get positive numbers, because you reach them by pressing $+$. Floors below it get negative numbers, because you reach them by pressing $-$.

Adding to keep track of movement

Suppose you start at the Food Court and press $+2$. You go up two floors from Floor $+1$, and you reach the Book Store on Floor $+3$. You can write this as an addition:

Starting Floor $+$ Movement $=$ Target Floor

$(+1) + (+2) = +3$
