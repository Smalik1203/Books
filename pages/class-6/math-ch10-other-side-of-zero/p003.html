Numbering the floors

You enter the building on the ground floor, at the Welcome Hall. From there, you press $+1$ to reach the Food Court and $+2$ to reach the Art Centre. So we say that the Food Court is on Floor $+1$ and the Art Centre is on Floor $+2$.

From the ground floor, you press $-1$ to reach the Toy Store, so the Toy Store is on Floor $-1$. You press $-2$ to reach Video Games, so Video Games is on Floor $-2$. The ground floor itself is called Floor 0. Can you see why?

The number $+3$ does two jobs. It is the floor number of the Book Store. It is also the number of floors you move when you press $+3$. In the same way, $-3$ is a floor number, and it is also a movement of three floors down.

Positive and negative numbers

A number with a $+$ sign in front is called a positive number. A number with a $-$ sign in front is called a negative number.

Zero is neither positive nor negative, so we write it with no sign.

The floors of the Building of Fun are numbered from the ground floor, Floor 0. The floors above it get positive numbers, because you reach them by pressing $+$. The floors below it get negative numbers, because you reach them by pressing $-$.

Adding to keep track of movement

Suppose you start at the Food Court and press $+2$. You go up two floors from Floor $+1$, and you reach the Book Store on Floor $+3$. You can write this as an addition:

Starting Floor $+$ Movement $=$ Target Floor

$(+1) + (+2) = +3$
