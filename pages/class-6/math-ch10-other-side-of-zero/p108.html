Example 10

Esha’s pocket holds 9 positive tokens and 4 negative tokens. Farid’s pocket holds 3 positive tokens and 8 negative tokens.

  1. The value of Esha’s pocket is
    1. $+13$
    2. $+4$
    3. $+5$
    4. $-5$
  2. Numerical answer. What is the value of both pockets together?
  3. Numerical answer. How many negative tokens must Farid take out to make his pocket worth 0?

Solution.

Step 1Esha: 4 zero pairs cancel and 5 positive tokens are left, $+5$. Farid: 3 zero pairs cancel and 5 negative tokens are left, $-5$.
Step 2Together: $(+5) + (-5) = 0$.
Step 3Farid must take out his 5 extra negative tokens, leaving 3 zero pairs.
Answer(i) (c) $+5$; (ii) 0; (iii) 5
