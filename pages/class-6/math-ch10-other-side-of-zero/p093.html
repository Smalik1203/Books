  1. A mall in Pune has floors from $-3$ to $+5$. The car park is on Floor $-3$. Kavita parks her car, takes the lift to a shop on Floor $+4$, and then goes to the food court on Floor $+1$.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Which button takes Kavita from the car park to Floor $+4$?
    2. Write the movement from Floor $+4$ to Floor $+1$.
    3. Write her movement back to the car. Why do her three movements add to 0?

  1. Rohit opens a bank account with ₹500. His passbook then shows, in order, a debit of ₹650 for school fees, a credit of ₹200 from a gift, and a debit of ₹120 for books.

    1. Find his balance after the first entry.
    2. Find his balance after all three entries.
    3. What credit must he get next, so that his balance becomes ₹100?
