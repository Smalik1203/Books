Changing subtraction into addition

Remember: Target $-$ Starting $=$ Movement needed, and Target $=$ Starting $+$ Movement needed.

Example 2

Start at 2 and go to $-3$. Find the movement needed in two ways.

Solution.

Step 1 First way: on the number line, $-3$ is 5 steps to the left of 2, so $-3 - 2 = -5$
Step 2 Second way, in two parts: from 2 to 0 is $0 - 2 = -2$, and from 0 to $-3$ is $-3 - 0 = -3$
Step 3 Total movement: $(-3) + (-2) = -5$ add the two movements
Check Start at 2 and move $-5$: $2 + (-5) = -3$, the target
Answer The movement needed is $-5$, both ways

Put $-3 - 2 = -5$ beside $(-3) + (-2) = -5$. The second one has no subtraction in it.

Subtraction and addition

To subtract a number, you can add its inverse instead. To add a number, you can subtract its inverse instead.

$(+7) - (+5) = (+7) + (-5)$

$(-3) - (+8) = (-3) + (-8)$

$(+8) - (-2) = (+8) + (+2)$

$(+6) - (-9) = (+6) + (+9)$
