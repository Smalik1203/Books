Changing subtraction into addition

Remember: Target $-$ Starting $=$ Movement needed, and Target $=$ Starting $+$ Movement needed.

Example 2

Start at 2 and go to $-3$. Find the movement needed in two ways.

Solution.

Step 1 On the number line, $-3$ is 5 steps to the left of 2 first way
Step 2 So $-3 - 2 = -5$
Step 3 From 2 to 0 the movement is $0 - 2 = -2$ second way: two parts
Step 4 From 0 to $-3$ it is $-3 - 0 = -3$
Step 5 Total movement: $(-3) + (-2) = -5$ add the two movements
Answer The movement needed is $-5$, both ways

Put $-3 - 2 = -5$ beside $(-3) + (-2) = -5$. The second one has no subtraction in it.

Subtraction and addition

To subtract a number, you can add its inverse instead. To add a number, you can subtract its inverse instead.

$(+7) - (+5) = (+7) + (-5)$

$(-3) - (+8) = (-3) + (-8)$

$(+8) - (-2) = (+8) + (+2)$

$(+6) - (-9) = (+6) + (+9)$

10.2The Token Model

Using tokens for addition

The lift attendant in the Building of Fun is bored. To pass the time, he keeps a box of green positive tokens and red negative tokens. Each time he presses $+$, he puts a positive token in his pocket. Each time he presses $-$, he puts a negative token in his pocket.

He starts on Floor 0 with an empty pocket. After an hour, he finds 5 positive tokens and 3 negative tokens in his pocket. So he pressed $+$ five times and $-$ three times, and $(+5) + (-3) = +2$. He is on Floor $+2$.
