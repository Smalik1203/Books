An amazing grid of numbers

Here is a game you can play on a grid of numbers. Follow the steps until no number is left.

First, circle any number, and strike out the row and the column it is in. Then circle any number that is not struck out, and strike out its row and column too. Keep going until every number is struck out. Then add up the circled numbers.

In Fig. 10.17, the circled numbers are $-1$, $9$, $-7$ and $-2$. Their sum is $(-1) + 9 + (-7) + (-2) = -1$. Look at where the circles are: there is exactly one circle in each row, and exactly one in each column. That is true of every game on a 4 by 4 grid, whichever numbers you choose.
