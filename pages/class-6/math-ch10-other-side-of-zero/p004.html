Combining button presses

Gurmit is in the Toy Store and wants to go down two floors. By mistake, he presses $+$ twice. He sees his mistake and quickly presses $-$ three times. How far does he move from the Toy Store?

Two presses up and three presses down leave him one floor lower than where he started. You can add two movements to find the total movement:

Movement 1 $+$ Movement 2 $=$ Total Movement

$(+2) + (-3) = -1$

Back to zero

Basant is on the ground floor and in a great hurry. By mistake, he presses $+3$. To stay on the ground floor, he can cancel it by pressing $-3$. That is, $(+3) + (-3) = 0$.

We call $-3$ the inverse of $+3$. In the same way, the inverse of $-3$ is $+3$. A number added to its inverse gives 0.

You can also think of an inverse from any floor. If you are on Floor $+6$ and press its inverse, $-6$, you come back to the ground floor. If you are on Floor $-2$ and press $+2$, you go to $(-2) + (+2) = 0$, the ground floor again.
