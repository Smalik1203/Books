Subtracting to find which button to press

In earlier classes, subtraction meant take away. There are 10 books on a shelf, and you take away 4 of them. The books left are $10 - 4 = 6$, or “ten take away four is six”.

Subtraction has a second meaning. It tells you how much is needed to make two amounts equal. Suppose you have ₹10 and your sister has ₹6. How much more money does she need, to have as much as you? You can write this as $6 + {?} = 10$, or as $10 - 6 = ?$. Finding the missing number to add is the same as subtracting.

For positive and negative numbers, we will use this second meaning of subtraction.

Say you start at the Art Centre and want to reach the Sports Centre. You need to go three floors up, so you press $+3$. You can write the movement needed as a subtraction:

Target Floor $-$ Starting Floor $=$ Movement needed

$(+5) - (+2) = +3$

Why does this work? You already know that Starting Floor $+$ Movement needed $=$ Target Floor. If the movement is the missing number, you have Starting Floor $+\ {?} =$ Target Floor. A missing number to add is found by subtracting, so the movement needed is Target Floor $-$ Starting Floor.

Example 1

Find the button to press in each case, and write the subtraction.

(a) Target Floor $-1$, Starting Floor $-2$. You go one floor up, so press $+1$:  $(-1) - (-2) = +1$

(b) Target Floor $-1$, Starting Floor $+3$. You go four floors down, so press $-4$:  $(-1) - (+3) = -4$

(c) Target Floor $+2$, Starting Floor $-2$. You go four floors up, so press $+4$:  $(+2) - (-2) = +4$
