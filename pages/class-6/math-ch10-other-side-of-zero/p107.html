Why the other options are wrong

A8 Between $-3$ and $2$ means the integers after $-3$ and before $2$, so neither end is included: $-2, -1, 0, 1$, option (d). Option (b) includes both ends. Option (a) leaves out 0, which is an integer even though it has no sign. Option (c) leaves out $-2$.
B2 Both statements are true. But the reason $-10$ is less than $-2$ is that Floor $-10$ is further below the ground. That $10$ is greater than $2$ points the other way, so R does not explain A, and the answer is (b).
