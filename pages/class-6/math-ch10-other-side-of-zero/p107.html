Type 7 · Patterns with integers

Example 15

The sequence $-20, -14, -8, -2, \ldots$ goes on in the same way. What is its 10th number?

Solution.

Step 1 $(-14) - (-20) = 6$ and $(-8) - (-14) = 6$ the same step each time
Step 2 $(-2) + 6 = 4$, then $4 + 6 = 10$, then $10 + 6 = 16$
Step 3 The 7th number is 16, so the 8th, 9th and 10th are 22, 28 and 34 keep adding 6
Answer The 10th number is 34
Example 16

Find $1 + (-2) + 3 + (-4) + 5 + (-6) + \cdots + 19 + (-20)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $1 + (-2) = -1$,  $3 + (-4) = -1$,  $5 + (-6) = -1$ group in pairs
Step 2 The last pair is $19 + (-20) = -1$
Step 3 20 numbers make 10 pairs, each worth $-1$
Answer $-10$

Ten pairs, each worth $-1$, are like ten negative tokens.

Example 17

The sequence $50, 43, 36, 29, \ldots$ goes on by taking away 7 each time. Which is its first negative number, and where in the sequence does it come?

Solution.

Step 1 After 29 come 22, 15, 8 and 1 still not negative
Step 2 $1 - 7 = -6$ the first below 0
Step 3 50, 43, 36, 29, 22, 15, 8, 1, $-6$ is nine numbers count them
Answer $-6$, the 9th number

The number before $-6$ is 1, which is less than 7. So taking away 7 once more is what crosses 0.
