Example 9

At 6 a.m. the temperature in Kargil was $-9$ °C. It rose by 4 degrees every hour until 11 a.m. Then it fell by 3 degrees every hour until 4 p.m.

  1. The temperature at 8 a.m. was
    1. $-1$ °C
    2. $-5$ °C
    3. $-17$ °C
    4. $-13$ °C
  2. Numerical answer. What was the temperature, in °C, at 11 a.m.?
  3. Numerical answer. What was the temperature, in °C, at 4 p.m.?

Solution.

Step 1By 8 a.m. it rose for 2 hours: $-9 + 4 + 4 = -1$.
Step 2By 11 a.m. it rose for 5 hours, 20 degrees in all: $-9 + 20 = 11$.
Step 3From 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. it fell for 5 hours, 15 degrees in all: $11 - 15 = -4$.
CheckIt rose 20 and fell 15, so at 4 p.m. it was 5 degrees warmer than at 6 a.m.: $(-4) - (-9) = 5$.
Answer(i) (a) $-1$ °C; (ii) 11; (iii) $-4$
