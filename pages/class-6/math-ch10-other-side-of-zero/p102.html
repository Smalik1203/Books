Riya writes $(-8) - (+5) = -3$. How can you tell at once that she has made a mistake?

Subtracting a positive number is the same as adding a negative one, so the answer must be further to the left than $-8$ on the number line. It must be less than $-8$. Riya’s answer, $-3$, is to the right of $-8$, so it cannot be right. The correct answer is $(-8) + (-5) = -13$. Knowing which direction the answer lies was enough to catch the mistake. Tokens agree: 8 negative tokens and 5 more negative tokens make 13 negative tokens, with no zero pairs to take away.

None of these questions needed a new rule. Each needed you to choose a picture: the lift, the number line or the tokens. Once the picture was chosen, it did most of the work. When a question about integers looks new, ask which of these pictures fits it best. Often two of them fit, and using both is a good way to check an answer.

Solved Examples

Type 1 · Comparing and ordering integers

Example 1

Write $-7$, $4$, $0$, $-12$, $-3$ and $9$ in order, from the smallest to the largest.

Solution.

Step 1 The negative numbers are $-12$, $-7$ and $-3$ all less than 0
Step 2 $-12 \lt -7 \lt -3$ further left is smaller
Step 3 $0 \lt 4 \lt 9$
Answer $-12 \lt -7 \lt -3 \lt 0 \lt 4 \lt 9$
Example 2

On the number line, which two integers are 5 steps away from $-3$? Which of the two is further from 0?

Solution.

Step 1 5 steps to the right: $-3 + 5 = 2$ forward is positive
Step 2 5 steps to the left: $-3 + (-5) = -8$ backward is negative
Step 3 2 is 2 steps from 0, and $-8$ is 8 steps from 0
Answer 2 and $-8$;  $-8$ is further from 0
