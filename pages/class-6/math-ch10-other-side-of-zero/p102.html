Riya writes $(-8) - (+5) = -3$. How can you tell at once that she has made a mistake?

Subtracting a positive number is the same as adding a negative one, so the answer must be further to the left than $-8$ on the number line. It must be less than $-8$. Riya’s answer, $-3$, is to the right of $-8$, so it cannot be right. The correct answer is $(-8) + (-5) = -13$. Knowing which direction the answer lies was enough to catch the mistake. Tokens agree: 8 negative tokens and 5 more negative tokens make 13 negative tokens, with no zero pairs to take away.

None of these questions needed a new rule. Each needed you to choose a picture: the lift, the number line or the tokens. Once the picture was chosen, it did most of the work. When a question about integers looks new, ask which of these pictures fits it best. Often two of them fit, and using both is a good way to check an answer.

Behind Each Answer
Five questions, with the reasoning as well as the working. Try each one before reading its solution.
Problem 1

A diver is 18 m below sea level. She swims up 7 m, and then dives down 12 m. Her height is now

  1. $-13$
  2. $-23$
  3. $-37$
  4. $+1$
Solution

Write each height and each movement as an integer. Below sea level is negative, up is positive and down is negative.

start: $-18$

swims up 7 m: $(-18) + (+7) = -11$

dives down 12 m: $(-11) + (-12) = -23$

Her height is $-23$ m, option (b). Option (a) swaps the two movements, going down 7 and up 12. Option (c) treats both movements as down. Option (d) treats both movements as up.

You can check the answer another way, as in the first question of Stage 1. Take the two movements together first: $(+7) + (-12) = -5$. So the diver ends 5 m lower than she started. Starting from $-18$ m, 5 m lower is $(-18) + (-5) = -23$ m. The two ways agree, which is a good sign that the answer is right. The order of the two movements does not change where she ends, because $(+7) + (-12)$ and $(-12) + (+7)$ are both $-5$. Only her depth on the way is different. Tokens give the same answer too: 7 positive and 12 negative tokens make 7 zero pairs, with 5 negative tokens left over.
