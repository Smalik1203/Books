Example 13

How many years passed from 45 BCE to 30 CE? Remember that there was no year 0.

Solution.

Step 1 write 45 BCE as $-45$ and 30 CE as $+30$ before and after, as on the number line
Step 2 $30 - (-45) = 30 + 45 = 75$ add the inverse
Step 3 the number line counts 0, but no year was 0: $75 - 1 = 74$ no year 0
Answer 74 years

Check by counting in parts: from 45 BCE to 1 BCE is 44 years, one more year reaches 1 CE, and 29 more reach 30 CE. That is $44 + 1 + 29 = 74$.

Example 14

In a hollow grid, the top left corner is $5$, the top right corner is $-2$ and the bottom left corner is $-6$. The border sum is $+1$. Find the middle number of the top row and the middle number of the left column.

Solution.

Step 1 $5 + (-2) = 3$, so the top middle is $1 - 3 = -2$ the row adds to 1
Step 2 $5 + (-6) = -1$, so the left middle is $1 - (-1) = 2$ the column adds to 1
Step 3 $5 + (-2) + (-2) = 1$ and $5 + 2 + (-6) = 1$ check
Answer Top middle $-2$, left middle 2
Example 15

The amazing grid game from Section 10.4 is played on a 3 by 3 grid whose rows are $2, -1, 4$ and $-3, -6, -1$ and $5, 2, 7$. Every game on this grid gives the same sum. Find it.

Solution.

Step 1 Circle 2, then $-6$, then 7 one in each row and column
Step 2 $2 + (-6) + 7 = 3$
Step 3 Another game: circle 4, then $-3$, then 2, and $4 + (-3) + 2 = 3$ check
Answer 3

Each row is the top row with one number added to every entry: $-5$ for the second row and 3 for the third. So every game gives $2 + (-1) + 4 = 5$, together with $0 + (-5) + 3 = -2$, and $5 + (-2) = 3$.
