Example 12 · Numerical answer

How many integers lie strictly between −8 and 6?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The integers are −7 through 5.
Step 2There are 7 negatives, zero, and 5 positives: 7 + 1 + 5 = 13.
Answer13
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A row of an integer puzzle must total zero. Its entries are −13, 8, −6 and x. Find x.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The three known entries total −13 + 8 − 6 = −11.
Step 2The missing entry must be 11 so that the total is zero.
Answer11

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3
