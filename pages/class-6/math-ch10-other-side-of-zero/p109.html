In Questions 13 to 16, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $(-8) - (-3) = -5$.

    Reason (R): Subtracting a negative number is the same as adding the matching positive number.

  1. Assertion (A): $-10 \lt -2$.

    Reason (R): $10 \gt 2$.

  1. Assertion (A): $-3 \gt -8$.

    Reason (R): $-3$ is further from 0 than $-8$ is.

  1. Assertion (A): $-5 \gt -2$.

    Reason (R): On the number line, $-5$ lies to the left of $-2$.
