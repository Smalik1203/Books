Integers

The Building of Fun has floors from $-5$ to $+6$, and the mine has levels from $-200$ to $+180$. But you can imagine a taller building or a deeper mine. The positive numbers $+1, +2, +3, \ldots$ go up without end, and the negative numbers $-1, -2, -3, \ldots$ go down without end.

The positive numbers, the negative numbers and zero together are called integers: $\ldots, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots$

Adding and subtracting any integers

To add and subtract even larger integers, imagine an even larger lift. You can even imagine a lift that goes up for ever and down for ever from Level 0, with no building or mine around it at all. Call it an infinite lift. With it, you can add and subtract any integers you like.

Take $(+2000) - (-200)$. Imagine a lift with 2000 levels above the ground and 200 below it. To go from the Starting Level $-200$ to the Target Level $+2000$, you press $+200$ to reach 0, and then $+2000$ more. That is $+2200$ in all, so $(+2000) - (-200) = +2200$. And $(+2000) + (+200)$ is also $+2200$.

Subtracting a negative number

$(+2000) - (-200)$ and $(+2000) + (+200)$ give the same answer. Subtracting a negative number is the same as adding the matching positive number.
