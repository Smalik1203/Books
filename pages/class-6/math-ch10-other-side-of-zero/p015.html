Example 4

Work out $(+4) - (-6)$ using tokens.

Solution.

Step 1 Start with 4 positive tokens
Step 2 You have to take away 6 negative tokens, but there are none
Step 3 So put down 6 zero pairs the value does not change
Step 4 Now there are 6 negative tokens. Take them away
Step 5 The 4 positive tokens and the 6 from the zero pairs are left see Fig. 10.12
Answer $(+4) - (-6) = +10$
