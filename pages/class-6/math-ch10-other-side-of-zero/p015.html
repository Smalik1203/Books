10.3Integers in Other Places

Integers are not only for lifts and number lines. You can use them whenever something can go above or below a starting point. Here are three places where they are used every day.

Money in the bank

Suppose you open a bank account with ₹100 that you saved last month. The money in your account is called your balance, so your balance starts at ₹100.

Money paid into the account is shown in your passbook as a credit. Money paid out of it is shown as a debit. Table 10.1 shows five days in your passbook.

Can a balance go below zero? Some banks allow it for a short time. They usually charge you extra, as a fee or as interest, while it is below zero. A large purchase can still be worth it, if it helps your business earn more.
