Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which statements hold for all integers a?

Select all correct options.

  1. a + 0 = a
  2. a − a = 0
  3. 0 − a = a
  4. a + (−a) = 0

Solution.

Step 1Adding zero keeps a unchanged; subtracting a from itself gives zero; opposite integers sum to zero.
Step 2But 0 − a is −a, not generally a. Taking a = 1 disproves the third statement.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

A diver is 12 m below sea level. Sea level is represented by 0 and upward motion is positive. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The diver’s position is −12 m.
  2. After rising 7 m, the position is −5 m.
  3. After descending a further 3 m from the original position, it is −9 m.
  4. The distance from the original position to sea level is 12 m.

Solution.

Step 1Below sea level is negative, so the initial position is −12 m. Rising gives −12 + 7 = −5 m.
Step 2Descending from the original position gives −12 − 3 = −15 m. The original distance to 0 is 12 m.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
