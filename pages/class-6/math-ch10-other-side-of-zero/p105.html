Example 6

The four numbers in a row of a puzzle must add up to 0. Three of them are $-13$, 8 and $-6$. Find the fourth number.

Solution.

Step 1The three numbers add up to $(-13) + 8 + (-6) = -11$.
Step 2The fourth number must be the inverse of $-11$, which is 11.
Check$(-13) + 8 + (-6) + 11 = 0$

Example 7

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) $(-7) + 12$(1) 19
(Q) $(-7) - 12$(2) $-5$
(R) $7 - (-12)$(3) 5
(S) $(-12) + 7$(4) $-19$
  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$(-7) + 12 = 5$; $(-7) - 12 = -19$; $7 - (-12) = 7 + 12 = 19$; and $(-12) + 7 = -5$.
CheckP and S are inverses: $5 + (-5) = 0$. So are Q and R: $(-19) + 19 = 0$.
