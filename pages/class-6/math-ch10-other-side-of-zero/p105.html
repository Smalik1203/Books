Example 9

A pocket holds 12 tokens, and their value is $-4$. How many of the tokens are positive, and how many are negative?

Solution.

Step 1 With the zero pairs taken away, 4 negative tokens are left the value is $-4$
Step 2 The other $12 - 4 = 8$ tokens make 4 zero pairs
Step 3 So there are 4 positive and $4 + 4 = 8$ negative tokens
Answer 4 positive tokens and 8 negative tokens

Example 10

A shop’s bank balance is ₹450. There is a debit of ₹600, then a debit of ₹275, and then a credit of ₹500. What is the balance at the end? When was it below zero?

Solution.

Step 1 $450 + (-600) = -150$ a debit is negative
Step 2 $(-150) + (-275) = -425$
Step 3 $(-425) + 500 = 75$ a credit is positive
Answer ₹75 at the end; below zero after each of the two debits
Example 11

At 4 a.m. it was $-6$ °C, and it grew 3 degrees warmer every hour. What was it at 9 a.m.?

Solution.

Step 1 From 4 a.m. to 9 a.m. is 5 hours
Step 2 $-6$,  $-3$,  $0$,  $3$,  $6$,  $9$ 3 degrees each hour
Step 3 The rise is $5 \times 3 = 15$, and $(-6) + 15 = 9$ check
Answer 9 °C
Example 12

A hill top is at $+650$ m and a nearby lake bed is at $-80$ m. How much higher is the hill top?

Solution.

Step 1 Difference: $650 - (-80) = 650 + 80 = 730$ higher minus lower
Answer 730 m
