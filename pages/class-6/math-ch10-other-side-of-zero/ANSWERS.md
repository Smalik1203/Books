### Exercise 10.19

1. *Answers will vary.* In the Building of Fun: adding two positives is
   going up and then up again, so you end above the ground (addition rule 1);
   adding two negatives is going down twice (rule 2); adding a positive and
   a negative is going up and down, and the larger move decides which side
   of the ground you end on (rule 3); a move and its inverse bring you back
   (rule 4); a move of 0 leaves you where you are (rule 5). Subtraction is
   the movement from the start to the target: from Floor 2 to Floor 3 is
   $+1$ (subtraction rule 1), from Floor 3 to Floor 2 is $-1$ (rule 2), from
   Floor $-3$ to Floor 2 is $+5$, the same as $2 + 3$ (rule 3), from a floor
   to itself is 0 (rule 4), and from Floor $-2$ to Floor 0 is $+2$ (rule 5).
2. *Answers will vary.* For example: $4 + 6 = 10$; $(-4) + (-6) = -10$;
   $(-9) + 4 = -5$; $7 + (-7) = 0$; $(-8) + 0 = -8$; $9 - 4 = 5$;
   $4 - 9 = -5$; $5 - (-4) = 9$; $(-6) - (-6) = 0$; $0 - (-7) = 7$.
