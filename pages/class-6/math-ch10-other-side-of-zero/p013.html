Using tokens for subtraction

You can subtract with tokens too. To work out $(+5) - (+4)$, put down 5 positive tokens and take 4 of them away. One positive token is left, so $(+5) - (+4) = +1$.

To work out $(-7) - (-5)$, put down 7 negative tokens and take 5 of them away. Two negative tokens are left, so $(-7) - (-5) = -2$. In Fig. 10.10 the tokens taken away are drawn dashed.

Think and Reflect
  1. Is $(-7) - (-5)$ the same as $(-7) + (+5)$? Use tokens to check.

Now try $(+5) - (+6)$. Put down 5 positive tokens. There are not enough of them to take 6 away.

So put down an extra zero pair: one positive token and one negative token. A zero pair is worth 0, so this does not change the value of the tokens. Now there are 6 positive tokens, and you can take them all away. One negative token is left, so $(+5) - (+6) = -1$.
