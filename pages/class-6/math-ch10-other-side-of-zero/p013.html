Using tokens for subtraction

You can subtract with tokens too. To work out $(+5) - (+4)$, put down 5 positive tokens and take 4 of them away. One positive token is left, so $(+5) - (+4) = +1$.

To work out $(-7) - (-5)$, put down 7 negative tokens and take 5 of them away. Two negative tokens are left, so $(-7) - (-5) = -2$. In Fig. 10.10 the tokens taken away are drawn dashed.
