Here is another way to see it. One press of $+$ and one press of $-$ cancel each other. So a positive token and a negative token together are worth 0. Such a pair is called a zero pair. Take away all the zero pairs in Fig. 10.7, and 2 positive tokens are left. So $(+5) + (-3) = +2$.

You can do any addition of integers with tokens.

Example 3

Add $+5$ and $-8$ using tokens.

Solution.

Step 1 Put down 5 positive tokens and 8 negative tokens as in Fig. 10.8
Step 2 You can take away 5 zero pairs each is worth 0
Step 3 Then 3 negative tokens are left
Answer $(+5) + (-8) = -3$
