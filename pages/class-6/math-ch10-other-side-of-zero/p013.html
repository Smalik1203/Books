10.2The Token Model

Using tokens for addition

The lift attendant in the Building of Fun is bored. To pass the time, he keeps a box of blue positive tokens and orange negative tokens. Each time he presses $+$, he puts a positive token in his pocket. Each time he presses $-$, he puts a negative token in his pocket. He starts on Floor 0 with an empty pocket. After an hour, he finds 5 positive tokens and 3 negative tokens in his pocket. So he pressed $+$ five times and $-$ three times, and $(+5) + (-3) = +2$. He is on Floor $+2$.

Here is another way to see it. One press of $+$ and one press of $-$ cancel each other. So a positive token and a negative token together are worth 0. Such a pair is called a zero pair. Take away all the zero pairs in Fig. 10.8, and 2 positive tokens are left. So $(+5) + (-3) = +2$.

You can do any addition of integers with tokens.

Example 3

Add $+5$ and $-8$ using tokens.

Solution.

Step 1 Put down 5 positive tokens and 8 negative tokens as in Fig. 10.9
Step 2 You can take away 5 zero pairs each is worth 0
Step 3 Then 3 negative tokens are left
Check On the number line, 8 steps to the left of $+5$ is $-3$
Answer $(+5) + (-8) = -3$
