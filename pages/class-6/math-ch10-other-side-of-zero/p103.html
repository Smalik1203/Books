Type 2 · Adding integers

Example 3

A diver is 18 m below sea level. She swims up 7 m, and then dives down 12 m. What is her height now?

Solution.

Step 1 She starts at $-18$ m below sea level
Step 2 $(-18) + (+7) = -11$ up is positive
Step 3 $(-11) + (-12) = -23$ down is negative
Step 4 $(+7) + (-12) = -5$ and $(-18) + (-5) = -23$ check: total movement
Answer $-23$ m, that is, 23 m below sea level
Example 4

Find $(-36) + 19 + (-14) + 25$.

Solution.

Step 1 $19 + 25 = 44$ positives together
Step 2 $(-36) + (-14) = -50$ negatives together
Step 3 $44 + (-50) = -6$ the larger is negative
Answer $(-36) + 19 + (-14) + 25 = -6$

Adding in any order gives the same total, so the positive numbers and the negative numbers can be added separately first.

Type 3 · Subtracting integers

Example 5

A submarine is 350 m below sea level. It rises until it is 120 m below sea level. What is its movement?

Solution.

Step 1 Starting height $-350$ m, target height $-120$ m
Step 2 Movement $= (-120) - (-350)$ target minus start
Step 3 $(-120) - (-350) = (-120) + (+350) = +230$ add the inverse
Answer $+230$ m, a rise of 230 m
