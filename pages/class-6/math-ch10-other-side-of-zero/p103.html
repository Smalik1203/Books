Example 2

The integer exactly halfway between $-14$ and 8 on the number line is

  1. $-11$
  2. $-3$
  3. $3$
  4. $11$

Solution.

Step 1From $-14$ to 8 is $8 - (-14) = 22$ steps.
Step 2Half of 22 is 11, and $-14 + 11 = -3$.
CheckFrom $-3$ to 8 is also $8 - (-3) = 11$ steps.
Answer(b) $-3$
