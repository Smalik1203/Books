10.1Bela’s Building of Fun

Bela owns an ice cream factory, and children love to visit it. To give them even more fun, she bought a tall building and filled every floor with something to do. She calls it Bela’s Building of Fun.

This is no ordinary building. Some of its floors are below the ground. Look at Fig. 10.2. Which shops are below the ground? What is on the ground floor?

A lift takes you up and down between the floors. It has two buttons: $+$ to go up one floor, and $-$ to go down one floor. In Fig. 10.2 the lift runs beside the building, and it can stop at every floor.

There are twelve floors in all, counting the ground floor.

To go from the Welcome Hall to the Art Centre, you press $+$ twice. We write this as $+2$. To go down two floors, you press $-$ twice, and we write this as $-2$. So pressing $+1$ takes you up one floor, and pressing $-1$ takes you down one floor.
