Now try $(+5) - (+6)$. Put down 5 positive tokens. There are not enough of them to take 6 away.

So put down an extra zero pair: one positive token and one negative token. A zero pair is worth 0, so this does not change the value of the tokens. Now there are 6 positive tokens, and you can take them all away. One negative token is left, so $(+5) - (+6) = -1$.

Example 4

Work out $(+4) - (-6)$ using tokens.

Solution.

Step 1 Start with 4 positive tokens
Step 2 You have to take away 6 negative tokens, but there are none
Step 3 So put down 6 zero pairs the value does not change
Step 4 Now there are 6 negative tokens. Take them away
Step 5 The 4 positive tokens and the 6 from the zero pairs are left see Fig. 10.12
Answer $(+4) - (-6) = +10$
