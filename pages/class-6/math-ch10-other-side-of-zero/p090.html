By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Find the value of $(-9) + (+4) - (-5)$.
  1. Which is smaller, $-101$ or $-99$? Give a reason.
  1. Write the inverse of $-12$. Then write the inverse of that number.
  1. A kite is 25 m above sea level. A fish is 10 m below sea level, straight below the kite. Find the distance between them.
  1. Write the next three numbers of the sequence $5, 2, -1, -4, \ldots$ and state its rule.
  1. Find the integer that must be added to $-18$ to get $-7$.
  1. Write the integer just before $-10$ and the integer just after $-10$. Find their sum.
  1. At 6 a.m. the temperature in Gulmarg was $-4$ °C. By noon it had risen by 7 degrees. Find the temperature at noon.
  1. Is $(-9) - (-4)$ equal to $(-9) + (+4)$? Justify your answer.
  1. A pocket holds 6 positive tokens and 10 negative tokens. Find the value of the tokens.
Short answer
  1. Asha’s bank balance is $-75$ rupees. Find the credit that will make her balance ₹40. Also find her balance if she then has a debit of ₹60.
  1. From the ground, the bottom of a well is at $-18$ m, a basement floor at $-4$ m and a roof at $+12$ m. Write the heights in order, from the lowest. How far is the bottom of the well below the roof?
  1. A pocket holds 5 positive tokens and 5 negative tokens. Ravi takes away 2 of the negative tokens. Find the value of the tokens now. Which subtraction does this show?
  1. A hollow 3 by 3 grid has border sum $-5$. Its top left corner is 4, its top right corner is $-6$ and its bottom right corner is 1. Find the middle number of the top row and the middle number of the right column.
  1. In Dras, the temperature was $-16$ °C at night and 7 °C the next afternoon. Find the rise. By evening it fell by 12 degrees. Find the evening temperature.
  1. Explain, using a number line, why $3 + (-5) = -2$. Draw the jump.
  1. Find the value of each of $7 - 11$, $11 - 7$, $(-7) - 11$ and $11 - (-7)$. Show that the four values make two pairs of inverses.
