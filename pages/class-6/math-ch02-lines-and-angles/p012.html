Right angles

A right angle is a quarter of a full turn, and two right angles make a straight angle. Two lines that meet at a right angle are called perpendicular lines.

In a figure, we mark a right angle with a small square at the vertex instead of a curve.

  1. Fold a sheet of paper to get a slanting crease. Now fold again to get a second crease that is perpendicular to the first.
