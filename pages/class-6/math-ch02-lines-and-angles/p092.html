Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): An angle of $250^\circ$ is a reflex angle.

    Reason (R): A reflex angle is more than $180^\circ$ and less than $360^\circ$.

  1. Assertion (A): An angle whose arms are 3 cm long can be equal to an angle whose arms are 8 cm long.

    Reason (R): The size of an angle depends only on the turn between its arms.

  1. Assertion (A): A straight angle measures $180^\circ$.

    Reason (R): A right angle is a quarter of a full turn.

  1. Assertion (A): Half of any obtuse angle is an acute angle.

    Reason (R): Double any acute angle is an obtuse angle.

  1. Assertion (A): Through two points we can draw two different lines.

    Reason (R): Through one point we can draw as many lines as we like.
