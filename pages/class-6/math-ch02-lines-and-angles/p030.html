  1. Puzzle. I am an acute angle. If you double my measure, you get an acute angle. If you triple it, you get an acute angle again. If you multiply it by 4, you still get an acute angle! But if you multiply my measure by 5, you get an obtuse angle. If my measure is a whole number of degrees, what can it be?
