1. Leela puts her protractor’s centre on the vertex of an angle, with one arm on the 0 of the outer scale. The other arm crosses the outer scale at 50 and the inner at 130. She writes $130^\circ$. Is she right? Give a reason, and find the correct measure.
  1. Four rays start at $O$ and make four angles that fill a full turn. Three of the angles are $80^\circ$, $95^\circ$ and $110^\circ$. Find the fourth angle. Is any of the four angles a right angle?
  1. An acute angle is doubled, and the result is still acute. What can you say about the angle? Give an example of an acute angle whose double is obtuse.
  1. Mohan says that a reflex angle is always bigger than an obtuse angle. Explain why he is right in every case.
  1. The minute hand of a clock turns $6^\circ$ each minute. Find the angle it turns through from 4:10 to 4:35, and name its type.

  1. $\angle AOB$ is a straight angle. The ray $\overrightarrow{OC}$ is drawn so that $\angle AOC$ is $40^\circ$ more than $\angle COB$. Find both angles. The ray $\overrightarrow{OD}$ bisects $\angle AOC$. Hence find $\angle DOB$, and name its type.
  1. A wheel has 36 equally spaced spokes. Find the angle between two spokes next to each other. Hence find how many gaps apart two spokes must be to make a right angle, and how many to make a straight angle.
  1. Draw a line $\overleftrightarrow{XY}$ and mark a point $O$ on it. Draw the ray $\overrightarrow{OP}$ with $\angle YOP = 50^\circ$. Find $\angle XOP$ without measuring, and check it by measuring. Then draw $\overrightarrow{OQ}$ to bisect $\angle XOP$. Find $\angle YOQ$, and name its type.
