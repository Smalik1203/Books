Table 2.57 Types of angles and their measures
Type of angleMeasure
Acute anglemore than $0^\circ$ and less than $90^\circ$
Right angleexactly $90^\circ$
Obtuse anglemore than $90^\circ$ and less than $180^\circ$
Straight angleexactly $180^\circ$
Reflex anglemore than $180^\circ$ and less than $360^\circ$
Full turnexactly $360^\circ$
