Can you draw the whole of a line? No, because it goes on without end. So we draw a part of it, with an arrowhead at each end to say that it carries on.

The line through $A$ and $B$ is written as $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$. Sometimes a line is named by one small letter, such as $l$ or $m$.

Two points and a line

Through any two points there is exactly one line.

2.4Ray

A ray is a part of a line that starts at one point and goes on without end in one direction. The point where it starts is called its starting point. A beam of light from a lighthouse, the light from a torch and the rays of the sun are all models of a ray.

The ray in Fig. 2.4 starts at $A$ and passes through $P$. We name it $\overrightarrow{AP}$.

Segment, ray and line
  • A line segment has two end points, a ray has one, and a line has none.
  • A ray is named with its starting point first: $\overrightarrow{AP}$ starts at $A$.
