Example 12

Three angles at a point together make a full turn. Two of them are $100^\circ$ and $125^\circ$. Find the third angle. What is the reflex angle made by the arms of the $100^\circ$ angle?

Solution.

Step 1 $100^\circ + 125^\circ = 225^\circ$
Step 2 third angle $= 360^\circ - 225^\circ = 135^\circ$ together a full turn
Step 3 reflex angle $= 360^\circ - 100^\circ = 260^\circ$ the long way round
Answer The third angle is $135^\circ$, and the reflex angle is $260^\circ$.

Check: going the long way round the $100^\circ$ angle passes over the other two angles, and $125^\circ + 135^\circ = 260^\circ$.

Type 9 · Bisecting and drawing angles

Example 13

$\angle AOB = 120^\circ$. The ray $\overrightarrow{OC}$ bisects $\angle AOB$, and the ray $\overrightarrow{OD}$ bisects $\angle AOC$. Find $\angle DOB$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle AOC = 120^\circ \div 2 = 60^\circ$
Step 2 $\angle AOD = 60^\circ \div 2 = 30^\circ$
Step 3 $\angle DOB = 120^\circ - 30^\circ = 90^\circ$ what is left of $\angle AOB$
Answer $\angle DOB = 90^\circ$
Example 14

Draw $\angle LMN = 65^\circ$ with the base $\overrightarrow{MN}$ pointing to the left.

Solution.

Step 1 draw $\overrightarrow{MN}$ pointing to the left, and put the centre of the protractor on $M$
Step 2 turn the protractor so that $\overrightarrow{MN}$ runs along the 0 line
Step 3 the 0 at the left end belongs to the outer scale outer goes up left to right
Step 4 count up the outer scale to 60, then 5 more marks; mark $L$ and join $M$ to $L$
Answer $\angle LMN = 65^\circ$

Check: $65^\circ$ is acute, so $\angle LMN$ must be narrower than the corner of a page. If it looks wide, you counted on the inner scale and drew $180^\circ - 65^\circ = 115^\circ$.
