  1. P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$360^\circ - 147^\circ = 213^\circ$, and $360^\circ \div 8 = 45^\circ$.
Step 2$180^\circ - 113^\circ = 67^\circ$, and $90^\circ - 28^\circ = 62^\circ$.
Check$213^\circ + 147^\circ = 360^\circ$ and $67^\circ + 113^\circ = 180^\circ$.
Answer(a) P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3
