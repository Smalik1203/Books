Why the other options are wrong

B2 A is true: three rays from $P$ make three angles, and all of them have the vertex $P$. R is true as well. But R is about the order of the letters, not about why one letter is not enough, so the answer is (b), not (a).
B3 A $190^\circ$ angle is more than a straight angle, so it is reflex, not obtuse. A is false. R is true, but it leaves out the other half of the rule: an obtuse angle is also less than $180^\circ$. The answer is (d).
