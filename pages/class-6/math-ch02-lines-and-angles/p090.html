By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Find the measure of three quarters of a full turn. What type of angle is it?
  1. How many right angles make a straight angle, and how many make a full turn? Give a reason for each.
  1. Is an angle of $180^\circ$ a reflex angle? Give a reason.
  1. $\angle AOB$ is a straight angle, and the ray $\overrightarrow{OC}$ makes $\angle AOC = 65^\circ$. Find $\angle COB$.
  1. The reflex angle between two arms is $235^\circ$. Find the smaller angle between the same arms, and name its type.
  1. The points $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ lie in that order on a line. Name all the line segments whose end points are two of these points.
  1. A straight angle is divided into 4 equal angles. Find the measure of each angle, and name its type.
  1. An angle is bisected, and each of the two new angles is $35^\circ$. Find the measure of the angle. Is it acute or obtuse?
  1. Write the vertex and the arms of $\angle PQR$. Also write one more name for the same angle.
  1. The minute hand of a clock turns from 12 to 3. Find the angle it turns through, and name its type.
Short answer
  1. $\angle AOB$ is a straight angle. The rays $\overrightarrow{OC}$ and $\overrightarrow{OD}$ lie on the same side of $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$, in the order $\overrightarrow{OA}$, $\overrightarrow{OC}$, $\overrightarrow{OD}$, $\overrightarrow{OB}$. If $\angle AOC = 40^\circ$ and $\angle DOB = 60^\circ$, find $\angle COD$. Also find $\angle AOD$ and name its type.
  1. Explain, with a figure, why a straight angle is made of two right angles.
  1. Ramesh draws an angle of $140^\circ$ and bisects it. Then he bisects one of the two new angles. Find the measures of the angles he gets at each step, and name the type of each.
  1. The spokes of a wheel are equally spaced, and two spokes next to each other make an angle of $36^\circ$. Find the number of spokes. Also find the angle between two spokes that have two other spokes between them.
  1. Two angles together make a full turn, and one of them is $70^\circ$. Find the other angle and name its type. Can both angles be obtuse? Justify your answer.
