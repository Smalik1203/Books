1. For Diwali, Meera draws a rangoli on the floor at her door. It is a wheel with 8 equally spaced spokes that meet at the centre.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the angle between two spokes next to each other.
    2. Find the angle between two spokes that have one spoke between them, and name its type.
    3. Her sister wants a wheel whose spokes are $40^\circ$ apart. How many spokes does she need? Do any two of her spokes make a straight angle? Justify your answer.

  1. A school gate turns about its hinge. When it is shut, it lies along the wall. In the morning, Mr Singh, the watchman, opens it through $35^\circ$. The gate can open until it lies along the wall on the other side.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. What type of angle is $35^\circ$?
    2. At noon he opens the gate three times as far. Find the angle.
    3. From its noon position, how much further can the gate open? Justify your answer.

  1. Near Jaisalmer, wind turbines turn in the desert wind to make electricity. Each turbine has 3 equally spaced blades. Arjun times one turbine: it turns through $45^\circ$ each second, at a steady speed.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the angle between two blades.
    2. In how many seconds does the turbine make a full turn?
    3. Through how many degrees does it turn in 5 seconds? What type of angle is this?

  1. A giant wheel at the Dussehra fair has 12 cabins, equally spaced round its rim. Each cabin hangs from a spoke that joins it to the centre. Riya’s cabin starts at the bottom.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the angle at the centre between two cabins next to each other.
    2. The wheel turns through $90^\circ$. How many cabin places has it moved?
