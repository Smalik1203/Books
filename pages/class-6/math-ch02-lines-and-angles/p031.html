2.11Types of Angles and their Measures

You already know that a straight angle is $180^\circ$ and a right angle is $90^\circ$. How can we describe acute and obtuse angles using degrees?

Acute angle. An angle that is bigger than $0^\circ$ and smaller than $90^\circ$ is called an acute angle.

Obtuse angle. An angle that is bigger than $90^\circ$ and smaller than $180^\circ$ is called an obtuse angle (Fig. 2.55).

Have we covered every size an angle can have? Not yet. Here is one more type.

Reflex angle. An angle that is bigger than $180^\circ$ and smaller than $360^\circ$ is called a reflex angle. The curve that marks it goes the long way round.

A right angle, a straight angle and a full turn each have one exact measure. Acute, obtuse and reflex angles each cover a range of measures: $1^\circ$ and $89^\circ$ are both acute. Table 2.57 puts all six types together.
