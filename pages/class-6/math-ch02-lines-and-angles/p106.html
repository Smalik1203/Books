Example 12 · Numerical answer

An angle of 152° is bisected, and one of the resulting angles is bisected again. Enter the measure, in degrees, of one of the smallest angles.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The first bisection gives 152 ÷ 2 = 76 degrees.
Step 2The second gives 76 ÷ 2 = 38 degrees.
Answer38
Example 13 · Numerical answer

Four rays make four consecutive angles around a point. Three angles measure 75°, 88° and 109°. Enter the fourth angle in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The angles around the point total 360°.
Step 2The known angles total 272°, leaving 360 − 272 = 88.
Answer88
Example 14 · Matching

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) Complement of 28°(1) 45°
(Q) Supplement of 113°(2) 213°
(R) Reflex angle paired with 147°(3) 62°
(S) One eighth of a full turn(4) 67°

Each entry has exactly one match

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Complements total 90°: 90 − 28 = 62. Supplements total 180°: 180 − 113 = 67.
Step 2The reflex angle is 360 − 147 = 213 degrees; one eighth-turn is 360 ÷ 8 = 45 degrees.
Answer(b) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
