Type 7 · Types of angles by their measure

Example 10

Say whether each angle is acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex or a full turn: $1^\circ,\ 179^\circ,\ 181^\circ,\ 90^\circ,\ 270^\circ,\ 360^\circ$.

Solution.

Step 1 $1^\circ$ is less than $90^\circ$ acute
Step 2 $179^\circ$ is between $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ obtuse
Step 3 $181^\circ$ and $270^\circ$ are between $180^\circ$ and $360^\circ$ reflex
Step 4 $90^\circ$ and $360^\circ$ are exact measures right, full turn
Answer acute, obtuse, reflex, right, reflex, full turn

$179^\circ$ and $181^\circ$ differ by only $2^\circ$. But the straight angle, $180^\circ$, lies between them, so they are of different types.

Type 8 · Angles that fill a straight angle or a full turn

Example 11

In Fig. 2.64, $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$ is a straight line through $O$, $\angle AOC = 35^\circ$ and $\angle DOB = 55^\circ$. Find $\angle COD$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle AOC + \angle COD + \angle DOB = 180^\circ$ together a straight angle
Step 2 $35^\circ + 55^\circ = 90^\circ$
Step 3 $\angle COD = 180^\circ - 90^\circ = 90^\circ$
Answer $\angle COD = 90^\circ$, a right angle
