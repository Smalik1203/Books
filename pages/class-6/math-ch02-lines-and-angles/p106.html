Example 6

Four rays start at a point and make four angles that fill a full turn. Three of the angles are $75^\circ$, $88^\circ$ and $109^\circ$. Find, in degrees, the fourth angle.

Solution.

Step 1The four angles add up to a full turn, $360^\circ$.
Step 2The three known angles add up to $75^\circ + 88^\circ + 109^\circ = 272^\circ$.
Step 3The fourth angle is $360^\circ - 272^\circ = 88^\circ$.
Answer88

  1. P–2, Q–3, R–1, S–4
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  3. P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2
  4. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2

Solution.

Step 1$89^\circ$ is less than $90^\circ$, so it is acute. $90^\circ$ is a right angle.
Step 2$179^\circ$ is between $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$, so it is obtuse. $181^\circ$ is between $180^\circ$ and $360^\circ$, so it is reflex.
Answer(d) P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
