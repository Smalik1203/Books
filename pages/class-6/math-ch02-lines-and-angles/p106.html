Example 10

A circle is cut from its centre into equal parts of $10^\circ$ each. How many parts are there? How many of them together make a straight angle?

Solution.

Step 1 number of parts $= 360^\circ \div 10^\circ = 36$ a full turn is $360^\circ$
Step 2 $180^\circ \div 10^\circ = 18$ a straight angle is $180^\circ$
Answer 36 parts, and 18 of them make a straight angle

Half of the parts make half a turn, and $36 \div 2 = 18$.

Example 11

Through how many degrees does the minute hand of a clock turn between 4:05 and 4:40? What type of angle is this turn?

Solution. The minute hand goes once round the dial, a full turn, in 60 minutes.

Step 1 one minute $= 360^\circ \div 60 = 6^\circ$ 60 equal parts of a turn
Step 2 from 4:05 to 4:40 is $40 - 5 = 35$ minutes
Step 3 $35 \times 6^\circ = 210^\circ$
Answer $210^\circ$, a reflex angle

Check: 30 minutes is half a turn, $180^\circ$, and 35 minutes is a little more, so the turn is just past a straight angle.

Example 12

Say whether each angle is acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex or a full turn: $1^\circ,\ 179^\circ,\ 181^\circ,\ 90^\circ,\ 270^\circ,\ 360^\circ$.

Solution.

Step 1 $1^\circ$ is less than $90^\circ$ acute
Step 2 $179^\circ$ is between $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ obtuse
Step 3 $181^\circ$ and $270^\circ$ are between $180^\circ$ and $360^\circ$ reflex
Step 4 $90^\circ$ and $360^\circ$ are exact measures right, full turn
Answer acute, obtuse, reflex, right, reflex, full turn

$179^\circ$ and $181^\circ$ differ by only $2^\circ$. But the straight angle, $180^\circ$, lies between them, so they are of different types.
