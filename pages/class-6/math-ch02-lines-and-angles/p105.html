Type 5 · Reading a protractor

Example 7

One arm of an angle passes through 0 on the outer scale of a protractor. The other arm passes through the mark that reads 140 on the outer scale and 40 on the inner scale. Find the angle, and say what type it is.

Solution.

Step 1 read the outer scale it is 0 on the first arm
Step 2 the outer scale reads 140 on the second arm
Step 3 $140^\circ$ is more than $90^\circ$ and less than $180^\circ$
Answer $140^\circ$, an obtuse angle

The inner reading, 40, counts from the other end of the protractor, and $140^\circ + 40^\circ = 180^\circ$.

Example 8

The centre of a protractor is on the vertex $O$. The arms of $\angle POQ$ cross the inner scale at 25 and at 110. Find $\angle POQ$.

Solution.

Step 1 neither arm is on 0, so subtract the two readings count the units between
Step 2 $110 - 25 = 85$
Step 3 outer scale: $180 - 25 = 155$ and $180 - 110 = 70$, and $155 - 70 = 85$ check on the other scale
Answer $\angle POQ = 85^\circ$

Type 6 · Equal parts of a turn

Example 9

A circle is cut from its centre into 20 equal parts. Find the angle of one part. How many parts together make a right angle?

Solution.

Step 1 one part $= 360^\circ \div 20 = 18^\circ$ a full turn is $360^\circ$
Step 2 $90^\circ \div 18^\circ = 5$
Answer One part is $18^\circ$, and 5 parts make a right angle.
