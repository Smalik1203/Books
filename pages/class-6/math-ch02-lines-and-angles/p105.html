Example 5

An angle of $152^\circ$ is bisected, and one of the two new angles is bisected again. Find, in degrees, the measure of one of the smallest angles.

Solution.

Step 1The first bisection gives two angles of $152^\circ \div 2 = 76^\circ$.
Step 2Bisecting one of them gives two angles of $76^\circ \div 2 = 38^\circ$.
CheckFour of the smallest angles make the first angle: $4 \times 38 = 152$.
Answer38
