Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which turns leave a pointer facing exactly opposite its starting direction?

Select all correct options.

  1. 180° clockwise
  2. 540° clockwise
  3. 360° anticlockwise
  4. 180° anticlockwise

Solution.

Step 1Either direction of a half-turn reverses the pointer.
Step 2540° is one full turn followed by a half-turn. A full turn of 360° alone restores the starting direction.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Three adjacent angles fill one straight angle without overlapping. Their measures are 42°, 68° and x°. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. x = 70.
  2. The largest of the three is obtuse.
  3. All three angles are acute.
  4. The two smallest angles together measure 110°.

Solution.

Step 1x = 180 − 42 − 68 = 70.
Step 2The measures 42°, 68° and 70° are all below 90°. The two smallest sum to 110°.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
