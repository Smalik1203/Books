Make your own protractor

How are the equal marks on a protractor made? You can make some of them yourself, just by folding paper.

Step 1. Draw a circle on paper and cut it out. The whole circle is one full turn, $360^\circ$.

Step 2. Fold the circle into two equal halves. Cut along the crease to get a half circle. Half a turn is $\frac{1}{2}$ of $360^\circ$, which is $180^\circ$. Write $0^\circ$ at the bottom right corner and $180^\circ$ at the bottom left corner.

Step 3. Fold the half circle in half to get a quarter circle. A quarter turn is $\frac{1}{4}$ of $360^\circ$, or $\frac{1}{2}$ of $180^\circ$. Fill in: a quarter turn is . Write this measure at the top of the half circle, where the crease meets the edge.
