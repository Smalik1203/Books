2.10Drawing Angles

Vidya wants to draw an angle of $30^\circ$ and name it $\angle TIN$. In $\angle TIN$, the vertex is $I$, and the arms are $\overrightarrow{IT}$ and $\overrightarrow{IN}$. She first draws one arm, $\overrightarrow{IN}$, as the base. The other arm, $\overrightarrow{IT}$, must be turned $30^\circ$ from the base.

Step 1. Draw the base, the ray $\overrightarrow{IN}$.

Step 2. Place the centre of the protractor on $I$. Turn it so that $\overrightarrow{IN}$ runs along the 0 line.

Step 3. Start from the 0 on $\overrightarrow{IN}$. Count up the scale in tens, 10, 20, 30, and mark a point $T$ at $30^\circ$.
