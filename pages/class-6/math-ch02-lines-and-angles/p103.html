Type 2 · Naming angles

Example 2

Name every angle at $A$ and every angle at $D$ in Fig. 2.63.

Solution.

Step 1 the rays from $A$ are $\overrightarrow{AB}$, $\overrightarrow{AD}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$
Step 2 $\angle BAD$, $\angle DAC$ and $\angle BAC$ one angle a pair
Step 3 the rays from $D$ are $\overrightarrow{DB}$, $\overrightarrow{DA}$ and $\overrightarrow{DC}$
Step 4 $\angle BDA$, $\angle ADC$ and $\angle BDC$ $\overrightarrow{DB}$, $\overrightarrow{DC}$ are opposite
Answer at $A$: $\angle BAD$, $\angle DAC$, $\angle BAC$; at $D$: $\angle BDA$, $\angle ADC$ and the straight angle $\angle BDC$

Type 3 · Comparing angles

Example 3

$\angle P$ is two thirds of a right angle. $\angle Q$ is one quarter of a straight angle. Which angle is bigger, and by how many degrees?

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle P = 90^\circ \div 3 \times 2 = 60^\circ$ a right angle is $90^\circ$
Step 2 $\angle Q = 180^\circ \div 4 = 45^\circ$ a straight angle is $180^\circ$
Step 3 $60^\circ - 45^\circ = 15^\circ$
Answer $\angle P$ is bigger, by $15^\circ$
