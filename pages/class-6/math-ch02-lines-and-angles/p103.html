Example 3 · Single correct

A robot faces east and turns clockwise through 225°. Which direction does it face?

Choose one correct option.

  1. North-east
  2. North-west
  3. South-west
  4. South-east

Solution.

Step 1A clockwise half-turn from east points west.
Step 2The remaining 45° turns from west towards north, reaching north-west.
Answer(b) North-west
Example 4 · Single correct

A protractor has opposite scales. One ray is aligned with the 0° mark on the right. The other ray reads 68° on the scale starting at the right and 112° on the other scale. What is the angle between the rays?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 44°
  2. 180°
  3. 112°
  4. 68°

Solution.

Step 1Use the scale whose zero is on the starting ray.
Step 2That scale reads 68° at the other ray.
Answer(d) 68°
Example 5 · Single correct

A straight angle is divided into three adjacent angles in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. What is the largest angle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 80°
  2. 40°
  3. 60°
  4. 90°

Solution.

Step 1The 9 equal parts together measure 180°, so each part measures 20°.
Step 2The largest angle has 4 parts: 4 × 20° = 80°.
Answer(a) 80°
