Problem 2

One arm of an angle passes through 0 on the inner scale of a protractor. The other arm passes through the mark where the scales read 65 and 115. The angle measures

  1. $65^\circ$
  2. $115^\circ$
  3. $50^\circ$
  4. $180^\circ$
Solution

Read the scale that starts from 0 on the first arm. That is the inner scale, and on the inner scale the second arm reads 115.

inner scale: 0 on the first arm, 115 on the second

angle $= 115 - 0 = 115^\circ$

The answer is $115^\circ$, option (b). Option (a) is the reading on the outer scale, which starts from 0 on the other side of the protractor. The two readings always add up to $180^\circ$, which is option (d). Option (c) is the difference of the two readings, which is not the angle at all.

Before you read a scale, look at the angle. An angle of $115^\circ$ is obtuse and an angle of $65^\circ$ is acute, so a quick look tells you which reading is the right one.

Problem 3

$\angle AOB$ is a straight angle. A ray $\overrightarrow{OC}$ makes $\angle AOC = 40^\circ$, and the ray $\overrightarrow{OD}$ bisects $\angle COB$. Then $\angle COD$ is

  1. $70^\circ$
  2. $140^\circ$
  3. $20^\circ$
  4. $110^\circ$
Solution

First find $\angle COB$ from the straight angle. Then halve it, because $\overrightarrow{OD}$ bisects it.

$\angle COB = 180^\circ - 40^\circ = 140^\circ$

$\angle COD = 140^\circ \div 2 = 70^\circ$

The answer is $70^\circ$, option (a). Option (b) is $\angle COB$, before it is halved. Option (c) halves $\angle AOC$ instead of $\angle COB$. Option (d) is $\angle AOD$, which is $40^\circ + 70^\circ$.

The ray $\overrightarrow{OD}$ halves $\angle COB$, not the whole straight angle. A rough figure, with the rays drawn in the right order, shows at once which angle is being halved. It also gives a check: $\angle AOC + \angle COD + \angle DOB = 40^\circ + 70^\circ + 70^\circ = 180^\circ$, which is the straight angle.
