Example 2

The smaller angle between two rays is $146^\circ$. The reflex angle between the same two rays is

  1. $34^\circ$
  2. $146^\circ$
  3. $214^\circ$
  4. $292^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1The smaller angle and the reflex angle together make a full turn, $360^\circ$.
Step 2So the reflex angle is $360^\circ - 146^\circ = 214^\circ$.
Step 3$214^\circ$ is more than $180^\circ$, so it is a reflex angle, as it must be.
Check$214^\circ + 146^\circ = 360^\circ$, a full turn.
Answer(c) $214^\circ$
