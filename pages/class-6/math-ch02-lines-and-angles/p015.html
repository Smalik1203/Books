Classifying angles

The angles in Fig. 2.27 are sorted into three groups. The middle group are all right angles. What do the angles in each of the other two groups have in common?

Every angle in the first group is smaller than a right angle. Each is less than a quarter turn. These are called acute angles.

Every angle in the third group is bigger than a right angle but smaller than a straight angle. Each is more than a quarter turn and less than a half turn. These are called obtuse angles.
