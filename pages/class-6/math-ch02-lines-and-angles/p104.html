Example 3

An angle measures $124^\circ$. Which of these statements are correct?

  1. It is an acute angle.
  2. It is an obtuse angle.
  3. Each half of it measures $62^\circ$.
  4. It is $24^\circ$ more than a right angle.

Solution.

Step 1$124^\circ$ is more than $90^\circ$ and less than $180^\circ$, so the angle is obtuse, not acute: (a) is wrong and (b) is right.
Step 2Each half is $124^\circ \div 2 = 62^\circ$.
Step 3$124^\circ - 90^\circ = 34^\circ$, not $24^\circ$.
Answer(b), (c)
Example 4

Three angles fill a straight angle without overlapping. They measure $42^\circ$, $68^\circ$ and $x^\circ$. Which of these statements are correct?

  1. $x = 70$.
  2. The largest angle is obtuse.
  3. All three angles are acute.
  4. The two smallest add up to $110^\circ$.

Solution.

Step 1The three angles add up to $180^\circ$, so $x = 180 - 42 - 68 = 70$.
Step 2$42^\circ$, $68^\circ$ and $70^\circ$ are all less than $90^\circ$, so all three are acute and none is obtuse: (b) is wrong.
Step 3The two smallest are $42^\circ + 68^\circ = 110^\circ$.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
