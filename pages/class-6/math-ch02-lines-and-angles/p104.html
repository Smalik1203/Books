Example 6 · Single correct

The smaller angle between two rays is 146°. What is the reflex angle formed by the same rays?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 34°
  2. 146°
  3. 214°
  4. 292°

Solution.

Step 1The smaller angle and the reflex angle make a full turn.
Step 2360° − 146° = 214°.
Answer(c) 214°
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which statements about lines, rays and segments are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. A segment has two endpoints.
  2. A ray has two endpoints.
  3. A line extends without end in both directions.
  4. Reversing the endpoints of a segment gives the same segment.

Solution.

Step 1A segment is the finite part between its two endpoints, independent of the order in which they are named.
Step 2A ray has one endpoint; a line has no endpoints and extends in both directions.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

An angle measures 124°. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It is acute.
  2. It is obtuse.
  3. Its two equal halves each measure 62°.
  4. It exceeds a right angle by 24°.

Solution.

Step 1124° lies between 90° and 180°, so it is obtuse.
Step 2124° ÷ 2 = 62°; 124° − 90° = 34°, not 24°.
Answer(b), (c)
