Type 4 · Turns, right angles and straight angles

Example 4

Asha faces east. She turns clockwise through three right angles. Which way does she face now? What part of a full turn has she made?

Solution.

Step 1 east to south, south to west, west to north clockwise quarter turns
Step 2 $3 \times 90^\circ = 270^\circ$
Step 3 four right angles make a full turn, so three make three quarters of it
Answer She faces north. She has made three quarters of a full turn, $270^\circ$.
Example 5

A ray $\overrightarrow{OC}$ divides the straight angle $\angle AOB$ so that $\angle AOC$ is 5 times $\angle COB$. Find both angles.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle COB$ is 1 part and $\angle AOC$ is 5 parts: 6 equal parts in all together a straight angle
Step 2 one part $= 180^\circ \div 6 = 30^\circ$
Step 3 $\angle AOC = 5 \times 30^\circ = 150^\circ$
Answer $\angle COB = 30^\circ$ and $\angle AOC = 150^\circ$

Check: $150^\circ + 30^\circ = 180^\circ$, the straight angle.

Type 5 · Reading a protractor

Example 6

One arm of an angle passes through 0 on the outer scale of a protractor. The other arm passes through the mark that reads 140 on the outer scale and 40 on the inner scale. Find the angle, and say what type it is.

Solution.

Step 1 read the outer scale it is 0 on the first arm
Step 2 the outer scale reads 140 on the second arm
Step 3 $140^\circ$ is more than $90^\circ$ and less than $180^\circ$
Answer $140^\circ$, an obtuse angle

The inner reading, 40, counts from the other end of the protractor, and $140^\circ + 40^\circ = 180^\circ$.
