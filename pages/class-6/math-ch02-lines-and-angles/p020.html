To bisect an angle drawn on paper, fold the paper through the vertex so that one arm lies exactly on the other. The crease is the angle bisector. If $\angle AOB$ in Fig. 2.38 is $70^\circ$, each of the two angles it is split into is $35^\circ$.

Equal angles in a figure are often marked with matching curves, each with a small stroke across it. When two curves are marked the same way, you know the angles are equal without measuring them.

Find the angle bisectors in your paper protractor. Then try to make other angles by folding, using angle bisectors.
