Objective questions
  1. The number of end points of a ray is
    1. 0
    2. 1
    3. 2
    4. many
  1. An angle of $90^\circ$ is
    1. acute
    2. obtuse
    3. right
    4. straight
  1. Three quarters of a full turn measures
    1. $270^\circ$
    2. $180^\circ$
    3. $90^\circ$
    4. $300^\circ$
  1. The vertex of $\angle XYZ$ is
    1. $X$
    2. $Z$
    3. $\overline{XZ}$
    4. $Y$
  1. If $\angle AOC + \angle COB = 180^\circ$ and $\angle AOC = 3 \times \angle COB$, then $\angle COB$ is
    1. $45^\circ$
    2. $60^\circ$
    3. $135^\circ$
    4. $90^\circ$
  1. The reflex angle between two arms is $300^\circ$. The smaller angle between the same arms is
    1. $30^\circ$
    2. $120^\circ$
    3. $300^\circ$
    4. $60^\circ$
  1. A full turn is divided into equal angles of $40^\circ$. The number of angles is
    1. 8
    2. 10
    3. 9
    4. 40
  1. One arm is on the inner 0 of a protractor. The other crosses 65 (inner) and 115 (outer). The angle is
    1. $65^\circ$
    2. $115^\circ$
    3. $50^\circ$
    4. $180^\circ$
  1. Which of these are true?
    1. A line segment has two end points.
    2. A line has one end point.
    3. A ray has one end point.
    1. (i) only
    2. (i) and (iii)
    3. (ii) and (iii)
    4. all three
  1. Priya says doubling an acute angle always gives an acute angle. Which is true?
    1. She is right: $2 \times 40^\circ = 80^\circ$.
    2. No: $80^\circ$ is not acute.
    3. No: $40^\circ$ is not acute.
    4. No: $2 \times 50^\circ = 100^\circ$ is obtuse.
