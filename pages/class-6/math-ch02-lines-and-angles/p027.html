Exercise Set 2.9
  1. List all the angles you can find in Fig. 2.52. Did you find them all? Guess the measure of each angle. Then measure each one with a protractor. Record your guesses and your measures in a table. How close were your guesses?
  1. Use a protractor to draw angles with these measures.
    1. $110^\circ$
    2. $40^\circ$
    3. $75^\circ$
    4. $112^\circ$
    5. $134^\circ$
  1. Draw an angle with the same measure as $\angle IHJ$ in Fig. 2.53. Write down the steps you followed.
  1. Draw an angle of $60^\circ$. Then draw its angle bisector by measuring. What is the measure of each of the two new angles?
  1. Make a right angle by folding paper, without a protractor. Then check it with a protractor. How close did you get?
  1. Draw a line $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$ and mark a point $O$ on it. Draw $\overrightarrow{OC}$ so that $\angle BOC = 70^\circ$. Now measure $\angle AOC$. What do you notice when you add the two angles?
  1. Draw a right angle with a protractor. Then draw its angle bisector. Measure the two new angles to check that each one is $45^\circ$. If they are not equal, which step went wrong?
