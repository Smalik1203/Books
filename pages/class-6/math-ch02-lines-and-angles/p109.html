Example 10

Divya cuts a round cake into 5 equal pieces, with every cut starting at the centre. Then she cuts each piece into 2 equal pieces in the same way.

  1. The angle at the centre of each final piece is
    1. $72^\circ$
    2. $36^\circ$
    3. $45^\circ$
    4. $18^\circ$
  2. Numerical answer. Divya serves 3 pieces that are next to each other. Find, in degrees, the angle they make together at the centre.
  3. Numerical answer. How many pieces next to each other make a straight angle at the centre of the cake?

Solution.

Step 1There are $5 \times 2 = 10$ pieces, and $360^\circ \div 10 = 36^\circ$.
Step 2Three pieces make $3 \times 36^\circ = 108^\circ$.
Step 3A straight angle is $180^\circ$, and $180 \div 36 = 5$.
Answer(i) (b) $36^\circ$; (ii) 108; (iii) 5
