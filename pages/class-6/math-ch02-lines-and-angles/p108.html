Example 9

The beam of a lighthouse turns round at a steady speed, through $20^\circ$ every second. At the start, the beam points along the shore.

  1. In how many seconds does the beam make a full turn?
    1. 9
    2. 18
    3. 36
    4. 20
  2. Numerical answer. Through how many degrees does the beam turn in 12 seconds?
  3. Numerical answer. After how many seconds does the beam first point exactly the opposite way to its start?

Solution.

Step 1A full turn is $360^\circ$, and $360 \div 20 = 18$, so it takes 18 seconds.
Step 2In 12 seconds the beam turns $12 \times 20^\circ = 240^\circ$.
Step 3Pointing the opposite way is a half turn, $180^\circ$, and $180 \div 20 = 9$.
Check9 seconds is half of 18 seconds, as a half turn is half of a full turn.
Answer(i) (b) 18; (ii) 240; (iii) 9
