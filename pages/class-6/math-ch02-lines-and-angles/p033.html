Measuring a reflex angle

A protractor measures angles only up to $180^\circ$. So how do you measure a reflex angle? Look at its two arms. They make two angles: the reflex angle the long way round, and a smaller angle the short way. Together the two angles make a full turn.

So measure the smaller angle with the protractor, and take it away from $360^\circ$. In Fig. 2.61 the smaller angle is $110^\circ$, so the reflex angle is $360^\circ - 110^\circ = 250^\circ$. Check: $250^\circ + 110^\circ = 360^\circ$, which is a full turn.

Angles that fill a turn

Two angles that together make a straight angle add up to $180^\circ$. Two angles that together make a full turn add up to $360^\circ$.
