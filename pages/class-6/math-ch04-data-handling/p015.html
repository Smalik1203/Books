Example 3

Draw a bar graph of the data in Table 4.21.

Solution.

Step 1 Draw a line across and a line going up. Mark the items along the line across, equally spaced
Step 2 Give every bar the same width, with equal gaps between the bars
Step 3 For the line going up, take 1 unit of length for ₹200, and mark ₹200, ₹400, and so on choose the scale
Step 4 House rent: $3000 \div 200 = 15$ units; Food: $3400 \div 200 = 17$ units bar heights
Step 5 Education: $800 \div 200 = 4$ units; Electricity: $400 \div 200 = 2$ units
Step 6 Transport: $600 \div 200 = 3$ units; Other items: $1200 \div 200 = 6$ units
Answer Drawing bars of these heights gives the bar graph in Fig. 4.22.
