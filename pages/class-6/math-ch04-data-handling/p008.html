Suppose Jarina and Sangita also use one symbol for each student, as Lakhanpal did. What problems will they run into?

There are many students, so the rows would be very long. Jarina has a plan. She lets one symbol stand for 5 students. This saves time, and it saves space on the page too.

Sangita tries one symbol for 10 students. Now 30 students are just 3 symbols. But how can she show 25 students, or 35? Then she sees that half a symbol can stand for 5 students.
