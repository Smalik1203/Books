Type 1 · Tally marks and frequency tables

Example 1

Arun asked 20 classmates which fruit they like best. In the order he asked, they said: mango, banana, apple, mango, guava, mango, banana, apple, mango, banana, mango, guava, apple, mango, banana, mango, apple, mango, banana, guava. Make a frequency table with tally marks. Which fruit do the most classmates like?

Solution.

Step 1 Take the answers one at a time, and draw a tally mark beside that fruit one mark for each answer
Step 2 Count the marks in each row, in fives the table below
Step 3 $8 + 5 + 4 + 3 = 20$ every answer counted once
Answer Mango, liked by 8 classmates
FruitTally marksFrequency
Mango8
Banana5
Apple4
Guava3
Example 2

In a quiz of 5 questions, the table shows how many students got each number of answers right.

Right answers012345
Number of students136852

(a) How many students took the quiz? (b) How many got at least 3 answers right? (c) Which number of right answers was the most common?

Solution.

Step 1 (a) $1 + 3 + 6 + 8 + 5 + 2 = 25$ add the frequencies
Step 2 (b) $8 + 5 + 2 = 15$ rows for 3, 4 and 5
Step 3 (c) the largest frequency is 8, in the row for 3 look for the largest
Answer (a) 25 students (b) 15 students (c) 3 right answers
