Example 1

A pictograph must show 18, 30 and 42 items, using whole symbols only. Which key uses the fewest symbols in all?

  1. 1 symbol for 2 items
  2. 1 symbol for 3 items
  3. 1 symbol for 6 items
  4. 1 symbol for 12 items

Solution.

Step 1A key for whole symbols must divide all three numbers. 2, 3 and 6 do; 12 does not divide 18
Step 2The largest of these keys uses the fewest symbols. With 6: $3 + 5 + 7 = 15$ symbols
CheckWith 3 the rows need $6 + 10 + 14 = 30$ symbols, twice as many
Answer(c) 1 symbol for 6 items
Example 2

A bar graph starts at 0, and 1 cm of height stands for 12 visitors. The bars for Monday and Tuesday are 3 cm and 5 cm tall. How many more visitors came on Tuesday?

  1. 2
  2. 12
  3. 60
  4. 24

Solution.

Step 1The bars differ in height by $5 - 3 = 2$ cm
Step 22 cm stand for $2 \times 12 = 24$ visitors
CheckMonday had $3 \times 12 = 36$ and Tuesday $5 \times 12 = 60$; $60 - 36 = 24$
Answer(d) 24
