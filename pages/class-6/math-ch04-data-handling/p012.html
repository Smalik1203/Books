Choosing a scale

Fig. 4.16 shows the population of India every ten years, from 1951 to 2001. The numbers are in crores.

Suppose one unit of length stood for one person. The bars would be far too long to draw! So this graph uses a scale of 1 unit of length for 10 crores. A bar 5 units tall stands for 50 crores, and a bar 8 units tall stands for 80 crores.
