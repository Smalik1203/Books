Type 2 · Reading a pictograph

Example 3

Fig. 4.23 shows how many coconuts a shop sold on five days. (a) How many coconuts were sold on Tuesday? (b) How many more were sold on Friday than on Thursday? (c) How many were sold in all five days?

Solution.

Step 1 (a) Tuesday: 2 whole symbols and half a symbol; $2 \times 20 = 40$, and half of 20 is 10 the key is 20
Step 2 $40 + 10 = 50$
Step 3 (b) Friday: $5 \times 20 = 100$; Thursday: $20 + 10 = 30$; $100 - 30 = 70$
Step 4 (c) symbols: 3, 2 and a half, 4, 1 and a half, 5, which make 16 symbols first
Step 5 $16 \times 20 = 320$
Answer (a) 50 coconuts (b) 70 coconuts (c) 320 coconuts
Example 4

In one pictograph, one symbol stands for 10 marbles, and Ravi’s row has 4 symbols. In another, one symbol stands for 5 marbles, and Sara’s row has 7 symbols. Which statement is true?

  1. Sara has more, as her row is longer.
  2. Ravi has 5 more than Sara.
  3. Both have the same number.
  4. Ravi has 3 fewer than Sara.

Solution.

Step 1 Ravi: $4 \times 10 = 40$ marbles use each key
Step 2 Sara: $7 \times 5 = 35$ marbles
Step 3 $40 - 35 = 5$ compare numbers, not rows
Answer (b)
