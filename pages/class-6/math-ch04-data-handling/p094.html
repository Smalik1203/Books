1. Meghalaya gets a lot of rain. A school there recorded the number of rainy days in four months: 26 in June, 28 in July, 25 in August and 21 in September.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Which month had the most rainy days?
    2. How many rainy days were there in the four months?
    3. The school draws a bar graph with 1 unit of length for 4 days. Which bars end part-way between two unit marks? Justify your answer.

  1. A school in Pune held a science fair for six days. Fig. 4.37 shows the number of visitors on each day, with 1 unit of length for 50 visitors.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many visitors came on Thursday?
    2. On which day did twice as many visitors come as on Friday?
    3. The school hoped for 1500 visitors in the six days. Did it reach this number? Justify your answer.
