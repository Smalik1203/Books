The rows would be very long — Class II would need 35 symbols — so the

### Think and Reflect (33 or 27 students)

With one symbol for 5 students, 33 is 6 symbols and three-fifths of a
symbol, since $33 = 6 \times 5 + 3$. With one symbol for 10 students, 27 is
2 symbols and seven-tenths of a symbol, since $27 = 2 \times 10 + 7$. Such
parts of a symbol cannot be drawn or read exactly; only whole and half
symbols can.

### Think and Reflect (population of India)

1. Answers will vary. For example: *In which ten years did the population
   grow the most?*
2. It grew by **66 crores**: $102 - 36 = 66$. In each ten years it grew by
