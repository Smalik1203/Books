Example 5

In a pictograph, 7 whole symbols stand for 84 bicycles. How many bicycles do 4 whole symbols stand for?

Solution.

Step 1One symbol stands for $84 \div 7 = 12$ bicycles
Step 2Four symbols stand for $4 \times 12 = 48$ bicycles
Check$48 \div 12 = 4$ symbols, as the question asks
Answer48
