Problem 5

In a pictograph, one symbol stands for 10 children. The row for Class 6 shows 3 whole symbols and half a symbol. Then 5 more children join Class 6. The row for Class 6 now shows

  1. 4 whole symbols
  2. 3 and a half symbols, and 5 more symbols
  3. 4 and a half symbols
  4. 3 whole symbols
Solution

Change the symbols into children, add the new children, and change back.

before: $3 \times 10 + 5 = 35$ children

after: $35 + 5 = 40$ children

$40 \div 10 = 4$ whole symbols

The answer is option (a). The half symbol and the 5 new children together make one whole symbol. Option (b) draws a symbol for each new child, as if the key were 1 child. Option (c) adds half a symbol but leaves the old half symbol as it was. Option (d) takes the half symbol away instead of completing it.
