Example 5

A dairy sold 120, 90, 150, 60 and 75 litres of milk on five days. Each row of a pictograph must use only whole and half symbols, and no row may have more than 5 symbols. Which key works?

  1. 1 symbol for 10 litres
  2. 1 symbol for 15 litres
  3. 1 symbol for 25 litres
  4. 1 symbol for 30 litres

Solution.

Step 1 150 litres: $150 \div 10 = 15$ symbols; $150 \div 15 = 10$ symbols rows too long
Step 2 $120 = 4 \times 25 + 20$, and 20 is not half of 25 not a half
Step 3 $120 \div 30 = 4$, $\ 90 \div 30 = 3$, $\ 150 \div 30 = 5$, $\ 60 \div 30 = 2$
Step 4 $75 = 2 \times 30 + 15$, which is 2 and a half symbols 15 is half of 30
Answer (d). Fig. 4.24 shows the pictograph.
Example 6

A pictograph shows the cars in a parking area. Monday’s row has 6 symbols, and the table beside it says that 48 cars were there on Monday. What is the key? How many symbols does Tuesday’s row need for 36 cars?

Solution.

Step 1 $48 \div 6 = 8$ cars for one symbol
Step 2 $36 = 4 \times 8 + 4$
Step 3 4 is half of 8, so the 4 cars are half a symbol
Answer One symbol stands for 8 cars; Tuesday needs 4 and a half symbols.
