Example 7 · Multiple correct

A frequency table gives red: 8, blue: 12, green: 5, yellow: 7. Each of 32 students chooses one colour. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Blue is the most frequent choice.
  2. Red and yellow together have frequency 15.
  3. Green is chosen by one quarter of the class.
  4. The frequencies add to 32.

Solution.

Step 1The largest count is 12 for blue; red plus yellow is 8 + 7 = 15.
Step 2The total is 8 + 12 + 5 + 7 = 32. One quarter of 32 is 8, not the green count of 5.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

A pictograph uses one full symbol for 10 trees and permits half-symbols. Which counts can be represented exactly with these symbols?

Select all correct options.

  1. 15 trees
  2. 22 trees
  3. 35 trees
  4. 40 trees

Solution.

Step 1Full and half-symbols represent multiples of 5 trees.
Step 215, 35 and 40 are multiples of 5; 22 is not.
