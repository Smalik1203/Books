4.5How a Graph Looks

A good graph needs more than correct bars. It must fit the space it is drawn in, and you can control that by choosing the scale. It should also be pleasant to look at and easy to understand, so that people want to read it.

Table 4.31 lists the tallest mountain on each continent, with its height in metres.

Table 4.31 The tallest mountain on each continent
ContinentTallest mountainHeight (metres)
AsiaEverest8848
South AmericaAconcagua6962

EuropeElbrus5642
AntarcticaVinson Massif4892
AustraliaKosciuszko2228

How much taller is Everest than Kosciuszko? Are Denali and Kilimanjaro very different in height? It is not easy to see this quickly from a table of numbers.
