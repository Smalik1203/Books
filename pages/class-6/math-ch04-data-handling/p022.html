In general, a height that is measured upwards from the ground is best shown with bars that stand up. A length that lies along the ground, such as the distance between two places, is best shown with bars lying on their side.

Infographics

When a graph is decorated with pictures and colour, it is called an information graphic, or an infographic for short. An infographic tries to catch your eye, and to give its information quickly and clearly.
