4.2Pictographs

A pictograph shows data with pictures instead of numbers. You may have seen one like Fig. 4.2 in earlier classes. It shows how the students of a class travel to school.

Think and Reflect
  1. Which way of travelling do the most students use?
  2. Which way of travelling do the fewest students use?

You did not need to count every symbol to answer. The longest row and the shortest row stand out at a glance. That is what a pictograph is for.

The key of a pictograph

A pictograph shows data with pictures or symbols. Its key, also called its scale, says how many things one symbol stands for.

In Fig. 4.2, one symbol stands for one student. In many pictographs, one symbol stands for several people or things.

Nand Kishor teaches at a middle school in Berasia. He asked the children how often they sleep for at least 9 hours at night. He drew Fig. 4.3 from their answers. In this pictograph, one symbol stands for 10 children.
