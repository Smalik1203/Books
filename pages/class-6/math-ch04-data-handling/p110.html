Assertion and reason

In Questions 13 to 16, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): If one symbol stands for 10 people, half a symbol stands for 5 people.

    Reason (R): Half of 10 is 5.

  1. Assertion (A): In a bar graph with 1 unit for 10 runs, a score of 0 runs has no bar.

    Reason (R): All the bars in a bar graph have the same width.

  1. Assertion (A): On a scale of 1 unit for 20, a bar 6 units tall stands for 120.

    Reason (R): To find the height of a bar, multiply its value by the scale.

  1. Assertion (A): The tally marks for 5 are five separate lines, side by side.

    Reason (R): In tally marks, the fifth mark is drawn across the first four.
