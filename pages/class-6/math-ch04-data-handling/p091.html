1. A bar graph shows the glasses of juice sold at a stall. The bar for Monday is 6 units tall and the bar for Tuesday is 9 units tall. The stall sold 72 glasses on Monday. Find the scale, and the number of glasses sold on Tuesday.
  1. The number of children in each of 15 families is 2, 1, 3, 2, 2, 4, 1, 2, 3, 2, 1, 2, 3, 2, 1. Make a frequency table. Which number of children is the most common? How many families have more than 2 children?
  1. A zoo had 400, 750, 600 and 250 visitors on four days. Nisha wants every bar to end exactly on a unit mark, and the tallest bar to be at most 20 units. Which of the scales 1 unit for 25, 1 unit for 50 and 1 unit for 100 can she use? Justify your answer.
  1. In a dance class, 4 children are 9 years old, 7 are 10 years old, 5 are 11 years old and 4 are 12 years old. Find the number of children in the class. What fraction of them are 10 years old or younger?
  1. Tanvi says that in a pictograph with one symbol for 5 people, a row of 7 symbols shows 12 people. Is she right? Justify your answer. Can this pictograph show 12 people exactly?
  1. A row of tally marks has 7 groups of five and 3 more marks. Find the frequency it shows. How many more marks are needed to make the frequency 50, and how many groups of five will there be then?

  1. A school library lent 45 books on Monday, 30 on Tuesday, 55 on Wednesday, 20 on Thursday and 50 on Friday. Choose a suitable scale and draw a bar graph of this data. Hence find the day on which the most books were lent and the day on which the fewest were lent. Also find the total number of books lent, and how many more were lent on Wednesday than on Thursday.
  1. A farmer picked 60 coconuts in Week 1, 45 in Week 2, 75 in Week 3 and 30 in Week 4. Show that a key of 10 coconuts cannot draw every row with whole symbols only, and that a key of 15 coconuts can. Draw the pictograph with the key of 15. Also find the total number of coconuts, and the week in which the farmer picked twice as many as in Week 4.

Draw the graph again correctly, from 0, with 1 unit of length for 50 pens. How many times as many pens were really sold in April as in March?
