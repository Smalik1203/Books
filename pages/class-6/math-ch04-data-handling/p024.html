Infographics

When a graph is decorated with pictures and colour, it is called an information graphic, or an infographic for short. An infographic tries to catch your eye, and to give its information quickly and clearly.

Think again about the column graph of the mountains. Mountains are not rectangles, so why not draw each column as a triangle, and add some colour? Fig. 4.34 does this.

This picture may look more attractive, but it has a problem. In a column graph every column has the same width, so that you compare only the heights. Here the taller triangles are also wider. Are taller mountains always wider? The picture suggests something that the data does not say, and it may not be true. A more attractive picture can mislead without meaning to.

You could go further, and draw the seven peaks as one imaginary mountain range, as in Fig. 4.35. The mountains look more real. But is the picture correct? In it, Everest looks about twice as tall as Elbrus.
