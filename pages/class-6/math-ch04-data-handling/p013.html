  1. Draw a line across the page, and a line going up from its left end. Along the line across, write the names of the sweets, equally spaced. The bars will stand on this line. Along the line going up, you will mark the number of students.
  2. Choose a scale. The scale says how many students one unit of length stands for, so that the graph fits on your paper. Here, take 1 unit of length for 1 student.
