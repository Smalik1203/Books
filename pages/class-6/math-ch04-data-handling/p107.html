Type 5 · Choosing a scale and drawing a bar graph

Example 9

A bar graph must show 450, 300, 750 and 600 tickets. Every bar must end exactly on a unit mark, and no bar may be taller than 16 units. Which scale works?

  1. 1 unit for 10 tickets
  2. 1 unit for 25 tickets
  3. 1 unit for 50 tickets
  4. 1 unit for 100 tickets

Solution.

Step 1 The tallest bar is for 750: $750 \div 10 = 75$ and $750 \div 25 = 30$ units too tall
Step 2 $450 \div 50 = 9$, $\ 300 \div 50 = 6$, $\ 750 \div 50 = 15$, $\ 600 \div 50 = 12$ all end on a mark
Step 3 1 unit for 100: 450 tickets would need 4 and a half units ends between marks
Answer (c)
Example 10

Five classes collected plastic bottles for recycling: Class 6A 35, Class 6B 50, Class 6C 20, Class 6D 45, Class 6E 30. Draw a bar graph of this data.

Solution.

Step 1 The largest value is 50, so take 1 unit of length for 5 bottles choose the scale
Step 2 $35 \div 5 = 7$, $\ 50 \div 5 = 10$, $\ 20 \div 5 = 4$, $\ 45 \div 5 = 9$, $\ 30 \div 5 = 6$ bar heights
Step 3 Mark 0, 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 on the line going up; draw bars of equal width with equal gaps
