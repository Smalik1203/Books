Example 12 · Numerical answer

The frequencies of four categories are 9, 14, 8 and x. Their total is 45. Find x.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The known frequencies total 9 + 14 + 8 = 31.
Step 2x = 45 − 31 = 14.
Answer14
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A zero-based bar graph uses one vertical interval for 6 students. A bar represents 42 students. How many intervals high is it?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Divide the count by the number per interval.
Step 242 ÷ 6 = 7 intervals.
Answer7
Example 14 · Matching

One full symbol represents 12 items; a half-symbol represents 6. Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) 3 full symbols(1) 30 items
(Q) 2 full symbols and a half(2) 18 items
(R) 4 full symbols(3) 48 items
(S) 1 full symbol and a half(4) 36 items

Each entry has exactly one match

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2
  2. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2
  3. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–1, R–3, S–4

Solution.

Step 1Use the key: one full symbol represents 12 items and a half-symbol represents 6.
Step 2The four counts are 36, 24 + 6 = 30, 48 and 12 + 6 = 18.
Answer(a) P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2
