Tally marks

Shri Nilesh is a teacher. He wants to bring sweets to class to celebrate the new year. The shop nearby sells jalebi, gulab jamun, gujiya, barfi and rasgulla. He writes the five names on the board, and asks each child which sweet they would like.

For each answer, he draws a short line beside that sweet. Each line is called a tally mark. When he reaches the fifth mark for a sweet, he draws it across the first four, as in Fig. 4.1. Then the marks are easy to count in fives.
