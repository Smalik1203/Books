Example 10

A class chose its monitor by a vote. The tally marks showed: Lata, 4 groups of five and 3 marks; Vikram, 3 groups of five and 4 marks; Zoya, 5 groups of five and 1 mark; Nitin, 2 groups of five.

  1. Who won the vote?
    1. Lata
    2. Vikram
    3. Zoya
    4. Nitin
  2. Numerical answer. How many votes were there in all?
  3. Numerical answer. How many more votes would Lata have needed to tie with Zoya?

Solution.

Step 1Lata $20 + 3 = 23$; Vikram $15 + 4 = 19$; Zoya $25 + 1 = 26$; Nitin 10
Step 2The largest is 26, so Zoya won. In all, $23 + 19 + 26 + 10 = 78$ votes
Step 3Lata needed $26 - 23 = 3$ more votes
Answer(i) (c) Zoya; (ii) 78; (iii) 3
