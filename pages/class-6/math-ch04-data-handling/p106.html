Example 9 · Multiple correct

A bar graph starts at zero, has equally spaced marks labelled 0, 5, 10, 15, …, and equal-width bars. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. A bar reaching the fourth interval represents 20.
  2. Twice the bar height represents twice the count.
  3. A wider bar would necessarily represent a larger count.
  4. A difference of two intervals represents 10.

Solution.

Step 1Each interval represents 5, so four represent 20 and two represent 10.
Step 2With a zero baseline and uniform scale, heights are proportional to counts. Counts are read from height, not width.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

The observations are 2, 3, 2, 4, 3, 2, 5, 4, 2, 3. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. There are 9 observations.
  2. The frequency of 2 is 4.
  3. The frequency of 3 is 3.
  4. Exactly 3 observations are greater than 3.

Solution.

Step 1Counting all entries gives 10 observations. The value 2 occurs four times and 3 occurs three times.
Step 2The values greater than 3 are 4, 5 and 4: three observations.
Answer(b), (c), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

In a pictograph, 7 full symbols represent 84 bicycles. How many bicycles do 4 full symbols represent?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1One symbol represents 84 ÷ 7 = 12 bicycles.
Step 2Four represent 4 × 12 = 48 bicycles.
Answer48
