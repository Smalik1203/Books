Type 4 · Reading a bar graph

Example 7

Fig. 4.25 shows the visitors to a school science fair on five days. (a) Which day had the most visitors, and how many? (b) How many more visitors came on Wednesday than on Monday? (c) How many visitors came in all?

Solution.

Step 1 (a) The longest bar is Friday’s, 8 units: $8 \times 50 = 400$ 1 unit, 50 visitors
Step 2 (b) $6 \times 50 = 300$ and $3 \times 50 = 150$; $300 - 150 = 150$
Step 3 (c) $3 + 5 + 6 + 2 + 8 = 24$ units, and $24 \times 50 = 1200$ add the units first
Answer (a) Friday, 400 visitors (b) 150 (c) 1200 visitors
Example 8

Use Fig. 4.25. Which statement is true?

  1. Friday had twice as many visitors as Tuesday.
  2. Monday and Tuesday together had as many visitors as Friday.
  3. Thursday had half as many visitors as Monday.
  4. Wednesday had 100 more visitors than Tuesday.

Solution.

Step 1 Mon 150, Tue 250, Wed 300, Thu 100, Fri 400 read every bar
Step 2 (a) $2 \times 250 = 500$, not 400; (c) half of 150 is 75, not 100
Step 3 (b) $150 + 250 = 400$; (d) $300 - 250 = 50$, not 100
Answer (b)
