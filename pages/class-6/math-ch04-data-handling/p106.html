Example 6

The frequencies of four items are 9, 14, 8 and one more number. The frequencies add up to 45. Find the missing frequency.

Solution.

Step 1The three known frequencies add up to $9 + 14 + 8 = 31$
Step 2The missing frequency is $45 - 31 = 14$
Check$9 + 14 + 8 + 14 = 45$

Example 7

One whole symbol stands for 12 items, and half a symbol for 6. Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) 3 whole symbols(1) 30 items
(Q) 2 whole symbols and a half(2) 18 items
(R) 4 whole symbols(3) 48 items
(S) 1 whole symbol and a half(4) 36 items
  1. P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2
  2. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2
  3. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–1, R–3, S–4

Solution.

Step 1P: $3 \times 12 = 36$. Q: $24 + 6 = 30$
Step 2R: $4 \times 12 = 48$. S: $12 + 6 = 18$
Answer(a) P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2
