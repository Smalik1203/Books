1. Assertion (A): In the data 5, 2, 5, 7, 5, 2, the frequency of 5 is 3.

    Reason (R): The frequency of a value is the number of times it appears in the data.

Case-based questions

  1. Ramesh sells mangoes at a market in Ratnagiri. Fig. 4.36 shows the number of mangoes he sold on five days. One symbol stands for 10 mangoes.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many mangoes did he sell on Wednesday?
    2. On which day did he sell the fewest mangoes?
    3. How many mangoes did he sell in the five days? How many more would he have needed to sell to reach 200?

  1. Students of a school in Jaipur collected old newspapers for recycling. They collected 24 kg in Week 1, 36 kg in Week 2, 30 kg in Week 3 and 42 kg in Week 4. They plan a pictograph with one symbol for 6 kg.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many symbols will the row for Week 2 have?
    2. How many kilograms did they collect in all?
    3. If they used one symbol for 12 kg instead, which weeks would need half a symbol? Show your working.

  1. A traffic warden in Kochi counted the vehicles at a crossing for 10 minutes. Her tally marks showed 17 cars, 23 autos, 8 buses and 32 motorbikes.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many complete groups of five are in the tally marks for autos?
    2. How many vehicles did she count in all?
    3. What fraction of the vehicles were motorbikes? Is it more than one-third? Justify your answer.
