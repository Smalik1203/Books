Example 9

A school canteen sold these plates of poha in one week. The data is to be drawn as a pictograph with one symbol for 10 plates, and as a bar graph with 1 unit for 5 plates.

DayMonTueWedThuFri
Plates4055356050
  1. How many symbols does the row for Thursday need?
    1. 5 and a half
    2. 60
    3. 6
    4. 7
  2. Numerical answer. How many plates were sold in the week?
  3. Numerical answer. How many units taller is Thursday’s bar than Wednesday’s?

Solution.

Step 1Thursday: $60 \div 10 = 6$ symbols
Step 2$40 + 55 + 35 + 60 + 50 = 240$ plates
Step 3Thursday: $60 \div 5 = 12$ units. Wednesday: $35 \div 5 = 7$ units. The difference is 5 units
CheckThe two days differ by $60 - 35 = 25$ plates, and $25 \div 5 = 5$
Answer(i) (c) 6; (ii) 240; (iii) 5
