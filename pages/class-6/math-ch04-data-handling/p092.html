1. Class 6A made a pictograph of how its students come to school, with one symbol for 4 students: walk 5 symbols, bus 3 and a half symbols, bicycle 2 symbols. Class 6B used one symbol for 6 students: walk 3 symbols, bus 2 and a half symbols, bicycle 1 and a half symbols. Find the number of students for each way in each class, and the number of students in each class. Which class has more students who come by bus, and how many more does it have?
  1. A school is 6 km from Village P, 9 km from Village Q, 4 km from Village R and 12 km from Village S. Should a bar graph of these distances have bars that stand up or bars that lie on their side? Give a reason. Draw it with 1 unit of length for 2 km. How much farther from the school is the farthest village than the nearest?
  1. Show that when one symbol stands for 10 things and half symbols may be used, a row can show only multiples of 5 exactly. Hence find which of 35, 42, 60 and 75 can be shown, and draw the row for each of them.
  1. 40 students each wrote how many books they read in a month: 6 read 0 books, 10 read 1 book, some read 2 books, 9 read 3 books and 5 read 4 books. Find the number of students who read 2 books. Find the total number of books read by the 40 students. How many students read at least 2 books?
  1. A bar graph with 1 unit of length for 15 trees shows the trees planted by four classes. The bars are 4, 6, 5 and 7 units tall. Find the number of trees planted by each class, and in all. The school wants 420 trees. Find how many more trees are needed, and the height of a bar that would show them.
  1. Five teams scored 120, 280, 360, 200 and 160 points in a quiz. Leela has graph paper 15 squares tall. Show that 1 square for 10 points and 1 square for 20 points do not fit on her paper. Show that 1 square for 40 points fits, with every bar ending on a whole square, and find the height of each bar.
Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): With one symbol for 10 students, 4 and a half symbols show 45 students.

    Reason (R): Half a symbol stands for half the number that a whole symbol stands for.

  1. Assertion (A): A bar graph of 30, 45 and 60 can use a scale of 1 unit for 15.

    Reason (R): All the bars of a bar graph have the same width.

  1. Assertion (A): On a scale of 1 unit for 25, a bar 8 units tall stands for 200.

    Reason (R): To find the number a bar stands for, divide its height by the scale.

  1. Assertion (A): A pictograph with one symbol for 4 books can show 10 books using whole symbols only.

    Reason (R): The key of a pictograph says how many things one symbol stands for.
