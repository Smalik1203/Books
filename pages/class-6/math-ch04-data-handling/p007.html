Example 1

Use Fig. 4.3 to answer these questions. (1) How many children always sleep for at least 9 hours? (2) How many sometimes do? (3) How many always sleep for less than 9 hours?

(1) Always: 5 symbols, so $5 \times 10 = 50$ children.

(2) Sometimes: 2 whole symbols and half a symbol. The whole symbols show $2 \times 10 = 20$ children, and half of 10 is 5. So $20 + 5 = 25$ children.

(3) Never: 4 symbols, so $4 \times 10 = 40$ children.

A child who never sleeps for 9 hours always sleeps for less than 9 hours. So the answer to (3) is 40 children.
