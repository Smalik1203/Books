Frequency

The frequency of an item is the number of times it appears in the data. Tally marks help you count frequencies, and a frequency table shows them all at a glance.

Collecting data of your own

In the next exercise set you will collect some data yourself. It helps to make a plan before you start.

  1. Decide the question you want to answer, such as Which colour do most students in my class like?
  2. Guess the answer before you begin.
  3. Collect the data. For example, ask each student, and write down every answer.
  4. Organise the data, using tally marks and a frequency table.
  5. Look at your table. Was your guess right?
  6. Show your data so that others can read it easily, and share what you found with your class. You will soon learn two good ways to show data: pictographs and bar graphs.
