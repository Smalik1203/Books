By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Draw the tally marks for 23. How many groups of five does the drawing have, and how many marks are left over?
  1. Arrange 5, 3, 7, 3, 9, 5, 3, 8 in ascending order. Which value appears most often?
  1. In a pictograph, one symbol stands for 6 books. Find the number of books shown by 4 and a half symbols.
  1. A bar graph uses 1 unit of length for 20 students. How long is the bar for 140 students?
  1. One symbol of a pictograph stands for 10 students, and half symbols may be used. Can a row show 45 students exactly? Give a reason.
  1. A bar 9 units tall stands for 360 kg of rice. Find the scale of the bar graph.
  1. 36 students chose a favourite colour: 9 chose red, 12 blue, 7 green and the rest yellow. Find the frequency of yellow.
  1. A pictograph uses one symbol for 8 mangoes. Find the number of mangoes shown by a row of 5 symbols and a row of 2 and a half symbols together.
  1. One symbol of a pictograph stands for 4 children. Find the number of symbols needed to show 18 children.
  1. In a class, 8 students walk to school, 12 come by bus and 10 come by bicycle. Which way of travelling has the highest frequency? Find the number of students in the class.
Short answer
  1. The marks out of 10 of 20 students in a test are 6, 8, 7, 6, 9, 5, 7, 8, 6, 10, 7, 6, 8, 9, 7, 6, 5, 8, 7, 6. Make a frequency table using tally marks. Find the number of students who scored more than 7.
  1. Kabir sold 30 notebooks on Monday, 45 on Tuesday, 20 on Wednesday and 35 on Thursday. Find the number of symbols needed for each day in a pictograph with one symbol for 10 notebooks. Also find the total number of notebooks sold.
  1. Explain why all the bars of a bar graph must have the same width. Use two bars, for 20 and for 40, as your example.
  1. In a pictograph, 3 and a half symbols stand for 70 bicycles. Find the key of the pictograph. Hence find the number of symbols that show 90 bicycles.
