4.5How a Graph Looks

A good graph needs more than correct bars. It must fit the space it is drawn in, and you can control that by choosing the scale. It should also be pleasant to look at and easy to understand, so that people want to read it.

Table 4.13 lists the tallest mountain on each continent, with its height in metres.

How much taller is Everest than Kosciuszko? Are Denali and Kilimanjaro very different in height? It is not easy to see this quickly from a table of numbers.

A bar graph makes it easier. In Fig. 4.19, each height is drawn as a bar lying on its side. A longer bar means a taller mountain, so you can compare all seven heights at a glance.

But these bars stand for heights. It looks more natural to turn the picture round, so that the bars grow upwards from the ground, like mountains. A bar graph whose bars stand up is also called a column graph. Columns are the pillars in a building that hold up its roof.
