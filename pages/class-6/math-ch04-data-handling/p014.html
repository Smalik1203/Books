Example 2

Table 4.19 shows the runs Smriti scored in 8 matches. Draw a bar graph of her runs.

Solution.

Step 1 Her lowest score is 0 and her highest score is 100. With 1 unit for 1 run, the line going up would need 100 marks
Step 2 So take 1 unit of length for 10 runs
Step 3 80 runs needs a bar $80 \div 10 = 8$ units tall; 100 runs needs a bar $100 \div 10 = 10$ units tall divide by the scale
Step 4 Match 6 has 0 runs, so it needs no bar at all. Leave its place empty, with its name below
Step 5 Mark 0, 10, 20, and so on up to 100 on the line going up, and draw the bars
Check Matches 5 and 7 both have 90 runs, so both bars are 9 units tall
Answer Fig. 4.20 shows the result.
