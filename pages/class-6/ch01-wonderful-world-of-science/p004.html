1.2 What This Book Will Ask You

Begin with the Earth — as far as we know, the only place in the universe where anything is alive.

A seed pushes a pale shoot up through dark soil. A caterpillar seals itself away and comes out with wings. A calf that could barely stand in the morning is running across the field by the end of the month. Growing is so ordinary that we stop noticing it happening. But how does a plant build an entire body out of soil, water and air?

Living things need materials and energy to grow. Green plants make sugars using light, water and carbon dioxide; animals obtain food by eating. In a country this large, that alone is a huge subject — pongal in one state, litti in another, a fish curry along the coast, a plate of rajma somewhere north. What are these dishes actually made of? And how could you find out for yourself, instead of being told?
