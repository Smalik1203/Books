Case-based questions

  1. Anjali makes a rangoli for Onam. She ties a string 30 cm long to a peg at $O$ and draws a circle with chalk at its other end. Then she moves the peg to a point $P$ on the circle and draws a second circle with the same string.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the radius of the first circle.
    2. How far is $P$ from $O$?
    3. The two circles cross at $A$ and $B$. Find $OA$ and $PA$. Justify your answer.

  1. Mr Das, the sports teacher, marks a kabaddi court on the school ground. It is a rectangle 13 m long and 10 m wide. He first draws a plan in his notebook, with 1 cm standing for 1 m.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. What are the lengths of the four sides of the court?
    2. What is the size of each angle at a corner of the court?
    3. Write the steps he should follow to construct the plan.

  1. A window grill in Jaipur is a rectangle 90 cm long and 30 cm high. It is made of identical squares that sit side by side in one row, each as high as the grill.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the length of a side of each square.
    2. How many squares are in the grill?
    3. A second grill is 120 cm long and 30 cm high, with the same squares. How many more squares does it have?

  1. Ramesh makes a model of a village hut on card. The floor is 8 cm, and each wall is 6 cm and stands at a right angle to the floor. The two roof lines are 5 cm each and meet at the top, $A$. The tops of the walls are $B$ and $C$.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How far apart are $B$ and $C$?
    2. Is $A$ equidistant from $B$ and $C$? Give a reason.
    3. Ramesh constructs the model and finds that $A$ is 3 cm above $BC$. How high is $A$ above the floor?
