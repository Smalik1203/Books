Example 10 · Multiple correct

Which measurements are sufficient to determine the size and shape of a rectangle, allowing rotations and reflections?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its length and breadth
  2. Its perimeter alone
  3. The length of a diagonal alone
  4. Its breadth and perimeter

Solution.

Step 1Length and breadth determine a rectangle. Breadth and perimeter also determine length because length = perimeter ÷ 2 − breadth.
Step 2Perimeter alone permits many side pairs. A diagonal alone permits different rectangles inscribed in the same circle.
Answer(a), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A square has perimeter 34 cm. Enter the compass opening in centimetres needed to copy the length of one side.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The four sides are equal, so each is 34 ÷ 4 = 8.5 cm.
Step 2To copy a segment, set the compass opening to its length.
Answer8.5
Example 12 · Numerical answer

A rectangle has perimeter 46 cm and one side 8 cm long. Enter the length in centimetres of an adjacent side.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Adjacent side lengths add to half the perimeter: 46 ÷ 2 = 23 cm.
Step 2The other side is 23 − 8 = 15 cm.
Answer15
