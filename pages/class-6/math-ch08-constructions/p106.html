Match List I with List II.

Solution.

Step 1The compass opening is the radius: 8 cm. Each square fills the width: 6 cm.
Step 2The widths add to $4 + 3 + 2 = 9$ cm, and each side of the square is $28 \div 4 = 7$ cm long.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
