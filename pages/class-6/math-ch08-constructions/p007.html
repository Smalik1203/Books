8.3Constructing Squares and Rectangles

Now you will construct squares and rectangles. How would you construct a square with sides of 6 cm? Figs. 8.14 and 8.15 show one way to construct a square $PQRS$ with sides of 6 cm.

Step 1. Draw a line $PQ$ that is 6 cm long.

Step 2. Place a protractor on $PQ$ with its centre at $P$, and mark a point at $90^\circ$. The line through $P$ and this point makes a right angle with $PQ$. We say it is perpendicular to $PQ$.

Step 3. Draw this perpendicular, and mark $S$ on it so that $PS = 6$ cm. You can measure $PS$ with a ruler. Or you can use a compass: open it to the length of $PQ$, put the needle on $P$, and draw a small arc across the perpendicular.
