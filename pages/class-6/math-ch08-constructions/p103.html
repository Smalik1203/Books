Example 2

A square $ABCD$ is to be constructed on a side $AB = 6$ cm. Which condition places the corner $D$ correctly?

  1. $D$ is 6 cm from $A$.
  2. $AD = 6$ cm, at right angles to $AB$.
  3. $D$ is the middle of $AB$.
  4. $AD = 6$ cm, along $AB$.

Solution.

Step 1The sides of a square are equal, and its angles are $90^\circ$.
Step 2So $AD$ must make a right angle with $AB$, and $AD$ must be 6 cm. Either condition alone is not enough.
Step 3So draw a perpendicular to $AB$ at $A$, and mark $D$ on it with the compass open at 6 cm.
CheckWith (a), $AD$ could make any angle with $AB$. With (c) and (d), $D$ lies on the line $AB$ and there is no corner at all.
