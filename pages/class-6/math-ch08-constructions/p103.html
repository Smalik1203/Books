Example 2 · Single correct

ABCD is a rectangle named in order. AB = 9 cm and BC = 6 cm. Which pair gives CD and DA?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 9 cm, 6 cm
  2. 6 cm, 9 cm
  3. 9 cm, 9 cm
  4. 6 cm, 6 cm

Solution.

Step 1Opposite sides of a rectangle are equal.
Step 2CD is opposite AB, so CD = 9 cm. DA is opposite BC, so DA = 6 cm.
Answer(a) 9 cm, 6 cm
Example 3 · Single correct

Two circles have centres A and B, radii 4 cm and 3 cm, and AB = 7 cm. How many points lie on both circles?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 2
  4. Infinitely many

Solution.

Step 1The centre distance equals the sum of the radii: 7 = 4 + 3.
Step 2The circles touch externally at one point on AB.
Answer(b) 1
Example 4 · Single correct

Two circles of radius 5 cm have centres 6 cm apart. A point P must be 5 cm from each centre. How many positions of P are possible?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 2
  4. 4

Solution.

Step 1Each condition places P on one of the two circles.
Step 2The centres are distinct and their distance is less than 5 + 5 = 10 cm, so these equal circles intersect at two points.
Answer(c) 2
