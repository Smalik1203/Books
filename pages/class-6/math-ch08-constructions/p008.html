8.4An Exploration in Rectangles

Construct a rectangle $ABCD$ with $AB = 7$ cm and $BC = 4$ cm.

Now imagine a point $X$ that can move anywhere along the side $AD$, and a point $Y$ that can move anywhere along the side $BC$. $X$ may also sit at an end, on $A$ or on $D$. In the same way, $Y$ may sit on $B$ or on $C$. Fig. 8.16 shows four places for $X$ and $Y$.
