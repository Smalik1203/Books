Think and Reflect
  1. Where should $X$ and $Y$ be so that they are as close together as possible? Where should they be so that they are as far apart as possible? Make a guess, and talk about it with your classmates.

Now check your guesses. Place $X$ and $Y$ on the sides, and measure the length of the line $XY$. How does the shortest length of $XY$ compare with the length of $AB$? Try other places too. Are there other positions where $X$ and $Y$ are nearest or farthest? You can construct several copies of the rectangle to try more places.

Recording in a table

A table keeps many tries in a short space. Each row is one try, and each column is one thing that changes from try to try.
