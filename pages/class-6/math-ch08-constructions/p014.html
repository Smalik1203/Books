A rectangle from the angles at a diagonal

Construct a rectangle in which one diagonal splits the opposite angles into $60^\circ$ and $30^\circ$.

Start with a rough diagram, as in Fig. 8.28. Then decide in what order to draw the parts.

Look at the rough diagram. At $A$, the diagonal $AC$ makes $60^\circ$ with the side $AB$, and $\angle B$ is a right angle. So if you draw $AB$ and the right angle at $B$ first, a $60^\circ$ line from $A$ will find $C$ for you.

Step 1. Draw $AB$ of any length you like. Draw a perpendicular to $AB$ through $B$. The corner $C$ is somewhere on this perpendicular.
