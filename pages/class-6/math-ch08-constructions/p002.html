Points at the same distance

Mark a point $P$ in your notebook. Now mark as many points as you can that are 4 cm away from $P$, in different directions. Fig. 8.3 shows two of them.

?
Think and Reflect
  1. Imagine marking every point that is 4 cm from $P$. What shape would all these points make?
  2. Draw the shape. Then choose a few points on it and measure how far each one is from $P$. Are they all 4 cm away?

A compass makes this job much easier. Open it against a ruler so that the needle and the pencil tip are 4 cm apart, as in Fig. 8.4. Place the needle on $P$ and mark a point with the pencil. Keep the needle still, move only the pencil, and mark another point. Then turn the pencil all the way round.

The curve you get is a circle. Take any point on it. How far is it from $P$: exactly 4 cm, less than 4 cm or more than 4 cm? Now take a different point on the circle. How far is that one from $P$?
