Long answer
  1. Construct a rectangle $ABCD$ with $AB = 12$ cm and a diagonal $AC = 13$ cm.
    1. Write your steps, in order.
    2. Measure the side $BC$.
    3. Draw the other diagonal, $BD$, and measure it.
  1. Construct a square $ABCD$ with sides of 6 cm. With the needle on $A$, draw the curve from $B$ to $D$.
    1. How wide must the compass be open? Why?
    2. Does the curve pass through $C$? Look at your drawing.
    3. Draw the diagonal $AC$ and measure how far $C$ is from the curve along it.
  1. Two points $P$ and $Q$ are 8 cm apart.
    1. Construct all the points that are 5 cm from $P$ and also 5 cm from $Q$.
    2. How many such points are there, and how far apart are they?
