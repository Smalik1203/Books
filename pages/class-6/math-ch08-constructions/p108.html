Example 15 · Matching

Two circles each have radius 4 cm. Match their centre separation in List I with the result in List II.

List IList II
(P) Centres 9 cm apart(1) One common point
(Q) Centres 8 cm apart(2) Two common points
(R) Centres 5 cm apart(3) No common point
(S) Centres coincide(4) Infinitely many common points

Each entry has exactly one match

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–2, R–1, S–4
  2. P–1, Q–3, R–2, S–4
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–2, S–4
  4. P–4, Q–1, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Both circles have radius 4 cm. At centre distance 9 cm they are separate; at 8 cm they touch externally.
Step 2At distance 5 cm they intersect twice. With the same centre and radius they coincide completely.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–1, R–2, S–4
