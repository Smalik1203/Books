Example 9

A builder draws a house front. The floor $DE$ and the walls $BD$ and $CE$ are 6 cm each, and the roof lines $AB$ and $AC$ are 5 cm each. $M$ is the middle of $BC$.

  1. The distance of $A$ from $C$ is
    1. 6 cm
    2. 5 cm
    3. 3 cm
    4. 4 cm
  2. Numerical answer. Construct the house. How long is $AM$, in centimetres?
  3. Numerical answer. How high is $A$ above the floor, in centimetres?

Solution.

Step 1Arcs of radius 5 cm about $B$ and $C$ find $A$, so $AC = 5$ cm.
Step 2$BM = 3$ cm, half of $BC$. In the drawing, $AM$ measures 4 cm.
Step 3$A$ is 4 cm above $BC$, which is 6 cm above the floor: $4 + 6 = 10$.
CheckIn the drawing, $M$ is 3 cm from $B$ and $A$ is 4 cm above $M$, and $AB$ measures 5 cm, as it must.
Answer(i) (b) 5 cm; (ii) 4; (iii) 10
