Example 5

Points $A$ and $B$ are 10 cm apart. A circle of radius 5 cm is drawn about each of them. How many points lie on both circles?

Solution.

Step 1Along $AB$, each circle reaches 5 cm towards the other centre, and $5 + 5 = 10$, which is exactly $AB$.
Step 2So the circles touch at the middle of $AB$, and nowhere else.
CheckIf the centres were 9 cm apart, $5 + 5 = 10$ would be more than 9, and the circles would cross at two points.
Answer1
Example 6

A rectangle has a perimeter of 46 cm, and one side is 8 cm long. How long, in centimetres, is a side next to it?

Solution.

Step 1Two sides next to each other make half the perimeter: $46 \div 2 = 23$ cm.
Step 2The other side is $23 - 8 = 15$ cm.
CheckThe four sides add up to the perimeter: $8 + 15 + 8 + 15 = 46$.
Answer15
