Example 6

Dot $B$ is 3 dots right of dot $A$ and 1 up. Construct the square $ABCD$ above $AB$ (Fig. 8.36). Where do $C$ and $D$ land?

Solution.

Step 1 join $AB$, and draw perpendiculars to it through $A$ and $B$ S2
Step 2 needle on $A$, pencil on $B$; arcs about $A$ and $B$ mark $D$ and $C$ S1: carry $AB$
Step 3 join $D$ to $C$: it goes 3 dots right and 1 dot up, just as $AB$ does
Answer On dots: $C$ is 1 dot left of $B$ and 3 up, and $D$ is 1 dot left of $A$ and 3 up

Example 7

Construct a rectangle $PQRS$ with $PQ = 5$ cm and $QR = 3$ cm. Use the compass, not the ruler, to mark both short sides.

Solution.

Step 1 draw $PQ = 5$ cm
Step 2 draw perpendiculars to $PQ$ through $P$ and through $Q$ R2 at $P$ and $Q$
Step 3 open the compass to 3 cm against the ruler
Step 4 arc about $Q$ marks $R$; arc about $P$ marks $S$ one opening, two sides
Step 5 join $S$ to $R$
Answer $SR$ measures 5 cm, and $\angle R$ and $\angle S$ measure $90^\circ$
