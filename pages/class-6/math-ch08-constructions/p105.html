Example 8

A rectangle 12 cm long and 6 cm wide is divided into identical squares with sides of 3 cm. How many squares are there?

Solution.

Step 1 along the length, $12 \div 3 = 4$ squares from a rough diagram
Step 2 across the width, $6 \div 3 = 2$ rows
Step 3 $2 \times 4 = 8$ two rows of four
Answer 8 squares
Example 9

Two squares with sides of 4 cm and one square with sides of 8 cm fit together, with no gaps, to make a rectangle. What are the sides of the rectangle?

Solution.

Step 1 stack the two small squares: $4 + 4 = 8$ cm tall as tall as the big one
Step 2 set the stack beside the big square
Step 3 length $= 8 + 4 = 12$ cm, width $= 8$ cm
Answer 12 cm and 8 cm

The rough diagram is what showed that the small squares had to be stacked. Placed in a row, they would not match the height of the big square.

Example 10

Construct a rectangle $ABCD$ in which the diagonal $AC$ splits the angle at $A$ into $20^\circ$ and $70^\circ$, with the $20^\circ$ part next to $AB$.

Solution.

Step 1 draw $AB$ of any length, and a perpendicular to $AB$ through $B$
Step 2 a line from $A$ at $20^\circ$ to $AB$ meets it at $C$ protractor at $A$
Step 3 draw the perpendicular to $AB$ through $A$ $D$ is on it
Step 4 compass open to $BC$, arc about $A$ marks $D$; join $C$ to $D$ $AD = BC$
Answer the rectangle $ABCD$; the other part of $\angle A$ is $90^\circ - 20^\circ = 70^\circ$
