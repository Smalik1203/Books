Example 8 · Multiple correct

A circle has centre O and radius 6 cm. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Every point on the circle is 6 cm from O.
  2. A point 4 cm from O is outside the circle.
  3. Every diameter is 12 cm long.
  4. A point 8 cm from O is outside the circle.

Solution.

Step 1Distance 6 cm defines the circle. A smaller distance is inside and a greater distance is outside.
Step 2The diameter is twice the radius: 12 cm.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

Two distinct points A and B are 8 cm apart. Which statements about points equidistant from A and B are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Their midpoint is equidistant from A and B.
  2. Every point on line AB is equidistant from A and B.
  3. Every point on the perpendicular bisector of AB is equidistant from A and B.
  4. There are exactly two such points in the plane.

Solution.

Step 1The midpoint is 4 cm from each endpoint.
Step 2All points on the perpendicular bisector have equal distances to A and B, giving infinitely many positions. On line AB, only the midpoint qualifies.
Answer(a), (c)
