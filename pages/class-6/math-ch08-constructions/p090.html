By the Book
Very short answer
  1. A compass is opened so that the needle and the pencil tip are 3 cm 5 mm apart, and a circle is drawn. Find the radius of the circle. Also find how far apart two points of the circle are when they lie in a straight line with the centre, on opposite sides of it.
  1. The corners of a rectangle, in order round it, are $K$, $L$, $M$ and $N$. Is $KMLN$ a name for this rectangle? Give a reason.
  1. In rectangle $ABCD$, $AB = 8$ cm and $BC = 5$ cm. Find the lengths of $CD$ and $DA$. Give a reason.
  1. A diagonal of a rectangle splits one of its corners into two angles. One of them is $35^\circ$. Find the other. Give a reason.
  1. A circle has its centre at $O$ and a radius of 4 cm. Point $P$ is 3 cm from $O$, and point $Q$ is 6 cm from $O$. Is each point inside the circle, on it or outside it? Give a reason.
  1. A rectangle is divided into 3 identical squares that sit side by side in one row. Each square has sides of 4 cm. Find the length and the width of the rectangle.
  1. A square with sides of 5 cm is turned on the page so that one of its corners points straight up. Find the length of each side and the size of each angle after it is turned. Give a reason.
  1. Point $A$ is equidistant from the points $B$ and $C$, and $AB = 7$ cm. Find $AC$. Give a reason.
  1. Two points of a circle lie in a straight line with its centre $O$, on opposite sides of it. They are 9 cm apart. Find the radius of the circle.
  1. Draw a rough diagram of a square $ABCD$ and its diagonal $AC$. Mark the equal sides and the right angles on it.
Short answer
  1. Write, in order, the steps to construct a square $PQRS$ with sides of 5 cm, using a ruler, a protractor and a compass.
  1. Construct a rectangle $ABCD$ with sides of 8 cm and 6 cm. Draw the diagonal $AC$ and measure it. Also measure the two angles into which $AC$ splits the corner at $A$.
  1. Explain why a square that is turned on the page is still a square. Use the two properties of a square in your answer.
