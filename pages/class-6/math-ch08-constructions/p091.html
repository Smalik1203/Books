1. Points $B$ and $C$ are 6 cm apart. Meena wants a point $A$ that is 4 cm from $B$ and 4 cm from $C$. Write the steps she should follow. How many such points are there?
  1. A rectangle has one side of 5 cm and a diagonal of 13 cm. Write the steps to construct it. Then measure its other side.
  1. A rectangle is divided into identical squares with sides of 3 cm, in 2 rows of 3 squares. Find the sides of the rectangle. Also find how many of these squares make a rectangle 12 cm long and 9 cm wide.
  1. Rohan draws a rectangle and its diagonal. He says that the diagonal splits one corner into $40^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Explain why his drawing cannot be correct.
  1. Squares with sides of 8 cm, 5 cm and 2 cm are placed as falling squares, each meeting the next only at a corner. Find the width and the height of the whole figure. Show your working.
  1. Draw a circle with centre $O$ and a radius of 3 cm. Mark a point $P$ on it. Keep the compass open at 3 cm, and with the needle at $P$ mark a point $Q$ on the circle. Find $OP$, $OQ$ and $PQ$. What do you notice?
  1. A square has a perimeter of 24 cm. Find the length of its side. Write how you would carry this length onto another line with a compass, without measuring it again.
Long answer
  1. Construct a rectangle $ABCD$ with $AB = 8$ cm and $BC = 5$ cm. Write the steps of construction. Check that your rectangle has both properties, R1 and R2. Also measure the diagonal $AC$.
  1. A house is drawn like the one in Section 8.6. Its floor $DE$ is 6 cm, its walls $BD$ and $CE$ are 4 cm each, and its roof lines $AB$ and $AC$ are 5 cm each. Write the steps to construct it, and explain why the point $A$ you find is 5 cm from both $B$ and $C$. Measure how high $A$ is above $BC$, and find how high it is above the floor.
  1. Construct a rectangle $ABCD$ with $AB = 6$ cm in which the diagonal $AC$ makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with $AB$. Write the steps of construction. Measure $BC$ and $AC$. Also find $\angle CAD$, and give a reason.
  1. A school garden is a rectangle 15 m long and 5 m wide. It is to be divided into identical square beds in one row. Draw its plan, with 1 cm standing for 1 m, and write your steps. Find the number of beds and the length of a side of each bed.
  1. Construct a rectangle 10 cm long and 4 cm wide. Inside it, construct a square with sides of 4 cm at each end. Write the steps. Find the sides of the part left in the middle, and show that this part is also a rectangle.
