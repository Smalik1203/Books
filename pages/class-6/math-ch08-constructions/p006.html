Squares and rectangles that are turned

Take a square piece of paper. All its sides are equal in length, and all its angles are $90^\circ$. Now turn it, as in Fig. 8.12. Is it still a square?

Check the two properties of a square. Turning the paper does not stretch it or bend it. So its sides are still equal, and its angles are still $90^\circ$. The turned figure has both properties, so it is still a square. In the same way, a rectangle that is turned is still a rectangle.

You can test this with a paper square. Trace round it, turn the paper, and then turn it back onto the tracing. It fits exactly, because none of its sides or corners changed.
