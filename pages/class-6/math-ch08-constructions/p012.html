You can even draw $AF$ without measuring it at all. Draw a perpendicular to $AF$ through $A$, long enough to hold the whole top side. Since $AB = AF$, you need to carry the length of $AF$ across to this line to find $B$. A compass does this without a ruler, as Fig. 8.21 shows.

Carrying a length

To copy a length onto a line, open the compass to that length. Put the needle on the line, and draw an arc across it. The arc marks the same length along the line.
