8.5Exploring Diagonals of Rectangles and Squares

Draw a rectangle $PQRS$. Join $P$ to $R$, and join $Q$ to $S$. These two lines are called the diagonals of the rectangle.

Are the two diagonals the same length? First predict the answer. Then construct a rectangle, mark it as in Fig. 8.24, and measure the diagonals.

In rectangle $PQRS$, the right angles at $P$ and at $R$ are called opposite angles. The right angles at $Q$ and at $S$ are the other pair of opposite angles.

A diagonal splits each angle of a pair of opposite angles into two smaller angles. The diagonal $PR$ splits $\angle R$ into the angles $g$ and $h$. It also splits $\angle P$ into $c$ and $d$. Are $g$ and $h$ equal? Are $c$ and $d$ equal? Predict first, and then measure. Which pairs of angles are equal?
