Step 3. You know the line that $D$ is on: the perpendicular to $AB$ through $A$. Draw it. Now $\angle A$ is split into two angles. One of them is $60^\circ$. How big is the other one?

There are at least two ways to find $D$. One uses the fact that all the angles of a rectangle are right angles. The other uses the fact that the opposite sides of a rectangle are equal.

Step 4, Method 1. Draw a perpendicular to $BC$ through $C$. It meets the line through $A$ at $D$.

Step 4, Method 2. Open the compass to the length of $BC$. With the needle on $A$, draw an arc across the line through $A$. It marks $D$, so that $AD = BC$. Join $C$ to $D$.
