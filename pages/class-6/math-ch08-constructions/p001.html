Playing with Constructions

A ruler and a compass are two of the oldest tools in mathematics. With only these two, you can draw circles, squares, rectangles and many beautiful designs. In this chapter you will learn how, and you will find out why each step works.

8.1Artwork

Look at the designs in Fig. 8.1. Try drawing them freehand in your notebook, without any instruments.

Now take a ruler and a compass. Look closely at the compass. One arm ends in a sharp needle, and the other arm holds a pencil. What can you draw with it? Explore for a few minutes before you read on.
