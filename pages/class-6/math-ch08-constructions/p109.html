Example 10

Leela makes a card: a rectangle $ABCD$ with $AB = 8$ cm and $BC = 6$ cm. With the needle on $A$ and the pencil on $C$, she draws a circle. It crosses the line $AB$ at $E$, a point past $B$ on that line.

  1. The diagonal $AC$ measures
    1. 14 cm
    2. 10 cm
    3. 12 cm
    4. 7 cm
  2. Numerical answer. What is the radius of the circle, in centimetres?
  3. Numerical answer. How far is $E$ from $B$, in centimetres?

Solution.

Step 1Construct the rectangle: the diagonal $AC$ measures 10 cm.
Step 2The compass is open from $A$ to $C$, so the radius is 10 cm.
Step 3$AE = 10$ cm and $AB = 8$ cm on one line, so $EB = 10 - 8 = 2$ cm.
Answer(i) (b) 10 cm; (ii) 10; (iii) 2
