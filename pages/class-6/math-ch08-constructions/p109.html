Assertion and reason

In Questions 13 to 16, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): A square that is turned on the page is still a square.

    Reason (R): Turning a figure does not change its side lengths or its angles.

  1. Assertion (A): Arcs of radius 3 cm drawn about two points 7 cm apart cross each other.

    Reason (R): $3 + 3$ is less than 7.

  1. Assertion (A): A rectangle can have a side of 8 cm and a diagonal of 6 cm.

    Reason (R): A diagonal of a rectangle is longer than each of its sides.

  1. Assertion (A): In the house of Section 8.6, the point $A$ is 5 cm from both $B$ and $C$.

    Reason (R): The floor of that house is 5 cm long.
