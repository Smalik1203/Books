Example 3

Which of these are true of every rectangle?

  1. All four angles are $90^\circ$.
  2. Opposite sides are equal.
  3. All four sides are equal.
  4. Turned, it is still a rectangle.

Solution.

Step 1Properties R1 and R2 give (a) and (b). Turning does not change sides or angles, so (d) holds.
Step 2A rectangle 9 cm long and 6 cm wide has sides that are not all equal, so (c) is false.
CheckA square is a rectangle whose sides are all equal, so (c) is true of some rectangles, but not of every one.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 4

A circle has its centre at $O$ and a radius of 6 cm. Which of these are true?

  1. Its points are 6 cm from $O$.
  2. A point 4 cm from $O$ is outside.
  3. Its width through $O$ is 12 cm.
  4. A point 8 cm from $O$ is outside.

Solution.

Step 1Points nearer to $O$ than 6 cm are inside; points farther than 6 cm are outside.
Step 2The width through $O$ is 6 cm on each side of $O$: $6 + 6 = 12$ cm.
CheckA point 4 cm from $O$ is nearer than 6 cm, so it is inside, and (b) is false.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
