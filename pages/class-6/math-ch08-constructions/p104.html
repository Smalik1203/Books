Example 5 · Single correct

The diagonals of a rectangle intersect at O. One diagonal is 14 cm long. What is the distance from O to any vertex?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 3.5 cm
  2. 14 cm
  3. 28 cm
  4. 7 cm

Solution.

Step 1A rectangle’s diagonals are equal and bisect each other.
Step 2Each half-diagonal measures 14 ÷ 2 = 7 cm.
Answer(d) 7 cm
Example 6 · Single correct

To construct a square ABCD on a given side AB = 6 cm, which condition correctly places D?

Choose one correct option.

  1. D lies anywhere 6 cm from A.
  2. AD is perpendicular to AB and AD = 6 cm.
  3. D is the midpoint of AB.
  4. AD is parallel to AB and AD = 6 cm.

Solution.

Step 1Adjacent sides of a square are equal and perpendicular.
Step 2Both the right angle at A and the length AD = 6 cm are required. Either side of AB gives a reflected square.
Answer(b) AD is perpendicular to AB and AD = 6 cm
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which properties hold for every rectangle?

Select all correct options.

  1. All four angles are right angles.
  2. All four sides have equal length.
  3. The diagonals have equal length.
  4. Opposite sides are parallel.

Solution.

Step 1Four right angles make opposite sides parallel. Rectangles also have equal diagonals.
Step 2A 9 cm by 6 cm rectangle shows that all four sides need not be equal.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
