Type 3 · Constructing from the sides

Example 5

Construct a rectangle $PQRS$ with $PQ = 5$ cm and $QR = 3$ cm. Use the compass, not the ruler, to mark both short sides.

Solution.

Step 1 draw $PQ = 5$ cm
Step 2 draw perpendiculars to $PQ$ through $P$ and through $Q$ R2 at $P$ and $Q$
Step 3 open the compass to 3 cm against the ruler
Step 4 arc about $Q$ marks $R$; arc about $P$ marks $S$ one opening, two sides
Step 5 join $S$ to $R$
Answer $SR$ measures 5 cm, and $\angle R$ and $\angle S$ measure $90^\circ$
Example 6

Construct a square $ABCD$ with sides of 5 cm. Measure only once, when you draw the first side.

Solution.

Step 1 draw $AB = 5$ cm the only measuring
Step 2 draw perpendiculars to $AB$ through $A$ and through $B$
Step 3 needle on $A$, pencil on $B$: the compass is open to $AB$ carry the length
Step 4 arc about $A$ marks $D$; arc about $B$ marks $C$
Step 5 join $D$ to $C$
Answer a square: $AD$, $BC$ and $DC$ all measure 5 cm

Type 4 · Planning with a rough diagram

Example 7

A rectangle is divided into 4 identical squares that sit side by side in one row. Its shorter side is 3 cm. Find its longer side, and the distance all the way round it.

Solution.

Step 1 each square has sides of 3 cm it fills the width
Step 2 longer side $= 4 \times 3 = 12$ cm four squares in a row
Step 3 all the way round $= 12 + 3 + 12 + 3 = 30$ cm
Answer 12 cm; 30 cm
