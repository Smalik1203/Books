What this chapter showed
  1. All the points of a circle are the same distance from its centre. That distance is the radius of the circle.
  2. A compass draws circles and parts of circles, and carries a length without a ruler.
  3. A rectangle has equal opposite sides, and all its angles are $90^\circ$. A square has all its sides equal, and all its angles are $90^\circ$. Turning a square or a rectangle does not change these properties.
  4. The name of a rectangle lists its corners in order round it.
  5. A rough diagram helps you plan the order of a construction.
  6. A rectangle can be constructed from the lengths of its sides, or from one side and a diagonal.
  7. To find a point at given distances from two points, draw an arc about each point. The point is where the arcs cross.

Draw your construction arcs lightly, and do not rub them out. They show how each point was found, and they let you check your work later.
