Centre and radius

All the points of a circle are the same distance from one fixed point. That point is the centre of the circle. The distance from the centre to any point on the circle is the radius.

A person

How would you draw the person on the left of Fig. 8.5? The figure has two parts: a head with a neck, and a body. The head is a circle and the neck is a straight line, so the first part is easy.

The top of the body is harder. It is a curve, and it is part of a circle. You have to find where to put the needle, and how wide to open the compass, so that the pencil passes through both top corners of the body. Fix one opening and try the needle in a few different places. Make a good guess first, and then adjust it.
