Type 6 · A point from two distances

Example 14

Draw $PQ = 6$ cm. Find a point $R$ that is 5 cm from $P$ and 4 cm from $Q$, and join it to $P$ and $Q$.

Solution.

Step 1 draw $PQ = 6$ cm
Step 2 arc of radius 5 cm about $P$ points 5 cm from $P$
Step 3 arc of radius 4 cm about $Q$, crossing it at $R$ points 4 cm from $Q$
Step 4 $5 + 4 = 9$, more than 6 so the arcs cross
Answer a triangle $PQR$ with $PR = 5$ cm and $QR = 4$ cm

The two arcs cross twice, once on each side of $PQ$, so there are two places for $R$. Either one gives a triangle with the same three sides.

Example 15

The tops of the two walls of a house, $B$ and $C$, are 6 cm apart. Each roof line is 5 cm long and meets the other at $A$. $M$ is the point halfway between $B$ and $C$. How high is the tip of the roof above $M$?

Solution.

Step 1 draw $BC = 6$ cm and mark $M$, 3 cm from $B$
Step 2 arcs of radius 5 cm about $B$ and about $C$ cross above $BC$ at $A$ $5 + 5 \gt 6$
Step 3 join $A$ to $M$ and measure it
Answer $AM$ measures 4 cm
