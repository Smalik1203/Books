Example 8

Two circles each have a radius of 4 cm. Match the distance between their centres in List I with the number of points on both circles in List II.

List IList II
(P) Centres 9 cm apart(1) One point
(Q) Centres 8 cm apart(2) Two points
(R) Centres 5 cm apart(3) No point
(S) The same centre(4) Every point of the circle
  1. P–3, Q–2, R–1, S–4
  2. P–1, Q–3, R–2, S–4
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–2, S–4
  4. P–4, Q–1, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The reaches add to $4 + 4 = 8$ cm. At 9 cm they miss; at 8 cm they touch once.
Step 2At 5 cm they cross twice. With one centre they are the same circle.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–1, R–2, S–4
