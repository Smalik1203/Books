Why the other options are wrong

B2 Two squares side by side make a rectangle whose long side is twice its short side, and 6 cm is not twice 4 cm. So A is false, and R is true: it is the very reason A fails. The answer is (d).
B4 Measure the diagonals of a rectangle 7 cm by 4 cm, and they come out equal, so A is true. R is false: only the opposite sides of a rectangle must be equal. The answer is (c).
B6 The square uses 6 cm of the 10 cm length. The 4 cm left over is shared between the two ends: 2 cm each, option (a). Option (b) does not share it.
B7 The arcs could meet only if $3 + 3$ were at least 7. It is 6, so the arcs never meet, and A is false. R is true, and it is the reason. The answer is (d).
