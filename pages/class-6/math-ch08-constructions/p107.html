Example 13 · Numerical answer

Points A and B are 10 cm apart. Two equal circles centred at A and B each have radius 5 cm. Enter the number of points common to the circles.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The centre distance equals the sum of the radii: 10 = 5 + 5.
Step 2They touch at the midpoint of AB, giving exactly one common point.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
