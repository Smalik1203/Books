Finding a point from two distances

To find a point at a given distance from each of two points, draw an arc of that radius about each point. Where the two arcs cross, the point is at the right distance from both.

Look again at Step 3, Method 1. The two circles cross at a second point, below $BC$. That point is also 5 cm from $B$ and 5 cm from $C$. A roof drawn up to it would point down into the house, so the upper point is the one you want. Two circles that cross always cross at two points, one on each side of the line that joins their centres. So there are exactly two points that are 5 cm from both $B$ and $C$.
