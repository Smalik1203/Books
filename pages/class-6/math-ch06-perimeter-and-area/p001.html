Perimeter and Area

Walk once around the edge of your school playground. Tie a ribbon around a gift box. Put a fence around a field. Each time, you go all the way around a shape. In this chapter you will measure the way around a shape, and the space it covers.

6.1Perimeter

The perimeter of a closed figure is the distance around its boundary, going around it once. A polygon is a closed figure made of line segments. To find its perimeter, add the lengths of all its sides.

Perimeter of a polygon

The perimeter of a polygon is the sum of the lengths of all its sides.

Perimeter of a rectangle

Look at rectangle $ABCD$ in Fig. 6.1. What is its perimeter?

The opposite sides of a rectangle are equal. So $CD = AB$ and $DA = BC$. Add the four sides in order.

Perimeter $= AB + BC + CD + DA$

$= AB + BC + AB + BC$

$= 2 \times AB + 2 \times BC$

$= 2 \times (AB + BC)$

$= 2 \times (12\text{ cm} + 8\text{ cm}) = 2 \times 20\text{ cm} = 40\text{ cm}$

For every rectangle, the length and the breadth are each added twice.
