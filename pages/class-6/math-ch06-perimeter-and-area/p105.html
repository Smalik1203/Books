Example 5

A rectangle 5 cm by 4 cm is cut from one corner of a rectangle 14 cm by 11 cm. Find the area that is left, in square centimetres.

Solution.

Step 1The whole rectangle has an area of 14 × 11 = 154 sq cm.
Step 2The corner cut away has an area of 5 × 4 = 20 sq cm.
Step 3The area left is 154 − 20 = 134 sq cm.
CheckSplit the shape into two rectangles instead: 14 × 7 = 98 and 9 × 4 = 36, and 98 + 36 = 134.
Answer134
Example 6

A regular octagon has a perimeter of 104 cm. Find the length of its side, in centimetres.

Solution.

Step 1A regular octagon has 8 equal sides, so its perimeter is 8 × its side.
Step 2So the side is the perimeter divided by 8.
Step 3Each side is 104 ÷ 8 = 13 cm.
Check8 × 13 = 104.
Answer13
