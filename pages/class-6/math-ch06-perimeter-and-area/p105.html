Example 8

A lawn is a rectangle 10 m long and 8 m wide. A square pond of side 2 m is dug in its middle, 4 m from each end and 3 m from each side. Find the area of grass that is left, in two ways.

Solution.

Step 1 lawn $= 10 \times 8 = 80$ sq m, pond $= 2 \times 2 = 4$ sq m first way
Step 2 grass $= 80 - 4 = 76$ sq m whole take away hole
Step 3 strips above and below the pond $= 2 \times (10 \times 3) = 60$ sq m second way
Step 4 pieces left and right of the pond $= 2 \times (4 \times 2) = 16$ sq m
Step 5 grass $= 60 + 16 = 76$ sq m the two ways agree
Answer 76 sq m
Example 9

A square card of side 4 cm is cut into four pieces, as in Fig. 6.23. Compare the pieces by placing them on one another, and then find the area of each piece.

Solution.

Step 1 $C$ placed on $D$ covers it exactly, so $C$ and $D$ have the same area one on the other
Step 2 $C$ and $D$ together cover $B$ exactly, so $B$ is 2 of $C$
Step 3 $B$, $C$ and $D$ together cover $A$ exactly, so $A$ is $2 + 1 + 1 = 4$ of $C$
Step 4 the whole card is $4 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 8$ of $C$ counting in $C$s
Step 5 card $= 4 \times 4 = 16$ sq cm, so $C = 16 \div 8 = 2$ sq cm
Answer $A$ 8 sq cm, $B$ 4 sq cm, $C$ and $D$ 2 sq cm each
