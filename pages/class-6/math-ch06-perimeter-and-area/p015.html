A triangle in a rectangle

A triangle drawn with one side of a rectangle as its base, and its top corner on the opposite side, has half the area of the rectangle.

Example 5

A rectangular garden is 12 m long and 9 m wide. A flower bed in the shape of a triangle has one long side of the garden as its base. Its top corner is on the opposite side. The rest of the garden is grass. Find the area of the grass.

Solution.

Step 1 Area of the garden $= 12\text{ m} \times 9\text{ m} = 108\text{ sq m}$
Step 2 Area of the flower bed $= 108\text{ sq m} \div 2 = 54\text{ sq m}$
Step 3 Area of the grass $= 108\text{ sq m} - 54\text{ sq m} = 54\text{ sq m}$
Check The grass is two triangles. Each is half of a smaller rectangle, and the two rectangles make up the garden.
Answer The grass covers 54 sq m, the same as the flower bed.
