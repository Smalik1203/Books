Example 3

A floor is 5 m long and 4 m wide. A square carpet of side 3 m is laid on the floor. What area of the floor is not covered by the carpet?

Solution.

Step 1 Area of the floor $= 5\text{ m} \times 4\text{ m} = 20\text{ sq m}$ length times width
Step 2 Area of the carpet $= 3\text{ m} \times 3\text{ m} = 9\text{ sq m}$ side times side
Step 3 Area not covered $= 20\text{ sq m} - 9\text{ sq m} = 11\text{ sq m}$ whole take away part
Answer 11 sq m of the floor is not carpeted.

To find the area of a part, you can often find the area of the whole and take away what you do not want.

Example 4

A piece of land is 12 m long and 10 m wide. There is a square flower bed of side 4 m in each of its four corners, as in Fig. 6.10. What is the area of the rest of the land?

Solution.

Step 1 Area of the whole land $= 12\text{ m} \times 10\text{ m} = 120\text{ sq m}$
Step 2 Area of one flower bed $= 4\text{ m} \times 4\text{ m} = 16\text{ sq m}$
Step 3 Area of four flower beds $= 4 \times 16\text{ sq m} = 64\text{ sq m}$ four equal beds
Step 4 Area left $= 120\text{ sq m} - 64\text{ sq m} = 56\text{ sq m}$ whole take away beds
Answer The rest of the land has an area of 56 sq m.

Look at Fig. 6.10. The land that is left is shaped like a plus sign. You can also find its area by splitting it into rectangles. The middle strip is 4 m wide and 10 m long, which is 40 sq m. The pieces to its left and right are each 4 m by 2 m, which is 8 sq m. That gives $40 + 8 + 8 = 56$ sq m, the same answer.
