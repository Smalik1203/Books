Why do we measure area in squares?

Draw a circle of diameter 3 units on squared paper. Count the squares to find its area. Now think: why not use small circles as units instead of squares?

Circles cannot be packed together without gaps. Fig. 6.14 shows the same rectangle packed with circles in two ways. One way fits 42 circles and the other fits 44, and both leave gaps. So circles do not give a clear count of area.
