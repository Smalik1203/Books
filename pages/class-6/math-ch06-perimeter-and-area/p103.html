Example 2 · Single correct

A square and a rectangle have the same perimeter, 48 cm. The rectangle measures 16 cm by 8 cm. How much larger is the square’s area?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 8 cm²
  2. 16 cm²
  3. 32 cm²
  4. 64 cm²

Solution.

Step 1The square’s side is 48 ÷ 4 = 12 cm and its area is 144 cm².
Step 2The rectangle’s area is 16 × 8 = 128 cm². The difference is 16 cm².
Answer(b) 16 cm²
Example 3 · Single correct

A rectangular garden measures 18 m by 12 m. A path 1 m wide runs all around its inside edge. What is the area of the path?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 30 m²
  2. 60 m²
  3. 56 m²
  4. 68 m²

Solution.

Step 1The inner rectangle measures 16 m by 10 m because each dimension loses 1 m at each end.
Step 2Path area = 18 × 12 − 16 × 10 = 216 − 160 = 56 m².
Answer(c) 56 m²
Example 4 · Single correct

A 4 cm by 3 cm rectangle is removed from a corner of a 10 cm by 8 cm rectangle. What is the perimeter of the remaining L-shape?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 22 cm
  2. 29 cm
  3. 43 cm
  4. 36 cm

Solution.

Step 1The original perimeter is 2 × (10 + 8) = 36 cm.
Step 2The removed outer lengths 4 cm and 3 cm are replaced by inner edges of the same lengths, so the perimeter stays 36 cm.
Answer(d) 36 cm
