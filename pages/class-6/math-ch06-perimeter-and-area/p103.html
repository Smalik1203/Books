Example 2

A rectangle 4 cm by 3 cm is cut from one corner of a rectangle 10 cm by 8 cm. What is the perimeter of the L-shape that is left?

  1. 22 cm
  2. 29 cm
  3. 43 cm
  4. 36 cm

Solution.

Step 1The perimeter of the whole rectangle is 2 × (10 + 8) = 36 cm.
Step 2The cut takes away 4 cm and 3 cm of the outside edge, and puts in two new edges of 4 cm and 3 cm. So the perimeter stays 36 cm.
Answer(d) 36 cm

Example 3

  1. 10 cm by 5 cm

  3. 12 cm by 3 cm

Step 1A perimeter of 30 cm means length + breadth = 30 ÷ 2 = 15 cm.
Step 210 + 5, 9 + 6 and 12 + 3 all make 15. But 8 + 8 = 16, so that square has a perimeter of 32 cm.
