Type 2 · Going round more than once

Example 3

Ravi walks round a park 90 m long and 60 m wide. How many full rounds make at least 2 km?

Solution.

Step 1 one round $= 2 \times (90 + 60) = 300$ m
Step 2 2 km $= 2 \times 1000 = 2000$ m same unit first
Step 3 $6 \times 300 = 1800$ m and $7 \times 300 = 2100$ m 6 rounds are too few
Answer 7 rounds

Type 3 · Straight units and diagonal units

Example 4

The dots in Fig. 6.22 are 1 unit apart. Write the perimeter of the shape in straight units and diagonal units. Then find its area.

Solution.

Step 1 straight sides: $3 + 2 + 3 + 2 = 10$ straight units
Step 2 two sides cross one square each, corner to corner 2 diagonal units
Step 3 the shape covers 10 full squares and 2 exactly half squares the rules for counting
Step 4 area $= 10 + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = 11$ square units
Answer perimeter $10s + 2d$ units; area 11 square units
