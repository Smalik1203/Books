1. A shape on squared paper covers 10 full squares, 4 squares more than half, 2 squares exactly half and 3 squares less than half. By the rules for counting squares, its area is
    1. 15 sq units
    2. 19 sq units
    3. 17 sq units
    4. 16 sq units
  1. Which of these are true?
    1. Two figures with the same area always have the same perimeter.
    2. The perimeter of a square is 4 times its side.
    3. Area is measured in square units.
    1. (i) only
    2. (ii) only
    3. (i) and (ii)
    4. (ii) and (iii)
  1. Tara says that the perimeter of a rectangle 4 cm long and 3 cm wide is 12 cm. Which of these is true?
    1. Tara is right.
    2. No: she multiplied the sides; the perimeter is 14 cm.
    3. No: the perimeter is 7 cm.
    4. No: the perimeter is 24 cm.
