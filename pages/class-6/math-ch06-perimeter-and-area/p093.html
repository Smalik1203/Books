Case-based questions

  1. For Diwali, Priya makes a rangoli on a square board of side 60 cm. She sticks a gold ribbon once around the edge of the board. In the middle she fills a square of side 20 cm with red powder.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the length of the ribbon.
    2. Find the area of the red square.
    3. Find the area of the board that is not red.

  1. The running track of a school is a rectangle 100 m long and 50 m wide. On sports day, Ravi runs 4 rounds of the track. Anil runs 1600 m on the same track, starting at the same corner.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the length of one round.
    2. Find the distance Ravi runs.
    3. How many full rounds does Anil finish? How far past the starting corner does he stop?

  1. Meena’s house fills a rectangular plot 40 ft long and 25 ft wide. It has a hall 20 ft by 15 ft, a kitchen 10 ft by 12 ft and a bedroom 15 ft by 12 ft. The rest of the plot is a garden.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the area of the plot.
    2. Find the area of the kitchen.
    3. Find the area of the garden.

  1. A bathroom wall is 3 m wide and 2 m high. It is covered exactly with square tiles of side 25 cm, with no gaps. Each tile costs ₹30.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many tiles fit along the width of the wall?
    2. How many tiles fit along the height of the wall?
    3. Find the number of tiles on the wall and their total cost.
