Area with tangram pieces

A tangram is a square cut into seven pieces, as in Fig. 6.12. The pieces are named by the letters $A$ to $G$. Trace the pieces onto thick paper and cut them out. You can compare the areas of two pieces by placing one on top of the other, or by covering one piece with other pieces. Two pieces have the same area if one covers the other exactly, even when it has to be turned over. A piece that is exactly covered by two others has as much area as those two together.
