Area on squared paper

How do you find the area of a shape that is not made of rectangles, such as a leaf? Trace the shape onto squared paper, where each square is 1 unit by 1 unit, or 1 square unit. Then count the squares inside the shape. Most shapes will cut through some squares, so follow these rules.

Counting squares

1. Count each full square as 1 square unit.

2. Leave out a part of a square that is less than half.

3. Count a part that is more than half a square as 1 square unit.

4. Count a part that is exactly half a square as $\frac{1}{2}$ square unit.
