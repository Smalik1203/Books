Example 2

Usha runs around a square park 3 times. Each side of the park is 75 m. How far does she run?

Solution.

Step 1 One round $= 4 \times 75\text{ m} = 300\text{ m}$ perimeter of the square
Step 2 Three rounds $= 3 \times 300\text{ m} = 900\text{ m}$ rounds times perimeter
Check 3 rounds pass $3 \times 4 = 12$ sides, and $12 \times 75\text{ m} = 900\text{ m}$
Answer Usha runs 900 m.
