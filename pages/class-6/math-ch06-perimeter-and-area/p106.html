Example 8

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) Side of a square of area 81 sq cm(1) 7 cm
(Q) Breadth of a rectangle of area 48 sq cm and length 8 cm(2) 11 cm
(R) Side of a regular pentagon of perimeter 35 cm(3) 9 cm
(S) Length of a rectangle of perimeter 30 cm and breadth 4 cm(4) 6 cm
  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Side of the square: 9 cm, as 9 × 9 = 81. Breadth: 48 ÷ 8 = 6 cm.
Step 2The side of the pentagon is 35 ÷ 5 = 7 cm. The length is 30 ÷ 2 − 4 = 11 cm.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
