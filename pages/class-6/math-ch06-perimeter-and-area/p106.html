Example 11 · Numerical answer

A runner completes five laps around a rectangular ground 85 m long and 45 m wide. Enter the total distance in metres.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1One lap is 2 × (85 + 45) = 260 m.
Step 2Five laps cover 5 × 260 = 1300 m.
Answer1300
Example 12 · Numerical answer

A 14 cm by 11 cm rectangle has a 5 cm by 4 cm rectangular corner removed. Enter the remaining area in square centimetres.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The original area is 14 × 11 = 154 cm².
Step 2Remove 5 × 4 = 20 cm² to leave 134 cm².
Answer134
Example 13 · Numerical answer

The perimeter of a regular octagon is 104 cm. Enter its side length in centimetres.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1A regular octagon has eight equal sides.
Step 2104 ÷ 8 = 13 cm.
Answer13
