Type 6 · Same perimeter, different areas

Example 10

Of all the rectangles with whole-number sides and a perimeter of 20 cm, which one has the greatest area?

Solution.

Step 1 length $+$ width $= 20 \div 2 = 10$ cm half the perimeter
Step 2 $1 \times 9 = 9$, $\ 2 \times 8 = 16$, $\ 3 \times 7 = 21$, $\ 4 \times 6 = 24$, $\ 5 \times 5 = 25$ every pair
Answer the square of side 5 cm, area 25 sq cm; the closer the sides, the bigger the area
Example 11

Fig. 6.24 shows 6 unit squares. Find the perimeter when a seventh is added at $P$, and at $Q$.

Solution.

Step 1 going round the figure, its perimeter is 14 units count once
Step 2 at $P$, 2 sides are covered and 2 added: $14 - 2 + 2 = 14$ units same perimeter
Step 3 at $Q$, 1 side is covered and 3 added: $14 - 1 + 3 = 16$ units
Answer at $P$, 14 units; at $Q$, 16 units

Type 7 · Area of a triangle in a rectangle

Example 12

Rectangle $PQRS$ is 8 cm long and 6 cm wide, with $PQ$ and $SR$ its long sides. Point $T$ is on $SR$, 3 cm from $S$. Find the areas of triangles $PQT$, $PST$ and $QRT$.

Solution.

Step 1 $PQT$ $= 8 \times 6 \div 2 = 24$ sq cm half the rectangle
Step 2 $PST$ is half of a 3 cm by 6 cm rectangle: $3 \times 6 \div 2 = 9$ sq cm
Step 3 $TR = 8 - 3 = 5$ cm, so $QRT$ $= 5 \times 6 \div 2 = 15$ sq cm
Step 4 $24 + 9 + 15 = 48 = 8 \times 6$ the three fill the rectangle
Answer 24 sq cm, 9 sq cm and 15 sq cm
