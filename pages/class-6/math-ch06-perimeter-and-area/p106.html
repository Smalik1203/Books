Example 11

Triangle $ABC$ has its base $AB$ 6 cm long. Its top corner $C$ is 4 cm straight above the point $F$ on $AB$, and $AF$ is 4 cm. Find the area of the triangle by cutting it along $CF$.

Solution.

Step 1 $FB = 6 - 4 = 2$ cm
Step 2 $AFC$ is half of a 4 cm by 4 cm rectangle: $4 \times 4 \div 2 = 8$ sq cm half a rectangle
Step 3 $FBC$ is half of a 2 cm by 4 cm rectangle: $2 \times 4 \div 2 = 4$ sq cm
Step 4 $ABC$ $= 8 + 4 = 12$ sq cm, and $6 \times 4 \div 2 = 12$ half the 6 by 4 rectangle
Answer 12 sq cm

Example 12

A flat fills a rectangle 40 ft long and 30 ft wide. It has a bedroom 15 ft by 12 ft, and a kitchen of area 120 sq ft with one side of 12 ft. The rest of the flat is the hall. Find the other side of the kitchen, and the area of the hall.

Solution.

Step 1 other side of the kitchen $= 120 \div 12 = 10$ ft
Step 2 flat $= 40 \times 30 = 1200$ sq ft, bedroom $= 15 \times 12 = 180$ sq ft
Step 3 hall $= 1200 - 180 - 120 = 900$ sq ft whole take away rooms
Answer 10 ft; the hall is 900 sq ft
Example 13

Three rectangles stand side by side, all with the same height. Their areas are 12 sq cm, 20 sq cm and 28 sq cm, and the first one is 3 cm wide. How wide is the whole figure?

Solution.

Step 1 height $= 12 \div 3 = 4$ cm from the first one
Step 2 widths $= 20 \div 4 = 5$ cm and $28 \div 4 = 7$ cm same height
Step 3 $3 + 5 + 7 = 15$ cm
Answer 15 cm
