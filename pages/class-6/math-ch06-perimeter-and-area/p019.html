Perimeter is a length, so it is measured in cm or m. Area is a number of squares, so it is measured in sq cm or sq m. Check the unit before you write an answer.

    3. The two perimeters of the rectangles added together are always $1\frac{1}{2}$ times the perimeter of the square.
