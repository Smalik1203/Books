Area maze puzzles

An area maze is a figure made of rectangles, with some areas and sides given. Find the missing one. You will not need every length in the figure. Rectangles side by side share a side, and that shared side links their areas.

Example 6

A rectangle is cut into four smaller rectangles by one line across and one line down. The top two have areas 8 sq cm and 12 sq cm. The bottom-left one has area 14 sq cm. Find the area of the bottom-right one.

Solution.

Step 1 $12 \div 8 = 1\frac{1}{2}$, so the right one is $1\frac{1}{2}$ times as wide same height
Step 2 the bottom two have the same two widths, and the same height
Step 3 Bottom-right area $= 1\frac{1}{2} \times 14\text{ sq cm} = 21\text{ sq cm}$
Check Columns: $12 + 21 = 33$, $8 + 14 = 22$, and $33 \div 22 = 1\frac{1}{2}$
Answer 21 sq cm

In Example 6 we found the area without finding a single length. If a length is missing instead, divide an area by the side you know. Example 7 shows how. It uses the side that the two rectangles share, which you find from the first rectangle.

Example 7

A rectangle of area 24 sq cm is 6 cm long. A second rectangle shares its shorter side and has an area of 20 sq cm. How long is the second rectangle?

Solution.

Step 1 Shorter side of the first $= 24 \div 6 = 4$ cm area divided by length
Step 2 Length of the second $= 20 \div 4 = 5$ cm the shared side is 4 cm
Check $4 \times 5 = 20$ sq cm, the area of the second rectangle
Answer The second rectangle is 5 cm long.

Perimeter is a length, so it is measured in cm or m. Area is a number of squares, so it is measured in sq cm or sq m. Check the unit before you write an answer.
