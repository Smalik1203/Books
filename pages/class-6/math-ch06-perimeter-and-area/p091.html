1. The length of a rectangle is 3 times its width, and its perimeter is 48 cm. Find its length and its width. Also find its area.
  1. Six unit squares are joined in a row. Find the area and the perimeter of the figure. One more unit square is now placed on top of the third square. Find the new perimeter.
  1. Rectangle $ABCD$ is 10 cm long and 6 cm wide. Find the area of triangle $ABD$. Also find the area of triangle $ABE$, where $E$ is any point on side $DC$.
  1. A regular pentagon has the same perimeter as a square of side 10 cm. Find the length of a side of the pentagon. Also find the length of a side of a regular octagon with the same perimeter.
  1. A garden is 30 m long and 20 m wide. A square pond of side 6 m is dug in it. Find the area of the garden that is left. Also find the cost of planting grass on it at ₹15 per sq m.

  1. A school ground is 120 m long and 80 m wide, and Arjun runs 5 rounds of it. His friend runs on a square track of side 90 m. Find the distance Arjun runs. Also find the least number of full rounds his friend must run to cover at least the same distance.
  1. A hall is 15 m long and 12 m wide. Its floor is to be covered with square tiles of side 50 cm, which cost ₹40 each. Find the number of tiles needed and the total cost of the tiles.
  1. A rectangular field is 50 m long and 30 m wide. It is fenced with 4 rounds of wire, and the wire costs ₹12 per metre. Find the cost of the wire. Also find the area of the field.
  1. A rectangular playground is 70 m long, and its perimeter is 240 m. Find its width and its area. Also find the cost of levelling the playground at ₹5 per sq m.
  1. A wall is 6 m long and 3 m high. It has a door 2 m high and 1 m wide, and a window 2 m wide and 1 m high. The rest of the wall is painted at ₹60 per sq m. Find the cost of painting. Also find the length of tape needed to go once around the door and once around the window.
  1. Kiran has 20 m of fencing to make a rectangular garden. Its length and width must be whole numbers of metres, and all the fencing must be used. List all the rectangles she can make, with the area of each. Which rectangle has the largest area?
  1. A flat is a rectangle 40 ft long and 30 ft wide. It has a hall 20 ft by 15 ft, two bedrooms of 12 ft by 15 ft each and a kitchen 15 ft by 12 ft. The rest is passage and bathrooms. Find the area of the rest. Also find the length of skirting along the walls of the hall, leaving out a doorway 3 ft wide.
