Who ran further?

Akshi and Toshi run on the two rectangular tracks in Fig. 6.3. Akshi runs on the outer track and finishes 5 rounds. Toshi runs on the inner track and finishes 7 rounds. Who ran the longer distance?

Each track is a rectangle. Akshi’s track is 70 m long and 40 m wide. In one round she runs its perimeter, $2 \times (70\text{ m} + 40\text{ m}) = 220\text{ m}$.

Both girls start at the corner marked for them and run in the direction of the arrows. After each full round, a runner is back at her starting corner.

Running in rounds

Distance run in full rounds $=$ number of rounds $\times$ perimeter of the track

A common finishing line

In a race, all the runners usually finish at the same line. Fig. 6.4 shows two square tracks. The inner track has sides of 100 m and the outer track has sides of 150 m. The finishing line for both runners crosses the middle of one side of each track.
