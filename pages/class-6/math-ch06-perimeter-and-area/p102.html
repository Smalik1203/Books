A photo 20 cm long and 15 cm wide is pasted on a card. The card leaves a border 5 cm wide all around the photo. What is the area of the border?

The border is not a rectangle, so find it from the whole and the part. The card is 5 cm wider on each side, so it is $20 + 5 + 5 = 30$ cm long and $15 + 5 + 5 = 25$ cm wide. Its area is $30 \times 25 = 750$ sq cm. The photo covers $20 \times 15 = 300$ sq cm. The border is what is left: $750 - 300 = 450$ sq cm.

A rectangle is 10 cm long and 6 cm wide. A triangle is drawn with one long side of the rectangle as its base, and its top corner somewhere on the opposite side. What is the area of the triangle?

We are not told where the top corner is. But from Section 6.3, a triangle like this always has half the area of the rectangle, wherever its top corner is. The rectangle has area $10 \times 6 = 60$ sq cm, so the triangle has area 30 sq cm. We did not need to know where the corner was.

None of these questions needed a new rule. Each needed you to ask what you already knew about the shape, and then to use it.

Example 1

A rectangular table top has a perimeter of 74 cm. It is 23 cm long. A cloth is cut to cover the top exactly. What is the area of the cloth?

  1. 322 sq cm
  2. 644 sq cm
  3. 1702 sq cm
  4. 148 sq cm

Solution.

Step 1Length + breadth is half the perimeter: 74 ÷ 2 = 37 cm.
Step 2The breadth is 37 − 23 = 14 cm.
Step 3Area = length × breadth = 23 × 14.
Step 423 × 14 = 23 × 10 + 23 × 4 = 230 + 92 = 322 sq cm.
Check2 × (23 + 14) = 2 × 37 = 74 cm, the perimeter given.
Answer(a) 322 sq cm
