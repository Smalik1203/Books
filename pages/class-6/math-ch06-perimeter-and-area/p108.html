Example 10

Salma has a wire 60 cm long. She bends it into different shapes. Each time she uses the whole wire, and then straightens it again.

  1. She makes an equilateral triangle. Each of its sides is
    1. 20 cm
    2. 15 cm
    3. 30 cm
    4. 12 cm
  2. Numerical answer. She makes a square. Find its area, in square centimetres.
  3. Numerical answer. She makes a rectangle 18 cm long. Find its area, in square centimetres.

Solution.

Step 1The triangle has 3 equal sides: 60 ÷ 3 = 20 cm.
Step 2The square has sides of 60 ÷ 4 = 15 cm, so its area is 15 × 15 = 225 sq cm.
Step 3For the rectangle, length + width = 60 ÷ 2 = 30 cm. The width is 30 − 18 = 12 cm, and the area is 18 × 12 = 216 sq cm.
Answer(i) (a) 20 cm; (ii) 225; (iii) 216
