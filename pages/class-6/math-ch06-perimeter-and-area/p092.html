Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): A square of side 5 cm has a perimeter of 20 cm.

    Reason (R): The perimeter of a square is 4 times the length of its side.

  1. Assertion (A): A rectangle 6 cm by 4 cm and a rectangle 8 cm by 3 cm have the same area.

    Reason (R): The two rectangles have the same perimeter.

  1. Assertion (A): A triangle drawn on a 10 cm side of a rectangle 10 cm by 4 cm, with its top corner on the opposite side, has an area of 20 sq cm.

    Reason (R): The perimeter of the rectangle is 28 cm.

  1. Assertion (A): Two figures with the same area always have the same perimeter.

    Reason (R): Figures with the same area can have different perimeters.

  1. Assertion (A): A floor 4 m long and 3 m wide can be covered exactly by 12 square tiles of side 1 m.

    Reason (R): The area of a rectangle is its length times its width, and each tile covers 1 sq m.
