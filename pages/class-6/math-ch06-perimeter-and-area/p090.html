By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Find the perimeter of a rectangle 18 cm long and 7 cm wide.
  1. A square field has a perimeter of 96 m. Find its area.
  1. A regular hexagon has a perimeter of 54 cm. Find the length of each of its sides.
  1. Is a rectangle 8 cm long and 2 cm wide equal in area to a square of side 4 cm? Give a reason.
  1. The area of a rectangle is 91 sq cm and its length is 13 cm. Find its perimeter.
  1. A triangle has one side of a rectangle 14 cm by 10 cm as its base, and its top corner on the opposite side. Find the area of the triangle.
  1. A shape on squared paper covers 12 full squares, 6 squares more than half, 4 squares exactly half and 5 squares less than half. Find its area, using the rules for counting squares.
  1. A triangle on dot paper has two sides of 2 straight units each, which meet at a right angle. Its third side joins their ends. Write its perimeter in straight and diagonal units.
  1. Leela walks 4 times around a rectangular park 60 m long and 40 m wide. Find the distance she walks.
  1. Find the number of square tiles of side 20 cm needed to cover a floor 2 m long and 1 m wide.
Short answer
  1. Neha fences a rectangular garden 12 m long with 40 m of fencing, using all of it. Find the width of the garden. Also find its area.
  1. Explain, with a drawing on squared paper, why a rectangle 6 units by 1 unit and a rectangle 3 units by 2 units have the same area but different perimeters.
  1. A rectangular room is 6 m long and 4 m wide. Find the cost of carpeting its floor at ₹250 per sq m. Also find the cost of fixing a wooden strip along the foot of all four walls at ₹30 per m.
  1. A square garden has a perimeter of 64 m. A rectangular garden 32 m long has the same area as the square garden. Find the perimeter of the rectangular garden.
  1. A rectangular plot is 25 m long and 16 m wide. A path 2 m wide runs along the inside of its boundary. Find the area of the path.
