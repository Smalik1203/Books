6.2Area

The amount of surface that a closed figure covers is called its area. We measure area by counting how many unit squares cover the figure. A square of side 1 cm has an area of 1 square centimetre, written 1 sq cm.

In earlier classes you found the area of a rectangle on squared paper. The rectangle in Fig. 6.9 is covered by 3 rows of 4 unit squares. That is $3 \times 4 = 12$ unit squares, so its area is 12 sq cm.
