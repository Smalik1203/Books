Perimeter of an equilateral triangle

Perimeter of an equilateral triangle $= 3 \times \text{length of a side}$

For example, an equilateral triangle with sides of 7 cm has a perimeter of $3 \times 7 = 21$ cm. You need to measure only one side.

Split and rejoin

A rectangular paper chit is 4 cm wide and 6 cm long. It is cut into two equal pieces, each 2 cm wide and 6 cm long. Then the pieces are joined in different ways, as in Fig. 6.7. In arrangement (a) they are joined end to end, and the perimeter is $12 + 2 + 12 + 2 = 28$ cm.

There is a second way to find the perimeter of (a). Each piece has a perimeter of $2 \times (6 + 2) = 16$ cm, so the two pieces have 32 cm of boundary between them. Where the pieces touch, a 2 cm side of each piece is now inside the figure, and is not part of its boundary. So

Perimeter of (a) $= 16 + 16 - 2 - 2 = 28$ cm

When two pieces are joined, the sides that touch are no longer on the boundary.
