6.2Area

The amount of surface that a closed figure covers is called its area. We measure area by counting how many unit squares cover the figure. A square of side 1 cm has an area of 1 square centimetre, written 1 sq cm.

In earlier classes you found the area of a rectangle on squared paper. The rectangle in Fig. 6.9 is covered by 3 rows of 4 unit squares. That is $3 \times 4 = 12$ unit squares, so its area is 12 sq cm.

Area of a rectangle and a square

Area of a rectangle $= \text{length} \times \text{width}$

Area of a square $= \text{side} \times \text{side}$

Example 3

A floor is 5 m long and 4 m wide. A square carpet of side 3 m is laid on the floor. What area of the floor is not covered by the carpet?

Solution.

Step 1 Area of the floor $= 5\text{ m} \times 4\text{ m} = 20\text{ sq m}$ length times width
Step 2 Area of the carpet $= 3\text{ m} \times 3\text{ m} = 9\text{ sq m}$ side times side
Step 3 Area not covered $= 20\text{ sq m} - 9\text{ sq m} = 11\text{ sq m}$ whole take away part
Answer 11 sq m of the floor is not carpeted.

To find the area of a part, you can often find the area of the whole and take away what you do not want.
