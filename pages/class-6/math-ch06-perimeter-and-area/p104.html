Example 4

Two squares of side 5 cm are joined along one whole side. Which statements are correct?

  1. The new figure has an area of 50 sq cm.
  2. The new figure has a perimeter of 40 cm.
  3. The new figure has a perimeter of 30 cm.
  4. The new figure is a rectangle.

Solution.

Step 1The joined figure is a rectangle 10 cm by 5 cm. Its area is 10 × 5 = 50 sq cm.
Step 2Its perimeter is 2 × (10 + 5) = 30 cm. The 5 cm side where the squares meet is inside, so it is not counted.
CheckThe two squares have perimeters of 20 cm each, 40 cm in all. Taking away the two hidden 5 cm sides leaves 40 − 10 = 30 cm.
