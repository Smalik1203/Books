Type 4 · Area of rectangles and squares

Example 5

A wall is 5 m long and 3 m high. It has a window 2 m by 1 m and a door 1 m by 2 m. Painting costs ₹30 for each sq m. What does it cost to paint the wall?

Solution.

Step 1 wall $= 5 \times 3 = 15$ sq m
Step 2 window $= 2 \times 1 = 2$ sq m, door $= 1 \times 2 = 2$ sq m
Step 3 to be painted $= 15 - 2 - 2 = 11$ sq m whole take away parts
Step 4 cost $= 11 \times 30 = 330$ rupees
Answer ₹330
Example 6

How many square tiles of side 25 cm are needed to cover a floor 3 m long and 2 m wide?

Solution.

Step 1 3 m $= 300$ cm and 2 m $= 200$ cm same unit first
Step 2 along the length, $300 \div 25 = 12$ tiles
Step 3 along the width, $200 \div 25 = 8$ tiles
Step 4 $12 \times 8 = 96$ tiles rows times columns
Answer 96 tiles

Type 5 · Areas made of pieces

Example 7

An L-shaped room is a rectangle 6 m by 5 m with a rectangle 2 m by 3 m cut away from one corner. Find the area and the perimeter of the room.

Solution.

Step 1 area $= 6 \times 5 - 2 \times 3 = 30 - 6 = 24$ sq m whole take away corner
Step 2 the cut takes 2 m and 3 m off two outer sides, and adds two new sides of 2 m and 3 m
Step 3 perimeter $= 2 \times (6 + 5) = 22$ m same as the rectangle
Answer area 24 sq m; perimeter 22 m

Cutting the corner away made the area smaller, but it did not change the perimeter.
