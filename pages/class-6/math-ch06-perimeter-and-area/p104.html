Example 5 · Single correct

A rectangular floor 6 m by 4 m is covered exactly by square tiles of side 50 cm. How many tiles are needed?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 24
  2. 48
  3. 96
  4. 192

Solution.

Step 150 cm is half a metre, so 12 tiles fit along the length and 8 along the breadth.
Step 212 × 8 = 96 tiles.
Answer(c) 96
Example 6 · Single correct

A diagonal cuts a rectangle of length 15 cm and breadth 8 cm into two triangles. What is the area of either triangle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 60 cm²
  2. 120 cm²
  3. 46 cm²
  4. 30 cm²

Solution.

Step 1The rectangle has area 15 × 8 = 120 cm².
Step 2The diagonal makes two equal-area triangles, each of area 120 ÷ 2 = 60 cm².
Answer(a) 60 cm²
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which rectangles have perimeter 30 cm?

Select all correct options.

  1. 10 cm by 5 cm

  3. 12 cm by 3 cm
  4. 8 cm by 8 cm

Solution.

Step 1A perimeter of 30 cm requires length + breadth = 15 cm.
