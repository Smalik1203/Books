Area maze puzzles

In an area maze, a figure is made of rectangles. Some areas and some side lengths are given. Use them to find the missing value. You will not need to find every length in the figure.

Example 5

A rectangle is cut into four smaller rectangles by one line across and one line down. The top two have areas 8 sq cm and 12 sq cm. The bottom-left one has area 14 sq cm. Find the area of the bottom-right one.

The top two rectangles have the same height. The right one has $12 \div 8 = 1\frac{1}{2}$ times the area, so it is $1\frac{1}{2}$ times as wide. The bottom two rectangles have the same two widths, and the same height as each other.

Bottom-right area $= 1\frac{1}{2} \times 14\text{ sq cm} = 21\text{ sq cm}$

We found the area without finding a single length.

In some puzzles a length is missing instead of an area. Then work the other way: divide an area by a side you know, to find the side you do not know.

Example 6

A rectangle of area 24 sq cm is 6 cm long. A second rectangle shares its shorter side and has an area of 20 sq cm. How long is the second rectangle?

Shorter side of the first $= 24 \div 6 = 4$ cm

Length of the second $= 20 \div 4 = 5$ cm

The second rectangle is 5 cm long.
