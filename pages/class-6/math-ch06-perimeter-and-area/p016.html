House plans

Fig. 6.20 is the plan of Charan’s house. The house fills a rectangular plot. Some measurements are given, and some are missing. What do you notice about the plan?

A plan is a drawing of a house seen from above, drawn to scale. Each room is drawn as a rectangle. Two rooms side by side share a wall, so a length you know for one room often gives you a length of the room next to it.

These plans measure length in feet, written ft. One foot is about 30 cm, the length of a long school ruler, so a room 10 ft long is about 3 m long. Areas on the plans are in square feet, written sq ft.
