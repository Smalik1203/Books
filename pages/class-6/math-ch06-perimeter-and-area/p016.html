Making it more or less

Look at the two figures in Fig. 6.18. How are they alike, and how are they different?

Both figures are made of 9 unit squares, so both have an area of 9 square units. But their perimeters are different. The first figure has a perimeter of 12 units and the second has a perimeter of 20 units.

Make other figures with 9 unit squares. Each square must share at least one whole side with another square. All the squares must make one figure with no holes in it.
