Example 9

A hall is 12 m long and 9 m wide. A carpet 10 m by 7 m covers the middle of its floor.

  1. What is the area of the carpet?
    1. 70 sq m
    2. 34 sq m
    3. 108 sq m
    4. 17 sq m
  2. Numerical answer. Find the area of the bare floor, in square metres.

Solution.

Step 1Area of the carpet = 10 × 7 = 70 sq m.
Step 2Area of the hall = 12 × 9 = 108 sq m, so the bare floor is 108 − 70 = 38 sq m.
Answer(i) (a) 70 sq m; (ii) 38
