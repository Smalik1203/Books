Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) Area of a 7 cm square(1) 26 cm

(R) Area of a 9 cm by 4 cm rectangle(3) 36 cm²
(S) Perimeter of a 9 cm by 4 cm rectangle(4) 28 cm

Each entry has exactly one match

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1

Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) Side of a square of area 81 cm²(1) 7 cm
(Q) Breadth of a rectangle of area 48 cm² and length 8 cm(2) 11 cm
(R) Side of a regular pentagon of perimeter 35 cm(3) 9 cm
(S) Length of a rectangle of perimeter 30 cm and breadth 4 cm(4) 6 cm

Each entry has exactly one match

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The square’s side is 9 cm because 9 × 9 = 81. The breadth is 48 ÷ 8 = 6 cm.
Step 2The pentagon’s side is 35 ÷ 5 = 7 cm. The last length is 30 ÷ 2 − 4 = 11 cm.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
