Two further components to consider are dietary fibre and water. Water is an essential nutrient even though it supplies no energy.

Dietary fibre, or roughage, comes mainly from plant foods such as whole grains, pulses, fruit and vegetables. Our own digestive enzymes do not break it down, although gut microbes can break down some fibre. Fibre helps bowel function; constipation can have several causes.

Water dissolves and transports substances, helps remove waste in urine and helps regulate temperature through sweating and evaporation.

Needs vary with activity and weather.

Water is a nutrient, not a source of food energy.
