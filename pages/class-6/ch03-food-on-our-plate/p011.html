Fats

How It Works

Ghee and cooking oils contain fats. Fat supplies about twice as much energy per gram as carbohydrate. The body can use fat for energy or store it; it does not automatically store all dietary fat. Stored fat also cushions organs and reduces heat loss. These functions do not establish a special need for laddoos in winter.

Fats come from plants and from animals. Plant sources include groundnut, walnut, cashew, almond, pistachio, coconut, sesame seed, pumpkin seed, sunflower seed, soya bean and the oils pressed from them. Animal sources include butter, ghee, curd and milk.
