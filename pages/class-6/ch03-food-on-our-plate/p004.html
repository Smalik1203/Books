Activity 3.2
1.

Choose four states of India. Include your own.

2.

For each one, find out: which crops are grown there, which dishes are traditionally eaten, and what people commonly drink.

3.

Use your library, the internet, and — best of all — people. Ask a classmate whose family comes from another state. Ask a neighbour.

4.

Record your findings in your notebook using the headings in Table 3.2. The printed rows are examples.
