Your diaries may share ingredients, but check rather than assume this. A food absent from four diaries may appear in another household. Four diaries cannot represent the whole class or region.

So even inside a single classroom, food is not one thing. It varies. The next question is why.

3.1.1 Food varies from place to place

A traveller eating in Amritsar, Bengaluru and Imphal could find different dishes and shared ingredients. A regional label does not describe every household. Compare examples before generalising.

That is not an accident, and it is not only a matter of taste. Let us gather the evidence first and explain it afterwards.

Activity 3.2
1.

Choose four states of India. Include your own.

2.

For each one, find out: which crops are grown there, which dishes are traditionally eaten, and what people commonly drink.

3.
