3.6 What Is a Balanced Diet?

Does everyone need the same food in the same amounts? Clearly not. A twelve-year-old is still growing and needs more protein for her size than her grandfather does. A farmhand doing heavy work all day needs more energy than someone at a desk. A person recovering from illness has different needs again. Requirements shift with age, sex, level of physical activity, state of health and way of life. A balanced diet is one that supplies all the nutrients a particular person needs, in the right amounts, together with enough roughage and water. Note the phrase a particular person. There is no single balanced diet for everybody — the idea only means anything once you say who is eating.
