Salt without a factory. The Apatani people of Arunachal Pradesh traditionally make a salt called tapyo from plant ash rather than from seawater or rock. Find out how, and why a community would develop such a method. Write a short account with pictures.

Wild food near you. In many parts of India people gather uncultivated greens, fruit and mushrooms — the ranbhajis of Maharashtra are one example. find out whether anything is gathered wild near your town. Ask an older person; they will know things the internet does not.

Write to your principal. With your classmates, list the junk foods most often eaten in your school. Then write a joint letter to your principal proposing which to remove from the canteen and what to offer instead. Cost your alternatives — a proposal nobody can afford will be refused.
