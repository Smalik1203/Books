Nutrients: sources, roles and deficiency
NutrientSources and roleDeficiency: possible signs, not a diagnosis
Iodine Iodised salt, seafood, dairy and eggs; amounts vary
What it does: Needed to make thyroid hormones, supporting growth and development 		Deficiency disorder: Goitre
Possible signs; not a diagnosis: Swelling at the front of the neck
Iron Pulses, leafy greens, meat and iron-fortified foods; absorption varies
What it does: Part of haemoglobin, which carries oxygen in blood 		Deficiency disorder: Anaemia
Possible signs; not a diagnosis: Weakness; shortness of breath
