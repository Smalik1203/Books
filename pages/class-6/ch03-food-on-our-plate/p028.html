Activity 3.6

Your teacher will demonstrate this activity. Read the precautions before anything else.

The teacher should include egg white as a positive control and water as a negative control. Keep sample amounts and waiting times alike. Record observations before interpreting them.

You will need: the same food samples; pestle and mortar; clean test tubes; two droppers; copper sulfate solution; caustic soda solution.

1.

Grind each food sample into a paste or powder with the pestle and mortar.

2.

Put about half a teaspoon of each into a separate clean test tube.

3.

Add two or three teaspoons of water to each tube and shake well.

4.

Using one dropper, add two drops of copper sulfate solution to each tube.

5.

Using a different dropper, add ten drops of caustic soda solution to each tube.

6.

Shake, then leave the tubes undisturbed for a few minutes.
