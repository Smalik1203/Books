3.8 Millets: The Grains That Came Back

Jowar, bajra, ragi, sanwa, kodo, kutki — these are millets, and they are among the oldest cultivated grains in India. For centuries they were ordinary food across large parts of the country. Then, within a few decades, they largely disappeared from urban plates, pushed aside by polished rice and refined wheat.
