Now put the two ideas together.

Everything on your plate carries months of a farmer's work and a long journey behind it. Food left uneaten on the plate throws all of that away — the water, the labour, the fuel, the months.

The remedy is not complicated and needs no equipment. Take what you will eat. Go back for more if you are still hungry. Finish what you have taken.
