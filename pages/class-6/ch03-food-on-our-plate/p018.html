Fig. 3.5 — Nutrient functions and possible deficiency signs; symptoms alone do not diagnose a deficiency

Nutrients: sources, roles and deficiency
NutrientSources and roleDeficiency: possible signs, not a diagnosis
Vitamin A Papaya, carrot, mango, milk
What it does: Keeps eyes and skin healthy 		Deficiency disorder: Loss of vision
Possible signs; not a diagnosis: Poor vision; loss of vision in dim light (night blindness); in severe cases, complete loss of vision
Vitamin B₁ Legumes, nuts, whole grains, seeds, milk products
What it does: Helps release energy from food and supports nerve function 		Deficiency disorder: Beriberi
Possible signs; not a diagnosis: Swelling, tingling or burning in the feet and hands; difficulty in breathing
Vitamin C Amla, guava, green chilli, orange, lemon
What it does: Needed to make collagen for connective tissues and wound healing 		Deficiency disorder: Scurvy
Possible signs; not a diagnosis: Bleeding gums; wounds heal slowly
