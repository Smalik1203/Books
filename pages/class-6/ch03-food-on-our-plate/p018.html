Nutrients: sources, roles and deficiency
NutrientSources and roleDeficiency: possible signs, not a diagnosis
Vitamin D Skin makes it in sunlight; oily fish, egg yolk, fortified milk
What it does: Helps the body absorb calcium for bones and teeth 		Deficiency disorder: Rickets
Possible signs; not a diagnosis: Soft, bent bones
Calcium Milk, curd, cheese, paneer; calcium-fortified soya milk
What it does: Keeps bones and teeth strong 		Deficiency disorder: Poor bone mineralisation
Possible signs; not a diagnosis: Low intake over time can weaken bones; tooth decay has other causes
