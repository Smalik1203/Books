3.7 Reading the Packet

Two snacks, both sold in a packet. Compare what is printed on them.

Table 3.4 · Nutritional information per 100 g
— Potato wafers Roasted chana
Energy 536 kcal 355 kcal
Fat 35.0 g 6.26 g
Carbohydrate 53.0 g 58.58 g
Protein 7.0 g 18.64 g
Dietary fibre 4.8 g 16.8 g

For these example labels, chana has more protein and fibre and less fat per 100 g. That supports a comparison on those measures, not a universal ranking of foods. Portion size, ingredients and the rest of the diet also matter. Foods high in free sugars, salt or unhealthy fats can displace needed nutrients when eaten frequently.
