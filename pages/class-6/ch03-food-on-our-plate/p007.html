3.1.2 Food also varies from time to time

Food varies across the map. Does it also vary across the years? Interview an older person about dishes, fuel and cooking tools. Record continuity as well as change; one memory cannot represent every household.

Activity 3.3
1.

Choose someone at least fifty years older than you — a grandparent, a neighbour, a retired teacher.

2.

Prepare your questions before you meet them. Write them down. Good starting points: What did you eat as a child that you still eat today? What did you eat then that you never eat now? How was the food cooked, and on what? How long did the cooking take? What changed, and when did it change?

3.

Listen more than you talk. Write their answers in their words, not yours.

4.

Bring your notes to class and pool them with your classmates'.
