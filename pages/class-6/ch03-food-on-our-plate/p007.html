Partly because transport improved. Roads, railways and cold storage mean that a fruit grown in Himachal can be sold in Chennai. Foods that were once purely local became available everywhere, and cuisines began to borrow from each other.

Television, phones and the internet can spread recipes beyond a region. To test a claim about how quickly one dish spread, we would need dated evidence from several places.

Change of this kind brings real gains. Cooking takes less time — which matters most to the women who have historically done most of it. Fuel-efficient stoves mean less smoke indoors, and less smoke means healthier lungs.

Changes can also reduce the use of familiar grains or cooking skills. Whether a replacement is more nourishing depends on its ingredients, preparation and quantity. Compare examples rather than assuming that older or newer food is better.

Smoke in the kitchen A wood or dung chulha burning indoors fills a kitchen with fine smoke. Breathing that smoke for years damages the lungs and the heart, and the person standing closest to the stove breathes the most of it. This is one reason governments have worked to bring clean cooking fuel to rural households: a better stove is a health measure, not just a convenience.

3.2 What Is Food Made Of?

Aarav misses breakfast and feels hungry during class. Ira shares a banana at break. Later he says he feels better.

Does that observation prove what caused the change? Rest, water and other factors may matter too. We need knowledge of digestion to explain what food can supply; this story is not a blood-sugar test.

Something in food, then, is not the same as food. When we eat a banana we are not eating "banana" as far as the body is concerned. We are eating a mixture of substances, each of which the body uses for a different job. These substances are the components of food.

Different foods carry different components in different amounts. That is why no single food keeps a person well, and why the mixture on the plate matters as much as the items on it.
