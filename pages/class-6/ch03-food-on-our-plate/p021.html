3.4 Cooking, Roughage and Water

Have you noticed that a raw carrot snaps and a boiled one bends? That spinach loses its bright green in the pan?

Heat changes food, and not only in ways we can see. Some nutrients — Vitamin C among them — are partly destroyed by high heat. Washing vegetables and fruit after cutting or peeling them washes some vitamins away as well.

Two sensible conclusions follow. First, include some fruit and raw vegetables in your diet so that not everything you eat has been through a hot pan. Second, wash produce before you cut it, not after.

But do wash it. Thoroughly, every time. The vitamins lost to washing are a small price against the dirt, pesticide residue and germs that washing removes.
