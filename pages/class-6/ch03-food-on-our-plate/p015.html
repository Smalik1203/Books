Nutrients: sources, roles and deficiency
NutrientSources and roleDeficiency: possible signs, not a diagnosis
Calcium
  • Milk, curd, cheese, paneer; calcium-fortified soya milk
  • What it does: Keeps bones and teeth strong
  • Deficiency disorder: Poor bone mineralisation
  • Possible signs; not a diagnosis: Low intake over time can weaken bones; tooth decay has other causes
Iodine
  • Iodised salt, seafood, dairy and eggs; amounts vary
  • What it does: Needed to make thyroid hormones, supporting growth and development
  • Deficiency disorder: Goitre
  • Possible signs; not a diagnosis: Swelling at the front of the neck
Iron
  • Pulses, leafy greens, meat and iron-fortified foods; absorption varies
  • What it does: Part of haemoglobin, which carries oxygen in blood
  • Deficiency disorder: Anaemia
  • Possible signs; not a diagnosis: Weakness; shortness of breath
