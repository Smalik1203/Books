Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which of these are geometric progressions?

Select all correct options.

  1. $1, -2, 4, -8, \ldots$
  2. $3, 6, 12, 20, \ldots$
  3. $0.1, 0.01, 0.001, \ldots$
  4. $2, 4, 6, 8, \ldots$

Solution.

Step 1The ratios are $-2$ throughout and $0.1$ throughout.
Step 2In (b) the ratios are $2, 2, \frac{5}{3}$, and in (d) $2, \frac{3}{2}, \frac{4}{3}$: not constant.
Answer(a), (c)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

A sequence has $t_n = 7 - 3n$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its first term is 4.
  2. It is an AP with common difference $-3$.
  3. $t_{10} = -23$
  4. 0 is one of its terms.

Solution.

Step 1$t_1 = 7 - 3 = 4$, and each term is 3 less than the one before; $t_{10} = 7 - 30 = -23$.
Step 2$7 - 3n = 0$ gives $n = \frac{7}{3}$, not a whole number, so 0 is not a term.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

A GP has first term 3 and common ratio 2. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its 5th term is 48.
  2. Its $n$th term is $3 \times 2^{n}$.
  3. 96 is one of its terms.
  4. 384 is one of its terms.

Solution.

Step 1$t_n = 3 \times 2^{n-1}$: $t_5 = 3 \times 16 = 48$, $t_6 = 96$ and $t_8 = 384$.
Step 2$3 \times 2^{n}$ would make the first term 6, not 3.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
