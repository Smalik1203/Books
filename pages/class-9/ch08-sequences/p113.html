Assertion and reason

In Questions 25 to 28, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): The sequence $2, 4, 8, 16, \ldots$ is an arithmetic progression.

    Reason (R): Each of its terms is twice the one before it.

  1. Assertion (A): An AP with common difference $-3$ must eventually have negative terms.

    Reason (R): Subtracting a fixed positive amount over and over takes any number below zero in the end.

  1. Assertion (A): The sequence $5, 5, 5, \ldots$ is both an AP and a GP.

    Reason (R): Its common difference is $0$ and its common ratio is $1$.

  1. Assertion (A): $1 + 2 + 3 + \cdots + 20 = 210$.

    Reason (R): The sum of any AP of $n$ terms is $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$.
