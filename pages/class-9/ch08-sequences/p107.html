Example 5 · Single correct

A sequence is given by $t_1 = 2$ and $t_n = 3t_{n-1} - 1$ for $n \geq 2$. What is $t_4$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 41
  2. 40
  3. 122
  4. 14

Solution.

Step 1$t_2 = 3 \times 2 - 1 = 5$ and $t_3 = 3 \times 5 - 1 = 14$.
Step 2$t_4 = 3 \times 14 - 1 = 41$.
Answer(a) 41
Example 6 · Single correct

How many terms has the AP $12, 17, 22, \ldots, 247$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 47
  2. 48
  3. 49
  4. 235

Solution.

Step 1From 12 to 247 is $247 - 12 = 235$, which is $235 \div 5 = 47$ steps of 5.
Step 247 steps visit $47 + 1 = 48$ terms.
Answer(b) 48
