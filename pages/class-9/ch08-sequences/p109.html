Example 11 · Numerical answer

Find the sum of all the multiples of 6 between 1 and 100.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The multiples run $6, 12, \ldots, 96$: an AP of $96 \div 6 = 16$ terms.
Step 2Sum $= \frac{16}{2} \times (6 + 96) = 8 \times 102 = 816$.

Example 13 · Numerical answer

The 5th term of an AP is 23 and its 12th term is 58. Find its 20th term.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Seven steps take 23 to 58, so $7d = 35$ and $d = 5$; then $a = 23 - 4 \times 5 = 3$.
Step 2$t_{20} = 3 + 19 \times 5 = 98$.
Answer98
