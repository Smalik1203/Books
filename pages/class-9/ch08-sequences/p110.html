Example 14 · Matching

Match each rule with its fifth term. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $t_n = 2n + 3$(1) 31
(Q) $t_n = n^2 - 1$(2) 81
(R) $t_1 = 1$, $t_n = 2t_{n-1} + 1$(3) 13
(S) $t_n = 3^{n-1}$(4) 24

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Put $n = 5$: $2 \times 5 + 3 = 13$, $25 - 1 = 24$ and $3^4 = 81$.
Step 2The recursion gives $1, 3, 7, 15, 31$.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $1 + 2 + \cdots + 30$(1) 420
(Q) $2 + 4 + \cdots + 40$(2) 225
(R) $1 + 3 + \cdots + 29$(3) 275
(S) $5 + 10 + \cdots + 50$(4) 465

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–2, R–1, S–3
  2. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3
  3. P–4, Q–1, R–2, S–3
  4. P–3, Q–1, R–2, S–4

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{30 \times 31}{2} = 465$, and $2 + 4 + \cdots + 40$ is twice $1 + \cdots + 20$: $2 \times 210 = 420$.
Step 2The first 15 odd numbers add to $15^2 = 225$, and $5 + \cdots + 50$ is 5 times $1 + \cdots + 10$: 275.
Answer(c) P–4, Q–1, R–2, S–3
