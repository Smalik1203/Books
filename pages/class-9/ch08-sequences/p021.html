Exercise Set 8.6
  1. Find the $10$th and the $n$th terms of the GP $5, 25, 125, \ldots$
  1. The $8$th term of a GP with common ratio $2$ is $192$. Find its $12$th term.
  1. Which term of the GP $2, 6, 18, \ldots$ is $4374$? Write both the explicit and the recursive rule for its $n$th term.
  1. Check whether each of these is a GP, and if so find its $n$th term.
    1. $2,\ 10,\ 50,\ 250,\ \ldots$
    2. $4,\ 2,\ 1,\ \tfrac12,\ \ldots$
    3. $3,\ 6,\ 9,\ 12,\ \ldots$
  1. A ball is dropped from $80$ metres and bounces back to $60\%$ of the height it fell from each time.
    1. What height does it reach after the $5$th bounce?
    2. What total vertical distance has it travelled by the time it hits the ground for the $6$th time?
  1. A sequence is given by $t_1 = 2$ and $t_{n+1} = 3t_n - 2$ for $n \geq 1$. Which term of the sequence is $730$?
  1. Find the first term of an AP whose $11$th term is $38$ and whose $16$th term is $73$.
  2. Determine the AP whose third term is $16$ and whose $7$th term exceeds the $5$th by $12$.
  3. How many three-digit numbers are divisible by $7$?

    They form an AP. Find the smallest and the largest of them.

  4. How many multiples of $4$ lie between $10$ and $250$?
  5. The number of bacteria in a culture doubles every hour. If there were $30$ to begin with, how many are there after $2$ hours, after $4$ hours, and after $n$ hours?
  6. The sum of the $4$th and $8$th terms of an AP is $24$, and the sum of the $6$th and $10$th is $44$. Find its first three terms.
  7. Find the smallest $n$ for which the sum of the first $n$ natural numbers is greater than $1000$.
  8. Find all the ways of writing $100$ as a sum of consecutive natural numbers.
