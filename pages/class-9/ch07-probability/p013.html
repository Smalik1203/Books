Exercise Set 7.2
  1. A large bag holds sweets of several colours. Priya takes a random sample of 30 and finds 12 green, 9 yellow, 6 red and 3 orange.
    1. What is the probability that a sweet picked from the sample is green?
    2. The bag holds 600 sweets in all. Estimate how many are yellow.
  1. A survey of a random sample of 40 students asks which club they prefer. Sixteen say Sports, twelve say Arts, eight say Science and four say Music.
    1. What is the probability that a student from the sample prefers the Arts Club?
    2. The school has 800 students. Estimate how many prefer the Sports Club.
  1. Toss a coin 20 times and record each result.
    1. How many heads did you get? How many tails?
    2. Write the experimental probability of heads from your 20 tosses.
    3. How far is it from $\tfrac12$? Would you expect it to be closer or further after 200 tosses?
  1. Toss a paper cup 100 times and record whether it lands on its base, on its top, or on its side. Write the experimental probability of each. Could you have predicted these by reasoning alone, as you can for a die?
  1. What is the probability of an even number on a roll of a fair six-sided die? Is your answer experimental or theoretical, and how do you know?
  1. Two students each toss the same coin 50 times. One gets 22 heads, the other 31. One of them says the coin must be biased. What would you say?
