  1. A die is rolled 300 times. The faces 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 come up 45, 54, 48, 51, 57 and 45 times.
    1. Find the experimental probability of each face, as a decimal.
    2. Compare each with the theoretical probability for a fair die. Which face is furthest from it?
    3. Do these results give a good reason to think the die is biased? Explain.
    4. If the die is fair, about how many 5s would you expect in 1200 rolls?
