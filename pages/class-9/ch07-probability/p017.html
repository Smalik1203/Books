A tree also grows in an orderly way. Toss the coin a third time and every tip sprouts two more, giving eight outcomes; a fourth toss gives sixteen. Two stages of two choices make $2 \times 2$, and three make $2 \times 2 \times 2$. The tree is showing you a multiplication.
