Example 14

Two dice are rolled. Find the probability that they show different numbers.

Solution.

Step 1 $6 \times 6 = 36$ equally likely pairs the first die, then the second
Step 2 the same number on both: $(1, 1), (2, 2), \ldots, (6, 6)$, which is 6 pairs the opposite is easier
Step 3 $P(\text{same}) = \tfrac{6}{36} = \tfrac16$
Step 4 $P(\text{different}) = 1 - \tfrac16 = \tfrac56$ $P(\text{not } E) = 1 - P(E)$
Answer $\tfrac56$
Check $36 - 6 = 30$ pairs differ, and $\tfrac{30}{36} = \tfrac56$
Example 15

The probability that a certain train is late is $0.15$. Over 60 journeys, the number of times it can be expected to be on time is about

  1. $9$
  2. $45$
  3. $51$
  4. $85$

Solution.

Step 1 $1 - 0.15 = 0.85$ on time is not late
Step 2 $0.85 \times 60 = 51$ rate times journeys
Answer (c)

Option (a) is $0.15 \times 60 = 9$, the late journeys. Option (b) is $60 - 15 = 45$, which takes the percentage away as if it were a count. Option (d) is $0.85 \times 100 = 85$, the percentage and not the number of journeys.
