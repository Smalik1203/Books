Case-based questions

  1. A school canteen wants to know which snack to make most of. It asks a random sample of 120 of the school's 900 students for their favourite.

    SnackStudents
    Samosa42
    Idli30
    Sandwich36
    Poha12
    1. Find the probability that a student in the sample prefers idli.
    2. Find the probability that a student in the sample does not prefer samosa.
    3. Estimate how many of the 900 students prefer sandwiches.
    4. Why does it matter that the 120 were chosen at random?

  1. At a school fair, a player draws one ball from a bag without looking and wins a prize if it is green. The bag holds these balls.

    ColourBalls
    Green4
    Yellow6
    White10
    1. Find the probability of winning a prize.
    2. Find the probability of not winning.
    3. The game is played 200 times, with the ball put back each time. About how many prizes will be given?
    4. How many green balls must be added to the bag to make the probability of winning $\tfrac12$?
