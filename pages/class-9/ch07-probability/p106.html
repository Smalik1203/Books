Example 3 · Single correct

A basketball player takes 80 shots and scores with 52. What is the experimental probability of scoring?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 0.52
  2. 0.8
  3. 0.65
  4. 0.35

Solution.

Step 1Experimental probability $= \frac{\text{successes}}{\text{trials}} = \frac{52}{80}$.
Step 2$\frac{52}{80} = \frac{13}{20} = 0.65$.
Answer(c) 0.65
Example 4 · Single correct

A letter is picked at random from the word ASSESSMENT. What is the probability that it is an S?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 0.1
  2. 0.3
  3. 0.5
  4. 0.4

Solution.

Step 1ASSESSMENT has 10 letters, and 4 of them are S.
Step 2$\frac{4}{10} = 0.4$.
Answer(d) 0.4
Example 5 · Single correct

Cards numbered 1 to 25 are shuffled, and one is drawn. What is the probability that its number is prime?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{9}{25}$
  2. $\frac{2}{5}$
  3. $\frac{8}{25}$
  4. $\frac{1}{5}$

Solution.

Step 1The primes up to 25 are 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19 and 23: nine of them.
Step 2The probability is $\frac{9}{25}$.
Answer(a) $\frac{9}{25}$
