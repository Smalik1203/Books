Example 12

A coin is tossed and a spinner with three equal sectors, coloured red, blue and green, is spun. Write the sample space, and find the probability of a head together with a colour that is not red.

Solution.

Step 1 $S = \{\text{H-red}, \text{H-blue}, \text{H-green}, \text{T-red}, \text{T-blue}, \text{T-green}\}$ every pairing
Step 2 $n(S) = 2 \times 3 = 6$ two stages multiply
Step 3 favourable: H-blue and H-green, 2 outcomes
Answer $\tfrac26 = \tfrac13$
Example 13

Spinner A has two equal sectors marked 1 and 2. Spinner B has three equal sectors marked 1, 2 and 3. Both are spun and the two numbers added. Draw a tree diagram, and find the probability that the sum is 3, and the probability that the sum is even.

Solution.

Step 1 $2 \times 3 = 6$ equally likely paths Fig. 7B.1
Step 2 the sums along the paths are 2, 3, 4, 3, 4 and 5 read each path
Step 3 a sum of 3 on two paths, so $\tfrac26 = \tfrac13$
Step 4 an even sum (2, 4, 4) on three paths, so $\tfrac36 = \tfrac12$
Answer $P(\text{sum is } 3) = \tfrac13$ and $P(\text{sum is even}) = \tfrac12$

The four possible sums 2, 3, 4 and 5 are not equally likely, since 3 and 4 each come two ways. Dividing by 4 would repeat the mistake of the two dice in Stage 1.
