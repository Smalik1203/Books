Example 12 · Numerical answer

Two dice are rolled. In how many of the 36 equally likely outcomes do they show different numbers?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The dice show the same number in 6 outcomes, $(1, 1)$ to $(6, 6)$.
Step 2$36 - 6 = 30$ outcomes show different numbers.
Answer30
Example 13 · Numerical answer

The probability that a train is late is 0.15. About how many times would you expect it to be late in 60 journeys?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Expected number $=$ probability $\times$ number of trials.
Step 2$0.15 \times 60 = 9$.
Answer9
