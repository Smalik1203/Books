Example 15 · Matching

A card is drawn from cards numbered 1 to 20. Match each event with its probability. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) A multiple of 5(1) $\frac{1}{4}$
(Q) A prime(2) $\frac{2}{5}$
(R) An even number(3) $\frac{1}{2}$
(S) A number greater than 15(4) $\frac{1}{5}$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  3. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–2, R–3, S–4

Solution.

Step 1Of the 20 cards, 4 are multiples of 5, 8 are prime (2 to 19), 10 are even and 5 are above 15.
Answer(b) P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
