Short answer
  1. A bag holds red, white and blue balls. The probability of drawing a red one is $\tfrac14$ and of a white one $\tfrac13$, and there are 10 blue balls. How many balls of each colour are there?
  1. Two spinners each have three equal sectors marked 1, 2 and 3. Both are spun and the two numbers multiplied. List the sample space, and find the probability that the product is even.
  1. In a random sample of 250 households in a town, 90 keep a pet. The town has 8000 households. Estimate how many keep a pet and how many do not.
  1. A card is drawn at random from cards numbered 1 to 40. Write each event as a set and find its probability.
    1. The number is a perfect square.
    2. The number is a multiple of 10.
    3. The number is a perfect square or a multiple of 10.
Long answer
  1. Two dice are rolled and the numbers added.
    1. Make a table of all the sums. How many entries does it have?
    2. Find the probability that the sum is 6.
    3. Find the probability that the sum is a prime number.
    4. Find the probability that the sum is a multiple of 4.
  1. A die is rolled 300 times. The faces 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 come up 45, 54, 48, 51, 57 and 45 times.
    1. Find the experimental probability of each face, as a decimal.
    2. Compare each with the theoretical probability for a fair die. Which face is furthest from it?
    3. Do these results give a good reason to think the die is biased? Explain.
    4. If the die is fair, about how many 5s would you expect in 1200 rolls?
