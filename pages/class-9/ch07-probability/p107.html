Example 6 · Single correct

A jar holds 24 sweets, some red and the rest green. The probability of picking a red one is $\frac{3}{8}$. How many are green?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 9
  2. 15
  3. 16
  4. 8

Solution.

Step 1Red sweets $= \frac{3}{8} \times 24 = 9$.
Step 2Green sweets $= 24 - 9 = 15$.
Answer(b) 15
Example 7 · Multiple correct

A spinner has ten equal sectors numbered 1 to 10. $A$ is "a multiple of 3" and $B$ is "an even number". Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $P(A) = \frac{3}{10}$
  2. $P(B) = \frac{1}{2}$
  3. $P(A \text{ and } B) = \frac{1}{10}$
  4. $P(A \text{ or } B) = \frac{4}{5}$

Solution.

Step 1$A = \{3, 6, 9\}$ and $B = \{2, 4, 6, 8, 10\}$; only 6 is in both.
Step 2$A$ or $B$ is $\{2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10\}$, 7 numbers: $\frac{7}{10}$, not $\frac{4}{5}$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

  2. 1.2
  3. $45\%$

Solution.

Step 21.2 is more than 1 and $-0.1$ is negative.
