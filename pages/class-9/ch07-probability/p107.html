Problem Sets
Three sets of multiple-choice questions, each a little harder than the last.
Set A · One result each
  1. The probability of an impossible event is
    1. $0$
    2. $\tfrac12$
    3. $1$
    4. $-1$
  1. A fair die is rolled once. $P(\text{an odd number})$ is
    1. $\tfrac16$
    2. $\tfrac13$
    3. $\tfrac12$
    4. $\tfrac23$
  1. A coin is tossed 60 times and gives 27 heads. The experimental probability of heads is
    1. $0.27$
    2. $0.45$
    3. $0.5$
    4. $0.55$
  1. Which of these cannot be a probability?
    1. $0$
    2. $0.999$
    3. $\tfrac54$
    4. $\tfrac{7}{8}$
  1. Two coins are tossed together. The sample size $n(S)$ is
    1. $2$
    2. $3$
    3. $4$
    4. $8$
  1. An event has probability $0.7$. The probability it does not happen is
    1. $0.3$
    2. $0.7$
    3. $1.7$
    4. $-0.7$
  1. A letter is chosen from the word CHANCE. $P(\text{C})$ is
    1. $\tfrac16$
    2. $\tfrac13$
    3. $\tfrac12$
    4. $\tfrac23$
  1. The sample space of a single roll of a die has size
    1. $1$
    2. $6$
    3. $12$
    4. $36$
  1. One card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck of 52. $P(\text{a heart})$ is
    1. $\tfrac{1}{52}$
    2. $\tfrac{1}{13}$
    3. $\tfrac14$
    4. $\tfrac12$
