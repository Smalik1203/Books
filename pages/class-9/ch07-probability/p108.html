Example 9 · Multiple correct

A coin is tossed and a spinner with three equal sectors, red, blue and green, is spun. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. There are 6 equally likely outcomes.
  2. $P(\text{head and red}) = \frac{1}{3}$
  3. $P(\text{tail}) = \frac{1}{2}$
  4. $P(\text{red or blue}) = \frac{2}{3}$

Solution.

Step 1Each of 2 faces goes with each of 3 colours: 6 outcomes, so head and red is $\frac{1}{6}$.
Step 2Tails are 3 of the 6 outcomes, and red or blue 4 of the 6.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Asha rolls a die 30 times and gets 9 sixes. Ravi rolls the same die 600 times and gets 102 sixes. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Asha's experimental probability of a six is 0.3.
  2. Ravi's is 0.17.
  3. Ravi's estimate is the more reliable.
  4. The die must be unfair.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{9}{30} = 0.3$ and $\frac{102}{600} = 0.17$; more trials give a steadier estimate.
Step 20.17 is close to $\frac{1}{6} \approx 0.167$, so nothing shows the die is unfair.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A factory tests a sample of 400 bulbs from a batch of 12 000 and finds 14 faulty. Estimate the number of faulty bulbs in the batch.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The fraction faulty in the sample is $\frac{14}{400}$.
Step 2Estimate: $\frac{14}{400} \times 12\,000 = 420$.
