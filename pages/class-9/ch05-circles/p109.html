Example 10

In Fig. 5B.4, the chords $AC$ and $BD$ of a circle cross at $E$, with $\angle BAC = 30^\circ$ and $\angle AEB = 100^\circ$. Find $\angle BDC$ and $\angle ACD$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle BDC = \angle BAC = 30^\circ$ same segment, chord $BC$
Step 2 in $\triangle ABE$, $\angle ABE = 180^\circ - 30^\circ - 100^\circ = 50^\circ$ angle sum
Step 3 $\angle ABD = \angle ABE = 50^\circ$ $E$ lies on $BD$
Step 4 $\angle ACD = \angle ABD = 50^\circ$ same segment, chord $AD$
Answer $\angle BDC = 30^\circ$ and $\angle ACD = 50^\circ$
Check in $\triangle CDE$, $\angle CED = 100^\circ$ (vertically opposite $\angle AEB$), and $30 + 50 + 100 = 180$
