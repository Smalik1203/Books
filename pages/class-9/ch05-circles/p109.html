B9 · Foundation Test
  1. A chord $30$ cm long lies $8$ cm from the centre. The radius is
    1. $15$ cm
    2. $17$ cm
    3. $19$ cm
    4. $23$ cm
  2. Prove that the perpendicular bisector of a chord passes through the centre of the circle.
  3. In a circle of radius $12$ cm with centre $O$, $\angle AOB$ is $60^\circ$. The chord $AB$ is
    1. $6$ cm
    2. $12$ cm
    3. $12\sqrt{3}$ cm
    4. $24$ cm
  4. A cyclic quadrilateral has $\angle A = 75^\circ$ and $\angle B = 110^\circ$. Find $\angle C$ and $\angle D$, and check your answer against the angle sum of a quadrilateral.
  5. The circumcentre of a right-angled triangle lies
    1. inside the triangle
    2. outside the triangle
    3. at the midpoint of the hypotenuse
    4. at the right-angled vertex
  6. A circle of radius $15$ cm has a chord $18$ cm long. Find its distance from the centre, and then the length of a chord of the same circle lying $9$ cm from the centre.
  7. An arc subtends $140^\circ$ at the centre. Find the angle it subtends at a point of the major arc, and the angle the other arc subtends at a point of the minor arc.
  8. Four points are concyclic when a segment joining two of them subtends, at the other two on the same side of it,
    1. supplementary angles
    2. equal angles
    3. right angles
    4. complementary angles
  9. Show that a rectangle can always be inscribed in a circle, and that a parallelogram which is not a rectangle never can.
  10. Two chords $AB$ and $AC$ of a circle have the same length. Explain why the centre must lie on the bisector of $\angle BAC$.
  11. A chord subtends a right angle at the centre of a circle of radius $8$ cm. Its length is
    1. $8$ cm
    2. $8\sqrt{2}$ cm
    3. $16$ cm
    4. $16\sqrt{2}$ cm
  12. A quadrilateral has angles $70^\circ$, $100^\circ$, $120^\circ$ and $70^\circ$, in that order round the shape. Can it be drawn at all? Can it be cyclic? Give reasons for both answers.
