Example 15 · Matching

In a circle of radius 25 cm, match each chord's distance from the centre with its length in cm. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) 7 cm from the centre(1) 30
(Q) 15 cm from the centre(2) 14
(R) 20 cm from the centre(3) 48
(S) 24 cm from the centre(4) 40

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Half a chord at distance $d$ is $\sqrt{625 - d^2}$: 24, 20, 15 and 7, so the chords are 48, 40, 30 and 14.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
