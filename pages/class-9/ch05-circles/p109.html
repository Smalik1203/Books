The Mixed Test
Everything these ten pages have covered, mixed.

No hints. Work straight through.

  1. A chord $30$ cm long lies $8$ cm from the centre. The radius is
    1. $15$ cm
    2. $17$ cm
    3. $19$ cm
    4. $23$ cm
  2. In a circle of radius $12$ cm, $\angle AOB = 60^\circ$. The chord $AB$ is
    1. $6$ cm
    2. $12$ cm
    3. $12\sqrt{3}$ cm
    4. $24$ cm
  3. The circumcentre of a right-angled triangle lies
    1. inside the triangle
    2. outside the triangle
    3. at the midpoint of the hypotenuse
    4. at the right-angled vertex
  4. Four points are concyclic when a segment joining two of them subtends, at the other two on the same side of it,
    1. supplementary angles
    2. equal angles
    3. right angles
    4. complementary angles
  5. A chord subtends a right angle at the centre of a circle of radius $8$ cm. Its length is
    1. $8$ cm
    2. $8\sqrt{2}$ cm
    3. $16$ cm
    4. $16\sqrt{2}$ cm
  6. $ABCD$ is cyclic and $AB$ is produced to $E$. If $\angle ADC = 104^\circ$, then $\angle CBE$ is
    1. $52^\circ$
    2. $76^\circ$
    3. $104^\circ$
    4. $128^\circ$
  7. In concentric circles of radii $17$ cm and $8$ cm, a chord of the larger touching the smaller at its midpoint is
    1. $15$ cm
    2. $25$ cm
    3. $30$ cm
    4. $34$ cm
  8. A cyclic quadrilateral has $\angle A = 75^\circ$ and $\angle B = 110^\circ$. Find $\angle C$ and $\angle D$, and check them against the angle sum of a quadrilateral.
  9. A circle of radius $15$ cm has a chord $18$ cm long. Find its distance from the centre, then the length of a chord of the same circle lying $9$ cm from the centre.
  10. A quadrilateral has angles $70^\circ$, $100^\circ$, $120^\circ$ and $70^\circ$ in order. Can it be drawn? Can it be cyclic? Give a reason for each answer.
