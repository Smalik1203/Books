Connect the Concepts
Two results of the chapter, one after the other.

Name both results as you go.

  1. A chord of a circle equals its radius. The angle it subtends at a point of the major arc is
    1. $30^\circ$
    2. $60^\circ$
    3. $120^\circ$
    4. $150^\circ$
  2. Two parallel chords of a circle of radius $10$ cm are $16$ cm and $12$ cm long. The distance between them is
    1. $2$ cm
    2. $14$ cm
    3. $2$ cm or $14$ cm
    4. $28$ cm
  3. A triangle has sides $6$ cm, $8$ cm and $10$ cm. The radius of the circle through its three vertices is
    1. $4$ cm
    2. $5$ cm
    3. $6$ cm
    4. $10$ cm
  4. $ABCD$ is cyclic and $AC$ is a diameter. If $\angle BAC = 35^\circ$, then $\angle ADB$ is
    1. $35^\circ$
    2. $45^\circ$
    3. $55^\circ$
    4. $70^\circ$
  5. Chords $AB$ and $CD$ of one circle are equal. Which follows without any further information?
    1. $AB \parallel CD$
    2. They are equidistant from the centre
    3. They meet inside the circle
    4. $\angle AOB + \angle COD = 180^\circ$
  6. Two chords of a circle of radius $25$ cm, of lengths $48$ cm and $14$ cm, lie on opposite sides of the centre. They are apart by
    1. $7$ cm
    2. $17$ cm
    3. $24$ cm
    4. $31$ cm
  7. The midpoint of a $24$ cm chord of a circle of radius $13$ cm is joined to the centre and the line produced to meet the circle. Find the two pieces it is cut into, naming the result that makes the join perpendicular to the chord.
  8. $ABCD$ is cyclic with $AB = AD$. Prove that $CA$ bisects $\angle BCD$, naming each theorem you use.
