Example 4 · Single correct

A circular window has radius 37 cm. A bar 70 cm long is fixed across it, with both ends on the rim. How far is the bar from the centre?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 35 cm
  2. 2 cm
  3. 24 cm
  4. 12 cm

Solution.

Step 1The perpendicular from the centre bisects the bar, making a right triangle with sides 35 cm and the distance, and hypotenuse 37 cm.
Step 2$\sqrt{37^2 - 35^2} = \sqrt{144} = 12$ cm.
Answer(d) 12 cm
Example 5 · Single correct

$A$, $B$ and $C$ lie on a circle with centre $O$, $C$ on the major arc $AB$. If $\angle AOB = 100^\circ$, what is $\angle ACB$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $50^\circ$
  2. $100^\circ$
  3. $130^\circ$
  4. $80^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1The angle an arc subtends at the centre is twice the angle it subtends at any point on the rest of the circle.
Step 2$\angle ACB = \frac{1}{2} \times 100^\circ = 50^\circ$.
Answer(a) $50^\circ$
Example 6 · Single correct

$ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral in which $AB$ is a diameter and $\angle ADC = 130^\circ$. What is $\angle BAC$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $50^\circ$
  2. $40^\circ$
  3. $130^\circ$
  4. $90^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add to $180^\circ$: $\angle ABC = 50^\circ$.
Step 2$\angle ACB = 90^\circ$, the angle in a semicircle, so $\angle BAC = 180^\circ - 90^\circ - 50^\circ = 40^\circ$.
Answer(b) $40^\circ$
