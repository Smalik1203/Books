Multiple Choice · Level 2
Two steps, and a figure to settle first.

Choose the correct option.

  1. Parallel chords $16$ cm and $12$ cm long lie on the same side of the centre of a circle of radius $10$ cm. They are apart by
    1. $2$ cm
    2. $4$ cm
    3. $8$ cm
    4. $14$ cm
  2. A chord $8$ cm from the centre of a circle of radius $17$ cm is
    1. $15$ cm
    2. $25$ cm
    3. $30$ cm
    4. $34$ cm
  3. In two concentric circles of radii $13$ cm and $5$ cm, a chord of the larger has its midpoint on the smaller. Its length is
    1. $12$ cm
    2. $18$ cm
    3. $24$ cm
    4. $26$ cm
  4. Circles of radii $10$ cm and $17$ cm have centres $21$ cm apart. Their common chord is
    1. $8$ cm
    2. $15$ cm
    3. $16$ cm
    4. $20$ cm
  5. A chord subtends $90^\circ$ at the centre of a circle of radius $r$. Its length is
    1. $r$
    2. $r\sqrt{2}$
    3. $r\sqrt{3}$
    4. $2r$
  6. $ABCD$ is cyclic and $BC$ is produced to $E$. If $\angle DAB = 92^\circ$, then $\angle DCE$ is
    1. $44^\circ$
    2. $88^\circ$
    3. $92^\circ$
    4. $96^\circ$
  7. $O$ is the centre and $\angle AOB = 130^\circ$. A point $C$ lies on the minor arc $AB$. Then $\angle ACB$ is
    1. $65^\circ$
    2. $115^\circ$
    3. $130^\circ$
    4. $230^\circ$
  8. The angles of a cyclic quadrilateral, in order, are in the ratio $1:2:3:k$. Then $k$ is
    1. $2$
    2. $4$
    3. $5$
    4. $6$
  9. Equal chords $AB$ and $CD$ of a circle meet at $P$ inside it. Then
    1. $PA = PC$ always
    2. $PA = PD$ always
    3. $AB \parallel CD$
    4. $\angle AOB = \angle COD$
