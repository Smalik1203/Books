B5 · Foundation MCQs, Level 2
  1. Two parallel chords of a circle of radius $10$ cm are $16$ and $12$ cm long. The distance between them is
    1. $2$ cm only
    2. $14$ cm only
    3. $2$ cm or $14$ cm
    4. $6$ cm or $8$ cm
  2. A chord of a circle of radius $25$ cm is $14$ cm long. Its distance from the centre is
    1. $7$ cm
    2. $11$ cm
    3. $20$ cm
    4. $24$ cm
  3. An arc subtends $250^\circ$ at the centre, the long way round. At a point of the circle outside it, that arc subtends
    1. $55^\circ$
    2. $110^\circ$
    3. $125^\circ$
    4. $235^\circ$
  4. In a circle of radius $r$, the midpoints of all chords of length $r$ lie on
    1. a circle of radius $\dfrac{r}{2}$
    2. a circle of radius $\dfrac{r\sqrt{3}}{2}$
    3. one diameter
    4. the circle itself
  5. $PQRS$ is cyclic, $\angle P = (2x + 10)^\circ$ and $\angle R = (3x - 20)^\circ$. Then $x$ is
    1. $30$
    2. $38$
    3. $42$
    4. $45$
  6. A parallelogram inscribed in a circle has to be
    1. a rhombus
    2. a rectangle
    3. a square
    4. none of these
  7. Two chords of a circle of radius $13$ cm, $24$ and $10$ cm long, lie on opposite sides of the centre, apart by
    1. $5$ cm
    2. $7$ cm
    3. $12$ cm
    4. $17$ cm
  8. In one circle, $AB$ lies twice as far from the centre as $CD$. It follows that
    1. $CD = 2\,AB$
    2. $AB = 2\,CD$
    3. $CD$ is the longer
    4. $AB$ is the longer
  9. $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ lie on a circle with $C$, $D$ on one arc, and $\angle ACB = 40^\circ$. Then $\angle ADB$ is
    1. $20^\circ$
    2. $40^\circ$
    3. $80^\circ$
    4. $140^\circ$
