Problem 2

$ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral in which $AB$ is a diameter and $\angle ADC = 130^\circ$. Then $\angle CAB$ equals

  1. $40^\circ$
  2. $50^\circ$
  3. $65^\circ$
  4. $80^\circ$
Solution

Two facts are given, and each belongs to a different result: $\angle ADC$ is opposite $\angle ABC$, and $AB$ is a diameter with $C$ on the circle.

$\angle ABC = 180^\circ - 130^\circ = 50^\circ$  (Theorem 11)

$\angle ACB = 90^\circ$  (angle in a semicircle)

$\angle CAB = 180^\circ - 90^\circ - 50^\circ = 40^\circ$

Option (a). Option (b) stops at $\angle ABC$. Option (c) halves $130^\circ$ as if it were an angle at the centre, but $D$ is a point of the circle. Option (d) is $130^\circ - 50^\circ$, a difference of two angles that share no triangle.
