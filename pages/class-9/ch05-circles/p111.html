1. Which of these statements is false?
    1. No chord of a circle is longer than a diameter.
    2. Equal chords of a circle lie at equal distances from the centre.
    3. The perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord.
    4. Any three points of a plane lie on some circle.
Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): The perpendicular bisectors of the three sides of a triangle pass through one point.

    Reason (R): The point where two of them meet is equidistant from all three vertices.

  1. Assertion (A): A chord $10$ cm from the centre of a circle of radius $26$ cm is $48$ cm long.

    Reason (R): No chord of a circle is longer than a diameter.

  1. Assertion (A): Two chords of one circle, $6$ cm and $9$ cm long, can lie at the same distance from the centre.

    Reason (R): Chords that lie at the same distance from the centre are equal in length.

  1. Assertion (A): The circumcentre of a right-angled triangle is the midpoint of its hypotenuse.

    Reason (R): The circumcentre of every triangle lies inside the triangle.

Very short answer
  1. A chord $12$ cm long is drawn in a circle of radius $6.5$ cm. Find its distance from the centre.
  1. The minor arc $AB$ of a circle subtends $136^\circ$ at the centre. Find the angle that $AB$ subtends at a point of the minor arc.
  1. In a cyclic quadrilateral $PQRS$, $\angle P$ is $40^\circ$ more than $\angle R$. Find $\angle P$ and $\angle R$.
Short answer
  1. Two parallel chords of a circle of radius $25$ cm are $30$ cm and $40$ cm long. Find the distance between them when they lie on opposite sides of the centre, and when they lie on the same side.
  1. $A$, $B$ and $C$ lie in that order on a circle with centre $O$, and $O$ lies inside triangle $ABC$. If $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$ and $\angle BOC = 120^\circ$, find the three angles of triangle $ABC$.
  1. $ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral with $\angle DBC = 55^\circ$ and $\angle BAC = 45^\circ$. Find $\angle BCD$.
  1. Prove that if two chords of a circle bisect each other, both of them are diameters.
Long answer
  1. $AB$ and $CD$ are equal chords of a circle. $P$ is a point of the major arc $AB$ and $Q$ is a point of the major arc $CD$. Prove that $\angle APB = \angle CQD$.
