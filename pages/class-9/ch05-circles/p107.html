B7 · The Challenge Zone
  1. Think Using $c = 2\sqrt{r^2 - d^2}$ and nothing else, prove that of two unequal chords of a circle the longer lies nearer the centre. Then follow the formula to both ends: what does it say as $d$ falls to $0$, and as $d$ climbs to $r$?
  2. Apply Prove that the midpoints of all the chords of one length $c$ in a circle of radius $r$ lie on a circle concentric with it.
    1. Give that circle's radius.
    2. What becomes of your answer when $c = 2r$, and what when $c$ shrinks towards $0$?
  3. Apply In Fig. 5.15, $O$ is the centre, $AB$ is a chord and $P$ lies on the major arc, with $\angle OAB = 25^\circ$. Find $\angle AOB$ and $\angle APB$, saying which theorem gives each — and then say what $\angle AQB$ would be for a point $Q$ on the minor arc.
  4. Challenge Two circles, of radii $10$ cm and $17$ cm, have their centres $21$ cm apart and cross at $P$ and $Q$. Find $PQ$.

    $PQ$ is a chord of both circles, and the line of centres meets it at right angles. Two applications of the chord formula, and a difference of squares.

  5. Challenge $ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral whose diagonals meet at $X$. Show that $\angle AXB$ is half the sum of the angles that arcs $AB$ and $CD$ subtend at the centre.
