The Challenge Zone
Think, then apply, then challenge.

Show every step of the reasoning.

  1. Think From $c = 2\sqrt{r^2 - d^2}$ alone, show that the longer of two chords lies nearer the centre. Then read the formula at both ends: $d \to 0$ and $d \to r$.
  2. Think In Fig. 5.15, $\angle OAB = 25^\circ$. Find $\angle AOB$ and $\angle APB$, naming the theorem behind each, and then $\angle AQB$ for a $Q$ on the minor arc.
  3. Apply Show that the midpoints of all chords of one length $c$ lie on a circle concentric with the first. Give its radius, and say what becomes of it at $c = 2r$ and as $c \to 0$.
  4. Challenge Circles of radii $10$ cm and $17$ cm have centres $21$ cm apart and cross at $P$ and $Q$. Find $PQ$.

    $PQ$ is a chord of both, cut at right angles by the line of centres. Two chord formulas and a difference of squares.

  5. Challenge A chord of a circle of radius $25$ cm is $14$ cm long. A second chord, parallel to it, is $48$ cm long. Find every possible distance between them, and say what in the question left more than one answer open.
  6. Think A circle of radius $r$ has chords of length $r$. How many are there, and what figure do their midpoints make?
  7. Apply Chords of $16$ cm and $30$ cm are drawn in one circle, and the $16$ cm chord lies $15$ cm from the centre. Find the radius, then the second chord's distance from the centre.
  8. Challenge $ABCD$ is cyclic and its diagonals meet at $X$. Show that $\angle AXB$ is half the sum of the angles that arcs $AB$ and $CD$ subtend at the centre.
