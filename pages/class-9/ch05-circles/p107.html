Example 10 · Multiple correct

Two circles of radii 10 cm and 17 cm have centres $A$ and $B$, 21 cm apart, and cross at two points. Which statements about their common chord are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It is 16 cm long.
  2. It is perpendicular to $AB$.
  3. It is 6 cm from $A$.
  4. It is 6 cm from $B$.

Solution.

Step 1The common chord is perpendicular to $AB$. If it is $x$ cm from $A$: $10^2 - x^2 = 17^2 - (21 - x)^2$, so $x = 6$.
Step 2Half the chord is $\sqrt{100 - 36} = 8$, so it is 16 cm long, and $21 - 6 = 15$ cm from $B$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

An arch is an arc of a circle. Its opening is 80 cm wide, and its top is 20 cm above the middle of the opening. Find the radius, in cm.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The centre is $r - 20$ below the top: $r^2 = 40^2 + (r - 20)^2$ gives $40r = 2000$, so $r = 50$ cm.
Answer50
Example 12 · Numerical answer

Two chords of a circle are 40 cm and 42 cm long. The 40 cm chord is 21 cm from the centre. How far is the 42 cm chord from the centre, in cm?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Radius$^2 = 20^2 + 21^2 = 841$, so the radius is 29 cm.
Step 2For the 42 cm chord: $\sqrt{841 - 21^2} = \sqrt{400} = 20$ cm.
Answer20
