Type 4 · Comparing chords by their distance from the centre

Example 7

Two parallel chords of a circle, $24$ cm and $18$ cm long, lie on the same side of the centre and $3$ cm apart (Fig. 5B.3). The radius of the circle is

  1. $12$ cm
  2. $15$ cm
  3. $21$ cm
  4. cannot be found

Solution.

Step 1 let the $24$ cm chord lie $d_1$ from the centre and the $18$ cm chord $d_2$; the longer chord is the nearer, so $d_2 - d_1 = 3$ Theorem 8
Step 2 $r^2 = d_1^2 + 12^2 = d_2^2 + 9^2$ one radius for both
Step 3 $d_2^2 - d_1^2 = 144 - 81 = 63$
Step 4 $(d_2 - d_1)(d_2 + d_1) = 3(d_2 + d_1) = 63$, so $d_2 + d_1 = 21$ difference of squares
Step 5 $d_2 = 12$ and $d_1 = 9$ from Steps 1 and 4
Step 6 $r^2 = 81 + 144 = 225$, so $r = 15$
Answer (b)

The other chord checks: $144 + 81 = 225$. Option (d) is chosen by seeing two unknown distances and stopping, but two unknowns need two equations, and the second is the chapter's own: one circle, one radius, for both chords. Option (a) is $d_2$, and option (c) is $d_1 + d_2$, which is not a length in the figure at all.
