Example 8

Two chords of a circle are $40$ cm and $42$ cm long. The $40$ cm chord lies $21$ cm from the centre. How far from the centre is the $42$ cm chord?

Solution.

Step 1 $r^2 = 21^2 + 20^2 = 441 + 400 = 841$, so $r = 29$ the $40$ cm chord
Step 2 $d^2 = 29^2 - 21^2 = 841 - 441 = 400$ the $42$ cm chord
Answer $d = 20$, so the $42$ cm chord lies $20$ cm from the centre
Check $20$ cm is less than $21$ cm: the longer chord is the nearer, as Theorem 8 says

Example 9

$A$, $B$ and $C$ are points of a circle with centre $O$, and $C$ lies on the major arc $AB$. If $\angle ACB = (3x)^\circ$ and $\angle AOB = (5x + 20)^\circ$, find $x$ and both angles.

Solution.

Step 1 $5x + 20 = 2 \times 3x$ Theorem 9
Step 2 $5x + 20 = 6x$, so $x = 20$
Answer $x = 20$, $\angle ACB = 60^\circ$ and $\angle AOB = 120^\circ$
Check $120 = 2 \times 60$
