8
The Trap Room
Two students, one question, and a mistake.

Answer (a) A only, (b) B only, (c) both, (d) neither — then say in one line what went wrong.

  1. A. Every chord is a diameter; both join two points of the circle.  B. A chord is a diameter only when it passes through the centre.
  2. Can a circle of radius $10$ cm hold a chord $21$ cm long? A. No — nothing beats the diameter, $20$ cm.  B. Yes — the circumference is over $60$ cm, so there is room.
  3. $ABCD$ is cyclic with $\angle A = 95^\circ$. A. $\angle C = 85^\circ$, and $\angle B$ is not fixed.  B. $\angle B = 85^\circ$, since opposite angles are supplementary.
  4. Parallel chords $16$ cm and $12$ cm long lie in a circle of radius $10$ cm. How far apart are they? A. $2$ cm.  B. $14$ cm.
  5. A chord subtends $110^\circ$ at the centre. A. It subtends the same angle at a point of the minor arc as at one of the major arc — angles in the same segment.  B. Those two angles are supplementary, $55^\circ$ and $125^\circ$.
  6. A point lies $10$ cm from the centre of a circle of radius $10$ cm. A. It is on the circle; the definition allows no near miss.  B. It is inside, since $10$ is not more than $10$.
  7. In a circle of radius $10$ cm a chord moves from $d = 3$ cm to $d = 6$ cm. A. Its length halves, by $c = 2\sqrt{r^2 - d^2}$.  B. Its length doubles, for the same reason.
  8. A. Equal chords of one circle subtend equal angles at its centre.  B. Equal chords of two different circles subtend equal angles at their centres.
  9. Can a parallelogram be cyclic? A. Yes.  B. Only when it is a rectangle.
  10. An arc subtends $100^\circ$ at the centre. A. At a point of the major arc it subtends $50^\circ$.  B. At a point of the minor arc it subtends $130^\circ$.
Three shapes of mistake
  1. A definition bent to fit (1, 6). A chord through the centre is a diameter and no other is; a point at distance exactly $r$ is on the circle. Definitions have no degrees.
  2. A theorem quoted without its condition (3, 5, 8, 9, 10). Opposite angles; one circle; points on the same arc. Drop a word and the claim fails half the time.
  3. An answer given before the figure is fixed (2, 4, 7). Two chords may share a side of the centre or straddle it; a square root is not a proportion.
