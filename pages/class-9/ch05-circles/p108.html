Example 13 · Numerical answer

The chords $AC$ and $BD$ of a circle cross at $E$, and $\angle BAC = 30^\circ$. Find $\angle BDC$, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$\angle BAC$ and $\angle BDC$ both stand on the arc $BC$, from points on the same side of $BC$.
Step 2Angles in the same segment are equal: $\angle BDC = 30^\circ$.
Answer30
Example 14 · Matching

$O$ is the centre and $\angle AOB = 120^\circ$. Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $\angle ACB$, $C$ on the major arc(1) $110^\circ$
(Q) $\angle ADB$, $D$ on the minor arc(2) $90^\circ$
(R) An angle in a semicircle(3) $60^\circ$
(S) Opposite $70^\circ$ in a cyclic quadrilateral(4) $120^\circ$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$\angle ACB = \frac{1}{2} \times 120^\circ = 60^\circ$ and $\angle ADB = 180^\circ - 60^\circ$; a semicircle gives $90^\circ$, and $180^\circ - 70^\circ = 110^\circ$.
Answer(b) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
