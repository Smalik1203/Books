Lines of symmetry of a circle

A line is a line of reflection symmetry of a circle exactly when it passes through the centre. The lines of symmetry of a circle are its diameters — and there are infinitely many of them.

5.4How Many Circles?

In Section 5.2 the word locus was introduced and then left alone: a circle is the locus of points at a given distance from a given point. Change the condition and you get a different locus. Here is the change worth making.

Instead of one fixed point, take two. Let $A$ and $B$ be points of the plane, and ask a question not about points but about circles: how many circles pass through both of them?
