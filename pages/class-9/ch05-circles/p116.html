1. In a cyclic quadrilateral $PQRS$, $\angle P$ is $40^\circ$ more than $\angle R$. Find $\angle P$ and $\angle R$.
Short answer
  1. Two parallel chords of a circle of radius $25$ cm are $30$ cm and $40$ cm long. Find the distance between them when they lie on opposite sides of the centre, and when they lie on the same side.
  1. $A$, $B$ and $C$ lie in that order on a circle with centre $O$, and $O$ lies inside triangle $ABC$. If $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$ and $\angle BOC = 120^\circ$, find the three angles of triangle $ABC$.
  1. $ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral with $\angle DBC = 55^\circ$ and $\angle BAC = 45^\circ$. Find $\angle BCD$.
  1. Prove that if two chords of a circle bisect each other, both of them are diameters.
Long answer
  1. $AB$ and $CD$ are equal chords of a circle. $P$ is a point of the major arc $AB$ and $Q$ is a point of the major arc $CD$. Prove that $\angle APB = \angle CQD$.
  1. Two circles, of radii $15$ cm and $20$ cm, cross at $P$ and $Q$, and $PQ = 24$ cm. Find the distance between their centres when the centres lie on opposite sides of $PQ$, and when they lie on the same side.
  1. In a quadrilateral $ABCD$, the bisectors of $\angle A$ and $\angle B$ meet at $P$, those of $\angle B$ and $\angle C$ at $Q$, those of $\angle C$ and $\angle D$ at $R$, and those of $\angle D$ and $\angle A$ at $S$. When these four points are different, prove that $PQRS$ is a cyclic quadrilateral.
