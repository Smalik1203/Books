Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these quadrilaterals always have all four vertices on one circle?

Select all correct options.

  1. a rectangle
  2. a square
  3. a rhombus that is not a square
  4. an isosceles trapezium

Solution.

Step 1Opposite angles adding to $180^\circ$ make (a), (b) and (d) cyclic; a rhombus with a $60^\circ$ angle has $60^\circ + 60^\circ \ne 180^\circ$.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

A circle has radius 15 cm, and two parallel chords of it are 24 cm and 18 cm long. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The 24 cm chord is 9 cm from the centre.
  2. The 18 cm chord is 12 cm from the centre.
  3. If they are on the same side of the centre, they are 3 cm apart.
  4. If they are on opposite sides, they are 15 cm apart.

Solution.

Step 1The distances are $\sqrt{225 - 144} = 9$ and $\sqrt{225 - 81} = 12$: 3 cm apart on one side, 21 cm on opposite sides.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which statements about a circle are always true?

Select all correct options.

  1. The centre's perpendicular bisects a chord.
  2. Equal chords are equally far from the centre.
  3. An arc makes equal angles at centre and rim.
  4. A chord farther from the centre is longer.

Solution.

Step 1(a) and (b) are the chord theorems; the angle at the centre is twice that at the rim, and chords shorten away from the centre.
Answer(a), (b)
