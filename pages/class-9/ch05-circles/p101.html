The Idea Underneath
One relation, doing the work of four theorems.

A chord at distance $d$ from the centre of a circle of radius $r$ has length

$$c = 2\sqrt{r^2 - d^2}$$

Fix $c$ and $d$ is fixed too, so every chord of one length keeps its midpoint the same distance from $O$ — and those midpoints trace a circle of their own.

Read it both ways
distance from centre $d$↑ chord length $c$↓
distance from centre $d$↓ chord length $c$↑

Neither is a proportion. Doubling $d$ does not halve $c$; the square root sees to that, and half the traps in this section are built on forgetting it.

The longer chord lies nearer the centre
Equal chords keep equal distances
