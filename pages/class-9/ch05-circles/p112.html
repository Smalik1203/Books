Type 7 · Two circles and their common chord

Example 14

Two circles of radii $10$ cm and $17$ cm have centres $A$ and $B$ that are $21$ cm apart. They cross at $P$ and $Q$ (Fig. 5B.5). The length of $PQ$ is

  1. $8$ cm
  2. $15$ cm
  3. $16$ cm
  4. $20$ cm

Solution.

Step 1 $A$ and $B$ both lie on the perpendicular bisector of $PQ$, so $AB$ is that bisector, and it meets $PQ$ at its midpoint $M$ Exercise Set 5.4, Q1
Step 2 let $AM = x$, so that $MB = 21 - x$
Step 3 $PM^2 = 10^2 - x^2 = 17^2 - (21 - x)^2$ two right-angled triangles
Step 4 $100 - x^2 = 289 - 441 + 42x - x^2$
Step 5 $42x = 252$, so $x = 6$
Step 6 $PM^2 = 100 - 36 = 64$, so $PM = 8$ cm and $PQ = 16$ cm $M$ is the midpoint
Answer (c)

From the other side, $289 - 225 = 64$. Option (a) is the half-chord, option (b) is $MB$, and option (d) is the diameter of the smaller circle, which $PQ$ cannot reach since it does not pass through $A$.
