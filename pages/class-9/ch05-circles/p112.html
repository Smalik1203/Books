  1. Challenge $P$ is a point $8$ cm from the centre of a circle of radius $17$ cm. The number of chords through $P$ that are exactly $30$ cm long is
    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $2$
    4. infinitely many

    How far from the centre must a $30$ cm chord lie? And how far from the centre can a line through $P$ lie?

  1. Challenge In triangle $ABC$, the perpendicular from $B$ meets $AC$ at $E$, and the perpendicular from $C$ meets $AB$ at $F$. Four points that must lie on one circle are
    1. $A$, $B$, $C$, $E$
    2. $B$, $C$, $E$, $F$
    3. $A$, $B$, $E$, $F$
    4. none of these

    Which segment is seen at a right angle from two points on the same side of it?
