1. Apply A regular hexagon is drawn with its six vertices on a circle of radius $10$ cm. The distance of each side from the centre is
    1. $5$ cm
    2. $5\sqrt{2}$ cm
    3. $5\sqrt{3}$ cm
    4. $10$ cm

    Each side subtends $60^\circ$ at the centre. Example 5 gives its length, and then the half-chord and the radius give the distance.

  1. Challenge $P$ is a point $8$ cm from the centre of a circle of radius $17$ cm. The number of chords through $P$ that are exactly $30$ cm long is
    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $2$
    4. infinitely many

    How far from the centre must a $30$ cm chord lie? And how far from the centre can a line through $P$ lie?

    2. $B$, $C$, $E$, $F$
    3. $A$, $B$, $E$, $F$
    4. none of these
