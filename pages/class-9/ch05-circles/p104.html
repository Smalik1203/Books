Example 1 · Single correct

Two points $A$ and $B$ are 16 cm apart. How many circles of radius 17 cm pass through both?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 2
  2. 1
  3. infinitely many
  4. none

Solution.

Step 1The centre is 17 cm from both, so it lies on the perpendicular bisector of $AB$, at $\sqrt{17^2 - 8^2} = 15$ cm from the midpoint.
Step 2There is one such point on each side of $AB$: two circles.
Answer(a) 2
Example 2 · Single correct

$AB$ and $CD$ are equal chords of a circle with centre $O$, and $\angle AOB = 70^\circ$. What is $\angle COD$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $35^\circ$
  2. $70^\circ$
  3. $110^\circ$
  4. $140^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1Equal chords subtend equal angles at the centre.
Step 2So $\angle COD = \angle AOB = 70^\circ$.
Answer(b) $70^\circ$
Example 3 · Single correct

The angles of a triangle are in the ratio $2 : 3 : 7$. Where does its circumcentre lie?

Choose one correct option.

  1. inside the triangle
  2. on a side
  3. outside the triangle
  4. at a vertex

Solution.

Step 1The angles are $30^\circ$, $45^\circ$ and $105^\circ$: the triangle is obtuse.
Step 2The circumcentre of an obtuse triangle lies outside it.
Answer(c) outside the triangle
