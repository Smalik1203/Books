Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): The perpendicular bisectors of the three sides of a triangle pass through one point.

    Reason (R): The point where two of them meet is equidistant from all three vertices.

  1. Assertion (A): A chord $10$ cm from the centre of a circle of radius $26$ cm is $48$ cm long.

    Reason (R): No chord of a circle is longer than a diameter.

  1. Assertion (A): Two chords of one circle, $6$ cm and $9$ cm long, can lie at the same distance from the centre.

    Reason (R): Chords that lie at the same distance from the centre are equal in length.

  1. Assertion (A): The circumcentre of a right-angled triangle is the midpoint of its hypotenuse.

    Reason (R): The circumcentre of every triangle lies inside the triangle.
