Example 7

If $a + b = 7$ and $a^3 + b^3 = 133$, the number of real pairs $(a, b)$ is

  1. $0$
  2. $1$
  3. $2$
  4. $4$

Solution.

Step 1 $a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)^3 - 3ab(a + b)$ links the sum, the cubes and the product
Step 2 $133 = 343 - 21ab$, so $ab = 10$
Step 3 $(a - b)^2 = (a + b)^2 - 4ab = 49 - 40 = 9$
Step 4 $a - b$ is $3$ or $-3$ a square has two square roots
Answer (c): the pairs are $(5, 2)$ and $(2, 5)$

Option (b) comes of taking $a - b = 3$ and stopping there. Option (a) would be right if $(a - b)^2$ had come out negative, and it can: with $a^3 + b^3 = 70$ instead, $343 - 21ab = 70$ gives $ab = 13$, and $49 - 52 = -3$. Option (d) gives $a + b$ two signs as well, but $a + b$ was given as $7$.

Example 8

$x^2 - 2x - 35$ is equal to

  1. $(x - 7)(x + 5)$
  2. $(x + 7)(x - 5)$
  3. $(x - 7)(x - 5)$
  4. $(x + 7)(x + 5)$

Solution.

Step 1 two numbers with sum $-2$ and product $-35$ compare with $x^2 + (a + b)x + ab$
Step 2 the numbers have opposite signs the product is negative
Step 3 $(-7) \times 5 = -35$ and $-7 + 5 = -2$ from the pairs $1, 35$ and $5, 7$
Answer (a)

Option (b) has the right product, but its numbers add to $2$, not $-2$. Options (c) and (d) have product $35$, not $-35$.
