Example 10 · Multiple correct

Which statements about $27x^3 - 54x^2y + 36xy^2 - 8y^3$ are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It equals $(3x - 2y)^3$.
  2. It is a perfect cube.
  3. It equals $(3x + 2y)^3$.
  4. Its value at $x = y = 1$ is $-1$.

Solution.

Step 1$(3x - 2y)^3 = 27x^3 - 3(9x^2)(2y) + 3(3x)(4y^2) - 8y^3$, which is the expression.
Step 2At $x = y = 1$: $(3 - 2)^3 = 1$, not $-1$; the signs rule out $(3x + 2y)^3$.
Answer(a), (b)

Example 12 · Numerical answer

The product of two consecutive positive odd numbers is 143. Find the larger number.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1As $n - 1$ and $n + 1$: $n^2 - 1 = 143$, so $n = 12$ and the numbers are 11 and 13.

Solution.
