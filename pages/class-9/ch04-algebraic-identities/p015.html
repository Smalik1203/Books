Think and Reflect

Algebra tiles can show a product, and can find factors.

  1. Work out the product of $x + 2$ and $x + 3$ using tiles.
  2. Lay out tiles for $x^2 + 11x + 30$ so that its factors become visible.

Two products worked out so far look like this:

$(x + 3)(x + 4) = x^2 + 7x + 12$

$(x + 6)(x + 7) = x^2 + 13x + 42$

Set them side by side and one thing stands out before any algebra: in each, the number at the end is the product of the two constants and the number in the middle is their sum. That is the whole of what the tiles were showing.
