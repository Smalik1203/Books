Selected solutions

B5.5 hint $a^4 + a^2 + 1$ is no difference of squares. Make one.
solution $\left(a^4+2a^2+1\right) - a^2 = \left(a^2+1\right)^2 - a^2 = \left(a^2+a+1\right)\left(a^2-a+1\right)$
key idea Complete a square to manufacture a difference of squares.
B7.4 hint The area is $xy$, and $2xy$ is the term the squared identity hides.
solution $x+y = 17$, $x^2+y^2 = 169$, so $2xy = 289 - 169 = 120$ and the area is $60$ cm$^2$.
key idea A quantity can be found without the numbers that make it — and with diagonal $\sqrt{200}$ that is the only route left.
What to take away
  1. Read the condition first: $x+y+z=0$ is the answer, not the first line of one.
  2. Work with the sum, the pairwise sum and the product.
  3. Complete a square to manufacture a difference of squares.
  4. An identity plus no square is negative gives an inequality.
  5. Cancel factors, never terms, and record what you excluded.
