Type 10 · Solving by factorising

Example 16

The product of two consecutive positive odd numbers is $143$. Find the numbers.

Solution.

Step 1 let the numbers be $x$ and $x + 2$
Step 2 $x(x + 2) = 143$, so $x^2 + 2x - 143 = 0$ $0$ on one side first
Step 3 $13 \times (-11) = -143$ and $13 + (-11) = 2$ sum $2$, product $-143$
Step 4 $(x + 13)(x - 11) = 0$
Step 5 $x = -13$ or $x = 11$ a product is zero only if a factor is
Answer the numbers are positive, so $x = 11$: they are $11$ and $13$
Check $11 \times 13 = 143$

Type 11 · Proofs with identities

Example 17

Prove that the product of four consecutive whole numbers, with $1$ added, is a perfect square.

Solution.

Step 1 take the numbers $n$, $n + 1$, $n + 2$, $n + 3$
Step 2 $n(n + 3) = n^2 + 3n$ and $(n + 1)(n + 2) = n^2 + 3n + 2$ pair the outer two and the inner two
Step 3 write $m$ for $n^2 + 3n$; the product with $1$ added is $m(m + 2) + 1$ one quantity, as in Section 4.4
Step 4 $m(m + 2) + 1 = m^2 + 2m + 1 = (m + 1)^2$ $(a + b)^2$ read backwards
Answer $n(n + 1)(n + 2)(n + 3) + 1 = \left(n^2 + 3n + 1\right)^2$, a perfect square
Check $1 \times 2 \times 3 \times 4 + 1 = 25 = 5^2$, and $1 + 3 + 1 = 5$

Pairing the first number with the last is what makes the two products differ only in their constant.
