Example 14

Find $28^3 + (-15)^3 + (-13)^3$ without working out any of the cubes.

Solution.

Step 1 $28 + (-15) + (-13) = 0$ the three numbers add to zero
Step 2 so $x^3 + y^3 + z^3 = 3xyz$ for these three Section 4.7
Step 3 $28^3 + (-15)^3 + (-13)^3 = 3 \times 28 \times (-15) \times (-13)$
Answer $28^3 + (-15)^3 + (-13)^3 = 16380$

Example 15

Simplify $\dfrac{3a^2 - 5a - 2}{9a^2 - 1}$, given that $9a^2 - 1 \neq 0$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3 \times (-2) = -6$, and $-6 + 1 = -5$ the pair for the middle term
Step 2 $3a^2 - 5a - 2 = 3a^2 - 6a + a - 2 = 3a(a - 2) + (a - 2) = (3a + 1)(a - 2)$
Step 3 $9a^2 - 1 = (3a + 1)(3a - 1)$ difference of squares
Step 4 $\dfrac{3a^2 - 5a - 2}{9a^2 - 1} = \dfrac{(3a + 1)(a - 2)}{(3a + 1)(3a - 1)}$
Step 5 cancel the factor $3a + 1$ not zero, since $9a^2 - 1 \neq 0$
Answer $\dfrac{3a^2 - 5a - 2}{9a^2 - 1} = \dfrac{a - 2}{3a - 1}$
Check at $a = 1$: $\dfrac{3 - 5 - 2}{9 - 1} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{1 - 2}{3 - 1} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$
