Example 13

A cube has volume $p^3 + 6p^2q + 12pq^2 + 8q^3$ cubic units. What is the length of its side?

The side is whatever this expression is the cube of.

Solution.

Step 1 $p^3 + 6p^2q + 12pq^2 + 8q^3 = p^3 + 3(p)^2(2q) + 3(p)(2q)^2 + (2q)^3$ set beside $a^3 + 3a^2b + 3ab^2 + b^3$
Step 2 $= (p + 2q)^3$ so $a = p$ and $b = 2q$
Answer the side is $p + 2q$ units

The step worth naming there is the one in the middle, where $6p^2q$ was rewritten as $3(p)^2(2q)$ and $8q^3$ as $(2q)^3$. Nothing changed in value; the expression was simply written in the shape of the identity so the two could be compared term by term. Most uses of an identity are that move.

It also gives you a check. If $a = p$ and $b = 2q$ are right, then every term must agree, not merely the first and the last — and here the middle two do: $3(p)(2q)^2 = 12pq^2$. An expression that matches at the ends and fails in the middle is not a cube, and the check costs a line.

Example 14

Write $8n^3 - 60n^2m + 150nm^2 - 125m^3$ in the form $(a - b)^3$. What are $a$ and $b$?

Solution.

Step 1 the signs alternate points to the difference identity
Step 2 $8n^3 = (2n)^3$ and $125m^3 = (5m)^3$ the two cubes at the ends
Step 3 $8n^3 - 60n^2m + 150nm^2 - 125m^3 = (2n)^3 - 3(2n)^2(5m) + 3(2n)(5m)^2 - (5m)^3$ try $a = 2n$ and $b = 5m$
Answer $8n^3 - 60n^2m + 150nm^2 - 125m^3 = (2n - 5m)^3$, so $a = 2n$ and $b = 5m$
Check $3(2n)^2(5m) = 60n^2m$ and $3(2n)(5m)^2 = 150nm^2$, the two middle terms

Playing with what you have

New identities also come from multiplying things out and seeing what survives. Try these two products with the distributive property:

$(x - y)(x^2 + xy + y^2) \qquad\text{and}\qquad (x + y)(x^2 - xy + y^2)$

Take the first. Multiply every term of the second bracket by $x$, then by $-y$, and line the results up:

$(x - y)(x^2 + xy + y^2) = x^3 + x^2y + xy^2 - x^2y - xy^2 - y^3$

$= x^3 - y^3$
