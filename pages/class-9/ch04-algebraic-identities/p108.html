Type 7 · Sums and differences of cubes

Example 12

Factorise $a^6 - b^6$ completely.

Solution.

Step 1 $a^6 - b^6 = \left(a^3\right)^2 - \left(b^3\right)^2 = \left(a^3 - b^3\right)\left(a^3 + b^3\right)$ difference of squares first
Step 2 $a^3 - b^3 = (a - b)\left(a^2 + ab + b^2\right)$ difference of cubes
Step 3 $a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)\left(a^2 - ab + b^2\right)$ sum of cubes
Answer $a^6 - b^6 = (a - b)(a + b)\left(a^2 + ab + b^2\right)\left(a^2 - ab + b^2\right)$
Check at $a = 2$, $b = 1$: $64 - 1 = 63$ and $1 \times 3 \times 7 \times 3 = 63$

Type 8 · Three cubes

Example 13

If $x + y + z = 9$ and $xy + yz + zx = 26$, then $x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz$ equals

  1. $9$
  2. $27$
  3. $81$
  4. cannot be found

Solution.

Step 1 $(x + y + z)^2 = x^2 + y^2 + z^2 + 2(xy + yz + zx)$ both conditions are in it
Step 2 $81 = x^2 + y^2 + z^2 + 52$, so $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 29$
Step 3 $x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz = (x + y + z)\left[x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - (xy + yz + zx)\right]$ both factors are now known
Step 4 $x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz = 9(29 - 26) = 27$
Answer (b)
Check $2$, $3$, $4$ add to $9$, their products to $26$, their cubes to $99$, and $99 - 72 = 27$

Option (d) is there because two conditions cannot fix three letters. They do not need to: the expression depends only on the two totals. Option (a) is $x + y + z$ alone, and (c) is its square.
