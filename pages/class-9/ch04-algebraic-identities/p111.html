What to take away
  1. A condition in a question is often one side of an identity. Read it first. Then ask which identity it belongs to.
  2. The sum and the product of two numbers fix every expression that treats them in the same way. The numbers themselves are rarely needed.
  3. A value can show that an equation is not an identity. Only algebra can show that it is. Also, a square is never negative. That one fact can prove an inequality, or show that a rectangle cannot exist.
