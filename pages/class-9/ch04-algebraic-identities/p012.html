4.5Factorisation Using Algebra Tiles

Take a rectangle with sides $x + 3$ and $x + 4$ units. Its area is $(x + 3)(x + 4)$ square units, and distributivity multiplies that out:

$(x + 3)(x + 4) = x^2 + 3x + 4x + 12 = x^2 + 7x + 12$
