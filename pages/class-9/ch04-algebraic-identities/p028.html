1. Each expression below is the volume of a cuboid, in cubic units. Find possible expressions for its length, breadth and height.
    1. $6a^2 - 24b^2$
    2. $3ps^2 - 15ps + 12p$
  1. A village playground is a square of side 40 metres. A path of width $s$ metres is laid all the way round the outside of it. Write an expression, in terms of $s$, for the area of the path.
  1. A number added to its own reciprocal gives $\frac{10}{3}$. Find the number. There are two answers; find both, and say how they are related.
  1. A rectangular pool has area $2x^2 + 7x + 3$ square hastas and width $2x + 1$ hastas. Find its length. the hasta was an old unit of length

The last four are harder. Each can be settled with an identity from this chapter — but deciding which one is part of the work.

  1. Both $x - 2$ and $x - \frac{1}{2}$ are factors of $px^2 + 5x + r$. Show that $p = r$.
  1. Given that $a + b + c = 5$ and $ab + bc + ca = 10$, prove that $a^3 + b^3 + c^3 - 3abc = -25$.
  1. Factorise $n^3 - n$, and use the factorisation to show that $n^3 - n$ is divisible by 6 for every natural number $n$. Give your reasons.

    The factors are three consecutive whole numbers. What must be true of any three whole numbers in a row?

  1. Find the value of
    1. $x^3 + y^3 - 12xy + 64$,  when $x + y = -4$
    2. $x^3 - 8y^3 - 36xy - 216$,  when $x = 2y + 6$

    In each part the constant is a cube and the middle term is $-3$ times a product of three things. Which identity has that shape — and what does the given condition do to its first bracket?

Between them these thirteen questions use every identity in the chapter. If one stalls, the last page has them all together.

One chapter, eleven identities, and one habit running through all of them: recognise the shape first, and the working follows.
