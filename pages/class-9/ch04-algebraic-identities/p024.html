Example 16

Simplify $\dfrac{x^2 - 7x + 12}{5x^2 + 5x - 100}$, given that $5x^2 + 5x - 100 \neq 0$.

Solution.

Step 1 numerator: sum $-7$ and product $12$, so the pair is $-3$ and $-4$ both negative: the product is positive, the sum is not
Step 2 $x^2 - 7x + 12 = (x - 3)(x - 4)$
Step 3 denominator: take out 5, then sum $1$ and product $-20$ give $5$ and $-4$ all three terms are multiples of 5
Step 4 $5x^2 + 5x - 100 = 5\left(x^2 + x - 20\right) = 5(x - 4)(x + 5)$
Step 5 $\dfrac{x^2 - 7x + 12}{5x^2 + 5x - 100} = \dfrac{(x - 3)(x - 4)}{5(x - 4)(x + 5)}$ a product of factors
Step 6 cancel $x - 4$, top and bottom $x - 4$ cannot be zero, or the denominator would be
Answer $\dfrac{x^2 - 7x + 12}{5x^2 + 5x - 100} = \dfrac{x - 3}{5(x + 5)}$

One caution. The two agree wherever the original makes sense, but not everywhere: at $x = 4$ the simplified form gives $\frac{1}{45}$, while the original reads $\frac{0}{0}$.
