Example 4 · Single correct

What is $28^3 + (-15)^3 + (-13)^3$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 1638
  2. 5460
  3. 3276
  4. 16380

Solution.

Step 1$28 + (-15) + (-13) = 0$, and when $a + b + c = 0$, $a^3 + b^3 + c^3 = 3abc$.
Step 2$3 \times 28 \times (-15) \times (-13) = 16\,380$.
Answer(d) 16380

Example 6 · Single correct

A rectangle has perimeter 34 cm and diagonal 13 cm. What is its area, in $\text{cm}^2$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 30
  2. 60
  3. 120
  4. 169

Solution.

Step 1Length + breadth $= 17$ and length$^2$ + breadth$^2 = 13^2 = 169$.
Step 2$2 \times$ area $= 17^2 - 169 = 120$, so the area is 60.
Answer(b) 60
