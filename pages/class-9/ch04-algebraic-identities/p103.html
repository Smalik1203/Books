Type 1 · Squares of sums and differences

Example 1

Find $996^2$ using an identity.

Solution.

Step 1 $996^2 = (1000 - 4)^2$ a round number less a small one
Step 2 $= 1000^2 - 2 \times 1000 \times 4 + 4^2$ $(a - b)^2$ identity
Step 3 $= 1000000 - 8000 + 16$
Answer $996^2 = 992016$
Example 2

Factorise $12a^2 - 60ab + 75b^2$.

Solution.

Step 1 $12a^2 - 60ab + 75b^2 = 3\left(4a^2 - 20ab + 25b^2\right)$ common factor first
Step 2 $4a^2 = (2a)^2$, $25b^2 = (5b)^2$, and twice $2a \times 5b$ is $20ab$ a perfect square, minus sign
Step 3 $12a^2 - 60ab + 75b^2 = 3\left[(2a)^2 - 2(2a)(5b) + (5b)^2\right]$ the square of a difference
Answer $12a^2 - 60ab + 75b^2 = 3(2a - 5b)^2$

Without the common factor, the first term is $12a^2$, which is the square of nothing you can write without a surd.
