The two sides are equal only when $a = b$ (Fig. 4B.3). Every identity in the chapter was an equation. This one fact turns it into an inequality.

The same fact answers the second question. Let the sides be $x$ and $y$. Then $x + y = 17$. By Pythagoras, $x^2 + y^2 = 144$. So $2xy = 289 - 144 = 145$. Then $(x - y)^2 = x^2 + y^2 - 2xy = 144 - 145 = -1$. A square cannot be $-1$. So there is no such rectangle. The algebra found this without finding a side.
