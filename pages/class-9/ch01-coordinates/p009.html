1.4Distance Between Two Points

Some distances we can already read straight off a plan. If two points lie on the same axis, or the segment joining them runs parallel to an axis, the distance is just the difference of the coordinates that change — the wardrobe’s width $W_1W_2$ and the bed’s width $S_1S_2$ in Fig. 1.5 are both of that kind.

The interesting case is the other one. What do we do when the segment is slanted, parallel to neither axis? We use the Baudhāyana–Pythagoras Theorem from Grade 8 — and, as it turns out, that one result is enough to give the distance between any two points of the xy-plane.
