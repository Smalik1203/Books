Type 9 · Whole-number points at a given distance

Example 15

The number of points with whole-number coordinates that are exactly $5$ units from the origin is

  1. $4$
  2. $8$
  3. $12$
  4. $16$

Solution.

Step 1 $(x, y)$ is $5$ from $O$ when $x^2 + y^2 = 25$ distance formula, squared
Step 2 $25 = 25 + 0$: the points $(\pm 5, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 5)$, $4$ in all one coordinate zero
Step 3 $25 = 9 + 16$: the points $(\pm 3, \pm 4)$ and $(\pm 4, \pm 3)$, $8$ in all two orders, four signs
Step 4 no other two of the squares $0, 1, 4, 9, 16, 25$ add to $25$
Answer (c)

Option (a) counts only the points on the axes. Option (b) forgets those four. Option (d) would allow $(\pm 5, \pm 5)$, which are $\sqrt{50}$ from the origin, not $5$.
