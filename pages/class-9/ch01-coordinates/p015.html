Where this goes next

Two numbers, and the whole plane is addressable: enough to locate a doorway, to measure a distance without a ruler, and to check whether three points fall in line. In Class 10 the same pair of numbers begins to carry equations rather than single points, and a line or a curve becomes something you can write down and work with algebraically.

The bridge between geometry and algebra that Descartes built, on foundations laid in Bhārat, is one you will cross in both directions from here on.
