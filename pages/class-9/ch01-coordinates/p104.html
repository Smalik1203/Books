B4 · Foundation MCQs, Level 1
  1. The point $(-3, 0)$ lies
    1. in Quadrant II
    2. in Quadrant III
    3. on the x-axis
    4. on the y-axis
  2. The distance between $(2, 3)$ and $(6, 6)$ is
    1. $5$
    2. $7$
    3. $\sqrt{13}$
    4. $25$
  3. The distance of $(-7, 24)$ from the origin is
    1. $17$
    2. $25$
    3. $31$
    4. $625$
  4. $P(x, y)$ lies in Quadrant III. Then
    1. $x > 0$, $y > 0$
    2. $x < 0$, $y > 0$
    3. $x < 0$, $y < 0$
    4. $x > 0$, $y < 0$
  5. The midpoint of the segment joining $(-3, 7)$ and $(5, -1)$ is
    1. $(1, 3)$
    2. $(2, 6)$
    3. $(4, -4)$
    4. $(-4, 4)$
  6. Reflecting $(4, -9)$ in the x-axis gives
    1. $(-4, -9)$
    2. $(4, 9)$
    3. $(-4, 9)$
    4. $(-9, 4)$
  7. A rectangle is drawn with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(6, 0)$, $(6, 4)$ and $(0, 4)$. Its perimeter, in units, is
    1. $10$
    2. $20$
    3. $24$
    4. $26$
  8. Which of these points lies on the y-axis?
    1. $(-6, 0)$
    2. $(0, -6)$
    3. $(6, 6)$
    4. $(-6, -6)$
  9. The distance between $(1, 1)$ and $(3, 3)$ is
    1. $2$
    2. $2\sqrt{2}$
    3. $\sqrt{6}$
    4. $4$
  10. The point $(5, -2)$ is reflected first in the y-axis, and the image is then reflected in the x-axis. It ends up at
    1. $(-5, 2)$
    2. $(5, 2)$
    3. $(-5, -2)$
    4. $(2, -5)$
