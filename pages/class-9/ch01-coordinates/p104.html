Example 2

A point lies on the x-axis, $4$ units from the origin, on the negative side. Its coordinates are

  1. $(0, -4)$
  2. $(-4, 0)$
  3. $(4, 0)$
  4. $(-4, -4)$

Solution.

Step 1 a point of the x-axis has the form $(x, 0)$ y-coordinate zero
Step 2 $4$ units on the negative side gives $x = -4$ left of $O$ is negative
Answer (b)

Option (a) is on the y-axis, below $O$. Option (c) is on the positive side. Option (d) is in Quadrant III, and is $\sqrt{16 + 16} = \sqrt{32}$ units from $O$, not $4$.

Example 3

$A(-3, -2)$, $B(5, -2)$ and $C(5, 4)$ are three corners of a rectangle $ABCD$. Find $D$, the length and breadth of the rectangle, its area, its perimeter and the length of its diagonal $AC$.

Solution.

Step 1 $A$ and $B$ share the y-coordinate $-2$; $B$ and $C$ share the x-coordinate $5$ $AB$ across, $BC$ up
Step 2 $D$ takes its x-coordinate from $A$ and its y-coordinate from $C$: $D(-3, 4)$ the corner opposite $B$
Step 3 length $|5 - (-3)| = 8$, breadth $|4 - (-2)| = 6$ sides parallel to the axes
Step 4 area $8 \times 6 = 48$; perimeter $2 \times (8 + 6) = 28$
Step 5 $AC$: $\sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{100} = 10$ Baudhāyana–Pythagoras
Answer $D(-3, 4)$; $8$ units by $6$ units; area $48$ square units, perimeter $28$ units, diagonal $10$ units
