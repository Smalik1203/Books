Example 12 · Numerical answer

Three villages stand at $A(0, 0)$, $B(8, 0)$ and $C(0, 6)$, in kilometres. A tank is to be equally far from all three. How far is that, in km?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The right angle at $A$ puts the point at the midpoint of $BC$, $(4, 3)$, which is $\sqrt{16 + 9} = 5$ km from each village.
Answer5
Example 13 · Numerical answer

On a town map one unit is one kilometre, and the streets run along the grid lines. How many kilometres is it by street from $(-3, 1)$ to $(4, 5)$?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1By street it is $4 - (-3) = 7$ across and $5 - 1 = 4$ up: $7 + 4 = 11$ km, whatever the turns.
Answer11
Example 14 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $(-3, 5)$(1) on the $x$-axis
(Q) $(4, -2)$(2) Quadrant IV
(R) $(0, -6)$(3) Quadrant II
(S) $(-7, 0)$(4) on the $y$-axis

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  2. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–2, R–4, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$x \lt 0 \lt y$ is Quadrant II and $y \lt 0 \lt x$ Quadrant IV; $x = 0$ is the $y$-axis and $y = 0$ the $x$-axis.
Answer(b) P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
