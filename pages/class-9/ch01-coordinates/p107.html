Example 8

Three villages stand at $A(0, 0)$, $B(8, 0)$ and $C(0, 6)$, in kilometres. A water tank is to be built at a point the same distance from all three. Where should it stand, and how far is it from each village?

Solution.

Step 1 $x^2 + y^2 = (x - 8)^2 + y^2$ as far from $A$ as from $B$
Step 2 $0 = -16x + 64$, so $x = 4$ $x^2$ and $y^2$ cancel
Step 3 $x^2 + y^2 = x^2 + (y - 6)^2$ as far from $A$ as from $C$
Step 4 $0 = -12y + 36$, so $y = 3$
Answer at $(4, 3)$, $5$ km from each village
Check $\sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5$, $\sqrt{(4 - 8)^2 + 3^2} = 5$ and $\sqrt{4^2 + (3 - 6)^2} = 5$

Example 9

$M(3, 1)$ is the midpoint of $A(-2, 5)$ and $B$. Find $B$.

Solution.

Step 1 shift from $A$ to $M$: $3 - (-2) = 5$ across and $1 - 5 = -4$ up subtract coordinates
Step 2 the same shift, taken again, carries $M$ on to $B$ $M$ is half way
Step 3 $3 + 5 = 8$ and $1 + (-4) = -3$
Answer $B(8, -3)$
Check $AM = \sqrt{5^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{41}$ and $MB = \sqrt{5^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{41}$

Half way along a segment means half of each shift. That is why one step, taken twice, runs from one end to the other.
