The Idea Underneath
One formula, and the question it does not answer.

Section 1.4 measured the straight line between two points:

$$d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$$

But nobody in a gridded city walks along a hypotenuse. Two honest numbers measure the same pair of points, and which one is wanted depends on what may travel along it.

What the square root does
both shifts double↑ the distance doubles↑
one shift doubles↑ the distance rises by less↑

The formula is not a proportion in either coordinate on its own. Half the traps in this section are built on treating it as one.

Two signs name the quadrant
A distance is one Pythagoras
A midpoint is an average
A walk is not a distance
