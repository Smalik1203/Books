Think and Reflect

Going from $A (3, 4)$ to $D (7, 1)$: how far have you travelled in the x-direction? How far in the y-direction?

Can those two numbers, between them, give you $AD$?

Fig. 1.7 supplies the missing line. Drop straight down from $A$ and across from $D$; they meet at $C (3, 1)$, and $ACD$ is a right-angled triangle whose two short sides run along the axes’ directions — exactly the case we already know how to measure.

The distance moved in the x-direction is the length $CD$, and the distance moved in the y-direction is the length $AC$. Both are differences of coordinates:

$CD = $ x-coordinate of $D - $ x-coordinate of $A = 7 - 3 = 4$

$AC = $ y-coordinate of $A - $ y-coordinate of $D = 4 - 1 = 3$
