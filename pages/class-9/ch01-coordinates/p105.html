Multiple Choice · Level 2
Two steps, and a figure to settle first.

Choose the correct option.

  1. The point on the x-axis equidistant from $(2, -5)$ and $(-2, 9)$ is
    1. $(-7, 0)$
    2. $(-3, 0)$
    3. $(3, 0)$
    4. $(7, 0)$
  2. The distance between $(3, a)$ and $(6, 5)$ is $5$. Then $a$ is
    1. $1$ only
    2. $9$ only
    3. $1$ or $9$
    4. $4$ or $6$
  3. $A(-2, -1)$, $B(1, 0)$, $C(4, 3)$ and $D(1, 2)$, joined in that order, form
    1. a square
    2. a rectangle
    3. a parallelogram, but no rectangle
    4. no closed figure at all
  4. The number of points with whole-number coordinates lying at distance $5$ from the origin is
    1. $4$
    2. $8$
    3. $12$
    4. $16$
  5. $M(2, -3)$ is the midpoint of $AB$ and $A$ is $(-1, 4)$. Then $B$ is
    1. $(5, -10)$
    2. $(3, -7)$
    3. $(1, 1)$
    4. $(-4, 11)$
  6. $(1, 2)$, $(3, 6)$ and $(k, 10)$ lie on one straight line when $k$ is
    1. $4$
    2. $5$
    3. $7$
    4. $8$
  7. In a city whose streets run only north–south and east–west, the walk from the crossing $(1, 2)$ to the crossing $(5, 7)$ is
    1. $6$ blocks
    2. $9$ blocks
    3. $\sqrt{41}$ blocks
    4. $41$ blocks
  8. A circle centred at the origin passes through $(6, 8)$. The point $(-9, 5)$ lies
    1. inside it
    2. on it
    3. outside it
    4. at the centre
  9. The area of the triangle with vertices $(2, 1)$, $(8, 1)$ and $(5, 7)$ is
    1. $18$
    2. $21$
    3. $24$
    4. $36$
  10. In the triangle with vertices $A(0, 0)$, $B(6, 0)$ and $C(6, 8)$, the length of the median drawn from $A$ is
    1. $5$
    2. $\sqrt{52}$
    3. $8$
    4. $10$
