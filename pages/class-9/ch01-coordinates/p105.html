Example 6 · Single correct

$P(-4, 3)$ is reflected in the $y$-axis to $P'$. What is the length $PP'$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 6
  2. 8
  3. 10
  4. 0

Solution.

Step 1Reflecting in the $y$-axis changes the sign of the $x$-coordinate: $P' = (4, 3)$.
Step 2$P$ and $P'$ are on one horizontal line, $4 - (-4) = 8$ apart.
Answer(b) 8
Example 7 · Multiple correct

The distance between $(a, 2)$ and $(7, 6)$ is 5. Which values can $a$ take?

Select all correct options.

  1. 4
  2. 10
  3. 7
  4. $-4$

Solution.

Step 1$(7 - a)^2 + 4^2 = 25$, so $(7 - a)^2 = 9$ and $7 - a = \pm 3$.
Step 2$a = 4$ or $a = 10$; there are two such points, one on each side.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

$A(-3, -2)$, $B(5, -2)$ and $C(5, 4)$ are three corners of rectangle $ABCD$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $D = (-3, 4)$
  2. $AB = 8$
  3. The diagonal $AC$ is 10.
  4. The area is 40.

Solution.

Step 1$D$ takes the $x$ of $A$ and the $y$ of $C$: $(-3, 4)$. $AB = 5 - (-3) = 8$ and $BC = 6$.
Step 2$AC = \sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = 10$, and the area is $8 \times 6 = 48$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
