Type 3 · The distance between two points

Example 4

Find the distance between $P(-3, 7)$ and $Q(5, -8)$.

Solution.

Step 1 shift in $x$: $5 - (-3) = 8$; shift in $y$: $-8 - 7 = -15$ subtract coordinates
Step 2 $\sqrt{8^2 + (-15)^2} = \sqrt{64 + 225} = \sqrt{289} = 17$ distance formula
Answer $PQ = 17$ units
Check from $Q$ to $P$ the shifts are $-8$ and $15$, and their squares are the same $64$ and $225$
Example 5

Which of these points is farthest from the origin?

  1. $(7, 1)$
  2. $(5, 5)$
  3. $(-6, 4)$
  4. $(0, -7)$

Solution.

Step 1 the larger distance has the larger square, so compare $x^2 + y^2$ no root needed
Step 2 $7^2 + 1^2 = 50$ and $5^2 + 5^2 = 50$
Step 3 $(-6)^2 + 4^2 = 52$ and $0^2 + (-7)^2 = 49$
Answer (c)

Option (d) has the largest single coordinate, but its other coordinate is $0$ and adds nothing. Options (a) and (b) are equally far from $O$, which is easy to miss without the squares. The negative coordinate in (c) makes no difference to the distance, because it is squared.
