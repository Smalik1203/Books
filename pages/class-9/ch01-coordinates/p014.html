End-of-Chapter Exercises
  1. What are the x-coordinate and the y-coordinate of the point where the two axes cross?
  1. The point $W$ has x-coordinate $-5$. A point $H$ lies on the line through $W$ parallel to the y-axis. What can you say about the coordinates of $H$? Which quadrants can it be in?
  1. Take the points $R (3, 0)$, $A (0, -2)$, $M (-5, -2)$ and $P (-5, 2)$, joined in that order. Before drawing anything, predict:
    1. two sides of $RAMP$ that are perpendicular to each other;
    2. one side of $RAMP$ that is parallel to an axis;
    3. two of the points that are mirror images of each other in one of the axes — and which axis that is.

    Now plot the points and check your predictions.

  1. Plot the point $Z (5, -6)$. Build a right-angled triangle $IZN$ on it, and find the lengths of all three sides. Your triangle need not match anybody else’s.
  1. What would a system of coordinates be like if we had no negative numbers? Could it still reach every point of a 2-D plane?
  1. Do the points $M (-3, -4)$, $A (0, 0)$ and $G (6, 8)$ lie on one straight line? Suggest a way of settling it without plotting the points and joining them up.
  1. Use your method from Question 6 on $R (-5, -1)$, $B (-2, -5)$ and $C (4, -12)$. Then plot both sets of points and see whether you were right.
