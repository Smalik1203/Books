Example 9 · Multiple correct

Consider $A(1, 0)$, $B(5, 3)$ and $C(-1, 11)$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $AB = 5$
  2. $BC = 10$
  3. Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $B$.
  4. Its area is 25.

Solution.

Step 1$AB = \sqrt{16 + 9} = 5$, $BC = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$ and $AC^2 = 4 + 121 = 125$.
Step 2$5^2 + 10^2 = 125 = AC^2$, so the right angle is at $B$, and the area is $\frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 10 = 25$.
Answer(a), (b), (c), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Points with integer coordinates lie exactly 5 units from the origin. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $(3, 4)$ is one of them.
  2. $(0, -5)$ is one of them.
  3. There are 12 of them.
  4. $(1, 4)$ is one of them.

Solution.

Step 1$x^2 + y^2 = 25$ holds for $(\pm 3, \pm 4)$, $(\pm 4, \pm 3)$, $(\pm 5, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 5)$: $4 + 4 + 2 + 2 = 12$ points.
Step 2$1 + 16 = 17$, so $(1, 4)$ is only $\sqrt{17}$ away.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
