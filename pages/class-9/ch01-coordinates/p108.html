Example 15 · Matching

Match each pair of points with the distance between them. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $(0, 0)$ to $(6, 8)$(1) 13
(Q) $(1, 2)$ to $(4, 6)$(2) 8
(R) $(-2, -3)$ to $(3, 9)$(3) 10
(S) $(2, -1)$ to $(2, 7)$(4) 5

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$\sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$, $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$ and $\sqrt{25 + 144} = 13$.
Step 2The last two points share $x = 2$, so the distance is $7 - (-1) = 8$.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
