Example 10

Find the two points that divide the segment from $A(-1, 4)$ to $B(8, -2)$ into three equal parts.

Solution.

Step 1 whole shift from $A$ to $B$: $8 - (-1) = 9$ across and $-2 - 4 = -6$ up
Step 2 one third of it: $9 \div 3 = 3$ and $-6 \div 3 = -2$ three equal steps
Step 3 first point: $-1 + 3 = 2$ and $4 + (-2) = 2$
Step 4 second point: $2 + 3 = 5$ and $2 + (-2) = 0$
Answer $P(2, 2)$ and $Q(5, 0)$
Check a third step gives $5 + 3 = 8$ and $0 + (-2) = -2$, which is $B$
Example 11

The midpoint of $P(a, 3)$ and $Q(5, b)$ is $M(2, -1)$. Then $(a, b)$ is

  1. $(-3, -4)$
  2. $(-1, -5)$
  3. $(-1, -4)$
  4. $(5, -5)$

Solution.

Step 1 across: $2 - a = 5 - 2$ shift $P$ to $M$ equals shift $M$ to $Q$
Step 2 $a = -1$
Step 3 up: $-1 - 3 = b - (-1)$ the same, for $y$
Step 4 $b = -5$
Answer (b)

Option (a) adds the two ends and forgets to halve: $a + 5 = 2$ and $3 + b = -1$ give $a = -3$ and $b = -4$. Option (c) gets $a$ right but stops at the shift $-1 - 3 = -4$ instead of the point it leads to. Option (d) turns one shift round: $a - 2 = 5 - 2$ gives $a = 5$, which would put $P$ directly above $Q$, with $M$ off to one side.
