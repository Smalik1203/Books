B9 · Foundation Test
  1. The distance between $(-2, 3)$ and $(4, -5)$ is
    1. $10$
    2. $8$
    3. $\sqrt{28}$
    4. $14$
  2. Find the point of the y-axis equidistant from $(-5, -2)$ and $(3, 2)$.
  3. $(a, 3)$ and $(4, b)$ have midpoint $(3, 5)$. Then $a + b$ is
    1. $7$
    2. $9$
    3. $11$
    4. $12$
  4. Find the point one third of the way from $(2, 3)$ to $(14, 9)$, and check it with two distances.
  5. The product of the coordinates of a point in Quadrant II is
    1. positive
    2. negative
    3. zero
    4. impossible to say
  6. Show that $A(3, 0)$, $B(6, 4)$, $C(-1, 3)$ form a triangle both isosceles and right-angled, naming the right angle.
  7. The points $(0, -10)$, $(6, 8)$ and $(-8, 6)$ lie on one circle centred at the origin. Give its radius, then decide where $(7, 7)$ lies with respect to that circle.
  8. The perimeter of the triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(3, 4)$ and $(-3, 4)$ is
    1. $14$
    2. $16$
    3. $18$
    4. $20$
  9. A distance does not depend on which point you call the first. Describe a situation in which the order does matter, and say what is being measured there.
  10. A rectangle has vertices $(1, 2)$, $(1, 8)$, $(9, 8)$, $(9, 2)$. Find a diagonal, and show both diagonals share a midpoint.
  11. In a city whose streets run only north–south and east–west, the walk from $(0, 0)$ to $(a, b)$, with $a$ and $b$ both positive, is
    1. $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$
    2. $a + b$
    3. $ab$
    4. $|a - b|$
  12. A screen is 800 pixels wide and 600 high, its origin at the bottom-left. An icon of radius 90 is centred at $(60, 500)$. Does any of it fall off, and which edges did you have to test?
  13. $A$ is $(2, 3)$ and $B$ is $(10, 9)$. Find every point of the x-axis that is equidistant from $A$ and $B$.
