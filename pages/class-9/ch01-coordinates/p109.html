  2. Apply $A (-2, -1)$, $B (1, 0)$, $C (4, 3)$ and $D (1, 2)$ are joined in that order. $ABCD$ is
    1. a square
    2. a rectangle that is not a square
    3. a parallelogram that is not a rectangle
    4. not a parallelogram

    Equal opposite sides make a parallelogram. A right angle at $B$ would need $AB^2 + BC^2 = AC^2$.

  3. Challenge Three villages stand at $A (0, 0)$, $B (8, 0)$ and $C (0, 6)$, in kilometres. A water tank is to be built at a point equidistant from all three. The tank stands at
