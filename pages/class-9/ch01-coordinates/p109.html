Type 7 · Right angles and shapes from their side lengths

Example 12

The points $A(2, 4)$, $B(5, 8)$, $C(9, 5)$ and $D(6, 1)$ are joined in that order (Fig. 1B.3). $ABCD$ is

  1. a square
  2. a rhombus that is not a square
  3. a rectangle that is not a square
  4. a parallelogram with no right angle

Solution.

Step 1 $AB^2$: $3^2 + 4^2 = 25$; $BC^2$: $4^2 + 3^2 = 25$ distance formula, squared
Step 2 $CD^2$: $3^2 + 4^2 = 25$; $DA^2$: $4^2 + 3^2 = 25$
Step 3 four equal sides, so $ABCD$ is a rhombus
Step 4 $AC^2$: $7^2 + 1^2 = 50$, and $25 + 25 = 50$ the diagonal $AC$
Step 5 $AB^2 + BC^2 = AC^2$, so the angle at $B$ is a right angle converse, from Class 8
Answer (a)

A rhombus with a right angle is a square. Option (b) stops at the four equal sides. Option (c) checks the right angle and not the sides, and option (d) the opposite sides alone.
