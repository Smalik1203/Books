Type 10 · Coordinates on a map

Example 16

On a town map, one unit is one kilometre and the streets run along the grid lines. A school stands at $S(2, 3)$ and a library at $L(8, 11)$. How far apart are they in a straight line, and how far is the shortest walk along the streets?

Solution.

Step 1 shifts: $8 - 2 = 6$ km east and $11 - 3 = 8$ km north
Step 2 straight line: $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{100} = 10$ distance formula
Step 3 along the streets: $6 + 8 = 14$ one shift, then the other
Answer $10$ km in a straight line; $14$ km along the streets

A walk along the grid lines is never shorter than the straight line, and is as short only when one of the two shifts is $0$.
