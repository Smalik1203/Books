Why the other options are wrong

B4 $AB = \sqrt{4 + 16} = 2\sqrt{5}$, $BC = \sqrt{1 + 4} = \sqrt{5}$ and $AC = \sqrt{9 + 36} = 3\sqrt{5}$. The two parts add to the whole, so the points are in line, but the parts are not equal: $B$ is two thirds of the way from $A$ to $C$. Option (d) assumes that three points in line must be evenly spaced.
B5 Arjun's $9$ is a real length, the walk along the grid lines, but it is not the distance between the two points. The distance is the straight line, and only the formula measures it. Option (c) treats two different lengths as the same thing.
C4 $AB = CD = \sqrt{10}$ and $BC = DA = \sqrt{18}$, so the opposite sides are equal, $ABCD$ is a parallelogram, and option (d) is out. But $AB^2 + BC^2 = 28$ while $AC^2 = 52$, so the angle at $B$ is not a right angle, and options (a) and (b) are out too.
C6 $625 = 25^2 + 0^2 = 7^2 + 24^2 = 15^2 + 20^2$. The first gives the four points on the axes. Each of the other two gives eight, with two orders and four choices of sign. Option (b) finds only one of the two ways with both squares non-zero.
