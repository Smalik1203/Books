Example 13

Prove that $A(1, 0)$, $B(5, 3)$ and $C(-1, 11)$ are the vertices of a right-angled triangle, and find its area.

Solution.

Step 1 $AB^2$: $(5 - 1)^2 + (3 - 0)^2 = 16 + 9 = 25$ distance formula, squared
Step 2 $BC^2$: $(-1 - 5)^2 + (11 - 3)^2 = 36 + 64 = 100$
Step 3 $AC^2$: $(-1 - 1)^2 + (11 - 0)^2 = 4 + 121 = 125$
Step 4 $25 + 100 = 125$, so $AB^2 + BC^2 = AC^2$
Step 5 the angle at $B$ is a right angle converse, from Class 8
Step 6 $AB = 5$ and $BC = 10$ are the base and height, so the area is $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 10 = 25$ the two shorter sides
Answer right-angled at $B$, with area $25$ square units

Type 8 · Reflection in an axis

Example 14

$P(-4, 3)$ is reflected in the y-axis to $P'$. Find $P'$, the length $PP'$, and the distance of each point from the origin.

Solution.

Step 1 $P'(4, 3)$ the x-coordinate changes sign
Step 2 $P$ and $P'$ share the y-coordinate $3$, so $PP'$ is $|4 - (-4)| = 8$ parallel to the x-axis
Step 3 $OP$: $\sqrt{(-4)^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5$
Step 4 $OP'$: $\sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5$
Answer $P'(4, 3)$; $PP'$ is $8$ units; each point is $5$ units from $O$

The y-axis crosses $PP'$ at $(0, 3)$, $4$ units from each end: a point and its image are the same distance from the mirror.
