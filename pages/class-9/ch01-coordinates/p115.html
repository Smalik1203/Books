Case-based questions

  1. A delivery app shows a town on a grid, with the railway station at the origin. One unit is one kilometre, and the streets run along the grid lines.

    PlaceCoordinates
    Shop$(3, 4)$
    Warehouse$(3, -2)$
    Customer$(-5, -2)$
    1. In which quadrant is the customer?
    2. How far is the shop from the station in a straight line?
    3. How far is the warehouse from the customer?
    4. A rider goes from the shop to the warehouse and then to the customer, along the streets. How far does the rider travel, and how far is the shop from the customer in a straight line?

  1. A classroom floor is drawn on axes with the origin at one corner. One unit is one metre.

    ItemCoordinates
    Corners of the floor$(0, 0)$, $(9, 0)$, $(9, 12)$, $(0, 12)$
    Teacher’s desk$(4.5, 11)$
    Anu’s desk$(1.5, 7)$
    1. Find the area of the floor.
    2. How far is Anu’s desk from the teacher’s desk?
    3. Find the length of a diagonal of the floor.
    4. The board is centred on the wall from $(0, 12)$ to $(9, 12)$. Where is its centre?
