Example 13

A cube has volume $p^3 + 6p^2q + 12pq^2 + 8q^3$ cubic units. What is the length of its side?

The side is whatever this expression is the cube of. Set it beside $a^3 + 3a^2b + 3ab^2 + b^3$ and look for $a$ and $b$:

$p^3 + 6p^2q + 12pq^2 + 8q^3 = p^3 + 3(p)^2(2q) + 3(p)(2q)^2 + (2q)^3$

$= (p + 2q)^3$

So $a = p$ and $b = 2q$, and the side is $p + 2q$ units.

Example 14

Write $8n^3 - 60n^2m + 150nm^2 - 125m^3$ in the form $(a - b)^3$. What are $a$ and $b$?

The alternating signs point to the difference identity. The two cubes at the ends are $8n^3 = (2n)^3$ and $125m^3 = (5m)^3$, so try $a = 2n$ and $b = 5m$:

$8n^3 - 60n^2m + 150nm^2 - 125m^3$

$= (2n)^3 - 3(2n)^2(5m) + 3(2n)(5m)^2 - (5m)^3$

$= (2n - 5m)^3$

The two middle terms confirm it: $3(2n)^2(5m) = 60n^2m$ and $3(2n)(5m)^2 = 150nm^2$.

Playing with what you have

New identities also come from multiplying things out and seeing what survives. Try these two products with the distributive property:

$(x - y)(x^2 + xy + y^2) \qquad\text{and}\qquad (x + y)(x^2 - xy + y^2)$

Take the first. Multiply every term of the second bracket by $x$, then by $-y$, and line the results up:

$(x - y)(x^2 + xy + y^2) = x^3 + x^2y + xy^2 - x^2y - xy^2 - y^3$

$= x^3 - y^3$

Everything in the middle cancels in pairs — $x^2y$ against $-x^2y$, and $xy^2$ against $-xy^2$ — and two terms are left.
