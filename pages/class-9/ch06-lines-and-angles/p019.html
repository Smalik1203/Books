  1. Prove that if a transversal cuts two parallel lines, the bisectors of a pair of corresponding angles are parallel to each other.
  1. In triangle $ABC$, $\angle A - \angle B = 15^\circ$ and $\angle B - \angle C = 25^\circ$. Find all three angles.
  1. A ladder leans against a wall, making $63^\circ$ with the level ground. The wall is vertical. What angle does the ladder make with the wall? Which fact of this chapter did you use?
  1. In triangle $ABC$, the bisector of $\angle A$ meets $BC$ at $D$. If $\angle B = 70^\circ$ and $\angle C = 50^\circ$, find $\angle ADB$ and $\angle ADC$, and check that they form a linear pair.
  1. Two lines $m$ and $n$ are cut by a transversal. A pair of co-interior angles are found to be $87^\circ$ and $91^\circ$. Are $m$ and $n$ parallel? If they meet, on which side of the transversal do they meet?
  1. In triangle $ABC$, the bisectors of the exterior angles at $B$ and $C$ meet at $O$. Prove that $\angle BOC = 90^\circ - \tfrac{1}{2}\angle A$. Compare this with the result of Example 10 and account for the difference.
  1. A line cuts two others so that the co-interior angles on one side add to $174^\circ$. Show that the two lines meet, and that they do so on that same side of the transversal.
  1. A triangle has all three angles equal to whole numbers of degrees, and the largest is exactly twice the smallest. How many such triangles are there?
