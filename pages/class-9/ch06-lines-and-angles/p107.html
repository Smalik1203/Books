Type 3 · Angles made by a transversal with parallel lines

Example 5

A transversal cuts two parallel lines, making alternate interior angles $(2x + 25)^\circ$ and $(3x - 5)^\circ$. Each of them is

  1. $30^\circ$
  2. $85^\circ$
  3. $95^\circ$
  4. $89^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1 $2x + 25 = 3x - 5$ alternate interior angles are equal
Step 2 $x = 30$
Step 3 $2(30) + 25 = 85$ and $3(30) - 5 = 85$
Answer (b)

Option (a) is $x$, not the angle. Option (c) is the co-interior angle at the same crossing. Option (d) comes from setting the sum to $180^\circ$, the co-interior rule: $5x + 20 = 180$ gives $x = 32$ and angles of $89^\circ$ and $91^\circ$. The name of the pair decides the equation.

Example 6

A transversal cuts two parallel lines. Two of the eight angles are unequal, and one is $40^\circ$ more than the other. Find both.

Solution.

Step 1 any two of the eight angles are equal or supplementary only two sizes occur
Step 2 these two are unequal, so $x + (x + 40^\circ) = 180^\circ$
Step 3 $2x = 140^\circ$, so $x = 70^\circ$
Answer $70^\circ$ and $110^\circ$

The question never says which pair the two angles form. It did not need to: once they are known to be unequal, supplementary is the only relation left.
