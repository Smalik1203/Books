Type 8 · Proofs about the angles of a triangle

Example 14

Prove that if one angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of the other two, the triangle is right-angled.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle A = \angle B + \angle C$ given
Step 2 $\angle A + \angle B + \angle C = 180^\circ$ angle sum
Step 3 $\angle A + \angle A = 180^\circ$ Step 1 in Step 2
Step 4 $\angle A = 90^\circ$
Answer The triangle has a right angle at $A$, so it is right-angled

The same fact can be read from the exterior angle theorem: the exterior angle at $A$ is $\angle B + \angle C$, which equals $\angle A$, and an angle equal to its own linear-pair partner is $90^\circ$.
