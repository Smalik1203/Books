Connect the Concepts
Two results of the chapter, one after the other.

Name both results as you go.

  1. Two lines cross and one of the four angles is $130^\circ$. The angle vertically opposite its neighbour is
    1. $50^\circ$
    2. $65^\circ$
    3. $130^\circ$
    4. $230^\circ$
  2. A transversal cuts two parallel lines, and one of the eight angles is $108^\circ$. The number of the eight that are $108^\circ$ is
    1. $2$
    2. $3$
    3. $4$
    4. $6$
  3. In triangle $ABC$ the exterior angle at $C$ is $110^\circ$ and $\angle A = 40^\circ$. Then $\angle C$ is
    1. $40^\circ$
    2. $70^\circ$
    3. $110^\circ$
    4. $140^\circ$
  4. A triangle has $\angle B = \angle C$, and its exterior angle at $A$ is $100^\circ$. Then $\angle B$ is
    1. $40^\circ$
    2. $50^\circ$
    3. $80^\circ$
    4. $100^\circ$
  5. Three angles round a point are $110^\circ$, $95^\circ$ and $x$. The supplement of $x$ is
    1. $25^\circ$
    2. $35^\circ$
    3. $155^\circ$
    4. $205^\circ$
  6. Lines $l$ and $n$ are each parallel to $m$. A transversal meets $l$ at $63^\circ$. The corresponding angle it makes with $n$ is
    1. $27^\circ$
    2. $63^\circ$
    3. $117^\circ$
    4. not fixed
  7. Two of the four angles round a point are right angles, and the other two are $4x$ and $5x$. Then $x$ is
    1. $20^\circ$
    2. $18^\circ$
    3. $36^\circ$
    4. $40^\circ$
  8. The exterior angles of triangle $ABC$ at $B$ and $C$ are $100^\circ$ and $130^\circ$. Then $\angle A$ is
    1. $30^\circ$
    2. $50^\circ$
    3. $70^\circ$
    4. $130^\circ$
  9. In triangle $ABC$ the bisectors of $\angle B$ and $\angle C$ meet at $O$. Prove that $\angle BOC = 90^\circ + \tfrac{1}{2}\angle A$, naming each result you use.
  10. Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. Prove that the bisectors of a pair of co-interior angles meet at right angles.
