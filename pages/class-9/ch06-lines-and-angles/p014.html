6.8The Angles of a Triangle

We can now do the thing promised on the first page. You have known since primary school that the three angles of a triangle add to $180^\circ$; you may even have torn the corners off a paper triangle and fitted them together along a line, which is a fine demonstration and not a proof. It shows that one triangle behaves that way. Here is why every triangle must.

Take any triangle $ABC$. Through the vertex $A$, draw the line $PQ$ parallel to the side $BC$. That is the construction, and it is the whole idea; the rest is bookkeeping with angles we already understand.
