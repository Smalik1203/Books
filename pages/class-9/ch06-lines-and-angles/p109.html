The Mixed Test
Everything these ten pages have covered, mixed.

No hints. Work straight through.

  1. An angle is $32^\circ$ more than its complement. It is
    1. $29^\circ$
    2. $58^\circ$
    3. $61^\circ$
    4. $74^\circ$
  2. A transversal cuts two parallel lines. Co-interior angles on one side are $5x$ and $4x$. Then $x$ is
    1. $18^\circ$
    2. $20^\circ$
    3. $36^\circ$
    4. $40^\circ$
  3. In a triangle, one angle equals the sum of the other two. The triangle is
    1. acute-angled
    2. right-angled
    3. obtuse-angled
    4. not determined
  4. Two angles of a triangle are equal and the third is $80^\circ$. Each of the equal angles is
    1. $40^\circ$
    2. $60^\circ$
    3. $100^\circ$
    4. $50^\circ$
  5. An exterior angle of a triangle equals one of its interior opposite angles. The other interior opposite angle is
    1. $0^\circ$
    2. $45^\circ$
    3. $90^\circ$
    4. impossible to say
  6. Two lines cross, and one angle is four times its neighbour. The smaller angle is
    1. $36^\circ$
    2. $45^\circ$
    3. $72^\circ$
    4. $144^\circ$
  7. The bisectors of a linear pair meet at
    1. $45^\circ$
    2. $60^\circ$
    3. $90^\circ$
    4. $180^\circ$
  8. In triangle $ABC$, $\angle B$ is twice $\angle A$ and $\angle C$ is three times $\angle A$. Find all three angles, and say which result you used to set up the equation.
  9. Two lines $l$ and $m$ are cut by a transversal so that a pair of alternate interior angles are $(3x - 20)^\circ$ and $(2x + 10)^\circ$. Find $x$, and say what you may — and may not — conclude about $l$ and $m$.
