Example 12

In triangle $ABC$, side $BC$ is produced to $D$, $\angle ACD = 115^\circ$ and $\angle A : \angle B = 2 : 3$. Find the three angles of the triangle.

Solution.

Step 1 $2k + 3k = 115^\circ$ exterior angle theorem
Step 2 $k = 23^\circ$, so $\angle A = 46^\circ$ and $\angle B = 69^\circ$
Step 3 $\angle ACB = 180^\circ - 115^\circ = 65^\circ$ linear pair
Answer $46^\circ$, $69^\circ$ and $65^\circ$
Check $46 + 69 + 65 = 180$

Example 13

In Fig. 6B.5, $AB \parallel CD$ and the point $E$ lies below both lines, with $\angle BAE = 70^\circ$ and $\angle DCE = 30^\circ$. Find $\angle AEC$.

Solution.

Step 1 draw $FE \parallel AB$, with $F$ on the side away from $B$; then $FE \parallel CD$ as well Section 6.7
Step 2 $\angle AEF = \angle BAE = 70^\circ$ alternate, $AB \parallel FE$
Step 3 $\angle CEF = \angle DCE = 30^\circ$ alternate, $CD \parallel FE$
Step 4 $\angle AEC = \angle AEF - \angle CEF$ $EC$ lies inside $\angle AEF$
Answer $\angle AEC = 70^\circ - 30^\circ = 40^\circ$

With $E$ between the lines, the two alternate angles were added. With $E$ outside them, both lie on the same side of $EF$, and one is taken from the other.
