Example 8

In a triangle the angles are $(x + 20)^\circ$, $(2x - 10)^\circ$ and $(3x + 5)^\circ$. Find them, and say what kind of triangle it is.

$(x + 20) + (2x - 10) + (3x + 5) = 180$
$6x + 15 = 180$, so $x = 27.5$

The angles are $47.5^\circ$, $45^\circ$ and $87.5^\circ$. All three are acute, so the triangle is acute-angled, and since no two are equal it is scalene.

Example 9

In triangle $ABC$, side $BC$ is produced to $D$. If $\angle ACD = 128^\circ$ and $\angle B = 47^\circ$, find $\angle A$ and $\angle ACB$.

The exterior angle at $C$ is the sum of the two interior opposite angles.

$\angle A + \angle B = 128^\circ$
$\angle A = 128^\circ - 47^\circ = 81^\circ$

And $\angle ACB$ makes a linear pair with $\angle ACD$, so it is $180^\circ - 128^\circ = 52^\circ$. Check: $81 + 47 + 52 = 180$. The check is not a formality — it catches a wrong subtraction at once.
