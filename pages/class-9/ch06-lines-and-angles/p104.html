The exterior angle at $B$ is $180^\circ - \angle B$, so its half is $90^\circ - \tfrac{1}{2}\angle B$, and the same at $C$. In triangle $BOC$ those two halves add to $180^\circ - \tfrac{1}{2}\left(\angle B + \angle C\right)$, which by the angle sum is $180^\circ - \tfrac{1}{2}(180^\circ - \angle A)$, or $90^\circ + \tfrac{1}{2}\angle A$. The third angle is what is left:

$$\angle BOC = 180^\circ - \left(90^\circ + \tfrac{1}{2}\angle A\right) = 90^\circ - \tfrac{1}{2}\angle A$$

The interior bisectors gave $90^\circ + \tfrac{1}{2}\angle A$ and the exterior ones give $90^\circ - \tfrac{1}{2}\angle A$. The two answers differ by $\angle A$, which is exactly what the two pairs of halves differ by, and the whole difference between the two questions came from replacing each angle by its supplement.

Look back at the five questions. Not one of them named a result, and not one of them needed a result the chapter had not already proved. Three of them turned on a total that was given indirectly — the angle sum, the straight angle, the supplementary pair — and were finished without ever finding the individual angles. The other two turned on drawing a line that was not there. That is most of what this chapter is for.

Problem 1 · Try it first

A transversal cuts two parallel lines, making alternate interior angles $(2x + 15)^\circ$ and $(3x - 25)^\circ$. Each of them is

  1. $40^\circ$
  2. $95^\circ$
  3. $85^\circ$
  4. $91^\circ$
Solution 1

The pair is named, and it is named alternate interior. Between parallel lines that pair is equal, so the two expressions are equal:

$2x + 15 = 3x - 25$,  so  $x = 40$

That is where option (a) comes from: $40$ is the value of $x$, not the size of the angle. Putting it back gives $2(40) + 15 = 95$ and $3(40) - 25 = 95$, which agree, so each angle is $95^\circ$ — option (b).

Option (c) is $85^\circ$, the co-interior angle at the same crossing. It belongs to the right figure and the wrong name: co-interior angles are supplementary, not equal. Option (d) is what that mistake produces if it is carried through, since $2x + 15 + 3x - 25 = 180$ gives $x = 38$ and an angle of $91^\circ$. Every wrong answer here comes of naming the pair wrongly, or of stopping at $x$.
