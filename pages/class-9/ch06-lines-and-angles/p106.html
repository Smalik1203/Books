Type 2 · Linear pairs, vertically opposite angles and angles round a point

Example 3

Five rays $OA$, $OB$, $OC$, $OD$ and $OE$ leave a point $O$ in that order. $\angle AOB = 2x$, $\angle BOC = 3x$, $\angle COD = 80^\circ$, $\angle DOE = 4x$ and $\angle EOA = 100^\circ$. Find $x$, and show that $A$, $O$ and $D$ lie in a straight line.

Solution.

Step 1 $2x + 3x + 80^\circ + 4x + 100^\circ = 360^\circ$ angles round a point
Step 2 $9x = 180^\circ$, so $x = 20^\circ$
Step 3 $\angle AOB + \angle BOC + \angle COD = 40^\circ + 60^\circ + 80^\circ = 180^\circ$
Step 4 turning from $OA$ to $OD$ is a turn of $180^\circ$, so $OD$ is the ray opposite $OA$ a straight angle
Answer $x = 20^\circ$, and $AOD$ is a straight line
Check on the other side, $\angle DOE + \angle EOA = 80^\circ + 100^\circ = 180^\circ$
Example 4

Lines $AB$ and $CD$ cross at $O$, and $\angle AOC = 70^\circ$. The ray $OE$ bisects $\angle BOD$. Find $\angle BOE$ and $\angle AOE$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle BOD = \angle AOC = 70^\circ$ vertically opposite
Step 2 $\angle BOE = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 70^\circ = 35^\circ$ $OE$ bisects it
Step 3 $\angle AOE = 180^\circ - 35^\circ = 145^\circ$ linear pair on $AB$
Answer $\angle BOE = 35^\circ$ and $\angle AOE = 145^\circ$
