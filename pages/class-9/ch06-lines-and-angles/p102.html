Think and Reason
Judge a claim — and the reason offered for it.

Questions 1–4 take this key. (a) both true, R explains A; (b) both true, R does not explain A; (c) A true, R false; (d) A false, R true.

  1. A. The three angles of a triangle add to $180^\circ$.  R. A line through one vertex parallel to the opposite side carries the other two up beside it, along a straight line.
  2. A. Two lines making equal corresponding angles with a transversal are parallel.  R. When two lines are parallel, corresponding angles are equal.
  3. A. Co-interior angles between parallel lines are equal.  R. Co-interior angles lie on the same side of the transversal.
  4. A. An exterior angle of a triangle is greater than either interior opposite angle.  R. It equals their sum, and no angle of a triangle is zero.
  5. A transversal cuts two parallel lines. Which must be true?
    1. Alternate interior angles are equal.
    2. Co-interior angles are equal.
    3. Corresponding angles are equal.
    1. (i) only
    2. (i) and (iii)
    3. (ii) and (iii)
    4. all three
  6. Two angles of a triangle are $40^\circ$ and $75^\circ$. Which must be true?
    1. The third angle is $65^\circ$.
    2. The exterior angle at that third vertex is $115^\circ$.
    3. The triangle is obtuse-angled.
    1. (i) only
    2. (ii) and (iii)
    3. (i) and (ii)
    4. all three
  7. Two adjacent angles add to $180^\circ$. Which must be true?
    1. They form a linear pair.
    2. Their outer arms lie in one straight line.
    3. Neither of them is reflex.
    1. (i) only
    2. (i) and (ii)
    3. (ii) and (iii)
    4. all three
  8. The corresponding-angles property is assumed, not proved. Say why something about parallel lines has to be.
