Multiple Choice · Level 2
Two steps, and a figure to settle first.

Choose the correct option.

  1. Two lines cross. One angle is $4x$ and the angle vertically opposite it is $2x + 40$. That angle is
    1. $80^\circ$
    2. $20^\circ$
    3. $40^\circ$
    4. $100^\circ$
  2. A linear pair is $3x$ and $2x$. The larger angle is
    1. $36^\circ$
    2. $72^\circ$
    3. $144^\circ$
    4. $108^\circ$
  3. A transversal cuts two parallel lines, making alternate interior angles $2x + 15$ and $3x - 25$. Each is
    1. $40^\circ$
    2. $95^\circ$
    3. $85^\circ$
    4. $115^\circ$
  4. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$. The largest is
    1. $40^\circ$
    2. $60^\circ$
    3. $80^\circ$
    4. $90^\circ$
  5. An exterior angle of a triangle is $3x$, and the two interior opposite angles are $x$ and $40^\circ$. The exterior angle is
    1. $20^\circ$
    2. $60^\circ$
    3. $80^\circ$
    4. $120^\circ$
  6. Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. The bisectors of a pair of co-interior angles meet at
    1. $45^\circ$
    2. $60^\circ$
    3. $120^\circ$
    4. $90^\circ$
  7. In triangle $ABC$, $\angle A = 60^\circ$ and the bisectors of $\angle B$ and $\angle C$ meet at $O$. Then $\angle BOC$ is
    1. $120^\circ$
    2. $60^\circ$
    3. $90^\circ$
    4. $150^\circ$
  8. Four angles round a point are in the ratio $1 : 2 : 3 : 4$. The largest is
    1. $72^\circ$
    2. $108^\circ$
    3. $144^\circ$
    4. $160^\circ$
  9. A transversal cuts two parallel lines and one pair of co-interior angles turns out to be equal. Each of them is
    1. $45^\circ$
    2. $90^\circ$
    3. $60^\circ$
    4. impossible
