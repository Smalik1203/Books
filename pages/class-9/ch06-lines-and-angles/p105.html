Type 1 · Complements and supplements

Example 1

The supplement of an angle is five times its complement. Find the angle.

Solution.

Step 1 let the angle be $x$; its supplement is $180^\circ - x$ and its complement is $90^\circ - x$
Step 2 $180^\circ - x = 5(90^\circ - x)$ as the question says
Step 3 $180^\circ - x = 450^\circ - 5x$, so $4x = 270^\circ$
Answer $x = 67.5^\circ$
Check supplement $112.5^\circ$, complement $22.5^\circ$, and $5 \times 22.5 = 112.5$
Example 2

Two angles are supplementary, and the larger is $38^\circ$ more than the smaller. The smaller angle is

  1. $71^\circ$
  2. $109^\circ$
  3. $142^\circ$
  4. $26^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1 $x + (x + 38^\circ) = 180^\circ$ supplementary
Step 2 $2x = 142^\circ$, so $x = 71^\circ$
Answer (a)

Option (b) is the larger angle, and (c) is $2x$, where the working stops one line early. Option (d) comes from reading the pair as complementary: $2x + 38 = 90$ gives $x = 26$.
