Case-based questions

  1. A school marks out a running track with two straight sections joined by two semicircular ends. The measurements of the inner edge are in the table.

    PartMeasurement
    each straight section$100$ m
    radius of each semicircular end$35$ m
    width of each lane$1.4$ m
    1. Find the length of one lap along the inner edge.
    2. How much longer is one lap along a line $1.4$ m further out?
    3. Find the area enclosed by the inner edge.
    4. In a race that runs round one semicircular end only, what stagger does a runner need, compared with the runner $1.4$ m further in?
