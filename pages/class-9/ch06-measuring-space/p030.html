  1. Three problems about fitting congruent shapes together:
    1. Rectangle $ABCD$ has sides $a$ and $b$, and rectangle $PQRS$ has sides $2a$ and $2b$. Show that $PQRS$ has $4$ times the area of $ABCD$. Does this mean that $4$ copies of $ABCD$ will fit into $PQRS$? Check and see!
    2. $\triangle ABC$ has sides $a$, $b$, $c$, and $\triangle PQR$ has sides $2a$, $2b$, $2c$. Show that $\triangle PQR$ has $4$ times the area of $\triangle ABC$. Does this mean that $4$ copies of $\triangle ABC$ will fit into $\triangle PQR$? Check and see!
    3. $\triangle ABC$ has sides $a$, $b$, $c$, and $\triangle PQR$ has sides $3a$, $3b$, $3c$. Show that $\triangle PQR$ has $9$ times the area of $\triangle ABC$. Does this mean that $9$ copies of $\triangle ABC$ will fit into $\triangle PQR$? Check and see!
