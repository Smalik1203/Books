Example 4 · Single correct

What is the area of a triangle with sides 5 cm, 5 cm and 6 cm?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 15 $\text{cm}^2$
  2. 30 $\text{cm}^2$
  3. 24 $\text{cm}^2$
  4. 12 $\text{cm}^2$

Solution.

Step 1$s = \frac{1}{2}(5 + 5 + 6) = 8$.
Step 2Area $= \sqrt{8 \times 3 \times 3 \times 2} = \sqrt{144} = 12$ $\text{cm}^2$.
Answer(d) 12 $\text{cm}^2$
Example 5 · Single correct

The radius of a circle is increased by 10%. By what percentage does its area increase?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 21%
  2. 10%
  3. 20%
  4. 11%

Solution.

Step 1The new radius is $1.1r$, so the new area is $\pi(1.1r)^2 = 1.21\pi r^2$.
Step 2The area grows by $0.21$ of itself, which is 21%.
Answer(a) 21%
Example 6 · Single correct

A sector of a circle of radius 6 cm has area $12\pi$ $\text{cm}^2$. How long is its arc?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $2\pi$ cm
  2. $4\pi$ cm
  3. $6\pi$ cm
  4. $8\pi$ cm

Solution.

Step 1$\pi \times 6^2 \times \frac{\theta}{360} = 12\pi$ gives $\theta = 120$, so the angle is $120^\circ$.
Step 2The arc is $2\pi \times 6 \times \frac{120}{360} = 4\pi$ cm.
Answer(b) $4\pi$ cm
