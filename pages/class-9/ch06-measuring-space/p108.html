Example 15 · Matching

The sides of each triangle are in cm. Match each with its area in $\text{cm}^2$. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) sides 5, 12, 13(1) 54
(Q) sides 10, 10, 12(2) 84
(R) sides 9, 12, 15(3) 30
(S) sides 10, 17, 21(4) 48

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$5^2 + 12^2 = 13^2$ and $9^2 + 12^2 = 15^2$: half base times height gives 30 and 54.
Step 2$\sqrt{16 \times 6 \times 6 \times 4} = 48$ and $\sqrt{24 \times 14 \times 7 \times 3} = 84$.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
