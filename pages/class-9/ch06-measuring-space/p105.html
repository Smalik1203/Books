Example 7 · Multiple correct

A circle has radius 17.5 cm. Which statements are correct? (Use $\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}$.)

Select all correct options.

  1. Its circumference is 110 cm.
  2. Its area is 962.5 $\text{cm}^2$.
  3. A semicircle of it has perimeter 55 cm.
  4. A quarter of its circumference is 27.5 cm.

Solution.

Step 1$C = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 17.5 = 110$ cm and $A = \frac{22}{7} \times 17.5^2 = 962.5$ $\text{cm}^2$.
Step 2A semicircle has perimeter $55 + 35 = 90$ cm, and $\frac{1}{4} \times 110 = 27.5$ cm.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

A triangle has sides 6 cm, 8 cm and 10 cm. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its area is 24 $\text{cm}^2$.
  2. Its semi-perimeter is 24 cm.
  3. Its altitude to the 10 cm side is 4.8 cm.
  4. Its area is 48 $\text{cm}^2$.

Solution.

Step 1$s = 12$, so the area is $\sqrt{12 \times 6 \times 4 \times 2} = \sqrt{576} = 24$ $\text{cm}^2$.
Step 2The altitude $h$ to the 10 cm side has $\frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times h = 24$, so $h = 4.8$ cm.
Answer(a), (c)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which statements about approximations of $\pi$ are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $\frac{22}{7} \gt \pi$
  2. $3.14 \lt \pi$
  3. $\frac{355}{113}$ is closer to $\pi$ than $\frac{22}{7}$ is.
  4. $\sqrt{10} \lt \pi$

Solution.

Step 1$\pi = 3.14159\ldots$ and $\frac{22}{7} = 3.14285\ldots$, so (a) and (b) hold.
Step 2$\frac{355}{113} = 3.1415929\ldots$ is far closer; $\sqrt{10} = 3.1622\ldots$ is more than $\pi$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
