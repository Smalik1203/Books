Example 10 · Multiple correct

A parallelogram has sides 10 cm and 6 cm. Which statements about its area are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It can be 30 $\text{cm}^2$.
  2. It can be 70 $\text{cm}^2$.
  3. It is at most 60 $\text{cm}^2$.
  4. It is 60 $\text{cm}^2$ only for a rectangle.

Solution.

Step 1On the 10 cm base the height is at most 6 cm, and is 6 cm only at a right angle.
Step 2So the area is at most $10 \times 6 = 60$ $\text{cm}^2$; a height of 3 cm gives 30 $\text{cm}^2$.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A chord of a circle of radius 14 cm subtends a right angle at the centre. Find the area of the minor segment, in $\text{cm}^2$. (Use $\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}$.)

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The sector is $\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 14^2 = 154$.
Step 2The triangle is $\frac{1}{2} \times 14 \times 14 = 98$, so the segment is $154 - 98 = 56$.
Answer56
Example 12 · Numerical answer

Find the area of a triangle with sides 9 cm, 10 cm and 17 cm, in $\text{cm}^2$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$s = \frac{1}{2}(9 + 10 + 17) = 18$.
Step 2Area $= \sqrt{18 \times 9 \times 8 \times 1} = \sqrt{1296} = 36$.
Answer36
