Area of a sector

If an arc $AB$ of a circle of radius $r$ subtends an angle $\theta^\circ$ at the centre $O$ (Fig. 6.38), the area of the sector $OAB$ is $\pi r^2 \times \dfrac{\theta^\circ}{360^\circ}$.

Look at how the rotational symmetry of the circle was used to find these formulas.

Related to a sector is another part of the region inside a circle, called a segment. A segment of a circle is the region bounded by an arc of the circle and the chord joining the ends of the arc. The smaller of the two segments cut off by a chord is the minor segment, and the larger is the major segment. In the same way, the sector with the smaller angle is the minor sector and the other is the major sector.

Exercise Set 6.3

Unless stated otherwise, use the approximation $\pi \approx \dfrac{22}{7}$.

  1. Find the area of a sector of a circle of radius $7$ cm when the angle of the sector is $60^\circ$.
  1. Find the area of a quadrant of a circle whose circumference is $44$ cm.
  1. The minute hand of a clock is $7$ cm long. Find the area swept by the minute hand in $10$ minutes.
  1. A chord of a circle of radius $10$ cm subtends a right angle at the centre. Find the area of
    1. the minor sector, which subtends $90^\circ$ at the centre
    2. the major sector, which subtends $270^\circ$ at the centre.
    (Use $\pi \approx 3.14$.)
  1. A chord of a circle of radius $15$ cm subtends an angle of $60^\circ$ at the centre. Find the areas of the minor and major segments of the circle. (Use $\pi \approx 3.14$ and $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.73$.)
  1. A car has two wipers, which do not overlap. Each wiper has a blade $28$ cm long and sweeps through an angle of $120^\circ$. Find the total area cleaned at each sweep of the blades.
  1. A chord of a circle of radius $r$ subtends an angle of $60^\circ$ at the centre. Show that the area of the minor segment is $r^2\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$.
  1. An equilateral triangle is inscribed in a circle of radius $r$. Show that the ratio of the area of the triangle to the area of the circle is $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{4\pi} \approx 0.413$.
  1. A square is inscribed in a circle of radius $r$. Show that the ratio of the area of the square to the area of the circle is $\dfrac{2}{\pi} \approx 0.637$.
