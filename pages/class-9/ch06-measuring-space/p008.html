Length of an arc

If an arc $AB$ of a circle of radius $r$ subtends an angle $\theta^\circ$ at the centre $O$, the length of the arc is $2\pi r \times \dfrac{\theta^\circ}{360^\circ}$.

A closer look at a 400 m athletics track

Fig. 6.11 shows a $400$ m athletics track. It has two straight sections, each $84.39$ m long, and two curved ends. Each end is made of semicircles with a common centre, $A$ at one end and $B$ at the other, and the innermost semicircle at each end has radius $36.5$ m. Each lane is $1.22$ m wide.

An athlete runs one complete lap of the track, keeping $0.3$ m from the inner border. What total distance does she run?

Step 1 the two straights: $2 \times 84.39 = 168.78$ m
Step 2 the two ends are semicircles of radius $36.5 + 0.3 = 36.8$ m, which together make one full circle
Step 3 its circumference: $2 \times \pi \times 36.8 = 2 \times 3.1416 \times 36.8 = 231.22$ m to two decimal places
Answer $168.78 + 231.22 = 400$ m
