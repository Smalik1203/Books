Example 2

In Fig. 6.13, two paths join the points $P$ and $Q$. The first path is the semicircle $a$. The second is made of three semicircles, $b$, $c$ and $d$. Which path is longer? Choose one: (i) path $a$ is longer; (ii) path $b + c + d$ is longer; (iii) the two paths have equal length.

Let the radii of the semicircles $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ be $a'$, $b'$, $c'$ and $d'$.

Solution.

Step 1 the semicircle $a$ has length $\frac{1}{2}(2\pi a') = \pi a'$; in the same way $b$, $c$, $d$ have lengths $\pi b'$, $\pi c'$, $\pi d'$
Step 2 the first path is $\pi a'$ long and the second is $\pi(b' + c' + d')$ long
Step 3 $PQ = 2a'$, and also $PQ = 2b' + 2c' + 2d'$, so $a' = b' + c' + d'$ two ways of measuring $PQ$
Answer (iii) The two paths have equal length.
