Example 1 · Single correct

A wheel of radius 35 cm turns 200 times as it rolls along a straight road. How far does it roll? (Use $\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}$.)

Choose one correct option.

  1. 440 m
  2. 220 m
  3. 880 m
  4. 44 m

Solution.

Step 1One turn covers one circumference: $2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 35 = 220$ cm.
Step 2200 turns cover $200 \times 220 = 44\,000$ cm, which is 440 m.
Answer(a) 440 m
Example 2 · Single correct

An arc of a circle of radius 21 cm is 22 cm long. What angle does it subtend at the centre? (Use $\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}$.)

Choose one correct option.

  1. $30^\circ$
  2. $60^\circ$
  3. $90^\circ$
  4. $120^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1The whole circumference is $2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 21 = 132$ cm.
Step 2The arc is $\frac{22}{132} = \frac{1}{6}$ of it, so the angle is $\frac{1}{6} \times 360^\circ = 60^\circ$.
Answer(b) $60^\circ$
Example 3 · Single correct

A circle has area 7546 $\text{cm}^2$. What is its circumference? (Use $\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}$.)

Choose one correct option.

  1. 98 cm
  2. 154 cm
  3. 308 cm
  4. 616 cm

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{22}{7}r^2 = 7546$ gives $r^2 = 2401$, so $r = 49$ cm.
Step 2$C = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 49 = 308$ cm.
Answer(c) 308 cm
