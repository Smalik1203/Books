1. Assertion (A): A median of a triangle divides it into two triangles of equal area.

    Reason (R): The two triangles made by a median are always congruent.

  1. Assertion (A): $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

    Reason (R): $\pi$ is an irrational number.

  1. Assertion (A): A circle of radius $21$ cm has area $1386$ cm$^2$.

    Reason (R): The ratio of the circumference to the diameter is the same for every circle.

Very short answer
  1. A circle has area $5544$ cm$^2$. Find its radius.
  1. An arc subtends $72^\circ$ at the centre of a circle of radius $35$ cm. Find its length.
  1. Find the area of an equilateral triangle of side $8$ cm. Leave $\sqrt{3}$ in your answer.
Short answer
  1. A horse is tied to a peg at one corner of a square field of side $20$ m, with a rope $14$ m long. Find the area of the field that the horse can graze.
  1. The sides of a triangular field are $26$ m, $28$ m and $30$ m. Find its area, and the cost of levelling it at ₹$5$ per m$^2$.
  1. A circular running track is the ring between two circles with the same centre. The inner circle has radius $70$ m and the track is $7$ m wide. How much longer is one lap along the outer edge than along the inner edge? Find the area of the track.
  1. In the 4-gon $ABCD$, $AB = 9$ cm, $BC = 40$ cm, $CD = 28$ cm, $DA = 15$ cm and $\angle ABC = 90^\circ$. Find its area.
Long answer
  1. $ABCD$ is a trapezium with $AB \parallel DC$, and its diagonals meet at $O$. Prove that the triangles $AOD$ and $BOC$ have equal areas.
  1. A chord of a circle of radius $12$ cm subtends an angle of $120^\circ$ at the centre. Find the area of the minor segment. (Use $\pi \approx 3.14$ and $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.73$.)
  1. A triangle has sides $13$ cm, $14$ cm and $15$ cm. Find its area, the radius $r$ of its incircle and the radius $R$ of its circumcircle, using the formulas of Section 6.8.1.
