Example 13 · Numerical answer

An isosceles trapezium has parallel sides 4 cm and 10 cm, and each slanting side is 5 cm. Use Brahmagupta’s formula to find its area, in $\text{cm}^2$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1An isosceles trapezium is cyclic, and $s = \frac{1}{2}(4 + 5 + 10 + 5) = 12$.
Step 2Area $= \sqrt{(12 - 4)(12 - 5)(12 - 10)(12 - 5)} = \sqrt{784} = 28$.
Answer28
Example 14 · Matching

A circle has radius 21 cm. Match List I with List II. (Use $\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}$.) Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) circumference (cm)(1) 346.5
(Q) area of a semicircle ($\text{cm}^2$)(2) 132
(R) perimeter of a semicircle (cm)(3) 108
(S) area of a quadrant ($\text{cm}^2$)(4) 693

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  2. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2

Solution.

Step 1$C = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 21 = 132$; the area is $\frac{22}{7} \times 21^2 = 1386$.
Step 2Semicircle: area 693, perimeter $66 + 42 = 108$. Quadrant: 346.5.
Answer(b) P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
