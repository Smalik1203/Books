Rationals on the number line

Integers are easy to place. Mark a point and call it 0, the origin; step one unit right for 1, another for 2, and left for $-1$, $-2$, and so on, each the same distance from the next. Rational numbers go on the same line, but they will generally fall between the integers, and the recipe has to say where.

To place $\dfrac{p}{q}$, cut the unit interval into $q$ equal parts, then step $p$ of those parts from 0 — right if the number is positive, left if it is negative. The denominator chooses the size of the step; the numerator says how many to take.
