Example 4

Which pair names the same rational number?

  1. $\dfrac{-3}{8}$, $\dfrac{6}{16}$
  2. $\dfrac{4}{-6}$, $\dfrac{-10}{15}$
  3. $\dfrac{5}{12}$, $\dfrac{15}{24}$
  4. $\dfrac{-2}{7}$, $\dfrac{6}{21}$

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{a}{b} = \dfrac{c}{d}$ exactly when $ad = bc$ the equality law
Step 2 for (b): $ad = 4 \times 15 = 60$ and $bc = (-6) \times (-10) = 60$ equal, so the same number
Step 3 for (a): $(-3) \times 16 = -48$ but $8 \times 6 = 48$ the signs differ
Answer (b)

Option (d) fails the same way as (a): $(-2) \times 21 = -42$ and $7 \times 6 = 42$. Option (c) gives $5 \times 24 = 120$ against $12 \times 15 = 180$; it doubled the bottom and tripled the top.

Type 3 · Absolute value and distance

Example 5

Find the two numbers on the number line whose distance from $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is $\dfrac{3}{4}$. Check each with $|a - b|$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{2}{4} + \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{5}{4}$ step right
Step 2 $\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{2}{4} - \dfrac{3}{4} = -\dfrac{1}{4}$ step left
Answer $\dfrac{5}{4}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{4}$
Check $\left|\dfrac{5}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2}\right| = \left|\dfrac{3}{4}\right| = \dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\left|-\dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2}\right| = \left|-\dfrac{3}{4}\right| = \dfrac{3}{4}$

A distance has no direction, so every distance but $0$ belongs to two numbers, one on each side.
