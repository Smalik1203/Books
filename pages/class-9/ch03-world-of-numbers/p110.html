Selected solutions

B5.4 hint One digit does not repeat and one does, so two multipliers are needed and they do different jobs.
solution $10x = 23.5\overline{5}$ and $100x = 235.5\overline{5}$, so $90x = 212$ and $x = \dfrac{212}{90} = \dfrac{106}{45}$.
key idea The denominator records the decimal's shape — a $9$ per repeating digit, a $10$ per digit that does not.
B7.2 hint Assume the opposite, and square it.
solution If $\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} = r$ were rational, squaring gives $5 + 2\sqrt{6} = r^2$, so $\sqrt{6} = \frac{r^2 - 5}{2}$ — rational, which it is not.
key idea Squaring turns a sum of two roots into one.
Foundation takeaways
  1. A decimal is a signature: terminating or repeating means rational, and nothing else does.
  2. The prime factors of the denominator — in lowest terms — decide which of the two happens.
  3. Rational $+$ irrational is settled; irrational $+$ irrational is not.
  4. Assume the opposite and square: it is the shortest route through most surd questions.
  5. No measurement can prove a number irrational. Only an argument can.
