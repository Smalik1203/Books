Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which statements about the numbers at a distance $\frac{3}{4}$ from $\frac{1}{2}$ on the number line are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $\frac{5}{4}$ is one of them.
  2. $-\frac{1}{4}$ is one of them.
  3. $\frac{1}{4}$ is one of them.
  4. Their sum is 1.

Solution.

Step 1They are $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{4} = \frac{5}{4}$ and $\frac{1}{2} - \frac{3}{4} = -\frac{1}{4}$.
Step 2Their sum is 1, twice $\frac{1}{2}$, since $\frac{1}{2}$ is their midpoint.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Which of these lie strictly between $0.\overline{2}$ and $0.25$?

Select all correct options.

  1. 0.23
  2. 0.24
  3. 0.22
  4. 0.25

Solution.

Step 1$0.\overline{2} = 0.2222\ldots$, so 0.23 and 0.24 lie between.
Step 2$0.22 \lt 0.222\ldots$, and 0.25 is an end, not between.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A submarine is at a depth of 120 m, written $-120$ m. It rises 45 m and then dives 70 m. How many metres below the surface is it now?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$-120 + 45 - 70 = -145$, so it is 145 m below the surface.
Answer145
