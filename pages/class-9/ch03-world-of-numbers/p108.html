Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{8}$(1) 3
(Q) $(\sqrt{5})^2$(2) 1
(R) $(2 + \sqrt{3})(2 - \sqrt{3})$(3) 4
(S) $\frac{\sqrt{18}}{\sqrt{2}}$(4) 5

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{8} = \sqrt{16} = 4$ and $(\sqrt{5})^2 = 5$.
Step 2$4 - 3 = 1$ and $\sqrt{\frac{18}{2}} = \sqrt{9} = 3$.
Answer(b) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
