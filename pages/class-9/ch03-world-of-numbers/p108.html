The Trap Room
Two students, one question, and a mistake.

Answer (a) A only, (b) B only, (c) both, (d) neither — then say in one line what went wrong.

  1. A. $\dfrac{22}{7}$ is irrational, because it is $\pi$.  B. It is rational, and it is not $\pi$.
  1. A. $0.\overline{9}$ is a little less than $1$: however far you go, a bit is missing.  B. $0.\overline{9}$ and $1$ are the same number.
  1. A. $\sqrt{16}$ is $4$, and rational.  B. It is irrational — it has a root sign.
  1. A. $\dfrac{6}{15}$ cannot terminate, because $15$ has a factor of $3$.  B. It terminates: in lowest terms it is $\dfrac{2}{5}$.
  1. A. $|x| = x$ exactly when $x$ is not negative.  B. $|x| = x$ for every $x$, since $|x|$ is never negative.
  1. A. A rational plus an irrational is always irrational.  B. Two irrationals may add to a rational.
  1. Between $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$: A. There are infinitely many rationals.  B. There are finitely many — over $6$ they are $\dfrac{2}{6}$ and $\dfrac{3}{6}$, with nothing between.
