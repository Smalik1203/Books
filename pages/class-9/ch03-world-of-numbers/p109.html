B9 · Foundation Test
  1. Which of these is rational?
    1. $\sqrt{3}$
    2. $\sqrt{0.09}$
    3. $\pi$
    4. $\sqrt{2} + 1$
  2. Write $0.\overline{18}$ in the form $\dfrac{p}{q}$, in lowest terms.
  3. The decimal expansion of $\dfrac{11}{80}$
    1. terminates after $2$ places
    2. terminates after $3$ places
    3. terminates after $4$ places
    4. repeats
  4. Prove that $\sqrt{7}$ is irrational.
  5. $\left|-3\right| \times \left|5 - 9\right|$ equals
    1. $12$
    2. $8$
    3. $2$
    4. $-12$
  6. Find four rationals strictly between $\dfrac{2}{7}$ and $\dfrac{3}{7}$, and say how you chose the denominator.
  7. Without dividing, decide which of $\dfrac{9}{16}$, $\dfrac{5}{12}$, $\dfrac{7}{80}$ and $\dfrac{11}{45}$ terminate, with reasons.
  8. The repeating block of $\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{7}$ has length
    1. $1$
    2. $3$
    3. $6$
    4. $21$
  9. A ledger reads $-450$, $+1200$, $-380$, $-90$. Where does the trader stand, does the order matter, and which law says so?
  10. A number $\dfrac{p}{q}$ in lowest terms has a repeating decimal exactly when
    1. $q$ is prime
    2. $q$ is odd
    3. $q$ has a prime factor other than $2$ and $5$
    4. $p$ and $q$ are co-prime
  11. Show that $\tfrac{1}{2}(a + b)$ lies strictly between $a$ and $b$ when $a \lt b$, and use it three times to find three rationals between $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
  12. Let $x = 0.\overline{36}$ and $y = 0.\overline{63}$. Find $x + y$ in lowest terms, and say what it tells you about $0.\overline{99}$.
