Example 12 · Numerical answer

How many numbers of the form $\frac{k}{24}$, with $k$ a whole number, lie strictly between $\frac{1}{3}$ and $\frac{3}{4}$?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{1}{3} = \frac{8}{24}$ and $\frac{3}{4} = \frac{18}{24}$, so $k = 9, 10, \ldots, 17$: 9 numbers.

Example 13 · Numerical answer

Baudhāyana gave $\frac{577}{408}$ for $\sqrt{2}$. Given $\left(\frac{577}{408}\right)^2 - 2 = \frac{1}{N}$, find $N$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$577^2 = 332\,929$ and $2 \times 408^2 = 2 \times 166\,464 = 332\,928$.
Step 2So the difference is $\frac{1}{408^2} = \frac{1}{166\,464}$: the square is too big by very little.

Example 14 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $0.\overline{3}$(1) $\frac{3}{8}$
(Q) $0.1\overline{6}$(2) $\frac{1}{7}$
(R) $0.375$(3) $\frac{1}{3}$
(S) $0.\overline{142857}$(4) $\frac{1}{6}$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2

Solution.

Step 1$0.\overline{3} = \frac{3}{9} = \frac{1}{3}$ and $0.1\overline{6} = \frac{16 - 1}{90} = \frac{1}{6}$.
Step 2$0.375 = \frac{375}{1000} = \frac{3}{8}$ and $0.\overline{142857} = \frac{142857}{999999} = \frac{1}{7}$.
Answer(d) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
