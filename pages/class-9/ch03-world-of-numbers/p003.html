Example 1

A merchant at Lothal trades spices for copper. He is offered 15 ingots for every 2 bags of spices. He brings 12 bags to the market. How many ingots does he leave with?

Nothing here is a single multiplication, so find what is fixed first. What does not change is the rate: 15 ingots for every 2 bags, whatever the size of the load.

the rate 15 ingots for every 2 bags
how many such lots $12 \div 2$ 6 lots
ingots in all $6 \times 15$ 90

He leaves with 90 ingots. Check it the other way round before trusting it: $90 \div 12 = 7.5$ ingots per bag, and the offered rate was $15 \div 2 = 7.5$ per bag. The two agree, so the rate has been kept.
