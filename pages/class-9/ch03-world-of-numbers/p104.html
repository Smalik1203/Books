Example 3 · Single correct

What kind of decimal expansion has $\frac{11}{80}$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. It terminates after 2 places.
  2. It repeats for ever.
  3. It terminates after 4 places.
  4. It terminates after 3 places.

Solution.

Step 1$80 = 2^4 \times 5$, with no prime but 2 and 5, so the decimal terminates.
Step 2$\frac{11}{80} = \frac{11 \times 125}{10\,000} = 0.1375$: four places.
Answer(c) It terminates after 4 places.
Example 4 · Single correct

$0.\overline{123}$ in lowest terms is

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{61}{495}$
  2. $\frac{122}{990}$
  3. $\frac{123}{1000}$
  4. $\frac{41}{333}$

Solution.

Step 1Three digits repeat, so $0.\overline{123} = \frac{123}{999}$.
Step 2$\frac{123}{999} = \frac{41}{333}$ on dividing by 3.
Answer(d) $\frac{41}{333}$
Example 5 · Single correct

$2.3\overline{5}$ in lowest terms is

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{106}{45}$
  2. $\frac{47}{20}$
  3. $\frac{212}{99}$
  4. $\frac{235}{99}$

Solution.

Step 1Let $x = 2.3\overline{5}$. Then $100x - 10x = 235.\overline{5} - 23.\overline{5} = 212$.
Step 2$x = \frac{212}{90} = \frac{106}{45}$.
Answer(a) $\frac{106}{45}$
