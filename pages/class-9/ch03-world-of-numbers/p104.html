Example 2

$(-8) \times (-3) - 5 \times (-4) + 0 \times (-7)$ equals

  1. $4$
  2. $37$
  3. $44$
  4. $-4$

Solution.

Step 1 $(-8) \times (-3) = 24$ a debt times a debt
Step 2 $5 \times (-4) = -20$, and taking away $-20$ adds $20$ subtracting a negative
Step 3 $0 \times (-7) = 0$ multiplying by zero
Step 4 $24 + 20 + 0 = 44$
Answer (c)

Option (a) subtracts $20$ instead of adding it: $24 - 20 = 4$. Option (b) reads $0 \times (-7)$ as $-7$: $44 - 7 = 37$. Option (d) makes a debt times a debt a debt: $-24 + 20 = -4$.

Example 3

Find the rational number $x$ for which $\dfrac{2}{3}x - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{5}{6}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{2}{3}x = \dfrac{5}{6} + \dfrac{1}{4}$ add $\tfrac{1}{4}$ to both sides
Step 2 $\dfrac{5}{6} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{10}{12} + \dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{13}{12}$ LCM of 6 and 4 is 12
Step 3 $x = \dfrac{13}{12} \div \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{13}{12} \times \dfrac{3}{2} = \dfrac{39}{24}$ dividing by a fraction
Answer $x = \dfrac{13}{8}$
Check $\dfrac{2}{3} \times \dfrac{13}{8} - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{13}{12} - \dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{10}{12} = \dfrac{5}{6}$
