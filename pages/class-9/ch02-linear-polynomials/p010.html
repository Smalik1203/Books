$$\text{tiles at Stage } n = 2n - 1$$

which is a polynomial of degree $1$ — a linear polynomial. That is the whole pattern in four characters, and it is the first time in this chapter that an expression has been found rather than handed over. The relationship between the stage number and the tile count is a linear relationship.

Now go the other way. Which stage has $21$ tiles? Which has $47$? Is there a stage with $50$?

A pattern need not grow. The next one shrinks, and the arithmetic is identical apart from a sign.

Example 7

Bela starts the month with ₹$100$ of pocket money and spends ₹$5$ a day. After how many days is she left with ₹$40$?

2$100 - 2 \times 5 = 90$
3$100 - 3 \times 5 = 85$
4$100 - 4 \times 5 = 80$

On day $n$ she has ₹$(100 - 5n)$ left. Setting that equal to $40$ gives $5n = 60$, so $n = 12$: on the twelfth day she is down to ₹$40$.
