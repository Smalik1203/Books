Why the other options are wrong

B3 R is true, but A is false. Two readings taken at the same $x$ give the same equation twice, and two copies of one equation fix nothing. The readings have to differ in $x$. Then, and only then, do two readings fix $a$ and $b$.
B5 Equal differences of $7$ fix the slope and nothing else, so (i) is true and (ii) is not. The rule is $7t + b$ for some $b$, and any one reading gives $b$. So (iii) is true: the answer is (c), not (a).
C1 Equal steps in $x$ mean equal rises in $y$, so $(3k - 2) - k = 16 - (3k - 2)$. That is $2k - 2 = 18 - 3k$, so $5k = 20$ and $k = 4$. The points are $(1, 4)$, $(4, 10)$ and $(7, 16)$, on the line $y = 2x + 2$. Option (b) makes the first rise $4$ and the second $9$.
C6 The first line has slope $8 \div 4 = 2$, so the second is $y = 2x + b$ with $-1 = 8 + b$, and $b = -9$. It crosses the $x$-axis where $2x - 9 = 0$, at $(4.5, 0)$. Option (a) is the $y$-crossing. Option (c) forgets to divide by the slope.
