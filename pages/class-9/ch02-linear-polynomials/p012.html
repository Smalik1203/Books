How far can you travel for ₹$130$?

In every example of this section the $n$th term has turned out to be a linear expression in $n$, and the sequence of terms a linear pattern. Sequences of this kind are taken further in the chapter on Sequences and Progressions.

Exercise Set 2.3
  1. A student has ₹$500$ in her savings account and adds ₹$150$ of pocket money every month. How much has she at the end of each month from the second onwards? Write a linear expression for the amount in the $n$th month.
  1. A rally starts with $120$ members and loses $9$ of them every hour. How many are left after $1$, $2$, $3$, … hours? Write a linear expression for the number left at the end of the $n$th hour.
  1. A rectangle is $13$ cm long. Find its area when the breadth is
    1. $12$ cm
    2. $10$ cm
    3. $8$ cm
    Then write the linear pattern the areas follow.
  1. A box is $7$ cm long and $11$ cm broad. Find its volume when the height is
    1. $5$ cm
    2. $9$ cm
    3. $13$ cm
    Then write the linear pattern the volumes follow.
  1. Sarita is reading a book of $500$ pages at $20$ pages a day. How many pages are left after $15$ days? Express the number left as a linear pattern.
