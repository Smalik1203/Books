  1. The degree of the polynomial $7 - 4x$ is
    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $4$
    4. $7$
  2. The value of $3x - 8$ at $x = -2$ is
    1. $-14$
    2. $-2$
    3. $2$
    4. $14$
  3. The slope of the line $y = 5 - 2x$ is
    1. $5$
    2. $2$
    3. $-2$
    4. $-5$
  4. The line $y = 4x - 7$ meets the y-axis at
    1. $(0, 4)$
    2. $(0, -7)$

    4. $(7, 0)$
  5. Which of these sequences is a linear pattern?
    1. $2, 4, 8, 16$

    2. $12d + 30$
    3. $42d$
    4. $12(d + 30)$
  7. The lines $y = 3x + 2$ and $y = 3x - 5$ are

    2. parallel

  8. If $p(x) = 2x + 9$ and $p(k) = 1$, then $k$ is
    1. $-5$
    2. $-4$
    3. $4$
    4. $5$
  9. A tank holds water $3$ m deep and loses $0.5$ m a month. By the formula, it is empty after
    1. $3$ months
    2. $5$ months
    3. $6$ months
    4. $8$ months
  10. A pattern of tiles has $2n - 1$ tiles at stage $n$. The stage with $47$ tiles is
    1. $23$
    2. $24$
    3. $46$
