Example 2

The degree of $4 - 3y^{2} + 2y^{3} - y$, and the coefficient of $y^{2}$ in it, are

  1. $3$ and $-3$
  2. $3$ and $3$
  3. $2$ and $-3$
  4. $4$ and $2$

Solution.

Step 1 the powers of $y$ in the four terms are $0$, $2$, $3$ and $1$
Step 2 the highest of them is $3$, so the degree is $3$ the highest power
Step 3 the term in $y^{2}$ is $-3y^{2}$, so its coefficient is $-3$ the sign goes with it
Answer (a)

Option (b) drops the minus sign. Option (c) takes the degree from $-3y^{2}$, the term written second. Option (d) reads the constant $4$ as the degree, and $2$, the coefficient of $y^{3}$, as the one asked for.

Type 2 · Finding the value of a polynomial

Example 3

For $p(x) = 3x^{2} - 5x + 2$, find $p(0)$, $p(2)$ and $p(-1)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $p(0) = 3(0)^{2} - 5(0) + 2 = 2$ only the constant is left
Step 2 $p(2) = 3(2)^{2} - 5(2) + 2 = 12 - 10 + 2 = 4$
Step 3 $p(-1) = 3(-1)^{2} - 5(-1) + 2 = 3 + 5 + 2 = 10$ $(-1)^{2} = 1$
Answer $p(0) = 2$, $p(2) = 4$ and $p(-1) = 10$
