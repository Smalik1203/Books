Example 2 · Single correct

For $p(x) = 3x^2 - 5x + 2$, what is $p(-1)$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 0
  2. 10
  3. 4
  4. 6

Solution.

Step 1$p(-1) = 3(-1)^2 - 5(-1) + 2 = 3 + 5 + 2 = 10$.
Answer(b) 10
Example 3 · Single correct

A linear polynomial has $p(2) = 7$ and $p(5) = 16$. What is $p(0)$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 3
  2. 4
  3. 1
  4. 10

Solution.

Step 1From $x = 2$ to $x = 5$ the value rises by 9, so it rises by 3 for each 1: $p(x) = 3x + b$.
Step 2$p(2) = 6 + b = 7$ gives $b = 1$, so $p(0) = 1$.
Answer(c) 1
Example 4 · Single correct

The points $(1, 2)$, $(4, 11)$ and $(6, k)$ lie on one straight line. What is $k$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 14
  2. 18
  3. 15
  4. 17

Solution.

Step 1From $(1, 2)$ to $(4, 11)$, $y$ rises 9 while $x$ rises 3: a slope of 3.
Step 2From $x = 4$ to $x = 6$, $y$ rises 6 more: $k = 17$.
Answer(d) 17
