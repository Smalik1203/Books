Example 9

A journey of $d$ km costs $C(d) = 100 + 60d$ rupees. Tabulate the cost as $d$ runs from $0$ to $5$.

Distance $d$ (km)Cost $C(d)$ (₹)
0100

5400

Every extra kilometre adds ₹$60$ — the same ₹$60$ each time. This is linear growth.
