Assertion and reason

In Questions 17 to 20, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): The graph of $y = 5x$ passes through the origin.

    Reason (R): Putting $x = 0$ in $y = ax$ gives $y = 0$, whatever $a$ is.

  1. Assertion (A): The graphs of $y = 4x - 3$ and $y = -4x - 3$ meet on the $y$-axis.

    Reason (R): The two lines have slopes $4$ and $-4$.

  1. Assertion (A): Any two readings of $y = ax + b$ fix $a$ and $b$.

    Reason (R): Two unknowns need two independent equations to fix them.

  1. Assertion (A): A linear polynomial $ax + b$ has exactly one zero.

    Reason (R): In a linear polynomial, $a$ may be $0$.
