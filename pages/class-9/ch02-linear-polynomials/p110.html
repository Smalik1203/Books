Type 8 · Slope, intercept and crossing points

Example 14

A line passes through $(2, 6)$ and $(5, 0)$. It crosses the $y$-axis at

  1. $(0, 10)$
  2. $(0, 8)$
  3. $(0, 12)$
  4. $(0, 6)$

Solution.

Step 1 $3a = 0 - 6 = -6$, so $a = -2$ three steps, from $x = 2$ to $x = 5$
Step 2 $b = 6 - 2(-2) = 10$ two steps back to $x = 0$
Step 3 the line is $y = -2x + 10$
Answer (a)
Check at $x = 5$ the line gives $-2 \times 5 + 10 = 0$, the second point

Option (b) goes back only one step. Option (c) goes back three steps, the gap between the two given points. Option (d) is the $y$-coordinate of the first point, as if that point were on the axis.

Example 15

Write $2y = -8x + 6$ in the form $y = ax + b$. Give its slope and its $y$-intercept, and find where the line crosses each axis.

Solution.

Step 1 $y = -4x + 3$ divide both sides by $2$
Step 2 the slope is $-4$ and the $y$-intercept is $3$, so the line crosses the $y$-axis at $(0, 3)$
Step 3 $-4x + 3 = 0$ on the $x$-axis, $y = 0$
Step 4 $4x = 3$, so $x = \tfrac{3}{4}$
Answer $y = -4x + 3$, with slope $-4$ and $y$-intercept $3$; it crosses the axes at $(0, 3)$ and $(\tfrac{3}{4}, 0)$
Check $-4 \times \tfrac{3}{4} + 3 = 0$
