Selected solutions

B5.1 hint Two values three apart, stepping by the same amount.
solution $16 - 7 = 9$ over three steps gives $a = 3$, so $p(0) = 1$.
key idea The slope is a rate. Never solve two equations where counting steps will do.
B7.5 hint Two polynomials agree everywhere only if their coefficients match.
solution $p(p(x)) = a^2x + ab + b$, so $a^2 = 4$ and $b(a + 1) = 9$: $a = 2$ gives $b = 3$, and $a = -2$ gives $b = -9$.
key idea "True for every $x$" turns one equation into a system.
What to take away
  1. Difference a table first; the differences name the degree.
  2. Two facts determine two unknowns, if they are independent facts.
  3. $a$ tilts, $b$ slides, and neither disturbs the other.
  4. A rule fitted to a range says nothing outside it.
  5. "For every $x$" lets you compare coefficients.
