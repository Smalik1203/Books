Type 6 · Finding $a$ and $b$ from two facts

Example 10

A linear polynomial has $p(2) = 7$ and $p(5) = 16$. Then $p(0)$ is

  1. $1$
  2. $3$
  3. $4$
  4. $10$

Solution.

Step 1 $2a + b = 7$ and $5a + b = 16$ $p(x) = ax + b$
Step 2 $3a = 9$, so $a = 3$ subtracting removes $b$
Step 3 $b = 7 - 2 \times 3 = 1$
Answer $p(0) = b = 1$: (a)
Check $p(x) = 3x + 1$ gives $p(5) = 3 \times 5 + 1 = 16$

Option (b) is the slope, not the value at $0$. Option (c) goes back one step of $3$ from $p(2)$ instead of two, and option (d) goes back two steps from $p(5)$ instead of from $p(2)$.

Example 11

A spring is $12$ cm long when a $2$ kg load hangs from it, and $15$ cm long with a $4$ kg load. Its length is $L = ak + b$ cm for a load of $k$ kg. Find $a$ and $b$, and say what $b$ is.

Solution.

Step 1 $12 = 2a + b$ and $15 = 4a + b$ one equation a reading
Step 2 $2a = 3$, so $a = 1.5$ subtracting removes $b$
Step 3 $b = 12 - 2 \times 1.5 = 9$
Answer $L = 1.5k + 9$; $b$ is the length with no load, $9$ cm
Check $1.5 \times 4 + 9 = 15$
