9
The Mixed Test
Everything these ten pages have covered, mixed.

No hints. Work straight through.

  1. The coefficient of $x$ in $9 - x$ is
    1. $9$
    2. $1$
    3. $-1$
    4. $0$
  2. A linear polynomial has $p(0) = -3$ and $p(4) = 9$. Find $p(x)$, and then $p(-2)$.
  3. The $n$th term of $8, 5, 2, -1, \ldots$ is
    1. $8 - 3n$
    2. $11 - 3n$
    3. $3n + 5$
    4. $5 - 3n$
  4. A gym charges a fixed fee plus an hourly rate for a court. Ten hours came to ₹$800$, fifteen hours to ₹$1100$. Find the fee, the rate, and the cost of $22$ hours.
  5. The lines $y = -x + 4$ and $y = -x - 4$
    1. meet at the origin
    2. meet on the x-axis
    3. are parallel
    4. are the same line
  6. Show that $(-1, -1)$, $(2, 5)$ and $(5, 11)$ lie on one straight line, and find its equation.
  7. A plant measured at the start of months $0$ to $4$ stands $1.75$, $2.25$, $2.75$, $3.25$ and $3.75$ feet tall. Write the rule relating height to month, and say in which month it first reaches $6.75$ ft.
  8. The graph of $y = ax + b$ passes through the origin exactly when
    1. $a = 0$
    2. $b = 0$
    3. $a = b$
    4. $a = 1$
  9. Two lines have equal slopes and different y-intercepts. Explain from the algebra, not from a drawing, why they never meet.
  10. A recharge of ₹$600$ loses ₹$15$ a day. Write $b(x)$, say why this is decay, and find the first day the balance falls below ₹$100$.
  11. A sequence has first differences $2, 4, 6, 8$. It is generated by a polynomial of degree
    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $3$
    4. $0$
  12. A line has y-intercept $-2$ and passes through $(4, 10)$. Find $a$ and $b$, and where it cuts the x-axis.
  13. $p(x) = ax + b$ satisfies $p(1) = 3$ and $p\bigl(p(1)\bigr) = 11$. Find $a$, $b$ and $p(0)$.
