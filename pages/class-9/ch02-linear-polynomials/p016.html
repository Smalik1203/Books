Example 11

A telecom company charges a fixed monthly fee plus a cost per GB of data. A student who used $10$ GB was billed ₹$350$; when she used $20$ GB the bill was ₹$550$. Taking the bill to be $y = ax + b$ for $x$ GB used, find $a$ and $b$.

Each observation is a pair of values that must satisfy the relation, so each gives an equation.

$x = 10$, $y = 350$ gives $350 = 10a + b$

$x = 20$, $y = 550$ gives $550 = 20a + b$

From the first, $b = 350 - 10a$. Substituting that into the second:

$550 = 20a + (350 - 10a)$

$550 = 10a + 350$

$10a = 200$

$a = 20$

and then $b = 350 - 10 \times 20 = 150$. So the bill is

$$y = 20x + 150$$

Two bills were enough to recover a rule that predicts all the others.
