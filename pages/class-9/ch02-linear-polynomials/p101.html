The Idea Underneath
Two points fix a line. A third can disagree.

Everything in the chapter came out of one expression:

$$p(x) = ax + b$$

Two readings fix $a$ and $b$ — two unknowns, two equations. A third reading can do something new: it can disagree. The bills $(10, 350)$ and $(20, 550)$ force $y = 20x + 150$, which predicts $750$ at $x = 30$, not $780$.

Which number does which job
$a$ grows↑ the line tilts steeper↑
$b$ grows↑ the line slides up, tilt unchanged↑

$a$ tilts and $b$ slides. Half the traps in this section come of swapping the two, because both numbers are in the equation somewhere and either answer looks plausible.

Equal steps across, equal steps up
$a$ is the tilt
$b$ is where it crosses
