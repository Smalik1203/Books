    1. $y = -3x + 4$
    2. $2y = 4x + 7$
    3. $5y = 6x - 10$

  1. A liquid's temperature is $x$ K on the Kelvin scale and $y$ °F on the Fahrenheit scale, and the two are related by $y = \frac{9}{5}(x - 273) + 32$.
    1. The liquid is at $313$ K. What is that in °F?
    2. The liquid is at $158$ °F. What is that in K?
  2. The work done by a constant force is the force times the distance moved in its direction. Write this as a linear equation in two variables — work $w$ and distance $d$ — and draw its graph for a constant force of $3$ units. How much work is done over a distance of $2$ units? Check your answer against the graph.
  3. The graph of a linear polynomial $p(x)$ passes through $(1, 5)$ and $(3, 11)$.
    1. Find $p(x)$.
    2. Find where its graph cuts each axis.
    3. Draw the graph and check both answers on it.
  4. Let $p(x) = ax + b$ and $q(x) = cx + d$ be linear polynomials with
    1. $p(0) = 5$;
    2. $p(x) - q(x)$ cutting the $x$-axis at $(3, 0)$;
    3. $p(x) + q(x) = 6x + 4$ for every real $x$.
    Find $p(x)$ and $q(x)$.
