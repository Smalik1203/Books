Example 5 · Single correct

A line passes through $(2, 6)$ and $(5, 0)$. Where does it cross the $y$-axis?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $(0, 10)$
  2. $(0, 8)$
  3. $(0, 12)$
  4. $(0, 6)$

Solution.

Step 1The slope is $\frac{0 - 6}{5 - 2} = -2$.
Step 2Going back from $x = 2$ to $x = 0$ adds $2 \times 2 = 4$: $y = 10$.
Answer(a) $(0, 10)$
Example 6 · Single correct

What is the slope of the line $2y = -8x + 6$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 3
  2. $-4$
  3. $-8$
  4. 4

Solution.

Step 1Divide by 2: $y = -4x + 3$.
Step 2The slope is $-4$ and the $y$-intercept 3.
Answer(b) $-4$
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these are linear polynomials in $x$?

Select all correct options.

  1. $3x - 7$
  2. $x^2 + 1$
  3. $\frac{x}{2} + 5$
  4. $\frac{2}{x} + 1$

Solution.

Step 1$3x - 7$ and $\frac{1}{2}x + 5$ have degree 1.
Step 2$x^2 + 1$ has degree 2, and $\frac{2}{x} = 2x^{-1}$ is not a polynomial term.
Answer(a), (c)
