Problem 3

A candle is $24$ cm tall two hours after it is lit and $15$ cm tall five hours after. It burns at a steady rate. Its height when it was lit was

  1. $18$ cm
  2. $27$ cm
  3. $30$ cm
  4. $33$ cm
Solution

A steady rate means the height is $h = at + b$. Three hours pass between the readings.

$3a = 15 - 24 = -9$

$a = -3$

It was lit at $t = 0$, two hours before the first reading.

$b = 24 - 2(-3) = 30$

Option (c). The rule is $h = 30 - 3t$, and at $t = 5$ it gives $15$. Option (b) adds only one hour's loss. Option (d) adds three hours' loss, the whole gap between the readings. Option (a) takes $6$ cm away, going forward in time instead of back.

Problem 4

The points $(1, 2)$, $(4, 11)$ and $(6, k)$ lie on one straight line. Then $k$ is

  1. $15$
  2. $16$
  3. $17$
  4. $18$
Solution

The first two points fix the line. Three steps in $x$ raise $y$ by $9$.

$3a = 11 - 2 = 9$, so $a = 3$

$2 = 3(1) + b$, so $b = -1$

The line is $y = 3x - 1$, and $k$ is its value at $x = 6$.

$k = 3(6) - 1 = 17$

Option (c). The line need not be found in full: from $x = 4$ to $x = 6$ is two steps, so $k = 11 + 2 \times 3 = 17$ as well. Option (d) is $3 \times 6$, which forgets $b$. Option (a) adds $4$, the difference in $x$, as if the slope were $2$. Option (b) adds the slope only once.
