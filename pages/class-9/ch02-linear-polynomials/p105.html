Type 3 · Word problems that become a linear equation

Example 4

A father is $24$ years older than his son. In $3$ years' time the father will be three times as old as the son. Find their present ages.

Solution.

Step 1 let the son be $x$ years old; the father is $x + 24$
Step 2 in $3$ years they will be $x + 3$ and $x + 27$
Step 3 $x + 27 = 3(x + 3)$ three times as old
Step 4 $x + 27 = 3x + 9$, so $2x = 18$
Step 5 $x = 9$
Answer the son is $9$ years old and the father $33$
Check in $3$ years they will be $12$ and $36$, and $36 = 3 \times 12$
Example 5

A purse holds only ₹$5$ coins and ₹$2$ coins, $25$ coins in all, worth ₹$77$. How many of each are there?

Solution.

Step 1 let there be $x$ five-rupee coins; then there are $25 - x$ two-rupee coins
Step 2 $5x + 2(25 - x) = 77$ their value in rupees
Step 3 $5x + 50 - 2x = 77$, so $3x = 27$
Step 4 $x = 9$
Answer $9$ five-rupee coins and $16$ two-rupee coins
Check $9 + 16 = 25$ coins, and $45 + 32 = 77$ rupees
