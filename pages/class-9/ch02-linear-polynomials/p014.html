Example 10

A cylindrical tank holds water $3$ m deep at the start of summer. After $t$ months the depth is $h(t) = 3 - 0.5t$ metres. Tabulate it.

Every month takes away $0.5$ m — the same $0.5$ m each time. This is linear decay.

Growth and decay

Linear growth is a pattern in which a quantity increases by a constant amount over equal intervals; linear decay is one in which it decreases by a constant amount over equal intervals. The two differ only in the sign of that constant.
