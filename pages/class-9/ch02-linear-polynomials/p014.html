Growth and decay

Linear growth is a pattern in which a quantity increases by a constant amount over equal intervals; linear decay is one in which it decreases by a constant amount over equal intervals. The two differ only in the sign of that constant.

Exercise Set 2.4
  1. A plant is $1.75$ feet tall and grows by $0.5$ feet a month.
    1. How tall is it after $7$ months?
    2. Tabulate the height for $t$ from $0$ to $10$ months.
    3. Write an expression relating the height $h$ to $t$, and say why it is linear growth.

    2. Tabulate its value for $t$ from $0$ to $8$ years.
    3. Write an expression relating the value $v$ to $t$, and say why it is linear decay.
  1. A village of $750$ people gains $50$ more every year, moving in from a nearby city.
