Example 8 · Multiple correct

The first row of a hall has 18 seats, and each row has 3 more than the row in front. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Row 5 has 30 seats.
  2. Row $n$ has $3n + 15$ seats.
  3. Row 10 has 45 seats.
  4. The first two rows have 36 seats together.

Solution.

Step 1Row $n$ has $18 + 3(n - 1) = 3n + 15$ seats: 30 in row 5 and 45 in row 10.
Step 2The first two rows have $18 + 21 = 39$ seats.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

A candle is 24 cm tall two hours after it is lit and 15 cm tall five hours after. It burns at a steady rate. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It burns 3 cm an hour.
  2. It was 30 cm tall when lit.
  3. After $t$ hours it is $30 - 3t$ cm tall.
  4. It burns out after 10 hours.

Solution.

Step 1In 3 hours it lost 9 cm, so 3 cm an hour; two hours earlier it was $24 + 6 = 30$ cm.
Step 2$30 - 3t = 0$ when $t = 10$.
Answer(a), (b), (c), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Consider the lines $y = 2x + 1$ and $y = 2x - 3$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. They have the same slope.
  2. They never meet.
  3. They meet at $(1, 3)$.
  4. Their $y$-intercepts are 1 and $-3$.

Solution.

Step 1Both have slope 2, and at every $x$ the first is 4 higher than the second, so they never meet.
Step 2$(1, 3)$ lies on the first line only: the second gives $-1$ at $x = 1$.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
