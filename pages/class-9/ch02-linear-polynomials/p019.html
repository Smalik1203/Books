Example 13

Plot $(-3, 6)$, $(-2, 4)$, $(0, 0)$, $(1, -2)$, $(2, -4)$ and $(3, -6)$, and join $(-3, 6)$ to $(3, -6)$. What is the equation of the line?

Solution.

Step 1 plot the six points and join $(-3, 6)$ to $(3, -6)$, as in Fig. 2.7
Step 2 again the ruler catches every point
Step 3 this time each $y$ is $-2$ times its $x$ look down the pairs
Answer the line is $y = -2x$

It falls from left to right, where $y = 3x$ climbed. The sign of the multiplier is doing that.

Both lines went through the origin, and both had the form $y = ax$. It is worth seeing several of these together before saying what $a$ does, because one line on its own has nothing to be compared with.
