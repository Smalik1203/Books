Example 11 · Numerical answer

A father is 24 years older than his son. In 3 years the father will be three times as old as the son. How old is the son now?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1With the son $s$ years old: $s + 24 + 3 = 3(s + 3)$.
Step 2$s + 27 = 3s + 9$, so $2s = 18$ and $s = 9$.
Answer9
Example 12 · Numerical answer

A purse holds only ₹5 and ₹2 coins, 25 coins worth ₹77 in all. How many ₹5 coins are there?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1With $a$ five-rupee coins: $5a + 2(25 - a) = 77$.
Step 2$3a + 50 = 77$, so $a = 9$.
Answer9
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A courier charges ₹40 for a parcel of up to 2 kg, and ₹15 for each kilogram above 2 kg. What does a 7 kg parcel cost, in rupees?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The charge for $w$ kg, above 2 kg, is $40 + 15(w - 2)$.
Step 2For $w = 7$: $40 + 15 \times 5 = 115$.
Answer115
