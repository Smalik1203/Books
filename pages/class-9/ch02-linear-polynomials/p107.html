The Challenge Zone
Think, then apply, then challenge.

Not one of these says which idea to use. Show every step of the reasoning.

  1. Think Show that if $p(x) = ax + b$ then $p(x + 1) - p(x) = a$ for every $x$, so that the constant difference of the pattern and the slope of the graph are the same number. Then find a polynomial for which $p(x+1) - p(x)$ is not constant, and say what its differences do instead.
  2. Apply Read the line in Fig. 2.13 off the grid: give its slope, its y-intercept, its equation, and the point where it cuts the x-axis. Then say which of those four you could have found without the picture, given only two of its points.
  3. Apply One candle is $30$ cm long and burns down $2$ cm an hour; another is $20$ cm and burns down $1$ cm an hour. Both are lit together. When are they the same height, what height is that, which is the taller before that moment, and which burns out first?
  4. Challenge The points $(1, k)$, $(4, 3k - 2)$ and $(7, 16)$ lie on one straight line. Find $k$, using the fact that the three x-values are equally spaced.
  5. Challenge A polynomial $p(x) = ax + b$ satisfies $p\bigl(p(x)\bigr) = 4x + 9$ for every $x$. Find every $p$ that works.

    Substitute and compare coefficients. Two equations come out, and one of them has two solutions.
