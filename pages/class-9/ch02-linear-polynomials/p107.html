Set B · Two ideas together

Questions 1 to 3 take this key. (a) both true, R explains A; (b) both true, R does not explain A; (c) A true, R false; (d) A false, R true.

  1. A. Evaluating a linear polynomial at $1, 2, 3, \ldots$ always gives a linear pattern.  R. $a(n + 1) + b - (an + b) = a$, the same number for every $n$.
  2. A. The graphs of $y = 4x - 3$ and $y = -4x - 3$ meet on the $y$-axis.  R. Both have the same constant term.
  3. A. Any two readings of $y = ax + b$ fix $a$ and $b$.  R. Two unknowns need two equations.
