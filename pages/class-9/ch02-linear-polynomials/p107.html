Type 5 · Linear growth and linear decay

Example 8

A candle is $24$ cm tall two hours after it is lit and $15$ cm tall five hours after. It burns at a steady rate. Its height when it was lit was

  1. $18$ cm
  2. $27$ cm
  3. $30$ cm
  4. $33$ cm

Solution.

Step 1 the height is $h = at + b$ a steady rate
Step 2 $3a = 15 - 24 = -9$, so $a = -3$ three hours between readings
Step 3 $b = 24 - 2(-3) = 30$ two hours back to $t = 0$
Answer (c)
Check $h = 30 - 3t$ gives $30 - 3 \times 5 = 15$ at $t = 5$

Option (b) adds back only one hour's loss, and (d) adds three, the whole gap between the readings. Option (a) takes $6$ cm away, going forward in time instead of back.

Example 9

A phone battery shows $100$ per cent, and falls by $8$ per cent for every hour of video. Write its charge after $t$ hours of video, say whether this is linear growth or linear decay, and find when the charge is down to $20$ per cent.

Solution.

Step 1 the charge is $c = 100 - 8t$ per cent $8$ lost each hour
Step 2 the same amount goes every hour, and the coefficient of $t$ is negative so it is linear decay
Step 3 $100 - 8t = 20$, so $8t = 80$
Answer $c = 100 - 8t$, a linear decay; the charge is down to $20$ per cent at $t = 10$ hours
Check $100 - 8 \times 10 = 20$
