Think and Reason
Judge a claim — and the reason offered for it.

Questions 1–4 take this key. (a) both true, R explains A; (b) both true, R does not explain A; (c) A true, R false; (d) A false, R true.

  1. A. Evaluating a linear polynomial at $1, 2, 3, \ldots$ always gives a linear pattern.  R. $a(n+1) + b - (an + b) = a$, the same number for every $n$.
  2. A. A sequence whose first differences are all equal comes from a polynomial of degree at most $1$.  R. A polynomial of degree $2$ has constant second differences.
  3. A. Any two readings determine $a$ and $b$.  R. Two unknowns need two equations.
  4. A. The graphs of $y = 4x - 3$ and $y = -4x - 3$ meet on the $y$-axis.  R. Both have the same constant term.
  5. For $y = -3x + 4$, which must be true?
    1. Its slope is $-3$.
    2. It meets the $y$-axis at $(0, 4)$.
    3. It passes through $(2, 2)$.
    1. (i) only
    2. (i) and (ii)
    3. (ii) and (iii)
    4. all three
  6. A quantity measured hourly has differences $7, 7, 7, 7$. Which must be true?
    1. The rule has the form $7t + b$.
    2. The rule is $7t$.
    3. One further reading would settle it.
    1. (i) only
    2. (ii) and (iii)
    3. (i) and (iii)
    4. all three
  7. In $y = ax + b$, $a$ is positive and $b$ is negative. Which must be true?
    1. It crosses the $x$-axis at a positive $x$.
    2. It crosses the $y$-axis below the origin.
    3. It passes through Quadrant III.
    1. (i) only
    2. (i) and (ii)
    3. (ii) and (iii)
    4. all three
  8. The chess club charged $200 + 50m$ and Bela's money ran $100 - 5n$. Both constants are what you have at the start, yet one is money owed and the other money held. Say what in the expression records the difference.
