Every linear polynomial is $ax + b$ with $a \ne 0$, and the two numbers do two different jobs. Put $x = 0$ and the whole thing collapses to $b$, so $b$ is the value you start from before the variable has done anything at all. Then $a$ is what each unit of $x$ adds. The perimeter $4x$ has $b = 0$, because a square of side nothing has no perimeter; most quantities in the world are not so obliging.

A candle $20$ cm tall that burns down $2$ cm an hour has height $20 - 2t$ after $t$ hours: it starts at $20$ and loses $2$ each hour, so here $a$ is negative. A taxi charging ₹$50$ to start and ₹$18$ a kilometre costs ₹$(50 + 18d)$ for $d$ kilometres. A temperature $C$ in degrees Celsius is $\tfrac{9}{5}C + 32$ in degrees Fahrenheit. Different subjects entirely, and the same two numbers doing the same two jobs in each.

Compare that with the area of the same square, $x^{2}$. Its side going up by half a centimetre at a time gives areas $1$, $2.25$, $4$, $6.25$, $9$ — and the steps between them are $1.25$, $1.75$, $2.25$, $2.75$, which are not equal. There is no starting amount and no amount-per-step to be had. That is the whole difference between degree $1$ and everything above it, and it is why the next few sections can say so much about so plain a family.
