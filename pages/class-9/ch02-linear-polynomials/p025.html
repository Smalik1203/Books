End-of-Chapter Exercises
  1. Write a polynomial of degree $3$ in $x$ in which the coefficient of $x^{2}$ is $-7$.
  1. Find the value of each polynomial at the value given.
    1. $5x^{2} - 3x + 7$ at $x = 1$
    2. $4t^{3} - t^{2} + 6$ at $t = a$
  1. Multiplying a number by $\frac{5}{2}$ and adding $\frac{2}{3}$ to the product gives $-\frac{7}{12}$. Find the number.
  1. One positive number is five times another. Adding $21$ to each makes one of the new numbers twice the other. Find the two numbers.
  1. You have ₹$800$ and save ₹$250$ every month. How much have you after
    1. $6$ months?
    2. $2$ years?
    Express the amount as a linear pattern.
  1. The two digits of a two-digit number differ by $3$. Reversing the digits and adding the result to the original number gives $143$. Find both numbers.
  1. Draw the graph of each equation. Give its slope, its $y$-intercept, and the coordinates of the point where it cuts the $y$-axis.
    1. $y = -3x + 4$
    2. $2y = 4x + 7$
    3. $5y = 6x - 10$
    4. $3y = 6x - 11$
    Are any of the four parallel?
  1. A liquid's temperature is $x$ K on the Kelvin scale and $y$ °F on the Fahrenheit scale, and the two are related by $y = \frac{9}{5}(x - 273) + 32$.
    1. The liquid is at $313$ K. What is that in °F?
    2. The liquid is at $158$ °F. What is that in K?
  1. The work done by a constant force is the force times the distance moved in its direction. Write this as a linear equation in two variables — work $w$ and distance $d$ — and draw its graph for a constant force of $3$ units. How much work is done over a distance of $2$ units? Check your answer against the graph.
  1. The graph of a linear polynomial $p(x)$ passes through $(1, 5)$ and $(3, 11)$.
    1. Find $p(x)$.
    2. Find where its graph cuts each axis.
    3. Draw the graph and check both answers on it.
