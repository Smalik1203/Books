Preface

Mathematics helps us describe patterns, compare quantities, and explain relationships. A calculation gives an answer; reasoning helps us understand why that answer makes sense. The LearnLab mathematics books bring these two parts of learning together.

Across the series, ideas are developed through explanations, examples, diagrams, and questions. A picture may make a relationship visible. A worked calculation may show how a rule is used. A question may ask the reader to connect ideas that first appeared separately. Each offers a different way into the same mathematics.

Understanding takes time. Students need opportunities to try an idea, make mistakes, discuss their reasoning, and return to what they have learnt. The book provides material for this work in the classroom and for practice at home. Teachers can guide the discussion, while students build confidence by explaining more of the reasoning for themselves.

LearnLab extends this learning through interactive simulations. Students can explore changes, compare cases, and investigate relationships that are difficult to see in a still picture. These experiences support the work on the page. Observations become more useful when students connect them to an explanation and test their understanding with a question.

Beyond the Book offers opportunities to explore the chapter's ideas further. It is an invitation to spend more time with a question, meet a different application, or discover an unexpected connection. Students can return to this material as their understanding grows.

Our aim is to help students become thoughtful and independent learners: willing to ask questions, careful with their reasoning, and able to explain what they know. We hope that the pages ahead encourage both steady practice and the pleasure of finding something out.
