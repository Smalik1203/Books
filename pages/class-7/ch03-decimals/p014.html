Which is larger?

Which is larger, 6.456 or 6.465? We could find both on a number line. We can also compare the digits place by place, as in Table 3.14.

Both numbers have 6 ones, so we look at the tenths. Both have 4 tenths, so we look at the hundredths. The first has 5 hundredths and the second has 6. So 6.465 is larger, and we do not need to look at the thousandths at all.

Comparing decimals

Compare the digits in the highest place first. If they are equal, move one place to the right. Keep going until the digits are different. The number with the larger digit in that place is the larger number.

Why can we stop at that place? Could the digits after it change the answer? Think about the largest amount that all the later places together can add.
