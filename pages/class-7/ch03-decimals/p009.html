3.5Units of Measurement

Length

We have measured with a ruler for years, and we know that 1 cm is 10 mm. So how many centimetres is 1 mm? A centimetre is split into 10 millimetres, so 1 mm is one-tenth of a centimetre.

$1 \text{ mm} = \tfrac{1}{10} \text{ cm} = 0.1 \text{ cm}$

$5 \text{ mm} = \tfrac{5}{10} \text{ cm} = 0.5 \text{ cm}$

$12 \text{ mm} = 10 \text{ mm} + 2 \text{ mm} = 1 \text{ cm} + \tfrac{2}{10} \text{ cm} = 1.2 \text{ cm}$

Going the other way, how many millimetres is 5.6 cm? It is 5 cm and 0.6 cm. Each centimetre has 10 mm, and 0.6 cm is 6 of those millimetres, so 5.6 cm is 56 mm.

We also know that 1 m is 100 cm. A metre is split into 100 centimetres, so 1 cm is one-hundredth of a metre: $1 \text{ cm} = \tfrac{1}{100} \text{ m} = 0.01 \text{ m}$.

How many metres is 10 cm? It is $\tfrac{10}{100}$ m, which is $\tfrac{1}{10}$ m, or 0.1 m. And 15 cm is 10 cm and 5 cm more:

$15 \text{ cm} = \dfrac{15}{100} \text{ m} = \dfrac{10}{100} \text{ m} + \dfrac{5}{100} \text{ m} = \dfrac{1}{10} \text{ m} + \dfrac{5}{100} \text{ m} = 0.15 \text{ m}$
