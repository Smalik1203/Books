3.2A Tenth Part

A pencil is $3\tfrac{4}{10}$ units long. That is 3 units and 4 tenths of a unit, or $(3 \times 1) + (4 \times \tfrac{1}{10})$ units.

Ten tenths make one unit, because adding $\tfrac{1}{10}$ ten times gives $10 \times \tfrac{1}{10} = 1$. So the 3 units in the pencil are 30 tenths, and the pencil is 34 tenths long:

$3\tfrac{4}{10} = 1 + 1 + 1 + \dfrac{4}{10} = \dfrac{10}{10} + \dfrac{10}{10} + \dfrac{10}{10} + \dfrac{4}{10} = \dfrac{34}{10}$
