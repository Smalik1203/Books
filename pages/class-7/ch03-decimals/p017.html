Decimal sequences

Look at the sequence 4.4, 4.8, 5.2, 5.6, 6.0, and so on. To find the change, subtract a term from the one after it: $4.8 - 4.4 = 0.4$. Check another pair before you trust it: $5.6 - 5.2 = 0.4$ as well. So each term is 0.4 more than the one before, and the next three terms are 6.4, 6.8 and 7.2. A sequence can also go down, when each term is less than the one before it.
