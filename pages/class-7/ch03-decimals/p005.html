Adding and subtracting with hundredths

Example 3

Find $15\tfrac{3}{10}\tfrac{4}{100} + 2\tfrac{6}{10}\tfrac{8}{100}$.

Add the units, the tenths and the hundredths separately. Then use the fact that 10 hundredths make 1 tenth, and 10 tenths make 1 unit.

Solution.

Step 1 $(15 + 2) + \left(\dfrac{3}{10} + \dfrac{6}{10}\right) + \left(\dfrac{4}{100} + \dfrac{8}{100}\right)$ each place apart
Step 2 $= 17 + \dfrac{9}{10} + \dfrac{12}{100}$
Step 3 $= 17 + \dfrac{9}{10} + \dfrac{1}{10} + \dfrac{2}{100}$ carry a tenth
Step 4 $= 17 + \dfrac{10}{10} + \dfrac{2}{100} = 18\dfrac{2}{100}$ carry a unit
Answer $18\dfrac{2}{100}$

We could also change both numbers to hundredths: $\dfrac{1534}{100} + \dfrac{268}{100} = \dfrac{1802}{100} = 18\dfrac{2}{100}$.

Compare this with adding whole numbers. For $483 + 268$ we add hundreds, tens and ones separately: $600 + 140 + 11$. Then 11 ones become 1 ten and 1 one, and 150 tens become 1 hundred and 50: the total is 751. Carrying works the same way with tenths and hundredths.
