Type 2 · Place value and the decimal point

Example 3

Write 3 tens, 4 ones, 2 hundredths and 6 thousandths as a decimal, and read it aloud.

Solution.

Step 1 $30 + 4 + \tfrac{0}{10} + \tfrac{2}{100} + \tfrac{6}{1000}$ no tenths, write 0
Step 2 $= 34.026$
Answer 34.026, read as “thirty-four point zero two six”
Example 4

Find the place value of each digit of 52.074. Then write 52.074 in expanded form.

Solution.

Step 1 5 tens $= 50$; 2 ones $= 2$; 0 tenths $= 0$
Step 2 7 hundredths $= \tfrac{7}{100}$; 4 thousandths $= \tfrac{4}{1000}$
Answer $52.074 = 50 + 2 + \tfrac{7}{100} + \tfrac{4}{1000}$
Example 5

Which number is equal to $3 + \tfrac{5}{100}$?

  1. 3.5
  2. 3.05
  3. 3.005
  4. 0.35

Solution.

Step 1 3 ones, 0 tenths and 5 hundredths
Step 2 $3 + \tfrac{0}{10} + \tfrac{5}{100} = 3.05$ 0 holds the tenths
Answer (b)

Type 3 · Changing units

Example 6

Write 7 cm 4 mm in centimetres, and 8 m 25 cm in metres.

Solution.

Step 1 $4 \text{ mm} = \tfrac{4}{10} \text{ cm} = 0.4 \text{ cm}$, so $7 \text{ cm } 4 \text{ mm} = 7.4 \text{ cm}$ 10 mm in 1 cm
Step 2 $25 \text{ cm} = \tfrac{25}{100} \text{ m} = 0.25 \text{ m}$, so $8 \text{ m } 25 \text{ cm} = 8.25 \text{ m}$ 100 cm in 1 m
Answer 7.4 cm; 8.25 m
