Problem 2

$7.2 - 3.45$ is equal to

  1. 4.25
  2. 3.85
  3. 4.75
  4. 3.75
Solution

Write 7.2 as 7.20 so that both numbers have hundredths. We cannot take 5 hundredths from 0, so a tenth becomes 10 hundredths. We cannot take 4 tenths from the 1 tenth left, so a one becomes 10 tenths.

$7.20 - 3.45 = 6\tfrac{11}{10}\tfrac{10}{100} - 3\tfrac{4}{10}\tfrac{5}{100} = 3\tfrac{7}{10}\tfrac{5}{100} = 3.75$

The answer is (d). Option (a) takes the smaller digit from the larger in every place, without borrowing. Option (b) borrows a tenth but forgets to take it away from the tenths. Option (c) borrows a one but forgets to take it away from the ones.

Problem 3

Which list is in increasing order?

  1. 0.5, 0.45, 0.405, 0.054
  2. 0.054, 0.45, 0.405, 0.5
  3. 0.054, 0.405, 0.45, 0.5
  4. 0.5, 0.405, 0.45, 0.054
Solution

Give every number three digits after the point, so that each can be read as a number of thousandths.

$0.5 = 0.500 \qquad 0.45 = 0.450 \qquad 0.405 \qquad 0.054$

Compared as 500, 450, 405 and 54 thousandths, the order from smallest to largest is 0.054, 0.405, 0.45, 0.5. The answer is (c). Option (a) is the same list in decreasing order. Option (b) puts 0.45 before 0.405, as though 45 were less than 405. Option (d) is in no order.

Problems 2 and 3 began in the same way: zeros were written at the end, so that the numbers had the same places. After that, the subtracting and the ordering were done exactly as with whole numbers.
