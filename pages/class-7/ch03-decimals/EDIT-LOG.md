## Fitting

Result: 21 pages of chapter proper and 8 of Beyond the Book. Every page is
at least 88% full except the two closing pages (page 21, which carries
`data-close`, and the answer page). No stranded openers, no colliding labels,
every option row fits.

The draft's six over-full pages and the new sections were split one question
per block and refitted. That left 13 short pages in the chapter proper, most
held open by a few tall blocks that cannot divide. The block heights were
measured from the build, and `repack`'s own packing rule was replayed on them
to test each fix before it was made. What closed the gaps:

- **Reformatted, content unchanged:** Set 3.3 Q3, Set 3.5 and End Q13 set in
  two columns instead of one; Set 3.4 split into sums and differences.
- **Written in:** a second question in Set 3.7; a two-line stem on Sets 3.10
  and 3.14; a fuller opening under *Decimal sequences* (how to find the
  change, and that a sequence can go down); a closing sentence on page 8; the
  estimating remark on page 18.
- **Taken out at the join:** one-line paragraphs folded into the sentence
  before them (234 tenths, 254 g, ₹0.75); the `h3` directly under 3.8's head;
  a line each from the zooming, carrying, doorway and 16th-century
  paragraphs; the jellyfish's tentacles.
- **Reordered:** Table 3.8 moved below the paragraph that explains it.
- **Dropped:** the closing tip, which repeated summary point 6 and was left
  alone on a page of its own.

Beyond the Book: a closing sentence after Problems 3 and 4, a second line of
working in Problem 5, a seventh question in Set C, and the last page trimmed
by a line to stop it overrunning. The options of Problems 2 and 3 were
reordered so the answers do not run (b), (b), (b).

**The proofs caught what the builder cannot.** Table 3.2 had eight columns and
ran 8 mm past the right edge of the text block — the builder measures height,
not width — so its *Ten thousands* column went. The octopus row of Table 3.6
wrapped with wide justified gaps and was shortened to one line. Both were
found by looking at the PNGs.

The builder still prints `~ page 8 runs 1.1mm into the bottom margin`. The
page is 90% full and its proof ends in several centimetres of white, so
the notice is not about anything that prints; it is a `~`, not a `!`, and it
was left.
