Assertion and reason

In Questions 22 to 27, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): 1.5 m is 150 cm.

    Reason (R): 1 kg is 1000 g.

  1. Assertion (A): $2.50 = 2.5$.

    Reason (R): Zeros at the right end of the decimal part do not change the value.

  1. Assertion (A): 250 g is 0.25 kg.

    Reason (R): 1 g is 0.01 kg.

  1. Assertion (A): 4.07 is less than 4.7.

    Reason (R): The tenths digit of 4.07 is 0, and the tenths digit of 4.7 is 7.

  1. Assertion (A): 2.5 hours is 2 hours 50 minutes.

    Reason (R): One hour is 60 minutes.

  1. Assertion (A): 0.05 mg is ten times 0.5 mg.

    Reason (R): When a digit moves one place to the right, it is worth one-tenth as much.
