Example 7

A packet of dal weighs 1 kg 75 g. Write its weight in kilograms.

Solution.

Step 1 $75 \text{ g} = \tfrac{75}{1000} \text{ kg} = \tfrac{0}{10} + \tfrac{7}{100} + \tfrac{5}{1000} = 0.075 \text{ kg}$ no tenths
Step 2 $1 \text{ kg} + 0.075 \text{ kg} = 1.075 \text{ kg}$
Answer 1.075 kg
Example 8

Which is heavier, a bag of 0.6 kg of onions or a bag of 540 g of tomatoes?

Solution.

Step 1 $0.6 \text{ kg} = \tfrac{600}{1000} \text{ kg} = 600 \text{ g}$ same unit first
Step 2 $600 \text{ g} \gt 540 \text{ g}$
Answer The onions are heavier.

Example 10

Between which two tenths does 2.36 lie? Which of them is it closer to?

Solution.

Step 1 $2.30 \lt 2.36 \lt 2.40$ tenths as hundredths
Step 2 $2.36 - 2.30 = 0.06$; $2.40 - 2.36 = 0.04$
Step 3 $0.04 \lt 0.06$ nearer to 2.4
Answer between 2.3 and 2.4; closer to 2.4
