Problem 4

Shreya’s bag weighs 2.4 kg. She puts in books weighing 850 g and a water bottle weighing 600 g. What does the bag weigh now?

  1. 3.85 kg
  2. 3.31 kg
  3. 2.545 kg
  4. 1452.4 kg
Solution

A gram is 0.001 kg, so 850 g is 0.850 kg and 600 g is 0.600 kg.

$2.400 + 0.850 + 0.600 = 3.850$

The answer is (a). Option (b) writes 600 g as 0.06 kg. Option (c) writes the 1450 g as 0.145 kg. Option (d) adds grams to kilograms without changing the unit.

Once every weight was in kilograms, with three digits after the point, the sum was an ordinary column addition. What made the question hard was the units, not the adding.

Problem 5

A film lasts 2.75 hours. It starts at 3:00 in the afternoon. When does it end?

  1. 5:45
  2. 6:15
  3. 5:42
  4. 5:07
Solution

An hour split into 10 parts gives parts of 6 minutes, so 0.7 hour is $7 \times 6 = 42$ minutes. And 0.05 hour is half of 0.1 hour, which is 3 minutes.

$0.75 \text{ hour} = 0.7 \text{ hour} + 0.05 \text{ hour} = 42 \text{ minutes} + 3 \text{ minutes} = 45 \text{ minutes}$

$2.75 \text{ hours} = 2 \text{ hours } 45 \text{ minutes}$

Two hours after 3:00 is 5:00, and 45 minutes later is 5:45. The answer is (a). Option (b) reads 0.75 hour as 75 minutes. Option (c) counts the 7 tenths but forgets the 5 hundredths. Option (d) reads the 7 tenths as 7 minutes.

In all five problems, the wrong options came from a digit put in the wrong place, a borrow forgotten, or a unit left unchanged. None of them needed a new idea to avoid. Before choosing an option, it helps to ask what each digit is worth, and in what unit.
