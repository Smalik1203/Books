Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these are equal to 0.6?

Select all correct options.

  1. $\frac{6}{10}$
  2. $\frac{60}{100}$
  3. 0.06
  4. 0.600

Solution.

Step 10.6 is 6 tenths, and 6 tenths = 60 hundredths = 600 thousandths, so $\frac{6}{10}$, $\frac{60}{100}$ and 0.600 are all equal to it.
Step 20.06 is only 6 hundredths.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
