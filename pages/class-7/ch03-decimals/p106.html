Example 12 · Numerical answer

Meena buys a notebook for ₹38.50 and a pen for ₹12.75, and pays with a ₹100 note. How much change does she get, in rupees?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Total cost = 38.50 + 12.75 = ₹51.25.
Step 2Change = 100.00 − 51.25 = ₹48.75.
Answer48.75
Example 13 · Numerical answer

On a number line, the stretch from 2 to 4 is divided into 10 equal parts. What number is at the 7th mark after 2?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The stretch is 2 units long, so each part is 2 ÷ 10 = 0.2.
Step 2The 7th mark is 7 × 0.2 = 1.4 after 2: 2 + 1.4 = 3.4.
Answer3.4

List IList II
(P) 7 cm 4 mm, in centimetres(1) 0.54
(Q) 8 m 25 cm, in metres(2) 7.4
(R) 1 kg 75 g, in kilograms(3) 1.075
(S) 540 g, in kilograms(4) 8.25

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2

Solution.
