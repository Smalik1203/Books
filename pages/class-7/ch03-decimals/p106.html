Example 14

$7.2 - 3.45$ is equal to

  1. 4.25
  2. 3.85
  3. 4.75
  4. 3.75

Solution.

Step 1 $7.2 = 7.20 = 6 + \tfrac{11}{10} + \tfrac{10}{100}$ borrow twice
Step 2 $(6 - 3) + \tfrac{11 - 4}{10} + \tfrac{10 - 5}{100} = 3.75$
Answer (d)
Example 15

Meena buys a notebook for ₹38.50 and a pen for ₹12.75. She pays with a ₹100 note. How much change does she get?

Solution.

Step 1 $38.50 + 12.75 = 51.25$ total cost
Step 2 $100.00 - 51.25 = 48.75$ change
Answer ₹48.75

Example 16

A scoreboard shows Overs: 7.4. Is this seven and four-tenths overs? How many balls have been bowled?

Solution.

Step 1 7.4 overs = 7 overs and 4 balls 6 balls an over
Step 2 $7 \times 6 + 4 = 42 + 4 = 46$
Answer No. 46 balls have been bowled.
