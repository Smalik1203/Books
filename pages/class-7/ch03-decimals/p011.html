Weight

We know that 1 kg is 1000 g. So a gram is one-thousandth of a kilogram: $1 \text{ g} = \tfrac{1}{1000} \text{ kg} = 0.001 \text{ kg}$. Then 5 g is $\tfrac{5}{1000}$ kg, or 0.005 kg. And 10 g is $\tfrac{10}{1000}$ kg, which is $\tfrac{1}{100}$ kg, or 0.01 kg.

$254 \text{ g} = \dfrac{254}{1000} \text{ kg} = \left(\dfrac{200}{1000} + \dfrac{50}{1000} + \dfrac{4}{1000}\right) \text{kg}$

$= \left(\dfrac{2}{10} + \dfrac{5}{100} + \dfrac{4}{1000}\right) \text{kg} = 0.254 \text{ kg}$
