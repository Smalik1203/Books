Example 11

Which list is in increasing order?

  1. 0.5, 0.45, 0.405, 0.054
  2. 0.054, 0.45, 0.405, 0.5
  3. 0.054, 0.405, 0.45, 0.5
  4. 0.5, 0.405, 0.45, 0.054

Solution.

Step 1 $0.5 = 0.500$; $0.45 = 0.450$ three places each
Step 2 500, 450, 405 and 54 thousandths
Step 3 $0.054 \lt 0.405 \lt 0.45 \lt 0.5$ compare thousandths
Answer (c)

Example 12

Find $14.6 + 3.852$.

Solution.

Step 1 $14.6 = 14.600$ same places
Step 2 $\begin{array}{r} 14.600 \\ +\,3.852 \\ \hline 18.452 \end{array}$ carry 1 one
Answer $14.6 + 3.852 = 18.452$
Example 13

Find $20 - 7.46$, and check the answer by adding.

Solution.

Step 1 $20 = 20.00 = 19 + \tfrac{9}{10} + \tfrac{10}{100}$ borrow across zeros
Step 2 $20.00 - 7.46 = 12.54$
Step 3 $12.54 + 7.46 = 20.00$ check by adding
Answer $20 - 7.46 = 12.54$
