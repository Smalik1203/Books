Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which weights are the same as 1.075 kg?

Select all correct options.

  1. 1 kg 75 g
  2. 1 kg 750 g
  3. 1075 g
  4. 1.75 kg

Solution.

Step 10.075 kg = $\frac{75}{1000}$ kg = 75 g, so 1.075 kg = 1 kg 75 g = 1075 g.
Step 21 kg 750 g and 1.75 kg are both 1750 g.
Answer(a), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

A sequence begins 7.8, 7.35, 6.9, … and changes by the same amount each time. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Each term is 0.45 less than the one before.
  2. The next term is 6.45.
  3. The fifth term is 6.
  4. The tenth term is 3.8.

Solution.

Step 17.80 − 7.35 = 0.45 and 7.35 − 6.90 = 0.45. The next terms are 6.45 and 6.00.
Step 2The tenth term is 9 steps after 7.8: 9 × 0.45 = 4.05 and 7.80 − 4.05 = 3.75, not 3.8.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.
