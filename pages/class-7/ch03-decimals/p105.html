Example 11

In Fig. 3.8, the stretch from 2 to 4 is split into 10 equal parts. What numbers are p and q?

Solution.

Step 1 From 2 to 4 is 2 units, or 20 tenths.
Step 2 20 tenths in 10 equal parts: each part is 2 tenths, or 0.2 not 1 tenth
Step 3 p is 3 parts after 2: 6 tenths, so p is $2 + 0.6 = 2.6$
Step 4 q is 8 parts after 2: 16 tenths, so q is $2 + 1.6 = 3.6$
Answer p is 2.6 and q is 3.6
Example 12

Which list is in increasing order?

  1. 0.5, 0.45, 0.405, 0.054
  2. 0.054, 0.45, 0.405, 0.5
  3. 0.054, 0.405, 0.45, 0.5
  4. 0.5, 0.405, 0.45, 0.054

Solution.

Step 1 $0.5 = 0.500$; $0.45 = 0.450$ three places each
Step 2 500, 450, 405 and 54 thousandths
Step 3 $0.054 \lt 0.405 \lt 0.45 \lt 0.5$ compare thousandths
Answer (c)
