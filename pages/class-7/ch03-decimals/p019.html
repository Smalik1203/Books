What this chapter showed
  1. Splitting a unit into smaller equal parts lets us measure more exactly.
  2. Place value carries on past the ones place: 1 one is 10 tenths, 1 tenth is 10 hundredths, and 1 hundredth is 10 thousandths.
  3. A decimal point separates the whole number part of a number from the part smaller than one.
  4. Zeros at the right end of the decimal part do not change the value: $0.2 = 0.20 = 0.200$.
  5. Decimals can be marked on a number line, and compared place by place, starting from the highest place.
  6. Decimals are added and subtracted in columns, like whole numbers, with the points kept in a line.
  7. Lengths, weights and money can be changed from one unit to another using decimals: $1 \text{ mm} = 0.1 \text{ cm}$, $1 \text{ cm} = 0.01 \text{ m}$, $1 \text{ g} = 0.001 \text{ kg}$ and 1 paisa is ₹0.01.
