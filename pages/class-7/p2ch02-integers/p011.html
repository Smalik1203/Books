  1. Here is a new version of the Collatz rule you met in Class 6. Start with any integer. If it is even, halve it; if it is odd, multiply it by $-3$ and add 1. Repeat. For example, starting from $-7$ gives $-7, 22, 11, -32, -16, -8, -4, -2, -1, 4, 2, 1, -2, \ldots$ Try other starting numbers, such as $-21$ and $-6$. What patterns do you see?
  1. In a test, a correct answer earns 4 marks and a wrong answer earns $-2$ marks. (a) Anita answered every question. She had 15 correct answers and scored 40 marks. Did he leave any questions unanswered? Check each answer by working out the score.
