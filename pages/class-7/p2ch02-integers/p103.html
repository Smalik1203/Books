Type 2 · The sign of a product

Example 5

Find (a) $(-7) \times 8$ and (b) $(-9) \times (-6)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $(-7) \times 8 = -(7 \times 8) = -56$ unlike signs, negative
Step 2 $(-9) \times (-6) = 9 \times 6 = 54$ like signs, positive
Answer (a) $-56$ (b) 54
Example 6

Without multiplying, say whether $(-13) \times 17$ and $13 \times (-17)$ are equal.

Solution.

Step 1 Each magnitude is $13 \times 17$
Step 2 Each product has one negative factor, so each is negative unlike signs
Answer Yes. Each is $-(13 \times 17) = -221$.
Example 7

$a$, $b$ and $c$ are integers. $a \times b$ is negative and $b \times c$ is positive. Then $a \times c$ is

  1. positive
  2. negative
  3. zero
  4. impossible to tell

Solution.

Step 1 $a \times b \lt 0$, so $a$ and $b$ have unlike signs
Step 2 $b \times c \gt 0$, so $b$ and $c$ have like signs
Step 3 $a$ and $c$ have unlike signs, so $a \times c \lt 0$
Answer (b)
