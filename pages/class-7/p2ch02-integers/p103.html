Which of these is equal to (−6) × (5 + (−3))?

Choose one correct option.

  1. (−30) + 18
  2. (−30) − 18
  3. 30 + (−18)
  4. (−30) + (−3)

Solution.

Step 1Multiply each term in the bracket by −6: (−6) × 5 + (−6) × (−3).
Step 2= (−30) + 18. Check: both sides equal −12.
Answer(a) (−30) + 18
Example 5 · Single correct

a, b and c are integers. a × b is negative and b × c is positive. Then a × c is

Choose one correct option.

  1. positive
  2. negative
  3. zero
  4. impossible to tell

Solution.

Step 1b × c is positive, so b and c have the same sign; neither is zero.
Step 2a × b is negative, so a has the other sign. So a and c have opposite signs, and a × c is negative.
Answer(b) negative
Example 6 · Single correct

What is (−72) ÷ 8 + 96 ÷ (−12)?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 17
  2. −1
  3. −17
  4. 1

Solution.

Step 1(−72) ÷ 8 = −9 and 96 ÷ (−12) = −8: unlike signs give a negative quotient.
Step 2(−9) + (−8) = −17.
Answer(c) −17
