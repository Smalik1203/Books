Problem 3

A diver starts 6 m above the water and goes down 4 m every minute. Taking positions above the water as positive, where is she after 5 minutes?

  1. $-26$ m
  2. $-14$ m
  3. 14 m
  4. $-20$ m
Solution

She moves $-4$ m every minute, so $5 \times (-4) = -20$ m in all.

$6 + 5 \times (-4) = 6 + (-20) = -14$

She is 14 m below the water, (b). Option (d) forgets the starting height, option (a) subtracts it, and option (c) has the wrong sign.

Problem 4

A test has 20 questions. A correct answer earns 3 marks and a wrong answer earns $-1$ mark. Priya answered every question and scored 40. How many did she get right?

  1. 13
  2. 14
  3. 15
  4. 20
Solution

With all 20 right, the score would be $20 \times 3 = 60$. Each answer that is wrong instead of right changes the score by $(-3) + (-1) = -4$. The score is 20 less than 60, so $20 \div 4 = 5$ answers are wrong and 15 are right. Check: $15 \times 3 + 5 \times (-1) = 45 - 5 = 40$.

The answer is (c); option (d) ignores the wrong answers.

Problem 5

$a$, $b$ and $c$ are integers. $a \times b$ is negative and $b \times c$ is positive. Then $a \times c$ is

  1. positive
  2. negative
  3. zero
  4. impossible to tell
Solution

$a \times b$ is negative, so $a$ and $b$ have different signs. $b \times c$ is positive, so $b$ and $c$ have the same sign. So $a$ and $c$ have different signs, and $a \times c$ is negative: (b). We did not need the numbers themselves, only their signs.
