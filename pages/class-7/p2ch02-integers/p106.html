  1. The product of three consecutive integers, which follow one another on the number line, is $-60$. The largest of the three is

  1. $a$ and $b$ are integers. $a \times b$ is positive and $a + b$ is negative. What can you say about the signs of $a$ and $b$?

    2. both $a$ and $b$ are negative
    3. one is positive and the other negative

  1. A test has 30 questions: 4 marks for a correct answer, $-1$ for a wrong answer and 0 for a question left out. Arun left out 3 questions and scored 58. How many did he get right?
    1. 19
    2. 15
    3. 17
    4. 21

    1. $-5$
