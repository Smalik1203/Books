Example 14

Which of these is equal to $(-6) \times (5 + (-3))$?

  1. $(-30) + 18$
  2. $(-30) - 18$
  3. $30 + (-18)$
  4. $(-30) + (-3)$

Solution.

Step 1 $(-6) \times 5 + (-6) \times (-3)$ distributive property
Step 2 $= (-30) + 18$
Step 3 $(-6) \times 2 = -12$ and $(-30) + 18 = -12$ check
Answer (a)
Example 15

In a class quiz, every wrong answer earns $-4$ marks. A class gives 23 wrong answers in the first week and 27 in the second. Find the total marks for the wrong answers in two ways.

Solution.

Step 1 $(-4) \times 23 + (-4) \times 27 = (-92) + (-108) = -200$ week by week
Step 2 $(-4) \times (23 + 27) = (-4) \times 50 = -200$ both weeks together
Answer $-200$ marks either way
