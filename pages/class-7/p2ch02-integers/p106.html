Example 13 · Numerical answer

A quiz has 30 questions. A right answer earns 3 marks and a wrong answer earns −2 marks. Arun answered every question and scored 40. How many did he get right?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1All 30 right would give 90. Each wrong answer instead of a right one changes the score by 3 + 2 = 5.
Step 290 − 40 = 50 = 10 × 5, so 10 are wrong and 20 are right. Check: 20 × 3 + 10 × (−2) = 40.
Answer20
Example 14 · Matching

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) (−9) − 14(1) 21
(Q) 6 − (−15)(2) 9
(R) (−7) × 8(3) −56
(S) (−135) ÷ (−15)(4) −23

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2
  3. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–1, R–3, S–4

Solution.

Step 1(−9) − 14 = (−9) + (−14) = −23, and 6 − (−15) = 6 + 15 = 21.
Step 2(−7) × 8 = −56, and (−135) ÷ (−15) = 9, since like signs give a positive quotient.
Answer(b) P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2
