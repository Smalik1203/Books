What this chapter showed
  1. A product of two integers is positive when both are positive or both are negative, and negative when one is positive and the other negative. Its magnitude is the product of the magnitudes.
  2. A quotient of two integers follows the same sign rules as a product.
  3. $1 \times a = a$ and $(-1) \times a = -a$ for every integer $a$.
  4. Multiplication of integers is commutative, $a \times b = b \times a$, and associative, $a \times (b \times c) = (a \times b) \times c$.
  5. Multiplication distributes over addition: $a \times (b + c) = a \times b + a \times c$.
