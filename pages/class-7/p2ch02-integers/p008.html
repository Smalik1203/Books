Signs of a quotient

For any positive integers $a$ and $b$: $a \div (-b) = -(a \div b)$, $(-a) \div b = -(a \div b)$, and $(-a) \div (-b) = a \div b$. The quotient is positive when the dividend and the divisor have the same sign, and negative when their signs are different.

2.4Expressions Using Integers

What is the value of $5 \times (-3) \times 4$? Does it matter whether we multiply $5 \times (-3)$ first and then multiply by 4, or multiply $(-3) \times 4$ first and then multiply 5 by the result?

$(5 \times (-3)) \times 4 = (-15) \times 4 = -60$

$5 \times ((-3) \times 4) = 5 \times (-12) = -60$

Try a few more products of three integers. You will find that grouping the multiplications in either way gives the same product.

Multiplication is associative

For any three integers $a$, $b$ and $c$, $a \times (b \times c) = (a \times b) \times c$.
