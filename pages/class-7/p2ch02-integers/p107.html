Why the other options are wrong

B2 $(-60) \div (-5) = 12$, and $12 \div (-4) = -3$. The answer is (b). Option (a) treats the second quotient as positive.
B4 The values are 30, 1, $-11$ and $-30$, so the answer is (a). Option (b) looks large, but $(-5) - (-6) = -5 + 6 = 1$.
B6 Twenty factors of $-1$ make ten pairs, and each pair is 1. R is true and is the reason. The answer is (a).
B8 $(-24) \div 6 = -4$, but $6 \div (-24)$ is not even an integer. Division is not commutative; the answer is (b).
C1 An odd number of negative factors is needed, and $60 = 3 \times 4 \times 5$, so the integers are $-5$, $-4$ and $-3$, the largest being $-3$. The answer is (a). Option (b) forgets that $3 \times 4 \times 5$ is positive.
C3 Pair the terms: $(1 - 2) + (3 - 4) + \cdots + (99 - 100)$ is fifty pairs of $-1$, which is $-50$. The answer is (b).
C4 He answered 27. All right would give 108, and each wrong answer changes the score by $-5$. Since $108 - 58 = 50$, 10 are wrong and 17 right. The answer is (c).
C5 A negative product needs one negative and one positive number, and the largest magnitudes give $(-4) \times 5 = -20$. The answer is (d). The other negative products possible, $(-4) \times 2 = -8$ and $(-1) \times 5 = -5$, are larger.
C6 $999 = 1000 - 1$, so $(-7) \times 999 = -7000 + 7$. The answer is (c). Option (a) gives $(-7) \times (-1)$ the wrong sign.

Three things from this section are worth keeping. Settle the sign of a product by counting its negative factors. Before multiplying, look for an order or a grouping that makes the numbers easy. And when a number is close to a round number, such as $-99$ or 999, split it and use the distributive property.
