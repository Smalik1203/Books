Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) The product of ten factors, each −1(1) 4
(Q) The product of eleven factors, each −1(2) −1
(R) (−2) × (−2) × (−2)(3) 1
(S) (−2) × (−2)(4) −8

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  2. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–2, R–4, S–3

Solution.

Step 1An even number of negative factors gives a positive product, and an odd number a negative one.
Step 2So ten factors of −1 give 1 and eleven give −1; (−2) × (−2) = 4 and (−2) × (−2) × (−2) = −8.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
