2.1A Quick Recap of Integers

Rakesh’s puzzle

Rakesh has a challenge for you. “I am thinking of two numbers,” he says. “Their sum is 25, and their difference is 11. What are my numbers?”

You do not need a formula. Try a pair of numbers, and check two things: do they add up to 25, and is the first number minus the second number equal to 11? Keep a record of your tries, as in Table 2.1.

Table 2.1 Pairs with a sum of 25
First numberSecond numberSumDifference
101525$-5$
2052515
1962513
1872511

The last row works: the numbers are 18 and 7. Now Rakesh gives a second challenge: find two numbers whose sum is 25 and whose difference is $-11$. Try pairs in the same way. You will find that swapping the two numbers from the first puzzle answers the second one: the first number is 7 and the second is 18, and $7 - 18 = -11$.
