Example 10 · Multiple correct

In Kargil the temperature at 6 pm was 6 °C. By 4 am it was −14 °C, and it fell by the same amount every hour. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It fell by 20 °C in all.
  2. It fell by 2 °C every hour.
  3. At 11 pm it was −4 °C.
  4. At midnight it was −8 °C.

Solution.

Step 1The change is (−14) − 6 = −20 °C over 10 hours, so −2 °C each hour.
Step 2At 11 pm, 5 hours after 6 pm: 6 + 5 × (−2) = −4 °C. At midnight, after 6 hours: 6 + 6 × (−2) = −6 °C.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

In Leh the temperature at 8 pm is −4 °C, and it falls by 3 °C every hour until 2 am. How many degrees below 0 °C is the temperature at 2 am?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 18 pm to 2 am is 6 hours, so the change is 6 × (−3) = −18 °C.
Step 2(−4) + (−18) = −22 °C, which is 22 degrees below zero.

Example 12 · Numerical answer

The sum of two integers is −3, and the first minus the second is 11. What is the first integer?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Adding the sum and the difference counts the first integer twice: (−3) + 11 = 8.
Step 2The first integer is 8 ÷ 2 = 4, and the second is −7. Check: 4 + (−7) = −3 and 4 − (−7) = 11.
Answer4
