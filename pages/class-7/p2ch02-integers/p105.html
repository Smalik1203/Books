Type 4 · Dividing integers

Example 11

Find (a) $(-72) \div 8$, (b) $96 \div (-12)$ and (c) $(-135) \div (-15)$. Check each answer by multiplying it by the divisor.

Solution.

Step 1 $(-72) \div 8 = -9$; $(-9) \times 8 = -72$ unlike signs
Step 2 $96 \div (-12) = -8$; $(-8) \times (-12) = 96$ unlike signs
Step 3 $(-135) \div (-15) = 9$; $9 \times (-15) = -135$ like signs
Answer (a) $-9$ (b) $-8$ (c) 9
Example 12

In Kargil the temperature at 6 pm was $6^\circ$C. By 4 am it was $-14^\circ$C. It fell by the same amount every hour. By how much did it change each hour?

Solution.

Step 1 6 pm to 4 am is 10 hours
Step 2 Change $= (-14) - 6 = -20$ later minus earlier
Step 3 $(-20) \div 10 = -2$ change each hour
Answer $-2^\circ$C each hour, a fall of $2^\circ$C

Type 5 · Many factors and the properties

Example 13

$(-3) \times (-4) \times (-5)$ is equal to

  1. 60
  2. $-60$
  3. $-12$
  4. 12

Solution.

Step 1 Three negative factors, so the product is negative odd count
Step 2 $3 \times 4 \times 5 = 60$ magnitudes
Answer (b)
