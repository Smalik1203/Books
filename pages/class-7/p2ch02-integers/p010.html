Pick the pattern

A pattern machine takes in three numbers, does some operations on them and gives out one result. Table 2.4 shows what Machine 1 does to five groups of numbers. Find its operations.

Machine 1 adds the first two numbers and subtracts the third. As an expression, the result is $a + b - c$, where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are the first, second and third numbers. For example, $5 + 8 - 3 = 10$ and $(-4) + (-1) - (-6) = 1$. So the result for the last group is $(-10) + (-12) - (-9)$. Find it.
