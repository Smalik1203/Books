Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these have the value −12?

Select all correct options.

  1. (−3) × 4
  2. (−3) × (−4)
  3. 48 ÷ (−4)
  4. (−20) − (−8)

Solution.

Step 1(−3) × 4 = −12, 48 ÷ (−4) = −12 and (−20) − (−8) = (−20) + 8 = −12.
Step 2(−3) × (−4) = 12: like signs give a positive product.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which statements are true for all integers a and b?

Select all correct options.

  1. a + b = b + a
  2. a − b = b − a
  3. a × b = b × a
  4. a ÷ b = b ÷ a whenever neither is zero

Solution.

Step 1Addition and multiplication of integers can be done in either order.
Step 25 − 8 = −3 but 8 − 5 = 3; 8 ÷ 4 = 2 but 4 ÷ 8 is not an integer.
Answer(a), (c)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which products are positive?

Select all correct options.

  1. (−2) × (−3) × (−4)
  2. (−1) × (−1) × (−1) × (−1)
  3. (−5) × 0
  4. (−7) × (−8)

Solution.

Step 1An even number of negative factors gives a positive product: four in (b), two in (d).
Step 2Three negative factors give a negative product, and any product with a factor 0 is 0, which is neither positive nor negative.
Answer(b), (d)
