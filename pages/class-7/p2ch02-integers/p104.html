Example 8

A diver starts 6 m above the water and goes down 4 m every minute. Taking positions above the water as positive, where is she after 5 minutes?

  1. $-26$ m
  2. $-14$ m
  3. 14 m
  4. $-20$ m

Solution.

Step 1 Change $= 5 \times (-4) = -20$ m going down is negative
Step 2 $6 + (-20) = -14$ start at 6 m
Answer (b)
Example 9

A test has 20 questions. A correct answer earns 3 marks and a wrong answer earns $-1$ mark. Priya answered every question and scored 40. How many did she get right?

  1. 13
  2. 14
  3. 15
  4. 20

Solution.

Step 1 All right: $20 \times 3 = 60$
Step 2 One wrong in place of right: $(-3) + (-1) = -4$ change per answer
Step 3 $(60 - 40) \div 4 = 5$ wrong, so $20 - 5 = 15$ right
Step 4 $15 \times 3 + 5 \times (-1) = 45 - 5 = 40$ check
Answer (c)

Example 10

Find (a) $(-72) \div 8$, (b) $96 \div (-12)$ and (c) $(-135) \div (-15)$. Check each answer by multiplying it by the divisor.

Solution.

Step 1 $(-72) \div 8 = -9$; $(-9) \times 8 = -72$ unlike signs
Step 2 $96 \div (-12) = -8$; $(-8) \times (-12) = 96$ unlike signs
Step 3 $(-135) \div (-15) = 9$; $9 \times (-15) = -135$ like signs
Answer (a) $-9$ (b) $-8$ (c) 9
