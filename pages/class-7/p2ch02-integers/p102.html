Solved Examples

Type 1 · Sums and differences of integers

Example 1

Find (a) $(-9) - 14$ and (b) $6 - (-15)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $(-9) - 14 = (-9) + (-14) = -23$ add the inverse
Step 2 $6 - (-15) = 6 + 15 = 21$ add the inverse
Answer (a) $-23$ (b) 21
Example 2

At 5 am Shimla was at $-3^\circ$C, and at noon at $8^\circ$C. Find the rise in temperature.

Solution.

Step 1 Rise $= 8 - (-3) = 8 + 3 = 11$ later minus earlier
Answer The temperature rose by $11^\circ$C.
Example 3

Is $a - b$ equal to $b - a$ for all integers $a$ and $b$? Give a reason.

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 5$, $b = 8$: $5 - 8 = -3$ and $8 - 5 = 3$ try one pair
Answer No. $a - b$ and $b - a$ are additive inverses, equal only when $a = b$.
Example 4

The sum of two integers is $-3$, and the first minus the second is 11. Find the two integers.

Solution.

Step 1 $0$ and $-3$: sum $-3$, difference $0 - (-3) = 3$ too small
Step 2 $2$ and $-5$: sum $-3$, difference $2 - (-5) = 7$ still too small
Step 3 $4$ and $-7$: sum $-3$, difference $4 - (-7) = 11$ both match
Answer The integers are 4 and $-7$.
