Problem 1

$(-3) \times (-4) \times (-5)$ is equal to

  1. 60
  2. $-60$
  3. $-12$
  4. 12
Solution

$(-3) \times (-4) = 12$

$12 \times (-5) = -60$

Pairing the first two negative factors gives a positive number, and the third negative factor makes the product negative.

The answer is (b). There are three negative factors, so the product is negative, which rules out (a) and (d). Options (c) and (d) add the numbers instead of multiplying them.

Problem 2

Which of these is equal to $(-6) \times (5 + (-3))$?

  1. $(-30) + 18$
  2. $(-30) - 18$
  3. $30 + (-18)$
  4. $(-30) + (-3)$
Solution

$(-6) \times (5 + (-3)) = (-6) \times 5 + (-6) \times (-3)$

$= (-30) + 18$

The answer is (a); both sides are $-12$. Option (b) gives $(-6) \times (-3)$ the wrong sign, option (c) gives $(-6) \times 5$ the wrong sign, and option (d) multiplies only the 5 by $-6$.
