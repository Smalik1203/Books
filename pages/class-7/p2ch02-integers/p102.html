Example 1 · Single correct

A diver starts 6 m above the water and goes down 4 m every minute. Taking positions above the water as positive, where is she after 5 minutes?

Choose one correct option.

  1. −26 m
  2. −14 m
  3. 14 m
  4. −20 m

Solution.

Step 1In 5 minutes she goes down 5 × 4 = 20 m, a change of −20 m.
Step 2Position = 6 + (−20) = −14 m, that is, 14 m below the water.
Answer(b) −14 m
Example 2 · Single correct

A test has 20 questions. A correct answer earns 3 marks and a wrong answer earns −1 mark. Priya answered every question and scored 40. How many did she get right?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 14
  2. 16
  3. 15
  4. 13

Solution.

Step 1Each right answer turned into a wrong one changes the score by 3 + 1 = 4 marks. All 20 right would give 60.
Step 260 − 40 = 20 = 5 × 4, so 5 are wrong and 15 are right. Check: 15 × 3 + 5 × (−1) = 40.
Answer(c) 15
