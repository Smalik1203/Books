Example 2

A lift in a mine shaft moves above and below the ground. Positions above the ground are positive and positions below it are negative. The lift moves at 3 metres per minute. (a) It goes down from ground level, 0. Where is it after one hour? (b) It starts at 15 m above the ground and goes down. Where is it after 45 minutes?

Solution.

Step 1 (a) $60 \times 3 = 180$ m one hour, 60 minutes
Step 2 $0 - 180 = -180$ going down from 0
Step 3 in 60 minutes, $60 \times (-3) = -180$ m $-3$ m every minute
Step 4 (b) it moves $45 \times (-3)$ m from 15 m the second way
Step 5 $15 + 45 \times (-3) = 15 + (-135)$
Step 6 $= -120$
Answer (a) 180 m below the ground; (b) 120 m below the ground

Solve part (b) by the first way too.
