Example 10 · Multiple correct

Which statements are true for every pair of counting numbers a and b?

Select all correct options.

  1. The HCF of a and b is a factor of their LCM.
  2. HCF × LCM = a × b
  3. The LCM of a and b is greater than both a and b.
  4. The HCF of a and a + 1 is 1.

Solution.

Step 1The HCF divides a, and a divides the LCM, so the HCF divides the LCM. The product rule holds for every pair of numbers.
Step 2For 4 and 12 the LCM is 12, which is not greater than 12. So (c) fails.
Step 3A common factor of a and a + 1 also divides their difference, 1.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

Find the smallest number that leaves a remainder of 7 when divided by each of 12, 16 and 20.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 112 = 2 × 2 × 3, 16 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 and 20 = 2 × 2 × 5, so their LCM is 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 = 240.
Step 2The number is 7 more than a common multiple, and the smallest is 240 + 7 = 247.
Answer247
Example 12 · Numerical answer

A floor 480 cm long and 336 cm wide is covered with the largest square tiles that fit exactly, without cutting any tile. How many tiles are used?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1480 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 and 336 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 7, so the side of a tile is the HCF, 48 cm.
Step 2480 ÷ 48 = 10 tiles along the length and 336 ÷ 48 = 7 across: 10 × 7 = 70 tiles.
Answer70
