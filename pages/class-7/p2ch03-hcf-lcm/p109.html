Assertion and reason

In Questions 17 to 20, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): The HCF of 15 and 45 is 15.

    Reason (R): If one number is a factor of another number, their HCF is the smaller number.

  1. Assertion (A): The LCM of 6 and 9 is 54.

    Reason (R): The product of two numbers is a common multiple of the two numbers.

  1. Assertion (A): 9 and 16 are co-prime.

    Reason (R): Two co-prime numbers must both be primes.

  1. Assertion (A): The HCF of 12 and 35 is 1.

    Reason (R): 12 and 35 are both composite numbers.
