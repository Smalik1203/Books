Case-based questions

  1. For Republic Day, 96 students of Class 7 and 120 students of Class 8 march in rows. Every row has the same number of students, and each row has students from one class only.

    1. What is the largest number of students that each row can have?
    2. How many rows are there then?
    3. If each row has 12 students, how many rows are there?
    4. Could each row have 16 students? Give a reason.
    5. Which row sizes between 10 and 20 are possible?

  1. At a bus stand in Mysuru, a bus to Bengaluru leaves every 20 minutes, a bus to Mandya every 30 minutes and a bus to Hassan every 45 minutes. At 6:00 a.m. all three buses leave together.

    1. When do the Bengaluru and Mandya buses next leave together?
    2. When do all three buses next leave together?
    3. How many times do all three buses leave together from 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., counting both of these times?
    4. How many Bengaluru buses leave after 6:00 a.m., up to and including 9:00 a.m.?
