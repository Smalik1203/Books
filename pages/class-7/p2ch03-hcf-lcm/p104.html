Example 7

The LCM of 12, 15 and 20 is

  1. 180
  2. 3600
  3. 60
  4. 120

Solution.

Step 1 $12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3$, $15 = 3 \times 5$, $20 = 2 \times 2 \times 5$
Step 2 $\text{LCM} = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5 = 60$ each prime, most times
Answer (c)
Example 8

Asha beats a drum every 6 seconds, Ravi strikes a cymbal every 10 seconds and Leela rings a bell every 15 seconds. They start together. After how many seconds do all three sound together again?

Solution.

Step 1 $6 = 2 \times 3$, $10 = 2 \times 5$, $15 = 3 \times 5$
Step 2 $\text{LCM} = 2 \times 3 \times 5 = 30$ first common time
Answer after 30 seconds

Type 4 · Dividing both numbers together, and doubling

Example 9

Find the HCF and the LCM of 72 and 120 by dividing both numbers together.

Solution.

Step 1 $72, 120 \to 36, 60 \to 18, 30 \to 9, 15 \to 3, 5$ divide by 2, 2, 2, 3
Step 2 $\text{HCF} = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 24$ the divisors
Step 3 $\text{LCM} = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5 = 360$ divisors, then 3 and 5
Answer HCF 24, LCM 360
Example 10

The HCF of 36 and 48 is 12. Without factorising again, find the HCF of 72 and 96.

Solution.

Step 1 $72 = 36 \times 2$, $96 = 48 \times 2$ both doubled
Step 2 $\text{HCF} = 12 \times 2 = 24$ the HCF doubles
Answer 24
