Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) Largest jug that measures 24 L and 36 L exactly (in litres)(1) 6
(Q) Seconds until two lights blinking every 24 s and 36 s next blink together(2) 72
(R) Shortest length (in cm) that can be cut exactly into 8 cm pieces or into 12 cm pieces(3) 12
(S) Largest number of identical bags using all of 18 pens and 30 pencils(4) 24

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–2, R–4, S–3
  2. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  4. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1

Solution.

Step 1The jug and the bags ask for a common factor that is as large as possible: HCF(24, 36) = 12 and HCF(18, 30) = 6.
Step 2The lights and the length ask for a common multiple that is as small as possible: LCM(24, 36) = 72 and LCM(8, 12) = 24.
Answer(d) P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
