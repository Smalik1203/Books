Why the other options are wrong

B1 $84 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 7$ and $126 = 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 7$, so the HCF is $2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$. The answer is (b). Option (d) is the LCM.
B3 The product is $9 \times 270 = 2430$, and $2430 \div 54 = 45$. The answer is (a).
B5 The LCM of 40 and 60 is 120 seconds, which is 2 minutes. The answer is (a). Option (b) adds the two times.
B7 The longest piece is the HCF, 24 cm, giving $72 \div 24 = 3$ and $120 \div 24 = 5$ pieces, 8 in all. The answer is (b). Option (a) is the length of a piece, not the number of pieces.
C1 Divide both by the HCF: the two quotients have no common factor and multiply to $60 \div 5 = 12$, so they are 1 and 12, or 3 and 4. The pairs are 5 and 60, and 15 and 20. The answer is (b).
C3 Each number is a multiple of 8, so the LCM is a multiple of 8. 60 is not. The answer is (c).
C4 The number divides $70 - 5 = 65$ and $125 - 8 = 117$, and $65 = 5 \times 13$, $117 = 3 \times 3 \times 13$, so it is 13. The answer is (c). Option (b) does not divide 117.
C6 Two odd numbers that differ by 2 have no common factor except 1, since a common factor would also divide 2. So the LCM is their product. The answer is (d).
C7 The numbers are $12a$ and $12b$, with $a + b = 8$ and no common factor. So $a$ and $b$ are 1 and 7, or 3 and 5, giving 12 and 84, and 36 and 60. The answer is (b).

Three things from this section are worth keeping. Decide first whether a question wants the largest thing that fits into both numbers, the HCF, or the first thing both numbers fit into, the LCM. Write prime factorisations one under the other, so the shared primes are easy to see. And remember that the HCF divides the LCM, and their product is the product of the two numbers.
