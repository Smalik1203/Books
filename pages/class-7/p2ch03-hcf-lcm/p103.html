Example 4

The HCF of $2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5$ and $2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 7$ is

  1. 12
  2. 7560
  3. 6
  4. 24

Solution.

Step 1 Common primes: 2 and 3
Step 2 2s: two and three, so take two fewest times
Step 3 3s: three and one, so take one fewest times
Step 4 $\text{HCF} = 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 12$
Answer (a)
Example 5

Neha has 60 red beads and 84 blue beads. She makes as many strings of beads as she can, all exactly alike, using every bead. How many strings does she make, and how many beads of each colour are on each string?

Solution.

Step 1 $60 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5$, $84 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 7$
Step 2 $\text{HCF} = 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 12$ most strings
Step 3 $60 \div 12 = 5$, $84 \div 12 = 7$ beads on a string
Answer 12 strings, each with 5 red beads and 7 blue beads

Example 6

The LCM of two numbers is 24, and one of the numbers is 8. What could the other number be? Find every answer.

Solution.

Step 1 $24 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3$, $8 = 2 \times 2 \times 2$
Step 2 Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 24 is its multiple
Step 3 Keep those with a 3: 3, 6, 12, 24 8 has no 3
Answer 3, 6, 12 or 24
