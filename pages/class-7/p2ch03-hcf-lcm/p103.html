Example 4 · Single correct

What is the largest number that divides 245 and 1029, leaving a remainder of 5 each time?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 16
  2. 48
  3. 8
  4. 32

Solution.

Step 1The number divides 245 − 5 = 240 and 1029 − 5 = 1024 exactly.
Step 2240 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 and 1024 is ten 2s multiplied together, so the HCF is 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16.
Answer(a) 16
Example 5 · Single correct

Three bells ring every 12, 18 and 30 minutes. They ring together at 8:00 a.m. When do they next ring together?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 9:00 a.m.
  2. 9:30 a.m.
  3. 10:30 a.m.
  4. 11:00 a.m.

Solution.

Step 112 = 2 × 2 × 3, 18 = 2 × 3 × 3 and 30 = 2 × 3 × 5, so the LCM is 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5 = 180.
Step 2180 minutes is 3 hours after 8:00 a.m.
Answer(d) 11:00 a.m.
Example 6 · Single correct

Two counting numbers have HCF 8 and sum 64. How many such pairs are there? Count a pair only once, whichever number is written first.

Choose one correct option.

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 4

Solution.

Step 1Write the numbers as 8 × p and 8 × q. Then p + q = 64 ÷ 8 = 8, and p and q have no common factor except 1.
Step 2Of 1 + 7, 2 + 6, 3 + 5 and 4 + 4, only 1 + 7 and 3 + 5 qualify. The pairs are 8, 56 and 24, 40.
Answer(b) 2
