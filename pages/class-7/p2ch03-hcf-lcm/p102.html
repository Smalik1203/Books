Type 1 · Prime factorisation and factors

Example 1

Find the prime factorisation of 252 by the division method.

Solution.

Step 1 $252 \div 2 = 126$ 252 is even
Step 2 $126 \div 2 = 63$
Step 3 $63 \div 3 = 21$
Step 4 $21 \div 3 = 7$ 7 is a prime
Answer $252 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 7$
Example 2

Use $252 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 7$ to find out whether 42 and 8 are factors of 252.

Solution.

Step 1 $42 = 2 \times 3 \times 7$ a part of 252
Step 2 $252 = (2 \times 3 \times 7) \times (2 \times 3) = 42 \times 6$
Step 3 $8 = 2 \times 2 \times 2$ 252 has only two 2s
Answer 42 is a factor of 252, and 8 is not.

Type 2 · Finding the HCF

Example 3

Three tanks hold 32 L, 48 L and 80 L. What is the largest jug that can measure each of them exactly, using whole jugfuls?

  1. 8 L
  2. 4 L
  3. 480 L
  4. 16 L

Solution.

Step 1 $32 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2$, $48 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3$, $80 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 5$
Step 2 $\text{HCF} = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 16$ fewest 2s: four
Step 3 $32 \div 16 = 2$, $48 \div 16 = 3$, $80 \div 16 = 5$ whole jugfuls
Answer (d)
