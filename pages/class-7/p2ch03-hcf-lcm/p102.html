Example 1 · Single correct

What is the HCF of 84 and 126?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 14
  2. 21
  3. 42
  4. 252

Solution.

Step 184 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 7 and 126 = 2 × 3 × 3 × 7.
Step 2The common primes, each taken the fewest times it occurs, are 2, 3 and 7: HCF = 2 × 3 × 7 = 42.
Answer(c) 42
Example 2 · Single correct

What is the LCM of 18, 24 and 30?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 180
  2. 360
  3. 720
  4. 120

Solution.

Step 118 = 2 × 3 × 3, 24 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 and 30 = 2 × 3 × 5.
Step 2Take each prime the most times it occurs: LCM = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5 = 360.
Answer(b) 360
Example 3 · Single correct

Two numbers have HCF 12 and LCM 180. One of them is 36. What is the other?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 60
  2. 48
  3. 72
  4. 90

Solution.

Step 1HCF × LCM equals the product of the two numbers: 12 × 180 = 2160.
Step 2The other number is 2160 ÷ 36 = 60. Check: 36 = 12 × 3 and 60 = 12 × 5 give HCF 12 and LCM 12 × 3 × 5 = 180.
Answer(a) 60
