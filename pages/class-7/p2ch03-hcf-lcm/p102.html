Two bells ring together at 9:00. One rings every 12 minutes and the other every 18 minutes. When do they next ring together?

This time we want a time that is a multiple of both 12 and 18, and the first such time: the LCM. Since $12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3$ and $18 = 2 \times 3 \times 3$, the LCM is $2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 36$. They ring together again at 9:36. “Largest piece that fits into both” asks for an HCF; “first time both happen” asks for an LCM.

Problem 1

The HCF of $2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5$ and $2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 7$ is

  1. 12
  2. 7560
  3. 6
  4. 24
Solution

The common primes are 2 and 3. The first number has two 2s and the second has three, so take two 2s. The first has three 3s and the second one, so take one 3.

$\text{HCF} = 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 12$

The answer is (a). Option (b) is the LCM, option (c) takes only one 2, and option (d) takes three 2s.

Problem 2

The LCM of 12, 15 and 20 is

  1. 180
  2. 3600
  3. 60
  4. 120
Solution

$12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3$, $15 = 3 \times 5$, $20 = 2 \times 2 \times 5$

$\text{LCM} = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5 = 60$

The answer is (c). Option (b) is the product of all three numbers, and options (a) and (d) are common multiples but not the lowest.
