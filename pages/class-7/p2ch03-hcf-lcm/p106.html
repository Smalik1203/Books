Example 14

The smallest number greater than 1 that leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by each of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 is

  1. 61
  2. 31
  3. 121
  4. 59

Solution.

Step 1 The number less 1 is a common multiple of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6. remainder 1
Step 2 $\text{LCM} = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5 = 60$
Step 3 $60 + 1 = 61$ smallest above 1
Answer (a)
