Example 13 · Numerical answer

Find the smallest square number that is divisible by each of 6, 8 and 15.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The number must be a multiple of the LCM, 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 = 120.
Step 2In a square every prime occurs an even number of times, so one more 2, 3 and 5 are needed: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5 × 5 = 3600 = 60 × 60.
Answer3600
