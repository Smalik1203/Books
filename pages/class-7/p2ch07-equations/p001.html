7.1Find the Unknowns

Unknown weights

Look at the hanging toys in Fig. 7.1. Each is a bar hung from its middle, with objects hanging from its two ends. The number in the disc at the top is the total weight of everything below it. All weights are in the same unit, and objects of the same shape always have the same weight.

A bar hangs level only when its two sides weigh the same. In (a), the total of 4 is shared as 2 and 2, so the bar is level. In (b), the total of 7 is shared as 4 and 3, and the bar tips down on the heavier side. So in a level toy, each side carries exactly half of the total weight.

Every toy in Fig. 7.2 and Fig. 7.3 hangs level. Under each toy, the weights of some of its shapes are given. Find the weights marked ?. Write down how you found each one, so that you can explain it to a classmate.
