A Magic Trick
  1. Think of any number.
  2. Multiply it by 2.
  3. Add 10.
  4. Divide by 2.
  5. Subtract the number you first thought of.
  6. Add 3.

I predict that you now have 8. Am I right? Try the trick on your friends and family. Can you explain why it works? (Hint: call the number you first thought of $x$.) Can you make up a trick of your own?
