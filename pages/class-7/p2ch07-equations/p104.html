Example 7

Solve $3 - 2x = 5x - 11$, and check your solution.

Solution.

Step 1 $3 + 11 = 5x + 2x$ move $-2x$ and $-11$
Step 2 $14 = 7x$
Step 3 $x = 14 \div 7 = 2$ divide both by 7
Step 4 LHS $= 3 - 2 \times 2 = -1$, RHS $= 5 \times 2 - 11 = -1$ check
Answer $x = 2$
Example 8

Solve $\frac{w}{4} - 1 = 5$, and check your solution.

Solution.

Step 1 $\frac{w}{4} = 5 + 1 = 6$ move $-1$ across
Step 2 $w = 6 \times 4 = 24$ move the divisor 4
Step 3 $\frac{24}{4} - 1 = 6 - 1 = 5$ check
Answer $w = 24$

Example 9

A pen costs ₹12 more than a pencil. Three pens and five pencils cost ₹84 in all. The cost of one pencil is

  1. ₹18
  2. ₹9
  3. ₹6
  4. ₹10.50

Solution.

Step 1 Pencil ₹$p$, pen ₹$(p + 12)$ ₹12 more
Step 2 $3(p + 12) + 5p = 84$ three pens, five pencils
Step 3 $8p + 36 = 84$, so $8p = 48$ and $p = 6$ move 36 across
Step 4 $3 \times 18 + 5 \times 6 = 54 + 30 = 84$ check
Answer (c)
