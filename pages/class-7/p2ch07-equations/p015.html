Example 13

Think of a real-life situation that the equation $100x + 75 = 250$ could describe.

Think of the numbers as amounts of money, with ₹250 in all. The LHS has two terms. The term 75 is fixed, while the value of $100x$ changes with $x$. So 75 could be a fixed charge, and $100x$ could be a charge of ₹100 for each unit of something. For example, a plumber may charge ₹75 to visit a house and ₹100 for each hour of work. If the bill comes to ₹250, then $x$ is the number of hours the plumber worked.
