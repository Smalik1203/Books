A father is 32 years older than his son. In 4 years, the father will be three times as old as his son. How old is the son now?

Let the son be $s$ years old now. In 4 years, he will be $s + 4$ and his father will be $s + 36$. So $s + 36 = 3(s + 4) = 3s + 12$. Subtracting $s$ and 12 from both sides gives $24 = 2s$, so $s = 12$. The son is 12 and his father is 44. In 4 years they will be 16 and 48, and 48 is three times 16.

Brahmagupta’s rule gives the solution of $Ax + B = Cx + D$ as $x = \dfrac{D - B}{A - C}$. What happens if we use it on $4x + 1 = 4x + 9$?

Here $A = C = 4$, so $A - C = 0$, and the rule asks us to divide by 0, which we cannot do. Look at the equation itself: subtracting $4x$ from both sides leaves $1 = 9$, so it has no solution. The rule works only when $A$ and $C$ are different, and the equation tells us why.

Problem 1

The solution of $4(x - 2) = 2x + 6$ is

  1. 7
  2. 4
  3. $-1$
  4. 14
Solution

$4x - 8 = 2x + 6$   (opening the bracket)

$2x = 14$   (subtracting $2x$ and adding 8 on both sides)

$x = 7$

The answer is (a). Option (b) comes from opening the bracket as $4x - 2$, multiplying only the $x$ by 4. Option (c) moves $-8$ to the RHS without changing its sign. Option (d) stops at $2x = 14$ and forgets to divide by 2.
