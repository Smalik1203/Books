What this chapter showed
  1. An equation is a statement that two algebraic expressions are equal.
  2. When the same operation is done on both sides of an equation, the two sides stay equal.
  3. Solving an equation means finding the values of the unknowns that make the LHS equal to the RHS.
  4. We can often solve an equation by doing the same operation on both sides, again and again, until the value of the unknown can be seen.
  5. A term can be removed from one side if its additive inverse is written on the other side; a factor or a divisor can be removed from one side if the other side is divided or multiplied by it.
