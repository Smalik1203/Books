In the second way, we notice that 28, 300 and 1000 can all be divided by 4. Dividing both sides by 4 divides each term by 4, and gives a simpler equation.

$7(x + 4) + 75 = 250$

$7(x + 4) = 175$   (subtracting 75 from both sides)

$7x + 28 = 175$

$7x = 147$   (subtracting 28 from both sides)

$x = 147 \div 7 = 21$

In the third way, we open the bracket first.

$28x + 112 + 300 = 1000$

$28x + 412 = 1000$

$28x = 1000 - 412 = 588$   (subtracting 412 from both sides)

$x = 588 \div 28 = 21$
