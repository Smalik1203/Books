Example 10

Rahul writes: $\frac{x}{3} - 2 = 5$, so $x - 2 = 15$, so $x = 17$. Which statement is true?

  1. Rahul’s solution is correct.
  2. His first step is wrong, and the solution is $x = 21$.
  3. His second step is wrong, and the solution is $x = 13$.
  4. Both steps are wrong, and the solution is $x = 7$.

Solution.

Step 1 $x - 6 = 15$, not $x - 2 = 15$ 3 multiplies every term
Step 2 $x = 15 + 6 = 21$ move $-6$ across
Step 3 $\frac{21}{3} - 2 = 7 - 2 = 5$ check
Answer (b)
Example 11

Kabir buys 3 pens at ₹$p$ each and a notebook for ₹12, and pays ₹42. He writes $3p + 12 = 42$, so $p + 12 = 14$, so $p = 2$. Is he right? Find the price of a pen.

Solution.

Step 1 $p + 4 = 14$, so $p = 10$ 3 divides every term
Step 2 $3 \times 10 + 12 = 42$, but $3 \times 2 + 12 = 18$ check both
Answer No, he did not divide 12 by 3. A pen costs ₹10.

Example 12

Solve $7x - 2 = 3x + 10$ by Brahmagupta’s rule, and check the solution.

Solution.

Step 1 $A = 7$, $B = -2$, $C = 3$, $D = 10$ compare with $Ax + B = Cx + D$
Step 2 $x = \dfrac{10 - (-2)}{7 - 3} = \dfrac{12}{4} = 3$ $x = \dfrac{D - B}{A - C}$
Step 3 $7 \times 3 - 2 = 19$ and $3 \times 3 + 10 = 19$ check
Answer $x = 3$
