Example 10

A pattern uses $5n + 2$ sticks at position $n$. Which of these numbers of sticks makes an arrangement in the pattern?

  1. 100
  2. 112
  3. 125
  4. 150

Solution.

Step 1 $5n + 2 = 112$ gives $5n = 110$ and $n = 22$ a counting number
Step 2 100, 125 and 150 give $5n = 98$, $123$ and $148$ not multiples of 5
Step 3 $5 \times 22 + 2 = 112$ check
Answer (b)

Example 11

Find the mistake in this solution, and correct it: $9 - 2y = 3$, so $2y = 3 - 9$, so $2y = -6$, so $y = -3$.

Solution.

Step 1 $9 - 2 \times (-3) = 15$, not 3 the check fails
Step 2 $-2y = 3 - 9 = -6$, so $y = 3$ $-2y$ keeps its sign
Step 3 $9 - 2 \times 3 = 3$ check
Answer The second line drops the minus sign of $-2y$; $y = 3$
Example 12

Rahul writes: $\frac{x}{3} - 2 = 5$, so $x - 2 = 15$, so $x = 17$. Which statement is true?

  1. Rahul’s solution is correct.
  2. His first step is wrong, and the solution is $x = 21$.
  3. His second step is wrong, and the solution is $x = 13$.
  4. Both steps are wrong, and the solution is $x = 7$.

Solution.

Step 1 $x - 6 = 15$, not $x - 2 = 15$ 3 multiplies every term
Step 2 $x = 15 + 6 = 21$ move $-6$ across
Step 3 $\frac{21}{3} - 2 = 7 - 2 = 5$ check
Answer (b)
