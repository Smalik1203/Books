Example 4

We know that $\frac{35}{113} \times 24 \times 14 \times \frac{8}{9} = \frac{94080}{1017}$. What is the value of $\frac{35}{113} \times 24 \times 14$?

To leave only this expression on the LHS, we remove the factor $\frac{8}{9}$ by dividing both sides by $\frac{8}{9}$:

$\frac{35}{113} \times 24 \times 14 \times \frac{8}{9} \div \frac{8}{9} = \frac{94080}{1017} \div \frac{8}{9}$

$\frac{35}{113} \times 24 \times 14 = \frac{94080}{1017} \times \frac{9}{8} = \frac{11760}{113}$

Solving an equation step by step

Let us use these ideas to solve $5x - 4 = 7$. What can we do so that only $5x$ is left on the LHS, while the two sides stay equal? We need to remove the term $-4$, and we can do this by adding 4 to both sides.

$5x - 4 + 4 = 7 + 4$

$5x = 11$

To leave only $x$ on the LHS, we remove the factor 5 by dividing both sides by 5.

$5x \div 5 = 11 \div 5$

$x = \frac{11}{5}$

Is $x = \frac{11}{5}$ correct? We can check by putting $\frac{11}{5}$ in place of $x$ in the LHS.

LHS $= 5 \times \frac{11}{5} - 4 = 11 - 4 = 7$

This is the RHS, so $\frac{11}{5}$ is indeed the solution of the equation.

Example 5

Solve the equation $11y + (-5) = 61$.

To leave only the term $11y$ on the LHS, we remove the term $-5$ by subtracting $-5$ from both sides.

$11y + (-5) - (-5) = 61 - (-5)$

$11y = 66$

Since $11 \times 6 = 66$, we can see that $y = 6$. We can also divide both sides by 11: $11y \div 11 = 66 \div 11$, so $y = 6$.
