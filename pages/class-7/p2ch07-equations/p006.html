Example 1

We know that $14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14 + 88 = 13353$. What is the value of $14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14$?

Solution.

Do we need to work the expression out, one term at a time? No. It is exactly the LHS without the term $+ 88$, and we already know the value of the LHS.

Step 1 $14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14 + 88 - 88 = 13353 - 88$ subtract 88 from both
Answer $14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14 = 13265$

This works because addition and subtraction are inverse operations: one undoes the other. Working the expression out term by term would have taken four calculations, and this took one.

Example 2

We know that $23 \times 41 \times 11 \times 8 \times 7 = 5{,}80{,}888$. What is the value of $23 \times 41 \times 11 \times 8$?

Solution.

Multiplication and division are inverse operations: dividing by 7 undoes multiplying by 7.

Step 1 $23 \times 41 \times 11 \times 8 = 5{,}80{,}888 \div 7 = 82{,}984$ divide by 7
Check $82{,}984 \times 7 = 5{,}80{,}888$
Answer $23 \times 41 \times 11 \times 8 = 82{,}984$
