Example 1

We know that $14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14 + 88 = 13353$. What is the value of $14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14$?

Do we need to work the expression out, one term at a time? No. It is exactly the LHS without the term $+ 88$, and we already know the value of the LHS. Subtracting 88 from both sides removes that term from the LHS:

$14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14 + 88 - 88 = 13353 - 88$

So $14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14 = 13265$. This works because addition and subtraction are inverse operations: one undoes the other. Working the expression out term by term would have taken four calculations, and this took one.

Example 2

We know that $23 \times 41 \times 11 \times 8 \times 7 = 5{,}80{,}888$. What is the value of $23 \times 41 \times 11 \times 8$?

Multiplication and division are inverse operations: dividing by 7 undoes multiplying by 7. So we can simply divide $5{,}80{,}888$ by 7. This gives $23 \times 41 \times 11 \times 8 = 82{,}984$. We can check the answer: $82{,}984 \times 7 = 5{,}80{,}888$.

Is dividing $5{,}80{,}888$ by 7 the same as dividing both sides of the equation by 7, so that the factor 7 is removed from the LHS?

Example 3

We know that $12345 - 5432 + 135 - 24 - (-67) = 7091$. What is the value of $12345 - 5432 + 135 - 24$?

We could again use the fact that addition and subtraction undo each other. Here is another way, which is easy to picture. We want to remove the term $-(-67)$ from the LHS. Adding $-67$ to both sides does this, because $-(-67) + (-67) = 0$:

$12345 - 5432 + 135 - 24 - (-67) + (-67) = 7091 + (-67)$

So $12345 - 5432 + 135 - 24 = 7024$. Adding the additive inverse of a term to both sides removes that term from one side and keeps the two sides equal. We will use this again to solve equations.
