Example 4

Write two equations whose solution is $m = -4$.

Solution.

Step 1 $m = -4$, so $2m = -8$ multiply both sides by 2
Step 2 $2m + 5 = -8 + 5 = -3$ add 5 to both sides
Step 3 $m = -4$, so $-m = 4$ and $6 - m = 10$ change both signs, add 6
Step 4 $2 \times (-4) + 5 = -3$ and $6 - (-4) = 10$ check
Answer $2m + 5 = -3$ and $6 - m = 10$
Example 5

Think of a real-life situation that the equation $3x - 20 = 70$ could describe, and find $x$ in it.

Solution.

Step 1 $3x$ changes with $x$; 20 is fixed and is taken away; 70 in all read the terms
Step 2 3 notebooks at ₹$x$ each, ₹20 off with a coupon, bill ₹70 one situation
Step 3 $3x = 70 + 20 = 90$, so $x = 30$ add 20, divide by 3
Answer For example, 3 notebooks with ₹20 off cost ₹70; each notebook costs ₹30.

Example 6

The solution of $4(x - 2) = 2x + 6$ is

  1. 7
  2. 4
  3. $-1$
  4. 14

Solution.

Step 1 $4x - 8 = 2x + 6$ open the bracket
Step 2 $4x - 2x = 6 + 8$ move $2x$ and $-8$
Step 3 $2x = 14$, so $x = 7$ divide both by 2
Step 4 LHS $= 4 \times 5 = 20$, RHS $= 2 \times 7 + 6 = 20$ check
Answer (a)
