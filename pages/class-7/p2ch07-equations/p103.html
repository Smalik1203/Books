Example 3 · Single correct

A pattern uses $5n + 2$ sticks at position $n$. Which of these numbers of sticks makes an arrangement in the pattern?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 100
  2. 112
  3. 125
  4. 150

Solution.

Step 1$5n + 2 = 112$ gives $5n = 110$ and $n = 22$, a counting number.
Step 2For 100, 125 and 150, the number minus 2 (98, 123, 148) is not a multiple of 5.
Answer(b) 112
Example 4 · Single correct

Rahul writes: $\frac{x}{3} - 2 = 5$, so $x - 2 = 15$, so $x = 17$. Which statement is true?

Choose one correct option.

  1. Rahul’s solution is correct.
  2. His first step is wrong, and the solution is 21.
  3. His first step is wrong, and the solution is 9.
  4. His second step is wrong, and the solution is 13.

Solution.

Step 1He multiplied only $\frac{x}{3}$ by 3, not the 2. Correctly, $\frac{x}{3} = 5 + 2 = 7$.
Step 2$x = 7 \times 3 = 21$. Check: $21 \div 3 - 2 = 5$.
Answer(b) His first step is wrong, and the solution is 21.
Example 5 · Single correct

What is the solution of $3 - 2x = 5x - 11$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 14
  2. −2
  3. 7
  4. 2

Solution.

Step 1Move $-2x$ and $-11$ across: $3 + 11 = 5x + 2x$, so $14 = 7x$.
Step 2$x = 2$. Check: $3 - 4 = -1$ and $10 - 11 = -1$.
Answer(d) 2
