Type 2 · Solving an equation step by step

Example 4

The solution of $4(x - 2) = 2x + 6$ is

  1. 7
  2. 4
  3. $-1$
  4. 14

Solution.

Step 1 $4x - 8 = 2x + 6$ open the bracket
Step 2 $4x - 2x = 6 + 8$ move $2x$ and $-8$
Step 3 $2x = 14$, so $x = 7$ divide both by 2
Step 4 LHS $= 4 \times 5 = 20$, RHS $= 2 \times 7 + 6 = 20$ check
Answer (a)
Example 5

Solve $3 - 2x = 5x - 11$, and check your solution.

Solution.

Step 1 $3 + 11 = 5x + 2x$ move $-2x$ and $-11$
Step 2 $14 = 7x$
Step 3 $x = 14 \div 7 = 2$ divide both by 7
Step 4 LHS $= 3 - 2 \times 2 = -1$, RHS $= 5 \times 2 - 11 = -1$ check
Answer $x = 2$
Example 6

Solve $\frac{w}{4} - 1 = 5$, and check your solution.

Solution.

Step 1 $\frac{w}{4} = 5 + 1 = 6$ move $-1$ across
Step 2 $w = 6 \times 4 = 24$ move the divisor 4
Step 3 $\frac{24}{4} - 1 = 6 - 1 = 5$ check
Answer $w = 24$
