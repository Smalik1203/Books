Assertion and reason

In Questions 22 to 25, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $x = 4$ is a solution of $5x - 8 = 12$.

    Reason (R): A solution of an equation makes the LHS equal to the RHS.

  1. Assertion (A): $x = 6$ is the solution of $2x + 3 = 15$.

    Reason (R): Multiplying both sides of an equation by the same number keeps the two sides equal.

  1. Assertion (A): $x = -2$ is a solution of $3x + 10 = 4$.

    Reason (R): The solution of an equation is always a positive number.

  1. Assertion (A): Dividing both sides of $4x + 8 = 20$ by 4 gives $x + 8 = 5$.

    Reason (R): When both sides of an equation are divided by a number, every term on each side is divided by it.
