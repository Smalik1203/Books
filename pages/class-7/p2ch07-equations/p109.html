Short answer
  1. When 7 is subtracted from 4 times a number, the result is 8 more than the number. Find the number.
  1. Solve $3(2x - 1) = 4x + 7$, and check your solution.
  1. The length of a rectangle is 5 cm more than its breadth. Its perimeter is 50 cm. Find its length and its breadth.
Long answer
  1. Look at the equation $5(x - 3) = 2x + 6$.
    1. Solve it, and check your solution.
    2. Open the bracket, write down $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$, and solve the equation by Brahmagupta’s rule.
    3. Change only the number 6 so that the solution is $x = 8$.
