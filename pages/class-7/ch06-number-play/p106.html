Example 13

Meena makes a border 6 cm long by laying red strips 1 cm long and blue strips 2 cm long end to end. How many different borders can she make?

Solution.

Step 1 first strip red: 5 cm left; first strip blue: 4 cm left as for rhythms
Step 2 borders of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 cm: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13 add the two before

Example 14

How many rhythms of 7 beats can be made from short syllables (1 beat) and long syllables (2 beats)?

  1. 21
  2. 13
  3. 14
  4. 34

Solution.

Step 1 begins short: 13 rhythms; begins long: 8 rhythms 6 or 5 beats left
Step 2 $13 + 8 = 21$ every rhythm begins one way
Answer (a)
Example 15

Show that two neighbouring Virahanka numbers are never both even.

Solution.

Step 1 the number just before two neighbours is their difference each is a sum
Step 2 if both are even, every number before them is even $-$ even $=$ even
Answer The first number, 1, is odd, so two neighbours are never both even.

Example 16

In the subtraction 7A $-$ A7 $=$ B5, find the digits A and B.

Solution.

Step 1 ones: A ${} - 7$ is at most 2, so 1 ten is borrowed to end in 5
Step 2 ones: $10 + \mathrm{A} - 7 = 5$, so A $= 2$
Step 3 tens: $7 - 1 - 2 = 4$, so B $= 4$ 1 ten borrowed
Answer A $= 2$ and B $= 4$. Check: $72 - 27 = 45$.
