Example 12

Can nine consecutive numbers make a $3 \times 3$ magic square whose magic sum is 40?

Solution.

Step 1 magic sum $= 3 \times$ centre general square
Step 2 $3 \times 13 = 39$ and $3 \times 14 = 42$ 40 lies between them
Answer No, such a magic square cannot be made.

Example 13

The 24th and 25th Virahanka numbers are 75,025 and 1,21,393. Find the next two.

Solution.

Step 1 26th $= 75{,}025 + 1{,}21{,}393 = 1{,}96{,}418$ sum of two before
Step 2 27th $= 1{,}21{,}393 + 1{,}96{,}418 = 3{,}17{,}811$
Answer 1,96,418 and 3,17,811
Example 14

Meena makes a border 6 cm long by laying red strips 1 cm long and blue strips 2 cm long end to end. How many different borders can she make?

Solution.

Step 1 first strip red: 5 cm left; first strip blue: 4 cm left as for rhythms
Step 2 borders of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 cm: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13 add the two before
Answer 13 borders
Example 15

How many rhythms of 7 beats can be made from short syllables (1 beat) and long syllables (2 beats)?

  1. 21
  2. 13
  3. 14
  4. 34

Solution.

Step 1 begins short: 13 rhythms; begins long: 8 rhythms 6 or 5 beats left
Step 2 $13 + 8 = 21$ every rhythm begins one way
Answer (a)
