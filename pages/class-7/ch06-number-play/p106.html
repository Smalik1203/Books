Example 12 · Numerical answer

A 3 × 3 magic square uses the nine consecutive numbers 21 to 29. What is its magic sum?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The centre of such a square is the middle number, 25.
Step 2The magic sum is 3 times the centre: 3 × 25 = 75.
Answer75

List IList II
(P) The 8th Virahanka number(1) 15
(Q) The magic sum of a magic square using 1 to 9(2) 45
(R) The number of rhythms of 5 beats(3) 34
(S) The sum of the numbers 1 to 9(4) 8

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–1, Q–3, R–4, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The Virahanka numbers run 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, and the 5th of them counts the rhythms of 5 beats: 8.
