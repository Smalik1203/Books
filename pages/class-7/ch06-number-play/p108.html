Example 19

Show that the cryptarithm AB $+$ B $=$ BCC has no solution.

Solution.

Step 1 AB $+$ B $\le 99 + 9 = 108$
Step 2 BCC has three digits and is at most 108, so B $= 1$
Step 3 A1 ${} + 1 = {}$ A2 only two digits
Answer A two-digit sum cannot be BCC, so there is no solution.
