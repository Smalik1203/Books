Assertion and reason

In Questions 13 to 17, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 + 11$ is even.

    Reason (R): A sum is even when an even number of the numbers in it are odd.

  1. Assertion (A): 21 is a Virahanka number.

    Reason (R): 21 is an odd number.

  1. Assertion (A): The product of two consecutive numbers is always even.

    Reason (R): The sum of two consecutive numbers is always even.

  1. Assertion (A): Nine consecutive numbers can make a $3 \times 3$ magic square whose magic sum is 50.

    Reason (R): The magic sum of a $3 \times 3$ magic square of consecutive numbers is 3 times its centre number.
