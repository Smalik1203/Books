C6 The switch is pressed $1 + 2 + \cdots + 10 = 55$ times, an odd number, so the bulb ends the opposite way from how it began: on. The answer is (a).
C7 The three row sums add up to 45, which is odd. Three even numbers always add up to an even number. So the row sums cannot all be even. The answer is (b).

Three things from this section are worth keeping. The parity of a sum depends only on how many odd numbers are in it. A total that is shared equally among rows tells you each row’s share. And a count that grows by adding the two counts before it can be carried as far as you like without listing anything.
