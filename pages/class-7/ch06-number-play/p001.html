Number Play

6.1Numbers That Tell Us Things

Seven children stand in a line, one behind another, all facing the front. Each child calls out a number, shown above their head in Fig. 6.1. What might the numbers mean? Could they be ages, or places in a race, or something else?

The children now change places, and each one calls out a new number (Fig. 6.2). The children are the same; only their order has changed. Look at both lines. Can you find the rule behind the numbers? One clue: the tallest child calls 0 in both lines.

Here is the rule. Each child calls out how many of the children in front of them are taller than they are. The child at the front has nobody in front, so that child always calls 0. Check the rule for every child in both lines. Notice too that no child can call a number larger than the number of children in front of them.
