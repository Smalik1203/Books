Case-based questions

  1. The school canteen sells a plate of idli for ₹7, a dosa for ₹12 and a cup of tea for ₹5. The cashier checks each bill without a calculator.

    1. Is the cost of 3 plates of idli odd or even?
    2. A bill is for some dosas and 2 cups of tea. Is it odd or even? Why?
    3. A bill for 5 plates of idli, 2 cups of tea and some dosas comes to ₹90. Show that this bill is wrong.
    4. Arjun buys only idli and tea, at least one of each, and pays exactly ₹36. How many plates of idli and how many cups of tea does he buy?

  1. A garden path is 1 m wide. It is paved in a row with square tiles 1 m long and long tiles 2 m long. Two pavings are different if the tiles come in a different order.

    1. In how many ways can a path 4 m long be paved?
    2. In how many ways can a path 8 m long be paved?
    3. How many of the pavings of the 8 m path begin with a long tile?
    4. A paving of the 8 m path has no square tiles. How many long tiles does it use?
    5. Is the number of pavings of a 9 m path odd or even?
