Example 16

Show that two neighbouring Virahanka numbers are never both even.

Solution.

Step 1 the number just before two neighbours is their difference each is a sum
Step 2 if both are even, every number before them is even $-$ even $=$ even
Answer The first number, 1, is odd, so two neighbours are never both even.

Example 17

In the subtraction 7A $-$ A7 $=$ B5, find the digits A and B.

Solution.

Step 1 ones: A ${} - 7$ is at most 2, so 1 ten is borrowed to end in 5
Step 2 ones: $10 + \mathrm{A} - 7 = 5$, so A $= 2$
Step 3 tens: $7 - 1 - 2 = 4$, so B $= 4$ 1 ten borrowed
Answer A $= 2$ and B $= 4$. Check: $72 - 27 = 45$.
Example 18

In PQ $+$ QP $= 121$, P and Q are digits, and PQ and QP are two-digit numbers. What is ${\mathrm{P} + \mathrm{Q}}$?

  1. 12
  2. 10
  3. 1
  4. 11

Solution.

Step 1 PQ $= 10 \times \mathrm{P} + \mathrm{Q}$ and QP $= 10 \times \mathrm{Q} + \mathrm{P}$ place value
Step 2 PQ $+$ QP $= 11 \times \mathrm{P} + 11 \times \mathrm{Q} = 11 \times (\mathrm{P} + \mathrm{Q})$
Step 3 $11 \times (\mathrm{P} + \mathrm{Q}) = 121$, so ${\mathrm{P} + \mathrm{Q} = 11}$ for example, $29 + 92$
Answer (d)
