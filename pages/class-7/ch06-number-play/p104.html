Example 6

Maya thinks of two counting numbers. She says that their product is odd and that their sum is odd too. Can she be right?

Solution.

Step 1 product odd, so both numbers are odd an even one makes even
Step 2 odd $+$ odd $=$ even
Answer No. The sum of the two numbers must be even.
Example 7

For $n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$, the expression $5n + 3$ gives

  1. only even numbers
  2. only odd numbers
  3. even and odd numbers in turn
  4. only multiples of 5

Solution.

Step 1 $n = 1, 2, 3, 4$ give $8, 13, 18, 23$
Step 2 each value is 5 more than the one before adding odd changes parity
Step 3 even, odd, even, odd, …
Answer (c)

Example 8

The numbers 1 to 9 are placed in a $3 \times 3$ grid, each once. Two of the row sums are 12 and 14. What is the third row sum? Can the three column sums be 9, 18 and 20?

Solution.

Step 1 Row sums together $= 1 + 2 + \cdots + 9 = 45$ each number once
Step 2 Third row sum $= 45 - (12 + 14) = 45 - 26 = 19$
Step 3 $9 + 18 + 20 = 47$ the columns must make 45 too
Answer The third row sum is 19. No: $47 \ne 45$.
