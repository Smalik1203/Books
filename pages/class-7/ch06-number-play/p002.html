6.2Odd and Even

Kishor has nine number cards: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15 and 17. He wants to choose five of them that add up to 30. Can he do it?

There are many ways to choose five cards, and checking every one would take a long time. Let us look for a reason instead.

An even number of objects can be put in pairs with nothing left over, as in Fig. 6.4. If we add even numbers together, every group is still in pairs, so the total can be put in pairs too. A sum of even numbers is always even. Join the pictures of 4 and 6, for example, and you get 5 pairs, which is 10.
