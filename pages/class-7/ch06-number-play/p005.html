Some expressions are even for every value of the letter-number: $100p$ and $48w - 2$ are two of them. Find some more. Can you find expressions that are always odd? And some that, like $3n + 4$, can be either?

The expression $6k + 2$ gives 8, 14, 20, and so on, for $k = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$ All of these are even, but many even numbers are missing. Is there an expression that gives every even number? Every even number has 2 as a factor, so the even numbers are $2 \times 1$, $2 \times 2$, $2 \times 3$, and so on. The $n$th even number is $2n$.

What is the 100th odd number? Write the odd numbers under the even numbers, position by position.

Even numbers: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, …

Odd numbers: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, …

At every position the odd number is 1 less than the even number. The 100th even number is $2 \times 100 = 200$, so the 100th odd number is $200 - 1 = 199$.

Even and odd numbers in order

The $n$th even number is $2n$, and the $n$th odd number is $2n - 1$.
