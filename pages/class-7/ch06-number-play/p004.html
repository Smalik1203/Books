Small squares in a grid

A $3 \times 3$ grid has 9 small squares, an odd number, and a $3 \times 4$ grid has 12, an even number (Fig. 6.7). Can you tell the parity of the number of squares from the two sides alone, without multiplying?

A grid of 3 rows and 4 columns holds 3 lots of 4. Each lot is even, so the total is even. A grid of 3 rows and 3 columns holds 3 lots of 3, which is an odd number of odd numbers, so the total is odd. A product of two numbers is odd only when both numbers are odd.
