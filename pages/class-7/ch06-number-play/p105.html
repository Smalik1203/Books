Example 9

A $3 \times 3$ magic square uses each of the numbers 11 to 19 once. Its magic sum is

  1. 15
  2. 30
  3. 45
  4. 135

Solution.

Step 1 $11 + 12 + \cdots + 19 = 135$ all nine numbers
Step 2 magic sum $= 135 \div 3 = 45$ three equal rows
Answer (c)
Example 10

A magic square uses the nine consecutive numbers 6 to 14. Its top row begins 13, 6. Find its centre, its magic sum and the last number in its top row.

Solution.

Step 1 centre $= 10$ middle of 6 to 14
Step 2 magic sum $= 3 \times 10 = 30$
Step 3 last number $= 30 - 13 - 6 = 11$ the row makes 30
Answer centre 10; magic sum 30; top row 13, 6, 11
Example 11

Can nine consecutive numbers make a $3 \times 3$ magic square whose magic sum is 40?

Solution.

Step 1 magic sum $= 3 \times$ centre general square
Step 2 $3 \times 13 = 39$ and $3 \times 14 = 42$ 40 lies between them
Answer No, such a magic square cannot be made.

The 24th and 25th Virahanka numbers are 75,025 and 1,21,393. Find the next two.

Solution.

Step 1 26th $= 75{,}025 + 1{,}21{,}393 = 1{,}96{,}418$ sum of two before

Answer 1,96,418 and 3,17,811
