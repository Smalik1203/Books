Problem 2

A $3 \times 3$ magic square uses each of the numbers 11 to 19 once. Its magic sum is

  1. 15
  2. 30
  3. 45
  4. 135
Solution

The nine numbers add up to $11 + 12 + \cdots + 19 = 135$. The three rows share that total equally. For any nine consecutive numbers, the magic sum is 3 times the middle number, here $3 \times 15$. The square of 11 to 19 is the square of 1 to 9 with 10 added to every number.

magic sum $= 135 \div 3 = 45$

The answer is (c). Option (a) is the magic sum for 1 to 9. Option (b) adds 15 to it once, as if only one number had grown. Option (d) is the total of all nine numbers, not of one row.

Problem 3

How many rhythms of 7 beats can be made from short syllables (1 beat) and long syllables (2 beats)?

  1. 21
  2. 13
  3. 14
  4. 34
Solution

A 7-beat rhythm begins with a short syllable, leaving 6 beats, or a long one, leaving 5 beats. So the count is the number of 6-beat rhythms plus the number of 5-beat rhythms.

$13 + 8 = 21$

The answer is (a). Option (b) is the count for 6 beats. Option (c) doubles 7, as if each beat had two choices of its own. Option (d) is the count for 8 beats, one place too far along the sequence.
