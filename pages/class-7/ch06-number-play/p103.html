Example 3 · Single correct

A 3 × 3 magic square uses each of the numbers 11 to 19 once. What is its magic sum?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 15
  2. 45
  3. 30
  4. 135

Solution.

Step 111 + 12 + … + 19 = 135, which is the total of the three rows.
Step 2The three rows have equal sums, so each is 135 ÷ 3 = 45.
Answer(b) 45
Example 4 · Single correct

How many rhythms of 7 beats can be made from short syllables of 1 beat and long syllables of 2 beats?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 21
  2. 13
  3. 14
  4. 34

Solution.

Step 1A rhythm begins short (6 beats left) or long (5 beats left), so each count is the sum of the two before it.
Step 2The counts for 1 to 7 beats are 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21.
Answer(a) 21
Example 5 · Single correct

In PQ + QP = 121, P and Q are digits, and PQ and QP are two-digit numbers. What is P + Q?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 12
  2. 10
  3. 1
  4. 11

Solution.

Step 1PQ = 10 × P + Q and QP = 10 × Q + P, so PQ + QP = 11 × P + 11 × Q = 11 × (P + Q).
Step 211 × (P + Q) = 121, so P + Q = 11.
Answer(d) 11
