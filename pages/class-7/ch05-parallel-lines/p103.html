Example 3 · Single correct

In the four-sided figure ABCD, AB is parallel to DC and AD is parallel to BC. If ∠DAB = 110°, what is ∠BCD?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 70°
  2. 20°
  3. 250°
  4. 110°

Solution.

Step 1AD is a transversal of the parallel lines AB and DC, so the interior angles ∠DAB and ∠ADC add to 180°: ∠ADC = 70°.
Step 2DC is a transversal of the parallel lines AD and BC, so ∠BCD = 180° − 70° = 110°.
Answer(d) 110°
Example 4 · Single correct

A transversal crosses lines l and m. The two interior angles on one side of it are 105° at l and 75° at m. Which is true?

Choose one correct option.

  1. l and m are parallel.
  2. l and m meet on that side of the transversal.
  3. l and m meet on the other side of the transversal.
  4. l and m are perpendicular.

Solution.

Step 1105° + 75° = 180°.
Step 2When the interior angles on the same side add to 180°, the lines are parallel.
Answer(a) l and m are parallel.
Example 5 · Single correct

A transversal crosses two parallel lines, and one of the eight angles formed is 58°. How many of the eight angles measure 122°?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 6

Solution.

Step 1At each crossing the four angles are 58°, 122°, 58° and 122°: two of each.
Step 2The corresponding angles at the second line are the same, so 2 + 2 = 4 angles measure 122°.
Answer(c) 4
