Example 4

Two lines cross, and one of the four angles is $47^\circ$. Going round the point from that angle, the other three angles are

  1. $133^\circ$, $47^\circ$, $133^\circ$
  2. $43^\circ$, $47^\circ$, $43^\circ$
  3. $47^\circ$, $47^\circ$, $47^\circ$
  4. $133^\circ$, $133^\circ$, $133^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1 next angle $= 180^\circ - 47^\circ = 133^\circ$ linear pair
Step 2 the angle after that $= 47^\circ$; the last angle $= 133^\circ$ vertically opposite angles
Answer (a)

Example 5

The capital letters E, H, L, N, T and Z are written with straight strokes. Which of them have a pair of parallel strokes? Which have a pair of perpendicular strokes?

Solution.

Step 1 E: the three level strokes are parallel, and each meets the upright stroke at $90^\circ$
Step 2 H: the two upright strokes are parallel, and the crossbar meets each at $90^\circ$
Step 3 L and T: the two strokes meet at $90^\circ$ perpendicular
Step 4 N and Z: one pair of strokes is parallel; the slanting stroke is neither
Answer parallel strokes: E, H, N, Z; perpendicular strokes: E, H, L, T
Example 6

ABCD is a rectangle. Name its pairs of parallel sides and its pairs of perpendicular sides. How many pairs of each are there?

Solution.

Step 1 AB is parallel to DC, and AD is parallel to BC opposite sides
Step 2 AB and BC, BC and CD, CD and DA, DA and AB are perpendicular a right angle at each corner
Answer 2 pairs of parallel sides; 4 pairs of perpendicular sides
