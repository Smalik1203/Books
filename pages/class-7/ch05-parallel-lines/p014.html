Example 4

In Fig. 5.25, AB is parallel to DC and AD is parallel to BC. $\angle DAC = 65^\circ$ and $\angle ADC = 60^\circ$. Find $\angle CAB$, $\angle ABC$ and $\angle BCD$.

First look at the parallel lines AB and DC, with AD as a transversal. $\angle ADC$ and $\angle DAB$ are interior angles on the same side, so they add up to $180^\circ$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle DAB = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ$ interior angles
Step 2 $\angle DAB = \angle DAC + \angle CAB$, so $\angle CAB = 120^\circ - 65^\circ = 55^\circ$

Now look at the parallel lines AD and BC, with DC as a transversal.

Step 3 $\angle ADC + \angle BCD = 180^\circ$, so $\angle BCD = 120^\circ$ interior angles
Step 4 $\angle ABC + \angle BCD = 180^\circ$, so $\angle ABC = 60^\circ$ BC as a transversal
Answer $\angle CAB = 55^\circ$, $\angle ABC = 60^\circ$, $\angle BCD = 120^\circ$
