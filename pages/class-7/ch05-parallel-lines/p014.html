Example 2

In Fig. 5.23, lines $l$ and $m$ are crossed by the transversal $t$. $\angle a = 120^\circ$ and $\angle f = 70^\circ$. Are $l$ and $m$ parallel?

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle b = 60^\circ$ linear pair with $\angle a$
Step 2 If $l$ and $m$ were parallel, $\angle b = \angle f$ corresponding angles
Step 3 But $60^\circ$ is not $70^\circ$
Answer $l$ and $m$ are not parallel.
Example 3

In Fig. 5.24, the parallel lines $l$ and $m$ are crossed by the transversal $t$, and $\angle 3 = 50^\circ$. Find $\angle 6$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle 2 = 180^\circ - 50^\circ = 130^\circ$ linear pair
Step 2 $\angle 6 = \angle 2 = 130^\circ$ corresponding angles
Answer $\angle 6 = 130^\circ$

We could also have used alternate angles: $\angle 3$ and $\angle 5$ are equal, and $\angle 5$ and $\angle 6$ form a linear pair.
