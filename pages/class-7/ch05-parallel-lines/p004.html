Think of the two rails of a straight railway track, the lines on a ruled notebook page, or the edges of a door. These lines never seem to meet, however far they go.

Parallel lines

Two lines on the same plane that do not meet, however far they are extended at both ends, are parallel lines.

The lines must lie on the same plane. A line drawn on your desk and a line drawn on the blackboard may never meet, but they are not parallel. Name some parallel lines you can see in your classroom.
