Assertion and reason

In Questions 15 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): A transversal crosses parallel lines $l$ and $m$, making $80^\circ$ with $l$. The corresponding angle at $m$ is $80^\circ$.

    Reason (R): When a transversal crosses two parallel lines, corresponding angles are equal.

  1. Assertion (A): When two lines intersect and one of the angles is $90^\circ$, all four angles are $90^\circ$.

    Reason (R): Vertically opposite angles add up to $180^\circ$.

  1. Assertion (A): When a transversal crosses two parallel lines, the interior angles on the same side are equal.

    Reason (R): When a transversal crosses two parallel lines, alternate angles are equal.

  1. Assertion (A): Two lines on a plane that are both perpendicular to a third line are parallel to each other.

    Reason (R): If a transversal makes a pair of equal corresponding angles with two lines, the lines are parallel.

  1. Assertion (A): If the two interior angles on one side of a transversal of lines $l$ and $m$ are $110^\circ$ and $70^\circ$, then $l$ and $m$ are parallel.

    Reason (R): Vertically opposite angles are equal.
