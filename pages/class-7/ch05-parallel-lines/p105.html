Problem 5

In the four-sided figure ABCD, AB is parallel to DC and AD is parallel to BC. $\angle DAB = 110^\circ$. What is $\angle BCD$?

  1. $70^\circ$
  2. $20^\circ$
  3. $250^\circ$
  4. $110^\circ$
Solution

Take one pair of parallel lines at a time, as in Example 4. With AD as a transversal of AB and DC, $\angle DAB$ and $\angle ADC$ are interior angles on the same side. With DC as a transversal of AD and BC, $\angle ADC$ and $\angle BCD$ are.

$\angle ADC = 180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ$, so $\angle BCD = 180^\circ - 70^\circ = 110^\circ$.

The answer is (d). Option (a) is $\angle ADC$, one step short. Option (b) takes $110^\circ$ from $90^\circ$ the wrong way round. Option (c) is $360^\circ - 110^\circ$, as if the two angles made a full turn.

In every one of these problems the answer came in two or three steps, and each step used one fact: a linear pair, vertically opposite angles, or one of the facts about parallel lines. The hard part was choosing which pair of angles to look at.
