Example 12 · Numerical answer

A transversal crosses two parallel lines. The interior angles on one side of it are in the ratio 4 : 5. Find the larger of them, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Interior angles on the same side of parallel lines add to 180°: 4 parts + 5 parts = 9 parts.
Step 2One part = 20°, so the larger angle is 5 × 20° = 100°.
Answer100
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A transversal makes an angle of 48° with one of two parallel lines. At the other line, find the sum, in degrees, of the angles that are greater than 90°.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The angles at the second line are again 48°, 132°, 48° and 132°: 132° + 132° = 264°.
Answer264
Example 14 · Matching

Two lines cross. Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) Vertically opposite to 65°(1) 115°
(Q) Linear pair with 65°(2) 360°
(R) Smaller angle, if one is 3 times the next(3) 65°
(S) The four angles at a crossing, added(4) 45°

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  2. P–1, Q–3, R–4, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Opposite: 65°. Linear pair: 180° − 65° = 115°. 4 parts of 180°: 45° each. Full turn: 360°.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
