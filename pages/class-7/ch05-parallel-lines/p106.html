Type 4 · Interior angles on the same side

Example 12

A transversal crosses lines $l$ and $m$. The two interior angles on one side of it are $105^\circ$ at $l$ and $75^\circ$ at $m$. Which is true?

  1. $l$ and $m$ are parallel.
  2. $l$ and $m$ meet on that side of the transversal.
  3. $l$ and $m$ meet on the other side of the transversal.
  4. We cannot tell without measuring more angles.

Solution.

Step 1 angle just above $l$, on the same side $= 180^\circ - 105^\circ = 75^\circ$ linear pair
Step 2 it corresponds to the $75^\circ$ angle at $m$, and $75^\circ = 75^\circ$ equal corresponding angles
Answer (a)
Example 13

In the four-sided figure ABCD, AB is parallel to DC and AD is parallel to BC. $\angle DAB = 110^\circ$. What is $\angle BCD$?

  1. $70^\circ$
  2. $20^\circ$
  3. $250^\circ$
  4. $110^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle ADC = 180^\circ - 110^\circ = 70^\circ$ AB parallel to DC
Step 2 $\angle BCD = 180^\circ - 70^\circ = 110^\circ$ AD parallel to BC
Answer (d)
