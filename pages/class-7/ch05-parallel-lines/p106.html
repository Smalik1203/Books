Type 5 · A path that bends between parallel lines

Example 13

In Fig. 5.35, lines $l$ and $m$ are parallel. Find the angle at the bend.

  1. $115^\circ$
  2. $15^\circ$
  3. $65^\circ$
  4. $90^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1 Draw a line through the bend, parallel to $l$ and $m$.
Step 2 upper part of the bend $= 40^\circ$; lower part $= 25^\circ$ alternate angles
Step 3 angle at the bend $= 40^\circ + 25^\circ = 65^\circ$
Answer (c)
