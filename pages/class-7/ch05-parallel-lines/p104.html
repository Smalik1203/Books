Type 3 · Corresponding angles and the test for parallel lines

Example 7

A transversal crosses parallel lines $l$ and $m$, with the angles numbered as in Fig. 5.14, and $\angle 1 = 70^\circ$. Find $\angle 5$, $\angle 6$ and $\angle 7$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle 5 = \angle 1 = 70^\circ$ corresponding angles
Step 2 $\angle 6 = 180^\circ - 70^\circ = 110^\circ$ linear pair
Step 3 $\angle 7 = \angle 5 = 70^\circ$ vertically opposite angles
Answer $\angle 5 = 70^\circ$, $\angle 6 = 110^\circ$, $\angle 7 = 70^\circ$
Example 8

In Fig. 5.34, lines $l$ and $m$ are parallel. Find the angle marked with a question mark.

  1. $115^\circ$
  2. $65^\circ$
  3. $25^\circ$
  4. $130^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1 top right angle at $m$ $= 65^\circ$ corresponding angles
Step 2 marked angle $= 65^\circ$ vertically opposite angles
Answer (b)
Example 9

Each rung of a ladder is fixed to a side rail at $90^\circ$. Why are the rungs parallel?

Solution.

Step 1 the side rail is a transversal of any two rungs
Step 2 the rungs make $90^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ with the rail, and $90^\circ = 90^\circ$ equal corresponding angles
Answer The rungs are parallel.
