Example 6 · Single correct

The blades of a pair of scissors are opened to 35°. What is the angle between one blade and its own handle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 35°
  2. 145°
  3. 55°
  4. 325°

Solution.

Step 1A blade and its own handle lie along one straight line.
Step 2The blade, the other blade and that straight line make a linear pair: 180° − 35° = 145°.
Answer(b) 145°
Example 7 · Multiple correct

A transversal crosses two parallel lines. Which statements are always correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Corresponding angles are equal.
  2. Alternate angles are equal.
  3. Interior angles on the same side are equal.
  4. Vertically opposite angles are equal.

Solution.

Step 1For parallel lines, corresponding angles are equal, and so are alternate angles. Vertically opposite angles are equal wherever two lines cross.
Step 2Interior angles on the same side add to 180°. They are equal only when each is 90°.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Capital letters are written with straight strokes. Which of these letters have a pair of parallel strokes?

Select all correct options.

  1. H
  2. T
  3. Z
  4. L

Solution.

Step 1H has two upright strokes, and Z has a top and a bottom stroke that are parallel.
Step 2T and L each have two strokes that meet at right angles, so they are perpendicular, not parallel.
Answer(a), (c)
