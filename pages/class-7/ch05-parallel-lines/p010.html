Parallel lines and corresponding angles

When a transversal crosses two parallel lines, each pair of corresponding angles are equal.

Copy Fig. 5.17, where $l$ and $m$ are not parallel. Try to draw a transversal that makes a pair of equal corresponding angles. Can you? When two lines are not parallel, the corresponding angles made by a transversal are never equal.

So we now have a test for parallel lines. Draw any transversal and compare one pair of corresponding angles. If they are equal, the lines are parallel; if they are not, the lines are not parallel. The rest of this chapter uses that test again and again.
