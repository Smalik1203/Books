Alternate angles

When a transversal crosses two parallel lines, each pair of alternate angles are equal.

Example 1

In Fig. 5.22, the parallel lines $l$ and $m$ are crossed by the transversal $t$, and $\angle 6 = 135^\circ$. Find the other seven angles.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle 2 = \angle 6 = 135^\circ$ corresponding angles
Step 2 $\angle 8 = \angle 6 = 135^\circ$ vertically opposite
Step 3 $\angle 4 = \angle 8 = 135^\circ$ corresponding angles
Step 4 $\angle 5 = 180^\circ - 135^\circ = 45^\circ$ linear pair
Step 5 $\angle 1 = \angle 3 = \angle 7 = 45^\circ$ in the same way
Answer $\angle 2 = \angle 4 = \angle 8 = 135^\circ$ and $\angle 1 = \angle 3 = \angle 5 = \angle 7 = 45^\circ$
Example 2

In Fig. 5.23, lines $l$ and $m$ are crossed by the transversal $t$. $\angle a = 120^\circ$ and $\angle f = 70^\circ$. Are $l$ and $m$ parallel?

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle b = 60^\circ$ linear pair with $\angle a$
Step 2 If $l$ and $m$ were parallel, $\angle b = \angle f$ corresponding angles
Step 3 But $60^\circ$ is not $70^\circ$
Answer $l$ and $m$ are not parallel.
