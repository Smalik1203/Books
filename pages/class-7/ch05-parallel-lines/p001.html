Parallel and Crossing Lines

5.1Lines That Cross

Take a sheet of square paper and fold it in different ways. Draw a pencil line along each crease, using a ruler. Now look at any two of the lines. Do they meet? If two lines do not meet on the paper, would they meet if we made the paper bigger and carried the lines on?

In this chapter we look at how lines lie on a flat surface. A table top, a sheet of paper and a blackboard are all flat surfaces. In mathematics a flat surface is called a plane.

When two lines on a plane meet, they meet at a point. We say that the lines intersect. How many angles do two intersecting lines make? In Fig. 5.2, line $l$ intersects line $m$, and four angles are formed: $\angle a$, $\angle b$, $\angle c$ and $\angle d$.
