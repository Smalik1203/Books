Example 15 · Matching

A transversal crosses two parallel lines. Match each angle in List I with its measure in List II.

List IList II
(P) The angle corresponding to 50°(1) 100°
(Q) The interior angle on the same side as 50°(2) 50°
(R) The angle alternate to 80°(3) 130°
(S) The interior angle on the same side as 80°(4) 80°

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–3, R–4, S–2
  2. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
  4. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1

Solution.

Step 1For parallel lines, corresponding and alternate angles are equal: 50° and 80°.
Step 2Interior angles on the same side add to 180°: 180° − 50° = 130° and 180° − 80° = 100°.
Answer(d) P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
