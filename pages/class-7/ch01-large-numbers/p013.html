One gram of healthy soil can hold between 100 million and 1 billion bacteria. It can also hold between 1 lakh and 1 million tiny fungi. They help plants to grow and stay healthy. Look for other large-number facts, and share them with your class.

1.6Did You Ever Wonder…?

These facts set Estu thinking: “Could everyone in Mumbai fit into 1 lakh buses?” To find out, we make a sensible guess, called an assumption: a bus holds 50 people.

People in 1 lakh buses $= 1{,}00{,}000 \times 50 = 50{,}00{,}000$

That is 50 lakh people. Table 1.5 shows that Mumbai had more than 1 crore 24 lakh people in 2011. So the people of Mumbai would not fit into 1 lakh buses.

The ship Titanic carried about 2500 passengers. Could the people of Mumbai fit into 5000 ships like it?

Roxie has a question too. “If I travelled 100 km every day, could I reach the Moon in 10 years?” The Moon is about 3,84,400 km from the Earth. How far would Roxie travel in 1 year? How far in 10 years?

Working in steps like this, first one year and then ten, keeps a large calculation easy to check. You can use steps for any large calculation.
