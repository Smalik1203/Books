Example 9

Meena says that the nearest lakh of 7,49,999 is 8,00,000, because 49,999 is almost 50,000. Is she right?

The lakhs digit is 7, and the next digit is 4, which is less than 5. So the number rounds down to 7,00,000. The two distances show why.

$7{,}49{,}999 - 7{,}00{,}000 = 49{,}999$

$8{,}00{,}000 - 7{,}49{,}999 = 50{,}001$

7,49,999 is nearer to 7,00,000, so Meena is not right. Almost halfway is still not halfway.

Estimating sums and differences

Roxie and Estu estimate $4{,}63{,}128 + 4{,}19{,}682$ without adding the numbers.

Roxie: “The sum is close to 8,00,000, and it is more than 8,00,000.”

Estu: “The sum is close to 9,00,000, and it is less than 9,00,000.”

Then they estimate $14{,}63{,}128 - 4{,}90{,}020$.

Roxie: “The difference is close to 10,00,000, and it is less than 10,00,000.”

Estu: “The difference is close to 9,00,000, and it is more than 9,00,000.”

Rounding the numbers first gives an estimate quickly. The estimate also tells you roughly what the exact answer should be, so it is a check on the exact working.
