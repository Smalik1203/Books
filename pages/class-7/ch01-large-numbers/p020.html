Example 13

About how many times does your heart beat in one day? Assume that it beats 80 times a minute.

In one hour: $80 \times 60 = 4{,}800$ beats

In one day: $4{,}800 \times 24 = 1{,}15{,}200$ beats

Your heart beats about 1,15,200 times a day, which is more than a lakh.

An assumption can also settle a question with a yes or no answer.

Example 14

Could one lakh people stand on a ground 100 m long and 70 m wide? Assume that 4 people can stand in one square metre.

Area of the ground $= 100 \times 70 = 7{,}000$ square metres

People who can stand there $= 7{,}000 \times 4 = 28{,}000$

28,000 is far less than 1,00,000, so one lakh people could not stand there.
