Is a lakh a large number?

Eshwarappa asks the children, “Is a lakh big or small?” They do not agree.

Roxie thinks a lakh is big. A lakh kinds of rice is a great many kinds. A lakh days is about 274 years, which is far longer than anyone lives. And if a lakh people stood in a line, shoulder to shoulder, the line would be about 38 km long.

Estu thinks a lakh is not so big. The cricket stadium in Ahmedabad has seats for more than a lakh people, and it fits in a small part of one city. Most people have between 80,000 and 1,20,000 hairs on their heads. Some fish lay about a lakh eggs at one time, and some lay many lakhs.

Reading and writing large numbers

In the Indian system, commas split a long number into groups. The first comma comes three digits from the right, and then one comes after every two digits. So one lakh is written 1,00,000.

12,78,830 is twelve lakh seventy-eight thousand eight hundred thirty.

15,75,000 is fifteen lakh seventy-five thousand.
