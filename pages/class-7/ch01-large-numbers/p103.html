Example 3 · Single correct

A 6-digit number is multiplied by a 4-digit number. How many digits does the product have?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 9 or 10
  2. exactly 10
  3. 10 or 11
  4. 24

Solution.

Step 1The smallest product is 1,00,000 × 1000 = 10,00,00,000, which has 9 digits.
Step 2Every product is less than 10,00,000 × 10,000 = 1,00,00,00,000, the smallest 11-digit number. The largest, 9,99,999 × 9999, has 10 digits.
Answer(a) 9 or 10
Example 4 · Single correct

You start at 1 and say one number every second, day and night. About how long will it take you to reach one crore?

Choose one correct option.

  1. about 12 days
  2. about 4 months
  3. about 1 year
  4. about 3 years

Solution.

Step 1Seconds in a day = 60 × 60 × 24 = 86,400, which is a little less than 1 lakh.
Step 21 crore ÷ 1 lakh = 100, so a little more than 100 days: 1,00,00,000 ÷ 86,400 is about 116 days, nearly 4 months.
Answer(b) about 4 months
