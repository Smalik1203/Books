1.5Patterns in Products

Multiplying by 10, 100 or 1000 is easy: you write one, two or three zeros at the end. Roxie and Estu use this to make other products easy too.

Roxie: $116 \times 5 = (116 \div 2) \times 10 = 58 \times 10 = 580$

Estu: $824 \times 25 = (824 \div 4) \times 100 = 206 \times 100 = 20{,}600$

Quick products

To multiply by 5, halve the number and multiply by 10. To multiply by 25, divide by 4 and multiply by 100. To multiply by 125, divide by 8 and multiply by 1000. Each works in one step when the division comes out exactly.

Example 11

Work out $348 \times 25$ quickly.

$348 \div 4 = 87$

$87 \times 100 = 8{,}700$

So $348 \times 25 = 8{,}700$.

The same idea works when neither number is 5, 25 or 125. Look for a part of one number that pairs with the other to make 10, 100, 1000 or 10,000.
