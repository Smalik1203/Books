Toothpick digits

Fig. 1.9 shows the ten digits made from sticks. Use toothpicks or matchsticks, or draw each stick as a line. The digit 7 needs three sticks.

  1. How many sticks do you need to write 5108? Check that 42,019 needs exactly 23 sticks.
  1. Add two sticks to 42,019 to make a bigger number, such as 42,078. Find more.
