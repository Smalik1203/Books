What this chapter showed
  1. A lakh has 5 zeros, a crore has 7 and an arab has 9. A million is ten lakh, and a billion is a hundred crore.
  2. The Indian system groups digits from the right as 3, 2, 2, …; the American system groups them in threes.
  3. Often a round number is enough. We round up or down, depending on what the number is for.
  4. A large number is easier to picture next to something familiar.
  5. Regrouping factors makes many products quick: $824 \times 25 = 206 \times 100$.
  6. A sensible assumption and a calculation in steps can answer questions such as whether a city fits into a lakh buses.
