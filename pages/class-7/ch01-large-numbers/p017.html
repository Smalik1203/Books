Example 12

A school prints 1250 copies of a magazine with 48 pages. How many pages does it print?

$1250 \times 48 = 1250 \times 8 \times 6$

$= 10{,}000 \times 6 = 60{,}000$

It prints 60,000 pages. Splitting 48 into 8 and 6 gave 1250 a partner, 8, that makes 10,000.

How long is a product?

In Question 1 below, work out each group of products and look for a pattern.

Then count digits. How many digits do the two numbers have, and how many does their product have? Is there a connection?

Roxie says that two 2-digit numbers always multiply to a 3-digit or a 4-digit number. We cannot try every pair, so she gives a reason.

The smallest 2-digit number is 10, and $10 \times 10 = 100$. So every product is at least 100, which has 3 digits.

Every 2-digit number is less than 100, and $100 \times 100 = 10{,}000$. So every product is less than 10,000, which means it has at most 4 digits.
