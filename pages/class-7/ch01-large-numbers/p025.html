Case-based questions
  1. Case study

    A town is raising money for a new library, which will cost ₹48,75,000. The town collects ₹12,48,300 in the first month, ₹9,86,700 in the second and ₹15,05,000 in the third.

    1. Estimate the money collected in the three months by rounding each amount off to the nearest lakh.
    2. Find the exact amount collected.
    3. How much more money is needed?
    4. From now on the town can collect about ₹4,00,000 a month. About how many more months will it need?
    5. Write the cost of the library in words.
