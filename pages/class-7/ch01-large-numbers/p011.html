1.5Patterns in Products

Multiplying by 10, 100 or 1000 is easy: you write one, two or three zeros at the end. Roxie and Estu use this to make other products easy too.

Roxie: $116 \times 5 = (116 \div 2) \times 10 = 58 \times 10 = 580$

Estu: $824 \times 25 = (824 \div 4) \times 100 = 206 \times 100 = 20{,}600$
