Example 10

A railway station sold 2,48,915 tickets in one month and 3,62,480 in the next. Estimate the total by rounding each number off to the nearest ten thousand. Then find the exact total.

2,48,915 rounds up to 2,50,000, and 3,62,480 rounds down to 3,60,000.

Estimate: $2{,}50{,}000 + 3{,}60{,}000 = 6{,}10{,}000$

Exact: $2{,}48{,}915 + 3{,}62{,}480 = 6{,}11{,}395$

The exact total is close to the estimate, as it should be.

Populations of cities

A census counts every person in the country. In India it is usually held once every ten years. India held a census in 2001 and another in 2011. Tables 1.5 and 1.6 give the populations of twenty Indian cities in those two years, with the largest city in 2011 first. Read a few of the numbers aloud in lakhs and crores before you answer the questions. Mumbai, for example, had about 1 crore 24 lakh people in 2011, and New Delhi about 1 crore 10 lakh.
