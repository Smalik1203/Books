Comparing and ordering

To compare two large numbers, count their digits first. The number with more digits is greater. If both have the same number of digits, compare them digit by digit from the left. The first place where the digits differ decides.

Numbers written from the smallest to the largest are in ascending order. Written from the largest to the smallest, they are in descending order.

Comparing numbers

A number with more digits is greater. For two numbers with the same number of digits, the first place from the left where the digits differ decides which is greater. Ascending order goes from the smallest to the largest; descending order goes from the largest to the smallest.

Example 7

Arrange 9,87,650, 10,02,345, 9,78,560 and 98,765 in ascending order.

98,765 has 5 digits, 9,87,650 and 9,78,560 have 6, and 10,02,345 has 7.

9,87,650 and 9,78,560 both start with 9. In the next place, 7 is less than 8, so 9,78,560 is the smaller.

In ascending order: 98,765, 9,78,560, 9,87,650, 10,02,345.
