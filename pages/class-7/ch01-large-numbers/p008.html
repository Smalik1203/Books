Reading a long number

A long number is easy to read once the commas are in. Take the number 9876501234.

Indian system: 9,87,65,01,234 is 9 arab 87 crore 65 lakh 1 thousand 234. We can also say 987 crore 65 lakh 1 thousand 234.

American system: 9,876,501,234 is 9 billion 876 million 501 thousand 234.

1.4Exact and Approximate Values

A newspaper says, “1 lakh people visited the book fair.” Did exactly 1,00,000 people go? Almost certainly not. The newspaper gives a round number that is close to the real one.

Very often we do not need the exact number. The 2011 census counted 76,068 people in Chintamani, but “about 75,000” already tells us how big the town is.

Sometimes it makes sense to round up, to a round number larger than the real one. A school of 732 people may order 750 sweets rather than 700, so that nobody goes without.
