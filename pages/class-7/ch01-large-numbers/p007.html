1.3Crores, Millions and Billions

There are two common ways to name large numbers. In India we use the Indian system. Many other countries use the American system, which is also called the International system. The two systems give the same number different names, and they put the commas in different places.

Table 1.3 The same numbers in the two systems
IndianNameAmericanName
1,000one thousand1,000one thousand
10,000ten thousand10,000ten thousand
1,00,000one lakh100,000one hundred thousand
10,00,000ten lakh1,000,000one million
1,00,00,000one crore10,000,000ten million
10,00,00,000ten crore100,000,000one hundred million
1,00,00,00,000one arab1,000,000,000one billion

Look at where the commas go. In the Indian system, the digits are grouped from the right as 3, then 2, then 2, and so on: thousands, lakhs, crores, arabs. In the American system, the digits are grouped in threes all the way: thousands, millions, billions.

Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, the Maldives, Afghanistan and Myanmar also use the Indian system. The words lakh and crore come from the Sanskrit words lakṣa and koṭi.

Lakh, crore and arab

A lakh is a hundred thousand, which is 1 followed by 5 zeros. A crore is a hundred lakh, which is 1 followed by 7 zeros. An arab is a hundred crore, which is 1 followed by 9 zeros.
