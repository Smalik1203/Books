Example 5

Chitti presses +10000 three times, +100 twenty-five times and +1 eight times. What number does it show?

$(3 \times 10{,}000) + (25 \times 100) + (8 \times 1)$

$= 30{,}000 + 2{,}500 + 8 = 32{,}508$

Chitti shows 32,508. The 25 presses of +100 make 2,500, which is 2 thousand and 5 hundred, so the numbers of presses are not the digits of the number.
