Large Numbers Around Us

1.1How Big Is a Lakh?

Eshwarappa grows rice near Chintamani, a town in Karnataka. At the seed shop, Ramanna tells him that India once grew about a lakh kinds of rice. Farmers saved their own seed each year. Today only a few kinds are grown, and most seed is bought.

Lakshmamma says a seed bank near her home keeps about a hundred kinds.

At home, Eshwarappa tells his children, Roxie and Estu. Estu asks, “If I ate a new kind every day, could I taste a lakh kinds in 100 years?” Guess first.

A lakh days

A year has 365 days, if we leave out leap years. A leap year has one extra day, and leaving it out changes the answer only a little. Two years have $365 \times 2$ days, and a life of $y$ years has $365 \times y$ days. Ten years have $365 \times 10 = 3650$ days, which is still far short of a lakh. The letter $y$ stands for the number of years, which can be any number we choose. Try Estu’s question yourself before you read the example.
