Example 5 · Single correct

How many 7-digit numbers are there?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 89,99,999
  2. 99,99,999
  3. 9,00,000
  4. 90,00,000

Solution.

Step 1The smallest 7-digit number is 10,00,000 and the largest is 99,99,999.
Step 2The count is 99,99,999 − 10,00,000 + 1 = 90,00,000.
Answer(d) 90,00,000
Example 6 · Single correct

What is the difference between the place values of the two 7s in 7,04,17,256?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 6,99,93,000
  2. 6,99,99,300
  3. 7,00,07,000
  4. 0

Solution.

Step 1The first 7 is in the crores place: 7,00,00,000. The second 7 is in the thousands place: 7,000.
Step 27,00,00,000 − 7,000 = 6,99,93,000.
Answer(a) 6,99,93,000
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these are equal to 1 crore?

Select all correct options.

  1. 100 lakh
  2. 10 million
  3. 1000 thousand
  4. 10,000 thousand

Solution.

Step 11 crore = 1,00,00,000 = 100 × 1,00,000 = 100 lakh, and 10 × 10,00,000 = 10 million.
Step 210,000 × 1000 = 1,00,00,000, but 1000 × 1000 = 10,00,000, which is only 10 lakh.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
