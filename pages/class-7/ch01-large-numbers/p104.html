Problem 4

A 6-digit number is multiplied by a 4-digit number. The product has

  1. exactly 10 digits
  2. 9 or 10 digits
  3. 10 or 11 digits
  4. 24 digits
Solution

Use Roxie’s reasoning: find the smallest possible product, and a number that every product is less than.

Smallest: $1{,}00{,}000 \times 1000 = 10{,}00{,}00{,}000$, which has 9 digits.

Every product is less than $10{,}00{,}000 \times 10{,}000 = 10{,}00{,}00{,}00{,}000$, which has 11 digits.

So the product has at least 9 digits and fewer than 11. The answer is (b). Option (a) is right for most pairs but forgets the small ones. Option (c) adds one digit too many at both ends. Option (d) multiplies the numbers of digits, $6 \times 4$, when the numbers of digits roughly add.

Problem 5

You start at 1 and say one number every second, day and night. About how long will it take you to reach one crore?

  1. about 12 days
  2. about 4 months
  3. about 1 year
  4. about 3 years
Solution

Find the number of seconds in a day, then compare it with one crore.

Seconds in a day $= 60 \times 60 \times 24 = 86{,}400$

$1{,}00{,}00{,}000 \div 86{,}400$ is about $116$ days

116 days is a little under 4 months, so the answer is (b). A day has a little less than a lakh seconds, and a crore is a hundred lakh. That is why the answer is a little more than 100 days. Option (a) is the time to reach ten lakh, not one crore. A year has about 3 crore seconds, so option (c) is three times too long and option (d) is about nine times too long.
