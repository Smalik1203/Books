Example 6

Sippy has the buttons +1, +10, +100, +1000, +10000 and +100000. What are the fewest presses it needs to make 2,60,408? Write the expression.

Solution.

Step 1 +100000 twice makes 2,00,000; 60,408 is left biggest button first
Step 2 +10000 six times makes 60,000; 408 is left
Step 3 +100 four times and +1 eight times make 408 no +1000 needed
Step 4 Presses $= 2 + 6 + 4 + 8 = 20$
Answer 20 presses: $(2 \times 1{,}00{,}000) + (6 \times 10{,}000) + (4 \times 100) + (8 \times 1)$

Type 3 · Comparing and ordering

Example 7

Arrange 9,87,650, 10,02,345, 9,78,560 and 98,765 in ascending order.

Solution.

Step 1 98,765 has 5 digits; 9,87,650 and 9,78,560 have 6; 10,02,345 has 7 more digits, greater
Step 2 $9{,}78{,}560 \lt 9{,}87{,}650$ 7 is less than 8
Answer 98,765, 9,78,560, 9,87,650, 10,02,345

Type 4 · Rounding off and estimating

Example 8

Round off 5,48,672 to the nearest (a) thousand (b) ten thousand (c) lakh.

Solution.

Step 1 Between 5,48,000 and 5,49,000; nearer 5,49,000 328 away, not 672
Step 2 Between 5,40,000 and 5,50,000; nearer 5,50,000 1,328 away, not 8,672
Step 3 Between 5,00,000 and 6,00,000; nearer 5,00,000 48,672 away, not 51,328
Answer (a) 5,49,000 (b) 5,50,000 (c) 5,00,000
