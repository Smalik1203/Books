Example 5

Chitti presses +10000 three times, +100 twenty-five times and +1 eight times. What number does it show?

Solution.

Step 1 $(3 \times 10{,}000) + (25 \times 100) + (8 \times 1)$
Step 2 $= 30{,}000 + 2{,}500 + 8 = 32{,}508$
Answer 32,508

Type 3 · Comparing and ordering

Example 6

Arrange 9,87,650, 10,02,345, 9,78,560 and 98,765 in ascending order.

Solution.

Step 1 98,765 has 5 digits; 9,87,650 and 9,78,560 have 6; 10,02,345 has 7 more digits, greater
Step 2 $9{,}78{,}560 \lt 9{,}87{,}650$ 7 is less than 8
Answer 98,765, 9,78,560, 9,87,650, 10,02,345

Type 4 · Rounding off and estimating

Example 7

Round off 5,48,672 to the nearest (a) thousand (b) ten thousand (c) lakh.

Solution.

Step 1 Nearest thousand: next digit 6, round up: 5,49,000 6 is 5 or more
Step 2 Nearest ten thousand: next digit 8, round up: 5,50,000 8 is 5 or more
Step 3 Nearest lakh: next digit 4, round down: 5,00,000 4 is less than 5
Answer (a) 5,49,000 (b) 5,50,000 (c) 5,00,000
