Words to know
arab
1,00,00,00,000, a hundred crore
ascending order
from the smallest number to the largest
assumption
a sensible guess, said out, that lets a question be worked out
billion
1,000,000,000 in the American system, a hundred crore
crore
1,00,00,000, a hundred lakh
descending order
from the largest number to the smallest
estimate
a number close to the exact answer, found quickly
expanded form
a number written as the sum of the place values of its digits
face value
the digit itself
Indian and American systems
two ways to name numbers and place commas; the American system is also called the International system
lakh
1,00,000, a hundred thousand
million
1,000,000 in the American system, ten lakh
nearest neighbour
the nearest thousand, lakh, crore and so on to a number
place value
a digit times the value of its place
predecessor
the number 1 less than a given number
round off
to replace a number by a nearby round number, by rounding up or down
successor
the number 1 more than a given number
