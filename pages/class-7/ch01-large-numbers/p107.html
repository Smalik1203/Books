Example 15

Could one lakh people stand on a ground 100 m long and 70 m wide? Assume that 4 people can stand in one square metre.

Solution.

Step 1 Area $= 100 \times 70 = 7{,}000$ square metres
Step 2 People $= 7{,}000 \times 4 = 28{,}000$
Answer No. $28{,}000 \lt 1{,}00{,}000$
Example 16

You start at 1 and say one number every second, day and night. About how long will it take you to reach one crore?

  1. about 12 days
  2. about 4 months
  3. about 1 year
  4. about 3 years

Solution.

Step 1 Seconds in a day $= 60 \times 60 \times 24 = 86{,}400$
Step 2 $1{,}00{,}00{,}000 \div 86{,}400 \approx 116$ days
Answer (b)
