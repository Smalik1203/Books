Example 1

Estu lives for 100 years and eats one new kind of rice every day. How many kinds does he taste?

Days in 100 years $= 365 \times 100 = 36{,}500$

He tastes 36,500 kinds. One lakh is 1,00,000, so he does not reach even half a lakh.

Roxie wonders what would happen if they ate 2 kinds every day. Work it out. Then try 3 kinds a day. How many kinds a day are needed to taste a lakh kinds in 100 years?

Now choose your own number for $y$. How close is $365 \times y$ to one lakh?

Getting a feel for large numbers

The Statue of Unity in Gujarat shows Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. It is about 180 m tall, and it is the tallest statue in the world. The Kunchikal waterfall in Karnataka drops about 450 m.

Heights like these are hard to picture. They become easier to picture next to something we know. Somu is 1 m tall. He lives in a building with 10 floors, and each floor is about 4 times his height. Fig. 1.1 draws all four heights on one scale.
