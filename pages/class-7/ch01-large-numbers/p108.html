C5 The fewest presses is the digit sum, and other ways add 9 presses at a time. So 40 minus the digit sum must be a multiple of 9. For 2344, $40 - 13 = 27$ works. The digit sums of 1000, 9999 and 4321 are 1, 36 and 10, and none works. The answer is (b).
C6 In crore, the four are 400, 450, a little under 500, and 500. The answer is (d). Option (c) has the most digits written out, but it is 1 less than 500 crore.

Three things from this section are worth keeping. A large number is easier to compare once it is written in one unit, such as crore. The first digits of a product, or of a sum, often settle a question without the full calculation. And when a question asks whether something is possible, it helps to ask what can change and what cannot.
