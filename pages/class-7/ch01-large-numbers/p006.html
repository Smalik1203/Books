Example 4

How many times must Handy Hundreds press +100 to show 72,500?

Each press adds 100, so the number of presses is the number of hundreds in 72,500.

$72{,}500 \div 100 = 725$

Handy Hundreds must press its button 725 times.

Many ways to the same number

Creative Chitti is a calculator with seven buttons: +1, +10, +100, +1000, +10000, +100000 and +1000000. It likes to reach the same number in more than one way.

To show 321, Chitti can press +100 three times, +10 two times and +1 once. It can also press +10 thirty-two times and +1 once. Or it can press +100 two times, +10 twelve times and +1 once. Check that all three give 321.
