The fewest presses

Systematic Sippy is another calculator. Its buttons are +1, +10, +100, +1000, +10000 and +100000. Sippy wants to press as few buttons as possible.

Here is one way for Sippy to make 5072. It presses +1000 five times, +10 six times and +1 twelve times.

$(5 \times 1000) + (6 \times 10) + (12 \times 1) = 5072$

Presses $= 5 + 6 + 12 = 23$

Can Sippy make 5072 with fewer than 23 presses? Find a way, and write its expression.
