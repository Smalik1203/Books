Work in steps, and change the units at the end.

Length of the line $= 140$ crore $\times\ 40$ cm $= 5600$ crore cm

$1$ km $= 1{,}00{,}000$ cm, so $5600$ crore cm $= 5{,}60{,}000$ km

$5{,}60{,}000 \div 40{,}000 = 14$

The line would go around the equator about 14 times. A hundred and forty crore people make a line longer than the distance to the Moon.

Work out $25 \times 64 \times 125$ in your head. Is it more than 1,50,000?

Look for pairs that make 100 or 1000. We know $25 \times 4 = 100$ and $125 \times 8 = 1000$. Now 64 is $4 \times 8 \times 2$, so the product can be regrouped.

$25 \times 64 \times 125 = (25 \times 4) \times (125 \times 8) \times 2$

$= 100 \times 1000 \times 2 = 2{,}00{,}000$

It is 2,00,000, which is more than 1,50,000. Nothing here needed long multiplication. The work was in splitting 64 so that each part had a partner.

Example 1

Write 7,05,430 in words. Then write “forty lakh six thousand twenty” in figures.

Solution.

Step 1 7,05,430 = 7 lakh, 05 thousand, 430 read the commas
Step 2 seven lakh five thousand four hundred thirty
Step 3 $40{,}00{,}000 + 6{,}000 + 20 = 40{,}06{,}020$
Answer seven lakh five thousand four hundred thirty; 40,06,020
