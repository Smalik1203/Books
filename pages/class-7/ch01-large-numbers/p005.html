1.2The Land of Tens

In the Land of Tens, a calculator starts at 0, and each press of a button adds that button’s amount. Thoughtful Thousands has only a +1000 button, Tedious Tens has only +10, and Handy Hundreds has only +100.

The buttons go up in tens because the places of a number do. Each place is worth ten of the place to its right.

Ten make one

Ten of one place make one of the next place to the left. 10 ones make 1 ten, 10 tens make 1 hundred, 10 hundreds make 1 thousand, 10 thousands make 1 ten thousand, and 10 ten thousands make 1 lakh.
