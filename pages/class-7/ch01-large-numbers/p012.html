Large numbers in the world

Each fact below hides a large number. Work out the product or the quotient to find it. Then read the number aloud in both the Indian and the American systems. Talk about each fact with your class.

$1250 \times 380$ is the number of songs that Purandaradāsa composed, according to legend. He was a singer and composer who lived about 500 years ago. The way he set out for teaching Carnatic music is still used today. Suppose he composed songs for 60 years. About how many songs is that each year?

$2100 \times 70{,}000$ is about the distance, in kilometres, between the Earth and the Sun. This distance changes during the year. At its largest, it is about 152 million km. How do you think anyone measured it?

$6400 \times 62{,}500$ is about the number of litres of water that the Amazon river pours into the Atlantic Ocean every second. So much water flows out that the sea is fresh enough to drink 160 km from the coast.

The next three facts hide quotients. Work out each division to find the number.

$13{,}95{,}000 \div 150$ is the length, in kilometres, of the longest train journey in the world without changing trains. The train runs across Russia, from Moscow to Vladivostok, and takes about 7 days. India’s longest train route runs from Dibrugarh in Assam to Kanyakumari in Tamil Nadu. It covers 4219 km in about 76 hours.

$10{,}50{,}00{,}000 \div 700$ is the weight, in kilograms, that an adult blue whale can be heavier than. A newborn blue whale weighs about 2700 kg, as much as an adult hippopotamus. A blue whale can eat up to 3500 kg of tiny sea animals called krill in one day. The heaviest known land animal, a dinosaur called Argentinosaurus, is thought to have weighed about 90,000 kg.

$52{,}00{,}00{,}00{,}000 \div 130$ is the weight, in tonnes, of the plastic waste made across the world in 2021.
