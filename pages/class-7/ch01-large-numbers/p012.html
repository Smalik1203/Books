Nearest neighbours

For a large number, it helps to know its nearest neighbours: the nearest thousand, lakh, crore and so on. Table 1.4 shows five of them for 6,72,85,183.

Table 1.4 The nearest neighbours of 6,72,85,183
NearestNumber
thousand6,72,85,000
ten thousand6,72,90,000
lakh6,73,00,000
ten lakh6,70,00,000
crore7,00,00,000

To find a nearest neighbour, look at the digit just to the right of the place you are rounding to. For the nearest ten thousand of 6,72,85,183, the ten thousands digit is 8 and the digit to its right is 5. That is 5 or more, so the number rounds up to 6,72,90,000. Finding a nearest neighbour this way is called rounding off.

Rounding off

To round off a number to a place, look at the next digit to the right. If it is 5 or more, round up. If it is less than 5, round down. Every digit to the right of that place becomes 0.

Example 8

Round off 5,48,672 to the nearest (a) thousand (b) ten thousand (c) lakh.

(a) The thousands digit is 8, and the next digit is 6, which is 5 or more. Round up: 5,49,000.

(b) The ten thousands digit is 4, and the next digit is 8. Round up: 5,50,000.

(c) The lakhs digit is 5, and the next digit is 4, which is less than 5. Round down: 5,00,000.

Common mistake

Rounding off 3,64,500 to the nearest thousand by looking at the last digit, 0, and keeping 3,64,000. The digit to look at is the one just right of the thousands place. It is 5, so the number rounds up to 3,65,000.
