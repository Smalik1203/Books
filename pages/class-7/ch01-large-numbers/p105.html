Example 9

Meena says that the nearest lakh of 7,49,999 is 8,00,000, because 49,999 is almost 50,000. Is she right?

Solution.

Step 1 $7{,}49{,}999 - 7{,}00{,}000 = 49{,}999$
Step 2 $8{,}00{,}000 - 7{,}49{,}999 = 50{,}001$
Step 3 $49{,}999 \lt 50{,}001$ nearer to 7,00,000
Answer No. The nearest lakh is 7,00,000.
Example 10

A railway station sold 2,48,915 tickets in one month and 3,62,480 in the next. Estimate the total by rounding each number off to the nearest ten thousand. Then find the exact total.

Solution.

Step 1 $2{,}48{,}915 \approx 2{,}50{,}000$ and $3{,}62{,}480 \approx 3{,}60{,}000$
Step 2 Estimate $= 2{,}50{,}000 + 3{,}60{,}000 = 6{,}10{,}000$
Step 3 Exact $= 2{,}48{,}915 + 3{,}62{,}480 = 6{,}11{,}395$
Answer about 6,10,000; exactly 6,11,395
