Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which numbers become 5,50,000 when rounded off to the nearest ten thousand?

Select all correct options.

  1. 5,46,120
  2. 5,54,999
  3. 5,44,999
  4. 5,55,010

Solution.

Step 1A number rounds to 5,50,000 when it is nearer to 5,50,000 than to 5,40,000 or 5,60,000: from 5,45,000 up to 5,54,999.
Step 25,44,999 rounds to 5,40,000 and 5,55,010 rounds to 5,60,000.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

Sippy has the buttons +1, +10, +100, +1000, +10000 and +100000, and makes 3,20,050 with the fewest presses. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It presses 10 times in all.
  2. It presses +10000 two times.
  3. It presses +10 five times.
  4. It presses +100 five times.

Solution.

Step 1Use the biggest button first: +100000 three times, +10000 twice and +10 five times make 3,00,000 + 20,000 + 50.
Step 2That is 3 + 2 + 5 = 10 presses. +100 is not pressed at all.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Assume that a heart beats 72 times a minute. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It beats 4,320 times in an hour.
  2. It beats 1,03,680 times in a day.
  3. It beats about 1 crore times in a day.
  4. It beats more than 7 lakh times in a week.

Solution.

Step 172 × 60 = 4,320 beats in an hour, and 4,320 × 24 = 1,03,680 beats in a day, about 1 lakh, not 1 crore.
Step 21,03,680 × 7 = 7,25,760 beats in a week.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
