Lakh, crore and arab

A lakh is a hundred thousand, which is 1 followed by 5 zeros. A crore is a hundred lakh, which is 1 followed by 7 zeros. An arab is a hundred crore, which is 1 followed by 9 zeros.

How many zeros does a thousand lakh have? How many zeros does a hundred thousand have? And how many lakhs make one million?

Reading a long number

A long number is easy to read once the commas are in. Take the number 9876501234.

Indian system: 9,87,65,01,234 is 9 arab 87 crore 65 lakh 1 thousand 234. We can also say 987 crore 65 lakh 1 thousand 234.

American system: 9,876,501,234 is 9 billion 876 million 501 thousand 234.

Example 6

Write 30,45,06,200 in words in the Indian system. Then write it with the commas of the American system, and in words again.

Indian: 30 crore, 45 lakh, 6 thousand and 200, so it is thirty crore forty-five lakh six thousand two hundred.

American: the digits in threes are 304,506,200, so it is three hundred four million five hundred six thousand two hundred.

The digits are the same in both systems. Only the groups, and so the names, change.
