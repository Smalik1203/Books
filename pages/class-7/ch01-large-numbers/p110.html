Assertion and reason

In Questions 26 to 31, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): 99,999 is the predecessor of 1,00,000.

    Reason (R): The predecessor of a number is 1 less than the number.

  1. Assertion (A): 6,48,000 rounded off to the nearest lakh is 7,00,000.

    Reason (R): 6,48,000 is nearer to 6,00,000 than to 7,00,000.

  1. Assertion (A): One crore is ten million.

    Reason (R): One lakh is a hundred thousand.

  1. Assertion (A): 1 billion is equal to 100 crore.

    Reason (R): 1 billion and 100 crore are each 1 followed by 9 zeros.

  1. Assertion (A): Two 2-digit numbers can have a product of 10,000.

    Reason (R): $99 \times 99$ is less than 10,000.

  1. Assertion (A): $4000 \times 25{,}000$ has 9 digits.

    Reason (R): A 4-digit number times a 5-digit number always has 9 digits.
