Type 5 · Quick products and digits in a product

Example 11

Work out $348 \times 25$ quickly.

Solution.

Step 1 $25 = 100 \div 4$
Step 2 $348 \div 4 = 87$
Step 3 $87 \times 100 = 8{,}700$
Answer $348 \times 25 = 8{,}700$
Example 12

A 6-digit number is multiplied by a 4-digit number. The product has

  1. exactly 10 digits
  2. 9 or 10 digits
  3. 10 or 11 digits
  4. 24 digits

Solution.

Step 1 Smallest: $1{,}00{,}000 \times 1000 = 10{,}00{,}00{,}000$ 9 digits
Step 2 Every product $\lt 10{,}00{,}000 \times 10{,}000 = 10{,}00{,}00{,}00{,}000$ 11 digits
Answer (b)

Type 6 · Estimating with an assumption

Example 13

About how many times does your heart beat in one day? Assume that it beats 80 times a minute.

Solution.

Step 1 $80 \times 60 = 4{,}800$ beats in one hour
Step 2 $4{,}800 \times 24 = 1{,}15{,}200$ beats in one day
Answer about 1,15,200 times
