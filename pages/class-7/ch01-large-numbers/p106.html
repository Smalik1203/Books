Example 12 · Numerical answer

Sippy has the buttons +1, +10, +100, +1000, +10000 and +100000. What is the fewest number of presses that makes 4,07,390?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Biggest button first: +100000 four times, +1000 seven times, +100 three times and +10 nine times.
Step 24 + 7 + 3 + 9 = 23. Each digit tells how many times its button is pressed.
Answer23
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A ground is 120 m long and 75 m wide. Assume that 4 people can stand in one square metre. How many people can stand on the ground?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Area = 120 × 75 = 9,000 square metres.
Step 2People = 9,000 × 4 = 36,000.
Answer36000
