Short answer
  1. The rainfall in a town on the seven days of a week was 12 mm, 0 mm, 5 mm, 18 mm, 0 mm, 9 mm and 19 mm. Find the mean and the median rainfall a day.
  1. Nine students scored 14, 17, 9, 12, 20, 15, 11, 16 and 12 marks out of 20 in a quiz. Find the mean and the median of the marks, and the gap between the highest and lowest marks.
  1. Shop P sold 40, 52, 47, 61 and 50 cups of tea on five days. Shop Q sold 55, 48, 58 and 51 cups on four days. Which shop sold more cups a day, on average? By how many?
  1. Kiran’s phone battery lasted 18, 20, 19, 21, 17 and 1 hours on six days. On the last day an app went wrong. Find the mean and the median. Which of them describes the battery better? Why?
  1. In a class, the favourite fruit of 18 boys is mango, of 10 banana, of 7 apple and of 5 guava. The favourite fruit of 22 girls is mango, of 8 banana, of 9 apple and of 6 guava. Draw a double bar graph with a suitable scale. Which fruit do the most girls like? For which fruit are there more boys than girls?
  1. Write five observations whose highest and lowest values have a gap of 0. What are their mean and median?
