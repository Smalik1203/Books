Case-based questions

  1. A school canteen counts the samosas it sells on each school day for two weeks. The table gives the counts.

    MonTueWedThuFriSat
    Week 1645870556251
    Week 2726669756054
    1. Find the mean number of samosas sold a day in each week.
    2. Find the median for Week 2.
    3. Find the gap between the highest and lowest counts in each week.
    4. On how many days did the canteen sell more samosas in Week 2 than on the same day of Week 1?
    5. The canteen draws a double bar graph of the table with the scale 1 unit for 5 samosas. How long is the longest bar?

  1. Two sections of Class 7 note how each student comes to school. The table gives the numbers.

    WalkCycleBusCar
    Section A129154
    Section B814117
    1. How many students are there in each section?
    2. Which way of coming to school is the most common in Section B?
    3. For which way of coming to school do the two sections differ the most?
    4. In a double bar graph with the scale 1 unit for 2 students, how long is the bar for the bus in Section A?
    5. Ravi says, “More students of Section A live far from school, because more of them come by bus.” Can the data justify this?
