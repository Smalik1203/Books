Looking at this data, you might wonder: Why are the students of the same class so much taller in one school than in the other? Where are these schools? How tall are the students of Classes 6 to 8 in my school? What is the average height of all the Class 6 boys and girls together? Could the students of School B simply be older, or could something else explain the difference? How could we find out?

We have seen that men are usually taller than women. But what about boys and girls? Are boys taller than girls? The data of one or two schools cannot tell us about all the children in our country, or in the world.

Table 5.8 gives the average heights, in centimetres, of boys and girls in India at each age from 5 to 19. The data comes from surveys, and is given for the years 1989, 1999, 2009 and 2019.
