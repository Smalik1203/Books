Section A has 20 students with a mean mark of 60. Section B has 30 students with a mean mark of 70. The mean mark of all 50 students is

The answer is (c). Option (a) takes the average of the two means, which would be right only if the sections were the same size; Section B is larger, so the mean is pulled towards 70. Option (b) gives each section the other’s size, and (d) adds the two means.
