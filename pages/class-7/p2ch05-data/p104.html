Example 6 · Single correct

Which of these is a statistical question?

Choose one correct option.

  1. How many pages does this maths book have?
  2. How tall is the school gate?
  3. How many hours do students in our class sleep on a school night?
  4. What is 25 × 4?

Solution.

Step 1A statistical question is answered by collecting data that vary.
Step 2Sleep differs from student to student; each of the others has one fixed answer.
Answer(c) How many hours do students in our class sleep on a school night?
Example 7 · Multiple correct

A library counts its visitors each day for a week: 38, 45, 29, 52, 41, 36 and 47. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The median is 41.
  2. The mean is 41.
  3. The difference between the highest and lowest is 23.
  4. On more than half of the days there were over 40 visitors.

Solution.

Step 1In order: 29, 36, 38, 41, 45, 47, 52. The median is the 4th value, 41, and 52 − 29 = 23.
Step 2The total is 288, so the mean is 288 ÷ 7, about 41.1, not 41. Four of the seven days had over 40 visitors.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
