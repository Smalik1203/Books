Example 8 · Multiple correct

Eight students scored 34, 41, 28, 45, 37, 30, 49 and 36 marks. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The median is 36.5.
  2. The difference between the highest and lowest mark is 21.
  3. The mean is 37.5.
  4. The median is one of the marks scored.

Solution.

Step 1In order: 28, 30, 34, 36, 37, 41, 45, 49. The median is (36 + 37) ÷ 2 = 36.5, which nobody scored.
Step 249 − 28 = 21, and the total is 300, so the mean is 300 ÷ 8 = 37.5.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which of these are statistical statements?

Select all correct options.

  1. Priya’s bicycle is red.
  2. Most students in our class reach school in less than 20 minutes.
  3. On average, the girls in our class sleep 8 hours a night.
  4. Today is Monday.

Solution.

Step 1(b) and (c) describe data collected from many people.
Step 2(a) and (d) are single facts about one thing.
Answer(b), (c)
