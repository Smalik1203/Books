Example 10

Five people work in a small workshop. Four workers earn ₹12,000, ₹14,000, ₹13,000 and ₹15,000 a month, and the owner earns ₹96,000. Which represents the monthly earnings better, the mean or the median?

Solution.

Step 1 Total $= 12{,}000 + 14{,}000 + 13{,}000 + 15{,}000 + 96{,}000 = 1{,}50{,}000$
Step 2 Mean $= 1{,}50{,}000 \div 5 = 30{,}000$
Step 3 In order: 12,000, 13,000, 14,000, 15,000, 96,000; median $= 14{,}000$
Step 4 $30{,}000$ is more than four of the five earn 96,000 is an outlier
Answer The median, ₹14,000.
