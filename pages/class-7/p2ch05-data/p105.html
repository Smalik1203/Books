Example 10

Five people work in a small workshop. Four workers earn ₹12,000, ₹14,000, ₹13,000 and ₹15,000 a month, and the owner earns ₹96,000. Which represents the monthly earnings better, the mean or the median?

Solution.

Step 1 Total $= 12{,}000 + 14{,}000 + 13{,}000 + 15{,}000 + 96{,}000 = 1{,}50{,}000$
Step 2 Mean $= 1{,}50{,}000 \div 5 = 30{,}000$
Step 3 In order: 12,000, 13,000, 14,000, 15,000, 96,000; median $= 14{,}000$
Step 4 $30{,}000$ is more than four of the five earn 96,000 is an outlier
Answer The median, ₹14,000.

Example 11

Ten students noted how many minutes they take to walk to school: 12, 8, 15, 10, 12, 21, 9, 12, 10 and 11. Show them as a dot plot on a number line from 5 to 25, with the mean and the median.

Solution.

Step 1 In order: 8, 9, 10, 10, 11, 12, 12, 12, 15, 21 one dot for each
Step 2 2 dots above 10, 3 dots above 12, 1 dot above each other value equal values stacked
Step 3 Mean $= 120 \div 10 = 12$
Step 4 Median $= (11 + 12) \div 2 = 11.5$ 5th and 6th values
Answer Fig. 5.14. The one long walk, 21 minutes, pulls the mean above the median.
