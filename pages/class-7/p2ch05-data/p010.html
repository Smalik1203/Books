A median of zero runs

In a cricket match, can the median of the runs scored by a team’s players be 0 while the team scores 407 runs for 10 wickets? Yes, it can. If 6 of the 11 players score 0, the median score is 0, and the other five players can still make the runs. The team’s 407 runs included 19 extras, so the average runs scored by a player were $(407 - 19) \div 11$, a little more than 35.

Zero, or no value?

A player’s scores in a series are 57, 13, 0, 84, —, 51 and 27. In Match 3 the player batted and scored 0 runs, but in Match 5 the player did not play. So the player played 6 matches, not 7, and the average runs scored per match are $(57 + 13 + 0 + 84 + 51 + 27) \div 6$.

Sita has a mango tree behind her house. From January to December last year, it gave 0, 0, 8, 24, 41, 16, 5, 0, 0, 0, 0 and 0 mangoes. To find the mean or the median number of mangoes a month, it makes sense to use only the summer months, when mangoes are expected to grow.

A mean foot

In Europe in the early 1500s, land was measured in rods, and a rod was 16 feet long. At that time a foot meant the length of a human foot. But feet come in different sizes, so whose foot should be used? To settle this, 16 grown men were asked to stand in a line, toe to heel, and the length of the line was taken as one rod. The rod was then divided into 16 equal parts, and each part was one foot. So a foot was the arithmetic mean of the 16 men’s feet, even though nobody used the word “mean”.
