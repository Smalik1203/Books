Example 10 · Multiple correct

In July, August, September and October, Class 6 borrowed 45, 60, 35 and 50 library books, and Class 7 borrowed 50, 55, 40 and 70. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Class 7 borrowed more books in total.
  2. Class 6 borrowed more than Class 7 in September.
  3. Together, the classes borrowed the most books in October.
  4. Class 7 borrowed more than Class 6 in every month.

Solution.

Step 1Totals: Class 6, 190; Class 7, 215. The monthly totals together are 95, 115, 75 and 120, the largest in October.
Step 2In September Class 7 borrowed more (40 against 35), but in August Class 6 did (60 against 55).
Answer(a), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

The mean of five numbers is 18. When one number is removed, the mean of the other four is 16. What number was removed?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The five numbers add to 5 × 18 = 90, and the four add to 4 × 16 = 64.
Step 2The number removed is 90 − 64 = 26.
Answer26
Example 12 · Numerical answer

Ten students take these numbers of minutes to walk to school: 12, 8, 15, 10, 12, 21, 9, 12, 10 and 11. Find the median, in minutes.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1In order: 8, 9, 10, 10, 11, 12, 12, 12, 15, 21. There are 10 values, so the median is halfway between the 5th and 6th.
Step 2(11 + 12) ÷ 2 = 11.5.
Answer11.5
