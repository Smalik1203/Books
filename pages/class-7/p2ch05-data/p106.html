Type 6 · Double bar graphs

Example 12

A school library lent books to Class 6 and Class 7 in four months. Class 6 borrowed 45 books in July, 60 in August, 35 in September and 50 in October. Class 7 borrowed 30, 55, 40 and 70 books in the same months. Draw a double bar graph of this data.

Solution.

Step 1 Largest value $= 70$, so take 1 unit for 10 books choose the scale
Step 2 Class 6 bars: 4.5, 6, 3.5 and 5 units
Step 3 Class 7 bars: 3, 5.5, 4 and 7 units
Answer Fig. 5.15, with a key for the two classes
Example 13

Use Fig. 5.15. (a) In which month did the two classes borrow the most books together? (b) In that month, how many more books did Class 7 borrow than Class 6?

Solution.

Step 1 July $45 + 30 = 75$; August $60 + 55 = 115$ add each pair
Step 2 September $35 + 40 = 75$; October $50 + 70 = 120$
Step 3 October: $70 - 50 = 20$
Answer (a) October (b) 20 books
