Example 4

A cricketer’s scores in a series are 45, 0, 30, — and 25, where the dash means that he did not play. His average score per match played is

  1. 25
  2. 20
  3. 27.5
  4. 33 and a third

Solution.

Step 1 Matches played $= 4$ 0 counts, — does not
Step 2 Runs $= 45 + 0 + 30 + 25 = 100$
Step 3 Average $= 100 \div 4 = 25$
Answer (a)
Example 5

Section A has 20 students with a mean mark of 60. Section B has 30 students with a mean mark of 70. The mean mark of all 50 students is

  1. 65
  2. 64
  3. 66
  4. 130

Solution.

Step 1 Total for Section A $= 20 \times 60 = 1200$ mean times count
Step 2 Total for Section B $= 30 \times 70 = 2100$
Step 3 Mean $= (1200 + 2100) \div 50 = 3300 \div 50 = 66$ all 50 together
Answer (c)

Example 6

A library counts its visitors each day for a week: 38, 45, 29, 52, 41, 36 and 47. Find the median number of visitors.

Solution.

Step 1 In order: 29, 36, 38, 41, 45, 47, 52 order first
Step 2 7 values, so the median is the 4th value odd number of values
Answer 41 visitors
