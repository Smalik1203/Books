Example 4 · Single correct

The scores of six players are 12, 15, 14, 13, 16 and 60. Which statement is true?

Choose one correct option.

  1. The mean is less than the median.
  2. The mean and the median are equal.
  3. Leaving out 60 changes the median more than the mean.
  4. The mean is greater than the median.

Solution.

Step 1Mean = 130 ÷ 6, about 21.7. In order, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 60, so the median is (14 + 15) ÷ 2 = 14.5.
Step 2Leaving out 60 makes the mean 14 and the median 14, so the mean changes far more.
Answer(d) The mean is greater than the median.
Example 5 · Single correct

Four workers in a workshop earn ₹12,000, ₹14,000, ₹13,000 and ₹15,000 a month, and the owner earns ₹96,000. Which value best represents what a worker there usually earns?

Choose one correct option.

  1. the mean, ₹30,000
  2. the median, ₹14,000
  3. the highest, ₹96,000
  4. the difference between highest and lowest, ₹84,000

Solution.

Step 1Mean = 1,50,000 ÷ 5 = ₹30,000, more than any worker earns, because of the one large value.
Step 2In order, 12, 13, 14, 15, 96 thousand: the median, ₹14,000, is close to the workers’ pay.
Answer(b) the median, ₹14,000
