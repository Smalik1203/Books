Why the other options are wrong

10 The 5 bags weigh $4 \times 5 + 5 = 25$ kg, and $25 \div 5 = 5$ kg: (b). A value equal to the mean leaves the mean unchanged. Option (d) is the total, not the mean.
13 The five numbers add up to $5 \times 20 = 100$. Without 40 the other four add up to 60, and $60 \div 4 = 15$: (c). Option (b) divides 60 by 5, as if there were still five numbers, and (d) forgets to take 40 away.
15 The mean goes from 12 to $136 \div 4 = 34$, up by 22. The median goes from 12 to $(12 + 14) \div 2 = 13$, up by 1: (a). Option (b) has the two the wrong way round.
17 Only (d) adds up to 50 with 8 in the middle. The data in (a) and (c) has a mean of 8, and the data in (b) has a median of 10.
18 The median is $(5 + x) \div 2$. For $x$ from 5 to 9 this runs from 5 to 7. A median of 8 needs $x = 11$, which is more than 9: (a).
20 A is true. But the mean of 1 and 2 is 1.5, which is not one of the values, so R is false: (c).
