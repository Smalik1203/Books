Exercise Set 5.1
  1. Shreyas is playing with a bat and a ball, but not playing cricket. He counts how many times he can bounce the ball on the bat before it falls. In 8 tries he gets 6, 2, 9, 5, 4, 6, 3 and 5 bounces. Find the average number of bounces. Is there a try in which he got exactly the average number of bounces, or is the average a number he never got?
  1. Try the same activity yourself. Record at least 7 tries, and find your average number of bounces. Compare your average with Shreyas’s average, and say what might explain any difference between the two.
  1. Choose a flowering plant near your home. During its flowering season, count the flowers that bloom on it each day for a week. What is the average number of flowers that bloom in a day?
  1. Nikhil and Sunil are training for a 100 m race. Their times in seconds over the past week were: Nikhil 17, 18, 17, 16, 19, 17, 18; Sunil 20, 18, 18, 17, 16, 16, 17. Which of them ran faster on average?
  1. The number of students in a school in six years in a row was 1555, 1670, 1750, 2013, 2040 and 2126. Find the mean number of students over these six years.
