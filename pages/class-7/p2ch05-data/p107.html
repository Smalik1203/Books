Example 13 · Numerical answer

A cycle shop sold 42 cycles in 6 days last week and 40 cycles in 5 days this week. How many more cycles a day, on average, did it sell this week?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Last week: 42 ÷ 6 = 7 cycles a day. This week: 40 ÷ 5 = 8 cycles a day.
Step 28 − 7 = 1 more cycle a day, although fewer were sold in all.

Example 14 · Matching

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) The mean of 4, 7, 7, 9, 13(1) 9
(Q) The median of 4, 7, 7, 9, 13(2) 40
(R) The difference between the highest and lowest of 4, 7, 7, 9, 13(3) 8
(S) The sum of 4, 7, 7, 9, 13(4) 7

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The sum is 4 + 7 + 7 + 9 + 13 = 40, so the mean is 40 ÷ 5 = 8.
Step 2The middle value of the five in order is 7, and 13 − 4 = 9.
Answer(b) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
