Assertion and reason

In Questions 19 to 21, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): The median of 4, 9, 6, 8 and 20 is 8.

    Reason (R): The median is the middle value when the data is put in order.

  1. Assertion (A): The mean of 5, 5, 5 and 5 is 5.

    Reason (R): The mean of some data is always one of its values.

  1. Assertion (A): The mean of 2, 4, 6 and 8 is 4.

    Reason (R): The mean is the sum of the values divided by the number of values.
