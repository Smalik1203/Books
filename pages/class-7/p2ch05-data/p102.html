Solved Examples

Type 1 · Statistical questions and statements

Example 1

Is each of these a statistical question? (a) How many pages does this maths book have? (b) How many pages do the books in our school library have?

Solution.

Step 1 (a) one book has one number of pages one fixed value
Step 2 (b) the library’s books have many different numbers of pages the values vary
Answer (a) is not a statistical question; (b) is.
Example 2

Which of these is a statistical statement? (a) Priya’s bicycle is red. (b) Most students in our class reach school in less than 20 minutes.

Solution.

Step 1 (a) is about one bicycle and gives no number
Step 2 (b) sums up the times of many students in a number, 20 minutes sums up data
Answer (b)

Type 2 · Finding and comparing means

Example 3

A cycle shop was open for 6 days last week and sold 42 cycles. This week it was open for 5 days and sold 40 cycles. The owner says that the shop did better last week, because it sold more cycles. Is he right?

Solution.

Step 1 Last week: $42 \div 6 = 7$ cycles a day
Step 2 This week: $40 \div 5 = 8$ cycles a day
Step 3 $8 \gt 7$ compare means, not totals
Answer No. This week the shop sold 1 cycle a day more.
