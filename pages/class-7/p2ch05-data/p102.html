Example 1 · Single correct

A cricketer’s scores in a series are 45, 0, 30, — and 25, where the dash means that he did not play. What is his average score per match played?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 25
  2. 20
  3. 27.5
  4. 33

Solution.

Step 1He played 4 matches; the 0 counts as a match played, and the dash does not.
Step 2(45 + 0 + 30 + 25) ÷ 4 = 100 ÷ 4 = 25.
Answer(a) 25
Example 2 · Single correct

Section A has 20 students with a mean mark of 60. Section B has 30 students with a mean mark of 70. What is the mean mark of all 50 students?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 65
  2. 64
  3. 66
  4. 130

Solution.

Step 1Total marks = 20 × 60 + 30 × 70 = 1200 + 2100 = 3300.
Step 2Mean = 3300 ÷ 50 = 66. It is nearer 70 because Section B is larger.
Answer(c) 66
Example 3 · Single correct

What is the median of 2, 3, 3, 5, 7, 10?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 3
  2. 4
  3. 5
  4. 8

Solution.

Step 1There are 6 values in order, so the middle two are the 3rd and 4th: 3 and 5.
Step 2Median = (3 + 5) ÷ 2 = 4.
Answer(b) 4
