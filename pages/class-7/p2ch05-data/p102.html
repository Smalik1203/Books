The data is 1, 2, 3, 4 and one more number. Can its mean be 10 while its median is 3?

A mean of 10 for five numbers needs a total of 50. The four numbers we know add up to 10, so the fifth would be 40. In order the data is 1, 2, 3, 4, 40, and the middle value is still 3. So yes. The one outlier, 40, has pulled the mean from 2.5 all the way up to 10 and has not moved the median at all. A median depends only on which value sits in the middle, so one value far out at either end cannot move it, however large that value is.
