Shreyas says, “They did about equally well. Yashasvi scored more in the first two matches, and Shubman scored more in the last two.”

Vaishnavi says, “I think Shubman did better. He made the highest score in a single match, 90!”

Shreyas replies, “No, Yashasvi did better. He made 175 runs in all, and Shubman made only 128.”

Vaishnavi adds, “Yashasvi was also steadier. The gap between his highest and lowest scores is smaller.”
