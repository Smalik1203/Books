Puzzle: queens that do not attack

Chess began in India and is played on an $8 \times 8$ board. A queen can move any distance along its row, its column or either diagonal. Can you place 4 queens on a $4 \times 4$ board so that no queen attacks another? Fig. 8.14 does not work, because two of its queens share a diagonal. Then try to place 8 queens on an $8 \times 8$ board in the same way.
