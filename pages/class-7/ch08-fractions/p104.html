Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which products are less than $\frac{7}{8}$?

Select all correct options.

  1. $\frac{7}{8}$ × $\frac{5}{3}$
  2. $\frac{7}{8}$ × $\frac{2}{3}$
  3. $\frac{7}{8}$ × 1
  4. $\frac{7}{8}$ × $\frac{9}{10}$

Solution.

Step 1Multiplying by a fraction less than 1 makes a number smaller; $\frac{2}{3}$ and $\frac{9}{10}$ are less than 1.
Step 2$\frac{5}{3}$ is more than 1, so it makes the product larger, and multiplying by 1 leaves $\frac{7}{8}$ unchanged.
Answer(b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which of these are equal to $\frac{4}{9}$ ÷ $\frac{2}{3}$?

Select all correct options.

  1. $\frac{4}{9}$ × $\frac{3}{2}$
  2. $\frac{2}{3}$
  3. $\frac{8}{27}$
  4. $\frac{12}{18}$

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{4}{9}$ ÷ $\frac{2}{3}$ = $\frac{4}{9}$ × $\frac{3}{2}$ = $\frac{12}{18}$.
Step 2$\frac{12}{18}$ = $\frac{2}{3}$. $\frac{8}{27}$ is $\frac{4}{9}$ × $\frac{2}{3}$, the product, not the quotient.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which quotients are greater than $\frac{2}{5}$?

Select all correct options.

  1. $\frac{2}{5}$ ÷ 4
  2. $\frac{2}{5}$ ÷ $\frac{1}{4}$
  3. $\frac{2}{5}$ ÷ $\frac{9}{10}$
  4. $\frac{2}{5}$ ÷ $\frac{2}{3}$

Solution.

Step 1Dividing by a number less than 1 gives a larger result; $\frac{1}{4}$, $\frac{9}{10}$ and $\frac{2}{3}$ are all less than 1.
Step 2Dividing by 4 gives $\frac{1}{10}$, which is smaller.
Answer(b), (c), (d)
