Example 8

$\frac{3}{4} \div \frac{1}{8}$ is equal to

  1. $\dfrac{3}{32}$
  2. 6
  3. $\dfrac{1}{6}$
  4. $\dfrac{3}{8}$

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{3}{4} \div \dfrac{1}{8} = \dfrac{3}{4} \times \dfrac{8}{1}$ reciprocal of divisor
Step 2 $= \dfrac{24}{4} = 6$
Answer (b)

Type 5 · Is the quotient larger or smaller?

Example 9

Without dividing, say whether $\frac{2}{5} \div 4$ is greater or less than $\frac{2}{5}$. Then find it.

Solution.

Step 1 $4 \gt 1$, so the quotient $\lt \frac{2}{5}$ divisor above 1
Step 2 $\dfrac{2}{5} \div 4 = \dfrac{2}{5} \times \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{2}{20} = \dfrac{1}{10}$
Step 3 $\dfrac{1}{10} \lt \dfrac{4}{10} = \dfrac{2}{5}$ check
Answer less; the quotient is $\frac{1}{10}$
Example 10

Which of these is the greatest?

  1. $5 \times \dfrac{7}{8}$
  2. $5 \div \dfrac{7}{8}$
  3. $5 \times 1$
  4. $5 \div \dfrac{8}{7}$

Solution.

Step 1 $5 \times \dfrac{7}{8} = \dfrac{35}{8} \lt 5$ multiplier below 1
Step 2 $5 \div \dfrac{8}{7} = 5 \times \dfrac{7}{8} = \dfrac{35}{8}$ same as (a)
Step 3 $5 \times 1 = 5$
Step 4 $5 \div \dfrac{7}{8} = 5 \times \dfrac{8}{7} = \dfrac{40}{7} \gt 5$ divisor below 1
Answer (b)
