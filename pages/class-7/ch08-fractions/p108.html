Long answer
  1. Ananya has ₹1200. She spends $\frac{1}{4}$ of it on books, and then $\frac{2}{3}$ of what is left on a school bag. How much money does she have left? What fraction of ₹1200 is that?
  1. A garden is a rectangle $12\frac{1}{2}$ m long and $8\frac{2}{5}$ m wide.
    1. Find the area of the garden.
    2. Vegetables are grown on $\frac{2}{5}$ of the garden. What area is that?
    3. The rest of the garden is divided into 7 flower beds of equal area. What is the area of each bed?

  1. Gurpreet’s family farms a field of 24 acres near Ludhiana. They grow wheat on $\frac{1}{2}$ of the field, mustard on $\frac{1}{3}$ of it, and vegetables on the rest.

    1. How many acres are under wheat?
    2. What fraction of the field is under vegetables? How many acres is that?
    3. The vegetable land is divided into plots of $\frac{2}{3}$ acre each. How many plots are there?
