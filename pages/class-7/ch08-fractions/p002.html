In $\frac{1}{5}$ hour: 3 km divided into 5 equal parts, which is $\frac{3}{5}$ km.

In $\frac{2}{5}$ hour: twice as far, $2 \times \frac{3}{5} = \frac{6}{5}$ km. So $\frac{2}{5} \times 3 = \frac{6}{5}$.

Look at how we found $\frac{2}{5} \times 3$. The first number, $\frac{2}{5}$, is the multiplier, and the second, 3, is the multiplicand. First we divided the multiplicand by the denominator of the multiplier, 5, to get $\frac{3}{5}$. Then we multiplied the result by the numerator of the multiplier, 2, to get $\frac{6}{5}$. We multiply any fraction and whole number in this way.

Example 1

A farmer has 5 grandchildren. She gives each of them $\frac{2}{3}$ of an acre of land. How much land does she give away in all?

$5 \times \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{10}{3}$

She gives away $\frac{10}{3}$ acres, which is $3\frac{1}{3}$ acres.

Example 2

One hour of internet time costs ₹8. How much does $1\frac{1}{4}$ hours cost?

As a fraction, $1\frac{1}{4}$ hours is $\frac{5}{4}$ hours. Divide the multiplicand, 8, by the denominator 4, and multiply the result by the numerator 5.

$\dfrac{5}{4} \times 8 = 5 \times (8 \div 4) = 5 \times 2 = 10$

It costs ₹10.
