In Questions 16 to 20, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $3 \times \frac{2}{5} = \frac{6}{5}$.

    Reason (R): To multiply a fraction by a whole number, multiply both its numerator and its denominator by the whole number.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{8}{9} = \frac{2}{3}$.

    Reason (R): A common factor of a numerator and a denominator can be cancelled before multiplying.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{2}$.

    Reason (R): $\frac{1}{2}$ is less than 1.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{4}{5} \div \frac{2}{3}$ is less than $\frac{4}{5}$.

    Reason (R): $\frac{2}{3}$ is less than 1.

  1. Assertion (A): $\frac{2}{3} \div \frac{1}{6} = 4$.

    Reason (R): Dividing by $\frac{1}{6}$ is the same as multiplying by 6.
