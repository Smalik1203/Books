Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) Pieces of $\frac{2}{5}$ m that can be cut from 8 m(1) 2
(Q) Length, in m, of $\frac{2}{5}$ of 8 m(2) 20
(R) Share, in m, when 8 m is divided equally among 5(3) $\frac{8}{5}$
(S) Total length, in m, of 5 pieces of $\frac{2}{5}$ m(4) $\frac{16}{5}$

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1

Solution.

Step 1How many pieces: 8 ÷ $\frac{2}{5}$ = 20. A share: 8 ÷ 5 = $\frac{8}{5}$.
Step 2Part of a length: $\frac{2}{5}$ × 8 = $\frac{16}{5}$. A total: 5 × $\frac{2}{5}$ = 2.
Answer(d) P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
