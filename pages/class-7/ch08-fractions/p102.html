Solved Examples

Type 1 · Multiplying by a fraction

Example 1

Meena says that $\frac{3}{4} \times 8$ must be less than 8. Is she right? Find the product.

Solution.

Step 1 One of 4 equal parts of 8: $8 \div 4 = 2$ divide by 4
Step 2 $\dfrac{3}{4} \times 8 = 3 \times 2 = 6$ take 3 parts
Step 3 $6 \lt 8$ 3 parts of 4
Answer Yes. The product is 6.
Example 2

Tara drinks $\frac{1}{2}$ of $\frac{1}{3}$ litre of juice. Ravi drinks $\frac{1}{3}$ of $\frac{1}{2}$ litre. Who drinks more?

Solution.

Step 1 Tara: $\dfrac{1}{2} \times \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ litre “of” means multiply
Step 2 Ravi: $\dfrac{1}{3} \times \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ litre order does not matter
Answer Neither. Each drinks $\frac{1}{6}$ litre.
