Example 1 · Single correct

$\frac{3}{5}$ × $\frac{10}{9}$ is equal to

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{13}{14}$
  2. $\frac{2}{3}$
  3. $\frac{27}{50}$
  4. $\frac{3}{2}$

Solution.

Step 1Multiply the numerators and the denominators: (3 × 10) ÷ (5 × 9) = $\frac{30}{45}$.
Step 2Cancel the common factor 15: $\frac{30}{45}$ = $\frac{2}{3}$.
Answer(b) $\frac{2}{3}$
Example 2 · Single correct

$\frac{3}{4}$ ÷ $\frac{1}{8}$ is equal to

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{3}{32}$
  2. $\frac{1}{6}$
  3. $\frac{3}{8}$
  4. 6

Solution.

Step 1Dividing by $\frac{1}{8}$ is multiplying by its reciprocal, 8.
Step 2$\frac{3}{4}$ × 8 = $\frac{24}{4}$ = 6. There are six eighths in three quarters.
Answer(d) 6
Example 3 · Single correct

Which of these is the greatest?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 5 × $\frac{7}{8}$
  2. 5 ÷ $\frac{7}{8}$
  3. 5 × 1
  4. 5 ÷ $\frac{8}{7}$

Solution.

Step 1Multiplying by $\frac{7}{8}$, which is less than 1, gives less than 5; so does dividing by $\frac{8}{7}$, which is more than 1.
Step 2Dividing by $\frac{7}{8}$, which is less than 1, gives more than 5: 5 × $\frac{8}{7}$ = $\frac{40}{7}$.
Answer(b) 5 ÷ $\frac{7}{8}$
