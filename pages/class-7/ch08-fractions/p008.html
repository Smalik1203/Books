The order of multiplication

We found that $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{8}$. What is $\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{1}{2}$? It is $\frac{1}{8}$ too. In Fig. 8.8 the two rectangles are the same rectangle turned on its side, and turning a rectangle does not change its area.

Order does not matter

$\dfrac{a}{b} \times \dfrac{c}{d} = \dfrac{c}{d} \times \dfrac{a}{b}$

This is true for any two fractions. Brahmagupta’s rule shows it as well, since $a \times c = c \times a$ and $b \times d = d \times b$.
