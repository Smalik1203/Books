What this chapter showed
  1. To multiply by a fraction, divide by its denominator and multiply by its numerator.
  2. Brahmagupta’s rule: $\frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \times c}{b \times d}$. The product of two fractions is the area of a rectangle with those sides.
  3. Common factors of a numerator and a denominator can be cancelled before multiplying.
  4. Multiplying by a number between 0 and 1 gives less than the other number; multiplying by a number greater than 1 gives more.
  5. The reciprocal of $\frac{a}{b}$ is $\frac{b}{a}$, and a fraction times its reciprocal is 1.
  6. To divide by a fraction, multiply by its reciprocal: $\frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \times d}{b \times c}$.
  7. Dividing by a number between 0 and 1 gives a quotient greater than the dividend; dividing by a number greater than 1 gives a quotient less than the dividend.
