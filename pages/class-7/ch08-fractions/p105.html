Example 10 · Multiple correct

A floor mat is $2\frac{1}{4}$ m long and $1\frac{1}{3}$ m wide. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its area is 3 square metres.
  2. Its area is $\frac{9}{4}$ × $\frac{4}{3}$ square metres.
  3. Its area is $2\frac{1}{12}$ square metres.
  4. Its perimeter is $7\frac{1}{6}$ m.

Solution.

Step 1$2\frac{1}{4}$ = $\frac{9}{4}$ and $1\frac{1}{3}$ = $\frac{4}{3}$, so the area is $\frac{9}{4}$ × $\frac{4}{3}$ = 3 square metres.
Step 2Perimeter = 2 × ($\frac{9}{4}$ + $\frac{4}{3}$) = 2 × $\frac{43}{12}$ = $\frac{43}{6}$ = $7\frac{1}{6}$ m. Multiplying the whole numbers and the fractions separately gives the wrong $2\frac{1}{12}$.

Example 11 · Numerical answer

How many packets of $\frac{3}{8}$ kg each can be filled from 6 kg of rice?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Packets = 6 ÷ $\frac{3}{8}$ = 6 × $\frac{8}{3}$.
Step 2= $\frac{48}{3}$ = 16.

Example 12 · Numerical answer

A car runs 16 km on 1 litre of petrol. How many kilometres does it run on $2\frac{3}{4}$ litres?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Distance = 16 × $2\frac{3}{4}$ = 16 × $\frac{11}{4}$.
Step 2= 4 × 11 = 44 km.
Answer44
