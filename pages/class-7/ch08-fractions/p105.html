Example 11

A ribbon $7\frac{1}{2}$ m long is cut into pieces $\frac{3}{4}$ m long. How many pieces are there?

  1. 10
  2. $\dfrac{45}{8}$
  3. $\dfrac{1}{10}$
  4. 30

Solution.

Step 1 Pieces $= 7\dfrac{1}{2} \div \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{15}{2} \div \dfrac{3}{4}$ how many fit
Step 2 $= \dfrac{15}{2} \times \dfrac{4}{3} = \dfrac{60}{6} = 10$
Answer (a)
Example 12

Half of the students in a class are girls, and $\frac{2}{5}$ of the girls play football. What fraction of the class are girls who play football?

  1. $\dfrac{9}{10}$
  2. $\dfrac{1}{5}$
  3. $\dfrac{3}{7}$
  4. $\dfrac{4}{5}$

Solution.

Step 1 Girls who play $= \dfrac{2}{5}$ of $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the class a part of a part
Step 2 $\dfrac{2}{5} \times \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{2}{10} = \dfrac{1}{5}$ “of” means multiply
Answer (b)
