Problem 3

A ribbon $7\frac{1}{2}$ m long is cut into pieces $\frac{3}{4}$ m long. How many pieces are there?

  1. 10
  2. $\dfrac{45}{8}$
  3. $\dfrac{1}{10}$
  4. 30
Solution

The question asks how many times $\frac{3}{4}$ fits into $\frac{15}{2}$, so we divide.

$\dfrac{15}{2} \div \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{15}{2} \times \dfrac{4}{3} = 10$

The answer is (a). (b) multiplies, (c) divides the wrong way round, (d) forgets the 3.

Problem 4

Which of these is the greatest?

  1. $5 \times \dfrac{7}{8}$
  2. $5 \div \dfrac{7}{8}$
  3. $5 \times 1$
  4. $5 \div \dfrac{8}{7}$
Solution

Multiplying 5 by $\frac{7}{8}$, a number less than 1, gives less than 5. Dividing 5 by $\frac{7}{8}$ gives more than 5. Dividing by $\frac{8}{7}$, a number greater than 1, gives less than 5. And $5 \times 1 = 5$.

The answer is (b). Options (a) and (d) are the same number, $\frac{35}{8}$, since dividing by $\frac{8}{7}$ is multiplying by $\frac{7}{8}$. Option (c) is 5, and (b) is $\frac{40}{7}$, which is more.

Problem 5

Half of the students in a class are girls, and $\frac{2}{5}$ of the girls play football. What fraction of the class are girls who play football?

  1. $\dfrac{9}{10}$
  2. $\dfrac{1}{5}$
  3. $\dfrac{3}{7}$
  4. $\dfrac{4}{5}$
Solution

We want $\frac{2}{5}$ of one half of the class, and “of” means multiply.

$\dfrac{2}{5} \times \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{1}{5}$

The answer is (b). (a) adds, (c) adds numerators and denominators, (d) divides.
