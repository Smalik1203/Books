Example 4

Find $\frac{9}{16} \times \frac{8}{15}$ in its lowest form.

Solution.

Step 1 9 and 15 share 3; 8 and 16 share 8 common factors
Step 2 $\dfrac{9}{16} \times \dfrac{8}{15} = \dfrac{3 \times 1}{2 \times 5} = \dfrac{3}{10}$ cancel
Answer $\frac{3}{10}$
Example 5

A floor mat is $2\frac{1}{4}$ m long and $1\frac{1}{3}$ m wide. Find its area.

Solution.

Step 1 Area $= 2\frac{1}{4} \times 1\frac{1}{3} = \frac{9}{4} \times \frac{4}{3}$ improper fractions
Step 2 $= 3$ cancel 4 and 3
Answer 3 square metres

Type 3 · Is the product larger or smaller?

Example 6

Without multiplying, compare $\frac{7}{8} \times \frac{5}{3}$ with $\frac{7}{8}$ and with $\frac{5}{3}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\frac{5}{3} \gt 1$, so the product $\gt \frac{7}{8}$ multiplier above 1
Step 2 $\frac{7}{8} \lt 1$, so the product $\lt \frac{5}{3}$ multiplier below 1
Answer greater than $\frac{7}{8}$ and less than $\frac{5}{3}$

Type 4 · Dividing fractions

Example 7

Find $\frac{4}{9} \div \frac{2}{3}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{4}{9} \div \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{4}{9} \times \dfrac{3}{2}$ reciprocal of divisor
Step 2 $= \dfrac{4 \times 3}{9 \times 2} = \dfrac{2 \times 1}{3 \times 1} = \dfrac{2}{3}$ cancel 2 and 3
Answer $\frac{2}{3}$
