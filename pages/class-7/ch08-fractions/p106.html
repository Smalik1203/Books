Example 13 · Numerical answer

Find $\frac{3}{4}$ of $\frac{2}{3}$ of 120.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{2}{3}$ of 120 = 120 ÷ 3 × 2 = 80.
Step 2$\frac{3}{4}$ of 80 = 80 ÷ 4 × 3 = 60.
Answer60

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  2. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2

Solution.
