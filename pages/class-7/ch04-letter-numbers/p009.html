Example 7

A shop rents out chairs for ₹40 each and tables for ₹75 each for a day. When they are returned, the shopkeeper gives back ₹6 for each chair and ₹10 for each table. Write an expression for the amount paid in all, if $x$ chairs and $y$ tables are rented.

Solution.

Step 1 Paid at the start $= 40x + 75y$ rupees
Step 2 Given back $= 6x + 10y$ rupees
Step 3 Paid in all $= (40x + 75y) - (6x + 10y)$
Step 4 $= 40x + 75y - 6x - 10y$ signs after the minus
Step 5 $= 40x + (-6x) + 75y + (-10y)$ like terms grouped
Step 6 $= (40 - 6)x + (75 - 10)y$
Answer $34x + 65y$ rupees

For 3 chairs and 2 tables, that is ₹232.

Could we also have written the amount as $(40x + 75y) + (-6x - 10y)$? Explain why. What does $-6x - 10y$ stand for in the shop?

Example 8

Charu takes part in a quiz with three rounds. Her scores are $7p - 3q$, $8p - 4q$ and $6p - 2q$. Here $p$ is the score for a correct answer, and $q$ is the penalty for a wrong one. What is her total score?

In the first round she got 7 answers right and 3 wrong: each right answer adds $p$ to her score, and each wrong answer takes away $q$.

Solution.

Step 1 At $p = 4$ and $q = 1$: $7 \times 4 - 3 \times 1 = 28 + (-3) = 25$ the first round
Step 2 Total $= (7p - 3q) + (8p - 4q) + (6p - 2q)$ the three scores added
Step 3 $= 7p + 8p + 6p + (-3q) + (-4q) + (-2q)$ so the brackets go
Step 4 $= (7 + 8 + 6)p + \big(-(3 + 4 + 2)\big)q$ added in any order
Answer $21p - 9q$

What were her scores in the second and third rounds, when $p = 4$ and $q = 1$? If there were no penalty, what would $q$ be? Her friend Krishita scored $23p - 7q$ in all. Give some scores Krishita might have had in the three rounds. Who scored more, and how much more? Find it by simplifying $23p - 7q - (21p - 9q)$. Then try a few values of $p$ and $q$, such as $p = 4$ and $q = 1$, to check your answer.
