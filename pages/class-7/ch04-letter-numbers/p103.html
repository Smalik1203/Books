Example 3 · Single correct

$3(2a - b) - 2(a - 2b)$ is equal to

Choose one correct option.

  1. $4a - 7b$
  2. $4a - b$
  3. $8a - 7b$
  4. $4a + b$

Solution.

Step 1$3(2a - b) = 6a - 3b$ and $-2(a - 2b) = -2a + 4b$.
Step 2$6a - 2a = 4a$ and $-3b + 4b = b$, giving $4a + b$.
Answer(d) $4a + b$
Example 4 · Single correct

A square of 9 dates, 3 rows by 3 columns, is chosen on a calendar. The sum of the 9 dates is always

Choose one correct option.

  1. 9 times the centre date
  2. 3 times the centre date
  3. the centre date plus 72
  4. 9 times the top-left date

Solution.

Step 1Call the centre date $c$. The dates above and below it are $c - 7$ and $c + 7$, and those beside it are $c - 1$ and $c + 1$; the corners are $c - 8$, $c - 6$, $c + 6$ and $c + 8$.
Step 2Every number taken away is added back by the date opposite, so the sum is $9c$.
Answer(a) 9 times the centre date
Example 5 · Single correct

Hexagons are made from matchsticks in a row, each sharing a side with the next. Which expression gives the number of matchsticks for $h$ hexagons?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $6h$
  2. $6h - 1$
  3. $5h + 6$
  4. $5h + 1$

Solution.

Step 1The first hexagon needs 6 sticks, and each new one shares a side, so it needs 5 more.
Step 2Sticks $= 6 + 5(h - 1) = 5h + 1$. Check: 2 hexagons use 11.
Answer(d) $5h + 1$
