Example 3

Sameer has ₹500. He buys 3 notebooks at ₹45 each and a pen for ₹28. Write an expression for the money he has left, and find its value.

Solution.

Step 1 Money left $= 500 - (3 \times 45 + 28)$ both are taken away
Step 2 $= 500 - (135 + 28) = 500 - 163 = 337$
Answer $500 - (3 \times 45 + 28)$; ₹337
Example 4

Is $50 - (20 - 6)$ equal to $50 - 20 - 6$? Give a reason.

Solution.

Step 1 $50 - (20 - 6) = 50 - 14 = 36$
Step 2 $50 - 20 - 6 = 30 - 6 = 24$
Step 3 $-(20 - 6) = -20 + 6$ every sign inside changes
Answer No. $50 - (20 - 6) = 50 - 20 + 6 = 36$

Example 6

Find the value of $3(a - b)$ when $a = 2$ and $b = -5$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3(a - b) = 3 \times \big(2 - (-5)\big)$ $-5$ in a bracket
Step 2 $= 3 \times (2 + 5) = 3 \times 7$ taking away $-5$ adds 5
Answer 21
