Short answer
  1. The length of a rectangle is $2x + 3$ cm and its breadth is $x - 1$ cm. Write its perimeter in simplest form. Find the perimeter when $x = 6$.
  1. The perimeter of a triangle is $9x + 4$ cm. Two of its sides are $2x + 3$ cm and $4x - 1$ cm. Find the third side.
Long answer
  1. Look at the expression $5(2p - q) - 3(p - 2q) + 4$.
    1. Simplify it.
    2. Find the value of the expression, and of your simplest form, when $p = 2$ and $q = -1$. Check that the two values agree.
    3. Neha got $7p - 11q + 4$. Find her mistake.
  1. Meena is $m$ years old. Her brother is 4 years younger than her. Their father’s age is twice the sum of their ages. Write each of these in simplest form.
    1. the brother’s age
    2. the father’s age
    3. the father’s age in 5 years
    4. the sum of the three ages
    5. all three ages when $m = 12$
Case-based questions

  1. For a class trip to a science centre, a bus costs ₹2500 for the day, however many students go. Each student also pays ₹150 for a ticket and lunch. A bus holds at most 50 students.

    1. Write an expression for the total cost, in rupees, when $s$ students go in one bus.
    2. Find the total cost for 40 students.
    3. When more than 50 students go, a second bus is needed. Write an expression for the total cost with two buses.
    4. For the same number of students, how much more does the trip cost with two buses than with one?
    5. Find the total cost for 60 students.
