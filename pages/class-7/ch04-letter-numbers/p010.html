Example 9

Simplify $4(x + y) - y$.

Solution.

Step 1 $4(x + y) - y = 4x + 4y - y$ both terms inside
Step 2 $= 4x + 4y + (-y)$
Step 3 $4y$ and $-y$ are like terms: $= 4x + (4 - 1)y$ joined into one
Answer $4x + 3y$
