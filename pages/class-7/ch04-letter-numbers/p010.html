Example 11

What is the sum of the numbers in Fig. 4.5? Some of the numbers are not known, so they are shown as letter-numbers.

There are many ways to add them. Adding row by row gives $(4 \times 3) + (r + s) + (r + s) + (4 \times 3)$. Adding like terms together gives $(8 \times 3) + (r + r) + (s + s)$. Adding the top half and doubling gives $2 \times (4 \times 3 + r + s)$. These look different, but each one simplifies to $2r + 2s + 24$. Check it with values: when $r = 1$ and $s = 2$, every way gives 30.
