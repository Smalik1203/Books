Example 9

Simplify $4(x + y) - y$.

The 4 outside the bracket multiplies both terms inside it, $x$ and $y$. After that, $4y$ and $-y$ are like terms, so they can be joined into one term.

$4(x + y) - y = 4x + 4y - y = 4x + 4y + (-y) = 4x + (4 - 1)y = 4x + 3y$
