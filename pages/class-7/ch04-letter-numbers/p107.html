Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) Cost in rupees of $n$ pens at ₹4 each and one bag at ₹20(1) $3n + 1$
(Q) Matchsticks for $n$ hexagons in a row(2) $4n + 20$
(R) People at $n$ square tables in a row(3) $2n + 2$
(S) Matchsticks for $n$ squares in a row(4) $5n + 1$

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1

Solution.

Step 1The pens and bag cost $4n + 20$ rupees, and $n$ tables seat $2n + 2$.
Step 2Each new hexagon adds 5 sticks to one side already there: $5n + 1$. Each new square adds 3: $3n + 1$.
Answer(d) P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
