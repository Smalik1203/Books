Example 15

A garland repeats four flowers in the order marigold, rose, jasmine, lily. Number the flowers 1, 2, 3, 4, and so on. Where is each flower for the $n$th time? Which flower is at position 150?

Solution.

Step 1 Lily is at 4, 8, 12, …, so its $n$th time is at $4n$ the multiples of 4
Step 2 Marigold at $4n - 3$, rose at $4n - 2$, jasmine at $4n - 1$ 3, 2, 1 places before
Step 3 $150 = 4 \times 37 + 2 = 4 \times 38 - 2$ remainder 2
Step 4 $4n - 2$ with $n = 38$ is 150 a rose position
Answer marigold $4n - 3$, rose $4n - 2$, jasmine $4n - 1$, lily $4n$; a rose is at 150
