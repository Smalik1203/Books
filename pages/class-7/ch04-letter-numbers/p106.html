Example 12 · Numerical answer

A garland repeats four flowers in the order marigold, rose, jasmine, lily. Numbering the flowers 1, 2, 3, … , at what position is the 38th rose?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The roses are at positions 2, 6, 10, … , four apart, so the $n$th rose is at $4n - 2$.
Step 2For $n = 38$: $4 \times 38 - 2 = 150$.
Answer150
Example 13 · Numerical answer

Find the value of $5p - 2(p - 4)$ when $p = 7$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$5p - 2(p - 4) = 5p - 2p + 8 = 3p + 8$.
Step 2When $p = 7$: $21 + 8 = 29$.
Answer29
