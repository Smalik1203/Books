Example 13

Square tables are placed in a row, each touching the next. One person sits at each free side of a table, so one table on its own seats 4 people. How many people can sit at $n$ tables in a row? How many at 15 tables?

Solution.

Step 1 Along the two long sides: $2n$ people one each side
Step 2 People at $n$ tables $= 2n + 2$ one at each end
Step 3 $n = 1$: $2 \times 1 + 2 = 4$ check one table
Step 4 $n = 15$: $2 \times 15 + 2 = 32$
Answer $2n + 2$ people; 32 people at 15 tables

Example 14

A number machine takes in two numbers, $a$ and $b$, and does the same operations on them every time. The table shows four pairs and the outputs. Find the rule as an algebraic expression.

$a$4536
$b$2706
output108912

Solution.

Step 1 $a + b$ fails: $4 + 2 = 6$, not 10 one pair rules it out
Step 2 When $b = 0$, the output is $9 = 3 \times 3$, so $a$ is multiplied by 3 $b$ plays no part
Step 3 $3 \times 4 = 12$ and the output is 10, which is $12 - 2$, so $b$ is taken away
Step 4 $3 \times 5 - 7 = 8$ and $3 \times 6 - 6 = 12$ check the other pairs
Answer $3a - b$
