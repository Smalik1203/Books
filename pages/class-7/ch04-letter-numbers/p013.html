Describing patterns

Example 12

Somjit notices that the border of a saree repeats three designs, A, B and C, as in Fig. 4.7. Where does each design appear for the $n$th time?

Design C first appears at position 3, then at position 6, then at 9. It appears at the multiples of 3, so its $n$th appearance is at position $3n$. Design B appears at 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, and so on, which is always one position before C. So B appears for the $n$th time at position $3n - 1$, and in the same way A appears at position $3n - 2$.

Can we go the other way? Given a position, which design is there? If the position is a multiple of 3, it holds C. If it is 1 less than a multiple of 3, it holds B, and if it is 2 less, it holds A. The remainder on dividing the position by 3 tells us which case we are in. Use Table 4.11 to find the designs at positions 99, 122 and 148.
