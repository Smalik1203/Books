Example 9 · Multiple correct

Square tables are placed in a row, each touching the next, with one person at each free side. One table on its own seats 4. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. 4 tables seat 10 people.
  2. 15 tables seat 32 people.
  3. The number of people who can sit is always even.
  4. 10 tables seat exactly 20 people.

Solution.

Step 1Each table has 2 free sides along the row, and the two end tables have one more each: $n$ tables seat $2n + 2$.
Step 2$2 \times 4 + 2 = 10$ and $2 \times 15 + 2 = 32$. $2n + 2 = 2(n + 1)$ is always even. 10 tables seat 22, not 20.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Step $y$ of a matchstick pattern uses $2y + 1$ matchsticks. Which of these could be the number of matchsticks at some step?

Select all correct options.

  1. 151
  2. 150
  3. 99
  4. 2

Solution.

Step 1$2y$ is even, so $2y + 1$ is always odd: 150 and 2 are impossible.
Step 2151 is Step 75 and 99 is Step 49.
Answer(a), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

An auto-rickshaw charges ₹30 to start a ride and ₹15 for every kilometre. What is the fare, in rupees, for a ride of 12 km?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The fare for $k$ km is $30 + 15k$ rupees.
Step 2For $k = 12$: $30 + 15 \times 12 = 30 + 180 = 210$.
Answer210
