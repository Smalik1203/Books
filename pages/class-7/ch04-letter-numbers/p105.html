Example 10

A square of 9 dates, 3 rows by 3 columns, is chosen in a calendar. The sum of the 9 dates is always

  1. 9 times the centre date
  2. 3 times the centre date
  3. the centre date plus 72
  4. 9 times the top-left date

Solution.

Step 1 Rows: $a - 8$, $a - 7$, $a - 6$; $a - 1$, $a$, $a + 1$; $a + 6$, $a + 7$, $a + 8$ centre date $a$
Step 2 Sum $= 9a + (-8 - 7 - 6 - 1 + 1 + 6 + 7 + 8) = 9a$ the numbers cancel
Step 3 $3 + 4 + 5 + 10 + 11 + 12 + 17 + 18 + 19 = 99 = 9 \times 11$ check a calendar
Answer (a)
Example 11

Hexagons are made with matchsticks, in a row, each one sharing a side with the next. Which expression gives the number of matchsticks for $h$ hexagons?

  1. $6h$
  2. $6h - 1$
  3. $5h + 6$
  4. $5h + 1$

Solution.

Step 1 First hexagon: 6 matchsticks
Step 2 Each of the other $h - 1$ hexagons: 5 more one side is shared
Step 3 $6 + 5(h - 1) = 6 + 5h - 5 = 5h + 1$
Step 4 $h = 2$: $5 \times 2 + 1 = 11$ check by counting
Answer (d)
