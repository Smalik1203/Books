Example 11

What is the sum of the numbers in Fig. 4.11? Some of the numbers are not known, so they are shown as letter-numbers.

Solution.

There are many ways to add them.

Step 1 $(4 \times 3) + (r + s) + (r + s) + (4 \times 3)$ row by row
Step 2 $(8 \times 3) + (r + r) + (s + s)$ like terms together
Step 3 $2 \times (4 \times 3 + r + s)$ top half, doubled
Step 4 Each one simplifies to $2r + 2s + 24$ they look different
Step 5 At $r = 1$ and $s = 2$, every way gives 30 checked with values
Answer $2r + 2s + 24$
