Example 2

How many matchsticks does Parthiv need for 5 Ls? For 7 Ls? For 45 Ls? Write an expression for the number of matchsticks.

Solution.

Step 1 Five Ls need $5 \times 2$ matchsticks
Step 2 7 Ls need $7 \times 2$, and 45 Ls need $45 \times 2$ every time the same
Step 3 If $n$ stands for the number of Ls 2 times the Ls
Answer $2 \times n$

To use the expression, we replace $n$ by the number of Ls.

Ketaki makes coconut-jaggery laddus. A coconut costs ₹35 and 1 kg of jaggery costs ₹60. How much does she pay for 10 coconuts and 5 kg of jaggery?

Solution.

Step 1 $10 \times 35 + 5 \times 60$ coconuts and jaggery
Step 2 $= 350 + 300 = 650$
Answer She pays ₹650.
