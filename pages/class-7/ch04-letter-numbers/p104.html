Example 7

An auto-rickshaw charges ₹30 to start a ride and ₹15 for every kilometre. Write an expression for the fare, in rupees, for a ride of $k$ km. What is the fare for 8 km?

Solution.

Step 1 Charge for $k$ km $= 15k$ ₹15 each kilometre
Step 2 Fare $= 30 + 15k$ add the starting charge
Step 3 $30 + 15 \times 8 = 30 + 120 = 150$ put $k = 8$
Answer $30 + 15k$ rupees; ₹150 for 8 km

Example 8

$5x - (2x - 3)$ is equal to

  1. $3x + 3$
  2. $3x - 3$
  3. $7x + 3$
  4. $3x$

Solution.

Step 1 $5x - (2x - 3) = 5x - 2x + 3$ every sign inside changes
Step 2 $= 3x + 3$ join like terms
Answer (a)
Example 9

$3(2a - b) - 2(a - 2b)$ is equal to

  1. $4a - 7b$
  2. $4a - b$
  3. $4a + b$
  4. $8a - 7b$

Solution.

Step 1 $3(2a - b) = 6a - 3b$ 3 multiplies both terms
Step 2 $-2(a - 2b) = -2a + 4b$ $-2 \times (-2b) = +4b$
Step 3 $(6a - 2a) + (-3b + 4b) = 4a + b$ join like terms
Step 4 $a = 1$, $b = 1$: $3 \times 1 - 2 \times (-1) = 5$ and $4 \times 1 + 1 = 5$ check with values
Answer (c)
