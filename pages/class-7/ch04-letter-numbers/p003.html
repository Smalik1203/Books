Here $c$ is the number of coconuts and $j$ is the number of kilograms of jaggery. The total amount is the cost of the coconuts plus the cost of the jaggery:

$c \times 35$ $+$ $j \times 60$

The shaded parts are the two terms of the expression, just as in Chapter 2. Use the expression to find the amount for 7 coconuts and 4 kg of jaggery. When $c$ and $j$ change, the value of the expression changes too.

Example 4

Write an expression for the perimeter of a square, and use it for a square of side 7 cm.

Solution.

Step 1 If $q$ stands for the side length, perimeter $= 4 \times q$ 4 times the side
Step 2 For a side of 7 cm, $4 \times 7 = 28$
Answer $4 \times q$; the perimeter is 28 cm.
