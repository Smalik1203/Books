Example 4

Write an expression for the perimeter of a square, and use it for a square of side 7 cm.

The perimeter of a square is 4 times the length of its side. If $q$ stands for the side length, the perimeter is $4 \times q$. For a side of 7 cm, the perimeter is $4 \times 7 = 28$ cm.

Letter-numbers let us write a general relationship in a few symbols. A relationship written in this way is often called a formula.
