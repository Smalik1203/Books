Like terms

Terms with the same letter-numbers, such as $5c$, $3c$ and $10c$, or $12n$ and $-4n$, are like terms. Terms with different letter-numbers, such as $18c$ and $11d$, are unlike terms. Like terms can be added into a single term. Unlike terms cannot.

Example 7

A shop rents out chairs for ₹40 each and tables for ₹75 each for a day. When they are returned, the shopkeeper gives back ₹6 for each chair and ₹10 for each table. Write an expression for the amount paid in all, if $x$ chairs and $y$ tables are rented.

The amount paid at the start is $40x + 75y$ rupees, and the amount given back is $6x + 10y$ rupees. So the amount paid in all is $(40x + 75y) - (6x + 10y)$. To simplify it, remove the brackets, changing the sign of each term after the minus sign, and then group the like terms.

$(40x + 75y) - (6x + 10y) = 40x + 75y - 6x - 10y$

$= 40x + (-6x) + 75y + (-10y) = (40 - 6)x + (75 - 10)y = 34x + 65y$

So the amount paid in all is $34x + 65y$ rupees. For 3 chairs and 2 tables, that is ₹232.

Could we also have written the amount as $(40x + 75y) + (-6x - 10y)$? Explain why. What does $-6x - 10y$ stand for in the shop?

Example 8

Charu takes part in a quiz with three rounds. Her scores are $7p - 3q$, $8p - 4q$ and $6p - 2q$. Here $p$ is the score for a correct answer, and $q$ is the penalty for a wrong one. What is her total score?

In the first round she got 7 answers right and 3 wrong: each right answer adds $p$ to her score, and each wrong answer takes away $q$. If $p = 4$ and $q = 1$, her first-round score is $7 \times 4 - 3 \times 1 = 28 + (-3) = 25$. For the total, add the three scores. The terms can be added in any order, so the brackets can go.

$(7p - 3q) + (8p - 4q) + (6p - 2q) = 7p + 8p + 6p + (-3q) + (-4q) + (-2q)$

$= (7 + 8 + 6)p + \big(-(3 + 4 + 2)\big)q = 21p - 9q$
