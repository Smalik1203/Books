Example 15 · Matching

List I gives two sides of a triangle. Match each with the range, in List II, in which the third side must lie.

List IList II
(P) 4 cm and 7 cm(1) between 8 cm and 12 cm
(Q) 5 cm and 5 cm(2) between 3 cm and 15 cm
(R) 6 cm and 9 cm(3) between 0 cm and 10 cm
(S) 2 cm and 10 cm(4) between 3 cm and 11 cm

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  3. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–3, R–2, S–4

Solution.

Step 1The third side is longer than the difference of the two sides and shorter than their sum.
Step 27 − 4 = 3 and 7 + 4 = 11; 5 − 5 = 0 and 10; 9 − 6 = 3 and 15; 10 − 2 = 8 and 12.
Answer(a) P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
