Problem 3

Two angles of a triangle are each $72^\circ$. The third angle is

  1. $36^\circ$
  2. $72^\circ$
  3. $108^\circ$
  4. $18^\circ$
Solution

third angle $= 180^\circ - 72^\circ - 72^\circ = 36^\circ$

The answer is (a). Option (b) makes all three angles equal, which would total $216^\circ$. Option (c) takes away only one angle, and (d) halves the answer.

Problem 4

In △ABC, side BC is extended to D. $\angle ACD = 130^\circ$ and $\angle A = 60^\circ$. Then $\angle B$ is

  1. $50^\circ$
  2. $130^\circ$
  3. $120^\circ$
  4. $70^\circ$
Solution

$\angle ACB$ makes a straight angle with the exterior angle. Then use the angle sum.

$\angle ACB = 180^\circ - 130^\circ = 50^\circ$

$\angle B = 180^\circ - 60^\circ - 50^\circ = 70^\circ$

The answer is (d). Check: $60^\circ + 70^\circ = 130^\circ$, the exterior angle. Option (a) is $\angle ACB$, one step short. Option (b) is the exterior angle itself. Option (c) takes only $\angle A$ from $180^\circ$.

Problem 5

Two sides of a triangle are 5 cm and 8 cm. How many whole-number lengths, in cm, can the third side have?

  1. 9
  2. 13
  3. 3
  4. 8
Solution

The third side must be less than $5 + 8 = 13$ cm and more than $8 - 5 = 3$ cm.

possible lengths: 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 cm

The answer is (a). Options (b) and (c) are the two limits, which are not allowed. Option (d) leaves out 12 cm.
