Example 4

Can bamboo strips of 20 cm, 35 cm and 60 cm make a triangle? Can 20 cm, 35 cm and 50 cm?

Solution.

Step 1 $60 \gt 20 + 35 = 55$ no triangle
Step 2 $50 \lt 20 + 35 = 55$ a triangle
Answer No with the 60 cm strip; yes with the 50 cm strip
Example 5

A house, a school and a bus stop are not in one line. The house is 3 km from the school, which is 5 km from the bus stop. Can the house be 9 km from the bus stop? Can it be 7 km?

Solution.

Step 1 $3 + 5 = 8$ km the other two sides
Step 2 $9 \gt 8$ no triangle
Step 3 $7 \lt 8$, and 7 km is the longest side a triangle
Answer It cannot be 9 km; it can be 7 km.
Example 6

The sides of a triangle add up to 20 cm. Can one side be 10 cm? Can one be 11 cm?

Solution.

Step 1 $20 - 10 = 10$, and 10 is not less than 10 no triangle
Step 2 $20 - 11 = 9$, and $11 \gt 9$ no triangle
Answer No to both: every side must be less than 10 cm, half of 20 cm.

Example 7

Construct △PQR with QR $=$ 6 cm, $\angle Q = 60^\circ$ and PQ $=$ 4.5 cm. Measure PR.

Solution.

Step 1 QR $=$ 6 cm; $\angle Q = 60^\circ$ at Q, between QR and PQ the included angle
Step 2 PQ $=$ 4.5 cm on the other arm; join PR mark P
Answer PR is about 5.4 cm
