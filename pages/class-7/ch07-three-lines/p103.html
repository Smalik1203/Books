Example 4 · Single correct

Two sides of a triangle are 5 cm and 9 cm. Which of these can be the third side?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 3 cm
  2. 4 cm
  3. 14 cm
  4. 8 cm

Solution.

Step 1The third side must be more than 9 − 5 = 4 cm, or 5 + (third side) would not exceed 9.
Step 2It must be less than 5 + 9 = 14 cm. Of the options, only 8 cm lies between 4 cm and 14 cm.
Answer(d) 8 cm
Example 5 · Single correct

The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. What is the largest angle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 40°
  2. 60°
  3. 80°
  4. 90°

Solution.

Step 12 + 3 + 4 = 9 parts make 180°, so one part is 20°.
Step 2The largest angle is 4 × 20° = 80°.
Answer(c) 80°
Example 6 · Single correct

Two angles of a triangle are 25° and 40°. What kind of triangle is it?

Choose one correct option.

  1. obtuse-angled
  2. acute-angled
  3. right-angled
  4. equilateral

Solution.

Step 1The third angle is 180° − 25° − 40° = 115°.
Step 2115° is more than 90°, so the triangle is obtuse-angled.
Answer(a) obtuse-angled
