When do the arms meet?

Is there a triangle for every pair of angles and every included side? If both angles are right angles, or larger, the arms at A and B go straight up or lean away from each other, and they never meet.

Suppose $\angle A = 40^\circ$, and call its arm $l$. How far must the arm at B lean to the right, so that it never meets $l$? Of all the arms that miss $l$, the one leaning least is the one parallel to $l$; call it $m$ (Fig. 7.13). AB is a transversal of the parallel lines $l$ and $m$, and interior angles on the same side of a transversal add up to $180^\circ$. So $\angle B = 180^\circ - 40^\circ = 140^\circ$.
