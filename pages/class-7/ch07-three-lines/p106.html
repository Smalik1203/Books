Example 13 · Numerical answer

In a triangle, one angle is twice the smallest angle, and the other is three times the smallest. Find the largest angle, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 11 + 2 + 3 = 6 parts make 180°, so the smallest angle is 30°.
Step 2The largest is 3 × 30° = 90°.
Answer90

(R) Two angles are 60° and 60°(3) right-angled

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–1, Q–3, R–4, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2

Step 1The third angles are 90°, 115°, 60° and 60°.
