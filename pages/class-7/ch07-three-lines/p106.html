Example 14

Two angles of a triangle are $25^\circ$ and $40^\circ$. Name the triangle by its angles.

Solution.

Step 1 third angle $= 180^\circ - 25^\circ - 40^\circ = 115^\circ$ angle sum
Step 2 $115^\circ \gt 90^\circ$ an obtuse angle
Answer $115^\circ$; the triangle is obtuse-angled.
