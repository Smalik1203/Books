7.3Triangles From Sides and Angles

So far every construction started from three lengths. In the constructions below, angles are given in place of some of the lengths.

Two sides and the angle between them

The angle between two given sides is called the included angle. Construct △ABC with AB $=$ 5 cm, AC $=$ 4 cm and $\angle A = 45^\circ$.

Step 1: Draw AB $=$ 5 cm.

Step 2: At A, draw an angle of $45^\circ$ with AB as one arm (Fig. 7.11 (i)).

Step 3: Mark C on the other arm, so that AC $=$ 4 cm.

Step 4: Join BC (Fig. 7.11 (ii)).
