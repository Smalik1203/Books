Example 1 · Single correct

Which set of lengths can be the sides of a triangle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 3 cm, 5 cm, 9 cm
  2. 4 cm, 4 cm, 8 cm
  3. 6 cm, 7 cm, 12 cm
  4. 2 cm, 4 cm, 7 cm

Solution.

Step 1The longest side must be less than the sum of the other two.
Step 29 > 3 + 5, 8 = 4 + 4 and 7 > 2 + 4 fail; 12 < 6 + 7 = 13 works.
Answer(c) 6 cm, 7 cm, 12 cm
Example 2 · Single correct

Two angles of a triangle are 48° and 67°. What is the third angle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 115°
  2. 65°
  3. 75°
  4. 113°

Solution.

Step 148° + 67° = 115°.
Step 2The angles of a triangle add to 180°: 180° − 115° = 65°.
Answer(b) 65°
Example 3 · Single correct

In △ABC, side BC is extended to D. If ∠ACD = 130° and ∠A = 60°, what is ∠B?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 50°
  2. 130°
  3. 120°
  4. 70°

Solution.

Step 1∠ACB and ∠ACD form a linear pair: ∠ACB = 180° − 130° = 50°.
Step 2Angle sum: ∠B = 180° − 60° − 50° = 70°.
Answer(d) 70°
