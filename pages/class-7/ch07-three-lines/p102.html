Type 1 · Constructing a triangle from its sides

Example 1

Construct △LMN with LM $=$ 7 cm, LN $=$ 4 cm and MN $=$ 5 cm.

Solution.

Step 1 $7 \lt 4 + 5 = 9$ the triangle exists
Step 2 LM $=$ 7 cm draw the base
Step 3 arc with centre L, radius 4 cm N is 4 cm from L
Step 4 arc with centre M, radius 5 cm, crossing it at N N is 5 cm from M
Answer join LN and MN to complete △LMN
Example 2

A paper flag is a triangle. Its top edge is 8 cm long, and its other two edges are 12 cm each. Construct the flag full size, and name the kind of triangle it is.

Solution.

Step 1 $12 \lt 8 + 12 = 20$ the triangle exists
Step 2 AB $=$ 8 cm draw the top edge
Step 3 arcs of radius 12 cm from A and B cross at C; join AC, BC
Answer AC $=$ BC $=$ 12 cm, so the flag is an isosceles triangle

Type 2 · Can three lengths make a triangle?

Example 3

Which set of lengths can be the sides of a triangle?

  1. 3 cm, 5 cm, 9 cm
  2. 4 cm, 4 cm, 8 cm
  3. 6 cm, 7 cm, 12 cm
  4. 2 cm, 4 cm, 7 cm

Solution.

Step 1 (a) $9 \gt 3 + 5$   (b) $8 = 4 + 4$   (d) $7 \gt 2 + 4$ longest not shorter
Step 2 (c) $12 \lt 6 + 7 = 13$ longest is shorter
Answer (c)
