Example 8

Draw a triangular roof with a 6 cm base and $30^\circ$ at each end, and measure its sloping sides.

Solution.

Step 1 $30^\circ + 30^\circ = 60^\circ \lt 180^\circ$ the arms will meet
Step 2 AB $=$ 6 cm the included side
Step 3 $30^\circ$ at A and $30^\circ$ at B, arms crossing at C
Answer AC and BC are each about 3.5 cm, so the sloping sides are equal.

Type 4 · The angle sum and exterior angles

Example 9

In △ABC, $\angle A = 64^\circ$ and $\angle B = 38^\circ$. Find $\angle C$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle A + \angle B + \angle C = 180^\circ$ angle sum
Step 2 $64^\circ + 38^\circ = 102^\circ$
Step 3 $\angle C = 180^\circ - 102^\circ = 78^\circ$
Answer $\angle C = 78^\circ$
Example 10

The ladder of a slide stands upright, at $90^\circ$ to the ground. The slide makes an angle of $35^\circ$ with the ground (Fig. 7.26). What angle does the slide make with the ladder at the top?

Solution.

Step 1 ladder, slide and ground make a triangle
Step 2 angle at the top $= 180^\circ - 90^\circ - 35^\circ = 55^\circ$ angle sum
Answer $55^\circ$
