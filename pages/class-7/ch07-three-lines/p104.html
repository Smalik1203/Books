Example 7 · Multiple correct

△ABC has AB = AC = 5 cm and BC = 6 cm, and AD is the altitude from A to BC. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. △ABC is isosceles.
  2. ∠B = ∠C
  3. △ABC is scalene.
  4. BD = DC = 3 cm

Solution.

Step 1Two sides are equal, so the triangle is isosceles and not scalene; the angles opposite the equal sides are equal.
Step 2Folding along AD puts AB on AC, so D is the midpoint of BC: BD = DC = 3 cm.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which sets of three angles can be the angles of a triangle?

Select all correct options.

  1. 60°, 60°, 60°
  2. 90°, 45°, 45°
  3. 100°, 50°, 40°
  4. 120°, 30°, 20°

Solution.

Step 160° + 60° + 60° = 180° and 90° + 45° + 45° = 180°.
Step 2100° + 50° + 40° = 190° and 120° + 30° + 20° = 170°, so neither adds to 180°.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

A house, a school and a bus stop are not in one line. The house is 3 km from the school, and the school is 5 km from the bus stop. Which could be the distance from the house to the bus stop?

Select all correct options.

  1. 9 km
  2. 7 km
  3. 2 km
  4. 4 km

Solution.

Step 1The three places make a triangle with sides 3 km, 5 km and the unknown distance.
Step 2It must be more than 5 − 3 = 2 km and less than 5 + 3 = 8 km: 7 km and 4 km are possible; 9 km and 2 km are not.
Answer(b), (d)
