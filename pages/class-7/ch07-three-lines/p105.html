Example 11

Two angles of a triangle are $48^\circ$ and $67^\circ$. The third angle is

  1. $115^\circ$
  2. $65^\circ$
  3. $75^\circ$
  4. $113^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1 $48^\circ + 67^\circ = 115^\circ$ the two given angles
Step 2 $180^\circ - 115^\circ = 65^\circ$ angle sum
Answer (b)
Example 12

In △ABC, side BC is extended to D. $\angle ACD = 130^\circ$ and $\angle A = 60^\circ$. Then $\angle B$ is

  1. $50^\circ$
  2. $130^\circ$
  3. $120^\circ$
  4. $70^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle ACB = 180^\circ - 130^\circ = 50^\circ$ straight angle at C
Step 2 $\angle B = 180^\circ - 60^\circ - 50^\circ = 70^\circ$ angle sum
Answer (d)

Type 5 · Altitudes and types of triangles

Example 13

Construct △ABC with BC $=$ 6 cm, AB $=$ AC $=$ 5 cm, and the altitude AD. Measure AD, BD and DC.

Solution.

Step 1 $6 \lt 5 + 5 = 10$; construct from the three sides, base BC the triangle exists
Step 2 set square on a ruler along BC, edge through A; draw AD a true right angle
Answer AD $=$ 4 cm, BD $=$ DC $=$ 3 cm: the altitude meets BC at its middle point
