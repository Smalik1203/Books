Example 10 · Multiple correct

Which statements are true for every triangle?

Select all correct options.

  1. At least two of its angles are acute.
  2. It can have two right angles.
  3. If it is equilateral, each angle is 60°.
  4. An exterior angle equals the sum of the two interior angles opposite it.

Solution.

Step 1Two angles of 90° or more would already make 180° or more, so at most one angle is not acute; the same shows (b) is false.
Step 2Equal angles adding to 180° are 60° each. An exterior angle and its neighbour add to 180°, and so do all three interior angles, so the exterior angle equals the other two.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

The ladder of a slide stands upright, at 90° to the ground. The slide makes an angle of 35° with the ground. What angle, in degrees, does the slide make with the ladder at the top?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The ladder, the slide and the ground make a triangle with angles 90° and 35°.
Step 2Third angle = 180° − 90° − 35° = 55°.
Answer55
