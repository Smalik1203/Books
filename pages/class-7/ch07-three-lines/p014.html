7.5Types of Triangles

We have met equilateral, isosceles and right-angled triangles. The first two were named by comparing their sides, and Fig. 7.24 shows a third kind. Sides of equal length are marked with the same number of ticks.

Triangles by their sides

An equilateral triangle has all three sides equal. An isosceles triangle has two equal sides. A scalene triangle has three sides of different lengths.

Could triangles also be sorted by comparing their angles, and would that sorting match the sorting by sides? We will return to this in a later chapter. Right-angled triangles were named by their angles, and sorting by angles gives three kinds.

Triangles by their angles

A right-angled triangle has one right angle. An obtuse-angled triangle has one obtuse angle. An acute-angled triangle has all three of its angles acute.
