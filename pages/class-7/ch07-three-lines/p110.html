Case-based questions

  1. Three villages, Amli, Bamni and Chandur, are joined by straight roads, and they are not on one straight line. Amli is 12 km from Bamni, and Bamni is 9 km from Chandur.

    1. Can the road from Amli to Chandur be 25 km long? Give a reason.
    2. That road is a whole number of kilometres long. How many lengths are possible?
    3. The road from Amli to Chandur is in fact 12 km long. Name the triangle by its sides.
    4. A road from Bamni meets the Amli–Chandur road at right angles. What is it called?

  1. A solar panel stands on a flat roof. The panel makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the roof, and an upright post joins its top edge to the roof, at $90^\circ$ to the roof. The panel, the post and the roof make a triangle.

    1. Find the angle between the panel and the post.
    2. Name the triangle by its angles.
    3. The line of the roof is extended past the foot of the panel. Find the exterior angle there, and check that it is the sum of its two interior opposite angles.
    4. Could the panel make an angle of $95^\circ$ with the roof while the post stays upright? Give a reason.
