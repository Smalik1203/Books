Example 10

Which expression is equal to $250 - (80 - 30)$?

  1. $250 - 80 - 30$
  2. $250 - 80 + 30$
  3. $250 + 80 - 30$
  4. $250 + 80 + 30$

Solution.

Step 1 $250 - (80 - 30) = 250 - 80 + 30$ minus in front: signs change
Step 2 $250 - 50 = 200$ and $250 - 80 + 30 = 200$ check
Answer (b)

Example 11

Work out $102 \times 45$ quickly.

Solution.

Step 1 $102 \times 45 = (100 + 2) \times 45$ split 102
Step 2 $= 100 \times 45 + 2 \times 45 = 4500 + 90$ multiple of a sum
Answer 4590
Example 12

Tinku says $27 \times 99 = 27 \times 100 - 99$. The correct value of $27 \times 99$ is

Example 13

Rehana knows that $25 \times 16 = 400$. Without multiplying from the start, find $25 \times 17$ and $24 \times 16$.

Solution.

Step 1 $25 \times 17 = 25 \times (16 + 1) = 400 + 25 = 425$ one more 25
Step 2 $24 \times 16 = (25 - 1) \times 16 = 400 - 16 = 384$ one fewer 16
Answer 425 and 384
