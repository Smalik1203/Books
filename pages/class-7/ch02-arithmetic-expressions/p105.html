Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which expressions have the value 0?

Select all correct options.

  1. 45 − (20 + 25)
  2. 45 − 20 − 25
  3. 7 × 8 − 8 × 7
  4. 12 − (5 − 7)

Solution.

Step 145 − (20 + 25) = 45 − 45 = 0, and 45 − 20 − 25 = 25 − 25 = 0.
Step 27 × 8 and 8 × 7 are both 56, so their difference is 0. But 12 − (5 − 7) = 12 − 5 + 7 = 14.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Which of these are terms of 25 − 4 × 3 + 8 ÷ 2?

Select all correct options.

  1. 25
  2. −4 × 3
  3. 3 + 8
  4. 8 ÷ 2

Solution.

Step 1Writing subtraction as adding the opposite: 25 − 4 × 3 + 8 ÷ 2 = 25 + (−4 × 3) + 8 ÷ 2.
Step 2The terms are 25, −4 × 3 and 8 ÷ 2. 3 + 8 is not a term, because × and ÷ bind 3 and 8 to their own terms.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

Find the value of 347 − 189 + 153 − 111, grouping the terms in an easy way.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Group the positive terms: 347 + 153 = 500.
Step 2Group the negative terms: (−189) + (−111) = −300. The value is 500 + (−300) = 200.
