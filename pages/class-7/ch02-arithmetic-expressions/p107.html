Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) 50 − (20 + 5)(1) 50 + 20 + 5
(Q) 50 − (20 − 5)(2) 50 − 20 − 5
(R) 50 + (20 − 5)(3) 50 + 20 − 5
(S) 50 − (−20 − 5)(4) 50 − 20 + 5

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1

Solution.

Step 1A plus sign before a bracket leaves the signs inside unchanged: 50 + (20 − 5) = 50 + 20 − 5.
Step 2A minus sign changes every sign inside: 50 − 20 − 5, 50 − 20 + 5 and 50 + 20 + 5.
Answer(d) P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
