Example 3 · Single correct

Tinku says that 27 × 99 = 27 × 100 − 99. What is the correct value of 27 × 99?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 2700
  2. 2727
  3. 2601
  4. 2673

Solution.

Step 127 × 99 = 27 × (100 − 1) = 27 × 100 − 27 × 1.
Step 22700 − 27 = 2673. Tinku took away 99 instead of 27.
Answer(d) 2673
Example 4 · Single correct

Which expression has the same value as 6 × 13 + 6 × 7?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 6 × (13 + 7)
  2. 6 × 13 + 7
  3. (6 + 6) × (13 + 7)
  4. 6 + 13 × 6 + 7

Solution.

Step 16 is a factor of both terms, so 6 × 13 + 6 × 7 = 6 × (13 + 7).
Step 2Both have the value 120. The others are 85, 240 and 91.
Answer(a) 6 × (13 + 7)
Example 5 · Single correct

Kiran had ₹500. He bought a book for ₹185 and a pen for ₹45, and then his aunt gave him ₹100. Which expression gives the money he has now?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 500 − 185 + 45 + 100
  2. 500 − (185 + 45) + 100
  3. 500 − (185 − 45) + 100
  4. 500 − 185 − 45 − 100

Solution.

Step 1He spends 185 + 45 = 230 rupees, which is taken away from 500; then 100 is added.
Step 2500 − (185 + 45) + 100 = 500 − 230 + 100 = ₹370.
Answer(b) 500 − (185 + 45) + 100
