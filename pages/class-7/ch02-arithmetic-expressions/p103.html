Type 2 · Brackets

Example 3

Find the value of $4 \times (12 - 5)$ and of $(4 \times 12) - 5$.

Solution.

Step 1 $4 \times (12 - 5) = 4 \times 7 = 28$ bracket first
Step 2 $(4 \times 12) - 5 = 48 - 5 = 43$ bracket first
Answer 28 and 43
Example 4

Kiran had ₹500. He bought a book for ₹185 and a pen for ₹45. Then his aunt gave him ₹100. Which expression gives the money he has now?

  1. $500 - 185 + 45 + 100$
  2. $500 - (185 + 45) + 100$
  3. $500 - (185 - 45) + 100$
  4. $500 - 185 - 45 - 100$

Solution.

Step 1 $185 + 45 = 230$ book and pen
Step 2 $500 - (185 + 45)$ both come off 500
Step 3 $500 - (185 + 45) + 100 = 500 - 230 + 100 = 370$ ₹100 is added
Answer (b)

Type 3 · Terms and the order of working

Example 5

Write the terms of $25 - 4 \times 3 + 8 \div 2$, and find its value.

Solution.

Step 1 $25 - 4 \times 3 + 8 \div 2 = 25 + (-4 \times 3) + 8 \div 2$ subtract as add
Step 2 $= 25 + (-12) + 4$ each term first
Step 3 $= 17$ add the terms
Answer terms $25$, $-4 \times 3$ and $8 \div 2$; value 17
