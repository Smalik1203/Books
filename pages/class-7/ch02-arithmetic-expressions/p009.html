Example 13

Write $500 - (250 - 100)$ without brackets.

The value is $500 - 150 = 350$. If we subtract the whole 250 from 500, we have taken away 100 too many, so we must add that 100 back.

$500 - (250 - 100) = 500 - 250 + 100$

Check that $500 - 250 - 100$ is not equal to 350. Again, removing the brackets after a minus sign changed the signs inside: 250 became $-250$, and $-100$ became $+100$.

Example 14

Hira has 28 rare coins in one bag and 35 in another. She gives her friend 10 coins from the second bag. How many coins does she have now?

She has $28 + (35 - 10)$ coins, which is $28 + (35 + (-10))$. The terms can be added in any order, so this is $28 + 35 + (-10) = 28 + 35 - 10 = 53$.

When the brackets do not have a minus sign in front of them, the terms inside keep their signs. You do not need to memorise when signs change and when they do not. Think about what the expression means, and you can work it out each time.
