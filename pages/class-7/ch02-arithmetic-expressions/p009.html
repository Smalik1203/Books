Removing brackets — I

Let us find the value of $200 - (40 + 3)$. We could work out the brackets first and subtract 43 from 200. But it is easier to subtract 40 from 200, which gives 160, and then subtract 3, which gives 157. What we did was $200 - 40 - 3$. We did not do $200 - 40 + 3$. So:

$200 - (40 + 3) = 200 - 40 - 3$

Example 12

Irfan bought biscuits for ₹15 and toor dal for ₹56, and paid with ₹100. We found his change as $100 - (15 + 56) = 29$. Find it another way.

Take away the biscuits first: $100 - 15 = 85$. That is what the shopkeeper would owe him for the biscuits alone. The toor dal also has to come out of that: $85 - 56 = 29$. So $100 - (15 + 56) = 100 - 15 - 56$.

When we removed brackets that had a minus sign in front of them, the terms inside changed sign. Look at 40 and 3 in the first case, and at 15 and 56 in Example 12.

Example 13

Write $500 - (250 - 100)$ without brackets.

The value is $500 - 150 = 350$. If we subtract the whole 250 from 500, we have taken away 100 too many, so we must add that 100 back.

$500 - (250 - 100) = 500 - 250 + 100$

Check that $500 - 250 - 100$ is not equal to 350. Again, removing the brackets after a minus sign changed the signs inside: 250 became $-250$, and $-100$ became $+100$.
