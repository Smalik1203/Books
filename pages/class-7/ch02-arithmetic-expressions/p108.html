Why the other options are wrong

B3 $99 \times 8 = 100 \times 8 - 1 \times 8 = 800 - 8 = 792$, so A is true. R takes away 1 instead of 8, and gives 799, so R is false. The answer is (c).
B6 $8 \times 7 + 3 = 59$ and $8 \times (7 + 3) = 80$, so A is false. R is true, and it is the reason the bracketed expression is larger. The answer is (d).
B8 Reorder the terms: $36 + (-36) + 64 + (-64) = 0 + 0 = 0$. A is true, and R is exactly what lets us reorder. The answer is (a).
C3 Each full day and night, the snail gains 2 cm. After 7 days and nights it is at 14 cm, and on day 8 it climbs only to 19 cm. After 8 days and nights it is at 16 cm, and on day 9 it climbs past 20 cm. The answer is (c). Option (a) forgets that the last climb needs no slide.
