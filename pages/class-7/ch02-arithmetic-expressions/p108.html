Assertion and reason

In Questions 17 to 21, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $36 + 64 - 36 - 64 = 0$.

    Reason (R): The terms of an expression can be added in any order.

  1. Assertion (A): $-8 + 23 = 23 - 8$.

    Reason (R): The inverse of $-8$ is 8.

  1. Assertion (A): $72 - (30 - 12) = 72 - 30 - 12$.

    Reason (R): When brackets with a minus sign in front of them are removed, each term inside changes its sign.

  1. Assertion (A): $99 \times 8 = 792$.

    Reason (R): $99 \times 8 = 100 \times 8 - 1$.

  1. Assertion (A): $8 \times 7 + 3$ is greater than $8 \times (7 + 3)$.

    Reason (R): In $8 \times (7 + 3)$, both 7 and 3 are multiplied by 8.
