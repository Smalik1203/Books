Example 12 · Numerical answer

Rehana knows that 25 × 16 = 400. Find 24 × 17.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 124 × 17 = (25 − 1) × 17 = 25 × 17 − 17.
Step 225 × 17 = 25 × 16 + 25 = 425, so 24 × 17 = 425 − 17 = 408.

Example 14 · Matching

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) 4 × (12 − 5)(1) 43
(Q) 4 × 12 − 5(2) 80
(R) 4 + 12 × 5(3) 28
(S) (4 + 12) × 5(4) 64

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–3, R–4, S–2
  2. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Brackets first: 4 × 7 = 28 and 16 × 5 = 80.
Step 2Without brackets, find each term first: 48 − 5 = 43 and 4 + 60 = 64.
Answer(b) P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
