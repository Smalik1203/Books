Example 14

Which expression has the same value as $6 \times 13 + 6 \times 7$?

  1. $6 \times 13 + 7$
  2. $(6 + 6) \times (13 + 7)$
  3. $6 \times (13 + 7)$
  4. $6 + 13 \times 6 + 7$

Solution.

Step 1 $6 \times 13 + 6 \times 7 = 6 \times (13 + 7)$ 6 in both terms
Step 2 $= 6 \times 20 = 120$
Answer (c)
