To reach 0 we need to take away 2 more, so $+1$ must become $-1$. Put 2 and 1 in a bracket after a minus sign: $8 - 5 - (2 + 1) = 3 - 3 = 0$. In both cases we looked at which sign had to change, and put the bracket there.

Pens cost ₹12 and notebooks cost ₹38. Asha buys 7 pens and 7 notebooks. What does she pay?

The long way is $7 \times 12 + 7 \times 38 = 84 + 266$. But both terms are 7 times something, so we can use the distributive property in reverse.

$7 \times 12 + 7 \times 38 = 7 \times (12 + 38) = 7 \times 50 = 350$

She pays ₹350. It also makes sense in the shop: 7 pens and 7 notebooks are 7 sets of one pen and one notebook, and one set costs ₹50. Grouping the terms first turned two multiplications into a single easy one.

Which expression is equal to 250 − (80 − 30)?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 250 − 80 − 30
  2. 250 + 80 − 30
  3. 250 − 80 + 30
  4. 250 + 80 + 30

Solution.

Step 1A minus sign in front of a bracket changes the sign of every term inside it: 250 − (80 − 30) = 250 − 80 + 30.
Step 2Check: 250 − 50 = 200 and 250 − 80 + 30 = 200.
Answer(c) 250 − 80 + 30
