Example 3

Which is greater, $113 - 25$ or $112 - 24$?

Solution.

Step 1 This time Raja has 113 marbles and loses 25, so he has $113 - 25$ left
Step 2 Joy has 112 marbles and loses 24, so he has $112 - 24$ left
Step 3 Raja starts with 1 more, but he also loses 1 more both change alike
Step 4 So they are left with the same number
Answer $113 - 25 = 112 - 24$

2.2Reading Longer Expressions

When an expression has no story around it, it can sometimes be read in more than one way. We need rules that say exactly how to work it out.

Language has the same problem. Read these two notices at a school gate: “No, parking here” and “No parking here”. One comma changes the meaning completely. In mathematics, brackets and terms do the job that commas do in a sentence.
