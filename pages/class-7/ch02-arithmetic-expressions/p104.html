Example 6

The value of $40 - 4 \times 6 + 12$ is

  1. 228
  2. 28
  3. 4
  4. 52

Solution.

Step 1 $40 - 4 \times 6 + 12 = 40 + (-4 \times 6) + 12$ find the terms
Step 2 $= 40 + (-24) + 12 = 28$ each term, then add
Answer (b)

Example 7

Find $136 + (-58) + 64 + (-42)$ in an easy way.

Solution.

Step 1 $= (136 + 64) + ((-58) + (-42))$ group round numbers
Step 2 $= 200 + (-100)$
Answer 100
Example 8

Find $487 - 256 + 113 - 44$ in an easy way.

Solution.

Step 1 $487 + 113 = 600$ terms 487 and 113
Step 2 $(-256) + (-44) = -300$ terms −256 and −44
Answer $600 + (-300) = 300$

Type 5 · Removing brackets

Example 9

Write $64 - (-18 + 30)$ without brackets, and find its value.

Solution.

Step 1 $64 - (-18 + 30) = 64 + 18 - 30$ every sign inside changes
Step 2 $= 82 - 30 = 52$ add the terms
Answer $64 + 18 - 30$; value 52
