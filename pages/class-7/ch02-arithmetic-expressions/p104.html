Example 6 · Single correct

What is the value of 100 − 3 × (20 − 4 × 3) + 6?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 34
  2. 70
  3. 82
  4. 782

Solution.

Step 1Inside the bracket: 20 − 4 × 3 = 20 − 12 = 8.
Step 2The terms are then 100, −3 × 8 and 6: 100 − 24 + 6 = 82.
Answer(c) 82
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which expressions have the same value as 64 − (−18 + 30)?

Select all correct options.

  1. 64 + 18 − 30
  2. 64 − 18 + 30
  3. 64 − 12
  4. 64 + (−30) + 18

Solution.

Step 1Removing the bracket changes each sign inside it: 64 − (−18 + 30) = 64 + 18 − 30 = 52.
Step 264 − 12 = 52 and 64 + (−30) + 18 = 52, but 64 − 18 + 30 = 76.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which expressions are equal to 102 × 45?

Select all correct options.

  1. 100 × 45 + 2 × 45
  2. 100 × 45 + 2
  3. 103 × 45 − 45
  4. 102 × 40 + 5

Solution.

Step 1102 × 45 = (100 + 2) × 45 = 4500 + 90 = 4590.
Step 2103 × 45 − 45 = 4635 − 45 = 4590. The others are 4502 and 4085.
Answer(a), (c)
