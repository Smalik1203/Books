End-of-Chapter Exercises
  1. For each situation, write an expression and find its value.
    1. The district market in Begur is open all seven days of the week. Every day, Rahim brings 9 kg of mangoes from his orchard and Shyam brings 11 kg from his. How many kilograms of mangoes do they bring in a week?
    2. Binu earns ₹20,000 a month. Every month she spends ₹5000 on rent, ₹5000 on food and ₹2000 on other things. How much does she save in a year?
    3. A snail climbs 3 cm up a pole during the day, and slides 2 cm back down while it sleeps at night. The pole is 10 cm high, and a tasty treat is at the top. On which day does the snail reach the treat?
  1. Melvin reads one story of two pages every day, except on Tuesdays and Saturdays. How many stories does he read in 8 weeks? Which of these expressions describe the situation?
    1. $5 \times 2 \times 8$
    2. $(7 - 2) \times 8$
    3. $8 \times 7$
    4. $7 \times 2 \times 8$
    5. $7 \times 5 - 2$
    6. $(7 + 2) \times 8$
    7. $7 \times 8 - 2 \times 8$
    8. $(7 - 5) \times 8$
