Expression engineer

With three 3s, the four operations and brackets, we can make many values: $(3 + 3) \div 3 = 2$, $3 + 3 - 3 = 3$ and $3 \times 3 + 3 = 12$ are three of them.

  1. Use four 4s to make every value from 1 to 20.
  1. Use each of the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 exactly once, in any order, to make as many of the values from $-10$ to 10 as you can.
  1. Use each of the digits 0 to 9 exactly once to make an expression whose value is 100.
  1. Make up a challenge like these, and try it on a classmate.
