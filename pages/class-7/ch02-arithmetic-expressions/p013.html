The distributive property

A multiple of a sum is the sum of the multiples, and a multiple of a difference is the difference of the multiples: $2 \times (43 + 24) = 2 \times 43 + 2 \times 24$.

Changing a number in a product

The distributive property is also useful when we already know one product and need another one close to it. We do not have to multiply again from the start. We look at how the new number differs from the old one, and change only that part. In Example 17 a few more copies are added to a product we know. In Example 18 a hard product is turned into an easy one, and a few copies are taken away.

Example 17

We know that $53 \times 18 = 954$. Find $63 \times 18$.

Solution.

Step 1 $63 \times 18$ is 63 times 18, and 63 is $53 + 10$
Step 2 $63 \times 18 = (53 + 10) \times 18$ a multiple of a sum
Answer $= 53 \times 18 + 10 \times 18 = 954 + 180 = 1134$
Example 18

Find a quick way to work out $97 \times 25$.

Solution.

Step 1 97 is $100 - 3$, so $97 \times 25$ is the difference of 100 times 25 and 3 times 25
Step 2 $97 \times 25 = (100 - 3) \times 25$ a multiple of a difference
Answer $= 100 \times 25 - 3 \times 25 = 2500 - 75 = 2425$

Use this method to find $95 \times 8$, $104 \times 15$ and $49 \times 50$.
