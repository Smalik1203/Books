C5 6 is always taken away, because nothing comes before it except 12. The largest value comes when 4 and 2 are both added, which happens in $12 - (6 - 4 - 2) = 12 - 0 = 12$. The answer is (b). Option (d) would need 6 to be added, which no bracket can do.
C6 $47 \times 24 - 47 = 47 \times 24 - 47 \times 1 = 47 \times (24 - 1) = 47 \times 23 = 1081$. The answer is (a). Option (b) is $47 \times 24$ itself, and (c) takes away 47 once too often.

Three things from this section are worth keeping. A bracket after a minus sign changes the sign of every term inside it, so choose where to put a bracket by asking which sign has to change. A product that is close to a round number can be split, as with $99 = 100 - 1$. And when every term is a multiple of the same number, that number can be taken outside a bracket.
