Long answer
  1. Sameer says $48 - 12 + 8 = 28$, because he adds 12 and 8 first. Reema says it is 44.
    1. Who is right? Explain your answer using the terms of the expression.
    2. Put brackets into $48 - 12 + 8$ so that its value is Sameer’s answer.
    3. Write $48 - 12 + 8$ with its terms in two other orders, keeping its value.
  1. Look at the expression $120 - (45 - 15) + 3 \times (20 - 8)$.
    1. Find its value by working out the brackets first.
    2. Remove all the brackets, and write the expression as a sum of five terms.
    3. Add those five terms in an easy order, and check your answer to (a).
Case-based questions

  1. At the school canteen, a dosa costs ₹30, a plate of idli costs ₹15 and a cup of tea costs ₹10. Four friends order 4 dosas, 2 plates of idli and 4 cups of tea.

    1. Write an expression for the bill, and name its terms.
    2. Find the bill.
    3. Show that $4 \times 30 + 4 \times 10 = 4 \times (30 + 10)$, and use it to find what the dosas and the tea cost together.
    4. They pay with a ₹500 note. Write an expression with brackets for their change, and find it.
    5. The next day a dosa costs ₹32. Without working out the whole bill again, find the new bill for the same order.

  1. In a building, the ground floor is floor 0, and the floors below the ground are numbered $-1$, $-2$ and so on. A lift starts at the ground floor. It goes up 7 floors, then down 3 floors, then down 6 floors, and then up 4 floors.

    1. Write an expression for the floor where the lift stops, and name its terms.
    2. Evaluate the expression.
    3. Add the terms in a different order to check your answer.
    4. On which floor was the lift after its first three moves?
    5. Riya writes $7 - (3 + 6) + 4$ for the same trip. Is her expression equal to yours? Give a reason.
