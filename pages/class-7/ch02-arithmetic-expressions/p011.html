Example 14

Hira has 28 rare coins in one bag and 35 in another. She gives her friend 10 coins from the second bag. How many coins does she have now?

Solution.

Step 1 She has $28 + (35 - 10)$ coins both bags
Step 2 which is $28 + (35 + (-10))$ as a sum of terms
Step 3 The terms can be added in any order
Answer $28 + 35 + (-10) = 28 + 35 - 10 = 53$ coins

When the brackets do not have a minus sign in front of them, the terms inside keep their signs. You do not need to memorise when signs change and when they do not. Think about what the expression means, and you can work it out each time.
