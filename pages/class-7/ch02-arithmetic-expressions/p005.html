What are the terms of $83 - 14$? Subtracting a number is the same as adding its inverse, the number with the opposite sign. The inverse of 14 is $-14$, and the inverse of $-14$ is 14. So:

$83 - 14 = $ $83$ $+$ $(-14)$

The terms of $83 - 14$ are 83 and $-14$. Try a few examples of your own to check that writing a subtraction as an addition in this way never changes the value. Every subtraction in an expression is turned into an addition like this before we pick out the terms.

$-18 - 3 = $ $-18$ $+$ $(-3)$

$6 \times 5 + 3 = $ $6 \times 5$ $+$ $3$

$2 - 10 + 4 \times 6 = $ $2$ $+$ $(-10)$ $+$ $4 \times 6$

Notice that $6 \times 5$ and $4 \times 6$ are single terms, because there is no + sign inside them.
