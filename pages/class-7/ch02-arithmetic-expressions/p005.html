Swapping and grouping

We start with expressions that have only additions, after every subtraction has been turned into an addition. Does changing the order in which we add the terms change the value?

Example 6

Madhu flies a drone from the terrace of her house. The drone goes 6 m up, and then 4 m down. How high above the terrace is it now?

It is $6 - 4 = 2$ m above the terrace. As a sum of terms, $6 + (-4) = 2$. If we swap the two terms, we get $(-4) + 6 = 2$ — the same value.

We already know that swapping two positive numbers does not change their sum. Take a few more pairs with negative numbers in them and check that swapping still makes no difference.

Term 1 + Term 2 = Term 2 + Term 1

Now take three terms, $(-7) + 10 + (-11)$. We can add the first two and then add the third: $3 + (-11) = -8$. Or we can add the last two and then add the first: $(-7) + (-1) = -8$. Both ways give $-8$. We can also add $-7$ and $-11$ first, to get $-18$, and then add 10: the answer is still $-8$.

(Term 1 + Term 2) + Term 3 = Term 1 + (Term 2 + Term 3)
