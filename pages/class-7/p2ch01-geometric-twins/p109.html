1. $\triangle ABC$ has AB = 5 cm, $\angle A = 45^\circ$ and AC = 6 cm. A triangle PQR is to be drawn congruent to it, with PQ = 6 cm and P corresponding to A. What must $\angle P$ and PR be? Write the congruence.
Long answer
  1. In $\triangle PQR$, PQ = PR. Points S and T lie on QR, with QS = RT. Show that $\triangle PQS \cong \triangle PRT$, and so that PS = PT.
  1. In $\triangle ABC$, AB = AC and $\angle A = 40^\circ$. A point D on AC is joined to B so that BD = BC. Find $\angle DBC$ and $\angle ABD$.
Case-based questions

    TileWhat is known
    1sides 10 cm, 12 cm and 15 cm
    2sides 15 cm, 10 cm and 12 cm
    3sides 10 cm and 12 cm, with an angle of $60^\circ$ between them
    4sides 12 cm and 10 cm, with an angle of $60^\circ$ between them
    5angles $40^\circ$, $60^\circ$ and $80^\circ$
    6angles $80^\circ$, $40^\circ$ and $60^\circ$
    1. Are tiles 1 and 2 congruent? Which condition tells you?
    2. Are tiles 3 and 4 congruent? Which condition tells you?
    3. Must tiles 5 and 6 be congruent? Give a reason.
    4. Two more tiles each have angles of $40^\circ$ and $60^\circ$, with a side of 9 cm between them. Are they congruent? Which condition tells you?

  1. A carpenter in Kerala builds a roof frame. Two rafters AB and AC of the same length meet at the top, A. Their feet B and C rest on a level beam BC, which is 8 m long. A post AD stands straight up from the beam to A, so that $\angle ADB = \angle ADC = 90^\circ$. Each rafter makes an angle of $32^\circ$ with the beam.

    1. Why is $\angle ABC = \angle ACB$?
    2. Find $\angle BAC$.
    3. Which condition shows that $\triangle ADB \cong \triangle ADC$?
    4. How far is D from B? Give a reason.
    5. Find $\angle BAD$.
