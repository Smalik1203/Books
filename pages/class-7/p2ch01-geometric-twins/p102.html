Example 1 · Single correct

One angle of an isosceles triangle is 100°. What are the other two angles?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 80° and 80°
  2. 40° and 40°
  3. 45° and 35°
  4. 50° and 30°

Solution.

Step 1A triangle cannot have two angles of 100°, so 100° is the angle between the equal sides and the two base angles are equal.
Step 2Each base angle = (180° − 100°) ÷ 2 = 40°.
Answer(b) 40° and 40°
Example 2 · Single correct

If △PQR ≅ △STU, which of these must be true?

Choose one correct option.

  1. PQ = TU
  2. ∠Q = ∠U
  3. QR = TU
  4. PR = ST

Solution.

Step 1The order of the letters gives the matching: P with S, Q with T and R with U.
Step 2So QR matches TU. PQ matches ST, ∠Q matches ∠T and PR matches SU.
Answer(c) QR = TU
Example 3 · Single correct

△ABC ≅ △DEF, ∠A = 50° and ∠E = 60°. What is ∠F?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 70°
  2. 60°
  3. 50°
  4. 110°

Solution.

Step 1∠D = ∠A = 50°, because D matches A.
Step 2∠F = 180° − 50° − 60° = 70°.
Answer(a) 70°
