Example 10 · Multiple correct

In one tick-shaped symbol, AB = 5 cm, BC = 9 cm and ∠ABC = 75°. In another, PQ = 9 cm, QR = 5 cm and ∠PQR = 75°. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The symbols are congruent.
  2. A matches P.
  3. AC = PR
  4. ∠BAC = ∠QRP

Solution.

Step 1BA = QR = 5 cm, ∠B = ∠Q and BC = QP = 9 cm, so the triangles ABC and RQP are congruent by SAS.
Step 2A matches R, not P, so ∠BAC = ∠QRP; and C matches P, so AC = RP.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A base angle of an isosceles triangle is 50°. Find, in degrees, the angle between its equal sides.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The two base angles are equal: 50° and 50°.
Step 2The third angle is 180° − 50° − 50° = 80°.
Answer80
Example 12 · Numerical answer

△ABC ≅ △PQR. AB = 7 cm, BC = 9 cm and the perimeter of △PQR is 24 cm. Find PR, in cm.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The perimeters are equal, so CA = 24 − 7 − 9 = 8 cm; PR matches AC, so PR = 8 cm.
Answer8
Example 13 · Numerical answer

In △ABC, AB = AC and ∠B = 65°. Find ∠A, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Equal sides face equal angles, so ∠C = ∠B = 65° and ∠A = 180° − 65° − 65° = 50°.
