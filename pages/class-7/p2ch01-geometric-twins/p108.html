1. Assertion (A): If the three angles of one triangle are equal to the three angles of another, the triangles are congruent.

    Reason (R): The three angles of a triangle add up to $180^\circ$.

  1. Assertion (A): Two triangles with two pairs of equal sides and one pair of equal angles are always congruent.

    Reason (R): The SAS condition needs the equal angle to be included between the equal sides.

Very short answer
  1. In $\triangle PQR$, which side is included between $\angle P$ and $\angle Q$?
  1. $\triangle BAT \cong \triangle RUG$. Which angle of $\triangle RUG$ is equal to $\angle T$?
  1. $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle PQR$ and $\angle A + \angle B = 115^\circ$. Find $\angle R$.
Short answer
  1. In $\triangle ABC$, AB = AC and $\angle A = \angle B$. Find all three angles. Give a reason.
  1. ABC is an equilateral triangle. The side BC is extended beyond C to a point D. Find $\angle ACD$.
  1. In $\triangle ABC$, AB = 3.5 cm, BC = 7.1 cm and AC = 5 cm. In $\triangle PQR$, PQ = 7.1 cm, QR = 5 cm and PR = 3.5 cm. Are the triangles congruent? If they are, write the congruence.
  1. In an isosceles triangle, the angle between the equal sides is $30^\circ$ more than each base angle. Find all three angles.
  1. Two triangular plots of land, ABC and XYZ, have $\angle B = \angle Y = 90^\circ$, AB = XY = 30 m and BC = YZ = 40 m. Are the plots congruent? Which condition tells you? Is it the RHS condition?
  1. $\triangle ABC$ has AB = 5 cm, $\angle A = 45^\circ$ and AC = 6 cm. A triangle PQR is to be drawn congruent to it, with PQ = 6 cm and P corresponding to A. What must $\angle P$ and PR be? Write the congruence.
