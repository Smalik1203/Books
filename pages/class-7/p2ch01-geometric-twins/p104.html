Type 3 · The SAS and ASA conditions

Example 7

In $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle DEF$, AB = DE, $\angle B = \angle E$ and BC = EF. Which condition shows that they are congruent, and how is the congruence written?

  1. SSS, $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$
  2. ASA, $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$
  3. SAS, $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle EDF$
  4. SAS, $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle B$ is between AB and BC; $\angle E$ is between DE and EF included angles
Step 2 $AB = DE$, $\angle B = \angle E$, $BC = EF$: SAS
Step 3 B corresponds to E, A to D, and C to F equal parts matched
Answer (d)
Example 8

Two triangular flags are cut for a school's Republic Day decorations. In flag $\triangle ABC$, $\angle B = 45^\circ$, BC = 6 cm and $\angle C = 65^\circ$. In flag $\triangle DEF$, $\angle E = 65^\circ$, EF = 6 cm and $\angle F = 45^\circ$. Are the flags congruent? Which side of $\triangle DEF$ is equal to AB?

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle B = \angle F = 45^\circ$ and $\angle C = \angle E = 65^\circ$ equal angles
Step 2 $BC = FE = 6$ cm the included sides
Step 3 $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DFE$, so $AB = DF$ ASA
Answer The flags are congruent, and DF = AB.

Type 4 · The AAS and RHS conditions

Example 9

In $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle PQR$, $\angle A = \angle P = 40^\circ$, $\angle B = \angle Q = 75^\circ$ and AC = PR = 6 cm. Are the triangles congruent? Name the condition, and write the congruence.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle A = \angle P$ and $\angle B = \angle Q$ two equal angles
Step 2 $AC = PR$, opposite $\angle B$ and $\angle Q$ side not included
Answer Congruent by AAS: $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle PQR$
