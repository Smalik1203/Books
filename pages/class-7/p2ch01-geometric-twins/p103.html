Example 4 · Single correct

In △ABC and △DEF, AB = DE, ∠B = ∠E and BC = EF. Which condition shows that the triangles are congruent?

Choose one correct option.

  1. SSS
  2. SAS
  3. ASA
  4. RHS

Solution.

Step 1Two pairs of sides are equal.
Step 2The equal angles, ∠B and ∠E, lie between those sides, so the condition is SAS: △ABC ≅ △DEF.
Answer(b) SAS
Example 5 · Single correct

△ABC is right-angled at B and △DEF is right-angled at E. AC = DF = 10 cm and AB = EF = 6 cm. How is the congruence written?

Choose one correct option.

  1. △ABC ≅ △DEF
  2. △ABC ≅ △EFD
  3. △ABC ≅ △DFE
  4. △ABC ≅ △FED

Solution.

Step 1The hypotenuses AC and DF are equal, and so are the sides AB and EF, so the triangles are congruent by RHS.
Step 2The right angles match, B with E; A, at the end of side AB, matches F, at the end of EF; and C matches D.
Answer(d) △ABC ≅ △FED
Example 6 · Single correct

ABC is an equilateral triangle. On the other side of BC from A, a triangle BCD is drawn with DB = DC and ∠BDC = 40°. What is ∠ABD?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 100°
  2. 130°
  3. 110°
  4. 120°

Solution.

Step 1△BCD is isosceles, so ∠DBC = (180° − 40°) ÷ 2 = 70°.
Step 2∠ABD = ∠ABC + ∠CBD = 60° + 70° = 130°.
Answer(b) 130°
