Type 2 · SSS and writing a congruence

Example 4

In $\triangle LMN$, LM = 4 cm, MN = 6 cm and NL = 5 cm. In $\triangle XYZ$, XY = 5 cm, YZ = 4 cm and ZX = 6 cm. Write the congruence of the two triangles.

Solution.

Step 1 $LM = YZ = 4$ cm, $MN = ZX = 6$ cm, $NL = XY = 5$ cm SSS
Step 2 M corresponds to Z between 4 cm and 6 cm
Step 3 L corresponds to Y between 4 cm and 5 cm
Step 4 N corresponds to X between 6 cm and 5 cm
Answer $\triangle LMN \cong \triangle YZX$
Example 5

If $\triangle PQR \cong \triangle STU$, which of these must be true?

  1. PQ = TU
  2. $\angle Q = \angle U$
  3. QR = TU
  4. PR = ST

Solution.

Step 1 P corresponds to S, Q to T, and R to U order of the letters
Step 2 $PQ = ST$, $QR = TU$, $PR = SU$ corresponding sides
Step 3 $\angle Q = \angle T$ corresponding angles
Answer (c)
Example 6

$\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$, $\angle A = 50^\circ$ and $\angle E = 60^\circ$. Find $\angle F$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle B = \angle E = 60^\circ$ B corresponds to E
Step 2 $\angle C = 180^\circ - 50^\circ - 60^\circ = 70^\circ$ angles add to $180^\circ$
Step 3 $\angle F = \angle C = 70^\circ$ F corresponds to C
Answer $\angle F = 70^\circ$
