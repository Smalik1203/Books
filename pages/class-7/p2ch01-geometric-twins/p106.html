Example 13

One angle of an isosceles triangle is $50^\circ$. Find its other two angles.

Solution.

Step 1 $50^\circ$ a base angle: $180^\circ - 50^\circ - 50^\circ = 80^\circ$ base angles equal
Step 2 $50^\circ$ between the equal sides: $(180^\circ - 50^\circ) \div 2 = 65^\circ$ the other case
Answer $50^\circ$ and $80^\circ$, or $65^\circ$ and $65^\circ$
Example 14

ABC is an equilateral triangle. On the other side of BC from A, a triangle BCD is drawn with DB = DC and $\angle BDC = 40^\circ$. Find $\angle ABD$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle ABC = 60^\circ$ equilateral triangle
Step 2 $\angle DBC = \angle DCB$ opposite equal sides
Step 3 $\angle DBC = (180^\circ - 40^\circ) \div 2 = 70^\circ$ angle sum of BCD
Step 4 $\angle ABD = \angle ABC + \angle DBC = 60^\circ + 70^\circ$ the two angles at B
Answer $\angle ABD = 130^\circ$
