The ASA condition

If two angles and the included side of one triangle are equal to two angles and the included side of another triangle, then the two triangles are congruent. This is called the ASA (angle, side, angle) condition for congruence.

In Fig. 1.17, O is the midpoint of both AD and BC. What can we say about the lengths AB and CD? Look for two triangles in the figure whose equal parts you already know, and decide before you read on.

Example 1

Solution.

Step 1 AO = OD midpoint of AD
Step 2 BO = OC midpoint of BC
Step 3 $\angle AOB = \angle DOC$ vertically opposite angles
Step 4 two sides and the included angle are equal $\triangle AOB$, $\triangle DOC$
Step 5 $\triangle AOB \cong \triangle DOC$ SAS condition
Step 6 AB and DC are corresponding sides A to D, B to C
Answer AB = CD
