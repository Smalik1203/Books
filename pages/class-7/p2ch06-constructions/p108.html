Assertion and reason

In Questions 10 to 13, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): An angle of $45^\circ$ can be constructed with a ruler and a compass.

    Reason (R): Bisecting an angle of $90^\circ$ gives $45^\circ$.

  1. Assertion (A): The perpendicular bisector of a segment 6 cm long divides it into parts of 2 cm and 4 cm.

    Reason (R): Every point on the perpendicular bisector of a segment is at the same distance from its two ends.

  1. Assertion (A): A $2 \times 1$ tile placed on a grid coloured like a chessboard covers one dark and one white square.

    Reason (R): A $4 \times 4$ grid coloured like a chessboard has 8 dark squares.
