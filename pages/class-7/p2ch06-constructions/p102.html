Example 1 · Single correct

A point A lies on the perpendicular bisector of XY, and AX = 7 cm. What is AY?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 7 cm
  2. 14 cm
  3. 3.5 cm
  4. It cannot be found.

Solution.

Step 1Every point on the perpendicular bisector of XY is the same distance from X as from Y.
Step 2So AY = AX = 7 cm.
Answer(a) 7 cm
Example 2 · Single correct

Angles of 80°, 95° and 110° are placed around a point without overlapping. What angle fills the gap?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 65°
  2. 75°
  3. 85°
  4. 105°

Solution.

Step 1The angles around a point add to 360°.
Step 280° + 95° + 110° = 285°, and 360° − 285° = 75°.
Answer(b) 75°
Example 3 · Single correct

Copies of which regular polygon can fit around a point with no gap and no overlap?

Choose one correct option.

  1. a regular pentagon, each angle 108°
  2. a regular octagon, each angle 135°
  3. a regular hexagon, each angle 120°
  4. a regular decagon, each angle 144°

Solution.

Step 1The angles meeting at the point must add to exactly 360°, so the angle must divide 360°.
Step 2360 ÷ 120 = 3; but 360 ÷ 108, 360 ÷ 135 and 360 ÷ 144 are not whole numbers.
Answer(c) a regular hexagon, each angle 120°
