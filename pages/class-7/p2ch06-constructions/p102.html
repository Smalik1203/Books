Type 1 · Perpendicular bisectors

Example 1

Draw a segment AB of 6 cm, and construct its perpendicular bisector. Check it by measuring.

Solution.

Step 1 AB $=$ 6 cm draw the segment
Step 2 arcs of radius 4 cm from A, above and below AB more than half of AB
Step 3 arcs of radius 4 cm from B, crossing them at P and Q the same radius
Step 4 line PQ, crossing AB at M the bisector
Answer AM $=$ MB $=$ 3 cm, and $\angle PMB = 90^\circ$
Example 2

A point A lies on the perpendicular bisector of XY, and AX $=$ 7 cm. Then AY is

  1. 7 cm
  2. 14 cm
  3. 3.5 cm
  4. cannot be found

Solution.

Step 1 A is on the perpendicular bisector of XY equally far from X, Y
Step 2 AY $=$ AX $=$ 7 cm
Answer (a)
Example 3

Rohan wants to construct the perpendicular bisector of a segment XY of 5 cm. He opens his compass to 2 cm, and the arcs from X and from Y do not meet. Why? What radius should he choose?

Solution.

Step 1 a meeting point would be 2 cm from X and 2 cm from Y if the arcs met
Step 2 path from X through it to Y $= 2 + 2 = 4$ cm
Step 3 $4 \lt 5$ XY is the shortest path
Step 4 radius $\gt 5 \div 2 = 2.5$ cm so the arcs cross
Answer 2 cm is less than half of XY, so there is no meeting point; a radius of 3 cm works.
