Example 12 · Numerical answer

How many 2 × 1 tiles are needed to cover a 6 × 8 grid completely?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The grid has 6 × 8 = 48 unit squares.
Step 2Each tile covers 2 of them: 48 ÷ 2 = 24 tiles.
Answer24
Example 13 · Numerical answer

At a point O on a line XY, an angle ∠AOY of 157.5° is constructed. What is ∠AOX, in degrees?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1∠AOY and ∠AOX form a linear pair and add to 180°.
Step 2∠AOX = 180° − 157.5° = 22.5°.
Answer22.5

List IList II
(P) Each angle of an equilateral triangle(1) 135°

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
