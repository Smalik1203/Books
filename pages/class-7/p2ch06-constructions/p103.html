Example 4 · Single correct

One unit square is removed from a 7 × 9 grid. Which square could be removed so that the rest can be tiled with 2 × 1 tiles?

Choose one correct option.

  1. the square in row 1, column 2
  2. the square in row 2, column 3
  3. the square in row 4, column 5
  4. the square in row 1, column 1

Solution.

Step 1Colour the grid like a chessboard, with the corner squares dark: there are 32 dark and 31 light squares. Each tile covers one of each.
Step 2So a dark square must be removed, one whose row and column add to an even number. Only row 1, column 1 qualifies.
Answer(d) the square in row 1, column 1
Example 5 · Single correct

At a point O on a line XY, the perpendicular OA is drawn. How many angle bisections are then needed to construct an angle of 157.5° at O?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. 4

Solution.

Step 1Bisecting the 90° angle ∠AOY once gives 45°, and 90° + 45° = 135°.
Step 2Bisecting that 45° angle gives 22.5°, and 135° + 22.5° = 157.5°: two bisections.
Answer(b) 2
Example 6 · Single correct

Rohan wants the perpendicular bisector of a segment XY of 5 cm. Which compass opening will give two arcs from X and from Y that cross twice?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 2 cm
  2. 2.5 cm
  3. 1.5 cm
  4. 3 cm

Solution.

Step 1Arcs from X and Y cross only if the radius is more than half of XY, that is, more than 2.5 cm.
Step 2At exactly 2.5 cm the arcs only touch at the midpoint. Only 3 cm works.
Answer(d) 3 cm
