Type 2 · Right angles and squares

Example 4

Construct an angle of $90^\circ$ at A, one end of a segment AB of 5 cm.

Solution.

Step 1 AB $=$ 5 cm, extended beyond A a line through A
Step 2 arc with centre A, radius 2 cm, cutting the line at X and Y AX $=$ AY
Step 3 arcs of radius 3 cm from X and from Y, meeting at C CX $=$ CY
Step 4 join AC bisector of XY
Answer $\angle CAB = 90^\circ$
Example 5

Meena wants a square of side 4 cm to start a kolam design on paper. Construct the square with a ruler and a compass.

Solution.

Step 1 AB $=$ 4 cm, extended beyond A and beyond B room for the arcs
Step 2 $90^\circ$ at A, as in Example 4; mark AD $=$ 4 cm the first side
Step 3 $90^\circ$ at B, on the same side; mark BC $=$ 4 cm the second side
Step 4 join DC
Answer DC measures 4 cm and $\angle C = \angle D = 90^\circ$, so ABCD is a square.

Type 3 · Bisecting and copying angles

Example 6

Draw $\angle PQR = 70^\circ$ with a protractor. Construct its bisector, and check it by measuring.

Solution.

Step 1 arc with centre Q, cutting QP at A and QR at B QA $=$ QB
Step 2 arcs of one radius from A and from B, meeting at S SA $=$ SB
Step 3 draw the ray QS SSS congruence
Answer $\angle PQS = \angle SQR = 70^\circ \div 2 = 35^\circ$
