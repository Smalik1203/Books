Example 7

Construct an angle of $135^\circ$ at a point O on a line XY.

Solution.

Step 1 the perpendicular OA to XY at O $\angle AOX = 90^\circ$
Step 2 bisect $\angle AOY$; name the bisector OB $\angle AOB = 45^\circ$
Answer $\angle BOX = 90^\circ + 45^\circ = 135^\circ$
Example 8

Rahul copies a $90^\circ$ angle at A to a ray from X, as in the chapter, but for the last arc he opens the compass to AB instead of BC. What angle does he get?

Solution.

Step 1 XY $=$ XZ $=$ YZ $=$ AB radii and the wrong opening
Answer $\triangle XYZ$ is equilateral, so $\angle YXZ = 60^\circ$, not $90^\circ$
Example 9

P is a point that is not on a line m. Construct a line through P parallel to m, with a ruler and a compass.

Solution.

Step 1 draw any line l through P, crossing m at A a transversal
Step 2 arc with centre A, cutting m at C and AP at D
Step 3 same radius from P, cutting l at F, beyond P as D lies from A
Step 4 radius CD from F, cutting that arc at E, on C’s side of l FE $=$ DC
Step 5 draw the line n through P and E the angle at A copied
Answer n makes with l the same corresponding angle as m does, so n is parallel to m.
