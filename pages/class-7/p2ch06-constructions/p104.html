Problem 5

To construct a $60^\circ$ angle at A on AX, an arc centred at A cuts AX at B, and an arc of the same radius centred at B cuts the first arc at C. Triangle ABC is

  1. equilateral
  2. isosceles, but not equilateral
  3. right-angled
  4. scalene
Solution

AB and AC are radii of the first arc, and BC is a radius of the second arc, which has the same radius. So $AB = AC = BC$, the triangle is equilateral, and each of its angles is $60^\circ$. The answer is (a). Option (b) notices only $AB = AC$ and misses BC; a triangle with all angles $60^\circ$ cannot be right-angled or scalene. This is why the construction gives exactly $60^\circ$, whatever radius is chosen for the two arcs.
