Example 8 · Multiple correct

At a vertex of a tiling, two squares and one equilateral triangle meet. Which of these could fill the angle that is left?

Select all correct options.

  1. one regular hexagon
  2. two equilateral triangles
  3. one square
  4. one regular octagon

Solution.

Step 190° + 90° + 60° = 240°, so 360° − 240° = 120° is left.
Step 2A regular hexagon has angles of 120°, and 60° + 60° = 120°. A square (90°) and an octagon (135°) do not fit.
Answer(a), (b)
