Example 7

Construct an angle of $135^\circ$ at a point O on a line XY.

Solution.

Step 1 the perpendicular OA to XY at O $\angle AOX = 90^\circ$
Step 2 bisect $\angle AOY$; name the bisector OB $\angle AOB = 45^\circ$
Answer $\angle BOX = 90^\circ + 45^\circ = 135^\circ$
Example 8

Rahul copies a $90^\circ$ angle at A to a ray from X, as in the chapter, but for the last arc he opens the compass to AB instead of BC. What angle does he get?

Solution.

Step 1 XY $=$ XZ $=$ YZ $=$ AB radii and the wrong opening
Answer $\triangle XYZ$ is equilateral, so $\angle YXZ = 60^\circ$, not $90^\circ$

Type 4 · Angles around a point

Example 9

Angles of $80^\circ$, $95^\circ$ and $110^\circ$ are placed around a point without overlapping. What angle fills the gap? Could an angle of $60^\circ$ fill it?

Solution.

Step 1 $80^\circ + 95^\circ + 110^\circ = 285^\circ$ the angles placed
Step 2 gap $= 360^\circ - 285^\circ = 75^\circ$ a complete turn
Answer $75^\circ$. A $60^\circ$ angle leaves $75^\circ - 60^\circ = 15^\circ$ uncovered.
Example 10

Construct an angle of $150^\circ$ at a point O on a line XY.

Solution.

Step 1 arc with centre O, cutting OY at P
Step 2 same radius from P, cutting it at A; join OA OPA is equilateral
Step 3 bisect $\angle AOY$; name the bisector OB $\angle BOY = 30^\circ$
Answer $\angle BOX = 180^\circ - 30^\circ = 150^\circ$
