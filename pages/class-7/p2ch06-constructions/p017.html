The pieces can be rearranged to make many interesting shapes, such as an arrow, a cat or a boat. Every shape uses all seven pieces, with no overlaps. A piece may be turned round or turned over, but it must always be used whole.

Tiling a region

Covering a region with shapes, with no gaps and no overlaps, is called tiling. A floor covered with square tiles is a tiling, and so is a wall of bricks.

Take a rectangular grid of unit squares with 4 rows and 6 columns. We call it a $4 \times 6$ grid. Can it be tiled with copies of a $2 \times 1$ tile? We are allowed to turn a tile, so it can lie across or stand upright. Fig. 6.30 shows one way. It is certainly not the only one.
