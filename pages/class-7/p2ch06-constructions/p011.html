Arches

Beautiful arches are found in many buildings, such as the Diwan-i-Aam in the Red Fort in Delhi. How were they made? The first step is to draw the arch on a flat surface, such as paper or stone. Let us construct one kind of arch on paper.

An arch like the one in Fig. 6.17 needs support lines. For it to be symmetrical, we need $AB = CD$ and $\angle BAD = \angle CDA$. How would you construct these support lines? Draw the base AD, construct equal angles at A and at D, and mark B and C with $AB = DC$.

Use the support lines to construct the arch. If you like, change the radii of the arcs until the arch looks pleasing to you.

Some arches come to a point at the top. How do we construct such a pointed arch? What support lines would you use? Remember Wavy Wave from the Class 6 book. Here the support lines are just two segments of equal length. If their midpoints are marked, can you construct a pointed arch?
