1. How many $2 \times 1$ tiles are needed to tile a $6 \times 7$ grid?
Short answer
  1. Draw a segment PQ of 7 cm and construct its perpendicular bisector. Measure the two parts of PQ.
  1. Draw an angle of $110^\circ$ with a protractor, and construct its bisector. Measure the two angles you get.
  1. Construct an angle of $67.5^\circ$ at a point O on a line XY, and say why it is $67.5^\circ$.
  1. Can a $5 \times 5$ grid with its centre square removed be tiled with $2 \times 1$ tiles? Give a reason.
  1. A rangoli has 16 petals, spaced evenly around its centre, one along each half of 8 lines through the centre. What angle do neighbouring lines make? Which constructions give this angle?
Long answer
  1. Construct $\triangle ABC$ with AB $=$ 6 cm, BC $=$ 5 cm and CA $=$ 4 cm, and the perpendicular bisectors of AB and BC, meeting at O. Measure OA, OB and OC. What do you notice, and why?
  1. Construct a regular hexagon ABCDEF with sides of 3.5 cm. Join AC, CE and EA.
    1. Measure AC, CE and EA.
    2. What kind of triangle is ACE? Explain why, using congruent triangles.
Case-based questions

  1. Rampur and Sonpur are two villages 6 km apart, joined by a straight road. The panchayat wants to dig a well at a point W that is 5 km from each village. On a map, 1 cm stands for 1 km.

    1. Why must W lie on the perpendicular bisector of the road between the two villages?
    2. Draw the road 6 cm long, and construct the points that could be W. How many such points are there?
    3. Measure how far W is from the road on the map. How many kilometres is that?
    4. Could the well be 2 km from each village? Give a reason.

  1. The floor of a school verandah is marked out as a grid of 4 rows and 9 columns of squares. It is to be covered with stone slabs, and each slab covers two squares next to each other.

    1. How many slabs cover the whole floor?
    2. Two pillars stand on the corner squares at the two ends of one long side. Can the rest of the floor be covered? Give a reason.
    3. Instead, the two pillars stand on the corner squares at the two ends of one short side. Can the rest of the floor be covered?
    4. For part (c), say how the slabs can be laid.
