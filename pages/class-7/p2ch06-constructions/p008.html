Repeating units and copying an angle

Look at the design in Fig. 6.13. One unit, a slice of a circle, is repeated in two positions: pointing up and pointing down. To construct the design, we must make exact copies of the unit. Each slice has two straight arms of the same length, and an arc of a circle joining their far ends, like a slice of a round cake. Some slices point up and some point down, and each slice shares a straight arm with the slice beside it.
