Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which of these can be tiled completely with 2 × 1 tiles?

Select all correct options.

  1. a 5 × 6 grid
  2. a 5 × 6 grid with one square removed
  3. a 4 × 5 grid with the squares in row 1, column 1 and row 2, column 2 removed
  4. a 3 × 4 grid

Solution.

Step 15 × 6 and 3 × 4 grids can be filled with rows of tiles laid along the side of even length.
Step 2(b) leaves 29 squares, an odd number. In (c) the removed squares share a chessboard colour, leaving 8 and 10, but each tile covers one of each.
Answer(a), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

P is a point on the perpendicular bisector of AB, other than the point where it meets AB. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. PA = PB
  2. The bisector meets AB at the midpoint of AB.
  3. The bisector makes a right angle with AB.
  4. P is the midpoint of AB.

Solution.

Step 1The perpendicular bisector passes through the midpoint of AB at 90°, and each of its points is equally far from A and B.
Step 2P is on the bisector but not on AB, so it is not the midpoint of AB.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
