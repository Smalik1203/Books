Example 15 · Matching

Match each construction in List I with the angle it gives in List II.

List IList II
(P) Bisect a right angle(1) 90°
(Q) Bisect a 45° angle(2) 135°
(R) A right angle and half a right angle, side by side(3) 45°
(S) Bisect a straight angle(4) 22.5°

Each entry has exactly one match.

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Half of 90° is 45°, and half of 45° is 22.5°.
Step 290° + 45° = 135°, and half of 180° is 90°.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
