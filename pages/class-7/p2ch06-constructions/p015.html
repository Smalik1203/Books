6.2Tiling

Tangrams are puzzles that began in China. A tangram uses 7 pieces, made by cutting a square as in Fig. 6.28. For the problems ahead you need a set of these pieces. You can cut them out of cardboard, copying the figure. The pieces are two large triangles, one medium triangle, two small triangles, a square and a parallelogram. Compare their sides: the side of the square, for instance, is as long as a short side of a small triangle. Before you start, check that the seven pieces fit back together into the square.
