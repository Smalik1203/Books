Type 5 · Leap years

Example 12

Which of the years 2032, 2200 and 2800 are leap years?

Solution.

Step 1 $2032 \div 4 = 508$, and 2032 is not divisible by 100 leap year
Step 2 $2200 \div 100 = 22$, but $2200 \div 400 = 5.5$ not a leap year
Step 3 $2800 \div 400 = 7$ leap year
Answer 2032 and 2800 are leap years; 2200 is not.
Example 13

Arun was born on 1 January 2000. How many days old was he on 1 January 2010?

Solution.

Step 1 10 years, 2000 to 2009; leap years 2000, 2004, 2008 2000 divisible by 400
Step 2 $10 \times 365 + 3 = 3653$
Answer 3653 days
Example 14

After 400 years of the full leap-year rule, is the calendar ahead or behind? By how much?

Solution.

Step 1 $400 \div 4 - 3 = 97$ leap years 100, 200, 300 dropped
Step 2 $400 \times 365 + 97 = 1{,}46{,}097$ days the calendar
Step 3 $400 \times 365.2422 = 1{,}46{,}096.88$ days the Earth
Answer $1{,}46{,}097 - 1{,}46{,}096.88 = 0.12$; the calendar is ahead by 0.12 of a day
