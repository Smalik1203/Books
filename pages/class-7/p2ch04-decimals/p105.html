Example 12

Which of these divisions never ends?

  1. $3 \div 8$
  2. $7 \div 20$
  3. $5 \div 12$
  4. $9 \div 25$

Solution.

Step 1 $\tfrac{3}{8} = \tfrac{375}{1000} = 0.375$ 8 divides 1000
Step 2 $\tfrac{7}{20} = \tfrac{35}{100} = 0.35$; $\tfrac{9}{25} = \tfrac{36}{100} = 0.36$
Step 3 $5 \div 12$: 50 tenths give 4, 2 left; 20 hundredths give 1, 8 left long division
Step 4 80 thousandths give 6, 8 left; 8 is left again at every step remainder 8 repeats
Answer (c)

Type 5 · Leap years

Example 13

Which of the years 2032, 2200 and 2800 are leap years?

Solution.

Step 1 $2032 \div 4 = 508$, and 2032 is not divisible by 100 leap year
Step 2 $2200 \div 100 = 22$, but $2200 \div 400 = 5.5$ not a leap year
Step 3 $2800 \div 400 = 7$ leap year
Answer 2032 and 2800 are leap years; 2200 is not.
