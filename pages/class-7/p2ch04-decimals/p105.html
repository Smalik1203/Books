Example 11 · Numerical answer

A rope 6.3 m long is cut into 4 equal pieces. Find the length of each piece, in metres.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 16.3 ÷ 4: 6 ones ÷ 4 = 1 one, remainder 2 ones = 20 tenths; 23 tenths ÷ 4 = 5 tenths, remainder 3 tenths.
Step 230 hundredths ÷ 4 = 7 hundredths, remainder 2; 20 thousandths ÷ 4 = 5 thousandths. Each piece is 1.575 m.
Answer1.575
