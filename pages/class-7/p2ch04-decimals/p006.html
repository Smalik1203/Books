4.3Dividing Decimals

Example 6

Anuja has a ribbon 3.9 m long and cuts it into 10 equal pieces. How long is each piece?

Solution.

Step 1 We find $3.9 \div 10$, and $3.9 = \frac{39}{10}$ as a fraction
Step 2 $3.9 \div 10 = \dfrac{39}{10} \times \dfrac{1}{10}$ multiply by its reciprocal
Step 3 $= \dfrac{39}{100} = 0.39$
Answer Each piece is 0.39 m long

If the ribbon were cut into 100 equal pieces, each would be $\frac{39}{10} \times \frac{1}{100} = \frac{39}{1000} = 0.039$ m long.

How much is 0.039 m in centimetres, and in millimetres? These divisions give a simple rule: to divide a decimal by 10, 100, 1000 and so on, move its decimal point as many places to the left as the divisor has zeros. Use it to complete Table 4.7.
