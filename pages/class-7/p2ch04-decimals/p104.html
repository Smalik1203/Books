Example 9

The product $4.5 \times 0.8$ is

  1. greater than both
  2. less than both
  3. between the two
  4. equal to 4.5

Solution.

Step 1 $4.5 \gt 1$ and $0 \lt 0.8 \lt 1$ one of each
Step 2 $45 \times 8 = 360$, so $4.5 \times 0.8 = 3.6$
Step 3 $0.8 \lt 3.6 \lt 4.5$ Table 4.4 agrees
Answer (c)

Example 10

$7.2 \div 0.08$ is equal to

  1. 0.9
  2. 9
  3. 900
  4. 90

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{7.2}{0.08} = \dfrac{7.2 \times 100}{0.08 \times 100}$ divisor made whole
Step 2 $= \dfrac{720}{8} = 90$
Step 3 $0.08 \times 90 = 7.2$ check by multiplying
Answer (d)
Example 11

Which of these divisions never ends?

  1. $3 \div 8$
  2. $7 \div 20$
  3. $5 \div 12$
  4. $9 \div 25$

Solution.

Step 1 $\tfrac{3}{8} = \tfrac{375}{1000} = 0.375$ 8 divides 1000
Step 2 $\tfrac{7}{20} = \tfrac{35}{100} = 0.35$; $\tfrac{9}{25} = \tfrac{36}{100} = 0.36$
Step 3 $12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3$ a factor 3
Step 4 $5 \div 12 = 0.41666\ldots$ remainder 8 repeats
Answer (c)
