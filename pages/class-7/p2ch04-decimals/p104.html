Example 9

The product $4.5 \times 0.8$ is

  1. greater than both
  2. less than both
  3. between the two
  4. equal to 4.5

Solution.

Step 1 $4.5 \gt 1$ and $0 \lt 0.8 \lt 1$ one of each
Step 2 $45 \times 8 = 360$, so $4.5 \times 0.8 = 3.6$
Step 3 $0.8 \lt 3.6 \lt 4.5$ Table 4.4 agrees
Answer (c)

Example 10

A rope 6.3 m long is cut into 4 equal pieces. Find the length of each piece by long division.

Solution.

Step 1 6 ones $\div$ 4: 1 one, and 2 ones left
Step 2 2 ones are 20 tenths; with 3 tenths, 23 tenths; $\div$ 4: 5 tenths, 3 left point in the quotient
Step 3 3 tenths become 30 hundredths; $\div$ 4: 7 hundredths, 2 left regroup
Step 4 2 hundredths become 20 thousandths; $\div$ 4: 5 thousandths, none left regroup
Step 5 $1.575 \times 4 = 6.3$ check by multiplying
Answer Each piece is 1.575 m long.
Example 11

$7.2 \div 0.08$ is equal to

  1. 0.9
  2. 9
  3. 900
  4. 90

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{7.2}{0.08} = \dfrac{7.2 \times 100}{0.08 \times 100}$ divisor made whole
Step 2 $= \dfrac{720}{8} = 90$
Step 3 $0.08 \times 90 = 7.2$ check by multiplying
Answer (d)
