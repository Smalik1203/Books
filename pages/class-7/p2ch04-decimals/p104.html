Which of these are equal to 23.45?

Select all correct options.

  1. 2345 ÷ 100
  2. 234.5 ÷ 10
  3. 2.345 × 100
  4. 0.2345 × 100

Solution.

Step 1Dividing by 100 moves the digits 2 places right of the point: 2345 ÷ 100 = 23.45. Also 234.5 ÷ 10 = 23.45.
Step 2Multiplying by 100 moves them 2 places left: 0.2345 × 100 = 23.45, but 2.345 × 100 = 234.5.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which products are less than 1?

Select all correct options.

  1. 0.5 × 1.8
  2. 1.2 × 0.9
  3. 0.25 × 3.9
  4. 2.5 × 0.4

Solution.

Step 10.5 × 1.8 = 0.9 and 0.25 × 3.9 = 0.975.
Step 21.2 × 0.9 = 1.08 and 2.5 × 0.4 = 1.00, which is not less than 1.
Answer(a), (c)

Which of these are leap years?

Select all correct options.

  1. 2032
  2. 2200
  3. 2800
  4. 2400

Solution.

Step 12032 is divisible by 4 and not by 100.
Step 22800 and 2400 are divisible by 400. 2200 is divisible by 100 but not by 400.
