C4 The choices are $2.1 \times 3 = 6.3$, $3.1 \times 2 = 6.2$, $1.2 \times 3 = 3.6$, $3.2 \times 1$, $1.3 \times 2$ and $2.3 \times 1$. The answer is (c).
C6 $a = 3.7 \div 0.01 = 370$, and $370 \div 0.01 = 37000$. The answer is (c). Option (b) is $a$ itself.
C7 $40 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 5$ divides 1000, so $\frac{7}{40} = \frac{175}{1000} = 0.175$. The other denominators have a factor 3. The answer is (b).

Three things from this section are worth keeping. Estimate before placing a decimal point. Change a division by a decimal into a division by a counting number, multiplying both numbers by the same power of ten. And a fraction in lowest form gives a decimal that ends only when its denominator has no prime factors other than 2 and 5.
