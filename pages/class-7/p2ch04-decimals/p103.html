Example 5

$0.3 \times 0.02$ is equal to

  1. 0.006
  2. 0.06
  3. 0.6
  4. 0.0006

Solution.

Step 1 $3 \times 2 = 6$, and $1 + 2 = 3$ digits after the point count both numbers
Step 2 $0.3 \times 0.02 = 0.006$
Answer (a)

Example 7

Jonali’s mother buys 2.5 kg of rice at ₹48.60 a kilogram. She has ₹120. Is that enough?

Solution.

Step 1 $2.5 \times 50 = 125$ estimate first
