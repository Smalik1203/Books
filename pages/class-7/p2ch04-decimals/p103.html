Problem 4

Petrol costs ₹104.50 a litre. What do 2.4 litres cost?

  1. ₹25.08
  2. ₹250.80
  3. ₹2508.00
  4. ₹248.80
Solution

$1045 \times 24 = 25{,}080$, and $104.5 \times 2.4$ has $1 + 1 = 2$ digits after the point, so it is 250.80. An estimate agrees: $100 \times 2.5 = 250$. The answer is (b). Options (a) and (c) put the decimal point in the wrong place, and (d) is a slip in multiplying.

Problem 5

The product $4.5 \times 0.8$ is

  1. greater than both
  2. less than both
  3. between the two
  4. equal to 4.5
Solution

One number is greater than 1 and the other is between 0 and 1, so the product lies between them. Indeed $4.5 \times 0.8 = 3.6$, which is less than 4.5 and greater than 0.8. The answer is (c).
