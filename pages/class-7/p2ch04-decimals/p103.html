Example 5 · Single correct

Which of these divisions never ends?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 5 ÷ 12
  2. 3 ÷ 8
  3. 7 ÷ 20
  4. 9 ÷ 25

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{3}{8}$ = $\frac{375}{1000}$ = 0.375, $\frac{7}{20}$ = $\frac{35}{100}$ = 0.35 and $\frac{9}{25}$ = $\frac{36}{100}$ = 0.36: 8, 20 and 25 each divide a power of 10.
Step 212 = 2 × 2 × 3 has the factor 3, so 5 ÷ 12 = 0.41666… goes on for ever.
Answer(a) 5 ÷ 12
Example 6 · Single correct

Which of these years is not a leap year?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 2024
  2. 2100
  3. 2000
  4. 2400

Solution.

Step 1A year divisible by 4 is a leap year, except a year divisible by 100, which must also be divisible by 400.
Step 22100 is divisible by 100 but not by 400, so it is not a leap year; 2000 and 2400 are.
Answer(b) 2100
