Another Peek Beyond the Point

4.1A Quick Recap of Decimals

Decimals carry the Indian place value system beyond the ones place, to show tenths, hundredths, thousandths and so on, and their sums. For example, 27.53 stands for 2 tens, 7 ones, 5 tenths and 3 hundredths. Each place is worth one tenth of the place to its left.

In Mathematics I we learnt to multiply and divide fractions. In this chapter we multiply and divide decimals. You will see that the methods grow naturally out of the ones we use for counting numbers.
