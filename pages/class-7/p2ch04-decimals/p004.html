Example 5

Find $5.8 \times 1.24$.

First, $58 \times 124 = 7192$. The numbers 5.8 and 1.24 have $1 + 2 = 3$ digits after their decimal points, so $5.8 \times 1.24 = 7.192$. Check this by writing both numbers as fractions.

Is the product always larger?

When we multiply two fractions, the product is not always larger than both of them. The same is true of decimals. $2.25 \times 8 = 18$ is larger than both numbers. $0.25 \times 8 = 2$ is larger than 0.25 but smaller than 8. And $0.25 \times 0.8 = 0.2$ is smaller than both. When is the product larger than both numbers, and when is it smaller than both? Decimals are fractions written another way, so the answer is the one we found for fractions (Table 4.4).
