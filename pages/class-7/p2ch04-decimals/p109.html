Assertion and reason

In Questions 22 to 25, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $1.5 \times 0.2 = 0.3$.

    Reason (R): A product has as many digits after the point as the two numbers have together.

  1. Assertion (A): $3.47 \times 100 = 34.7$.

    Reason (R): To multiply a decimal by 100, move its decimal point two places to the right.

  1. Assertion (A): $5 \div 0.5$ is greater than 5.

    Reason (R): 0.5 is less than 5.

  1. Assertion (A): The year 2400 will be a leap year.

    Reason (R): Every year divisible by 100 is a leap year.
