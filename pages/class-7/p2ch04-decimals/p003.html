Example 3

Ajay’s school is 827 m from his home. He walks there and back once a day, 6 days a week. How far does he walk in a week, in kilometres?

Solution.

Step 1 Each way is 827 m, which is 0.827 km metres to kilometres
Step 2 $0.827 \times 2 = \dfrac{827 \times 2}{1000} = \dfrac{1654}{1000} = 1.654$ in a day
Step 3 $1.654 \times 6 = \dfrac{1654 \times 6}{1000} = \dfrac{9924}{1000} = 9.924$ in a week
Answer He walks 9.924 km in a week
