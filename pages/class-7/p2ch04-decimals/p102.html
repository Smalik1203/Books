Type 1 · Dividing by 10, 100 and 1000

Example 1

Find $2345 \div 100$.

Solution.

Step 1 $2345 = 2345.$, and 100 has 2 zeros move 2 places left
Answer $2345 \div 100 = 23.45$
Example 2

Find $7 \div 1000$. Why do we write zeros in front of the 7?

Solution.

Step 1 1000 has 3 zeros move 3 places left
Step 2 $7 = 007.$ only one digit
Answer $7 \div 1000 = 0.007$; the zeros hold the tenths and hundredths places
Example 3

Priya walks 1250 m from her home in Shillong to school. How far is that in kilometres?

Solution.

Step 1 $1 \text{ km} = 1000 \text{ m}$ divide by 1000
Step 2 $1250 \div 1000 = 1.250 = 1.25$ move 3 places left
Answer 1.25 km

Type 2 · Multiplying decimals

Example 4

Find $3.6 \times 100$ and $0.058 \times 1000$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3.6 \times 100 = 3.60 \times 100 = 360$ move 2 places right
Step 2 $0.058 \times 1000 = 0058 = 58$ move 3 places right
Answer 360 and 58
