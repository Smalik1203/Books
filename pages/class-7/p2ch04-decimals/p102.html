Example 1 · Single correct

0.3 × 0.02 is equal to

Choose one correct option.

  1. 0.006
  2. 0.06
  3. 0.6
  4. 0.0006

Solution.

Step 13 × 2 = 6, and the two numbers have 1 + 2 = 3 digits after the decimal point.
Step 2So the product has 3 decimal places: 0.006.
Answer(a) 0.006

Example 3 · Single correct

The product 4.5 × 0.8 is

Choose one correct option.

  1. greater than both numbers
  2. less than both numbers
  3. between the two numbers
  4. equal to 4.5

Solution.

Step 1Multiplying by 0.8, which is less than 1, makes 4.5 smaller; multiplying by 4.5, which is more than 1, makes 0.8 larger.
Step 2So the product lies between them: 4.5 × 0.8 = 3.6.
Answer(c) between the two numbers
