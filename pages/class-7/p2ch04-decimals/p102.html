Behind Each Answer
Five multiple-choice problems, each solved, with the reason for every option.
Problem 1

$0.3 \times 0.02$ is equal to

  1. 0.006
  2. 0.06
  3. 0.6
  4. 0.0006
Solution

$3 \times 2 = 6$, and there are $1 + 2 = 3$ digits after the decimal points, so the product is 0.006: (a). Options (b) and (c) count too few places, and (d) too many.

Problem 2

$7.2 \div 0.08$ is equal to

  1. 0.9
  2. 9
  3. 900
  4. 90
Solution

$7.2 \div 0.08 = \dfrac{7.2 \times 100}{0.08 \times 100} = \dfrac{720}{8} = 90$

The answer is (d). The divisor is less than 1, so the quotient must be greater than 7.2, which rules out (a) and (b). Option (c) multiplies 7.2 by 1000 instead of 100.

Problem 3

Which of these divisions never ends?

  1. $3 \div 8$
  2. $7 \div 20$
  3. $5 \div 12$
  4. $9 \div 25$
Solution

A division ends when the fraction can be given a denominator of 10, 100, 1000 and so on. 8 divides 1000, 20 divides 100 and 25 divides 100, so (a), (b) and (d) end: 0.375, 0.35 and 0.36. But 12 divides none of these numbers, because of its factor 3, and $5 \div 12 = 0.41666\ldots$ The answer is (c).
