Dividing by a decimal

Example 12

Ravi rode his scooter 126 km from Pune to Matheran in 2.5 hours. What was his average speed?

Average speed is distance divided by time, $126 \div 2.5$. Write the divisor as a fraction and multiply by its reciprocal.

$126 \div \dfrac{25}{10} = 126 \times \dfrac{10}{25} = \dfrac{1260}{25} = 50.4$

The last step is done by long division. His average speed was 50.4 km per hour.

Example 13

Find $4.68 \div 1.3$ and $4.68 \div 0.13$.

$4.68 \div 1.3 = 4.68 \times \dfrac{10}{13} = \dfrac{46.8}{13} = 3.6$

$4.68 \div 0.13 = 4.68 \times \dfrac{100}{13} = \dfrac{468}{13} = 36$
