Example 11

Find $0.06 \div 5$.

Solution.

Step 1 0.06 is 0 ones, 0 tenths and 6 hundredths by place value
Step 2 0 ones $\div$ 5 is 0 ones; moving to tenths, 0 tenths $\div$ 5 is 0 tenths put the decimal point
Step 3 6 hundredths $\div$ 5 is 1 hundredth, with 1 hundredth left, which becomes 10 thousandths
Step 4 10 thousandths $\div$ 5 is 2 thousandths
Answer $0.06 \div 5 = 0.012$
