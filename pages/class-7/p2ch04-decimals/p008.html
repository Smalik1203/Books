So $1325 \div 4 = 3$ hundreds $+ 3$ tens $+ 1$ one $+ 2$ tenths $+ 5$ hundredths $= 331.25$. Check it with an equal fraction: $\frac{1325}{4} = \frac{1325 \times 25}{4 \times 25} = \frac{33125}{100} = 331.25$. Long division carries on past the ones place: ones regroup as tenths, tenths as hundredths, and so on.

Example 9

Find $237 \div 8$.

Solution.

Step 1 2 hundreds become 20 tens, making 23 tens. 23 tens $\div$ 8: 2 tens, and 7 tens left
Step 2 7 tens become 70 ones, making 77 ones. 77 ones $\div$ 8: 9 ones, and 5 ones left
Step 3 5 ones become 50 tenths. 50 tenths $\div$ 8: 6 tenths, and 2 tenths left point in the quotient
Step 4 2 tenths become 20 hundredths. 20 hundredths $\div$ 8: 2 hundredths, and 4 hundredths left
Step 5 4 hundredths become 40 thousandths. 40 thousandths $\div$ 8: 5 thousandths, and nothing left
Answer $237 \div 8 = 29.625$
Example 10

A shopkeeper packs 9.5 kg of sugar equally into 4 bags. How much is in each bag?

Solution.

Step 1 We find $9.5 \div 4$. 9 ones $\div$ 4 gives 2 ones with 1 one left
Step 2 The 1 one becomes 10 tenths, which with 5 tenths makes 15 tenths point in the quotient
Step 3 15 tenths $\div$ 4 gives 3 tenths with 3 left
Step 4 30 hundredths $\div$ 4 gives 7 hundredths with 2 left, and 20 thousandths $\div$ 4 gives 5 thousandths
Answer Each bag holds 2.375 kg
