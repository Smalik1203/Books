Example 15

After 400 years of the full leap-year rule, is the calendar ahead or behind? By how much?

Solution.

Step 1 $400 \div 4 - 3 = 97$ leap years 100, 200, 300 dropped
Step 2 $400 \times 365 + 97 = 1{,}46{,}097$ days the calendar
Step 3 $400 \times 365.2422 = 1{,}46{,}096.88$ days the Earth
Answer $1{,}46{,}097 - 1{,}46{,}096.88 = 0.12$; the calendar is ahead by 0.12 of a day
