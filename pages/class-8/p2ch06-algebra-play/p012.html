6.7The Largest Product

Put the digits $4$, $6$ and $7$ in the boxes of $\square\,\square \times \square$, using each digit once. Which arrangement gives the largest product?

There are six arrangements. Any of the three digits can go first, and the other two can then be placed in two ways.

multiplier 4 $67 \times 4 = 268$ and $76 \times 4 = 304$
multiplier 6 $47 \times 6 = 282$ and $74 \times 6 = 444$
multiplier 7 $46 \times 7 = 322$ and $64 \times 7 = 448$

The largest is $64 \times 7 = 448$. It is worth seeing why. The products are in pairs with the same multiplier. In each pair, the larger two-digit number wins, so three products can be dropped at once. That leaves $76 \times 4$, $74 \times 6$ and $64 \times 7$.
