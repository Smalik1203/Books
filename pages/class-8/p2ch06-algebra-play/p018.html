Exercise Set 6.5
  1. Each evening, Kavya’s aunt doubles the number of stickers Kavya has. The next morning, Kavya gives the same number of stickers to a friend. She does this for three days, and after the third gift she has none left. What is the smallest number of stickers she could have started with? How many did each friend get?
  1. A farm keeps cows and ducks. Together the animals have $40$ heads and $110$ legs. How many cows are there?
    1. Answer it with a letter-number.
    2. Answer it again without one, by first working out how many legs there would be if every animal were a duck.
  1. Salma’s aunt is three times Salma’s age. In ten years, she will be twice Salma’s age. How old is Salma now?
  1. Harpreet and Divya sell mangoes. Divya says, “I have three times as many baskets as you.” Harpreet says, “If you gave me $4$ of yours, we would have the same number.” How many baskets does each have?
  1. A juice stall costs ₹$800$ a day to rent, and each glass of juice costs ₹$15$ to make.
    1. The stall sells $80$ glasses a day. What price gives a profit of ₹$1{,}200$ a day?
    2. If customers will pay only ₹$35$ a glass, how many glasses a day give the same profit?
  1. Work out $4 \times 4 - 3 \times 5$, then $8 \times 8 - 7 \times 9$, then $11 \times 11 - 10 \times 12$. What do you notice? Write one more of your own, and use a letter-number $n$ to explain why it happens.
  1. In Farhan’s game, suppose the fee had been $24$ tokens in place of $16$. After the third doubling he again had exactly the fee he owed. How many tokens did he start with?
  1. Kavya does the same as in Question 1, but for four days. What is the smallest number of stickers she can start with? Do the same for $n$ days, and say what the two numbers have to do with powers of $2$.
