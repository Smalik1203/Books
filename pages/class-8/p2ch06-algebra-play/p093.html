    Reason (R): The largest digit should always be placed in the tens place.

  1. Assertion (A): The top of the three-row pyramid with the bottom row $4$, $6$, $5$ is $21$.

    Reason (R): In a three-row pyramid, the middle number of the bottom row is counted twice in the top.

  1. Assertion (A): $845845$ is divisible by $13$.

    Reason (R): $13$ is a prime number.
