Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): A two-digit number minus its reverse can be $35$.

    Reason (R): A two-digit number minus its reverse is always a multiple of $9$.

  1. Assertion (A): The four dates of any $2 \times 2$ calendar square add to an even number.

    Reason (R): Their total is $4a + 16 = 4(a + 4)$, a multiple of $4$.

  1. Assertion (A): A two-digit number plus its reverse is always a multiple of $11$.

    Reason (R): $11$ is a prime number.

  1. Assertion (A): From the digits $1$, $2$ and $3$, the largest product of the form $\square\,\square \times \square$ is $21 \times 3 = 63$.

    Reason (R): The largest digit should always go in the tens place.
