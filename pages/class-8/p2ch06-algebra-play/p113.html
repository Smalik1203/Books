Short answer
  1. A trick runs: think of a number, multiply by $6$, add $18$, divide by $3$, subtract twice the number you thought of. Show that the answer is always $6$. What should be added in place of $18$ to make the trick end at $10$?
  1. The bottom row of a three-row pyramid is $x$, $2x$, $x + 6$, and its top is $48$. Find $x$ and complete the pyramid.
  1. The digits of a two-digit number add to $11$. Reversing the digits gives a number $27$ more. Find the number.
  1. Riya spends half her money and then ₹$10$ more. She does the same again with what is left, and ends with ₹$6$. How much did she start with?
Long answer
  1. The nine dates of a $3 \times 3$ calendar square add to $117$. Find all nine dates and check their total. Then show, with the top-left date $a$, why the total of any such square is a multiple of $9$.
  1. A four-row pyramid has bottom row $x$, $3$, $5$, $x$ and top $60$. Find $x$ and build the whole pyramid to check. By how much does the top change if both end entries are raised by $1$?
  1. A traveller starts with $x$ coins. At each round a genie doubles his coins and then takes $12$.
    1. Write his coins after one, two and three rounds.
    2. After three rounds he has $52$ coins. Find $x$.
    3. For which starting numbers is he better off after each round than before it?
