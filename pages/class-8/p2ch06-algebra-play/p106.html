Type 3 · Filling in a pyramid

Example 5

A three-row pyramid has $35$ at the top, $20$ on the left of the middle row, and $6$ at the right-hand end of the bottom row. Complete it.

Solution.

Step 1 middle right: $35 - 20 = 15$ the top is their sum
Step 2 bottom middle: $15 - 6 = 9$
Step 3 bottom left: $20 - 9 = 11$
Step 4 $11 + 9 = 20$ and $9 + 6 = 15$ check
Answer Bottom row $11$, $9$, $6$; middle row $20$, $15$; top $35$
Example 6

The bottom row of a three-row pyramid is $x$, $2x$, $3x$, and the top is $40$. Find $x$ and complete the pyramid.

Solution.

Step 1 top $= x + 2(2x) + 3x = 8x$ top is $a + 2b + c$
Step 2 $8x = 40$, so $x = 5$
Step 3 bottom $5$, $10$, $15$; middle $15$, $25$
Answer $x = 5$, and $15 + 25 = 40$ at the top

Type 4 · The top from the bottom row

Example 7

Find the top of the four-row pyramid whose bottom row is $2$, $4$, $6$, $8$ without building it. Then build it to check.

Solution.

Step 1 $a + 3b + 3c + d = 2 + 12 + 18 + 8 = 40$ multipliers $1, 3, 3, 1$
Step 2 second row: $6$, $10$, $14$ building it
Step 3 third row: $16$, $24$; top $16 + 24 = 40$
Answer The top is $40$ both ways
