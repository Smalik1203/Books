Example 5 · Single correct

The digits of a two-digit number add to 12. Reversing the digits gives a number 18 less. What is the number?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 75
  2. 57
  3. 84
  4. 66

Solution.

Step 1With digits $a$ and $b$: $(10a + b) - (10b + a) = 9(a - b) = 18$, so $a - b = 2$.
Step 2$a + b = 12$ and $a - b = 2$ give $a = 7$ and $b = 5$: the number is 75.
Answer(a) 75
Example 6 · Single correct

In a pyramid of numbers each box is the sum of the two below it. The bottom row of a four-row pyramid is 2, 4, 6, 8. What is at the top?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 20
  2. 40
  3. 80
  4. 36

Solution.

Step 1With bottom row $a, b, c, d$, the top is $a + 3b + 3c + d$.
Step 2$2 + 12 + 18 + 8 = 40$.
Answer(b) 40
Example 7 · Multiple correct

A six-digit number is made by writing a three-digit number twice, such as 385 385. By which of these is every such number divisible?

Select all correct options.

  1. 7
  2. 11
  3. 13
  4. 17

Solution.

Step 1Writing $abc$ twice gives $abc \times 1000 + abc = abc \times 1001$.
Step 2$1001 = 7 \times 11 \times 13$, and 17 is not a factor: $385\,385 \div 17$ is not whole.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
