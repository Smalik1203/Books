6.5A Square on a Calendar

Ask a friend to draw a square round four dates on a calendar page, and to tell you their total. From the total alone, you can name all four dates.

The trick works because the four dates are not four free numbers. Along a row, the dates go up by $1$. Down a column, they go up by $7$. So once you know the top-left date, you know the other three.

Call the top-left date $a$. The date to its right is $a + 1$. The date below it is $a + 7$, and the date in the far corner is $a + 8$. The four dates add to

$$a + (a+1) + (a+7) + (a+8) = 4a + 16.$$

For the shaded square, $a = 8$, and $4 \times 8 + 16 = 48$, the total on the calendar. Now go the other way. If the total is $60$, take away $16$ and divide by $4$. So $a = 11$, and the square holds $11$, $12$, $18$ and $19$.

Example 4

One friend says their square adds to $78$. Another says theirs adds to $54$. What do you tell them?

Solution.

Step 1 first friend: $4a + 16 = 78$, so $4a = 62$ not a multiple of 4
Step 2 second friend: $4a + 16 = 54$, so $4a = 38$ not a multiple of 4
Answer Neither total can come from a square, so both friends have added wrongly

Every possible total is $4a + 16$, which is a multiple of $4$. So no square on any calendar page can add to $78$ or $54$.
