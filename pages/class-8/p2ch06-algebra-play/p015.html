  1. Show, using algebra, that a three-digit number minus the number made by reversing all three of its digits is always a multiple of $99$.

6.10Undoing What Was Done

Karim fell asleep under a tree and woke to find a genie standing over him. “Walk once round that banyan,” said the genie, “and the money in your pocket will double. But since I am making you rich, you will give me eight coins after each round.” Eight coins seemed a trifle, and Karim agreed.

He walked round. His coins doubled, and he handed over eight. He walked round again; they doubled again, and again he paid eight. He walked round a third time, and the coins doubled a third time — and he was left holding exactly eight, which was precisely what he now owed. The genie laughed and vanished.

How many coins did Karim start with? Nothing in the story says, and the only number given is the eight at the end — so the natural thing is to walk the story backwards, undoing each step as you come to it.
