6.9Undoing What Was Done

At a school fair, one stall runs a doubling game. In each round, the stall doubles the tokens a player has. The player then pays a fee of $16$ tokens to go on. Farhan plays three rounds.

Twice, his tokens double and he pays $16$. After the third doubling he has exactly $16$ tokens, the fee he owes. He pays it and has nothing left.

How many tokens did Farhan start with? The only number we know is the $16$ at the end. So walk back through the story, and undo each step.

at the end $16$ tokens, just after the third doubling
before that doubling $16 \div 2 = 8$
before the second fee $8 + 16 = 24$, so before that doubling $12$
before the first fee $12 + 16 = 28$, so before that doubling $14$
