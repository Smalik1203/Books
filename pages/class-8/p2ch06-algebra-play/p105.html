Example 2

A trick runs: think of a number, multiply by $4$, add $k$, divide by $2$, subtract twice the number you thought of. It always ends at $9$. Find $k$, and check with the number $5$.

Solution.

Step 1 $4x + k$, then $\dfrac{4x + k}{2} = 2x + \dfrac{k}{2}$
Step 2 $2x + \dfrac{k}{2} - 2x = \dfrac{k}{2}$ the unknown cancels
Step 3 $\dfrac{k}{2} = 9$, so $k = 18$
Step 4 with $5$: $20 \to 38 \to 19 \to 19 - 10 = 9$ check
Answer $k = 18$

Example 3

Take your age, double it, add $5$, multiply by $50$, and add your house number, which is below $100$. A friend's answer is $1537$. Find the age and the house number.

Solution.

Step 1 $(2A + 5) \times 50 + h = 100A + 250 + h$ age $A$, house $h$
Step 2 $1537 - 250 = 1287$ take off the constant
Step 3 $1287 = 100 \times 12 + 87$ $h$ is below $100$
Answer Age $12$, house number $87$
Example 4

A different date trick: multiply the month by $4$, add $3$, multiply by $25$, and add the day. What must be subtracted from the answer, and what date gives $1004$?

Solution.

Step 1 $(4m + 3) \times 25 + d = 100m + 75 + d$ $4 \times 25 = 100$
Step 2 subtract $75$: $1004 - 75 = 929$
Step 3 $929 = 100 \times 9 + 29$ split at the hundreds
Answer Subtract $75$; the date is the twenty-ninth of September
