Example 2 · Single correct

A trick runs: think of a number, multiply by 4, add $k$, divide by 2, and subtract twice the number. What must $k$ be for the answer always to be 7?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 7
  2. 14
  3. 28
  4. 3.5

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{4n + k}{2} - 2n = 2n + \frac{k}{2} - 2n = \frac{k}{2}$.
Step 2$\frac{k}{2} = 7$, so $k = 14$.
Answer(b) 14
Example 3 · Single correct

A date trick: multiply the month by 4, add 3, multiply by 25, and add the day. What must be taken from the result to show the month followed by the two digits of the day?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 25
  2. 3
  3. 75
  4. 100

Solution.

Step 1$(4m + 3) \times 25 + d = 100m + 75 + d$.
Step 2Taking away 75 leaves $100m + d$, the month followed by the day.
Answer(c) 75
Example 4 · Single correct

The nine dates of a $3 \times 3$ square on a calendar add to 153. What is the smallest date in the square?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 17
  2. 13
  3. 1
  4. 9

Solution.

Step 1The nine dates are the centre $c$ and pairs around it that add to $2c$, so their sum is $9c = 153$, and $c = 17$.
Step 2The smallest, top left, is $c - 8 = 9$.
Answer(d) 9
