Example 5

Veena thinks of a number, multiplies it by 3, subtracts 5 and doubles the result. She gets 38. What number did she think of?

Solution.

Step 1Undo the steps in reverse: $38 \div 2 = 19$ and $19 + 5 = 24$.
Step 2$24 \div 3 = 8$.
Check$8 \times 3 = 24$, $24 - 5 = 19$ and $19 \times 2 = 38$.
Answer8
Example 6

The digits 2, 7 and 8 are placed in $\square\,\square \times \square$, one in each box. What is the largest product?

Solution.

Step 1The largest digit should multiply: try $72 \times 8 = 576$ and $82 \times 7 = 574$.
Step 2The other four arrangements are smaller: $27 \times 8 = 216$, $28 \times 7 = 196$, $87 \times 2 = 174$ and $78 \times 2 = 156$.
Answer576
