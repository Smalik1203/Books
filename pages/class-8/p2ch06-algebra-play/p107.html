Example 8

The digits $1$, $2$, $3$ and $4$ are placed along the bottom of a four-row pyramid, one in each box. What is the largest top that can be made, and the smallest?

Solution.

Step 1 top $= a + 3b + 3c + d$ the middle two count three times
Step 2 largest: $4$ and $3$ in the middle, $1 + 3 \times 4 + 3 \times 3 + 2 = 24$
Step 3 smallest: $1$ and $2$ in the middle, $3 + 3 \times 1 + 3 \times 2 + 4 = 16$
Answer Largest $24$, smallest $16$

The order of the two middle digits does not matter, and nor does the order of the two ends.

Type 5 · Squares on a calendar

Example 9

A $2 \times 2$ square on a calendar page adds to $104$. Find its four dates.

Solution.

Step 1 $4a + 16 = 104$ $a$ is the top-left date
Step 2 $4a = 88$, so $a = 22$
Step 3 $a + 1 = 23$, $a + 7 = 29$, $a + 8 = 30$
Answer $22$, $23$, $29$, $30$

This needs a month with $30$ days or more, and $22$ must not fall on the last day of its week.

Example 10

The nine dates of a $3 \times 3$ square on a calendar add to $153$. Find the nine dates.

Solution.

Step 1 total $= 9 \times$ centre $9a + 72 = 9(a + 8)$
Step 2 centre $= 153 \div 9 = 17$
Step 3 rows: $9, 10, 11$; $\ 16, 17, 18$; $\ 23, 24, 25$ a week up and down
Step 4 $30 + 51 + 72 = 153$ check
Answer $9$ to $11$, $16$ to $18$ and $23$ to $25$
