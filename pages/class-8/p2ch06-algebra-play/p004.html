Example 2

Use the trick on Teachers’ Day, the $5$th day of the $9$th month, and check that the date comes back.

Solution.

Step 1 the six steps: $9 \to 36 \to 43 \to 215 \to 218 \to 1090 \to 1095$
Step 2 take away $190$: $1095 - 190 = 905$ the added numbers removed
Step 3 read it: $9$ hundreds and $5$ more split at the hundreds
