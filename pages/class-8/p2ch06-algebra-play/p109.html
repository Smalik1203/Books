Example 14

A three-digit number and the two numbers made by cycling its digits add to $2220$. What do its digits add to? Give one number that works.

Solution.

Step 1 the total is $111(a + b + c)$ each digit in each place
Step 2 $a + b + c = 2220 \div 111 = 20$
Step 3 try $488$: $488 + 884 + 848 = 2220$ $4 + 8 + 8 = 20$
Answer The digits add to $20$; $488$ is one such number

Answers will vary: any three digits adding to $20$ will do, such as $596$ or $875$.

Type 9 · Undoing a chain of steps

Example 15

Veena thinks of a number, multiplies it by $3$, subtracts $5$ and doubles the result. She gets $38$. What was her number?

Solution.

Step 1 undo the doubling: $38 \div 2 = 19$ last step first
Step 2 undo the subtraction: $19 + 5 = 24$
Step 3 undo the multiplying: $24 \div 3 = 8$
Step 4 $2(3 \times 8 - 5) = 38$ check forwards
Answer Her number was $8$
Example 16

A genie doubles a traveller's coins at each round and then takes $10$. After two rounds the traveller has $22$ coins. How many did he start with?

Solution.

Step 1 $2^{\,2}(x - 10) + 10 = 22$ $n = 2$, $c = 10$
Step 2 $4(x - 10) = 12$, so $x - 10 = 3$
Step 3 $x = 13$
Step 4 $13 \to 26 - 10 = 16 \to 32 - 10 = 22$ check
Answer He started with $13$ coins
