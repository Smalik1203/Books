Type 8 · Reversing and cycling digits

Example 13

The digits of a two-digit number add to $12$. Reversing the digits gives a number $18$ less. Find the number.

Solution.

Step 1 $(10a + b) - (10b + a) = 9(a - b) = 18$ the reverse is smaller
Step 2 $a - b = 2$, and $b = 12 - a$ the digits add to 12
Step 3 $a - (12 - a) = 2$, so $2a - 12 = 2$ put in $b$
Step 4 $2a = 14$, so $a = 7$ and $b = 12 - 7 = 5$
Answer $75$, and indeed $75 - 57 = 18$
Example 14

A three-digit number and the two numbers made by cycling its digits add to $2220$. What do its digits add to? Give one number that works.

Solution.

Step 1 the total is $111(a + b + c)$ each digit in each place
Step 2 $a + b + c = 2220 \div 111 = 20$
Step 3 try $488$: $488 + 884 + 848 = 2220$ $4 + 8 + 8 = 20$
Answer The digits add to $20$; $488$ is one such number

Answers will vary: any three digits adding to $20$ will do, such as $596$ or $875$.

Example 15

A six-digit number is made by writing a three-digit number twice over. Divided by $77$, it gives $6019$. What number was written twice?

Solution.

Step 1 the six-digit number is the three-digit number $\times 1001$ what repeating does
Step 2 $1001 = 7 \times 11 \times 13 = 77 \times 13$ three small primes
Step 3 so dividing by $77$ leaves the number $\times 13$, which is $6019$
Step 4 $6019 \div 13 = 463$ undo the 13
Step 5 $463 \times 1001 = 463463$, and $463463 \div 77 = 6019$ check
Answer $463$, written twice as $463463$
