Example 13 · Numerical answer

The digits 2, 7 and 8 are placed in $\square\,\square \times \square$, one in each box. What is the largest product?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The largest digit should multiply: try $72 \times 8 = 576$ and $82 \times 7 = 574$.
Step 2The other arrangements give at most $87 \times 2 = 174$, so the largest is 576.
Answer576
Example 14 · Matching

In a three-row pyramid each box is the sum of the two below it. The bottom row is $x$, $2x$, $3x$ and the top is 40. Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $x$(1) 25
(Q) The left box of the middle row(2) 40
(R) The right box of the middle row(3) 5
(S) The top(4) 15

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The middle row is $x + 2x = 3x$ and $2x + 3x = 5x$, and the top is $8x = 40$, so $x = 5$.
Step 2So the middle row is 15 and 25.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
