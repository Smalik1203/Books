the top row three triangles make $27$, so a triangle is $9$
the second row two circles and a $9$ make $19$, so two circles make $10$
giving a circle is $5$

Written with letters, the two rows are $3t = 27$ and $2c + t = 19$, which is a pair of equations in two unknowns — and the pair was easy only because the first one had a single unknown to begin with. A grid in which every row mixes both shapes is a harder problem. The way into it is the one used on the pyramid: write both rows down, use one to say what $t$ is in terms of $c$, and put that into the other.
