With letters, the two rows are $3t = 24$ and $2c + t = 22$. This is a pair of equations in two unknowns. It was easy only because the first equation had just one unknown. When every row mixes both shapes, use the method from the pyramid. Use one equation to write $t$ in terms of $c$, and put that into the other equation.

Example 5

In a grid, two stars and a moon total $17$. A star and three moons total $21$. Find both shapes.

Solution. No row has only one kind of shape, so write one unknown in terms of the other. Let a star be $s$ and a moon be $m$.

Step 1 first row: $2s + m = 17$, so $m = 17 - 2s$
Step 2 second row: $s + 3(17 - 2s) = 21$ substitute
Step 3 $51 - 5s = 21$, so $5s = 30$ and $s = 6$
Step 4 $m = 17 - 12 = 5$
Check $6 + 3 \times 5 = 21$
Answer a star is $6$ and a moon is $5$
Shapes are letter-numbers

A shape in a grid works like a letter. Start with a row that has only one unknown. If there is no such row, write one unknown in terms of the other and substitute.
