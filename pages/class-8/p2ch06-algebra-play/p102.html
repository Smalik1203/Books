Where the constant ends up

Follow the five steps on $x$, keeping $k$ as it stands. The doubling and the halving between them leave $x$ untouched, so the subtraction at the end removes it entirely and only the constants survive.

after doubling $2(x + k) - 8 = 2x + 2k - 8$
halving $x + k - 4$
subtracting $x$ $k - 4$, which is to be $6$, so $k = 10$

The $8$ was halved to $4$ on its way through and the $k$ was not, because $k$ was added before the doubling and $8$ after it. Where a constant is inserted decides how much of it survives, and that is the whole of the designer's control.
