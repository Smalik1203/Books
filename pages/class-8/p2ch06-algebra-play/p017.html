Think and Reflect

The genie wanted Karim cleaned out on the third round exactly — not the second, and not the fourth.

Using $2^{\,n}(x - c) + c$, work out what charge would take everything from a traveller with $x$ coins in exactly three rounds. Check your answer against $x = 7$ and $c = 8$. What charge would do it in two rounds instead, and why is the greedier genie the one that waits?

  1. Three shrines each have a magical pond before them, and flowers dipped in any of the ponds double in number. A pilgrim dips his flowers in the first pond and leaves some at the first shrine; dips what is left in the second pond and leaves the same number at the second shrine; dips what is left in the third pond and leaves all of them at the third shrine. What is the smallest number of flowers he could have started with, and how many did each shrine receive?
