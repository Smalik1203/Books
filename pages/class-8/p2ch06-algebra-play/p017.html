Exercise Set 6.5
  1. Three shrines each have a magical pond before them, and flowers dipped in any of the ponds double in number. A pilgrim dips his flowers in the first pond and leaves some at the first shrine; dips what is left in the second pond and leaves the same number at the second shrine; dips what is left in the third pond and leaves all of them at the third shrine. What is the smallest number of flowers he could have started with, and how many did each shrine receive?
  1. A farm keeps horses and hens. Between them the animals have $55$ heads and $150$ legs. How many are horses?
    1. Answer it with a letter-number.
    2. Answer it again without one, by first working out how many legs there would be if every animal were a hen.
  1. A mother is five times her daughter's age. In six years she will be three times her daughter's age. How old is the daughter now?
  1. Gauri and Naina pass on the road with their cows. Gauri says, “You have twice as many cows as I do.” Naina says, “True — but if I gave you three of mine we would have the same number.” How many cows has each?
  1. A dosa cart costs ₹$5{,}000$ a day to rent, and each dosa costs ₹$10$ to make.
    1. Selling $100$ dosas a day, what price gives a profit of ₹$2{,}000$?
    2. If customers will pay only ₹$50$, how many dosas a day are needed for the same profit?
  1. Work out each of these fractions. $$\dfrac{1}{3}, \qquad \dfrac{1 + 3}{5 + 7}, \qquad \dfrac{1 + 3 + 5}{7 + 9 + 11}$$ What do you notice? Write the next one in the pattern, and use what you know about the sum of the first $n$ odd numbers to explain why it happens.
  1. In the story of Karim and the genie, suppose the charge had been sixteen coins instead of eight, and that he still ended the third round holding exactly what he owed. How many coins did he start with?
  1. A pilgrim visits four shrines with four magical ponds, under the same rule as question $1$. What is the smallest number of flowers he can start with? Do the same for $n$ shrines, and say what the two numbers have to do with powers of two.
