Objective questions
  1. Think of a number, add $9$, double it, take away $6$, halve it and take away the number you thought of. The answer is always
    1. $3$
    2. $6$
    3. $9$
    4. $12$
  1. The number made by reversing the digits of the two-digit number $10a + b$ is
    1. $10b + a$
    2. $10a - b$
    3. $ab$
    4. $10(a + b)$
  1. The date trick of this chapter gives $100m + 190 + d$. A friend’s answer is $1005$. The date is
    1. $5$ October
    2. $5$ August
    3. $25$ August
    4. $15$ August
  1. The top of the four-row number pyramid with the bottom row $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$ is
    1. $10$
    2. $15$
    3. $20$
    4. $40$
  1. A trick says: think of a number, multiply by $5$, add $k$, divide by $5$ and take away the number. The answer is always $7$. The value of $k$ is
    1. $7$
    2. $12$
    3. $2$
    4. $35$
  1. The four dates of a $2 \times 2$ square on a calendar add to $76$. The smallest of the four dates is
    1. $15$
    2. $19$
    3. $23$
    4. $60$
  1. A two-digit number minus its reverse is $45$. The two digits of the number differ by
    1. $4$
    2. $5$
    3. $9$
    4. $45$
