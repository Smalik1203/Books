By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Find the top of the three-row number pyramid whose bottom row is $7$, $9$, $4$, without filling in the middle row. Show how you found it.
  1. Think of a number $x$, multiply it by $4$, add $20$, divide by $4$ and take away $x$. Show that the answer is always $5$.
  1. The top-left date of a $2 \times 2$ square on a calendar is $13$. Find the total of its four dates without adding them.
  1. The four dates of a $2 \times 2$ square on a calendar add to $88$. Find the four dates.
  1. A two-digit number has tens digit $p$ and units digit $q$. Write the number and its reverse, and find their sum in its simplest form.
  1. Can $75$ be the difference between a two-digit number and its reverse? Give a reason.
  1. Using each of the digits $3$, $6$ and $8$ once, find the largest product of the form $\square\,\square \times \square$. Give a reason for your choice.
  1. A three-row number pyramid has the top $40$ and the bottom row $9$, $c$, $11$. Find $c$.
  1. Find $472 + 724 + 247$ without adding column by column. Give a reason.
  1. In a game, Rohan’s tokens are doubled and he then pays $6$ tokens. After one round he has $20$ tokens. Find the number of tokens he started with.
Short answer
  1. Show that the sum of any three consecutive whole numbers is $3$ times the middle number. Hence find three consecutive whole numbers that add to $72$.
  1. A trick says: think of a number, multiply by $6$, add $18$, divide by $3$ and take away twice the number you thought of. Show that the answer is always $6$. Also find the number to add in place of $18$ so that the trick ends at $10$.
  1. The bottom row of a three-row number pyramid is $x$, $2x$, $x + 6$, and its top is $48$. Find $x$ and complete the pyramid.
  1. The digits of a two-digit number add to $11$. The number made by reversing its digits is $27$ more than the number. Find the number.
