Example 9

A four-row number pyramid has the bottom row $2$, $x$, $y$, $3$. Each box is the sum of the two boxes just below it, so the next row up is $2 + x$, $x + y$ and $y + 3$.

  1. The top of the pyramid, in terms of $x$ and $y$, is
    1. $5 + x + y$
    2. $5 + 3x + 3y$
    3. $5 + 2x + 2y$
    4. $6 + 3x + 3y$
  2. Numerical answer. The top is $50$ and $x = 7$. Find $y$.
  3. Numerical answer. With these values, what is the left-hand number in the row just below the top?

Solution.

Step 1The multipliers for four rows are $1$, $3$, $3$, $1$. So $2 + 3x + 3y + 3 = 5 + 3x + 3y$ is the top.
Step 2$5 + 21 + 3y = 50$, so $3y = 24$ and $y = 8$.
Step 3The rows are $2, 7, 8, 3$; then $9, 15, 11$; then $24, 26$; and $24 + 26 = 50$.
CheckWith $x = 7$ and $y = 8$, the top is $5 + 21 + 24 = 50$.
Answer(i) (b); (ii) 8; (iii) 24
