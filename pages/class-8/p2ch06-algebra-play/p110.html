Type 10 · Word problems with a letter-number

Example 17

A cycle shop has $20$ bicycles and tricycles, with $48$ wheels between them. How many of each are there?

Solution.

Step 1 $b$ bicycles and $20 - b$ tricycles one letter only
Step 2 $2b + 3(20 - b) = 48$ count the wheels
Step 3 $60 - b = 48$, so $b = 12$
Step 4 $2 \times 12 + 3 \times 8 = 48$ check
Answer $12$ bicycles and $8$ tricycles
