Example 10

In each round of a game, a stall doubles a player’s tokens, and the player then pays a fee of $c$ tokens. After $n$ rounds, a player who started with $x$ tokens has $2^{\,n}(x - c) + c$ tokens.

  1. With a fee of $10$, a player who starts with $13$ tokens has, after $3$ rounds,
    1. $34$
    2. $24$
    3. $16$
    4. $104$
  2. Numerical answer. A player starts with $21$ tokens and has nothing left after exactly $3$ rounds. What is the fee?
  3. Numerical answer. With a fee of $24$, a player starts with $25$ tokens. After how many rounds does the player first have more than $100$ tokens?

Solution.

Step 1$8 \times (13 - 10) + 10 = 34$. Check: $13 \to 16 \to 22 \to 34$.
Step 2$8(21 - c) + c = 0$, so $168 = 7c$ and $c = 24$.
Step 3The bracket is $25 - 24 = 1$, so after $n$ rounds the player has $2^{\,n} + 24$. After $6$ rounds this is $88$, and after $7$ rounds it is $152$.
Answer(i) (a); (ii) 24; (iii) 7
