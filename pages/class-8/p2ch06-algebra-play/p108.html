Example 10 · Multiple correct

A cycle shop has 20 bicycles and tricycles, with 48 wheels between them. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. There are 12 bicycles.
  2. There are 8 tricycles.
  3. If all 20 were bicycles there would be 40 wheels.
  4. If all 20 were tricycles there would be 60 wheels.

Solution.

Step 1All bicycles would give 40 wheels; each tricycle adds 1 more, so there are $48 - 40 = 8$ tricycles and 12 bicycles.
Step 2Check: $12 \times 2 + 8 \times 3 = 48$; all tricycles would give 60.
Answer(a), (b), (c), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

Veena thinks of a number, multiplies it by 3, subtracts 5 and doubles the result. She gets 38. What number did she think of?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Undo the steps in reverse: $38 \div 2 = 19$ and $19 + 5 = 24$.
Step 2$24 \div 3 = 8$.
Answer8
Example 12 · Numerical answer

A genie doubles a traveller's coins at each round and then takes 10. After two rounds the traveller has 30 coins. How many did the traveller start with?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Before the second round: $(30 + 10) \div 2 = 20$.
Step 2Before the first: $(20 + 10) \div 2 = 15$.
Answer15
