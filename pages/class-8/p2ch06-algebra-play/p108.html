Example 7

In a three-row pyramid each box is the sum of the two below it. The bottom row is $x$, $2x$, $3x$ and the top is 40. Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $x$(1) 25
(Q) The left box of the middle row(2) 40
(R) The right box of the middle row(3) 5
(S) The top(4) 15
  1. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The middle row is $x + 2x = 3x$ and $2x + 3x = 5x$.
Step 2The top is $3x + 5x = 8x$, and $8x = 40$, so $x = 5$.
Step 3So the bottom row is 5, 10, 15, and the middle row is $3 \times 5 = 15$ and $5 \times 5 = 25$, as in List II.
CheckThe bottom row 5, 10, 15 gives the middle row 15 and 25, and $15 + 25 = 40$. So each item in List I has exactly one value in List II.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
