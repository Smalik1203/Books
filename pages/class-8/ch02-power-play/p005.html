2.3The Laws of Indices

On a festival morning, Asha sends a greeting to $3$ friends. An hour later, each of them sends it on to $3$ new friends. Every hour, each person who got the greeting in the last hour sends it to $3$ more people. How many people get it in the fourth hour?

In the first hour, $3$ people get it. In the second hour, each of those $3$ sends it to $3$ more, so $9$ people get it. In the third hour $27$ people get it, and in the fourth hour $81$. And $81 = 3^4$: one factor of $3$ for each hour.
