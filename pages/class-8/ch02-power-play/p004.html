The brackets are not decoration. $(-4)^2 = 16$, but $-4^2 = -16$, because without brackets the power applies to the $4$ alone and the minus sign is applied afterwards.

The exponent $0$ is a different kind of question, since $n$ multiplied by itself no times is not something you can picture. We come to it in § 2.4, where the laws of the next section will settle it for us, and settle it in only one possible way.

2.3The Laws of Indices

A king had three daughters. He gave each daughter three baskets. Each basket held three keys, and each key opened three rooms. How many rooms were there?

Three daughters, three baskets each, so nine baskets. Three keys to a basket, so twenty-seven keys. Three rooms to a key, so eighty-one rooms — and $81 = 3^4$, one factor of three for each layer of the story.
