Type 2 · Negative bases and brackets

Example 2

Find $(-2)^4 - 2^4$, then $(-1)^{25} + (-1)^{26}$, then $-3^2 \times (-3)^2$.

Solution.

Step 1 $(-2)^4 = 16$ and $2^4 = 16$ even power, positive
Step 2 $(-1)^{25} = -1$ and $(-1)^{26} = 1$ odd, then even
Step 3 $-3^2 = -9$ and $(-3)^2 = 9$ brackets decide the sign
Answer $16 - 16 = 0$,  $-1 + 1 = 0$,  $-9 \times 9 = -81$

Type 3 · Multiplying powers, and a power of a power

Example 3

Simplify $(a^2)^3 \times a^4 \times a$.

Solution.

Step 1 $(a^2)^3 = a^{2 \times 3} = a^6$ multiply the exponents
Step 2 $a^6 \times a^4 \times a^1 = a^{6+4+1}$ a lone $a$ is $a^1$
Answer $a^{11}$
Example 4

Find $n$ if $(2^3)^n \times 2^2 = 2^{14}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $(2^3)^n \times 2^2 = 2^{3n} \times 2^2 = 2^{3n+2}$ both laws
Step 2 $3n + 2 = 14$ same base, so equal exponents
Step 3 $3n = 12$
Answer $n = 4$
