Example 1

What is $\left(3^{-1} + 4^{-1}\right)^{-1}$?

  1. $\frac{7}{12}$
  2. 7
  3. $\frac{1}{7}$
  4. $\frac{12}{7}$

Solution.

Step 1$3^{-1} + 4^{-1} = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{7}{12}$.
Step 2The power $-1$ takes the reciprocal: $\frac{12}{7}$.
CheckThe reciprocal of $\frac{12}{7}$ is $\frac{7}{12}$, which is $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4}$.
Answer(d) $\frac{12}{7}$
Example 2

What is the last digit of $3^{25} + 7^{25}$?

  1. 0
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 8

Solution.

Step 1The last digits of the powers of 3 repeat 3, 9, 7, 1, and of 7 repeat 7, 9, 3, 1: every 4 powers.
Step 2$25 = 4 \times 6 + 1$, so $3^{25}$ ends in 3 and $7^{25}$ in 7; $3 + 7 = 10$ ends in 0.
Check$3^5 = 243$ and $7^5 = 16807$ end in 3 and 7, as the cycles say.
Answer(a) 0
