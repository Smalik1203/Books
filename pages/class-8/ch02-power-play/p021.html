Think and Reflect

What date was one billion seconds ago? Estimate it before you calculate it — a year, a decade, a century?

Then work it out: a year is about $3.15 \times 10^7$ seconds. Most people are badly wrong on this one, and the reason is the same reason they were wrong about the folded sheet.

The reason, in both cases, is that we have no feel for repeated multiplication. Our instincts were built for adding. A number that doubles forty-six times, or a billion seconds laid end to end, is outside anything experience has prepared us for — and the only reliable way through is to stop guessing and let the notation do the work.
