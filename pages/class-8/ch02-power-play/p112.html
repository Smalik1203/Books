Example 20

How many seconds are there in a week? Give the answer in standard form, and then to one figure.

Solution.

Step 1 $7 \times 24 \times 60 \times 60 = 604\,800$ days, hours, minutes
Step 2 $604\,800 = 6.048 \times 10^5$
Answer $6.048 \times 10^5$ seconds, or about $6 \times 10^5$

Type 11 · The last digit of a power

Example 21

Find the last digit of $3^{25} + 7^{25}$.

Solution.

Step 1 last digits of powers of $3$: $3, 9, 7, 1$; of $7$: $7, 9, 3, 1$ each cycle has length $4$
Step 2 $25 = 4 \times 6 + 1$ one past a whole cycle
Step 3 $3^{25}$ ends in $3$ and $7^{25}$ ends in $7$
Answer $3 + 7 = 10$, so the last digit is $0$
