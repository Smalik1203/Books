Example 5

Simplify $\dfrac{5^{40}}{5^{12}}$ and $\dfrac{3^8 \times 3^5}{3^{10}}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $5^{40} \div 5^{12} = 5^{40-12} = 5^{28}$ subtract the exponents
Step 2 $3^8 \times 3^5 = 3^{13}$ join the top first
Step 3 $3^{13} \div 3^{10} = 3^3$ then subtract
Answer $5^{28}$ and $27$

The first needs only a subtraction. Writing out forty fives would take far longer.
