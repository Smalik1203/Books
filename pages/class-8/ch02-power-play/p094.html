1. Paper decorations are hung in many homes at Diwali. Sneha takes a square sheet of coloured paper of side $64$ cm and cuts it into $4$ equal squares. She then cuts each piece into $4$ equal squares, and she goes on in this way. The table shows the number of pieces and their sides after the first rounds.

    Round012
    Pieces1416
    Side (cm)643216

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Write the number of pieces after $5$ rounds as a power of $4$ and as a power of $2$.
    2. Find the side of each piece after $5$ rounds.
    3. After how many rounds is the side of each piece $2^{-1}$ cm? How many pieces are there then?
Objective questions
  1. $2^5 \times 2^3$ is equal to
    1. $2^{15}$
    2. $4^8$
    3. $2^2$
    4. $2^8$
  1. $(5^2)^3$ is equal to
    1. $5^5$
    2. $5^6$
    3. $5^8$
    4. $5^9$
  1. $0.00058$ in standard form is
    1. $58 \times 10^{-5}$
    2. $5.8 \times 10^{4}$
    3. $5.8 \times 10^{-4}$
    4. $5.8 \times 10^{-3}$
  1. If $2^n = 32$, then the value of $n$ is
    1. $5$
    2. $16$
    3. $6$
    4. $4$
  1. $\left(\dfrac{2}{5}\right)^{-3}$ is equal to
    1. $\dfrac{8}{125}$
    2. $-\dfrac{8}{125}$
    3. $\dfrac{125}{8}$
    4. $\dfrac{6}{15}$
  1. Which of these is not a power of $2$?
    1. $8^4$
    2. $16^3$
    3. $32^2$
    4. $6^2$
