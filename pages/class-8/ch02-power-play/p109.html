Example 11 · Numerical answer

Find the value of $\frac{2^5 \times 3^4 \times 7}{6^3 \times 14}$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$6^3 = 2^3 \times 3^3$ and $14 = 2 \times 7$, so the denominator is $2^4 \times 3^3 \times 7$.
Step 2Divide: $2^{5 - 4} \times 3^{4 - 3} = 2 \times 3 = 6$.
Answer6
Example 12 · Numerical answer

Find $m$ if $5^m \div 5^{-3} = 5^5$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$5^m \div 5^{-3} = 5^{m - (-3)} = 5^{m + 3}$.
Step 2$m + 3 = 5$, so $m = 2$.
Answer2
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A locker code is three letters, each from A to E, followed by two digits. How many codes are there?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Each letter can be chosen in 5 ways and each digit in 10.
Step 2$5^3 \times 10^2 = 125 \times 100 = 12\,500$.
Answer12500
