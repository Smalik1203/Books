Example 11

Find $m$ if $5^m \div 5^{-3} = 5^5$.

Solution.

Step 1 $5^m \div 5^{-3} = 5^{m-(-3)} = 5^{m+3}$ subtract the exponents
Step 2 $m + 3 = 5$ same base
Answer $m = 2$
Example 12

Simplify $\dfrac{(x^{-2})^3 \times x^4}{x^{-5}}$, for $x \ne 0$.

Solution.

Step 1 $(x^{-2})^3 = x^{-6}$ multiply the exponents
Step 2 $x^{-6} \times x^4 = x^{-2}$ add the exponents
Step 3 $x^{-2} \div x^{-5} = x^{-2-(-5)}$ subtract the exponents
Answer $x^3$

Example 13

Write as an ordinary number: $(3 \times 10^4) + (5 \times 10^2) + (7 \times 10^{-1}) + (2 \times 10^{-3})$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3 \times 10^4 = 30000$ and $5 \times 10^2 = 500$ whole-number columns
Step 2 $7 \times 10^{-1} = 0.7$ and $2 \times 10^{-3} = 0.002$ tenths and thousandths
Step 3 the thousands, tens, units and hundredths columns hold $0$ no term for them
Answer $30500.702$
