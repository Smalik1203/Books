1. Computers store photos, songs and videos as bytes. Aarav looks after the computers in his school’s laboratory and plans how much storage to buy. Storage is measured in kilobytes, megabytes, gigabytes and terabytes. In this question, take each unit to be $2^{10}$ times the unit before it, as the table shows.

    UnitKBMBGBTB
    Bytes$2^{10}$$2^{20}$$2^{30}$$2^{40}$

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the number of bytes in $16$ GB, as a power of $2$.
    2. A song takes $4$ MB. Find the number of such songs that fit in $16$ GB.
    3. A hard disk holds $2^{41}$ bytes. How many TB is this? Using $2^{10} \approx 10^3$, write the number of bytes in standard form.

  1. The planets move round the Sun at very different distances. Neha is making a model of the solar system for her school’s science fair. She finds the distances of four planets from the Sun and writes them in standard form, as in the table. She wants her model to fit inside the school hall.

    PlanetMercuryEarthJupiterNeptune
    Distance (km)$5.8 \times 10^{7}$$1.5 \times 10^{8}$$7.8 \times 10^{8}$$4.5 \times 10^{9}$

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Write the Earth’s distance from the Sun as an ordinary number.
    2. How many times as far from the Sun as the Earth is Neptune?
    3. In her model, $1$ cm stands for $10^{7}$ km. Find the distance of Neptune from the Sun in the model, in metres.

  1. Every vehicle in India carries a registration number. In one state, the last part of each number is three capital letters, each from A to Z, followed by three digits, each from 0 to 9. Joseph works at the transport office. He must plan how many different numbers can be given out in one district before a new series is needed.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many different endings of three digits, from 000 to 999, are there?
    2. Find the number of different sets of three letters.
    3. Find the number of vehicle numbers possible for the district, as a product of powers and in standard form.
