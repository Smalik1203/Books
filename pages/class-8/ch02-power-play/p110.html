Example 9

The last digits of the powers of $2$ repeat as $2, 4, 8, 6$, and of $9$ as $9, 1$.

  1. The last digit of $2^{50}$ is
    1. $2$
    2. $4$
    3. $8$
    4. $6$
  2. Numerical answer. Find the last digit of $2^{50} \times 9^{99}$.

Solution.

Step 1$50 = 4 \times 12 + 2$, so $2^{50}$ ends in 4; $9^{99}$ ends in 9; $4 \times 9 = 36$.
Answer(i) (b) 4; (ii) 6
