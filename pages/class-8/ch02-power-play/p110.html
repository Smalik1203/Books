Example 14

Write $0.000\,000\,052$ and $3\,250\,000\,000$ in standard form, and find their product in standard form.

Solution.

Step 1 $0.000\,000\,052 = 5.2 \times 10^{-8}$ point moves $8$ right
Step 2 $3\,250\,000\,000 = 3.25 \times 10^9$ point moves $9$ left
Step 3 $5.2 \times 3.25 = 16.9$ and $10^{-8} \times 10^9 = 10^1$ multiply, and add exponents
Step 4 $16.9 \times 10^1 = 1.69 \times 10^2$ coefficient below $10$
Answer $5.2 \times 10^{-8}$, $\ 3.25 \times 10^9$; product $1.69 \times 10^2$
Example 15

Arrange in ascending order: $3.2 \times 10^{-3}$, $4.1 \times 10^{-4}$, $2.9 \times 10^{-3}$, $1.5 \times 10^{-2}$.

Solution.

Step 1 exponents $-4$, then $-3$ and $-3$, then $-2$ exponents first
Step 2 between the two with exponent $-3$: $2.9 \lt 3.2$ then coefficients
Answer $4.1 \times 10^{-4}$, $\ 2.9 \times 10^{-3}$, $\ 3.2 \times 10^{-3}$, $\ 1.5 \times 10^{-2}$
Example 16

A factory makes $4.5 \times 10^5$ bottle caps a day. How many does it make in $300$ days? Give the answer in standard form.

Solution.

Step 1 $300 = 3 \times 10^2$
Step 2 $(4.5 \times 10^5) \times (3 \times 10^2) = 13.5 \times 10^7$ multiply, and add exponents
Step 3 $13.5 \times 10^7 = 1.35 \times 10^8$ coefficient below $10$
Answer $1.35 \times 10^8$ bottle caps
