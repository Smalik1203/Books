$2^{46}$ $= 2^{40} \times 2^6 = (2^{10})^4 \times 64$
$\approx (10^3)^4 \times 64 = 6.4 \times 10^{13}$

Multiply by $0.001$ and the thickness is about $6.4 \times 10^{10}$ cm, which is $6.4 \times 10^{5}$ km. The exact figure is $7.04 \times 10^5$ km, so the estimate is out by about a tenth — and it was got without multiplying anything larger than $64$.

That is what this chapter buys you. A number too big to write became a number you could reason about, and the reasoning was three laws and a rounding.
