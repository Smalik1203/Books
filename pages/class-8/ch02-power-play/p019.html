1. If everybody in India lay end to end in a line, how long would the line be? Give the answer in kilometres, in standard form, and compare it with the distance to the Moon.
  1. Estimate the total time a person spends eating in a lifetime, in seconds. Then write it as a fraction of the total seconds in that lifetime.
  1. What date was one billion seconds ago? Take a year to be $3.15 \times 10^7$ seconds.
  1. A sheet of paper $0.001$ cm thick is folded $n$ times. Write an expression for its thickness, and find the smallest $n$ for which it would pass the top of a $100$ m building.
  1. Two children play a game: in ten seconds, write down the largest number you can using only digits and the ordinary operations. One writes $10\,000\,000\,000\,000$ and the other writes $999\,999 \times 999\,999$. Who wins, and how can you tell without multiplying?
