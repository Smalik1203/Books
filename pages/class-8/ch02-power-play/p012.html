2.5Powers of Ten

Our numerals are built on the base $10$. Writing its powers shows what place value really means. Take $36\,208$ in expanded form:

$(3 \times 10000) + (6 \times 1000) + (2 \times 100) + (0 \times 10) + 8$

Every multiplier here is a power of ten, so

$36208 = (3 \times 10^4) + (6 \times 10^3) + (2 \times 10^2) + (0 \times 10^1) + (8 \times 10^0)$

Look at the exponents: $4, 3, 2, 1, 0$, one for each column, counting down from the left. The exponent tells you the place of the digit.
