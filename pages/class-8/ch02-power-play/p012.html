Example 5

Simplify, leaving each answer as a single power with a positive exponent.

$2^{-4} \times 2^7$ $= 2^{-4+7} = 2^3$ $8$
$3^2 \times 3^{-5} \times 3^6$ $= 3^{2-5+6} = 3^3$ $27$
$p^3 \div p^{10}$ $= p^{-7} = \dfrac{1}{p^7}$
$(13^{-2})^{-3}$ $= 13^{(-2) \times (-3)} = 13^6$

Every line is the same three laws. The only new thing is that the arithmetic on the exponents is now arithmetic with negative numbers, which you can already do.
