Example 5

Write $27^4 \times 9^3$ as a single power of $3$.

Solution.

Step 1 $27^4 = (3^3)^4 = 3^{12}$ $27 = 3^3$
Step 2 $9^3 = (3^2)^3 = 3^6$ $9 = 3^2$
Step 3 $3^{12} \times 3^6 = 3^{12+6}$ add the exponents
Answer $3^{18}$

Example 6

Find $8^4 \times 125^4$ as a power of $10$.

Solution.

Step 1 $8^4 \times 125^4 = (8 \times 125)^4$ the exponents match
Step 2 $= 1000^4 = (10^3)^4$
Answer $10^{12}$
Example 7

Find the value of $\dfrac{15^4}{5^4 \times 3^2}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{15^4}{5^4} = \left(\dfrac{15}{5}\right)^4 = 3^4$ the exponents match
Step 2 $\dfrac{3^4}{3^2} = 3^{4-2} = 3^2$ subtract the exponents
Answer $9$
