Very short answer
  1. Write $4^3 \times 2^5$ as a single power of $2$.
  1. Find the value of $3^{-2} + 2^{-2} + 6^0$.
  1. Is $(-1)^{25} + (-1)^{24}$ positive, negative or zero? Give a reason.
  1. Write $0.000\,305$ in standard form, and $7.02 \times 10^6$ as an ordinary number.
  1. If $5^{x} \times 5^{3} = 5^{11}$, find the value of $x$.
  1. Find the value of $\left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-3}$.
  1. Show that $(2^3)^4 = (2^4)^3$, and find their common value as a power of $2$.
  1. Write $3375$ as a product of powers of primes, and hence as a single power.
  1. Which is larger, $2^{30}$ or $8^{9}$? Give a reason.
  1. A number multiplied by $10^{-3}$ gives $4.2$. Find the number.
Short answer
  1. Simplify $\dfrac{9^3 \times 27 \times 3^{-2}}{81^2}$ and find its value.
  1. Find $x$ if $\left(\tfrac{3}{5}\right)^{x} \times \left(\tfrac{3}{5}\right)^{-4} = \left(\tfrac{3}{5}\right)^{3}$. Also find the value of $\left(\tfrac{3}{5}\right)^{x-5}$.
  1. A school code is two capital letters (A to Z) followed by three digits. Find the number of codes, as a product of powers and in standard form.
  1. A cell splits into two every $20$ minutes. Starting from one cell, find the number of cells after $4$ hours. Give the answer as a power of $2$ and as a number.
  1. Show that $\dfrac{a^{m} \times a^{n}}{a^{m-n}} = a^{2n}$ for any $a \ne 0$. Hence find the value of $\dfrac{2^{7} \times 2^{3}}{2^{4}}$.
