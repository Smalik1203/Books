Case-based questions

  1. A laboratory counts the bacteria in a dish every hour. The number doubles each hour.

    HourBacteria
    0500
    11000
    22000
    34000
    1. Write the number of bacteria after $n$ hours, using a power of $2$.
    2. How many bacteria are there after $10$ hours? Give the answer in standard form.
    3. After how many whole hours does the number first go past $10^6$?

  1. Computer storage is counted in bytes. In this question, take each unit to be $2^{10}$ of the unit before it.

    UnitBytes
    1 kilobyte (KB)$2^{10}$
    1 megabyte (MB)$2^{20}$
    1 gigabyte (GB)$2^{30}$
    1 terabyte (TB)$2^{40}$
    1. How many bytes are there in $16$ GB? Give the answer as a power of $2$.
    2. A song takes $4$ MB. How many such songs fit in $16$ GB?
    3. Using $2^{10} \approx 10^3$, about how many bytes are there in $1$ TB? Give the answer in standard form.
