Think and Reflect

How old is someone who has lived for one crore minutes? Guess first: a month, a year, or twenty years?

Then work it out. A year has about $5.3 \times 10^5$ minutes. Was your guess too small or too large?

One crore minutes is about nineteen years, and most people guess far less. We have little feel for large numbers made by multiplying. Our sense of size was built for adding. The safe way is to stop guessing and let the notation do the work.
