Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $(-1)^{50} = 1$.

    Reason (R): A negative number raised to an even power is positive.

  1. Assertion (A): $(2^3)^2 = 2^6$.

    Reason (R): $2^3 \times 2^2 = 2^5$.

  1. Assertion (A): $10^{-2} = 0.01$.

    Reason (R): A negative exponent makes a number negative.

  1. Assertion (A): $5^{-2}$ is a negative number.

    Reason (R): $5^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{25}$.

Very short answer
  1. Write $125 \times 25 \times 5$ as a single power of $5$.
  1. Find the value of $2^{-1} + 3^0 + 4^{-1}$.
  1. Write $0.000\,305$ in standard form.
Short answer
  1. Simplify $\dfrac{9^3 \times 27 \times 3^{-2}}{81^2}$, and give its value.
  1. Find $x$ if $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^{x} \times \left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^{-4} = \left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^{3}$.
  1. A school gives each student a code of two capital letters (A to Z) followed by three digits. How many codes are possible? Write the answer as a product of powers, and in standard form.
  1. A cell splits into two every $20$ minutes. Starting from one cell, how many cells are there after $4$ hours? Give the answer as a power of $2$ and as a number.
Long answer
  1. Find the value of $\dfrac{(2^5)^2 \times 7^3 \times 3^{-2}}{8^3 \times 7^2 \times 3^{-4}}$, showing each law you use.
  1. Without evaluating any of them, decide which is larger: $2^{75}$ or $3^{50}$; and then $2^{75}$ or $5^{25}$. Explain each step.
  1. Suppose one grain of sand has a mass of $6.5 \times 10^{-4}$ g. About how many grains are there in a bag of $26$ kg of sand? Give the answer in standard form, and say which law of exponents you used.
