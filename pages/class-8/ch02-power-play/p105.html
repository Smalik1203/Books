How fast a rumour travels

Read the situation carefully before writing anything, because the obvious answer is the wrong one. If everybody who knows tells three people who do not, then after an hour there are the original knowers and three times as many new ones — so the number knowing is multiplied by $4$, not by $3$.

start $1$
after 1 hour $1 + 3 = 4$
after 2 hours $4 + 12 = 16 = 4^2$
after $n$ hours $4^n$

We need $4^n \ge 10^6$. Since $4^{10} = 2^{20} = (2^{10})^2$, and $2^{10}$ is just over $10^3$, we get $4^{10}$ just over $10^6$ — in fact $1\,048\,576$. And $4^9$ is a quarter of that, about $2.6 \times 10^5$, which is not enough.

answer $10$ hours a city of a million

Ten hours, from one person, using nothing but conversation. The result is not really about rumours — it is the same arithmetic that governs how an epidemic spreads, how a chain letter propagates, and how a video goes around the world overnight. In each case what looks like an explosion is only a modest number multiplied a modest number of times.

The rice on the chessboard

The squares hold $1, 2, 4, 8, \ldots$ so the sixty-fourth holds $2^{63}$ and the total is

$1 + 2 + 4 + \cdots + 2^{63} = 2^{64} - 1$

The sum is one short of the next power, which you can see by noticing that $1 + 2 = 3$, $1 + 2 + 4 = 7$, $1+2+4+8 = 15$ — always one less than the doubling that comes next.

Estimate it with the same trick as before: $2^{64} = (2^{10})^6 \times 2^4 \approx (10^3)^6 \times 16 = 1.6 \times 10^{19}$, and the true value is $1.8 \times 10^{19}$.

Is it payable? A grain of rice weighs about $0.025$ g, so this is around $4.6 \times 10^{14}$ kg, or $4.6 \times 10^{11}$ tonnes. The world grows about $5 \times 10^8$ tonnes of rice a year, so the debt is roughly a thousand years of the entire world harvest. The king should have read the second half of the contract.
