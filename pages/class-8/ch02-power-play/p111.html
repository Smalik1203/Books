Example 10

Light travels about $3 \times 10^{5}$ km in one second. A year has about $3.2 \times 10^{7}$ seconds. A light-year is the distance that light travels in one year.

  1. In standard form, the distance light travels in one minute is
    1. $1.8 \times 10^{7}$ km
    2. $1.8 \times 10^{6}$ km
    3. $18 \times 10^{6}$ km
    4. $5 \times 10^{3}$ km
  2. Numerical answer. A light-year is about $k \times 10^{12}$ km. Find $k$.
  3. Numerical answer. A star is about $4 \times 10^{13}$ km away. To the nearest whole number, how many years does its light take to reach us?

Solution.

Step 1$3 \times 10^5 \times 60 = 180 \times 10^5 = 1.8 \times 10^7$ km.
Step 2$3 \times 10^5 \times 3.2 \times 10^7 = 9.6 \times 10^{12}$, so $k = 9.6$.
Step 3$4 \times 10^{13} \div (9.6 \times 10^{12}) \approx 4.2$, so about 4 years.
Answer(i) (a) $1.8 \times 10^{7}$ km; (ii) 9.6; (iii) 4
