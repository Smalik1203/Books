Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $(-1)^{50} = 1$.

    Reason (R): A negative number raised to an even power is positive.

  1. Assertion (A): $(2^3)^2 = 2^6$.

    Reason (R): $2^3 \times 2^2 = 2^5$.

  1. Assertion (A): $10^{-2} = 0.01$.

    Reason (R): A negative exponent makes a number negative.

  1. Assertion (A): $5^{-2}$ is a negative number.

    Reason (R): $5^{-2} = \dfrac{1}{25}$.

  1. Assertion (A): $2^{10} \times 5^{10} = 10^{10}$.

    Reason (R): $m^a \times n^a = (mn)^a$.

Case-based questions

  1. Bacteria are tiny living things that grow by splitting. In a school laboratory, Ms Iyer’s class puts a few bacteria in a dish of food and counts them every hour. Each bacterium splits into two every hour, so the number in the dish doubles each hour. The class counts $500$ bacteria at the start, $1000$ after one hour and $2000$ after two hours.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Write the number of bacteria after $n$ hours, using a power of $2$.
    2. Find the number of bacteria after $10$ hours, in standard form.
    3. After how many whole hours does the number first pass $10^6$? Justify your answer.
