  1. The number of people who have watched a video triples every day. On day $0$, $200$ people have watched it. (i) Write the number who have watched it after $n$ days. (ii) Hence find the number after $5$ days. (iii) On which day does the number first pass one lakh? Show your working.
  1. A circular park has a radius of $7 \times 10^{2}$ m. Find its area in standard form. One tree is planted for every $1.1 \times 10^{2}$ m² of the park. Find the number of trees that are planted in the park. (Take $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$)
