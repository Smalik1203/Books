2.7Back to the Folded Sheet

Now we can answer the opening question with powers. After $n$ folds, the thickness of the sheet is

$0.1 \times 2^{n}$ mm

After forty-two folds it is $0.1 \times 2^{42}$ mm. We do not need the exact value of $2^{42}$. The laws give a good estimate in two lines. Since $2^{10} = 1024$, which is a little more than $10^3$,

$2^{42}$ $= 2^{40} \times 2^2 = (2^{10})^4 \times 4$
$\approx (10^3)^4 \times 4 = 4 \times 10^{12}$
