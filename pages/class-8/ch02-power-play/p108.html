Example 5

Find the value of $\frac{2^5 \times 3^4 \times 7}{6^3 \times 14}$.

Solution.

Step 1$6^3 = 2^3 \times 3^3$ and $14 = 2 \times 7$, so the denominator is $2^4 \times 3^3 \times 7$.
Step 2The $7$ on top cancels the $7$ below.
Step 3Divide: $2^{5 - 4} \times 3^{4 - 3} = 2 \times 3 = 6$.
Check$2^5 \times 3^4 \times 7 = 18144$ and $6^3 \times 14 = 3024$; $18144 \div 3024 = 6$.
Answer6
Example 6

A locker code is three letters, each from A to E, followed by two digits. How many codes are there?

Solution.

Step 1Each letter can be chosen in 5 ways and each digit in 10.
Step 2There are $5^3 = 125$ groups of letters and $10^2 = 100$ pairs of digits.
Step 3$5^3 \times 10^2 = 125 \times 100 = 12\,500$.
CheckThe first code is AAA00 and the last is EEE99. Every group of letters meets every pair of digits exactly once.
Answer12500
