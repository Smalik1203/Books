Type 5 · Dividing powers

Example 8

Simplify $\dfrac{5^8 \times 25^2}{125^3}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $25^2 = (5^2)^2 = 5^4$ and $125^3 = (5^3)^3 = 5^9$ one base throughout
Step 2 $5^8 \times 5^4 = 5^{12}$ gather the top
Step 3 $5^{12} \div 5^9 = 5^{12-9} = 5^3$ subtract the exponents
Answer $5^3 = 125$
Example 9

Find the value of $\dfrac{2^5 \times 3^4 \times 7}{6^3 \times 14}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $6^3 = 2^3 \times 3^3$ and $14 = 2 \times 7$ break the bottom into primes
Step 2 $6^3 \times 14 = 2^4 \times 3^3 \times 7^1$
Step 3 $2^{5-4} \times 3^{4-3} \times 7^{1-1} = 2^1 \times 3^1 \times 7^0$ one prime at a time
Answer $2 \times 3 \times 1 = 6$

Type 6 · Zero and negative exponents

Example 10

Find the value of $\left(3^{-1} + 4^{-1}\right)^{-1}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3^{-1} = \dfrac13$ and $4^{-1} = \dfrac14$ a reciprocal each
Step 2 $\dfrac13 + \dfrac14 = \dfrac{7}{12}$ add inside the bracket
Step 3 $\left(\dfrac{7}{12}\right)^{-1} = \dfrac{12}{7}$ then invert
Answer $\dfrac{12}{7}$

The power $-1$ outside the bracket does not reach inside it: $3^{-1} + 4^{-1}$ has to be added first.
