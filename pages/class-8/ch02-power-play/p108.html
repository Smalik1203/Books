Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which statements are true for every number $x \ne 0$?

Select all correct options.

  1. $x^0 = 1$
  2. $x^{-2} = \frac{1}{x^2}$
  3. $\frac{(x^{-2})^3 \times x^4}{x^{-5}} = x^3$
  4. $x^2 \times x^3 = x^6$

Solution.

Step 1$x^0 = 1$ and $x^{-2} = \frac{1}{x^2}$ by definition. In (c) the exponents give $-6 + 4 - (-5) = 3$.
Step 2$x^2 \times x^3 = x^5$, not $x^6$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which of these numbers are written in standard form?

Select all correct options.

  1. $3.25 \times 10^9$
  2. $32.5 \times 10^8$
  3. $5.2 \times 10^{-8}$
  4. $0.52 \times 10^{-7}$

Solution.

Step 1Standard form is a number from 1 up to (but not including) 10, times a power of 10.
Step 232.5 is too large and 0.52 too small, although both numbers are correct in value.
Answer(a), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Consider $3.2 \times 10^{-3}$, $4.1 \times 10^{-4}$, $2.9 \times 10^{-3}$ and $1.5 \times 10^{-2}$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The smallest is $4.1 \times 10^{-4}$.
  2. The largest is $1.5 \times 10^{-2}$.
  3. $3.2 \times 10^{-3} < 2.9 \times 10^{-3}$
  4. $3.2 \times 10^{-3} > 1.5 \times 10^{-2}$

Solution.

Step 1Compare the powers of 10 first: $10^{-4} < 10^{-3} < 10^{-2}$.
Step 2So $4.1 \times 10^{-4}$ is smallest and $1.5 \times 10^{-2}$ largest; with the same power, $2.9 < 3.2$.
Answer(a), (b)
