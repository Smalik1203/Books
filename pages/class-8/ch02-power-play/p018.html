Multiply by $0.1$ mm. The thickness is about $4 \times 10^{11}$ mm, which is $4 \times 10^{5}$ km. The exact value is $4.4 \times 10^5$ km, so the estimate is out by less than a tenth. We found it without multiplying anything larger than $4$.

Ten doublings make about a thousand

$2^{10} = 1024$, which is a little more than $10^3$. So every ten doublings multiply a number by a little more than a thousand.

Look at what this estimate did not need. It needed no calculator, no long multiplication and no exact value of $2^{42}$. It needed only the fact that $2^{10}$ is a little more than $10^3$. With this fact, you can change powers of two into powers of ten in your head.

Example 9

Use $2^{10} \approx 10^3$ to estimate $2^{25}$ and $2^{-20}$ in standard form.

Solution.

Step 1 $2^{25} = (2^{10})^2 \times 2^5 \approx 10^6 \times 32 = 3.2 \times 10^7$ split off tens
Step 2 $2^{-20} = (2^{10})^{-2} \approx (10^3)^{-2} = 10^{-6}$ the same for negatives
Answer about $3.2 \times 10^7$ and about $1 \times 10^{-6}$

The exact values are $33\,554\,432$ and about $9.5 \times 10^{-7}$. Each estimate is out by less than a tenth of the true value.
