Notice what made the count a power rather than a plain product: every slot offered the same ten options, so the same number was multiplied in each time. That is worth checking before reaching for an exponent. A four-digit PIN gives $10^4$, but a number plate of two letters followed by four digits gives $26^2 \times 10^4$ — a product of two powers, and not a single power at all.
