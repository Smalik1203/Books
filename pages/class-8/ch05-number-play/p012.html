Example 2

Find the remainder when $538$ is divided by $9$.

Solution.

Step 1 $500 = 5 \times 99 + 5$ 5 hundreds leave 5
Step 2 $30 = 3 \times 9 + 3$ 3 tens leave 3
Step 3 $8$ units leave $8$
Step 4 $5 + 3 + 8 = 16 = 9 + 7$ one more group of 9
Answer The remainder is $7$.

This works for bigger numbers too, because every place value is one more than a multiple of $9$: $10 = 9 + 1$, $100 = 99 + 1$, $1000 = 999 + 1$, and so on. So each digit leaves itself as a remainder, and we only need to add the digits.

Example 3

Find the remainder when $8465$ is divided by $9$.

Solution.

Step 1 $8465 = 8 \times 1000 + 4 \times 100 + 6 \times 10 + 5$
Step 2 $= 8 \times (999 + 1) + 4 \times (99 + 1) + 6 \times (9 + 1) + 5$
Step 3 $= (8 \times 999 + 4 \times 99 + 6 \times 9) + (8 + 4 + 6 + 5)$
Step 4 $8 + 4 + 6 + 5 = 23$, and $2 + 3 = 5$ the first bracket is a multiple of 9
Answer The remainder is $5$.

In Step 4, $23$ is $5$ more than a multiple of $9$, and adding its digits gives that $5$ at once.
