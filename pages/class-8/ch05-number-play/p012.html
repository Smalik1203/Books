Example 1

Find the remainder when $427$ is divided by $9$.

Solution.

Step 1 $400 = 4 \times 99 + 4$ 4 hundreds leave 4
Step 2 $20 = 2 \times 9 + 2$ 2 tens leave 2
Step 3 $7$ units leave $7$
Step 4 $4 + 2 + 7 = 13 = 9 + 4$ one more group of 9
Answer The remainder is $4$.

Will this work for bigger numbers? It does, because every place value is one more than a multiple of $9$: $1 = 0 + 1$, $10 = 9 + 1$, $100 = 99 + 1$, $1000 = 999 + 1$, $10000 = 9999 + 1$, and so on. So each digit gives the remainder that its own place leaves on division by $9$, and to find the remainder when $7309$ is divided by $9$ we just add the digits: $7 + 3 + 0 + 9 = 19$. Example 2 shows why.

Example 2

Find the remainder when $7309$ is divided by $9$.

Solution.

Step 1 $7309 = 7 \times 1000 + 3 \times 100 + 0 \times 10 + 9 \times 1$
Step 2 $= 7 \times (999 + 1) + 3 \times (99 + 1) + 0 \times (9 + 1) + 9 \times (0 + 1)$
Step 3 $= (7 \times 999 + 3 \times 99 + 0 \times 9 + 9 \times 0) + (7 + 3 + 0 + 9)$
Step 4 $7 + 3 + 0 + 9 = 19$ the first bracket is a multiple of 9
Step 5 $1 + 9 = 10$, then $1 + 0 = 1$ add the digits again
Answer The remainder is $1$.

So $7309$ is $19$ more than a multiple of $9$. In the same way $19$ is $10$ more than a multiple of $9$, and $10$ is $1$ more. So $7309$ is $1$ more than a multiple of $9$.
