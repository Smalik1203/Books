Example 4

Use alternating signs to find how far $328105$ is from a multiple of $11$.

Solution.

Step 1 $-3 + 2 - 8 + 1 - 0 + 5$ signs alternate, $+$ before the units digit
Step 2 $-3 + 2 - 8 + 1 - 0 + 5 = -3$
Answer $328105$ is $3$ less than a multiple of $11$, or $8$ more than one: the remainder is $8$.

Divisibility by 11

Starting from the units digit, add and subtract the digits in turn. A number is divisible by $11$ if and only if the result is $0$ or a multiple of $11$. Otherwise the result tells you how far the number is from a multiple of $11$.
