Example 6 · Single correct

In the cryptarithm $\text{AB} \times 7 = \text{CBA}$, what is the two-digit number AB?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 79
  2. 69
  3. 96
  4. 97

Solution.

Step 1The units digit of $7 \times \text{B}$ is A, and the product starts with C and ends with A.
Step 2Trying the options: $97 \times 7 = 679$, which is C $= 6$, B $= 7$, A $= 9$. The others give 553, 483 and 672, which do not fit CBA.
Answer(d) 97

Example 8 · Multiple correct

$M$ and $N$ are both multiples of 7, and $N$ is not 0. Which of these must be multiples of 7?

Select all correct options.

  1. $M + N$
  2. $M - N$
  3. $M \times N$
  4. $M \div N$

Solution.

Step 1Write $M = 7a$ and $N = 7b$. Then $M + N = 7(a + b)$, $M - N = 7(a - b)$ and $M \times N = 7(7ab)$.
Step 2$M \div N$ need not be: $M = 14$ and $N = 7$ give $14 \div 7 = 2$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
