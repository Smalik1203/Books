Example 5

How many whole numbers from 1 to 200 have digital root 7?

Solution.

Step 1A digital root of 7 means a remainder of 7 on division by 9. So the number is 7 more than a multiple of 9, giving the numbers $7, 16, 25, \ldots$, each $9$ more than the one before.
Step 2The largest up to 200 is $196 = 9 \times 21 + 7$, so the numbers are $9k + 7$ for $k = 0$ to 21.
Step 3From 0 to 21 there are 22 whole numbers, so the answer is 22.
CheckCount another way. Each block of $9$ consecutive numbers holds exactly one number with digital root 7. From 1 to 198 there are $198 \div 9 = 22$ such blocks, and the last two numbers, 199 and 200, have digital roots 1 and 2, so they add nothing. So the count is 22 once again.
