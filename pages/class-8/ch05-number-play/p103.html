Writing $abc$ twice gives $abcabc$. As place values, the first copy is $abc$ thousands and the second is $abc$ units:

$abcabc = abc \times 1000 + abc = abc \times 1001$

And $1001 = 7 \times 11 \times 13$. So $abcabc$ is a multiple of $1001$, and every multiple of $1001$ is divisible by each factor of $1001$. For example, $527527 = 527 \times 1001 = 527 \times 7 \times 11 \times 13$.

Single correct
Example 1

Which of these expressions is odd for every whole number $n$?

  1. $n + 7$
  2. $3n + 1$
  3. $2n^2 + 7$
  4. $5n + 2$

Solution.

Step 1$2n^2$ is even for every $n$, and even $+$ odd is odd, so $2n^2 + 7$ is always odd.
Step 2The others change with $n$: $n + 7$ and $3n + 1$ are even when $n$ is odd, and $5n + 2$ is even when $n$ is even.
Answer(c) $2n^2 + 7$
Example 2

In the cryptarithm $\text{AB} \times 7 = \text{CBA}$, what is the two-digit number AB?

  1. 79
  2. 69
  3. 96
  4. 97

Solution.

Step 1Here $7 \times \text{B}$ must end in A, and the product must begin with C.
Step 2Trying the options: $97 \times 7 = 679$, which is C $= 6$, B $= 7$, A $= 9$. The others give 553, 483 and 672, which do not fit CBA.
Answer(d) 97
