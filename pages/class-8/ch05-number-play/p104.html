Example 3

The digital root of a number $N$ is 6. Which statements are correct?

  1. $N$ leaves a remainder of 6 when divided by 9.
  2. $N + 3$ is divisible by 9.
  3. $2N$ has digital root 3.
  4. $N - 6$ is divisible by 18.

Solution.

Step 1A digital root of 6 means $N = 9k + 6$. So $N + 3 = 9(k + 1)$, and $2N = 18k + 12$, which is $9(2k + 1) + 3$.
Step 2$N - 6 = 9k$ is a multiple of 9 but not always of 18: for example, if $N = 15$ then $N - 6 = 9$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 4

Which statements are true for every set of five consecutive whole numbers?

  1. Their sum is a multiple of 5.
  2. Their sum is odd.
  3. Exactly one of them is a multiple of 5.
  4. At least two of them are even.

Solution.

Step 1The sum is $(m - 2) + (m - 1) + m + (m + 1) + (m + 2) = 5m$, where $m$ is the middle number.
Step 2$5m$ is odd only when $m$ is odd: $1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 15$ but $2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 = 20$. Five numbers in a row meet exactly one multiple of 5, and contain two or three even numbers.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
