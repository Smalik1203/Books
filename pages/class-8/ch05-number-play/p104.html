Example 3 · Single correct

What is the remainder when 70413 is divided by 11?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 1
  2. 3
  3. 13
  4. 2

Solution.

Step 1From the units digit, add and subtract in turn: $3 - 1 + 4 - 0 + 7 = 13$.
Step 213 is 2 more than 11, so 70413 is 2 more than a multiple of 11: $70413 = 11 \times 6401 + 2$.
Answer(d) 2
Example 4 · Single correct

A number leaves a remainder of 5 when divided by 8. What remainder does 3 times the number leave when divided by 8?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 15
  2. 7
  3. 5
  4. 3

Solution.

Step 1Write the number as $8k + 5$. Then $3(8k + 5) = 24k + 15$.
Step 2$24k + 15 = 8(3k + 1) + 7$, so the remainder is 7. A remainder is always less than 8, so 15 cannot be one.
Answer(b) 7
Example 5 · Single correct

A number leaves a remainder of 4 when divided by 9. What is the digital root of the number that is 7 more than it?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 2
  2. 11
  3. 4
  4. 7

Solution.

Step 1Write the number as $9k + 4$. Then $(9k + 4) + 7 = 9k + 11 = 9(k + 1) + 2$.
Step 2So the new number leaves a remainder of 2 when divided by 9, and its digital root is 2. For example, $13 + 7 = 20$ and $2 + 0 = 2$.
Answer(a) 2
