Example 10

A list of numbers starts at $7$ and goes up by $8$ each time: $7, 15, 23, 31$, and so on.

  1. The digital root of $31$, the 4th number, is
    1. $4$
    2. $3$
    3. $13$
    4. $7$
  2. Numerical answer. What is the digital root of the 20th number?
  3. Numerical answer. How many of the first $100$ numbers are multiples of $9$?

Solution.

Step 1Adding $8$ is adding $9$ and taking away $1$, so each digital root is $1$ less than the one before: $7, 6, 5, 4, \ldots$ So $31$ has digital root $4$.
Step 2The 20th number is $7 + 19 \times 8 = 159$, and $1 + 5 + 9 = 15$, $1 + 5 = 6$.
Step 3The digital roots run $7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 9, 8$ and repeat every $9$ numbers. The digital root is $9$ at the 8th, 17th, 26th, $\ldots$, 98th numbers: $11$ of them.
CheckThe 8th number is $7 + 7 \times 8 = 63 = 9 \times 7$.
Answer(i) (a) $4$; (ii) $6$; (iii) $11$
