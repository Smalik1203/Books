Example 11 · Numerical answer

Find the digit $d$ for which the five-digit number $6d875$ is divisible by 11.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1From the units digit: $5 - 7 + 8 - d + 6 = 12 - d$.
Step 2For a digit $d$, $12 - d$ is a multiple of 11 only when $d = 1$: $61875 = 11 \times 5625$.
Answer1
Example 12 · Numerical answer

How many whole numbers from 1 to 200 have digital root 7?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1A digital root of 7 means the number is 7 more than a multiple of 9: $7, 16, 25, \ldots$
Step 2The largest up to 200 is $196 = 9 \times 21 + 7$, so the numbers are $9k + 7$ for $k = 0$ to 21: 22 of them.
Answer22
Example 13 · Numerical answer

What is the remainder when $10^{25}$ is divided by 11?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The place values 10, 1000, 100000, … — odd powers of 10 — are each 1 less than a multiple of 11.
Step 2$10^{25}$ is an odd power, so it is 1 less than a multiple of 11, which is 10 more than the one before.
Answer10
