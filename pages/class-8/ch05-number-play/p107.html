Example 8

Here $k$, $j$ and $n$ are whole numbers. Match each number in List I with its remainder in List II.

List IList II
(P) $3(4k + 3)$, divided by $4$(1) $1$
(Q) $(6k + 5) + (6j + 4)$, divided by $6$(2) $3$
(R) $n + (n + 1) + (n + 2) + (n + 3)$, divided by $4$(3) $5$
(S) $2(9k + 7)$, divided by $9$(4) $2$
  1. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  2. P–1, Q–2, R–4, S–3
  3. P–1, Q–3, R–2, S–4
  4. P–1, Q–2, R–3, S–4

Solution.

Step 1(P) $3(4k + 3) = 12k + 9 = 4(3k + 2) + 1$
Step 2(Q) $(6k + 5) + (6j + 4) = 6(k + j + 1) + 3$
Step 3(R) the sum is $4n + 6 = 4(n + 1) + 2$
Step 4(S) $2(9k + 7) = 18k + 14 = 9(2k + 1) + 5$
Answer(b) P–1, Q–2, R–4, S–3
