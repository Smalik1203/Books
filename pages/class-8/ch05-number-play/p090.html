  1. Is $918$ divisible by $18$? Give a reason.
  1. A number leaves a remainder of $3$ when divided by $7$. Write the number in algebraic form, and find the remainder when twice the number is divided by $7$.

  1. Show that $4(3m + 1) + 2(m - 2)$ is a multiple of $14$ for every whole number $m$.

  1. Show that the product of a multiple of $6$ and a multiple of $10$ is always a multiple of $60$. Also show, with an example, that their sum need not be a multiple of $60$.
  1. A number leaves a remainder of $7$ when divided by $12$. Find the remainders when it is divided by $3$, by $4$ and by $6$.

    Step 4So $58432$ is not divisible by $11$.
