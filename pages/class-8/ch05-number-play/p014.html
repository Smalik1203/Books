Divisibility by 3

A number is divisible by $3$ if and only if the sum of its digits is divisible by $3$.

A Test for 11

The test for $11$ also comes from what the place values leave. Table 5.11 compares each place value with the nearest multiple of $11$.

Table 5.11 Place values and the multiples of 11
place valuenearest multiple of $11$written with itso the place value is
$1$$0$$1 = 0 + 1$$1$ more than a multiple
$10$$11$$10 = 11 - 1$$1$ less than a multiple
$100$$99$$100 = 99 + 1$$1$ more than a multiple
$1000$$1001$$1000 = 1001 - 1$$1$ less than a multiple

The pattern carries on: $10000 = 9999 + 1$, $100000 = 100001 - 1$, and so on, with $9999 = 11 \times 909$ and $100001 = 11 \times 9091$.

Now take $583$. Its $5$ hundreds are $5$ more than a multiple of $11$. Its $8$ tens are $8$ less than a multiple of $11$. Its $3$ units are $3$ more. Altogether it is $5 + 3 - 8 = 0$ away from a multiple of $11$, so $583$ is divisible by $11$. Indeed, $583 = 11 \times 53$.

Example 4

Find how far $416253$ is from a multiple of $11$, and the remainder when it is divided by $11$.

Solution.

Step 1 places $1$, $100$, $10000$ hold $3$, $2$, $1$: excess $3 + 2 + 1 = 6$ 1 more each
Step 2 places $10$, $1000$, $100000$ hold $5$, $6$, $4$: short $5 + 6 + 4 = 15$ 1 less each
Step 3 $6 - 15 = -9$ excess minus short
Answer $416253$ is $9$ less than a multiple of $11$, so it is $2$ more than the multiple before it. The remainder is $2$.
