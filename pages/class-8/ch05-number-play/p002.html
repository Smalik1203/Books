Signs Between Numbers

Choose four consecutive numbers, such as $7, 8, 9$ and $10$. Put a $+$ or a $-$ sign in each of the three gaps between them. Two of the ways are $7 - 8 + 9 + 10$ and $7 - 8 - 9 - 10$.

Each gap has two choices of sign, so there are $2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8$ ways in all. The tree in Fig. 5.1 lists all eight without missing one. Each fork chooses the sign in front of the next number.

Find the value of each of the eight expressions. Then do the same for four other consecutive numbers. Table 5.2 starts two sets for you. What do you notice about the values?

Every value is an even number. Negative numbers such as $-2$ and $-20$ count as even too, since $2$ is a factor of each. Whether a number is even or odd is called its parity. So all eight expressions have even parity, whichever four consecutive numbers you start with.

Is this only luck? Try four numbers that are not consecutive, such as $3, 8, 10$ and $15$. Place the signs in the eight ways and look at the parity of each value. Then try another set of four. We can explain what happens in two ways.
