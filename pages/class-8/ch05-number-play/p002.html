Some values come up whichever four consecutive numbers you choose. Do these patterns hold for every choice? Is there a way to find out by reasoning, rather than by trying more cases? Here is a hint: use letter-numbers to write the eight expressions in a general form.

You may have noticed that every value is an even number. An even number has $2$ as a factor, and that includes negative numbers such as $-2$, $-4$ and $-6$. Did anyone in your class get an odd number?

Whether a number is even or odd is called its parity. Whichever four consecutive numbers you choose, and however you place the signs, every one of the eight expressions has even parity.

Now take any four numbers, not necessarily consecutive. Place the signs in the eight ways and work out each value. What do you notice about their parities? Try it with other sets of four numbers.

Can you explain why this happens? Here is a hint: think of the rules for the parity of a sum or a difference of two numbers.
