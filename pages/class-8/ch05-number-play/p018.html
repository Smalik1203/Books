Try this one now: $\text{GH} \times \text{H} = 9\text{K}$.

It says that a two-digit number times a one-digit number gives another two-digit number, in the $90$s. Look at the letters in the units places. Then pick the solution from these:

  1. $11 \times 9 = 99$
  2. $12 \times 8 = 96$
  3. $46 \times 2 = 92$
  4. $24 \times 4 = 96$
  5. $47 \times 2 = 94$
  6. $31 \times 3 = 93$
  7. $16 \times 6 = 96$

Here is one more: $\text{BYE} \times 6 = \text{RAY}$.

Anshu says, “The product has three digits, so B cannot be $2$ or more. If B is $2$, that is $2$ hundreds, the product will be more than $1200$. So B is $1$.”

What can you say about Y? Which digits are possible for Y, and which are not?

“Y cannot be $7$ or more,” Anshu explains, “because if Y is $7$, then $170 \times 6 = 1020$, and we want a three-digit product. Also, Y must be even.”
