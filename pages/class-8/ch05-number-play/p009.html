What Remains?

Find a number that leaves a remainder of $3$ when divided by $5$. Write some more numbers like it.

Which of these algebraic expressions describe all such numbers?

  1. $3k + 5$
  2. $3k - 5$
  3. $3k$
  4. $5k + 3$
  5. $5k - 2$
  6. $5k - 3$

The numbers that leave a remainder of $0$ when divided by $5$ are the multiples of $5$, and they have the form $5k$. The numbers we want leave a remainder of $3$, so they are $3$ more than a multiple of $5$. So they have the form $5k + 3$ (Fig. 5.9).

Now take another expression, $5k - 2$, and look at the values it takes. A number that leaves a remainder of $3$ when divided by $5$ is also $2$ less than a multiple of $5$. So these numbers can be written as $5k - 2$, where $k \geq 1$. Table 5.10 puts the two side by side.
