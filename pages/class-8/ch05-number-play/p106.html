Example 6

What is the remainder when $10^{25}$ is divided by 11?

Solution.

Step 1The place values 10, 1000, 100000, … — odd powers of 10 — are each 1 less than a multiple of 11.
Step 2$10^{25}$ is an odd power, so it is 1 less than a multiple of 11, which is 10 more than the one before.
CheckA smaller odd power behaves the same way: $1000 = 11 \times 90 + 10$.
Answer10

Example 7

Match each number in List I with the remainder it leaves when divided by 9 in List II.

List IList II
(P) 4512(1) 5
(Q) 8807(2) 0
(R) 7777(3) 3
(S) 36036(4) 1
  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–1, Q–3, R–4, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The digit sums are 12, 23, 28 and 18, with digital roots 3, 5, 1 and 9.
Step 2A digital root of 9 means a remainder of 0; the others are the remainders.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
