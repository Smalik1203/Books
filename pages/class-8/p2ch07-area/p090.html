By the Book
Very short answer
  1. The height of a triangle is half of its base, and the base is $13$ cm. Find the area of the triangle in square centimetres.
  1. A parallelogram has an area of $91$ cm2 and a base of $13$ cm. Find its height. Also find the area of a triangle on the same base and between the same parallels.
  1. Find the area of a rhombus whose diagonals are $11$ cm and $8$ cm. Is it larger than a square of side $6$ cm? Give a reason.
  1. Can two rectangles, each with a perimeter of $30$ cm, have areas of $50$ cm2 and $56$ cm2? Justify your answer.
  1. The area of a trapezium is $60$ cm2, and its parallel sides are $9$ cm and $6$ cm. Find the distance between the parallel sides.
  1. Write $3.5$ m2 in cm2. Give a reason for the number you multiply by.
  1. A square has the same area as a rectangle $16$ cm by $4$ cm. Find the side of the square. Which of the two has the shorter perimeter?
  1. The diagonals from one corner cut a polygon with $8$ sides into triangles. Find the number of triangles. If each triangle has an area of $12$ cm2, find the area of the polygon.
  1. Show that $1$ ft2 $= 144$ in2, and hence find the number of square inches in $5$ ft2.
  1. A triangle has an area of $36$ cm2 and a height of $9$ cm. Find its base. Is the base longer than the height?
Short answer
  1. Meera's rectangular garden is $18$ m by $12$ m. She makes a triangular flower bed with one $18$ m side as its base and its top corner on the opposite side. Find the area of the bed. Also find the area of the garden that is left.
  1. Show that the diagonals of a parallelogram cut it into four triangles of equal area.
  1. A field is a trapezium with parallel sides of $50$ m and $34$ m, which are $24$ m apart. Find its area and the cost of levelling it at ₹$12$ per m2.
