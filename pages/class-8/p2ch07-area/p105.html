Example 3 · Single correct

A right-angled triangle has sides 9 cm, 12 cm and 15 cm. What is the perpendicular from the right angle to the longest side?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 54 cm
  2. 6 cm
  3. 7.2 cm
  4. 7.5 cm

Solution.

Step 1The area is $\frac{1}{2} \times 9 \times 12 = 54 \text{ cm}^2$.
Step 2Taking the 15 cm side as base: $\frac{1}{2} \times 15 \times h = 54$, so $h = 7.2$ cm.
Answer(c) 7.2 cm
Example 4 · Single correct

A parallelogram has area $84 \text{ cm}^2$ and sides 7 cm and 14 cm. What is its height on the 14 cm side?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 12 cm
  2. 7 cm
  3. 14 cm
  4. 6 cm

Solution.

Step 1Area $=$ base $\times$ height: $14 \times h = 84$.
Step 2$h = 6$ cm.
Answer(d) 6 cm
Example 5 · Single correct

A rhombus has sides 25 cm and one diagonal 48 cm. What is its area?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $336 \text{ cm}^2$
  2. $600 \text{ cm}^2$
  3. $1200 \text{ cm}^2$
  4. $672 \text{ cm}^2$

Solution.

Step 1The diagonals bisect each other at right angles: half the other diagonal is $\sqrt{25^2 - 24^2} = 7$, so it is 14 cm.
Step 2Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times 48 \times 14 = 336 \text{ cm}^2$.
Answer(a) $336 \text{ cm}^2$
