Example 2

A right-angled triangle has sides 9 cm, 12 cm and 15 cm. Find the height to the 15 cm side.

  1. 54 cm
  2. 6 cm
  3. 7.2 cm
  4. 7.5 cm

Solution.

Step 1The area is $\frac{1}{2} \times 9 \times 12 = 54 \text{ cm}^2$.
Step 2Taking the 15 cm side as base: $\frac{1}{2} \times 15 \times h = 54$, so $h = 7.2$ cm.
Answer(c) 7.2 cm

Example 3

$ABCD$ is a parallelogram of area $50 \text{ cm}^2$, and $P$ is any point on side $CD$. Which statements are correct?

  1. $APB$ has area $25 \text{ cm}^2$.
  2. $APB$ has the same area for every $P$.
  3. $APD$ and $BPC$ make $25 \text{ cm}^2$.
  4. $APB$ has area $50 \text{ cm}^2$.

Solution.

Step 1$APB$ has base $AB$ and the full height: half of $ABCD$, for any $P$.
Step 2So $APD$ and $BPC$ make the other half, $25 \text{ cm}^2$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
