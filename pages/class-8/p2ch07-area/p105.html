Type 2 · A triangle's area, and a height from it

Example 3

A right-angled triangle has sides $9$ cm, $12$ cm and $15$ cm. Find its area, and the perpendicular height on its longest side.

Solution.

Step 1 $\tfrac12 \times 9 \times 12 = 54$ cm2 the two shorter sides meet square
Step 2 $\tfrac12 \times 15 \times h = 54$ same area, base 15 cm
Step 3 $h = 108 \div 15 = 7.2$ cm
Answer Area 54 cm2; height 7.2 cm on the 15 cm side

In a right-angled triangle each of the two shorter sides is the height on the other, which is why the first step needed no measuring.

Example 4

In triangle $ABC$, $BC = 6$ cm. The perpendicular from $A$ meets $CB$ extended at $D$, beyond $B$, with $AD = 5$ cm and $DB = 3$ cm. Find the area of triangle $ABC$ by subtraction, and check it against the formula.

Solution.

Step 1 $DC = 3 + 6 = 9$ cm $D$ lies outside $BC$
Step 2 $\tfrac12 \times 5 \times 9 = 22.5$ cm2 right triangle $ADC$
Step 3 $\tfrac12 \times 5 \times 3 = 7.5$ cm2 right triangle $ADB$
Step 4 $22.5 - 7.5 = 15$ cm2 take the smaller away
Step 5 $\tfrac12 \times 6 \times 5 = 15$ cm2 the formula agrees
Answer 15 cm2
