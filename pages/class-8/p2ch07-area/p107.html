Example 8

A parallelogram has area $84$ cm2 and sides of $7$ cm and $14$ cm. Find the perpendicular height on each side.

Solution.

Step 1 $7 \times h = 84$, so $h = 12$ cm on the 7 cm side
Step 2 $14 \times h = 84$, so $h = 6$ cm on the 14 cm side
Answer 12 cm on the 7 cm side, and 6 cm on the 14 cm side

Each height is shorter than the other side, as it must be: the height on one side is measured across to the opposite side, and the other pair of sides is a slanting path between the same two lines.

Type 5 · The rhombus and the kite

Example 9

A rhombus has sides of $25$ cm and one diagonal of $48$ cm. Find the other diagonal, the area, and the perpendicular height.

Solution.

Step 1 $48 \div 2 = 24$ cm the diagonals bisect
Step 2 $\sqrt{25^2 - 24^2} = \sqrt{49} = 7$ cm right angle at the centre
Step 3 $2 \times 7 = 14$ cm the other diagonal
Step 4 $\tfrac12 \times 48 \times 14 = 336$ cm2 half the diagonals' product
Step 5 $25 \times h = 336$, so $h = 13.44$ cm as a parallelogram
Answer Diagonal 14 cm; area 336 cm2; height 13.44 cm
Example 10

A kite has diagonals of $14$ cm and $10$ cm, crossing at right angles. A rhombus with one diagonal of $20$ cm has the same area. Find the rhombus's other diagonal.

Solution.

Step 1 $\tfrac12 \times 14 \times 10 = 70$ cm2 the kite obeys the rule
Step 2 $\tfrac12 \times 20 \times d = 70$ the same area
Step 3 $10 \times d = 70$, so $d = 7$ cm
Answer 7 cm
