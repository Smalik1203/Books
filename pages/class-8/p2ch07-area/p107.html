Example 8 · Multiple correct

A kite has diagonals 14 cm and 10 cm. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its area is $70 \text{ cm}^2$.
  2. A 20 cm by 3.5 cm rectangle has equal area.
  3. It equals a 14 cm by 10 cm rectangle.
  4. Its area is $140 \text{ cm}^2$.

Solution.

Step 1Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times 14 \times 10 = 70 = 20 \times 3.5$, half the rectangle round the kite.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

On a map, 1 cm stands for 2 km. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $1 \text{ m}^2 = 10\,000 \text{ cm}^2$
  2. $7.5 \text{ m}^2 = 75\,000 \text{ cm}^2$
  3. $1 \text{ km}^2 = 1000 \text{ m}^2$
  4. $1 \text{ cm}^2$ of map is $4 \text{ km}^2$.

Solution.

Step 1An area unit is a length unit squared: $100^2 = 10\,000$, $2^2 = 4$, but $1000^2 = 10^6$, not 1000.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

A trapezium has parallel sides 14 cm and 10 cm, 6 cm apart. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its area is $72 \text{ cm}^2$.
  2. Triangle: base 24 cm, height 6 cm, equal area.
  3. Rectangle: 12 cm by 6 cm, equal area.
  4. Its area is $144 \text{ cm}^2$.

Solution.

Step 1Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times (14 + 10) \times 6 = 72$, and $\frac{1}{2} \times 24 \times 6 = 12 \times 6 = 72$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
