Example 5

A rectangular farm plot is 330 ft by 264 ft. An acre is $43\,560 \text{ ft}^2$. Find the area of the plot in acres.

Solution.

Step 1Area $= 330 \times 264 = 87\,120 \text{ ft}^2$.
Step 2$87\,120 \div 43\,560 = 2$ acres.
CheckAn acre is $330 \times 132$ ft2, and $264$ is twice $132$, so the plot is two strips of one acre each.

Example 6

A garden is a pentagon: a rectangle 14 m by 9 m with a triangle on top, whose base is one 14 m side and whose height is 6 m. Find its area, in $\text{m}^2$.

Solution.

Step 1Rectangle: $14 \times 9 = 126 \text{ m}^2$. Triangle: $\frac{1}{2} \times 14 \times 6 = 42 \text{ m}^2$.
Step 2$126 + 42 = 168 \text{ m}^2$.
CheckThe diagonals from the top corner cut the pentagon into $5 - 2 = 3$ triangles, and they also add up to $168 \text{ m}^2$.
Answer168
