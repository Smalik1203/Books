7.5One Triangle, Three Ways

A triangle has three sides, and any of them may be called the base. Each base has its own height, drawn from the opposite corner at a right angle to that base. So a triangle has three base-and-height pairs. All three pairs must give the same answer, because the triangle has only one area. This simple fact is one of the most useful in the chapter.
