1. Kabir finds the area and the height of a rhombus with sides of $10$ cm and diagonals of $12$ cm and $16$ cm. His working is shown below.
    Step 1The area of a rhombus is the product of its diagonals.
    Step 2$12 \times 16 = 192$, so the area is $192$ cm2.
    Step 3A rhombus is a parallelogram, so $10 \times h$ is its area.
    Step 4$h = 192 \div 10 = 19.2$ cm.
    Identify the step in which Kabir has made an error, if any. Correct it and find the right area and height.
