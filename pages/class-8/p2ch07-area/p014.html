7.8Shapes Exchanged for Shapes

The Śulbasūtras are old Indian texts about building fire altars. Chapter 2 met them as the source of the theorem about the diagonal. An altar had to have a fixed shape and also a fixed area. So these texts have many problems of one kind: change this shape into that shape, and keep the area the same.

Section 7.6 has already solved one such problem: a parallelogram was changed into a rectangle. Here is a second one, which makes the others easy. We change a triangle into a parallelogram of the same area.
