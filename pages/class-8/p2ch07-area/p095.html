1. A school in Shillong has a garden in the shape of a parallelogram. Its sides are $20$ m and $12$ m long, and the distance between the two $20$ m sides is $9$ m. The students grow vegetables there, and they plan to put a fence all round the garden to keep out goats.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the area of the garden.
    2. Find the distance between the two $12$ m sides.
