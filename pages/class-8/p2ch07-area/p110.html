Example 17

A rectangular farm plot is $330$ ft by $264$ ft. An acre is $43{,}560$ ft2. How many acres is the plot?

Solution.

Step 1 $330 \times 264 = 87{,}120$ ft2
Step 2 $87{,}120 \div 43{,}560 = 2$ ft2 in one acre
Answer 2 acres
