Why the other options are wrong

Set A, 10. Each of the first three options is contradicted by something in the chapter. The four triangles inside a rectangle have equal areas and are not congruent; a square and a rectangle of equal area have different perimeters; and a triangle and a parallelogram of equal area are not the same kind of shape at all. Equal area is a weaker claim than any of them, which is exactly what makes dissection possible.

Set A, 12. Option (a), $12$, is the length conversion doing duty for the area one. A foot is $12$ inches, so a square foot is a square of side $12$ inches, and it holds $12 \times 12 = 144$ of them.

Set B, 5. Option (a) is the mistake section 7.3 was written to head off. The two rectangles have equal perimeters — $18$ cm each — and areas of $8$ and $20$ cm2, so (a) and (c) both fail and only (b) survives.
