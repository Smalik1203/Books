Example 3

A rhombus has diagonals of $24$ cm and $18$ cm. Find its area. Its side is $15$ cm. Find its height on that side.

Solution.

Step 1 $\tfrac12 \times 24 \times 18 = 216$ cm2 half the product of the diagonals
Step 2 $15 \times h = 216$ as a parallelogram
Step 3 $h = 14.4$ cm divide by 15
Answer Area 216 cm2; height 14.4 cm

The side of $15$ cm was given, but it can also be found from the diagonals. The half-diagonals are $12$ cm and $9$ cm, and they meet at a right angle. By the theorem of Chapter 2, the side is $\sqrt{144 + 81} = 15$ cm. So the diagonals alone give the area, the side and the height of a rhombus.

Diagonals at right angles

If the diagonals of a quadrilateral cross at right angles, its area is half the product of the diagonals. This is true for a rhombus, a square and a kite.
