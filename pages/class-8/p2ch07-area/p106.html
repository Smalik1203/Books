Example 6 · Single correct

A field shaped like a trapezium has area $91 \text{ m}^2$ and parallel sides 16 m and 10 m. How far apart are the parallel sides?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 3.5 m
  2. 7 m
  3. 14 m
  4. 5.7 m

Solution.

Step 1Area $= \frac{1}{2} \times (16 + 10) \times h = 13h$.
Step 2$13h = 91$, so $h = 7$ m.
Answer(b) 7 m
Example 7 · Multiple correct

$ABCD$ is a parallelogram of area $50 \text{ cm}^2$, and $P$ is any point on side $CD$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Triangle $APB$ has area $25 \text{ cm}^2$.
  2. $APB$ has the same area for every $P$.
  3. $APD$ and $BPC$ together make $25 \text{ cm}^2$.
  4. Triangle $APB$ has area $50 \text{ cm}^2$.

Solution.

Step 1$APB$ has base $AB$ and the parallelogram's height, so it is half of it wherever $P$ is; the other two make the rest.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
