Type 3 · Figures between the same parallels

Example 5

$ABCD$ is a parallelogram of area $50$ cm2, and $P$ is any point on the side $CD$. Find the area of triangle $APB$, and the total area of triangles $APD$ and $BPC$.

Solution.

Step 1 $APB$ and $ABCD$ both stand on $AB$, with $P$ on the line $CD$ same base, same parallels
Step 2 $50 \div 2 = 25$ cm2 a triangle is half
Step 3 $50 - 25 = 25$ cm2 what the triangle leaves
Answer Triangle $APB$ is 25 cm2, and the other two together are 25 cm2, wherever $P$ is
Example 6

$ABCD$ is a trapezium with $AB$ parallel to $DC$, and its diagonals cross at $O$. Triangle $ABD$ has area $30$ cm2 and triangle $AOB$ has area $12$ cm2. Find the areas of triangles $AOD$ and $BOC$.

Solution.

Step 1 $30 - 12 = 18$ cm2 $ABD$ less $AOB$ is $AOD$
Step 2 triangles $ABD$ and $ABC$ stand on $AB$, with $D$ and $C$ on one parallel so both are 30 cm2
Step 3 $30 - 12 = 18$ cm2 $ABC$ less $AOB$ is $BOC$
Answer Both are 18 cm2

The two side triangles of any trapezium cut by its diagonals are equal in area, although they are usually not the same shape.

Type 4 · The parallelogram

Example 7

A plot of land is a parallelogram with a base of $25$ m and a perpendicular height of $14$ m. Levelling it costs ₹$12$ for each square metre. Find the cost.

Solution.

Step 1 $25 \times 14 = 350$ m2 base times height
Step 2 $350 \times 12 = 4200$ area times rate
Answer ₹$4200$
