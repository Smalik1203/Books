Type 8 · Changing the unit of area

Example 14

A carpet is $3$ m by $2.5$ m. Give its area in m2 and in cm2.

Solution.

Step 1 $3 \times 2.5 = 7.5$ m2
Step 2 $1$ m2 is $100 \times 100 = 10{,}000$ cm2 square the factor
Step 3 $7.5 \times 10{,}000 = 75{,}000$ cm2
Answer 7.5 m2, or $75{,}000$ cm2

Working in centimetres from the start gives the same answer: $300 \times 250 = 75{,}000$.

Example 15

On a map, $1$ cm stands for $2$ km. A lake covers $6$ cm2 of the map. What is the lake's real area?

Solution.

Step 1 $1$ cm2 on the map is $2 \times 2 = 4$ km2 a square of side 2 km
Step 2 $6 \times 4 = 24$ km2
Answer 24 km2

Multiplying by $2$ alone gives $12$ km2, which is the length factor used on an area.

Example 16

A rug is $72$ in long and $48$ in wide. Find its area in in2, in ft2, and to the nearest cm2.

Solution.

Step 1 $72 \times 48 = 3456$ in2
Step 2 $1$ ft2 is $12 \times 12 = 144$ in2 square the factor
Step 3 $3456 \div 144 = 24$ ft2 in2 to ft2: divide
Step 4 $3456 \times 6.4516 = 22{,}296.7296$ cm2 in2 to cm2: multiply
Answer 3456 in2, 24 ft2, and about $22{,}297$ cm2

Check: the rug is $6$ ft by $4$ ft, and $6 \times 4 = 24$.
