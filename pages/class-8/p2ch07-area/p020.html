  1. A trapezium has parallel sides of $11$ cm and $7$ cm, which are $6$ cm apart. Find its area. A rectangle has the same area and the same height. Find its length.
  1. A floor $4$ m by $3$ m is covered with square tiles of side $25$ cm. How many tiles are needed? The tiles come in boxes of $16$. How many boxes must be bought?
  1. A plot of land is half an acre. How many square feet is that? If the plot is a rectangle $132$ ft wide, how long is it? Find also the length of fence needed to go all the way round it.
  1. Which is larger, $2$ m2 or $20$ ft2? Show your working, taking $1$ ft $= 30.48$ cm. By how many square centimetres is it larger?
  1. The diagonals from one corner cut a field with five sides into triangles of $120$ m2, $150$ m2 and $90$ m2. Find the area of the field. Into how many triangles would the diagonals from one corner cut a field with seven sides?
