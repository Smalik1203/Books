Example 7

All lengths are in cm. Match each figure with its area, in square centimetres.

List IList II
(P) Parallelogram, base 25, height 14(1) 91
(Q) Rhombus, diagonals 14 and 48(2) 15
(R) Trapezium, parallel sides 16 and 10, height 7(3) 350
(S) Triangle, base 6, height 5(4) 336
  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$25 \times 14 = 350$ and $\frac{1}{2} \times 14 \times 48 = 336$.
Step 2$\frac{1}{2} \times 26 \times 7 = 91$ and $\frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 5 = 15$.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
Example 8

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $1 \text{ m}^2$, in $\text{cm}^2$(1) 100
(Q) $1 \text{ ft}^2$, in $\text{in}^2$(2) 1 000 000
(R) $1 \text{ km}^2$, in $\text{m}^2$(3) 10 000
(S) $1 \text{ cm}^2$, in $\text{mm}^2$(4) 144
  1. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1

Solution.

Step 11 m is 100 cm and 1 ft is 12 in, so $100^2 = 10\,000$ and $12^2 = 144$.
Step 21 km is 1000 m and 1 cm is 10 mm: $1000^2 = 1\,000\,000$ and $10^2 = 100$.
Answer(d) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
