Example 11 · Numerical answer

On a map, 1 cm stands for 2 km. A lake covers $6 \text{ cm}^2$ of the map. What is its real area, in $\text{km}^2$?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$1 \text{ cm}^2$ on the map stands for $2 \times 2 = 4 \text{ km}^2$.
Step 2$6 \times 4 = 24 \text{ km}^2$.

Example 12 · Numerical answer

A rectangular farm plot is 330 ft by 264 ft. An acre is $43\,560 \text{ ft}^2$. How many acres is the plot?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Area $= 330 \times 264 = 87\,120 \text{ ft}^2$.
Step 2$87\,120 \div 43\,560 = 2$ acres.
Answer2
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A garden is a pentagon: a rectangle 14 m by 9 m with a triangle on top, whose base is one 14 m side and whose height is 6 m. Find its area, in $\text{m}^2$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Rectangle: $14 \times 9 = 126 \text{ m}^2$. Triangle: $\frac{1}{2} \times 14 \times 6 = 42 \text{ m}^2$.
Step 2$126 + 42 = 168 \text{ m}^2$.
Answer168
