Exercise Set 7.1
  1. Find the area of each triangle. Lengths are in centimetres.
    1. base $14$, height $6$
    2. base $7.5$, height $8$
    3. base $11$, height $5.2$
  1. A triangle with an area of $48$ cm2 stands on a base of $16$ cm. Find its height. Another side of the same triangle is $12$ cm. Find the height to that side. Which of the two sides has the shorter height?
  1. Find two rectangles with whole-metre sides and a perimeter of $24$ m whose areas differ by more than $20$ m2.
  1. A rectangular card is $12$ cm by $8$ cm.
    1. Find the area of the triangle cut off by one diagonal.
    2. Both diagonals are drawn. Find the area of each of the four triangles.
  1. In a triangle $ABC$, $BC = 18$ cm and the height from $A$ to $BC$ is $10$ cm. If $AB = 15$ cm, find the height from $C$ to $AB$.
  1. Four triangles stand on the same base of $9$ cm. Their top corners lie on a line parallel to the base and $6$ cm from it. Find the area of each triangle. Do they all have the same perimeter? Give a reason for your answer.
  1. A triangle has sides $17$ cm, $17$ cm and $16$ cm, and the height to the $16$ cm side is $15$ cm. Find its area and the height to each $17$ cm side. Check that the longer sides have the shorter height.
  1. A triangular flower bed has a base of $6$ m and an area of $21$ m2. Find its height. A second bed has the same height as the first and twice its area. Find the base of the second bed.
  1. In a triangle $XQR$ of area $50$ cm2, $P$ is the midpoint of $QR$. Show that $XP$ cuts the triangle into two parts of equal area. $K$ is the midpoint of $XP$. Find the area of triangle $QKR$.
