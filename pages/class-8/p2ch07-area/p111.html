Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): Two rectangles with the same perimeter can have different areas.

    Reason (R): The area of a rectangle is fixed once its perimeter is known.

  1. Assertion (A): A triangle and a rectangle on the same base and between the same parallels have areas in the ratio $1 : 2$.

    Reason (R): The area of a triangle is $\tfrac12 \times$ base $\times$ height, and the rectangle's is base $\times$ height.

  1. Assertion (A): A square of side $1$ m has an area of $100$ cm2.

    Reason (R): $1$ m is $100$ cm.

  1. Assertion (A): A kite with diagonals of $12$ cm and $8$ cm has an area of $48$ cm2.

    Reason (R): A kite has two pairs of equal sides next to each other.
