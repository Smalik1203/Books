Example 10

The sail of a boat is a triangle with sides of $13$ m, $14$ m and $15$ m. The height from the top corner to the $14$ m side is $12$ m.

  1. The area of the sail is
    1. 84 m2
    2. 168 m2
    3. 91 m2
    4. 105 m2
  2. Numerical answer. Find the height to the $15$ m side, in metres.
  3. Numerical answer. Find the height to the $13$ m side, in metres, to two decimal places.

Solution.

Step 1$\tfrac12 \times 14 \times 12 = 84$ m2, so twice the area is $168$.
Step 2On the $15$ m side: $168 \div 15 = 11.2$ m.
Step 3On the $13$ m side: $168 \div 13 \approx 12.92$ m.
CheckThe longest side, $15$ m, has the shortest height, $11.2$ m.
Answer(i) (a) 84 m2; (ii) 11.2; (iii) 12.92
