1. The Sundarbans in West Bengal has many small islands. Mr Mondal, a forest officer, uses a map on which $1$ cm stands for $4$ km. On the map, one island is drawn as a triangle with a base of $3$ cm and a height of $2$ cm. A square of side $1$ cm on this map stands for a square of side $4$ km on the ground. He wants to know the real area of the whole island.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the area of the triangle on the map.
    2. How many square kilometres does $1$ cm2 of the map stand for?
    3. Find the real area of the island. Is it more than $50$ km2? Justify your answer.

  1. On Uttarayan, the kite festival, the sky over Ahmedabad fills with kites. Vikram makes kites from paper. Each kite has two sticks, $60$ cm and $40$ cm long, which cross at right angles and form its diagonals. Each stick runs from one corner of the kite to the opposite corner. Paper costs ₹$2$ for every $100$ cm2 used.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the area of one kite.
    2. Find the area of the smallest rectangle of paper from which one kite can be cut.
    3. Vikram covers $5$ kites and pays only for the paper in the kites. Find the total cost of the paper.
