Example 6

Change $20$ in2 to cm2, and change $322.58$ cm2 to in2.

Solution.

Step 1 $20 \times 6.4516 = 129.032$ cm2 in2 to cm2: multiply
Step 2 $322.58 \div 6.4516 = 50$ in2 cm2 to in2: divide
Answer 129.032 cm2, and 50 in2

To check, ask which number should be bigger. A square inch is larger than a square centimetre, so the number of square inches is always the smaller number: $50$ against $322.58$. If you multiply instead of dividing, you get more than $2{,}000$, which fails this check at once.

Larger areas use other units. A floor is often given in square feet, and a foot is $12$ inches, so $1$ ft2 is $12 \times 12 = 144$ in2. Land is often sold by the acre, which is $43{,}560$ ft2. Farms are also measured in hectares: a hectare is a square of side $100$ m, which is $10{,}000$ m2. A city or a district is measured in square kilometres, and $1$ km2 is $1000 \times 1000 = 1{,}000{,}000$ m2.

Example 7

A school playground is $120$ m by $75$ m. Find its area in square metres and in hectares. Is it larger than $2$ acres? (One acre is about $4047$ m2.)

Solution.

Step 1 $120 \times 75 = 9000$ m2 length times width
Step 2 $9000 \div 10{,}000 = 0.9$ hectare 1 hectare is 10,000 m2
Step 3 $2 \times 4047 = 8094$ m2, which is less than $9000$ two acres
Answer 9000 m2, or 0.9 hectare; yes, it is larger than 2 acres

Many parts of India also use local units for land, such as the bigha, the gaj and the cent. The size of a bigha is not the same in every state. This is one reason why standard units are used in official records.

Changing units of area

To change a unit of area, square the factor for the unit of length. Since $1$ m is $100$ cm, $1$ m2 is $100 \times 100 = 10{,}000$ cm2.
