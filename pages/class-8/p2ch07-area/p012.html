7.8Shapes Exchanged for Shapes

The Śulbasūtras are ancient Indian texts on the building of altars — the same texts Chapter 2 took the theorem of the diagonal from. An altar had to be built to a prescribed shape and a prescribed area, and the two requirements do not always arrive together, so the Śulbasūtras are full of problems of one kind: given this shape, construct that one, with the area unchanged.

Section 7.6 has already solved one of them — a parallelogram exchanged for a rectangle. Here is a second, and it is the one that makes the rest easy: turn a triangle into a parallelogram.
