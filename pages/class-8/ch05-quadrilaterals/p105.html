Example 2

How many more diagonals has a $9$-gon than a heptagon?

Solution.

Step 1 $9$-gon: $\dfrac{9 \times 6}{2} = 27$
Step 2 heptagon: $\dfrac{7 \times 4}{2} = 14$ seven sides
Step 3 $27 - 14 = 13$
Answer 13 more diagonals

Type 2 · The angle sum of a polygon

Example 3

The angles of a pentagon are $x^\circ$, $(x + 10)^\circ$, $(x + 20)^\circ$, $(x + 30)^\circ$ and $(x + 40)^\circ$. Find them.

Solution.

Step 1 $(5-2) \times 180 = 540$ the angle sum
Step 2 $5x + 100 = 540$ add the five
Step 3 $5x = 440$, so $x = 88$
Step 4 $88 + 98 + 108 + 118 + 128 = 540$ check
Answer $88^\circ$, $98^\circ$, $108^\circ$, $118^\circ$ and $128^\circ$
Example 4

Five angles of a hexagon are each $112^\circ$. Find the sixth.

Solution.

Step 1 $(6-2) \times 180 = 720$ the angle sum
Step 2 $5 \times 112 = 560$ the five given
Step 3 $720 - 560 = 160$
Answer The sixth angle is $160^\circ$
