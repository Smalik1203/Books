Example 6

In parallelogram $ABCD$, $AB = 3x + 4$ and $CD = 5x - 6$ centimetres. Find $x$ and the length of $AB$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3x + 4 = 5x - 6$ opposite sides are equal
Step 2 $10 = 2x$ gather the terms
Step 3 $x = 5$
Step 4 $AB = 3(5) + 4 = 19$ cm
Step 5 $5(5) - 6 = 19$ check with $CD$
Answer $x = 5$ and $AB = 19$ cm

Both expressions must give the same length. An answer that does not survive that check is an answer with an arithmetic slip in it.
