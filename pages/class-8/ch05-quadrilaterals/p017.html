These run backwards as tests. A quadrilateral whose diagonals bisect each other is a parallelogram; if they also cross at right angles it is a rhombus; if they are also equal it is a rectangle. That is often the quickest way to identify a figure you have been given by its corners rather than by its name.

Example 7

The diagonals of a rhombus are $16$ cm and $12$ cm. Find the length of a side.

They bisect each other at right angles, so each side is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle whose legs are half of each diagonal.

half-diagonals $8$ cm and $6$ cm
the side $8^2 + 6^2 = 64 + 36 = 100$
so side $= 10$ cm

All four sides come out at $10$, which they had to: the four small triangles have the same two legs, so they are the same triangle four times over.
