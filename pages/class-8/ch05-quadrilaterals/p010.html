5.5A Family, Not a List

Quadrilaterals have names — trapezium, kite, parallelogram, rhombus, rectangle, square — and they are usually met as a list of six shapes to be learnt one at a time. They are not a list. Each name is a condition laid on the figure, and the conditions stack: adding one takes you a step down a family in which everything below inherits everything above it, and inherits it for free, without any further proof.

trapezium one pair of opposite sides parallel
kite two pairs of adjacent sides equal
parallelogram both pairs of opposite sides parallel
rhombus a parallelogram with all four sides equal
rectangle a parallelogram with all four angles right
square both at once — all sides equal, all angles right
