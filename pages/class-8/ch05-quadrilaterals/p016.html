Example 7

The diagonals of a rhombus are $16$ cm and $12$ cm. Find the length of a side.

Solution.

Step 1 half-diagonals $8$ cm and $6$ cm the diagonals bisect each other
Step 2 each side is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle with these legs they cross at right angles
Step 3 $8^2 + 6^2 = 64 + 36 = 100$
Answer side $= 10$ cm

All four sides come out at $10$, which they had to: the four small triangles have the same two legs, so they are the same triangle four times over.
