Type 6 · Angles and sides of a parallelogram

Example 11

In parallelogram $ABCD$, $\angle A = (3x + 10)^\circ$ and $\angle C = (5x - 30)^\circ$. Find all four angles.

Solution.

Step 1 $3x + 10 = 5x - 30$ opposite angles are equal
Step 2 $40 = 2x$, so $x = 20$
Step 3 $3 \times 20 + 10 = 70$ and $5 \times 20 - 30 = 70$ both give the same
Step 4 $180 - 70 = 110$ adjacent angles
Answer $\angle A = \angle C = 70^\circ$ and $\angle B = \angle D = 110^\circ$
Example 12

The perimeter of a parallelogram is $60$ cm and two adjacent sides are in the ratio $2 : 3$. Find the sides.

Solution.

Step 1 sides $2k$ and $3k$, each twice opposite sides are equal
Step 2 $2 \times (2k + 3k) = 60$, so $10k = 60$ and $k = 6$
Step 3 $2 \times 6 = 12$ and $3 \times 6 = 18$
Step 4 $2 \times (12 + 18) = 60$ check
Answer The sides are 12 cm and 18 cm
Example 13

The diagonals of parallelogram $ABCD$ meet at $O$. $AO = (x + 3)$ cm, $OC = (2x - 1)$ cm and $BD = 14$ cm. Find $x$, $AC$ and $BO$. What more can you say about the figure?

Solution.

Step 1 $x + 3 = 2x - 1$, so $x = 4$ the diagonals bisect each other
Step 2 $AO = 4 + 3 = 7$, so $AC = 2 \times 7 = 14$
Step 3 $BO = 14 \div 2 = 7$
Step 4 $AC = BD$ equal diagonals
Answer $x = 4$, $AC = 14$ cm, $BO = 7$ cm; the diagonals are equal, so $ABCD$ is a rectangle
