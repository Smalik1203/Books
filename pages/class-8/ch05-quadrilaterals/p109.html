Type 7 · Diagonals of a rhombus, a rectangle and a square

Example 14

A rhombus has a side of $25$ cm and one diagonal of $14$ cm. Find the other diagonal.

Solution.

Step 1 half of the known diagonal is $7$ cm the diagonals bisect each other
Step 2 the side is the hypotenuse, with legs $7$ and the other half they cross at right angles
Step 3 $25^2 - 7^2 = 625 - 49 = 576 = 24^2$
Step 4 $2 \times 24 = 48$ double the half
Answer The other diagonal is 48 cm
Example 15

The diagonals of rectangle $ABCD$ meet at $O$, and $\angle AOB = 110^\circ$. Find $\angle OAB$ and $\angle OAD$.

Solution.

Step 1 $OA = OB$ equal diagonals, bisected
Step 2 $\angle OAB = (180 - 110) \div 2 = 35$ isosceles triangle $AOB$
Step 3 $\angle OAD = 90 - 35 = 55$ $\angle A$ is a right angle
Answer $\angle OAB = 35^\circ$ and $\angle OAD = 55^\circ$
Example 16

In rhombus $ABCD$ the diagonals meet at $O$, and $\angle ABC = 70^\circ$. Find $\angle BAC$ and $\angle ABO$.

Solution.

Step 1 $AB = BC$ all sides equal
Step 2 $\angle BAC = (180 - 70) \div 2 = 55$ isosceles triangle $ABC$
Step 3 $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$ diagonals cross at right angles
Step 4 $\angle ABO = 180 - 90 - 55 = 35$ triangle $AOB$
Answer $\angle BAC = 55^\circ$ and $\angle ABO = 35^\circ$

So the diagonal $BD$ cuts $\angle ABC$ exactly in half.
