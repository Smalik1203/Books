Example 14

The diagonals of parallelogram $ABCD$ meet at $O$. $AO = (x + 3)$ cm, $OC = (2x - 1)$ cm and $BD = 14$ cm. Find $x$, $AC$ and $BO$. What more can you say about the figure?

Solution.

Step 1 $x + 3 = 2x - 1$, so $x = 4$ the diagonals bisect each other
Step 2 $AO = 4 + 3 = 7$, so $AC = 2 \times 7 = 14$
Step 3 $BO = 14 \div 2 = 7$
Step 4 $AC = BD$ equal diagonals
Answer $x = 4$, $AC = 14$ cm, $BO = 7$ cm; the diagonals are equal, so $ABCD$ is a rectangle

Example 15

The diagonals of square $ABCD$ meet at $O$. $OA = (2x + 1)$ cm and $OB = (x + 4)$ cm. Find $x$, the length of each diagonal and $\angle AOB$.

Solution.

Step 1 $OA = OB$ equal diagonals, bisected
Step 2 $2x + 1 = x + 4$, so $x = 3$
Step 3 $OA = 2 \times 3 + 1 = 7$, so $AC = 2 \times 7 = 14$ and $BD = AC$
Step 4 $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$ a square is a rhombus
Answer $x = 3$; each diagonal is 14 cm; $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$
Example 16

The diagonals of rectangle $ABCD$ meet at $O$, and $\angle AOB = 110^\circ$. Find $\angle OAB$ and $\angle OAD$.

Solution.

Step 1 $OA = OB$ equal diagonals, bisected
Step 2 $\angle OAB = (180 - 110) \div 2 = 35$ isosceles triangle $AOB$
Step 3 $\angle OAD = 90 - 35 = 55$ $\angle A$ is a right angle
Answer $\angle OAB = 35^\circ$ and $\angle OAD = 55^\circ$
