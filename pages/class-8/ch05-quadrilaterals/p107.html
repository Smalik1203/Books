Example 8

Each interior angle of a regular polygon is $11$ times each exterior angle. How many sides has it, and what is each interior angle?

Solution.

Step 1 $11e + e = 180$ the two add to $180^\circ$
Step 2 $12e = 180$, so $e = 15$
Step 3 $360 \div 15 = 24$ the number of sides
Step 4 $11 \times 15 = 165$
Answer 24 sides; each interior angle is $165^\circ$

Type 5 · Naming a quadrilateral from what is given

Example 9

In quadrilateral $PQRS$, $PQ = QR = 5$ cm and $RS = SP = 8$ cm. Name the figure, and say whether it is a parallelogram.

Solution.

Step 1 $PQ = QR$ and $RS = SP$ are two pairs of adjacent sides a kite
Step 2 $PQ$ and $RS$ are opposite sides, and $5 \ne 8$
Step 3 a parallelogram has its opposite sides equal so it is not one
Answer A kite, and not a parallelogram
Example 10

The angles of quadrilateral $ABCD$ are $\angle A = 70^\circ$, $\angle B = 110^\circ$, $\angle C = 70^\circ$ and $\angle D = 110^\circ$. Show that it is a parallelogram.

Solution.

Step 1 $70 + 110 + 70 + 110 = 360$ a real quadrilateral
Step 2 $\angle A + \angle B = 180^\circ$, so $AD$ is parallel to $BC$ interior angles, same side
Step 3 $\angle B + \angle C = 180^\circ$, so $AB$ is parallel to $DC$ the same, along $BC$
Answer Both pairs of opposite sides are parallel, so $ABCD$ is a parallelogram
