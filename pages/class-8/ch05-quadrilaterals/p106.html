Example 5

Four angles of a pentagon are $90^\circ$, $90^\circ$, $90^\circ$ and $70^\circ$. Find the fifth, and say whether the pentagon is convex or concave.

Solution.

Step 1 $(5-2) \times 180 = 540$ the angle sum
Step 2 $90 + 90 + 90 + 70 = 340$ the four given
Step 3 $540 - 340 = 200$
Step 4 $200^\circ$ is more than $180^\circ$ the test for concave
Answer The fifth angle is $200^\circ$, so the pentagon is concave

Its fifth corner points inwards. A segment joining points just either side of that corner leaves the figure and comes back.

Type 3 · Each angle of a regular polygon

Example 6

Find each interior angle of a regular $16$-gon in two ways.

Solution.

Step 1 $(16-2) \times 180 = 2520$ the angle sum
Step 2 $2520 \div 16 = 157.5$ shared equally
Step 3 $360 \div 16 = 22.5$ each exterior angle
Step 4 $180 - 22.5 = 157.5$ the same answer
Answer Each interior angle is $157.5^\circ$
Example 7

Each interior angle of a regular polygon is $168^\circ$. How many sides has it?

Solution.

Step 1 $180 - 168 = 12$ each exterior angle
Step 2 $360 \div 12 = 30$ the turns make $360^\circ$
Step 3 $(30-2) \times 180 = 5040$ and $5040 \div 30 = 168$ check
Answer 30 sides
