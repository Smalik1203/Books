Type 3 · Each angle of a regular polygon

Example 5

Find each interior angle of a regular $16$-gon in two ways.

Solution.

Step 1 $(16-2) \times 180 = 2520$ the angle sum
Step 2 $2520 \div 16 = 157.5$ shared equally
Step 3 $360 \div 16 = 22.5$ each exterior angle
Step 4 $180 - 22.5 = 157.5$ the same answer
Answer Each interior angle is $157.5^\circ$
Example 6

Each interior angle of a regular polygon is $168^\circ$. How many sides has it?

Solution.

Step 1 $180 - 168 = 12$ each exterior angle
Step 2 $360 \div 12 = 30$ the turns make $360^\circ$
Step 3 $(30-2) \times 180 = 5040$ and $5040 \div 30 = 168$ check
Answer 30 sides

Type 4 · Exterior angles

Example 7

The exterior angles of a hexagon, taken in order, are $x$, $2x$, $3x$, $x$, $2x$ and $3x$. Find every interior angle.

Solution.

Step 1 $12x = 360$, so $x = 30$ the exterior sum
Step 2 exterior angles $30^\circ$, $60^\circ$, $90^\circ$, $30^\circ$, $60^\circ$, $90^\circ$
Step 3 $180 - 30 = 150$, $\ 180 - 60 = 120$, $\ 180 - 90 = 90$ interior plus exterior
Step 4 $150 + 120 + 90 + 150 + 120 + 90 = 720$ check
Answer $150^\circ$, $120^\circ$, $90^\circ$, $150^\circ$, $120^\circ$, $90^\circ$
