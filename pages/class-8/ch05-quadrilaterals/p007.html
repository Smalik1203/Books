In a regular polygon the angles are all the same, so each one is the total shared out equally.

$$\text{each angle of a regular } n\text{-gon} = \frac{(n-2) \times 180^\circ}{n}$$

A regular hexagon therefore has angles of $\dfrac{720^\circ}{6} = 120^\circ$, and a regular pentagon $\dfrac{540^\circ}{5} = 108^\circ$ — a number worth remembering, because it is not one of the friendly ones and it decides which shapes will tile a floor.
