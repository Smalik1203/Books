Example 12

What number does the binary numeral $110101_{\,2}$ stand for?

Solution.

Step 1 the places are $32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1$ powers of two
Step 2 the ones stand in the $32$, $16$, $4$ and $1$ places
Step 3 $32 + 16 + 4 + 1 = 53$
Answer $53$

Type 7 · Counting in sixties

Example 13

Write 4000 the Mesopotamian way, giving the count in each place, and say how many wedge marks it takes.

Solution.

Step 1 $4000 = 1 \times 3600 + 400$ the $3600$ place
Step 2 $400 = 6 \times 60 + 40$ the $60$ place
Step 3 1 narrow wedge; 6 narrow wedges; 4 sideways wedges $40$ is four tens
Answer $1 \mid 6 \mid 40$, written with $1 + 6 + 4 = 11$ marks
Example 14

A film runs for 10 000 seconds. Write its length in hours, minutes and seconds.

Solution.

Step 1 $10\,000 = 2 \times 3600 + 2800$ an hour is $3600$ seconds
Step 2 $2800 = 46 \times 60 + 40$ a minute is $60$
Answer 2 hours 46 minutes 40 seconds

This is the Mesopotamian numeral $2 \mid 46 \mid 40$, still in use.
