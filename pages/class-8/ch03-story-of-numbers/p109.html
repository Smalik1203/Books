Example 14 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) XLIX(1) 1900
(Q) XCIV(2) 49
(R) CDXL(3) 440
(S) MCM(4) 94

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  2. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2

Solution.

Step 1XL + IX = 49, XC + IV = 94, CD + XL = 440 and M + CM = 1900.
