3.7Zero, and the System We Use

Everything the last section wanted is delivered by one further idea: give the empty place a symbol of its own, use it everywhere including at the end, and treat it as a digit like any other.

That symbol is $0$, and with it the system is finished. Ten digits, a base of ten, and position doing the rest:

$3605$ $= (3 \times 10^3) + (6 \times 10^2) + (0 \times 10^1) + (5 \times 10^0)$
