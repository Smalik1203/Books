Type 10 · Comparing the systems

Example 18

Write 2764 in the Roman, Egyptian and Hindu systems, and count the symbols each one uses.

Solution.

Step 1 Roman: MM DCC LX IV, that is MMDCCLXIV, which is $2 + 3 + 2 + 2 = 9$ symbols
Step 2 Egyptian: 2 lotuses, 7 coils, 6 arches, 4 strokes, which is $2 + 7 + 6 + 4 = 19$ one per unit of each
Step 3 Hindu: $2764$, which is 4 digits one per place
Answer Hindu 4, Roman 9, Egyptian 19

The Egyptian count is the sum of the digits, and the Roman count depends on how each digit breaks into landmarks. Ours is one symbol a place, whatever the digit.
