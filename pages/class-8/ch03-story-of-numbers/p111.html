Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): No place of a base-$9$ numeral holds a digit greater than $8$.

    Reason (R): Nine of any landmark in base $9$ make one of the next landmark.

  1. Assertion (A): The base-$6$ numeral $100$ stands for $36$.

    Reason (R): A base-$6$ system needs six different symbols.

  1. Assertion (A): The base-$4$ numeral $33$ stands for $15$.

    Reason (R): In base $4$, the second place from the right is the sixteens place.

  1. Assertion (A): The binary numeral $1000$ stands for one thousand.

    Reason (R): In base $2$, the fourth place from the right is the eights place.
