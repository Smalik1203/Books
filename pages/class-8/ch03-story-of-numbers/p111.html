Example 9

A toy counter has three wheels. Each wheel shows a digit from $0$ to $4$. When a wheel passes $4$, it goes back to $0$ and turns the wheel on its left on by one, as the wheels of a water meter do. The counter starts at $000$, and each click adds one. Fig. 3.10 shows it part-way through a count. The wheels are read from left to right, like the digits of a numeral.

  1. After $38$ clicks the counter shows
    1. 038
    2. 123
    3. 133
    4. 113
  2. Numerical answer. After how many clicks does the counter first come back to $000$?
  3. Numerical answer. The counter shows $342$, as in Fig. 3.10, and has not yet gone back to $000$. How many clicks has it counted?

Solution.

Step 1No wheel ever shows $5$, because five clicks on a wheel make one on the wheel to its left. So the counter counts in base $5$.
Step 2The wheels are the places of base $5$: $1$, $5$ and $25$. $38 = 1 \times 25 + 2 \times 5 + 3$, so the counter shows $123$.
Step 3The largest reading is $444$, which is $4 \times 25 + 4 \times 5 + 4 = 124$. One more click makes $125$ and shows $000$.
Step 4$342$ stands for $3 \times 25 + 4 \times 5 + 2 = 97$ clicks.
Check$97 + 28 = 125$: after $28$ more clicks the counter shows $000$ again, just as $124$ more clicks after $000$ bring it to $444$.
Answer(i) (b) 123; (ii) 125; (iii) 97
