It is worth ending on how ordinary each step looks in isolation. Nobody inventing the five-bar gate was trying to found modern science. They were trying to read a tally without counting it. And yet the line from that mark to the calculations that fly aircraft and run banks is unbroken, and every link in it was somebody solving a small, practical, immediate problem.

The next time you write a number down, look at what you are doing. Ten shapes, each meaning something different depending on where you put it, and one of them meaning nothing at all — and between them they can express any quantity in the universe. It took the whole world a very long time to think of that.
