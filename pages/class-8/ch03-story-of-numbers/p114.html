Long answer
  1. A farm packs eggs in boxes of $12$, puts $12$ boxes in a carton and $12$ cartons in a crate. An order for $2000$ eggs is packed with as few containers as possible. How many crates, cartons and boxes are used, and how many eggs are left loose? Which base is the farm using?
  1. Write $5000$ using the Mayan landmarks $1$, $20$, $360$ and $7200$, giving the count in each place. Then write it in a true base-$20$ system, whose landmarks are $1$, $20$, $400$ and $8000$. Why do the two answers differ?
  1. Show that the largest three-place numeral in base $4$, which is $333$, stands for one less than the base-$4$ numeral $1000$. Then find the largest four-place numeral in base $3$, and the number it stands for.
Case-based questions

    RiderSeconds
    P3725
    Q5400
    R7384
    1. Write each time in hours, minutes and seconds.
    2. Written in places of $3600$, $60$ and $1$, which rider's time has an empty place?
    3. How much longer did R take than P? Give the answer in hours, minutes and seconds.
