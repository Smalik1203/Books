Case-based questions

  1. Three friends time a long bicycle ride. Each stopwatch shows the time in seconds.

    RiderSeconds
    P3725
    Q5400
    R7384
    1. Write each time in hours, minutes and seconds.
    2. Written in places of $3600$, $60$ and $1$, which rider's time has an empty place?
    3. How much longer did R take than P? Give the answer in hours, minutes and seconds.

  1. A panel of six lamps shows a number in base $2$: a lamp that is on is a $1$, a lamp that is off is a $0$, and the rightmost lamp is the units place.

    PanelLamps
    X1 0 1 1 0 0
    Y0 1 1 1 1 1
    Z1 1 0 0 1 0
    1. What number does each panel show?
    2. What is the largest number six lamps can show?
    3. How many lamps would a panel need to show $200$?
