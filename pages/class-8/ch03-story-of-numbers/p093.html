1. Assertion (A): In the base-$6$ numeral $505$, the $0$ shows that there are no sixes.

    Reason (R): A place value system with no zero can always be read in only one way.

  1. Assertion (A): $8000$ is a landmark of the Mayan system.

    Reason (R): In a base system, every landmark is the base times the one before.

  1. Assertion (A): The base-$2$ numeral $1111$ stands for $15$.

    Reason (R): $1111$ in base $2$ is one less than $10000$ in base $2$, which stands for $16$.
