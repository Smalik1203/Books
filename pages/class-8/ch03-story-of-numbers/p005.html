3.4Landmark Numbers

Grouping by fives is a start, but a number like $1580$ written as three hundred and sixteen groups of five and no more is not much of an improvement on writing it in strokes. What is missing is a second level of grouping — groups of groups.

The Roman system does exactly that. It grew out of the older numerals of the Etruscans, Rome's neighbours in Italy, and was used across Europe for nearly two thousand years. It has a short list of numbers with symbols of their own:

IVXLCDM
1510501005001000

Call these the landmark numbers of the system. To write any number, take as many of the largest landmark as will fit, then as many of the next, and so on down.
