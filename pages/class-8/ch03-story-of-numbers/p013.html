3.6Letting Position Do the Work

A base fixes the arithmetic, but one problem remains, and it is the problem this chapter opened with. Egypt needed a new picture for every new power of ten: a stroke, an arch, a coil, a lotus, a finger, a tadpole, a kneeling figure — and after a million, nothing. Push far enough and the system runs out of symbols, just as the alphabet did.
