1. Invent a base-$3$ place value system with your own three symbols, one of which must be a zero. Write the numbers $1$ to $10$ in it, and then write $2025$.
  1. Base $60$ divides into halves, thirds, quarters, fifths and sixths without a remainder; base $10$ manages only halves and fifths. List every factor of each, and use them to explain why an hour splits more easily than a metre.
  1. Computers use base $2$, which has the fewest factors of any base. Suggest why that is not a disadvantage for a machine, and give the base-$2$ form of $10$, $16$ and $100$.
  1. A friend claims that a number system needs a zero. Is that true of every system in this chapter? Say which ones managed without one, and what they gave up in exchange.
