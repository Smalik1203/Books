3.5The Idea of a Base

The trouble with the Roman landmarks is that they are a list somebody just chose. Fix that, and almost everything else fixes itself.

Around 3000 BCE the Egyptians were using a system whose landmarks were chosen by a rule rather than picked by hand. Start at $1$. Ten of those make the next landmark, $10$. Ten of those make the next, $100$. Ten of those, $1000$ — and so on, each landmark ten times the last.

Each landmark got a picture of its own — a stroke, a heel bone, a coil of rope, a lotus — and a number was written by drawing as many of each as it contained.
