  1. $10102_{3}$
  2. $10201_{3}$

Solution.

Step 1Landmarks 81, 27, 9, 3, 1: $100 = 1 \times 81 + 0 \times 27 + 2 \times 9 + 0 \times 3 + 1$.
Check$81 + 18 + 1 = 100$, and every digit is less than 3.

Example 3

A film runs for 10 000 seconds. Which statements are correct?

  1. It runs for 2 h 46 min 40 s.
  2. It runs for 166 min 40 s.
  3. It runs for more than 3 hours.
  4. $10\,000 = 2 \times 60^2 + 46 \times 60 + 40$

Solution.

Step 1$10\,000 = 166 \times 60 + 40$, so 166 min 40 s; and $166 = 2 \times 60 + 46$, so 2 h 46 min 40 s.
Step 2That is the base-sixty form in (d), and 3 hours would be $10\,800$ seconds.
Check$2 \times 3600 + 46 \times 60 + 40 = 7200 + 2760 + 40 = 10\,000$.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
