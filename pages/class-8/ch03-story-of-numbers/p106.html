Example 3

What number is MMCDXCVI?

Solution.

Step 1 MM is $2000$
Step 2 CD is $500 - 100 = 400$ C before D subtracts
Step 3 XC is $100 - 10 = 90$ X before C subtracts
Step 4 VI is $5 + 1 = 6$
Answer $2000 + 400 + 90 + 6 = 2496$

Example 4

Add CLXVI and CCLXXVII without converting either to our numerals.

Solution.

Step 1 Cs: C and CC make three; Ls: L and L make two gather like symbols
Step 2 Xs: X and XX make three; Vs: V and V make two; Is: I and II make three
Step 3 two Ls make a C, so four Cs trade upward
Step 4 two Vs make an X, so four Xs
Step 5 CCCCXXXXIII is written CDXLIII subtractive rule
Answer CDXLIII

As a check, $166 + 277 = 443$, and CDXLIII is $400 + 40 + 3$.

Example 5

Is $\text{V} \times \text{C}$ a Roman landmark? Is $\text{L} \times \text{C}$? Then say whether $10 \times 1000$ is an Egyptian landmark.

Solution.

Step 1 $5 \times 100 = 500$, which is D a landmark
Step 2 $50 \times 100 = 5000$ the list stops at M
Step 3 $10 \times 1000 = 10^1 \times 10^3 = 10^4$ exponents add
Answer Yes; no; yes, $10^4$ is the finger

The Roman answer depended on the numbers chosen. The Egyptian one could not have come out any other way.
