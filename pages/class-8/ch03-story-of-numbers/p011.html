3.6Letting Position Do the Work

A base fixes the arithmetic, but one problem remains, and it is the problem this chapter opened with. Egypt needed a new picture for every new power of ten: a stroke, an arch, a coil, a lotus, a finger, a tadpole, a kneeling figure — and after a million, nothing. Push far enough and the system runs out of symbols, just as the alphabet did.

The way out is startling once you see it, and it was found more than once. Look again at how a number is written:

$324$ in Egypt coil coil coil, arch arch, stroke stroke stroke stroke

Every symbol in that line is doing two jobs at once. It says how many by being repeated, and it says of what by being the shape it is. Separate the two jobs, and the second one can be done without a symbol at all — by position.
