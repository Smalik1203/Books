Example 10 · Multiple correct

The Egyptian symbols stand for 1, 10, 100, 1000, 10 000 and 100 000. Which statements about writing 40 506 are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It needs 4 of the symbol for 10 000.
  2. It needs 5 of the symbol for 100.
  3. It needs 15 symbols in all.
  4. It needs 1 of the symbol for 10.

Solution.

Step 1$40\,506 = 4 \times 10\,000 + 5 \times 100 + 6 \times 1$.
Step 2That is $4 + 5 + 6 = 15$ symbols; the tens digit is 0, so no symbol for 10 is used.

Example 11 · Numerical answer

Using the Mayan landmarks 1, 20, 360 and 7200, $1000 = 2 \times 360 + k \times 20$. Find $k$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$1000 - 2 \times 360 = 1000 - 720 = 280$.
Step 2$280 \div 20 = 14$, with nothing left for the ones.

Example 12 · Numerical answer

In the Mesopotamian base-sixty system, $4000 = a \times 3600 + b \times 60 + c$, with $a$, $b$ and $c$ each less than 60. Find $b$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$4000 - 3600 = 400$, so $a = 1$.
Step 2$400 = 6 \times 60 + 40$, so $b = 6$ and $c = 40$.
Answer6
