Example 9

List the landmarks of a base-4 system up to 1000. Then write 300 as a sum of them, using as few as possible.

Solution.

Step 1 $1, \ 4, \ 16, \ 64, \ 256$ next is $1024$, too big
Step 2 one $256$ leaves $300 - 256 = 44$ largest first
Step 3 no $64$ fits in $44$
Step 4 two $16$s leave $44 - 32 = 12$
Step 5 three $4$s leave $12 - 12 = 0$, so no ones
Answer $300 = 256 + 16 + 16 + 4 + 4 + 4$

No landmark is used four or more times, as the base requires.

Example 10

Write 100 in base 3.

Solution.

Step 1 $100 \div 3 = 33$ remainder $1$ the units digit
Step 2 $33 \div 3 = 11$ remainder $0$
Step 3 $11 \div 3 = 3$ remainder $2$
Step 4 $3 \div 3 = 1$ remainder $0$, and $1 \div 3 = 0$ remainder $1$
Step 5 $1 \times 81 + 0 \times 27 + 2 \times 9 + 0 \times 3 + 1 = 100$ check
Answer $100 = 10201_{\,3}$, the remainders read upwards
Example 11

What number does the base-5 numeral $3042_{\,5}$ stand for?

Solution.

Step 1 the places are $125$, $25$, $5$ and $1$ powers of five
Step 2 $3 \times 125 + 0 \times 25 + 4 \times 5 + 2 \times 1$ expanded form
Step 3 $375 + 0 + 20 + 2 = 397$
Answer $397$
