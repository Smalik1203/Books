Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $1011_{2}$(1) 15
(Q) $32_{4}$(2) 24
(R) $44_{5}$(3) 11
(S) $120_{3}$(4) 14

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$8 + 2 + 1 = 11$, $3 \times 4 + 2 = 14$, $4 \times 5 + 4 = 24$ and $9 + 2 \times 3 = 15$.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
