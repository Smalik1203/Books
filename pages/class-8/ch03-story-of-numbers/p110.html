Example 14

A film runs for 10 000 seconds. Write its length in hours, minutes and seconds.

Solution.

Step 1 $10\,000 = 2 \times 3600 + 2800$ an hour is $3600$ seconds
Step 2 $2800 = 46 \times 60 + 40$ a minute is $60$
Answer 2 hours 46 minutes 40 seconds

This is the Mesopotamian numeral $2 \mid 46 \mid 40$, still in use.

Type 8 · The Mayan landmarks

Example 15

Using the Mayan landmarks 1, 20, 360 and 7200, write 1000 in Mayan style.

Solution.

Step 1 $1000 = 2 \times 360 + 280$ $7200$ is too big
Step 2 $280 = 14 \times 20 + 0$
Step 3 $14$ is two bars and four dots; $0$ is the shell bar is $5$, dot is $1$
Answer from the top: two dots; two bars and four dots; a shell

Type 9 · Chinese rod numerals

Example 16

A rod numeral has one empty place. From left to right it shows four rods lying one way, a blank, three rods lying the same way, and two rods turned the other way. What number is it?

Solution.

Step 1 the two rods on the right are the units: $2$ the last place
Step 2 the three rods, turned the other way, are the tens: $3$ places alternate
Step 3 the four lie like the tens, so two places on: thousands the hundreds would lie like the units
Step 4 the blank is the hundreds place: $0$
Answer $4 \times 1000 + 0 \times 100 + 3 \times 10 + 2 = 4032$

Without the question's word one, the numeral could also be $400\,032$, with three empty places: the rods would lie the same way again. That is why the alternation is a good fix and not the right one.
