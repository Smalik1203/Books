Exercise Set 3.5
  1. Write each number in expanded form using powers of ten, showing every zero.
    1. $5030$
    2. $70\,009$
    3. $800\,400$
  1. Write $6 \times 10^4 + 2 \times 10^2 + 9$ as an ordinary numeral.
  1. What does each $0$ in $4040$ tell you? What number would a reader see if both zeros were left out?
  1. Write two thousand and forty in Egyptian, Roman and Hindu numerals. How many symbols does each use?
  1. Say whether each system uses place value, and whether it has a base.
    1. Roman
    2. Egyptian
    3. Mesopotamian
    4. Mayan
    5. Hindu
  1. Write $30$ in base $2$, in base $4$ and in base $8$. How many different digits does each of these systems need?
  1. Tanvi says a zero is only ever needed at the end of a number, as in $50$. Give a number that shows she is wrong.
  1. Why is the name Arabic numerals misleading? Give a better name and say why.
  1. List the factors of $60$ and of $10$. How many minutes are there in a sixth of an hour? Why can a metre not be split into six equal parts each a whole number of centimetres long?
  1. Invent a base-$3$ system with three symbols of your own, one of them a zero. Write the numbers $1$ to $9$ in it, and then write $20$. Check your answer by expanding it.
  1. Of the five ideas in Fig. 3.6, which does each of these have? Answer with the numbers of the ideas.
    1. a tally
    2. Roman
    3. Egyptian
    4. Hindu
