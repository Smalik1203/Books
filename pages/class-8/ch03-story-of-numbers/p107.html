Example 4

The landmarks of base 4 are 1, 4, 16, 64, 256, … . Which statements are correct?

  1. 256 is a landmark.
  2. 128 is a landmark.
  3. $300 = 256 + 2 \times 16 + 3 \times 4$
  4. In base 4, 300 is written $10230_{4}$.

Solution.

Step 1The landmarks are powers of 4, each 4 times the one before. 128 lies between 64 and 256, so it is not one.
Step 2$256 + 32 + 12 = 300$ uses 1 of 256, 0 of 64, 2 of 16, 3 of 4 and 0 ones: the digits 1, 0, 2, 3, 0.
Step 3So (a), (c) and (d) are true. (b) is false: $128 = 2 \times 64$ is two of a landmark, not a landmark. And $300$ needs five digits in base 4, since 300 lies between 256 and 1024.
Check$1 \times 256 + 0 \times 64 + 2 \times 16 + 3 \times 4 + 0 = 300$, and every digit is less than 4.
