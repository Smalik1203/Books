Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these are correctly formed Roman numerals?

Select all correct options.

  1. XL
  2. IC
  3. XC
  4. VX

Solution.

Step 1XL is $50 - 10 = 40$ and XC is $100 - 10 = 90$: X may stand before L and C.
Step 2I may stand only before V and X, and V is never subtracted, so IC and VX are not allowed; 99 is XCIX and 5 is V.
Answer(a), (c)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

A film runs for 10 000 seconds. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It runs for 2 h 46 min 40 s.
  2. It runs for 166 min 40 s.
  3. It runs for more than 3 hours.
  4. $10\,000 = 2 \times 60^2 + 46 \times 60 + 40$

Solution.

Step 1$10\,000 \div 60 = 166$ remainder 40, so 166 min 40 s; and $166 \div 60 = 2$ remainder 46, so 2 h 46 min 40 s.
Step 2That is the base-sixty form in (d), and 3 hours would be $10\,800$ seconds.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

The landmarks of base 4 are 1, 4, 16, 64, 256, … . Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. 256 is a landmark.
  2. 128 is a landmark.
  3. $300 = 256 + 2 \times 16 + 3 \times 4$
  4. In base 4, 300 is written $10230_{4}$.

Solution.

Step 1The landmarks are powers of 4, and 128 is not one.
Step 2$256 + 32 + 12 = 300$ uses 1 of 256, 0 of 64, 2 of 16, 3 of 4 and 0 ones: the digits 1, 0, 2, 3, 0.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
