Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): At step $3$ the Sierpiński carpet has $512$ squares.

    Reason (R): At each step every square is replaced by $8$ smaller squares.

  1. Assertion (A): A pyramid can have $15$ edges.

    Reason (R): A pyramid on an $n$-sided base has $2n$ edges.

  1. Assertion (A): The Koch snowflake has $48$ sides at step $2$.

    Reason (R): The perimeter of the Koch snowflake is multiplied by $\tfrac43$ at each step.

  1. Assertion (A): The shortest route over the surface of a $6 \text{ cm} \times 3 \text{ cm} \times 2 \text{ cm}$ box, between opposite corners, is $\sqrt{61}$ cm.

    Reason (R): The shortest route over the surface of a box always runs along its edges.

  1. Assertion (A): After $2$ steps the Sierpiński triangle keeps $\tfrac{9}{16}$ of its area.

    Reason (R): Each step keeps three quarters of the area that is left.
