4.7Unfolding a Solid

A sweet box from a shop is not made face by face. It is cut from one flat sheet of card, printed while it is flat, stored flat, and folded only when it is needed. A flat shape that folds up into a solid is called a net of the solid. Every solid with flat faces has a net, and most solids have several nets.

Not every arrangement of six squares folds into a cube. Some leave a gap, and some put two squares on the same face. Exactly eleven arrangements work, if we count two arrangements as the same when one is a turned or flipped copy of the other. Finding all eleven takes squared paper, scissors and some patience.

Net

A net is a flat shape that folds into a solid. One solid can have many nets; a cube has exactly eleven.
