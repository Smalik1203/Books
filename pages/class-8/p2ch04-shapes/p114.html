Why the other options are wrong

2 (a) is the side after one step. (b) halves the side once and (c) halves it twice, which is the Sierpiński triangle's rule, not the carpet's. The carpet divides by three: $9$, then $3$, then $1$.
3 (b) is the area after one step and (d) is the part thrown away at the first step. (a) is the part thrown away after two steps. What is left is $\tfrac89 \times \tfrac89 = \tfrac{64}{81}$.
7 (b) belongs to a prism with $36$ edges and (c) to one with $18$. (d) is the number of edges itself. The rule is $3n$, so $24$ edges means $n = 8$.
11 (a) and (c) are the other two unfoldings, with sides $13$ and $3$, and $12$ and $4$. (d) is the walk along three edges. The balanced pair is $9$ against $4 + 3$, giving $81 + 49 = 130$.
