Example 12

A net is made of one regular pentagon with an identical triangle on each of its five sides. What solid does it fold into, and how many faces, edges and vertices has that solid?

Solution.

Step 1 the five triangles fold up and their tips meet at one point one base, one point above
Step 2 a pyramid on a pentagon: $n = 5$ named after its base
Step 3 faces $5 + 1 = 6$, edges $2 \times 5 = 10$, vertices $5 + 1 = 6$ $n + 1$, $2n$, $n + 1$
Step 4 $6 + 6 - 10 = 2$ check
Answer A pentagonal pyramid, with 6 faces, 10 edges and 6 vertices

A net with two matching polygons and rectangles between them would have folded into a prism instead.

Type 7 · The shortest route across a box

Example 13

A box is $15$ cm by $6$ cm by $3$ cm. An ant crosses its surface from one corner to the opposite one. Work out all three unfoldings and find the shortest route.

Solution.

Step 1 $\sqrt{225 + 81} = \sqrt{306} \approx 17.5$ cm sides $15$ and $6 + 3$
Step 2 $\sqrt{441 + 9} = \sqrt{450} \approx 21.2$ cm sides $15 + 6$ and $3$
Step 3 $\sqrt{324 + 36} = \sqrt{360} \approx 19.0$ cm sides $15 + 3$ and $6$
Answer About 17.5 cm, from the first unfolding

The longest side set against the other two added together wins again.
