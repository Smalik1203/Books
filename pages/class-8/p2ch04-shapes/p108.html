Example 13 · Numerical answer

A Sierpiński carpet is begun from a square of side 81 cm. What area, in $\text{cm}^2$, is left at step 2?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Each step keeps $\frac{8}{9}$ of the area, since 8 of the 9 small squares stay.
Step 2$81^2 \times \frac{8}{9} \times \frac{8}{9} = 6561 \times \frac{64}{81} = 5184$.
Answer5184
Example 14 · Matching

Match each solid in List I with its number of edges in List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Triangular prism(1) 12
(Q) Square pyramid(2) 6
(R) Cube(3) 9
(S) Tetrahedron(4) 8

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1A triangular prism has $3 + 3 + 3 = 9$ edges and a square pyramid $4 + 4 = 8$.
Step 2A cube has 12 edges and a tetrahedron 6.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
