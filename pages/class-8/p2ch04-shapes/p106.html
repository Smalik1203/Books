Example 6

A Koch snowflake is begun from a triangle of side $27$ cm. How many sides are added between step $2$ and step $3$, and how much longer does the perimeter get?

Solution.

Step 1 step 2: $48$ sides of $3$ cm, so $48 \times 3 = 144$ cm $27 \div 9 = 3$
Step 2 step 3: $192$ sides of $1$ cm, so $192 \times 1 = 192$ cm $27 \div 27 = 1$
Step 3 $192 - 48 = 144$ sides added
Step 4 $192 - 144 = 48$ cm longer a third of $144$
Answer 144 sides are added, and the perimeter grows by 48 cm

Each step multiplies the perimeter by $\tfrac43$, so each step adds a third of what was there.

Type 4 · Counting the parts of prisms and pyramids

Example 7

A pyramid has $10$ vertices. What is its base, and how many faces and edges has it?

Solution.

Step 1 $n + 1 = 10$, so $n = 9$ $n + 1$ vertices
Step 2 $9 + 1 = 10$ faces 9 triangles and the base
Step 3 $2 \times 9 = 18$ edges 9 round the base, 9 up
Step 4 $10 + 10 - 18 = 2$ check
Answer A nine-sided base, 10 faces and 18 edges
Example 8

A prism and a pyramid are built on the same polygon. Between them they have $40$ edges. What is the polygon, and how many faces, edges and vertices has each solid?

Solution.

Step 1 $3n + 2n = 40$ prism $3n$, pyramid $2n$
Step 2 $5n = 40$, so $n = 8$
Step 3 prism: $8 + 2 = 10$ faces, $3 \times 8 = 24$ edges, $2 \times 8 = 16$ vertices
Step 4 pyramid: $8 + 1 = 9$ faces, $2 \times 8 = 16$ edges, $9$ vertices
Step 5 $24 + 16 = 40$ check
Answer An octagon. Prism: 10, 24, 16. Pyramid: 9, 16, 9
