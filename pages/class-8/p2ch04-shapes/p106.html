Example 7 · Multiple correct

A net is made of one regular pentagon with an identical triangle on each of its five sides. Which statements about the solid it folds into are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It is a pentagonal pyramid.
  2. It has 6 faces.
  3. It has 10 edges.
  4. It has 5 vertices.

Solution.

Step 1The triangles meet at one apex over the pentagon: a pentagonal pyramid, with $1 + 5 = 6$ faces.
Step 2It has 5 base edges and 5 slant edges, and $5 + 1 = 6$ vertices. Check: $6 + 6 - 10 = 2$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which statements about a hexagonal prism are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It has 12 vertices.
  2. It has 18 edges.
  3. It has 6 faces.
  4. $F + V - E = 2$ for it.

Solution.

Step 1Two hexagons give $6 + 6 = 12$ vertices, and $6 + 6 + 6 = 18$ edges.
Step 2It has 2 hexagons and 6 rectangles, 8 faces: $8 + 12 - 18 = 2$.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

A solid has a square for its top view and a triangle for both its front and its side view. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It can be a square pyramid.
  2. It can be a cube.
  3. If it is a square pyramid, it has 5 faces.
  4. It can be a cone.

Solution.

Step 1A square pyramid seen from above shows its square base, and from the front and side shows triangles; it has $1 + 4 = 5$ faces.
Step 2A cube shows squares from the front, and a cone shows a circle from above.
Answer(a), (c)
