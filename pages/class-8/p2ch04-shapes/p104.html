Look back over the eight and notice how few pictures were needed: once a rule has been written down as a formula, a question about step $40$ is no harder than one about step $2$, and that is exactly what a formula is for.

Single correct
Example 1

At each step of a Sierpiński triangle, every triangle becomes 3 smaller ones. A Sierpiński triangle has 2187 triangles. Which step is it at?

  1. 6
  2. 7
  3. 8
  4. 729

Solution.

Step 1The counts are $1, 3, 9, 27, \ldots$: step $n$ has $3^n$ triangles.
Step 2$2187 = 3^7$, so it is step 7.
Answer(b) 7
Example 2

A prism and a pyramid are built on the same polygon. Between them they have 40 edges. What is the polygon?

  1. a hexagon
  2. an octagon
  3. a pentagon
  4. a decagon

Solution.

Step 1On an $n$-sided polygon a prism has $3n$ edges and a pyramid $2n$.
Step 2$5n = 40$, so $n = 8$: an octagon.
Answer(b) an octagon
