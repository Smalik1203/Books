Example 1 · Single correct

A Sierpiński carpet is begun from a square of side 54 cm. At step 2, what is the side of each remaining square?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 6 cm
  2. 18 cm
  3. 2 cm
  4. 27 cm

Solution.

Step 1At each step every square is cut into 9 squares with a third of its side, and the middle one is removed.
Step 2After two steps the side is $54 \div 3 \div 3 = 6$ cm.
Answer(a) 6 cm
Example 2 · Single correct

At each step of a Sierpiński triangle, every triangle becomes 3 smaller ones. A Sierpiński triangle has 2187 triangles. Which step is it at?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 6
  2. 7
  3. 8
  4. 729

Solution.

Step 1The counts are $1, 3, 9, 27, \ldots$: step $n$ has $3^n$ triangles.
Step 2$2187 = 3^7$, so it is step 7.
Answer(b) 7
Example 3 · Single correct

A pyramid has 10 vertices. How many edges has it?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 10
  2. 20
  3. 18
  4. 9

Solution.

Step 1A pyramid has one apex, so its base has $10 - 1 = 9$ corners: it is a nonagonal pyramid.
Step 2It has 9 base edges and 9 slant edges: $9 + 9 = 18$.
Answer(c) 18
