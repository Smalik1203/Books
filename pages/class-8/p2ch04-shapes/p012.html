4.9Three Views

Hold a solid in front of a lamp, and it throws a flat shadow on the wall. In mathematics, a flat picture of this kind is called a projection. It shows what you would see looking at the solid straight on, with all the depth lost.

One projection is never enough, because many different solids throw the same shadow. A ball and a flat round plate both give a circle. So an object is usually drawn from three directions. The front view looks at it from the front. The top view looks straight down on it. The side view looks at it from one end.
