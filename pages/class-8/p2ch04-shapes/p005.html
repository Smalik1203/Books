Coastlines behave a little like this, which is why the length of a country's coast depends on the length of the ruler used to measure it. Measure Britain's coast in hundred-kilometre steps and you get one figure; measure it in ten-kilometre steps, going into every bay the first ruler stepped over, and you get a much larger one. The coast has not changed. The question was less well posed than it looked.

4.5Fractals People Built

Sierpiński and von Koch gave these shapes names and formulas, but builders and weavers were putting them into work a long time earlier.

The Kandariya Mahadev temple at Khajuraho, finished around 1025 CE, is the clearest example. Its tower is a mountain of stone whose outline is repeated in smaller towers set against it, and those carry smaller towers again, so that the whole silhouette appears at three or four scales at once. The same idea shapes temples at Madurai, Hampi, Rameswaram and Varanasi. A visitor sees the design of the building in every part of the building.

Fulani wedding blankets from Nigeria carry rows of diamonds with smaller diamonds inside them and smaller ones inside those. The Dutch artist M. C. Escher made the idea the whole subject of a print called Smaller and Smaller, in which the same lizard is repeated at scales that halve towards the centre until the drawing gives out.
