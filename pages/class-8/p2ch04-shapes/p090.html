By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Find the number of faces, edges and vertices of a prism whose ends are hexagons.
  1. A Sierpiński carpet starts from a square of side $81$ cm. Find the side of each square left at step $3$.
  1. Can a prism have $25$ edges? Justify your answer.
  1. Taking the area of the first triangle as $1$, find the area of the Sierpiński triangle left at step $3$.
  1. A pyramid has $11$ vertices. Find the number of sides of its base, and the number of edges it has.
  1. At some step, a Koch snowflake has $768$ sides. Find the step.
  1. A solid has $7$ faces and $10$ vertices. Find the number of its edges, and name a solid with these counts.
  1. Find the area of card in a net of a cube of edge $7$ cm.
  1. Write the front view and the top view of a cone standing on its base. Give a reason why the two views are different.
  1. Find the length of the shortest route over the surface of a cube of edge $3$ cm, from one corner to the opposite corner.
Short answer
  1. Using $n$ for the number of sides of each end, show that faces $+$ vertices $-$ edges $= 2$ for every prism.
  1. Show that the Sierpiński carpet keeps $\left(\tfrac89\right)^{n}$ of its area at step $n$. Hence find the area left at step $2$ from a square of side $36$ cm.
  1. Arjun finds the area left at step $3$ of a Sierpiński triangle of area $128$ cm².
    Step 1Each step removes $\tfrac14$ of the area.
    Step 2Three steps remove $3 \times \tfrac14 = \tfrac34$ of the area.
    Step 3So $\tfrac14$ of the area is left.
    Step 4Area left $= \tfrac14 \times 128 = 32$ cm².
    Identify the step in which Arjun has made an error, if any. Correct it and find the right answer for the area.
