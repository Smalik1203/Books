Type 9 · Isometric drawing

Example 17

A cuboid $3$ units by $2$ units by $1$ unit is drawn on isometric paper, with its hidden edges left out. How many edges are drawn, and what is their total length on the paper?

Solution.

Step 1 $12$ edges, $4$ in each of the three directions
Step 2 $12 - 3 = 9$ edges drawn 3 meet the hidden corner
Step 3 $3 \times 3 + 3 \times 2 + 3 \times 1 = 18$ units three of each length
Answer 9 edges, 18 units of line in all

Each edge is drawn at its true length, which is why the total can be read off the paper.
