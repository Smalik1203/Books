Example 10

A gift box is a closed cuboid $8$ cm long, $6$ cm wide and $4$ cm high. A ladybird at one bottom corner walks over the surface of the box to the opposite top corner.

  1. The shortest route is a straight line on a net, across a right-angled triangle whose shorter sides are
    1. 8 cm, 10 cm
    2. 6 cm, 12 cm
    3. 4 cm, 14 cm
    4. 8 cm, 6 cm
  2. Numerical answer. Find the length of the shortest route, in cm, to one decimal place.
  3. Numerical answer. How many cm² of card does the box use, with no overlaps?

Solution.

Step 1Each unfolding puts one measurement against the other two added: $8$ and $10$, $6$ and $12$, $4$ and $14$.
Step 2$8^2 + 10^2 = 164$, $6^2 + 12^2 = 180$ and $4^2 + 14^2 = 212$. The smallest is $164$, and $\sqrt{164} \approx 12.8$.
Step 3The card is $2 \times (48 + 24 + 32) = 208$ cm².
Answer(i) (a) 8 cm, 10 cm; (ii) 12.8; (iii) 208
