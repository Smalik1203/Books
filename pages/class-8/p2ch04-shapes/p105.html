Example 3

A net is made of one regular pentagon with an identical triangle on each of its five sides. Which statements about the solid it folds into are correct?

  1. It is a pentagonal pyramid.
  2. It has 6 faces.
  3. It has 10 edges.
  4. It has 5 vertices.

Solution.

Step 1The triangles meet at one apex over the pentagon: a pentagonal pyramid, with $1 + 5 = 6$ faces.
Step 2It has 5 base edges and 5 slant edges, and $5 + 1 = 6$ vertices, and $6 + 6 - 10 = 2$ as it must be.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 4

A box with no lid is 10 cm long, 6 cm wide and 4 cm high. Which statements are correct?

  1. Its net uses $188 \text{ cm}^2$ of card.
  2. With a lid, the net would use $228 \text{ cm}^2$.
  3. Its volume is $240 \text{ cm}^3$.
  4. Its net has 5 rectangles.

Solution.

Step 1Base $60$, two long sides $2 \times 40 = 80$ and two short sides $2 \times 24 = 48$: $188 \text{ cm}^2$, in 5 rectangles.
Step 2The volume is $10 \times 6 \times 4 = 240 \text{ cm}^3$. A lid would add another 60, giving $248 \text{ cm}^2$ of card.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
