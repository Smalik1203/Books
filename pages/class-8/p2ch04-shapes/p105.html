Example 4 · Single correct

A solid of the kind in this chapter has 20 faces and 30 edges. How many vertices has it?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 10
  2. 50
  3. 8
  4. 12

Solution.

Step 1For these solids $F + V - E = 2$.
Step 2$20 + V - 30 = 2$, so $V = 12$.
Answer(d) 12
Example 5 · Single correct

The net of a cube has an area of $150 \text{ cm}^2$. What is the volume of the cube?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $125 \text{ cm}^3$
  2. $25 \text{ cm}^3$
  3. $150 \text{ cm}^3$
  4. $216 \text{ cm}^3$

Solution.

Step 1The net is 6 equal squares, each $150 \div 6 = 25 \text{ cm}^2$, so the edge is 5 cm.
Step 2Volume $= 5^3 = 125 \text{ cm}^3$.
Answer(a) $125 \text{ cm}^3$
Example 6 · Single correct

A prism and a pyramid are built on the same polygon. Between them they have 40 edges. What is the polygon?

Choose one correct option.

  1. a hexagon
  2. an octagon
  3. a pentagon
  4. a decagon

Solution.

Step 1On an $n$-sided polygon a prism has $3n$ edges and a pyramid $2n$.
Step 2$5n = 40$, so $n = 8$: an octagon.
Answer(b) an octagon
