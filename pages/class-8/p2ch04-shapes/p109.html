Example 15 · Matching

Match each net in List I with the solid it folds into. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Six squares(1) triangular prism
(Q) Two triangles and three rectangles(2) cube
(R) One square and four triangles(3) triangular pyramid
(S) Four triangles(4) square pyramid

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–2, R–4, S–3
  2. P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3
  3. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3
  4. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2

Solution.

Step 1Six squares fold into a cube, and two triangles with three rectangles into a triangular prism.
Step 2A square with four triangles makes a square pyramid, and four triangles a triangular pyramid.
Answer(b) P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3
