Example 9

Meenakshi makes a wall hanging from felt. She starts with an equilateral triangle of side $64$ cm and follows the rule of the Sierpiński triangle: at each step, the middle triangle is cut out of every triangle that is left.

  1. How many triangles are left at step $3$?
    1. 9
    2. 27
    3. 12
    4. 81
  2. Numerical answer. What is the side, in cm, of each triangle left at step $3$?
  3. Numerical answer. She goes on to step $4$. How many triangles has she cut out in all?

Solution.

Step 1Each step turns every triangle into $3$, so step $3$ has $3^3 = 27$ triangles.
Step 2Each step halves the side: $64 \to 32 \to 16 \to 8$ cm.
Step 3Every triangle left at one step loses its middle at the next. So steps $1$ to $4$ cut out $1 + 3 + 9 + 27 = 40$ triangles.
CheckAt step $4$, each of the $27$ triangles left at step $3$ loses its middle and becomes $3$, giving $27 \times 3 = 81$ triangles.
Answer(i) (b) 27; (ii) 8; (iii) 40
