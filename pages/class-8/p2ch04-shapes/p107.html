Example 10 · Multiple correct

A box with no lid is 10 cm long, 6 cm wide and 4 cm high. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its net uses $188 \text{ cm}^2$ of card.
  2. With a lid, the net would use $228 \text{ cm}^2$.
  3. Its volume is $240 \text{ cm}^3$.
  4. Its net has 5 rectangles.

Solution.

Step 1Base $60$, two long sides $2 \times 40 = 80$ and two short sides $2 \times 24 = 48$: $188 \text{ cm}^2$, in 5 rectangles.
Step 2A lid adds another 60, giving $248 \text{ cm}^2$; the volume is $10 \times 6 \times 4 = 240 \text{ cm}^3$.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A box is 12 cm long, 5 cm wide and 4 cm high. An ant walks on its surface from one corner to the opposite corner. Find the length of the shortest route, in cm.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Unfold two faces into one rectangle; the three ways give $\sqrt{12^2 + 9^2} = 15$, $\sqrt{17^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{305}$ and $\sqrt{16^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{281}$.
Step 2$15^2 = 225$ is the smallest of 225, 305 and 281, so the shortest route is 15 cm.
Answer15
Example 12 · Numerical answer

At each step of a Sierpiński triangle, every triangle becomes 3 smaller ones. How many triangles are there at step 4?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Step $n$ has $3^n$ triangles.
Step 2$3^4 = 81$.
Answer81
