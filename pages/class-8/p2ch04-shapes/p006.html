A long edge round a small patch

The perimeter is multiplied by $\tfrac43$ at every step, so it grows without limit. It passes a hundred, then a million, then any number you name. The area does not grow like this. Each new bump is smaller than the last, and the whole snowflake stays inside a circle drawn round the first triangle.

So the finished snowflake would have an edge of endless length round an area that fits in a circle. You could never walk all the way round its edge, but you could paint its inside with one tin of paint.

A coastline behaves a little like this. A ruler $100$ km long steps straight across the small bays of the coast of India. A ruler $10$ km long goes into many of them, and gives a longer total. The length depends on the ruler.
