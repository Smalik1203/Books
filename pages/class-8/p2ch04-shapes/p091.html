Find the lengths of the three routes that are straight lines on a net, and state the shortest. How much shorter is it than a route along three edges?
  1. The number of holes in the Sierpiński carpet at step $n$ is $H_n = 1 + 8 + 8^2 + \cdots + 8^{\,n-1}$. Write out $H_4$ and $8H_4$, and subtract to show that $8H_4 - H_4 = 8^4 - 1$. Hence find $H_4$. Use the same method to find $H_6$.
