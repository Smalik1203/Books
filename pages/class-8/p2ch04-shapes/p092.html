  1. A sweet shop packs laddus in closed boxes $20$ cm long, $12$ cm wide and $5$ cm high. Draw a rough net of one box and find its area. The shop adds $10\%$ extra card for flaps. Find the card needed for one box, and for $50$ boxes in square metres.
  1. Anil builds steps from unit cubes, all one cube deep. The bottom row has $4$ cubes. On it, flush at the left, is a row of $3$, then a row of $2$, then $1$ cube at the top. Find the number of cubes. Draw the front, top and side views, and say how many squares each shows. Anil now makes the steps two cubes deep. Find the number of cubes, and say which views change.
  1. A solid has $6$ faces, and every face is a triangle. Each edge is shared by two faces. Show that the solid has $9$ edges. Hence use faces $+$ vertices $-$ edges $= 2$ to find its vertices, and describe how two pyramids can be joined to make it.
  1. A weaver makes a square rug of side $180$ cm with the pattern of step $2$ of the Sierpiński carpet. The holes are filled with red cloth that costs ₹$2$ for every $100$ cm². Find the side of each hole, the total area of the holes and the cost of the red cloth. Check the area with the formula for the carpet.
  1. A $3 \times 3 \times 3$ block of unit cubes is painted on all its outside faces and then taken apart. Find the number of small cubes with paint on $3$, $2$, $1$ and $0$ faces, and check that they add up to $27$. Explain how the counts are linked to the vertices, edges and faces of the block.
