1.6A Pinch of History

The first known lists of perfect squares and perfect cubes were compiled by the Babylonians, as far back as 1700 BCE. Pressed into clay tablets, they were used to look up square roots and cube roots quickly in problems of land measurement, building and anything else where a geometric calculation was needed.

In ancient Sanskrit works the word varga was used both for the square figure, or its area, and for the square power; and ghana both for the solid cube and for the product of a number with itself three times.
