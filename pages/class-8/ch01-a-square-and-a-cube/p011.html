Example 8

Aribam and Bijou play a game: one says a number, the other replies with its square root. Aribam says 25; Bijou answers 5. Bijou says 81; Aribam answers 9. Then Aribam says 250, and Bijou is stuck, because 250 is not a perfect square. Aribam asks for a number that is at least close to $\sqrt{250}$.

first bracket $100 < 250 < 400$, and $\sqrt{100} = 10$, $\sqrt{400} = 20$, so $10 < \sqrt{250} < 20$
narrower $15^2 = 225$ and $16^2 = 256$, so $15 < \sqrt{250} < 16$

256 is much closer to 250 than 225 is, so $\sqrt{250}$ is approximately 16 — and we know it is a little less than 16.

Example 9

Akhil has a square piece of cloth of area 125 cm². Can he cut a square handkerchief of side 15 cm from it? If not, what is the largest handkerchief with a whole-number side that he can cut?

A 15 cm square has area $15^2 = 225$ cm², more than the cloth, so no. The nearest squares to 125 are $11^2 = 121$ and $12^2 = 144$: a 12 cm square needs more cloth than he has, and an 11 cm square does not. The largest handkerchief has side 11 cm.
