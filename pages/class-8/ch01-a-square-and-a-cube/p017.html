Or gather the primes into triplets: $3375 = (3 \times 3 \times 3) \times (5 \times 5 \times 5) = 3^3 \times 5^3$. Either way, 3375 is a perfect cube and $\sqrt[3]{3375} = 15$.

For 500, the factorisation is $2 \times 2 \times 5 \times 5 \times 5$. The 5s make a triplet but the 2s do not — there are only two of them. The factors cannot be split into three identical groups, so 500 is not a perfect cube.

Perfect cubes by prime factors

Each prime factor of a number appears three times in the prime factorisation of its cube. So a number is a perfect cube exactly when its prime factors can be split into three identical groups — every prime occurs a multiple of three times — and the cube root is the product of one group.
