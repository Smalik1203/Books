1.3Square Roots

The area of a square is 49 sq cm. What is the length of its side? We know that $7 \times 7 = 49$, or $7^2 = 49$, so the side is 7 cm. We call 7 the square root of 49: squaring takes 7 to 49, and taking the square root brings it back.

Square root

If $y = x^2$, then $x$ is a square root of $y$. The square root is written with the sign $\sqrt{\phantom{x}}$: $\sqrt{49} = 7$, $\sqrt{64} = 8$, $\sqrt{100} = 10$, and in general $\sqrt{n^2} = n$.

What is the square root of 64? We know $8 \times 8 = 64$. But $(-8) \times (-8)$ is 64 too, since a negative times a negative is positive: $8^2 = 64$ and $(-8)^2 = 64$. So every perfect square has two integer square roots, one positive and one negative — the square roots of 64 are $+8$ and $-8$. In this chapter $\sqrt{\phantom{x}}$ will always mean the positive one, so $\sqrt{64} = 8$.
