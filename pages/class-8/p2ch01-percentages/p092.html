1. In the given figure, a rectangular garden is $40$ m long and $25$ m wide. A path $5$ m wide runs along one long side, and a square flower bed of side $10$ m is in a corner. The rest is grass. Find the percentage of the garden covered by the path and by the flower bed. Also find the percentage covered by grass, and the cost of laying the grass at ₹$60$ per m².
  1. In an election between two candidates, $10\%$ of the voters on the list did not vote, and $90$ of the votes cast were invalid. The winner got $55\%$ of the valid votes and won by $450$ votes. Find the number of voters on the list. Also find the number of votes the winner got.
  1. The population of a town grows by $5\%$ every year. At the end of $2$ years it is $44{,}100$. Find the population at the start. Also find the population at the end of the third year.
  1. In a school, $60\%$ of the students are girls. $75\%$ of the girls and $50\%$ of the boys joined a sports club, and $390$ students joined in all. Find the number of students in the school. Also find the percentage of students who did not join.
  1. A dealer buys a television and a fridge for ₹$60{,}000$ together. He sells the television at a profit of $10\%$ and the fridge at a loss of $5\%$, and gains ₹$2{,}400$ in all. Find the cost price of each. Also find the selling price of each.
Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $150\%$ of $60$ is $90$, which is more than $60$.

    Reason (R): A percentage above $100$ is a share larger than the whole.

  1. Assertion (A): A profit of $20\%$ on a cost price of ₹$500$ is ₹$100$.

    Reason (R): Profit per cent is worked out on the selling price.

  1. Assertion (A): A discount of $10\%$ followed by a further $10\%$ off is the same as a single discount of $20\%$.

    Reason (R): Changes one after another are combined by multiplying their multipliers.

  1. Assertion (A): ₹$1{,}000$ at $10\%$ per annum, compounded yearly, grows to ₹$1{,}210$ in two years.

    Reason (R): $10\%$ of ₹$1{,}000$ is ₹$100$.
