Example 3 · Single correct

A fare rises by $20\%$ one year and by a further $5\%$ the next. What single percentage rise has the same effect?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $25\%$
  2. $24\%$
  3. $26\%$
  4. $30\%$

Solution.

Step 1The multipliers are $1.2$ and $1.05$, and one rise follows the other: $1.2 \times 1.05 = 1.26$.
Step 2A multiplier of $1.26$ is a rise of $26\%$, not $20\% + 5\%$.
Answer(c) $26\%$
Example 4 · Single correct

₹12 000 is deposited for 3 years at $5\%$ a year, and each year's interest is added to the deposit. What is the amount at the end?

Choose one correct option.

  1. ₹13 800
  2. ₹13 230
  3. ₹12 600
  4. ₹13 891.50

Solution.

Step 1Each year multiplies the deposit by $1.05$: $12\,000 \times 1.05^3$.
Step 2$1.05^3 = 1.157625$, so the amount is ₹13 891.50. Interest paid out each year would give only ₹13 800.
Answer(d) ₹13 891.50
Example 5 · Single correct

Brass is made of copper and zinc in the ratio $13 : 7$. What percentage of brass is zinc?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $35\%$
  2. $65\%$
  3. $53.8\%$
  4. $7\%$

Solution.

Step 1Brass is $13 + 7 = 20$ parts, of which 7 are zinc.
Step 2$\frac{7}{20} = \frac{35}{100} = 35\%$.
Answer(a) $35\%$
