Example 3

A school had 640 students last year and has 544 this year. Which statements are correct?

  1. The number fell by 96.
  2. The number fell by $15\%$.
  3. 544 is $85\%$ of 640.
  4. A rise of $15\%$ next year would bring it back to 640.

Solution.

Step 1$640 - 544 = 96$, so (a) is correct.
Step 2$\frac{96}{640} \times 100 = 15$, so the fall is $15\%$ and 544 is $100\% - 15\% = 85\%$ of 640.
Step 3$15\%$ of 544 is only 81.6, and $544 + 81.6 = 625.6$, so (d) fails.
CheckTo get back from 544 to 640 needs a rise of $\frac{96}{544} \times 100 \approx 17.6\%$, not $15\%$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
