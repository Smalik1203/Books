Type 2 · A percentage of a quantity, and the whole from a part

Example 3

A school has $1250$ students, and $36\%$ of them are in the primary section. How many primary students are there?

Solution.

Step 1 $36\%$ of $1250 = \dfrac{36}{100} \times 1250$ of means times
Step 2 $36 \times 12.5 = 450$
Answer $450$ primary students
Example 4

At a concert, $369$ seats are taken, and that is $45\%$ of the seats in the hall. How many seats does the hall have, and how many are empty?

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{45}{100} \times q = 369$ $q$ is the whole
Step 2 $q = 369 \times \dfrac{100}{45} = 820$
Step 3 $820 - 369 = 451$ the empty seats
Answer $820$ seats, of which $451$ are empty

The whole had to be more than $369$, because $45\%$ is less than all of it.

Type 3 · Ratios and mixtures

Example 5

Brass is made of copper and zinc in the ratio $13 : 7$. What percentage of brass is zinc? How much copper is there in $3$ kg of brass?

Solution.

Step 1 $13 + 7 = 20$ parts the whole mixture
Step 2 $\dfrac{7}{20} \times 100 = 35$ zinc
Step 3 copper is $100 - 35 = 65$ per cent
Step 4 $\dfrac{65}{100} \times 3 = 1.95$ copper in $3$ kg
Answer Zinc is $35\%$; $3$ kg of brass holds $1.95$ kg of copper
