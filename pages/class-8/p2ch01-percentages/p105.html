Counting the years by multiplying

Each year multiplies the deposit by $1.1$, so the question is how many tens per cent it takes to get from $10{,}000$ past $15{,}000$ — that is, when $1.1^{\,t}$ first passes $1.5$.

after 3 years $10{,}000 \times 1.331 = $ ₹$13{,}310$
after 4 years ₹$14{,}641$ — still short
after 5 years ₹$16{,}105$

Five years. Simple interest would have taken it to ₹$15{,}000$ in exactly five as well — but a year later the two are ₹$16{,}000$ and ₹$17{,}716$ apart, and the gap widens every year after that.
