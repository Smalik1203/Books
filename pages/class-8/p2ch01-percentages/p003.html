A decimal goes the same way, and faster. Multiplying by $100$ moves the point two places to the right, so $0.6$ becomes $60\%$ and $0.125$ becomes $12.5\%$. Going back, $4\% = 0.04$.

A few are worth knowing by sight, because they come up often and save you the working.

$\dfrac12 = 0.5$ $50\%$
$\dfrac14 = 0.25$ $25\%$
$\dfrac15 = 0.2$ $20\%$
$\dfrac{1}{10} = 0.1$ $10\%$
$\dfrac18 = 0.125$ $12.5\%$
$\dfrac13 = 0.333\ldots$ $33.33\ldots\%$, usually written $33\tfrac13\%$

Look again at the last row. A percentage does not have to be a whole number. $\tfrac13$ is not $33\%$. It is a third of a hundred, and its decimal never ends. Round it when you must, and say that you have.
