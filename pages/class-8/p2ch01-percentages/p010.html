Example 4

The rent of a flat is ₹$12{,}000$ a month. It is raised by $10\%$. What is the new rent? A year later the new rent is cut by $10\%$. What is it then?

Solution.

Step 1 $12{,}000 \times 1.1 = 13{,}200$ the rise
Step 2 $13{,}200 \times 0.9 = 11{,}880$ the cut
Answer ₹$13{,}200$ after the rise, ₹$11{,}880$ after the cut

The rent is not back to ₹$12{,}000$. It is ₹$120$ less. The rise was $10\%$ of $12{,}000$, and the cut was $10\%$ of $13{,}200$. Those are different amounts of money. Section 1.8 looks at this more closely.
