By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Express $\dfrac38$ and $0.045$ as percentages.
  1. Show that $18\%$ of $450$ is equal to $450\%$ of $18$.
  1. If $35\%$ of a number is $84$, find the number.
  1. Write the multiplier for a rise of $15\%$. Hence find the value of ₹$800$ after a rise of $15\%$.
  1. The price of a pen rises from ₹$40$ to ₹$46$. Find the percentage increase.
  1. A batter scored $60$ runs, and $42$ of them came from boundaries. Find the percentage of her runs that did not come from boundaries.
  1. An article bought for ₹$250$ is sold for ₹$290$. Find the profit per cent.
  1. A jacket marked at ₹$1{,}600$ is sold at a discount of $15\%$. Find its selling price.
  1. After a discount of $20\%$, a book costs ₹$240$. Find its marked price.
  1. Two quantities are in the ratio $3 : 7$. Find the first quantity as a percentage of the two together.
Short answer
  1. Lalit sells a phone cover for ₹$546$ at a profit of $5\%$. Find its cost price. Also find the selling price that would give him a profit of $15\%$.
  1. In an examination, $68\%$ of the candidates passed and $256$ failed. Find the number of candidates. Also find how many passed.
  1. Show that a rise of $r\%$ followed by a fall of $r\%$ is a fall of $\dfrac{r^2}{100}\%$ in all. Hence find the overall change when $r = 20$.
  1. Rakesh's income is $25\%$ more than Sunil's. Taking Sunil's income as $x$, find by what percentage Sunil's income is less than Rakesh's.
  1. ₹$15{,}000$ is deposited for $2$ years at $8\%$ per annum, compounded yearly. Find the amount. Also find how much more it is than the amount with simple interest.
  1. A number is increased by $20\%$, and the result is then decreased by $25\%$. Find the single percentage change that has the same effect.
  1. Priya earns ₹$32{,}000$ a month. She spends $35\%$ of it on rent and $25\%$ of the rest on food. Find how much is left. Also find what percentage of her income is left.
