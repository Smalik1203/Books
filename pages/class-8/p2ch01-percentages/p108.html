Example 7

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) $64\%$(1) $4.5\%$
(Q) $0.4\%$(2) $\frac{16}{25}$
(R) $2\tfrac{1}{4}$(3) $225\%$
(S) $0.045$(4) $\frac{1}{250}$
  1. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{64}{100} = \frac{16}{25}$, $\frac{0.4}{100} = \frac{1}{250}$, $2\tfrac{1}{4} = 225\%$ and $0.045 = 4.5\%$.
Answer(a) P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
Example 8

Match each change with the single multiplier that has the same effect.

List IList II
(P) A rise of $20\%$(1) 1
(Q) A fall of $20\%$(2) 0.96
(R) A rise of $20\%$, then a fall of $20\%$(3) 1.2
(S) A rise of $25\%$, then a fall of $20\%$(4) 0.8
  1. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1A rise of $20\%$ multiplies by $1.2$ and a fall by $0.8$; in turn, $1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96$ and $1.25 \times 0.8 = 1$.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
