Example 12

A phone is marked ₹$15{,}000$. The shop gives $10\%$ off, and GST at $18\%$ is added to the price after the discount. What does the buyer pay?

Solution.

Step 1 $15{,}000 \times 0.9 = 13{,}500$ the discount first
Step 2 $13{,}500 \times 0.18 = 2430$ the tax
Step 3 $13{,}500 + 2430 = 15{,}930$ or $13{,}500 \times 1.18$
Answer ₹$15{,}930$
Example 13

A bag sold for ₹$1{,}088$ gives a loss of $15\%$. What did it cost? At what price should it be sold for a profit of $10\%$?

Solution.

Step 1 $0.85 \times \text{cost} = 1088$ loss is on the cost
Step 2 cost $= \dfrac{1088}{0.85} = 1280$
Step 3 $1280 \times 1.1 = 1408$ $10\%$ on the cost
Answer It cost ₹$1{,}280$; it should be sold for ₹$1{,}408$

Example 14

₹$12{,}000$ is deposited for $3$ years at $5\%$ a year. Find the amount if the interest is paid out each year, and if it is left in. How far apart are they?

Solution.

Step 1 $5\%$ of $12{,}000 = 600$ one year's interest
Step 2 $12{,}000 + 3 \times 600 = 13{,}800$ paid out
Step 3 $12{,}000 \times 1.05 = 12{,}600$, then $12{,}600 \times 1.05 = 13{,}230$ left in
Step 4 $13{,}230 \times 1.05 = 13{,}891.50$
Step 5 $13{,}891.50 - 13{,}800 = 91.50$
Answer ₹$13{,}800$ and ₹$13{,}891.50$, which are ₹$91.50$ apart
