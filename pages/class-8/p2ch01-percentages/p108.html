Example 12 · Numerical answer

A scooter bought for ₹80 000 loses $20\%$ of its value every year. What is it worth after 2 years, in rupees?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Each year the value is multiplied by $0.8$.
Step 2$80\,000 \times 0.8 \times 0.8 = 51\,200$.
Answer51200
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A trader wants to allow $10\%$ off the marked price and still make a profit of $17\%$. By what percentage above the cost price must the marked price be?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Selling price $= 1.17 \times$ cost, and it is also $0.9 \times$ marked price.
Step 2Marked price $= \frac{1.17}{0.9} \times$ cost $= 1.3 \times$ cost: $30\%$ above.
Answer30
Example 14 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $64\%$(1) $4.5\%$
(Q) $0.4\%$(2) $\frac{16}{25}$
(R) $2\tfrac{1}{4}$(3) $225\%$
(S) $0.045$(4) $\frac{1}{250}$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{64}{100} = \frac{16}{25}$, $\frac{0.4}{100} = \frac{1}{250}$, $2\tfrac{1}{4} = 225\%$ and $0.045 = 4.5\%$.
Answer(a) P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
