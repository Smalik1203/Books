what is known $\dfrac{20}{100} \times q = 30$
so $q = 30 \times \dfrac{100}{20} = 150$ ml

This time the unknown is the whole, and the equation works just the same. In Class 7 you learnt that you may do anything to an equation if you do it to both sides. That is the rule at work here.

A ratio becomes a percentage in the same way. A lemon sherbet is made from lemon juice and water in the ratio $3 : 11$. What percentage of the sherbet is lemon juice?

It is easy to write $\tfrac{3}{11}$ here, but that is wrong. The ratio compares lemon juice with water. A percentage compares lemon juice with the whole sherbet, which is $3 + 11 = 14$ parts. So lemon juice is $3$ parts in $14$.

$$\frac{3}{14} \times 100 = 21.428\ldots \approx 21.4\%$$

Water is the rest, $78.6\%$, and the two add up to $100\%$ as they must. It helps to estimate first. Half of $14$ is $7$, so $3$ is well under half. A fifth of $14$ is $2.8$, so $3$ is a little over a fifth. The answer had to lie between $20\%$ and $25\%$, and it does.

Estimate before you calculate. Some percentages are easy to find: $50\%$ is half, $25\%$ is half of that, $10\%$ moves the point one place and $1\%$ moves it two places. Most answers can be placed between two of these in your head. If your answer falls outside them, it is wrong.
