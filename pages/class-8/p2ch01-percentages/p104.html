₹$10{,}000$ is deposited at $10\%$ a year with the interest left in. After how many whole years does the deposit first pass ₹$15{,}000$?

Each year multiplies the deposit by $1.1$, so the question is how many tens per cent it takes to get from $10{,}000$ past $15{,}000$ — that is, when $1.1^{\,t}$ first passes $1.5$.

after 3 years $10{,}000 \times 1.331 = $ ₹$13{,}310$
after 4 years ₹$14{,}641$ — still short
after 5 years ₹$16{,}105$, to the nearest rupee

Five years. With the interest paid out each year, it would have reached ₹$15{,}000$ in exactly five as well — but a year later the two stand at ₹$16{,}000$ and ₹$17{,}716$, to the nearest rupee, and the gap widens every year after that.

Single correct
Example 1

After a cut of $15\%$, a pressure cooker costs ₹595. What did it cost before the cut?

  1. ₹684.25
  2. ₹700
  3. ₹680
  4. ₹610

Solution.

Step 1After a $15\%$ cut the price is $85\%$ of the old price: $0.85 \times P = 595$.
Step 2$P = 595 \div 0.85 = 700$. Adding $15\%$ of 595 back gives the wrong ₹684.25.
Answer(b) ₹700
Example 2

The rent of a shop in the market is $150\%$ more than the rent of a shop at the edge of town. By what percentage is the edge-of-town rent less than the market rent?

  1. $150\%$
  2. $60\%$
  3. $40\%$
  4. $66\tfrac{2}{3}\%$

Solution.

Step 1If the edge rent is 100, the market rent is $100 + 150 = 250$.
Step 2The edge rent is $150$ less than 250, and $\frac{150}{250} = 60\%$.
Check$60\%$ off 250 leaves $250 \times 0.4 = 100$, the edge rent.
Answer(b) $60\%$
