after 3 years $10{,}000 \times 1.331 = $ ₹$13{,}310$
after 4 years ₹$14{,}641$ — still short
after 5 years ₹$16{,}105$, to the nearest rupee

Five years. With the interest paid out each year, it would have reached ₹$15{,}000$ in exactly five as well — but a year later the two stand at ₹$16{,}000$ and ₹$17{,}716$, to the nearest rupee, and the gap widens every year after that.

Type 1 · Fractions, decimals and percentages

Example 1

Write $\dfrac78$, $0.045$ and $2\tfrac14$ as percentages.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac78 \times 100 = 87.5$ multiply by $100$
Step 2 $0.045 \times 100 = 4.5$ point two places right
Step 3 $2\tfrac14 = \dfrac94$, and $\dfrac94 \times 100 = 225$ more than one whole
Answer $87.5\%$, $4.5\%$ and $225\%$
Example 2

Write $64\%$ and $0.4\%$ as fractions in lowest terms.

Solution.

Step 1 $64\% = \dfrac{64}{100} = \dfrac{16}{25}$ divide both by $4$
Step 2 $0.4\% = \dfrac{0.4}{100} = \dfrac{4}{1000} = \dfrac{1}{250}$ clear the decimal first
Answer $\dfrac{16}{25}$ and $\dfrac{1}{250}$

A percentage below $1$ is a fraction smaller than a hundredth, and $\tfrac{1}{250}$ is.
