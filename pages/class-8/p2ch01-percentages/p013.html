1.7The Same Rise, Again

A bank pays $10\%$ per annum on a fixed deposit. Per annum means for each year. So ₹$8{,}000$ kept in the bank for a year earns $10\%$ of $8{,}000 = $ ₹$800$. The ₹$8{,}000$ is the principal, the $10\%$ is the rate, and the ₹$800$ is the interest.

Keep the money there for three years, and the answer depends on something the rate does not tell you: what happens to each year's interest.
