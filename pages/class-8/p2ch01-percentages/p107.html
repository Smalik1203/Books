Example 9

After a cut of $15\%$, a pressure cooker costs ₹$595$. What did it cost before the cut?

Solution.

Step 1 $0.85 \times \text{price} = 595$ the cut is a multiplier
Step 2 price $= \dfrac{595}{0.85} = 700$ divide to undo it
Answer ₹$700$

Adding $15\%$ of ₹$595$ back on would give a different, wrong figure: the $15\%$ was of the old price, not the new one.

Example 10

A fare rises by $20\%$ one year and by a further $5\%$ the next. What single percentage rise has the same effect?

Solution.

Step 1 the multipliers are $1.2$ and $1.05$
Step 2 $1.2 \times 1.05 = 1.26$ changes in a row multiply
Answer A single rise of $26\%$, not $25\%$

Type 6 · Profit, loss, discount and tax

Example 11

A shopkeeper buys a mixer for ₹$2{,}400$ and marks it $25\%$ above that. He then allows a discount of $8\%$. Find the selling price and his profit per cent.

Solution.

Step 1 $2400 \times 1.25 = 3000$ the marked price
Step 2 $3000 \times 0.92 = 2760$ discount on the marked price
Step 3 $2760 - 2400 = 360$ the profit
Step 4 $\dfrac{360}{2400} \times 100 = 15$ profit on the cost
Answer He sells it for ₹$2{,}760$, a profit of $15\%$
