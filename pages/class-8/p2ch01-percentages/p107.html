Example 9 · Multiple correct

A shopkeeper buys a mixer for ₹2400, marks it $25\%$ above that, and then allows a discount of $10\%$ on the marked price. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The marked price is ₹3000.
  2. The selling price is ₹2700.
  3. The profit is $15\%$.
  4. The profit is $25\%$.

Solution.

Step 1Marked price $= 2400 \times 1.25 = 3000$; selling price $= 3000 \times 0.9 = 2700$.
Step 2Profit $= 300$ on a cost of 2400, which is $12.5\%$.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

In Village 1, 312 of the 480 homes have a tap; in Village 2, 406 of the 580 homes do. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Village 1: $65\%$ of homes have a tap.
  2. Village 2 has the larger share.
  3. Village 2 has more homes with a tap.
  4. The shares differ by 10 points.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{312}{480} = 65\%$ and $\frac{406}{580} = 70\%$, only 5 points apart; and $406 > 312$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A syrup of 400 g is $12\%$ sugar. How many grams of sugar must be stirred in to make it $20\%$ sugar?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1It has $48$ g of sugar. Adding $s$ g gives $\frac{48 + s}{400 + s} = \frac{20}{100}$.
Step 2Multiply out: $5(48 + s) = 400 + s$, so $240 + 5s = 400 + s$, $4s = 160$ and $s = 40$.
Answer40
