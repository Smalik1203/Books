Case-based questions

  1. A shop runs a sale on school things. GST at $12\%$ is added to the bill after the discounts.

    ItemMarked priceDiscount
    School bag₹1,20015%
    Water bottle₹40010%
    Lunch box₹25020%
    1. Find the price after the discount for each item.
    2. A student buys one of each. What is the bill with GST?
    3. Taking the three items together, what percentage of the marked prices was taken off? Give the answer to one decimal place.

  1. Meena leaves ₹$25{,}000$ in a bank at $8\%$ a year, with the interest added in. The bank's statement for the first year reads:

    YearAt the startInterestAt the end
    1₹25,000₹2,000₹27,000
    1. Write the row for Year 2.
    2. Had the interest been paid out each year, what would Meena have after two years in all, and how much less is that?
    3. What will the deposit be at the end of Year 3?
