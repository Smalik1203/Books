  1. What multiplier gives a fall of $12\%$?

Short answer
  1. A shop sells a bicycle for ₹$4{,}410$ at a profit of $5\%$. Find its cost price, and the selling price that would give a profit of $20\%$.
  1. The price of petrol rose from ₹$96$ to ₹$102$ a litre. Find the percentage increase.
  1. In an examination, $72\%$ of the candidates passed and $196$ failed. How many candidates were there?
  1. ₹$15{,}000$ is left for $2$ years at $8\%$ a year with the interest added in. Find the amount, and how much more it is than if the interest had been paid out each year.
Long answer
  1. A shopkeeper buys $80$ pens at ₹$12$ each. He sells $50$ of them at a profit of $25\%$ and the rest at a loss of $10\%$. Find his overall profit or loss per cent.
  1. $60$ litres of a mixture of milk and water is $20\%$ water. How much milk must be added to make the water $12\%$ of the mixture?
  1. A town has $64{,}000$ people. Its population grows by $5\%$ in one year and by $8\%$ in the next. Find the population after the two years, and the overall percentage rise.
