Assertion and reason

In Questions 15 to 18, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $150\%$ of a number is larger than the number.

    Reason (R): A percentage above $100$ means more than the whole.

  1. Assertion (A): A profit of $20\%$ on a cost of ₹$500$ is ₹$100$.

    Reason (R): Profit per cent is worked out on the selling price.

  1. Assertion (A): A discount of $10\%$ followed by a further $10\%$ off is the same as a single discount of $20\%$.

    Reason (R): Changes one after another are combined by multiplying their multipliers.
