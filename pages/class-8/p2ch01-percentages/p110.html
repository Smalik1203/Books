Example 18

A trader wants to allow $10\%$ off the marked price and still make a profit of $17\%$. How far above the cost price should he mark his goods?

Solution.

Step 1 $0.9 \times m = 1.17$ $m$ is the markup multiplier
Step 2 $m = \dfrac{1.17}{0.9} = 1.3$
Step 3 cost $100$, marked $130$, sold at $130 \times 0.9 = 117$ check
Answer $30\%$ above the cost price

Adding $10$ and $17$ gives $27\%$, which is too little, because the discount is taken of the larger, marked price.
