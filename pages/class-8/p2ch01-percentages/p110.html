Example 9

A shop buys shoes at ₹800 a pair and marks them $50\%$ above cost. In a sale it takes $20\%$ off and then adds $5\%$ GST.

  1. The marked price of a pair is
    1. ₹1,000
    2. ₹1,200
    3. ₹960
    4. ₹1,040
  2. Numerical answer. The price in rupees a customer pays, with GST

Solution.

Step 1Marked price $= 800 \times 1.5 = 1200$; sale price $= 1200 \times 0.8 = 960$; with GST, $960 \times 1.05 = 1008$.
Answer(i) (b) ₹1,200; (ii) 1008
Example 10

A lake had 5,000 fish at the start of 2024. During each year the number of fish grows by $20\%$, and then 600 fish are caught at the end of the year.

  1. The number of fish at the end of 2024 is
    1. 6,000
    2. 5,280
    3. 5,400
    4. 5,600
  2. Numerical answer. How many fish are there at the end of 2025?
  3. Numerical answer. By what percentage has the number of fish grown over the two years?

Solution.

Step 1End of 2024: $5000 \times 1.2 - 600 = 6000 - 600 = 5400$.
Step 2End of 2025: $5400 \times 1.2 - 600 = 6480 - 600 = 5880$.
Step 3$5880 - 5000 = 880$, and $\frac{880}{5000} \times 100 = 17.6$.
CheckWith no fish caught, the lake would hold $5000 \times 1.2 \times 1.2 = 7200$ fish; the catches keep it far below that.
Answer(i) (c) 5,400; (ii) 5880; (iii) 17.6
