Example 6

A $400$ g syrup is $12\%$ sugar. How much sugar must be stirred in to make it $20\%$ sugar?

Solution.

Step 1 water is $88\%$ of $400 = 352$ g the water does not change
Step 2 in the new syrup the water is $80\%$: $0.8 \times s = 352$
Step 3 $s = \dfrac{352}{0.8} = 440$ g
Step 4 $440 - 400 = 40$ g the sugar added
Answer $40$ g of sugar

Check: the syrup now holds $48 + 40 = 88$ g of sugar, and $\dfrac{88}{440} \times 100 = 20$.

Type 4 · Comparing with percentages

Example 7

In one village, $312$ of the $480$ homes have a tap. In another, $406$ of the $580$ homes do. Which village has the larger share of homes with a tap?

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{312}{480} \times 100 = 65$ the first village
Step 2 $\dfrac{406}{580} \times 100 = 70$ the second village
Answer The second village, $70\%$ against $65\%$

It also has more homes without a tap, $174$ against $168$, so counts alone settle nothing.

Type 5 · Percentage change and multipliers

Example 8

A school had $640$ students last year and has $544$ this year. Find the percentage decrease, and the multiplier that takes $640$ to $544$.

Solution.

Step 1 $640 - 544 = 96$ the decrease
Step 2 $\dfrac{96}{640} \times 100 = 15$ on the starting number
Step 3 $640 \times 0.85 = 544$ check the multiplier
Answer A decrease of $15\%$; the multiplier is $0.85$
