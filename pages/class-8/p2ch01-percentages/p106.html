Example 6 · Single correct

The rent of a shop in the market is $150\%$ more than the rent of a shop at the edge of town. By what percentage is the edge-of-town rent less than the market rent?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $150\%$
  2. $60\%$
  3. $40\%$
  4. $66\tfrac{2}{3}\%$

Solution.

Step 1If the edge rent is 100, the market rent is $100 + 150 = 250$.
Step 2The edge rent is $150$ less than 250, and $\frac{150}{250} = 60\%$.
Answer(b) $60\%$

Example 8 · Multiple correct

Select all correct options.

  1. The number fell by 96.
  2. The number fell by $15\%$.
  3. 544 is $85\%$ of 640.
  4. A rise of $15\%$ next year would bring it back to 640.

Solution.

Step 196 is $15\%$ of 640, so 544 is $85\%$ of it; but $15\%$ of 544 is only 81.6, so (d) fails.
