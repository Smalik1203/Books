Example 15

A scooter bought for ₹$80{,}000$ loses $20\%$ of its value every year. What is it worth after $2$ years, and what percentage of its price has it lost?

Solution.

Step 1 $80{,}000 \times 0.8 = 64{,}000$ after 1 year
Step 2 $64{,}000 \times 0.8 = 51{,}200$ after 2 years
Step 3 $80{,}000 - 51{,}200 = 28{,}800$
Step 4 $\dfrac{28{,}800}{80{,}000} \times 100 = 36$ on the price paid
Answer ₹$51{,}200$; it has lost $36\%$, not $40\%$
Example 16

A town of $25{,}000$ people grows by $4\%$ a year. What will its population be in $2$ years?

Solution.

Step 1 $25{,}000 \times 1.04 = 26{,}000$ after 1 year
Step 2 $26{,}000 \times 1.04 = 27{,}040$ $4\%$ of the larger town
Answer $27{,}040$ people

Example 17

The rent of a shop in the market is $150\%$ more than the rent of a shop at the edge of town. By what percentage is the edge-of-town rent less than the market rent?

Solution.

Step 1 take the edge-of-town rent as $100$; the market rent is $250$ $100$ plus $150$
Step 2 $250 - 100 = 150$ the same gap
Step 3 $\dfrac{150}{250} \times 100 = 60$ counted out of the market rent
Answer $60\%$ less
