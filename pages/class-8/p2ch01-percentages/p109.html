Example 15 · Matching

Match each change with the single multiplier that has the same effect. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) A rise of $20\%$(1) 1
(Q) A fall of $20\%$(2) 0.96
(R) A rise of $20\%$, then a fall of $20\%$(3) 1.2
(S) A rise of $25\%$, then a fall of $20\%$(4) 0.8

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1A rise of $20\%$ multiplies by $1.2$ and a fall by $0.8$; in turn, $1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96$ and $1.25 \times 0.8 = 1$.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
