1.2A Hundred Underneath

Gauri is mixing paint for a poster. Three fifths of the mixture is blue. What percentage of the mixture is blue?

A percentage counts out of a hundred. So the question really asks this: which fraction equal to $\tfrac35$ has the denominator $100$? Equivalent fractions take you there.

$$\frac35 \;=\; \frac{6}{10} \;=\; \frac{12}{20} \;=\; \frac{60}{100} \;=\; 60\%$$

Three out of every five is six out of every ten, twelve out of every twenty and sixty out of every hundred. Only the size of the group we count in has changed.

There is a shorter way, and it is the one to remember. A fraction is a share of one. A percentage is a share of a hundred. So to change a fraction into a percentage, multiply it by a hundred.

Both ways along the same road
  • Fraction to percentage: multiply by $100$. So $\tfrac35 \times 100 = 60$, and $\tfrac35 = 60\%$.
  • Percentage to fraction: write it over $100$. So $15\% = \tfrac{15}{100} = \tfrac{3}{20}$.
Example 1

Imran spends $\tfrac{7}{20}$ of his pocket money on books. What percentage is that?

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{7}{20} = \dfrac{35}{100} = 35\%$ by equal fractions
Step 2 $\dfrac{7}{20} \times 100 = 35$, so $\dfrac{7}{20} = 35\%$ by multiplying
Answer $35\%$

Both ways give the same answer. They are the same working done in a different order.
