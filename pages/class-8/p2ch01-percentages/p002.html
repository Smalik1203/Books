Three out of every four is six out of every eight, thirty out of every forty, seventy-five out of every hundred. Nothing changed but the size of the group we counted in.

There is a shorter route, and it is the one to remember. A fraction is a share of one. A percentage is a share of a hundred. So to turn the first into the second, take a hundred times as much.

Both ways along the same road

To express a fraction as a percentage, multiply it by $\mathbf{100}$: $\ \tfrac34 \times 100 = 75$, so $\tfrac34 = 75\%$. To express a percentage as a fraction, divide it by $\mathbf{100}$ — which is only writing the hundred back underneath: $\ 24\% = \tfrac{24}{100} = \tfrac{6}{25}$.

Example 1

Surya keeps $\tfrac25$ of his prize money to buy a canvas. What percentage is that?

by equal fractions $\dfrac25 = \dfrac{20}{50} = \dfrac{40}{100} = 40\%$
by multiplying $\dfrac25 \times 100 = 40$, so $\dfrac25 = 40\%$

The same answer twice, which is what you would hope for: the two methods are the same arithmetic done in a different order.

A decimal takes the same road, and takes it faster. Multiplying by $100$ moves the point two places to the right, so $0.4$ becomes $40\%$ and $0.375$ becomes $37.5\%$. Going back, $6\% = 0.06$.

A few of these are worth knowing by sight, because they turn up constantly and because recognising one saves you the arithmetic.
