1.8A Rise and a Fall Do Not Cancel

Harish sells shoes. He marks every pair $40\%$ above what he paid for it. When some shoes do not sell, he offers $40\%$ off. He thinks that $40\%$ added and $40\%$ taken away must bring him back to what he paid, with no profit and no loss.

He is wrong, and it is worth finding out by how much. Call the price he paid $x$ and follow it.
