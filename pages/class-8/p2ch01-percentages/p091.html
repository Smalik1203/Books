1. The population of a village is $12{,}500$. It falls by $4\%$ in one year and rises by $5\%$ in the next. Find the population after the two years. By what percentage is it more or less than at the start?
  1. A $75$-litre mixture of milk and water is $20\%$ water. Find how much water must be added to make the water $25\%$ of the mixture. Also find the quantity of the new mixture.
  1. A bill comes to ₹$1{,}180$ after GST at $18\%$ has been added. Aarav finds the price before tax like this.
    Step 1The bill is the price before tax plus the GST.
    Step 2The GST is $18\%$ of the bill.
    Step 3$18\%$ of $1180$ is $212.40$
    Step 4Price before tax $= 1180 - 212.40 = 967.60$
    Identify the step in which Aarav has made an error, if any. Correct it and find the right answer.
Long answer
  1. A shopkeeper buys $120$ kg of rice at ₹$40$ a kilogram. He sells $70$ kg at a profit of $20\%$ and the rest at a loss of $10\%$. Find his total profit. Also find his profit per cent on the whole. At what price per kilogram should he have sold all $120$ kg to make the same profit?
  1. A trader marks an article $x\%$ above its cost price and allows a discount of $20\%$ on the marked price. He still makes a profit of $12\%$. Find $x$. Also find the marked price and the selling price when the cost price is ₹$1{,}500$.
  1. Kavita deposits ₹$20{,}000$ in a bank at $10\%$ per annum, compounded yearly. Find the amount after $3$ years and the compound interest. Also find how much more this is than the simple interest for the same time, and explain why it is more.
  1. A machine is bought for ₹$2{,}50{,}000$. Its value falls by $20\%$ in the first year and by $10\%$ in each year after that. Find its value after $3$ years. By what percentage has its value fallen in all? Is this the same as $20\% + 10\% + 10\%$? Justify your answer.
  1. Show that when a price rises by $r\%$, a family must cut the amount it buys by $\tfrac{100r}{100 + r}\%$ to spend the same money. Using this, find the cut needed when the price of sugar rises by $25\%$, and check it for a price of ₹$40$ a kilogram. Also find how much sugar a family that used to buy $12$ kg can now buy.
