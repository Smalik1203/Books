Changes in a row multiply
  • A rise and a fall of the same percentage leave a quantity lower than it started.
  • To undo a change, divide by its multiplier. Do not subtract the same percentage.

Think and Reflect
  1. One shop raises a price by $25\%$ and later cuts it by $25\%$. A second shop cuts first and raises after, by the same two percentages. Which shop's final price is lower? Can you say why without working it out twice?
  2. Is there a markup that no discount can undo? Explain.
  3. Divya is $20\%$ older than Arjun. Is Arjun $20\%$ younger than Divya? Explain.
Exercise Set 1.7
  1. A price of ₹$1{,}200$ is raised by $10\%$ and then cut by $10\%$. Find the final price, and the single percentage change that gives it.
  1. A shopkeeper marks up by $20\%$ and then offers $20\%$ off. Is this a profit or a loss, and of what percentage?
  1. A tower is $150\%$ as tall as the building beside it. What percentage of the tower's height is the building's, to one decimal place?
  1. What discount undoes a markup of $20\%$? Of $60\%$? Write a rule that connects a markup and the discount that undoes it.
  1. A price is cut by $25\%$. By what percentage must the new price be raised to bring it back to where it was?
  1. The length of a rectangle is increased by $20\%$ and its breadth is decreased by $20\%$. Does its area go up or down, and by what percentage?
