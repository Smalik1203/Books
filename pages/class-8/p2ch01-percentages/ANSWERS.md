## 1.2 A Hundred Underneath

### In the text (Gauri's paint)

$\frac35 = \frac{60}{100}$, so **$60\%$** of the mixture is blue.

### Think and Reflect

1. $\frac38 \times 100 = 3 \times 12.5 = 37.5$, so **$37.5\%$**; $\frac{3}{10} \times 100 = 3 \times 10 = 30$, so **$30\%$**.
2. $\frac12$, $\frac14$, $\frac15$ and $\frac{1}{10}$ give $50\%$, $25\%$,
   $20\%$ and $10\%$. These denominators divide $100$ exactly; $3$, $6$, $7$,
   $8$ and $9$ do not, so their percentages are not whole numbers.

## 1.8 A Rise and a Fall Do Not Cancel

### Think and Reflect

1. **The same.** $1.25 \times 0.75 = 0.9375$ and $0.75 \times 1.25 = 0.9375$:
   both shops end $6.25\%$ below the first price. The order of two
   multiplications does not matter.
2. **No.** A markup of $m\%$ is undone by a discount of
   $\frac{100m}{100 + m}\%$, which is always less than $100\%$.
3. **No.** If Arjun is $10$, Divya is $12$, and Arjun is $2$ years younger:
   $\frac{2}{12} \times 100 \approx 16.7$, so about $16.7\%$ younger.
