## 1.8 A Rise and a Fall Do Not Cancel

### Think and Reflect

**Both shops end at the same price.** The first multiplies by $1.2$ and then
$0.8$, the second by $0.8$ and then $1.2$, and a product does not depend on
the order: $1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96$ either way. Both finish $4\%$ below where
they began.

### Exercise Set 1.7

1. $800 \times 1.25 = 1000$ and $1000 \times 0.75 = 750$: **₹750**. As one
   step, $1.25 \times 0.75 = 0.9375$, a **fall of $6.25\%$**.

2. $1.4 \times 0.6 = 0.84$: a **loss of $16\%$**.

3. $\frac{100}{110} \times 100 = 90.90\ldots$: **$90.9\%$** to one decimal
   place.

4. A markup of $25\%$: $1.25 \times m = 1$, so $m = 0.8$, a **discount of
   $20\%$**. A markup of $100\%$: $2 \times m = 1$, so $m = 0.5$, a
   **discount of $50\%$**. The rule: a markup of $M\%$ is undone by a
   discount of $\frac{100M}{100 + M}\%$. (Check with the chapter's own case:
   $M = 50$ gives $33\frac13\%$.)
