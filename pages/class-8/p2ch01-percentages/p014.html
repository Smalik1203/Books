Surbhi sells cookware. On everything she buys from the wholesaler she puts a profit margin of $50\%$. When some stock will not move she offers $50\%$ off, and she reasons that a half added and a half taken away must bring her back to what she paid — no profit, but no loss either.

She is wrong, and it is worth finding out by how much. Call the wholesale price of the stock $x$ and follow it.
