Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): There is no regular polygon with an exterior angle of $16^\circ$.

    Reason (R): The exterior angle of a regular polygon must divide $360^\circ$ exactly.

  1. Assertion (A): In rectangle $ABCD$, if $AC = 14$ cm, then $BD = 14$ cm.

    Reason (R): The diagonals of every parallelogram are equal.

  1. Assertion (A): The interior angles of a hexagon add to $720^\circ$.

    Reason (R): A hexagon has 9 diagonals.
