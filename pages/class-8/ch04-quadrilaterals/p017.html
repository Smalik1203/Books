Example 8

The diagonals of rhombus $ABCD$ meet at $O$, and $\angle ABC = 110^\circ$. Find $\angle AOB$, $\angle ABO$ and $\angle BAO$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$ diagonals of a rhombus
Step 2 $\angle ABO = 110^\circ \div 2 = 55^\circ$ $BD$ halves $\angle B$
Step 3 $\angle BAO = 180^\circ - 90^\circ - 55^\circ = 35^\circ$ triangle $AOB$
Answer $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$, $\angle ABO = 55^\circ$, $\angle BAO = 35^\circ$
Example 9

The diagonals of rectangle $PQRS$ meet at $O$, and $\angle OPQ = 35^\circ$. Find the angles $\angle POQ$ and $\angle OQR$.

Solution.

Step 1 $OP = OQ$ equal diagonals, bisecting each other
Step 2 $\angle OQP = \angle OPQ = 35^\circ$ triangle $OPQ$ is isosceles
Step 3 $\angle POQ = 180^\circ - 35^\circ - 35^\circ = 110^\circ$
Step 4 $\angle OQR = 90^\circ - 35^\circ = 55^\circ$ $\angle PQR$ is a right angle
Answer $\angle POQ = 110^\circ$ and $\angle OQR = 55^\circ$
Diagonals
  • The diagonals of a rhombus cross at right angles; those of a rectangle are equal.
  • The diagonals of a square are equal and cross at right angles.
