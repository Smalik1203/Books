Example 11 · Numerical answer

How many more diagonals has a 9-sided polygon than a 7-sided one?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1A 9-gon has $\frac{9 \times 6}{2} = 27$ diagonals and a heptagon $\frac{7 \times 4}{2} = 14$.
Step 2$27 - 14 = 13$.
Answer13
Example 12 · Numerical answer

The angles of a pentagon are $x^\circ$, $(x + 10)^\circ$, $(x + 20)^\circ$, $(x + 30)^\circ$ and $(x + 40)^\circ$. Find the largest angle, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The angles of a pentagon add to $540^\circ$: $5x + 100 = 540$, so $x = 88$.
Step 2The largest is $88 + 40 = 128$.
Answer128
Example 13 · Numerical answer

The perimeter of a parallelogram is 60 cm, and two adjacent sides are in the ratio $2 : 3$. Find the longer side, in cm.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Two adjacent sides make half the perimeter: 30 cm, in $2 + 3 = 5$ parts.
Step 2One part is 6 cm, so the longer side is $3 \times 6 = 18$ cm.
Answer18
