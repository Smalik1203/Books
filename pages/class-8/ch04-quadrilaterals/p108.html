Example 7

Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) Each angle of a regular hexagon(1) 20
(Q) Exterior angle of a regular 120-gon(2) $540^\circ$
(R) Angle sum of a pentagon(3) $120^\circ$
(S) Diagonals of an octagon(4) $3^\circ$
  1. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$720^\circ \div 6 = 120^\circ$, $360^\circ \div 120 = 3^\circ$, $3 \times 180^\circ = 540^\circ$, and $\frac{8 \times 5}{2} = 20$.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
Example 8

List I describes the diagonals of a quadrilateral. Match it with List II.

List IList II
(P) They bisect each other.(1) square
(Q) As (P), and they are equal.(2) parallelogram
(R) As (P), equal and perpendicular.(3) rectangle
  1. P–3, Q–2, R–1
  2. P–1, Q–3, R–2
  3. P–2, Q–3, R–1
  4. P–2, Q–1, R–3

Solution.

Step 1Bisecting diagonals give a parallelogram; equal ones, a rectangle; equal and perpendicular ones, a square.
Answer(c) P–2, Q–3, R–1
