Example 7

In parallelogram $ABCD$, $AB = (4x - 3)$ cm and $CD = (2x + 9)$ cm. Find $x$ and the length of $AB$.

Solution.

Step 1 $4x - 3 = 2x + 9$ opposite sides are equal
Step 2 $2x = 12$, so $x = 6$
Step 3 $AB = 4 \times 6 - 3 = 21$
Check $CD = 2 \times 6 + 9 = 21$
Answer $x = 6$ and $AB = 21$ cm
