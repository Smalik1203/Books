4.4Exterior Angles

Extend one side of a polygon beyond a vertex. The angle between this extension and the next side is an exterior angle at that vertex. The interior angle and the exterior angle sit side by side on a straight line, so they always add to $180^\circ$.

$$\text{interior angle} + \text{exterior angle} = 180^\circ \text{ at every vertex}$$

Now imagine walking along the edge of a park shaped like a pentagon. At each corner you turn, and the amount you turn is the exterior angle at that corner. When you get back to where you started, you face the same way as at the start. So you have turned through one full turn, which is $360^\circ$.

$$\text{the exterior angles of any polygon add to } 360^\circ$$

The interior angle sum grows with the number of sides. The exterior angle sum is always $360^\circ$: more sides give more turns, but smaller ones.

Example 3

Three exterior angles of a quadrilateral are $70^\circ$, $95^\circ$ and $110^\circ$. Find the fourth exterior angle and all four interior angles.

Solution.

Step 1 $360 - (70 + 95 + 110) = 360 - 275 = 85$ exterior angles add to $360^\circ$
Step 2 $180 - 70 = 110$ and $180 - 95 = 85$ on a line
Step 3 $180 - 110 = 70$ and $180 - 85 = 95$
Answer The fourth exterior angle is $85^\circ$. The interior angles are $110^\circ$, $85^\circ$, $70^\circ$ and $95^\circ$
