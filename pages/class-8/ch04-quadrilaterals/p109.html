Example 9

In one tiled floor, two regular octagons and one other regular polygon meet at every corner. In a second floor, two regular 12-gons and one other regular polygon meet at every corner.

  1. Each angle of a regular octagon is
    1. $120^\circ$
    2. $135^\circ$
    3. $144^\circ$
    4. $150^\circ$
  2. Numerical answer. Find the number of sides of the other polygon in the first floor.
  3. Numerical answer. Do the same for the second floor.

Solution.

Step 1Octagon: $180^\circ - 360^\circ \div 8 = 135^\circ$. Then $360 - 270 = 90$, the angle of a square: 4 sides.
Step 212-gon: $180^\circ - 30^\circ = 150^\circ$. Then $360 - 300 = 60$, an equilateral triangle: 3 sides.
Answer(i) (b) $135^\circ$; (ii) 4; (iii) 3
