Example 1

A polygon has 44 diagonals. How many sides has it?

  1. 9
  2. 10
  3. 11
  4. 12

Solution.

Step 1A polygon with $n$ sides has $\frac{n(n - 3)}{2}$ diagonals, so $n(n - 3) = 88$.
Step 2$11 \times 8 = 88$, so $n = 11$.
Check$\frac{11 \times 8}{2} = 44$. Ten sides would give only $\frac{10 \times 7}{2} = 35$.
Answer(c) 11
Example 2

Five angles of a hexagon are each $112^\circ$. What is the sixth angle?

  1. $160^\circ$
  2. $148^\circ$
  3. $120^\circ$
  4. $112^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1The angles of a hexagon add to $(6 - 2) \times 180^\circ = 720^\circ$.
Step 2$720^\circ - 5 \times 112^\circ = 720^\circ - 560^\circ = 160^\circ$.
Answer(a) $160^\circ$
