Example 2 · Single correct

Five angles of a hexagon are each $112^\circ$. What is the sixth angle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $160^\circ$
  2. $148^\circ$
  3. $120^\circ$
  4. $112^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1The angles of a hexagon add to $(6 - 2) \times 180^\circ = 720^\circ$.
Step 2$720^\circ - 5 \times 112^\circ = 720^\circ - 560^\circ = 160^\circ$.
Answer(a) $160^\circ$
Example 3 · Single correct

Each interior angle of a regular polygon is $168^\circ$. How many sides has it?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 24
  2. 30
  3. 28
  4. 36

Solution.

Step 1Each exterior angle is $180^\circ - 168^\circ = 12^\circ$.
Step 2The exterior angles add to $360^\circ$, so there are $360 \div 12 = 30$ sides.
Answer(b) 30
Example 4 · Single correct

In parallelogram $ABCD$, $\angle A = (3x + 10)^\circ$ and $\angle C = (5x - 30)^\circ$. What is $\angle B$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $70^\circ$
  2. $140^\circ$
  3. $20^\circ$
  4. $110^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1Opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal: $3x + 10 = 5x - 30$, so $x = 20$ and $\angle A = 70^\circ$.
Step 2Adjacent angles add to $180^\circ$: $\angle B = 180^\circ - 70^\circ = 110^\circ$.
Answer(d) $110^\circ$
