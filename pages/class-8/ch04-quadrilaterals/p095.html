1. Tanvi says, “A quadrilateral whose diagonals are equal must be a rectangle.” Which of these is true?
    1. Tanvi is right.
    2. No: a square has equal diagonals but is not a rectangle.
    3. No: the diagonals must also bisect each other.
    4. No: a rhombus always has equal diagonals.
