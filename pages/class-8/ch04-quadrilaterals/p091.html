  1. A field is shaped like parallelogram $ABCD$. Its perimeter is 128 m, and $AB$ is 16 m longer than $BC$. Taking $BC = x$ m, form an equation and find $AB$ and $BC$. Fencing costs ₹45 per metre. Find the cost of fencing the field.
  1. Prove that the angles of a quadrilateral add to $360^\circ$. Using this, find the angles of quadrilateral $PQRS$ in which $\angle R = \angle P$, $\angle Q = 2\angle P$ and $\angle S = 2\angle P + 30^\circ$.
  1. Using the angle-sum formula $(n - 2) \times 180^\circ$, prove that the exterior angles of an $n$-gon add to $360^\circ$. Hence find the number of sides of a regular polygon whose interior angles are all $176^\circ$.
  1. The diagonals of rhombus $PQRS$ meet at $O$. $PR = (3a + 4)$ cm and $OR = (a + 5)$ cm. Find $a$ and the length of $PR$. If $\angle PQR = 108^\circ$, find $\angle QPO$ and $\angle PQO$. Hence verify that $\angle POQ = 90^\circ$.
  1. The diagonals of rectangle $ABCD$ meet at $O$. $\angle AOB = (4x + 12)^\circ$ and $\angle BOC = (2x + 18)^\circ$. Find $x$, $\angle AOB$ and $\angle BOC$. Hence find $\angle OAB$ and $\angle OBC$, and check that $\angle ABC$ is a right angle.
