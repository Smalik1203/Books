The angle sum
  • The angles of any quadrilateral add to $360^\circ$.
  • The angles of an $n$-gon add to $(n - 2) \times 180^\circ$.
?
Think and Reflect
  1. Can all four angles of a quadrilateral be acute? Give a reason.
  2. Draw a quadrilateral and cut it with the other diagonal. Does the angle sum change? Why not?
  3. A pentagon has three right angles. What do its other two angles add to?

    2. $45^\circ$, $135^\circ$, $90^\circ$

  1. Find each angle of a regular polygon with

    4. $30$ sides
  1. The angles of a quadrilateral are $x^\circ$, $2x^\circ$, $2x^\circ$ and $4x^\circ$. Find $x$ and the four angles.
