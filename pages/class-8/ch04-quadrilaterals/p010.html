Exterior angles
  • At each vertex, the interior and exterior angles add to $180^\circ$.
  • The exterior angles of any polygon add to $360^\circ$. In a regular $n$-gon, each exterior angle is $\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}$.

Think and Reflect
  1. A regular polygon gets more and more sides. What happens to each exterior angle? What happens to each interior angle?
  2. Can a regular polygon have an exterior angle of $100^\circ$? Give a reason.
Exercise Set 4.3
  1. Find each exterior angle of a regular polygon with
    1. $5$ sides
    2. $8$ sides
    3. $20$ sides
    4. $36$ sides
  1. Find the number of sides of a regular polygon whose exterior angle is
    1. $60^\circ$
    2. $36^\circ$
    3. $20^\circ$
    4. $15^\circ$
  1. Four exterior angles of a pentagon are $70^\circ$, $60^\circ$, $85^\circ$ and $75^\circ$. Find the fifth exterior angle. Then find all five interior angles.
  1. Each interior angle of a regular polygon is $165^\circ$. Use the exterior angle to find the number of sides.
  1. Is there a regular polygon with an exterior angle of $50^\circ$? Is there one with an interior angle of $110^\circ$? Give reasons.
  1. Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 3 times its exterior angle. Find the number of sides.
  1. The exterior angles of a hexagon are $x^\circ$, $x^\circ$, $2x^\circ$, $2x^\circ$, $3x^\circ$ and $3x^\circ$. Find $x$ and the largest interior angle.
