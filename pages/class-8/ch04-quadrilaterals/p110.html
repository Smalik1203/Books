Example 10

A welder makes a gate frame shaped like parallelogram $ABCD$, with $AB = (3x + 2)$ cm, $CD = (5x - 38)$ cm and $\angle A = 58^\circ$. Its diagonal braces meet at $O$, and the brace $BD$ is 90 cm long.

  1. The length of $AB$ is
    1. 20 cm
    2. 62 cm
    3. 38 cm
    4. 60 cm
  2. Numerical answer. Find $\angle B$, in degrees.
  3. Numerical answer. Find the length of $BO$, in cm.

Solution.

Step 1$3x + 2 = 5x - 38$, so $x = 20$ and $AB = 3 \times 20 + 2 = 62$ cm.
Step 2Adjacent angles: $\angle B = 180^\circ - 58^\circ = 122^\circ$.
Step 3The diagonals bisect each other: $BO = 90 \div 2 = 45$ cm.
Answer(i) (b) 62 cm; (ii) 122; (iii) 45
