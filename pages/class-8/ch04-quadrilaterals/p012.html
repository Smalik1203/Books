A family of shapes
  • Each named quadrilateral is a condition, and the conditions build on one another.
  • Every square is a rectangle and a rhombus, and every rectangle and rhombus is a parallelogram.
?
Think and Reflect
  1. Which of these are always true, and which are only sometimes true? A rhombus is a kite. A kite is a rhombus. A rectangle is a square. A square is a rhombus.
  2. For each one that is only sometimes true, draw a figure for which it is false.
Exercise Set 4.4
  1. Say whether each statement is always, sometimes or never true.
    1. A rhombus is a parallelogram.
    2. A parallelogram is a rectangle.
    3. A rectangle is a kite.
    4. A trapezium is a parallelogram.
  1. Name the quadrilateral.
    1. Opposite sides parallel, all angles right, sides not all equal.
    2. All four sides equal, and no angle a right angle.
    3. One pair of opposite sides parallel, the other pair not.
    4. Sides of 6 cm, 6 cm, 9 cm and 9 cm, in order round the figure.
  1. Neha says, “My quadrilateral has four right angles, so it must be a square.” Is she right? Draw a figure to explain.
  1. A kite has four right angles. What kind of quadrilateral is it? Give a reason.
  1. In trapezium $PQRS$, $PQ$ is parallel to $SR$ and $\angle P = 64^\circ$. Find $\angle S$, and give a reason.
