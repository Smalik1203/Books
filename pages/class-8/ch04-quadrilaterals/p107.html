Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which statements are true for every parallelogram?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its opposite sides are equal.
  2. Its diagonals bisect each other.
  3. Its diagonals are equal.
  4. Its adjacent angles add to $180^\circ$.

Solution.

Step 1(a), (b) and (d) hold in every parallelogram.
Step 2Equal diagonals make it a rectangle; a parallelogram with angles $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$ has unequal diagonals.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

In rhombus $ABCD$ the diagonals meet at $O$, and $\angle ABC = 70^\circ$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$
  2. $\angle ABO = 35^\circ$
  3. $\angle BAC = 55^\circ$
  4. $\angle BAD = 70^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1The diagonals of a rhombus meet at right angles and bisect its angles, so $\angle ABO = 35^\circ$.
Step 2$AB = BC$, so $\angle BAC = (180^\circ - 70^\circ) \div 2 = 55^\circ$; and $\angle BAD = 180^\circ - 70^\circ = 110^\circ$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Which statements about polygons are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Their exterior angles add to $360^\circ$.
  2. Regular 7-gon: exterior angle $50^\circ$.
  3. Regular 9-gon: exterior angle $40^\circ$.
  4. An octagon's angles add to $1080^\circ$.

Solution.

Step 1Exterior angles make one full turn: $360 \div 9 = 40$, but $360 \div 7 \approx 51.4$. An octagon has $6 \times 180^\circ = 1080^\circ$.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
