Example 3

In quadrilateral $PQRS$, $PQ = QR = 5$ cm and $RS = SP = 8$ cm. Which statements are correct?

  1. $PQRS$ is a kite.
  2. $PQRS$ must be a parallelogram.
  3. Its diagonals are perpendicular.
  4. It can be a rhombus.

Solution.

Step 1Two pairs of equal adjacent sides make a kite, and the diagonals of a kite meet at right angles.
Step 2Its opposite sides are 5 cm and 8 cm, not equal, so it is neither a parallelogram nor a rhombus.
Answer(a), (c)
Example 4

Which statements about polygons are correct?

  1. Their exterior angles add to $360^\circ$.
  2. Regular 7-gon: exterior angle $50^\circ$.
  3. Regular 40-gon: exterior angle $9^\circ$.
  4. An octagon’s angles add to $1080^\circ$.

Solution.

Step 1Exterior angles make one full turn: $360 \div 40 = 9$, but $360 \div 7$ is not 50, since $7 \times 50 = 350$.
Step 2An octagon has $6 \times 180^\circ = 1080^\circ$.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
