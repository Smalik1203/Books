Example 5 · Single correct

Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 11 times each exterior angle. How many sides has it?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 24
  2. 22
  3. 12
  4. 11

Solution.

Step 1Interior + exterior $= 180^\circ$, and here that is 12 exterior angles, so each exterior angle is $15^\circ$.
Step 2$360 \div 15 = 24$ sides.
Answer(a) 24
Example 6 · Single correct

The diagonals of rectangle $ABCD$ meet at $O$, and $\angle AOB = 110^\circ$. What is $\angle OAB$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $55^\circ$
  2. $35^\circ$
  3. $70^\circ$
  4. $110^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1The diagonals of a rectangle are equal and bisect each other, so $OA = OB$.
Step 2Triangle $OAB$ is isosceles: $\angle OAB = (180^\circ - 110^\circ) \div 2 = 35^\circ$.
Answer(b) $35^\circ$
Example 7 · Multiple correct

In quadrilateral $PQRS$, $PQ = QR = 5$ cm and $RS = SP = 8$ cm. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $PQRS$ is a kite.
  2. $PQRS$ must be a parallelogram.
  3. Its diagonals are perpendicular.
  4. It can be a rhombus.

Solution.

Step 1Two pairs of equal adjacent sides make a kite, and the diagonals of a kite meet at right angles.
Step 2Its opposite sides are 5 cm and 8 cm, not equal, so it is neither a parallelogram nor a rhombus.
Answer(a), (c)
