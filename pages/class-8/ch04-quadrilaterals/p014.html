The diagonals bisect each other. Draw both diagonals and let them meet at $O$. Compare triangles $AOB$ and $COD$. We know $AB = CD$. Also $\angle OAB = \angle OCD$ and $\angle OBA = \angle ODC$, because they are alternate angles between the parallel lines $AB$ and $CD$. By the ASA rule the triangles are congruent. So $AO = OC$ and $BO = OD$.

Example 6

In parallelogram $PQRS$, $\angle P = 72^\circ$. Find the other three angles.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle R = \angle P = 72^\circ$ opposite angles
Step 2 $\angle Q = 180^\circ - 72^\circ = 108^\circ$ adjacent angles
Step 3 $\angle S = \angle Q = 108^\circ$ opposite angles
Check $72 + 108 + 72 + 108 = 360$
Answer $\angle Q = 108^\circ$, $\angle R = 72^\circ$, $\angle S = 108^\circ$

So one angle of a parallelogram fixes all four.
