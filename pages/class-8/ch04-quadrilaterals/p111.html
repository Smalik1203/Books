Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): A regular polygon can have an exterior angle of $70^\circ$.

    Reason (R): The exterior angle of a regular polygon must divide $360^\circ$ exactly.

  1. Assertion (A): In parallelogram $PQRS$, if $\angle P = 100^\circ$ then $\angle R = 100^\circ$.

    Reason (R): Adjacent angles of a parallelogram are equal.

  1. Assertion (A): The interior angles of a pentagon add to $540^\circ$.

    Reason (R): A pentagon has five diagonals.

  1. Assertion (A): Rods of $2$, $3$, $4$ and $9$ cm cannot be pinned into a quadrilateral.

    Reason (R): A diagonal cuts a quadrilateral into two triangles, and in a triangle each side is shorter than the other two together.
