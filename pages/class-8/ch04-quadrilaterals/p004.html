4.3The Angle Sum

We use one fact about triangles: the three angles of a triangle add to $180^\circ$. Everything in this section comes from that fact.

4.3.1 A quadrilateral is two triangles

Draw a quadrilateral $ABCD$ and its diagonal $AC$. The diagonal cuts the figure into triangle $ABC$ and triangle $ACD$. The angle at $B$ is an angle of triangle $ABC$, and the angle at $D$ is an angle of triangle $ACD$. The diagonal splits the angles at $A$ and $C$ into two parts each, one part in each triangle.
