1. For Makar Sankranti, Imran makes a kite $ABCD$ from paper and two sticks. The sides are $AB = AD = 30$ cm and $CB = CD = 50$ cm. The sticks lie along the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$. The angle at $A$ is $110^\circ$ and the angle at $C$ is $50^\circ$.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Which side of the kite is equal to $CB$?
    2. Find $\angle ABC + \angle ADC$.
    3. Triangles $ABC$ and $ADC$ are congruent. Find $\angle ABC$, and give a reason.

  1. A fountain in a city park has a stone edge shaped like a regular polygon with 16 sides. Sameer walks along the edge. At each corner he turns to follow the next side.

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Through what angle does he turn at each corner?
    2. Find each interior angle of the edge.
    3. He walks round the fountain three times. Through what angle does he turn in all, and how many corners does he pass?
