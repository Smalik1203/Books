NCERT, Figure it Out, §4.7

4.8Why Frames Need Triangles

Go back to the strips of card from the start of the chapter. Three strips pinned into a triangle cannot be pushed into another shape. Four strips pinned into a quadrilateral can take many shapes. The reason is about how much three lengths decide.

Give someone three lengths and ask for a triangle. Only one triangle can be made. It may be turned round or flipped over, but its angles are fixed before it is drawn. Give someone four lengths and ask for a quadrilateral. Many quadrilaterals can be made, and pushing a corner moves from one of them to another.
