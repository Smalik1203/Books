By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Find the number of diagonals of a regular polygon each of whose exterior angles is $72^\circ$.
  1. Is there a polygon whose interior angles add to $1530^\circ$? Justify your answer.
  1. The angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio $3 : 4 : 5 : 6$. Find its largest angle.
  1. In parallelogram $ABCD$, $\angle A = (5x - 20)^\circ$ and $\angle B = (3x + 40)^\circ$. Find $x$ and $\angle A$.
  1. Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 7 times its exterior angle. Find the number of its sides.
  1. Show that no regular polygon has an interior angle of $145^\circ$.
  1. The diagonals of rectangle $PQRS$ meet at $O$. $OP = (2y + 3)$ cm and $OQ = (4y - 5)$ cm. Find $y$ and the length of $PR$.
  1. A polygon has 35 diagonals. Find the number of its sides.
  1. Two adjacent angles of a parallelogram differ by $40^\circ$. Find all four angles of the parallelogram.
  1. Can the exterior angles of a quadrilateral be $80^\circ$, $95^\circ$, $105^\circ$ and $90^\circ$? Justify your answer.
Short answer
  1. The angles of a hexagon are $x^\circ$, $x^\circ$, $(x + 20)^\circ$, $(x + 20)^\circ$, $(x + 40)^\circ$ and $(x + 40)^\circ$. Find $x$. Also find the smallest exterior angle of the hexagon.
  1. Aarav walks along the fence of a garden shaped like a regular polygon. At each corner he turns through $8^\circ$. Find the number of sides of the garden and each of its interior angles. Also find the sum of its interior angles.
  1. In parallelogram $PQRS$, $PQ = (3x + 1)$ cm and $QR = (2x - 1)$ cm. The perimeter of $PQRS$ is 60 cm. Find $x$, and the lengths of $PQ$ and $QR$.
  1. The angles of a triangle are $a$, $b$ and $c$. Show that the three exterior angles of the triangle, one at each vertex, add to $360^\circ$.
  1. In rhombus $ABCD$, $\angle DAB = 64^\circ$ and the diagonals meet at $O$. Find $\angle ABC$, $\angle ABD$ and $\angle AOB$.
  1. The angles of a quadrilateral are $2x^\circ$, $3x^\circ$, $(x + 30)^\circ$ and $(2x + 10)^\circ$. Find $x$, and show that the quadrilateral has exactly one right angle.
