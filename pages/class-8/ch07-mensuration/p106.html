Example 6

The diagonals of a rhombus are in the ratio $3 : 4$, and its area is $384$ cm2. Find the diagonals.

Solution.

Step 1 diagonals $3x$ and $4x$ from the ratio
Step 2 $\tfrac12 \times 3x \times 4x = 6x^2 = 384$ half the product
Step 3 $x^2 = 64$, so $x = 8$ a length is positive
Answer 24 cm and 32 cm
Example 7

A square field has a diagonal of $30$ m. Find its area, and the cost of levelling it at ₹$12$ a square metre.

Solution.

Step 1 $\tfrac12 \times 30^2 = \tfrac12 \times 900 = 450$ m2 a square is $\tfrac12 d^2$
Step 2 $450 \times 12 = 5400$ area times rate
Answer 450 m2, costing ₹5400

Example 8

A closed wooden box measures $50$ cm by $40$ cm by $30$ cm on the outside. Find the cost of painting its outside at ₹$40$ a square metre.

Solution.

Step 1 $2(2000 + 1200 + 1500) = 9400$ cm2 all six faces
Step 2 $9400 \div 10\,000 = 0.94$ m2 1 m2 is 10 000 cm2
Step 3 $0.94 \times 40 = 37.6$ area times rate
Answer ₹37.60

A square metre is $100 \times 100$ square centimetres, not $100$ of them.
