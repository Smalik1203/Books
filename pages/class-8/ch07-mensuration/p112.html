C6 $2(lb + bh + hl)$ is the whole surface of the cuboid — six faces. Four walls are $2(l+b)h$, and Ravi has bought paint for the floor and ceiling as well.
C8 The volume is $\pi \times 4 \times 2 = 8\pi$ and the curved surface is $2\pi \times 2 \times 2 = 8\pi$. The numbers agree because $r = 2$; the quantities do not, one being cubic centimetres and the other square.
C12 Volumes grow by $k^3 = 8000$. Option (b) is $k^2$, what happens to areas.

What to carry forward

Every area here came from one idea — cut the figure into pieces you know — and every volume from another: the area of the base times the height. The formulas are those two with a shape's own facts put in, and anyone who has them can rebuild a forgotten one, or draw the net and add it up.

The habit worth keeping is to ask which of the two questions is being asked before doing any arithmetic. Covering or filling? A label, a lid, four walls and a lining are all covering; water, earth, wax and rice are all filling. Nearly every mark lost here is lost by answering the other one, and the unit at the end will tell you which you did.

And keep the last section, because it outlives the formulas. Multiply every length by $k$ and areas go up by $k^2$ while volumes go up by $k^3$. That is why a cake bakes more slowly than a bun, why crushed ice works faster than a block, and why nothing the size of an elephant is built like a mouse.
