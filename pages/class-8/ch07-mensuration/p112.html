Short answer
  1. The parallel sides of a trapezium are $30$ cm and $20$ cm, and its area is $450$ cm2. Find the distance between the parallel sides.
  1. Find the total surface area of a closed cylinder $14$ cm across and $6$ cm high. Take $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.
  1. A wall is $6$ m long, $3$ m high and $24$ cm thick. How many bricks $24$ cm by $12$ cm by $8$ cm does it take, leaving out the mortar?
  1. A cylinder of height $14$ cm has a curved surface of $88$ cm2. Find its radius and its volume. Take $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.
Long answer
  1. A godown is $30$ m long, $25$ m broad and $10$ m high. How many cartons $1.5$ m by $1.25$ m by $0.5$ m can it hold? Show that they fit exactly.
  1. A closed cylindrical tank of radius $3.5$ m and height $4$ m is made of sheet metal. How many square metres of sheet does it take, and how many litres does it hold? Take $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.
  1. A field is a trapezium with parallel sides $48$ m and $32$ m, $30$ m apart. A square pond of side $10$ m is dug in it. Find the area of the field left for grass, and the cost of turfing it at ₹$15$ a square metre.
