Type 2 · The trapezium

Example 3

A trapezium has area $180$ cm2 and height $12$ cm. One parallel side is $6$ cm longer than the other. Find both parallel sides.

Solution.

Step 1 sides $x$ and $x + 6$ name the unknown
Step 2 $\tfrac12 (x + x + 6) \times 12 = 180$ the area formula
Step 3 $6(2x + 6) = 180$, so $2x + 6 = 30$
Step 4 $2x = 24$, so $x = 12$
Answer 12 cm and 18 cm
Example 4

A trapezium has parallel sides $16$ cm and $10$ cm, height $4$ cm, and its two slanting sides are equal. Cut it into a rectangle and two triangles, find its area, and check with the formula.

Solution.

Step 1 $(16 - 10) \div 2 = 3$ cm each triangle's base
Step 2 $10 \times 4 = 40$ cm2 the rectangle
Step 3 $\tfrac12 \times 3 \times 4 = 6$ cm2 each the two triangles
Step 4 $40 + 6 + 6 = 52$ add the pieces
Step 5 $\tfrac12 (16 + 10) \times 4 = 52$ the formula agrees
Answer 52 cm2

Type 3 · Any quadrilateral, the rhombus and the square

Example 5

A quadrilateral of area $210$ cm2 has a diagonal of $28$ cm. The perpendicular onto it from one vertex is $8$ cm. Find the perpendicular from the opposite vertex.

Solution.

Step 1 $\tfrac12 \times 28 \times (8 + x) = 210$ half the diagonal, both heights
Step 2 $14(8 + x) = 210$, so $8 + x = 15$
Answer $x = 7$, so the perpendicular is 7 cm
