The same litre, three boxes

Each box holds $1000$ cm3. The surface areas are not alike at all.

$10 \times 10 \times 10$ $6 \times 100 = 600$ cm2
$20 \times 10 \times 5$ $2(200 + 50 + 100) = 700$ cm2
$50 \times 20 \times 1$ $2(1000 + 20 + 50) = 2140$ cm2

The cube uses least, and the flattest box more than three times as much for the same contents. The nearer a box is to a cube, the less cardboard it needs: stretching one direction adds surface faster than squashing another takes it away. A tall thin packet is usually a decision about shelves rather than about cardboard.
