Example 5

A closed cylindrical tin has radius $7$ cm and height $10$ cm. Find its total surface area, taking $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.

formula $2\pi r(h + r)$
substitute $2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times (10 + 7)$
the sevens go $2 \times 22 \times 17$
answer $748$ cm2

The radius was seven, so $\tfrac{22}{7}$ cancelled and no decimal appeared anywhere. That is not luck; it is how the question was built.

Think and Reflect

A label is to be printed for that tin, going once round it with no overlap. Which of the three areas in the last section does the printer need?

Now suppose the tin is open at the top, like a mug. Which faces does its surface area have, and what is the formula?

An open cylinder — a pipe, a mug, a bucket without a lid — is the total less the face that is not there. Nobody needs a separate formula for it; they need to look at the object and count what it actually has.
