Example 9

A room is $8$ m long, $6$ m broad and $4$ m high. It has one door $1$ m by $2$ m and two windows, each $1.5$ m by $1$ m. Find the area of the walls to be painted.

Solution.

Step 1 $2(8 + 6) \times 4 = 112$ m2 the four walls
Step 2 $1 \times 2 + 2 \times 1.5 \times 1 = 5$ m2 the door and windows
Step 3 $112 - 5 = 107$
Answer 107 m2
Example 10

A square card $30$ cm by $30$ cm has a $5$ cm square cut from each corner. The sides are folded up to make a box with no lid. Find the box's measurements, its volume and the area of card it uses.

Solution.

Step 1 $30 - 2 \times 5 = 20$ each side of the base
Step 2 $20 \times 20 \times 5 = 2000$ cm3 the volume
Step 3 $30^2 - 4 \times 5^2 = 900 - 100 = 800$ cm2 the card is the net
Step 4 $20^2 + 4 \times 20 \times 5 = 800$ check: base and four sides
Answer 20 cm by 20 cm by 5 cm; 2000 cm3; 800 cm2

Example 11

A garden roller is a cylinder $1.4$ m across and $2$ m long. What area does it level in $25$ full turns? Take $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $r = 0.7$ m half the width across
Step 2 $2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 0.7 \times 2 = 8.8$ m2 one turn lays the curved surface
Step 3 $8.8 \times 25 = 220$
Answer 220 m2
