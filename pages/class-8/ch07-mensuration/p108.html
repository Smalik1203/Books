Example 12

The curved surface of a cylinder is $660$ cm2 and its height is $15$ cm. Find its radius. Take $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times r \times 15 = 660$ curved surface is $2\pi r h$
Step 2 $\tfrac{660}{7} \, r = 660$ collect the numbers
Answer $r = 7$ cm

Type 6 · Volume and capacity

Example 13

A container $30$ cm by $25$ cm by $20$ cm is full of juice. How many $250$ mL cartons can be filled from it?

Solution.

Step 1 $30 \times 25 \times 20 = 15\,000$ cm3 the volume
Step 2 $15\,000$ cm3 is $15\,000$ mL 1 cm3 is 1 mL
Step 3 $15\,000 \div 250 = 60$
Answer 60 cartons
Example 14

A field $40$ m by $25$ m receives $5$ cm of rain. How many litres of water fell on it?

Solution.

Step 1 $5$ cm $= 0.05$ m one unit throughout
Step 2 $40 \times 25 \times 0.05 = 50$ m3 a very flat cuboid
Step 3 $50 \times 1000 = 50\,000$ 1 m3 is 1000 litres
Answer 50 000 litres
