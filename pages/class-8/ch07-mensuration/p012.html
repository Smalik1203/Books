Exercise Set 7.4
  1. Find the volume of each cuboid. Lengths are in centimetres.
    1. $8$ by $5$ by $3$
    2. $12$ by $10$ by $4$
    3. $7$ by $7$ by $2$
  1. Find the volume of a cube of side
    1. $4$ cm
    2. $6$ cm
    3. $10$ cm
    4. $0.5$ m
  1. A cube has volume $343$ cm3. Find its side, and then its surface area.
  1. Find the volume of a cylinder of radius $7$ cm and height $15$ cm, taking $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.
  1. Convert.
    1. $5$ m3 into litres
    2. $2500$ cm3 into litres
    3. $4.5$ litres into cm3
  1. A water tank $3$ m by $2$ m by $2$ m is full. How many litres does it hold, and how long will it take to empty at $50$ litres a minute?
  1. A cuboid of volume $360$ cm3 has a base $12$ cm by $5$ cm. Find its height.
  1. Two cylinders have the same height. One has twice the radius of the other. How many times the volume does it hold? Answer before calculating, then check with $r = 3$, $r = 6$ and $h = 10$.
  1. A rectangular sheet $22$ cm by $10$ cm is rolled into a cylinder with the $22$ cm edge going round. Find the radius, and then the volume. Take $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.
  1. The same sheet is rolled the other way, with the $10$ cm edge going round. Does it hold the same amount? Work it out, and then say which way to roll a sheet to hold the most.
