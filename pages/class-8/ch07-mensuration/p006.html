Example 3

A quadrilateral has a diagonal of $18$ cm, and the perpendiculars onto it from the other two vertices are $6$ cm and $9$ cm. Find its area.

rule $\tfrac12 \times 18 \times (6 + 9)$
answer $9 \times 15 = 135$ cm2

Where a shape is not one of these at all, go back to the only method there is: cut it into pieces you know. A field shaped like a house — a rectangle with a triangle on top — is a rectangle plus a triangle, and there is nothing more to it.
