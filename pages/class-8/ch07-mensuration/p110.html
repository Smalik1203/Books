Example 18

A model water tank is made to a scale of $1 : 20$. The model holds $2$ litres and its outside has $150$ cm2 of paint. How much does the real tank hold, and how many square metres of paint does it need?

Solution.

Step 1 $20^3 = 8000$ and $20^2 = 400$ volume and area factors
Step 2 $2 \times 8000 = 16\,000$ litres the capacity
Step 3 $150 \times 400 = 60\,000$ cm2 the paint
Step 4 $60\,000 \div 10\,000 = 6$ m2 into square metres
Answer 16 000 litres, and 6 m2 of paint
