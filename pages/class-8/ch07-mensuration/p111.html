Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): For a cube of side $4$ cm, the surface area is a larger number than the volume.

    Reason (R): $6a^2$ is greater than $a^3$ whenever $a$ is less than $6$.

  1. Assertion (A): The four walls of a room $l$ by $b$ by $h$ have area $2(l + b)h$.

    Reason (R): The total surface area of a cuboid is $2(lb + bh + hl)$.

  1. Assertion (A): A cylinder of radius $7$ cm and height $2$ cm holds $308$ cm3, with $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$.

    Reason (R): The volume of a cylinder is $2\pi r h$.

  1. Assertion (A): If every edge of a cube is doubled, its surface area is doubled.

    Reason (R): An area is found by multiplying two lengths.
