Example 4

Show that the three expressions for the painted area are equal. Find the painted area when $x = 20$ cm and $y = 6$ cm.

Solution.

Step 1 Neha: $2xy - y^2$. Arjun: $xy + y(x - y) = xy + xy - y^2 = 2xy - y^2$.
Step 2 Sana: $x^2 - (x - y)^2 = x^2 - (x^2 - 2xy + y^2) = 2xy - y^2$. Identity 1B
Step 3 With $x = 20$ and $y = 6$: $2 \times 20 \times 6 - 6^2 = 240 - 36 = 204$.
Check Sana’s way: $20^2 - 14^2 = 400 - 196 = 204$.
Answer All three equal $2xy - y^2$; the painted area is $204$ cm².
