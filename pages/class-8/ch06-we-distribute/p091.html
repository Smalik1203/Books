1. If $a - b = 5$ and $ab = 24$, find $a^2 + b^2$. Also find $(a + b)^2$.
  1. Using the distributive property, find $5047 \times 101$ and $638 \times 99$. Show your working.
  1. Each side of a square is increased by $3$ cm, and its area increases by $57$ cm². Find the side of the original square.
  1. Without working out either product in full, find how much greater $38 \times 57$ is than $37 \times 55$. Show your working.
Long answer
  1. A square plot has side $x$ m. One pair of opposite sides is made $5$ m longer and the other pair $5$ m shorter, so that the plot becomes a rectangle. Show that the area of the plot goes down by $25$ m², whatever $x$ is. The new plot has an area of $119$ m². Find $x$, and the length and breadth of the new plot.
  1. A photograph $(2x + 1)$ cm long and $(x + 3)$ cm wide is mounted on a card that leaves a border $2$ cm wide all round it. Write the length, breadth and area of the card as expressions. Show that the area of the border is $(12x + 32)$ cm². Hence find the area of the border when $x = 5$, and check it by finding both areas.
  1. In the given figure, a square of side $(p + 3)$ cm is divided into a square of side $p$ cm, a square of side $3$ cm and two rectangles, each $p$ cm by $3$ cm.
    Write the area of the whole square in two ways, and hence show that $(p + 3)^2 = p^2 + 6p + 9$. If the square of side $p$ cm has an area of $49$ cm², find the area of the whole square in cm².
  1. Show that $(a + b)^2 - (a - b)^2 = 4ab$. Two numbers have a sum of $17$ and a difference of $7$. Using this result, find their product without finding the numbers. Then find the two numbers and check your answer.
  1. Write an odd number as $2n + 1$. Show that its square is $4n(n + 1) + 1$, and explain why the square of every odd number is $1$ more than a multiple of $8$. Hence find the remainder when $37^2$ is divided by $8$, and check it by working out $37^2$.
