1. Tara buys $(x - 8)$ notebooks at ₹$(x + 8)$ each. Write her bill as an expression. Show that she would pay ₹$64$ more if she bought $x$ notebooks at ₹$x$ each. Her bill is ₹$336$. Find $x$, the number of notebooks and the price of one notebook.
  1. A square tile has side $(x + 4)$ cm, and a rectangular tile is $(x + 7)$ cm long and $(x + 1)$ cm wide. Find the area of each tile as an expression. Show that the square tile is always larger, and find by how much. Check your answer when $x = 6$, by working out both areas in square centimetres.
  1. Think of a number. Add $3$ and square the result. Subtract $9$, divide by the number you thought of, and then subtract $6$. Using $n$ for the number, show that the answer is always $n$. Check each step with the number $7$, and then with the number $10$.
  1. The floor of a school hall is a square of side $(x + 3)$ m. A square carpet of side $(x - 3)$ m is laid on it. Find the area of the floor not covered by the carpet as an expression in $x$. If this area is $96$ m², find $x$, the side of the hall and the side of the carpet. Check your answer by finding the two areas.
  1. A two-digit number with tens digit $a$ and units digit $b$ is $10a + b$. Show that $(10a + b) \times 11 = 100a + 10(a + b) + b$. Use this to explain why $43 \times 11 = 473$. Then find $76 \times 11$ in the same way, and explain what happens to the digits when $a + b$ is $10$ or more.
Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $203 \times 197 = 39991$.

    Reason (R): $(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$ for all numbers $a$ and $b$.

  1. Assertion (A): $501 \times 499 = 249999$.

    Reason (R): $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$ for all numbers $a$ and $b$.

  1. Assertion (A): $(3m)^2 = 9m^2$.

    Reason (R): Squaring a term squares its letter and leaves its number part as it is.

  1. Assertion (A): $4p + 5q = 9pq$.

    Reason (R): Only like terms can be added to make a single term.

  1. Assertion (A): $(x + 1)(x + 1) - x \times x = 2x + 1$.

    Reason (R): When both numbers of a product $ab$ are increased by $1$, the product increases by $a + b + 1$.
