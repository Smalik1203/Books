Case-based questions

  1. Floor tiles are sold in many shapes and sizes. A tile shop in Jaipur sells three kinds, and the sides of all three depend on one length, $x$ cm, as the table shows. Kavita wants to compare the areas of the tiles before she chooses one for her kitchen floor.

    TileShapeSides
    Psquare$(x + 3)$ cm
    Qsquare$(x - 3)$ cm
    Rrectangle$(x + 3)$ cm by $(x - 3)$ cm

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Write the area of Tile R as an expression in $x$.
    2. Find the area of Tile P when $x = 12$.
    3. Show that the areas of Tiles P and Q differ by $12x$ cm². Find the value of $x$ if they differ by $180$ cm².

  1. Many people find products in their heads by using identities. In a mental maths quiz at a school in Pune, each team is given one product and must find it without writing anything down. The table shows the product that each of the three teams was given. A team that uses the right identity can answer in a few seconds.

    TeamProduct
    A$48 \times 52$
    B$71^2$
    C$99^2$

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Which identity suits Team A’s product?
    2. Find Team B’s product.
    3. Team C wrote $99^2 = 100^2 - 1^2 = 9999$. Is this right? Justify your answer, and find the correct value of $99^2$.
