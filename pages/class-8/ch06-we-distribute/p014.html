Why is this true? It lets us choose $b$ so that one of $a + b$ and $a - b$ is a round number. For example, $41^2$ can be found by taking $a = 41$ and $b = 1$, and $296^2$ can be found by taking $a = 296$ and $b = 4$.

$41^2$ $= (41 + 1)(41 - 1) + 1^2$
$= 42 \times 40 + 1 = 1681$
$296^2$ $= (296 + 4)(296 - 4) + 4^2$
$= 300 \times 292 + 16 = 87616$
