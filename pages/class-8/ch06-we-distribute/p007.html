6.1.2 Fast multiplication using the distributive property

The distributive property gives quick methods of multiplication when one of the numbers is of a particular kind.

When one of the numbers is $11$, $101$, $1001$, … Use the following multiplications to find a way of multiplying a number by $11$ in a single step: (a) $3874 \times 11$ and (b) $5678 \times 11$.

Take the first one. Since $11 = 10 + 1$,

$3874 \times 11 = 3874(10 + 1) = 38740 + 3874$

$\begin{array}{r} 38740 \\ +\;\;\; 3874 \\ \hline \end{array}$

Look at how the digits are being added: each digit of $3874$ is added to the digit on its right. To see this in general, take a four-digit number $dcba$, the number with $d$ in the thousands place, $c$ in the hundreds place, $b$ in the tens place and $a$ in the units place.

$dcba \times (10 + 1) = dcba \times 10 + dcba$

$\begin{array}{cccccc} & d & c & b & a & 0 \\ + & & d & c & b & a \\ \hline & d & (c + d) & (b + c) & (a + b) & a \end{array}$

So the product can be written in one line, working from the right and carrying as usual whenever a sum is $10$ or more.
