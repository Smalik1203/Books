Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $43 \times 101$(1) 2704
(Q) $57 \times 1001$(2) 4343
(R) $1003^2 - 3^2$(3) 1006000
(S) $52^2$(4) 57057

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1

Solution.

Step 1$43 \times 101 = 4300 + 43$ and $57 \times 1001 = 57000 + 57$.
Step 2$1003^2 - 3^2 = (1003 + 3)(1003 - 3) = 1006 \times 1000$, and $52^2 = (50 + 2)^2 = 2500 + 200 + 4$.
Answer(d) P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
