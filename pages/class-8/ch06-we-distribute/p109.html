Example 9

A square courtyard has side $a$ m. A builder adds a strip $2$ m wide along one side and a strip $2$ m wide along the side next to it, so that the courtyard becomes a square with each side $(a + 2)$ m long.

  1. The increase in the area of the courtyard, in m², is
    1. $4$
    2. $2a + 4$
    3. $4a + 4$
    4. $4a$
  2. Numerical answer. If $a = 15$, what is the new area, in m²?
  3. Numerical answer. The area went up by $84$ m². What was $a$?

Solution.

Step 1$(a + 2)^2 - a^2 = a^2 + 4a + 4 - a^2 = 4a + 4$.
Step 2With $a = 15$, the new area is $17^2 = 289$ m².
Step 3$4a + 4 = 84$, so $4a = 80$ and $a = 20$.
Check$22^2 - 20^2 = 484 - 400 = 84$.
Answer(i) (c) $4a + 4$; (ii) 289; (iii) 20
