By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Expand and simplify $(3x - 2)(x + 5)$.
  1. Using a suitable identity, find $104 \times 96$.
  1. Find the value of $a^2 + 2ab + b^2$ when $a = 23$ and $b = 17$ without squaring them.
  1. Is $(y - 3)^2 = y^2 - 9$ an identity? Give a reason.
  1. The product of two numbers is $180$. When the first number is increased by $1$, the product becomes $195$. Find the second number.
  1. Evaluate $7.2^2 - 2.8^2$ using a suitable identity.
  1. If $x + y = 9$ and $xy = 14$, find the value of $x^2 + y^2$.
  1. Find $3572 \times 11$ in one line, showing how each digit is found.
  1. Show that $(m + 6)^2 - (m - 6)^2 = 24m$.
  1. Find $k$ if $(x + 4)(x - k) = x^2 + x - 12$ for every value of $x$.
Short answer
  1. Expand $(2a - b + 3)(a + 2b)$ and simplify. Check your answer at $a = b = 1$.
  1. Simplify $(3p + 4)^2 - (3p - 4)^2$. Also find its value when $p = 5$.
  1. Using identities, find (i) $10.3^2$ and (ii) $49.5 \times 50.5$.
  1. Show that $(4n + 1)^2 - (4n - 1)^2$ is a multiple of $16$ for every whole number $n$. Hence find $n$ when the expression is equal to $144$.
  1. Meera’s rectangular garden is $(x + 6)$ m by $(x - 2)$ m. Find its area as an expression in $x$, and its value when $x = 10$.
  1. Rohan expands $(2x - 3)^2$ and finds its value at $x = 2$ like this.
    Step 1$(2x - 3)^2 = (2x)^2 - 2 \times 2x \times 3 + 3^2$
    Step 2$= 2x^2 - 12x + 9$
    Step 3At $x = 2$: $2 \times 4 - 12 \times 2 + 9 = 8 - 24 + 9 = -7$
    Identify the step in which Rohan has made an error, if any. Correct it and find the right value at $x = 2$.
