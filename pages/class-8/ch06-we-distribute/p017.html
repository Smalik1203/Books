Tadang’s method. The whole figure is a square of side $m + n$, so its area is $(m + n)^2$. Taking away the areas of the four rectangles leaves the shaded region: its area is $(m + n)^2 - 4mn$.

Yusuf’s method. The shaded region is a square of side $n - m$, so its area is $(n - m)^2$.

Expand both expressions and check that $(m + n)^2 - 4mn = (n - m)^2$.

Next, find the area of the region with slanting lines in the first drawing of Fig. 6.13. The three rectangles that make the I shape are all the same, $x$ long and $y$ wide. The other two drawings show how two more students saw the same figure.

Anusha’s method. The region is what is left of $ABCD$ when $EFGH$ is taken away. $ABCD$ is a square of side $x$ and $EFGH$ is a rectangle $x$ by $y$.

required area $= \text{area}(ABCD) - \text{area}(EFGH) = x^2 - xy$

Vaishnavi’s method. The side $QS$ is $y + x + y = x + 2y$, so the area of $PQSR$ is $x(x + 2y)$. The region is $PQSR$ with the three rectangles taken away.

required area $= x(x + 2y) - 3xy$

Aditya’s method. The region is two copies of $JKML$, which is $\frac{x - y}{2}$ wide and $x$ tall.

area $(JKML)$ $= x\left(\frac{x - y}{2}\right)$
required area $= 2 \times \text{area}(JKML) = 2x\left(\frac{x - y}{2}\right) = x(x - y)$

Expand the three expressions and check that they are equal. If $x = 8$ and $y = 3$, find the area of the region with slanting lines.
