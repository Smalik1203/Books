Exercise Set 6.3
  1. Use Identity 1A to find $53^2$ and Identity 1B to find $79^2$. Use Identity 1C to find $296 \times 304$ and $61 \times 63$.
  1. Use a suitable identity or the distributive property to find each product.
    1. $(q - 2)(q + 13)$
    2. $(4m - 7n)(4m + 7n)$
    3. $-(3x + 2)(x + 5)$
    4. $(5a + 3b)^2$
    5. $\left(3y - \frac{1}{3}\right)^2$
    6. $(4s) \times (2t) \times (s - 3)$
  1. Priya thinks of a number, adds $5$ and squares the result. Ravi takes the same number, subtracts $5$ and squares the result. Show that Priya’s answer is always $20$ times the number more than Ravi’s.
  1. Find out which of these statements are true for every whole number.
    1. $(m + 2)(m + 3) - m(m + 5)$ is always $6$.
    2. $(2k + 1)(2k + 3)$ is always even.
    3. $(n + 4)^2 - (n + 2)^2$ is always a multiple of $8$.
    4. $(3p + 1)^2 - (3p - 1)^2$ is always a multiple of $12$.
  1. Show that the product of any two odd numbers is odd. Write the two numbers as $2m + 1$ and $2n + 1$.
  1. A number ending in $5$ can be written $10n + 5$. Show that its square is $100n(n + 1) + 25$. Use this to find $65^2$ and $105^2$.
  1. Without working out the products in full, find which is larger, and by how much.
    1. $32 \times 48$ or $35 \times 45$
    2. $58 \times 62$ or $59 \times 61$
  1. A rectangular garden is $(x + 5)$ m long and $(x + 2)$ m wide. A square lawn of side $x$ m fills one corner, and the rest is a flower bed. Find the area of the flower bed in two ways, and show that both give $7x + 10$. Check both ways when $x = 4$.
  1. Expand $(a + b + c)^2$ by writing it as $\big((a + b) + c\big)^2$ and using Identity 1A twice.

NCERT, Figure it Out, pp. 154–156, Q1–11
