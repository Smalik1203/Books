Example 10

To multiply two numbers close to $100$, Deepa writes them as $100 + p$ and $100 + q$, where $p$ and $q$ may be negative. By Identity 1, $(100 + p)(100 + q) = 10000 + 100(p + q) + pq$, and she uses this to multiply.

  1. Her method gives $104 \times 107$ as
    1. 11128
    2. 11028
    3. 11100
    4. 10128
  2. Numerical answer. Find $97 \times 94$ by her method.
  3. Numerical answer. Find $103 \times 98$ by her method.

Solution.

Step 1$p = 4$, $q = 7$: $10000 + 1100 + 28 = 11128$.
Step 2$p = -3$, $q = -6$: $10000 - 900 + 18 = 9118$.
Step 3$p = 3$, $q = -2$: $10000 + 100 - 6 = 10094$.
Answer(i) (a) 11128; (ii) 9118; (iii) 10094
