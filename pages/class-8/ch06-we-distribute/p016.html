Does your method match one of these, or is it different? The four expressions look different, but are they? They all count the same circles, so they ought to be equal. Simplify each one and find out.

Method 1 $(k + 1)^2 - 1 = k^2 + 1 + 2k - 1 = k^2 + 2k$
Method 2 $k^2 + 2 \times k = k^2 + 2k$
Method 3 $k \times (k + 1) + k = k^2 + k + k = k^2 + 2k$
Method 4 $k \times (k + 2) = k^2 + 2k$

Worked out correctly, every method leads to the same answer, $k^2 + 2k$. This expression gives the number of circles at Step $k$ of the pattern.

In mathematics there are often several ways of looking at a pattern, and several ways of solving the same problem. Finding them takes creativity and imagination. You may have a favourite way, and it is still worth exploring the others.
