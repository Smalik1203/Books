The three expressions look different, but they count the same dots. Simplify each one.

Method 1 $k^2 + 4 \times k = k^2 + 4k$
Method 2 $(k + 2)^2 - 4 = k^2 + 4k + 4 - 4 = k^2 + 4k$
Method 3 $k(k + 2) + 2k = k^2 + 2k + 2k = k^2 + 4k$

Worked out correctly, every method gives $k^2 + 4k$ dots in Step $k$. Does your own way of seeing the pattern match one of these?

Many ways, one expression

There are often several ways of counting the same things, and each gives an expression. The expressions may look different, but when they are simplified correctly they are equal.
