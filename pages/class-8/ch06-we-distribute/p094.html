  1. A park in Mysuru has a square lawn of side $a$ m. A path $b$ m wide runs all round the outside of the lawn, so that the lawn and the path together make a larger square. The gardener, Ramesh, has to pave the path, and paving costs ₹$50$ per m².

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Write the side of the larger square.
    2. Show that the area of the path is $(4ab + 4b^2)$ m².
    3. If $a = 20$ and $b = 2$, find the cost of paving the path.
