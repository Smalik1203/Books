A quick way to test an expansion is to put a small number in place of each letter. For example, in Item 3 put $p = 1$. The left side is $(1 + 3)^2 = 16$ and the right side is $1 + 9 = 10$. The two values differ, so the expansion is wrong.

Checking an expansion
  • Every term of one bracket multiplies every term of the other.
  • If the two sides differ for one value of a letter, the expansion is wrong.
