Case-based questions

  1. Harpreet grows vegetables on a farm near Ludhiana in Punjab. The farm has three square plots, P, Q and R. The plots stand side by side in a straight row, with no gaps between them, and their lower edges lie along one straight line. The table gives the area of each plot.

    PlotPQR
    Area (m²)144225400

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. Find the side of plot Q.
    2. Find the length of the row of plots.
    3. A new square plot is to have the same area as P and Q together. Between which two whole numbers of metres does its side lie? Which of the two is it nearer?

  1. Many schools hold a mathematics quiz every year. At a quiz in a school in Guwahati, Dev is told that $804\,357$ is a perfect cube. He must find its cube root without factorising it. To help himself, he writes down the last digits of some cubes.

    $n$ ends in123789
    $n^3$ ends in187329

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. What is the last digit of $73^3$?
    2. What is the last digit of the cube root of $804\,357$?
    3. Find the cube root of $804\,357$. Show your working.
