Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $16\,000$ is not a perfect cube.

    Reason (R): A perfect cube ends in a multiple of three zeros.

  1. Assertion (A): $2^6 \times 3^3$ is a perfect cube.

    Reason (R): In a perfect cube, every prime appears a multiple of three times.

  1. Assertion (A): $7744$ could be a perfect square.

    Reason (R): Every number that ends in $4$ is a perfect square.
