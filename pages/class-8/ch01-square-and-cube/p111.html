Type 8 · A cube root from its digits

Example 18

Given that 238 328 is a perfect cube, find its cube root without factorising.

Solution.

Step 1 it ends in 8, so the root ends in 2 $2^3 = 8$
Step 2 strike out the last three digits: 238 remains
Step 3 $6^3 = 216$ fits and $7^3 = 343$ does not the tens digit is 6
Step 4 $62^3 = 238\,328$ check
Answer 62

Type 9 · Taking differences

Example 19

Take differences of the list 5, 8, 13, 20, 29, 40 until they go flat. How was the list built?

Solution.

Step 1 first differences: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11
Step 2 second differences: 2, 2, 2, 2 flat after two rounds
Step 3 two rounds, settling on 2, is how the squares behave
Step 4 $5 - 1 = 4$, $\ 8 - 4 = 4$, $\ 13 - 9 = 4$, and so on compare with the squares
Answer Each entry is a square plus 4: $n^2 + 4$
