Example 11

Find the smallest number by which 3380 must be divided to leave a perfect square, and the square root of the result.

Solution.

Step 1 $3380 = 2^2 \times 5 \times 13^2$
Step 2 the 5 has no partner divide it out
Step 3 $3380 \div 5 = 676 = 2^2 \times 13^2 = (2 \times 13)^2$
Answer Divide by 5. $676 = 26^2$, so the root is 26

Example 12

Given that 2809 is a perfect square, find $\sqrt{2809}$ without factorising.

Solution.

Step 1 $50^2 = 2500$ and $60^2 = 3600$ root between 50 and 60
Step 2 2809 ends in 9, so the root ends in 3 or 7: 53 or 57
Step 3 $55^2 = 3025$, more than 2809 the root is below 55
Step 4 $53^2 = 2809$ check
Answer $\sqrt{2809} = 53$
Example 13

A square garden has an area of 500 m². Between which two whole numbers of metres does its side lie, and which of the two is it nearer?

Solution.

Step 1 $22^2 = 484$ and $23^2 = 529$ 500 lies between
Step 2 $500 - 484 = 16$ and $529 - 500 = 29$ 500 is nearer 484
Answer Between 22 m and 23 m, nearer 22 m
