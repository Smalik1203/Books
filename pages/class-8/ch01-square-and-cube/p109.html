Example 12 · Numerical answer

Given that $60^2 = 3600$, find $61^2$ without multiplying.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Going from $60^2$ to $61^2$ adds the 61st odd number, $2 \times 61 - 1 = 121$.
Step 2$61^2 = 3600 + 121 = 3721$.
Answer3721
Example 13 · Numerical answer

By what smallest number must 4375 be divided so that the quotient is a perfect cube?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$4375 = 5^4 \times 7$.
Step 2Three of the 5s make a cube; divide by the spare 5 and the 7: $4375 \div 35 = 125 = 5^3$.
Answer35
