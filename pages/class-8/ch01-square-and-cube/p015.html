1.4Cubic Numbers

Everything so far has been flat. A square number counts the unit squares in a square; now stack that square into the third dimension and count the unit cubes in a cube.

A cube of edge $2$ is built from $8$ unit cubes — two layers of $2 \times 2$. A cube of edge $3$ takes $27$ — three layers of $3 \times 3$. A cube of edge $4$ takes four layers of $16$, which is $64$.
