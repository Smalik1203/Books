Example 9

A square floor of 324 m² is covered with square tiles of side 50 cm.

  1. The side of the floor is
    1. 16 m
    2. 18 m
    3. 81 m
    4. 162 m
  2. Numerical answer. How many tiles are needed?

Solution.

Step 1$324 = 18^2$: the side is 18 m, or 36 tiles, so $36^2 = 1296$ tiles.
Answer(i) (b) 18 m; (ii) 1296
