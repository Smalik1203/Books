By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Find the smallest number by which $72$ must be multiplied to give a perfect square. Also find the square root of the product.
  1. Show that $6084$ is a perfect square, and find its square root.
  1. Write the $15$th odd number, and find the sum of the first $15$ odd numbers.
  1. The sum of the first $n$ odd numbers is $361$. Find $n$.
  1. Find $\sqrt[3]{-2744}$.
  1. Find the number of whole numbers that lie strictly between $30^2$ and $31^2$. Give a reason.
  1. Evaluate $\sqrt[3]{27 \times 64}$.
  1. Is $72\,000$ a perfect cube? Give a reason.
  1. Find the last digit of $57^2$ and of $57^3$ without multiplying.
  1. A square has an area of $1089$ m². Find its perimeter.
Short answer
  1. Find the smallest number by which $1350$ must be divided so that the quotient is a perfect cube. Also find the cube root of the quotient.
  1. Show that $(n + 1)^2 - n^2$ is odd for every natural number $n$. Hence find $41^2 - 40^2$ without squaring.
  1. Meera has $1000$ square tiles. She lays the largest square floor she can with them. Find the number of tiles along each side of the floor, and the number of tiles left over.
  1. Find the smallest perfect square that is divisible by each of $6$, $9$ and $14$.
  1. Rohit finds the smallest number by which $1176$ must be multiplied to give a perfect square. His working is shown.
    Step 1$1176 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 7 \times 7$
    Step 2The pairs are $(2 \times 2)$ and $(7 \times 7)$.
    Step 3The primes left without a partner are $2$ and $3$.
    Step 4So he multiplies by $2 + 3 = 5$.
    Identify the step in which Rohit has made an error, if any. Correct the step and find the right answer.
