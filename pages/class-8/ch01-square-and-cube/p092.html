  1. A square hall has an area of $500$ m². Show that its side lies between $22$ m and $23$ m, and then between $22.3$ m and $22.4$ m. Hence find the least whole number of metres of tape that is sure to go once round the hall.
  1. In a class of $x$ students, each student gave as many rupees as there were students. The class collected ₹$1296$. A second class, with $4$ more students, did the same. Find $x$. Show that the second class collected $8x + 16$ rupees more, and find this amount.
  1. A cubical tank has a volume of $3375$ m³. Find its edge. A second cubical tank holds $8$ times as much. Show that its edge is twice as long, and find the area of all six faces of the second tank.
Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $1331$ is a perfect cube.

    Reason (R): In a perfect cube, every prime in the prime factorisation appears a multiple of three times.

  1. Assertion (A): $7056$ is a perfect square.

    Reason (R): $7056$ ends in the digit $6$.

  1. Assertion (A): $2000$ is not a perfect cube.

    Reason (R): A number that ends in exactly three zeros is always a perfect cube.

  1. Assertion (A): $(-5)^3 = 125$.

    Reason (R): The cube of a negative number is negative.

  1. Assertion (A): There are $40$ whole numbers strictly between $20^2$ and $21^2$.

    Reason (R): There are $2n$ whole numbers strictly between $n^2$ and $(n + 1)^2$.
