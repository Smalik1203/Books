Example 6 · Single correct

By what smallest number must 2560 be multiplied so that the product is a perfect cube?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 5
  2. 10
  3. 25
  4. 50

Solution.

Step 1$2560 = 2^9 \times 5$, and $2^9 = (2^3)^3$ is already a cube.
Step 2The single 5 needs two more: multiply by $5^2 = 25$, giving $64\,000 = 40^3$.
Answer(c) 25
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these could be the last digit of a perfect square?

Select all correct options.

  1. 2
  2. 4
  3. 7
  4. 9

Solution.

Step 1The last digit of a square depends only on the last digit of the number: the squares of 0 to 9 end in 0, 1, 4, 9, 6, 5, 6, 9, 4, 1.
Step 2So 4 and 9 are possible; 2 and 7 never occur.
Answer(b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which statements about 196 are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. 196 has an odd number of factors.
  2. $\sqrt{196} = 14$
  3. $196 = 2^2 \times 7^2$
  4. 196 is a perfect cube.

Solution.

Step 1$196 = 14^2 = 2^2 \times 7^2$. A perfect square has an odd number of factors, because its square root pairs with itself.
Step 2$5^3 = 125$ and $6^3 = 216$, so 196 is not a cube.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
