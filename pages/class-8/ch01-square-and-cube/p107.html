Type 3 · Odd numbers and the gaps between squares

Example 5

Find $1 + 3 + 5 + \cdots + 79$.

Solution.

Step 1 $79 = 2 \times 40 - 1$ the 40th odd number
Step 2 sum of the first 40 odd numbers $= 40^2$
Answer $40^2 = 1600$
Example 6

Given that $60^2 = 3600$, find $61^2$ and $59^2$ without multiplying.

Solution.

Step 1 61st odd number $= 2 \times 61 - 1 = 121$
Step 2 $61^2 = 3600 + 121 = 3721$ add the next one
Step 3 60th odd number $= 2 \times 60 - 1 = 119$
Step 4 $59^2 = 3600 - 119 = 3481$ take off the last one
Answer $61^2 = 3721$, $\ 59^2 = 3481$
Example 7

How many whole numbers lie strictly between $25^2$ and $26^2$?

Solution.

Step 1 $26^2 - 25^2 = 2 \times 25 + 1 = 51$ the gap is $2n + 1$
Step 2 $51 - 1 = 50$ the ends are not counted
Answer 50, namely 626 to 675
