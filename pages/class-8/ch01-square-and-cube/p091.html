Long answer
  1. At a school assembly, Mr Deshpande arranges the students in a square of $x$ rows. When $31$ more students arrive, they all fit in a square with one more row. Form an equation in $x$ and solve it. Find the number of students before and after.
  1. Kabir has wooden blocks $12$ cm long, $18$ cm wide and $8$ cm high. He stacks them, all the same way up, to make a solid cube. Find the edge of the smallest such cube, and the number of blocks needed.
  1. Show that the $12$ consecutive odd numbers centred on $12^2$ add up to $12^3$. Hence find the first and last numbers of the block of $15$ consecutive odd numbers that adds up to $15^3$, and check that they add up to $2 \times 15^2$.
  1. In the given figure, a square lawn of area $1764$ m² has a path $3$ m wide all round it, outside the lawn. Find the side of the lawn, the side of the outer square and the area of the path. Also find the cost of paving the path at ₹$50$ per m².
  1. Sunita has a square plot of side $x$ m. She adds $5$ m to each side, and the area of the plot goes up by $325$ m². Form an equation in $x$ and solve it. Find the new area of the plot.
  1. The cubes $1, 8, 27, 64, 125$ have first differences $7, 19, 37, 61$. Find the second and the third differences. Use them to extend the list, and find $6^3$ and $7^3$ without multiplying. Check your value of $6^3$ by multiplying.
