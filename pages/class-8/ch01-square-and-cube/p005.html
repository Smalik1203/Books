Example 1

Which of $5382$, $7043$, $6917$ and $2209$ are certainly not perfect squares?

Read the last digit of each and check it against the six that are possible.

Solution.

Step 1 $5382$ ends in $2$ impossible
Step 2 $7043$ ends in $3$ impossible
Step 3 $6917$ ends in $7$ impossible
Step 4 $2209$ ends in $9$ possible, so look further
Answer $5382$, $7043$ and $6917$

Three are settled without a calculation. The fourth is not settled at all; in fact $2209 = 47^2$, but the last digit could never have told us that.

Two more habits of squares are worth knowing. Both save work later.

Zeros come in pairs. Squaring $10$ gives $100$: one zero becomes two. Squaring $200$ gives $40\,000$: two zeros become four.

$10^2$$= 100$
$200^2$$= 40\,000$
$900^2$$= 810\,000$

Each trailing zero of $n$ is a factor of $10$, and squaring uses it twice — so the square has exactly twice as many. A number with three zeros at the end has a square with six. Read backwards: a perfect square can only end in an even number of zeros, so $2000$ is not a square and neither is any number ending in an odd run of them.

Squaring keeps a number odd or even. An even number is $2k$, and $(2k)^2 = 4k^2$, which is even. An odd number is $2k+1$, and its square is $4k^2 + 4k + 1$, which is odd. So a square is even exactly when the number it came from is even — $6^2 = 36$, $7^2 = 49$ — and there are no exceptions at all.

Both habits can be read backwards to rule numbers out, or to spot a square quickly:

$25\,000$ three zeros, an odd run not a square
$250\,000$ four zeros, and $25 = 5^2$ $= 500^2$
$4900$ two zeros, and $49 = 7^2$ $= 70^2$, an even root
