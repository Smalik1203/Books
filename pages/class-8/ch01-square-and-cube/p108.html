Example 5

By what smallest number must 4375 be divided so that the quotient is a perfect cube?

Solution.

Step 1$4375 = 5^4 \times 7$.
Step 2In a cube every prime appears a multiple of three times. Keep three 5s; the spare 5 and the 7 must go.
Step 3$4375 \div (5 \times 7) = 4375 \div 35 = 125 = 5^3$.
CheckDividing by 7 alone leaves $625 = 5^4$, and by 5 alone leaves $875 = 5^3 \times 7$. Neither is a cube.
Answer35
Example 6

Find $\sqrt{3136}$ by prime factorisation.

Solution.

Step 1$3136 = 2^6 \times 7^2$.
Step 2Every prime is paired: six 2s make three pairs, and two 7s make one.
Step 3Take one prime from each pair: $\sqrt{3136} = 2^3 \times 7 = 56$.
Check$3136$ ends in $6$, so the root ends in $4$ or $6$, and $56$ does.
Answer56
