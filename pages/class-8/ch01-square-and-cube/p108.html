Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $11^3 = 1221$
  2. $11^3$ is the sum of the 11 consecutive odd numbers from 111 to 131.
  3. $\sqrt[3]{-1331} = -11$
  4. $(-0.2)^3 = -0.08$

Solution.

Step 1$11^3 = 1331$. The 11 odd numbers from 111 to 131 have average 121, so their sum is $11 \times 121 = 1331$.
Step 2$(-11)^3 = -1331$, and $(-0.2)^3 = -0.008$, not $-0.08$.
Answer(b), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

In the corridor of 100 lockers, the lockers that end open are those with a perfect-square number. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Locker 81 ends open.
  2. Locker 72 ends open.
  3. Exactly 10 lockers end open.
  4. Locker 100 ends open.

Solution.

Step 1A locker ends open when its number has an odd number of factors, which happens only for perfect squares.
Step 2The squares up to 100 are $1^2, 2^2, \ldots, 10^2$: ten of them, including 81 and 100 but not 72.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

Find $\sqrt{1764}$ by prime factorisation.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$1764 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 7^2$.
Step 2$\sqrt{1764} = 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$.
Answer42
