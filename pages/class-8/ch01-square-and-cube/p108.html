Example 8

144 is the sum of two consecutive triangular numbers. Which two?

Solution.

Step 1 $144 = 12^2$ a square array of side 12
Step 2 with the diagonal: $1 + 2 + \cdots + 12 = 78$
Step 3 without it: $1 + 2 + \cdots + 11 = 66$
Step 4 $66 + 78 = 144$ check
Answer 66 and 78, the 11th and 12th triangular numbers

Type 4 · Square roots by prime factorisation

Example 9

Find $\sqrt{1764}$ by prime factorisation.

Solution.

Step 1 $1764 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 7 \times 7$
Step 2 $1764 = (2 \times 3 \times 7) \times (2 \times 3 \times 7)$ two identical piles
Answer $\sqrt{1764} = 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$
Example 10

Find the smallest number by which 1800 must be multiplied to give a perfect square, and the square root of the product.

Solution.

Step 1 $1800 = 2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5^2$
Step 2 one 2 has no partner multiply by 2
Step 3 $1800 \times 2 = 2^4 \times 3^2 \times 5^2 = (2^2 \times 3 \times 5)^2$
Answer Multiply by 2. $3600 = 60^2$, so the root is 60
