Example 3 · Single correct

How many whole numbers lie strictly between $25^2$ and $26^2$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 49
  2. 51
  3. 50
  4. 52

Solution.

Step 1$26^2 - 25^2 = 2 \times 25 + 1 = 51$.
Step 2The numbers strictly between are one fewer than this gap: $51 - 1 = 50$.
Answer(c) 50
Example 4 · Single correct

By what smallest number must 3380 be divided so that the quotient is a perfect square?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 5
  2. 2
  3. 13
  4. 10

Solution.

Step 1$3380 = 2^2 \times 5 \times 13^2$.
Step 2Only the 5 lacks a partner, so divide by 5: $3380 \div 5 = 676 = 26^2$.
Answer(a) 5
Example 5 · Single correct

238 328 is a perfect cube. What is its cube root?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 58
  2. 62
  3. 68
  4. 72

Solution.

Step 1It ends in 8, and only $2^3$ ends in 8, so the root ends in 2.
Step 2238 lies between $6^3 = 216$ and $7^3 = 343$, so the tens digit is 6: the root is 62, and $62^3 = 238\,328$.
Answer(b) 62
