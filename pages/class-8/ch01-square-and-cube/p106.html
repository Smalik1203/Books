Example 1

The smallest number that divides 3380 to leave a perfect square is

  1. 5
  2. 2
  3. 13
  4. 10

Solution.

Step 1$3380 = 2^2 \times 5 \times 13^2$; only the 5 has no partner.
Step 2$3380 \div 5 = 676 = 26^2$.
Answer(a) 5
Example 2

238 328 is a perfect cube. What is its cube root?

  1. 58
  2. 62
  3. 68
  4. 72

Solution.

Step 1It ends in 8, and only $2^3$ ends in 8, so the root ends in 2.
Step 2238 lies between $6^3 = 216$ and $7^3 = 343$, so the tens digit is 6 and the root is 62.
Check$62^2 = 3844$ and $3844 \times 62 = 238\,328$.
Answer(b) 62
