Example 2

Without listing them, say whether 196 has an odd or an even number of factors. Then list them to check.

Solution.

Step 1 $196 = 14 \times 14$ a square
Step 2 14 is its own partner, so the count is odd
Step 3 1 and 196, 2 and 98, 4 and 49, 7 and 28, then 14 alone
Answer Odd: 196 has 9 factors

Example 3

Which of 3528, 6724, 5000 and 91 003 cannot be perfect squares?

Solution.

Step 1 3528 ends in 8 not a square
Step 2 91 003 ends in 3 not a square
Step 3 5000 ends in three zeros an odd count
Step 4 6724 ends in 4 possible
Answer 3528, 5000 and 91 003

The tests say nothing more about 6724. It is in fact $82^2$.

Example 4

The square of a number ends in 9. What can the last digit of the number be? Give one such square greater than 1000.

Solution.

Step 1 $3^2 = 9$ and $7^2 = 49$ the only endings giving 9
Step 2 $43^2 = 1849$ ends in 3
Answer 3 or 7; for example $43^2 = 1849$
