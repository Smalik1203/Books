Three habits from this division will last longer than the questions that carried them. The first is to look inside a number rather than at it: the factorisation answered nearly every hard question here, and the digits answered almost none. The second is that an impossibility needs an argument and not a search — $n^2 + n$ was settled in three lines, and no amount of checking would have settled it. The third is that two conditions on one number can often be read one prime at a time, as they were for $2n$ and $3n$.
