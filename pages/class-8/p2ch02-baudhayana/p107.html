Example 10 · Multiple correct

A carpenter makes a frame 60 cm by 80 cm and measures its diagonal as 99 cm. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. A frame with right-angled corners would have a diagonal of 100 cm.
  2. The frame is not a true rectangle.
  3. Every corner of the frame is exactly $90^\circ$.
  4. The diagonal is 1 cm shorter than it should be.

Solution.

Step 1$60^2 + 80^2 = 3600 + 6400 = 10\,000 = 100^2$.
Step 2A diagonal of 99 cm means the corners are not right angles; it is 1 cm short.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A rectangle is 7 cm wide, and its diagonal is 1 cm longer than its length. Find its length, in cm.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1With length $l$: $(l + 1)^2 = l^2 + 7^2$.
Step 2$2l + 1 = 49$, so $l = 24$ cm, and the diagonal is 25 cm.
Answer24
Example 12 · Numerical answer

A rectangular panel is 1.2 m wide and 0.5 m high. How long is a bar fixed along its diagonal, in metres?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$1.2^2 + 0.5^2 = 1.44 + 0.25 = 1.69$.
Step 2$\sqrt{1.69} = 1.3$ m.
Answer1.3
