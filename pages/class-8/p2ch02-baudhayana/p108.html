Example 13 · Numerical answer

A multiple of the triple $(12, 35, 37)$ has largest number 111. What is its smallest number?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$111 = 3 \times 37$, so the multiple is 3 times the triple.
Step 2$3 \times 12 = 36$; the triple is $(36, 105, 111)$.
Answer36
