  1. A wire $26$ m long runs from the top of an upright pole to a peg $10$ m from its foot. Find the height of the pole. Also find the length of wire needed if the peg is moved $8$ m further out.
  1. In triangle PQR, PQ $= 20$ cm, QR $= 99$ cm and PR $= 101$ cm. Show that the triangle is right-angled, and name the right angle. Also find its area.
  1. In the given figure, $\angle$ABC $= 90^\circ$, AB $= 12$ cm, and D is a point on BC with BD $= 5$ cm and DC $= 11$ cm.
    Find AD and AC.
  1. Trap $\sqrt{10}$ between two numbers with one decimal place. Then trap it between two numbers with two decimal places. Show the squares you use.
  1. A box is $12$ cm long, $4$ cm wide and $3$ cm high. Find the length of the longest rod that fits inside it. Between which two whole numbers does the diagonal of its base lie?
  1. Sana’s rectangular garden is $21$ m wide. A straight path along its diagonal is $29$ m long. Find the length of the garden. Also find its perimeter.

  1. The string of Farhan’s kite is pulled tight. The kite is directly above a point $15$ m from his hand, level with it. The string is $5$ m longer than the height of the kite above his hand. Taking this height as $h$ m, find the height of the kite and the length of the string.
