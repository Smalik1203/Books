1. Assertion (A): $(20, 21, 29)$ is a Baudhāyana triple.

    Reason (R): Every Baudhāyana triple has at least one even number in it.

  1. Assertion (A): Doubling each side of a square doubles its area.

    Reason (R): The square built on the diagonal of a square has twice its area.

  1. Assertion (A): $\sqrt{20}$ lies between $4$ and $5$.

    Reason (R): The square root of an even number is always a whole number.

  1. Assertion (A): The diagonal of a rectangle $5$ cm by $12$ cm is $13$ cm long.

    Reason (R): The diagonal of a rectangle is the hypotenuse of a right triangle whose short sides are the sides of the rectangle.
