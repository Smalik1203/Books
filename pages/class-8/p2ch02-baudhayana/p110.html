Example 10

Neel has a square sheet of paper of side 16 cm. He folds its four corners in to the centre, which makes a smaller square. Then he folds the four new corners in to the centre, and goes on in the same way.

  1. The area of the square after the first fold is
    1. 64 cm²
    2. 128 cm²
    3. 32 cm²
    4. 192 cm²
  2. Numerical answer. What is the side, in cm, of the square after the second fold?
  3. Numerical answer. What is the area, in cm², of the square after the fourth fold?

Solution.

Step 1Each fold joins the midpoints of the sides, so it halves the area: $256 \div 2 = 128$ cm².
Step 2After two folds the area is $256 \div 4 = 64$ cm², so the side is $\sqrt{64} = 8$ cm.
Step 3After four folds the area is $256 \div 16 = 16$ cm².
CheckTwo folds halve the side, from 16 cm to 8 cm, and two more halve it again, to 4 cm. A square of side 4 cm has area 16 cm².
Answer(i) (b) 128 cm²; (ii) 8; (iii) 16
