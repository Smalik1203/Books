Example 15 · Matching

Match each square root with the two numbers, to two decimal places, between which it lies. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $\sqrt{2}$(1) 1.73 and 1.74
(Q) $\sqrt{3}$(2) 2.82 and 2.83
(R) $\sqrt{5}$(3) 2.23 and 2.24
(S) $\sqrt{8}$(4) 1.41 and 1.42

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2
  3. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–1, R–3, S–4

Solution.

Step 1Square the ends: $1.41^2 \lt 2 \lt 1.42^2$, $1.73^2 \lt 3 \lt 1.74^2$, $2.23^2 \lt 5 \lt 2.24^2$, $2.82^2 \lt 8 \lt 2.83^2$.
Answer(b) P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2
