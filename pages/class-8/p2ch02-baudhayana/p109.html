Example 16

Which whole numbers $m$ and $n$ make the machine give the triple $(13, 84, 85)$?

Solution.

Step 1 $2mn = 84$, so $mn = 42$ the even number
Step 2 $42 = 42 \times 1 = 21 \times 2 = 14 \times 3 = 7 \times 6$ list the pairs
Step 3 $7^2 + 6^2 = 49 + 36 = 85$ only this pair gives $85$
Step 4 $7^2 - 6^2 = 49 - 36 = 13$ check
Answer $m = 7$, $n = 6$
