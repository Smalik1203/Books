Why the other options are wrong

Set A, 2. Option (a), $8$, is $13 - 5$. Lengths in a right triangle do not subtract like that; their squares do, and $169 - 25 = 144$ gives $12$. A check settles it: $8$, $5$ and $13$ cannot even be a triangle, since $8 + 5$ is exactly $13$.

Set A, 4. Option (d), $81$, squares the area instead of doubling it, and option (a) takes the square root of it. The square on a diagonal is twice the square, always — the answer is $18$ whatever the shape of the arithmetic that tempted you.
