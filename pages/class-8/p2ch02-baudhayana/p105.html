Example 3

A box is 2 cm by 3 cm by 6 cm. Which statements are correct?

  1. The diagonal of its 2 cm by 6 cm face is $\sqrt{40}$ cm.
  2. The longest straight rod that fits inside is 7 cm.
  3. The longest straight rod that fits inside is 11 cm.
  4. The diagonal of its 2 cm by 3 cm face is 5 cm.

Solution.

Step 1$2^2 + 6^2 = 40$, so the diagonal of the 2 cm by 6 cm face is $\sqrt{40}$ cm.
Step 2Corner to corner, $\sqrt{4 + 9 + 36} = \sqrt{49} = 7$ cm; but the 2 cm by 3 cm face has diagonal $\sqrt{13}$ cm, not 5 cm.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 4

A carpenter makes a frame 60 cm by 80 cm and measures its diagonal as 99 cm. Which statements are correct?

  1. A frame with right-angled corners would have a diagonal of 100 cm.
  2. The frame is not a true rectangle.
  3. Every corner of the frame is exactly $90^\circ$.
  4. The diagonal is 1 cm shorter than it should be.

Solution.

Step 1$60^2 + 80^2 = 3600 + 6400 = 10\,000 = 100^2$.
Step 2So a 99 cm diagonal means the corners are not right angles.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
