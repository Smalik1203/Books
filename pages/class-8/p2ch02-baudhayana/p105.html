Example 4 · Single correct

The diagonals of a rhombus are 18 cm and 80 cm. What is the length of a side?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 49 cm
  2. 45 cm
  3. 40 cm
  4. 41 cm

Solution.

Step 1The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles, making right triangles with short sides 9 cm and 40 cm.
Step 2$9^2 + 40^2 = 81 + 1600 = 1681 = 41^2$.
Answer(d) 41 cm
Example 5 · Single correct

Which set of sides makes a right-angled triangle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 33, 56, 65
  2. 6, 7, 9
  3. 5, 11, 13
  4. 8, 14, 17

Solution.

Step 1$33^2 + 56^2 = 1089 + 3136 = 4225 = 65^2$.
Step 2$36 + 49 = 85 \ne 81$, $25 + 121 = 146 \ne 169$ and $64 + 196 = 260 \ne 289$.
Answer(a) 33, 56, 65
Example 6 · Single correct

A square has an area of $18 \text{ cm}^2$. What is the side of the square built on its diagonal?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $3\sqrt{2}$ cm
  2. 6 cm
  3. 9 cm
  4. 36 cm

Solution.

Step 1The square on the diagonal has twice the area: $36 \text{ cm}^2$.
Step 2Its side is $\sqrt{36} = 6$ cm.
Answer(b) 6 cm
