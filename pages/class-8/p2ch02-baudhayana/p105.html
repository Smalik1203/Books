The short way across a field

A hectare is $10{,}000$ m2, so the field is $100$ m square. Two sides come to $200$ m; the diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle with two sides of $100$.

diagonal $d^2 = 100^2 + 100^2 = 20{,}000$, so $d \approx 141.4$ m
saving $200 - 141.4 = 58.6$ m
as a percentage $\dfrac{58.6}{200} \times 100 \approx 29\%$

The diagonal of any square is $\sqrt2$ times its side, so cutting the corner off a square always saves the same share of the walk — about $29\%$ — whether the field is a hectare or a hundred. That is why the worn path across a lawn is always the diagonal.
