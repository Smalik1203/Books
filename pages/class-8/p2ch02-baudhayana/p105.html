Example 4

Between which two numbers with two decimal places does the diagonal of a square of side $3$ cm lie?

Solution.

Step 1 the square on the diagonal has area $2 \times 9 = 18$ twice the square
Step 2 $4.2^2 = 17.64$ and $4.3^2 = 18.49$ first trap
Step 3 $4.24^2 = 17.9776$ and $4.25^2 = 18.0625$ tighter trap
Answer Between $4.24$ cm and $4.25$ cm

Example 5

A right-angled triangle has short sides $20$ cm and $48$ cm. Find its hypotenuse.

Solution.

Step 1 $c^2 = 20^2 + 48^2 = 400 + 2304 = 2704$ add the two squares
Step 2 $50^2 = 2500$, $\ 52^2 = 2704$ find the root
Answer $c = 52$ cm
Example 6

A rectangular wooden panel is $1.2$ m wide and $0.5$ m high. How long is a bar fixed along its diagonal?

Solution.

Step 1 $d^2 = 1.2^2 + 0.5^2 = 1.44 + 0.25 = 1.69$ the theorem
Step 2 $1.3^2 = 1.69$ find the root
Answer The bar is $1.3$ m long
Example 7

The diagonals of a rhombus are $18$ cm and $80$ cm long. Find the length of a side.

Solution.

Step 1 half-diagonals $9$ cm and $40$ cm they bisect at right angles
Step 2 $s^2 = 9^2 + 40^2 = 81 + 1600 = 1681$ side is the hypotenuse
Step 3 $41^2 = 1681$, so $s = 41$ cm find the root
Answer Each side is $41$ cm
