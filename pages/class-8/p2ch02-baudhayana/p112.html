Case-based questions

  1. A class marks corners on the school ground with knotted cords, pegged out as triangles. The table gives the three sides of each cord.

    CordSides (m)
    P15, 20, 25
    Q10, 12, 16
    R24, 32, 40
    1. Which cords make a right angle? Show the test for each.
    2. How long is cord P altogether?
    3. Each cord that makes a right angle is a multiple of $(3, 4, 5)$. By what number is each one multiplied?

  1. A shop sells rectangular screens. Screen sizes are given by the diagonal. The table gives the width and height of three screens.

    ScreenWidth (cm)Height (cm)
    A4030
    B8060
    C4820
    1. Find the diagonal of each screen.
    2. Screen B is twice as wide and twice as high as Screen A. How do their diagonals compare, and how do their areas compare?
    3. Another screen has a diagonal of $65$ cm and a height of $25$ cm. How wide is it?
