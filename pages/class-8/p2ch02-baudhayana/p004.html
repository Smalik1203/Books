2.4How Long Is the Diagonal?

Baudhāyana's construction answers the question it was asked without ever saying how long the new side is. That is a strength, not a gap — a rule you can carry out with a cord and two pegs is worth more on a building site than a decimal. But the length is interesting, and finding it needs nothing beyond what we have.

Take a square of side $1$, and cut it along a diagonal. Each half is a right triangle with two sides of length $1$ and a longer third side. That longest side, the one opposite the right angle, is called the hypotenuse. Call it $c$.
