  1. Between which two consecutive whole numbers does $\sqrt{40}$ lie? Give a reason.

  1. Write the triple $m^2 - n^2$, $2mn$, $m^2 + n^2$ for $m = 7$ and $n = 2$, and verify that it is a Baudhāyana triple.
  1. The two short sides of a right-angled triangle are equal, and its hypotenuse is $8$ cm. Find the area of the square on one short side.
