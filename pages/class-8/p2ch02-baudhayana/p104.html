Example 1 · Single correct

A right-angled triangle has short sides 20 cm and 48 cm. What is its hypotenuse?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 52 cm
  2. 68 cm
  3. 56 cm
  4. 44 cm

Solution.

Step 1$20^2 + 48^2 = 400 + 2304 = 2704$.
Step 2$\sqrt{2704} = 52$.
Answer(a) 52 cm
Example 2 · Single correct

A right-angled triangle has hypotenuse 65 and one short side 16. What is the other short side?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 49
  2. 63
  3. 61
  4. 81

Solution.

Step 1$65^2 - 16^2 = (65 - 16)(65 + 16) = 49 \times 81$.
Step 2$\sqrt{49 \times 81} = 7 \times 9 = 63$.
Answer(b) 63
Example 3 · Single correct

A ladder 8.9 m long leans against a wall with its foot 3.9 m from the wall. How high up the wall does it reach?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 5 m
  2. 7.5 m
  3. 8 m
  4. 9.8 m

Solution.

Step 1$8.9^2 - 3.9^2 = (8.9 - 3.9)(8.9 + 3.9) = 5 \times 12.8 = 64$.
Step 2The height is $\sqrt{64} = 8$ m.
Answer(c) 8 m
