diagonal $d^2 = 100^2 + 100^2 = 20{,}000$, so $d \approx 141.4$ m
saving $200 - 141.4 = 58.6$ m
as a percentage $\dfrac{58.6}{200} \times 100 \approx 29\%$

The diagonal of any square is $\sqrt2$ times its side, so cutting the corner off a square always saves the same share of the walk — about $29\%$ — whether the field is a hectare or a hundred. That is why the worn path across a lawn is always the diagonal.

Example 1

A right triangle has hypotenuse 65 and a short side 16. The other short side is

  1. 49
  2. 63
  3. 61
  4. 81

Solution.

Step 1$65^2 - 16^2 = (65 - 16)(65 + 16) = 49 \times 81$.
Step 2$\sqrt{49 \times 81} = 7 \times 9 = 63$.
Answer(b) 63
Example 2

A ladder 8.9 m long leans against a wall with its foot 3.9 m from the wall. How high up the wall does it reach?

  1. 5 m
  2. 7.5 m
  3. 8 m
  4. 9.8 m

Solution.

Step 1$8.9^2 - 3.9^2 = (8.9 - 3.9)(8.9 + 3.9) = 5 \times 12.8 = 64$.
Step 2The height is $\sqrt{64} = 8$ m.
Answer(c) 8 m
