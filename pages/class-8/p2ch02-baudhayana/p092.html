Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): A triangle with sides $9$ cm, $40$ cm and $41$ cm is right-angled.

    Reason (R): If the square of the longest side of a triangle equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides, the angle opposite the longest side is a right angle.
