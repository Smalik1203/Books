Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these are Baudhāyana triples?

Select all correct options.

  1. $(27, 36, 45)$
  2. $(12, 35, 37)$
  3. $(6, 7, 9)$
  4. $(13, 84, 85)$

Solution.

Step 1$27^2 + 36^2 = 2025 = 45^2$, $12^2 + 35^2 = 1369 = 37^2$ and $13^2 + 84^2 = 7225 = 85^2$.
Step 2$6^2 + 7^2 = 85$, but $9^2 = 81$.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

The triple machine uses $m^2 - n^2$, $2mn$ and $m^2 + n^2$. With $m = 8$ and $n = 3$, which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $m^2 - n^2 = 55$
  2. $2mn = 24$
  3. $m^2 + n^2 = 73$
  4. $55^2 + 48^2 = 73^2$

Solution.

Step 1$64 - 9 = 55$, $2 \times 8 \times 3 = 48$ and $64 + 9 = 73$.
Step 2$3025 + 2304 = 5329 = 73^2$. $2mn$ is 48, not 24.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

A box is 2 cm by 3 cm by 6 cm. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The diagonal of its 2 cm by 6 cm face is $\sqrt{40}$ cm.
  2. The longest straight rod that fits inside is 7 cm.
  3. The longest straight rod that fits inside is 11 cm.
  4. The diagonal of its 2 cm by 3 cm face is 5 cm.

Solution.

Step 1$2^2 + 6^2 = 40$, and corner to corner $\sqrt{4 + 9 + 36} = 7$ cm; but $\sqrt{4 + 9} = \sqrt{13}$, not 5.
Answer(a), (b)
