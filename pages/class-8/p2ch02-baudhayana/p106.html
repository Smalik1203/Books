Example 5

A rectangle is 7 cm wide. Its diagonal is 1 cm longer than its length. Find the length in cm.

Solution.

Step 1With length $l$: $(l + 1)^2 = l^2 + 7^2$.
Step 2$2l + 1 = 49$, so $l = 24$ cm. The diagonal is 25 cm long.
Check$24^2 + 7^2 = 576 + 49 = 625 = 25^2$.
Answer24
