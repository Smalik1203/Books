Type 4 · Finding a short side

Example 7

A right-angled triangle has a hypotenuse of $65$ and one short side of $16$. Find the other short side.

Solution.

Step 1 $16^2 + b^2 = 65^2$ hypotenuse alone on one side
Step 2 $b^2 = 4225 - 256 = 3969$ subtract
Step 3 $63^2 = 3969$ find the root
Answer $b = 63$
Example 8

A ladder $8.9$ m long leans against a wall with its foot $3.9$ m from the wall. How high up the wall does it reach?

Solution.

Step 1 the ladder is the hypotenuse opposite the right angle
Step 2 $h^2 = 8.9^2 - 3.9^2 = 79.21 - 15.21 = 64$ subtract
Answer $h = 8$ m

Type 5 · Testing for a right angle

Example 9

Which of these triangles is right-angled: sides $33$, $56$, $65$, or sides $6$, $7$, $9$?

Solution.

Step 1 $33^2 + 56^2 = 1089 + 3136 = 4225 = 65^2$ the two squares match
Step 2 $6^2 + 7^2 = 36 + 49 = 85$, but $9^2 = 81$ they do not
Answer The triangle $33$, $56$, $65$ is right-angled, with the right angle opposite $65$; the other is not

Always put the longest side on its own: it is the only one that can be the hypotenuse.
