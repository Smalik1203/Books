Type 4 · Finding a short side

Example 8

A right-angled triangle has a hypotenuse of $65$ and one short side of $16$. Find the other short side.

Solution.

Step 1 $16^2 + b^2 = 65^2$ hypotenuse alone on one side
Step 2 $b^2 = 4225 - 256 = 3969$ subtract
Step 3 $63^2 = 3969$ find the root
Answer $b = 63$
Example 9

A ladder $8.9$ m long leans against a wall with its foot $3.9$ m from the wall. How high up the wall does it reach?

Solution.

Step 1 the ladder is the hypotenuse opposite the right angle
Step 2 $h^2 = 8.9^2 - 3.9^2 = 79.21 - 15.21 = 64$ subtract
Answer $h = 8$ m

Type 5 · The unknown in a side and the hypotenuse

Example 10

A rectangle is $7$ cm wide, and its diagonal is $1$ cm longer than its length. Find its length and its diagonal.

Let the length be $x$ cm. The diagonal is then $x + 1$ cm, and it is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle with short sides $7$ and $x$.

Solution.

Step 1 $7^2 + x^2 = (x+1)^2$ the theorem
Step 2 $49 + x^2 = x^2 + 2x + 1$ expand
Step 3 $49 = 2x + 1$ take $x^2$ off both sides
Step 4 $x = 24$ solve
Answer The length is $24$ cm and the diagonal $25$ cm

Check: $49 + 576 = 625 = 25^2$. The $x^2$ was on both sides, so it went, and a first-degree equation was left.
