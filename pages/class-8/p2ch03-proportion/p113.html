Three things from this division will last longer than the questions that carried them. Decide the kind before you calculate: ask what happens to the second quantity when the first is doubled. Find what one part is worth: a ratio becomes a set of amounts the moment you know the value of a single part. And ratios survive multiplication but not addition: whenever a question adds something to both parts, write the quantities as $ax$ and $bx$ and turn it into an equation.
