1. A car covers a journey in $6$ hours at $55$ km/h. Find the speed at which it would cover the journey in $5$ hours. Also find the time it would take at $60$ km/h.
  1. A map is drawn at $1 : 2{,}50{,}000$. Two villages are $7.2$ cm apart on it. Find the real distance between them. Also find the length on the map of a lake that is $4.5$ km long.
  1. In a survey of $240$ students, the numbers who prefer tea, coffee and milk are in the ratio $3 : 2 : 5$. Find the number of students in each group, and the angles of a pie chart of this survey data.
  1. $x$ and $y$ are in inverse proportion. Show that if $x$ is increased by $25\%$, then $y$ decreases by $20\%$.
  1. Two numbers are in the ratio $4 : 7$. If $9$ is added to each, the ratio becomes $5 : 8$. Find the numbers.
Long answer
  1. A school has enough rice to feed $450$ students for $28$ days. After $8$ days, $90$ students leave for a camp. Let the remaining rice last $d$ more days. Form an equation in $d$ and solve it. Also find the total number of days the rice lasts.
  1. Taps A and B fill a tank in $12$ minutes and $18$ minutes. A drain C empties the full tank in $36$ minutes. All three are opened on an empty tank. Find the time the tank takes to fill. Also find how many minutes would be saved if C were kept shut.
  1. A cart wheel has a radius of $35$ cm, and a bicycle wheel has a radius of $28$ cm. Find the number of turns each wheel makes in covering $1.1$ km. Show that the radius times the number of turns is the same for both wheels, and hence that the two are in inverse proportion. (Take $\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$)
  1. In the given figure, a rectangular plot is drawn on a plan at a scale of $1 : 500$. On the plan the plot is $6$ cm long and $4.2$ cm wide. Find the real length and breadth of the plot in metres, and its area. Also find the cost of fencing it at ₹$120$ a metre.
