Behind Each Answer
The same eight questions, worked — and the reasoning that finds them.

The eight are answered below in the order they were asked. Each one begins the same way: decide, in a sentence, whether the two quantities grow together or pull apart. Everything after that is a single multiplication or a single division, and getting the first sentence wrong is the only way to get the answer wrong.

More people, more rice

Twice the eaters, twice the rice: direct. The quotient — grams of rice per person — is what stays the same.

per person $750 \div 6 = 125$ g
for eight $125 \times 8 = 1000$ g, that is $1$ kg

Or in one step: $750 \times \tfrac86 = 1000$. Going by way of one person is slower and safer, and it is the same move as finding the value of one part.

More workers, fewer days

The wall does not get bigger when more people build it, so the two quantities pull apart: inverse. The product is the size of the job.

the job $12 \times 10 = 120$ worker-days
so $15 \times y = 120$, giving $y = 8$ days

A quarter more workers, and a fifth off the time. Those two fractions differ, and both are right, for the reason Chapter 1 gave: they are measured against different starting numbers.
