3.4Dividing a Whole

The questions so far gave one amount and asked for the rest. The other common question gives the total and asks how it splits.

Divide $12$ sweets between two children in the ratio $2 : 1$. There are $2 + 1 = 3$ parts altogether, so one part is $12 \div 3 = 4$ sweets, and the two shares are $8$ and $4$. Adding the terms first is the whole method, and it works however many terms there are.

Dividing $x$ in the ratio $a : b : c$

Add the terms. Each share is the whole times that term's fraction of the total:

$\ x \times \dfrac{a}{a+b+c}$, $\quad x \times \dfrac{b}{a+b+c}$, $\quad x \times \dfrac{c}{a+b+c}$.

The three fractions add to $1$, which is the check: the shares must come back to the whole.
