on foot, $5$ km/h $12$ hours    $5 \times 12 = 60$
by bicycle, $15$ km/h $4$ hours    $15 \times 4 = 60$
by scooter, $30$ km/h $2$ hours    $30 \times 2 = 60$
by bus, $40$ km/h $1.5$ hours    $40 \times 1.5 = 60$

The two quantities change by the same factor. A bicycle is three times as fast as walking, and it takes a third of the time. But they change in opposite directions. Multiply the speed by $n$, and the time is multiplied by $\tfrac1n$.

Look at the right-hand column and the reason is clear. Speed times time is $60$ every time, because $60$ km is the distance, and the distance does not change. Here the product stays the same, just as the quotient stayed the same in Section 3.2.

Two kinds of proportion
  • Direct proportion: $\dfrac{y}{x} = k$. Both grow together.
  • Inverse proportion: $xy = k$. One grows as the other shrinks.

So in an inverse proportion, any two pairs of values give $x_1 y_1 = x_2 y_2$. Nothing in the numbers tells you which kind a pair of quantities is. You have to think about the situation. More workers move more bricks: direct. More workers finish a job in fewer days: inverse. The same word more appears in both, pulling in opposite directions.

Example 8

Eighteen volunteers take $5$ days to paint the walls of a school. How long would $15$ volunteers take?

Fewer volunteers need more days: inverse. So the product stays the same.

Solution.

Step 1 $18 \times 5 = 90$ volunteer-days the constant
Step 2 $15 \times y = 90$ same product
Step 3 $y = 6$ divide by fifteen
Answer $6$ days

The constant has a meaning worth keeping: ninety volunteer-days is the size of the job, however it is shared out.
