Ram, in an hour $1$ unit
Shyam, in an hour $\dfrac{1}{1.5} = \dfrac23$ of a unit
together, in an hour $1 + \dfrac23 = \dfrac53$ units

Work done and time taken are in direct proportion — twice the time, twice the work — so the last step is the rule of three.

as a proportion $\dfrac53 : 1 \ :: \ 1 : x$
so $x = \dfrac35$ of an hour, that is $36$ minutes

Rates add; times do not. That is what the question was testing, and why the average was wrong. The same reasoning answers every question about two taps, two printers or two people working at once: turn each rate into work-per-hour, add them, and turn the total back into a time at the end.
