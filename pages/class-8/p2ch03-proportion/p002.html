$$a \times d = b \times c, \qquad\text{or, in the same way,}\qquad \frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}.$$

Such ratios are called proportional, and we write $a : b :: c : d$. For the sherbet, $2 \times 12 = 24$ and $8 \times 3 = 24$, so the ratios agree. Also $\tfrac82 = \tfrac{12}{3} = 4$, which is four times as much water as syrup written as a number.

The quotient stays the same
  • $a : b$ and $c : d$ are proportional when $a \times d = b \times c$.
  • In direct proportion, $y \div x$ is a fixed number: the constant.

For the sherbet, the constant is $4$: water is always four times the syrup, in a jug of five cups or of fifty. Double one quantity, and the other doubles too.

Example 1

Are $8 : 14$ and $20 : 35$ proportional?

Solution.

Step 1 $8 \times 35 = 280$ and $14 \times 20 = 280$ multiply across
Answer Yes. Both reduce to $4 : 7$

The test also finds a missing number. If three of $a$, $b$, $c$ and $d$ are known, $ad = bc$ gives the fourth. This is called the rule of three.

Example 2

Six tailors stitch $150$ school shirts in a week. How many tailors are needed to stitch $450$ shirts in a week?

Solution.

Step 1 $150 : 450 \ :: \ 6 : x$ as a proportion
Step 2 $150\,x = 450 \times 6 = 2700$ multiply across
Step 3 $x = \dfrac{2700}{150} = 18$ divide
Answer $18$ tailors

Three times the shirts needs three times the tailors. Once the four numbers are in the right places, the arithmetic cannot go wrong.
