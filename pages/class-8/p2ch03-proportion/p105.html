Example 4

A printer prints $1{,}350$ pages in $25$ minutes. How many pages does it print in $40$ minutes at the same speed?

Solution.

Step 1 $1350 \div 25 = 54$ pages a minute the constant
Step 2 $54 \times 40 = 2160$ forty minutes
Answer $2{,}160$ pages

Type 3 · Ratios with more than two terms

Example 5

A fruit drink is mixed mango : orange : pineapple in the ratio $4 : 3 : 1$. There is $1.2$ litres of orange juice. How much of the other two is needed, and how much drink is made?

Solution.

Step 1 $3$ parts are $1.2$ litres, so one part is $0.4$ litres orange is given
Step 2 mango: $4 \times 0.4 = 1.6$ litres
Step 3 pineapple: $1 \times 0.4 = 0.4$ litres
Step 4 $1.6 + 1.2 + 0.4 = 3.2$ litres add all three
Answer $1.6$ litres of mango and $0.4$ of pineapple; $3.2$ litres of drink
Example 6

One batch of concrete is mixed cement : sand : gravel as $6 : 9 : 15$, and another as $4 : 6 : 10$. Are they the same mix? Write it in its simplest form.

Solution.

Step 1 $6 \div 4 = 1.5$, $\ 9 \div 6 = 1.5$, $\ 15 \div 10 = 1.5$ matching quotients
Step 2 all three quotients agree so proportional
Step 3 $6 : 9 : 15 = 2 : 3 : 5$ and $4 : 6 : 10 = 2 : 3 : 5$ divide by 3, and by 2
Answer Yes, the same mix: $2 : 3 : 5$

With three terms there is no cross-multiplying to fall back on; the quotients are the test.
