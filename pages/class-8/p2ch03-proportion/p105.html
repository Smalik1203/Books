Example 4 · Single correct

A map is drawn at $1 : 30\,00\,000$. Two towns are 7.5 cm apart on it. How far apart are they on the ground?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 22.5 km
  2. 2250 km
  3. 30 km
  4. 225 km

Solution.

Step 11 cm on the map is $30\,00\,000$ cm $= 30$ km on the ground.
Step 2$7.5 \times 30 = 225$ km.
Answer(d) 225 km
Example 5 · Single correct

A bus at 40 km/h takes 6 hours over a journey. How long does it take at 48 km/h?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 5 hours
  2. 7.2 hours
  3. 4.8 hours
  4. 6 hours

Solution.

Step 1The distance is fixed, $40 \times 6 = 240$ km, so speed and time are in inverse proportion.
Step 2$240 \div 48 = 5$ hours.
Answer(a) 5 hours
Example 6 · Single correct

Asha can paint a wall in 10 hours and Bina in 15 hours. How long do they take working together?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 12.5 hours
  2. 6 hours
  3. 25 hours
  4. 5 hours

Solution.

Step 1In one hour Asha paints $\frac{1}{10}$ of the wall and Bina $\frac{1}{15}$: together $\frac{3}{30} + \frac{2}{30} = \frac{1}{6}$.
Step 2So the whole wall takes 6 hours.
Answer(b) 6 hours
