Example 3

In a pie chart of the 1250 students of a school, the slice for students who cycle is $72^\circ$. Which statements are correct?

  1. 250 students cycle.
  2. $20\%$ of the students cycle.
  3. 500 students would get $144^\circ$.
  4. $90^\circ$ stands for 300 students.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{72}{360} = 20\%$, and $20\%$ of 1250 is 250; twice as many is $144^\circ$.
Step 2But $\frac{90}{360} \times 1250 = 312.5$, so (d) is false.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 4

A rectangle has a perimeter of 84 cm, and its length and breadth are in the ratio $4 : 3$. Which statements are correct?

  1. Its length is 24 cm.
  2. Its breadth is 18 cm.
  3. Its area is $432 \text{ cm}^2$.
  4. Its diagonal is 30 cm.

Solution.

Step 1Length + breadth $= 42$ cm in 7 parts of 6 cm: 24 cm and 18 cm, and the area is $24 \times 18 = 432$ cm².
Step 2$24^2 + 18^2 = 576 + 324 = 900 = 30^2$.
Answer(a), (b), (c), (d)
