Exercise Set 3.5
  1. Which of these pairs are in inverse proportion? Check by multiplying.
    1. $x = 20, 40, 50$ with $y = 30, 15, 12$
    2. $x = 8, 16, 24$ with $y = 9, 4.5, 4$
    3. $x = 25, 50, 125$ with $y = 12, 6, 2.4$
  1. $x$ and $y$ are in inverse proportion, and $x = 18$ when $y = 8$. Find $y$ when $x = 24$, and find $x$ when $y = 36$.
  1. Say whether each pair is in direct proportion, in inverse proportion, or neither.
    1. the kilograms of rice bought and the bill, at a fixed price
    2. the number of people sharing a cake equally and the size of each share
    3. a child's age and her shoe size
  1. Eight machines of the same kind pack a day's orders in $45$ minutes. How long would $6$ machines take? How many machines would pack them in half an hour?
  1. A camp has food for $60$ hikers for $18$ days. After $6$ days, $20$ more hikers join. How much longer will the remaining food last?
  1. One pipe fills a tank in $4$ hours, and a second pipe fills it in $12$ hours. How long do the two take together? Check that your answer is less than $4$ hours.
  1. A bus takes $5$ hours for a journey at $48$ km/h. At what speed would it take $4$ hours? Are speed and time here in direct or inverse proportion? Give a reason.
  1. Two pipes together fill a tank in $6$ hours. One of them alone fills it in $10$ hours. How long does the other pipe take alone?
  1. A tap fills a tank in $5$ hours, and a drain empties the full tank in $20$ hours. Both are opened on an empty tank. How long does the tank take to fill? Give your answer in hours and minutes.
