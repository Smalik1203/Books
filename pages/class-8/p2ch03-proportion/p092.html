Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): If $15$ workers take $8$ days over a job, then $10$ workers take $12$ days.

    Reason (R): For a fixed job, the number of workers and the number of days are in inverse proportion.

  1. Assertion (A): $3 : 4$ and $9 : 16$ are proportional.

    Reason (R): $a : b$ and $c : d$ are proportional exactly when $ad = bc$.

  1. Assertion (A): On a map drawn at $1 : 50{,}000$, a line $4$ cm long stands for $2$ km.

    Reason (R): The scale of a map is written without units.

  1. Assertion (A): In a pie chart of $90$ students, the slice for $30$ students is $120^\circ$.

    Reason (R): Two quantities that increase together are always in direct proportion.
