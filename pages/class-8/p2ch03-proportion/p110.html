Example 10

The water tank on a school roof is filled by pumps of the same kind. With 3 pumps running, the empty tank fills in 40 minutes.

  1. With 5 pumps running, the empty tank fills in
    1. 24 minutes
    2. 66.7 minutes
    3. 20 minutes
    4. 8 minutes
  2. Numerical answer. How many pumps are needed to fill the empty tank in 15 minutes?
  3. Numerical answer. The tank starts to fill with 3 pumps. After 10 minutes, 3 more pumps are switched on. In how many minutes in all is the tank full?

Solution.

Step 1The job is $3 \times 40 = 120$ pump-minutes. So 5 pumps take $120 \div 5 = 24$ minutes to fill it.
Step 2To finish in 15 minutes needs $120 \div 15 = 8$ pumps.
Step 3In 10 minutes, 3 pumps do 30 pump-minutes, leaving 90. Six pumps do these in $90 \div 6 = 15$ minutes, so $10 + 15 = 25$ minutes in all.
Answer(i) (a) 24 minutes; (ii) 8; (iii) 25
