Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): If $8$ workers take $9$ days over a job, then $4$ workers take $18$ days.

    Reason (R): For a fixed job, the number of workers and the number of days are in inverse proportion.

  1. Assertion (A): $2 : 3$ and $4 : 9$ are proportional.

    Reason (R): $a : b$ and $c : d$ are proportional exactly when $ad = bc$.

  1. Assertion (A): The angles of the slices of a pie chart add up to $360^\circ$.

    Reason (R): The slices of a pie chart are in the ratio of the parts they stand for.

  1. Assertion (A): The side of a square and its area are not in direct proportion.

    Reason (R): Two quantities that increase together are always in direct proportion.
