Very short answer
  1. Find the value of $x$ if $6 : x :: 15 : 25$.
  1. Are $2.4 : 3.6$ and $10 : 15$ proportional? Give a reason.
  1. Write the ratio of $75$ cm to $2$ m in its simplest form.
  1. $y$ is in direct proportion to $x$, and $y = 42$ when $x = 6$. Find the constant. Also find $x$ when $y = 91$.
  1. On a map, $1$ cm stands for $8$ km. Write the scale of the map in the form $1 : n$.
  1. Divide ₹$1{,}560$ in the ratio $5 : 7$.
  1. $x$ and $y$ are in inverse proportion, and $y = 15$ when $x = 8$. Find $y$ when $x = 20$.
  1. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio $3 : 4 : 5$. Find its largest angle.
  1. In a pie chart, a slice of $108^\circ$ stands for $45$ students. Find the total number of students.
  1. Is the ratio $3 : 5 : 7$ proportional to $12 : 20 : 30$? Give a reason.
Short answer
  1. Rehana buys $12$ m of cloth for ₹$2{,}160$. Find the cost of $7.5$ m of the same cloth. Also find the length she can buy for ₹$3{,}420$.
  1. Show that if $a : b :: c : d$, then $(a + b) : b :: (c + d) : d$.
  1. ₹$8{,}100$ is shared among three sisters in the ratio $\tfrac13 : \tfrac14 : \tfrac16$. Find each share.

  1. Twelve workers finish a job in $15$ days. Manoj finds how long $20$ workers would take.

    Step 1$12 : 20 :: 15 : x$
    Step 2$12x = 20 \times 15 = 300$
    Step 3$x = 300 \div 12 = 25$
    Step 4So $20$ workers take $25$ days.

    Identify the step in which Manoj has made an error, if any. Correct it and find the right answer.

  1. The ages of Aditi and her grandmother are in the ratio $2 : 9$. After $12$ years the ratio will be $1 : 3$. Find their present ages.
