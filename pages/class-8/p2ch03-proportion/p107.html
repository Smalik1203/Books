Example 10

The plan of a hall is drawn at $1 : 250$. The hall is $20$ m long. How long is it on the plan? A door is $0.48$ cm wide on the plan. How wide is it really?

Solution.

Step 1 $20$ m $= 2000$ cm same units first
Step 2 $2000 \div 250 = 8$ cm ground to paper
Step 3 $0.48 \times 250 = 120$ cm paper to ground
Answer $8$ cm on the plan; the door is $120$ cm, that is $1.2$ m, wide

Example 11

Of $1{,}200$ people asked how they travel to work, $480$ go by bus, $300$ by train, $240$ by car and $180$ on foot. Find the angles of the pie chart.

Solution.

Step 1 $360 \div 1200 = 0.3$ degrees for each person one part
Step 2 bus $480 \times 0.3 = 144$, train $300 \times 0.3 = 90$
Step 3 car $240 \times 0.3 = 72$, foot $180 \times 0.3 = 54$
Step 4 $144 + 90 + 72 + 54 = 360$ the check
Answer $144^\circ$, $90^\circ$, $72^\circ$ and $54^\circ$
Example 12

In a pie chart of the $1{,}250$ students of a school, the slice for students who cycle is $72^\circ$. How many students cycle, and what percentage is that?

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{72}{360} = \dfrac{1}{5}$ share of the circle
Step 2 $\dfrac{1}{5} \times 1250 = 250$ same share of students
Step 3 $\dfrac{1}{5} \times 100 = 20$ as a percentage
Answer $250$ students, which is $20\%$
