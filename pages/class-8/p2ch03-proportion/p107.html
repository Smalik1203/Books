Example 10 · Multiple correct

A rectangle has a perimeter of 84 cm, and its length and breadth are in the ratio $4 : 3$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its length is 24 cm.
  2. Its breadth is 18 cm.
  3. Its area is $432 \text{ cm}^2$.
  4. Its diagonal is 30 cm.

Solution.

Step 1Length + breadth $= 42$ cm in 7 parts of 6 cm: 24 cm and 18 cm, and $24 \times 18 = 432$.
Step 2$24^2 + 18^2 = 576 + 324 = 900 = 30^2$.
Answer(a), (b), (c), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A pipe fills a tank in 4 hours, and a drain empties the full tank in 6 hours. Both are left open. How many hours does the empty tank take to fill?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1In one hour the tank gains $\frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3}{12} - \frac{2}{12} = \frac{1}{12}$ of itself.
Step 2So it fills in 12 hours.
Answer12
Example 12 · Numerical answer

A printer prints 1350 pages in 25 minutes. How many pages does it print in 40 minutes at the same rate?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1It prints $1350 \div 25 = 54$ pages a minute.
Step 2In 40 minutes: $54 \times 40 = 2160$ pages.
Answer2160
