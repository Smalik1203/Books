Example 6

A fruit drink is mixed mango, orange and pineapple in the ratio $4 : 3 : 1$. There is 1.2 litres of orange. How many litres of drink are there in all?

Solution.

Step 13 parts are 1.2 litres, so 1 part is 0.4 litres.
Step 2The drink is $4 + 3 + 1 = 8$ parts: $8 \times 0.4 = 3.2$ litres.

Example 7

A plan is drawn at a scale of $1 : 250$. Match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) A 20 m wall, drawn on the plan(1) 30 m
(Q) A 1 cm line on the plan, on the ground(2) 8 cm
(R) A 4 cm line on the plan, on the ground(3) 2.5 m
(S) A 12 cm line on the plan, on the ground(4) 10 m
  1. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
  2. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–3, R–4, S–2

Solution.

Step 11 cm on the plan is 250 cm, that is 2.5 m, on the ground. So a 20 m wall is 8 cm on the plan.
Step 24 cm and 12 cm on the plan are $4 \times 2.5 = 10$ m and $12 \times 2.5 = 30$ m.
Answer(c) P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
