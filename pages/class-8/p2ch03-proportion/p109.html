Example 16

A pipe fills a tank in $4$ hours, and a drain empties the full tank in $6$ hours. Both are left open on an empty tank. How long does it take to fill?

Solution.

Step 1 the pipe adds $\tfrac14$ of the tank an hour; the drain takes away $\tfrac16$ rates
Step 2 $\tfrac14 - \tfrac16 = \tfrac{3}{12} - \tfrac{2}{12} = \tfrac{1}{12}$ the drain subtracts
Answer $12$ hours

A drain works against the pipe, so its rate is taken away rather than added.
