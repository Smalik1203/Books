Example 15 · Matching

A pie chart shows how 1200 people travel. Match each group with its angle. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Bus, 480 people(1) $72^\circ$
(Q) Train, 300 people(2) $54^\circ$
(R) Car, 240 people(3) $144^\circ$
(S) Walk, 180 people(4) $90^\circ$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Each person is $\frac{360}{1200} = 0.3$ degrees, so 480, 300, 240 and 180 people take $144^\circ$, $90^\circ$, $72^\circ$, $54^\circ$.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
