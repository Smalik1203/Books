Example 9

Three friends start a stall at a fair. They put in ₹1,500, ₹2,500 and ₹3,000, and they agree to share any profit in the ratio of the money each put in. The profit this year is ₹5,600.

  1. The ratio of the money they put in, in its simplest form, is
    1. $3 : 5 : 6$
    2. $15 : 25 : 3$
    3. $1 : 2 : 3$
    4. $5 : 3 : 6$
  2. Numerical answer. What is the largest share of this year’s profit, in rupees?
  3. Numerical answer. Next year the first friend’s share is ₹1,800. What is the whole profit that year, in rupees?

Solution.

Step 1Divide each amount by 500: $1500 : 2500 : 3000 = 3 : 5 : 6$.
Step 2There are $3 + 5 + 6 = 14$ parts of $5600 \div 14 = 400$, so the largest share is $6 \times 400 = 2400$.
Step 3Next year 3 parts are ₹1,800, so one part is ₹600, and the whole profit is $14 \times 600 = 8400$ rupees.
CheckThis year the shares are ₹1,200, ₹2,000 and ₹2,400, and these add up to ₹5,600 in all.
Answer(i) (a) 3 : 5 : 6; (ii) 2400; (iii) 8400
