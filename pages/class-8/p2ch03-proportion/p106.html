Type 4 · Dividing a whole in a given ratio

Example 7

Divide ₹3,000 among three friends in the ratio $3 : 5 : 7$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3 + 5 + 7 = 15$ total parts
Step 2 $3000 \div 15 = 200$ one part
Step 3 $3 \times 200 = 600$, $\ 5 \times 200 = 1000$, $\ 7 \times 200 = 1400$
Step 4 $600 + 1000 + 1400 = 3000$ the check
Answer ₹600, ₹1,000 and ₹1,400
Example 8

A rectangle has a perimeter of $84$ cm, and its length and breadth are in the ratio $4 : 3$. Find its area.

Solution.

Step 1 $84 \div 2 = 42$ cm length plus breadth
Step 2 $42 \div 7 = 6$ cm one part of $4 + 3$
Step 3 length $4 \times 6 = 24$ cm, breadth $3 \times 6 = 18$ cm
Step 4 $24 \times 18 = 432$ the area
Answer $432$ cm²

The whole to be divided was half the perimeter, not the perimeter: the ratio compares one length with one breadth.

Type 5 · Scale on a map or a plan

Example 9

A map is drawn at $1 : 30{,}00{,}000$. How far apart on the ground are two towns that are $7.5$ cm apart on the map? How far apart on the map are two cities $540$ km apart?

Solution.

Step 1 $30{,}00{,}000$ cm $= 30$ km, so $1$ cm stands for $30$ km divide by 1,00,000
Step 2 $7.5 \times 30 = 225$ km paper to ground
Step 3 $540 \div 30 = 18$ cm ground to paper
Answer $225$ km apart; $18$ cm apart on the map
