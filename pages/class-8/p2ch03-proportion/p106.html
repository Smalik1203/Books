Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which ratios are proportional to $6 : 15$?

Select all correct options.

  1. $14 : 35$
  2. $2 : 5$
  3. $12 : 25$
  4. $18 : 45$

Solution.

Step 1$6 : 15 = 2 : 5$, and $14 : 35$ and $18 : 45$ also reduce to $2 : 5$.
Step 2$12 : 25$ does not: $12 \times 5 = 60$ but $25 \times 2 = 50$.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which pairs of quantities are in inverse proportion?

Select all correct options.

  1. Speed and time, for a fixed distance
  2. Number of workers and days to finish a fixed job
  3. Number of notebooks and their total cost
  4. Distance on a map and distance on the ground

Solution.

Step 1In (a) and (b) the product stays fixed: doubling one halves the other.
Step 2In (c) and (d) the ratio stays fixed, so they are in direct proportion.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

In a pie chart of the 1250 students of a school, the slice for students who cycle is $72^\circ$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. 250 students cycle.
  2. $20\%$ of the students cycle.
  3. 500 students would get $144^\circ$.
  4. $90^\circ$ stands for 300 students.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{72}{360} = 20\%$, and $20\%$ of 1250 is 250; twice as many is $144^\circ$. But $\frac{90}{360} \times 1250 = 312.5$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
