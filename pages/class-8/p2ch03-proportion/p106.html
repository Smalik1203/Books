Example 5

A pipe fills a tank in 4 hours, and a drain empties the full tank in 6 hours. Both are left open. How many hours does the empty tank take to fill?

Solution.

Step 1In one hour the pipe fills $\frac{1}{4}$ of the tank, and the drain empties $\frac{1}{6}$ of it.
Step 2So each hour the tank gains $\frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3}{12} - \frac{2}{12} = \frac{1}{12}$ of itself.
Step 3So it fills in 12 hours.
CheckIn 12 hours the pipe fills 3 tanks and the drain empties 2, which leaves one full tank.
Answer12
