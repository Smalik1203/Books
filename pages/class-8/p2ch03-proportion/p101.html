Using What You Know
The chapter's ideas, in questions that do not say which one to use.

Two things came out of this chapter and everything below is one of them. In a direct proportion the quotient holds still, so both quantities grow together. In an inverse proportion the product holds still, so one grows as the other shrinks. The whole difficulty is deciding which you are looking at, and no formula will decide it for you.

So none of the questions below says which kind it is, and two of them are not proportional at all until you have made them so. Try each before turning the page.

A recipe for six people uses $750$ g of rice. How much rice for eight?

Twelve workers build a wall in ten days. How long would fifteen workers take?

Two landmarks are $6$ cm apart on a map drawn at $1 : 50{,}000$. How far apart would they be on a map drawn at $1 : 2{,}00{,}000$?

A pie chart of a survey of $720$ people gives one answer a slice of $150^\circ$. How many people gave that answer?

A car travels $15$ km on a litre of petrol, and petrol costs ₹$105$ a litre. What does a journey of $240$ km cost in fuel?

Six taps fill a tank in $40$ minutes. Two of them are shut off. How long does the tank take now?
