Think and Reflect
  1. Share $100$ sweets equally among three children, in the ratio $1 : 1 : 1$. Why can the shares not be whole numbers?
  2. A share is $3$ parts out of a total of $8$ parts. What fraction of the whole is it?
  3. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio $1 : 1 : 1$. What kind of triangle is it?
Exercise Set 3.2
  1. Divide as asked.
    1. ₹$640$ in the ratio $3 : 5$
    2. $84$ kg in the ratio $1 : 2 : 4$
    3. $360^\circ$ in the ratio $2 : 3 : 7$
  1. A school day of $360$ minutes is divided into lessons, breaks, games and assembly in the ratio $12 : 2 : 3 : 1$. How long is each?
  1. A shop has blue, black and red pens in the ratio $5 : 4 : 1$. There are $120$ black pens. How many blue and red pens are there, and how many pens in all?
  1. A money box holds $60$ coins of ₹$5$, ₹$2$ and ₹$1$, whose numbers are in the ratio $2 : 3 : 5$. How much money is in the box?
  1. Can a triangle have sides in the ratio $2 : 3 : 6$? Try to draw one, and then explain what stops you.
  1. Two sisters share ₹$900$ so that the elder gets ₹$180$ more than the younger. In what ratio is the money divided? If the difference were ₹$300$, what would the ratio be?
  1. A sum of money is divided in the ratio $4 : 7$, and the smaller share is ₹$1{,}200$. Find the whole sum.
  1. Boys and girls in a class are in the ratio $5 : 4$, and there are $8$ more boys than girls. How many students are in the class?
