Type 7 · Inverse proportion

Example 13

A bus at $40$ km/h takes $6$ hours over a journey. How long does it take at $48$ km/h?

Solution.

Step 1 faster means less time, over the same distance inverse
Step 2 $40 \times 6 = 240$ km the constant
Step 3 $48 \times t = 240$, so $t = 5$ same product
Answer $5$ hours
Example 14

Show that $x$ and $y$ are in inverse proportion, and find $y$ when $x = 4$.

$x$ $3 \qquad 5 \qquad 12$
$y$ $20 \qquad 12 \qquad 5$

Solution.

Step 1 $3 \times 20 = 60$, $\ 5 \times 12 = 60$, $\ 12 \times 5 = 60$ multiply each pair
Step 2 the product is always $60$ so inverse
Step 3 $4 \times y = 60$, so $y = 15$
Answer Inverse, with $xy = 60$; $y = 15$ when $x = 4$

Type 8 · Working together

Example 15

Asha can paint a wall in $10$ hours and Bina in $15$ hours. How long do they take working together?

Solution.

Step 1 Asha does $\tfrac{1}{10}$ of the wall an hour, Bina $\tfrac{1}{15}$ rates
Step 2 $\tfrac{1}{10} + \tfrac{1}{15} = \tfrac{3}{30} + \tfrac{2}{30} = \tfrac{1}{6}$ add the rates
Step 3 a sixth of the wall an hour takes $6$ hours rate back to time
Answer $6$ hours
