Example 5

A job needs $110$ units of concrete, mixed cement : sand : gravel in the ratio $1 : 1.5 : 3$. How much of each?

Solution.

Step 1 $1 + 1.5 + 3 = 5.5$ total parts
Step 2 $110 \div 5.5 = 20$ units one part
Step 3 $20$ cement, $30$ sand, $60$ gravel 1, 1.5 and 3 parts
Step 4 $20 + 30 + 60 = 110$ the check
Answer $20$ units of cement, $30$ of sand and $60$ of gravel
Example 6

The three angles of a triangle are in the ratio $1 : 3 : 5$. Find them.

Here the whole is not given by the question but by geometry: the angles of a triangle add to $180^\circ$, and Class 7 established why.

Solution.

Step 1 $1 + 3 + 5 = 9$ total parts
Step 2 one part is $20^\circ$ $180^\circ$ in nine parts
Step 3 $20^\circ$, $60^\circ$ and $100^\circ$ 1, 3 and 5 parts
Answer The angles are $20^\circ$, $60^\circ$ and $100^\circ$

Every triangle with angles in this ratio has these three angles, whatever its size. Sides behave differently — a triangle with sides in the ratio $3 : 4 : 5$ can be drawn at any scale, and those are not all the same triangle.
