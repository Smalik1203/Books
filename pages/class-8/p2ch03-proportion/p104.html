Example 1 · Single correct

If $7 : x :: 21 : 36$, what is $x$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 12
  2. 108
  3. 15
  4. 9

Solution.

Step 1In a proportion the product of the extremes equals the product of the means: $7 \times 36 = 21 \times x$.
Step 2$252 = 21x$, so $x = 12$.
Answer(a) 12
Example 2 · Single correct

Nine notebooks cost ₹315. What do fourteen cost?

Choose one correct option.

  1. ₹450
  2. ₹490
  3. ₹520
  4. ₹441

Solution.

Step 1One notebook costs $315 \div 9 = 35$ rupees.
Step 2Fourteen cost $14 \times 35 = 490$ rupees.
Answer(b) ₹490
Example 3 · Single correct

₹3000 is divided among three friends in the ratio $3 : 5 : 7$. What is the largest share?

Choose one correct option.

  1. ₹1000
  2. ₹1200
  3. ₹1400
  4. ₹1500

Solution.

Step 1There are $3 + 5 + 7 = 15$ parts, so one part is $3000 \div 15 = 200$ rupees.
Step 2The largest share is $7 \times 200 = 1400$ rupees.
Answer(c) ₹1400
