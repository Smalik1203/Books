The $\pi$ cancelled before it was ever written down, which is worth remembering: when two quantities are compared by a ratio, any factor they both carry can be left out of the working.

Single correct
Example 1

₹3000 is divided among three friends in the ratio $3 : 5 : 7$. What is the largest share?

  1. ₹1000
  2. ₹1200
  3. ₹1400
  4. ₹1500

Solution.

Step 1There are $3 + 5 + 7 = 15$ parts, so one part is $3000 \div 15 = 200$ rupees.
Step 2The largest share is $7 \times 200 = 1400$ rupees.
Step 3The other two shares are $3 \times 200 = 600$ and $5 \times 200 = 1000$ rupees.
Check$600 + 1000 + 1400 = 3000$, so the three shares make up the whole of the sum.
Answer(c) ₹1400
Example 2

Asha can paint a wall in 10 hours and Bina in 15 hours. How long do the two of them take together?

  1. 12.5 hours
  2. 6 hours
  3. 25 hours
  4. 5 hours

Solution.

Step 1In one hour Asha paints $\frac{1}{10}$ of the wall and Bina $\frac{1}{15}$: together $\frac{3}{30} + \frac{2}{30} = \frac{1}{6}$ of the wall.
Step 2So the whole wall takes 6 hours.
Answer(b) 6 hours
