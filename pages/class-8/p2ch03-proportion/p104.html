Taps that were shut off

Six taps became four, and the tank did not change size: inverse. The only care needed is with the number four, which the question never states.

the constant $6 \times 40 = 240$ tap-minutes
so $4 \times y = 240$, giving $y = 60$ minutes

An hour. Reading two are shut off as two are left would have given three hundred and twenty minutes, an answer that is wrong by more than arithmetic.

A ratio that changes when you add

This one is not a proportion question at all until it is set up. Ratios do not survive addition — adding the same number to both terms of $5 : 7$ gives something else — so the two numbers have to be named before anything can be done.

let them be $5x$ and $7x$
the condition $\dfrac{5x+6}{7x+6} = \dfrac34$
cross-multiply $20x + 24 = 21x + 18$, so $x = 6$

The numbers are $30$ and $42$, and the check is quick: $36 : 48$ is $3 : 4$. Writing the pair as $5x$ and $7x$ is what made a ratio into an equation.

Two wheels over the same road

A bigger wheel covers more ground per turn, so over a fixed distance it turns fewer times: inverse. And what matters is not the radius but the circumference — except that the two are themselves in direct proportion, since every circumference is $2\pi$ times its radius. So the radii may be used as they stand.

inverse $35 \times 60 = 21 \times y$
so $y = \dfrac{2100}{21} = 100$ turns

The $\pi$ cancelled before it was ever written down, which is worth remembering: when two quantities are compared by a ratio, any factor they both carry can be left out of the working.
