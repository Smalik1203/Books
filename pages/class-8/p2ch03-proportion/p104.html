Type 1 · Proportional ratios and the missing term

Example 1

Are $6 : 15$ and $14 : 35$ proportional?

Solution.

Step 1 $6 \times 35 = 210$ first times fourth
Step 2 $15 \times 14 = 210$ second times third
Answer Yes. The products agree, and both ratios reduce to $2 : 5$
Example 2

Find $x$ if $7 : x :: 21 : 36$.

Solution.

Step 1 $7 \times 36 = 21 \times x$ $ad = bc$
Step 2 $252 = 21x$
Step 3 $x = 252 \div 21 = 12$ divide by 21
Answer $x = 12$

Type 2 · Direct proportion through the value of one

Example 3

Nine notebooks cost ₹315. What do fourteen cost?

Solution.

Step 1 more notebooks, more money direct
Step 2 $315 \div 9 = 35$ the cost of one
Step 3 $35 \times 14 = 490$ fourteen of them
Answer ₹490
