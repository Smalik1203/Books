1. Assertion (A): If $y$ is in direct proportion to $x$ and $y = 12$ when $x = 3$, then $y = 20$ when $x = 5$.

    Reason (R): When $y$ is in direct proportion to $x$, the quotient $y \div x$ stays the same.

    CurdMango pulpSugar
