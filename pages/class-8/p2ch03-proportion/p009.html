3.6A Slice of the Pie

Seventy-two students of a school each named their favourite game. Cricket was named by $24$ students, kabaddi by $18$, football by $12$, badminton by $12$ and chess by $6$. A table shows this clearly. A picture can show at a glance which game was named most often, and roughly what share of the students named it.

A pie chart is a circle cut into slices, one for each part. The angle of each slice is proportional to the part it stands for. So the question is how to divide $360^\circ$ in the ratio $24 : 18 : 12 : 12 : 6$. This is the arithmetic of Section 3.4, with a whole that is an angle.

Reducing the ratio first makes the numbers smaller. All five terms can be divided by $6$, so the ratio is $4 : 3 : 2 : 2 : 1$, and these add to $12$. One part is therefore $360^\circ \div 12 = 30^\circ$.
