3.7The Other Kind

Suresh cycles from his village to the town market at $12$ km/h, and it takes him $2$ hours. On a scooter at $36$ km/h, how long does it take?

Write it as a proportion in the usual way, $12 : 36 :: 2 : x$, and the answer comes out as $6$ hours. That is plainly wrong. Going three times as fast cannot take three times as long. Something about this pair of quantities is different, and we need to see exactly what.
