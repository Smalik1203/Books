Example 2

Which of $x^2 - \dfrac{3}{x}$,   $\tfrac12 y^3 + y$,   $\sqrt{m} + 4$   and   $9$ are polynomials? Give the degree of each one that is.

Solution.

Step 1 $x^2 - \dfrac{3}{x}$ has $x$ in a denominator not a polynomial
Step 2 $\tfrac12 y^3 + y$ has powers $3$ and $1$ a fraction may be a coefficient
Step 3 $\sqrt{m} + 4$ has $m$ under a root not a polynomial
Step 4 $9 = 9x^0$ a constant
Answer $\tfrac12 y^3 + y$, of degree $3$, and $9$, of degree $0$

Example 3

Add $2p^2 - 3pq + q^2$,   $-p^2 + 5pq$   and   $4pq - 3q^2$.

Solution.

Step 1 $2p^2 - p^2 = p^2$ the $p^2$ terms
Step 2 $-3pq + 5pq + 4pq = 6pq$ the $pq$ terms
Step 3 $q^2 - 3q^2 = -2q^2$ the $q^2$ terms
Answer $p^2 + 6pq - 2q^2$

Check at $p = q = 1$: the three expressions are $0$, $4$ and $1$, which add to $5$, and $1 + 6 - 2 = 5$.

Example 4

By how much is $2a^2 + ab - 4$ less than $5a^2 - 2ab + 3$?

Solution.

Step 1 $(5a^2 - 2ab + 3) - (2a^2 + ab - 4)$ larger minus smaller
Step 2 $5a^2 - 2ab + 3 - 2a^2 - ab + 4$ every sign changes
Answer $3a^2 - 3ab + 7$
