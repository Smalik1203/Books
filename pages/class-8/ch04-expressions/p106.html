Type 3 · Multiplying by a single term

Example 5

Simplify $3x(2x - y) - 2y(x - 4y) + xy$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3x(2x - y) = 6x^2 - 3xy$
Step 2 $-2y(x - 4y) = -2xy + 8y^2$ every sign reversed
Step 3 $6x^2 - 3xy - 2xy + 8y^2 + xy$ three $xy$ terms
Answer $6x^2 - 4xy + 8y^2$

Type 4 · Multiplying two brackets

Example 6

Expand $(3a - 2b)(a + 4b)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3a^2 + 12ab - 2ab - 8b^2$ the four products
Answer $3a^2 + 10ab - 8b^2$

Check at $a = b = 1$: the brackets are $1$ and $5$, and $3 + 10 - 8 = 5$.

Example 7

Expand $(x + 2)(x^2 - 3x + 1)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $2 \times 3 = 6$ products count them first
Step 2 $x^3 - 3x^2 + x + 2x^2 - 6x + 2$ $x$, then $2$, times each
Step 3 $-3x^2 + 2x^2 = -x^2$ and $x - 6x = -5x$ gather like terms
Answer $x^3 - x^2 - 5x + 2$
