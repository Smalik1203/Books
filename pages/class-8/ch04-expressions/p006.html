Taking away a parcel of two things takes away both of them. If you owe ₹$b$ and ₹$c$ and clear the debt, both go. So the minus sign in front of a bracket reverses the sign of everything inside it, not merely of the first thing it happens to meet.

Example 2

Add $3x^2 - 5x + 2$ and $x^2 + 7x - 9$.

write out $(3x^2 - 5x + 2) + (x^2 + 7x - 9)$
gather $x^2$ $3x^2 + x^2 = 4x^2$
gather $x$ $-5x + 7x = 2x$
gather units $2 - 9 = -7$
answer $4x^2 + 2x - 7$

Nothing there needed a rule beyond the one about pencils. Subtraction is the same job with one extra step at the start, and the step is where the marks are lost.

Example 3

Subtract $4ab - 3b^2 + 5$ from $7ab + b^2 - 2$.

From tells you which way round: the second expression is the one being reduced.

write out $(7ab + b^2 - 2) - (4ab - 3b^2 + 5)$
change all three signs $7ab + b^2 - 2 - 4ab + 3b^2 - 5$
gather $3ab + 4b^2 - 7$

The $-3b^2$ became $+3b^2$ and the $+5$ became $-5$. A subtraction in which only the first sign changes is the error to watch for; checking at $a = b = 1$ catches it at once, since $7+1-2 = 6$ and $4-3+5 = 6$, so the answer must come to $0$ — and $3 + 4 - 7$ does.
