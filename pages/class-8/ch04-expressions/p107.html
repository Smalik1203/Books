Type 5 · Expanding with the three identities

Example 8

Expand $\left(2m - \tfrac12\right)^2$.

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 2m$,   $b = \tfrac12$ square of a difference
Step 2 $a^2 = 4m^2$,   $2ab = 2 \times 2m \times \tfrac12 = 2m$,   $b^2 = \tfrac14$
Answer $4m^2 - 2m + \tfrac14$
Example 9

Expand $(x^2 + 3)(x^2 - 3)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $a = x^2$,   $b = 3$ difference of squares
Step 2 $a^2 = x^2 \times x^2 = x^4$ add the exponents
Answer $x^4 - 9$

A term that is itself a power is still one term. Here $a$ is $x^2$, so $a^2$ is a fourth power.

Type 6 · Reading an identity backwards

Example 10

Write $9a^2 - 30ab + 25b^2$ as a square, and $81 - 4y^2$ as a product of two brackets.

Solution.

Step 1 $9a^2 = (3a)^2$ and $25b^2 = (5b)^2$ two squares at the ends
Step 2 $2 \times 3a \times 5b = 30ab$ the middle term fits
Step 3 $9a^2 - 30ab + 25b^2 = (3a - 5b)^2$ the middle sign is minus
Step 4 $81 - 4y^2 = 9^2 - (2y)^2$ difference of squares
Answer $(3a - 5b)^2$ and $(9 + 2y)(9 - 2y)$
