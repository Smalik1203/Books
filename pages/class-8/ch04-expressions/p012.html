Negative terms need no new rule, only care. A bracket like $(x - 3)$ has second term $-3$, and $-3$ is what multiplies.

Example 7

Expand $(2m - 5)(3m + 4)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $6m^2 + 8m - 15m - 20$ the four products
Answer $6m^2 - 7m - 20$

Check it at $m = 1$: the brackets are $-3$ and $7$, whose product is $-21$; and $6 - 7 - 20 = -21$. A single value cannot prove an expansion right, but it catches almost every arithmetic slip, and it costs one line.

Where a bracket has three terms, count before you gather: three times two is six products, and six is what should be on the page before any of them are joined.

$(a + b + c)(a - b)$ $= a^2 - ab + ab - b^2 + ac - bc$
gather $= a^2 - b^2 + ac - bc$

Notice that the $-ab$ and the $+ab$ cancelled. That is not luck: the two middle products of $(a+b)(a-b)$ always cancel, and § 4.6.3 turns that into a result worth having on its own.
