Exercise Set 4.1
  1. Write down the terms of each expression, each with its own sign.
    1. $4p - 7q$
    2. $m^2 - mn + n^2$
    3. $-3t^2 + t - 8$
    4. $ab + bc + ca$
  1. Give the coefficient of $x$ in each.
    1. $5x + 2$
    2. $7 - x$
    3. $3yx$
    4. $x^2 + x$
  1. Classify each as a monomial, a binomial or a trinomial.
    1. $9pq$
    2. $a + b - c$
    3. $2x^2 - 5$
    4. $-t^3$
  1. Which of these are polynomials? For each one that is not, say which rule it breaks.
    1. $x^3 - 2x$
    2. $\dfrac{4}{y} + y$
    3. $6$
    4. $\sqrt{n} + n^2$
  1. State the degree of each expression.
    1. $7ab$
    2. $x^2y + xy^2$
    3. $5 - 2m$
    4. $-11$
  1. Write an expression for each. A number is $n$.
    1. Seven more than twice the number.
    2. The number squared, less five.
    3. Half the number, added to a third of it.
  1. A term has coefficient $-6$ and degree $4$, and uses only the letters $x$ and $y$. Write down three different terms it could be.
  1. Somebody says that $2x$ and $x^2$ are two ways of writing the same thing. Show that they are not, by choosing a value of $x$ for which they differ — and then find the two values of $x$ for which they do agree.
