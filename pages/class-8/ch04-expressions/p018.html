4.7Putting Them to Work

An identity is true for every value of its letters, and numbers are values. So the three results are arithmetic as well as algebra, and they are quicker than the arithmetic you would otherwise do — provided you can see the sum or the difference hiding in the numbers.

The trick, and it is the only one, is to write each number as a round number plus or minus a small one.

Example 9

Find $102^2$, $97^2$ and $103 \times 97$ without a written multiplication.

$102^2$ $= (100+2)^2 = 10000 + 400 + 4 = 10404$
$97^2$ $= (100-3)^2 = 10000 - 600 + 9 = 9409$
$103 \times 97$ $= (100+3)(100-3) = 10000 - 9 = 9991$

The third is the striking one. Two three-figure numbers multiplied, and the whole calculation is $10000 - 9$ — because they happen to sit the same distance either side of $100$.
