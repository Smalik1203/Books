Why the other options are wrong

1 The sign in front of a term belongs to it, so the coefficient is $-5$. (a) drops the sign; (c) and (d) take the number to be $1$ because $x$ and $y$ carry none.
2 $ab^4$ has degree $1 + 4 = 5$, the largest. (a) and (b) count one power of one term; (d) adds the constant $9$, which has degree $0$.
3 $zy$ and $yz$ are the same product, so (d) is a like pair. In (a) the powers sit on different letters; in (b) and (c) the letter parts differ.
5 $-2a \times (-5) = +10a$. (a) keeps the minus; (b) leaves the $a$ off the second product; (d) changes the wrong sign.
