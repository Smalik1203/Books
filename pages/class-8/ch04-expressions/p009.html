Doing this is called expanding, and the brackets are said to have been removed. It is worth being clear that they have not been ignored: every term inside has been used, and the bracket has been paid for term by term.

Example 4

Expand $4x(2x^2 - 3x + 5)$.

Three terms inside, so three multiplications.

$4x \times 2x^2$ $= 8x^3$
$4x \times (-3x)$ $= -12x^2$
$4x \times 5$ $= 20x$
answer $8x^3 - 12x^2 + 20x$

Every term of the answer has degree one more than the term it came from, because $4x$ has degree $1$.

When the term outside is negative, every sign inside is reversed — which is the rule about subtracting a bracket, arrived at a second time and from a different direction.

Example 5

Simplify $2a(a - 3b) - 5b(2a - b)$.

Expand each bracket first, then gather.

first $2a(a - 3b) = 2a^2 - 6ab$
second $-5b(2a - b) = -10ab + 5b^2$
gather $2a^2 - 6ab - 10ab + 5b^2$
answer $2a^2 - 16ab + 5b^2$

The $-5b$ turned the $-b$ inside into $+5b^2$. Two negatives multiplied give a positive here for the same reason they do in arithmetic.
