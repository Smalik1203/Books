Type 7 · The identities as arithmetic

Example 11

Find $59^2$ and $7.9^2$ without a written multiplication.

Solution.

Step 1 $59^2 = (60 - 1)^2 = 3600 - 120 + 1$ square of a difference
Step 2 $7.9^2 = (8 - 0.1)^2 = 64 - 1.6 + 0.01$ the same shape
Answer $59^2 = 3481$ and $7.9^2 = 62.41$
Example 12

Find $64^2 - 36^2$. Then use the same idea to find $6.4^2 - 3.6^2$.

Solution.

Step 1 $64^2 - 36^2 = (64 + 36)(64 - 36) = 100 \times 28$ difference of squares
Step 2 $6.4^2 - 3.6^2 = (6.4 + 3.6)(6.4 - 3.6) = 10 \times 2.8$
Answer $64^2 - 36^2 = 2800$ and $6.4^2 - 3.6^2 = 28$

Type 8 · A product of the form $(x + a)(x + b)$

Example 13

Use $(x + a)(x + b) = x^2 + (a + b)x + ab$ to write down $(y - 7)(y + 3)$ and $(2t + 5)(2t - 1)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $a = -7$, $b = 3$:   $a + b = -4$,   $ab = -21$
Step 2 $2t$ in place of $x$, and $a = 5$, $b = -1$:   $a + b = 4$,   $ab = -5$
Step 3 $(2t)^2 = 4t^2$ and $4 \times 2t = 8t$ the whole term $2t$
Answer $y^2 - 4y - 21$ and $4t^2 + 8t - 5$
