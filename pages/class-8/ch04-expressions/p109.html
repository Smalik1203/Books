Type 9 · A value without finding the letters

Example 14

If $x + y = 8$ and $x^2 + y^2 = 34$, find $xy$ and $(x - y)^2$.

Solution.

Step 1 $(x + y)^2 = x^2 + y^2 + 2xy$ square of a sum
Step 2 $64 = 34 + 2xy$, so $2xy = 30$ put in the values
Step 3 $(x - y)^2 = x^2 + y^2 - 2xy = 34 - 30$ square of a difference
Answer $xy = 15$ and $(x - y)^2 = 4$
Example 15

Two positive numbers $a$ and $b$ have $a - b = 3$ and $ab = 28$. Find $a^2 + b^2$ and $a + b$.

Solution.

Step 1 $a^2 + b^2 = (a - b)^2 + 2ab = 9 + 56 = 65$ square of a difference
Step 2 $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + b^2 + 2ab = 65 + 56 = 121$ square of a sum
Step 3 $121 = 11^2$ $a + b$ is positive
Answer $a^2 + b^2 = 65$ and $a + b = 11$

The numbers are $7$ and $4$, but neither was needed.

Type 10 · An identity or an equation?

Example 16

Say whether each is an identity or an equation. (a) $(x - 2)^2 = x^2 - 4x + 4$   (b) $(x - 2)(x + 2) = x^2 - 4x + 4$

Solution.

Step 1 (a) at $x = 0$, $1$ and $5$ the two sides give $4$ and $4$, $1$ and $1$, $9$ and $9$ true each time
Step 2 (a) is the square of a difference, with $a = x$ and $b = 2$ true for every $x$
Step 3 (b) at $x = 0$ the two sides give $-4$ and $4$ one failure is enough
Step 4 (b) at $x = 2$ the two sides give $0$ and $0$ true for this value
Answer (a) is an identity; (b) is an equation, true only when $x = 2$

The left side of (b) is $x^2 - 4$, so (b) says $-4 = -4x + 4$, which holds only for $x = 2$.
