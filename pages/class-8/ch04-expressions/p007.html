Exercise Set 4.2
  1. Pick out the pairs of like terms.
    1. $6m$, $-m$
    2. $2p^2$, $2p$
    3. $xy^2$, $y^2x$
    4. $5a^2b$, $5ab^2$
  1. Simplify by gathering like terms.
    1. $8y - 3y + y$
    2. $4ab + 2ba$
    3. $m^2 + 5m - 2m^2$
    4. $7 - 3t + 4 + t$
  1. Add.
    1. $5x + 3y$ and $2x - 8y$
    2. $a^2 - 4a + 1$ and $3a^2 + 4a - 6$
    3. $pq + qr + rp$ and $2pq - qr$
  1. Subtract.
    1. $3n - 7$ from $9n + 2$
    2. $x^2 - x + 5$ from $2x^2 + 3x - 1$
    3. $6ab - b^2$ from $ab + 4b^2$
  1. Remove the brackets and simplify.
    1. $(2a + b) - (a - b)$
    2. $5 - (t - 3)$
    3. $-(x + y) - (x - y)$
    4. $(m - n) - (n - m)$
  1. What must be added to $3x^2 - 2x + 4$ to give $5x^2 + x - 1$?
  1. A triangle has sides $2x + 1$, $3x - 4$ and $x + 9$ centimetres. Write its perimeter in simplest form, and find it when $x = 5$.
  1. Rohan writes $9y - (4y - 2) = 5y - 2$. Find his mistake, give the correct answer, and show that a value of $y$ of your own choosing tells the two answers apart.
  1. Two expressions add to $7a - 3b$, and one of them is $4a + b$. Find the other. Then find what must be subtracted from $4a + b$ to leave $7a - 3b$. Your two answers differ only in sign — say why they must.
