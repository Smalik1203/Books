One case of this deserves its own mention, because it answers a question that sounds harder than it is: by how much does a product grow when one of its two numbers goes up by one? Writing it out settles it in a line.

one number up by 1 $a(b + 1) = ab + a$
both up by 1 $(a+1)(b+1) = ab + a + b + 1$

A product grows by the other number. So $23 \times 28$ is $23 \times 27$ with another $23$ added, and $24 \times 28$ is $23 \times 27$ plus $23$ plus $27$ plus $1$ — which is worth checking on the two products themselves, since $621 + 23 + 27 + 1$ is $672$.
