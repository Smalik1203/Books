Solved Examples

Type 1 · Checking a solution

Example 1

Is $x = -3$ a solution of $4x + 7 = x - 2$?

Solution.

Step 1 left side: $4(-3) + 7 = -12 + 7 = -5$ put in $x = -3$
Step 2 right side: $-3 - 2 = -5$
Answer Yes. Both sides come to $-5$
Example 2

Which of $x = 2$ and $x = 5$ is a solution of $3(x - 1) = 2x + 2$?

Solution.

Step 1 $x = 2$:   $3(2 - 1) = 3$,   $2(2) + 2 = 6$ the sides differ
Step 2 $x = 5$:   $3(5 - 1) = 12$,   $2(5) + 2 = 12$ the sides agree
Answer $x = 5$ is the solution; $x = 2$ is not

Type 2 · The letter on one side

Example 3

Solve $6 - 5x = 21$.

Solution.

Step 1 $-5x = 15$ take 6 from both
Step 2 $x = -3$ $\div (-5)$ both sides
Check $6 - 5(-3) = 6 + 15 = 21$ true
Answer $x = -3$
