Example 2

Which of $x = 2$ and $x = 5$ is a solution of $3(x - 1) = 2x + 2$?

Solution.

Step 1 $x = 2$:   $3(2 - 1) = 3$,   $2(2) + 2 = 6$ the sides differ
Step 2 $x = 5$:   $3(5 - 1) = 12$,   $2(5) + 2 = 12$ the sides agree
Answer $x = 5$ is the solution; $x = 2$ is not

Example 3

Solve $6 - 5x = 21$.

Solution.

Step 1 $-5x = 15$ take 6 from both
Step 2 $x = -3$ $\div (-5)$ both sides
Check $6 - 5(-3) = 6 + 15 = 21$ true
Answer $x = -3$
Example 4

Solve $8x + 3 = 7$.

Solution.

Step 1 $8x = 4$ take 3 from both
Step 2 $x = \dfrac{4}{8} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ divide both by 8
Check $8 \times \dfrac{1}{2} + 3 = 4 + 3 = 7$ true
Answer $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$
