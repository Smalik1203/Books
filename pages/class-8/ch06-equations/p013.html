Example 12

A rectangle is $5$ cm longer than it is wide, and its perimeter is $46$ cm. Find its sides.

Solution.

Step 1 $w$ = the width in centimetres name the unknown
Step 2 length $w + 5$
Step 3 $2\big(w + (w+5)\big) = 46$ the perimeter
Step 4 $2(2w + 5) = 46$,   $4w + 10 = 46$ simplify
Step 5 $w = 9$ solve
Answer The rectangle is $9$ cm by $14$ cm

Its perimeter is $2(9+14) = 46$. Answering with $w = 9$ alone would not have answered the question, which asked for both sides.
