Type 3 · Letters on both sides

Example 5

Solve $9x - 7 = 4x + 13$.

Solution.

Step 1 $5x - 7 = 13$ take $4x$ from both
Step 2 $5x = 20$ add 7 to both
Step 3 $x = 4$ divide both by 5
Check $9(4) - 7 = 29$ and $4(4) + 13 = 29$ true
Answer $x = 4$
Example 6

Solve $3 - 2x = 15 - 6x$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3 + 4x = 15$ add $6x$ to both
Step 2 $4x = 12$ take 3 from both
Step 3 $x = 3$ divide both by 4
Check $3 - 2(3) = -3$ and $15 - 6(3) = -3$ true
Answer $x = 3$

The left side has $-2x$ and the right $-6x$, so the left has more $x$s. Adding $6x$ gathers them there with a positive coefficient.

Type 4 · Brackets

Example 7

Solve $4(2x - 3) = 3(x + 6)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $8x - 12 = 3x + 18$ expand both
Step 2 $5x - 12 = 18$ take $3x$ from both
Step 3 $5x = 30$,   $x = 6$ add 12, divide by 5
Check $4(9) = 36$ and $3(12) = 36$ true
Answer $x = 6$
