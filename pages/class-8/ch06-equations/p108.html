Example 11

Solve $\dfrac{x - 2}{3} + 1 = \dfrac{x}{2}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $2(x - 2) + 6 = 3x$ $\times 6$, the $1$ too
Step 2 $2x + 2 = 3x$ expand and gather
Step 3 $x = 2$ take $2x$ from both
Check $0 + 1 = 1$ and $\dfrac{2}{2} = 1$ true
Answer $x = 2$

Example 12

The sum of three consecutive odd numbers is $63$. Find them.

Solution.

Step 1 $n$ = the smallest; the others are $n + 2$ and $n + 4$ odd numbers go up in twos
Step 2 $n + (n + 2) + (n + 4) = 63$ the sentence
Step 3 $3n + 6 = 63$,   $3n = 57$,   $n = 19$
Check $19 + 21 + 23 = 63$
Answer 19, 21 and 23
Example 13

A mother is $26$ years older than her son. In $4$ years she will be three times as old as he will be. Find their ages now.

Solution.

Step 1 $x$ = the son's age now, in years; the mother is $x + 26$ name the unknown
Step 2 in 4 years: son $x + 4$, mother $x + 30$ both get older
Step 3 $x + 30 = 3(x + 4)$ the sentence
Step 4 $x + 30 = 3x + 12$,   $18 = 2x$,   $x = 9$
Check in 4 years: 13 and 39, and $39 = 3 \times 13$
Answer The son is 9 and the mother is 35
