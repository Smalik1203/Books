Example 10

Solve $\dfrac{3x + 1}{4} = \dfrac{x + 5}{2}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3x + 1 = 2(x + 5)$ $\times 4$, keep the bracket
Step 2 $3x + 1 = 2x + 10$ expand
Step 3 $x = 9$ take $2x$ and 1
Check $\dfrac{28}{4} = 7$ and $\dfrac{14}{2} = 7$ true
Answer $x = 9$
Example 11

Solve $\dfrac{x - 2}{3} + 1 = \dfrac{x}{2}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $2(x - 2) + 6 = 3x$ $\times 6$, the $1$ too
Step 2 $2x + 2 = 3x$ expand and gather
Step 3 $x = 2$ take $2x$ from both
Check $0 + 1 = 1$ and $\dfrac{2}{2} = 1$ true
Answer $x = 2$

Example 12

The sum of three consecutive odd numbers is $63$. Find them.

Solution.

Step 1 $n$ = the smallest; the others are $n + 2$ and $n + 4$ odd numbers go up in twos
Step 2 $n + (n + 2) + (n + 4) = 63$ the sentence
Step 3 $3n + 6 = 63$,   $3n = 57$,   $n = 19$
Check $19 + 21 + 23 = 63$
Answer 19, 21 and 23
