6.3The Same to Both Sides

Trying whole numbers found the answers in the last exercise, and it will keep working for as long as the answers stay small and whole. It fails the moment they do not, and the solution of $7x + 4 = 25$ is not a number anybody guesses. What is needed is a method that walks to the answer instead of hunting for it.

The method is the balance. An equation says the two sides are equal; any operation carried out on both sides leaves them equal, and so leaves the solution untouched.

Four operations are allowed, and they are the four you would expect. Only one carries a condition.

add the same number to both sides
subtract the same number from both sides
multiply both sides by the same number
divide both sides by the same number, not zero

Dividing by zero is not allowed, here or anywhere else, and the reason matters here in particular: multiplying both sides by zero turns every equation into $0 = 0$, which is true and tells you nothing at all. An operation that throws the question away is not a step towards its answer.

The aim of every step is the same — get the letter by itself on one side — and the way to choose a step is to look at what is standing in the way and undo it.
