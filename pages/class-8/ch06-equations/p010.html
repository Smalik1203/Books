Where a whole numerator is a bracketed expression, keep the bracket while you multiply and expand afterwards. The fraction bar acts as a bracket whether one is written or not, and half of the errors in this section come from forgetting that.

Example 9

Solve $\dfrac{2x - 1}{3} = \dfrac{x + 4}{5}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $5(2x - 1) = 3(x + 4)$ $\times 15$ both sides
Step 2 $10x - 5 = 3x + 12$ expand
Step 3 $7x = 17$ gather
Answer $x = \dfrac{17}{7}$

Both numerators kept their brackets across the multiplication. Without them the second line would read $10x - 1 = 3x + 4$, which is a different equation with a different answer.
