Type 4 · Brackets

Example 7

Solve $4(2x - 3) = 3(x + 6)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $8x - 12 = 3x + 18$ expand both
Step 2 $5x - 12 = 18$ take $3x$ from both
Step 3 $5x = 30$,   $x = 6$ add 12, divide by 5
Check $4(9) = 36$ and $3(12) = 36$ true
Answer $x = 6$
Example 8

Solve $6 - 3(x - 2) = 2(x + 1)$.

Solution.

Step 1 $6 - 3x + 6 = 2x + 2$ the minus reverses both signs
Step 2 $12 - 3x = 2x + 2$ gather the numbers
Step 3 $10 = 5x$ add $3x$, take 2
Step 4 $x = 2$ divide both by 5
Check $6 - 3(0) = 6$ and $2(3) = 6$ true
Answer $x = 2$

Type 5 · Fractions

Example 9

Solve $\dfrac{x}{4} - \dfrac{x}{6} = 3$.

Solution.

Step 1 the lowest common multiple of 4 and 6 is 12
Step 2 $3x - 2x = 36$ $\times 12$ every term
Step 3 $x = 36$ gather
Check $\dfrac{36}{4} - \dfrac{36}{6} = 9 - 6 = 3$ true
Answer $x = 36$
