Example 14

A show sells adult tickets at ₹$50$ and child tickets at ₹$30$. It sells $12$ more child tickets than adult tickets and takes ₹$1160$. How many of each did it sell?

Solution.

Step 1 $a$ = the number of adult tickets; child tickets $a + 12$ one letter is enough
Step 2 $50a + 30(a + 12) = 1160$ the money taken
Step 3 $80a + 360 = 1160$,   $80a = 800$,   $a = 10$
Check $50 \times 10 + 30 \times 22 = 500 + 660 = 1160$
Answer 10 adult tickets and 22 child tickets
Example 15

The angles of a triangle are $x$, $2x$ and $3x - 30$ degrees. Find them.

Solution.

Step 1 $x + 2x + (3x - 30) = 180$ angle sum of a triangle
Step 2 $6x - 30 = 180$,   $6x = 210$,   $x = 35$
Check $35 + 70 + 75 = 180$
Answer $35^\circ$, $70^\circ$ and $75^\circ$
