Think and Reflect

Find a value of $k$ for which $3x + 7 = 3x + k$ has no solution, and the one value of $k$ for which it has every number as a solution.

Now try $kx + 7 = 3x + 7$. This time there is one value of $k$ that makes every number a solution — and for every other $k$ there is exactly one solution. What is it?

These two cases are not a curiosity got up for a textbook. When two prices, two journeys or two plans are compared by setting their expressions equal, no solution means the two never meet and every solution means they were the same plan under two names — and both of those are answers a person might actually want.
