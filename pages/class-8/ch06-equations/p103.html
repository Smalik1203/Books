Five numbers, and only the middle one matters

Name the middle number rather than the smallest, and the five become $n-2$, $n-1$, $n$, $n+1$, $n+2$. Their total is $5n$, because the twos and the ones cancel in pairs.

average $\dfrac{5n}{5} = n = 23$

So the numbers are $21, 22, 23, 24, 25$. The average of a run of consecutive numbers is just its middle number, because everything on one side of the middle is cancelled by something the same distance on the other.

Thirty coins, one letter

Two kinds of coin, and the temptation is two letters. The thirty is a total, so once the five-rupee coins are named the others are counted for you.

let $x$ = the number of five-rupee coins
then $30 - x$ two-rupee coins
value $5x + 2(30 - x) = 99$
expand $5x + 60 - 2x = 99$
solve $3x = 39$,   $x = 13$

Thirteen fives and seventeen twos: $65 + 34 = 99$, and thirty coins in all. Both given facts were used, a count and a value, each in a different place.

A number and its reversal

A two-digit number is not its digits written side by side but ten times the tens digit plus the units digit. Getting that line right is the whole question.

let $t$ = tens digit, so units digit $= 9 - t$
the number $10t + (9 - t) = 9t + 9$
reversed $10(9 - t) + t = 90 - 9t$
the sentence $90 - 9t = 9t + 9 + 27$
solve $54 = 18t$,   $t = 3$

The number is $36$; reversed it is $63$, which is $27$ more. The digit sum let one letter do the work of two, as the count of coins did before.
