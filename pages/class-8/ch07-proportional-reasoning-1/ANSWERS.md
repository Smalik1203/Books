## 7.1 Observing Similarity in Change

### Think and Reflect

1. P is $80 : 60$, which is $4 : 3$. A copy $20$ mm wide must be $15$ mm
   tall, since $4 : 3 :: 20 : 15$.
2. Taking the same amount from both measurements does not keep the shape.
   From P to R the width is multiplied by $\frac{3}{4}$ but the height by
   $\frac{2}{3}$, so the factors differ.

## 7.2 Ratios

### Think and Reflect

1. Girls to boys is $18 : 16$, which is $9 : 8$. Boys to girls is
   $16 : 18$, which is $8 : 9$. They are **not** the same ratio: the order of
   the terms matters.
2. *Answers will vary with the measuring.* The printed page is $196$ mm by
   $276$ mm, a ratio of $49 : 69$.

## 7.3 Ratios in their Simplest Form

### Think and Reflect

1. Turned on its side, T is $60$ mm wide and $45$ mm high: $60 : 45$ is
   $4 : 3$, the shape of P, with the boat lying on its side.
2. **No.** $4 \times 4 = 16$ but $3 \times 3 = 9$. The simplest forms
   $4 : 3$ and $3 : 4$ are different.

## 7.5 Sharing, but Not Equally!

### Think and Reflect

1. $30$ in the ratio $1 : 2$ gives $10$ and $20$; in the ratio $2 : 1$ it
   gives $20$ and $10$. The two parts change places; the size of one group,
   $10$, stays the same.
2. The difference is $5 - 3 = 2$ parts, which is ₹$40$, so one part is ₹$20$.
   The whole is $8 \times 20 = $ **₹$160$** (₹$100$ and ₹$60$).

### Exercise Set 7.3

1. $3600 \div 12 = 300$: **₹$1{,}500$ and ₹$2{,}100$**.
2. $400 \div 8 = 50$: **$50$ mL of cleaner and $350$ mL of water**.
3. $60 \div 5 = 12$: **$24$ mL of red and $36$ mL of white**. With $12$ mL

5. $36 \div 9 = 4$: $16$ boys and $20$ girls. Afterwards $20$ boys and $16$
