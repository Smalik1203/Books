Now suppose you want to share $42$ counters between the two of you in the ratio $4 : 3$. How will you do it? Taking counters round by round would take a long time. There is a quicker way to divide a whole into parts in a given ratio.

Divide the $42$ counters into equal groups, so that your partner gets $4$ groups and you get $3$ groups. What is the size of each group? There are $4 + 3 = 7$ groups in all, so each group holds $42 \div 7 = 6$ counters.

Now multiply the number of groups by the size of a group. Your partner gets $4 \times 6 = 24$ counters and you get $3 \times 6 = 18$ counters, when $42$ counters are shared in the ratio $4 : 3$.

In general, to divide a quantity $x$ in the ratio $m : n$, we do this.

Step 1 split $x$ into equal groups, so that the first part has $m$ groups and the second part has $n$ groups
Step 2 there are $m + n$ groups, so each group is $\dfrac{x}{m + n}$
Step 3 the first part is $m \times \dfrac{x}{m + n}$ and the second part is $n \times \dfrac{x}{m + n}$
Dividing in a given ratio

To divide a quantity $x$ in the ratio $m : n$, add the terms. The two parts are $m \times \frac{x}{m + n}$ and $n \times \frac{x}{m + n}$. The parts add up to $x$, and they are in the ratio $m : n$:

$m \times \dfrac{x}{m + n} : n \times \dfrac{x}{m + n} :: m : n$
