Short answer
  1. Shreya's recipe for $6$ people uses $450$ g of paneer and $150$ g of peas. Find the amounts of paneer and peas she needs for $10$ people. Also write the ratio of paneer to peas in its simplest form.
  1. Show that if $a : b :: c : d$, then $(a + b) : b :: (c + d) : d$.
  1. Nikhil finds $x$ in $3 : 8 :: x : 56$ like this.
    Step 1The second term changes from $8$ to $56$.
    Step 2It has increased by $56 - 8 = 48$.
    Step 3So the first term also increases by $48$, and $x = 3 + 48 = 51$.
    Step 4Hence $3 : 8 :: 51 : 56$.
    Identify the step in which Nikhil has made an error, if any. Correct it and find the right value of $x$.
  1. A plot of land is $60$ m long and $45$ m wide. Find its area in hectares. Also find its area in acres, correct to two decimal places. (Use $1$ hectare $= 2.471$ acres.)
