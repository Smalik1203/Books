7.1Observing Similarity in Change

We often change the size of a picture on a phone or a computer. We make it bigger to see a detail, or smaller to fit it on a page. Fig. 7.1 shows five copies of one picture, each in a frame of a different size.

Which images look alike, and which look different? Images A, C and D look alike, even though they are of different sizes. Images B and E do not look like the other three. In B the hut and the tree look stretched sideways, and in E they look squeezed, taller and thinner than in A.

Why does this happen? You may notice that A, C and D are rectangles, while E is a square. Perhaps that is why E looks different. But B is a rectangle too. Why does it look different from the other rectangles? To find a pattern, let us measure the frames.
