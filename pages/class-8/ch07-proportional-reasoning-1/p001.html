Proportional Reasoning-1

7.1Observing Similarity in Change

Aarav is making a notice for his school's Sports Day. He has drawn a sailing boat on the sea, and he wants to use the drawing in several places: on the notice, on a badge and on a banner. On the computer he can change the width and the height of the drawing. Fig. 7.1 shows five copies he made, P to T.

Which pictures look alike, and which look different? Pictures P, Q and S look alike, though their sizes are different. Pictures R and T look wrong. In R the boat looks stretched sideways. In T it looks squeezed, too tall and too thin.

Why does this happen? Every frame is a rectangle, so the shape of the frame alone does not explain it. To find the reason, Aarav measures the frames.
