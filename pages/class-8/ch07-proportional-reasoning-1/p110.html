Example 10

A tanker fills the overhead tank of a block of flats. It pumps water at a steady rate: $1{,}500$ litres every $12$ minutes. The tank holds $9{,}000$ litres.

  1. The time taken to fill the empty tank is
    1. $60$ min
    2. $72$ min
    3. $75$ min
    4. $90$ min
  2. Numerical answer. How many litres does the tanker pump in $20$ minutes?
  3. Numerical answer. On another day the tank already holds $2{,}250$ litres. How many minutes does it take to fill?

Solution.

Step 1$12 : 1500 :: t : 9000$, so $1500t = 12 \times 9000$ and $t = 72$.
Step 2$12 : 1500 :: 20 : v$, so $12v = 1500 \times 20$ and $v = 2500$.
Step 3$9000 - 2250 = 6750$ litres; $12 : 1500 :: t : 6750$ gives $t = 54$.
Answer(i) (b) $72$ min; (ii) $2500$; (iii) $54$
