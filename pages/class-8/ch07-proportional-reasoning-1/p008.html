Trairāśika — the rule of three

In Examples 2 and 7, three terms of a proportion were known and the fourth was found from the factor of change. Problems of this kind are very common: in cooking, in building, in buying and selling, and in travel. A recipe for four people is changed for ten; the cost of five metres of cloth gives the cost of eight; the time for one journey gives the time for a longer one at the same speed.
