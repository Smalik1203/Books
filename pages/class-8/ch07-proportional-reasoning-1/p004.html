Simplest form and proportion

A ratio is in its simplest form when the HCF of its terms is $1$. Two ratios are in proportion, or proportional, when they are the same in their simplest forms. We write $a : b :: c : d$, and read it a is to b as c is to d.

So $80 : 60 :: 40 : 30$ and $80 : 60 :: 100 : 75$. Ratios with the same simplest form, such as $80 : 60$, $40 : 30$ and $4 : 3$, are also called equivalent ratios.

7.4Problem Solving with Proportional Reasoning

The simplest form gives a quick test for proportion. The examples in this section use it, and then use the factor of change to find a missing term.

Example 1

Are the ratios $5 : 8$ and $65 : 104$ proportional?

Solution.

Step 1 $5 : 8$ is already in its simplest form HCF of $5$ and $8$ is $1$
Step 2 the HCF of $65$ and $104$ is $13$
Step 3 $65 \div 13 = 5$ and $104 \div 13 = 8$, giving $5 : 8$ divide by $13$
Answer Yes. Both are $5 : 8$ in simplest form, so $5 : 8 :: 65 : 104$

Look at how the terms are linked. $65 = 5 \times 13$ and $104 = 8 \times 13$: both terms of $5 : 8$ have been multiplied by the same factor, $13$. Now try $5 : 8$ and $60 : 104$. Here $60 = 5 \times 12$ but $104 = 8 \times 13$, so the factors are different. In simplest form $60 : 104$ is $15 : 26$, not $5 : 8$, and the two ratios are not proportional.
