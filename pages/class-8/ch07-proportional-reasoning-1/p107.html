Example 5

A can holds 45 litres of milk and water in the ratio $4 : 1$. How many litres of water must be added to make the ratio $3 : 2$?

Solution.

Step 1The can holds $4 + 1 = 5$ parts, so one part is $45 \div 5 = 9$ litres.
Step 2Milk is $4 \times 9 = 36$ litres and water is 9 litres.
Step 3The milk stays 36 litres, so $3 : 2 :: 36 : w$ gives $w = 24$.
Step 4The water must go from 9 to 24 litres: add $24 - 9 = 15$ litres.
CheckAfter the water is added, the can holds 36 litres of milk and 24 litres of water, and $36 : 24 = 3 : 2$, as required.
Answer15
Example 6

$\frac{3}{4}$ kg of dry fruit costs ₹510. What does 2.5 kg cost, in rupees?

Solution.

Step 1Weight to price: $\frac{3}{4} : 510 :: 2.5 : x$.
Step 2Cross multiply: $\frac{3}{4} \times x = 510 \times 2.5 = 1275$.
Step 3$x = 1275 \times \frac{4}{3} = 1700$.
CheckOne kilogram costs $510 \times \frac{4}{3} = 680$ rupees, and $2.5 \times 680 = 1700$.
Answer1700
