Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which of these unit conversions are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. 1 hectare = 2.471 acres
  2. 1 litre = 1000 cc
  3. 1 square metre = 3.281 square feet
  4. 5 hectares = 50 000 square metres

Solution.

Step 1(a) and (b) are in the table of conversions, and $5 \times 10\,000 = 50\,000$.
Step 23.281 changes metres into feet. A square metre is $3.281 \times 3.281$, about 10.764 square feet.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Rekha is 8 years old and her father is 32. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Their ages are now in the ratio $1 : 4$.
  2. In 8 years their ages will be in the ratio $1 : 4$.
  3. In 8 years their ages will be in the ratio $2 : 5$.
  4. Four years ago their ages were in the ratio $1 : 7$.

Solution.

Step 1Now: $8 : 32 = 1 : 4$. In 8 years: $16 : 40 = 2 : 5$, not $1 : 4$.
Step 2Four years ago: $4 : 28 = 1 : 7$. Adding the same number to both ages changes the ratio.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A can holds 45 litres of milk and water in the ratio $4 : 1$. How many litres of water must be added to make the ratio $3 : 2$?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Milk is $\frac{4}{5} \times 45 = 36$ litres and water is 9 litres.
Step 2The milk stays 36 litres, so $3 : 2 :: 36 : w$ gives $w = 24$. Add $24 - 9 = 15$ litres.
Answer15
