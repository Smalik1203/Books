    BatchWhite (g)Red (g)

  1. The mid-day meal kitchen of a school in Kolkata cooks khichdi. For every $60$ students the cook uses $5$ kg of rice and dal together, mixed in the ratio $3 : 2$. The table shows the number of students on three days.

    DayMondayTuesdayWednesday
    Students240300180

    Based on the above information, answer the following questions.

    1. How many kilograms of rice and dal together are needed on Monday?
    2. How much rice is there in $5$ kg of the mixture?
    3. The cook has $12$ kg of rice on Tuesday. Is it enough? Justify your answer.
