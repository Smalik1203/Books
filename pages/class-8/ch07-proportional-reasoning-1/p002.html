Compare image A with image C. The width of C is half the width of A, and the height of C is also half the height of A. Both measurements have been multiplied by the same number, $\frac{1}{2}$. We say that they have changed by the same factor. Since the width and the height changed by the same factor, the two images look alike.

Now compare image A with image B. The width of B is $20$ mm less than the width of A, and the height of B is also $20$ mm less than the height of A. The difference is the same, and yet the images look different. So look at the factors instead. The height of B is half the height of A, but the width of B, $40$ mm, is not half of $60$ mm. The width and the height have not changed by the same factor, and so the images look different.

Check image D against image A in the same way. By what factor has the width changed, and by what factor has the height changed? Are the two factors the same?

Images A, C and D look alike because their widths and heights have changed by the same factor. We say that the changes in their widths and heights are proportional.

Changing in proportion

Two measurements change proportionally when both are multiplied by the same factor. A picture keeps its shape when its width and height change proportionally. Adding or taking away the same amount from both does not keep the shape.

7.2Ratios

In mathematics we describe such a relationship with a ratio. The ratio of the width to the height of image A is written $60 : 40$, and read 60 to 40. The numbers $60$ and $40$ are called the terms of the ratio.

In the same way, the ratio of width to height is $30 : 20$ for image C and $90 : 60$ for image D.

Ratio

A ratio $a : b$ says that for every $a$ units of the first quantity there are $b$ units of the second quantity. The numbers $a$ and $b$ are the terms of the ratio. The order of the terms matters: $60 : 40$ and $40 : 60$ are different ratios.

So in image A, for every $60$ mm of width there are $40$ mm of height.

The ratios of width to height for images A, C and D are proportional, because their terms change by the same factor. Here is how. Image A has the ratio $60 : 40$. Multiply both terms by $\frac{1}{2}$:

$60 \times \frac{1}{2} : 40 \times \frac{1}{2}$, which is $30 : 20$
