Example 7

Match each ratio in List I with its simplest form in List II.

List IList II
(P) the ratio $45 : 60$(1) $3 : 8$
(Q) 2 m to 80 cm(2) $3 : 4$
(R) 750 g to 2 kg(3) $5 : 2$
  1. P–3, Q–2, R–1
  2. P–2, Q–3, R–1
  3. P–2, Q–1, R–3
  4. P–1, Q–3, R–2

Solution.

Step 1$45 : 60 = 3 : 4$; in centimetres, $200 : 80 = 5 : 2$.
Step 2In grams, $750 : 2000 = 3 : 8$.
CheckFor P, cross multiply: $3 \times 60 = 180 = 4 \times 45$.
Answer(b) P–2, Q–3, R–1
Example 8

Match each ratio in List I with the smaller share of ₹360 in List II.

List IList II
(P) $1 : 2$(1) ₹90
(Q) $1 : 3$(2) ₹160
(R) $4 : 5$(3) ₹120
  1. P–3, Q–2, R–1
  2. P–2, Q–1, R–3
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–2
  4. P–1, Q–3, R–2

Solution.

Step 1One part: $360 \div 3 = 120$ and $360 \div 4 = 90$ rupees.
Step 2$360 \div 9 = 40$, so the smaller share for $4 : 5$ is $4 \times 40 = 160$.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–1, R–2
