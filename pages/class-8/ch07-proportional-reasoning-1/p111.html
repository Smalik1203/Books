Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $2 : 3$ and $20 : 30$ are proportional.

    Reason (R): Multiplying both terms of a ratio by the same number gives a proportional ratio.

  1. Assertion (A): Adding $4$ to both terms of $5 : 9$ gives a ratio proportional to $5 : 9$.

    Reason (R): Adding the same number to both terms of a ratio usually changes the ratio.

  1. Assertion (A): $50$ °C is $122$ °F.

    Reason (R): $0$ °C is $32$ °F.

  1. Assertion (A): The simplest form of $20 : 30$ is $2 : 3$.

    Reason (R): A ratio is reduced to its simplest form by taking the same number away from both terms.
