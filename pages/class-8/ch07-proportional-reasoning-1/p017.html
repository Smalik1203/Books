Puzzle: Binairo

Binairo, also called Takuzu, is a logic puzzle with simple rules. It is usually played on a square grid, which can be of any size. At the start some cells hold one of two symbols, here a horizontal line or a vertical line, and the rest are empty. Fill the empty cells so that

  1. every row and every column has as many horizontal lines as vertical lines;
  2. no more than two of the same symbol are next to each other in a row or a column;
  3. no two rows are the same, and no two columns are the same.
