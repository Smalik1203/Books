Exercise Set 7.4
  1. Mehul mixes $450$ mL of guava juice with $750$ mL of orange juice. Write the ratio of guava juice to orange juice in its simplest form.
  1. Last month $4$ full vans carried $96$ children to a science fair. Each van seats the same number. This month $150$ children are going. How many vans are needed? Will every van be full?
  1. Village A has $1{,}800$ people living on $4$ sq km. Village B has $2{,}500$ people on $6$ sq km. Which village is more crowded? Give a reason.
  1. On a model of a temple, $1$ cm stands for $3$ m. The model is $18$ cm tall. How tall is the temple?
  1. A normal body temperature is about $98.6$ °F. What is it in degrees Celsius?
  1. Kabir is $3$ years old and his sister is $11$. How old will Kabir be when the ratio of his age to his sister's age is $3 : 5$?
  1. Equal volumes of iron and water have masses in the ratio $79 : 10$. One litre of water has a mass of $1$ kg. What is the mass of $250$ mL of iron?
  1. A farmer spreads $8$ tonnes of compost on each hectare. Her field is $250$ m long and $160$ m wide. How much compost does she need?
  1. A water cooler fills a $250$ mL glass in $5$ seconds. How long does it take to fill a $20$-litre can? Give the answer in minutes and seconds.
  1. A flat has a floor area of $90$ square metres. What is its area in square feet?
  1. Brass is made by mixing copper and zinc in the ratio $7 : 3$. A brass lamp has a mass of $1.5$ kg. Copper costs ₹$800$ a kilogram and zinc costs ₹$300$ a kilogram. What is the cost of the metal in the lamp?
