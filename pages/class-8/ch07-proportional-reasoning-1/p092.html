1. A television screen has its width and height in the ratio $16 : 9$, and it is $80$ cm wide. A film made for a $4 : 3$ screen is shown on it using the full height of the screen, without being stretched. Find the width of the picture. Also find the area of the screen that is left dark, and the fraction of the screen that is used.
  1. The Fahrenheit reading of a temperature is twice its Celsius reading. Using $F = \frac{9}{5} \times C + 32$, form an equation and find this temperature on both scales. Also find the Fahrenheit reading when the Celsius reading is half as much.
  1. ₹$9{,}000$ is divided between Anil and Farah in the ratio $4 : 5$. Farah then shares her part with her brother in the ratio $3 : 2$. Find the amount each of the three people gets. Also find the ratio of Anil's amount to her brother's amount.
  1. A rectangular farm is $330$ m long and $150$ m wide. Find its area in hectares and in acres. (Use $1$ hectare $= 2.471$ acres.) The farmer sows $30$ kg of seed on each acre, and the seed costs ₹$45$ a kilogram. Find the cost of the seed, to the nearest rupee.
  1. The present ages of Meenakshi and her son are in the ratio $7 : 2$. After $10$ years the ratio of their ages will be $9 : 4$. Taking their present ages as $7x$ and $2x$ years, form an equation and find their present ages. Also find the ratio of their ages $5$ years ago.
  1. A train covers $210$ km in $2$ hours $20$ minutes. At the same speed, find the distance it covers in $3$ hours $40$ minutes. Also find, in hours, the time it takes to cover $450$ km.
Assertion and reason

Choose (a) if A and R are both true and R is the correct explanation of A; (b) if A and R are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): $12 : 18 :: 30 : 45$.

    Reason (R): Two ratios are proportional when the product of the extremes equals the product of the means.

  1. Assertion (A): $25$ °C is $77$ °F.

    Reason (R): The Celsius and Fahrenheit scales give the same reading at $-40$ degrees.

  1. Assertion (A): Adding $5$ to both terms of $3 : 4$ gives a ratio proportional to $3 : 4$.

    Reason (R): Multiplying both terms of a ratio by the same non-zero number gives a proportional ratio.

  1. Assertion (A): When ₹$600$ is divided in the ratio $1 : 2$, the shares are ₹$200$ and ₹$400$.

    Reason (R): When $x$ is divided in the ratio $m : n$, the first part is $\frac{x}{m}$.

  1. Assertion (A): One hectare is more than two acres.

    Reason (R): $1$ hectare $= 2.471$ acres.
