Example 7

Fill in the missing numbers so that each ratio is proportional to $14 : 21$.

 $: 42$    $6 :$     $2 :$ 

Solution.

Step 1 $42 = 21 \times 2$, so the first term is $14 \times 2 = 28$ factor $2$
Step 2 $14 \times y = 6$, so $y = \frac{6}{14} = \frac{3}{7}$ a fraction this time
Step 3 $21 \times \frac{3}{7} = 9$ the same factor
Step 4 $14 \div 7 = 2$ and $21 \div 7 = 3$ $7$ is the HCF
Answer $28 : 42$, $\ 6 : 9$ and $2 : 3$

Filter coffee

Filter coffee is made by mixing coffee decoction with milk. Manjunath runs a coffee shop. For one cup of filter coffee he usually mixes $15$ mL of decoction with $35$ mL of milk. So the ratio of decoction to milk is $15 : 35$.

When a customer wants stronger coffee, Manjunath mixes $20$ mL of decoction with $30$ mL of milk, a ratio of $20 : 30$. When a customer wants lighter coffee, he mixes $10$ mL of decoction with $40$ mL of milk, a ratio of $10 : 40$.

Why is the second coffee stronger than the regular one? Why is the third one lighter?
