7.5Sharing, but Not Equally!

Activity 3. Work in pairs. Collect $12$ counters: coins, seeds or pebbles will do. Share them between the two of you in different ways.

If you share them equally, what is the ratio of your partner's counters to yours? Each of you gets $6$, so the ratio is $6 : 6$, which is $1 : 1$ in its simplest form.

Now share them unequally. If your partner gets $5$ counters, how many do you get, and what is the ratio? You get $7$, and the ratio of your partner's counters to yours is $5 : 7$.

Suppose you want to share the $12$ counters in the ratio $3 : 1$. How many would each of you get? Try different ways of sharing, and see which one is in the ratio $3 : 1$. Here is one way:

Round 1 your partner takes $3$ and you take $1$; $8$ are left
Round 2 your partner takes $3$ more and you take $1$ more; $4$ are left
Round 3 your partner takes $3$ more and you take $1$ more; none are left

So your partner gets $9$ counters in all, and you get $3$. When $12$ counters are divided in the ratio $3 : 1$ between two people, one gets $9$ and the other gets $3$.
