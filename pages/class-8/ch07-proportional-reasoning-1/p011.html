Example 10

A village cooperative sells honey in $250$ g jars for ₹$150$ each. A shop in the town sells honey in $1$ kg jars for ₹$520$ each. Are the ratios of weight to price proportional? Which honey costs more?

Solution.

Step 1 cooperative: $250 : 150$, which is $5 : 3$ grams to rupees
Step 2 shop: $1000 : 520$, which is $25 : 13$, not $5 : 3$ in grams, not $1 : 520$
Step 3 $250 : 150 :: 1000 : x$ gives $x = \dfrac{150 \times 1000}{250} = 600$
Answer Not proportional. The cooperative's honey costs more: ₹$600$ a kg against the shop's ₹$520$

To decide which honey costs more, we compared the prices of the same weight, $1$ kg. Finding the value of one unit first, and then of as many units as are needed, is called the unitary method.
