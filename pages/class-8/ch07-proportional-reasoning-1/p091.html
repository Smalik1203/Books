1. The ratio of boys to girls in a class is $7 : 6$. There are $8$ more boys than girls. Find the number of boys and the number of girls.
  1. At the same time of day, a pole $2.4$ m tall casts a shadow $1.8$ m long, and a tower casts a shadow $27$ m long. Find the height of the tower. Also find the length of the shadow of a tree $6$ m tall.
  1. A shop sells rice at ₹$162$ for $3$ kg. Find the cost of $7.5$ kg of rice. Also find how many kilograms of rice can be bought for ₹$999$.
  1. Each day Priya and Sameer read pages in the ratio $4 : 3$, and Priya reads $12$ pages more than Sameer. Find the number of pages each of them reads in a week.
  1. A $20$-litre drum of paint covers $180$ m². Find the paint needed for a wall $12$ m long and $4.5$ m high. Also find the area that $7.5$ litres cover.
  1. Show that the perimeters of two squares with sides $6$ cm and $9$ cm are in the same ratio as their sides, but that their areas are not.
