Example 7

Fill in the missing numbers so that each ratio is proportional to $12 : 18$.

 $: 54$    $8 :$     $2 :$ 

Solution.

Step 1 $54 = 18 \times 3$, so the first term is $12 \times 3 = 36$ factor $3$
Step 2 $12 \times y = 8$, so $y = \frac{8}{12} = \frac{2}{3}$ a fraction this time
Step 3 $18 \times \frac{2}{3} = 12$ the same factor
Step 4 $12 \div 6 = 2$ and $18 \div 6 = 3$ $6$ is the HCF
Answer $36 : 54$, $\ 8 : 12$ and $2 : 3$

Sweeter or lighter?

Gopal sells rose sherbet from a stall near a bus stand. A glass holds $200$ mL. For his regular sherbet he pours $40$ mL of rose syrup and then $160$ mL of water. So the ratio of syrup to water is $40 : 160$.

When a customer asks for a sweeter glass, Gopal pours $50$ mL of syrup and $150$ mL of water, a ratio of $50 : 150$. For a lighter glass he pours $25$ mL of syrup and $175$ mL of water: $25 : 175$.

Write each ratio in its simplest form: $40 : 160$ is $1 : 4$, $50 : 150$ is $1 : 3$, and $25 : 175$ is $1 : 7$. In the sweeter glass there is $1$ mL of syrup for every $3$ mL of water, which is more syrup for the same water than $1$ mL for every $4$. In the lighter glass there is $1$ mL of syrup for every $7$ mL of water, which is less.
