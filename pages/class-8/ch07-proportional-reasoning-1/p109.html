Example 9

A paint shop mixes blue and white paint. Its Sky shade has blue and white in the ratio $1 : 4$, and its Ocean shade $2 : 3$. A painter pours $5$ litres of each into one drum.

  1. The blue paint in the drum is
    1. $3$ litres
    2. $2.5$ litres
    3. $1$ litre
    4. $2$ litres
  2. Numerical answer. How many litres of white paint make the drum Sky shade?

Solution.

Step 1Blue: $1 + 2 = 3$ litres. White: $4 + 3 = 7$ litres.
Step 2$1 : 4 :: 3 : 12$, so add $12 - 7 = 5$ litres of white.
Answer(i) (a) $3$ litres; (ii) $5$
