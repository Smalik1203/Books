Example 15 · Matching

₹360 is divided in each ratio of List I. Match it with the smaller share in List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $1 : 2$(1) ₹150
(Q) $1 : 3$(2) ₹120
(R) $4 : 5$(3) ₹160
(S) $5 : 7$(4) ₹90

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  3. P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2

Solution.

Step 1One part is $360 \div 3 = 120$ and $360 \div 4 = 90$ rupees, so the smaller shares are ₹120 and ₹90.
Step 2One part is $360 \div 9 = 40$, so $4 \times 40 = 160$; and $360 \div 12 = 30$, so $5 \times 30 = 150$.
Answer(c) P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
