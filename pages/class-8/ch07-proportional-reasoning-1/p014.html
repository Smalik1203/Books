In general, to divide a quantity $x$ in the ratio $m : n$, we do this.

Step 1 split $x$ into equal groups, so that the first part has $m$ groups and the second part has $n$ groups
Step 2 there are $m + n$ groups, so each group is $\dfrac{x}{m + n}$
Step 3 the first part is $m \times \dfrac{x}{m + n}$ and the second part is $n \times \dfrac{x}{m + n}$
Dividing in a given ratio
  • To divide $x$ in the ratio $m : n$, first find one group: $\dfrac{x}{m + n}$.
  • The parts, $m$ groups and $n$ groups, add up to $x$ and are in the ratio $m : n$.
Example 11

Tenzin and Rukhsar ran a snack stall at their school fair. Tenzin put in ₹$600$ and Rukhsar put in ₹$400$. They made a profit of ₹$750$, and agreed to share it in the same ratio as the money they put in. What is each person's share?

Solution.

Step 1 $600 : 400$ is $3 : 2$ divide by $200$
Step 2 $3 + 2 = 5$ groups, and $750 \div 5 = 150$ one group
Step 3 Tenzin: $3 \times 150 = 450$; Rukhsar: $2 \times 150 = 300$
Answer Tenzin gets ₹$450$ and Rukhsar gets ₹$300$

Sometimes a mixture has to be changed to a new ratio. The part that is not added to stays the same, and that is the place to start.
