Example 11

Prashanti and Bhuvan started a food cart near their school. Prashanti put in ₹$75{,}000$ and Bhuvan put in ₹$25{,}000$. At the end of the first month they made a profit of ₹$4{,}000$. They agreed to share the profit in the same ratio as the money they put in. What is each person's share?

Solution.

Step 1 $75000 : 25000$ is $3 : 1$ divide by $25000$
Step 2 $3 + 1 = 4$ groups, and $4000 \div 4 = 1000$ one group
Step 3 Prashanti: $3 \times 1000 = 3000$; Bhuvan: $1 \times 1000 = 1000$
Answer Prashanti gets ₹$3{,}000$ and Bhuvan gets ₹$1{,}000$

Sometimes a mixture has to be changed to a new ratio. The part that is not added to stays the same, and that is the place to start.

Example 12

A $40$ kg mixture contains sand and cement in the ratio $3 : 1$. How much cement should be added to make the ratio of sand to cement $5 : 2$?

Solution.

Step 1 sand $= \dfrac{3}{3 + 1} \times 40 = 30$ kg the first part
Step 2 cement $= \dfrac{1}{3 + 1} \times 40 = 10$ kg the second part
Step 3 $5 : 2 :: 30 : \,?$ the sand stays $30$ kg
Step 4 $\frac{2}{5} \times 30 = 12$ second term is $\frac{2}{5}$ of the first
Step 5 $12 - 10 = 2$ cement already there
Answer $2$ kg of cement
