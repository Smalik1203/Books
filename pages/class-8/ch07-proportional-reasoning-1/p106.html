Example 6 · Single correct

A patient's temperature is 104 °F. What is it in degrees Celsius?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 72 °C
  2. 40 °C
  3. 58 °C
  4. 26 °C

Solution.

Step 1$C = \frac{5}{9} \times (F - 32) = \frac{5}{9} \times (104 - 32)$.
Step 2$\frac{5}{9} \times 72 = 40$.
Answer(b) 40 °C
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these rectangles have the same shape as a rectangle 6 cm wide and 4 cm high?

Select all correct options.

  1. 9 cm wide, 6 cm high
  2. 8 cm wide, 6 cm high
  3. 3 cm wide, 2 cm high
  4. 15 cm wide, 10 cm high

Solution.

Step 1Width to height is $6 : 4$, which is $3 : 2$.
Step 2$9 : 6$, $3 : 2$ and $15 : 10$ all reduce to $3 : 2$; $8 : 6$ reduces to $4 : 3$, a different shape.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which statements about the ratio $3 : 5$ are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $(3 + 2) : (5 + 2)$ is proportional to $3 : 5$.
  2. $(3 \times 4) : (5 \times 4)$ is proportional to $3 : 5$.
  3. $3 : 5 :: 4.5 : 7.5$
  4. $3 : 5 :: 5 : 3$

Solution.

Step 1Multiplying both terms by 4 gives $12 : 20$, which reduces to $3 : 5$; and $3 \times 7.5 = 22.5 = 5 \times 4.5$.
Step 2Adding 2 gives $5 : 7$, and $3 \times 7 = 21$ but $5 \times 5 = 25$. And $5 : 3$ is the ratio reversed.
Answer(b), (c)
