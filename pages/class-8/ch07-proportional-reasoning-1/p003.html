That is the ratio of width to height for image C. By what factor should both terms of $60 : 40$ be multiplied to give $90 : 60$, the ratio for image D?

Finding the factor each time works, but there is a more orderly way to test whether two ratios are proportional. Reduce each ratio to its simplest form, and see whether the simplest forms are the same.

7.3Ratios in their Simplest Form

We reduce a ratio to its simplest form by dividing both terms by their HCF.

For image A the terms are $60$ and $40$. Their HCF is $20$. Dividing both terms by $20$ gives $3 : 2$. This is the ratio for image A in its simplest form.

For image D the ratio is $90 : 60$. The HCF of $90$ and $60$ is $30$, and dividing both terms by $30$ gives $3 : 2$ again. So the ratios for images A and D are proportional as well.

What are the simplest forms of the ratios for images B and E? For image B, $40 : 20$ becomes $2 : 1$. For image E, $60 : 60$ becomes $1 : 1$. Neither of these is $3 : 2$. So the ratios of width to height for images B and E are not proportional to the ratios for images A, C and D.

Simplest form and proportion

A ratio is in its simplest form when the HCF of its terms is $1$. Two ratios are in proportion, or proportional, when they are the same in their simplest forms. We write $a : b :: c : d$, and read it a is to b as c is to d.

So $60 : 40 :: 30 : 20$ and $60 : 40 :: 90 : 60$. Ratios with the same simplest form, such as $60 : 40$, $30 : 20$ and $3 : 2$, are also called equivalent ratios.

7.4Problem Solving with Proportional Reasoning

Example 1

Are the ratios $3 : 4$ and $72 : 96$ proportional?

Solution.

Step 1 $3 : 4$ is already in its simplest form HCF of $3$ and $4$ is $1$
Step 2 the HCF of $72$ and $96$ is $24$
Step 3 $72 \div 24 = 3$ and $96 \div 24 = 4$, so $72 : 96$ becomes $3 : 4$ divide by the HCF
Answer Yes. Both are $3 : 4$ in simplest form, so $3 : 4 :: 72 : 96$
