Ratio
  • A ratio $a : b$ says that for every $a$ units of the first quantity there are $b$ units of the second.
  • The order of the terms matters: $80 : 60$ and $60 : 80$ are different ratios.

The ratios of width to height for pictures P, Q and S are proportional, because their terms change by the same factor. Picture P has the ratio $80 : 60$. Multiply both terms by $\frac{1}{2}$:

$80 \times \frac{1}{2} : 60 \times \frac{1}{2}$, which is $40 : 30$

That is the ratio for picture Q. To reach $100 : 75$, the ratio for picture S, both terms of $80 : 60$ are multiplied by $\frac{5}{4}$.

Finding the factor each time works, but there is a more orderly way to test whether two ratios are proportional. Write each ratio in its simplest form, and see whether the simplest forms are the same.

7.3Ratios in their Simplest Form

We reduce a ratio to its simplest form by dividing both terms by their HCF.

For picture P the terms are $80$ and $60$. Their HCF is $20$. Dividing both terms by $20$ gives $4 : 3$. This is the ratio for picture P in its simplest form.

For picture S the ratio is $100 : 75$. The HCF of $100$ and $75$ is $25$, and dividing both terms by $25$ gives $4 : 3$ again. For picture Q, $40 : 30$ also becomes $4 : 3$. So the three are proportional.

What about pictures R and T? For picture R, $60 : 40$ becomes $3 : 2$. For picture T, $45 : 60$ becomes $3 : 4$. Neither of these is $4 : 3$. So the ratios of width to height for R and T are not proportional to the ratio for P.
