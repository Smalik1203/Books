Amar puts ₹$20{,}000$ into a business for $12$ months. Bela puts in ₹$30{,}000$, but only for the last $6$ months. They share a profit of ₹$14{,}000$ in the ratio of money × months for each of them. How much does each get?

Bela put in more money, but for half the time, so it is not clear at the start who should get more. The ratio settles it.

Amar $20000 \times 12 = 240000$
Bela $30000 \times 6 = 180000$
the ratio $240000 : 180000$ is $4 : 3$ divide by $60000$
one part $14000 \div 7 = 2000$ ₹$8{,}000$ and ₹$6{,}000$

Amar gets ₹$8{,}000$ and Bela gets ₹$6{,}000$. Example 11 shared a profit in the ratio of the money put in. This question needed one more idea: money that stays in a business for longer does more work, so time counts too.

Example 1

In its simplest form, the ratio of 1.2 m to 45 cm is

  1. $8 : 3$
  2. $3 : 8$
  3. $24 : 9$
  4. $4 : 15$

Solution.

Step 11.2 m is 120 cm, so in one unit the ratio is $120 : 45$.
Step 2The HCF of 120 and 45 is 15: $120 \div 15 = 8$ and $45 \div 15 = 3$. The ratio $24 : 9$ is equal to it but is not in simplest form.
Answer(a) $8 : 3$
