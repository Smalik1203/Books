Example 1 · Single correct

In its simplest form, the ratio of 1.2 m to 45 cm is

Choose one correct option.

  1. $8 : 3$
  2. $3 : 8$
  3. $24 : 9$
  4. $4 : 15$

Solution.

Step 11.2 m is 120 cm, so in one unit the ratio is $120 : 45$.
Step 2The HCF of 120 and 45 is 15: $120 \div 15 = 8$ and $45 \div 15 = 3$. The ratio $24 : 9$ is equal to it but is not in simplest form.
Answer(a) $8 : 3$
Example 2 · Single correct

Which ratio is proportional to $18 : 27$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $4 : 9$
  2. $10 : 15$
  3. $6 : 8$
  4. $27 : 18$

Solution.

Step 1Dividing by the HCF, 9, reduces $18 : 27$ to $2 : 3$.
Step 2Only $10 : 15$ also reduces to $2 : 3$. The ratio $27 : 18$ is $3 : 2$, the terms the other way round.
Answer(b) $10 : 15$
