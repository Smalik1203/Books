Example 12

A $60$ kg mixture has sand and cement in the ratio $4 : 1$. How much cement should be added to make the ratio of sand to cement $3 : 1$?

Solution.

Step 1 sand $= \dfrac{4}{4 + 1} \times 60 = 48$ kg the first part
Step 2 cement $= \dfrac{1}{4 + 1} \times 60 = 12$ kg the second part
Step 3 $3 : 1 :: 48 : \,?$ the sand stays $48$ kg
Step 4 $48 \div 3 = 16$ cement needed
Step 5 $16 - 12 = 4$ cement already there
Answer $4$ kg of cement
