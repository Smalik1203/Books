7.6Unit Conversions

Two ratios can be compared only when their terms are in the same units. In Example 9 the time was given in minutes and in hours, and the hours had to be changed to minutes. In Example 10 the weight was given in grams and in kilograms, and the kilograms had to be changed to grams. Problems of proportion often need a change of units like this before anything else is done. Table 7.13 gives some important conversions, for reference.

Table 7.13 Some unit conversions
QuantityConversion
Length1 metre = 3.281 feet
Area1 square metre = 10.764 square feet
Area1 acre = 43,560 square feet
Area1 hectare = 10,000 square metres
Area1 hectare = 2.471 acres
Volume1 millilitre (mL) = 1 cubic centimetre (cc)
Volume1 litre = 1,000 mL = 1,000 cc

Changing a temperature between degrees Fahrenheit (°F) and degrees Celsius (°C) takes one more step, because the two scales do not start at the same place: $0$ °C is $32$ °F. If $C$ is a temperature in degrees Celsius and $F$ is the same temperature in degrees Fahrenheit, then

$F = \dfrac{9}{5} \times C + 32 \qquad \text{and} \qquad C = \dfrac{5}{9} \times (F - 32)$

For example, $25$ °C is $\frac{9}{5} \times 25 + 32 = 45 + 32 = 77$ °F.
