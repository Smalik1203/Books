Example 2

A machine caps 360 bottles in 15 minutes. Working at the same rate, how many bottles does it cap in $1\tfrac{1}{4}$ hours?

  1. 30
  2. 450
  3. 1500
  4. 1800

Solution.

Step 1$1\tfrac{1}{4}$ hours is 75 minutes, so the proportion is $15 : 360 :: 75 : x$.
Step 2$15x = 360 \times 75$, so $x = 1800$.
Answer(d) 1800

Example 3

A rectangle is 6 cm wide and 4 cm high. Which of these rectangles, given as width by height in centimetres, have the same shape?

  1. 9 by 6
  2. 8 by 6
  3. 3 by 2
  4. 15 by 10

Solution.

Step 1Width to height is $6 : 4$, which is $3 : 2$.
Step 2$9 : 6$, $3 : 2$ and $15 : 10$ all reduce to $3 : 2$; $8 : 6$ reduces to $4 : 3$, a different shape.
