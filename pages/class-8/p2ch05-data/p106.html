Type 5 · A missing or a wrong value

Example 6

The mean of six numbers is $18$. Five of them are $12$, $15$, $20$, $22$ and $25$. Find the sixth.

Solution.

Step 1 $6 \times 18 = 108$ the sum of all six
Step 2 $12 + 15 + 20 + 22 + 25 = 94$ the five we know
Step 3 $108 - 94 = 14$
Answer The sixth number is $14$
Example 7

The mean of $20$ observations was found to be $45$. It was later noticed that one observation, $64$, had been copied as $46$. Find the correct mean.

Solution.

Step 1 $20 \times 45 = 900$ the recorded total
Step 2 $900 - 46 + 64 = 918$ take out the wrong, put in the right
Step 3 $918 \div 20 = 45.9$
Answer The correct mean is $45.9$

The total was short by $18$, and $18 \div 20 = 0.9$ is exactly the change in the mean.

Type 6 · Mean and median from a frequency table

Example 8

The shoe sizes of $30$ students are given in the table. Find the mean and the median size.

Shoe size45678
Students381063
Running total311212730

Solution.

Step 1 $(4 \times 3) + (5 \times 8) + (6 \times 10) + (7 \times 6) + (8 \times 3) = 178$ value times frequency
Step 2 $178 \div 30 = 5.93$, to two places divide by 30, not 5
Step 3 positions $12$ to $21$ are all size $6$ read the running total
Answer Mean $5.93$; the 15th and 16th are both $6$, so the median is $6$
