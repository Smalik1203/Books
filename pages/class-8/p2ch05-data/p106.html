Example 7 · Multiple correct

Consider the values 9, 12, 13, 15 and 21. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Their differences from 14 add to 0.
  2. Their mean is 14.
  3. Their median is 13.
  4. Their mean is 15.

Solution.

Step 1The differences from 14 are $-5, -2, -1, +1, +7$, which add to 0, so 14 is the balance point, the mean.
Step 2The middle of the five values in order is 13.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

5 is added to every value in a set of data. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The mean goes up by 5.
  2. The median goes up by 5.
  3. The difference between the highest and lowest value goes up by 5.
  4. The mean doubles.

Solution.

Step 1Every value, and so the middle value and the balance point, moves up by 5.
Step 2The highest and lowest both move up by 5, so their difference is unchanged; nothing doubles.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

In a frequency table the values 1, 2 and 3 occur 4, $f$ and 6 times, and the mean is 2.1. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $f = 10$
  2. There are 20 observations.
  3. The median is 2.
  4. The most common value is 3.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{4 + 2f + 18}{10 + f} = 2.1$ gives $f = 10$: 20 values, whose 10th and 11th, and most common, are all 2.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
