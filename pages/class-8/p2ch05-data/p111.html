  1. The mean of $11$ numbers is $35$. The mean of the first six is $32$, and the mean of the last six is $37$. Find the sixth number.
  1. A patient's temperature, in degrees Fahrenheit, was taken every two hours: $101.5$ at 8 am, $102.5$ at 10 am, $103$ at noon, $102$ at 2 pm, $100.5$ at 4 pm and $99.5$ at 6 pm.
    1. Draw the line graph with the vertical axis starting at $98$, and say why a cut axis is allowed here.
    2. Between which two readings did the temperature fall fastest?
    3. Estimate the temperature at 11 am.
    4. Find the mean of the six readings.
    5. Does the graph show that the patient's highest temperature that day was $103$ degrees?
  1. Twelve students scored these marks out of $10$: $4$, $7$, $8$, $8$, $5$, $9$, $8$, $6$, $7$, $10$, $3$, $9$.
    1. Find the mean and the median.
    2. Another student, who scored $7$, joins. Find the new mean and the new median.
