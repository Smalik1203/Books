Example 10

Neha's height is measured on each birthday from her 8th to her 12th: $124$, $130$, $135$, $141$ and $148$ cm. Her mother joins the five readings to make a line graph, with her age along the bottom axis.

  1. Neha grew most between her birthdays
    1. 8 and 9
    2. 9 and 10
    3. 10 and 11
    4. 11 and 12
  2. Numerical answer. Estimate her height, in cm, at the age of ten and a half.
  3. Numerical answer. Find her mean growth per year, in cm, from 8 to 12.

Solution.

Step 1The yearly gains are $6$, $5$, $6$ and $7$ cm, so the largest is between 11 and 12
Step 2Halfway between $135$ and $141$ is $138$
Step 3She grew $148 - 124 = 24$ cm in $4$ years: $24 \div 4 = 6$
CheckThe four gains, $6 + 5 + 6 + 7$, also add to $24$
Answer(i) (d); (ii) $138$; (iii) $6$
