The third one is not a trick. Twelve students are between $140$ and $145$ cm, and the histogram does not say where in that stretch any of them stands — that is precisely the detail grouping threw away. If the question matters, the raw measurements must be kept, and the graph is not a substitute for them.

Think and Reflect

The forty heights were put into five classes of $5$ cm. Suppose they had been put into one class of $25$ cm instead, or into twenty-five classes of $1$ cm.

Draw both in your head. What does each of the two graphs tell you, and what has each of them lost? Then say which of the two is closer to the raw list of forty measurements — and why the answer to that is not the same as which is more useful.
