Behind Each Answer
The same eight questions, worked — and the reasoning that finds them.

The eight are answered below in the order they were asked. Notice how often the first move is to write down the rule going forwards, and only then to put the unknown where the answer usually goes. None of them needs a new rule, and none of them needs a picture drawn: a scale, an angle and a probability are all statements about proportion, and a statement about proportion can be entered from either end.

Two of the eight have no number for an answer at all. They ask what can be said, and the honest reply is a range or a caution rather than a figure — which is not a weaker answer but a more careful one, and the sort a reader of graphs has to be willing to give.
