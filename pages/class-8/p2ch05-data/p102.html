Behind Each Answer
The same eight questions, worked — and the reasoning that finds them.

The eight are answered below in the order they were asked. Most of them can be done twice — once by finding the sum and once by thinking about what the change does to the balance — and where both are short, both are given.

A value removed, and how far the mean falls

The direct route is to recover the sum, take the value away, and divide by what is left.

the sum $8 \times 15 = 120$
without the 22 $120 - 22 = 98$ over $7$ values
the new mean $98 \div 7 = 14$

The balance route says the same thing faster. The value $22$ stood $7$ above the mean, and removing it takes those $7$ out of the pile — leaving $7$ less to be shared among the $7$ that remain, which is exactly $1$ less each. The mean falls by $1$, from $15$ to $14$.
