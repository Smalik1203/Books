Case-based questions

  1. A stationery shop records the notebooks it sells on six days.

    DayMonTueWedThuFriSat
    Notebooks423845404956
    1. Find the mean and the median number sold.
    2. Which of the two is larger, and which day makes it so?
    3. On the next Monday the shop sells $45$. Find the mean and the median of all seven days.

  1. A town records its rainfall for four months of the monsoon.

    MonthJuneJulyAugustSeptember
    Rainfall (mm)130290220160
    1. Find the median rainfall.
    2. Check by balancing, without adding, that the mean is $200$ mm.
    3. A poster draws the four months as bars on an axis that starts at $100$ mm. How many times as tall as June's bar is July's? How many times as much rain as June did July really have?
