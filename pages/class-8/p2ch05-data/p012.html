Example 8

A hockey team scored $0$, $1$, $2$ or $3$ goals in each match of a season. The table shows the number of matches, but one number is missing. The mean is $1.4$ goals per match. Find the missing number, and the median.

Goals0123
Matches47$f$3

Solution.

Step 1 Goals: $0 + 7 + 2f + 9 = 16 + 2f$. Matches: $14 + f$
Step 2 $16 + 2f = 1.4(14 + f) = 19.6 + 1.4f$, so $0.6f = 3.6$ and $f = 6$
Step 3 $20$ matches; running totals $4, 11, 17, 20$. The 10th and 11th values are both $1$
Answer The team scored $2$ goals in $6$ matches. The median is $1$ goal
