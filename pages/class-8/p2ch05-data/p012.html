Here is why the order matters. A shop's sales over four years were $96$, $98$, $101$ and $104$ lakh rupees, and the two graphs below are both of those four numbers. Every bar in each is drawn to its own axis correctly, and no figure has been altered.

The left graph says the shop is steady. The right graph says the last bar is five times the first, and a reader who skipped step one will believe it — because that is what bars are for, comparing lengths without thinking. What has gone wrong is that a bar means what it means only when its length is proportional to its value, and an axis beginning at $94$ chops the same $94$ off every bar. The first keeps $2$ of its $96$ and the last keeps $10$ of its $104$: five times, from a rise of about eight per cent.

A cut axis is not always dishonest. A graph of body temperature through a fever would be a flat line drawn from zero, and the interesting range is two degrees wide. The rule is about bars in particular, because a bar carries the promise that its length is the quantity. Cut the axis of a line graph when you must, and say so; never cut the axis of a bar graph.

Step two has its own discipline. Given a graph, some statements follow from it, some are contradicted by it, and some — the awkward ones — are simply not settled either way. Look again at Fig. 5.4 and consider three claims.

the day warmed until 3 pm not settled — nothing was measured between the readings
it was warmer at noon than at 6 pm true, and read straight off the graph
the day never passed 34° not settled — no reading did, which is a different claim

The middle one is the only inference here, and the other two are guesses dressed as inferences. Both are probably right, and probably right is not what a graph offers: it offers what was measured, joined by a line that says nothing except that the quantity got from one reading to the next somehow.

Not settled is a legitimate answer and the most common correct one. It is also the answer a reader is least willing to give, because a graph looks so complete. The way to give it is to have done step one, where the six readings were noticed to be six.
