Example 8

A set of data has a mean of $20$. Match each change in List I with the new mean in List II.

List IList II
(P) Add 5 to every value(1) 12
(Q) Multiply every value by 3(2) 44
(R) Double every value, then add 4(3) 25
(S) Take 8 from every value(4) 60
  1. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The mean changes as each value does: $20 + 5 = 25$ and $20 \times 3 = 60$
Step 2$20 \times 2 + 4 = 44$ and $20 - 8 = 12$
CheckIn (R), doubling gives a mean of $40$, and adding $4$ gives $44$
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
