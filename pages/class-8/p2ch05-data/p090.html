By the Book
Very short answer
  1. Find the mean and the median of $13$, $8$, $21$, $8$ and $15$.
  1. The mean of $9$ numbers is $16$. When a tenth number is included, the mean becomes $17$. Find the tenth number.
  1. Is $11$ the mean of $5$, $9$, $14$ and $16$? Justify your answer by balancing, without adding all four values.
  1. Every value in a set with mean $48$ is divided by $6$, and then $3$ is added to it. Find the mean of the new values.
  1. Find the median of $27$, $19$, $33$, $41$, $25$ and $30$.
  1. The mean of $4$, $7$, $x$, $10$ and $12$ is $9$. Find the value of $x$.
  1. Forty-one values are arranged in order. Write the place of the median. If the two largest values are removed, write the new place of the median.
  1. The table shows a set of data.
    Value123
    Frequency532
    Find the mean of the data.
  1. Can four values, each greater than $10$, have a mean of $10$? Give a reason.
  1. A line graph shows the depth of water in a tank: $30$ cm at $2$ pm and $36$ cm at $4$ pm. Estimate the depth at $3{:}30$ pm, and state what your estimate assumes.
Short answer
  1. Meera scored $72$, $65$, $80$ and $71$ in four tests. Let her score in the fifth test be $x$. Find $x$ if the mean of the five scores is $75$. Also find whether a mean of $80$ is possible when each test is out of $100$.
  1. A class has two sections. The $20$ students of Section A have a mean mark of $45$, and the $30$ students of Section B have a mean mark of $50$. Find the mean mark of all $50$ students. Explain why it is not $47.5$.
