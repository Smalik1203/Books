Example 9

A cycle shop in Ludhiana records the number of bicycles it repairs on each day of one week. The owner wants to know how busy a usual day is.

DayMonTueWedThuFriSatSun
Bicycles69712859
  1. The median number of bicycles repaired is
    1. $8$
    2. $9$
    3. $7$
    4. $8.5$
  2. Numerical answer. Find the mean number repaired per day.
  3. Numerical answer. The shop repairs some bicycles on the next Monday, and the mean for all eight days becomes $8.5$. How many did it repair that Monday?

Solution.

Step 1In order: $5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 9, 12$. The 4th value is $8$
Step 2The seven numbers add to $56$, and $56 \div 7 = 8$
Step 3Eight days need a total of $8 \times 8.5 = 68$, so Monday brings $68 - 56 = 12$
Check$12$ is above the old mean of $8$, so the mean had to rise, as it did
Answer(i) (a) $8$; (ii) $8$; (iii) $12$
