In Questions 11 to 14, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): The values $4$, $9$, $11$ and $16$ have a mean of $10$.

    Reason (R): The distances of these values below $10$ add up to their distances above $10$.

  1. Assertion (A): Removing a value equal to the mean leaves the mean unchanged.

    Reason (R): The median of a collection is always one of its values.

  1. Assertion (A): The median of $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $100$ is $3$.

    Reason (R): The mean of $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $100$ is $22$.

    Reason (R): In a frequency table, each value is multiplied by its frequency before adding.
