5.8Lines for Things That Change

Every collection so far has been a heap. The thirty household sizes could have been collected in any order and the mean and the median would be the same, which is exactly why a frequency table was allowed to throw the order away.

Some data is not like that. Think of a temperature taken every three hours, a price recorded every year, a patient's pulse through a night. Here the readings come in an order that is part of the information. The thing being measured also has a value at every moment in between, not only at the moments somebody wrote it down. That is what allows the dots to be joined.
