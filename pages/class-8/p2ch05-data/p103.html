A difference of lengths is a difference of values

A scale converts centimetres to people, and it converts a difference of centimetres to a difference of people for the same reason: both bars are measured from the same zero, so the part they share cancels.

so $1.8 \times 250 = 450$ people

This is one of the things a graph starting at zero buys you. On a cut axis the two heights are still $1.8$ cm apart and the answer is still $450$ — but their ratio is no longer the ratio of the towns, which is the comparison the eye actually makes.

An angle is a fraction of the whole

A slice's angle is the same fraction of $360^\circ$ that its part is of the total. Chapter 3 used that to turn money into an angle; here it is used the other way, so reduce the angle to a fraction first.

the fraction $\dfrac{135}{360} = \dfrac{3}{8}$
so the food bill $\dfrac{3}{8} \times 32{,}000 = 12{,}000$, so ₹$12{,}000$

The ₹$32{,}000$ was essential, and it is exactly what a pie chart does not show. Without it the slice says only three eighths of whatever this is, which is why the chapter asked for a total on every one.

A straight line is a rule, not a guess

Falling from $9$ m to $3$ m is a fall of $6$ m, and over five months in a straight line that is a fixed rate.

rate $6 \div 5 = 1.2$ m a month
after 2.5 months $9 - 1.2 \times 2.5 = 9 - 3 = 6$ m

The second half of the question is the point of it. On Fig. 5.5 a reading between two dots was an estimate, because the temperature was free to wander. Here the question says the fall is a straight line, and a straight line is a rule that holds at every moment — so $6$ m is not a guess but a consequence.
