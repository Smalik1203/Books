Example 10 · Multiple correct

Shop A sold 210 school bags and Shop B sold 240. A poster shows both as bars on an axis that starts at 200. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Shop B sold 30 more bags.
  2. Shop B sold $30\%$ more bags.
  3. Shop B's bar looks 4 times as tall as Shop A's.
  4. Shop B sold 4 times as many bags.

Solution.

Step 1$240 - 210 = 30$ more, which is $\frac{30}{210}$, about $14\%$, not $30\%$.
Step 2Above 200 the bars are 10 and 40 tall, so B's looks 4 times as tall; a cut axis exaggerates the difference.
Answer(a), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

The value 40 is included with 14, 9, 21, 9, 17, 30, 12 and 25. What is the median now?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1In order: 9, 9, 12, 14, 17, 21, 25, 30, 40.
Step 2With 9 values the median is the 5th: 17.
Answer17
Example 12 · Numerical answer

The mean of 4, 6, 8 and $x$ is 7. Find $x$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The four values add to $4 \times 7 = 28$.
Step 2$x = 28 - 4 - 6 - 8 = 10$.
Answer10
