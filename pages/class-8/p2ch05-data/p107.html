Eight players score $14$, $9$, $21$, $9$, $17$, $30$, $12$ and $25$ runs. A ninth player scores $40$. What is the median score of all nine players?

Solution.

Step 1In order: $9, 9, 12, 14, 17, 21, 25, 30, 40$
Step 2With $9$ values the median is the 5th: $17$
