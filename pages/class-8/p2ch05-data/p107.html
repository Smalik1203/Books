Example 9

In a frequency table the values $1$, $2$ and $3$ occur $4$, $f$ and $6$ times, and the mean is $2.1$. Find $f$.

Solution.

Step 1 sum $= 4 + 2f + 18 = 22 + 2f$ value times frequency
Step 2 count $= 10 + f$, so $22 + 2f = 2.1 \times (10 + f)$ mean times count
Step 3 $22 + 2f = 21 + 2.1f$, so $0.1f = 1$
Answer $f = 10$. Check: $42 \div 20 = 2.1$

Example 10

Use Fig. 5.7. In which week did the plant grow most? Estimate its height halfway through the third week, and find its mean growth per week.

Solution.

Step 1 readings: $2, 5, 9, 12, 14, 15$ cm read off the dots
Step 2 growth each week: $3, 4, 3, 2, 1$ cm the steepest segment
Step 3 $(9 + 12) \div 2 = 10.5$ cm halfway along the segment
Step 4 $(15 - 2) \div 5 = 2.6$ cm total growth over five weeks
Answer The second week; about $10.5$ cm, an estimate; $2.6$ cm a week
