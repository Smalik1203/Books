Example 2

A teacher found the mean of $20$ observations to be $45$. Later she noticed that one value, $64$, had been read as $46$. What is the correct mean?

  1. $44.1$
  2. $45.9$
  3. $46.8$
  4. $45$

Solution.

Step 1The recorded total is $20 \times 45 = 900$
Step 2The correct total is $64 - 46 = 18$ more: $918$
Step 3$918 \div 20 = 45.9$
Check$45.9$ is more than $45$, as it must be, since the true total is larger
CheckThe total rose by $18$, so the mean rose by $18 \div 20 = 0.9$
Answer(b) $45.9$

Only the total needed mending. The wrong value was $18$ too small, and $18$ shared among $20$ values is $0.9$, which is how far the mean moves. The other nineteen values did not matter at all.
