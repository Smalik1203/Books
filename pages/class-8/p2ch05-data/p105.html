Example 4 · Single correct

What is the median of 14, 9, 21, 9, 17, 30, 12 and 25?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 14
  2. 17
  3. 16
  4. 15.5

Solution.

Step 1In order: 9, 9, 12, 14, 17, 21, 25, 30. With 8 values the median is halfway between the 4th and 5th.
Step 2$\frac{14 + 17}{2} = 15.5$.
Answer(d) 15.5
Example 5 · Single correct

The mean of six numbers is 18. Five of them are 12, 15, 20, 22 and 25. What is the sixth?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 14
  2. 18
  3. 16
  4. 12

Solution.

Step 1The six add to $6 \times 18 = 108$, and the five given add to 94.
Step 2The sixth is $108 - 94 = 14$.
Answer(a) 14
Example 6 · Single correct

The mean of 20 observations was found to be 45. Later it was noticed that 64 had been read as 46. What is the correct mean?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 44.1
  2. 45.9
  3. 46.8
  4. 45

Solution.

Step 1The total was $20 \times 45 = 900$, but it should be $64 - 46 = 18$ more: 918.
Step 2$918 \div 20 = 45.9$.
Answer(b) 45.9
