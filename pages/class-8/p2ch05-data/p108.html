Example 6

The mean of $4$, $6$, $8$ and $x$ is $7$. Find $x$.

Solution.

Step 1The four values add to $4 \times 7 = 28$
Step 2$x = 28 - 4 - 6 - 8 = 10$
Check$4 + 6 + 8 + 10 = 28$, and $28 \div 4 = 7$
Answer$10$

Example 7

For the data $2$, $3$, $3$, $5$, $7$, $10$, match List I with List II.

List IList II
(P) The mean(1) 8
(Q) The median(2) 30
(R) The largest minus the smallest(3) 5
(S) The sum(4) 4
  1. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2

Solution.

Step 1The sum is $30$, so the mean is $30 \div 6 = 5$
Step 2The median is $\frac{3 + 5}{2} = 4$, and $10 - 2 = 8$
Answer(d) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
