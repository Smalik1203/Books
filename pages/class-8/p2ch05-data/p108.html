Example 13 · Numerical answer

One section of 25 students has a mean mark of 40, and another of 15 students has a mean mark of 48. Find the mean mark of all 40 students.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Total $= 25 \times 40 + 15 \times 48 = 1000 + 720 = 1720$.
Step 2$1720 \div 40 = 43$.
Answer43
Example 14 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) The mean of 2, 3, 3, 5, 7, 10(1) 8
(Q) The median of 2, 3, 3, 5, 7, 10(2) 30
(R) The difference between the highest and lowest of 2, 3, 3, 5, 7, 10(3) 5
(S) The sum of 2, 3, 3, 5, 7, 10(4) 4

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2

Solution.

Step 1The sum is 30, the mean $30 \div 6 = 5$, the median $\frac{3 + 5}{2} = 4$, and $10 - 2 = 8$.
Answer(d) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
