Example 12

Shop A sold $210$ school bags in a year and Shop B sold $240$. A poster shows both as bars on an axis that starts at $200$. How many times as tall as A's bar is B's, and how do the sales really compare?

Solution.

Step 1 $210 - 200 = 10$ and $240 - 200 = 40$ what each bar shows
Step 2 $40 \div 10 = 4$ the look of the bars
Step 3 $240 - 210 = 30$ and $30 \div 210 = \tfrac{1}{7}$ the true comparison
Answer B's bar is $4$ times as tall, but B sold only $\tfrac{1}{7}$ more than A

Example 13

Priya colours a strip of $48$ half-hour boxes for one day, as Manoj did. Her key gives one colour each to sleep, school and travel. She colours $18$ boxes for sleep, $14$ for school and $4$ for travel, and the rest are other things. How many hours went to each?

Solution.

Step 1 one box is half an hour the key's rule
Step 2 $18 \div 2 = 9$, $\ 14 \div 2 = 7$, $\ 4 \div 2 = 2$ boxes to hours
Step 3 $48 - 18 - 14 - 4 = 12$ boxes, and $12 \div 2 = 6$ everything else
Answer Sleep $9$ hours, school $7$, travel $2$, other things $6$

The four answers add to $24$ hours, which is the check that no box was missed.
