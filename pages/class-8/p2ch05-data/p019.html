Exercise Set 5.3
  1. Meera measures a plant's height every Monday for six weeks: $4$, $6$, $9$, $13$, $16$ and $18$ cm. Draw the line graph. Between which two Mondays did the plant grow most? Estimate its height halfway between the third and fourth Mondays, and say what your estimate assumes.
  1. Say whether a line graph or a bar graph suits each set of data, and give a reason.
    1. A baby's mass each month
    2. Students present each day
    3. A candle's height every $10$ minutes
    4. The favourite sports of a class
  1. A line graph joins these readings of a city's temperature: $19^\circ$ at $6$ am, $24^\circ$ at $10$ am, $29^\circ$ at $2$ pm and $25^\circ$ at $6$ pm. Say whether each claim follows from the graph, goes against it, or is not settled by it.
    1. It was warmer at $2$ pm than at $10$ am.
    2. It was exactly $26.5^\circ$ at noon.
    3. The temperature was never above $29^\circ$.
    4. It was colder at $6$ pm than at $6$ am.
  1. Say what is wrong with each graph.
    1. A bar graph of five students' heights whose vertical axis runs from $130$ cm to $160$ cm.
    2. A line graph of a town's rainfall in $2000$, $2005$, $2015$ and $2020$, with the four years spaced evenly.
    3. A line graph of the favourite colours of a class.
  1. A poster shows a bar graph of a shop's sales. Last year's bar is $2$ cm tall and this year's is $5$ cm tall, under the words sales more than doubled. The axis begins at $400$, and $1$ cm stands for $25$ sales. Find the two sales figures, and the true rise as a fraction of last year's sales.
  1. On a strip of $48$ half-hour boxes, Ishita colours $16$ boxes for sleep, $12$ for school, $3$ for travel, $5$ for eating and the rest for other things. How many hours did she spend on other things? What fraction of the day was she asleep?
