1. A square sheet of paper is folded in half and cut along the fold into two rectangles. Whatever the size of the square, one of these statements is always true. Which one?
    1. The area of each rectangle is more than the area of the square.
    2. The square’s perimeter is more than the sum of the rectangles’ perimeters.
    3. The rectangles’ perimeters always add up to $1\frac{1}{2}$ times the square’s perimeter.
    4. The square’s area is always three times the sum of the rectangles’ areas.
