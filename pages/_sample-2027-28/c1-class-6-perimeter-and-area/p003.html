Who ran further?

Akshi and Toshi run on the two rectangular tracks in Fig. 6.3. Akshi runs on the outer track and finishes 5 rounds. Toshi runs on the inner track and finishes 7 rounds. Who ran the longer distance?

Each track is a rectangle. Akshi’s track is 70 m long and 40 m wide. In one round she runs its perimeter, $2 \times (70\text{ m} + 40\text{ m}) = 220\text{ m}$. Toshi’s inner track is 60 m long and 30 m wide, so her round is shorter.
