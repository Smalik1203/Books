Example 1 · Single correct

A rectangle has perimeter 74 cm and length 23 cm. What is its area?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 322 cm²
  2. 644 cm²
  3. 1702 cm²
  4. 148 cm²

Solution.

Step 1Length + breadth = 74 ÷ 2 = 37 cm, so breadth = 37 − 23 = 14 cm.
Step 2Area = 23 × 14 = 322 cm².
Answer(a) 322 cm²
Example 2 · Single correct

A 4 cm by 3 cm rectangle is removed from a corner of a 10 cm by 8 cm rectangle. What is the perimeter of the remaining L-shape?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 22 cm
  2. 29 cm
  3. 43 cm
  4. 36 cm

Solution.

Step 1The original perimeter is 2 × (10 + 8) = 36 cm.
Step 2The removed outer lengths 4 cm and 3 cm are replaced by inner edges of the same lengths, so the perimeter stays 36 cm.
Answer(d) 36 cm
Example 3 · Multiple correct

Which rectangles have perimeter 30 cm?

Select all correct options.

  1. 10 cm by 5 cm
  2. 9 cm by 6 cm
  3. 12 cm by 3 cm
  4. 8 cm by 8 cm

Solution.

Step 1A perimeter of 30 cm requires length + breadth = 15 cm.
Step 2The first three pairs add to 15. The last adds to 16 and has perimeter 32 cm.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
