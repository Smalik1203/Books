Example 9 · Paragraph-based

A farmer uses all of her 24 m of fencing to make a rectangular pen whose sides are whole metres.

  1. Which pen has the largest area?
    1. 11 m by 1 m
    2. 8 m by 4 m
    3. 6 m by 6 m
    4. 7 m by 5 m
  2. Numerical answer. How many differently shaped pens can she make?

Solution.

Step 1Length + breadth = 12 m. The options have areas 11, 32, 36 and 35 m².
Step 2The pairs are 11 and 1, 10 and 2, 9 and 3, 8 and 4, 7 and 5, 6 and 6.
Answer(i) (c) 6 m by 6 m; (ii) 6
