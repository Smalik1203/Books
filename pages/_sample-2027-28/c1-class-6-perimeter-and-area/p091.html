1. Assertion (A): A triangle with one side of a 6 cm by 4 cm rectangle as its base, and its top corner on the opposite side, has an area of 12 sq cm.

    Reason (R): Such a triangle always has half the area of the rectangle.

  1. Assertion (A): Doubling the side of a square doubles its area.

    Reason (R): Doubling the side of a square doubles its perimeter.

  1. Assertion (A): A rectangle 8 cm by 2 cm has a longer perimeter than a square of side 4 cm.

    Reason (R): The rectangle and the square have the same area.
