Case-based questions

  1. Class 7 of a school is going on a trip to a museum. The bus costs a fixed ₹2500, and a museum ticket costs ₹40 a student. The total cost of the trip is ₹4500.

    1. Write an expression for the total cost if $s$ students go.
    2. Write an equation for the number of students. Solve it, and check your answer by substituting it.
    3. The students share the total cost equally. How much does each student pay?
    4. If 10 more students had gone, what would the total cost have been?
