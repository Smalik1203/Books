Ancient Indian mathematicians were very good at turning hard mathematical ideas into simple methods, so that anyone could use them to solve problems.

Bijaganita, or algebra, uses letters to solve problems. With it we find general patterns in numbers, in shapes and in everyday situations, and show why a claim is true, such as the claim that the sum of two odd numbers is always even. We hope that you enjoy using it!
