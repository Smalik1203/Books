Example 3 · Multiple correct

Which equations have the solution $m = -4$?

Select all correct options.

  1. $2m + 5 = -3$
  2. $m - 4 = 0$
  3. $3m = -12$
  4. $10 - m = 14$

Solution.

Step 1Put $m = -4$: $2 \times (-4) + 5 = -3$, $3 \times (-4) = -12$ and $10 - (-4) = 14$.
Step 2But $-4 - 4 = -8$, not 0.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
Example 4 · Multiple correct

Brahmagupta’s rule solves $Ax + B = Cx + D$ as $x = (D - B) \div (A - C)$. Use it on $7x - 2 = 3x + 10$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $x = (10 - (-2)) \div (7 - 3)$
  2. $x = 3$
  3. $x = (10 + 2) \div (7 + 3)$
  4. When $x = 3$, both sides equal 19.

Solution.

Step 1Here $A = 7$, $B = -2$, $C = 3$ and $D = 10$, so $x = 12 \div 4 = 3$.
Step 2Check: $7 \times 3 - 2 = 19$ and $3 \times 3 + 10 = 19$. The denominator is $A - C$, not $A + C$.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 5 · Numerical answer

A father is 3 times as old as his son. In 12 years he will be twice as old as his son. How old is the son now, in years?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Let the son be $s$ years old. Then $3s + 12 = 2(s + 12)$.
Step 2$3s + 12 = 2s + 24$, so $s = 12$. Check: 36 and 12 now; 48 and 24 in 12 years.
