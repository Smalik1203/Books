In Method 1, Step $k$ has a middle tile with three arms of $k$ tiles, so it has $k + k + k + 1 = 3k + 1$ tiles. In Method 2, Step $k$ has a top row of $2k + 1$ tiles and $k$ tiles below it, so it has $k + (2k + 1) = 3k + 1$ tiles. Both ways give $3k + 1$ tiles at Step $k$.

To find out whether some Step $k$ uses 100 tiles, we can solve the equation $3k + 1 = 100$. Find the value of $k$.

Example 6

Two friends are saving money. Jahnavi starts with ₹4000 and saves ₹650 more every month. Sunita starts with ₹5050 and saves ₹500 every month. After how many months will they have the same amount of money?

Let $m$ be the number of months after which their savings are equal. After $m$ months, Jahnavi has $4000 + 650m$ rupees and Sunita has $5050 + 500m$ rupees. These amounts are equal, so we get this equation.

$4000 + 650m = 5050 + 500m$

$4000 + 650m - 500m = 5050$   (subtracting $500m$ from both sides)

$4000 + 150m = 5050$

$150m = 5050 - 4000$   (subtracting 4000 from both sides)

$150m = 1050$

$m = 1050 \div 150 = 7$   (dividing both sides by 150)

So after 7 months, they will both have the same amount of money. Check the answer by working out how much each of them has after 7 months.
