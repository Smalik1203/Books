  1. A class is seated in a hall with $r$ equal rows of chairs. When 12 children sit in each row, 8 children are left standing. When 14 children sit in each row instead, every child has a seat and 4 chairs are empty.

    1. Which equation is true?
      1. $12r - 8 = 14r + 4$
      2. $12r + 8 = 14r + 4$
      3. $12r + 8 = 14r - 4$
      4. $12r - 8 = 14r - 4$
    2. How many children are in the class?
    3. One more row of 14 chairs is added, and all the children sit down. How many chairs are now empty?
