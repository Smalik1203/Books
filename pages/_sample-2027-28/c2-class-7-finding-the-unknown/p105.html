Example 8 · Paragraph-based

A pan balance has 5 identical packets and a 200 g weight on its left pan, and 2 of the same packets and an 800 g weight on its right pan. The scale is balanced.

  1. What does one packet weigh?
    1. 250 g
    2. 200 g
    3. 300 g
    4. 120 g
  2. Numerical answer. The 800 g weight is taken off the right pan. How many more packets must be put on that pan to balance the scale again?

Solution.

Step 1Let one packet weigh $p$ g. Then $5p + 200 = 2p + 800$. So $3p = 600$, and one packet weighs $p = 200$ g.
Step 2The 800 g weight must be replaced by $800 \div 200 = 4$ packets.
Answer(i) (b) 200 g; (ii) 4
