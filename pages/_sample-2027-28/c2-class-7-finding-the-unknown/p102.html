Let the son be $s$ years old now. In 4 years, he will be $s + 4$ and his father will be $s + 36$. So $s + 36 = 3(s + 4) = 3s + 12$. Subtracting $s$ and 12 from both sides gives $24 = 2s$, so $s = 12$. The son is 12 and his father is 44. In 4 years they will be 16 and 48, and 48 is three times 16.

Example 1 · Single correct

A pen costs ₹12 more than a pencil. Three pens and five pencils cost ₹84 in all. What does one pencil cost?

Choose one correct option.

  1. ₹18
  2. ₹9
  3. ₹6
  4. ₹10.50

Solution.

Step 1Let a pencil cost ₹$p$; a pen costs ₹$(p + 12)$. Then $3(p + 12) + 5p = 84$.
Step 2$8p + 36 = 84$, so $8p = 48$ and $p = 6$.
Step 3Check: a pen costs ₹18, and $3 \times 18 + 5 \times 6 = 54 + 30 = 84$.
Answer(c) ₹6
Example 2 · Single correct

Rahul writes: $\frac{x}{3} - 2 = 5$, so $x - 2 = 15$, so $x = 17$. Which statement is true?

Choose one correct option.

  1. Rahul’s solution is correct.
  2. His first step is wrong, and the solution is 21.
  3. His first step is wrong, and the solution is 9.
  4. His second step is wrong, and the solution is 13.

Solution.

Step 1He multiplied only $\frac{x}{3}$ by 3, not the 2. Correctly, $\frac{x}{3} = 5 + 2 = 7$.
Step 2$x = 7 \times 3 = 21$.
Step 3Check: $21 \div 3 - 2 = 7 - 2 = 5$, so 21 is correct.
Answer(b) His first step is wrong, and the solution is 21.
