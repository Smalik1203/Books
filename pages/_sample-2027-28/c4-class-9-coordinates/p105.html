Example 6 · Multiple correct

Consider $A(1, 0)$, $B(5, 3)$ and $C(-1, 11)$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $AB = 5$
  2. $BC = 10$
  3. Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $B$.
  4. Its area is 25.

Solution.

Step 1$AB = \sqrt{16 + 9} = 5$, $BC = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$ and $AC^2 = 4 + 121 = 125$.
Step 2$5^2 + 10^2 = 125 = AC^2$, so the right angle is at $B$, and the area is $\frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 10 = 25$.
Answer(a), (b), (c), (d)
Example 7 · Numerical answer

Three villages stand at $A(0, 0)$, $B(8, 0)$ and $C(0, 6)$, in kilometres. A tank is to be equally far from all three. How far is that, in km?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The right angle at $A$ puts the point at the midpoint of $BC$, $(4, 3)$, which is $\sqrt{16 + 9} = 5$ km from each village.
Answer5
Example 8 · Numerical answer

On a town map one unit is one kilometre, and the streets run along the grid lines. How many kilometres is it by street from $(-3, 1)$ to $(4, 5)$?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1By street it is $4 - (-3) = 7$ across and $5 - 1 = 4$ up: $7 + 4 = 11$ km, whatever the turns.
Answer11
