Where this goes next

With two numbers you can name any point of the plane. That is enough to locate a doorway, to measure a distance without a ruler, and to check whether three points lie on one line. In Class 10 you will use the same pair of numbers in equations, not just for single points. Then a line or a curve becomes an equation that you can work with using algebra.

Descartes joined geometry and algebra, using ideas that began in Bhārat. From now on you will often move between the two: from shapes to equations, and from equations back to shapes.
