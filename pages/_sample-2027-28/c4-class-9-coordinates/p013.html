The images are $A' (-3, 4)$, $D' (-7, 1)$ and $M' (-9, 6)$. Find the legs of the right-angled triangle exactly as before, keeping the negative signs:

$C'D' =$ x-coordinate of $A' -$ x-coordinate of $D' = -3 - (-7) = 4$

$A'C' =$ y-coordinate of $A' -$ y-coordinate of $D' = 4 - 1 = 3$

We get the same 4 and the same 3, so the Baudhāyana–Pythagoras Theorem gives the same answer: $A'D' = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5$ units. The other two sides work the same way, because squaring removes the minus signs:

$D'M' = \sqrt{(-2)^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{29}$ units

$M'A' = \sqrt{(-6)^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{40}$ units

Reflection has moved the triangle without changing the length of a single side.
