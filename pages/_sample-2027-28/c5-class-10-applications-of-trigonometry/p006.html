Example 4

From a point on a bridge across a river, the angles of depression of the banks on opposite sides of the river are $30^\circ$ and $45^\circ$. If the bridge is 3 m above the banks, find the width of the river.

Solution.

Step 1 in Fig. 9.10, A and B are on opposite banks, so AB is the width of the river; P on the bridge is 3 m above the banks, so ${DP = 3}$ m
Step 2 we need the side AB of $\triangle APB$, and ${AB = AD + DB}$
Step 3 in right $\triangle APD$, ${\angle A = 30^\circ}$ alternate angles
Step 4 $\tan 30^\circ = \dfrac{PD}{AD}$,   so   $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{3}{AD}$,   which gives   ${AD = 3\sqrt{3}}$
Step 5 in right $\triangle PBD$, ${\angle B = 45^\circ}$, so ${BD = PD = 3}$ m
Step 6 ${AB = BD + AD = 3 + 3\sqrt{3} = 3(1 + \sqrt{3})}$
Answer The river is ${3(1 + \sqrt{3})}$ m wide
