1. An 8 m ladder stands in a street between two walls. Against one wall it makes $60^\circ$ with the ground; turned about its foot to the other, $30^\circ$. Which statements are correct?
    1. The street is ${4(1 + \sqrt{3})}$ m wide.
    2. The ladder reaches 4 m up the first wall.
    3. The two positions of the ladder are at right angles to each other.
    4. The two points where the top of the ladder touches the walls are 8 m apart.
  1. A man on a jetty has his eyes 20 m above the still water of a lake. He sees a cloud at an elevation of $30^\circ$, and its reflection in the water at a depression of $60^\circ$. The reflection is as far below the surface as the cloud is above it. Which statements are correct?
    1. The cloud is 40 m above the water.
    2. The cloud is 20 m from the man, measured horizontally.
    3. The line of sight to the reflection is 40 m long.
    4. The line of sight to the cloud is 40 m long.
