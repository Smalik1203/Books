Example 6 · Numerical answer

A tower's top is at an elevation of $45^\circ$ from 50 m away. Find its height, in m.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$\tan 45^\circ = \frac{h}{50}$.
Step 2$h = 50$ m.
Answer50
Example 7 · Numerical answer

A 9 m pole casts a $3\sqrt{3}$ m shadow. Find the sun’s elevation, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$\tan\theta = 9 \div 3\sqrt{3} = \sqrt{3}$.
Step 2So $\theta = 60$.
Answer60
Example 8 · Matching

A tower's top is seen from 30 m away. Match each elevation with its height, in m.

List IList II
(P) Elevation $30^\circ$(1) $30\sqrt{3}$
(Q) Elevation $45^\circ$(2) 60
(R) Elevation $60^\circ$(3) $10\sqrt{3}$
(S) An elevation whose tangent is 2(4) 30
  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The height is $30\tan\theta$: $30 \div \sqrt{3} = 10\sqrt{3}$ and $30 \times 1 = 30$.
Step 2$30 \times \sqrt{3} = 30\sqrt{3}$ and $30 \times 2 = 60$.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
