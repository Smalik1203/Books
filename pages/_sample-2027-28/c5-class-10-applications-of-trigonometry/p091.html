1. Assertion (A): The angle of elevation of the top of a tower gets larger as an observer walks towards it on level ground.

    Reason (R): The angle of elevation of an object from a point is always greater than the angle of depression of the point from the object.

  1. Assertion (A): A ladder 8 m long that makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the ground reaches ${4\sqrt{3}}$ m up the wall.

    Reason (R): ${\cos 60^\circ = \frac{1}{2}}$.

Very short answer
  1. A ladder makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the ground, and its foot is 2.5 m from the wall. Find the length of the ladder.
  1. From a point on the ground, the angle of elevation of the top of a tower 33 m high is $60^\circ$. How far is the point from the foot of the tower?
Short answer
  1. A boy whose eyes are 1.2 m above the ground stands 18.8 m from a tree. The angle of elevation of the top of the tree from his eyes is $45^\circ$. Find the height of the tree.
  1. From the top of a tower 90 m high, the angles of depression of the top and the bottom of a pole are $45^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Find the height of the pole. (Take ${\sqrt{3} = 1.732}$.)
  1. Two men stand on opposite sides of a tower 60 m high, in a line with its foot. They see its top at angles of elevation of $30^\circ$ and $45^\circ$. Find the distance between the men. (Take ${\sqrt{3} = 1.732}$.)
  1. An observer whose eyes are 1.5 m above the ground is 20.5 m away from a tower 22 m high. Find the angle of elevation of the top of the tower from her eyes.
Long answer
  1. From the top of a tower, the angles of depression of the top and the bottom of a building 10 m high are $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Find the height of the tower and the distance between the tower and the building. (Take ${\sqrt{3} = 1.732}$.)
  1. A pole $h$ m tall stands on top of a tower. From a point on the ground, the angles of elevation of the bottom and the top of the pole are $\alpha$ and $\beta$. Prove that the height of the tower is $\dfrac{h\tan\alpha}{\tan\beta - \tan\alpha}$ m.
  1. A balloon rises straight up from a point A on level ground. From a point B, 100 m from A, the angle of elevation of the balloon changes from $30^\circ$ to $60^\circ$ in 20 seconds. Find the speed at which the balloon rises. (Take ${\sqrt{3} = 1.732}$.)
