Example 9 · Paragraph-based

From a roof 20 m high, a water tower's top is at an elevation of $60^\circ$ and its foot at a depression of $30^\circ$.

  1. The horizontal distance to the tower is
    1. 20 m
    2. ${20\sqrt{3}}$ m
    3. ${10\sqrt{3}}$ m
    4. 60 m
  2. Numerical answer. How tall is the water tower, in metres?

Solution.

Step 1${d \tan 30^\circ = 20}$, so the tower is ${d = 20\sqrt{3}}$ m away.
Step 2Its top is ${20\sqrt{3} \tan 60^\circ = 60}$ m above the roof, so it is 80 m tall.
Answer(i) (b) ${20\sqrt{3}}$ m; (ii) 80
