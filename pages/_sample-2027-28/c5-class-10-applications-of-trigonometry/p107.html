Paragraph-based

  1. A drone hovers at a point P above a straight, level road. D is the point on the road directly below it. Ravi stands at A and Meena at B, on the road on opposite sides of D, and ${AB = 80}$ m. Ravi sees the drone at an angle of elevation of $30^\circ$, and Meena sees it at $60^\circ$.

    1. The height of the drone above the road is
      1. 20 m
      2. ${20\sqrt{3}}$ m
      3. 40 m
      4. ${40\sqrt{3}}$ m
    2. How far is the drone from Meena, in metres?
    3. The drone now comes straight down until Meena sees it at an angle of elevation of $45^\circ$. How high above the road is it now, in metres?
