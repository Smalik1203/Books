Example 6 · Matching

Match the short sides of each right-angled triangle with its hypotenuse.

List IList II
(P) Short sides 5 and 12(1) 25
(Q) Short sides 8 and 15(2) 29
(R) Short sides 7 and 24(3) 13
(S) Short sides 20 and 21(4) 17

Each entry has exactly one match. Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2

Solution.

Step 1$5^2 + 12^2 = 13^2$ and $8^2 + 15^2 = 17^2$.
Step 2$7^2 + 24^2 = 25^2$ and $20^2 + 21^2 = 29^2$.
Answer(d) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
Example 7 · Paragraph-based

A laptop screen is 32 cm wide and 24 cm tall. Screens are sold by their diagonal.

  1. What is the diagonal of this screen?
    1. 28 cm
    2. 40 cm
    3. 56 cm
    4. 48 cm
  2. Numerical answer. A screen of the same shape has a diagonal of 50 cm. What is its area in cm²?

Solution.

Step 1$32^2 + 24^2 = 1024 + 576 = 1600 = 40^2$, so the diagonal is 40 cm.
Step 2A 50 cm diagonal is $\tfrac54$ of 40 cm, so each side is $\tfrac54$ as long: 40 cm and 30 cm.
Step 3Area $= 40 \times 30 = 1200$ cm².
Answer(i) (b) 40 cm; (ii) 1200
