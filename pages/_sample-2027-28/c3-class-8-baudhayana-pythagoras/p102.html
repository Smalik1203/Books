Example 1 · Single correct

A right-angled triangle has short sides 20 cm and 48 cm. What is its hypotenuse? Choose one correct option.

  1. 52 cm
  2. 68 cm
  3. 56 cm
  4. 44 cm

Solution.

Step 1$20^2 + 48^2 = 400 + 2304 = 2704$.
Step 2$\sqrt{2704} = 52$.
Answer(a) 52 cm
Example 2 · Single correct

A square has an area of $18 \text{ cm}^2$. What is the side of the square built on its diagonal? Choose one correct option.

  1. $3\sqrt{2}$ cm
  2. 6 cm
  3. 9 cm
  4. 36 cm

Solution.

Step 1The square on the diagonal has twice the area: $36 \text{ cm}^2$.
Step 2Its side is $\sqrt{36} = 6$ cm.
Answer(b) 6 cm
