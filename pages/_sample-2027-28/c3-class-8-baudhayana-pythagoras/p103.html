Example 3 · Multiple correct

Which of these are Baudhāyana triples? Select all correct options.

  1. $(27, 36, 45)$
  2. $(12, 35, 37)$
  3. $(6, 7, 9)$
  4. $(13, 84, 85)$

Solution.

Step 1$27^2 + 36^2 = 2025 = 45^2$ and $12^2 + 35^2 = 1369 = 37^2$.
Step 2$13^2 + 84^2 = 7225 = 85^2$.
Step 3$6^2 + 7^2 = 85$, but $9^2 = 81$, so $(6, 7, 9)$ is not a triple.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 4 · Multiple correct

A carpenter makes a frame 60 cm by 80 cm and measures its diagonal as 99 cm. Which statements are correct? Select all correct options.

  1. A frame with right-angled corners would have a diagonal of 100 cm.
  2. The frame is not a true rectangle.
  3. Every corner of the frame is exactly $90^\circ$.
  4. The diagonal is 1 cm shorter than it should be.

Solution.

Step 1$60^2 + 80^2 = 3600 + 6400 = 10\,000 = 100^2$.
Step 2A diagonal of 99 cm is 1 cm short of that.
Step 3So the corners are not right angles, and the frame is not a true rectangle.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 5 · Numerical answer

A rectangle is 7 cm wide, and its diagonal is 1 cm longer than its length. Find its length, in cm. Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1With length $l$: $(l + 1)^2 = l^2 + 7^2$.
Step 2$2l + 1 = 49$, so $l = 24$ cm, and the diagonal is 25 cm.
Check$7^2 + 24^2 = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25^2$.
Answer24
