Assertion and reason

In Questions 13 to 16, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): A triangle with sides $6$, $7$ and $10$ is not right-angled.

    Reason (R): In a right-angled triangle, the square on the longest side equals the sum of the squares on the other two.

  1. Assertion (A): $(30, 72, 78)$ is a Baudhāyana triple.

    Reason (R): Every Baudhāyana triple is primitive.

  1. Assertion (A): The square built on the diagonal of a square of side $4$ cm has an area of $16$ cm².

    Reason (R): The square built on the diagonal of a square has twice its area.

  1. Assertion (A): $2.2 < \sqrt5 < 2.3$.

    Reason (R): $\sqrt5$ is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle with short sides $1$ and $2$.
