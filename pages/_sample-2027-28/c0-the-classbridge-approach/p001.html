Part 1
The ClassBridge Approach

How the books are built, how each topic is taught, and what every part of a page is for, shown with pages from the sample chapters in Part 2.

ClassBridge Mathematics follows the prescribed syllabus for every class from 6 to 10, chapter for chapter and in the order it is taught. Nothing in a chapter is moved or cut to make room for something else. What the series adds is placed after the chapter, in two parts of its own, so that a teacher can teach the chapter as it is written and a student can see at once which kind of work a page is asking for.

This part of the book explains that design. Section 1 shows how a chapter is built, and Section 2 how each topic inside it is taught. Sections 3 to 5 take the three parts of a chapter in turn and show each of their components as it prints, with a note on what it is for. Section 6 shows how the mathematics grows from Class 6 to Class 10.

1How a chapter is built

Every chapter has three parts, and they are read in order. Each part opens with a band that names it, and its name is carried in the running head at the top of every page, so a reader who opens the book anywhere knows which part they are in.
