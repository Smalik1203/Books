LearnLab

The experiential learning app, beside all three parts. Every chapter has lessons, interactive simulations and practice on screen. A student can change a value, watch what happens, and return to the page to explain why.

The three parts serve three needs, and keeping them apart is what lets each one be done properly. The chapter is taught in class and is the same for every student. By the Book is the practice a school examination asks for, in the forms its papers use. Beyond the Book is for the student who is ready to go further, and it goes there without the chapter changing to make room for it.

2How a topic is taught

Inside the chapter, every topic follows the same path. A student who knows the path knows where to look: for the rule when revising, for a worked example when stuck, and for questions when ready to practise.

1
Begin with a question

A situation from everyday life, or a question to try. A student who has made a guess reads on to find out whether it was right.

2
Explain in plain words

Short sentences and the words a student already knows. A new term is set in colour where it first appears, and a figure is drawn beside the text that needs it.

3
State the key idea

The rule or definition in its exact wording, between two rules. It is what a student revises from the night before a test.

4
Work an example

What is given, then one step to a line with its reason beside it, then the answer. A student can follow the method as well as the result.

5
Stop and think

Think and Reflect asks why a method works, or what would change if the question did. It turns a rule that has been used into one that is understood.

6
Practise

An exercise set at the end of the topic, rising from routine questions to one that needs thought.
