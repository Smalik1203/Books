3Inside the chapter

These are the components of a chapter, each shown as it prints in the sample chapters, with a note on what it does. Every chapter in the series is built from the same few, so a student meets the same parts in the same places from Class 6 to Class 10. The excerpts come from the Class 6 and Class 7 chapters in Part 2, where each can be found in its place, and they are printed here exactly as they are printed there.

1
A section and its key idea

A numbered section head, the idea explained with its new term in colour, and the key idea set between two rules in its exact wording.

The perimeter of a closed figure is the distance around its boundary, going around it once. A polygon is a closed figure made of line segments. To find its perimeter, add the lengths of all its sides.

Perimeter of a polygon

The perimeter of a polygon is the sum of the lengths of all its sides.

2
A figure and stepped working

The figure is drawn for the question beside it, and the working is set one step to a line, so a student can see where each line comes from.

Perimeter $= AB + BC + CD + DA$

$= AB + BC + AB + BC$

$= 2 \times (AB + BC)$

$= 2 \times (12\text{ cm} + 8\text{ cm}) = 40\text{ cm}$
