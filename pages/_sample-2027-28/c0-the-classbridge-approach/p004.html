A worked example

The question, then the thinking, then each step with its reason, then the answer.

Example 1

We know that $14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14 + 88 = 13353$. What is the value of $14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14$?

Solution.

The expression is exactly the left-hand side without the term $+ 88$, and we already know the value of the left-hand side.

Step 1 $14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14 + 88 - 88 = 13353 - 88$ subtract 88 from both
Answer $14593 - 1459 + 145 - 14 = 13265$

A tip

A short reminder beside the questions it helps with, often about an easy slip.

Perimeter is a length, so it is measured in cm or m. Area is a number of squares, so it is measured in sq cm or sq m. Check the unit before you write an answer.

What a page leaves out is designed as carefully. No question carries a mark or a label saying how hard it is; the order of the questions does that. A page uses two typefaces and three working colours, the same way in every chapter, so the colour of a box tells a student what kind of box it is before a word of it is read.
