5Beyond the Book

Beyond the Book is where a chapter meets the competitive examinations. It adds nothing to the syllabus: every question uses the chapter's own mathematics. What changes is the kind of question. It joins two ideas, or gives no hint of which idea to use, and it is answered with a choice of options or a single number rather than a written solution. In Classes 6 to 8 the questions lean towards the reasoning of the mathematics olympiads; in Classes 9 and 10, towards the longer, multi-step problems of engineering and other entrance examinations. It has three stages, each opened by a numbered head.
