Example 1

In a Mathematics paper out of 100 marks, 30 students of Class 10 in a school got the marks below. Find the mean of their marks.

Marks ($x_i$)10203640505660
Number of students ($f_i$)1134324
Marks ($x_i$)707280889295
Number of students ($f_i$)411231

Solution.

Step 1 multiply each mark $x_i$ by its frequency $f_i$, and put the products in a third column, as in Table 13.1 the sum of all the marks
Step 2 $\bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum f_ix_i}{\sum f_i} = \dfrac{1779}{30} = 59.3$ the totals of Table 13.1
Answer The mean mark of the students is 59.3
