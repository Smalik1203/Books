Example 8

The median of the data below is 525, and the total frequency is 100. Find the values of $x$ and $y$.

Solution.

Step 1 add a row of cumulative frequencies, as in the tables below
Step 2 $76 + x + y = 100$,   that is,   $x + y = 24$   (1) given that ${n = 100}$
Step 3 525 lies in the class 500 – 600, so ${l = 500}$, ${f = 20}$, ${cf = 36 + x}$ and ${h = 100}$ the median class
Step 4 $525 = 500 + \left(\dfrac{50 - 36 - x}{20}\right) \times 100$ the median formula
Step 5 $525 - 500 = (14 - x) \times 5$
Step 6 $25 = 70 - 5x$
Step 7 $5x = 70 - 25 = 45$,   so   $x = 9$
Step 8 ${9 + y = 24}$, so ${y = 15}$ from (1)
Answer $x = 9$ and ${y = 15}$

You have now studied all three measures of central tendency. Which one suits a particular purpose best?

The mean is the measure used most often. It uses every observation, it lies between the largest and the smallest, and it lets you compare distributions: the mean results of different schools in one examination show which school did better.
