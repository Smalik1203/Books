Remark. All three methods give the same result, so choose a method by looking at the numbers. If the $x_i$ and $f_i$ are small, the direct method is a good choice. If they are large, use the assumed mean method or the step-deviation method. If the classes are of different sizes and the $x_i$ are large, you can still use the step-deviation method: take $h$ to be a number that divides every $d_i$.
