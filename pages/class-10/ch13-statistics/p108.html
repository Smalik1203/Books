Example 15 · Matching

For the data 2, 4, 4, 5, 7, 8, match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) The mean(1) 4
(Q) The median(2) 6
(R) The mode(3) 5
(S) The range(4) 4.5

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The sum is 30, so the mean is 5; the middle two values are 4 and 5, so the median is 4.5.
Step 24 occurs most often, and $8 - 2 = 6$.
Answer(b) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
