Example 11

The less than cumulative frequencies of the classes 10 – 20, 20 – 30, 30 – 40, 40 – 50 and 50 – 60 are 4, 16, 34, 45 and 50. The median class and its frequency are

  1. 30 – 40; 34
  2. 30 – 40; 18
  3. 20 – 30; 12
  4. 50 – 60; 5

Solution.

Step 1 $n = 50$, so $\frac{n}{2} = 25$ the last cumulative frequency
Step 2 the first cumulative frequency greater than 25 is 34, so the median class is 30 – 40
Step 3 its frequency is ${34 - 16 = 18}$ take off the class before
Answer (b)

Option (a) gives the cumulative frequency in place of the frequency, (c) stops at a class whose cumulative frequency, 16, is still below 25, and (d) looks for $n$ instead of $\frac{n}{2}$.

Example 12

A distribution has 50 observations. Its median class is 30 – 40, with frequency 10, and the cumulative frequency of the class before it is 22. The median is

  1. 58
  2. 23
  3. 33
  4. 35

Solution.

Step 1 $\frac{n}{2} = 25$, $l = 30$, $cf = 22$, $f = 10$, $h = 10$
Step 2 $\text{Median} = 30 + \left(\dfrac{25 - 22}{10}\right) \times 10 = 30 + 3 = 33$ the median formula
Answer (c)

Option (a) uses $n$ in place of $\frac{n}{2}$, (b) uses the cumulative frequency of the median class itself, 32, and (d) is only the class mark.
