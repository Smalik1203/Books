Example 5

In the step-deviation method, ${a = 45}$, ${h = 10}$, ${\sum f_i = 40}$ and ${\sum f_iu_i = -8}$. The mean is

  1. 43
  2. 47
  3. 44.8
  4. 37

Solution.

Step 1 $\bar{u} = \dfrac{\sum f_iu_i}{\sum f_i} = \dfrac{-8}{40} = -0.2$
Step 2 $\bar{x} = a + h\bar{u} = 45 + 10 \times (-0.2) = 45 - 2 = 43$ undo the shift and scale
Answer (a)

Option (b) adds the 2, (c) forgets $h$, and (d) forgets both $h$ and $\sum f_i$.

Example 6

The daily wages of 50 workers are given below. Find the mean daily wage by the step-deviation method.

Daily wage (in ₹)200 – 240240 – 280280 – 320320 – 360360 – 400
Number of workers81215105

Solution.

Step 1 the class marks are 220, 260, 300, 340 and 380; take $a = 300$ and $h = 40$ the class size
Step 2 $u_i = \dfrac{x_i - 300}{40}$ is $-2$, $-1$, 0, 1 and 2
Step 3 $\sum f_iu_i = -16 - 12 + 0 + 10 + 10 = -8$
Step 4 $\bar{x} = 300 + 40 \times \dfrac{-8}{50} = 300 - 6.4 = 293.6$ $\bar{x} = a + h\bar{u}$
Answer The mean daily wage is ₹$293.60$
