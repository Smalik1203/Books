Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which statements about grouped data are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. A class mark is (lower limit + upper limit) $\div$ 2.
  2. The modal class has the highest frequency.
  3. The median class contains the $\frac{n}{2}$th value.
  4. The mean is always one of the class marks.

Solution.

Step 1(a), (b) and (c) are the definitions the chapter uses.
Step 2The mean is a weighted average of the class marks, and is usually none of them.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

The classes 0–10, 10–20, 20–30, 30–40 and 40–50 have frequencies 5, 8, 12, 7 and 8. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. There are 40 values.
  2. The modal class is 20–30.
  3. The cumulative frequency up to 30 is 23.
  4. The median class is 30–40.

Solution.

Step 1$5 + 8 + 12 + 7 + 8 = 40$, and 12 is the largest frequency.
Step 2The cumulative frequencies are 5, 13, 25, 32, 40: up to 30 it is 25, and the 20th value is in 20–30.
Answer(a), (b)
