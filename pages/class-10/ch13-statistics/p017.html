Example 7

A survey of the heights, in centimetres, of 51 girls of Class 10 in a school gave these results. Find the median height.

Height less than (in cm)140145150155160165
Number of girls41129404651

Solution.

Step 1 140, 145, 150, …, 165 are the upper limits of the classes, so the classes are below 140, 140 – 145, 145 – 150, …, 160 – 165 a less than type distribution
Step 2 4 girls are shorter than 140 cm, so the class below 140 has frequency 4; 11 are shorter than 145 cm, so ${11 - 4 = 7}$ girls are in 140 – 145 subtract the counts
Step 3 the frequency of 145 – 150 is ${29 - 11 = 18}$, the frequency of 150 – 155 is ${40 - 29 = 11}$, and so on, as in Table 13.16
Step 4 ${n = 51}$, so ${\frac{n}{2} = \frac{51}{2} = 25.5}$, which lies in the class 145 – 150 the median class
Step 5 $l = 145$,   $cf = 11$,   $f = 18$,   $h = 5$
Step 6 $\text{Median} = l + \left(\dfrac{\frac{n}{2} - cf}{f}\right) \times h = 145 + \left(\dfrac{25.5 - 11}{18}\right) \times 5 = 145 + \dfrac{72.5}{18} = 149.03$ the formula
Answer The median height of the girls is 149.03 cm: about half the girls are shorter than 149.03 cm, and about half are taller
