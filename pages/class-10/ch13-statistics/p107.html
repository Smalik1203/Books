Example 9

The waiting times of 40 patients at a clinic are given below. Find the modal waiting time.

Waiting time (in minutes)0 – 2020 – 4040 – 6060 – 8080 – 100
Number of patients1510852

Solution.

Step 1 the highest frequency, 15, is in the first class, so the modal class is 0 – 20
Step 2 $l = 0$, $h = 20$, $f_1 = 15$, $f_0 = 0$, $f_2 = 10$ no class comes before it
Step 3 $\text{Mode} = 0 + \left(\dfrac{15 - 0}{30 - 0 - 10}\right) \times 20 = \dfrac{15}{20} \times 20 = 15$
Answer 15 minutes

Example 10

The marks of 40 students are given below. Make a less than and a more than cumulative frequency table. How many students got 20 marks or more?

Marks0 – 1010 – 2020 – 3030 – 4040 – 50
Number of students391585

Solution.

Step 1 less than 10, 20, 30, 40, 50:   3, ${3 + 9 = 12}$, ${12 + 15 = 27}$, ${27 + 8 = 35}$, ${35 + 5 = 40}$ add each frequency on
Step 2 at least 0, 10, 20, 30, 40:   40, ${40 - 3 = 37}$, ${37 - 9 = 28}$, ${28 - 15 = 13}$, ${13 - 8 = 5}$ take each frequency off
Step 3 20 marks or more is the more than entry for 20
Answer 28 students

The less than table gives the same count: $40 - 12 = 28$.
