Example 12 · Numerical answer

The values 5, 10 and 15 occur 2, $k$ and 3 times, and their mean is 10.5. Find $k$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{10 + 10k + 45}{5 + k} = 10.5$, so $55 + 10k = 52.5 + 10.5k$.
Step 2$0.5k = 2.5$, so $k = 5$.
Answer5
Example 13 · Numerical answer

The classes 0–10, 10–20, 20–30, 30–40 and 40–50 have frequencies 5, 8, 12, 7 and 8. Find the median, to one decimal place.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$n = 40$; the median class is 20–30, with $cf = 13$ before it and $f = 12$.
Step 2Median $= 20 + \frac{20 - 13}{12} \times 10 = 20 + 5.83 \approx 25.8$.
Answer25.8
Example 14 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Class mark of 20–30(1) 50
(Q) Class size of 20–30(2) 30
(R) Upper limit of 20–30(3) 25
(S) Class mark of 40–60(4) 10

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The class mark is the mid-point: 25, and 50 for 40–60.
Step 2The class size is $30 - 20 = 10$, and the upper limit is 30.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
