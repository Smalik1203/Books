Type 2 · The direct and assumed mean methods

Example 3

The numbers of books read in a year by 40 students are given below. Find the mean number of books by the direct method.

Books read0 – 1010 – 2020 – 3030 – 4040 – 50
Number of students5812105

Solution.

Step 1 the class marks are 5, 15, 25, 35 and 45
Step 2 $\sum f_ix_i = 25 + 120 + 300 + 350 + 225 = 1020$ each $f_i \times x_i$
Step 3 $\bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum f_ix_i}{\sum f_i} = \dfrac{1020}{40} = 25.5$ the direct method
Answer 25.5 books
Example 4

The monthly electricity bills of 50 homes in a colony are given below. Find the mean bill by the assumed mean method.

Bill (in ₹)400 – 500500 – 600600 – 700700 – 800800 – 900
Number of homes61014128

Solution.

Step 1 the class marks are 450, 550, 650, 750 and 850; take $a = 650$ the middle class mark
Step 2 $d_i = x_i - 650$ is $-200$, $-100$, 0, 100 and 200
Step 3 $\sum f_id_i = -1200 - 1000 + 0 + 1200 + 1600 = 600$
Step 4 $\bar{x} = a + \dfrac{\sum f_id_i}{\sum f_i} = 650 + \dfrac{600}{50} = 650 + 12 = 662$
Answer The mean bill is ₹$662$
