Example 4 · Single correct

For grouped data, the modal class is 20–30, with frequency 12; the classes before and after have frequencies 8 and 7. What is the mode, to one decimal place?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 24
  2. 25
  3. 22.5
  4. 24.4

Solution.

Step 1Mode $= l + \frac{f_1 - f_0}{2f_1 - f_0 - f_2} \times h = 20 + \frac{12 - 8}{24 - 8 - 7} \times 10$.
Step 2$= 20 + \frac{40}{9} \approx 24.4$.
Answer(d) 24.4
Example 5 · Single correct

For some data the mean is 20 and the median is 22. Using $3\,\text{Median} = \text{Mode} + 2\,\text{Mean}$, what is the mode?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 26
  2. 24
  3. 18
  4. 22

Solution.

Step 1Mode $= 3 \times 22 - 2 \times 20$.
Step 2$= 66 - 40 = 26$.
Answer(a) 26
Example 6 · Single correct

The classes 0–10, 10–20, 20–30 and 30–40 have frequencies 5, 8, 12 and 15. What is the median class?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 10–20
  2. 20–30
  3. 30–40
  4. 0–10

Solution.

Step 1$n = 40$, so the median is the 20th value. The cumulative frequencies are 5, 13, 25 and 40.
Step 2The 20th value lies where the total first reaches 20: in 20–30.
Answer(b) 20–30
