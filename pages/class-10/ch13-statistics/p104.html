Problem 4

A distribution has 50 observations. Its median class is 30 – 40, with frequency 10, and the cumulative frequency of the class before it is 22. The median is

  1. 58
  2. 23
  3. 33
  4. 35
Solution

${30 + \left(\frac{25 - 22}{10}\right) \times 10 = 30 + 3 = 33}$

The answer is (c). Option (a) uses $n$ in place of $\frac{n}{2}$, (b) uses the cumulative frequency of the median class itself, 32, and (d) is only the class mark.

Problem 5

The less than cumulative frequencies of the classes 10 – 20, 20 – 30, 30 – 40, 40 – 50 and 50 – 60 are 4, 16, 34, 45 and 50. The median class and its frequency are

  1. 30 – 40; 34
  2. 30 – 40; 18
  3. 20 – 30; 12
  4. 50 – 60; 5
Solution

${\frac{n}{2} = 25}$;   the first cumulative frequency above 25 is 34, so the class is 30 – 40, with frequency ${34 - 16 = 18}$

The answer is (b). Option (a) gives the cumulative frequency in place of the frequency, (c) stops at a class whose cumulative frequency, 16, is still below 25, and (d) looks for $n$ instead of $\frac{n}{2}$.
