Example 2

The table gives the percentage of female teachers in the rural primary schools of different states and union territories (U.T.) of India. Find the mean percentage of female teachers by each of the three methods of this section. (The data are from the Seventh All India School Education Survey conducted by NCERT.)

Female teachers (%)15 – 2525 – 3535 – 4545 – 5555 – 6565 – 7575 – 85
States/U.T.61174421

Solution.

Step 1 find the class mark $x_i$ of each class, as in Table 13.6
Step 2 take ${a = 50}$ and ${h = 10}$, so that ${d_i = x_i - 50}$ and ${u_i = \frac{x_i - 50}{10}}$; each $d_i$ and $u_i$ is in Table 13.7 a middle class mark
Step 3 ${\sum f_i = 35}$, ${\sum f_ix_i = 1390}$, ${\sum f_id_i = -360}$ and ${\sum f_iu_i = -36}$ the totals of Table 13.7
Step 4 $\bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum f_ix_i}{\sum f_i} = \dfrac{1390}{35} = 39.71$ direct method
Step 5 $\bar{x} = a + \dfrac{\sum f_id_i}{\sum f_i} = 50 + \dfrac{-360}{35} = 39.71$ assumed mean method
Step 6 $\bar{x} = a + h\left(\dfrac{\sum f_iu_i}{\sum f_i}\right) = 50 + 10 \times \dfrac{-36}{35} = 39.71$ step-deviation method
Answer The mean percentage of female teachers in the rural primary schools is 39.71
