$\bar{u} = \dfrac{-106}{45}$,   so   $\bar{x} = 200 + 20 \times \dfrac{-106}{45} = 200 - 47.11 = 152.89$

So, on average, each of these 45 bowlers took 152.89 wickets in one-day cricket.
