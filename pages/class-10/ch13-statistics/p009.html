Activity 2. Divide your class into three groups. Each group does one of the tasks below.

  1. Collect the Mathematics marks of every student in your class in the latest school examination, and make a grouped frequency distribution of them.
  2. Collect the highest temperature in your city on each of 30 days, and make a grouped frequency table of them.
  3. Measure the height in centimetres of every student in your class, and make a grouped frequency distribution table of the heights.

When every group has made its table, each group finds the mean of its data by the method it thinks best.
