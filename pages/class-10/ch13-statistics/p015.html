From Table 13.11, the 50th observation is 28 and the 51st observation is 29. (Why?) So

$$\text{Median} = \dfrac{28 + 29}{2} = 28.5$$

Remark. Rows 1 and 3 of Table 13.11 together make a cumulative frequency table. The median mark 28.5 tells you that about half the students got less than 28.5 marks, and the other half got more.
