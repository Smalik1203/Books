What this chapter showed
  1. The mean of grouped data can be found by the direct method, ${\bar{x} = \frac{\sum f_ix_i}{\sum f_i}}$; by the assumed mean method, ${\bar{x} = a + \frac{\sum f_id_i}{\sum f_i}}$; or by the step-deviation method, ${\bar{x} = a + h\left(\frac{\sum f_iu_i}{\sum f_i}\right)}$. All three assume that the frequency of each class is centred at its mid-point, its class mark.
  2. The mode of grouped data is ${l + \left(\frac{f_1 - f_0}{2f_1 - f_0 - f_2}\right) \times h}$, with the symbols as in Section 13.3.
  3. The cumulative frequency of a class is the sum of its own frequency and the frequencies of all the classes before it.
  4. The median of grouped data is ${l + \left(\frac{\frac{n}{2} - cf}{f}\right) \times h}$, with the symbols as in Section 13.4.
  5. Before you use the formulas for the mode and the median, make sure that the classes are continuous, with no gaps between them.
