13.4Median of Grouped Data

You studied in Class 9 that the median is the measure of central tendency that gives the value of the middle observation in the data. To find the median of ungrouped data, first arrange the observations in ascending order. If the number of observations $n$ is odd, the median is the $\left(\frac{n + 1}{2}\right)$th observation. If $n$ is even, the median is the average of the $\left(\frac{n}{2}\right)$th and the $\left(\frac{n}{2} + 1\right)$th observations.
