Example 15

For a distribution, the median is 45 and the mode is 42. Estimate the mean.

Solution.

Step 1 $3 \times 45 = 42 + 2\,\text{Mean}$ the empirical relationship
Step 2 $2\,\text{Mean} = 135 - 42 = 93$
Answer The mean is about 46.5
Example 16

A shopkeeper counted the customers in his shop each day for 50 days. Find the mean, the median and the mode of the counts, and check them against the empirical relationship.

Customers10 – 2020 – 3030 – 4040 – 5050 – 60
Number of days61016126

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 35$, $h = 10$:   $\sum f_iu_i = -12 - 10 + 0 + 12 + 12 = 2$ $u_i$ from $-2$ to 2
Step 2 $\bar{x} = 35 + 10 \times \dfrac{2}{50} = 35.4$ the mean
Step 3 cumulative frequencies 6, 16, 32, 44, 50; $\frac{n}{2} = 25$, so the median class is 30 – 40
Step 4 $\text{Median} = 30 + \left(\dfrac{25 - 16}{16}\right) \times 10 = 35.63$ the median
Step 5 $\text{Mode} = 30 + \left(\dfrac{16 - 10}{32 - 10 - 12}\right) \times 10 = 36$ the mode
Step 6 $3 \times 35.63 = 106.89$ and $36 + 2 \times 35.4 = 106.8$ nearly equal
Answer Mean 35.4, median 35.63 and mode 36 customers; the relationship holds roughly
