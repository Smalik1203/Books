Behind Each Answer
Five multiple-choice problems, each solved, with the reason for every option.
Problem 1

The class marks of a distribution are 12, 17, 22, 27 and 32. The class size and the first class are

  1. 5, and 12 – 17
  2. 10, and 7 – 17
  3. 5, and 9.5 – 14.5
  4. 2.5, and 10.75 – 13.25
Solution

class size ${= 17 - 12 = 5}$;   the first class has mid-point 12, so it is ${12 - 2.5}$ to ${12 + 2.5}$

The answer is (c); (a) starts the class at 12, (b) doubles the size, and (d) halves it.

Problem 2

In the step-deviation method, ${a = 45}$, ${h = 10}$, ${\sum f_i = 40}$ and ${\sum f_iu_i = -8}$. The mean is

  1. 43
  2. 47
  3. 44.8
  4. 37
Solution

${\bar{x} = a + h\left(\frac{\sum f_iu_i}{\sum f_i}\right) = 45 + 10 \times \frac{-8}{40} = 45 - 2 = 43}$

The answer is (a); (b) adds the 2, (c) forgets $h$, and (d) forgets both $h$ and $\sum f_i$.

Problem 3

The classes 0 – 10, 10 – 20, 20 – 30, 30 – 40 and 40 – 50 have frequencies 5, 8, 12, 10 and 4. The mode is

  1. 23.33
  2. 26.67
  3. 25
  4. 6.67
Solution

modal class 20 – 30:   ${20 + \left(\frac{12 - 8}{24 - 8 - 10}\right) \times 10 = 20 + \frac{40}{6} = 26.67}$

The answer is (b). Option (a) swaps $f_0$ and $f_2$, (c) is the class mark of the modal class, and (d) leaves out $l$.
