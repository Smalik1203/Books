Example 1 · Single correct

What is the class mark of the class 10–20?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 15
  2. 10
  3. 20
  4. 5

Solution.

Step 1The class mark is the mid-point of the class.
Step 2$\frac{10 + 20}{2} = 15$.
Answer(a) 15
Example 2 · Single correct

The values 2, 4, 6 and 8 occur 3, 2, 4 and 1 times. What is their mean?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 5
  2. 4.6
  3. 4
  4. 5.2

Solution.

Step 1$\sum fx = 6 + 8 + 24 + 8 = 46$ and $\sum f = 10$.
Step 2Mean $= \frac{46}{10} = 4.6$.
Answer(b) 4.6
Example 3 · Single correct

The classes 0–10, 10–20, 20–30 and 30–40 have frequencies 5, 8, 12 and 7. What is the modal class?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 10–20
  2. 30–40
  3. 20–30
  4. 0–10

Solution.

Step 1The modal class is the class with the highest frequency.
Step 2That is 20–30, with 12.
Answer(c) 20–30
