Example 13

Find the median of the distribution below.

Class interval10 – 2020 – 3030 – 4040 – 5050 – 6060 – 70
Frequency481012106

Solution.

Step 1 the cumulative frequencies are 4, 12, 22, 34, 44 and 50
Step 2 $n = 50$ and $\frac{n}{2} = 25$; the first cumulative frequency greater than 25 is 34, so the median class is 40 – 50 the median class
Step 3 $l = 40$, $cf = 22$, $f = 12$, $h = 10$
Step 4 $\text{Median} = 40 + \left(\dfrac{25 - 22}{12}\right) \times 10 = 40 + 2.5 = 42.5$
Answer 42.5
Example 14

The median of the distribution below is 32, and the total frequency is 50. Find $x$ and $y$.

Class interval0 – 1010 – 2020 – 3030 – 4040 – 5050 – 60
Frequency5$x$1215$y$4

Solution.

Step 1 $5 + x + 12 + 15 + y + 4 = 50$, so $x + y = 14$ the total
Step 2 32 lies in 30 – 40, so $l = 30$, $f = 15$, $cf = 17 + x$, $h = 10$ the median class
Step 3 $32 = 30 + \left(\dfrac{25 - 17 - x}{15}\right) \times 10$
Step 4 $2 \times 15 = (8 - x) \times 10$, so $8 - x = 3$
Step 5 $x = 5$ and $y = 14 - 5 = 9$
Answer $x = 5$, $y = 9$

Check: the cumulative frequency up to 30 is 22, below 25, and up to 40 it is 37, above 25, so 30 – 40 is indeed the median class.
