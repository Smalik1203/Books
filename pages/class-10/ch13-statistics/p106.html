Type 4 · A missing frequency

Example 7

The mean of the distribution below is 24.2. Find $k$.

Class interval0 – 1010 – 2020 – 3030 – 4040 – 50
Frequency46$k$82

Solution.

Step 1 $\sum f_i = 20 + k$
Step 2 $\sum f_ix_i = 20 + 90 + 25k + 280 + 90 = 480 + 25k$ class marks 5, 15, 25, 35, 45
Step 3 $\dfrac{480 + 25k}{20 + k} = 24.2$, so $480 + 25k = 484 + 24.2k$ the direct method
Step 4 $0.8k = 4$, so $k = 5$
Answer $k = 5$. Check: $\dfrac{480 + 125}{25} = \dfrac{605}{25} = 24.2$

Type 5 · The mode of grouped data

Example 8

The classes 0 – 10, 10 – 20, 20 – 30, 30 – 40 and 40 – 50 have frequencies 5, 8, 12, 10 and 4. The mode is

  1. 23.33
  2. 26.67
  3. 25
  4. 6.67

Solution.

Step 1 modal class 20 – 30:   $l = 20$, $h = 10$, $f_1 = 12$, $f_0 = 8$, $f_2 = 10$ the highest frequency
Step 2 $\text{Mode} = 20 + \left(\dfrac{12 - 8}{24 - 8 - 10}\right) \times 10 = 20 + \dfrac{40}{6} = 26.67$
Answer (b)

Option (a) swaps $f_0$ and $f_2$, (c) is the class mark of the modal class, and (d) leaves out $l$.
