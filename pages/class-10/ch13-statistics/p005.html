$$\bar{x} = a + \bar{d} = a + \dfrac{\sum f_id_i}{\sum f_i}$$

Putting in the values of $a$, $\sum f_id_i$ and $\sum f_i$ from Table 13.4,

$$\bar{x} = 47.5 + \dfrac{435}{30} = 47.5 + 14.5 = 62$$

So the mean mark of the students is 62. This way of finding the mean is called the assumed mean method.
