Assertion and reason

In Questions 15 to 18, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): The mean found by the step-deviation method does not depend on the choice of $a$.

    Reason (R): ${\bar{x} = a + h\bar{u}}$, and when $a$ changes, $h\bar{u}$ changes by the same amount the other way.

  1. Assertion (A): The classes 0 – 10, 10 – 20 and 20 – 30 have frequencies 4, 9 and 4, so the mode is 15.

    Reason (R): The mode of grouped data is always the class mark of the modal class.

  1. Assertion (A): The median of grouped data can be found from a more than type cumulative frequency table.

    Reason (R): The mean of grouped data is found by taking each class to be centred at its class mark.

  1. Assertion (A): If every frequency of a distribution is doubled, its mean is doubled.

    Reason (R): The mean of grouped data is $\dfrac{\sum f_ix_i}{\sum f_i}$.
