Example 13 · Numerical answer

A quadratic polynomial has zeroes 2 and 5, and its value at $x = 1$ is 8. Find its value at $x = 0$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1It is $a(x - 2)(x - 5)$, and at $x = 1$ this is $a \times (-1) \times (-4) = 4a = 8$, so $a = 2$.
Step 2At $x = 0$: $2 \times (-2) \times (-5) = 20$.
Answer20
