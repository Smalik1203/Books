Example 8

If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 3x - 2$, find $\dfrac{\alpha}{\beta} + \dfrac{\beta}{\alpha}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\alpha + \beta = 3$ and $\alpha\beta = -2$
Step 2 $\dfrac{\alpha}{\beta} + \dfrac{\beta}{\alpha} = \dfrac{\alpha^2 + \beta^2}{\alpha\beta}$ common denominator
Step 3 $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = (\alpha + \beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta = 9 + 4 = 13$
Answer $\dfrac{13}{-2} = -\dfrac{13}{2}$

Example 9

The zeroes of $x^2 - kx + 6$ are in the ratio $3 : 2$. The value of $k$ is

  1. 5 only
  2. $-5$ only
  3. 5 or $-5$
  4. 6

Solution.

Step 1 let the zeroes be $3m$ and $2m$ ratio $3 : 2$
Step 2 product $6m^2 = 6$, so $m = 1$ or $m = -1$
Step 3 sum $5m = k$, so $k = 5$ or $k = -5$
Answer (c)

With $k = -5$ the polynomial is $x^2 + 5x + 6$, whose zeroes $-3$ and $-2$ are also in the ratio $3 : 2$. Options (a) and (b) keep only one value of $m$, and (d) is the product.

Example 10

The sum of the squares of the zeroes of $x^2 - 8x + k$ is 40. Find $k$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\alpha + \beta = 8$ and $\alpha\beta = k$
Step 2 $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = (\alpha + \beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta = 64 - 2k$
Step 3 $64 - 2k = 40$, so $2k = 24$ given sum of squares
Answer $k = 12$

Check: $x^2 - 8x + 12 = (x - 2)(x - 6)$, and $2^2 + 6^2 = 40$.
