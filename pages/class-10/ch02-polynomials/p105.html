Example 10 · Multiple correct

For the cubic $x^3 - 3x^2 - x + 3$, which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The sum of its zeroes is 3.
  2. The product of its zeroes is $-3$.
  3. The sum of the products of its zeroes taken two at a time is $-1$.
  4. 2 is one of its zeroes.

Solution.

Step 1For $ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d$ the three are $-\frac{b}{a}$, $-\frac{d}{a}$ and $\frac{c}{a}$: $3$, $-3$ and $-1$. The zeroes are 3, 1 and $-1$.
Step 2At 2: $8 - 12 - 2 + 3 = -3$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

$\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 7x + 12$. Find $(\alpha - \beta)^2$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$(\alpha - \beta)^2 = (\alpha + \beta)^2 - 4\alpha\beta$.
Step 2$= 49 - 48 = 1$. (The zeroes are 3 and 4.)

Example 12 · Numerical answer

The zeroes of $x^2 - 8x + k$ are $\alpha$ and $\alpha + 2$. Find $k$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Their sum is $2\alpha + 2 = 8$, so $\alpha = 3$ and the zeroes are 3 and 5.
Step 2$k$ is their product: 15.
