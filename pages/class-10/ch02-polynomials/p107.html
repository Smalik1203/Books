Example 15 · Matching

Match the sum and product of the zeroes with the quadratic polynomial. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Sum 4, product 3(1) $x^2 - 4$
(Q) Sum $-4$, product 3(2) $x^2 - 4x - 3$
(R) Sum 4, product $-3$(3) $x^2 + 4x + 3$
(S) Sum 0, product $-4$(4) $x^2 - 4x + 3$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  3. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–3, R–2, S–4

Solution.

Step 1A quadratic whose zeroes have sum $s$ and product $p$ is $x^2 - sx + p$.
Step 2So the four are $x^2 - 4x + 3$, $x^2 + 4x + 3$, $x^2 - 4x - 3$ and $x^2 - 4$.
Answer(c) P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
