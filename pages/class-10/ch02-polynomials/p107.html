Type 7 · Cubic polynomials

Example 11

If $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ are the zeroes of $x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6$, then $\alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha$ is

  1. $-11$
  2. $6$
  3. $11$
  4. $-6$

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 1$, $b = -6$, $c = 11$ and $d = -6$
Step 2 $\alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha = \dfrac{c}{a} = 11$ the cubic relation
Answer (c)

The zeroes are 1, 2 and 3. Option (a) is $-\frac{c}{a}$, (b) is the sum and (d) is the constant term.

Example 12

Find a cubic polynomial whose zeroes are 2, $-1$ and 3.

Solution.

Step 1 sum $= 2 + (-1) + 3 = 4$
Step 2 $2 \times (-1) + (-1) \times 3 + 3 \times 2 = -2 - 3 + 6 = 1$ products two at a time
Step 3 product $= 2 \times (-1) \times 3 = -6$
Step 4 take $a = 1$: then $b = -4$, $c = 1$ and $d = 6$ from the three relations
Answer $x^3 - 4x^2 + x + 6$

Check: $2^3 - 4 \times 2^2 + 2 + 6 = 8 - 16 + 2 + 6 = 0$.

Type 8 · Proving a property of every quadratic polynomial

Example 13

The two zeroes of $ax^2 + bx + c$, $a \neq 0$, are equal in size and opposite in sign. Prove that $b = 0$.

Solution.

Step 1 let the zeroes be $\alpha$ and $-\alpha$ given
Step 2 $\alpha + (-\alpha) = -\dfrac{b}{a}$ sum of the zeroes
Step 3 $0 = -\dfrac{b}{a}$ since $\alpha - \alpha = 0$
Step 4 $b = 0$ multiply both sides by $-a$
Answer So $b = 0$, as was to be proved
