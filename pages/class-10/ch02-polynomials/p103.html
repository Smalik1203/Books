Example 4 · Single correct

One zero of $2x^2 - 3x + k$ is 1. What is $k$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $-1$
  2. 5
  3. 2
  4. 1

Solution.

Step 1A zero makes the polynomial 0: $2 - 3 + k = 0$.
Step 2So $k = 1$.
Answer(d) 1
Example 5 · Single correct

The graph of $y = p(x)$ meets the $x$-axis at exactly three points. How many zeroes has $p(x)$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 3
  2. 2
  3. 0
  4. 6

Solution.

Step 1A zero of $p(x)$ is an $x$ where $p(x) = 0$, that is, where the graph meets the $x$-axis.
Step 2Three meeting points give three zeroes.
Answer(a) 3
Example 6 · Single correct

$\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 + 7x + 10$. What is $\frac{1}{\alpha} + \frac{1}{\beta}$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{7}{10}$
  2. $-\frac{7}{10}$
  3. $\frac{10}{7}$
  4. $-\frac{10}{7}$

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{1}{\alpha} + \frac{1}{\beta} = \frac{\alpha + \beta}{\alpha\beta}$, with $\alpha + \beta = -7$ and $\alpha\beta = 10$.
Step 2$= -\frac{7}{10}$.
Answer(b) $-\frac{7}{10}$
