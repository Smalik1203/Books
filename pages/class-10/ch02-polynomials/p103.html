Example 3

Fig. 2.10 shows the graph of $y = p(x)$, where $p(x) = x^2 + bx + c$. Read the zeroes of $p(x)$ from the graph, and find $b$ and $c$.

Solution.

Step 1 the graph cuts the $x$-axis at $(-2, 0)$ and $(3, 0)$, so the zeroes are $-2$ and 3 read from the graph
Step 2 sum $= -2 + 3 = 1 = -\frac{b}{1}$, so $b = -1$
Step 3 product $= -2 \times 3 = -6 = \frac{c}{1}$, so $c = -6$
Answer The zeroes are $-2$ and 3; $b = -1$ and $c = -6$, so $p(x) = x^2 - x - 6$
