Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these are zeroes of $x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6$?

Select all correct options.

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. $-1$

Solution.

Step 1$1 - 6 + 11 - 6 = 0$, $8 - 24 + 22 - 6 = 0$ and $27 - 54 + 33 - 6 = 0$.
Step 2At $-1$: $-1 - 6 - 11 - 6 = -24$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

For $p(x) = 3x^2 - 5x - 2$, which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its zeroes are 2 and $-\frac{1}{3}$.
  2. The sum of its zeroes is $\frac{5}{3}$.
  3. The product of its zeroes is $\frac{2}{3}$.
  4. $p(1) = -4$

Solution.

Step 1$3x^2 - 5x - 2 = (x - 2)(3x + 1)$, so the zeroes are 2 and $-\frac{1}{3}$, with sum $\frac{5}{3}$.
Step 2Their product is $-\frac{2}{3}$, not $\frac{2}{3}$; and $p(1) = 3 - 5 - 2 = -4$.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

For which of these polynomials do the zeroes add to 0?

Select all correct options.

  1. $x^2 - 9$
  2. $x^2 + 4x$
  3. $4x^2 - 1$
  4. $x^2 + x - 2$

Solution.

Step 1The sum of the zeroes of $ax^2 + bx + c$ is $-\frac{b}{a}$, which is 0 exactly when there is no $x$ term.
Step 2$x^2 + 4x$ has sum $-4$ and $x^2 + x - 2$ has sum $-1$.
Answer(a), (c)
