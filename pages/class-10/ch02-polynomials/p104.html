Type 3 · Finding the zeroes and checking the relationship

Example 4

Find the zeroes of $5x^2 - 13x - 6$, and check the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.

Solution.

Step 1 $5x^2 - 13x - 6 = 5x^2 - 15x + 2x - 6$ splitting the middle term
Step 2 $= 5x(x - 3) + 2(x - 3) = (5x + 2)(x - 3)$
Step 3 zero when $x - 3 = 0$ or $5x + 2 = 0$, that is, $x = 3$ or $x = -\frac{2}{5}$
Step 4 sum $= 3 + \left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right) = \dfrac{13}{5} = \dfrac{-(-13)}{5} = -\dfrac{b}{a}$
Step 5 product $= 3 \times \left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right) = -\dfrac{6}{5} = \dfrac{-6}{5} = \dfrac{c}{a}$
Answer The zeroes are 3 and $-\frac{2}{5}$, and both relationships hold

Type 4 · A quadratic polynomial from its zeroes

Example 5

A quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are $-3$ and 4 is

  1. $x^2 - x - 12$
  2. $x^2 + x - 12$
  3. $x^2 - x + 12$
  4. $x^2 + 7x + 12$

Solution.

Step 1 sum $= -3 + 4 = 1$ and product $= -3 \times 4 = -12$
Step 2 $x^2 - 1x + (-12) = x^2 - x - 12$ using $x^2 - Sx + P$
Answer (a)

Option (b) puts the sum in without its minus sign, and (c) loses the sign of the product. Option (d) is $(x + 3)(x + 4)$, whose zeroes are $-3$ and $-4$.
