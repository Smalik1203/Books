Exercise Set 2.2
  1. Find the zeroes of each polynomial, and check how the zeroes are related to the coefficients.
    1. $x^2 - 2x - 8$
    2. $4s^2 - 4s + 1$
    3. $6x^2 - 3 - 7x$
    4. $4u^2 + 8u$
    5. $t^2 - 15$
    6. $3x^2 - x - 4$
  1. Find a quadratic polynomial for each pair of numbers below, where the first number is the sum of its zeroes and the second is their product.
    1. $\dfrac{1}{4}$, $-1$
    2. $\sqrt{2}$, $\dfrac{1}{3}$
    3. $0$, $\sqrt{5}$
    4. $1$, $1$
    5. $-\dfrac{1}{4}$, $\dfrac{1}{4}$
    6. $4$, $1$

A sum and a product are enough to rebuild a quadratic polynomial, as Example 4 showed.
