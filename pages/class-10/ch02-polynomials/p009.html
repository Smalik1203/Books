Example 2

Find the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial $x^2 + 7x + 10$, and check the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.

Solution.

Step 1 $x^2 + 7x + 10 = (x + 2)(x + 5)$ factorising
Step 2 $x^2 + 7x + 10$ is zero when $x + 2 = 0$ or $x + 5 = 0$, that is, when $x = -2$ or $x = -5$
Step 3 sum of the zeroes $= -2 + (-5) = -7 = \dfrac{-(7)}{1} = \dfrac{-(\text{coefficient of } x)}{\text{coefficient of } x^2}$
Step 4 product of the zeroes $= (-2) \times (-5) = 10 = \dfrac{10}{1} = \dfrac{\text{constant term}}{\text{coefficient of } x^2}$
Answer The zeroes are $-2$ and $-5$, and both relationships hold
Example 3

Find the zeroes of the polynomial $x^2 - 3$, and check the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.

Solution.

Step 1 $x^2 - 3 = \left(x - \sqrt{3}\right)\left(x + \sqrt{3}\right)$ by $a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$
Step 2 $x^2 - 3$ is zero when $x = \sqrt{3}$ or $x = -\sqrt{3}$
Step 3 sum of the zeroes $= \sqrt{3} + \left(-\sqrt{3}\right) = 0 = \dfrac{-(\text{coefficient of } x)}{\text{coefficient of } x^2}$ no term in $x$
Step 4 product of the zeroes $= \sqrt{3} \times \left(-\sqrt{3}\right) = -3 = \dfrac{-3}{1} = \dfrac{\text{constant term}}{\text{coefficient of } x^2}$
Answer The zeroes are $\sqrt{3}$ and $-\sqrt{3}$, and both relationships hold
Example 4

Find a quadratic polynomial the sum and product of whose zeroes are $-3$ and 2 respectively.

Solution.

Step 1 let the polynomial be $ax^2 + bx + c$, with zeroes $\alpha$ and $\beta$
Step 2 $\alpha + \beta = -3 = -\frac{b}{a}$ and $\alpha\beta = 2 = \frac{c}{a}$
Step 3 take $a = 1$; then $b = 3$ and $c = 2$ any $a$ other than 0 will do
Answer One such polynomial is $x^2 + 3x + 2$

You can check that any other quadratic polynomial with this sum and product of zeroes has the form $k(x^2 + 3x + 2)$, where $k$ is a real number other than 0.
