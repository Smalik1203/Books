Here $\alpha + \beta = -3$ and $\alpha\beta = 2$. The new zeroes have sum $2\alpha + 2\beta = 2(\alpha + \beta) = -6$ and product $2\alpha \times 2\beta = 4\alpha\beta = 8$. So one such polynomial is $x^2 + 6x + 8$.

What are the zeroes of $x^2 - 2x - 8$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $4$ and $-2$
  2. $-4$ and $2$
  3. $8$ and $-1$
  4. $2$ and $4$

Step 2It is 0 when $x = 4$ or $x = -2$.
Answer(a) $4$ and $-2$
Example 2 · Single correct

Which quadratic polynomial has zeroes whose sum is $-3$ and product is 2?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $x^2 - 3x + 2$
  2. $x^2 + 3x + 2$
  3. $x^2 + 2x - 3$
  4. $x^2 - 2x + 3$

Solution.

Step 1A quadratic with sum of zeroes $s$ and product $p$ is $x^2 - sx + p$.
Step 2$x^2 - (-3)x + 2 = x^2 + 3x + 2$, with zeroes $-1$ and $-2$.
Answer(b) $x^2 + 3x + 2$
Example 3 · Single correct

$\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the zeroes of $x^2 - 5x + 6$. What is $\alpha^2 + \beta^2$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 25
  2. 19
  3. 13
  4. 1

Solution.

Step 1$\alpha + \beta = 5$ and $\alpha\beta = 6$.
Step 2$\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = (\alpha + \beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta = 25 - 12 = 13$.
Answer(c) 13
