2.3Relationship between Zeroes and Coefficients of a Polynomial

You have already seen that the zero of the linear polynomial $ax + b$ is $-\frac{b}{a}$. Now let us return to the question from Section 2.1: how are the zeroes of a quadratic polynomial related to its coefficients? Take the quadratic polynomial $p(x) = 2x^2 - 8x + 6$. In Class IX you learnt to factorise a quadratic polynomial by splitting the middle term. Here we split $-8x$ into two terms whose product is $6 \times 2x^2 = 12x^2$.
