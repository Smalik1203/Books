  1. A stone is thrown upwards from the top of a building 15 m tall. After $t$ seconds its height above the ground is $h(t) = -5t^2 + 10t + 15$ metres.

    Time $t$ (s)0123
    Height (m)1520150
    1. What kind of polynomial is $h(t)$, and what is its degree?
    2. Find the zeroes of $-5t^2 + 10t + 15$.
    3. After how many seconds does the stone reach the ground?
    4. Check that the sum and product of the zeroes agree with the coefficients.
