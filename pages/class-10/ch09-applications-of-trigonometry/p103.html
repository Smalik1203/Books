Example 2 · Single correct

From a point 20 m from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of its top is $60^\circ$. How tall is the tower?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 20 m
  2. $20\sqrt{3}$ m
  3. 40 m
  4. $\frac{20}{\sqrt{3}}$ m

Solution.

Step 1$\tan 60^\circ = \frac{h}{20}$.
Step 2$h = 20\sqrt{3}$ m.
Answer(b) $20\sqrt{3}$ m
Example 3 · Single correct

A pole 12 m tall casts a shadow $12\sqrt{3}$ m long. What is the angle of elevation of the sun?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $60^\circ$
  2. $45^\circ$
  3. $30^\circ$
  4. $90^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1$\tan\theta = \frac{12}{12\sqrt{3}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$.
Step 2So $\theta = 30^\circ$.
Answer(c) $30^\circ$
Example 4 · Single correct

From the top of a building 30 m tall, the angle of depression of a car on the ground is $30^\circ$. How far is the car from the foot of the building?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 30 m
  2. 15 m
  3. 60 m
  4. $30\sqrt{3}$ m

Solution.

Step 1The angle of depression from the top equals the angle of elevation from the car: $\tan 30^\circ = \frac{30}{d}$.
Step 2$d = 30\sqrt{3}$ m.
Answer(d) $30\sqrt{3}$ m
