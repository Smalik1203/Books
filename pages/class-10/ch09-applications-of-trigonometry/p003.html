If you know these three, how can you find the height of the minar? From the figure, ${CD = CB + BD}$, and BD is equal to AE, the height of the student. So you need only BC.

To find BC, use a trigonometric ratio of $\angle BAC$, that is, of angle A. In $\triangle ABC$, BC is the side opposite to angle A and AB is the side adjacent to it. You know AB, because it is equal to DE, and you want BC. The two ratios that use only these two sides are $\tan A$ and $\cot A$. So write

$\tan A = \dfrac{BC}{AB}$   or   $\cot A = \dfrac{AB}{BC}$

and solve for BC. Adding AE to BC then gives the height of the minar. The examples below work through this method.

In every problem, draw a rough figure first. Mark the right angle, the angle you know and the length you know. Then choose the ratio that uses the side you know and the side you want.
