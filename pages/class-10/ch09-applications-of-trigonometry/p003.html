Example 1

A tower stands vertically on the ground. From a point on the ground 15 m away from the foot of the tower, the angle of elevation of the top of the tower is $60^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.

Solution.

Step 1 in Fig. 9.4, AB is the tower, C is the point on the ground and CB is its distance from the foot, so $\angle ACB$ is the angle of elevation draw a simple figure
Step 2 the triangle ACB is right-angled at B, and we need the height AB
Step 3 $\tan 60^\circ = \dfrac{AB}{BC}$   (or use $\cot 60^\circ$) it uses AB and BC
Step 4 $\sqrt{3} = \dfrac{AB}{15}$, which gives ${AB = 15\sqrt{3}}$
Answer The height of the tower is ${15\sqrt{3}}$ m
