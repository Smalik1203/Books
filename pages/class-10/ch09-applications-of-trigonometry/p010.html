1. A tree breaks in a storm. The broken part bends so that its top touches the ground, making an angle of $30^\circ$ with it. The top touches the ground 8 m from the foot of the tree. Find the height of the tree.
  1. A contractor plans to put up two slides in a park. For children below 5 years of age, she wants a slide whose top is 1.5 m high and which is inclined at $30^\circ$ to the ground. For older children, she wants a steeper slide whose top is 3 m high and which is inclined at $60^\circ$ to the ground. How long should each slide be?
  1. From a point on the ground 30 m from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of the top of the tower is $30^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.
  1. A kite is flying 60 m above the ground. Its string is tied for a while to a point on the ground, and makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the ground. Find the length of the string, taking it to be straight, with no slack.
  1. A boy 1.5 m tall is standing some distance away from a building 30 m tall. As he walks towards the building, the angle of elevation of its top from his eyes increases from $30^\circ$ to $60^\circ$. How far did he walk towards the building?
  1. A transmission tower stands on top of a building 20 m high. From a point on the ground, the angles of elevation of the bottom and the top of the tower are $45^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Find the height of the tower.
  1. A statue 1.6 m tall stands on top of a pedestal. From a point on the ground, the angle of elevation of the top of the statue is $60^\circ$. From the same point, the angle of elevation of the top of the pedestal is $45^\circ$. Find the height of the pedestal.
  1. The angle of elevation of the top of a building from the foot of a tower is $30^\circ$. The angle of elevation of the top of the tower from the foot of the building is $60^\circ$. Take the tower and the building to stand on the same level ground. If the tower is 50 m high, find the height of the building.
  1. Two poles of equal height stand opposite each other, on either side of a road 80 m wide. From a point on the road between them, the angles of elevation of the tops of the poles are $60^\circ$ and $30^\circ$. Find the height of the poles and the distances of the point from the poles. Take the point and the feet of the two poles to lie on one straight line across the road.
  1. A TV tower stands vertically on one bank of a canal. From a point on the other bank, directly opposite the tower, the angle of elevation of its top is $60^\circ$. From another point 20 m further away, on the line joining the first point to the foot of the tower, the angle of elevation of the top is $30^\circ$ (Fig. 9.12). Find the height of the tower and the width of the canal.
