B4 The height is ${s\sqrt{3}}$, so the new shadow is ${s\sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{3} = 3s}$: (b). Option (c) takes the height to be $s$.

Three things from this section are worth keeping. Draw the figure first, and mark which angles are at the ground and which are at the top. An angle of depression is equal to an angle of elevation, so you can always move it inside the right triangle. And look at the angles before writing equations: an isosceles triangle, or the same angle in two triangles, can give the answer in one line.
