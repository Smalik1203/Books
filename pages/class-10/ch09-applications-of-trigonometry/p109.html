Type 8 · Proving a result

Example 15

From the top A of a tower AB, $h$ m high, the angles of depression of two objects C and D on the ground are $\alpha$ and $\beta$, where $\alpha$ is greater than $\beta$. The objects are on the same side of the tower, in a line with its foot B. Prove that the distance between them is ${h(\cot\beta - \cot\alpha)}$ m.

Solution.

Step 1 ${\angle ACB = \alpha}$ and ${\angle ADB = \beta}$ alternate angles
Step 2 in right $\triangle ABC$, ${\cot\alpha = \frac{BC}{AB}}$, so ${BC = h\cot\alpha}$ adjacent over opposite
Step 3 in right $\triangle ABD$, ${\cot\beta = \frac{BD}{AB}}$, so ${BD = h\cot\beta}$
Step 4 $\alpha$ is the larger angle, so C is the nearer object, and ${CD = BD - BC}$ same side, so subtract
Answer ${CD = h\cot\beta - h\cot\alpha = h(\cot\beta - \cot\alpha)}$ m
