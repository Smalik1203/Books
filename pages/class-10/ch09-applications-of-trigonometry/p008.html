Example 6

From the top of a multi-storeyed building, the angles of depression of the top and the bottom of a building 8 m tall are $30^\circ$ and $45^\circ$. Find the height of the multi-storeyed building and the distance between the two buildings.

Solution.

Step 1 in Fig. 9.9, PC is the multi-storeyed building and AB is the building 8 m tall; we need the height PC and the distance AC
Step 2 PQ and BD are both horizontal, so they are parallel, and PB is a transversal to them
Step 3 ${\angle PBD = 30^\circ}$,   and in the same way   ${\angle PAC = 45^\circ}$ $\angle QPB$ and $\angle PBD$ are alternate angles
Step 4 in right $\triangle PBD$,   $\dfrac{PD}{BD} = \tan 30^\circ = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$,   so   ${BD = PD\sqrt{3}}$
Step 5 in right $\triangle PAC$,   $\dfrac{PC}{AC} = \tan 45^\circ = 1$,   so   ${PC = AC}$
Step 6 ${PC = PD + DC}$,   so   ${PD + DC = AC}$
Step 7 ${AC = BD}$ and ${DC = AB = 8}$ m, so ${PD + 8 = BD = PD\sqrt{3}}$. (Why?)
Step 8 ${PD(\sqrt{3} - 1) = 8}$,   so   $PD = \dfrac{8}{\sqrt{3} - 1} = \dfrac{8(\sqrt{3} + 1)}{(\sqrt{3} + 1)(\sqrt{3} - 1)} = 4(\sqrt{3} + 1)$
Answer The multi-storeyed building is ${PC = 4(\sqrt{3} + 1) + 8 = 4(3 + \sqrt{3})}$ m high, and the distance between the buildings is also ${AC = PC = 4(3 + \sqrt{3})}$ m
