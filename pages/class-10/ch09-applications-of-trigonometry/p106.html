Example 10 · Multiple correct

Two poles of equal height stand on opposite sides of a road 40 m wide. From a point on the road between them, the angles of elevation of their tops are $60^\circ$ and $30^\circ$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The point is 10 m from one pole.
  2. Each pole is $10\sqrt{3}$ m tall.
  3. The point is 30 m from the other pole.
  4. Each pole is 20 m tall.

Solution.

Step 1With distances $x$ and $40 - x$: $h = x\sqrt{3} = \frac{40 - x}{\sqrt{3}}$, so $3x = 40 - x$ and $x = 10$.
Step 2So $h = 10\sqrt{3}$ m, and the other pole is 30 m away.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

From a point 50 m from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of its top is $45^\circ$. Find the height of the tower, in m.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$\tan 45^\circ = \frac{h}{50}$.
Step 2$h = 50$ m.
Answer50
