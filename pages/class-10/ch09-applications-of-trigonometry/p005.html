Example 3

An observer 1.5 m tall is 28.5 m away from a chimney. The angle of elevation of the top of the chimney from her eyes is $45^\circ$. What is the height of the chimney?

In Fig. 9.6, AB is the chimney, CD is the observer and $\angle ADE$ is the angle of elevation. The triangle ADE is right-angled at E, and we need the height of the chimney.

${AB = AE + BE = AE + 1.5}$   and   ${DE = CB = 28.5}$ m

To find AE, choose a ratio that uses both AE and DE. The tangent of the angle of elevation does this.

$\tan 45^\circ = \dfrac{AE}{DE}$,   so   $1 = \dfrac{AE}{28.5}$,   which gives ${AE = 28.5}$

So the height of the chimney is ${AB = 28.5 + 1.5 = 30}$ m.
