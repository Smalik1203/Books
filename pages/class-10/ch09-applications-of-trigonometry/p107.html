Type 6 · Two heights seen from one point

Example 11

From the top of a building 9 m high, the angle of elevation of the top of a tower is $60^\circ$, and the angle of depression of its foot is $30^\circ$. The height of the tower is

  1. 27 m
  2. ${9\sqrt{3}}$ m
  3. 36 m
  4. ${18\sqrt{3}}$ m

Solution.

Step 1 ${\tan 30^\circ = \frac{9}{d}}$, so the distance between them is ${d = 9\sqrt{3}}$ m the angle of depression
Step 2 the tower rises ${d \tan 60^\circ = 9\sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{3} = 27}$ m above the building the angle of elevation
Step 3 height of the tower ${= 27 + 9 = 36}$ m
Answer (c)

Option (a) leaves out the 9 m below the level of the eye. Option (b) is the distance between the building and the tower, and (d) is twice that distance.

Example 12

A mobile tower 24 m tall stands on the roof of a building. From a point P on the ground, the angles of elevation of the bottom and the top of the mobile tower are $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Find the height of the building and its distance from P.

Solution.

Step 1 let the building be $h$ m high and $d$ m from P; ${\tan 30^\circ = \frac{h}{d}}$, so ${d = h\sqrt{3}}$ the bottom of the tower
Step 2 ${\tan 60^\circ = \frac{h + 24}{d}}$, so ${h + 24 = \sqrt{3} \times h\sqrt{3} = 3h}$ the top of the tower
Step 3 ${2h = 24}$, so ${h = 12}$ and ${d = 12\sqrt{3}}$
Answer The building is 12 m high and ${12\sqrt{3}}$ m, about 20.78 m, from P
