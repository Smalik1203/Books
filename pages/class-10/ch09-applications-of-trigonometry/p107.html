Example 13 · Numerical answer

Walking 20 m towards a tower, the angle of elevation of its top changes from $30^\circ$ to $60^\circ$. Find the height of the tower, in m. Take $\sqrt{3} = 1.732$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1If the nearer point is $x$ m away: $h = x\sqrt{3} = \frac{x + 20}{\sqrt{3}}$, so $3x = x + 20$ and $x = 10$.
Step 2$h = 10\sqrt{3} = 17.32$ m.
Answer17.32
Example 14 · Matching

A tower's top is seen from 30 m away. Match each angle of elevation with the tower's height in m. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Elevation $30^\circ$(1) $30\sqrt{3}$
(Q) Elevation $45^\circ$(2) 60
(R) Elevation $60^\circ$(3) $10\sqrt{3}$
(S) An elevation whose tangent is 2(4) 30

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The height is $30\tan\theta$: $30 \times \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = 10\sqrt{3}$ and $30 \times 1 = 30$.
Step 2$30 \times \sqrt{3} = 30\sqrt{3}$ and $30 \times 2 = 60$.
Answer(a) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
