Problem 5

A man in a boat rows away from a cliff 150 m high. In 2 minutes, the angle of elevation of the top of the cliff changes from $60^\circ$ to $45^\circ$. His speed is

  1. ${50(3 - \sqrt{3})}$ m per minute
  2. ${25(3 - \sqrt{3})}$ m per minute
  3. ${50\sqrt{3}}$ m per minute
  4. 75 m per minute
Solution

at $60^\circ$ he is ${\frac{150}{\sqrt{3}} = 50\sqrt{3}}$ m from the foot of the cliff; at $45^\circ$ he is 150 m from it

he rows ${150 - 50\sqrt{3} = 50(3 - \sqrt{3})}$ m in 2 minutes

so his speed is ${25(3 - \sqrt{3})}$ m per minute

The answer is (b), about 31.7 m per minute. Option (a) is the distance, not the speed. Option (c) is his first distance from the cliff, and (d) is half the height of the cliff.
