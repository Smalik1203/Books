Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The angle of depression of $B$ from $A$ equals the angle of elevation of $A$ from $B$.
  2. Walking towards a tower, the angle of elevation of its top increases.
  3. When a pole's shadow equals its height, the sun's elevation is $60^\circ$.
  4. An angle of depression is measured from the vertical.

Solution.

Step 1The two angles are alternate angles between horizontal lines, so they are equal; and as the distance shrinks, $\tan\theta = \frac{h}{d}$ grows.
Step 2Equal shadow and height give $\tan\theta = 1$, so $45^\circ$; and both angles are measured from the horizontal.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

A kite flies 60 m above the ground, and its string makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the ground. Taking the string as straight, which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The string is $40\sqrt{3}$ m long.
  2. The kite is $20\sqrt{3}$ m from the flier horizontally.
  3. The string is 120 m long.
  4. The string is about 69.3 m long.

Solution.

Step 1String $= \frac{60}{\sin 60^\circ} = \frac{120}{\sqrt{3}} = 40\sqrt{3} \approx 69.3$ m.
Step 2Horizontal distance $= \frac{60}{\tan 60^\circ} = 20\sqrt{3}$ m.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
