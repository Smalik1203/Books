Example 5 · Single correct

A ladder 10 m long leans against a wall and makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the ground. How high up the wall does it reach?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $5\sqrt{3}$ m
  2. 5 m
  3. $10\sqrt{3}$ m
  4. 10 m

Solution.

Step 1The height is opposite the $60^\circ$ angle: $h = 10 \sin 60^\circ$.
Step 2$h = 10 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 5\sqrt{3}$ m.
Answer(a) $5\sqrt{3}$ m
Example 6 · Single correct

What is the angle of elevation of the top of a pole 10 m tall from a point on the ground 10 m from its foot?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $30^\circ$
  2. $45^\circ$
  3. $60^\circ$
  4. $90^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1$\tan\theta = \frac{10}{10} = 1$.
Step 2So $\theta = 45^\circ$.
Answer(b) $45^\circ$
Example 7 · Multiple correct

The top of a tower 30 m tall is seen at $30^\circ$ from a point $A$ and at $60^\circ$ from a point $B$, both on the same side of the tower and in line with its foot. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $A$ is $30\sqrt{3}$ m from the foot.
  2. $B$ is $10\sqrt{3}$ m from the foot.
  3. $AB = 20\sqrt{3}$ m
  4. $AB = 30$ m

Solution.

Step 1From $A$: $\frac{30}{\tan 30^\circ} = 30\sqrt{3}$; from $B$: $\frac{30}{\tan 60^\circ} = \frac{30}{\sqrt{3}} = 10\sqrt{3}$.
Step 2$AB = 30\sqrt{3} - 10\sqrt{3} = 20\sqrt{3}$ m.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
