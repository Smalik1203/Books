Example 15 · Matching

A pole 15 m tall casts a shadow. Match each elevation of the sun with the shadow's length in m. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Sun at $30^\circ$(1) 0
(Q) Sun at $45^\circ$(2) $5\sqrt{3}$
(R) Sun at $60^\circ$(3) $15\sqrt{3}$
(S) Sun at $90^\circ$(4) 15

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The shadow is $\frac{15}{\tan\theta}$: $15\sqrt{3}$, then 15.
Step 2Then $\frac{15}{\sqrt{3}} = 5\sqrt{3}$; with the sun overhead there is no shadow.
Answer(b) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
