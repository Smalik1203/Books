Why the other options are wrong

A1 ${\tan\theta = \frac{6}{2\sqrt{3}} = \sqrt{3}}$, so ${\theta = 60^\circ}$: (c). Option (a) needs a longer shadow.
A3 The distance is the side adjacent to the angle of $60^\circ$, so it is ${12\cos 60^\circ = 6}$ m: (a). Option (b) is the height the ladder reaches.
A5 ${\sin 60^\circ = \frac{45}{L}}$, so ${L = \frac{90}{\sqrt{3}} = 30\sqrt{3}}$ m: (d). Option (b) uses $\cos 60^\circ$.
A6 The two horizontal lines are parallel, so these are alternate angles: (d).
B1 The building is $\frac{20}{\sqrt{3}}$ m high and the top of the flagstaff is 20 m high, so the flagstaff is ${20 - \frac{20}{\sqrt{3}}}$ m: (c). Option (d) is the height of the building.
B2 The part left standing is ${10\tan 60^\circ = 10\sqrt{3}}$ m, and the broken part is ${\frac{10}{\cos 60^\circ} = 20}$ m: (b). Option (c) takes the broken part as 10 m.
