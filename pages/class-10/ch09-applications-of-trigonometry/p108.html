Type 7 · Speed and time

Example 13

A man in a boat rows away from a cliff 150 m high. In 2 minutes, the angle of elevation of the top of the cliff changes from $60^\circ$ to $45^\circ$. His speed is

  1. ${50(3 - \sqrt{3})}$ m per minute
  2. ${25(3 - \sqrt{3})}$ m per minute
  3. ${50\sqrt{3}}$ m per minute
  4. 75 m per minute

Solution.

Step 1 at $60^\circ$ he is ${\frac{150}{\sqrt{3}} = 50\sqrt{3}}$ m from the foot of the cliff; at $45^\circ$ he is 150 m from it
Step 2 he rows ${150 - 50\sqrt{3} = 50(3 - \sqrt{3})}$ m in 2 minutes
Step 3 speed ${= \frac{50(3 - \sqrt{3})}{2} = 25(3 - \sqrt{3})}$ m per minute distance over time
Answer (b), about 31.7 m per minute

Option (a) is the distance, not the speed. Option (c) is his first distance from the cliff, and (d) is half the height of the cliff.

Example 14

An aeroplane flies horizontally at a height of ${1500\sqrt{3}}$ m. From a point on the ground, its angle of elevation is $60^\circ$, and 15 seconds later it is $30^\circ$. Find the speed of the aeroplane in km/h.

Solution.

Step 1 at $60^\circ$, the spot below the aeroplane is ${\frac{1500\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}} = 1500}$ m from the point distance ${= \frac{\text{height}}{\tan\theta}}$
Step 2 at $30^\circ$, it is ${1500\sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{3} = 4500}$ m from the point
Step 3 it flies ${4500 - 1500 = 3000}$ m in 15 seconds, so ${\frac{3000}{15} = 200}$ m in a second
Step 4 ${200 \times 3600 = 720000}$ m in an hour 3600 seconds in an hour
Answer 720 km/h
