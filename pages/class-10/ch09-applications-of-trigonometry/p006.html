Example 4

From a point P on the ground, the angle of elevation of the top of a building 10 m tall is $30^\circ$. A flag is hoisted at the top of the building, and the angle of elevation of the top of the flagstaff from P is $45^\circ$. Find the length of the flagstaff and the distance of the building from P. (You may take ${\sqrt{3} = 1.732}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 in Fig. 9.7, AB is the building, BD is the flagstaff and P is the given point; PAB and PAD are right triangles, and we need DB and PA
Step 2 $\tan 30^\circ = \dfrac{AB}{AP}$ in PAB, AB is known
Step 3 $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{10}{AP}$,   which gives   ${AP = 10\sqrt{3}}$, or 17.32 m
Step 4 let ${DB = x}$ m; then ${AD = (10 + x)}$ m
Step 5 $\tan 45^\circ = \dfrac{AD}{AP}$,   so   $1 = \dfrac{10 + x}{10\sqrt{3}}$ in the right triangle PAD
Step 6 ${10 + x = 10\sqrt{3}}$,   so   ${x = 10(\sqrt{3} - 1) = 7.32}$
Answer The flagstaff is 7.32 m long, and the building is ${10\sqrt{3}}$ m, or 17.32 m, from P
