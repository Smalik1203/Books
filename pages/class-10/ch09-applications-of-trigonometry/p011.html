1. A straight highway leads to the foot of a tower. A man at the top of the tower sees a car at an angle of depression of $30^\circ$. The car is moving towards the foot of the tower at a steady speed. Six seconds later, the angle of depression of the car is $60^\circ$. How much longer will the car take to reach the foot of the tower?
What this chapter showed
  1. The line of sight is the line from the eye of an observer to the point being looked at.
  2. The angle of elevation of a point is the angle between the line of sight and the horizontal, when the point is above the horizontal level of the eye. We raise our head to look at it.
  3. The angle of depression of a point is the angle between the line of sight and the horizontal, when the point is below the horizontal level of the eye. We lower our head to look at it.
  4. You can find the height or length of an object, or the distance between two objects far apart, with the trigonometric ratios.
