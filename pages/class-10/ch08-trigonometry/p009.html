Example 4

In a right triangle ABC, right-angled at B, if ${\tan A = 1}$, verify that ${2 \sin A \cos A = 1}$.

Solution.

Step 1 In $\triangle ABC$, ${\tan A = \frac{BC}{AB} = 1}$ (Fig. 8.11), so ${BC = AB}$
Step 2 ${AB = BC = k}$ $k$ is a positive number
Step 3 ${AC = \sqrt{AB^2 + BC^2} = \sqrt{k^2 + k^2} = k\sqrt{2}}$ the Pythagoras theorem
Step 4 $\sin A = \dfrac{BC}{AC} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$   and   $\cos A = \dfrac{AB}{AC} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$
Step 5 $2 \sin A \cos A = 2\left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right) = 1$
Answer ${2 \sin A \cos A = 1}$, which is the required value
