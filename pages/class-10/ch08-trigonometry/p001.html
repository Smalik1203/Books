Introduction to Trigonometry

8.1Introduction

You have studied triangles, and right triangles in particular, in earlier classes. Right triangles can be imagined in many situations around us. Here are three of them.

1. The students of a school are visiting the Qutub Minar. When a student looks at the top of the Minar, we can imagine a right triangle, as in Fig. 8.1. Can the student find the height of the Minar without actually measuring it?

2. A girl is sitting on the balcony of her house, on the bank of a river. She is looking down at a flower pot placed on a step of a temple on the other bank. Again we can imagine a right triangle, as in Fig. 8.2. If you know the height at which she is sitting, can you find the width of the river?

3. A hot air balloon is flying in the sky. A girl sees it and runs to tell her mother, who rushes out of the house to look at it. When the girl first saw the balloon, it was at the point A. By the time the mother and the daughter came out, it had moved to another point B. Can you find the height of B above the ground?
