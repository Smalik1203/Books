What this chapter showed
  1. In a right triangle ABC, right-angled at B, ${\sin A = \frac{BC}{AC}}$, ${\cos A = \frac{AB}{AC}}$ and ${\tan A = \frac{BC}{AB}}$.
  2. ${\operatorname{cosec} A = \frac{1}{\sin A}}$, ${\sec A = \frac{1}{\cos A}}$, ${\tan A = \frac{1}{\cot A}}$ and ${\tan A = \frac{\sin A}{\cos A}}$.
  3. If one of the trigonometric ratios of an acute angle is known, the remaining trigonometric ratios of the angle can easily be found.
  4. Table 8.1 gives the trigonometric ratios of $0^\circ$, $30^\circ$, $45^\circ$, $60^\circ$ and $90^\circ$.
  5. $\sin A$ and $\cos A$ are never more than 1; $\sec A$ (${0^\circ \leq A \lt 90^\circ}$) and $\operatorname{cosec} A$ (${0^\circ \lt A \leq 90^\circ}$) are never less than 1.
  6. ${\sin^2 A + \cos^2 A = 1}$; ${\sec^2 A - \tan^2 A = 1}$ for ${0^\circ \leq A \lt 90^\circ}$; ${\operatorname{cosec}^2 A = 1 + \cot^2 A}$ for ${0^\circ \lt A \leq 90^\circ}$.
