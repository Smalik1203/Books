Example 5

In $\triangle OPQ$, right-angled at P, ${OP = 7}$ cm and ${OQ - PQ = 1}$ cm (Fig. 8.12). Find the values of $\sin Q$ and $\cos Q$.

Solution.

Step 1 ${OQ^2 = OP^2 + PQ^2}$ the Pythagoras theorem
Step 2 ${(1 + PQ)^2 = OP^2 + PQ^2}$ Why?
Step 3 ${1 + PQ^2 + 2PQ = OP^2 + PQ^2}$ expanding
Step 4 ${1 + 2PQ = 7^2}$ Why?
Step 5 ${PQ = 24}$ cm   and   ${OQ = 1 + PQ = 25}$ cm
Answer $\sin Q = \dfrac{7}{25}$   and   $\cos Q = \dfrac{24}{25}$
