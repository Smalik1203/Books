Example 14

If ${x = a\sec\theta}$ and ${y = b\tan\theta}$, where $a$ and $b$ are not zero, prove that ${\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} - \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1}$.

Solution.

Step 1 ${\dfrac{x}{a} = \sec\theta}$   and   ${\dfrac{y}{b} = \tan\theta}$ dividing by $a$ and by $b$
Step 2 ${\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} - \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = \sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta}$ squaring, then subtracting
Step 3 ${\sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta = 1}$ ${1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta}$
Answer ${\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} - \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1}$, as required; $\theta$ has been removed
Example 15

If ${\sin\theta + \sin^2\theta = 1}$, prove that ${\cos^2\theta + \cos^4\theta = 1}$.

Solution.

Step 1 ${\sin\theta = 1 - \sin^2\theta = \cos^2\theta}$ from the condition, then the identity
Step 2 ${\cos^4\theta = \sin^2\theta}$ squaring Step 1
Step 3 ${\cos^2\theta + \cos^4\theta = \sin\theta + \sin^2\theta}$ Steps 1 and 2
Answer ${\cos^2\theta + \cos^4\theta = 1}$, by the condition
