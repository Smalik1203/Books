Type 5 · One ratio in terms of another

Example 8

For an acute angle $\theta$, the value of ${(1 - \sin^2\theta)\sec^2\theta}$ is

  1. $0$
  2. $1$
  3. $\tan^2\theta$
  4. $\cos^2\theta$

Solution.

Step 1 ${1 - \sin^2\theta = \cos^2\theta}$ ${\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1}$
Step 2 ${(1 - \sin^2\theta)\sec^2\theta = \cos^2\theta \times \frac{1}{\cos^2\theta} = 1}$ $\sec\theta$ is the reciprocal
Answer (b)

Option (d) stops after the first step and forgets $\sec^2\theta$, and (c) mixes up ${\sec^2\theta - 1}$ with the product.

Example 9

Express $\cos A$ and $\sin A$ in terms of $\tan A$, for an acute angle A.

Solution.

Step 1 ${\sec^2 A = 1 + \tan^2 A}$, so ${\sec A = \sqrt{1 + \tan^2 A}}$ $\sec A$ is positive
Step 2 $\cos A = \dfrac{1}{\sec A} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 + \tan^2 A}}$
Step 3 $\sin A = \tan A \cos A = \dfrac{\tan A}{\sqrt{1 + \tan^2 A}}$ $\tan A = \dfrac{\sin A}{\cos A}$
Answer $\cos A = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 + \tan^2 A}}$   and   $\sin A = \dfrac{\tan A}{\sqrt{1 + \tan^2 A}}$
Example 10

If ${\tan\theta = 2}$ for an acute angle $\theta$, find ${\sec^2\theta + \operatorname{cosec}^2\theta}$ without finding $\theta$.

Solution.

Step 1 ${\sec^2\theta = 1 + \tan^2\theta = 1 + 4 = 5}$ the second identity
Step 2 ${\cot\theta = \frac{1}{2}}$, so ${\operatorname{cosec}^2\theta = 1 + \cot^2\theta = 1 + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{5}{4}}$ the third identity
Answer ${\sec^2\theta + \operatorname{cosec}^2\theta = 5 + \frac{5}{4} = \frac{25}{4}}$
