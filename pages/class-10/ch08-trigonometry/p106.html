Example 12 · Numerical answer

$A$ is acute and $\cos A = 0.6$. Find $5\sin A + 3\tan A$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$\sin A = \sqrt{1 - 0.36} = 0.8$ and $\tan A = \frac{0.8}{0.6} = \frac{4}{3}$.
Step 2$5 \times 0.8 + 3 \times \frac{4}{3} = 4 + 4 = 8$.
Answer8
Example 13 · Numerical answer

$\theta$ is acute and $2\sin\theta = \sqrt{3}$. Find $\theta$, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$\sin\theta = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
Step 2That is $\sin 60^\circ$, so $\theta = 60$.
Answer60

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $\sin 60^\circ$(1) 1
(Q) $\tan 30^\circ$(2) 2
(R) $\cos 0^\circ$(3) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3
