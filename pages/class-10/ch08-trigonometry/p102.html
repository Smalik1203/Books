If ${\sec\theta + \tan\theta = p}$, find ${\sec\theta - \tan\theta}$ and $\sec\theta$ in terms of $p$.

Multiply the two expressions: ${(\sec\theta + \tan\theta)(\sec\theta - \tan\theta) = \sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta = 1}$, by the identity ${1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta}$. So ${\sec\theta - \tan\theta = \frac{1}{p}}$. Adding the two equations gives ${2\sec\theta = p + \frac{1}{p}}$, so ${\sec\theta = \frac{p^2 + 1}{2p}}$. Here the identity was used as a difference of two squares. As a check, ${p = 7}$ gives ${\sec\theta = \frac{25}{7}}$ and ${\tan\theta = \frac{24}{7}}$, and ${\frac{625}{49} - \frac{576}{49} = 1}$.

Type 1 · The ratios from the sides of a right triangle

Example 1

In $\triangle ABC$, right-angled at B, ${AB = 2}$ cm and ${AC = 3}$ cm. Find the six trigonometric ratios of the angle A.

Solution.

Step 1 ${BC = \sqrt{AC^2 - AB^2} = \sqrt{9 - 4} = \sqrt{5}}$ cm the Pythagoras theorem
Step 2 $\sin A = \dfrac{BC}{AC} = \dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$   and   $\cos A = \dfrac{AB}{AC} = \dfrac{2}{3}$ the definitions
Step 3 $\tan A = \dfrac{BC}{AB} = \dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$
Step 4 $\operatorname{cosec} A = \dfrac{3}{\sqrt{5}}$,   $\sec A = \dfrac{3}{2}$,   $\cot A = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{5}}$ the reciprocals
Answer $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$, $\dfrac{2}{3}$, $\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$, $\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{5}}$, $\dfrac{3}{2}$, $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{5}}$, in the order sin, cos, tan, cosec, sec, cot
