If ${\sec\theta + \tan\theta = p}$, find ${\sec\theta - \tan\theta}$ and $\sec\theta$ in terms of $p$.

Multiply the two expressions: ${(\sec\theta + \tan\theta)(\sec\theta - \tan\theta) = \sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta = 1}$, by the identity ${1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta}$. So ${\sec\theta - \tan\theta = \frac{1}{p}}$. Adding the two equations gives ${2\sec\theta = p + \frac{1}{p}}$, so ${\sec\theta = \frac{p^2 + 1}{2p}}$. Here the identity was used as a difference of two squares. As a check, ${p = 7}$ gives ${\sec\theta = \frac{25}{7}}$ and ${\tan\theta = \frac{24}{7}}$, and ${\frac{625}{49} - \frac{576}{49} = 1}$.

Example 1 · Single correct

$A$ is acute and $\sin A = \frac{3}{5}$. What is $\cos A$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{4}{5}$
  2. $\frac{3}{4}$
  3. $\frac{5}{4}$
  4. $\frac{5}{3}$

Solution.

Step 1Take a right triangle with opposite side 3 and hypotenuse 5: the adjacent side is $\sqrt{25 - 9} = 4$.
Step 2$\cos A = \frac{4}{5}$.
Answer(a) $\frac{4}{5}$
Example 2 · Single correct

$A$ is acute and $\tan A = \frac{5}{12}$. What is $\sec A$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{12}{13}$
  2. $\frac{13}{12}$
  3. $\frac{5}{13}$
  4. $\frac{13}{5}$

Solution.

Step 1With opposite side 5 and adjacent side 12, the hypotenuse is $\sqrt{25 + 144} = 13$.
Step 2$\sec A = \frac{\text{hypotenuse}}{\text{adjacent}} = \frac{13}{12}$.
Answer(b) $\frac{13}{12}$
