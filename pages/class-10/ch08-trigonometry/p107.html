Example 15 · Matching

$A$ is acute and $\sin A = \frac{8}{17}$. Match each ratio with its value. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $\cos A$(1) $\frac{15}{8}$
(Q) $\tan A$(2) $\frac{17}{15}$
(R) $\sec A$(3) $\frac{8}{15}$
(S) $\cot A$(4) $\frac{15}{17}$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–2, R–3, S–1
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  3. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–3, R–2, S–4

Solution.

Step 1With opposite side 8 and hypotenuse 17, the adjacent side is $\sqrt{289 - 64} = 15$.
Step 2So $\cos A = \frac{15}{17}$, $\tan A = \frac{8}{15}$, $\sec A = \frac{17}{15}$ and $\cot A = \frac{15}{8}$.
Answer(c) P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
