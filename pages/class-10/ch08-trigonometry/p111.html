Case-based questions

  1. A geometry box holds two set squares, each a right triangle.

    Set squareAnglesWhat is known
    P$45^\circ$, $45^\circ$, $90^\circ$each shorter side is 12 cm
    Q$30^\circ$, $60^\circ$, $90^\circ$the longest side is 20 cm
    1. Find the longest side of P.
    2. Find the two shorter sides of Q.
    3. Using the sides of Q, find ${\tan 30^\circ \times \tan 60^\circ}$.
    4. Find the area of P.
