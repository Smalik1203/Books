Example 2

If ${\cos A = \frac{9}{41}}$ for an acute angle A, then $\tan A$ is

  1. $\dfrac{40}{41}$
  2. $\dfrac{40}{9}$
  3. $\dfrac{9}{40}$
  4. $\dfrac{41}{40}$

Solution.

Step 1 take ${AB = 9k}$ and ${AC = 41k}$ in a triangle right-angled at B $\cos A = \dfrac{AB}{AC}$
Step 2 ${BC = \sqrt{1681k^2 - 81k^2} = \sqrt{1600k^2} = 40k}$ the Pythagoras theorem
Step 3 $\tan A = \dfrac{BC}{AB} = \dfrac{40k}{9k} = \dfrac{40}{9}$
Answer (b)

Option (a) is $\sin A$, (c) is $\cot A$, and (d) is $\operatorname{cosec} A$. All four come from the triangle with sides 9, 40 and 41.

Example 3

In $\triangle ABC$, right-angled at B, ${BC = 35}$ cm and ${AC - AB = 25}$ cm. Find $\sin A$, $\cos A$ and $\tan A$.

Solution.

Step 1 ${AC^2 - AB^2 = BC^2 = 35^2 = 1225}$ the Pythagoras theorem
Step 2 ${(AC - AB)(AC + AB) = 1225}$, so ${AC + AB = 1225 \div 25 = 49}$ difference of two squares
Step 3 ${AC = (49 + 25) \div 2 = 37}$ and ${AB = (49 - 25) \div 2 = 12}$ adding, then subtracting
Answer $\sin A = \dfrac{35}{37}$,   $\cos A = \dfrac{12}{37}$,   $\tan A = \dfrac{35}{12}$
