Example 3 · Single correct

What is $\sin 30^\circ + \cos 60^\circ$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{1}{2}$
  2. $\sqrt{3}$
  3. 1
  4. 0

Solution.

Step 1$\sin 30^\circ = \frac{1}{2}$ and $\cos 60^\circ = \frac{1}{2}$.
Step 2Their sum is 1.
Answer(c) 1
Example 4 · Single correct

What is $2\tan 45^\circ + \cos 30^\circ \sin 60^\circ$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 2
  2. 3
  3. $\frac{7}{4}$
  4. $\frac{11}{4}$

Solution.

Step 1$\tan 45^\circ = 1$ and $\cos 30^\circ = \sin 60^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
Step 2$2 + \frac{3}{4} = \frac{11}{4}$.
Answer(d) $\frac{11}{4}$
Example 5 · Single correct

$\theta$ is acute and $\sin\theta = \cos\theta$. What is $\theta$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $45^\circ$
  2. $30^\circ$
  3. $60^\circ$
  4. $90^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1Dividing by $\cos\theta$ gives $\tan\theta = 1$.
Step 2So $\theta = 45^\circ$.
Answer(a) $45^\circ$
