Table 8.1 gives the values of all the trigonometric ratios of $0^\circ$, $30^\circ$, $45^\circ$, $60^\circ$ and $90^\circ$, for ready reference.

Remark. From the table, you can see that as $\angle A$ increases from $0^\circ$ to $90^\circ$, $\sin A$ increases from 0 to 1 and $\cos A$ decreases from 1 to 0.
