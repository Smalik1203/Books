In this chapter you will study quadratic equations and ways of finding their roots. You will also see how quadratic equations are used in everyday situations.

4.2Quadratic Equations

Quadratic equation

A quadratic equation in the variable $x$ is an equation of the form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are real numbers and $a \neq 0$.

For example, $2x^2 + x - 300 = 0$ is a quadratic equation. So are $2x^2 - 3x + 1 = 0$, $4x - 3x^2 + 2 = 0$ and $1 - x^2 + 300 = 0$.

In fact, any equation $p(x) = 0$, where $p(x)$ is a polynomial of degree 2, is a quadratic equation. When we write the terms of $p(x)$ in descending order of their degrees, we get $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, with $a \neq 0$. This is called the standard form of a quadratic equation.

Quadratic equations arise in many situations in the world around us, and in different parts of mathematics. Let us look at a few examples.

Example 1

Write each situation as an equation.

(i) John and Jivanti together have 45 marbles. Each of them loses 5 marbles, and the product of the numbers of marbles they now have is 124. We want to find how many marbles each had to start with.

(ii) A cottage industry makes some toys in a day. The cost of making each toy (in rupees) is 55 minus the number of toys made that day. On one day, the total cost was ₹750. We want to find the number of toys made that day.

Solution.

Step 1 (i) John had $x$ marbles and Jivanti $45 - x$ (Why?); after each loses 5, they have $x - 5$ and $45 - x - 5 = 40 - x$
Step 2 product $= (x - 5)(40 - x) = 40x - x^2 - 200 + 5x = -x^2 + 45x - 200$
Step 3 $-x^2 + 45x - 200 = 124$, so $-x^2 + 45x - 324 = 0$ the product is 124
Step 4 (ii) $x$ toys were made, each costing $55 - x$ rupees, so $x(55 - x) = 750$
Step 5 $55x - x^2 = 750$, that is, $-x^2 + 55x - 750 = 0$
Answer (i) $x^2 - 45x + 324 = 0$   (ii) $x^2 - 55x + 750 = 0$

The first equation is satisfied by the number of marbles John had, and the second by the number of toys made that day. Each describes its problem mathematically.
