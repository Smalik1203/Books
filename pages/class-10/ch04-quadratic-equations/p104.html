Problem 5

The sum of the squares of two consecutive even natural numbers is 340. The numbers are

  1. 12, 14
  2. 10, 12
  3. 14, 16
  4. $-14$, $-12$
Solution

Let the numbers be $x$ and $x + 2$. Then $x^2 + (x + 2)^2 = 340$, which gives $(x + 14)(x - 12) = 0$. Natural numbers are positive, so $x = 12$: (a). Option (d) fits the equation, but not the words natural numbers. The squares in (b) and (c) do not add up to 340.
