Example 6 · Single correct

What are the roots of $x^2 + x - 12 = 0$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $-3$ and $4$
  2. $3$ and $-4$
  3. $2$ and $-6$
  4. $-2$ and $6$

Solution.

Step 1Two numbers with product $-12$ and sum 1: 4 and $-3$.
Step 2$x^2 + x - 12 = (x + 4)(x - 3)$, so the roots are 3 and $-4$.
Answer(b) $3$ and $-4$
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these are quadratic equations?

Select all correct options.

  1. $(x + 1)^2 = 2(x - 3)$
  2. $x(x + 1) + 8 = (x + 2)(x - 2)$
  3. $x^2 - 2x = (-2)(3 - x)$
  4. $(x + 2)^3 = x^3 - 4$

Solution.

Step 1Simplify each: (a) $x^2 + 7 = 0$; (c) $x^2 - 4x + 6 = 0$; (d) the cubes cancel, leaving $6x^2 + 12x + 12 = 0$.
Step 2(b) becomes $x + 12 = 0$: the $x^2$ terms cancel, so it is linear.
Answer(a), (c), (d)
