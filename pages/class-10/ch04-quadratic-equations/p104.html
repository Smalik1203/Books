Example 6

The sum of the squares of two consecutive even natural numbers is 340. The numbers are

  1. 12, 14
  2. 10, 12
  3. 14, 16
  4. $-14$, $-12$

Solution.

Step 1 $x^2 + (x + 2)^2 = 340$ the numbers $x$ and $x + 2$
Step 2 $2x^2 + 4x - 336 = 0$, that is, $x^2 + 2x - 168 = 0$
Step 3 $(x + 14)(x - 12) = 0$, so $x = 12$ or $x = -14$
Step 4 $x = 12$ natural numbers are positive
Answer (a)

Option (d) fits the equation, but not the words natural numbers. The squares in (b) add up to 244 and those in (c) to 452.

Example 7

A train travels 360 km at a uniform speed. If its speed had been 5 km/h more, it would have taken 1 hour less. Its speed, in km/h, is

  1. 40
  2. 45
  3. $-45$
  4. 36

Solution.

Step 1 speed $u$ km/h: $\dfrac{360}{u} - \dfrac{360}{u + 5} = 1$ the times differ by 1 hour
Step 2 $360(u + 5) - 360u = u(u + 5)$, so $u^2 + 5u - 1800 = 0$ multiply by $u(u + 5)$
Step 3 $(u + 45)(u - 40) = 0$, so $u = 40$ or $u = -45$
Answer (a), since a speed cannot be negative

Option (c) is the other root, which the question rules out, and (b) is that root with its sign dropped. With a speed of 36 km/h the two times are 10 hours and $\dfrac{360}{41}$ hours, which do not differ by 1 hour.
