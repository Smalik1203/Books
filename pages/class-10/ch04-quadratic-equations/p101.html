Using What You Know

The chapter showed you how to solve a quadratic equation by factorising and by the formula, and how the discriminant tells you what kind of roots to expect. What changes now is that a question may ask whether something is possible, or ask about a letter in the equation instead of $x$.

For what values of $k$ does $x^2 + kx + 9 = 0$ have two equal roots? What is the root in each case?

Equal roots need the discriminant to be 0, so $k^2 - 4 \times 1 \times 9 = 0$, that is, $k^2 = 36$, and $k = 6$ or $k = -6$. Both values work. When the roots are equal, each root is $-\frac{b}{2a} = -\frac{k}{2}$. So for $k = 6$ the root is $-3$, since $x^2 + 6x + 9 = (x + 3)^2$, and for $k = -6$ the root is 3, since $x^2 - 6x + 9 = (x - 3)^2$.

One root of $x^2 - 5x + k = 0$ is 2. Find $k$ and the other root.

A root satisfies the equation, so $2^2 - 5 \times 2 + k = 0$, which gives $k = 6$. For the other root, we need not solve $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$ from the start. From Chapter 2, the sum of the roots is $-\frac{b}{a} = 5$, so the other root is $5 - 2 = 3$. Check: $3^2 - 5 \times 3 + 6 = 0$.

Can the sum of a number and its reciprocal be $\frac{3}{2}$?

Let the number be $x$, which cannot be 0. Then $x + \frac{1}{x} = \frac{3}{2}$. Multiplying both sides by $2x$ gives $2x^2 + 2 = 3x$, that is, $2x^2 - 3x + 2 = 0$. Its discriminant is $9 - 16 = -7$, which is negative, so there is no real number $x$. The answer is no. We did not try a single number: the discriminant settled the question.

The product of two consecutive odd integers is 143. Find the integers.

Let the integers be $x$ and $x + 2$. Then $x(x + 2) = 143$, that is, $x^2 + 2x - 143 = 0$, or $(x + 13)(x - 11) = 0$. So $x = 11$ or $x = -13$. Both roots answer this question: 11 and 13 work, and so do $-13$ and $-11$, whose product is also 143. If the question had said positive integers, only 11 and 13 would be allowed. Whether a negative root is kept depends on the question, not on the equation. Check both pairs: $11 \times 13 = 143$ and $(-13) \times (-11) = 143$.
