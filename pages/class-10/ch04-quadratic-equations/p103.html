Problem 2

The equation $4x^2 - 12x + 9 = 0$ has

  1. two distinct real roots
  2. two equal real roots
  3. no real roots
  4. more than two roots
Solution

The discriminant is $(-12)^2 - 4 \times 4 \times 9 = 144 - 144 = 0$, so the roots are equal: (b). Option (a) comes from taking $b^2 + 4ac$, and (c) from getting the sign of $b^2$ wrong. Option (d) cannot happen.

Problem 3

The equation $kx^2 + 4x + 1 = 0$ is a quadratic equation with real roots when

  1. $k \leq 4$
  2. $k \lt 4$
  3. $k \geq 4$
  4. $k \leq 4$, $k \neq 0$
Solution

Real roots need $b^2 - 4ac \geq 0$, that is, $16 - 4k \geq 0$, so $k \leq 4$. A quadratic equation also needs $k \neq 0$. The answer is (d). Option (a) forgets $k \neq 0$, (b) leaves out the equal roots at $k = 4$, and (c) turns the inequality round.

Problem 4

A train travels 360 km at a uniform speed. If its speed had been 5 km/h more, it would have taken 1 hour less. Its speed, in km/h, is

  1. 40
  2. 45
  3. $-45$
  4. 36
Solution

speed $u$ km/h:   $\dfrac{360}{u} - \dfrac{360}{u + 5} = 1$

$u^2 + 5u - 1800 = 0$, that is, $(u + 45)(u - 40) = 0$, so $u = 40$ or $u = -45$

A speed cannot be negative, so the answer is (a). Option (c) is the other root, which the question rules out, and (b) is that root with its sign dropped. Option (d) does not satisfy the equation.
