Example 3 · Single correct

For what value of $k$ does $x^2 - 6x + k = 0$ have two equal roots?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 6
  2. 36
  3. 9
  4. 3

Solution.

Step 1Equal roots need $D = 0$: $36 - 4k = 0$.
Step 2$k = 9$, and then $x^2 - 6x + 9 = (x - 3)^2$.
Answer(c) 9

Example 5 · Single correct

The product of two consecutive positive integers is 306. What is the smaller?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 17
  2. 18
  3. 16
  4. 34

Solution.

Step 1$x(x + 1) = 306$, so $x^2 + x - 306 = 0 = (x + 18)(x - 17)$.
Step 2The positive root is 17: $17 \times 18 = 306$.
Answer(a) 17
