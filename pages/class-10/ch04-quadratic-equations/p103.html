Type 2 · Roots by factorisation

Example 3

The roots of $2x^2 - 7x + 3 = 0$ are

  1. $3$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$
  2. $-3$ and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$
  3. $\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $1$
  4. $6$ and $1$

Solution.

Step 1 $2x^2 - 7x + 3 = 2x^2 - 6x - x + 3$ split the middle term
Step 2 $= 2x(x - 3) - 1(x - 3) = (2x - 1)(x - 3)$
Step 3 $2x - 1 = 0$ gives $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$, and $x - 3 = 0$ gives $x = 3$
Answer (a)

Option (b) gets the signs wrong: those are the roots of $(2x + 1)(x + 3) = 0$. Option (c) gives the roots of $2x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$, and (d) the roots of $x^2 - 7x + 6 = 0$, which forgets the coefficient 2.

Example 4

Find the roots of $x^2 - (\sqrt{2} + 1)x + \sqrt{2} = 0$.

Solution.

Step 1 $x^2 - \sqrt{2}x - x + \sqrt{2} = 0$ split the middle term
Step 2 $x(x - \sqrt{2}) - 1(x - \sqrt{2}) = 0$
Step 3 $(x - \sqrt{2})(x - 1) = 0$
Answer the roots are $\sqrt{2}$ and 1
Example 5

Solve $x - \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{3}{2}$, where $x \neq 0$.

Solution.

Step 1 $2x^2 - 2 = 3x$ multiply both sides by $2x$
Step 2 $2x^2 - 3x - 2 = 0$
Step 3 $2x^2 - 4x + x - 2 = 2x(x - 2) + 1(x - 2) = (2x + 1)(x - 2)$
Step 4 $x = 2$ or $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ neither is 0
Answer $x = 2$ or $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$
