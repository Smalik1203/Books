Why the other options are wrong

1 In (b) the $x^3$ terms cancel, leaving $-x^2 - 1 = 0$, which is quadratic. In (a) and (c) the $x^2$ terms cancel instead, leaving linear equations, and (d) is linear.
2 $x^2 - 9x + 20 = (x - 4)(x - 5)$. Option (a) has the signs wrong. The numbers in (b) and (d) multiply to 20, but their sums are 12 and 21, not 9.
3 $b^2 - 4ac = 25 - 24 = 1$. Option (a) adds $4ac$ instead of taking it away, (b) leaves out the 4, and (c) subtracts the wrong way round.
4 $4 + 2k - 6 = 0$ gives $k = 1$. Option (b) gets the sign wrong, (c) takes the 4 to the wrong side, and (d) leaves out the 4.
6 $x(2x + 3) = 0$ gives $x = 0$ or $x = -\frac{3}{2}$. Option (a) divides by $x$ and loses the root 0, and (b) gets the sign wrong.
7 $(2k)^2 - 4 \times 16 = 0$ gives $k^2 = 16$, so $k = 4$ or $k = -4$. Option (a) keeps one value only, and (c) and (d) take $b$ as $k$ instead of $-2k$.
11 $x^2 + x - 42 = (x + 7)(x - 6) = 0$, so $x = 6$ or $x = -7$, and both work. Options (a) and (d) keep one root, and (c) gets both signs wrong.
12 $x(x + 30) = 400$ gives $(x + 40)(x - 10) = 0$, so the son is 10. Option (b) is the father’s age, and also the negative root with its sign dropped.
14 No real roots needs $p^2 - 64 \lt 0$, that is, $p$ between $-8$ and 8. For (d) the roots are equal, and for (a) and (c) they are distinct.
15 $x^2 + (x + 5)^2 = 625$ gives $x^2 + 5x - 300 = (x + 20)(x - 15) = 0$, so the sides are 15 cm and 20 cm. Option (a) is the other side, and (b) and (c) do not satisfy the equation.
17 $x(x + 3) = x^2 - 2$ becomes $3x + 2 = 0$, which is linear, so A is false. R is the definition, so it is true: the answer is (d).
19 $36 - 36 = 0$, so A is true. R is false: a positive discriminant gives two distinct roots. The answer is (c).

Three things from this section are worth keeping. The discriminant can show that something is impossible, or always possible, without solving anything. A root of the equation must still be checked against the question: a length or a speed cannot be negative, but an integer can. And an equation with fractions or with a letter in it becomes an ordinary quadratic equation once it is written in the form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$.
