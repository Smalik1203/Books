Example 12 · Numerical answer

The squares of two consecutive positive odd integers add to 290. Find the larger integer.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$x^2 + (x + 2)^2 = 290$ gives $2x^2 + 4x - 286 = 0$, or $x^2 + 2x - 143 = 0$.
Step 2$(x + 13)(x - 11) = 0$, so the integers are 11 and 13.
Answer13
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A train covers 360 km at a steady speed. Had it gone 5 km/h faster, it would have taken 1 hour less. Find its speed in km/h.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{360}{v} - \frac{360}{v + 5} = 1$ gives $1800 = v^2 + 5v$.
Step 2$v^2 + 5v - 1800 = (v + 45)(v - 40) = 0$, so $v = 40$.
Answer40
