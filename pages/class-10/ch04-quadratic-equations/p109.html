1. Assertion (A): $x(x + 3) = x^2 - 2$ is a quadratic equation.

    Reason (R): A quadratic equation is an equation $p(x) = 0$ where $p(x)$ is a polynomial of degree 2.

  1. Assertion (A): 3 is a root of $x^2 - x - 6 = 0$.

    Reason (R): A quadratic equation has at most two roots.

  1. Assertion (A): $x^2 - 6x + 9 = 0$ has two equal roots.

    Reason (R): A quadratic equation has two equal roots when $b^2 - 4ac \gt 0$.
