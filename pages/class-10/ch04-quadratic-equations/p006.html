The roots of $6x^2 - x - 2 = 0$ are the values of $x$ for which $(3x - 2)(2x + 1) = 0$, that is, $3x - 2 = 0$ or $2x + 1 = 0$. So the roots are $\frac{2}{3}$ and $-\frac{1}{2}$.

Example 5

Find the roots of the quadratic equation $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$.

$3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 3x^2 - \sqrt{6}x - \sqrt{6}x + 2$
$= \sqrt{3}x\left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right) - \sqrt{2}\left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right)$
$= \left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right)\left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right)$

So the roots are the values of $x$ for which $\left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right)\left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right) = 0$. Now $\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2} = 0$ when $x = \sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}$. The same factor appears twice, so this root is repeated, once for each factor. The roots of $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$ are $\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}$ and $\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}$.

Example 6

Find the length and breadth of the prayer hall in Section 4.1.

In Section 4.1 we found that if the breadth of the hall is $x$ m, then $2x^2 + x - 300 = 0$. Factorising,

$2x^2 - 24x + 25x - 300 = 0$
$2x(x - 12) + 25(x - 12) = 0$
$(x - 12)(2x + 25) = 0$

So the roots of the equation are $x = 12$ and $x = -12.5$. The breadth of the hall cannot be negative, so the breadth is 12 m, and the length is $2x + 1 = 25$ m.
