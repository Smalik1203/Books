4.4Nature of Roots

Not every quadratic polynomial can be factorised easily, so we also need a way of finding the roots of any quadratic equation. It is given by the quadratic formula.

The quadratic formula

The roots of the quadratic equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, $a \neq 0$, are given by

$$x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}, \qquad \text{provided } b^2 - 4ac \geq 0$$

You can check that these two numbers fit what you learnt in Chapter 2. Their sum is $\frac{-2b}{2a} = -\frac{b}{a}$, and their product is $\frac{b^2 - (b^2 - 4ac)}{4a^2} = \frac{c}{a}$, which are exactly the sum and product of the zeroes of $ax^2 + bx + c$.
