Step 4 uses one fact: a product of two numbers is 0 only when one of them is 0. Check that both roots satisfy the equation. We found these roots by factorising $2x^2 - 5x + 3$ into two linear factors, and setting each factor equal to zero.

Example 4

Find the roots of the quadratic equation $6x^2 - x - 2 = 0$.

Solution.

Step 1 $6x^2 - x - 2 = 6x^2 + 3x - 4x - 2$ split the middle term
Step 2 $= 3x(2x + 1) - 2(2x + 1)$
Step 3 $= (3x - 2)(2x + 1)$
Step 4 $(3x - 2)(2x + 1) = 0$, that is, $3x - 2 = 0$ or $2x + 1 = 0$ a zero product
Answer the roots of $6x^2 - x - 2 = 0$ are $\frac{2}{3}$ and $-\frac{1}{2}$
Example 5

Find the roots of the quadratic equation $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$.

Solution.

Step 1 $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 3x^2 - \sqrt{6}x - \sqrt{6}x + 2$ split the middle term
Step 2 $= \sqrt{3}x\left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right) - \sqrt{2}\left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right)$ take out common factors
Step 3 $= \left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right)\left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right)$
Step 4 the roots are the values of $x$ for which $\left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right)\left(\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2}\right) = 0$
Step 5 $\sqrt{3}x - \sqrt{2} = 0$ when $x = \sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}$ once for each factor
Answer the roots of $3x^2 - 2\sqrt{6}x + 2 = 0$ are $\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}$ and $\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}$

The same factor appears twice, so this root is repeated, once for each factor.

Example 6

Find the length and breadth of the prayer hall in Section 4.1.

Solution.

Step 1 breadth $x$ m gives $2x^2 + x - 300 = 0$ from Section 4.1
Step 2 $2x^2 - 24x + 25x - 300 = 0$, so $2x(x - 12) + 25(x - 12) = 0$
Step 3 $(x - 12)(2x + 25) = 0$, so $x = 12$ or $x = -12.5$
Answer the breadth is 12 m, since it cannot be negative, and the length is $2x + 1 = 25$ m
