Exercise Set 4.3
  1. Find the nature of the roots of each quadratic equation. If it has real roots, find them.
    1. $2x^2 - 3x + 5 = 0$
    2. $3x^2 - 4\sqrt{3}x + 4 = 0$
    3. $2x^2 - 6x + 3 = 0$
  1. For each quadratic equation, find the values of $k$ for which it has two equal roots.
    1. $2x^2 + kx + 3 = 0$
    2. $kx(x - 2) + 6 = 0$
  1. Is it possible to design a rectangular mango grove whose length is twice its breadth and whose area is 800 m²? If so, find its length and breadth.
  1. The sum of the ages of two friends is 20 years. Four years ago, the product of their ages in years was 48. Is this situation possible? If so, find their ages now.
  1. Is it possible to design a rectangular park with a perimeter of 80 m and an area of 400 m²? If so, find its length and breadth.

A root that solves the equation does not always answer the question.
