Example 12

Find the values of $k$ for which $(k + 1)x^2 - 2(k - 1)x + 1 = 0$ has two equal roots, and find the roots in each case.

Solution.

Step 1 $a = k + 1$, $b = -2(k - 1)$, $c = 1$
Step 2 $b^2 - 4ac = 4(k - 1)^2 - 4(k + 1) = 4k^2 - 12k$ expand
Step 3 $4k(k - 3) = 0$, so $k = 0$ or $k = 3$ equal roots need zero
Step 4 $k = 0$ gives $x^2 + 2x + 1 = 0$; $k = 3$ gives $4x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0$
Answer $k = 0$, with both roots $-1$; or $k = 3$, with both roots $\dfrac{1}{2}$

Here $k = 0$ is allowed, because the coefficient of $x^2$ is then 1, not 0.

Example 13

One root of $x^2 + kx + 12 = 0$ is 2. Find $k$. Then find the value of $q$ for which $x^2 + kx + q = 0$ has two equal roots.

Solution.

Step 1 $2^2 + 2k + 12 = 0$, so $k = -8$ a root satisfies it
Step 2 $x^2 - 8x + q = 0$ has $b^2 - 4ac = 64 - 4q$
Step 3 $64 - 4q = 0$, so $q = 16$ equal roots
Answer $k = -8$ and $q = 16$

Check: $x^2 - 8x + 16 = (x - 4)^2$, so both roots are 4.

Example 14

Solve $x^2 - 4x - 1 = 0$ by the quadratic formula.

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 1$, $b = -4$, $c = -1$
Step 2 $b^2 - 4ac = 16 + 4 = 20$ positive: two roots
Step 3 $x = \dfrac{4 \pm \sqrt{20}}{2} = \dfrac{4 \pm 2\sqrt{5}}{2}$ simplify the surd
Answer $x = 2 + \sqrt{5}$ or $x = 2 - \sqrt{5}$
