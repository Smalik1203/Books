Example 10 · Multiple correct

A rectangle is 5 m longer than it is broad, and its area is $84 \text{ m}^2$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its breadth is 7 m.
  2. Its length is 12 m.
  3. Its perimeter is 40 m.
  4. Its diagonal is 15 m.

Solution.

Step 1$b(b + 5) = 84$ gives $b^2 + 5b - 84 = (b + 12)(b - 7) = 0$, so $b = 7$ and the length is 12.
Step 2The perimeter is $2(7 + 12) = 38$ m, and the diagonal $\sqrt{49 + 144} = \sqrt{193}$ m.
