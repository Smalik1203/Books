Example 8

A rectangular park is 20 m long and 14 m wide. A path of the same width all round runs inside the park along its four sides, and the area of the path is 120 m². Find the width of the path.

Solution.

Step 1 path $w$ m wide: the part inside it is $(20 - 2w)$ m by $(14 - 2w)$ m
Step 2 $(20 - 2w)(14 - 2w) = 280 - 120 = 160$ the park less the path
Step 3 $280 - 68w + 4w^2 = 160$, so $w^2 - 17w + 30 = 0$
Step 4 $(w - 2)(w - 15) = 0$, so $w = 2$ or $w = 15$
Step 5 $w = 15$ would make $14 - 2w$ negative set it aside
Answer the path is 2 m wide

Example 9

The equation $4x^2 - 12x + 9 = 0$ has

  1. two distinct real roots
  2. two equal real roots
  3. no real roots
  4. more than two roots

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 4$, $b = -12$, $c = 9$
Step 2 $b^2 - 4ac = (-12)^2 - 4 \times 4 \times 9 = 144 - 144 = 0$ zero, so equal roots
Answer (b)

Option (a) comes from taking $b^2 + 4ac$, and (c) from taking $b^2$ as negative. Option (d) cannot happen: a quadratic equation has at most two roots.
