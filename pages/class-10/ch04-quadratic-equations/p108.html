Example 15

Solve $\dfrac{1}{x} - \dfrac{1}{x - 2} = 3$, where $x \neq 0$ and $x \neq 2$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{(x - 2) - x}{x(x - 2)} = 3$ one fraction
Step 2 $-2 = 3x^2 - 6x$, so $3x^2 - 6x + 2 = 0$
Step 3 $b^2 - 4ac = 36 - 24 = 12$
Step 4 $x = \dfrac{6 \pm \sqrt{12}}{6} = \dfrac{6 \pm 2\sqrt{3}}{6}$ the quadratic formula
Answer $x = 1 + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ or $x = 1 - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$; neither is 0 or 2

Example 16

Is it possible to mark out a rectangle with a perimeter of 28 m and an area of 50 m²?

Solution.

Step 1 the sides are $x$ m and $(14 - x)$ m half the perimeter is 14 m
Step 2 $x(14 - x) = 50$, so $x^2 - 14x + 50 = 0$
Step 3 $b^2 - 4ac = 196 - 200 = -4$ negative: no real roots
Answer No. No real number $x$ can be a side
