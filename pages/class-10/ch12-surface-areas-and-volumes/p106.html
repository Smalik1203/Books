Example 11 · Numerical answer

Find the surface area, in $\text{cm}^2$, of a sphere of radius 7 cm. Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Surface area $= 4\pi r^2$.
Step 2$= 4 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 49 = 616$.
Answer616
Example 12 · Numerical answer

A cylindrical tank has radius 70 cm and height 1 m. How many litres does it hold? Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$V = \frac{22}{7} \times 4900 \times 100 = 1\,540\,000 \text{ cm}^3$.
Step 2$1000 \text{ cm}^3$ is one litre, so the tank holds 1540 litres.
Answer1540
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A cylinder of radius 2 cm and height 9 cm is melted and recast into spheres of radius 1 cm. How many spheres are made?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The cylinder holds $36\pi$ and each sphere $\frac{4}{3}\pi$: $36\pi \div \frac{4}{3}\pi = 27$.
Answer27
