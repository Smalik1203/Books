Example 3

A wooden toy rocket is shaped like a cone mounted on a cylinder (Fig. 12.8). The whole rocket is 26 cm high, and the conical part is 6 cm high. The base of the conical part has a diameter of 5 cm, and the base of the cylindrical part has a diameter of 3 cm. The conical part is to be painted orange and the cylindrical part yellow. Find the area painted in each colour. (Take ${\pi = 3.14}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 the cone has radius $r$, height $h$ and slant height $l$; the cylinder has radius $r'$ and height $h'$
Step 2 ${r = 2.5}$ cm,   ${h = 6}$ cm,   ${r' = 1.5}$ cm,   ${h' = 26 - 6 = 20}$ cm radius is half the diameter
Step 3 ${l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2} = \sqrt{2.5^2 + 6^2} = 6.5}$ cm
Step 4 area painted orange $=$ CSA of the cone $+$ base area of the cone $-$ base area of the cylinder a ring of the base shows
Step 5 $= \pi rl + \pi r^2 - \pi (r')^2 = \pi[(2.5 \times 6.5) + 2.5^2 - 1.5^2]$ cm2
Step 6 $= 3.14 \times 20.25$ cm2 $= 63.585$ cm2
Step 7 area painted yellow $=$ CSA of the cylinder $+$ area of one base of the cylinder the top base is covered
Step 8 $= 2\pi r'h' + \pi (r')^2 = \pi r'(2h' + r') = (3.14 \times 1.5)(2 \times 20 + 1.5)$ cm2
Step 9 $= 4.71 \times 41.5$ cm2 $= 195.465$ cm2
Answer orange 63.585 cm2, yellow 195.465 cm2

The base of the cone rests on the top of the cylinder, but it is larger than the base of the cylinder. So a ring of the base of the cone is left uncovered, and it has to be painted orange too.
