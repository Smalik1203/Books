Example 8 · Multiple correct

A cube has edge 6 cm. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its volume is $216 \text{ cm}^3$.
  2. Its total surface area is $216 \text{ cm}^2$.
  3. The largest sphere inside it has radius 6 cm.
  4. Its diagonal is $6\sqrt{2}$ cm.

Solution.

Step 1$6^3 = 216$ and $6 \times 6^2 = 216$.
Step 2The largest sphere has diameter 6, so radius 3; the cube's diagonal is $6\sqrt{3}$.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

A cone has radius 6 cm and height 8 cm. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its slant height is 10 cm.
  2. Its curved surface is $60\pi \text{ cm}^2$.
  3. Its volume is $96\pi \text{ cm}^3$.
  4. Its total surface is $100\pi \text{ cm}^2$.

Solution.

Step 1$l = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10$, $\pi r l = 60\pi$ and $\frac{1}{3}\pi \times 36 \times 8 = 96\pi$.
Step 2The total surface is $60\pi + 36\pi = 96\pi$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

A cone of the same base and height is hollowed out of a cylinder of radius 3 cm and height 7 cm. Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The cylinder's volume is $198 \text{ cm}^3$.
  2. The cone's volume is $66 \text{ cm}^3$.
  3. The volume left is $132 \text{ cm}^3$.
  4. The cone is half the cylinder.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{22}{7} \times 9 \times 7 = 198$; the cone is a third of it, 66, and $198 - 66 = 132$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
