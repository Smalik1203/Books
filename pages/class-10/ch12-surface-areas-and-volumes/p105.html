Example 7

An ice-cream cone of radius 3.5 cm and height 6 cm is filled with ice cream, and a hemisphere of ice cream of the same radius sits on top. Find the volume of the ice cream, to two decimal places. (Take ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 cone ${= \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 3.5 \times 3.5 \times 6 = 77}$ cm3
Step 2 hemisphere ${= \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 3.5 \times 3.5 \times 3.5 \approx 89.83}$ cm3
Step 3 ${77 + 89.83 = 166.83}$ cm3 volumes add
Answer about 166.83 cm3
Example 8

The container on a water tanker is a cylinder 3 m long and 1.4 m in diameter, with a hemisphere of the same diameter at each end. How many litres of water does it hold, to the nearest litre? (Take ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$, and use 1 m3 $=$ 1000 litres.)

Solution.

Step 1 radius ${= 1.4 \div 2 = 0.7}$ m
Step 2 cylinder ${= \frac{22}{7} \times 0.7 \times 0.7 \times 3 = 4.62}$ m3
Step 3 two hemispheres ${= \frac{4}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 0.7 \times 0.7 \times 0.7 \approx 1.4373}$ m3 together, one sphere
Step 4 ${4.62 + 1.4373 = 6.0573}$ m3
Step 5 ${6.0573 \times 1000 = 6057.3}$ litres 1 m3 is 1000 litres
Answer about 6057 litres

Example 9

A wooden block is a cuboid 10 cm long, 8 cm wide and 5 cm high. A hemispherical hollow of radius 2.1 cm is cut into its top face. Find the volume of wood left. (Take ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 cuboid ${= 10 \times 8 \times 5 = 400}$ cm3
Step 2 hollow ${= \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 2.1 \times 2.1 \times 2.1 = 19.404}$ cm3
Step 3 ${400 - 19.404 = 380.596}$ cm3 the hollow is subtracted
Answer 380.596 cm3
