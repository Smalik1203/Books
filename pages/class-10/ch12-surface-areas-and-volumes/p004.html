Example 1

Rasheed got a playing top (a lattu) as a birthday present. It had no colour on it, and he wanted to colour it with his crayons. The top is shaped like a cone with a hemisphere on top of it (Fig. 12.6). The whole top is 5 cm high, and its diameter is 3.5 cm. Find the area he has to colour. (Take ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 TSA of the top $=$ CSA of the hemisphere $+$ CSA of the cone like the toy in Fig. 12.5
Step 2 CSA of the hemisphere $= \dfrac{1}{2}(4\pi r^2) = 2\pi r^2 = \left(2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times \dfrac{3.5}{2} \times \dfrac{3.5}{2}\right)$ cm2 radius is half the diameter
Step 3 height of the cone $= \left(5 - \dfrac{3.5}{2}\right)$ cm $= 3.25$ cm whole height less the radius
Step 4 slant height of the cone, $l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2} = \sqrt{\left(\dfrac{3.5}{2}\right)^2 + (3.25)^2}$ cm $= 3.7$ cm   (approx.)
Step 5 CSA of the cone $= \pi rl = \left(\dfrac{22}{7} \times \dfrac{3.5}{2} \times 3.7\right)$ cm2
Step 6 TSA of the top $= \left(2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times \dfrac{3.5}{2} \times \dfrac{3.5}{2}\right)$ cm2 $+ \left(\dfrac{22}{7} \times \dfrac{3.5}{2} \times 3.7\right)$ cm2 Steps 2 and 5 added
Step 7 $= \dfrac{22}{7} \times \dfrac{3.5}{2} \times (3.5 + 3.7)$ cm2 $= \dfrac{11}{2} \times 7.2$ cm2 $= 39.6$ cm2   (approx.)
Answer about 39.6 cm2

Notice that the total surface area of the top is not the sum of the total surface areas of the cone and the hemisphere.
