1. A wooden pen stand is a cuboid with four conical hollows to hold pens. The cuboid is 15 cm by 10 cm by 3.5 cm. Each hollow has radius 0.5 cm and depth 1.4 cm. Find the volume of wood in the whole stand (Fig. 12.16).
  1. A vessel is an inverted cone, 8 cm high, with an open top of radius 5 cm. It is filled with water up to the brim. Lead shots, each a sphere of radius 0.5 cm, are dropped into the vessel, and one fourth of the water flows out. Find the number of lead shots dropped into the vessel.
  1. A solid iron pole is a cylinder of height 220 cm and base diameter 24 cm, with another cylinder of height 60 cm and radius 8 cm on top of it. Find the mass of the pole, given that 1 cm3 of iron has a mass of about 8 g. (Use ${\pi = 3.14}$.)
  1. A solid is a right circular cone of height 120 cm and radius 60 cm, standing on a hemisphere of radius 60 cm. It is placed upright in a right circular cylinder full of water, so that it touches the bottom. The cylinder has radius 60 cm and height 180 cm. Find the volume of water left in the cylinder.
  1. A spherical glass vessel has a cylindrical neck 8 cm long and 2 cm in diameter, and the diameter of the spherical part is 8.5 cm. By measuring the amount of water it holds, a child finds its volume to be 345 cm3. Taking these as the inside measurements and ${\pi = 3.14}$, check whether she is correct.
What this chapter showed
  1. The surface area of a solid made by joining basic solids (cuboid, cone, cylinder, sphere, hemisphere) is the sum of the areas of the parts of their surfaces that can still be seen.
  2. The volume of a solid made by joining basic solids is the sum of their volumes; when one solid is cut out of another, its volume is subtracted.
