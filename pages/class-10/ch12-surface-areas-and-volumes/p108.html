Type 6 · Cost from a surface area

Example 14

A water tank is a cylinder of radius 0.7 m and height 2 m, with a hemispherical top. Its outside, but not its base, is painted at ₹$40$ per m2. Find the cost of the painting. (Take ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 CSA of the cylinder ${= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 0.7 \times 2 = 8.8}$ m2
Step 2 CSA of the hemisphere ${= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 0.7 \times 0.7 = 3.08}$ m2 the top of the cylinder is hidden
Step 3 area painted ${= 8.8 + 3.08 = 11.88}$ m2
Step 4 ${11.88 \times 40 = 475.2}$ area times the rate
Answer ₹$475.20$
