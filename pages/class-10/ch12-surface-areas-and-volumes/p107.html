Example 14 · Matching

A sphere has radius 21 cm; take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$. Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Volume of the sphere(1) 5544
(Q) Volume of the hemisphere(2) 4158
(R) Surface area of the sphere(3) 38808
(S) Total surface area of the hemisphere(4) 19404

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = 38\,808$ and half of it is 19 404.
Step 2$4\pi r^2 = 5544$, and a hemisphere's curved half plus its flat circle is $3\pi r^2 = 4158$.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
Example 15 · Matching

A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm; take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$. Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Curved surface area(1) 154
(Q) Total surface area(2) 1540
(R) Volume(3) 440
(S) Area of one end(4) 748

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1

Solution.

Step 1$2\pi rh = 440$ and $\pi r^2 = 154$, so the total is $440 + 2 \times 154 = 748$.
Step 2$\pi r^2 h = 1540$.
Answer(d) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
