Three things from this section are worth keeping. A surface is counted only where it can be seen, so joining solids hides area and cutting them can add it. Volume is never hidden: when solids are joined or cut, their volumes only add and subtract. And a question in metres and litres, or in grams and kilograms, is worked in one unit and changed at the end.
