1. A hemisphere is placed on top of a cubical block of side 7 cm. What is the greatest diameter the hemisphere can have? Find the surface area of the solid.
  1. A hemispherical hollow is cut out of one face of a cubical wooden block, so that the diameter $l$ of the hemisphere is equal to the edge of the cube. Find the surface area of the remaining solid.
  1. A medicine capsule is a cylinder with a hemisphere stuck on each end (Fig. 12.10). The whole capsule is 14 mm long and 5 mm in diameter. Find its surface area.
  1. A tent is a cylinder with a conical top. The cylindrical part is 2.1 m high and 4 m in diameter, and the slant height of the top is 2.8 m. Find the area of the canvas used to make the tent. Also find the cost of the canvas at ₹500 per m2. (The base of the tent is not covered with canvas.)
  1. From a solid cylinder of height 2.4 cm and diameter 1.4 cm, a conical hollow of the same height and the same diameter is cut out. Find the total surface area of the remaining solid, to the nearest cm2.
  1. A wooden article is made by scooping out a hemisphere from each end of a solid cylinder (Fig. 12.11). The cylinder is 10 cm high, and its base has radius 3.5 cm. Find the total surface area of the article.
