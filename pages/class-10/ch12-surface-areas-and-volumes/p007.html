Example 4

Mayank made a bird-bath for his garden, shaped like a cylinder with a hemispherical hollow at one end (Fig. 12.9). The cylinder is 1.45 m high, and its radius is 30 cm. Find the total surface area of the bird-bath. (Take ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 let $h$ be the height of the cylinder, 145 cm, and $r$ the common radius of the cylinder and the hemisphere, 30 cm 1.45 m in centimetres
Step 2 total surface area of the bird-bath $=$ CSA of the cylinder $+$ CSA of the hemisphere
Step 3 $= 2\pi rh + 2\pi r^2 = 2\pi r(h + r) = 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 30 \times (145 + 30)$ cm2
Step 4 $= 33000$ cm2 $= 3.3$ m2 10000 cm2 make 1 m2
Answer 3.3 m2
