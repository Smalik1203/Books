Example 6 · Single correct

A sphere of radius 3 cm is melted and recast into a cylinder of radius 2 cm. How tall is the cylinder?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 3 cm
  2. 9 cm
  3. 6 cm
  4. 12 cm

Solution.

Step 1The volume is unchanged: $\frac{4}{3}\pi \times 27 = \pi \times 4 \times h$.
Step 2$36\pi = 4\pi h$, so $h = 9$ cm.
Answer(b) 9 cm
Example 7 · Multiple correct

A solid is a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm with a hemisphere of radius 7 cm on top. Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The cylinder's curved surface is $440 \text{ cm}^2$.
  2. The hemisphere's curved surface is $308 \text{ cm}^2$.
  3. The whole outer surface, base included, is $902 \text{ cm}^2$.
  4. The volume is $1540 \text{ cm}^3$.

Solution.

Step 1$2\pi rh = 440$, $2\pi r^2 = 308$ and the base is $\pi r^2 = 154$: $440 + 308 + 154 = 902$.
Step 2The volume is the cylinder's 1540 plus the hemisphere's $718\tfrac{2}{3}$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
