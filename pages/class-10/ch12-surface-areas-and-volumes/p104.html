Example 5

A cube of edge 10 cm has a cylindrical hole of radius 1.4 cm drilled straight through it, from the middle of one face to the middle of the opposite face. The total surface area of the solid left is (Take ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

  1. 688 cm2
  2. 587.68 cm2
  3. 675.68 cm2
  4. 663.36 cm2

Solution.

Step 1 TSA of the cube ${= 6 \times 10 \times 10 = 600}$ cm2
Step 2 two circles removed ${= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 1.4 \times 1.4 = 12.32}$ cm2 one on each face
Step 3 inside of the hole ${= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 1.4 \times 10 = 88}$ cm2 a cylinder 10 cm long
Step 4 ${600 - 12.32 + 88 = 675.68}$ cm2
Answer (c)

Option (a) forgets to take away the two circles, (b) takes them away but forgets the inside of the hole, and (d) takes the circles away twice.

Example 6

A capsule is 8 cm long: a cylinder of radius 1 cm with a hemisphere on each end. Its volume is

  1. $8\pi$ cm3
  2. $6\pi$ cm3
  3. $\dfrac{22\pi}{3}$ cm3
  4. $\dfrac{26\pi}{3}$ cm3

Solution.

Step 1 the cylinder is ${8 - 2 = 6}$ cm long each end takes 1 cm
Step 2 the two hemispheres make a sphere of volume $\frac{4}{3}\pi$ cm3
Step 3 volume ${= \pi \times 1 \times 6 + \frac{4}{3}\pi = \frac{22\pi}{3}}$ cm3
Answer (c)

Option (a) treats all 8 cm as cylinder, (b) leaves out the ends, and (d) counts two whole spheres.
