Problem 5

A hemispherical bowl of inner radius 9 cm is full of oil, which is poured into cylindrical bottles of radius 1.5 cm and height 4 cm. The number of bottles needed is

  1. 108
  2. 27
  3. 81
  4. 54
Solution

bowl ${= \frac{2}{3}\pi \times 729 = 486\pi}$;   one bottle ${= \pi \times 2.25 \times 4 = 9\pi}$;   ${486\pi \div 9\pi = 54}$

The answer is (d). Option (a) uses a whole sphere, (c) forgets the $\frac{2}{3}$, and (b) takes bottles 8 cm high.
