The hemisphere hides part of the top face but adds its own curved surface. So

surface area of the block $=$ TSA of the cube $-$ base area of the hemisphere $+$ CSA of the hemisphere

$= 150 - \pi r^2 + 2\pi r^2 = (150 + \pi r^2)$ cm2

$= 150$ cm2 $+ \left(\dfrac{22}{7} \times \dfrac{4.2}{2} \times \dfrac{4.2}{2}\right)$ cm2 $= (150 + 13.86)$ cm2 $= 163.86$ cm2

Example 3

A wooden toy rocket is shaped like a cone mounted on a cylinder (Fig. 12.8). The whole rocket is 26 cm high, and the conical part is 6 cm high. The base of the conical part has a diameter of 5 cm, and the base of the cylindrical part has a diameter of 3 cm. The conical part is to be painted orange and the cylindrical part yellow. Find the area painted in each colour. (Take ${\pi = 3.14}$.)

Let the radius, height and slant height of the cone be $r$, $h$ and $l$, and let the radius and height of the cylinder be $r'$ and $h'$. Then

${r = 2.5}$ cm,   ${h = 6}$ cm,   ${r' = 1.5}$ cm,   ${h' = 26 - 6 = 20}$ cm

${l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2} = \sqrt{2.5^2 + 6^2} = 6.5}$ cm

The base of the cone rests on the top of the cylinder, but it is larger than the base of the cylinder. So a ring of the base of the cone is left uncovered, and it has to be painted orange too.

area painted orange $=$ CSA of the cone $+$ base area of the cone $-$ base area of the cylinder

$= \pi rl + \pi r^2 - \pi (r')^2 = \pi[(2.5 \times 6.5) + 2.5^2 - 1.5^2]$ cm2

$= 3.14 \times 20.25$ cm2 $= 63.585$ cm2
