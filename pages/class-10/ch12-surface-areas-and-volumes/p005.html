Example 2

The decorative block in Fig. 12.7 is made of two solids, a cube and a hemisphere. The base of the block is a cube with edge 5 cm, and the hemisphere fixed on top has a diameter of 4.2 cm. Find the total surface area of the block. (Take ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 total surface area of the cube $= 6 \times (\text{edge})^2 = 6 \times 5 \times 5$ cm2 $= 150$ cm2
Step 2 surface area of the block $=$ TSA of the cube $-$ base area of the hemisphere $+$ CSA of the hemisphere part of the top is hidden
Step 3 $= 150 - \pi r^2 + 2\pi r^2 = (150 + \pi r^2)$ cm2
Step 4 $= 150$ cm2 $+ \left(\dfrac{22}{7} \times \dfrac{4.2}{2} \times \dfrac{4.2}{2}\right)$ cm2 $= (150 + 13.86)$ cm2 $= 163.86$ cm2 radius 2.1 cm
Answer 163.86 cm2

The hemisphere hides part of the top face but adds its own curved surface.
