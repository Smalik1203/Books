We now have a round-bottomed toy. How much paint would we need to colour its surface? We would need the surface area of the toy, which is made up of the CSA of the hemisphere and the CSA of the cone. So

total surface area of the toy $=$ CSA of the hemisphere $+$ CSA of the cone

Surface area of a combination

Add the areas of only those parts of the surfaces that can still be seen. A face that is covered where two solids are joined is not part of the surface of the new solid.
