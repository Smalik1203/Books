Every length of a capsule is doubled, so that it keeps its shape. What happens to its surface area and to its volume?

Every area in the working, such as $2\pi rh$ or $4\pi r^2$, is a product of two lengths, so each is multiplied by ${2 \times 2 = 4}$. Every volume, such as $\pi r^2 h$ or $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$, is a product of three lengths, so each is multiplied by ${2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8}$. So the surface area becomes 4 times as large and the volume 8 times as large. This is true for a solid of any shape.

A hemisphere is scooped out of one face of a cube. Does the surface area go up or down?

The volume goes down, but the surface area goes up. The flat circle of area $\pi r^2$ is removed from the face, and the curved inside of the hollow, of area $2\pi r^2$, is added. So the surface area increases by $\pi r^2$. Cutting a solid away can add surface.

Example 1 · Single correct

What is the volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm? Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Choose one correct option.

  1. $1540 \text{ cm}^3$
  2. $440 \text{ cm}^3$
  3. $3080 \text{ cm}^3$
  4. $770 \text{ cm}^3$

Solution.

Step 1$V = \pi r^2 h = \frac{22}{7} \times 49 \times 10 = 1540 \text{ cm}^3$.
Answer(a) $1540 \text{ cm}^3$
Example 2 · Single correct

What is the volume of a cone of radius 3 cm and height 7 cm? Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Choose one correct option.

  1. $198 \text{ cm}^3$
  2. $66 \text{ cm}^3$
  3. $33 \text{ cm}^3$
  4. $132 \text{ cm}^3$

Solution.

Step 1$V = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 9 \times 7 = 66 \text{ cm}^3$, a third of the cylinder on the same base.
Answer(b) $66 \text{ cm}^3$
