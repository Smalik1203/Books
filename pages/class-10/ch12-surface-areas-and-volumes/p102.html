When a solid is melted and remade, its volume does not change, although its surface area usually does. So ${\pi \times 2^2 \times h = \frac{4}{3}\pi \times 3^3}$, that is, ${4\pi h = 36\pi}$, and ${h = 9}$ cm. The $\pi$ on both sides cancelled, so no value of $\pi$ was needed.

Every length of a capsule is doubled, so that it keeps its shape. What happens to its surface area and to its volume?

Every area in the working, such as $2\pi rh$ or $4\pi r^2$, is a product of two lengths, so each is multiplied by ${2 \times 2 = 4}$. Every volume, such as $\pi r^2 h$ or $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$, is a product of three lengths, so each is multiplied by ${2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8}$. So the surface area becomes 4 times as large and the volume 8 times as large. This is true for a solid of any shape.

A hemisphere is scooped out of one face of a cube. Does the surface area go up or down?

The volume goes down, but the surface area goes up. The flat circle of area $\pi r^2$ is removed from the face, and the curved inside of the hollow, of area $2\pi r^2$, is added. So the surface area increases by $\pi r^2$, as in Question 5 of Exercise Set 12.1. Cutting a solid away can add surface.

Problem 1

Two cubes of edge 5 cm are joined end to end. The surface area of the cuboid they form is

  1. 300 cm2
  2. 250 cm2
  3. 150 cm2
  4. 275 cm2
Solution

the cuboid is 10 cm by 5 cm by 5 cm: ${2(10 \times 5 + 5 \times 5 + 10 \times 5) = 250}$ cm2

The answer is (b). Option (a) forgets the two hidden faces, (d) takes away only one, and (c) is one cube.
