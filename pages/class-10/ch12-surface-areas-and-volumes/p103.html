Example 3

A solid is a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 20 cm, with a cone of radius 7 cm and height 24 cm standing on its top. Find the total surface area of the solid. (Take ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 ${l = \sqrt{7^2 + 24^2} = \sqrt{625} = 25}$ cm slant height of the cone
Step 2 CSA of the cone ${= \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 25 = 550}$ cm2
Step 3 CSA of the cylinder ${= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 20 = 880}$ cm2
Step 4 base of the cylinder ${= \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 7 = 154}$ cm2 the top base is hidden
Step 5 ${550 + 880 + 154 = 1584}$ cm2
Answer 1584 cm2

Type 2 · Surface area when a part is cut away

Example 4

A solid cylinder has radius 3 cm and height 8 cm. A conical hollow of radius 3 cm and depth 4 cm is cut out of its top face. Find the total surface area of the solid that is left. (Take ${\pi = 3.14}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 ${l = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5}$ cm slant height of the hollow
Step 2 CSA of the cylinder ${= 2\pi \times 3 \times 8 = 48\pi}$ cm2
Step 3 bottom face ${= \pi \times 3^2 = 9\pi}$ cm2 the top face is gone
Step 4 inside of the hollow ${= \pi \times 3 \times 5 = 15\pi}$ cm2 the CSA of the cone
Step 5 ${48\pi + 9\pi + 15\pi = 72\pi = 72 \times 3.14 = 226.08}$ cm2
Answer 226.08 cm2
