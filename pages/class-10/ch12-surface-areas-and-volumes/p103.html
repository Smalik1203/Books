Problem 2

A toy is a cone of radius 3 cm and height 4 cm on a hemisphere of radius 3 cm. Its total surface area is

  1. $33\pi$ cm2
  2. $51\pi$ cm2
  3. $24\pi$ cm2
  4. $42\pi$ cm2
Solution

${l = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5}$;   cone ${= \pi \times 3 \times 5 = 15\pi}$,   hemisphere ${= 2\pi \times 9 = 18\pi}$,   total $33\pi$

The answer is (a). Option (b) adds both total surface areas, (d) counts the flat face of the hemisphere, and (c) is the cone alone.

Problem 3

A capsule is 8 cm long: a cylinder of radius 1 cm with a hemisphere on each end. Its volume is

  1. $8\pi$ cm3
  2. $6\pi$ cm3
  3. $\dfrac{22\pi}{3}$ cm3
  4. $\dfrac{26\pi}{3}$ cm3
Solution

the cylinder is ${8 - 2 = 6}$ cm long;   volume ${= \pi \times 1 \times 6 + \frac{4}{3}\pi = \frac{22\pi}{3}}$ cm3

The answer is (c). Option (a) treats all 8 cm as cylinder, (b) leaves out the ends, and (d) counts two whole spheres.

Problem 4

A solid sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and made into cones of radius 3 cm and height 4 cm. The number of cones is

  1. 12
  2. 24
  3. 8
  4. 32
Solution

sphere ${= \frac{4}{3}\pi \times 216 = 288\pi}$;   one cone ${= \frac{1}{3}\pi \times 9 \times 4 = 12\pi}$;   ${288\pi \div 12\pi = 24}$

The answer is (b). Option (a) uses a hemisphere, (c) forgets the $\frac{1}{3}$, and (d) forgets the height.
