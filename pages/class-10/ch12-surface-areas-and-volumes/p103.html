Example 3 · Single correct

What is the volume of a hemisphere of radius 21 cm? Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Choose one correct option.

  1. $38808 \text{ cm}^3$
  2. $9702 \text{ cm}^3$
  3. $19404 \text{ cm}^3$
  4. $1386 \text{ cm}^3$

Solution.

Step 1$V = \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 9261$.
Step 2$= 19\,404 \text{ cm}^3$.
Answer(c) $19404 \text{ cm}^3$
Example 4 · Single correct

Two cubes of edge 4 cm are joined face to face. What is the surface area of the cuboid formed?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $192 \text{ cm}^2$
  2. $128 \text{ cm}^2$
  3. $96 \text{ cm}^2$
  4. $160 \text{ cm}^2$

Solution.

Step 1The cuboid is 8 cm by 4 cm by 4 cm.
Step 2$2(8 \times 4 + 4 \times 4 + 8 \times 4) = 2 \times 80 = 160 \text{ cm}^2$; the two joined faces are hidden.
Answer(d) $160 \text{ cm}^2$
Example 5 · Single correct

What is the curved surface area of a cone of radius 7 cm and slant height 10 cm? Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Choose one correct option.

  1. $220 \text{ cm}^2$
  2. $440 \text{ cm}^2$
  3. $154 \text{ cm}^2$
  4. $374 \text{ cm}^2$

Solution.

Step 1Curved surface $= \pi r l$.
Step 2$= \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 10 = 220 \text{ cm}^2$.
Answer(a) $220 \text{ cm}^2$
