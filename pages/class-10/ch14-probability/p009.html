Example 13

Two dice, one blue and one grey, are thrown at the same time. Write down all the possible outcomes. What is the probability that the sum of the two numbers on top is (i) 8? (ii) 13? (iii) less than or equal to 12?

Solution.

Step 1 when the blue die shows 1, the grey die can show any of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6; the same is true when the blue die shows 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6
Step 2 Table 14.1 lists them; in each pair the first number is on the blue die, the second on the grey
Step 3 (1, 4) differs from (4, 1), so there are ${6 \times 6 = 36}$ possible outcomes the dice can be told apart
Step 4 (i) (2, 6), (3, 5), (4, 4), (5, 3), (6, 2) are favourable to E, the sum is 8: $P(E) = \dfrac{5}{36}$
Step 5 (ii) no outcome in Table 14.1 is favourable to F, the sum is 13: $P(F) = \dfrac{0}{36} = 0$
Step 6 (iii) all are favourable to G, the sum is at most 12: $P(G) = \dfrac{36}{36} = 1$ no sum is above 12
Answer the 36 outcomes are in Table 14.1; (i) $\dfrac{5}{36}$   (ii) 0   (iii) 1
