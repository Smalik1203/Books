  1. One card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck of 52 cards. Find the probability of getting
    1. a red king

    3. a red face card
    4. the jack of hearts
    5. a spade
    6. the queen of diamonds
  1. The ten, jack, queen, king and ace of diamonds are shuffled well and laid face down. One card is picked up at random.
    1. What is the probability that the card is the queen?
    2. If the queen is drawn and put aside, what is the probability that the second card picked up is (a) an ace? (b) a queen?
  1. 12 defective pens have been mixed by mistake with 132 good ones. You cannot tell by looking at a pen whether it is defective. One pen is taken out of this lot at random. Find the probability that the pen is a good one.
