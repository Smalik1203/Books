Example 10 · Multiple correct

Two dice are thrown. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. There are 36 equally likely outcomes.
  2. $P(\text{a doublet}) = \frac{1}{6}$
  3. $P(\text{sum } 13) = 0$
  4. $P(\text{sum } 7) = \frac{1}{6}$

Solution.

Step 1$6 \times 6 = 36$, and 6 of them are doublets.
Step 2The largest sum is 12, and the sum 7 comes 6 ways.
Answer(a), (b), (c), (d)

Example 12 · Numerical answer

A bag has only red and blue balls. The probability of drawing a red one is $\frac{1}{4}$, and there are 15 blue balls. How many red balls are there?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Blue balls are $\frac{3}{4}$ of all, so there are $15 \div \frac{3}{4} = 20$ balls, and $20 - 15 = 5$ are red.
Answer5
