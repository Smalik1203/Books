Example 10

In a game of musical chairs, the person playing the music is told to stop it at any moment within 2 minutes of starting. What is the probability that the music stops within the first half-minute?

This example and the next are not needed for the examination.

Solution.

Step 1 the outcomes are the numbers between 0 and 2, the line from 0 to 2 (Fig. 14.1)
Step 2 let E be the event the music stops within the first half-minute; the outcomes favourable to E are the points of the number line from 0 to $\frac{1}{2}$
Step 3 the length from 0 to 2 is 2, and the length from 0 to $\frac{1}{2}$ is $\frac{1}{2}$; so of the total length 2, a length of $\frac{1}{2}$ is favourable to E all outcomes equally likely
Answer $P(E) = \dfrac{\text{length favourable to } E}{\text{total length in which the outcomes can lie}} = \dfrac{\frac{1}{2}}{2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$
