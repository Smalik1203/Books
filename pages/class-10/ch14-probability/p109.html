Assertion and reason

In Questions 12 to 15, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): When two dice are thrown, the probability that the product of the two numbers is 13 is 0.

    Reason (R): For every event E, $0 \leq P(E) \leq 1$.

  1. Assertion (A): If a day of the week is picked at random, the probability that it is a Sunday or a Monday is $\frac{1}{7}$.

    Reason (R): The seven days of the week are equally likely to be picked.

  1. Assertion (A): The probability that a card drawn from a well-shuffled deck of 52 cards is black is $\frac{1}{2}$.

    Reason (R): 26 of the 52 equally likely cards are black.
