1. Assertion (A): A ball is drawn at random from a bag of 4 red and 5 green balls. The probability that it is red is $\frac{4}{9}$.

    Reason (R): The outcomes a red ball and a green ball are equally likely.

Very short answer
  1. A die is thrown once. Find the probability of getting a number that is a factor of 12.
  1. A number is chosen at random from 1 to 100. Find the probability that it is a multiple of 7.
  1. One card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck of 52 cards. Find the probability that it is a black card numbered 2, 3, 4 or 5.
  1. In a lucky draw, 10 of the 250 tickets carry a prize. Rita buys one ticket. Find the probability that she does not win a prize.
  1. Two dice are thrown. Find the probability that both numbers are 5 or more.
Short answer
  1. A box holds 20 cards numbered 1 to 20, and one card is drawn at random. Find the probability that its number is (i) a prime (ii) divisible by 3 (iii) a multiple of both 4 and 5.
  1. A coin is tossed and a die is thrown at the same time. List all the outcomes, and find the probability of getting a head or a number greater than 4.
  1. All the aces and kings are removed from a deck of 52 cards. One card is drawn from the rest. Find the probability that it is (i) a heart (ii) a face card (iii) a black card numbered 2 to 10.
  1. The faces of a die are marked 0, 1, 1, 1, 6 and 6. Two such dice are thrown, and the numbers on top are added. Find the probability that the total is (i) 7 (ii) 2.
  1. Meena plays a game that cannot end in a draw. The probability that she wins is 3 times the probability that she loses. Find the probability that she wins.
Long answer
  1. Two dice are thrown. Find the probability that
    1. the product of the two numbers is a perfect square
    2. the product is more than 20
    3. the larger of the two numbers is 4
    4. one number is double the other.
  1. A box holds cards marked with the odd numbers 3, 5, 7, …, 35, one number on each card. One card is drawn at random. Find the probability that its number is (i) a prime (ii) divisible by both 3 and 5 (iii) a perfect square (iv) not a multiple of 7.
