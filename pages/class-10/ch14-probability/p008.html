Let E be the event the music stops within the first half-minute. The outcomes favourable to E are the points of the number line from 0 to $\frac{1}{2}$. The length from 0 to 2 is 2, and the length from 0 to $\frac{1}{2}$ is $\frac{1}{2}$. All the outcomes are equally likely, so of the total length 2, a length of $\frac{1}{2}$ is favourable to E. So

$$P(E) = \dfrac{\text{length favourable to } E}{\text{total length in which the outcomes can lie}} = \dfrac{\frac{1}{2}}{2} = \dfrac{1}{4}$$

Can the same idea give a probability as the ratio of a favourable area to a total area?

Example 11

A missing helicopter is reported to have crashed somewhere in the rectangular region shown in Fig. 14.2. What is the probability that it crashed inside the lake shown in the figure?

The helicopter is equally likely to have crashed anywhere in the region. From the figure, the lake is ${9 - 6 = 3}$ km long and ${4.5 - 2 = 2.5}$ km wide.

area of the whole region ${= 4.5 \times 9}$ km2 ${= 40.5}$ km2

area of the lake ${= 2.5 \times 3}$ km2 ${= 7.5}$ km2

$P(\text{helicopter crashed in the lake}) = \dfrac{7.5}{40.5} = \dfrac{75}{405} = \dfrac{5}{27}$
