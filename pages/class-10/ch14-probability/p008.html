Can the same idea give a probability as the ratio of a favourable area to a total area?

Example 11

A missing helicopter is reported to have crashed somewhere in the rectangular region shown in Fig. 14.2. What is the probability that it crashed inside the lake shown in the figure?

Solution.

Step 1 the helicopter is equally likely to have crashed anywhere in the region
Step 2 from the figure, the lake is ${9 - 6 = 3}$ km long and ${4.5 - 2 = 2.5}$ km wide
Step 3 area of the whole region ${= 4.5 \times 9}$ km2 ${= 40.5}$ km2
Step 4 area of the lake ${= 2.5 \times 3}$ km2 ${= 7.5}$ km2
Step 5 $P(\text{helicopter crashed in the lake}) = \dfrac{7.5}{40.5} = \dfrac{75}{405} = \dfrac{5}{27}$
Answer the probability is $\dfrac{5}{27}$
Example 12

A carton holds 100 shirts: 88 are good, 8 have minor defects and 4 have major defects. Jimmy, a trader, will accept only the good shirts. Sujatha, another trader, will reject only the shirts with major defects. One shirt is drawn at random from the carton. What is the probability that it is acceptable (i) to Jimmy? (ii) to Sujatha?

Solution.

Step 1 one shirt is drawn at random from 100 shirts, so there are 100 equally likely outcomes
Step 2 (i) 88 outcomes are acceptable to Jimmy, so $P(\text{acceptable to Jimmy}) = \dfrac{88}{100} = 0.88$
Step 3 (ii) ${88 + 8 = 96}$ outcomes are acceptable to Sujatha, so $P(\text{acceptable to Sujatha}) = \dfrac{96}{100} = 0.96$
Answer (i) 0.88   (ii) 0.96
