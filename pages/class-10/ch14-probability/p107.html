Type 8 · Finding an unknown number of objects

Example 13

A box has 12 balls, $x$ of them black. When 6 more black balls are put in, the probability of drawing a black ball doubles. The value of $x$ is

  1. 6
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 2

Solution.

Step 1 before: $P(\text{black}) = \dfrac{x}{12}$; after: $P(\text{black}) = \dfrac{x + 6}{18}$ the total becomes 18
Step 2 ${\frac{x + 6}{18} = 2 \times \frac{x}{12}}$, so ${12x + 72 = 36x}$ multiply by 36
Step 3 ${24x = 72}$, so ${x = 3}$
Answer (b)

Check: $\frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}$ and $\frac{9}{18} = \frac{1}{2}$. Option (a) forgets that the total becomes 18, and (c) and (d) do not satisfy the equation.

Example 14

A piggy bank has 60 one-rupee coins, 40 two-rupee coins and $x$ five-rupee coins. The probability that a coin falling out is a five-rupee coin is $\frac{1}{6}$. The value of $x$ is

  1. 17
  2. 16
  3. 20
  4. 25

Solution.

Step 1 there are ${60 + 40 + x = 100 + x}$ coins
Step 2 ${\frac{x}{100 + x} = \frac{1}{6}}$, so ${6x = 100 + x}$
Step 3 ${5x = 100}$, so ${x = 20}$
Answer (c)

Option (b) is $\frac{100}{6}$ rounded down, which forgets the five-rupee coins in the total, and (d) makes the total 125, where $\frac{25}{125} = \frac{1}{5}$.
