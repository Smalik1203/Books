Example 15 · Matching

A card is drawn from a deck of 52. Match each event with its probability. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) A heart(1) $\frac{1}{13}$
(Q) An ace(2) $\frac{1}{52}$
(R) A black face card(3) $\frac{3}{26}$
(S) The queen of spades(4) $\frac{1}{4}$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–1, R–3, S–4
  2. P–1, Q–4, R–3, S–2
  3. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  4. P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2

Solution.

Step 113 hearts and 4 aces: $\frac{1}{4}$ and $\frac{1}{13}$.
Step 26 black face cards, $\frac{6}{52} = \frac{3}{26}$, and one queen of spades, $\frac{1}{52}$.
Answer(d) P–4, Q–1, R–3, S–2
