Example 3 · Single correct

A card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck of 52. What is the probability that it is a king?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{1}{52}$
  2. $\frac{4}{13}$
  3. $\frac{1}{13}$
  4. $\frac{1}{4}$

Solution.

Step 1There are 4 kings in 52 cards: $\frac{4}{52} = \frac{1}{13}$.
Answer(c) $\frac{1}{13}$
Example 4 · Single correct

$P(E) = 0.35$. What is $P(\text{not } E)$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 0.35
  2. 1.35
  3. $-0.35$
  4. 0.65

Solution.

Step 1$P(E) + P(\text{not } E) = 1$, so $1 - 0.35 = 0.65$.
Answer(d) 0.65
Example 5 · Single correct

Two dice are thrown. What is the probability that the sum is 8?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{5}{36}$
  2. $\frac{1}{6}$
  3. $\frac{1}{9}$
  4. $\frac{7}{36}$

Solution.

Step 1The sum 8 comes from $(2, 6)$, $(3, 5)$, $(4, 4)$, $(5, 3)$ and $(6, 2)$: $\frac{5}{36}$.
Answer(a) $\frac{5}{36}$
Example 6 · Single correct

A bag has 5 red and 3 blue balls. What is the probability that a ball drawn is blue?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\frac{5}{8}$
  2. $\frac{3}{8}$
  3. $\frac{3}{5}$
  4. $\frac{1}{3}$

Solution.

Step 1There are 8 balls, and 3 are blue: $\frac{3}{8}$.
Answer(b) $\frac{3}{8}$
