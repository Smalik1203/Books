Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these can be the probability of an event?

Select all correct options.

  1. 0.7
  2. $15\%$
  3. $-1.5$
  4. $\frac{2}{3}$

Solution.

Step 1A probability lies between 0 and 1, inclusive: 0.7, 0.15 and $\frac{2}{3}$ do.
Step 2$-1.5$ is negative.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

A die is thrown once. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $P(\text{even}) = \frac{1}{2}$
  2. $P(\text{more than } 6) = 0$
  3. $P(\text{at most } 6) = 1$
  4. $P(3) = \frac{1}{3}$

Solution.

Step 1Three of six faces are even; no face is more than 6, and every face is at most 6.
Step 2$P(3) = \frac{1}{6}$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 9 · Multiple correct

A card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck of 52. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $P(\text{red}) = \frac{1}{2}$
  2. $P(\text{face card}) = \frac{3}{13}$
  3. $P(\text{red king}) = \frac{1}{13}$
  4. $P(\text{spade}) = \frac{1}{2}$

Solution.

Step 126 cards are red and 12 are face cards, $\frac{3}{13}$; but 2 are red kings, $\frac{1}{26}$, and 13 spades, $\frac{1}{4}$.
Answer(a), (b)
