Problem 5

A box has 12 balls, $x$ of them black. When 6 more black balls are put in, the probability of drawing a black ball doubles. The value of $x$ is

  1. 6
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 2
Solution

${\frac{x + 6}{18} = 2 \times \frac{x}{12}}$, so ${12x + 72 = 36x}$ and ${x = 3}$

The answer is (b); (a) forgets that the total becomes 18, and (c) and (d) do not satisfy the equation.
