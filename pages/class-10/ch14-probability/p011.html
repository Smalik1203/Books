1. A bag contains only lemon-flavoured sweets. Malini takes out one sweet without looking into the bag. What is the probability that she takes out
    1. an orange-flavoured sweet?
    2. a lemon-flavoured sweet?
  1. In a group of 3 students, the probability that 2 students do not have the same birthday is 0.992. What is the probability that the 2 students have the same birthday? (Ignore leap years, and assume that every day of the year is equally likely to be a birthday.)
  1. A bag contains 3 red balls and 5 black balls. A ball is drawn at random from the bag. What is the probability that the ball is (i) red? (ii) not red? The balls are all of the same size.
  1. A box contains 5 red, 8 white and 4 green marbles. One marble is taken out of the box at random. What is the probability that it is (i) red? (ii) white? (iii) not green?
  1. A piggy bank contains a hundred 50p coins, fifty ₹1 coins, twenty ₹2 coins and ten ₹5 coins. When the bank is turned upside down, each coin is equally likely to fall out. What is the probability that the coin that falls out (i) is a 50p coin? (ii) is not a ₹5 coin?
  1. Gopi buys a fish for his aquarium. The shopkeeper takes out one fish at random from a tank holding 5 male fish and 8 female fish (Fig. 14.4). What is the probability that the fish taken out is a male fish?
  1. In a game of chance, an arrow is spun and comes to rest pointing at one of the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 (Fig. 14.5). These outcomes are equally likely. What is the probability that the arrow points at
    1. 8?
    2. an odd number?
    3. a number greater than 2?
    4. a number less than 9?
