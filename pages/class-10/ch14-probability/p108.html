Type 9 · Data in a table

Example 15

The 60 students of a class are in three houses, as the table shows. One student is chosen at random to hoist the flag. Find the probability that the student is (i) a girl of Red house (ii) not in Blue house (iii) a boy.

HouseBoysGirls
Red812
Blue105
Green1411

Solution.

Step 1 ${8 + 12 + 10 + 5 + 14 + 11 = 60}$ equally likely outcomes the table agrees
Step 2 (i) $P(\text{Red girl}) = \dfrac{12}{60} = \dfrac{1}{5}$
Step 3 (ii) Blue has ${10 + 5 = 15}$, so $P(\text{not Blue}) = 1 - \dfrac{15}{60} = \dfrac{3}{4}$
Step 4 (iii) boys: ${8 + 10 + 14 = 32}$, so $P(\text{boy}) = \dfrac{32}{60} = \dfrac{8}{15}$
Answer (i) $\dfrac{1}{5}$   (ii) $\dfrac{3}{4}$   (iii) $\dfrac{8}{15}$
