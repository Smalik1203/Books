Example 14 · Matching

A die is thrown once. Match each event with its probability. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $P(6)$(1) 1
(Q) $P(\text{odd})$(2) $\frac{1}{3}$
(R) $P(\text{more than } 4)$(3) $\frac{1}{6}$
(S) $P(\text{less than } 7)$(4) $\frac{1}{2}$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1One face shows 6, and three are odd.
Step 2Two faces (5 and 6) are more than 4, and every face is less than 7.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
