Assertion and reason

In Questions 15 to 18, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): The length of the tangent from a point 41 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 9 cm is 40 cm.

    Reason (R): The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.

  1. Assertion (A): If PA, 7 cm long, is one of the two tangents from a point P to a circle, then the other tangent from P is also 7 cm long.

    Reason (R): Any two tangents to a circle are equal in length.

  1. Assertion (A): Two tangents to a circle of radius 6 cm can be drawn from a point 6 cm from its centre.

    Reason (R): Exactly one tangent to a circle passes through each point of the circle.

  1. Assertion (A): No tangent to a circle passes through a point inside the circle.

    Reason (R): The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.

  1. A tangent from a point P to a circle of radius 12 cm is 35 cm long. How far is P from the centre?
  1. PA and PB are the tangents from P to a circle with centre O, and ${\angle APO = 25^\circ}$. Find $\angle APB$ and $\angle AOP$.
  1. Find the length of the tangent from a point 10 cm from the centre of a circle of diameter 12 cm.

  1. Two concentric circles have radii 53 cm and 28 cm. Find the length of the chord of the larger circle that touches the smaller circle.
