Type 6 · A circle inside a triangle

Example 14

A circle with centre O touches all three sides of a triangle ABC, in which ${AB = AC = 10}$ cm and ${BC = 12}$ cm. Find the radius of the circle.

Solution. Let the radius be $r$ cm.

Step 1 the height from A to BC is ${\sqrt{10^2 - 6^2} = 8}$ cm it bisects BC
Step 2 area of ABC ${= \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 8 = 48}$ cm2
Step 3 OA, OB and OC cut ABC into three triangles, each of height $r$ Theorem 10.1
Step 4 area of ABC ${= \frac{1}{2}r(10 + 10 + 12) = 16r}$ adding the three areas
Step 5 ${16r = 48}$, so ${r = 3}$
Answer 3 cm

Step 4 works for any triangle: its area is half the radius of this circle times its perimeter.
