Type 3 · Equal tangents from a point

Example 8

A quadrilateral ABCD circumscribes a circle, with ${AB = 6}$ cm, ${BC = 7}$ cm and ${CD = 4}$ cm. Then AD is

  1. 9 cm
  2. 3 cm
  3. 17 cm
  4. 5 cm

Solution.

Step 1 ${AB + CD = AD + BC}$ Exercise Set 10.2, Question 8
Step 2 ${6 + 4 = AD + 7}$, so ${AD = 3}$ cm
Answer (b)

Option (a) is ${AB + BC - CD}$, (c) adds all three sides, and (d) averages AB and CD.

Example 9

A circle touches the sides BC, CA and AB of a triangle ABC at D, E and F. If ${AB = 10}$ cm, ${AC = 12}$ cm and ${BD = 4}$ cm, then BC is

  1. 10 cm
  2. 12 cm
  3. 8 cm
  4. 14 cm

Solution.

Step 1 ${BF = BD = 4}$, so ${AF = 10 - 4 = 6}$ tangents from B
Step 2 ${AE = AF = 6}$, so ${CE = 12 - 6 = 6}$ tangents from A
Step 3 ${CD = CE = 6}$ tangents from C
Step 4 ${BC = BD + CD = 4 + 6 = 10}$ cm
Answer (a)

Option (b) copies AC, (c) is ${AC - BD}$, and (d) is ${AB + BD}$.
