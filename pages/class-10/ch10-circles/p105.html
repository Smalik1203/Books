Example 9 · Multiple correct

A quadrilateral $ABCD$ is drawn around a circle, with $AB = 6$ cm, $BC = 7$ cm and $CD = 4$ cm. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $AB + CD = BC + AD$
  2. $AD = 3$ cm
  3. Its perimeter is 18 cm.
  4. $AD = 5$ cm

Solution.

Step 1The tangents from each vertex are equal, so opposite sides have equal sums: $6 + 4 = 7 + AD$.
Step 2$AD = 3$ cm, and the perimeter is $6 + 7 + 4 + 3 = 20$ cm.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

A circle is drawn inside $\triangle ABC$, touching all three sides. The tangents from $A$, $B$ and $C$ are 4 cm, 6 cm and 8 cm long. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $AB = 10$ cm
  2. $BC = 14$ cm
  3. The perimeter is 36 cm.
  4. $CA = 14$ cm

Solution.

Step 1Each side is the sum of the tangents from its ends: 10, 14 and 12 cm, so the perimeter is 36 cm.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

Find the length of the tangent from a point 17 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 8 cm, in cm.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Tangent$^2 = 17^2 - 8^2 = 289 - 64 = 225$.
Step 2The tangent is 15 cm.
