Example 3 · Single correct

Tangents $PA$ and $PB$ to a circle with centre $O$ make an angle of $80^\circ$ at $P$. What is $\angle AOB$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $80^\circ$
  2. $50^\circ$
  3. $100^\circ$
  4. $90^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1In quadrilateral $OAPB$, the angles at $A$ and $B$ are $90^\circ$ each.
Step 2$\angle AOB = 360^\circ - 90^\circ - 90^\circ - 80^\circ = 100^\circ$.
Answer(c) $100^\circ$
Example 4 · Single correct

How many tangents can be drawn to a circle from a point inside it?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. infinitely many
  4. 0

Solution.

Step 1Every line through a point inside a circle meets the circle twice.
Step 2So no line through it is a tangent.
Answer(d) 0
Example 5 · Single correct

The tangent from a point 25 cm from the centre of a circle is 24 cm long. What is the radius?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 7 cm
  2. 1 cm
  3. 49 cm
  4. 24.5 cm

Solution.

Step 1Radius, tangent and the line to the centre make a right triangle with hypotenuse 25.
Step 2$r = \sqrt{25^2 - 24^2} = \sqrt{49} = 7$ cm.
Answer(a) 7 cm
