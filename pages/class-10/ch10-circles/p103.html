Example 2

The tangent at a point T of a circle with centre O and radius 9 cm passes through a point P, with ${OP = 15}$ cm. Find the length PT and the area of the triangle OTP.

Solution.

Step 1 ${\angle OTP = 90^\circ}$ Theorem 10.1
Step 2 ${PT = \sqrt{15^2 - 9^2} = \sqrt{144} = 12}$ cm Pythagoras theorem
Step 3 area ${= \frac{1}{2} \times 9 \times 12 = 54}$ cm2 OT and PT are the legs
Answer PT is 12 cm, and the area is 54 cm2
Example 3

A straight road touches a circular park of radius 20 m at the point T. A lamp post L stands on the road, 29 m from the centre O of the park. How far along the road is the lamp post from T?

Solution.

Step 1 ${OT \perp TL}$ the road is a tangent
Step 2 ${TL^2 = OL^2 - OT^2 = 29^2 - 20^2 = 441}$ Pythagoras theorem
Step 3 ${TL = \sqrt{441} = 21}$ m
Answer 21 m
Example 4

A circle has centre O and radius 5 cm. The points P, Q and R are 3 cm, 5 cm and 13 cm from O. The numbers of tangents to the circle that pass through P, Q and R are

  1. 2, 1, 0
  2. 0, 1, 2
  3. 0, 2, 2
  4. 1, 1, 2

Solution.

Step 1 ${OP = 3}$ cm, less than 5 cm, so P is inside: no tangent Case 1
Step 2 ${OQ = 5}$ cm, the radius, so Q is on the circle: one tangent Case 2
Step 3 ${OR = 13}$ cm, more than 5 cm, so R is outside: two tangents Case 3
Answer (b)

Option (a) reverses the order, (c) gives Q two tangents, and (d) gives P one. Each tangent from R is ${\sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = 12}$ cm long.
