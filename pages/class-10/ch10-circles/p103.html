Problem 2

TP and TQ are tangents from T to a circle with centre O, and ${\angle PTQ = 50^\circ}$. Then $\angle OPQ$ is

  1. $50^\circ$
  2. $65^\circ$
  3. $25^\circ$
  4. $130^\circ$
Solution

${TP = TQ}$, so ${\angle TPQ = \frac{1}{2}(180^\circ - 50^\circ) = 65^\circ}$

${\angle OPT = 90^\circ}$, so ${\angle OPQ = 90^\circ - 65^\circ = 25^\circ}$

The answer is (c). Option (b) is $\angle TPQ$, (d) is $\angle POQ$ and (a) is $\angle PTQ$.

Problem 3

A quadrilateral ABCD circumscribes a circle, with ${AB = 6}$ cm, ${BC = 7}$ cm and ${CD = 4}$ cm. Then AD is

  1. 9 cm
  2. 3 cm
  3. 17 cm
  4. 5 cm
Solution

the two tangents from each vertex are equal, so ${AB + CD = AD + BC}$

${6 + 4 = AD + 7}$, so ${AD = 3}$ cm

The answer is (b). Option (a) is ${AB + BC - CD}$, (c) adds all three sides, and (d) averages AB and CD.

Problem 4

Two concentric circles have radii 13 cm and 5 cm. The chord of the larger circle that touches the smaller circle has length

  1. 12 cm
  2. 24 cm
  3. 18 cm
  4. ${2\sqrt{194}}$ cm
Solution

the radius to the point of contact bisects the chord at right angles (Example 1)

half the chord ${= \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = 12}$ cm, so the chord is 24 cm

The answer is (b). Option (a) is half the chord, (c) adds the radii and (d) their squares.
