Example 12 · Numerical answer

$PA$ and $PB$ are tangents from $P$ to a circle with centre $O$, and $\angle AOB = 110^\circ$. Find $\angle APB$, in degrees.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The angles at $A$ and $B$ are right angles.
Step 2$\angle APB = 360 - 90 - 90 - 110 = 70$.
Answer70
Example 13 · Numerical answer

Two concentric circles have radii 13 cm and 5 cm. Find the length, in cm, of the chord of the larger circle that touches the smaller.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The radius to the point of contact bisects the chord at right angles: half of it is $\sqrt{169 - 25} = 12$, so the chord is 24 cm.
Answer24
Example 14 · Matching

A tangent is drawn from a point at distance $d$ from the centre of a circle of radius $r$ (in cm). Match each with the tangent's length. Each entry has exactly one match.

(P) Radius 3, distance 5(1) 15

(R) Radius 8, distance 17(3) 4
(S) Radius 7, distance 25(4) 12

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The tangent is $\sqrt{d^2 - r^2}$: $\sqrt{16} = 4$, $\sqrt{144} = 12$, $\sqrt{225} = 15$ and $\sqrt{576} = 24$.
Answer(c) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
