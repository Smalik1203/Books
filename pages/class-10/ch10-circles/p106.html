Example 10

PA and PB are the tangents from a point P to a circle, with ${PA = 10}$ cm. A third tangent touches the circle at a point E of the arc that faces P, and meets PA at C and PB at D. Find the perimeter of the triangle PCD.

Solution.

Step 1 ${CE = CA}$ and ${DE = DB}$ tangents from C and from D
Step 2 ${PC + CD + DP = PC + CE + ED + DP}$ E lies on CD
Step 3 ${PC + CE + ED + DP = PC + CA + DB + DP}$ Step 1
Step 4 ${PC + CA + DB + DP = PA + PB = 10 + 10 = 20}$ ${PA = PB}$
Answer 20 cm

The perimeter does not depend on where E is on that arc.

Example 11

Two concentric circles have radii 13 cm and 5 cm. The chord of the larger circle that touches the smaller circle has length

  1. 12 cm
  2. 24 cm
  3. 18 cm
  4. ${2\sqrt{194}}$ cm

Solution.

Step 1 the radius to the point of contact bisects the chord at right angles Example 1 of the chapter
Step 2 half the chord ${= \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = 12}$ cm Pythagoras theorem
Step 3 the chord ${= 2 \times 12 = 24}$ cm
Answer (b)

Option (a) is half the chord, (c) adds the radii, and (d) adds their squares.
