10.3Number of Tangents from a Point on a Circle

How many tangents to a circle can pass through a given point? Try this activity to find out.

Activity 3. Draw a circle on paper. Take a point P inside it. Can you draw a tangent to the circle through P? You will find that every line through P meets the circle in two points [Fig. 10.6 (i)]. So no tangent to a circle passes through a point inside it.

Next take a point P on the circle, and draw tangents through it. You have already seen that there is only one tangent to the circle at such a point [Fig. 10.6 (ii)].

Last, take a point P outside the circle, and try to draw tangents to the circle through P. What do you see? You can draw exactly two tangents to the circle through this point [Fig. 10.6 (iii)].
