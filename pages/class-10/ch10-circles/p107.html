Type 5 · Proofs with tangents

Example 12

Prove that the tangents drawn at the ends of a chord of a circle make equal angles with the chord.

Solution. Let the tangents at the ends P and Q of a chord PQ meet at T. We need to prove that ${\angle TPQ = \angle TQP}$.

Step 1 ${TP = TQ}$ Theorem 10.2
Step 2 the triangle TPQ is isosceles two equal sides
Step 3 ${\angle TPQ = \angle TQP}$ angles opposite equal sides
Answer The two tangents make equal angles with the chord PQ
Example 13

A circle touches the side BC of a triangle ABC at P, and touches AB produced at Q and AC produced at R. Prove that AQ is half the perimeter of the triangle ABC.

Solution. We need to prove that ${AQ = \frac{1}{2}(AB + BC + CA)}$.

Step 1 ${AQ = AR}$, ${BQ = BP}$ and ${CR = CP}$ tangents from A, B and C
Step 2 ${AB + BC + CA = AB + BP + PC + CA}$ P lies on BC
Step 3 ${AB + BP + PC + CA = AB + BQ + CR + CA}$ Step 1
Step 4 ${AB + BQ + CR + CA = AQ + AR = 2AQ}$ Q and R on the sides produced
Answer ${AQ = \frac{1}{2}(AB + BC + CA)}$
