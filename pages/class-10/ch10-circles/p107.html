Three things are worth keeping. Draw the radius to the point of contact: it brings a right angle, and with it the Pythagoras theorem and the ratios of Chapter 8. The two tangents from an outside point are equal, so a figure made of tangents can be read one vertex at a time. And a length that is hard to reach can often be found from an area worked out in two ways.
