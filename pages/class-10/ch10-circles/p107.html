Example 15 · Matching

$PA$ and $PB$ are tangents to a circle with centre $O$. Match each $\angle APB$ with $\angle AOB$. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $\angle APB = 40^\circ$(1) $90^\circ$
(Q) $\angle APB = 90^\circ$(2) $140^\circ$
(R) $\angle APB = 70^\circ$(3) $60^\circ$
(S) $\angle APB = 120^\circ$(4) $110^\circ$

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  2. P–1, Q–2, R–4, S–3
  3. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3
  4. P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The angles at $A$ and $B$ are right angles, so $\angle AOB = 180^\circ - \angle APB$: $140^\circ$, $90^\circ$, $110^\circ$, $60^\circ$.
Answer(d) P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3
