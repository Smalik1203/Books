Let the tangents touch the circle at A and B, and let O be the centre. OP bisects $\angle APB$, so ${\angle APO = 30^\circ}$, and ${\angle OAP = 90^\circ}$ by Theorem 10.1. In the right triangle OAP, ${\sin 30^\circ = \frac{OA}{OP}}$, that is, ${\frac{1}{2} = \frac{6}{OP}}$, so ${OP = 12}$ cm, and ${\tan 30^\circ = \frac{OA}{AP}}$ gives ${AP = 6\sqrt{3}}$ cm.

A circle is drawn inside a right triangle whose shorter sides are 3 cm and 4 cm, touching all three sides. Find its radius.

In the triangle ABC, let ${BC = 3}$ cm, ${CA = 4}$ cm and ${\angle C = 90^\circ}$, and let the circle, with centre O and radius $r$, touch BC and CA at D and E. ODCE has three right angles and ${OD = OE = r}$, so it is a square and ${CD = CE = r}$. The tangents from B and A, ${3 - r}$ and ${4 - r}$, make up the hypotenuse: ${(3 - r) + (4 - r) = 5}$, so ${r = 1}$ cm. The same steps give ${r = \frac{a + b - c}{2}}$ in any right triangle with hypotenuse $c$.

Example 1 · Single correct

A point is 13 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 5 cm. How long is the tangent from it?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 12 cm
  2. 8 cm
  3. 18 cm
  4. 13 cm

Solution.

Step 1The tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact, so the radius, the tangent and the line to the centre make a right triangle.
Step 2Tangent $= \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{144} = 12$ cm.
Answer(a) 12 cm

What is the angle between a tangent to a circle and the radius at the point of contact?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $60^\circ$
  2. $90^\circ$
  3. $45^\circ$
  4. $180^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through that point.
Step 2So the angle is $90^\circ$.
Answer(b) $90^\circ$
