Example 6 · Single correct

Two concentric circles have radii 5 cm and 3 cm. How long is a chord of the larger circle that touches the smaller?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 4 cm
  2. 8 cm
  3. 6 cm
  4. 10 cm

Solution.

Step 1The chord touches the smaller circle, so the radius 3 to that point is perpendicular to the chord and bisects it.
Step 2Half the chord is $\sqrt{25 - 9} = 4$, so the chord is 8 cm.
Answer(b) 8 cm
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which statements are true of every circle?

Select all correct options.

  1. A tangent meets it at exactly one point.
  2. The tangents from an external point are equal.
  3. It has exactly two parallel tangents.
  4. The tangent at a point is perpendicular to the radius there.

Solution.

Step 1(a), (b) and (d) are the chapter's facts about tangents.
Step 2Every diameter gives a pair of parallel tangents at its ends, so there are infinitely many pairs.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

$PA$ and $PB$ are tangents from $P$ to a circle with centre $O$ and radius 5 cm, and $\angle APB = 60^\circ$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $\angle AOB = 120^\circ$
  2. $\angle OPA = 30^\circ$
  3. $OP = 10$ cm
  4. $PA = 5$ cm

Solution.

Step 1$\angle AOB = 120^\circ$ and $\angle OPA = 30^\circ$, so $OP = \frac{5}{\sin 30^\circ} = 10$ and $PA = 5\sqrt{3}$ cm.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
