Type 2 · Angles made by tangents

Example 5

TP and TQ are tangents from T to a circle with centre O, and ${\angle PTQ = 50^\circ}$. Then $\angle OPQ$ is

  1. $50^\circ$
  2. $65^\circ$
  3. $25^\circ$
  4. $130^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1 ${TP = TQ}$, so ${\angle TPQ = \frac{1}{2}(180^\circ - 50^\circ) = 65^\circ}$ equal tangents
Step 2 ${\angle OPT = 90^\circ}$, so ${\angle OPQ = 90^\circ - 65^\circ = 25^\circ}$ Theorem 10.1
Answer (c)

Option (b) is $\angle TPQ$, (a) is $\angle PTQ$, and (d) is ${180^\circ - 50^\circ}$.

Example 6

PQ is a chord of a circle with centre O, and PT is the tangent at P. If ${\angle QPT = 50^\circ}$, find $\angle POQ$.

Solution.

Step 1 ${\angle OPT = 90^\circ}$ Theorem 10.1
Step 2 ${\angle OPQ = 90^\circ - 50^\circ = 40^\circ}$
Step 3 ${\angle OQP = \angle OPQ = 40^\circ}$ ${OP = OQ}$, radii
Step 4 ${\angle POQ = 180^\circ - 40^\circ - 40^\circ = 100^\circ}$ angle sum of triangle OPQ
Answer ${\angle POQ = 100^\circ}$

The angle at the centre is twice the angle between the tangent and the chord.

Example 7

The tangents PA and PB from a point P to a circle with centre O and radius 3 cm make an angle of $60^\circ$ with each other. Show that the triangle APB is equilateral, and find AB.

Solution.

Step 1 ${PA = PB}$ Theorem 10.2
Step 2 ${\angle PAB = \angle PBA = \frac{1}{2}(180^\circ - 60^\circ) = 60^\circ}$, so APB is equilateral angles opposite equal sides
Step 3 ${\angle APO = 30^\circ}$ and ${\angle OAP = 90^\circ}$ OP bisects the angle; Theorem 10.1
Step 4 ${\tan 30^\circ = \frac{OA}{PA}}$, that is, ${\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{3}{PA}}$, so ${PA = 3\sqrt{3}}$ cm
Answer The triangle is equilateral, and ${AB = PA = 3\sqrt{3}}$ cm
