Problem 5

A circle touches the sides BC, CA and AB of a triangle ABC at D, E and F. If ${AB = 10}$ cm, ${AC = 12}$ cm and ${BD = 4}$ cm, then BC is

  1. 10 cm
  2. 12 cm
  3. 8 cm
  4. 14 cm
Solution

${BF = BD = 4}$, so ${AF = 10 - 4 = 6}$;   ${AE = AF = 6}$, so ${CE = 12 - 6 = 6}$

${CD = CE = 6}$, so ${BC = BD + CD = 4 + 6 = 10}$ cm

The answer is (a). Option (b) copies AC, (c) is ${AC - BD}$, and (d) is ${AB + BD}$. Follow the equal tangents round the triangle one vertex at a time.
