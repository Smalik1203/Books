What this chapter showed
  1. A tangent to a circle is a line that meets the circle in exactly one point, its point of contact.
  2. The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.
  3. The lengths of the two tangents from an external point to a circle are equal.
