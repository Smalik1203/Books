Example 2

Two tangents TP and TQ are drawn to a circle with centre O from an external point T. Prove that ${\angle PTQ = 2\angle OPQ}$.

We are given a circle with centre O, an external point T, and two tangents TP and TQ with points of contact P and Q (Fig. 10.9). We need to prove that ${\angle PTQ = 2\angle OPQ}$.

Let ${\angle PTQ = \theta}$. By Theorem 10.2, ${TP = TQ}$, so TPQ is an isosceles triangle.

$\angle TPQ = \angle TQP = \dfrac{1}{2}(180^\circ - \theta) = 90^\circ - \dfrac{1}{2}\theta$   (angles opposite equal sides)

${\angle OPT = 90^\circ}$   (Theorem 10.1)

$\angle OPQ = \angle OPT - \angle TPQ = 90^\circ - \left(90^\circ - \dfrac{1}{2}\theta\right) = \dfrac{1}{2}\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}\angle PTQ$

So ${\angle PTQ = 2\angle OPQ}$.
