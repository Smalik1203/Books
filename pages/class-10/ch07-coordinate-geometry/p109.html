Assertion and reason

In Questions 16 to 19, choose (a) if both A and R are true and R explains A; (b) if both are true but R does not explain A; (c) if A is true but R is false; (d) if A is false but R is true.

  1. Assertion (A): The point $(-3, 0)$ lies on the $x$-axis.

    Reason (R): Every point on the $x$-axis has $y$-coordinate 0.

  1. Assertion (A): The distance of the point $(5, 12)$ from the origin is 13 units.

    Reason (R): The mid-point of the line segment joining $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ is ${\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)}$.

  1. Assertion (A): The point $(2, 3)$ divides the line segment joining $(1, 1)$ and $(4, 7)$ in the ratio ${1 : 2}$.

    Reason (R): The mid-point of a line segment divides it in the ratio ${2 : 1}$.

  1. Assertion (A): The distance between the points $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 6)$ is 7 units.

    Reason (R): The distance between $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ is ${\sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}}$.
