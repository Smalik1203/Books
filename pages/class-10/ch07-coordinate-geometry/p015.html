1. Find the ratio in which the line segment joining the points $(-3, 10)$ and $(6, -8)$ is divided by $(-1, 6)$.
  1. Find the ratio in which the line segment joining $A(1, -5)$ and $B(-4, 5)$ is divided by the $x$-axis. Also find the coordinates of the point of division.
  1. If $(1, 2)$, $(4, y)$, $(x, 6)$ and $(3, 5)$, taken in order, are the vertices of a parallelogram, find $x$ and $y$.
  1. AB is a diameter of a circle whose centre is $(2, -3)$, and B is the point $(1, 4)$. Find the coordinates of the point A.
  1. A and B are the points $(-2, -2)$ and $(2, -4)$. Find the coordinates of the point P on the line segment AB such that ${AP = \frac{3}{7}AB}$.
  1. Find the coordinates of the points which divide the line segment joining $A(-2, 2)$ and $B(2, 8)$ into four equal parts.
  1. The vertices of a rhombus, taken in order, are $(3, 0)$, $(4, 5)$, $(-1, 4)$ and $(-2, -1)$. Find its area. [Hint: area of a rhombus ${= \frac{1}{2} \times}$ (product of its diagonals)]
