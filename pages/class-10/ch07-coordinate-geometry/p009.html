7.3Section Formula

Go back to the two towns of Section 7.2. Suppose a telephone company wants to put up a relay tower at a point P between A and B, so that the distance of the tower from B is twice its distance from A. If P lies on AB, it divides AB in the ratio 1 : 2 (Fig. 7.9). Take A as the origin O, and 1 km as one unit on both axes. Then B is the point $(36, 15)$ on these axes. To know where the tower stands, we must know the coordinates of P. How do we find them?

Let the coordinates of P be $(x, y)$. Draw perpendiculars from P and B to the $x$-axis, meeting it at D and E. Draw PC perpendicular to BE. Then, by the AA similarity criterion of Chapter 6, $\triangle POD$ and $\triangle BPC$ are similar. So

$\dfrac{OD}{PC} = \dfrac{OP}{PB} = \dfrac{1}{2}$   and   $\dfrac{PD}{BC} = \dfrac{OP}{PB} = \dfrac{1}{2}$

so $\dfrac{x}{36 - x} = \dfrac{1}{2}$   and   $\dfrac{y}{15 - y} = \dfrac{1}{2}$

The first equation gives ${2x = 36 - x}$, so ${x = 12}$. The second gives ${2y = 15 - y}$, so ${y = 5}$. You can check that $P(12, 5)$ satisfies the condition ${OP : PB = 1 : 2}$.

Now let us use what this example has shown to get a general formula.

Take any two points $A(x_1, y_1)$ and $B(x_2, y_2)$, and suppose that $P(x, y)$ divides AB internally in the ratio ${m_1 : m_2}$, that is, ${\dfrac{PA}{PB} = \dfrac{m_1}{m_2}}$ (Fig. 7.10).
