Example 5

Find a point on the $y$-axis which is equidistant from the points $A(6, 5)$ and $B(-4, 3)$.

We know that a point on the $y$-axis is of the form $(0, y)$. So let the point $P(0, y)$ be equidistant from A and B. Then

${(6 - 0)^2 + (5 - y)^2 = (-4 - 0)^2 + (3 - y)^2}$

${36 + 25 + y^2 - 10y = 16 + 9 + y^2 - 6y}$

${61 - 10y = 25 - 6y}$, so ${4y = 36}$

${y = 9}$

So the point we want is $(0, 9)$. Let us check the answer:

${AP = \sqrt{(6 - 0)^2 + (5 - 9)^2} = \sqrt{36 + 16} = \sqrt{52}}$

${BP = \sqrt{(-4 - 0)^2 + (3 - 9)^2} = \sqrt{16 + 36} = \sqrt{52}}$

Note. From the remark above, $(0, 9)$ is the point where the $y$-axis meets the perpendicular bisector of AB.
