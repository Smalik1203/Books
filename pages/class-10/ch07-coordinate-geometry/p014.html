Example 10

If the points $A(6, 1)$, $B(8, 2)$, $C(9, 4)$ and $D(p, 3)$, taken in order, are the vertices of a parallelogram, find the value of $p$.

Solution.

Step 1 the mid-point of AC and the mid-point of BD are the same point diagonals bisect each other
Step 2 $\left(\dfrac{6 + 9}{2},\ \dfrac{1 + 4}{2}\right) = \left(\dfrac{8 + p}{2},\ \dfrac{2 + 3}{2}\right)$ mid-point formula
Step 3 $\left(\dfrac{15}{2},\ \dfrac{5}{2}\right) = \left(\dfrac{8 + p}{2},\ \dfrac{5}{2}\right)$
Step 4 so $\dfrac{15}{2} = \dfrac{8 + p}{2}$, that is, ${p = 7}$
Answer ${p = 7}$
