Type 2 · An unknown coordinate from a distance

Example 3

The distance between the points $(4, p)$ and $(1, 0)$ is 5 units. Then $p$ is

  1. 4 only
  2. $-4$ only
  3. 4 or $-4$
  4. 0

Solution.

Step 1 ${(4 - 1)^2 + (p - 0)^2 = 5^2}$ square both sides
Step 2 ${9 + p^2 = 25}$, so ${p^2 = 16}$
Step 3 ${p = 4}$ or ${p = -4}$ 16 has two square roots
Answer (c)

Options (a) and (b) each keep only one of the two roots; both $(4, 4)$ and $(4, -4)$ are 5 units from $(1, 0)$. Option (d) gives a distance of 3.

Example 4

Find the values of $x$ for which the distance between the points $(x, -1)$ and $(3, 2)$ is 5 units.

Solution.

Step 1 ${(x - 3)^2 + (-1 - 2)^2 = 5^2}$ distance formula, squared
Step 2 ${(x - 3)^2 + 9 = 25}$, so ${(x - 3)^2 = 16}$
Step 3 ${x - 3 = 4}$ or ${x - 3 = -4}$
Answer ${x = 7}$ or ${x = -1}$

Type 3 · Proving the shape of a figure

Example 5

Show that the points $A(1, -1)$, $B(5, 2)$ and $C(9, 5)$ are collinear.

Solution.

Step 1 ${AB = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5}$ distance formula
Step 2 ${BC = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5}$
Step 3 ${AC = \sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = 10}$
Step 4 ${AB + BC = 5 + 5 = 10 = AC}$ the two parts make the whole
Answer A, B and C are collinear, with B between A and C
