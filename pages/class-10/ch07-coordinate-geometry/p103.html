Example 3 · Single correct

What is the mid-point of $(-3, 10)$ and $(6, -8)$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $(4.5, 9)$
  2. $(3, 2)$
  3. $(1.5, 1)$
  4. $(-1.5, 1)$

Solution.

Step 1Mid-point $= \left(\frac{-3 + 6}{2}, \frac{10 - 8}{2}\right)$.
Step 2$= (1.5, 1)$.
Answer(c) $(1.5, 1)$
Example 4 · Single correct

Which point on the $x$-axis is equidistant from $(2, -5)$ and $(-2, 9)$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $(7, 0)$
  2. $(0, -7)$
  3. $(-7, 7)$
  4. $(-7, 0)$

Solution.

Step 1For $(x, 0)$: $(x - 2)^2 + 25 = (x + 2)^2 + 81$.
Step 2$-4x + 29 = 4x + 85$, so $x = -7$.
Answer(d) $(-7, 0)$
Example 5 · Single correct

In what ratio does the $y$-axis divide the join of $A(5, -6)$ and $B(-1, -4)$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $5 : 1$
  2. $1 : 5$
  3. $6 : 1$
  4. $3 : 2$

Solution.

Step 1Take the ratio $k : 1$. On the $y$-axis $x = 0$: $\frac{k \times (-1) + 5}{k + 1} = 0$.
Step 2So $k = 5$: the ratio is $5 : 1$.
Answer(a) $5 : 1$
