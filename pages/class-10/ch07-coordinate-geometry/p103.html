Problem 2

A is $(-4, 0)$ and B is $(8, 6)$. The point P on AB with ${AP : PB = 1 : 2}$ is

  1. $(4, 4)$
  2. $(2, 3)$
  3. $(0, 2)$
  4. $(0, 3)$
Solution

$x = \dfrac{1(8) + 2(-4)}{1 + 2} = 0$   and   $y = \dfrac{1(6) + 2(0)}{1 + 2} = 2$

The answer is (c). Option (a) is the point with ${AP : PB = 2 : 1}$, which is nearer B, and (b) is the mid-point. Option (d) has the right $x$-coordinate but divides the rise of 6 by 2 instead of by 3.

Problem 3

The $x$-axis divides the line segment joining $(2, -3)$ and $(5, 6)$ in the ratio

  1. ${2 : 1}$
  2. ${1 : 2}$
  3. ${1 : 3}$
  4. ${1 : 1}$
Solution

with the ratio ${k : 1}$, the $y$-coordinate is $\dfrac{6k - 3}{k + 1} = 0$

so ${k = \frac{1}{2}}$, and the ratio is ${1 : 2}$

The answer is (b), and the point is $(3, 0)$. Option (a) turns the ratio round. Option (c) comes from seeing that the axis is $\frac{1}{3}$ of the way up, but $\frac{1}{3}$ of the whole segment is a ratio of ${1 : 2}$ between the two parts.

Problem 4

The perimeter of the triangle with vertices $O(0, 0)$, $A(0, 6)$ and $B(8, 0)$ is

  1. 14
  2. 24
  3. 20
  4. 48
Solution

${OA = 6}$ and ${OB = 8}$, since both lie along an axis

${AB = \sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{100} = 10}$, so the perimeter is ${6 + 8 + 10 = 24}$

The answer is (b). Option (a) leaves out the side AB, and (c) is twice AB. Option (d) is twice the perimeter.
