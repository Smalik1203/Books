Example 15 · Matching

Match each point with its distance from the origin. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $(5, 12)$(1) 25
(Q) $(-8, 6)$(2) 15
(R) $(7, -24)$(3) 13
(S) $(-9, 12)$(4) 10

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2

Solution.

Step 1The distance from the origin is $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$: $\sqrt{169} = 13$ and $\sqrt{100} = 10$.
Step 2$\sqrt{625} = 25$ and $\sqrt{225} = 15$.
Answer(d) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
