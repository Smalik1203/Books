Case-based questions

  1. A town map is drawn on a grid in which 1 unit stands for 1 km. Three places are marked on it.

    PlacePosition
    School$(2, 3)$
    Hospital$(8, 11)$
    Park$(-4, 11)$
    1. How far is the hospital from the school?
    2. A bus stop is on the straight road from the school to the hospital, and divides it in the ratio ${1 : 3}$ from the school. Where is the bus stop?
    3. Is the park as far from the school as the hospital is?
    4. A water tank stands halfway between the hospital and the park. Where is it?

  1. Three saplings are planted in a school garden. Their positions, in metres from one corner of the garden, are shown below. A tap is to be fixed at the mid-point M of AC.

    SaplingPosition
    A$(1, 2)$
    B$(5, 2)$
    C$(5, 8)$
    1. Find AB and BC.
    2. Find AC.
    3. Find the position of the tap M.
    4. Show that the tap is equally far from all three saplings.
