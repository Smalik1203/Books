Problem 5

If ${\left(\frac{a}{3}, 4\right)}$ is the mid-point of the line segment joining $(-6, 5)$ and $(-2, 3)$, then $a$ is

  1. $-4$
  2. 12
  3. $-12$
  4. $-6$
Solution

the mid-point is ${\left(\frac{-6 - 2}{2}, \frac{5 + 3}{2}\right) = (-4, 4)}$

so ${\frac{a}{3} = -4}$ and ${a = -12}$

The answer is (c). Option (a) is the $x$-coordinate of the mid-point, which is $\frac{a}{3}$ and not $a$. Option (b) loses the minus sign, and (d) is the $x$-coordinate of one end.
