Example 6 · Single correct

The distance between $(x, 7)$ and $(1, 3)$ is 5, and $x \gt 1$. What is $x$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $-2$
  2. 4
  3. 5
  4. 6

Solution.

Step 1$(x - 1)^2 + 4^2 = 25$, so $(x - 1)^2 = 9$ and $x - 1 = \pm 3$.
Step 2$x = 4$ or $-2$, and $x \gt 1$ gives 4.
Answer(b) 4
Example 7 · Multiple correct

For $A(0, 0)$, $B(4, 0)$, $C(4, 3)$ and $D(0, 3)$, which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $AB = 4$
  2. $AC = 5$
  3. The diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ are equal.
  4. $ABCD$ is a square.

Solution.

Step 1$AB = 4$, $BC = 3$, and $AC = BD = \sqrt{16 + 9} = 5$.
Step 2Adjacent sides 4 and 3 differ, so it is a rectangle, not a square.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

For $A(1, 1)$, $B(3, 5)$ and $C(5, 9)$, which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $AB = BC$
  2. $AB + BC = AC$
  3. $AC = 2\sqrt{5}$
  4. $A$, $B$ and $C$ lie on one line.

Solution.

Step 1$AB = BC = \sqrt{4 + 16} = 2\sqrt{5}$ and $AC = \sqrt{16 + 64} = 4\sqrt{5}$.
Step 2So $AB + BC = AC$, which puts $B$ on the segment $AC$: the points are collinear.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
