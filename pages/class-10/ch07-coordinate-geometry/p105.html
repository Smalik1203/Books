Example 9 · Multiple correct

$P(2, -3)$ is the mid-point of $A(x, 1)$ and $B(5, y)$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $x = -1$
  2. $y = -7$
  3. $AB = 10$
  4. $A$ lies on the $y$-axis.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{x + 5}{2} = 2$ gives $x = -1$, and $\frac{1 + y}{2} = -3$ gives $y = -7$.
Step 2$AB = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 10$; $A(-1, 1)$ is not on the $y$-axis.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

The points of trisection of the join of $A(-3, 6)$ and $B(6, -3)$ are found. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $(0, 3)$ is one of them.
  2. $(3, 0)$ is one of them.
  3. $(1.5, 1.5)$ is one of them.
  4. Each lies on an axis.

Solution.

Step 1They divide $AB$ in $1 : 2$ and $2 : 1$: $(-3 + 3, 6 - 3) = (0, 3)$ and $(-3 + 6, 6 - 6) = (3, 0)$.
Step 2$(1.5, 1.5)$ is the mid-point, not a point of trisection.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
