Type 5 · The point that divides a segment in a given ratio

Example 9

A is $(-4, 0)$ and B is $(8, 6)$. The point P on AB with ${AP : PB = 1 : 2}$ is

  1. $(4, 4)$
  2. $(2, 3)$
  3. $(0, 2)$
  4. $(0, 3)$

Solution.

Step 1 $x = \dfrac{1(8) + 2(-4)}{1 + 2} = 0$ section formula, ${m_1 = 1}$, ${m_2 = 2}$
Step 2 $y = \dfrac{1(6) + 2(0)}{1 + 2} = 2$
Answer (c)

Option (a) is the point with ${AP : PB = 2 : 1}$, which is nearer B, and (b) is the mid-point. Option (d) has the right $x$-coordinate but divides the rise of 6 by 2 instead of by 3.

Example 10

On a map where 1 unit stands for 100 m, a straight road runs from a school at $S(2, 1)$ to a library at $L(14, 9)$. A bus stop B is to be put on the road so that ${SB : BL = 3 : 1}$. Find the position of the bus stop, and its distance from the library.

Solution.

Step 1 $x = \dfrac{3(14) + 1(2)}{3 + 1} = \dfrac{44}{4} = 11$ section formula
Step 2 $y = \dfrac{3(9) + 1(1)}{3 + 1} = \dfrac{28}{4} = 7$
Step 3 ${BL = \sqrt{3^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{13}}$ units distance formula
Answer The bus stop is at $B(11, 7)$, $100\sqrt{13}$ m, about 361 m, from the library
