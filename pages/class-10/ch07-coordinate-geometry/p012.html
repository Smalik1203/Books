Example 7

In what ratio does the point $(-4, 6)$ divide the line segment joining the points $A(-6, 10)$ and $B(3, -8)$?

Solution.

Step 1 let $(-4, 6)$ divide AB internally in the ratio ${m_1 : m_2}$
Step 2 $(-4, 6) = \left(\dfrac{3m_1 - 6m_2}{m_1 + m_2},\ \dfrac{-8m_1 + 10m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\right) \qquad (1)$ section formula
Step 3 $-4 = \dfrac{3m_1 - 6m_2}{m_1 + m_2}$ if ${(x, y) = (a, b)}$, then ${x = a}$ and ${y = b}$
Step 4 $6 = \dfrac{-8m_1 + 10m_2}{m_1 + m_2}$
Step 5 ${-4m_1 - 4m_2 = 3m_1 - 6m_2}$ from Step 3
Step 6 ${7m_1 = 2m_2}$, so ${m_1 : m_2 = 2 : 7}$
Check $\dfrac{-8m_1 + 10m_2}{m_1 + m_2} = \dfrac{-8 \cdot \frac{m_1}{m_2} + 10}{\frac{m_1}{m_2} + 1}$ top and bottom divided by $m_2$
Check $= \dfrac{-8 \cdot \frac{2}{7} + 10}{\frac{2}{7} + 1} = \dfrac{54}{7} \div \dfrac{9}{7} = 6$ so Step 4 holds too
Answer $(-4, 6)$ divides AB in the ratio ${2 : 7}$

Another way. The ratio ${m_1 : m_2}$ can also be written as ${\frac{m_1}{m_2} : 1}$, or ${k : 1}$. Let $(-4, 6)$ divide AB internally in the ratio ${k : 1}$. Using the section formula, we get

$$(-4, 6) = \left(\dfrac{3k - 6}{k + 1},\ \dfrac{-8k + 10}{k + 1}\right) \qquad (2)$$

so $-4 = \dfrac{3k - 6}{k + 1}$

${-4k - 4 = 3k - 6}$

${7k = 2}$, that is, ${k : 1 = 2 : 7}$

You can check the $y$-coordinate as well, and it gives the same ratio ${2 : 7}$.

Note. You can also find this ratio by working out the distances PA and PB and taking their ratio, as long as you know that A, P and B are collinear.
