${-4m_1 - 4m_2 = 3m_1 - 6m_2}$

${7m_1 = 2m_2}$

${m_1 : m_2 = 2 : 7}$

You should check that this ratio satisfies the $y$-coordinate too. Dividing the top and the bottom by $m_2$,

$\dfrac{-8m_1 + 10m_2}{m_1 + m_2} = \dfrac{-8 \cdot \frac{m_1}{m_2} + 10}{\frac{m_1}{m_2} + 1} = \dfrac{-8 \cdot \frac{2}{7} + 10}{\frac{2}{7} + 1} = \dfrac{54}{7} \div \dfrac{9}{7} = 6$

So the point $(-4, 6)$ divides the line segment joining the points $A(-6, 10)$ and $B(3, -8)$ in the ratio ${2 : 7}$.

Another way. The ratio ${m_1 : m_2}$ can also be written as ${\frac{m_1}{m_2} : 1}$, or ${k : 1}$. Let $(-4, 6)$ divide AB internally in the ratio ${k : 1}$. Using the section formula, we get

$$(-4, 6) = \left(\dfrac{3k - 6}{k + 1},\ \dfrac{-8k + 10}{k + 1}\right) \qquad (2)$$

so $-4 = \dfrac{3k - 6}{k + 1}$

${-4k - 4 = 3k - 6}$

${7k = 2}$, that is, ${k : 1 = 2 : 7}$

You can check the $y$-coordinate as well. So the point $(-4, 6)$ divides the line segment joining $A(-6, 10)$ and $B(3, -8)$ in the ratio ${2 : 7}$.

Note. You can also find this ratio by working out the distances PA and PB and taking their ratio, as long as you know that A, P and B are collinear.
