What this chapter showed
  1. The distance between the points $P(x_1, y_1)$ and $Q(x_2, y_2)$ is ${\sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}}$.
  2. The distance of a point $P(x, y)$ from the origin is ${\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}}$.
  3. The coordinates of the point $P(x, y)$ which divides the line segment joining the points $A(x_1, y_1)$ and $B(x_2, y_2)$ internally in the ratio ${m_1 : m_2}$ are ${\left(\frac{m_1x_2 + m_2x_1}{m_1 + m_2},\ \frac{m_1y_2 + m_2y_1}{m_1 + m_2}\right)}$.
  4. The mid-point of the line segment joining the points $P(x_1, y_1)$ and $Q(x_2, y_2)$ is ${\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2},\ \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)}$.
