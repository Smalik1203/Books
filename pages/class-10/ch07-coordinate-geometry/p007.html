Example 5

Find a point on the $y$-axis which is equidistant from the points $A(6, 5)$ and $B(-4, 3)$.

Solution.

Step 1 let the point $P(0, y)$ be equidistant from A and B a point on the $y$-axis is $(0, y)$
Step 2 ${(6 - 0)^2 + (5 - y)^2 = (-4 - 0)^2 + (3 - y)^2}$ ${AP^2 = BP^2}$
Step 3 ${36 + 25 + y^2 - 10y = 16 + 9 + y^2 - 6y}$ expanding
Step 4 ${61 - 10y = 25 - 6y}$, so ${4y = 36}$
Step 5 ${y = 9}$, so the point is $(0, 9)$
Check ${AP = \sqrt{(6 - 0)^2 + (5 - 9)^2} = \sqrt{36 + 16} = \sqrt{52}}$
Check ${BP = \sqrt{(-4 - 0)^2 + (3 - 9)^2} = \sqrt{16 + 36} = \sqrt{52}}$ the same, as it should be
Answer $(0, 9)$

Note. From the remark above, $(0, 9)$ is the point where the $y$-axis meets the perpendicular bisector of AB.
