In Fig. 7.3, the points $P(4, 6)$ and $Q(6, 8)$ lie in the first quadrant. How do we use the Pythagoras theorem to find the distance between them? Draw PR and QS perpendicular to the $x$-axis from P and Q. Also draw a perpendicular from P to QS, meeting QS at T. Then R is the point $(4, 0)$ and S is the point $(6, 0)$, so ${RS = 2}$ units. Also ${QS = 8}$ units and ${TS = PR = 6}$ units.

So ${QT = 8 - 6 = 2}$ units and ${PT = RS = 2}$ units. Now, using the Pythagoras theorem in the right triangle PTQ, we have

${PQ^2 = PT^2 + QT^2 = 2^2 + 2^2 = 8}$

so ${PQ = 2\sqrt{2}}$ units
