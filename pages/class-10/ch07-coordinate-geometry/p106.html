Example 12 · Numerical answer

The point $(3, y)$ is 13 units from $(-2, 1)$, and $y \gt 0$. Find $y$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$5^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 169$, so $(y - 1)^2 = 144$.
Step 2$y - 1 = 12$ (as $y \gt 0$), so $y = 13$.
Answer13
Example 13 · Numerical answer

Find the perimeter of the triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(8, 0)$ and $(0, 6)$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Two sides lie along the axes: 8 and 6.
Step 2The third is $\sqrt{64 + 36} = 10$, so the perimeter is $8 + 6 + 10 = 24$.
Answer24
