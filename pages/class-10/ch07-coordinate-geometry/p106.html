Type 6 · The ratio in which a point or an axis divides a segment

Example 11

The $x$-axis divides the line segment joining $(2, -3)$ and $(5, 6)$ in the ratio

  1. ${2 : 1}$
  2. ${1 : 2}$
  3. ${1 : 3}$
  4. ${1 : 1}$

Solution.

Step 1 with the ratio ${k : 1}$, the $y$-coordinate is $\dfrac{6k - 3}{k + 1}$ section formula
Step 2 $\dfrac{6k - 3}{k + 1} = 0$, so ${k = \frac{1}{2}}$ on the $x$-axis, $y$ is 0
Step 3 the ratio is ${\frac{1}{2} : 1 = 1 : 2}$, and the point is $(3, 0)$
Answer (b)

Option (a) turns the ratio round. Option (c) comes from seeing that the axis is $\frac{1}{3}$ of the way up, but $\frac{1}{3}$ of the whole segment is a ratio of ${1 : 2}$ between the two parts.

Example 12

The point $P(-2, y)$ lies on the line segment joining $A(-5, 8)$ and $B(3, -4)$. Find the ratio in which P divides AB, and the value of $y$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{3k - 5}{k + 1} = -2$ ratio ${k : 1}$, $x$-coordinates
Step 2 ${3k - 5 = -2k - 2}$, so ${5k = 3}$ and ${k = \frac{3}{5}}$
Step 3 the ratio is ${3 : 5}$
Step 4 $y = \dfrac{3(-4) + 5(8)}{3 + 5} = \dfrac{28}{8} = \dfrac{7}{2}$ section formula
Answer ${3 : 5}$, and ${y = \frac{7}{2}}$
