Type 3 · Area of a segment

Example 6

A chord of a circle of radius 14 cm subtends $60^\circ$ at the centre. The area of the minor segment is (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$ and ${\sqrt{3} = 1.73}$.)

  1. 17.9 cm2
  2. 102.7 cm2
  3. 84.8 cm2
  4. 187.4 cm2

Solution.

Step 1 sector ${= \frac{1}{6} \times 616 = 102.67}$ cm2 $60^\circ$ is a sixth of the circle
Step 2 triangle ${= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \times 196 = 84.77}$ cm2 it is equilateral
Step 3 segment ${= 102.67 - 84.77 = 17.90}$ cm2 sector less triangle
Answer (a)

Option (b) is the sector, (c) is the triangle, and (d) adds them.

Example 7

A chord of a circle of radius 21 cm subtends a right angle at the centre. Find the areas of the minor and the major segments. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 sector $= \dfrac{90}{360} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 21 \times 21 = 346.5$ cm2
Step 2 triangle $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 21 \times 21 = 220.5$ cm2 right angle at the centre
Step 3 minor segment $= 346.5 - 220.5 = 126$ cm2
Step 4 circle $= \dfrac{22}{7} \times 21 \times 21 = 1386$ cm2
Step 5 major segment $= 1386 - 126 = 1260$ cm2 the rest of the circle
Answer minor segment 126 cm2, major segment 1260 cm2
