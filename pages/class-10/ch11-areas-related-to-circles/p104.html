Problem 5

The hour hand of a clock is 6 cm long. The area it sweeps from 12 o'clock to 4 o'clock is

  1. $4\pi$ cm2
  2. $24\pi$ cm2
  3. $12\pi$ cm2
  4. $36\pi$ cm2
Solution

the hour hand turns $30^\circ$ an hour, so in 4 hours it turns $120^\circ$

area ${= \frac{120}{360} \times \pi \times 36 = 12\pi}$ cm2

The answer is (c). Option (a) takes 4 hours as $40^\circ$, (b) uses $2\pi r^2$ in place of $\pi r^2$, and (d) is the whole circle.
