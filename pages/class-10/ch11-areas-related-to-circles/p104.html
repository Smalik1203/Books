Example 6 · Single correct

A sector of a circle of radius 6 cm has area $6\pi \text{ cm}^2$. What is its angle?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $30^\circ$
  2. $60^\circ$
  3. $90^\circ$
  4. $120^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi \times 36 = 6\pi$, so $\theta = 60^\circ$.
Answer(b) $60^\circ$
Example 7 · Multiple correct

A sector $OAB$ of a circle of radius 14 cm has angle $90^\circ$ at the centre $O$. Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The arc $AB$ is 22 cm long.
  2. The sector has area $154 \text{ cm}^2$.
  3. $\triangle OAB$ has area $196 \text{ cm}^2$.
  4. The minor segment has area $56 \text{ cm}^2$.

Solution.

Step 1Arc $= \frac{1}{4} \times 88 = 22$, sector $= \frac{1}{4} \times 616 = 154$, triangle $= \frac{1}{2} \times 14 \times 14 = 98$, segment $= 56$.

Example 8 · Multiple correct

For a sector of radius $r$ and angle $\theta$ (in degrees), which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its arc is $\frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r$.
  2. Its area is $\frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2$.
  3. Its area is its arc times $r$.
  4. Doubling $r$ doubles its area.

Solution.

Step 1A sector is $\frac{\theta}{360}$ of the circle in length and area; its area is half the arc times $r$, and doubling $r$ quadruples it.
Answer(a), (b)
