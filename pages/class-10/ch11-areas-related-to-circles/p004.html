Example 2

Find the area of the segment AYB in Fig. 11.6, if the radius of the circle is 21 cm and ${\angle AOB = 120^\circ}$. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 area of the segment AYB $=$ area of the sector OAYB $-$ area of $\triangle OAB$ (1)
Step 2 area of the sector OAYB $= \dfrac{120}{360} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 21 \times 21$ cm2 $= 462$ cm2 (2)
Step 3 draw ${OM \perp AB}$ (Fig. 11.7); since ${OA = OB}$, triangles AMO and BMO are congruent RHS
Step 4 M is the mid-point of AB, and ${\angle AOM = \angle BOM = \frac{1}{2} \times 120^\circ = 60^\circ}$ congruent parts
Step 5 in $\triangle OMA$,   $\dfrac{OM}{OA} = \cos 60^\circ$,   so   $\dfrac{x}{21} = \dfrac{1}{2}$   and   $OM = x = \dfrac{21}{2}$ cm let ${OM = x}$ cm
Step 6 $\dfrac{AM}{OA} = \sin 60^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$,   so   $AM = \dfrac{21\sqrt{3}}{2}$ cm
Step 7 ${AB = 2AM = 21\sqrt{3}}$ cm M is the mid-point
Step 8 area of $\triangle OAB = \dfrac{1}{2} \times AB \times OM = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 21\sqrt{3} \times \dfrac{21}{2}$ cm2
Step 9 $= \dfrac{441\sqrt{3}}{4}$ cm2 (3)
Step 10 area of the segment AYB $= \left(462 - \dfrac{441\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$ cm2 from (1), (2) and (3)
Answer $\dfrac{21}{4}(88 - 21\sqrt{3})$ cm2
