Problem 2

A chord of a circle of radius 14 cm subtends $60^\circ$ at the centre. The area of the minor segment is (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$ and ${\sqrt{3} = 1.73}$.)

  1. 17.9 cm2
  2. 102.7 cm2
  3. 84.8 cm2
  4. 187.4 cm2
Solution

sector ${= \frac{1}{6} \times 616 = 102.67}$ cm2; the triangle is equilateral, of area ${\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \times 196 = 84.77}$ cm2

segment ${= 102.67 - 84.77 = 17.90}$ cm2

The answer is (a). Option (b) is the sector, (c) is the triangle, and (d) adds them.

Problem 3

A sector of a circle of radius 21 cm has an arc 22 cm long. Its area is (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

  1. 462 cm2
  2. 231 cm2
  3. 1386 cm2
  4. 60 cm2
Solution

area of the sector = half the arc times the radius ${= \frac{1}{2} \times 22 \times 21 = 231}$ cm2

The answer is (b). Option (a) forgets the half, (c) is the whole circle, and (d) is the angle, $60^\circ$, written as an area.

Problem 4

A goat is tied with a rope 7 m long to one corner of a rectangular field 20 m by 15 m. The area of the field that the goat cannot graze is (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

  1. 38.5 m2
  2. 261.5 m2
  3. 146 m2
  4. 154 m2
Solution

the goat grazes a quadrant: ${\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 49 = 38.5}$ m2

the rest of the field ${= 20 \times 15 - 38.5 = 261.5}$ m2

The answer is (b). Option (a) is the part it can graze, (d) is a whole circle, and (c) subtracts that whole circle.
