Example 3 · Single correct

The minute hand of a clock is 14 cm long. What area does it sweep in 15 minutes? Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Choose one correct option.

  1. $308 \text{ cm}^2$
  2. $616 \text{ cm}^2$
  3. $154 \text{ cm}^2$
  4. $77 \text{ cm}^2$

Solution.

Step 1In 15 minutes the hand turns through a quarter turn, $90^\circ$.
Step 2Area $= \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 196 = 154 \text{ cm}^2$.
Answer(c) $154 \text{ cm}^2$
Example 4 · Single correct

A chord of a circle of radius 10 cm subtends a right angle at the centre. What is the area of the minor segment? Take $\pi = 3.14$.

Choose one correct option.

  1. $78.5 \text{ cm}^2$
  2. $50 \text{ cm}^2$
  3. $128.5 \text{ cm}^2$
  4. $28.5 \text{ cm}^2$

Solution.

Step 1The sector is $\frac{1}{4} \times 3.14 \times 100 = 78.5$, and the triangle is $\frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 10 = 50$.
Step 2Segment $= 78.5 - 50 = 28.5 \text{ cm}^2$.
Answer(d) $28.5 \text{ cm}^2$
Example 5 · Single correct

What is the perimeter of a sector of radius 7 cm and angle $90^\circ$? Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Choose one correct option.

  1. 25 cm
  2. 11 cm
  3. 18 cm
  4. 32 cm

Solution.

Step 1The arc is $\frac{1}{4} \times 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7 = 11$ cm.
Step 2The perimeter adds the two radii: $11 + 7 + 7 = 25$ cm.
Answer(a) 25 cm
