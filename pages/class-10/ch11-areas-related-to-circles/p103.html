Example 3

The hour hand of a clock is 6 cm long. The area it sweeps from 12 o'clock to 4 o'clock is

  1. $4\pi$ cm2
  2. $24\pi$ cm2
  3. $12\pi$ cm2
  4. $36\pi$ cm2

Solution.

Step 1 in 4 hours the hand turns ${4 \times 30^\circ = 120^\circ}$ $360^\circ$ in 12 hours
Step 2 area ${= \frac{120}{360} \times \pi \times 36 = 12\pi}$ cm2
Answer (c)

Option (a) takes 4 hours as $40^\circ$, (b) uses $2\pi r^2$ in place of $\pi r^2$, and (d) is the whole circle.

Example 4

A sector of a circle of radius 35 cm has an arc 22 cm long. Its area is (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

  1. 770 cm2
  2. 385 cm2
  3. 3850 cm2
  4. 192.5 cm2

Solution.

Step 1 area of a sector $=$ half the arc times the radius from Stage 1
Step 2 area ${= \frac{1}{2} \times 22 \times 35 = 385}$ cm2
Step 3 the circumference is ${2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 35 = 220}$, so the sector is $\frac{1}{10}$ of the circle, and ${\frac{1}{10} \times 3850 = 385}$ check
Answer (b)

Option (a) leaves out the half, (c) is the whole circle, and (d) takes the half twice.

Example 5

An arc 22 cm long subtends an angle of $140^\circ$ at the centre of a circle. Find the radius of the circle and the area of the sector. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{140}{360} \times 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times r = 22$ length of the arc
Step 2 $\dfrac{22}{9} r = 22$, so $r = 9$ cm $\frac{7}{18} \times \frac{44}{7} = \frac{22}{9}$
Step 3 area $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 22 \times 9 = 99$ cm2 half the arc times the radius
Step 4 $\dfrac{140}{360} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 9 \times 9 = 99$ check
Answer radius 9 cm, area 99 cm2
