area of the segment APB $=$ area of the sector OAPB $-$ area of $\triangle OAB$

$\phantom{\text{area of the segment APB}} = \dfrac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2 -$ area of $\triangle OAB$

Note. From Fig. 11.3 and Fig. 11.4, you can also see that

area of the major sector OAQB $= \pi r^2 -$ area of the minor sector OAPB

area of the major segment AQB $= \pi r^2 -$ area of the minor segment APB

Example 1

Find the area of the sector of a circle of radius 4 cm with an angle of $30^\circ$. Also find the area of the corresponding major sector. (Use ${\pi = 3.14}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 sector OAPB $= \dfrac{30}{360} \times 3.14 \times 4 \times 4$ cm2 $= \dfrac{12.56}{3}$ cm2 $\approx 4.19$ cm2
Step 2 major sector $= \pi r^2 -$ OAPB $= (3.14 \times 16 - 4.19)$ cm2 $= 46.05$ cm2
Step 3 $\dfrac{330}{360} \times 3.14 \times 16$ cm2 $= 46.05$ cm2, or 46.1 cm2 check: ${360^\circ - 30^\circ = 330^\circ}$
Answer 4.19 cm2; major sector 46.05 cm2, about 46.1 cm2
