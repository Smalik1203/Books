Example 9 · Multiple correct

A circle has radius 21 cm. Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its circumference is 132 cm.
  2. Its area is $1386 \text{ cm}^2$.
  3. A $120^\circ$ sector has area $462 \text{ cm}^2$.
  4. A $120^\circ$ arc is 66 cm long.

Solution.

Step 1$2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 21 = 132$ and $\frac{22}{7} \times 441 = 1386$; a third of these is 44 cm of arc and $462 \text{ cm}^2$.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

The minute hand of a clock is 21 cm long. Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$. In 20 minutes, which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. It turns through $120^\circ$.
  2. It sweeps $462 \text{ cm}^2$.
  3. Its tip moves 22 cm.
  4. It sweeps $231 \text{ cm}^2$.

Solution.

Step 120 minutes is $120^\circ$, a third of a turn: a third of 1386 is 462, and a third of 132 is 44 cm.
Answer(a), (b)
