Example 8

A chord AB of a circle with centre O and radius $r$ subtends $60^\circ$ at O. Show that the area of the minor segment is $r^2\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$.

Solution.

Step 1 area of the sector OAB $= \dfrac{60}{360} \times \pi r^2 = \dfrac{\pi r^2}{6}$ area of a sector
Step 2 ${OA = OB = r}$, so $\angle OAB = \angle OBA$ angles opposite equal sides
Step 3 $\angle OAB + \angle OBA = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ$, so each is $60^\circ$ angle sum of a triangle
Step 4 $\triangle OAB$ is equilateral with side $r$, so its area is $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} r^2$ all three angles are $60^\circ$
Step 5 segment $= \dfrac{\pi r^2}{6} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} r^2 = r^2\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$ sector less triangle
Answer the area of the minor segment is $r^2\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$, as was to be shown

Example 9

A goat is tied with a rope 7 m long to one corner of a rectangular field 20 m by 15 m. The area of the field that the goat cannot graze is (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

  1. 38.5 m2
  2. 261.5 m2
  3. 146 m2
  4. 154 m2

Solution.

Step 1 grazed ${= \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 49 = 38.5}$ m2 a quadrant: the corner is a right angle
Step 2 the rest ${= 20 \times 15 - 38.5 = 261.5}$ m2
Answer (b)

Option (a) is the part it can graze, (d) is a whole circle, and (c) subtracts that whole circle.
