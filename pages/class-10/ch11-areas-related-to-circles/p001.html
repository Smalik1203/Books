Areas Related to Circles

11.1Areas of Sector and Segment of a Circle

You have met the words sector and segment of a circle in earlier classes. Recall that a sector of a circle is the part of the circular region enclosed by two radii and the arc between them. A segment of a circle is the part of the circular region enclosed between a chord and its arc.

In Fig. 11.1, the shaded region OAPB is a sector of the circle with centre O. $\angle AOB$ is called the angle of the sector. The unshaded region OAQB is also a sector of the circle. OAPB is the smaller of the two, so it is called the minor sector, and OAQB is called the major sector. The angle of the major sector is ${360^\circ - \angle AOB}$.

Now look at Fig. 11.2, in which AB is a chord of the circle with centre O. The shaded region APB is a segment of the circle. The unshaded region AQB is another segment, formed by the same chord AB. APB is called the minor segment, and AQB the major segment.

Remark. From now on, "sector" and "segment" mean the minor sector and the minor segment, unless something else is said.
