1. Assertion (A): In a circle of radius 28 cm, a chord that subtends a right angle at the centre cuts off a minor segment of area 224 cm2. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

    Reason (R): The area of a segment is the area of its sector plus the area of its triangle.

  1. Assertion (A): The perimeter of a semicircular region of radius 3.5 cm is 11 cm. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

    Reason (R): The arc of a semicircle of radius $r$ has length $\pi r$.

Very short answer
  1. The area of a sector is one ninth of the area of its circle. Find the angle of the sector.
  1. Find, in terms of $\pi$, the area of a sector of angle $36^\circ$ in a circle of radius 5 cm.
  1. Find, in terms of $\pi$, the length of an arc that subtends $40^\circ$ at the centre of a circle of radius 9 cm.
Short answer
  1. A sector of angle $72^\circ$ has an area of 30.8 cm2. Find the radius of its circle and the length of its arc. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)
  1. A chord of a circle of radius 14 cm subtends an angle of $120^\circ$ at the centre. Find the area of the minor segment. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$ and ${\sqrt{3} = 1.73}$.)
  1. A pendulum 42 cm long swings through an angle of $60^\circ$. Find the length of the arc described by its tip, and the area it sweeps. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)
  1. The minute hand of a clock is 12 cm long. Find the area it sweeps between 9:00 a.m. and 9:25 a.m. (Use ${\pi = 3.14}$.)
Long answer
  1. A chord of a circle of radius $r$ subtends a right angle at the centre. Show that the area of the minor segment is $\frac{r^2}{4}(\pi - 2)$. Hence find this area when $r = 14$ cm. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)
  1. A chord AB of a circle of radius 35 cm subtends $60^\circ$ at the centre. Find the length of the minor arc AB, the perimeter of the minor segment, and the area of the major segment. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$ and ${\sqrt{3} = 1.73}$.)
  1. Two circles have the same centre and radii 21 cm and 14 cm. A piece of the ring between them is cut off by two radii of the larger circle at right angles. Find the area and the perimeter of the piece. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)
