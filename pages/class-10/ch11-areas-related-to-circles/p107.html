Why the other options are wrong

A2 ${\frac{45}{360} \times 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 14 = 11}$ cm: (b). Option (a) forgets that $45^\circ$ is one eighth of the circle, not one quarter.
A6 In 10 minutes the hand turns $60^\circ$, so the area is ${\frac{1}{6} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 441 = 231}$ cm2: (a). Option (b) takes $120^\circ$.
A7 The curved edge is ${\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 14 = 22}$ cm and the straight edge is 14 cm: (b). Option (a) leaves out the diameter.
B1 One twelfth of $360^\circ$ is $30^\circ$, so the arc is ${\frac{1}{12} \times 132 = 11}$ cm: (a). Option (b) takes $60^\circ$.
B2 The minor segment is ${38.5 - 24.5 = 14}$ cm2, so the major segment is ${154 - 14 = 140}$ cm2: (b). Option (a) is the major sector, and (d) subtracts the triangle from the circle.
B4 The arc is ${50 - 28 = 22}$ cm, so the area is ${\frac{1}{2} \times 22 \times 14 = 154}$ cm2: (c). Option (a) uses the whole perimeter as the arc.
B6 The three $60^\circ$ sectors make half a circle, 77 cm2, and the triangle is ${49 \times 1.73 = 84.77}$ cm2: (d). Option (a) is the triangle alone.
C1 The four quadrants inside the square make one circle, 38.5 cm2, so ${49 - 38.5 = 10.5}$ cm2: (b). Option (a) is the covered part.
C2 The circumference is 132 cm, and 44 is a third of it, so the angle is $120^\circ$: (c). Option (d) takes two thirds.
C3 The radius is 14 cm, the quadrant is 154 cm2 and the triangle is 98 cm2, so the segment is 56 cm2: (d).
C4 The hour hand goes round twice a day, so the tip travels ${2 \times 2\pi \times 6 = 24\pi}$ cm: (b). Option (a) counts one turn only.

Three things are worth keeping. A sector is the fraction $\frac{\theta}{360}$ of its circle, in area and in arc, so an area or an arc can be turned back into an angle. The area of a sector is half the arc times the radius. And when a shape is made of sectors, add their angles first: pieces that meet at one centre, or share one radius, often make up a whole circle.
