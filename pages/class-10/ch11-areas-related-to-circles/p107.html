Example 15 · Matching

A circle has radius 10 cm; take $\pi = 3.14$. Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Its area(1) 78.5
(Q) Its circumference(2) 28.5
(R) A quarter-circle's area(3) 314
(S) A quarter-circle segment's area(4) 62.8

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
  4. P–2, Q–4, R–1, S–3

Solution.

Step 1$3.14 \times 100 = 314$ and $2 \times 3.14 \times 10 = 62.8$.
Step 2A quarter of 314 is 78.5, and less the triangle of area 50 it is 28.5.
Answer(b) P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
