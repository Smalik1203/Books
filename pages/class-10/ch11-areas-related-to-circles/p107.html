Example 12

AB is a diameter of a circle with centre O and radius 7 cm. C is a point on the circle with ${AC = BC}$. Find the area of the part of the semicircle ACB that lies outside $\triangle ABC$. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 O is the mid-point of AB and ${AC = BC}$, so ${OC \perp AB}$ median of an isosceles triangle
Step 2 area of $\triangle ABC$ $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 14 \times 7 = 49$ cm2 base AB, height OC
Step 3 semicircle $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 7 = 77$ cm2
Step 4 the part outside $= 77 - 49 = 28$ cm2
Answer 28 cm2
