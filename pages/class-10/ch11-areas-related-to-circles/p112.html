Three things are worth keeping. A sector is the fraction $\frac{\theta}{360}$ of its circle, in area and in arc, so an area or an arc can be turned back into an angle. The area of a sector is half the arc times the radius. And a segment is a sector less a triangle, so work out the two parts separately: changing the angle changes them in different ways.
