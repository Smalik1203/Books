Example 12 · Numerical answer

Find the length, in cm, of an arc of radius 35 cm and angle $72^\circ$. Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Arc $= \frac{72}{360} \times 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 35 = \frac{1}{5} \times 220 = 44$.
Answer44
Example 13 · Numerical answer

A chord of a circle of radius 14 cm subtends a right angle at the centre. Find the area of the minor segment, in $\text{cm}^2$. Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Sector $= \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 196 = 154$, less the triangle $\frac{1}{2} \times 14 \times 14 = 98$: 56.
Answer56
Example 14 · Matching

A circle has radius 7 cm; take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$. Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) Area of a $90^\circ$ sector(1) 44
(Q) Area of a $180^\circ$ sector(2) 11
(R) Length of a $90^\circ$ arc(3) 38.5
(S) The circumference(4) 77

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  2. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1The whole circle has area $154$ and circumference $44$.
Step 2A quarter and a half of 154 are 38.5 and 77, and a quarter of 44 is 11.
