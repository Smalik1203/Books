Example 10

A flower bed is a sector of radius 10.5 m and angle $120^\circ$. Find the cost of fencing its whole boundary at ₹$25$ a metre, and of laying grass on it at ₹$12$ a square metre. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 arc $= \dfrac{120}{360} \times 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 10.5 = 22$ m
Step 2 boundary $= 22 + 10.5 + 10.5 = 43$ m the arc and two radii
Step 3 fencing: $43 \times 25 = 1075$, that is ₹$1075$
Step 4 area $= \dfrac{120}{360} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 10.5 \times 10.5 = 115.5$ m2
Step 5 grass: $115.5 \times 12 = 1386$, that is ₹$1386$
Answer fencing ₹$1075$, grass ₹$1386$

Example 11

A round mirror of radius 35 cm is trimmed along a straight line. The line is a chord that subtends a right angle at the centre. Find the area of glass that is trimmed away, and the area of the mirror that is left. (Use ${\pi = \frac{22}{7}}$.)

Solution.

Step 1 sector $= \dfrac{90}{360} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 35 \times 35 = 962.5$ cm2
Step 2 triangle $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 35 \times 35 = 612.5$ cm2 right angle at the centre
Step 3 trimmed piece $= 962.5 - 612.5 = 350$ cm2 the minor segment
Step 4 whole mirror $= \dfrac{22}{7} \times 35 \times 35 = 3850$ cm2
Step 5 left $= 3850 - 350 = 3500$ cm2 the major segment
Answer trimmed away 350 cm2, left 3500 cm2
