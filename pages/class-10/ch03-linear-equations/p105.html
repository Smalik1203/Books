Example 10 · Multiple correct

The digits of a two-digit number add to 9, and reversing them adds 27 to the number. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. Its tens digit is 3.
  2. The number is 36.
  3. The reversed number is 72.
  4. The number is 45.

Solution.

Step 1With digits $t$ and $u$: $t + u = 9$ and $(10u + t) - (10t + u) = 9(u - t) = 27$, so $u - t = 3$.
Step 2$t = 3$ and $u = 6$: the number is 36, and reversed it is 63.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

A father is three times as old as his son. In 10 years he will be twice as old as his son. How old is the father now?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1With the son $s$ years old: $3s + 10 = 2(s + 10)$.
Step 2$s = 10$, so the father is 30.
