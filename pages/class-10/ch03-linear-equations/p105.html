Type 3 · The substitution method

Example 6

Solve $3x - 5y = -8$ and $2x - y = 4$ by the substitution method.

Solution.

Step 1 $y = 2x - 4$ from the second
Step 2 $3x - 5(2x - 4) = -8$ putting this in the first
Step 3 $3x - 10x + 20 = -8$, so $-7x = -28$ and $x = 4$
Step 4 $y = 2 \times 4 - 4 = 4$
Answer $x = 4$, $y = 4$
Example 7

Solve $\dfrac{x}{3} + \dfrac{y}{4} = 2$ and $x - y = -1$ by the substitution method.

Solution.

Step 1 $4x + 3y = 24$ multiplying the first by 12
Step 2 $x = y - 1$ from the second
Step 3 $4(y - 1) + 3y = 24$, so $7y = 28$ and $y = 4$
Step 4 $x = 4 - 1 = 3$
Answer $x = 3$, $y = 4$

Type 4 · The elimination method

Example 8

The solution of $2x + 3y = 12$ and $3x - 2y = 5$ is

  1. $x = 2$, $y = 3$
  2. $x = 3$, $y = -2$
  3. $x = 3$, $y = 2$
  4. $x = -3$, $y = 2$

Solution.

Step 1 $4x + 6y = 24$ and $9x - 6y = 15$ multiplying by 2 and by 3
Step 2 $13x = 39$, so $x = 3$ adding
Step 3 $6 + 3y = 12$, so $y = 2$ putting $x = 3$ in the first
Answer (c)

Options (a), (b) and (d) fit neither equation: (a) swaps $x$ and $y$, and (b) and (d) each change a sign.
