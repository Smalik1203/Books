Example 3

Champa went to a sale to buy some pants and skirts. When her friends asked how many of each she had bought, she said, “The number of skirts is two less than twice the number of pants. Also, the number of skirts is four less than four times the number of pants.” Help her friends find how many pants and skirts Champa bought.

Solution.

Step 1 let the number of pants be $x$ and the number of skirts be $y$
Step 2 $y = 2x - 2$   (1)   and   $y = 4x - 4$   (2) her two statements
Step 3 find two solutions of each equation, as in Table 3.3, then plot the points and draw the lines through them, as in Fig. 3.2
Step 4 the two lines intersect at the point $(1, 0)$ the common point
Answer $x = 1$, $y = 0$: Champa bought 1 pair of pants and no skirts
