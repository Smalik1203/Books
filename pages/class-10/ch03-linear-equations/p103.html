Problem 2

The solution of $2x + 3y = 12$ and $3x - 2y = 5$ is

  1. $x = 2$, $y = 3$
  2. $x = 3$, $y = -2$
  3. $x = 3$, $y = 2$
  4. $x = -3$, $y = 2$
Solution

$4x + 6y = 24$ and $9x - 6y = 15$   (multiplying by 2 and by 3)

$13x = 39$, so $x = 3$   (adding), and then $6 + 3y = 12$ gives $y = 2$

The answer is (c). Option (a) swaps $x$ and $y$, which fits the first equation but not the second. Options (b) and (d) change a sign, and fit neither.

Problem 3

2 kg of apples and 1 kg of grapes cost ₹160. 4 kg of apples and 2 kg of grapes cost ₹300. Then

  1. apples cost ₹70 a kg and grapes ₹20 a kg
  2. apples cost ₹75 a kg and grapes ₹10 a kg
  3. many different prices fit both statements
  4. no prices fit both statements
Solution

Let apples cost ₹$x$ a kg and grapes ₹$y$ a kg. Then $2x + y = 160$ and $4x + 2y = 300$. Here $\frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$ but $\frac{160}{300} \neq \frac{1}{2}$, so the lines are parallel and no prices fit: (d). Doubling the first statement says 4 kg and 2 kg cost ₹320, not ₹300. Options (a) and (b) fit the first statement only, and (c) would need the costs to be ₹320.

Problem 4

The lines $3x + y = 9$ and $x - y = -1$ form a triangle with the $y$-axis. Its vertices are

  1. $(3, 0)$, $(-1, 0)$ and $(2, 3)$
  2. $(0, 9)$, $(0, 1)$ and $(2, 3)$
  3. $(0, 9)$, $(0, -1)$ and $(2, 3)$
  4. $(9, 0)$, $(1, 0)$ and $(2, 3)$
