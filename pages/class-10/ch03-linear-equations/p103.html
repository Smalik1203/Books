Example 3 · Single correct

What are the lines $2x + 3y = 7$ and $4x + 6y = 14$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. intersecting
  2. parallel
  3. coincident
  4. perpendicular

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{2}{4} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{7}{14}$: all three ratios are equal.
Step 2The second equation is twice the first, so they are the same line.
Answer(c) coincident
Example 4 · Single correct

The sum of two numbers is 50 and their difference is 12. What is the larger number?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 19
  2. 25
  3. 38
  4. 31

Solution.

Step 1$x + y = 50$ and $x - y = 12$; adding, $2x = 62$.
Step 2$x = 31$ and $y = 19$.
Answer(d) 31
Example 5 · Single correct

5 pens and 3 notebooks cost ₹120, and 3 pens and 5 notebooks cost ₹136. What does one notebook cost?

Choose one correct option.

  1. ₹20
  2. ₹12
  3. ₹32
  4. ₹24

Solution.

Step 1Adding the equations: $8(p + n) = 256$, so $p + n = 32$. Subtracting: $2(p - n) = -16$, so $p - n = -8$.
Step 2So $n = 20$ and $p = 12$. Check: $5 \times 12 + 3 \times 20 = 120$.
Answer(a) ₹20
