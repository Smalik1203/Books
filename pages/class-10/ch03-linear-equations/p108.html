Type 6 · Reading a pair from data

Example 14

A mobile plan charges a fixed amount each month and a fixed rate for each GB of data used. Two of Asha’s bills are shown.

MonthData usedBill
March6 GB₹390
April10 GB₹510
  1. Find the fixed charge and the rate for each GB.
  2. What is the bill for a month in which she uses 15 GB?
  3. In May her bill was ₹600. How much data did she use?

Solution.

Step 1 fixed charge ₹$f$, rate ₹$r$ a GB: $f + 6r = 390$ and $f + 10r = 510$
Step 2 $4r = 120$, so $r = 30$ and $f = 390 - 180 = 210$ subtracting
Step 3 $210 + 15 \times 30 = 660$ part (b)
Step 4 $210 + 30n = 600$ for $n$ GB, so $30n = 390$ and $n = 13$ part (c)
Answer (a) ₹210, and ₹30 a GB   (b) ₹660   (c) 13 GB
