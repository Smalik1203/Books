Example 4

For what values of $a$ and $b$ does the pair $3x + 4y = 12$ and $(a + b)x + 2(a - b)y = 5a - 1$ have infinitely many solutions?

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{3}{a + b} = \dfrac{4}{2(a - b)} = \dfrac{12}{5a - 1}$ all three ratios equal
Step 2 $6(a - b) = 4(a + b)$, so $2a = 10b$, that is, $a = 5b$ the first two ratios
Step 3 $3(5a - 1) = 12(a + b)$, so $3a - 12b = 3$, that is, $a - 4b = 1$ the first and third
Step 4 $5b - 4b = 1$, so $b = 1$ and $a = 5$ putting $a = 5b$
Step 5 $a + b = 6$, $2(a - b) = 8$, $5a - 1 = 24$, and $\dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{4}{8} = \dfrac{12}{24}$ check
Answer $a = 5$, $b = 1$
Example 5

2 kg of apples and 1 kg of grapes cost ₹160. 4 kg of apples and 2 kg of grapes cost ₹300. Then

  1. apples cost ₹70 a kg and grapes ₹20 a kg
  2. apples cost ₹75 a kg and grapes ₹10 a kg
  3. many different prices fit both statements
  4. no prices fit both statements

Solution.

Step 1 let apples cost ₹$x$ a kg and grapes ₹$y$ a kg: $2x + y = 160$ and $4x + 2y = 300$
Step 2 $\dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ but $\dfrac{160}{300} \neq \dfrac{1}{2}$ compare the ratios
Step 3 the lines are parallel, so no prices fit
Answer (d)

Doubling the first statement says that 4 kg of apples and 2 kg of grapes cost ₹320, not ₹300. Options (a) and (b) fit the first statement only, and (c) would need the second cost to be ₹320.
