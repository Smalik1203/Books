Solution

Adding the equations gives $4x = 8$, so the lines meet at $(2, 3)$. On the $y$-axis $x = 0$, so the first line meets it at $(0, 9)$ and the second at $(0, 1)$. The answer is (b). Option (a) uses the points on the $x$-axis, (c) gets a sign wrong, and (d) swaps the coordinates.

Problem 5

A fraction becomes $\frac{1}{3}$ when 1 is subtracted from its numerator, and $\frac{1}{4}$ when 8 is added to its denominator. The fraction is

  1. $\dfrac{4}{12}$
  2. $\dfrac{12}{5}$
  3. $\dfrac{5}{20}$
  4. $\dfrac{5}{12}$
Solution

Let the fraction be $\frac{x}{y}$. Then $3(x - 1) = y$, that is, $3x - y = 3$, and $4x = y + 8$, that is, $4x - y = 8$. Subtracting the first from the second gives $x = 5$, and then $y = 12$. The answer is (d). Options (a) and (c) are the fraction after each change, and (b) turns the answer upside down.
