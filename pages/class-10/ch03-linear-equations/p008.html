Example 7

Two rails lie along the lines $x + 2y - 4 = 0$ and $2x + 4y - 12 = 0$. Will they cross each other?

Solution.

Step 1 $x = 4 - 2y$ from the first equation
Step 2 $2(4 - 2y) + 4y - 12 = 0$ putting this in the second
Step 3 $8 - 12 = 0$, that is, $-4 = 0$ a false statement
Answer the two equations have no common solution, so the rails will not cross each other
