Example 12 · Numerical answer

Adult tickets cost ₹40 and child tickets ₹25. 50 tickets bring in ₹1550. How many adult tickets were sold?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$a + c = 50$ and $40a + 25c = 1550$.
Step 2$40a + 25(50 - a) = 1550$ gives $15a = 300$, so $a = 20$.
Answer20
Example 13 · Numerical answer

For what value of $k$ do $2x + ky = 5$ and $4x + 6y = 10$ have infinitely many solutions?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Infinitely many solutions needs $\frac{2}{4} = \frac{k}{6} = \frac{5}{10}$.
Step 2$\frac{k}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$ gives $k = 3$.
Answer3
