Example 9

Solve $47x + 31y = 63$ and $31x + 47y = 15$.

Solution.

Step 1 $78x + 78y = 78$, so $x + y = 1$ adding the equations
Step 2 $16x - 16y = 48$, so $x - y = 3$ subtracting the second
Step 3 $2x = 4$, so $x = 2$, and then $y = -1$ adding these two
Answer $x = 2$, $y = -1$

The coefficients swap places, so adding and subtracting give two short equations at once.

Example 10

A fraction becomes $\frac{1}{3}$ when 1 is subtracted from its numerator, and $\frac{1}{4}$ when 8 is added to its denominator. The fraction is

  1. $\dfrac{4}{12}$
  2. $\dfrac{12}{5}$
  3. $\dfrac{5}{20}$
  4. $\dfrac{5}{12}$

Solution.

Step 1 let the fraction be $\dfrac{x}{y}$: $3(x - 1) = y$ and $4x = y + 8$ cross-multiplying
Step 2 $3x - y = 3$ and $4x - y = 8$
Step 3 $x = 5$, and then $y = 3 \times 5 - 3 = 12$ subtracting the first
Answer (d)

Options (a) and (c) are the fraction after each change, and (b) turns the answer upside down.

Example 11

4 notebooks and 3 pens cost ₹255, and 3 notebooks and 5 pens cost ₹260. Find the cost of one notebook and of one pen.

Solution.

Step 1 let a notebook cost ₹$x$ and a pen ₹$y$: $4x + 3y = 255$ and $3x + 5y = 260$
Step 2 $12x + 9y = 765$ and $12x + 20y = 1040$ multiplying by 3 and by 4
Step 3 $11y = 275$, so $y = 25$ subtracting the first
Step 4 $4x = 255 - 75 = 180$, so $x = 45$ in the first equation
Answer a notebook costs ₹45 and a pen ₹25
