Adding the equations gives $2x = 6$, so the lines meet at $(3, 2)$. The first line meets the $x$-axis where $y = 0$, at $(5, 0)$, and the second at $(1, 0)$. So the triangle has a base of $5 - 1 = 4$ units along the $x$-axis and a height of 2 units, the $y$-coordinate of the top vertex. Its area is $\frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 2 = 4$ square units.

The sum of the ages of a mother and her daughter is 50 years. In 5 years, the mother will be three times as old as her daughter was 5 years ago. How old are they now?

Let the mother be $m$ years old and the daughter $d$ years old. Then $m + d = 50$, and $m + 5 = 3(d - 5)$, that is, $m - 3d = -20$. Subtracting the second equation from the first gives $4d = 70$, so $d = 17.5$ and $m = 32.5$. The answer is correct, but it is worth noticing that it is not a whole number of years: a pair of equations can have a solution that does not fit the everyday meaning we expected.
