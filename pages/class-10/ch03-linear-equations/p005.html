The two lines intersect at the point $(1, 0)$. So $x = 1$, $y = 0$ is the solution of the pair of linear equations: Champa bought 1 pair of pants and no skirts.

Exercise Set 3.1
  1. Form a pair of linear equations for each problem, and find the solution graphically.
    1. 10 students of Class X took part in a Mathematics quiz. The number of girls is 4 more than the number of boys. Find the number of boys and the number of girls who took part in the quiz.
    2. 5 pencils and 7 pens together cost ₹50, and 7 pencils and 5 pens together cost ₹46. Find the cost of one pencil and the cost of one pen.
  1. Using the ratios, do the lines of each pair intersect, lie parallel or coincide?
    1. $5x - 4y + 8 = 0$
      $7x + 6y - 9 = 0$
    2. $9x + 3y + 12 = 0$
      $18x + 6y + 24 = 0$
    3. $6x - 3y + 10 = 0$
      $2x - y + 9 = 0$
  1. Using the ratios, is each pair of linear equations consistent or inconsistent?
    1. $3x + 2y = 5$;   $2x - 3y = 7$
    2. $2x - 3y = 8$;   $4x - 6y = 9$
    3. $\dfrac{3}{2}x + \dfrac{5}{3}y = 7$;   $9x - 10y = 14$
    4. $5x - 3y = 11$;   $-10x + 6y = -22$
    5. $\dfrac{4}{3}x + 2y = 8$;   $2x + 3y = 12$
