Exercise Set 3.1
  1. Form a pair of linear equations for each problem, and find the solution graphically.
    1. 10 students of Class X took part in a Mathematics quiz. The number of girls is 4 more than the number of boys. Find the number of boys and the number of girls who took part in the quiz.
    2. 5 pencils and 7 pens together cost ₹50, and 7 pencils and 5 pens together cost ₹46. Find the cost of one pencil and the cost of one pen.
  1. Using the ratios, do the lines of each pair intersect, lie parallel or coincide?
    1. $5x - 4y + 8 = 0$
      $7x + 6y - 9 = 0$
    2. $9x + 3y + 12 = 0$
      $18x + 6y + 24 = 0$
    3. $6x - 3y + 10 = 0$
      $2x - y + 9 = 0$
  1. Using the ratios, is each pair of linear equations consistent or inconsistent?
    1. $3x + 2y = 5$;   $2x - 3y = 7$
    2. $2x - 3y = 8$;   $4x - 6y = 9$
    3. $\dfrac{3}{2}x + \dfrac{5}{3}y = 7$;   $9x - 10y = 14$
    4. $5x - 3y = 11$;   $-10x + 6y = -22$
    5. $\dfrac{4}{3}x + 2y = 8$;   $2x + 3y = 12$
  1. Which of these pairs of linear equations are consistent, and which are inconsistent? If a pair is consistent, find the solution graphically.
    1. $x + y = 5$;   $2x + 2y = 10$
    2. $x - y = 8$;   $3x - 3y = 16$
    3. $2x + y - 6 = 0$;   $4x - 2y - 4 = 0$
    4. $2x - 2y - 2 = 0$;   $4x - 4y - 5 = 0$
  1. Half the perimeter of a rectangular garden is 36 m, and its length is 4 m more than its width. Find the length and the width of the garden.
  1. Take the linear equation $2x + 3y - 8 = 0$. Write another linear equation in two variables so that the graph of the pair you make is
    1. intersecting lines
    2. parallel lines
    3. coincident lines
  1. Draw the graphs of the equations $x - y + 1 = 0$ and $3x + 2y - 12 = 0$. Find the coordinates of the vertices of the triangle formed by these two lines and the $x$-axis, and shade the triangle.
