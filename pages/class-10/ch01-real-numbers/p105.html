Type 5 · Showing that a number is composite

Example 9

Explain why $11 \times 13 \times 17 + 17$ is a composite number.

Solution.

Step 1 $11 \times 13 \times 17 + 17 = 17 \times (11 \times 13 + 1)$ 17 is common
Step 2 $17 \times (11 \times 13 + 1) = 17 \times 144$
Answer It has the factor 17, which is neither 1 nor the number itself, so it is composite

Type 6 · Proving that a number is irrational

Example 10

Prove that $\sqrt{7}$ is irrational.

Solution.

Step 1 $\sqrt{7} = \frac{a}{b}$, for coprime integers $a$ and $b$ with $b \neq 0$ assume it is rational
Step 2 $b\sqrt{7} = a$, so $7b^2 = a^2$ squaring both sides
Step 3 7 divides $a^2$, so 7 divides $a$ Theorem 1.2, 7 is prime
Step 4 $a = 7c$, so $7b^2 = 49c^2$, that is, $b^2 = 7c^2$
Step 5 7 divides $b^2$, so 7 divides $b$ Theorem 1.2 again
Step 6 7 is a common factor of $a$ and $b$ contradicts Step 1
Answer The assumption is false, so $\sqrt{7}$ is irrational
