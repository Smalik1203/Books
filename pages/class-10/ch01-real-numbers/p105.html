Example 9 · Multiple correct

Which statements are true for every pair of positive integers $a$ and $b$?

Select all correct options.

  1. HCF $\times$ LCM $= ab$
  2. The HCF divides the LCM.
  3. The LCM of $a$ and $b$ equals $ab$.
  4. $\text{HCF}(a, b) = \text{HCF}(a, b - a)$ when $b \gt a$.

Solution.

Step 1(a) and (b) hold for every pair, and any common factor of $a$ and $b$ also divides $b - a$, which gives (d).
Step 2For 4 and 6 the LCM is 12, not 24.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Which statements about $6^n$, for natural numbers $n$, are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $6^n$ never ends in the digit 0.
  2. $6^n$ always ends in the digit 6.
  3. $6^n$ is divisible by 5 for some $n$.
  4. $6^n + 1$ is always even.

Solution.

Step 1$6^n = 2^n \times 3^n$, with no factor 5, so it never ends in 0 and is never divisible by 5.
Step 2$6 \times 6$ ends in 6 again, so every power does; then $6^n + 1$ ends in 7 and is odd.
Answer(a), (b)
