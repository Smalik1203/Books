Problem 5

Copy the proof that $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational, with 4 in place of 2. Which step fails?

  1. writing $\sqrt{4} = \frac{a}{b}$, with $a$ and $b$ coprime
  2. squaring to get $4b^2 = a^2$
  3. saying that 4 divides $a$, because 4 divides $a^2$
  4. saying that $a$ and $b$ have a common factor
Solution

Theorem 1.2 is about a prime, and 4 is not a prime. The step fails: 4 divides $6^2 = 36$, but 4 does not divide 6. So the answer is (c). Steps (a) and (b) are correct, since $\sqrt{4} = \frac{2}{1}$ and squaring is always allowed, and (d) only follows from (c).
