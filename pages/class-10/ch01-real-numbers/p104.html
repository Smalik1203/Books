Example 6

The HCF of two numbers is 14 and their LCM is 1260. One of the numbers is 126. Find the other.

Solution.

Step 1 $126 \times \text{other} = 14 \times 1260$ HCF × LCM is the product
Step 2 $\text{other} = \dfrac{14 \times 1260}{126} = 140$
Step 3 $126 = 2 \times 3^2 \times 7$ and $140 = 2^2 \times 5 \times 7$ give HCF 14 and LCM 1260 check
Answer 140

Example 7

Two ropes, 945 cm and 1155 cm long, are to be cut into pieces of equal length, as long as possible, with nothing left over. How long is each piece, and how many pieces are there?

Solution.

Step 1 $945 = 3^3 \times 5 \times 7$ and $1155 = 3 \times 5 \times 7 \times 11$
Step 2 $\text{HCF} = 3 \times 5 \times 7 = 105$ the longest length that fits both
Step 3 $945 \div 105 = 9$ and $1155 \div 105 = 11$
Answer Each piece is 105 cm; there are $9 + 11 = 20$ pieces
Example 8

Two runners go round a track in 84 seconds and 108 seconds. They start together from the same point, running the same way. After how long are they next together at the start?

Solution.

Step 1 $84 = 2^2 \times 3 \times 7$ and $108 = 2^2 \times 3^3$
Step 2 $\text{LCM} = 2^2 \times 3^3 \times 7 = 756$ the first time common to both
Answer After 756 seconds, which is 12 minutes 36 seconds
