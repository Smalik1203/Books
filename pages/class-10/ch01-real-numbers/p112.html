Three things from this section are worth keeping. The HCF of two numbers divides their LCM, and their product is $\text{HCF} \times \text{LCM}$, so one fact can be found or ruled out from the others. A question about remainders becomes an HCF or LCM question once the remainders are taken away, or added back. And to show that a number is irrational, assume it is rational and rearrange until a number known to be irrational equals a rational one.
