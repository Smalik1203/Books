Example 3 · Single correct

Two numbers have HCF 9 and LCM 459. One of them is 27. What is the other?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 51
  2. 81
  3. 153
  4. 459

Solution.

Step 1HCF $\times$ LCM equals the product of the two numbers: $9 \times 459 = 4131$.
Step 2The other number is $4131 \div 27 = 153$. Check: $153 = 9 \times 17$ and $27 = 9 \times 3$.
Answer(c) 153
Example 4 · Single correct

Which of these numbers is irrational?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $\sqrt{49}$
  2. $0.\overline{36}$
  3. $\frac{22}{7}$
  4. $3 + \sqrt{5}$

Solution.

Step 1$\sqrt{49} = 7$, and $0.\overline{36} = \frac{36}{99}$ and $\frac{22}{7}$ are quotients of integers.
Step 2If $3 + \sqrt{5}$ were rational, so would be $\sqrt{5}$, which is irrational.
Answer(d) $3 + \sqrt{5}$
Example 5 · Single correct

What is the smallest number divisible exactly by each of 8, 12 and 18?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 72
  2. 144
  3. 36
  4. 216

Solution.

Step 1$8 = 2^3$, $12 = 2^2 \times 3$ and $18 = 2 \times 3^2$.
Step 2The LCM is $2^3 \times 3^2 = 72$.
Answer(a) 72
