Which of these numbers is irrational?

  1. $\sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{27}$
  2. $(2 - \sqrt{3})^2$
  3. $\dfrac{\sqrt{12}}{\sqrt{3}}$
  4. $\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{18}$

Solution.

Step 1 (a) $\sqrt{81} = 9$   (c) $\sqrt{4} = 2$   (d) $\sqrt{36} = 6$ integers
Step 2 (b) $(2 - \sqrt{3})^2 = 4 - 4\sqrt{3} + 3 = 7 - 4\sqrt{3}$
Step 3 $4\sqrt{3}$ is irrational, so $7 - 4\sqrt{3}$ is irrational rational minus irrational
Answer (b)
