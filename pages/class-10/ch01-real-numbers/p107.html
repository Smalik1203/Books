Example 15 · Matching

Match each fraction with the number of decimal places after which its expansion ends. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $\frac{13}{3125}$(1) 4
(Q) $\frac{17}{8}$(2) 2
(R) $\frac{7}{25}$(3) 5
(S) $\frac{3}{16}$(4) 3

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–3, R–2, S–1
  2. P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
  3. P–3, Q–2, R–4, S–1
  4. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3

Solution.

Step 1Write each denominator as a power of 10: $\frac{13}{5^5} = \frac{416}{10^5}$ and $\frac{17}{2^3} = \frac{2125}{10^3}$.
Step 2$\frac{7}{5^2} = \frac{28}{10^2}$ and $\frac{3}{2^4} = \frac{1875}{10^4}$; the power is the number of decimal places.
Answer(b) P–3, Q–4, R–2, S–1
