Case-based questions

  1. Three traffic signals on one road change at fixed intervals. All three change together at 7:00 a.m.

    SignalChanges every
    A48 seconds
    B72 seconds
    C108 seconds
    1. Write 48, 72 and 108 as products of primes.
    2. After how many seconds do all three next change together?
    3. At what time is that?
    4. How many times after 7:00 a.m. do all three change together before 8:00 a.m.?

  1. A school puts the students of three sections into groups for a quiz. Every group has the same number of students, a group holds students of one section only, and the groups are as large as possible.

    SectionStudents
    A60
    B84
    C108
    1. Write 60, 84 and 108 as products of primes.
    2. How many students are there in each group?
    3. How many groups are there in all?
