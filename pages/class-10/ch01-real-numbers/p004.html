HCF and LCM from prime factorisations

The HCF of two or more numbers is the product of the smallest power of each prime factor that is common to all the numbers.

The LCM is the product of the greatest power of each prime factor that occurs in any of the numbers.

From Example 2, you can see that $\text{HCF}(6, 20) \times \text{LCM}(6, 20) = 2 \times 60 = 120$, which is equal to $6 \times 20$. In fact, for any two positive integers $a$ and $b$,

$$\text{HCF}(a, b) \times \text{LCM}(a, b) = a \times b$$

So once we have found the HCF of two positive integers, we can use this result to find their LCM.

Example 3

Find the HCF of 96 and 404 by the prime factorisation method. Then find their LCM.

Solution.

Step 1 $96 = 2^5 \times 3$ and $404 = 2^2 \times 101$
Step 2 the only common prime factor is 2; its smallest power is $2^2$
Step 3 HCF of 96 and 404 $= 2^2 = 4$
Step 4 $\text{LCM}(96, 404) = \dfrac{96 \times 404}{\text{HCF}(96, 404)} = \dfrac{96 \times 404}{4} = 9696$ HCF × LCM is the product
Answer HCF 4, LCM 9696
Example 4

Find the HCF and LCM of 6, 72 and 120, using the prime factorisation method.

Solution.

Step 1 $6 = 2 \times 3$, $72 = 2^3 \times 3^2$ and $120 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 5$
Step 2 common primes 2 and 3, smallest powers $2^1$ and $3^1$
Step 3 primes 2, 3 and 5, greatest powers $2^3$, $3^2$ and $5^1$
Step 4 $\text{HCF}(6, 72, 120) = 2^1 \times 3^1 = 2 \times 3 = 6$
Step 5 $\text{LCM}(6, 72, 120) = 2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5^1 = 360$
Answer HCF 6, LCM 360
