Example 12 · Numerical answer

Three bells ring every 18, 24 and 32 minutes. They ring together at 8 a.m. After how many minutes do they next ring together?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$18 = 2 \times 3^2$, $24 = 2^3 \times 3$ and $32 = 2^5$.
Step 2LCM $= 2^5 \times 3^2 = 288$ minutes.
Answer288
Example 13 · Numerical answer

Find the largest number that divides 398, 436 and 542, leaving remainders 7, 11 and 15.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The number divides $398 - 7 = 391$, $436 - 11 = 425$ and $542 - 15 = 527$ exactly.
Step 2$391 = 17 \times 23$, $425 = 5^2 \times 17$ and $527 = 17 \times 31$, so the HCF is 17.
Answer17

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) HCF of 12 and 18(1) 36
(Q) LCM of 12 and 18(2) 1
(R) HCF of 17 and 23(3) 60
(S) LCM of 4, 6 and 10(4) 6

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–1, R–2, S–3
  2. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3
  3. P–4, Q–2, R–1, S–3
  4. P–3, Q–1, R–2, S–4

Solution.

Step 1$2^2 \times 3$ and $2 \times 3^2$ give HCF 6 and LCM 36; 17 and 23 share no factor; and $2^2 \times 3 \times 5 = 60$.
