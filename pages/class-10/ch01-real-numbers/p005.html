Example 4

Find the HCF and LCM of 6, 72 and 120, using the prime factorisation method.

We have $6 = 2 \times 3$, $72 = 2^3 \times 3^2$ and $120 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 5$. The common prime factors are 2 and 3, and their smallest powers in the three numbers are $2^1$ and $3^1$. The prime factors 2, 3 and 5 occur with greatest powers $2^3$, $3^2$ and $5^1$. So

$\text{HCF}(6, 72, 120) = 2^1 \times 3^1 = 2 \times 3 = 6$

$\text{LCM}(6, 72, 120) = 2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5^1 = 360$

Check that $6 \times 72 \times 120 = 51840$, while $\text{HCF}(6, 72, 120) \times \text{LCM}(6, 72, 120) = 6 \times 360 = 2160$. For three numbers, the product of the numbers is not equal to the product of their HCF and LCM.
