Example 7

Show that $3\sqrt{2}$ is irrational.

Solution.

Step 1 $3\sqrt{2} = \frac{a}{b}$, for coprime integers $a$ and $b$ with $b \neq 0$ assume it is rational
Step 2 $\sqrt{2} = \frac{a}{3b}$ rearranging
Step 3 $\frac{a}{3b}$ is rational, so $\sqrt{2}$ is rational 3, $a$ and $b$ are integers
Step 4 but $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational Theorem 1.3
Answer The assumption is false, so $3\sqrt{2}$ is irrational
