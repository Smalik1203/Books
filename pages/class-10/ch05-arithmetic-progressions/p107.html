Example 14

Raju repays a loan of ₹$3250$ in monthly instalments. He pays ₹$20$ in the first month, and each instalment is ₹$15$ more than the one before. In how many months does he repay the loan?

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 20$, $d = 15$ and $S_n = 3250$
Step 2 $3250 = \frac{n}{2}[40 + 15(n - 1)] = \frac{n}{2}(15n + 25)$ the sum formula
Step 3 $6500 = 15n^2 + 25n$, that is, $3n^2 + 5n - 1300 = 0$
Step 4 $(n - 20)(3n + 65) = 0$, so $n = 20$ $n$ is a positive integer
Check $S_{20} = 10 \times (40 + 19 \times 15) = 3250$
Answer In 20 months
Example 15

A clerk’s monthly salary is ₹$15000$ in the first year, and it rises by ₹$900$ every year. In which year does it become ₹$24900$?

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 15000$, $d = 900$ and $a_n = 24900$
Step 2 $24900 = 15000 + (n - 1) \times 900$ the $n$th term formula
Step 3 $(n - 1) \times 900 = 9900$, so $n - 1 = 11$ and $n = 12$
Answer In the 12th year
