Example 15 · Matching

Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $1 + 2 + \cdots + 100$(1) 2550
(Q) $2 + 4 + \cdots + 100$(2) 1050
(R) $1 + 3 + \cdots + 99$(3) 5050
(S) $5 + 10 + \cdots + 100$(4) 2500

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–2, Q–1, R–4, S–3
  2. P–1, Q–3, R–4, S–2
  3. P–3, Q–4, R–1, S–2
  4. P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{100}{2}(1 + 100) = 5050$ and $\frac{50}{2}(2 + 100) = 2550$.
Step 2$\frac{50}{2}(1 + 99) = 2500$ and $\frac{20}{2}(5 + 100) = 1050$.
Answer(d) P–3, Q–1, R–4, S–2
