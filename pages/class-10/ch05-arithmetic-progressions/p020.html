1. A small terrace at a football ground has 15 steps. Each step is 50 m long and is built of solid concrete. Each step has a rise of $\frac{1}{4}$ m and a tread of $\frac{1}{2}$ m (see Fig. 5.8). Find the total volume of concrete needed to build the terrace.

    Hint: the volume of concrete for the first step is $\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{1}{2} \times 50$ m³.

What this chapter showed
  1. An arithmetic progression (AP) is a list of numbers in which each term, except the first, is got by adding a fixed number $d$ to the term before it. The fixed number $d$ is called the common difference. The general form of an AP is $a, a + d, a + 2d, a + 3d, \ldots$
  2. A list of numbers $a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots$ is an AP if the differences $a_2 - a_1$, $a_3 - a_2$, $a_4 - a_3$, … are all equal, that is, if $a_{k+1} - a_k$ is the same for every $k$.
  3. In an AP with first term $a$ and common difference $d$, the $n$th term, or general term, is ${a_n = a + (n - 1)d}$.
  4. The sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP is $S = \dfrac{n}{2}[2a + (n - 1)d]$.
  5. If $l$ is the last term of a finite AP with $n$ terms, the sum of all its terms is $S = \dfrac{n}{2}(a + l)$.

One more result comes straight from the meaning of an AP.

If $a$, $b$ and $c$ are in AP, then $b - a = c - b$, so $b = \frac{a + c}{2}$. The middle number $b$ is called the arithmetic mean of $a$ and $c$.
