Let $a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots$ be an AP whose first term $a_1$ is $a$ and whose common difference is $d$. Then

the second term is $a_2 = a + d = a + (2 - 1)d$

the third term is $a_3 = a_2 + d = (a + d) + d = a + 2d = a + (3 - 1)d$

the fourth term is $a_4 = a_3 + d = (a + 2d) + d = a + 3d = a + (4 - 1)d$

Following the pattern, the $n$th term is ${a_n = a + (n - 1)d}$.

The nth term of an AP

The $n$th term $a_n$ of the AP with first term $a$ and common difference $d$ is given by

$$a_n = a + (n - 1)d$$

The $n$th term $a_n$ is also called the general term of the AP. If an AP has $m$ terms, then $a_m$ is its last term, which is sometimes written as $l$.

Let us look at some examples.

Example 3

Find the 10th term of the AP $2, 7, 12, \ldots$

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 2$, $d = 7 - 2 = 5$ and $n = 10$
Step 2 ${a_{10} = 2 + (10 - 1) \times 5 = 2 + 45 = 47}$ using ${a_n = a + (n - 1)d}$
Check the first ten terms are 2, 7, 12, 17, 22, 27, 32, 37, 42, 47
Answer The 10th term of the AP is 47
Example 4

Which term of the AP $21, 18, 15, \ldots$ is $-81$? Is any term of this AP equal to 0? Give a reason for your answer.

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 21$, $d = 18 - 21 = -3$ and $a_n = -81$ we have to find $n$
Step 2 $-81 = 21 + (n - 1)(-3) = 24 - 3n$ since ${a_n = a + (n - 1)d}$
Step 3 $-105 = -3n$, so $n = 35$
Step 4 $21 + (n - 1)(-3) = 0$ is there an $n$ with $a_n = 0$?
Step 5 $3(n - 1) = 21$, so $n - 1 = 7$ and $n = 8$
Answer The 35th term is $-81$. Yes: the 8th term of the AP is 0
