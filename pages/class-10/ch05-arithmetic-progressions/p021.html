The last exercise set is optional. Its questions are not set with examinations in mind, but they use the ideas of this chapter in less direct ways.

Exercise Set 5.4 (Optional)
  1. Which term of the AP $121, 117, 113, \ldots$ is its first negative term?

    Hint: find $n$ for which $a_n \lt 0$.

  1. The sum of the third and seventh terms of an AP is 6, and their product is 8. Find the sum of the first sixteen terms of the AP.
  1. A ladder has rungs 25 cm apart (see Fig. 5.7). The rungs get shorter by the same amount each time, from 45 cm at the bottom to 25 cm at the top. If the top and bottom rungs are $2\frac{1}{2}$ m apart, what length of wood is needed for the rungs?

    Hint: the number of rungs is $\frac{250}{25} + 1$.

  1. The houses in a row are numbered 1 to 49 in order. Show that there is a value of $x$ such that the sum of the numbers of the houses before house $x$ is equal to the sum of the numbers of the houses after it. Find this value of $x$.

    Hint: $S_{x-1} = S_{49} - S_x$.
