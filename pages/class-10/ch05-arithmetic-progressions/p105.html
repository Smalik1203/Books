Example 9 · Multiple correct

Three numbers in AP add to 24 and multiply to 440. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. The middle number is 8.
  2. The numbers are 5, 8 and 11.
  3. The common difference may be $-3$.
  4. The largest number is 12.

Solution.

Step 1Write them $a - d$, $a$, $a + d$: $3a = 24$, so $a = 8$, and $8(64 - d^2) = 440$ gives $d^2 = 9$.
Step 2$d = 3$ or $-3$, giving 5, 8, 11 in one order or the other; the largest is 11.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

Which statements about the two-digit numbers divisible by 7 are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. There are 13 of them.
  2. The smallest is 14.
  3. Their sum is 700.
  4. The largest is 99.

Solution.

Step 1They are $14, 21, \ldots, 98$: $\frac{98 - 14}{7} + 1 = 13$ terms.
Step 2Their sum is $\frac{13}{2}(14 + 98) = 728$, and 99 is not a multiple of 7.
Answer(a), (b)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

Find the sum of the first 40 positive integers divisible by 6.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1They are $6, 12, \ldots, 240$: an AP with $a = 6$, $d = 6$, $n = 40$.
Step 2$S_{40} = 20 \times (6 + 240) = 4920$.
