  1. A sum of ₹700 is to be given as seven cash prizes to students of a school for their overall performance in studies. Each prize is ₹20 less than the prize before it. Find the value of each prize.
  1. To reduce air pollution, the students of a school decide to plant trees in and around the school. Each section of each class will plant as many trees as the number of its class: a section of Class I will plant 1 tree, a section of Class II will plant 2 trees, and so on up to Class XII. Each class has three sections. How many trees will the students plant?
