Example 15

Find the sum of the first 24 terms of the list of numbers whose $n$th term is given by $a_n = 3 + 2n$.

Solution.

Step 1 $a_1 = 3 + 2 \times 1 = 5$,   $a_2 = 3 + 2 \times 2 = 7$,   $a_3 = 3 + 2 \times 3 = 9$ since $a_n = 3 + 2n$
Step 2 the list is $5, 7, 9, 11, \ldots$ and $7 - 5 = 9 - 7 = 11 - 9 = 2$, and so on an AP with common difference 2
Step 3 $n = 24$, $a = 5$ and $d = 2$
Step 4 $S_{24} = \dfrac{24}{2}[2 \times 5 + (24 - 1) \times 2] = 12(10 + 46) = 672$ the sum formula
Answer The sum of the first 24 terms of the list is 672
Example 16

A factory made 600 TV sets in its third year and 700 TV sets in its seventh year. Its production rises by the same number of sets every year. Find

(i) the production in the 1st year   (ii) the production in the 10th year   (iii) the total production in the first 7 years.

Solution.

Step 1 the numbers of TV sets made in the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, … years form an AP; let $a_n$ be the number made in the $n$th year a fixed rise every year
Step 2 $a_3 = 600$ and $a_7 = 700$, that is, $a + 2d = 600$ and $a + 6d = 700$
Step 3 (i) $d = 25$ and $a = 550$ solving these equations
Step 4 (ii) $a_{10} = a + 9d = 550 + 9 \times 25 = 775$
Step 5 (iii) $S_7 = \dfrac{7}{2}[2 \times 550 + (7 - 1) \times 25] = \dfrac{7}{2}(1100 + 150) = 4375$
Answer (i) 550 sets   (ii) 775 sets in the 10th year   (iii) 4375 sets in the first 7 years
