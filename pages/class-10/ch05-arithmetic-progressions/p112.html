Three things from this section are worth keeping. When three numbers are in AP, calling them $a - d$, $a$ and $a + d$ lets their sum give the middle one at once. A question about a sum may have two answers for $n$, or none, and $n$ must always be a positive whole number. And $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1}$ turns a formula for sums into a formula for terms.
