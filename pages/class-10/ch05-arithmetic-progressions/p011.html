Example 10

A flower bed has 23 rose plants in its first row, 21 in the second row, 19 in the third row, and so on. The last row has 5 rose plants. How many rows are there in the flower bed?

Solution.

Step 1 the numbers of rose plants in the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, … rows are $23, 21, 19, \ldots, 5$ these form an AP. (Why?)
Step 2 $a = 23$, ${d = 21 - 23 = -2}$ and $a_n = 5$, where $n$ is the number of rows
Step 3 $5 = 23 + (n - 1)(-2)$ since ${a_n = a + (n - 1)d}$
Step 4 $-18 = (n - 1)(-2)$, so $n - 1 = 9$ and $n = 10$
Answer There are 10 rows in the flower bed
