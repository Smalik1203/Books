Example 12

The sum of the first 14 terms of an AP is 1050, and its first term is 10. Find its 20th term.

Solution.

Step 1 $S_{14} = 1050$, $n = 14$ and $a = 10$
Step 2 $1050 = \dfrac{14}{2}[20 + 13d] = 140 + 91d$ using ${S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n - 1)d]}$
Step 3 $910 = 91d$, so $d = 10$
Step 4 $a_{20} = 10 + (20 - 1) \times 10 = 200$ the $n$th term
Answer The 20th term of the AP is 200
Example 13

How many terms of the AP $24, 21, 18, \ldots$ must be taken so that their sum is 78?

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 24$, $d = 21 - 24 = -3$ and $S_n = 78$ we need to find $n$
Step 2 $78 = \dfrac{n}{2}[48 + (n - 1)(-3)] = \dfrac{n}{2}[51 - 3n]$ using ${S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n - 1)d]}$
Step 3 $156 = 51n - 3n^2$, that is, $3n^2 - 51n + 156 = 0$
Step 4 $n^2 - 17n + 52 = 0$, that is, $(n - 4)(n - 13) = 0$
Step 5 $n = 4$ or $n = 13$ both values are possible
Answer The number of terms is either 4 or 13

Two answers make sense here. The sum of the first 4 terms and the sum of the first 13 terms are both 78, because the terms from the 5th to the 13th add up to 0. Since $a$ is positive and $d$ is negative, the later terms are negative, and they cancel the positive ones.

Example 14

Find the sum of (i) the first 1000 positive integers (ii) the first $n$ positive integers.

Solution.

Step 1 (i) $S = 1 + 2 + 3 + \cdots + 1000$
Step 2 $S_{1000} = \dfrac{1000}{2}(1 + 1000) = 500 \times 1001 = 500500$ using ${S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a + l)}$
Step 3 (ii) $S_n = 1 + 2 + 3 + \cdots + n$, with $a = 1$ and last term $l$ equal to $n$
Step 4 $S_n = \frac{n(1 + n)}{2}$ the same formula
Answer (i) The sum of the first 1000 positive integers is 500500   (ii) the sum of the first $n$ positive integers is $S_n = \dfrac{n(n + 1)}{2}$
