Example 6 · Single correct

The sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP is $n^2 + 2n$. What is its 5th term?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 35
  2. 11
  3. 9
  4. 13

Solution.

Step 1$a_5 = S_5 - S_4$.
Step 2$= (25 + 10) - (16 + 8) = 35 - 24 = 11$.
Answer(b) 11
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these are APs?

Select all correct options.

  1. $2, 4, 8, 16, \ldots$
  2. $-10, -6, -2, 2, \ldots$
  3. $\sqrt{3}, \sqrt{12}, \sqrt{27}, \sqrt{48}, \ldots$
  4. $1^2, 3^2, 5^2, 7^2, \ldots$

Solution.

Step 1The second has $d = 4$, and the third is $\sqrt{3}, 2\sqrt{3}, 3\sqrt{3}, 4\sqrt{3}$, with $d = \sqrt{3}$.
Step 2The first has differences $2, 4, 8$ and the last $8, 16, 24$: not constant.
Answer(b), (c)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

An AP has first term 5 and common difference 3. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $a_{10} = 32$
  2. $S_{10} = 185$
  3. 100 is one of its terms.
  4. 104 is one of its terms.

Solution.

Step 1$a_{10} = 5 + 27 = 32$ and $S_{10} = 5 \times (10 + 27) = 185$.
Step 2$5 + 3(n - 1) = 100$ gives $n = 32\tfrac{2}{3}$, not whole; $= 104$ gives $n = 34$.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
