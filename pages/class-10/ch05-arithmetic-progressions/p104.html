Problem 5

The first term of an AP is 4, and the sum of its first 10 terms is 175. Its 10th term is

  1. 35
  2. 34
  3. 17.5
  4. 31
Solution

$175 = \frac{10}{2}(4 + a_{10})$, so $4 + a_{10} = 35$ and $a_{10} = 31$

The answer is (d), and then $4 + 9d = 31$ gives $d = 3$. Option (a) is $a + a_{10}$, (b) is ${4 + 10 \times 3}$, using $n$ in place of $n - 1$, and (c) is the average of the ten terms. The AP is $4, 7, 10, \ldots, 31$, and $5 \times (4 + 31) = 175$ checks the sum.
