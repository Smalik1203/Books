Example 6

The sum of the 5th and 9th terms of an AP is 72, and the sum of its 7th and 12th terms is 97. Find the AP.

Solution.

Step 1 $(a + 4d) + (a + 8d) = 2a + 12d = 72$
Step 2 $(a + 6d) + (a + 11d) = 2a + 17d = 97$
Step 3 $5d = 25$, so $d = 5$ subtracting
Step 4 $2a = 72 - 60 = 12$, so $a = 6$
Answer The AP is $6, 11, 16, 21, \ldots$

Example 7

Find the 12th term from the end of the AP $3, 5, 7, \ldots, 201$.

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 3$, $d = 2$ and $l = 201$
Step 2 $201 = 3 + (n - 1) \times 2$, so $n - 1 = 99$ and $n = 100$ the number of terms
Step 3 the 12th term from the end is the 89th term counting back from the 100th
Step 4 $a_{89} = 3 + 88 \times 2 = 179$
Check counting back from 201 in steps of 2: $201 - 11 \times 2 = 179$
Answer 179
Example 8

How many numbers between 100 and 300 are divisible by 6?

Solution.

Step 1 the first is $102 = 6 \times 17$ and the last is $294 = 6 \times 49$
Step 2 $294 = 102 + (n - 1) \times 6$ an AP with $d = 6$
Step 3 $(n - 1) \times 6 = 192$, so $n - 1 = 32$ and $n = 33$
Answer 33 numbers
