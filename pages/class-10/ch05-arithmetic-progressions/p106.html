Example 13 · Numerical answer

Riya saves ₹50 in the first month and ₹20 more each month than the month before. In which month does she save ₹330?

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Her savings form an AP: $50 + 20(n - 1) = 330$.
Step 2$20(n - 1) = 280$, so $n = 15$.
Answer15
