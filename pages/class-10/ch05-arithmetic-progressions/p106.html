Type 6 · Terms from a formula for the sum

Example 12

The sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP is $5n^2 - 3n$. Find the AP and its 12th term.

Solution.

Step 1 $S_1 = 5 - 3 = 2$, so $a = 2$ the first sum is the first term
Step 2 $S_2 = 20 - 6 = 14$, so $a_2 = 14 - 2 = 12$ second sum minus first
Step 3 $d = 12 - 2 = 10$
Step 4 $a_{12} = 2 + 11 \times 10 = 112$
Check $S_{12} - S_{11} = 684 - 572 = 112$
Answer The AP is $2, 12, 22, \ldots$, and its 12th term is 112

Type 7 · Three numbers in AP

Example 13

For what value of $p$ are $2p + 1$, $13$ and $5p - 3$ three consecutive terms of an AP?

Solution.

Step 1 $13 - (2p + 1) = (5p - 3) - 13$ equal differences
Step 2 $12 - 2p = 5p - 16$
Step 3 $7p = 28$, so $p = 4$
Check the terms are 9, 13 and 17, which go up by 4
Answer $p = 4$
