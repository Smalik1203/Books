Problem 2

The $n$th term of an AP is $7 - 4n$. Its common difference is

  1. 4
  2. $-4$
  3. 3
  4. 7
Solution

Here $a_1 = 3$ and $a_2 = -1$, so $d = -4$. In fact $a_{n+1} - a_n = [7 - 4(n + 1)] - [7 - 4n] = -4$ for every $n$: (b). Option (a) subtracts the wrong way round, (c) is the first term, and (d) is the fixed number 7 in the formula.

Problem 3

How many terms of the AP $54, 51, 48, \ldots$ must be taken so that their sum is 513?

  1. 18 only
  2. 19 only
  3. 18 or 19
  4. 17 or 19
Solution

$513 = \frac{n}{2}[108 + (n - 1)(-3)] = \frac{n}{2}(111 - 3n)$

$3n^2 - 111n + 1026 = 0$, so $n^2 - 37n + 342 = (n - 18)(n - 19) = 0$

The 19th term is $54 + 18 \times (-3) = 0$, so the sums of 18 and of 19 terms are equal, and both answers work: (c). Options (a) and (b) keep one root each, and (d) comes from a wrong factorisation.

Problem 4

The sum of all the odd two-digit numbers is

  1. 2500
  2. 2475
  3. 4905
  4. 2420
Solution

the numbers are $11, 13, \ldots, 99$, and $99 = 11 + (n - 1) \times 2$ gives $n = 45$

$S = \frac{45}{2}(11 + 99) = 45 \times 55 = 2475$

The answer is (b). Option (a) also counts the odd one-digit numbers, (c) is the sum of all the two-digit numbers, and (d) takes 44 terms, forgetting to add 1.
