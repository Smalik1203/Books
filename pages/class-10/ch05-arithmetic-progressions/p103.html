Example 3 · Single correct

What is the sum of the first 20 terms of $1, 4, 7, \ldots$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 570
  2. 610
  3. 590
  4. 1180

Solution.

Step 1$S_n = \frac{n}{2}\left[2a + (n - 1)d\right]$ with $a = 1$, $d = 3$, $n = 20$.
Step 2$S_{20} = 10 \times (2 + 57) = 590$.
Answer(c) 590
Example 4 · Single correct

The $n$th term of an AP is $5 - 2n$. What is its common difference?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 5
  2. 2
  3. 3
  4. $-2$

Solution.

Step 1$a_{n+1} - a_n = \left(5 - 2(n + 1)\right) - (5 - 2n)$.
Step 2$= -2$ for every $n$.
Answer(d) $-2$
Example 5 · Single correct

What is the 11th term from the end of the AP $3, 8, 13, \ldots, 253$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 203
  2. 198
  3. 208
  4. 53

Solution.

Step 1Read backwards, the AP is $253, 248, 243, \ldots$ with $d = -5$.
Step 2Its 11th term is $253 + 10 \times (-5) = 203$.
Answer(a) 203
