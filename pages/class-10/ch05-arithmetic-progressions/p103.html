Type 2 · Finding a term, and which term

Example 3

The 25th term of the AP $-5, -\frac{5}{2}, 0, \frac{5}{2}, \ldots$ is

  1. 55
  2. 60
  3. 57.5
  4. 62.5

Solution.

Step 1 $a = -5$ and $d = -\frac{5}{2} - (-5) = \frac{5}{2}$
Step 2 $a_{25} = -5 + (25 - 1) \times \frac{5}{2} = -5 + 60 = 55$ the $n$th term formula
Answer (a)

Option (b) is $24 \times \frac{5}{2}$, leaving out the first term. Option (c) uses 25 in place of $n - 1 = 24$, and (d) is $25 \times \frac{5}{2}$, which makes both mistakes.

Example 4

Which term of the AP $5, 13, 21, \ldots$ is 181? Is 250 a term of this AP?

Solution.

Step 1 $a = 5$ and $d = 13 - 5 = 8$
Step 2 $181 = 5 + (n - 1) \times 8$, so $n - 1 = 22$ and $n = 23$
Step 3 $250 = 5 + (n - 1) \times 8$ gives $n - 1 = \frac{245}{8}$ not a whole number
Answer 181 is the 23rd term; 250 is not a term of the AP

Type 3 · Finding an AP from two conditions

Example 5

The 7th term of an AP is 37 and its 13th term is 67. Find the AP.

Solution.

Step 1 $a + 6d = 37$ and $a + 12d = 67$ the $n$th term formula
Step 2 $6d = 30$, so $d = 5$ subtracting
Step 3 $a = 37 - 6 \times 5 = 7$
Answer The AP is $7, 12, 17, 22, \ldots$
