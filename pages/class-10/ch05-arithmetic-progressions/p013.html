5.4Sum of First n Terms of an AP

Go back to Shakila from Section 5.1. She put ₹100 into her daughter’s money box when the daughter was one year old, ₹150 on her second birthday, ₹200 on her third birthday, and so on. How much money will be in the money box by the time her daughter is 21 years old?

The amounts, in rupees, put in on the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, … birthdays are $100, 150, 200, 250, \ldots$, up to the 21st birthday. To find the total, we could write down all 21 numbers and add them.
