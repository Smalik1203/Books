Example 2

Which of these pairs of figures are always similar?

  1. two rectangles
  2. two right triangles
  3. two regular hexagons
  4. two trapeziums

Solution.

Step 1 every angle of a regular hexagon is $120^\circ$ angles are equal
Step 2 all six sides of each hexagon are equal, so every pair of corresponding sides is in the same ratio sides are proportional
Answer (c)

A rectangle 2 cm by 3 cm and one 2 cm by 5 cm have equal angles but sides not in proportion, so (a) fails. Right triangles with angles $30^\circ$, $60^\circ$ and $45^\circ$, $45^\circ$ are not equiangular, and two trapeziums can have different angles.

Example 3

In $\triangle ABC$, $DE \parallel BC$ with D on AB and E on AC. If AD = 3 cm, DB = 5 cm and AC = 12 cm, then AE is

  1. 7.2 cm
  2. 20 cm
  3. 4.5 cm
  4. 7.5 cm

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{AD}{AB} = \dfrac{AE}{AC}$ Example 1 of the chapter
Step 2 AB = 3 + 5 = 8 cm
Step 3 AE = $\dfrac{3}{8} \times 12$ = 4.5 cm
Answer (c)

Option (d) is EC, and (a) takes AC as if it were EC. Option (b) turns the ratio $\frac{AD}{DB}$ upside down.
