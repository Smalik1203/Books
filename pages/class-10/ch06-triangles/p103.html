Example 3 · Single correct

The sides of two similar triangles are in the ratio $3 : 5$. What is the ratio of their perimeters?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $9 : 25$
  2. $5 : 3$
  3. $3 : 5$
  4. $27 : 125$

Solution.

Step 1Every side of the second is $\frac{5}{3}$ times the matching side of the first.
Step 2So the sum of the sides, the perimeter, is too: $3 : 5$.
Answer(c) $3 : 5$
Example 4 · Single correct

A girl 1.5 m tall casts a shadow 2 m long. At the same time a tower casts a shadow 40 m long. How tall is the tower?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 20 m
  2. 53.3 m
  3. 25 m
  4. 30 m

Solution.

Step 1The sun's rays make the same angle with the ground, so the two right triangles are similar (AA).
Step 2$\frac{h}{40} = \frac{1.5}{2}$, so $h = 30$ m.
Answer(d) 30 m
Example 5 · Single correct

In $\triangle ABC$, $D$ is on $AB$ and $E$ on $AC$ with $\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC} = \frac{2}{3}$. What follows?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $DE \parallel BC$
  2. $DE \perp BC$
  3. $DE = BC$
  4. $AD = AE$

Solution.

Step 1A line dividing two sides of a triangle in the same ratio is parallel to the third side.
Step 2That is the converse of the basic proportionality theorem, so $DE \parallel BC$.
Answer(a) $DE \parallel BC$
