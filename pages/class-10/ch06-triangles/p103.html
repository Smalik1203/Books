Problem 2

If ${\triangle ABC \sim \triangle QRP}$, $\angle A = 50^\circ$ and $\angle C = 70^\circ$, then $\angle R$ is

  1. $70^\circ$
  2. $60^\circ$
  3. $50^\circ$
  4. $120^\circ$
Solution

The order of the letters gives the correspondence: $A \leftrightarrow Q$, $B \leftrightarrow R$, $C \leftrightarrow P$. So $\angle R = \angle B = 180^\circ - 50^\circ - 70^\circ = 60^\circ$: (b). Option (a) matches R with C because R comes after Q, and (c) is $\angle A$. Option (d) is $\angle A + \angle C$, not $\angle B$. As a check, $50^\circ + 60^\circ + 70^\circ = 180^\circ$. The same order also pairs the sides: AB with QR, BC with RP, and CA with PQ.

Problem 3

A triangle has sides 4 cm, 6 cm and 8 cm. The sides of a triangle similar to it could be

  1. 6, 8, 10 cm
  2. 8, 10, 12 cm
  3. 5, 7, 9 cm
  4. 6, 9, 12 cm
Solution

For similar triangles the sides must be in the same ratio. In (d), $\frac{6}{4} = \frac{9}{6} = \frac{12}{8} = \frac{3}{2}$, so the answer is (d) by SSS. Options (a), (b) and (c) add the same number, 2, 4 or 1, to each side, which keeps the differences but not the ratios. For example, in (b) the longest side is $\frac{12}{8}$ of the old one, but the shortest is $\frac{8}{4}$ of it.

Problem 4

A man 1.8 m tall casts a shadow 2.4 m long. At the same time, a building casts a shadow 20 m long. The height of the building is

  1. 26.7 m
  2. 15 m
  3. 19.4 m
  4. 36 m
Solution

The sun’s rays make the same angle with the ground for both, and both stand upright, so the two triangles are similar (AA). So $\frac{h}{20} = \frac{1.8}{2.4}$, and $h = 15$ m: (b). Option (a) uses $\frac{2.4}{1.8}$ instead, (c) takes away the difference of 0.6 m, and (d) is $1.8 \times 20$. The sun is so far away that its rays reaching the man and the building can be taken as parallel.
