Type 6 · The SSS, SAS and RHS criteria

Example 9

A triangle has sides 4 cm, 6 cm and 8 cm. The sides of a triangle similar to it could be

  1. 6, 8, 10 cm
  2. 8, 10, 12 cm
  3. 5, 7, 9 cm
  4. 6, 9, 12 cm

Solution.

Step 1 in (d), $\dfrac{6}{4} = \dfrac{9}{6} = \dfrac{12}{8} = \dfrac{3}{2}$ shortest to shortest
Step 2 in (b), $\dfrac{8}{4} = 2$ but $\dfrac{12}{8} = \dfrac{3}{2}$ ratios differ
Answer (d), by the SSS criterion

Options (a), (b) and (c) add the same number, 2, 4 or 1, to each side. That keeps the differences between the sides, but not their ratios.

Example 10

In $\triangle PQR$, PQ = 4 cm, PR = 6 cm and $\angle P = 55^\circ$. In $\triangle XYZ$, XY = 6 cm, XZ = 9 cm, YZ = 7.5 cm, $\angle X = 55^\circ$ and $\angle Y = 70^\circ$. Find $\angle Q$, $\angle R$ and QR.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{PQ}{XY} = \dfrac{4}{6} = \dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{PR}{XZ} = \dfrac{6}{9} = \dfrac{2}{3}$
Step 2 $\angle P = \angle X$, between the proportional sides, so ${\triangle PQR \sim \triangle XYZ}$ SAS similarity
Step 3 $\angle Q = \angle Y = 70^\circ$ and $\angle R = 180^\circ - 55^\circ - 70^\circ = 55^\circ$ corresponding angles
Step 4 QR = $\dfrac{2}{3} \times 7.5$ = 5 cm sides in the ratio 2 : 3
Answer $\angle Q = 70^\circ$, $\angle R = 55^\circ$, QR = 5 cm
Example 11

P and Q are points on the sides AB and AC of $\triangle ABC$ such that $AP \cdot AB = AQ \cdot AC$. Prove that ${\triangle APQ \sim \triangle ACB}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $AP \cdot AB = AQ \cdot AC$, so $\dfrac{AP}{AC} = \dfrac{AQ}{AB}$ given; dividing by $AC \cdot AB$
Step 2 $\angle PAQ = \angle CAB$ the same angle
Step 3 the angle A lies between AP and AQ, and between AC and AB included angle
Answer ${\triangle APQ \sim \triangle ACB}$, by the SAS similarity criterion

The order matters: P goes with C and Q with B, because AP is compared with AC.
