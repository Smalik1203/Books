Example 12 · Numerical answer

A vertical pole 6 m tall casts a shadow 4 m long, and at the same time a tower casts a shadow 28 m long. Find the height of the tower, in m.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1The triangles formed by the objects, their shadows and the rays are similar.
Step 2$\frac{h}{28} = \frac{6}{4}$, so $h = 42$ m.
Answer42
Example 13 · Numerical answer

$\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$. Their perimeters are 30 cm and 45 cm, and $AB = 8$ cm. Find $DE$, in cm.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1Perimeters are in the ratio of the sides, $\frac{45}{30} = \frac{3}{2}$, so $DE = \frac{3}{2} \times 8 = 12$ cm.
Answer12
