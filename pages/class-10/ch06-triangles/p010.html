Example 3

In Fig. 6.16, ${\frac{PS}{SQ} = \frac{PT}{TR}}$ and $\angle PST = \angle PRQ$. Prove that PQR is an isosceles triangle.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{PS}{SQ} = \dfrac{PT}{TR}$, so $ST \parallel QR$ given; Theorem 6.2
Step 2 $\angle PST = \angle PQR$   (1) corresponding angles
Step 3 $\angle PST = \angle PRQ$   (2) given
Step 4 $\angle PRQ = \angle PQR$ from (1) and (2)
Step 5 $PQ = PR$ sides opposite equal angles
Answer PQR is an isosceles triangle
