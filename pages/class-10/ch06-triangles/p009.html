Example 2

ABCD is a trapezium with $AB \parallel DC$. E and F are points on the non-parallel sides AD and BC such that EF is parallel to AB (Fig. 6.14). Show that ${\frac{AE}{ED} = \frac{BF}{FC}}$.

Solution.

Step 1 join AC, and let it meet EF at G (Fig. 6.15)
Step 2 $AB \parallel DC$ and $EF \parallel AB$, so $EF \parallel DC$ given; both parallel to AB
Step 3 in $\triangle ADC$, $EG \parallel DC$, so $\dfrac{AE}{ED} = \dfrac{AG}{GC}$   (1) Theorem 6.1
Step 4 in $\triangle CAB$, $GF \parallel AB$, so $\dfrac{CG}{GA} = \dfrac{CF}{FB}$ Theorem 6.1
Step 5 that is, $\dfrac{AG}{GC} = \dfrac{BF}{FC}$   (2) both fractions upside down
Answer from (1) and (2), $\frac{AE}{ED}$ and $\frac{BF}{FC}$ are both equal to $\frac{AG}{GC}$, so ${\frac{AE}{ED} = \frac{BF}{FC}}$
