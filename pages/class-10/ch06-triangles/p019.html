Example 6

In Fig. 6.31, $OA \cdot OB = OC \cdot OD$. Show that $\angle A = \angle C$ and $\angle B = \angle D$.

Solution.

Step 1 $OA \cdot OB = OC \cdot OD$, so $\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OD}{OB}$   (1) given
Step 2 $\angle AOD = \angle COB$   (2); by (1) and (2), ${\triangle AOD \sim \triangle COB}$ vertically opposite; SAS
Answer $\angle A = \angle C$ and $\angle D = \angle B$, as corresponding angles of similar triangles
