6.4Criteria for Similarity of Triangles

In the previous section we said that two triangles are similar if (i) their corresponding angles are equal and (ii) their corresponding sides are in the same ratio. That is, in $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle DEF$, if

(i) $\angle A = \angle D$, $\angle B = \angle E$, $\angle C = \angle F$ and

(ii) $\dfrac{AB}{DE} = \dfrac{BC}{EF} = \dfrac{CA}{FD}$

then the two triangles are similar (Fig. 6.22).
