There is one more criterion, for right triangles.

If the hypotenuse and one side of a right triangle are proportional to the hypotenuse and one side of another right triangle, then the two triangles are similar. This is called the RHS similarity criterion.

Let us now look at some examples that use these criteria.

Example 4

In Fig. 6.29, $PQ \parallel RS$. Prove that ${\triangle POQ \sim \triangle SOR}$.

$PQ \parallel RS$   (given)

so $\angle P = \angle S$ and $\angle Q = \angle R$   (alternate angles)

also $\angle POQ = \angle SOR$   (vertically opposite angles)

so ${\triangle POQ \sim \triangle SOR}$   (AAA similarity criterion)
