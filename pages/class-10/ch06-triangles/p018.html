Example 5

Look at Fig. 6.30 and find $\angle P$.

Solution.

Step 1 in $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle PQR$, $\dfrac{AB}{RQ} = \dfrac{3.8}{7.6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$
Step 2 $\dfrac{BC}{QP} = \dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}$   and   $\dfrac{CA}{PR} = \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{6\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{1}{2}$
Step 3 $\dfrac{AB}{RQ} = \dfrac{BC}{QP} = \dfrac{CA}{PR}$, so ${\triangle ABC \sim \triangle RQP}$ SSS similarity
Step 4 $\angle C = 180^\circ - \angle A - \angle B = 180^\circ - 80^\circ - 60^\circ = 40^\circ$ angle sum property
Answer $\angle P = \angle C = 40^\circ$, as corresponding angles of similar triangles
Example 6

In Fig. 6.31, $OA \cdot OB = OC \cdot OD$. Show that $\angle A = \angle C$ and $\angle B = \angle D$.

Solution.

Step 1 $OA \cdot OB = OC \cdot OD$, so $\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OD}{OB}$   (1) given
Step 2 $\angle AOD = \angle COB$   (2); by (1) and (2), ${\triangle AOD \sim \triangle COB}$ vertically opposite; SAS
Answer $\angle A = \angle C$ and $\angle D = \angle B$, as corresponding angles of similar triangles
