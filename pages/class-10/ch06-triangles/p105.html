Type 4 · Reading a similarity statement

Example 7

If ${\triangle ABC \sim \triangle QRP}$, $\angle A = 50^\circ$ and $\angle C = 70^\circ$, then $\angle R$ is

  1. $70^\circ$
  2. $60^\circ$
  3. $50^\circ$
  4. $120^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1 $A \leftrightarrow Q$, $B \leftrightarrow R$, $C \leftrightarrow P$ the order of the letters
Step 2 $\angle B = 180^\circ - 50^\circ - 70^\circ = 60^\circ$ angle sum property
Step 3 $\angle R = \angle B = 60^\circ$ corresponding angles
Answer (b)

Option (a) matches R with C because R comes after Q, (c) is $\angle A$, and (d) is $\angle A + \angle C$. The same order pairs the sides: AB with QR, BC with RP, and CA with PQ.

Type 5 · The AA criterion

Example 8

In $\triangle ABC$, D is on AB and E is on AC, with $DE \parallel BC$. Prove that ${\triangle ADE \sim \triangle ABC}$, and so that $\dfrac{AD}{AB} = \dfrac{DE}{BC}$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle ADE = \angle ABC$ corresponding angles, $DE \parallel BC$
Step 2 $\angle DAE = \angle BAC$ the same angle
Step 3 ${\triangle ADE \sim \triangle ABC}$ AA similarity
Answer corresponding sides are in the same ratio, so $\dfrac{AD}{AB} = \dfrac{DE}{BC}$

Note that the ratio is $\frac{AD}{AB}$, not $\frac{AD}{DB}$: the sides DE and BC belong to $\triangle ADE$ and $\triangle ABC$.
