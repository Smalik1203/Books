Example 9 · Multiple correct

In $\triangle ABC$, $DE \parallel BC$, with $AD = 4$ cm, $DB = 6$ cm and $BC = 15$ cm. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $\frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{2}{5}$
  2. $DE = 6$ cm
  3. $\frac{AE}{EC} = \frac{3}{2}$
  4. $\frac{DE}{BC} = \frac{AD}{AB}$

Solution.

Step 1$\triangle ADE \sim \triangle ABC$ (AA), so $\frac{DE}{BC} = \frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{4}{10} = \frac{2}{5}$ and $DE = 6$ cm.
Step 2$\frac{AE}{EC} = \frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{2}{3}$, not $\frac{3}{2}$.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 10 · Multiple correct

$\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$ with $AB : DE = 2 : 3$. Which statements are correct?

Select all correct options.

  1. $BC : EF = 2 : 3$
  2. $\angle C = \angle F$
  3. Their perimeters are in the ratio $2 : 3$.
  4. $AB = 2$ cm and $DE = 3$ cm

Solution.

Step 1Corresponding sides share the ratio $2 : 3$, corresponding angles are equal, and so the perimeters are in $2 : 3$ too.
Step 2The ratio fixes no length: $AB$ could be 4 cm and $DE$ 6 cm.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
Example 11 · Numerical answer

In $\triangle ABC$, $DE \parallel BC$ with $AD = 3.6$ cm, $DB = 5.4$ cm and $AE = 2.4$ cm. Find $EC$, in cm.

Enter the numerical value.

Solution.

Step 1$\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}$ gives $\frac{3.6}{5.4} = \frac{2.4}{EC}$.
Step 2$EC = \frac{2.4 \times 5.4}{3.6} = 3.6$ cm.
Answer3.6
