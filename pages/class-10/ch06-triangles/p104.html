Problem 5

ABCD is a trapezium with $AB \parallel DC$, AB = 12 cm and DC = 8 cm. Its diagonals meet at O, and AC = 15 cm. Then OC is

  1. 9 cm
  2. 10 cm
  3. 7.5 cm
  4. 6 cm
Solution

$\angle OAB = \angle OCD$ and $\angle OBA = \angle ODC$ (alternate angles), so ${\triangle AOB \sim \triangle COD}$

$\frac{AO}{OC} = \frac{AB}{CD} = \frac{12}{8}$, so OC = $\frac{8}{20} \times 15$ = 6 cm

The answer is (d). Option (a) is AO, (c) is half of AC, and (b) uses $\frac{8}{12}$ in place of $\frac{8}{20}$.
