Example 6 · Single correct

$\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR$, $\angle A = 50^\circ$ and $\angle Q = 70^\circ$. What is $\angle R$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. $50^\circ$
  2. $60^\circ$
  3. $70^\circ$
  4. $80^\circ$

Solution.

Step 1Corresponding angles are equal: $\angle P = \angle A = 50^\circ$.
Step 2$\angle R = 180^\circ - 50^\circ - 70^\circ = 60^\circ$.
Answer(b) $60^\circ$
Example 7 · Multiple correct

Which of these pairs are always similar?

Select all correct options.

  1. two equilateral triangles
  2. two squares
  3. two rectangles
  4. two circles

Solution.

Step 1Equilateral triangles, squares and circles each have one shape; only their size varies.
Step 2Rectangles 1 by 1 and 1 by 5 have equal angles but sides out of proportion.
Answer(a), (b), (d)
Example 8 · Multiple correct

Which of these facts prove $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$?

Select all correct options.

  1. $\angle A = \angle D$ and $\angle B = \angle E$
  2. $\frac{AB}{DE} = \frac{BC}{EF} = \frac{CA}{FD}$
  3. $\frac{AB}{DE} = \frac{AC}{DF}$ and $\angle A = \angle D$
  4. $\frac{AB}{DE} = \frac{BC}{EF}$ and $\angle A = \angle D$

Solution.

Step 1These are AA, SSS and SAS: in (c) the equal angles lie between the proportional sides.
Step 2In (d) $\angle A$ is not between $AB$ and $BC$, so it proves nothing.
Answer(a), (b), (c)
