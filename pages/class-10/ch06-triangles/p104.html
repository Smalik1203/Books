Example 4

In $\triangle ABC$, $DE \parallel BC$ with D on AB and E on AC. If AD = 3 cm, AE = 2.5 cm and EC = 4 cm, find AB.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$, so $\dfrac{3}{DB} = \dfrac{2.5}{4}$ Theorem 6.1
Step 2 $DB = \dfrac{3 \times 4}{2.5} = 4.8$ cross-multiplying
Step 3 AB = AD + DB = 3 + 4.8
Answer AB = 7.8 cm

Type 3 · The converse: showing lines are parallel

Example 5

In $\triangle XYZ$, L is on XY and M is on XZ, with XL = 3 cm, LY = 4.5 cm, XM = 4 cm and MZ = 6 cm. Is LM parallel to YZ?

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{XL}{LY} = \dfrac{3}{4.5} = \dfrac{2}{3}$
Step 2 $\dfrac{XM}{MZ} = \dfrac{4}{6} = \dfrac{2}{3}$
Step 3 LM divides XY and XZ in the same ratio Theorem 6.2
Answer yes, $LM \parallel YZ$
Example 6

D and E are points on the sides AB and AC of $\triangle ABC$ such that $\dfrac{AD}{AB} = \dfrac{AE}{AC}$. Prove that $DE \parallel BC$.

Solution.

Step 1 $\dfrac{AB}{AD} = \dfrac{AC}{AE}$ both fractions upside down
Step 2 $\dfrac{AB}{AD} - 1 = \dfrac{AC}{AE} - 1$ taking 1 from both sides
Step 3 $\dfrac{AB - AD}{AD} = \dfrac{AC - AE}{AE}$, that is, $\dfrac{DB}{AD} = \dfrac{EC}{AE}$ $AB - AD = DB$
Step 4 $\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}$ both fractions upside down
Answer DE divides AB and AC in the same ratio, so $DE \parallel BC$ by Theorem 6.2
