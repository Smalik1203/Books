1. The sides AB and BC and the median AD of a triangle ABC are proportional to the sides PQ and QR and the median PM of $\triangle PQR$ (Fig. 6.41). Show that ${\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR}$.
  1. D is a point on the side BC of a triangle ABC such that $\angle ADC = \angle BAC$. Draw a figure first, marking the two equal angles and the point D on the side BC. Show that $CA^2 = CB \cdot CD$.
  1. The sides AB and AC and the median AD of a triangle ABC are proportional to the sides PQ and PR and the median PM of another triangle PQR. Draw both triangles with their medians, and mark the equal ratios on your figure before you begin. Show that ${\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR}$.
  1. A vertical pole 6 m long casts a shadow 4 m long on the ground, and at the same time a tower casts a shadow 28 m long. Find the height of the tower. Draw a rough figure first.
  1. AD and PM are medians of the triangles ABC and PQR, and ${\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR}$. Draw both triangles, mark the mid-points D and M, and join them to A and P. Prove that ${\frac{AB}{PQ} = \frac{AD}{PM}}$.
