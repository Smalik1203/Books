Example 7

A girl 90 cm tall is walking away from the base of a lamp-post at a speed of 1.2 m/s. The lamp is 3.6 m above the ground. Take the ground to be level and the lamp-post to be upright, and assume that she walks in a straight line directly away from the foot of the lamp-post. Find the length of her shadow after 4 seconds, in metres, and show each step of your working.

Solution.

Step 1 AB is the lamp-post, CD the girl after 4 seconds, and DE her shadow (Fig. 6.32); let DE be $x$ metres
Step 2 in 4 seconds she walks 1.2 m × 4 = 4.8 m, so BD = 4.8 m distance = speed × time
Step 3 in $\triangle ABE$ and $\triangle CDE$, $\angle B = \angle D$, each is $90^\circ$ both stand upright
Step 4 $\angle E = \angle E$ the same angle
Step 5 ${\triangle ABE \sim \triangle CDE}$ AA similarity criterion
Step 6 $\dfrac{BE}{DE} = \dfrac{AB}{CD}$, that is, $\dfrac{4.8 + x}{x} = \dfrac{3.6}{0.9}$ 90 cm = 0.9 m
Step 7 $4.8 + x = 4x$, so $3x = 4.8$ and $x = 1.6$
Answer after walking for 4 seconds, the girl’s shadow is 1.6 m long
