Type 8 · Two lines crossing between parallels

Example 15

The segments AD and BC cross at O, and $AB \parallel CD$. If AB = 12 cm, CD = 8 cm and BC = 15 cm, then OC is

  1. 9 cm
  2. 10 cm
  3. 7.5 cm
  4. 6 cm

Solution.

Step 1 ${\triangle AOB \sim \triangle DOC}$ as in Example 4 of the chapter
Step 2 $\dfrac{OB}{OC} = \dfrac{AB}{DC} = \dfrac{12}{8}$ corresponding sides
Step 3 BC is split in the ratio 12 : 8, so OC = $\dfrac{8}{20} \times 15$ = 6 cm
Answer (d)

Option (a) is OB, (c) is half of BC, and (b) uses $\frac{8}{12}$ in place of $\frac{8}{20}$.
