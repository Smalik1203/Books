Example 15 · Matching

$\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$, with $AB = 4$ cm, $BC = 6$ cm, $CA = 8$ cm and $DE = 6$ cm. Match List I with List II. Each entry has exactly one match.

List IList II
(P) $EF$(1) 12 cm
(Q) $FD$(2) 27 cm
(R) The perimeter of $\triangle DEF$(3) $55^\circ$
(S) $\angle E$, if $\angle B = 55^\circ$(4) 9 cm

Choose the correct combination.

  1. P–4, Q–2, R–1, S–3
  2. P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3
  3. P–4, Q–1, R–2, S–3
  4. P–3, Q–1, R–2, S–4

Solution.

Step 1The scale is $\frac{6}{4} = \frac{3}{2}$: $EF = 9$, $FD = 12$, perimeter 27, and $\angle E = \angle B$.
Answer(c) P–4, Q–1, R–2, S–3
