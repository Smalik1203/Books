Example 12

In $\triangle ABC$, $\angle B = 90^\circ$, AC = 10 cm, AB = 6 cm and BC = 8 cm. In $\triangle PQR$, $\angle Q = 90^\circ$, PR = 15 cm and PQ = 9 cm. Show that ${\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR}$, and find QR.

Solution.

Step 1 $\angle B = \angle Q = 90^\circ$ both right triangles
Step 2 $\dfrac{AC}{PR} = \dfrac{10}{15} = \dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{AB}{PQ} = \dfrac{6}{9} = \dfrac{2}{3}$ hypotenuse, then a side
Step 3 ${\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR}$ RHS similarity
Step 4 $\dfrac{BC}{QR} = \dfrac{2}{3}$, so QR = $\dfrac{3}{2} \times 8$ = 12 cm corresponding sides
Answer the triangles are similar, and QR = 12 cm

Example 13

A man 1.8 m tall casts a shadow 2.4 m long. At the same time, a building casts a shadow 20 m long. The height of the building is

  1. 26.7 m
  2. 15 m
  3. 19.4 m
  4. 36 m

Solution.

Step 1 both stand upright, and the sun’s rays meet the ground at one angle AA similarity
Step 2 $\dfrac{h}{20} = \dfrac{1.8}{2.4}$ height to shadow
Step 3 $h = 15$
Answer (b)

Option (a) uses $\frac{2.4}{1.8}$ instead, (c) takes away the difference of 0.6 m, and (d) is $1.8 \times 20$.

Example 14

A lamp is fixed 5 m above level ground on an upright post. A man 1.6 m tall stands upright some distance from the post, and his shadow is 2.4 m long. How far is he from the foot of the post?

Solution.

Step 1 let him be $d$ m from the post; the shadow’s tip is $d + 2.4$ m from the post
Step 2 the post and the man make similar right triangles with the shadow’s tip AA: right angles, common angle
Step 3 $\dfrac{5}{d + 2.4} = \dfrac{1.6}{2.4}$, so $d + 2.4 = 7.5$ height to base
Answer $d = 5.1$, so he is 5.1 m from the post
