In a right-angled isosceles triangle, the two equal angles add up to $90^\circ$, so each is $45^\circ$. So every such triangle has angles $45^\circ$, $45^\circ$ and $90^\circ$, and any two of them are similar by the AA criterion.

On level ground, an upright stick 1.2 m tall casts a shadow 1.6 m long. At the same moment, the shadow of an upright flagpole is 10 m longer than the flagpole itself. How tall is the flagpole?

The sun’s rays make the same angle with the ground at the tip of each shadow, and the stick and the flagpole both stand at right angles to the ground. So the triangle made by the stick and its shadow and the triangle made by the flagpole and its shadow are similar by the AA criterion, and height divided by shadow is the same for both: $\frac{1.2}{1.6} = \frac{3}{4}$. If the flagpole is $h$ m tall, its shadow is $h + 10$ m, so $\frac{h}{h + 10} = \frac{3}{4}$. Then $4h = 3h + 30$, and $h = 30$. The flagpole is 30 m tall, with a shadow 40 m long. Neither was measured: one stick and one difference were enough.

Example 1 · Single correct

In $\triangle ABC$, $DE \parallel BC$ with $D$ on $AB$ and $E$ on $AC$. If $AD = 2$ cm, $DB = 3$ cm and $AE = 4$ cm, what is $EC$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 6 cm
  2. 4 cm
  3. 3 cm
  4. 8 cm

Solution.

Step 1By the basic proportionality theorem, $\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}$.
Step 2$\frac{2}{3} = \frac{4}{EC}$, so $EC = 6$ cm.
Answer(a) 6 cm
Example 2 · Single correct

$\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$, with $AB = 6$ cm, $DE = 9$ cm and $BC = 8$ cm. What is $EF$?

Choose one correct option.

  1. 10 cm
  2. 12 cm
  3. 16 cm
  4. 6 cm

Solution.

Step 1Corresponding sides are in one ratio: $\frac{DE}{AB} = \frac{9}{6} = \frac{3}{2}$.
Step 2$EF = \frac{3}{2} \times 8 = 12$ cm.
Answer(b) 12 cm
