No chord is longer. A chord that misses the centre still has both ends at distance r from it, so going from one end to the other by way of the centre measures 2r — and going straight across is shorter. Nothing here was measured: the argument used the definition, and that a straight path is the shortest one.

5.3Symmetries of a Circle

A circle looks the same from every direction, and that is not a vague compliment — it is a statement with consequences, and it follows from the definition in a line. There are two kinds of sameness to separate.

Watch a wheel go past. At one moment some point of the rim is touching the road; a moment later some point of the rim is touching the road again. Is it the same point? Nothing you can see decides it. Chalk a mark on the rim and you can follow it, as in Fig. 5.2 — but rub the mark out and the two pictures become one picture.
