The longer chord sits 7 cm out and the shorter 24 cm, a difference of 17 cm — the ordering Theorem 8 promised before any arithmetic began.

5.8Angles Subtended by an Arc

Everything so far has been about chords. But a chord comes with arcs attached — two of them — and an arc subtends angles of its own.

An arc is a connected piece of a circle, fixed by its two end points. Two points A and B cut a circle into two arcs, and unless AB is a diameter one is shorter than the other: the shorter is the minor arc, the longer the major arc. Naming a third point says which you mean — in Fig. 5.10, arc AXB is the minor one and arc AYB the major.
