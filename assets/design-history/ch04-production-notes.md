## Reference checks

- Magnetic and geographic north differ: [NOAA magnetic declination](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/geomag/declination.shtml).
- Austenitic stainless steels need not be strongly magnetic: [British Stainless Steel Association](https://bssa.org.uk/bssa_articles/austenitic-stainless-steels/).
- Ring magnets can have different magnetisation directions: [K&J Magnetics](https://www.kjmagnetics.com/magdir.asp).

in this pass. The passage avoids a precise date and distinguishes the working
