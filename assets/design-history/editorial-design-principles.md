# Editorial principles applied to Chapter 2

Research reviewed September 2026:
- Adobe, magazine layout: https://www.adobe.com/learn/indesign/web/design-magazine-layout — common margins and modular guides support varied compositions.
- Nielsen Norman Group, visual hierarchy: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-hierarchy-ux-definition/ — scale, contrast and grouping guide attention. This is general visual-design guidance, not a school-textbook learning trial.
- Cambridge Handbook of Multimedia Learning, chapter 12: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-handbook-of-multimedia-learning/principles-for-reducing-extraneous-processing-in-multimedia-learning-coherence-signaling-redundancy-spatial-contiguity-and-temporal-contiguity-principles/CD5B7AE1279A9AB81F8EEBB53DBEC86E — coherence, signaling and spatial contiguity support removing extraneous material and placing related words and pictures together.

Editorial application (our judgment, not numerical rules from those sources):
- Remove automatic 510-unit image growth. Fix each picture's size according to what the pupil must see.
- Keep anatomical seed section legible; reduce exercise seed pictures, goat comparison and sacred grove.
- Use scene, comparison, detail and reference compositions with a shared grid, rather than equally large pictures throughout.
- No enlarged art, inflated paragraph gaps or decorative prose to pass a page-fill threshold.
- Judge each spread's focal point, caption proximity, reading order and relative scale at printed size.
- Chapter 2 now uses comparison columns on plant forms, climbers, leaf veins, roots and camel pages; the conservation page pairs the grove with its explanation. This pilot does not change the other chapters.
- Narrow comparison columns use a natural right edge at the full body size; full-width prose retains measured justification.
- Fixed-page compositions use absolute sheet coordinates so table framing and artwork checks share the same geometry.
- Final validation: 27 pages, 1,001 rendered lines; no overflow, artwork overlap or uneven leading. Diversity lesson checks and 10 science contract regressions pass.
