# V1 furniture blend and continuous pagination

User request: bring V1 header/footer styling into V2 and correct large white areas.

Cause: the V2 layout preserved almost every manuscript-page boundary. Removing unnecessary headings shortened the content without removing those imposed page breaks. The ordinary builder measures the outer full-sheet SVG, so its reported 100% fill did not reveal the unused reading area.

Change: retain V1's leaf ribbon, chapter label and mirrored outer folio tabs, drawn with V2-scoped colours and type. Preserve the aligned V2 chapter opener. Refit the main lesson from measured blocks across former page boundaries. Keep all illustrations at their authored sizes, all prose at the existing scale, panels whole, and glossary/summary on dedicated pages.

The page map now records occupied body height, actual fill, source provenance and the next block behind each page break. Some lower margin remains when the next complete investigation or illustrated comparison cannot fit; it is not converted into larger pictures, stretched paragraph gaps or filler copy.
