# V2 book furniture correction

The user rejected the amber dashes above headings, broad green section bars, the invented “Science Field Journal” name and the detached opener number. Those treatments are removed from the compositor and stylesheet, not merely covered in the exported PDF.

The replacement uses book conventions: actual book/chapter running heads, outer folios mirrored across facing pages, fine rules within the text measure, open section typography, and an aligned chapter numeral/title group. The opener has no running head. Existing body text, illustrations, type sizes and learning-panel meanings are preserved.

Open comparison summaries replace the nonessential rounded cards. The previously requested investigation and thinking panels remain recognisable learning components.

## Research applied

- [Fiona Raven and Glenna Collett, Book Design Made Simple: running heads](https://www.bookdesignmadesimple.com/book/book-running-heads/) — choose actual book and chapter information that helps readers navigate nonfiction; left/right arrangements are conventions, not universal laws.
- [Joel Friedlander, chapter and part openers](https://www.thebookdesigner.com/book-design-chapter-openers-part-openers/) — compose chapter numbers, display typography and content-related imagery as a coordinated opening page.
- [Joel Friedlander, book construction guide](https://www.thebookdesigner.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/03/Book-Construction.pdf) — running heads orient readers and are normally omitted on chapter display pages.
- [Matthew Butterick, headings](https://practicaltypography.com/headings.html) — establish hierarchy with restrained, consistent typography and spacing; a heading does not require a coloured container.

These sources support the editorial principles. The precise geometry, green ink, aligned two-line title and mirrored footer are our application to this science textbook. They do not establish that these are the only valid choices for books or magazines.
