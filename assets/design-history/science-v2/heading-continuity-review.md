# Chapter 2 V2 heading continuity

The repeated page titles made each leaf read as a separate slide. This revision changes heading decisions in the V2 compositor; V1 pages remain unchanged.

| Treatment | Headings |
|---|---|
| Remove manufactured page titles | Read the field records; A record is evidence; Compare the evidence; Continue the investigation; Put your ideas to work |
| Remove breaks within a continuing investigation or explanation | A Grouping Needs a Clear Rule; Herbs, Shrubs and Trees; Two Patterns; Two Kinds of Root; The Two Go Together; What Movement Tells Us; Built for the Place |
| Remove redundant local label | One tree, several relationships |
| Make the investigation heading neutral | A Pattern Appears becomes Comparing Leaves and Roots; the heading no longer announces that a pattern must occur |
| Move to the actual topic start | What Is Inside a Seed precedes the seed investigation, within the ongoing page; the anatomy page continues directly |
| Retain at smaller subtopic size | Plant and animal grouping, plant support, leaves, roots, comparison, seed anatomy, camel comparison, adaptations, habitat and conservation |
| Retain large section size | Sections 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3; Keywords; Summary; assessment opening; Learning Further |

Continuation pages start at the normal body-text position. No empty title slot, enlarged image, inflated paragraph gaps or filler copy compensates for a removed heading. Investigation panels and associated recording tables stay together where they fit. The V2 regression check rejects the retired headings and verifies the retained hierarchy and seed-topic sequence.
